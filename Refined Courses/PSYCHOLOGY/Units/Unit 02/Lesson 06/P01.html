P01 Lesson Overview

Perception, Attention, and Interpretation

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PSY.BIO.2: Analyze sensation, perception, sleep, consciousness, stress, health, and biological regulation using evidence and models.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how perception, attention, and interpretation helps explain behavior and mental processes using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded perception process diagram, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped psychology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches sensation, perception, and regulation.
