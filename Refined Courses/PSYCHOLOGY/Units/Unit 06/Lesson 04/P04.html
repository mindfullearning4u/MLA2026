Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the behavior or mental process, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without diagnosing or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the psychology evidence to explain the behavior within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a personal opinion, diagnosis, or label without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the behavior or process, read the evidence, apply the correct concept, and state a careful conclusion.
