P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1

Prompt: A social situation shows how a person, group, message, or culture affects behavior.

Step 1: Separate person and situation. This identifies the psychology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Identify internal and external factors. This ties the answer to the embedded model, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Social psychology avoids one-cause answers. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PSY.SOC.2.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A social situation shows how a person, group, message, or culture affects behavior.

Step 1: Use the social evidence. This identifies the psychology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Read the group, norm, or message. This ties the answer to the embedded model, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Context changes behavior. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PSY.SOC.2.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A social situation shows how a person, group, message, or culture affects behavior.

Step 1: Choose responsible action. This identifies the psychology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Apply communication or bias-reduction evidence. This ties the answer to the embedded model, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Psychology can guide better choices. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PSY.SOC.2.

Embedded Psychology Support: communication conflict-resolution organizer

Step or EvidenceWhat the Student Should Notice

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the behavior or mental process, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without diagnosing or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the psychology evidence to explain the behavior within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a personal opinion, diagnosis, or label without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the behavior or process, read the evidence, apply the correct concept, and state a careful conclusion.
