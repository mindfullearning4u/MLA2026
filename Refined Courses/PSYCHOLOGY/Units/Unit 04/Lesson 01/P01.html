P01 Lesson Overview

Developmental Research and Lifespan Thinking

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PSY.DEV.1: Analyze human development across the lifespan, including prenatal, physical, cognitive, social, emotional, and moral development.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how developmental research and lifespan thinking helps explain behavior and mental processes using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded lifespan development timeline, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped psychology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches research design and ethical evidence.

Embedded Psychology Support: lifespan development timeline

Step or EvidenceWhat the Student Should Notice
QuestionWhat is being studied.
VariableWhat is measured or changed.
DesignSurvey, observation, correlation, or experiment.
EthicsConsent, privacy, protection from harm, and debriefing.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the behavior or mental process, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without diagnosing or guessing.
