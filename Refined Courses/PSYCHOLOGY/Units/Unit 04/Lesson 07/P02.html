Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Name the behavior, mental process, research feature, or social context in the prompt.
  2. Identify the exact psychology concept involved: need, motive, emotion, trait, regulation.
  3. Read the embedded personality perspective comparison chart before choosing or writing an answer.
  4. Connect one piece of evidence to MLA.PSY.DEV.2 and stay inside the mapped lesson standard.
  5. Explain carefully: describe evidence, avoid labels or unsupported diagnosis, and state the psychological reasoning.
