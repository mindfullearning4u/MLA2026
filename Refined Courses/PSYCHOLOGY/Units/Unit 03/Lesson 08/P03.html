P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A review case asks students to combine MLA.PSY.COG.1, MLA.PSY.COG.2 without adding unmapped content.

  1. Underline the behavior, mental process, research feature, or social context.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: MLA.PSY.COG.1, MLA.PSY.COG.2.
  3. Use the embedded learning cognition application matrix to identify the evidence.
  4. Match the evidence to MLA.PSY.COG.1, MLA.PSY.COG.2.
  5. Write one sentence explaining the psychology reasoning without overclaiming.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the behavior or mental process, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without diagnosing or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the psychology evidence to explain the behavior within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a personal opinion, diagnosis, or label without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

The strongest answer starts with evidence, identifies the correct psychology concept, respects the limits of the evidence, and avoids diagnosing, labeling, or assuming information not provided.
