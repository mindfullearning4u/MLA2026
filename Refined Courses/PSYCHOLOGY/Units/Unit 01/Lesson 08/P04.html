Worked Example 1

Prompt: A review case asks students to combine MLA.PSY.FND.1, MLA.PSY.FND.2 without adding unmapped content.

Step 1: Identify each standard. This identifies the psychology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Name the exact mapped standard being used. This ties the answer to the embedded model, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Synthesis requires standard-by-standard evidence. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PSY.FND.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A review case asks students to combine MLA.PSY.FND.1, MLA.PSY.FND.2 without adding unmapped content.

Step 1: Explain ethically. This identifies the psychology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Avoid diagnosis, labeling, or unsupported assumptions. This ties the answer to the embedded model, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Psychology explanations must be careful and evidence-based. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PSY.FND.1.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the behavior or mental process, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without diagnosing or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the psychology evidence to explain the behavior within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a personal opinion, diagnosis, or label without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the behavior or process, read the evidence, apply the correct concept, and state a careful conclusion.
