Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson is focused on Data, Models, Graphs, and Medical Evidence. The standard is MLA.ANP.LAB.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: medical models, graphs, tables, and evidence limits.

A strong answer does more than name a term. It explains what is visible in the required model, how that structure or process works, and why that evidence supports the claim.

  1. read the title and units before interpreting data
  2. identify the trend or structure shown
  3. connect the visible evidence to the claim
  4. state what the data cannot prove

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: treating any medical-looking image or graph as proof

Correct Thinking

Correct: models and graphs support claims only when the evidence directly matches the question
