Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson is focused on Human Cells and Cell Regulation. The standard is MLA.ANP.TIS.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: cell cycle regulation and uncontrolled cell growth.

A strong answer does more than name a term. It explains what is visible in the required model, how that structure or process works, and why that evidence supports the claim.

  1. trace interphase, mitosis, and cytokinesis
  2. identify where regulation checks DNA or cell readiness
  3. explain how mutation can affect regulation
  4. connect uncontrolled division to tissue-level consequences

Common Confusion to Avoid

Correct Thinking

Correct: regulated mitosis is normal repair and growth; uncontrolled regulation is the problem
