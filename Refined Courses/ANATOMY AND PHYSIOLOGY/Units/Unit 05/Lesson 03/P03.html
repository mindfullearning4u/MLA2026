Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson is focused on Blood Flow and Cardiovascular Factors. The standard is MLA.ANP.CRI.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: blood flow, pressure, and resistance.

A strong answer does more than name a term. It explains what is visible in the required model, how that structure or process works, and why that evidence supports the claim.

  1. identify pressure difference, vessel diameter, and resistance
  2. read the graph or data table
  3. connect vessel changes to flow changes
  4. stay within model evidence instead of diagnosing

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: thinking blood pressure and blood flow mean the same thing

Correct Thinking

Correct: pressure helps drive flow, but resistance and vessel diameter also matter
