Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe model analysis, anatomy diagrams, physiology data, case evidence, or public-source reasoning. Do not perform dissections, specimen handling, body testing, exercise trials, medical procedures, or physical investigations unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives explicit safety clearance.
