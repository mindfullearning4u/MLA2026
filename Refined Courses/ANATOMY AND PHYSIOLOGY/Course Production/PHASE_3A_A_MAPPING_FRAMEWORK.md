# Phase 3A-A: Anatomy and Physiology Mapping Framework

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the official mapping rules for Anatomy and Physiology. It preserves the approved MLA science structure:

1. Six units.
2. Eight lessons per unit.
3. Lessons 1-7 contain Lesson Quiz evidence.
4. Lesson 8 is always titled `Putting It All Together`.
5. Lesson 8 contains Unit Assessment evidence.
6. Lesson 8 introduces no new primary standard.
7. Lab and virtual lab expectations must be visible across all six units.

This is architecture only. No lessons, assessments, HTML, JSON, GIFT, or LMS pages are created.

## Required Lab, Visual, Simulation, and Resource Matrix

The mapping framework must be used with:

- `PHASE_3A_B_3_LAB_VISUAL_SIMULATION_MAPPING.md`

That companion matrix is required before lesson development, assessment development, Moodle XML production, or Moodle transfer. It controls lesson-level anatomy labs, case-data investigations, diagrams, histology images, physiological graphs, medical case stimuli, body-system models, simulation review, safety controls, direct resource links, step-by-step student resource directions, and assessment stimulus requirements.

## Mapping Philosophy

| Mapping Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Primary standard | The main standard taught and assessed for mastery in a lesson. |
| Support standard | A standard used to support reading, data, model use, communication, lab reasoning, or prerequisite understanding. |
| Spiral standard | A standard that recurs across units for reinforcement, especially inquiry, lab/data, literacy, quantitative reasoning, and scientific communication. |
| Lesson 8 | Synthesis only. Lesson 8 may assess standards taught earlier in the unit but may not introduce a new primary standard. |
| Lab/data evidence | Every unit must include at least one identifiable lab-style, virtual lab, case-data, model, or physiological data investigation. |
| Lab, visual, simulation, and resource matrix | Verify `PHASE_3A_B_3_LAB_VISUAL_SIMULATION_MAPPING.md` is used before lesson, assessment, or Moodle transfer work. |

## Lesson Load Rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Most lessons | One primary standard. |
| Natural pairings | Two closely related primary standards may be paired when one standard is anatomical structure and the other is immediate physiological function. |
| Support standards | Support standards should reinforce lab/data, literacy, quantitative reasoning, or prerequisite body-system understanding without becoming hidden primary standards. |
| Workload protection | Do not overload a lesson with multiple body systems unless the lesson purpose is explicit integration. |
| Lab protection | Lab/data skills must appear as direct primary instruction in Unit 1 and support every later unit through models, simulations, case studies, and physiological data. |

## Lab and Virtual Lab Mapping Rules

Every unit must visibly include:

- one or more lab-style investigation opportunities
- model or diagram interpretation
- physiological data, case data, or graph analysis
- evidence-based scientific communication
- safety/procedure awareness where appropriate
- reflection on uncertainty, error, or model limitations where appropriate

Virtual labs may include anatomical models, histology images, body-system simulations, physiological data sets, diagnostic data, and graph-based investigations.

## Workload Protection Rules

| Risk | Protection |
|---|---|
| Too many systems in one lesson | Keep lessons organized by structure/function cluster. |
| Anatomy memorization without physiology | Require structure/function reasoning and evidence where possible. |
| Physiology without anatomical grounding | Teach structure before function when required. |
| Lab designation not visible | Maintain lab/data emphasis in unit and lesson mapping. |
| Health course drift | Use health benchmarks only as medical/physiological support. |
| General biology drift | Use cellular, biochemical, and disease concepts only to explain human physiology. |
