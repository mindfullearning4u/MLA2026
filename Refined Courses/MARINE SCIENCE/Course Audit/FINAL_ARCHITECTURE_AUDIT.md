## 4. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Drift into Biology | Marine biodiversity and adaptation remain tied to marine organisms, habitats, and ecosystem evidence. |
| Drift into Environmental Science | Human impacts remain tied to oceans, fisheries, coastal systems, marine monitoring, and conservation. |
| Drift into Earth/Space Science | Geology and climate support remain tied to ocean basins, coasts, currents, and ocean-atmosphere interactions. |
| Drift into Chemistry | Chemistry remains tied to salinity, density, pH, nutrients, dissolved gases, and water-quality indicators. |
| Unit 2 workload density | Lesson mapping separates ocean basins, zones, currents, climate links, chemistry, and geology across seven lessons. |
| Unit 5 evidence complexity | Fisheries, conservation, monitoring, policy, and sustainability are sequenced before synthesis. |
