Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Marine Process Data Synthesis. The standard is MLA.MAR.ECO.03; MLA.MAR.ECO.04; MLA.MAR.ECO.05, so the work must stay inside this lesson: Synthesize population, food web, productivity, and cycle data..

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the marine science question being asked.
  2. Read the assigned map, graph, model, data table, source, case, diagram, or technology evidence.
  3. Name the evidence pattern or relationship.
  4. Explain the ocean process, organism relationship, system connection, impact, or technology limit.
  5. State one limitation so the conclusion does not go beyond the evidence.
