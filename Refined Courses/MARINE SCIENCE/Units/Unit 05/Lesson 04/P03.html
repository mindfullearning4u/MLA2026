Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Marine Conservation and Restoration. The standard is MLA.MAR.IMP.03, so the work must stay inside this lesson: human impacts, fisheries, conservation, monitoring, policy, and sustainability decisions.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the impact or decision.
  2. Read the evidence source or data display.
  3. Compare benefits, risks, and tradeoffs.
  4. Justify the decision with marine evidence.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: choosing the solution that sounds best without weighing marine evidence and tradeoffs

Correct Thinking

Correct: marine decisions require evidence about impact, risk, benefits, tradeoffs, monitoring, and long-term sustainability
