Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Ocean Maps, Models, and Data Displays. The standard is MLA.MAR.LAB.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: ocean maps, bathymetry, sonar evidence, scale, and seafloor features.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Read the map title, scale, and legend.
  2. Identify the seafloor or ocean feature.
  3. Use depth, location, or pattern evidence.
  4. Explain what the map can and cannot prove.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: reading a marine map as a picture instead of using scale, labels, depth, and evidence

Correct Thinking

Correct: marine maps and bathymetry models must be read with scale, labels, depth values, and feature evidence
