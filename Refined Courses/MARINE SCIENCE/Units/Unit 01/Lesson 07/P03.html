Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Marine Investigation Design. The standard is MLA.MAR.SCI.01; MLA.MAR.LAB.03, so the work must stay inside this lesson: safe marine investigation planning and valid evidence collection.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the investigation question.
  2. Name the variable, comparison, or control.
  3. Check the safety and data-quality concern.
  4. Explain what evidence would support the claim.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: thinking a marine investigation is valid just because data were collected

Correct Thinking

Correct: a valid marine investigation needs safe procedures, clear variables, reliable data, and evidence that matches the question
