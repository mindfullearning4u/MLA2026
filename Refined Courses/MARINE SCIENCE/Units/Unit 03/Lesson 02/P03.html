Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Marine Ecosystem Components and Niches. The standard is MLA.MAR.ECO.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: marine habitats, organisms, biodiversity, adaptation, and ecosystem change.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the habitat or organism.
  2. Name the abiotic and biotic conditions.
  3. Connect adaptation, niche, or biodiversity evidence to survival.
  4. Explain how change could affect the system.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: naming an organism or habitat without explaining the evidence that supports survival, distribution, or change

Correct Thinking

Correct: marine life explanations must connect organism traits, habitat conditions, and evidence from the model or data
