Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Nutrients, Dissolved Gases, pH, and Water Quality. The standard is MLA.MAR.CHEM.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: physical and chemical ocean conditions shown by models, graphs, and data.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the physical or chemical variable.
  2. Read the data value, pattern, or model relationship.
  3. Connect the pattern to water movement, density, zones, chemistry, or climate.
  4. State the effect on ocean systems.
