Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Marine Science Careers and Technical Communication. The standard is MLA.MAR.TECH.03, so the work must stay inside this lesson: marine exploration technology, remote evidence, tool limits, and technical communication.

A strong Marine Science answer does not guess. It reads the marine evidence, names the pattern, explains the ocean-system relationship, and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the tool, data source, or communication task.
  2. Name what evidence it provides.
  3. Name one tool or data limitation.
  4. Explain how the evidence supports the marine conclusion.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: assuming technology gives complete evidence without limits

Correct Thinking

Correct: marine technology extends observation but every tool has limits, resolution, sampling boundaries, or interpretation needs
