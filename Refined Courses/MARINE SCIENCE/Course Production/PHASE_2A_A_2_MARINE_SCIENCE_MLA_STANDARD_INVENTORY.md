# Marine Science Phase 2A-A-2: MLA Standard Inventory

## Source Control

Official source basis: Florida CPALMS Marine Science course record, course number `2002500`, Florida Next Generation Sunshine State Standards for Science, with embedded ELA, ELD, and Mathematical Thinking support expectations used in MLA science production architecture.

Course identity control: this inventory is for Marine Science only. Biology, Environmental Science, Earth/Space Science, and Chemistry concepts are included only where they directly support ocean systems, marine ecosystems, marine chemistry, coastal systems, fisheries, conservation, exploration, and marine technology.

The MLA Marine Science inventory consolidates official Florida/CPALMS benchmark expectations into 30 teachable MLA Marine Science standards. Florida benchmarks remain traceable in the crosswalk and mapping documents.
