# World History Visual QA Audit

Updated: 2026-08-10

## Required production standard

- A map, photograph, artwork, artifact, diagram, timeline, chart, or table must be present wherever a student must inspect it to complete a lesson task or answer an assessment question.
- Visuals are instructional evidence, not decoration. Each must have readable color contrast, labels, directions for use, and meaningful historical content.
- Every externally sourced visual must record its title, creator, date, repository, source URL or identifier, and rights status.
- Every image must include useful alternative text. Color may organize information but may not be the only way information is communicated.
- An assessment question may not refer students to a map, image, table, chart, timeline, diagram, or passage that is not contained in that question.
- Public-domain and properly licensed sources are preferred. Quotations and historical images may not be fabricated or materially altered.

## Assessment review priority

1. Confirm that every question whose answer depends on a visual embeds that visual inside its Moodle XML question text.
2. Confirm that the embedded visual is historically accurate, readable at student scale, and color-supported.
3. Confirm that the prompt tells the student what feature to inspect without revealing the answer.
4. Confirm that all four answer choices can be evaluated using the included stimulus and lesson knowledge.
5. Preview the imported question in Moodle; XML presence alone is not sufficient.
