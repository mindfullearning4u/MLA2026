Guided Cause-and-Effect Chain

Background condition: Medieval stories preserved accounts of westward Norse voyages.

Immediate cause: Investigators used the stories and geographic clues to search the coast.

Event: Complete a sentence describing what investigators found beginning in 1960.

Effect: Complete a sentence explaining how the discovery changed the accepted historical conclusion.

Mechanism: The stories influenced where investigators looked; the archaeological evidence—not the stories alone—supported the conclusion.
