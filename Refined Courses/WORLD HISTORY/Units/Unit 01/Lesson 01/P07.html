Objective Success Criteria

Your response must:

  • Directly answer the investigation question.
  • Distinguish evidence from unsupported opinion.
  • Use accurate and relevant evidence.
  • Explain how the evidence supports the conclusion.
  • Identify a limitation or remaining uncertainty.
  • Use at least four assigned vocabulary terms accurately.
  • Reflect your own understanding and independent reasoning.
