Unit 1 Synthesis: Evidence, Maps, and Medieval Turning Points

Historical Thinking

Investigation Question

Which developments most significantly transformed the transition from the Roman world to medieval and early modern societies?

How to Rank Turning Points

  1. Scale: How broad was the change?
  2. Duration: How long did effects continue?
  3. People affected: How many communities or groups?
  4. Degree of change: How deeply did institutions or daily life change?
  5. Evidence quality: How strong and varied is the support?

Worked Model

A turning point cannot rank first merely because it is dramatic. A strong ranking applies all five criteria, uses evidence from multiple lessons, and explains why the top development exceeds the others.
