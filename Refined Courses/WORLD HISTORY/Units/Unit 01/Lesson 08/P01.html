Unit 1 Synthesis: Evidence, Maps, and Medieval Turning Points

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Which developments most significantly transformed the transition from the Roman world to medieval and early modern societies?

Unit Synthesis Purpose

This lesson introduces no new standards. You will connect inquiry, geography, Byzantine history, medieval institutions, law, crisis, and turning points across Lessons 1–7.

Learning Objectives

  • Compare evidence from at least three lessons.
  • Apply scale, duration, people affected, degree of change, and evidence quality.
  • Rank historical turning points using consistent criteria.
  • Construct a cross-unit argument using maps, sources, timelines, and data.
