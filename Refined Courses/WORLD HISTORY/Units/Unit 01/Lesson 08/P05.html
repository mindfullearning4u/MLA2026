Unit 1 Synthesis: Evidence, Maps, and Medieval Turning Points

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question

Which developments most significantly transformed the transition from the Roman world to medieval and early modern societies?

Guided Investigation

Given comparison: Magna Carta and the fall of Constantinople.

  1. Identify each development’s political effect.
  2. Compare scale and duration.
  3. Use a dated detail for each.
  4. State one continuity and one change.
  5. Explain why different criteria could change the ranking.

Required Notebook Summary

Write four sentences: one comparison claim, one evidence detail for each development, and one sentence explaining which criterion most affects the ranking.
