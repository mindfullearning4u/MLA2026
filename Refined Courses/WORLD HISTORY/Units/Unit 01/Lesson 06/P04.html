Medieval Monarchies, Law, and National Identity

Historical Thinking

Investigation Question

How did medieval rulers strengthen monarchies while law began to limit royal power?

How to Apply Comparison

  1. Establish the question and context. Name the conditions surrounding the development.
  2. Use shared categories. Compare evidence using the same criteria or trace a complete causal relationship.
  3. Separate evidence from explanation. Evidence states what the lesson shows; explanation states why it supports the conclusion.
  4. Qualify the claim. Avoid “always,” “everywhere,” or one-cause explanations when evidence shows variation.

Worked Example

Claim: One important development must be understood in its historical context.

Evidence: The timeline, map, and written source show conditions before and after the development.

Explanation: The development was a human response to specific political, geographic, social, or economic conditions; it did not appear identically everywhere.
