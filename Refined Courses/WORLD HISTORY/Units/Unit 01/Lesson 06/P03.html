Medieval Monarchies, Law, and National Identity

Historical Evidence Lab

Investigation Question

How did medieval rulers strengthen monarchies while law began to limit royal power?

Evidence Set C: Written Sources

Magna Carta states, in medieval legal language, that certain free men should not be imprisoned or dispossessed except through lawful judgment or the law of the land. This is an instructional paraphrase of Clause 39, not a quotation. A parliamentary record shows monarchs seeking consent for taxation; a chronicle perspective presents warfare and loyalty through the author’s political viewpoint.

Source method: Identify creator and context, extract a useful detail, and state one limitation. Never treat a single source as the whole story.
