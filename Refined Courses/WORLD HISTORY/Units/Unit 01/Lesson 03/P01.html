Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Lesson Overview

After the western Roman government collapsed, the Eastern Roman Empire continued from Constantinople. Its people preserved Roman government and law while adapting those traditions to a Greek-speaking, Eastern Orthodox, commercially connected civilization. You will learn to distinguish continuity, adaptation, and change.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain why the Eastern Roman Empire continued after the West declined.
  • Identify Roman political and legal continuities.
  • Identify Byzantine religious, linguistic, artistic, and geographic changes.
  • Classify features as continuity, adaptation, or distinctive change.
  • Support a continuity-and-change claim with evidence from maps, sources, timelines, and architecture.
