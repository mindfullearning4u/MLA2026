Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Independent Investigation

FeatureEvidence supplied
Imperial governmentAn emperor and centralized bureaucracy governed the state.
Justinian CodeRoman laws were selected and reorganized for current use.
Greek languageGreek became dominant in administration and culture.
Eastern Orthodox ChristianityEastern worship and patriarchal leadership developed distinctive forms.
Hagia SophiaRoman engineering supported a distinct Byzantine Christian space.
Constantinople tradeThe capital linked European, Asian, Black Sea, and Mediterranean routes.

Your Required Work

Create a three-column organizer labeled Feature | Classification | Evidence-Based Justification.

  1. Classify all six features as Roman continuity, Byzantine adaptation, or distinctive Byzantine change.
  2. Write one complete justification sentence for every feature.
  3. Each justification must cite the supplied evidence and explain why it fits the category.
  4. Use at least six assigned vocabulary terms across the organizer.

A defensible classification may differ for a complex feature such as Hagia Sophia, but your evidence and reasoning must support your choice.

Before You Continue

Check that you completed all six rows. A label without a justification is incomplete. This organizer is required Historian’s Notebook evidence and prepares you for the Mastery Checkpoint.
