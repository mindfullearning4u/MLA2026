Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Copy the four organizer headings.
  2. Add all six required feature rows.
  3. Choose one classification for each feature.
  4. Write one accurate historical fact in the Evidence column.
  5. Write a separate complete sentence in the Explanation column that tells why the evidence proves the classification.
  6. Use and mark at least six different vocabulary terms across your explanations.
  7. Review all six rows before continuing.

A complex feature such as Hagia Sophia may support more than one defensible classification. Your evidence and explanation must make your choice clear.
