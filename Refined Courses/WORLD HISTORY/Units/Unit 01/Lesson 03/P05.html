Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Guided Investigation: The Justinian Code

Context: Centuries of Roman laws had accumulated before Justinian’s reign. Justinian appointed legal experts to select, organize, and clarify that material for use in his empire.

Given claim: The Justinian Code was both a continuity of Roman civilization and an adaptation to Byzantine needs.

  1. Identify continuity: The laws, legal concepts, and imperial authority came from Rome.
  2. Identify adaptation: Justinian’s jurists reorganized and clarified the law for a sixth-century eastern empire.
  3. Use the excerpt: The statement about justice shows that Byzantine law preserved a Roman idea about legal obligation.
  4. Connect context: The western empire had ended, yet an eastern emperor continued to rule through Roman legal traditions.
  5. Form the reasoning: Preserving older legal principles is continuity; reorganizing them for new circumstances is adaptation.
