Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Mastery Checkpoint Task

Write 200–250 words answering: Explain how the Byzantine Empire both preserved and transformed Roman civilization.

Submit one polished response in the separate U1 L3 Mastery Checkpoint Submission activity.

Exactly What You Will Submit

Your final product is a four-paragraph historical explanation. Use this structure:

ParagraphWhat it must containModel sentence frame
1. IntroductionHistorical context and a claim that answers the question.After the western Roman Empire fell, the Byzantine Empire continued in the east. It preserved Roman traditions through ___ and ___, while developing distinct features such as ___ and ___.
2. Roman continuityTwo examples of continuity, supporting evidence, and an explanation of why each example shows continuity.One important continuity was ___. Evidence shows ___. This preserved Roman civilization because ___.
3. Byzantine changeTwo changes or adaptations, supporting evidence, and an explanation of why each made Byzantium distinct.However, Byzantium also transformed Roman civilization by ___. The evidence ___ demonstrates this change because ___.
4. ConclusionRestate the claim in new words and explain the historical significance.Therefore, the Byzantine Empire was both Roman and distinctively Byzantine because ___.

Important: The sentence frames show the required organization. Replace every blank with your own evidence and reasoning; do not submit the frames with blanks.

Evidence and Success Requirements

  • 200–250 words in four developed paragraphs.
  • At least two examples of continuity.
  • At least two examples of change or adaptation.
  • At least one map, legal, religious, architectural, or artistic detail.
  • Evidence from at least two source types.
  • At least five assigned vocabulary terms used accurately and marked so the TOR can locate them.
  • Use Continuity and Change, Comparison, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Checkpoint Checklist

  • □ My final product is one polished, four-paragraph response.

  • □ I used and marked at least five assigned vocabulary terms.
