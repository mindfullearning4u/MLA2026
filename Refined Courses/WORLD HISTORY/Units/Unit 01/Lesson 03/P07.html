Byzantine Empire: Continuity and Change

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

In what ways did the Byzantine Empire preserve Roman traditions while creating a distinct civilization?

Mastery Checkpoint Task

Write 200–250 words answering: Explain how the Byzantine Empire both preserved and transformed Roman civilization.

Submit your response in the separate U1 L3 Mastery Checkpoint Submission activity.

Organize Your Writing

  1. Introduction and claim: Establish the division of Rome and state how Byzantium combined continuity with transformation.
  2. Continuity paragraph: Explain at least two Roman continuities, such as imperial government or Roman law.
  3. Change/adaptation paragraph: Explain at least two changes or adaptations, such as Greek language, Eastern Orthodoxy, Hagia Sophia, or Constantinople’s eastern trade setting.
  4. Conclusion: Return to the claim and explain why Byzantium was both Roman and distinctively Byzantine.

Use transitions such as continued, preserved, adapted, transformed, in contrast, and therefore.

Evidence and Success Requirements

  • 200–250 words.
  • At least two examples of continuity.
  • At least two examples of change or adaptation.
  • At least one map, legal, religious, architectural, or artistic detail.
  • Evidence from at least two source types.
  • At least five assigned vocabulary terms used accurately.
  • Clear Continuity and Change, Comparison, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Checkpoint Checklist

  • □ I answered the investigation question with a clear claim.
  • □ I established historical context.
  • □ I distinguished continuity from change or adaptation.
  • □ I included the required evidence and explained why it matters.
  • □ I organized my response into an introduction, developed body paragraphs, and a conclusion.
  • □ I stayed within 200–250 words and proofread my work.
  • □ I submitted it to the U1 L3 Mastery Checkpoint Submission activity.

Mastery and Progression

You must earn at least 80% mastery on the checkpoint before the Lesson 3 Quiz opens. If you earn below 80%, meet with your Teacher of Record for feedback and remediation, then complete the authorized resubmission.
