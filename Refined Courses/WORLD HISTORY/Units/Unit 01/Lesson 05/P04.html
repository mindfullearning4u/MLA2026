Worked Examples

Western Europe After Rome

Example 1: Build a Cause-and-Effect Explanation

  1. Question: What caused the change?
  2. Evidence: Identify a relevant event, policy, belief, technology, or geographic factor.
  3. Reasoning: Explain how the evidence produced an effect.
  4. Mastery answer: The change occurred because the evidence created pressure, opportunity, or conflict that affected people's choices.

Example 2: Compare Two Societies or Systems

  1. Name both societies or systems.
  2. Use the same categories for both sides: government, economy, social structure, religion, geography, or culture.
  3. Explain one similarity and one difference.
  4. Finish with why the comparison matters historically.

Example 3: Interpret a Source or Visual

  1. Identify what the source or visual shows.
  2. Ask who created it or what data it uses.
  3. Find the claim it supports.
  4. Explain the limit: what the source does not prove by itself.
Common mistake: Treating one fact as the whole explanation.
Correction: A strong World History answer uses context, evidence, and reasoning. Explain what happened, why it happened, and why it mattered.
