Western Europe After Rome

Historical Evidence Lab

Investigation Question

How did Western European societies reorganize after the collapse of Roman authority?

Evidence Set A: Timeline

DateDevelopment
476Western imperial government ends.
c. 500–800Germanic kingdoms develop across former Roman territories.
800Charlemagne is crowned emperor.
843Treaty of Verdun divides Charlemagne’s empire.
800s–900sViking, Magyar, and Muslim raids intensify local security pressures.
900s–1100sFeudal and manorial relationships become influential in many regions.

Evidence Set B: Instructional Map

What to notice: Identify location, movement, connections, and geographic limits. This schematic teaches relationships and is not a boundary-precise political map.

Evidence Set C: Written Sources

A feudal oath records reciprocal promises of loyalty and protection; a manor record lists rents, labor services, fields, and common resources; a monastic source shows religious communities preserving books and providing education or care. These source summaries are instructional paraphrases, not quotations.

Source method: Identify creator and context, extract a useful detail, and state one limitation. Never treat a single source as the whole story.

Required Historian’s Notebook Evidence

  1. Copy every timeline entry.
  2. Record three direct map observations.
  3. Create a source table: Source | Context | Useful evidence | Limitation.
  4. Write a 3–4 sentence summary explaining the most important historical relationship shown by the evidence.
