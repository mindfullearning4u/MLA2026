Geography, Regions, and Historical Change

Historical Evidence Lab

Investigation Question

How does geography influence where societies develop and how they change?

Evidence Lab A: Physical Map

Read the map again on this page. Record three direct observations. Each must name a labeled feature and describe its location or relationship to another feature. Example structure: ‘The Alps lie north of the Italian Peninsula.’ Do not explain effects yet.

Evidence Lab B: Trade Network

Record three direct observations from the trade map. Name the locations connected and the route pattern shown. Keep observation separate from inference.

Notebook Summary: How to Read Geographic Evidence

  1. Read the map title and period.
  2. Use the legend to identify symbols and colors.
  3. Locate the region and nearby features.
  4. Record what the map directly shows.
  5. Identify an opportunity or constraint.
  6. Explain a human response.
  7. Connect that response to a historical effect.
Required notebook evidence: Copy this seven-step summary, your three physical-map observations, and your three trade-map observations. These notes will support later analysis.
