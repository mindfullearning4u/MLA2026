Geography, Regions, and Historical Change

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How does geography influence where societies develop and how they change?

Lesson Overview

Geography creates opportunities and constraints, but people decide how to respond. In this lesson you will use physical, regional, and trade maps to explain relationships among place, settlement, trade, defense, migration, and historical change.

Learning Objectives

  • Distinguish physical geography from human geography.
  • Read map titles, legends, labels, scale cues, and spatial relationships.
  • Explain how geographic features create opportunities and limitations.
  • Compare two regions without treating geography as destiny.
  • Support a geographic claim with specific map evidence.

Standards and Investigation Path

MLA.WH.GEO.01 and MLA.WH.GEO.02

  1. P02: Learn vocabulary and core concepts.
  2. P03: Record direct map observations.
  3. P04: Study a worked geographic-reasoning example.
  4. P05: Complete a guided investigation and meaningful summary.
  5. P06: Conduct an independent regional investigation.
  6. P07: Analyze a provided annotated map and complete the mastery checkpoint.
