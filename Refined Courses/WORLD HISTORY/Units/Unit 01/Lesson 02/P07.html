Geography, Regions, and Historical Change

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

How does geography influence where societies develop and how they change?

Mastery Checkpoint Task

Use the provided annotated map to write a 150–200-word explanation answering: How did geography influence the development of Constantinople?

Your job is to analyze the map—not create one. Use all three numbered annotations as evidence. Explain at least two geographic features, one opportunity, one limitation, one human response, and one historical consequence. Use at least four assigned vocabulary terms correctly.

How to Read the Annotations

  1. Annotation 1—Strategic location: Identify where Constantinople is located and explain why connecting regions and seas mattered.
  2. Annotation 2—Water-route opportunity: Explain how sea routes supported trade and cultural diffusion.
  3. Annotation 3—Exposure and response: Explain why a valuable location could also create danger and how people responded through defense.

Do not merely repeat the annotation labels. Explain the cause-and-effect relationship shown by each one.

Organize Your Writing

Use the same structure for future evidence-based writing assignments:

  1. Introduction: Name Constantinople and state a direct claim answering the investigation question.
  2. Body paragraph 1: Use Annotations 1 and 2 to explain the strategic location and trade opportunity. Cite specific map evidence.
  3. Body paragraph 2: Use Annotation 3 to explain the limitation, human response, and historical consequence.
  4. Conclusion: Restate the finding and clarify that geography influenced choices rather than determining them.

Use transitions such as because, therefore, however, as a result, and in contrast to make the reasoning visible.
