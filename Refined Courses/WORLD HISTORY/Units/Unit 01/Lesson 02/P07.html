Geography, Regions, and Historical Change

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

How does geography influence where societies develop and how they change?

Mastery Checkpoint Task

Create an annotated map and a 150–200-word explanation answering: How did geography influence the development of one medieval region?

Your map must identify the region and annotate at least three accurate map details. Your explanation must discuss at least two geographic features, one opportunity, one limitation, one human response, and one historical consequence. Use at least four vocabulary terms.

Organize Your Writing

Use the same structure for future evidence-based writing assignments:

  1. Introduction: Name the region and state a direct claim answering the question.
  2. Body paragraph 1: Explain the first geographic feature, cite map evidence, and connect it to an opportunity or limitation.
  3. Body paragraph 2: Explain another feature, the human response, and the resulting historical consequence.
  4. Conclusion: Restate the finding and clarify that geography influenced choices rather than determining them.

Use transitions such as because, therefore, however, as a result, and in contrast to make the reasoning visible.

Checkpoint Checklist

  • I answered the investigation question with a clear claim.
  • My annotated map contains at least three accurate details.
  • I explained at least two geographic features.
  • I included an opportunity and a limitation.
  • I explained a human response and a historical consequence.
  • I used Geographic Reasoning and Cause and Effect.
  • I used at least four assigned vocabulary terms correctly.
  • My explanation is 150–200 words and organized into an introduction, body, and conclusion.
