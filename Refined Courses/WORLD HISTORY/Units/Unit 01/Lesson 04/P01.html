Byzantine Figures, Achievements, Decline, and Ottoman Transition

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Why did the Byzantine Empire endure for centuries and eventually fall?

Lesson Overview

The Byzantine Empire endured for more than a thousand years by combining defensible geography, organized government, diplomacy, military adaptation, commerce, and a powerful cultural identity. Its achievements outlasted its political borders. This lesson shifts from Lesson 3’s continuity-and-change focus to causation: why Byzantine power weakened, why the events of 1453 occurred, and why the Ottoman transition mattered.

Learning Objectives

  • Explain how Justinian, Theodora, Belisarius, law, art, diplomacy, and defensive organization supported Byzantine endurance.
  • Distinguish long-term causes from immediate causes.
  • Analyze the Great Schism, Fourth Crusade, internal weakness, territorial loss, and Ottoman pressure.
  • Explain why the fall of Constantinople was a turning point.
  • Construct a ranked, evidence-based causal explanation.
