Byzantine Figures, Achievements, Decline, and Ottoman Transition

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question

Why did the Byzantine Empire endure for centuries and eventually fall?

Guided Investigation: Rank One Cause

Given claim: The Fourth Crusade should rank above the Great Schism as a cause of Byzantine decline.

  1. Define the comparison: Both are long-term causes, so compare their direct effect on Byzantine power.
  2. Evidence for the Great Schism: The 1054 division deepened religious and political separation between eastern and western churches.
  3. Evidence for the Fourth Crusade: In 1204 crusaders captured and looted Constantinople, removed Byzantine rule, and contributed to territorial fragmentation.
  4. Compare impact: The Schism complicated cooperation; the Fourth Crusade directly damaged the capital, treasury, institutions, and territorial unity.
  5. Reasoned judgment: The Fourth Crusade ranks higher because its direct political and economic damage more clearly reduced the empire’s ability to resist later pressure.

Required Historian’s Notebook Summary

Write a useful four-sentence causal summary in your own words:

  1. State which cause ranks higher.
  2. Give one dated piece of evidence.
  3. Explain why its impact was greater.
  4. Name one limitation: ranking causes requires judgment because causes interacted.

Use at least three terms: long-term cause, decline, Crusade, Great Schism, evidence, or effect.
