Step-by-Step Directions

  1. Decide your ranking before copying numbers into the first column.
  2. Label every cause as long-term or immediate. Ottoman military pressure may include both the long-term expansion and the immediate 1453 siege; state which part your evidence addresses.
  3. Give one specific evidence detail for every row.
  4. Write a separate 2–3 sentence explanation for every row.
  5. Compare adjacent ranks: explain why rank 1 had more influence than rank 2, and so on.
  6. Use and mark at least six different vocabulary terms across the organizer.

Submission-Ready Checklist

  • □ I ranked all four required causes from 1 through 4.
  • □ Every row identifies the type of cause.
  • □ Every row includes accurate, specific evidence.
  • □ Every explanation connects the evidence to Byzantine decline.
  • □ My explanations justify the order, not merely describe events.
  • □ I used and marked six assigned vocabulary terms.
  • □ My organizer is complete, organized, and easy for the TOR to grade.
