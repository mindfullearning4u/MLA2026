Required P04 Modeled Example

Both Haiti and Spanish American independence movements challenged European colonial authority, but their social foundations differed. Haiti's revolution was driven largely by enslaved and free African-descended people and resulted in abolition of slavery. Many Spanish American movements were led substantially by Creole elites and often preserved important elements of existing social hierarchy. Both achieved independence, but independence did not produce the same degree of social change.

Required P04 Common Misconceptions
Misconception 1

All Latin American revolutions were led by BolÃ­var.

Correction: Independence movements had numerous leaders, regions, and political paths.

Misconception 2

Haiti was simply another European-style independence movement.

Correction: Haiti uniquely combined anti-colonial revolution with a successful large-scale revolution against slavery.

Misconception 3

Independence automatically created equality.

Correction: Colonial rule ended in many places while social and economic inequalities often continued.

Misconception 4

Enlightenment ideas alone caused independence.

Correction: Colonial inequality, slavery, economic restrictions, political crises, local leadership, and international events also mattered.

Required P04 Modeled Example

Britain's coal reserves helped support industrialization because coal could provide concentrated energy for steam-powered machinery. However, coal alone did not cause industrialization. Britain also had investment capital, commercial networks, transportation infrastructure, labor, and technological innovation. Geography therefore provided an important opportunity that interacted with economic and technological conditions.

Required P04 Common Misconceptions
Misconception 1

The Industrial Revolution began because someone invented the steam engine.

Correction: Industrialization resulted from interacting geographic, economic, technological, social, and political conditions.

Misconception 2

James Watt invented the steam engine.

Correction: Watt significantly improved existing steam-engine technology.

Misconception 3

Everyone immediately became poorer because of industrialization.

Correction: Industrialization created severe costs for many workers while also increasing production and eventually contributing to changing living standards.

Misconception 4

Industrialization happened entirely inside Britain.

Correction: British industry depended partly on global trade, imported raw materials, overseas markets, and imperial economic networks.
