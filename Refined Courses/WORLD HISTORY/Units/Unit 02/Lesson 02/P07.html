Islamic Expansion and Cultural Diffusion

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

How did political expansion, trade, and cultural exchange spread Islamic civilization?

Mastery Checkpoint Task

Task Explain how political expansion, trade, and cultural exchange spread Islamic civilization. Response Format 225–275 words Two or three developed paragraphs Required Evidence At least two map details At least two causes of expansion At least one trade-network detail At least one example of cultural diffusion Evidence from at least two source types Required Reasoning Cause and Effect Geographic Reasoning Claim-Evidence-Reasoning Success Requirements Distinguishes political control from religious and cultural diffusion Explains multiple causes Uses geographic evidence Avoids reducing expansion to a single cause Uses at least five vocabulary terms accurately

Submit only the completed checkpoint response in the separate U2 L2 Checkpoint Submission activity.

Writing Organization

  1. Introduction: Establish context and state a defensible thesis.
  2. Body: Present evidence in the required categories. After every detail, explain why it supports the claim.
  3. Conclusion: Return to the investigation question and state the most defensible historical conclusion.

Use this same introduction–body–conclusion approach whenever a World History assignment requires developed writing.
