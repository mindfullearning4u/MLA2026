Mesoamerican and Andean Civilizations

Historical Thinking

Investigation Question

How did the Maya, Aztec, and Inca adapt to geography and organize complex societies?

Apply Comparison

  1. Restate the historical question.
  2. Establish relevant context and chronology.
  3. Choose specific evidence from two different types.
  4. Explain why each detail supports the claim.
  5. Apply Comparison and the supporting skills.
  6. State one qualification or source limitation.

Worked Example

Claim: Geography, human choices, and institutions interacted to shape this society.

Evidence: A map identifies a location or network; a dated source or artifact supplies a specific historical detail.

Reasoning: The evidence supports the claim because it shows both the conditions people faced and the choices they made. The conclusion remains limited by the perspective and survival of evidence.
