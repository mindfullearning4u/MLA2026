Mesoamerican and Andean Civilizations

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did the Maya, Aztec, and Inca adapt to geography and organize complex societies?

Lesson Purpose

This lesson teaches students to answer the investigation question through historically accurate content, maps, timelines, sources, and explicit reasoning. Primary skill: Comparison. Supporting skills: Geographic Reasoning, Historical Significance, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Required Learning

  • Geographic regions of Mesoamerica and the Andes
  • Maya city-states
  • Maya writing, mathematics, calendar, and architecture
  • Aztec Empire and Tenochtitlan
  • Tribute system
  • Chinampa agriculture
  • Inca Empire
  • Andes geography
  • Road system
  • Terrace farming
  • Quipu
  • Political and religious organization
  • Engineering and environmental adaptation
  • Similarities and differences among Maya, Aztec, and Inca
  • Limits of relying only on later European accounts
