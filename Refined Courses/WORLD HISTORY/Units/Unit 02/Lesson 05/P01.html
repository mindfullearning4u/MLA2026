West African Kingdoms: Ghana, Mali, and Songhai

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did geography, trade, and political leadership support the rise of West African kingdoms?

Lesson Purpose

This lesson teaches students to answer the investigation question through historically accurate content, maps, timelines, sources, and explicit reasoning. Primary skill: Comparison. Supporting skills: Geographic Reasoning, Cause and Effect, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Required Learning

  • Sahel and savanna geography
  • Trans-Saharan trade
  • Gold and salt
  • Ghana
  • Mali
  • Mansa Musa
  • Timbuktu
  • Songhai
  • Sunni Ali and Askia Muhammad
  • Taxation and control of trade routes
  • Islam and regional cultural exchange
  • Oral traditions and griots
  • Similarities and differences among the three kingdoms
  • Avoiding the misconception that one kingdom directly replaced another in a simple identical pattern
