Islamic Golden Age and Cross-Cultural Exchange

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Why were the achievements of the Islamic Golden Age significant to world history?

Lesson Purpose

This lesson teaches students to answer the investigation question through historically accurate content, maps, timelines, sources, and explicit reasoning. Primary skill: Historical Significance. Supporting skills: Contextualization, Comparison, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Required Learning

  • Abbasid Baghdad
  • House of Wisdom
  • Translation movement
  • Preservation and critique of Greek, Persian, Indian, and other knowledge
  • Algebra and mathematical development
  • Medicine and hospitals
  • Astronomy and observatories
  • Geography and cartography
  • Literature and philosophy
  • Ibn Sina, al-Khwarizmi, Ibn al-Haytham, or other representative scholars
  • Paper production and libraries
  • Transmission of knowledge through trade, translation, and contact
  • Difference between preservation and original innovation
  • Historical significance criteria: scale, duration, influence, and reach
