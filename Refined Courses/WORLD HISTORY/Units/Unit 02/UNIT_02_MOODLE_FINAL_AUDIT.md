## Assessment configuration

- Unit 2 Pretest: 10 questions, one attempt, diagnostic, 0% course weight.
- Lesson 1-7 quizzes: 25-question bank per lesson, five random questions delivered, one initial attempt, 80% passing score.
- Unit Assessment: 40-question bank, ten questions delivered, one initial attempt, 80% passing score.
- Unit Assessment delivery is stratified: one draw from each Lesson 1-7 core category and one draw from each of three rotating extra categories.
- Source, map, schematic, timeline, chart, and table evidence needed to answer an assessment question is embedded in the question content.
- Answer choices use A-D numbering and shuffled answer order.

## Release sequence

- Lesson 1 opens after completion of the Unit 2 Pretest.
- Each notebook submission opens after its lesson is complete.
- Each checkpoint opens after its lesson is complete and its notebook submission is passed.
- Each lesson quiz opens after its checkpoint is passed at 80% or higher.
- The next lesson opens after the preceding quiz is passed at 80% or higher.
- The Unit Assessment opens after Lesson 8 and the Lesson 8 notebook submission requirements are complete.
