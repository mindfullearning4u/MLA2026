Required Source-Limitation Instruction

You must understand:

  • European explorer accounts may reflect colonial goals and assumptions.
  • Indigenous sources may describe one community or experience rather than all Indigenous societies.
  • Maps simplify complex routes and political boundaries.
  • Population estimates can vary.
  • A crop-transfer chart shows movement but not automatically the full social consequences.
  • Later colonial outcomes were not inevitable at the moment of first contact.
