Age of Exploration and the Columbian Exchange

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did oceanic exploration create lasting global connections and consequences?

What You Will Learn

You investigate why European states expanded oceanic exploration and how new maritime routes connected previously separate regional systems more directly.

  • The lesson emphasizes both causes and consequences.

You examine:

Economic motivations

Political competition

Religious motivations

Navigation and ship technology

Geographic knowledge

Portuguese and Spanish exploration

Atlantic crossings

Columbian Exchange

Disease

Agricultural transfer

Animal transfer

Demographic change

Environmental change

Indigenous perspectives

African consequences

European consequences

You must understand that exploration created global connections but also produced conquest, violence, population loss, exploitation, and long-term environmental and social transformation.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Cause and Effect

Supporting skills: Geographic Reasoning, Perspective, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Standards: MLA.WH.EXP.01, MLA.WH.EXP.02
