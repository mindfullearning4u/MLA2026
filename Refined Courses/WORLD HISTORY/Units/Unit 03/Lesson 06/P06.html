Age of Exploration and the Columbian Exchange

Independent Investigation

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question: How did oceanic exploration create lasting global connections and consequences?

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which consequence of oceanic exploration had the greatest long-term impact on the Atlantic world?

You must evaluate at least four:

New trade routes

Columbian Exchange of crops and animals

Disease and demographic change

Colonization and political expansion

  • Detailed systems of forced labor and Atlantic slavery will be addressed in Lesson 7, so they may be acknowledged but should not dominate this investigation.

Required Product

Part A — Consequence Evidence

  • For each of the four consequences, students identify:

One specific example

One piece of evidence

One immediate effect

One long-term effect

Part B — Ranking

  • Rank all four from most to least historically consequential.

Part C — Perspective Check

You answer:

  • Would the ranking be the same from a European and Indigenous perspective?
  • Explain why or why not.

Part D — Interaction

You identify at least two consequences that reinforced one another.

  • Example:
  • Epidemic population decline affected European colonization because...

Part E — Final Conclusion

  • Students write a short evidence-based conclusion defending the highest-ranked consequence.
