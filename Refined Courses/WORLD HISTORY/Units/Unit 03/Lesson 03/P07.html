Renaissance Contributions in Art, Literature, and Technology

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Mastery Checkpoint

Investigation Question: Which Renaissance developments most significantly changed European culture and communication?

Investigation Question

  • Which Renaissance development most significantly changed European culture and communication?

Required Task

You construct a historical argument comparing major Renaissance developments and defend which one had the greatest historical significance.

You may select:

Artistic development

Literary development

Printing/communication development

  • but they must compare their selected development against at least two alternatives.

Response Format

  • 225–275 words

Two or three developed paragraphs

  • Recommended structure:

Paragraph 1

  • Claim + historical context + significance criteria

Paragraph 2

Evidence supporting the selected development

Paragraph 3

  • Comparison with alternatives + conclusion
  • Alternative structures are acceptable if all requirements are met.

Required Checkpoint Evidence

You must include:

At least three Renaissance developments

  • At least one specific artwork, architecture, or artistic-technique detail

At least one literature or written-source detail

At least one printing or communication detail

Evidence from at least two different source types

  • Explicit use of at least two historical-significance criteria

Required Historical Reasoning

You must demonstrate:

Historical Significance

Comparison

Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

  • Continuity and Change may support the argument where appropriate.

Mastery Success Criteria

  • A mastery response must:

Directly answer the Investigation Question

Make a defensible historical claim

Compare at least three developments

Establish criteria for significance

Use at least two significance criteria explicitly

Use evidence rather than fame as the basis for ranking

  • Accurately explain printing in European historical context
  • Connect the selected development to longer-term consequences

Use specific lesson evidence

Use at least five assigned vocabulary terms accurately

Meet the required response length

Represent the student's own understanding

Checkpoint Organization

  1. Introduction: establish context and state a direct claim.
  2. Body: present the exact required evidence and explain how it supports the claim.
  3. Conclusion: answer the question, show the required historical-thinking skill, and explain significance or limits.

Use this introduction–body–conclusion approach whenever a World History assignment requires developed writing.
