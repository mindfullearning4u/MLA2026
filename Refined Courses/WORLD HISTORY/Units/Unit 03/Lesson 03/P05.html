Renaissance Contributions in Art, Literature, and Technology

Guided Investigation

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question: Which Renaissance developments most significantly changed European culture and communication?

This is guided notebook work. Follow the reasoning sequence below. You are not expected to invent the process on your own.

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which Renaissance development had the greatest long-term historical significance?

You must evaluate at least three developments.

Required categories include:

Artistic innovation

Literature or vernacular writing

Printing and communication

You may identify a more specific development within one category.

Required Product

Part A — Evidence Collection

  • For each development, students identify:

Specific example

Contemporary effect

Evidence

Later influence

Part B — Significance Ratings

  • Students rate each development from 1–5 for:

Scale

Reach

Duration

Degree of change

Later influence

You must justify ratings with evidence.

Part C — Ranking

  • Rank the three developments from:

Most historically significant

Second

Third

Part D — Final Argument

  • Students write a short notebook conclusion explaining:

Which development ranks first

Which significance criteria matter most

Which historical evidence best supports the ranking

Why another development ranks lower

Meaningful Notebook Summary

Do not copy every instruction. Record one organized summary containing: the claim being tested, the strongest evidence, the reasoning that connects that evidence to the claim, and one limitation. This summary is required notebook evidence.
