Visual 4 — Manuscript Versus Printed Book

  • Required.
  • Comparison should include:
  • Feature Manuscript Printed Book
  • Production method Hand copied Mechanically reproduced
  • Production speed Slower Faster
  • Number of copies Limited Larger quantities possible
  • Cost Generally higher Could decline with larger production
  • Consistency Could vary between copies Greater consistency
  • Distribution More limited Potentially broader
  • Add the clarification:
  • Printing did not immediately eliminate manuscripts.

Required Source Limitation Instruction

You should understand examples such as:

  • One artwork cannot represent all Renaissance art.
  • A political writer represents one perspective.
  • Surviving printed books disproportionately reflect literate groups.
  • Famous elite works tell less about people who left few written records.
  • Later historical influence cannot be established from one contemporary source alone.

Notebook Evidence to Submit
