Renaissance Contributions in Art, Literature, and Technology

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Which Renaissance developments most significantly changed European culture and communication?

What You Will Learn

You examine major Renaissance developments in art, literature, architecture, printing, and communication and determine which developments had the greatest historical significance.

  • The lesson moves beyond identifying famous people or inventions. Students must evaluate developments using explicit significance criteria:

Scale

Reach

Duration

Degree of change

Influence on later developments

You should also understand that Renaissance innovation frequently involved adapting or building upon earlier knowledge and technologies rather than creating developments with no historical precedent.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Historical Significance

Supporting skills: Comparison, Continuity and Change, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Standards: MLA.WH.REN.03
