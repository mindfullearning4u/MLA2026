Medieval Japan: Geography, Society, and Cultural Exchange

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did geography and cultural exchange shape medieval Japanese society?

What You Will Learn

You investigate how the physical geography of the Japanese archipelago influenced settlement, agriculture, transportation, political organization, defense, and economic activity.

  • Students also examine Japan's connections with China and Korea and analyze how Japanese society borrowed, adapted, and transformed outside cultural influences.
  • The lesson introduces the political and social organization of medieval Japan, including the emperor, shogun, daimyo, and samurai, and compares Japanese feudal relationships with the European systems studied earlier in the course.

You should understand that geography influenced Japanese development but did not determine it. Human decisions, political circumstances, cultural exchange, and historical change also shaped Japanese society.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Geographic Reasoning

Supporting skills: Comparison, Contextualization, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Standards: MLA.WH.JPN.01, MLA.WH.JPN.02
