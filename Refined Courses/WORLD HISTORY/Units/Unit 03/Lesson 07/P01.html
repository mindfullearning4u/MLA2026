Colonial Systems, Slavery, and Forced Labor

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did colonial systems use labor, race, and economic power to reshape societies across the Atlantic world?

What You Will Learn

You investigate how European colonial powers organized labor and production in the Americas and Atlantic world.

  • The lesson examines:

Colonization

Encomienda

Repartimiento

Plantation systems

Forced Indigenous labor

Atlantic slavery

African forced migration

Racial hierarchy

Mercantilism

Cash crops

Colonial extraction

Resistance

Demographic and social consequences

You must understand that colonial labor systems were not interchangeable. They developed in different places, under different legal structures, and affected Indigenous peoples and Africans in different ways.

  • The lesson must also distinguish between:

Slavery

Forced labor

Tribute labor

Wage labor

Contract labor

  • where historically appropriate.
