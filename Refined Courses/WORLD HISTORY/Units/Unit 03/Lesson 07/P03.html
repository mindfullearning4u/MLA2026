Required Source-Limitation Instruction

You must understand:

  • Colonial officials often documented systems from the perspective of administrators or owners.
  • Reform critics may emphasize abuses for persuasive purposes.
  • Enslaved-person testimony reflects lived experience but may not represent every location or period.
  • Quantitative records may undercount people or omit illegal activity.
  • Maps simplify routes, legal systems, and regional differences.
  • Surviving evidence is uneven because powerful institutions preserved more records.
