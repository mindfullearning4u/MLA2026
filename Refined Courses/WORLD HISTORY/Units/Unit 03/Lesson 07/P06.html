Colonial Systems, Slavery, and Forced Labor

Independent Investigation

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question: How did colonial systems use labor, race, and economic power to reshape societies across the Atlantic world?

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which factor was most important in the expansion of forced labor and slavery in the Atlantic colonial system?

You must evaluate at least four factors:

European demand for cash crops and resources

Colonial political and legal power

Labor demands in plantations and mines

Atlantic trade and shipping networks

You may also use Indigenous demographic decline as an additional factor, but they must not treat it as a complete explanation.

Required Product

Part A — Factor Evidence

  • For each factor, students identify:

One specific historical example

One source, map, data, or lesson detail

How the factor contributed to coercive labor

Part B — Ranking

  • Rank the factors from most to least influential.

Part C — System Comparison

  • Students explain one important difference between:

Encomienda or repartimiento

  • and

Chattel slavery

Part D — Resistance

You identify one example of resistance and explain what it reveals about agency.

Part E — Final Conclusion

  • Students write a short evidence-based conclusion explaining:

Which factor mattered most

How it interacted with at least one other factor

Why no single cause fully explains the system
