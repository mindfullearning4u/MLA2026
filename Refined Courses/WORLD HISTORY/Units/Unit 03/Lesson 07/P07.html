Colonial Systems, Slavery, and Forced Labor

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Mastery Checkpoint

Investigation Question: How did colonial systems use labor, race, and economic power to reshape societies across the Atlantic world?

Investigation Question

  • How did colonial systems use labor, race, and economic power to reshape societies across the Atlantic world?

Required Task

You construct a historical explanation showing how colonial governments and economic systems created and expanded coercive labor systems.

  • The response must address:

Economic demand

Colonial political/legal power

At least two forms of coerced labor

Racial hierarchy

Resistance or agency

Long-term social or demographic consequences

Response Format

  • 275–325 words

Three developed paragraphs recommended

  • Suggested organization:

Paragraph 1

  • Historical context + economic/political causes

Paragraph 2

  • Labor systems + racial hierarchy + evidence

Paragraph 3

  • Resistance + long-term consequences + conclusion
  • Alternative organization is acceptable if all requirements are met.

Required Checkpoint Evidence

You must include:

At least one cash-crop or resource-production example

At least one colonial political or legal factor

At least one example of forced Indigenous labor

At least one example of African chattel slavery

At least one Atlantic map, route, or quantitative detail

  • At least one source representing an Indigenous, enslaved African, or African-diaspora perspective

At least one example of resistance or agency

  • At least one long-term demographic, social, or economic consequence
  • Evidence must come from at least three different evidence types.

Required Historical Reasoning

You must demonstrate:

Cause and Effect

Perspective

Comparison

Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Mastery Success Criteria

  • A mastery response must:

Directly answer the Investigation Question

Establish appropriate historical context

  • Explain how economic demand contributed to labor exploitation

Explain the role of colonial political/legal power

Distinguish forced Indigenous labor from chattel slavery

  • Explain how racial hierarchy became connected to colonial labor systems

Use Atlantic geographic evidence accurately

  • Include the perspective of people subjected to colonial labor systems

Include resistance or agency

Explain at least one long-term consequence

Avoid implying racialized slavery was inevitable

Use at least six assigned vocabulary terms accurately

Meet the required response length

Represent the student's own understanding

Checkpoint Organization

  1. Introduction: establish context and state a direct claim.
  2. Body: present the exact required evidence and explain how it supports the claim.
  3. Conclusion: answer the question, show the required historical-thinking skill, and explain significance or limits.

Use this introduction–body–conclusion approach whenever a World History assignment requires developed writing.
