Reformation and Catholic Response

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Why did religious reform divide Western Christianity and transform European society?

What You Will Learn

You investigate the religious, political, technological, intellectual, and economic conditions that contributed to the Protestant Reformation.

  • They examine how reform movements challenged established religious authority, how different reformers developed distinct theological and political positions, and how Catholic leaders responded through reform and institutional action.

You must understand that the Reformation was not caused by one person or one event. It developed from multiple long-term and immediate causes.

  • The lesson must also distinguish among:

Protestant reform movements

Catholic reform

Political interests

Religious beliefs

Institutional responses

Social consequences

  • The lesson must use neutral academic language and must not evaluate the truth of competing religious doctrines.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Cause and Effect

Supporting skills: Perspective, Contextualization, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Standards: MLA.WH.REF.01, MLA.WH.REF.02
