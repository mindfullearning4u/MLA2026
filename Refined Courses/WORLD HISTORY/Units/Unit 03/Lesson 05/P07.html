Reformation and Catholic Response

Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Mastery Checkpoint

Investigation Question: Why did religious reform divide Western Christianity and transform European society?

Investigation Question

  • Why did religious reform divide Western Christianity and transform European society?

Required Task

You construct a historical explanation showing:

Why the Reformation developed

Why it spread

How Catholic leaders responded

  • How religious reform produced broader political or social consequences

Response Format

  • 250–300 words

Two or three developed paragraphs

Recommended organization:

Paragraph 1

  • Historical context + overall claim

Paragraph 2

Causes and spread of reform

Paragraph 3

  • Catholic response + political/social consequences

You may use another organization if all requirements are met.

Required Checkpoint Evidence

You must include:

At least two distinct causes of the Reformation

At least one specific Martin Luther detail

At least one printing-related detail

At least one political factor

  • At least one Catholic response, such as Council of Trent or Jesuits

Evidence from one Protestant source

Evidence from one Catholic or political source

At least one consequence of religious division

Required Historical Reasoning

You must demonstrate:

Cause and Effect

Perspective

Contextualization

Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Mastery Success Criteria

  • A mastery response must:

Directly answer the Investigation Question

Establish historical context

Explain multiple interacting causes

  • Distinguish an underlying cause from an accelerating factor
  • Explain why printing mattered without claiming it caused the Reformation alone

Accurately explain Luther's role

Include at least one political dimension

Accurately explain Catholic reform/response

Represent Protestant and Catholic perspectives fairly

  • Connect religious change to political or social consequences

Use at least five assigned vocabulary terms accurately

Meet the required response length

Represent the student's own understanding

Checkpoint Organization

  1. Introduction: establish context and state a direct claim.
  2. Body: present the exact required evidence and explain how it supports the claim.
  3. Conclusion: answer the question, show the required historical-thinking skill, and explain significance or limits.

Use this introduction–body–conclusion approach whenever a World History assignment requires developed writing.

Checkpoint Checklist

  • I explained evidence instead of listing facts.
  • I used the required historical-thinking skill and lesson vocabulary.
