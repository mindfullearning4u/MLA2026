Required Source-Limitation Instruction

You must understand:

  • Luther does not represent every Protestant reformer.
  • A Catholic council document represents institutional Catholic authority, not every Catholic person's experience.
  • A political ruler may have religious and political motives at the same time.
  • Propaganda or polemical images may exaggerate opponents.
  • Later outcomes should not be assumed to have been inevitable.
