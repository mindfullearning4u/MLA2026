Reformation and Catholic Response

Guided Investigation

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question: Why did religious reform divide Western Christianity and transform European society?

This is guided notebook work. Follow the reasoning sequence below. You are not expected to invent the process on your own.

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which factor was most important in allowing the Reformation to transform from a religious controversy into a major European movement?

You evaluate:

Religious criticism and reform ideas

Printing

Political support

Economic/institutional interests

Required Product

Part A — Evidence for Each Factor

  • For each factor students identify:

One specific historical example

One piece of evidence

How the factor contributed

Part B — Ranking

  • Rank the four factors from most to least influential.

Part C — Interaction

You identify two factors that depended on one another and explain the relationship.

  • Example structure:
  • Printing was more influential because ______, but it became especially powerful when combined with ______.

Part D — Alternative Perspective

You identify one reason another historian might rank a different cause first.

Part E — Conclusion

  • Students write a short evidence-based conclusion defending their ranking.

Meaningful Notebook Summary

Do not copy every instruction. Record one organized summary containing: the claim being tested, the strongest evidence, the reasoning that connects that evidence to the claim, and one limitation. This summary is required notebook evidence.
