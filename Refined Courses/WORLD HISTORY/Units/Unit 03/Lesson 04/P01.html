Scientific Revolution: New Methods and Challenges

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

How did evidence and new methods change European understanding of the natural world?

What You Will Learn

You investigate how new observations, mathematical models, experimentation, and systematic reasoning contributed to major changes in European understandings of the natural world.

  • The lesson must emphasize that the Scientific Revolution was not a simple story of "old ideas being replaced by correct ones." Instead, students examine:
  • Continuity with earlier classical, medieval, and Islamic scholarship

New observations

New instruments

Mathematical reasoning

Revision of earlier scientific models

Debate among scholars

Institutional and religious context

Gradual development of scientific practices

You must learn to distinguish:

Claim

Evidence

Reasoning

  • and apply this structure to historical scientific debates.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Supporting skills: Continuity and Change, Comparison, Contextualization

Standards: MLA.WH.SCI.01, MLA.WH.SCI.02
