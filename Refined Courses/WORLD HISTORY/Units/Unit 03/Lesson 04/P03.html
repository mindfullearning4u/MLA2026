Required Source-Limitation Instruction

You should understand:

  • A scientific observation may support a claim without proving every part of a theory.
  • One scientist's writing represents a particular historical perspective.
  • A diagram may simplify more complicated evidence.
  • Later scientific success should not cause students to assume earlier scientists already knew the final outcome.
  • Secondary historians interpret evidence and may emphasize different causes or consequences.
