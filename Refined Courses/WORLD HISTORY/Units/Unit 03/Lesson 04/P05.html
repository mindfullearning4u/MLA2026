Scientific Revolution: New Methods and Challenges

Guided Investigation

Guided Investigation

Investigation Question: How did evidence and new methods change European understanding of the natural world?

This is guided notebook work. Follow the reasoning sequence below. You are not expected to invent the process on your own.

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which mattered more in transforming European science: new evidence or new methods of investigation?

You must evaluate both.

Required Product

Part A — New Evidence

You identify at least two examples such as:

Telescopic observations

Planetary observations

Improved measurements

Other lesson evidence

  • For each:
  • What was observed?
  • Which claim did it support or challenge?

Part B — New Methods

You identify at least two developments such as:

Systematic observation

Empiricism

Experimentation

Mathematical modeling

  • For each:
  • How did it change investigation?
  • Which scientist or source illustrates it?

Part C — Comparison

You must determine:

  • Which had greater influence?
  • Why?
  • What evidence supports that conclusion?
  • How did evidence and method depend on one another?

Part D — Limitation

You identify one limitation of arguing that either evidence or methods alone caused the Scientific Revolution.

Meaningful Notebook Summary

Do not copy every instruction. Record one organized summary containing: the claim being tested, the strongest evidence, the reasoning that connects that evidence to the claim, and one limitation. This summary is required notebook evidence.
