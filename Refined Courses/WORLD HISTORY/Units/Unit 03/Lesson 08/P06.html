Unit 3 Synthesis: Innovation, Reform, and Global Exchange

Independent Investigation

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question: Which developments in Unit 3 most transformed societies, ideas, and global connections?

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which three developments from Unit 3 most transformed societies, ideas, or global connections?

You must select exactly three developments from Lessons 1–7.

  • Possible choices include:

Japanese political/cultural development

Renaissance humanism

Renaissance art/literature

Printing

Scientific Revolution

Reformation

Oceanic exploration

Columbian Exchange

Colonial labor systems

Atlantic slavery

Required Product

Part A — Select Three Developments

  • For each development:

Identify the lesson

Describe the development

Identify one specific piece of evidence

  • Identify the best historical-thinking skill for analyzing it

Part B — Historical Significance Evaluation

  • Rate each development using:

Reach

Duration

Scale

Degree of change

Later influence

Part C — Cross-Unit Connections

  • For each selected development, identify at least one connection to another Unit 3 lesson.

You must create at least three cross-lesson connections total.

Part D — Ranking

  • Rank the three developments:

Most transformative

Second most transformative

Third most transformative

Part E — Counterargument

You must identify:

One development not selected

Why another historian might rank it in the top three

One piece of evidence supporting that alternative

Part F — Final Notebook Conclusion

  • Students write a short synthesis paragraph defending their top-ranked development.

Example reasoning sentence: “The evidence shows ___; this matters because ___; however, this evidence alone cannot prove ___.” Replace each blank with lesson-specific information.
