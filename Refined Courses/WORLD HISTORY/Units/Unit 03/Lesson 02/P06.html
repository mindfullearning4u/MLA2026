Italian City-States and Renaissance Humanism

Independent Investigation

Independent Investigation

Investigation Question: Why did Renaissance culture develop first in Italian city-states?

Investigation Task

You answer:

  • Which two factors were most important in explaining why Renaissance culture developed first in Italian city-states?

You must evaluate all five:

Mediterranean trade and wealth

Urbanization

Classical heritage

Patronage

Political competition

Required Product

Part A — Evidence Ranking

  • Rank all five from most to least influential.

Part B — Top Two Causes

  • For each of the top two:

Identify specific historical evidence

Explain how it contributed

Explain how it interacted with another cause

Part C — Challenge the Ranking

You must identify:

  • One reason another historian might choose a different cause

One piece of evidence supporting that alternative

Part D — Final Conclusion

  • Write a short conclusion explaining why a single-cause explanation is insufficient.
