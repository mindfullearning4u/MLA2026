Italian City-States and Renaissance Humanism

Historical Evidence Lab

Evidence Set

Visual 1 — Map of Italian City-States

  • Required.
  • Must identify at minimum:

Florence

Venice

Milan

Rome

Italian Peninsula

Mediterranean Sea

  • Additional historically relevant political entities may be included if the map remains readable.

Student purpose

You must answer:

  • Where were major Renaissance centers located?
  • How might access to Mediterranean trade have benefited them?
  • Why did political fragmentation matter?

Visual 2 — Mediterranean Trade Map

  • Required.
  • Must show meaningful trade connections among:

Italian commercial centers

Eastern Mediterranean

Western Europe

North Africa where historically appropriate

  • The map should include representative trade routes rather than becoming an overloaded commodity map.

Student purpose

You analyze:

  • How could trade generate both wealth and access to ideas?

Visual 3 — Renaissance Development Timeline

  • Required.
  • Possible chronological anchors include:

Growth of Italian commercial centers

Important artistic developments

  • Printing as later Renaissance context if used only as preview
  • Do not teach Lesson 3 content in detail here.
  • The timeline's primary purpose is chronology and context.

Visual 6 — Humanism Instructional Visual

  • Required.
  • Distinguish:
  • Humanism emphasizes:

Classical texts

Education

Rhetoric

History

Human experience

Civic involvement

Individual achievement

  • Humanism does not automatically mean:

Rejection of religion

Modern secularism

Opposition to Christianity

  • This distinction is important for historical accuracy.

Visual 7 — Renaissance Artwork as Evidence

  • Required.
  • Use at least one open-license Renaissance artwork.
  • The artwork must be analyzed for historical evidence such as:

Patronage

Classical influence

Status

Religious continuity

Human representation

Political or civic messaging

  • The artwork must not be included merely as decoration.
