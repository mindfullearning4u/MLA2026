Mastery Checkpoint

Investigation Question: Why did Renaissance culture develop first in Italian city-states?

Investigation Question

  • Why did Renaissance culture develop first in Italian city-states?

Required Task

You construct a historical explanation showing how multiple economic, geographic, political, and intellectual factors contributed to Renaissance development.

Response Format

  • 225–275 words

Two or three developed paragraphs

Recommended organization:

Paragraph 1

  • Historical context + overall claim

Paragraph 2

Economic/geographic causes

Paragraph 3

  • Intellectual/political causes + interaction + conclusion

You may organize differently if all requirements are satisfied.

Required Checkpoint Evidence

You must use:

At least one specific map detail

At least two economic or political factors

At least one humanism or classical-learning detail

At least one specific example of patronage

Evidence from at least two different source types

  • At least one explanation showing how two causes interacted

Required Historical Reasoning

You must demonstrate:

Cause and Effect

Contextualization

Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Mastery Success Criteria

  • A mastery response must:

Directly answer the Investigation Question

Establish appropriate historical context

Explain at least three contributing causes

Identify relationships among causes

Use map evidence accurately

Connect wealth to patronage

Explain humanism accurately

  • Avoid treating the Renaissance as a sudden complete rejection of medieval society

Use specific historical evidence

Use at least five assigned vocabulary terms accurately

Meet the required response length

Represent the student's own understanding

Checkpoint Organization

  1. Introduction: establish context and state a direct claim.
  2. Body: present the exact required evidence and explain how it supports the claim.
  3. Conclusion: answer the question, show the required historical-thinking skill, and explain significance or limits.

Use this introduction–body–conclusion approach whenever a World History assignment requires developed writing.
