Italian City-States and Renaissance Humanism

Lesson Overview

Investigation Question

Why did Renaissance culture develop first in Italian city-states?

What You Will Learn

You investigate why Renaissance culture developed first in Italian city-states.

  • They examine the interaction of:

Mediterranean trade

Urban development

Merchant wealth

Banking

Political competition

Patronage

Classical Greek and Roman heritage

Humanism

You must understand that the Renaissance was not caused by one factor and did not appear suddenly. It developed through interacting economic, political, geographic, intellectual, and cultural conditions.

  • The lesson must also establish continuity between medieval Europe and the Renaissance rather than teaching the Renaissance as a complete break with the medieval period.

Historical Thinking

Primary skill: Cause and Effect

Supporting skills: Contextualization, Geographic Reasoning, Claim-Evidence-Reasoning

Standards: MLA.WH.REN.01, MLA.WH.REN.02
