Effects of World War I and the Treaty Settlement

Your Exact Notebook Product

Use the completed 1919 Europe map and evidence table on P02. You are not creating a new map. In your notebook, write one organized evidence analysis with the four labeled parts below.

Part A — Map Detail

Copy this heading: Map evidence. Identify one specific border, new state, or unsettled territory shown on the 1919 map. Write one complete sentence explaining what changed after World War I.

Part B — Treaty Evidence

Copy this heading: Treaty evidence. Record two accurate effects of the peace settlement from the lesson. For each effect, add a sentence explaining who or what was affected.

Part C — Cause and Effect

Write one developed paragraph of 6–8 sentences. Begin with a claim about how the World War I settlement shaped later instability. Use your map detail and both treaty details as evidence. Use because to explain the cause and therefore to explain the consequence.
