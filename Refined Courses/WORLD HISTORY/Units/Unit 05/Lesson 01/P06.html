Model developed statements—organization only

Trigger: “The assassination triggered the crisis by giving Austria-Hungary a reason to confront Serbia; it was not an underlying cause because militarism, alliances, nationalism, imperial rivalry, and Balkan tension already existed.”

Interaction: “Militarism strengthened alliance tension because leaders feared that delaying mobilization would give opposing states a military advantage.”

Technology: “Machine guns and artillery increased defensive firepower, encouraging trenches and contributing to stalemate on the Western Front.”
