P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question: Why did a regional crisis in 1914 develop into a major world war, and how did industrial technology change the nature of warfare?

What you will submit

Write a 300–350 word historical explanation organized into three developed paragraphs. Submit the completed response in the separate Unit 5 Lesson 1 Mastery Checkpoint Submission assignment.

Required organization

Paragraph 1—Introduction and causation claim

  • Answer the full investigation question with a clear claim.
  • Distinguish at least three long-term causes from the Sarajevo assassination as the trigger.
  • Preview how decisions during the July Crisis produced escalation.

Paragraph 2—Evidence and explanation

  • Explain at least two July Crisis decisions.
  • Include at least one specific Europe/Balkans map detail.
  • Use at least one primary-source detail.
  • Explain why the conflict became global rather than remaining only European.

Paragraph 3—Changing warfare and conclusion

  • Explain how at least two industrial military technologies changed combat.
  • Explain why trench warfare and stalemate developed on the Western Front.
  • Connect the warfare analysis back to your claim and end with a supported conclusion.

How to write each body section

Use Claim–Evidence–Reasoning: state the idea, provide an exact historical detail, and explain how that detail supports the claim. Do not leave evidence unexplained. This paragraph structure is the same approach you should use in future historical writing assignments.

Checkpoint checklist

Before submitting, confirm every item:

  • □ My response is 300–350 words.
  • □ I wrote three developed paragraphs.
  • □ I identified at least three long-term causes.
  • □ I identified the assassination as the trigger, not the sole cause.
  • □ I explained at least two July Crisis decisions.
  • □ I used one specific map detail.
  • □ I used at least one primary-source detail.
  • □ I explained global involvement.
  • □ I explained at least two industrial technologies.
  • □ I explained trench warfare or stalemate.
  • □ I used Cause and Effect, Chronology, Geographic Reasoning, and Claim–Evidence–Reasoning.
  • □ I used at least eight lesson vocabulary terms accurately.
  • □ My evidence is followed by reasoning, not just listed.
  • □ I answered the entire investigation question.

Mastery and progression

You must earn at least 80% mastery on the Mastery Checkpoint to progress to the Lesson 1 Quiz. If you do not yet earn 80%, you will meet with the Teacher of Record (TOR), receive feedback and remediation, and then resubmit the checkpoint.
