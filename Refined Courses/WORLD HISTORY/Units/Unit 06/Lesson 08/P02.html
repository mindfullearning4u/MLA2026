Notebook task: synthesis vocabulary

Write a one-sentence meaning for each previously learned term: Cold War, containment, proxy war, nuclear deterrence, decolonization, self-determination, nationalism, sovereignty, globalization, interdependence, migration, refugee, demography, genocide, ethnic cleansing, peacekeeping, humanitarian intervention, terrorism, trade bloc, historical significance, Cause and Effect, Continuity and Change, Perspective, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Seven-lesson review

  1. Cold War Europe: rivalry, security, containment, Berlin, alliances.
  2. China/proxy wars: Mao, Sino-Soviet split, deterrence, Korea, Vietnam, Soviet collapse and China’s different path.
  3. Middle East: Zionism, Palestinian/Arab nationalism, 1948 displacement, 1967, territory, diplomacy.
  4. Decolonization: India/Pakistan, Africa, nonalignment, apartheid and democratic reform.
  5. Global change: population, migration, Green Revolution, technology, public health, pandemics, inequality.
  6. Mass atrocity: legal distinctions, Cambodia, Bosnia, Rwanda, response and accountability.
  7. Globalization/conflict: nationalism, supply chains, blocs, terrorism, 9/11, security.
