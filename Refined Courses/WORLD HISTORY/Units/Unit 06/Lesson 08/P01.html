Unit 6 Synthesis: The Modern World and Historical Evidence

Investigation Question

Which forces have most significantly shaped the modern world since 1945, and how can historical evidence be used to evaluate their impact?

Skills: Comparison, Cause and Effect, Continuity and Change, CER; supported by significance, geography, perspective, context, and chronology.
