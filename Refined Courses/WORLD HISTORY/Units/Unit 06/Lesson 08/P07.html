P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Unit 6 Synthesis: The Modern World and Historical Evidence

Checkpoint

Which forces have most significantly shaped the modern world since 1945, and how can evidence evaluate their impact?

Submit 425–500 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 8 Mastery Checkpoint Submission. There is no Lesson 8 quiz; the Unit Assessment replaces it.

Four or five paragraphs

  1. Thesis identifying and ranking exactly three forces.
  2. Force 1 with significance, cause/change, source and geography.
  3. Forces 2–3 with direct comparison and interaction.
  4. Continuities, affected-community perspective, source limitations/corroboration.
  5. Counterargument and defended conclusion.

Checkpoint checklist

  • Exactly three forces; clear ranking; 425–500 words.
  • Evidence from five lessons and two regions; two map details; chronology detail.
  • Two written primary-source details; one quantitative detail; one affected perspective; one government source.
  • Two cause/effect and two continuity/change relationships; direct comparison.
  • Two significance criteria; four cross-lesson connections; counterargument.
  • Nine Unit 6 terms; recognize source limitations.
Mastery: Earn 80% to progress to the Unit Assessment. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise, and resubmit.
