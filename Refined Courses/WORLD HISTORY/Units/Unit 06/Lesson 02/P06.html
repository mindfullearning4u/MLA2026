Example row

Factor: Nationalism/local goals. Example: Vietnamese movements sought independence and reunification. Evidence: The conflict grew from French colonial rule and Vietnamese political struggle before major U.S. escalation. Effect: local goals sustained conflict beyond superpower plans. Limitation: nationalism alone does not explain the scale of foreign aid and intervention.
