China, Arms Race, Proxy Wars, and the Fall of Communism

Claim to investigate

Cold War rivalry spread through regional conflicts and nuclear competition, but local history and different reform choices produced different outcomes.

Follow the reasoning

  1. Use China’s civil war and nationalism to explain why the PRC was not a Soviet creation.
  2. Use the Sino-Soviet split to disprove automatic communist unity.
  3. Compare Korea and Vietnam: identify colonial/postwar context, local goals, outside support, and different outcomes.
  4. Explain deterrence: fear of retaliation discouraged direct attack while creating catastrophic risk.
  5. Trace Soviet and Eastern European change through multiple causes.
  6. Contrast that result with China’s economic reform and continued one-party political rule.
