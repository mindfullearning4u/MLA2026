P01 Lesson Overview

China, Arms Race, Proxy Wars, and the Fall of Communism

Investigation Question

How did Cold War rivalry spread beyond Europe, and why did communist governments follow different paths in China, the Soviet Union, and Eastern Europe?

Primary skill: Comparison. Supporting skills: Cause and Effect, Chronology, Geographic Reasoning, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Two rules for this lesson: local people were not puppets, and communist governments were not politically identical. Domestic history, nationalism, leadership, geography, and economics mattered.
