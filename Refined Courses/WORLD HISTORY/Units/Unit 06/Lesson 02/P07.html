P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

China, Arms Race, Proxy Wars, and the Fall of Communism

Checkpoint question

How did Cold War rivalry spread beyond Europe, and why did communist governments follow different paths in China, the Soviet Union, and Eastern Europe?

Submit: 350–400 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 2 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Recommended four-paragraph organization

  1. Introduction: claim explaining globalization and different paths.
  2. China and rivalry: PRC, one Mao-era campaign, Sino-Soviet split, arms race or Cuban crisis.
  3. Proxy conflicts: compare Korea and Vietnam, include a local perspective and geography.
  4. Reform and outcomes: Gorbachev, a 1989 example, Berlin Wall, Soviet dissolution, and China’s continuing party rule.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 350–400 words; four developed paragraphs.
  • PRC plus Great Leap Forward or Cultural Revolution.
  • Sino-Soviet split and nuclear-deterrence paradox.
  • Korea and Vietnam accurately compared; Korean armistice not called a peace treaty.
  • At least one Korean or Vietnamese local perspective.
  • Gorbachev reforms, one 1989 case, Berlin Wall, and multiple causes of Soviet collapse.
  • China’s post-Mao economic reform and continued Communist Party rule.
  • Two map/timeline details and one meaningful comparison.
  • At least eight vocabulary terms.
Mastery requirement: Earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise using feedback, and resubmit.
