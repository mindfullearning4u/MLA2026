P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Nationalist Conflicts, Globalization, Trade Blocs, and Terrorism

Checkpoint

How have nationalism, globalization, economic integration, and political violence shaped the modern world?

Submit 350–425 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 7 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Four paragraphs

  1. Claim connecting nationalism and globalization.
  2. Nationalist conflict, territorial/geographic detail, and response.
  3. Trade trend, supply chain/multinational, trade bloc, benefit, cost, and worker/migrant perspective.
  4. 9/11, terrorism distinction, response/civil liberty, cooperation-vulnerability comparison, and conclusion.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 350–425 words; post-Cold War conflict; map detail.
  • Trade trend, quantitative source, supply chain/multinational, and trade bloc.
  • Economic benefit and social/economic cost; affected perspective.
  • 9/11, accurate terrorism definition, and government/international response.
  • Cause/effect, comparison, geographic reasoning, perspective, CER.
  • At least eight vocabulary terms and no religion/population stereotyping.
Mastery: Earn 80% to progress. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise, and resubmit.
