Nationalist Conflicts, Globalization, Trade Blocs, and Terrorism

Notebook evidence

Complete one concise row per source: claim, benefit/conflict/response, inference, and limitation.

Economic data: WTO/World Bank series show major long-run expansion in cross-border trade, with recessions and disruptions interrupting the trend.

Worker/community perspective: oral histories show that investment may create jobs while factory closure or outsourcing can harm other communities. Editorial synthesis, not quotation.

Trade agreement: NAFTA’s stated purpose included reducing trade barriers among Canada, Mexico, and the United States.

Nationalist source: competing claims in former Yugoslavia linked self-determination to disputed territory and minority security.

9/11 response: government and civilian records show grief, security action, alliance cooperation, and civil-liberty debate; extremist propaganda is not reproduced.
