P04 Historical Thinking

Nationalist Conflicts, Globalization, Trade Blocs, and Terrorism

Cause and Effect

  1. Nationalism: grievance/identity → territorial claim → state/international response → outcome.
  2. Globalization: technology + transport + trade policy + investment → economic and social effects.
  3. Terrorism: political objective + context + organization/enabling conditions → violence/fear → government response and consequences.
  4. Separate necessary evidence from coincidence, identify multiple causes, and test unintended effects.

Modeled reasoning

Improved transportation, digital communication, agreements, and investment spread production across countries. This created opportunity and made shocks cross borders faster. Technology did not automatically cause prosperity or political violence; institutions and choices shaped outcomes.

Example only; no notebook writing required.

Globalization’s gains are uneven; national identity persists; terrorism is not Islam; and separatism may use elections, negotiation, protest, insurgency, or sometimes terrorism.
