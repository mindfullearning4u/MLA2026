P05 Guided Investigation

Nationalist Conflicts, Globalization, Trade Blocs, and Terrorism

Claim

Modern interdependence increased cooperation and opportunity while also transmitting economic shocks and security risks; nationalism continued to shape territorial conflict and political choice.

  1. Use Yugoslavia to trace nationalism, state fragmentation, territorial claims, and response.
  2. Use the trade network and supply chain to explain interdependence.
  3. Compare EU, NAFTA, and ASEAN without treating them as equally integrated.
  4. Identify one benefit and one cost from data and community perspective.
  5. Define terrorism as a tactic; use 9/11 and a response while separating religion from extremism.
  6. Evaluate a security/civil-liberty tradeoff.

Notebook summary

Write 7–9 sentences connecting one nationalist conflict, one trade trend, one supply-chain effect, one trade bloc, 9/11, one government response, and one civil-liberty debate. Include both benefit and vulnerability and six vocabulary terms.
