What a completed row looks like

Factor: Security. Western example: fear of further Soviet political expansion. Soviet example: desire for friendly border governments after devastating invasions. Evidence: Churchill described a continental division. Contribution: each side treated measures described as defensive by the other as threatening. Limitation: security alone does not explain ideological conflict or every political decision.

Use this structure, but supply your own evidence and reasoning for every row.
