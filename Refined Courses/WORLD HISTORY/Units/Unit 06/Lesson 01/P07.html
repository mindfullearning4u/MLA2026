P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Cold War Europe, Ideology, and Early Conflict

Checkpoint question

Why did the wartime alliance between the United States and Soviet Union become a Cold War rivalry, and how did that rivalry divide Europe?

Submit: a 300–350 word response in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 1 Mastery Checkpoint Submission assignment.

Recommended organization

  1. Paragraph 1 — Introduction and causes: Answer the question with a defensible claim. Explain at least two postwar conditions, one ideological difference, and competing security concerns.
  2. Paragraph 2 — Evidence and escalation: Explain containment, the Truman Doctrine, the Marshall Plan, and one Western and one Soviet/Eastern perspective. Trace at least one action-response relationship.
  3. Paragraph 3 — Geography and division: Use a Germany/Berlin map detail, explain the Blockade and Airlift, and distinguish NATO in 1949 from the Warsaw Pact in 1955. End by explaining how rivalry divided Europe.

Sentence framework—not a response to copy

Claim: The wartime alliance broke down because ___, ___, and ___ interacted. Evidence: One source shows ___. The Berlin map shows ___. Reasoning: This mattered because ___. Qualification: Although ___, the evidence most strongly supports ___.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 300–350 words and three developed paragraphs.
  • At least two postwar conditions and one ideological difference.
  • Soviet security/influence and U.S./Western containment accurately explained.
  • Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, Berlin Blockade/Airlift, NATO, and Warsaw Pact used accurately.
  • At least one map detail, one Western primary-source detail, and one Soviet/Eastern perspective.
  • At least one action-response relationship.
  • Cause and Effect, Comparison, Geographic Reasoning, Perspective, and CER are visible.
  • At least seven vocabulary terms used accurately.
  • No claim that capitalism versus communism alone explains everything.
Mastery requirement: You must earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. If you do not reach 80%, meet with your TOR for feedback, revise, and resubmit the checkpoint.
