WORLD HISTORY • UNIT 6 • LESSON 1

P01 Lesson Overview

Cold War Europe, Ideology, and Early Conflict

Investigation Question

Why did the wartime alliance between the United States and Soviet Union become a Cold War rivalry, and how did that rivalry divide Europe?

Primary skill: Cause and Effect. Supporting skills: Comparison, Geographic Reasoning, Perspective, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Connection to Unit 5

Nazi Germany and Japan had been defeated, Britain and France were weakened, Germany was occupied, and the United States and Soviet Union emerged as superpowers. Their wartime cooperation against a common enemy did not erase earlier ideological differences or competing security interests.

Important: “Cold” does not mean peaceful. The two superpowers generally avoided direct large-scale war with each other, but Cold War rivalry contributed to proxy wars, repression, displacement, crises, and political violence.
