Completed example row

Factor: Weak mandate/resources. Example: Rwanda. Evidence: the UN mission had limited authority and resources during rapid escalation. Effect: peacekeepers could not automatically conduct a broad military campaign. Limitation: mandate weakness alone does not explain why member states failed to strengthen the response; political will also mattered.
