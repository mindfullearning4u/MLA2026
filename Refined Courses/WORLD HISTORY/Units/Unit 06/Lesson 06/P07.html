P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Genocide, Ethnic Cleansing, and Global Responses

Checkpoint question

Why do genocide and ethnic cleansing occur, and why have international responses sometimes failed to prevent mass atrocities?

Submit: 350–425 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 6 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Four-paragraph organization

  1. Introduction: precise definitions and a multi-cause claim.
  2. Cases: Cambodia, Bosnia/Srebrenica, and Rwanda; compare targeting, institutions, propaganda, and context.
  3. Response: two international-response examples, sovereignty/mandate/political-will limits, refugee consequence, and survivor perspective.
  4. Conclusion: prevention versus tribunal accountability, counterargument, and historical significance.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 350–425 words; four developed paragraphs recommended.
  • Accurate genocide/ethnic-cleansing distinction and Genocide Convention.
  • Cambodia, Bosnia/Srebrenica, and Rwanda.
  • Propaganda/dehumanization and a state/institutional role.
  • One survivor/victim-source detail and one refugee/displacement consequence.
  • Two international-response examples and one tribunal/accountability example.
  • Explicit comparison, cause/effect, perspective, historical significance, and CER.
  • At least eight vocabulary terms; no “inevitable hatred” or one-cause explanation.
Mastery requirement: Earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise using feedback, and resubmit.
