P05 Guided Investigation

Genocide, Ethnic Cleansing, and Global Responses

Claim to investigate

Mass atrocities result from interacting political conditions and organized human choices, while international protection can fail when authority, resources, political will, and timely action do not align.

Follow the reasoning

  1. Start with the Convention: classification requires protected group, prohibited act, and specific intent.
  2. For Cambodia, separate mass crimes against political/social groups from genocide findings involving protected groups.
  3. For Bosnia, connect forced displacement to ethnic cleansing and the Srebrenica evidence to tribunal genocide rulings.
  4. For Rwanda, explain historically shaped identities, extremist organization, propaganda, war, and individual/institutional choices.
  5. Compare what the UN and member states knew, what mandates/resources existed, and why action differed.
  6. Distinguish prevention from later accountability through tribunals.

Notebook summary

Write one 7–9 sentence comparison explaining: the legal distinction; one cause shared by Bosnia and Rwanda; one important difference; one human choice; two reasons international protection failed; and why later prosecution is not the same as prevention. Use at least six vocabulary terms.
