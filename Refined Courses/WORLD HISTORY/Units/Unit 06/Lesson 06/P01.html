P01 Lesson Overview

Genocide, Ethnic Cleansing, and Global Responses

Investigation Question

Why do genocide and ethnic cleansing occur, and why have international responses sometimes failed to prevent mass atrocities?

Primary skill: Comparison. Supporting skills: Cause and Effect, Perspective, Historical Significance, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Care and precision: This lesson discusses mass violence in age-appropriate language. Genocide is a specific legal category, not a synonym for every atrocity.
Use the legal terms precisely
CategoryGenocideEthnic cleansing
Primary intentDestroy, in whole or in part, a protected national, ethnic, racial, or religious groupRemove a population from a territory
MethodsActs listed in the Genocide Convention when joined to specific intentCoercion, intimidation, forced displacement, and violence
RelationshipThey can overlap, but they are not automatically identical. “Mass atrocity” is a broader analytical term.
