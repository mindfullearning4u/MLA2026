WORLD HISTORY • UNIT 6 • LESSON 5

P01 Lesson Overview

Science, Technology, Demography, Migration, and Pandemics

Investigation Question

How did scientific and technological change reshape population patterns, migration, communication, health, and daily life in the modern world?

Primary skill: Continuity and Change. Supporting skills: Cause and Effect, Geographic Reasoning, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Core idea: Innovation created benefits and unintended consequences, and it did not reach every society or population equally.

Demographic transition model

1
High birth
High death
2
Death rates fall
3
Rapid growth
4
Birth rates fall
5
Slower growth/stabilization

Not every society follows the same sequence or timing. Policy, culture, economics, migration, war, and public health affect patterns.
