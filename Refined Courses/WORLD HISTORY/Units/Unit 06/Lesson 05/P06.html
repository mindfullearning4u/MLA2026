Finished product checklist

  • Rank all five developments.
  • Use at least one quantitative source.
  • Connect migration or demography.
  • Address one counterargument.
  • Write a 6–8 sentence conclusion.

Example row

Development: Communication. Evidence: satellites, internet, and mobile networks accelerated information exchange. Benefit: faster education, commerce, and connection. Cost: misinformation and surveillance. Continuity: income and infrastructure still limit access through the digital divide.
