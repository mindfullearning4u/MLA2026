WORLD HISTORY • UNIT 6 • LESSON 5

P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Science, Technology, Demography, Migration, and Pandemics

Checkpoint question

How did scientific and technological change reshape population patterns, migration, communication, health, and daily life in the modern world?

Submit: 325–400 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 5 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Recommended organization

  1. Introduction: claim about transformation and uneven effects.
  2. Population and health: medicine/public health, demographic trend, Green Revolution, and quantitative evidence.
  3. Movement and connection: migration, urbanization, transportation, and communication technology.
  4. Tradeoffs and continuity: pandemic example, unequal access/digital divide, and one continuity.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 325–400 words and three or four developed paragraphs.
  • Medical/public-health development and demographic trend.
  • Migration example and transportation development.
  • Communication technology and Green Revolution.
  • Pandemic/public-health example presented historically.
  • One quantitative point or trend.
  • At least one cost, one unequal-access example, and one continuity.
  • Continuity and Change, Cause and Effect, Geographic Reasoning, and CER are visible.
  • At least seven vocabulary terms.
Mastery requirement: Earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise using feedback, and resubmit.
