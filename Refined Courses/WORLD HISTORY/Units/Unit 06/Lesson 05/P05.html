P05 Guided Investigation

Science, Technology, Demography, Migration, and Pandemics

Claim to investigate

Modern science and technology changed population, work, movement, communication, and health while preserving—and sometimes increasing—important inequalities and risks.

Follow the reasoning

  1. Use population data to describe change without claiming the graph alone proves causation.
  2. Connect lower mortality and food production to demographic change.
  3. Use migration and urbanization to show geographic consequences.
  4. Compare a benefit and cost of transportation, communication, and agriculture.
  5. Use a pandemic/public-health example historically, not as medical advice.
  6. Identify one continuity: unequal access, disease, migration, or infrastructure gaps.

Notebook summary

Write 5–7 sentences: two major changes, one quantitative trend, two causes, two benefits, two costs, and one continuing inequality. Use at least five vocabulary terms. Preserve useful reasoning; do not copy the whole guided process.
