P01 Lesson Overview

Modern Israel, Middle East Conflict, and Religious Fundamentalism

Investigation Question

How did nationalism, competing territorial claims, religion, foreign intervention, and historical experience contribute to conflict in the modern Middle East?

Primary skill: Cause and Effect. Supporting skills: Perspective, Geographic Reasoning, Contextualization, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Historical framing: Modern Israeli-Palestinian conflict is not proof that “Jews and Arabs have always fought.” It developed through modern nationalism, Ottoman collapse, imperial administration, migration, persecution, state formation, war, displacement, territory, security, religion, and foreign intervention.
