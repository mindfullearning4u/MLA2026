P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Modern Israel, Middle East Conflict, and Religious Fundamentalism

Checkpoint question

How did nationalism, competing territorial claims, religion, foreign intervention, and historical experience contribute to conflict in the modern Middle East?

Submit: 350–425 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 3 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Recommended four-paragraph organization

  1. Context and claim: Ottoman collapse, mandate rule, Zionism, and Arab/Palestinian nationalism.
  2. 1917–1949: Balfour, Holocaust/postwar migration, partition proposal, Israel’s creation, war, and Palestinian displacement.
  3. Territory and perspectives: a 1967 map detail, Israeli security, Palestinian sovereignty/occupation/refugees, and religion as one factor.
  4. Diplomacy and conclusion: Camp David or Oslo, unresolved issues, and a multi-causal judgment.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 350–425 words and four developed paragraphs.
  • Zionism and Arab/Palestinian nationalism accurately distinguished.
  • British Mandate/Balfour and Holocaust/postwar migration context.
  • UN proposal, 1948 war, Nakba/displacement, and one 1967 territorial detail.
  • At least two map details.
  • One Israeli source detail and one Palestinian source detail.
  • Israeli security and Palestinian sovereignty/occupation/refugee concerns.
  • One peace-process example.
  • Religion treated as one factor, never natural hatred.
  • At least eight vocabulary terms and no collective blame.
Mastery requirement: Earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise using feedback, and resubmit.
