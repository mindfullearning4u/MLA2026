P01 Lesson Overview

Decolonization, Nationalist Leaders, and Democratic Reform

Investigation Question

Why did European colonial empires decline after World War II, and why did newly independent states follow different political paths?

Primary skill: Comparison. Supporting skills: Cause and Effect, Perspective, Contextualization, and Claim-Evidence-Reasoning.

Core rule: Decolonization was not one uniform or automatically peaceful process. Leaders mattered, but mass movements, institutions, imperial responses, World War II, economics, social divisions, and international conditions also mattered.
