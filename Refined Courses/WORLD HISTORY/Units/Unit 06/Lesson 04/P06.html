Required notebook product

Question: Which factor most strongly determined whether decolonization produced a relatively stable transition or prolonged conflict?

Evaluate colonial institutions, nationalist leadership, mass participation, ethnic/religious divisions, colonial-power response, and Cold War/international involvement. Use India/Pakistan, Ghana, Kenya or Algeria, and South Africa.

Finished product checklist

  • Address one counterargument.

Example row

Factor: Colonial-power response. Example: French determination to retain settler-colonial Algeria intensified armed conflict. Evidence: sustained FLN warfare and French military response preceded 1962 independence. Effect: limited negotiated space and increased violence. Limitation: response alone does not explain Algerian nationalism or social organization.
