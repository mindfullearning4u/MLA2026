P07 Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Decolonization, Nationalist Leaders, and Democratic Reform

Checkpoint question

Why did European colonial empires decline after World War II, and why did newly independent states follow different political paths?

Submit: 350–425 words in the separate Unit 6 Lesson 4 Mastery Checkpoint Submission.

Recommended four-paragraph organization

  1. Claim and causes: explain at least three postwar causes.
  2. South Asia: Gandhi, one additional Indian/Pakistani leader, partition, and one map detail.
  3. African comparison: two cases; compare nonviolent/negotiated and armed strategies; add Cold War or nonalignment.
  4. Outcomes and reform: post-independence challenge, apartheid/South Africa, and a direct comparison.

Checkpoint checklist

  • 350–425 words and four developed paragraphs.
  • Three broad causes of decolonization.
  • India, Gandhi, and Nehru or Jinnah.
  • Partition as multi-causal plus one map detail.
  • At least two African cases.
  • One nonviolent/negotiated and one armed strategy.
  • Cold War or nonalignment and one post-independence challenge.
  • South African apartheid-to-democracy example.
  • Three primary-source details from different cases.
  • One direct outcome comparison and at least eight vocabulary terms.
Mastery requirement: Earn at least 80% to progress to the quiz. Below 80%, meet with your TOR, revise using feedback, and resubmit.
