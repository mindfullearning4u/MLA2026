P05 Guided Investigation

Decolonization, Nationalist Leaders, and Democratic Reform

Claim to investigate

Colonial empires declined through interacting global and local pressures, while differences in institutions, strategy, social divisions, and imperial response shaped each transition.

Follow the reasoning

  1. Identify at least three postwar pressures on empire.
  2. Compare India/Pakistan with Ghana, Kenya, and Algeria using the same categories.
  3. Separate “nonviolent strategy” from “absence of violence.”
  4. Explain local agency and nonalignment rather than treating new states as Cold War pieces.
  5. Distinguish independence from stable democratic institution-building.
  6. Use South Africa as a later democratic-reform comparison.

Notebook summary

Write 5–7 sentences: three shared causes, one negotiated/nonviolent pathway, one armed pathway, one reason outcomes differed, and one post-independence or democratic-reform challenge. Use at least five vocabulary terms. Do not copy the whole guided process.
