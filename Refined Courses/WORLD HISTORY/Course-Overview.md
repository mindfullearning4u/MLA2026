# World History Course Overview

Course: World History
Course number: 2109310
Credits: 1.0
Structure: 6 units, 8 lessons per unit
Production format: Moodle HTML lessons and Moodle XML assessments
Official standards source: CPALMS World History (#2109310), 2024-2026 version, retrieved 2026-07-08
Official source URL: https://www.cpalms.org/PreviewCourse/Preview/22644

## Course Purpose

World History develops students' ability to explain global change over time using historical evidence, geography, chronology, primary and secondary sources, maps, timelines, data, and comparison. The course covers civilizations and societies through the beginning of the 21st century and integrates World History, Geography, Humanities, ELA evidence expectations, and ELD support.

## Mastery Model

Each lesson is self-contained and written for asynchronous learning. The lesson pages provide the teaching. Students use the Teacher of Record for support, checkpoint feedback, intervention, retake workflow, and clarification when needed.

## Unit Sequence

1. Historical Thinking, Geography, and Medieval Foundations - Build historical inquiry, map skills, source analysis, and the transition from the Roman world to medieval societies.
2. Islamic Civilizations, African Kingdoms, and American Civilizations - Study Islamic expansion, African empires, and major Mesoamerican and Andean civilizations through cultural, political, economic, and geographic evidence.
3. Japan, Renaissance, Reformation, Scientific Revolution, and Exploration - Connect medieval Japan, Renaissance creativity, religious reform, scientific change, exploration, colonization, and forced labor systems.
4. Absolutism, Enlightenment, Revolutions, Industrialization, and Imperialism - Analyze political authority, Enlightenment ideas, revolutionary movements, industrialization, reform, nationalism, and imperialism.
5. World War I, Interwar Crisis, World War II, and the Holocaust - Examine global conflict, total war, authoritarianism, the Holocaust, Allied strategy, atomic warfare, and the consequences of World War II.
6. Cold War, Decolonization, Globalization, Genocide, and Contemporary World Issues - Study Cold War systems, decolonization, nationalism, conflict, globalization, terrorism, pandemics, science, and responses to genocide.

## Assessment Model

Each unit includes a pretest, guided practice for every lesson, lesson quizzes for Lessons 1-7, and a unit assessment in Lesson 8. Moodle XML is the only production assessment format. Lesson 8 is synthesis and does not have a lesson quiz.
