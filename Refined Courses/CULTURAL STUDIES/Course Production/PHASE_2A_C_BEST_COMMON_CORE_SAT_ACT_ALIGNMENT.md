# Cultural Studies Florida B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, and ACT Alignment

## Alignment Principle

Cultural Studies uses MLA.CS standards as the primary content standards. Florida/CPALMS, Florida B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, and ACT entries are support and readiness alignments only. They strengthen evidence reading, source analysis, interpretation of visuals and data, research, writing, communication, ethical reasoning, and college-readiness skills. They do not replace MLA.CS standards and must not be used to add content outside the approved unit and lesson mapping.

## Official Source Verification

| Support Layer | Official Source Checked | Course Use | Required Production Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Florida / CPALMS Social Studies | CPALMS standards/course search and related Social Studies benchmarks documented in the crosswalk | Culture, sociology, civics, geography, media, history, and globalization support | Use as support only; do not treat as a single governing Cultural Studies course record. |
| Florida B.E.S.T. ELA | CPALMS English Language Arts B.E.S.T. standards search | Evidence, argument, research, presentation, perspective, and digital media support | Every lesson must teach students how to read, cite, explain, and limit evidence. |
| Common Core Literacy | Official Common Core ELA-Literacy support families for history/social studies and writing | Sourcing, corroboration, evidence, research, argument, presentation, and source synthesis | Common Core support must not be treated as a primary course standard. |
| SAT Reading and Writing | College Board SAT Reading and Writing content domains | Information and Ideas, Craft and Structure, Expression of Ideas, Standard English Conventions, and informational graphics | Assessment questions should include short sources, visuals, charts, tables, or evidence prompts when needed. |
| ACT College and Career Readiness | ACT College and Career Readiness Standards | Reading, English, Writing, and Science-style data reasoning support | Lessons must require evidence, organization, interpretation, and teachable reasoning rather than recall only. |

## Required Support Carry-Through

Every unit and lesson must document:

- MLA.CS standard alignment
- Florida/CPALMS and Florida B.E.S.T. support when applicable
- Common Core literacy support when applicable
- SAT and ACT readiness support when applicable
- embedded source or visual support
- assessment stimulus needs
- student misconception prevention
- Teacher of Record support workflow

## Non-Negotiable Boundary

The crosswalk, unit mapping, and lesson mapping are the source of truth. Support standards may help students read, write, reason, cite, compare, interpret, and communicate, but they may not introduce unmapped content.
