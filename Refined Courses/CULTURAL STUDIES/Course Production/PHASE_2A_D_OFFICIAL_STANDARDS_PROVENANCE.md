# Cultural Studies Official Standards Provenance

## Source-Control Decision

Cultural Studies is an MLA catalog elective. Active CPALMS course and standard search pathways were checked for social studies, humanities, sociology, media literacy, history, geography, civics, and literacy support. No single official CPALMS Cultural Studies high school course record was located or used as a one-to-one governing source.

The course therefore uses internally numbered `MLA.CS` primary content standards. Florida/CPALMS Social Studies, Florida B.E.S.T. ELA, Common Core literacy, SAT Reading and Writing, and ACT college-readiness references are support layers only. They may strengthen reading, writing, source analysis, evidence reasoning, data/visual interpretation, communication, research, and readiness skills, but they must not add content outside the approved Cultural Studies unit and lesson mapping.
