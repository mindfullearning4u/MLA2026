Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Step 1 - Read the standard before the source. The lesson is limited to MLA.CS.HIS.1; MLA.CS.ID.2. That means your answer must focus on students analyze colonization, resistance, and cultural survival through multiple sources.
  2. Step 2 - Identify the cultural evidence. Use the resistance and survival timeline as the required support. Name the exact detail before writing a conclusion.
  3. Step 3 - Add context. Ask where, when, who, for what purpose, and for which audience. Context prevents guessing and keeps the answer accurate.
  4. Step 4 - Explain perspective. Decide whose viewpoint is visible and whose viewpoint may be missing. This keeps the analysis from becoming a stereotype.
  5. Step 5 - Connect evidence to reasoning. Use a because statement: the evidence suggests ___ because ___.
  6. Step 6 - Add a limitation. State what the evidence cannot prove alone and what additional source would help.
  7. Step 7 - Check for mastery. A complete answer includes evidence, context, perspective, standard alignment, and limitation.

Notebook Task

Write a five-sentence analysis for Colonization, Resistance, and Cultural Survival. Sentence 1 names the evidence. Sentence 2 gives context. Sentence 3 explains perspective. Sentence 4 connects to MLA.CS.HIS.1; MLA.CS.ID.2. Sentence 5 states a limitation or additional source needed.
