# Science Laboratory Architecture Compliance Audit

**Courses Reviewed:** Biology, Chemistry, Physics  
**Audit Purpose:** Determine whether current science course architecture and production packages explicitly support high school laboratory science designation, accreditation readiness, enrollment readiness, LMS deployment readiness, and MLA science department standards.  
**Audit Type:** Architecture compliance audit only. No lesson, assessment, HTML, JSON, GIFT, or LMS production files were created or modified.

---

## Executive Summary

Biology, Chemistry, and Physics all identify themselves as high school laboratory science courses in their Course Overview documents and include laboratory expectations in their architecture packages. Biology and Chemistry provide the clearest architecture-level lab controls because their Mapping Framework documents include explicit laboratory and virtual lab control sections and their Unit-Level Mapping files include lab/data emphasis across all six units.

Physics includes lab and virtual lab expectations in the Course Overview, Phase 1B architecture, lesson-level mapping, and audit reports. However, its Mapping Framework and Unit-Level Mapping are less explicit than Biology and Chemistry. Physics should be strengthened before lesson production by adding a dedicated laboratory and virtual lab controls section and a unit-level lab/data emphasis matrix.

**Final Course Decisions**

| Course | Final Status | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Biology | PASS | Lab designation, lab expectations, virtual lab expectations, unit integration, and lesson-level lab/data evidence are explicit enough for architecture approval. |
| Chemistry | PASS | Lab designation, lab expectations, virtual lab expectations, controlled conditions, measurement precision, and data emphasis are explicit and well integrated. |
| Physics | PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS | Lab identity is present, but lab/virtual lab architecture should be made more explicit in the Mapping Framework and Unit-Level Mapping before lesson production. |

---

## Audit Methodology

Reviewed the following architecture document types for each course where available:

- Course Overview
- Phase 1B Curriculum Architecture Analysis
- Standard Inventory
- Crosswalk Draft
- Mapping Framework
- Unit-Level Mapping
- Lesson-Level Mapping
- Architecture Audit Reports

Audit criteria included:

- Explicit laboratory science designation
- Lab safety, variables, controls, measurement, data collection, and data analysis
- Evidence-based claims and scientific communication
- Experimental design and scientific modeling
- Error, uncertainty, precision, and reliability
- Virtual labs, simulations, data-based investigations, graph analysis, and technology-supported investigation
- Unit-level and lesson-level integration
- Reviewer visibility from architecture documents alone

# Physics

## Laboratory Designation Status

**Status: PASS**

Physics is explicitly identified as a high school laboratory science course.

Evidence reviewed:

- `Refined Courses/PHYSICS/Course Production/Course-Overview.md:4` identifies the course as **High School Laboratory Science**.
- `Refined Courses/PHYSICS/Course Production/PHASE_1B_CURRICULUM_ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS.md:17` describes Physics as a quantitative laboratory-centered physical science.
- `Refined Courses/PHYSICS/Course Audit/FINAL_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md:27` verifies lab science expectations as passing.
- `Refined Courses/PHYSICS/Course Audit/PHYSICS_ARCHITECTURE_CONSISTENCY_AUDIT.md:77` and `:78` mark lab and virtual lab expectations as passing.

## Lab Expectation Status

**Status: PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS**

Physics architecture includes core lab expectations, especially in the Course Overview and Phase 1B analysis.

Explicitly present:

- Lab safety
- Measurement
- Procedures
- Tools and apparatus
- Data displays
- Error
- Interpretation
- Communication
- Variables
- Physical models
- Precision and reliability

Evidence reviewed:

- `Course-Overview.md:139` includes a dedicated **Lab Expectations** section.
- `PHASE_1B_CURRICULUM_ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS.md:26` requires lab safety, measurement, procedures, tools, apparatus, data displays, error, interpretation, and communication.
- `PHASE_3A_A_MAPPING_FRAMEWORK.md:38` states lab science expectations are supported across Units 1-6 through lab support.

Concern:

- Physics does not provide the same level of explicit lab-control detail in its Mapping Framework as Biology and Chemistry.
- The Unit-Level Mapping does not include a dedicated **Lab/Data Emphasis** column, so unit-by-unit lab visibility is weaker for accreditation review.

## Virtual Lab Status

**Status: PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS**

Physics architecture includes virtual lab expectations, but they should be made more explicit in the Mapping Framework and Unit-Level Mapping.

Explicitly present:

- Simulations
- Models
- Motion data
- Wave data
- Circuit simulations
- Optics diagrams
- Authentic data sets
- Video analysis
- Graph-based investigations
- Technology-system case data

Evidence reviewed:

- `Course-Overview.md:156` includes a dedicated **Virtual Lab Expectations** section.
- `PHASE_1B_CURRICULUM_ARCHITECTURE_ANALYSIS.md:27` states virtual investigation, simulation, graphical interpretation, and data analysis must be represented.
- `PHASE_3A_A_MAPPING_FRAMEWORK.md:39` includes graphing, simulation, modeling, circuit, wave, and motion data expectations.

Concern:

- Virtual lab expectations are present but are not organized into a dedicated controls section comparable to the stronger Biology, Chemistry, Earth/Space Science, and Marine Science models.

## Unit Integration

**Status: PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS**

Physics unit-level mapping supports lab and data expectations, but the unit mapping is less explicit than Biology and Chemistry.

| Unit | Lab Expectations Present? | Virtual Lab Expectations Present? | Evidence/Data Expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Yes | Yes | Lab safety, measurement, data displays, models, evidence, error |
| Unit 2 | Yes | Yes | Forces, orbital models, momentum evidence, motion/force data |
| Unit 3 | Yes | Yes | Energy, work, power, thermal data, conservation reasoning |
| Unit 4 | Yes | Yes | Wave behavior, sound/light data, optics models |
| Unit 5 | Yes | Yes | Circuit models, electrical data, magnetic field models |
| Unit 6 | Yes | Yes | Modern physics evidence, nuclear processes, cosmic systems, applied technology |

Concern:

- The lab/data expectations are inferable from standards and lesson evidence, but they are not as clearly visible in the Unit-Level Mapping file as they are in Biology and Chemistry.

## Lesson-Level Integration

**Status: PASS**

Physics lesson-level mapping supports:

- Lab investigations
- Lab checkpoints
- Data interpretation
- Mathematical and graphical models
- Scientific reasoning
- Error and evidence interpretation
- Virtual investigation through simulations and modeled systems

Examples:

- Unit 1 Lesson 2 includes lab safety, tools, and measurement.
- Unit 1 Lesson 3 includes data tables, graphs, and models.
- Unit 1 Lesson 4 includes precision, error, and evidence.
- Later units include motion data, force analysis, wave data, optics models, circuit evidence, and technology-supported applications.

## Accreditation Readiness

**Status: PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS**

A reviewer can identify Physics as a lab science course from the Course Overview, Phase 1B architecture, Lesson-Level Mapping, and audit reports. However, the lab architecture is not as reviewer-visible at the unit mapping and mapping framework level as Biology and Chemistry.

## Physics Findings

Strengths:

- Lab designation is explicit.
- Course Overview includes Lab Expectations and Virtual Lab Expectations.
- Phase 1B architecture includes lab, simulation, graphing, and data analysis expectations.
- Lesson-level mapping supports lab and data-based scientific reasoning.
- Architecture audit reports already identify lab expectations as passing.

Deficiencies requiring recommendation before lesson production:

1. The Mapping Framework should include a dedicated **Laboratory and Virtual Lab Controls** section, similar to Biology, Chemistry, Earth/Space Science, and Marine Science.
2. The Unit-Level Mapping should include a unit-by-unit **Lab/Data Emphasis** field or a separate Physics lab matrix so accreditation reviewers can see lab integration without inferring it from lesson evidence.

## Physics Required Recommendations

Before Physics lesson production begins, add one of the following:

1. A dedicated section in `PHASE_3A_A_MAPPING_FRAMEWORK.md` titled **Laboratory and Virtual Lab Controls** that explicitly includes:
   - Lab safety
   - Measurable variables
   - Controlled conditions
   - Measurement precision
   - Data collection
   - Graph analysis
   - Mathematical modeling
   - Error and uncertainty
   - Evidence-based claims
   - Scientific communication
   - Virtual labs, simulations, and technology-supported investigations

2. A unit-level lab visibility tool, either by:
   - Adding a **Lab/Data Emphasis** column to `PHASE_3A_B_1_UNIT_LEVEL_MAPPING.md`, or
   - Creating a separate `PHYSICS_LAB_AND_VIRTUAL_LAB_MATRIX.md`.

## Physics Final Status

**PASS WITH RECOMMENDATIONS**
