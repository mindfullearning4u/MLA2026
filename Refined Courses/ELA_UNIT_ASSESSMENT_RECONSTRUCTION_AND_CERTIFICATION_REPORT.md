# ELA Department Unit Assessment Reconstruction and Certification Report

## Executive Summary

The ELA Department Unit Assessments were reconstructed and re-audited against MLA requirements. The final certification audit was run after reconstruction and returned zero findings.

All reviewed Unit Assessments now contain exactly 40 Moodle-ready multiple-choice questions with standards, four answer choices, one correct answer, feedback for every answer choice, no HTML, no duplicate question prompts, no duplicate answer choices, and standards present in Lessons 1-7 metadata.

## Reconstruction Scope

- Repaired English I Unit Assessments from 25 to 40 questions.
- Repaired English II Unit Assessments from 25 to 40 questions.
- Repaired English III Unit Assessments from 25 to 40 questions.
- Repaired English IV Unit Assessments from 25 to 40 questions.
- Repaired Creative Writing Unit Assessments from 25 to 40 questions.
- Repaired Journalism Unit Assessments from 30 to 40 questions.
- Speech Communication was not part of the count repair scope, but Unit 3 was corrected after a duplicate/generic-question issue was discovered during certification.

## Reconstruction Method

Existing valid Unit Assessment questions were preserved where possible. New or replacement questions were drawn from the same unit's Lessons 1-7 Guided Practice and Lesson Quiz sources, with standard tags corrected from lesson metadata when needed. No future-unit standards were introduced.

## Final Certification Checks

- 40 questions exactly
- Lessons 1-7 represented through detected lesson standards
- Standards present on every question
- Standards found in Lessons 1-7 lesson metadata
- No duplicate question prompts
- No duplicate answer choices
- One correct answer only
- Four answer choices per question
- Feedback for every answer choice
- Moodle-compatible GIFT formatting
- No HTML in GIFT files
