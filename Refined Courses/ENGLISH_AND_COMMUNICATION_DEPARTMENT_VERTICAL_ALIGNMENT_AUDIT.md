# English & Communication Department Vertical Alignment Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

This department-level audit reviewed the certified architecture packages for English I, English II, English III, English IV, Speech Communication, Creative Writing, and Journalism. The review used only certified local architecture documents: architecture analysis, standard inventory, crosswalk draft, mapping framework, unit-level mapping, lesson-level mapping, course overview, and final architecture audit or approved local audit trail.

Audit result: `PASS`

The English & Communication department is architecture-ready. All seven courses preserve the MLA mastery model, use a six-unit structure, map 48 lessons, preserve Lesson 8 as Putting It All Together, place Unit Assessment in Lesson 8, and prevent new primary standards from being introduced in Lesson 8. Standards coverage, course identity, workload controls, publication pathways, accreditation documentation, and enrollment-facing architecture documentation are sufficient for production architecture lock.

English & Communication Department:

PASS — APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION AND DEPARTMENT ARCHITECTURE LOCK

## 11. Strengths

- The department uses a consistent MLA mastery architecture across all seven courses.
- All seven courses use 6 units and 48 mapped lessons.
- Lesson 8 is consistently reserved for Putting It All Together and Unit Assessment.
- Standards coverage is auditable from inventory to crosswalk to unit map to lesson map.
- English I-IV create a coherent ELA progression from foundations to advanced analysis, research, effectiveness evaluation, and publication.
- Speech Communication, Creative Writing, and Journalism have distinct identities and appropriate production pathways.
- Specialized courses extend, rather than duplicate, English I-IV: Speech extends oral/professional communication, Creative Writing extends creative production, and Journalism extends factual media publication.

## 14. Recommended Improvements

- Create `ENGLISH_AND_COMMUNICATION_TEXT_SELECTION_MATRIX.md` as a department-level planning-control document before lesson production.

- Add a department text-bank audit after text selection but before lesson drafting.
- Add course identity checks to future lesson-production audits so specialized courses do not drift into English literature, Speech, Creative Writing, or Journalism unintentionally.
- Maintain a publication pathway tracker for English IV, Speech Communication, Creative Writing, and Journalism.
