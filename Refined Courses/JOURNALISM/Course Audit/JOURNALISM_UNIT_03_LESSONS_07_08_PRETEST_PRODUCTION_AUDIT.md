## Instructional Quality Review

Lesson 7 accurately teaches oral presentation of reporting as a journalism communication skill. The lesson focuses on verified facts, speaker notes, listener needs, source attribution, pacing, and clear audience action without turning the course into a general speech course.

Lesson 8 functions as Unit 3 synthesis and introduces no new primary standard. It requires students to apply Unit 3 news, feature, editorial, revision, conventions, vocabulary, style, and oral briefing skills to one journalism package scenario.

Both lessons include embedded source material, explicit vocabulary, step-by-step instruction, modeled examples, common mistake correction, guided practice, independent application, checkpoint criteria, and Moodle-ready assessments.
