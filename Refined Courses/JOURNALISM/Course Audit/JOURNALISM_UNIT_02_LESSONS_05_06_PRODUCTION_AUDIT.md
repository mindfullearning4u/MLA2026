## Instructional Quality Review

Lessons 5 and 6 support independent mastery for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners. Each lesson includes explicit explanation, sequenced teaching, embedded source material, modeled examples, guided reasoning, and clear checkpoint expectations.

Lesson 5 teaches students how to preserve source meaning through accurate notes, paraphrase, and quotation decisions. The lesson repeatedly distinguishes reporting from rewriting and reinforces that paraphrase must keep the source's meaning, scope, and attribution intact.

Lesson 6 teaches students how evidence and reasoning work together in reporting. The lesson moves students from identifying relevant evidence to explaining how evidence supports a claim, while also teaching students to acknowledge limits in available information.
