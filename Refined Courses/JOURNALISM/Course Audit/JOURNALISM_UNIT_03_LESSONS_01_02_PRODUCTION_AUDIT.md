## Instructional Quality Review

Lesson 1 teaches expository news writing step by step. It supports independent learners by explicitly identifying the news, selecting lead facts, ordering supporting details, using source attribution, and avoiding unsupported opinion.

Lesson 2 teaches feature writing and point of view step by step. It supports independent learners by distinguishing straight news from feature writing, identifying a human-interest angle, selecting accurate source details, and maintaining respectful journalistic tone without inventing facts.

Both lessons include embedded source material, explicit vocabulary, modeled examples, common mistake correction, guided practice, independent application, checkpoint criteria, and Moodle-ready assessment files.
