## Instructional Quality Review

Lesson 7 teaches integrated source verification and interview planning step by step. It supports remedial learners by explicitly separating confirmed facts, verification gaps, and follow-up interview questions. It supports standard learners through modeled reporting decisions and guided practice. It supports accelerated learners by requiring purposeful source selection, neutral question design, and evidence-based reasoning.

Lesson 8 functions as Unit 2 synthesis and does not introduce a new primary standard. It reviews the full Unit 2 reporting process: source verification, source comprehension, inference, paraphrase, evidence, reasoning, and interview planning. The lesson prepares students for the Unit Assessment without becoming a catch-all for untaught standards.

## Corrective Actions During Audit

The first audit pass confirmed the required page structure and assessment counts. A content-compliance review then identified that GIFT questions needed the source scenario embedded directly in each item. The GIFT files were revised so every Guided Practice, Lesson Quiz, Unit Assessment, and Pretest question includes the relevant source text inside the question.

After revision, the audit was rerun.
