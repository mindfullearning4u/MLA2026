## Instructional Quality Review

Lessons 3-4 meet the MLA self-paced mastery model.

- Lessons teach source comprehension and inference explicitly before independent application.
- Embedded source material is included directly in the lesson pages.
- Instruction distinguishes stated facts, supporting details, paraphrase, inference, assumption, evidence, reasoning, and limits.
- Notebook tasks require students to show thinking step by step.
- Worked examples model source reading and evidence-based reasoning.
- Common mistakes use red/green contrast with teachable explanations.
- Guided Practice and quizzes align only to the approved lesson standards.
- Assessment feedback explains how to self-correct.
