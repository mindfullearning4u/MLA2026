## Embedded Source and Passage Audit

The course was checked against the MLA requirement that any passage, scenario, source, or media package needed for a task or assessment item must be included directly where the student needs it.

Findings:

- Source-based lessons embed the required source material.
- Scenario-based Guided Practice and assessment items include the needed scenario/source context.
- Students are not required to search elsewhere for required source material.
- Standalone journalism-knowledge questions do not require artificial source labels.

Embedded source/passage decision: **PASS**

## Self-Paced Learning Audit

The course was reviewed through the MLA mastery-based online model. Students learn primarily through the curriculum itself, while the Teacher of Record monitors progress, provides feedback, grades submissions, and supports intervention.

Findings:

- Lessons provide step-by-step instruction rather than only directions.
- Notebook tasks guide students through the thinking process.
- Worked examples model journalism reasoning.
- Common mistake sections provide corrective feedback.
- Checkpoints clearly explain what students submit and how mastery is judged.
- Course design supports remedial learners through explicit instruction.
- Course design supports standard learners through guided application.
- Course design supports accelerated learners through independent production and synthesis tasks.

Self-paced learning decision: **PASS**
