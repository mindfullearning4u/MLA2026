## Content Alignment Certification

The actual lesson pages were reviewed for alignment to the mapped standard and lesson focus. The review included instructional text, examples, notebook tasks, independent work, and checkpoint tasks.

The audit confirmed:

- Content directly teaches the mapped primary standard.
- Support standards are represented through appropriate lesson context.
- Lesson titles, learning targets, tasks, examples, independent work, and checkpoints align to the certified lesson mapping.
- P04 examples model the lesson skill through journalism-specific reasoning.
- P06 independent work applies the mapped lesson skill.
- P07 checkpoint tasks align to the mapped standards and lesson purpose.
- No future-unit standards or non-journalism course drift were identified.
- The course remains Journalism-focused and does not drift into general English, Creative Writing, or Speech Communication.

Some lessons are concise, but targeted review showed the pages still align to the mapped Journalism skill. For example:

- Unit 2 Lesson 2 P04 teaches reliable and valid source selection through official records, expert sources, and bias checks.
- Unit 3 Lesson 7 P06 applies oral presentation through a reporting briefing plan, speaker notes, and listener-focused organization.
- Unit 5 Lesson 2 P04 teaches sequential images and media in motion through shot choice, close-ups, narration, and accuracy.
- Unit 5 Lesson 5 P04 teaches production workflow through export timing, parallel task management, and caption revision.
