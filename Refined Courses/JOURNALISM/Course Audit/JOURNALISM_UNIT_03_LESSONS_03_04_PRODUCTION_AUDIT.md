## Instructional Quality Review

Lesson 3 accurately teaches editorial claims, evidence, counterclaims, and rebuttal as journalism skills. The lesson keeps editorial writing grounded in source evidence and explicitly prevents unsupported opinion, overstatement, and unfair dismissal of opposing concerns.

Lesson 4 accurately teaches revision for clarity and cohesion. The lesson distinguishes revision from simple proofreading, teaches students to remove unsupported opinion, improve information order, preserve source meaning, and prepare writing for publication.

Both lessons include embedded source material, explicit vocabulary, step-by-step instruction, modeled examples, common mistake correction, guided practice, independent application, checkpoint criteria, and Moodle-ready assessments.
