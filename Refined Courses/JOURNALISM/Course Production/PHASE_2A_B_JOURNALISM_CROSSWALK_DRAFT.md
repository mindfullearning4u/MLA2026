# Phase 2A-B: Journalism Crosswalk Draft

## Executive Summary

This crosswalk aligns the verified Journalism MLA standards to the official CPALMS `Journalism 1 (#1006300)` benchmark set and to broad Common Core, SAT, ACT, college readiness, and workforce readiness categories. The alignments are planning-level architecture alignments, not assessment items or lesson content.

All 33 official CPALMS-listed standards appear exactly once.
