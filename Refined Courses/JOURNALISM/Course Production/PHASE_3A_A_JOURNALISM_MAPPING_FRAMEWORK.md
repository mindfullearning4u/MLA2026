# Phase 3A-A: Journalism Mapping Framework

## Executive Summary

This mapping framework defines how Journalism standards are distributed across the six-unit, eight-lesson MLA production structure. It preserves the approved architecture format while adapting the progression to reporting, source verification, news writing, ethics, media literacy, digital journalism, and publication.

This document is mapping only. It does not create lessons, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, LMS pages, or student-facing instructional content.

## Mapping Principles

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| Standards-first architecture | All unit and lesson placements derive from the verified CPALMS Journalism 1 standard list. |
| Journalism identity | ELA and visual/media standards are applied to factual reporting, media production, ethics, and publication. |
| Mastery model | Standards are introduced, practiced, reinforced, and synthesized across units. |
| Lesson 8 control | Lesson 8 remains Putting It All Together, contains Unit Assessment only, and introduces no new primary standard. |
| Publication pathway | The course progresses from foundations and reporting into writing, media literacy, digital production, and publication. |

## Unit Progression Model

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Domains | Progression Function |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of Journalism, News Judgment, and Ethics | ETH, NEWS, MED, INT | Establishes journalism purpose, ethics, format, media context, newsroom norms, and audience-aware factual communication. |
| 2 | Reporting, Source Verification, and Interviewing | REP, INT, MED | Builds inquiry, source verification, evidence, paraphrase, inference, complex text comprehension, and interviewing. |
| 3 | News, Feature, and Editorial Writing | NEWS, REP, COM | Develops factual writing forms: news/expository, features/narrative, editorial/argument, revision, tone, and conventions. |
| 4 | Media Literacy, Rhetoric, and Audience | MED, NEWS, ETH | Analyzes media structure, support, purpose, rhetoric, opposing claims, mood/effect, media history, and design trends. |
| 5 | Digital Journalism, Multimedia, and Publication | DIG, PUB, ETH, REP | Builds digital presentation, sequential media, visual accuracy, online collaboration, production management, and publication. |
| 6 | Newsroom Portfolio, Publication, and Multimedia Synthesis | All | Synthesizes the full journalism workflow without new instruction in Lesson 8. |
