📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the drama club draft and source facts to prepare a publication-ready correction plan.

Part A

Find five convention errors in the rough draft. Label each error as capitalization, punctuation, quote accuracy, sentence boundary, title formatting, or source fact check.

Part B

Rewrite the draft as a polished short news brief. Preserve every source fact and use accurate quotation punctuation.

Part C

Choose two corrections and explain why each one matters for publication readiness.

Mastery Support Checklist

  • Did I compare the draft to source facts?
  • Did I correct capitalization and punctuation?
  • Did I preserve the exact quote?
  • Did I fix sentence boundaries?
  • Did I avoid adding new information?
