Checkpoint Task

Submit your polished drama club news brief and a correction explanation. Include at least five convention corrections and explain two of them in detail.

Mastery Criteria

  • The edited brief preserves source facts.
  • Publication conventions are corrected.
  • The direct quote is accurate.
  • Sentence boundaries are clear.
  • The explanation shows why conventions affect credibility.
