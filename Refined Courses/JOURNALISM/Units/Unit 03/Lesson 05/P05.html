Guided Practice Strategy

Use this process before answering:

  1. Identify the convention problem.
  2. Check the source facts.
  3. Choose the correction that fixes the error without changing meaning.
  4. Reject answers that introduce new facts or weaken attribution.

Publication conventions are part of credibility. Careless copy can make accurate reporting look unreliable.
