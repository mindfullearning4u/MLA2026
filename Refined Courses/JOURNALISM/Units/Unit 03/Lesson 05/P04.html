P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Correct Capitalization

Original: the drama club will perform friday.

Modeled thinking: The sentence begins with a lowercase word, and Friday is a proper day name.

Correction: The Drama Club will perform Friday.

Reasoning: Capitalization makes the copy publication-ready and helps readers recognize proper names.

Example 2: Fix Sentence Boundaries

Original: The play is Friday tickets are five dollars.

Modeled thinking: This contains two complete ideas without correct punctuation.

Correction: The play is Friday at 7:00 p.m. Tickets cost $5.

Reasoning: Two clear sentences help readers understand schedule and cost.

Example 3: Check Direct Quotation

Original: The club president said, we want people to see how much work everyone has done.

Modeled thinking: The source gives the speaker's name and exact words.

Correction: Club president Lena Ortiz said, "We want people to see how much work everyone has done."

Reasoning: Direct quotations need quotation marks and accurate attribution.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Correct grammar by changing facts or rewriting the source quote in your own words.

Correct: Correct conventions while preserving the source facts and exact quotation.

Teachable explanation: Copyediting is not a license to alter reporting. It improves presentation while protecting accuracy.
