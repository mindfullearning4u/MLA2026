📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the rough library draft and source facts to create a revision plan. Your work should show how you improve clarity, cohesion, conventions, and accuracy.

Part A

Identify three problems in the original draft. Label each as clarity, cohesion, convention, or unsupported opinion.

Part B

Rewrite the draft as a clear four- to five-sentence news brief. Use only the source facts.

Part C

Write a short explanation of two revision choices you made. Explain how each choice helps readers understand the information.

Mastery Support Checklist

  • Did I remove unsupported opinion?
  • Did I keep all facts accurate?
  • Did I organize information logically?
  • Did I fix sentence boundaries and conventions?
  • Did I explain why my revisions help readers?
