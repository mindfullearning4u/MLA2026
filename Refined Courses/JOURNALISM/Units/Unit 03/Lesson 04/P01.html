P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Revision for clarity and cohesion

This lesson teaches how journalists revise writing so readers can follow the information easily. Revision is not just fixing commas. It includes improving clarity, order, transitions, sentence focus, and publication readiness.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04 - Revision for Clarity and Cohesion.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.05 - Conventions for Publication; MLA.JOUR.ETH.01 - Accuracy and Responsible Publication.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify unclear or unfocused news writing.
  • How to revise sentence order for stronger reader understanding.
  • How cohesion connects ideas from one sentence to the next.
  • How conventions and accuracy support publication readiness.

What You Will Do

  • Study a rough news draft and source facts.
  • Identify opinion, weak organization, and unclear wording.
  • Practice revising for clarity and cohesion.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Revise a draft without changing source meaning.
  • Improve order, clarity, transitions, and conventions.
  • Remove unsupported opinion from news writing.
  • Submit a checkpoint revision with explanation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can revise journalism so it is accurate, clear, connected, and ready for readers.
