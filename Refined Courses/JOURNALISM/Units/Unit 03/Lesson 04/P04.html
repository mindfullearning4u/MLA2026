📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Remove Unsupported Opinion

Original: It is a really good idea.

Modeled thinking: A news article should not tell readers what opinion to have.

Revision: The pilot is intended to give students a quiet place to finish homework after clubs and tutoring.

Reasoning: The revision replaces opinion with an attributed purpose from the source.

Example 2: Improve Cohesion

Original order: buses, librarian, sign-ins, opinion, schedule.

Modeled thinking: Readers need the main schedule change before the smaller details.

Revised order: schedule change, purpose, sign-in rule, bus time, review process.

Reasoning: This order helps readers move from main news to practical details.

Example 3: Fix Sentence Control

Original problem: One sentence contains several unrelated details joined by and.

Modeled thinking: Each sentence should have a clear job.

Revision: Students must sign in at the front desk. Activity buses will leave at 5:10 p.m.

Reasoning: Separating the details makes instructions easier to follow.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Revise by adding exciting details that are not in the source.

Correct: Revise by making the verified source details clearer, better ordered, and grammatically correct.

Teachable explanation: Revision improves communication. It does not give a writer permission to invent information.
