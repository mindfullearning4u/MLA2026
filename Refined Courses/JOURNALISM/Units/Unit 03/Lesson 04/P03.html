P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now move from diagnosing problems to making revisions. A strong revision should improve the writing without changing the facts.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Revise for factual focus. Replace opinion with verified information.

Step 2: Revise for sentence control. Break long, crowded sentences into shorter sentences with one main job each.

Step 3: Revise for cohesion. Use connected order: main news, reason, directions, next step.

Step 4: Revise for conventions. Check punctuation, capitalization, spelling, and sentence completeness.

Step 5: Compare the revision to the source. Make sure the revision did not add, remove, or distort important facts.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Draft for Revision

Original draft: The library pilot is starting next week and it is a really good idea because students need places and the buses are leaving at 5:10 and the librarian said the library is quiet and the school will look at sign-ins. Also students should use it because it helps.

Source facts available: The library will stay open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays for four weeks. Students must sign in at the front desk. Activity buses leave at 5:10 p.m. The librarian says the purpose is to give students a quiet place to finish homework after clubs and tutoring. The principal says the school will review sign-in numbers before deciding whether to continue the schedule.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The library pilot is amazing and all students should use it because it helps everyone.

Correct: The library will remain open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays during a four-week pilot program designed to give students a quiet place to finish homework.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect revision adds opinion and overstates the source. The correct revision uses verified facts, clear order, and neutral news tone.

Notebook Directions

Revise the first sentence of the original draft. Then explain which problem you fixed: clarity, cohesion, convention, or unsupported opinion.
