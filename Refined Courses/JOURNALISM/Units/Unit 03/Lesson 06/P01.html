📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Vocabulary, Derivations, and News Style

This lesson teaches how journalists choose precise words, explain unfamiliar terms, and use derivations to understand and write news clearly.

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.06 - Academic and Journalistic Vocabulary; MLA.JOUR.NEWS.07 - Word Derivations and Meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.08 - Voice, Tone, and Style; MLA.JOUR.INT.02 - Professional Communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How vocabulary affects reader understanding.
  • How to define technical or academic terms in context.
  • How prefixes, roots, and suffixes can help explain meaning.
  • How news style uses precise, concise, and audience-friendly wording.

What You Will Do

  • Study source notes about a recycling initiative.
  • Analyze words such as initiative, contamination, recyclable, and concise.
  • Practice choosing precise news-style wording.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Define unfamiliar vocabulary using context.
  • Use word parts to support meaning.
  • Revise vague wording into precise news style.
  • Submit a checkpoint vocabulary and style explanation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use precise words and explain vocabulary so readers understand the news without confusion.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot explain a vocabulary word in student-friendly language or if your wording sounds too vague for news style.
