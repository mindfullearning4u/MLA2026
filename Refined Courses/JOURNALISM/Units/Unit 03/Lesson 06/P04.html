P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Use Context to Define a Term

Term: contamination

Modeled thinking: The sentence explains that contamination happens when non-recyclable items are mixed with recyclable materials.

News-style definition: Contamination occurs when the wrong items are placed with recyclable materials.

Reasoning: This definition is accurate and understandable for readers.

Example 2: Use Derivation

Word: recyclable

Modeled thinking: The suffix -able means able to be. The root connects to recycle.

Meaning: able to be recycled.

Reasoning: Word parts help readers understand precise vocabulary.

Example 3: Revise for News Style

Vague wording: Students should not put random stuff in bins.

Modeled thinking: Random stuff is too informal and unclear.

Revision: Students should keep non-recyclable items out of the recycling bins.

Reasoning: The revision is specific, concise, and respectful.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use technical vocabulary without explaining it because it sounds official.

Correct: Use precise vocabulary and explain unfamiliar terms in context.

Teachable explanation: News style should help readers understand. A precise term is useful only when the reader can follow it.
