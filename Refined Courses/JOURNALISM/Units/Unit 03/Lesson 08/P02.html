P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Unit 3 Journalism Package Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: combining several skills to complete a larger task.
  • News brief: a concise factual explanation of important information.
  • Feature angle: the human-interest focus of a story.
  • Editorial claim: a supportable opinion for an editorial.
  • Publication readiness: accuracy, clarity, conventions, and style needed before readers see the work.

Embedded Source

Embedded Unit 3 Synthesis Scenario

The student newspaper is preparing a package about a new school recycling initiative. Source notes show that labeled bins will be placed near the cafeteria, gym, and library. The environmental club adviser says the goal is to reduce contamination, which happens when non-recyclable items are mixed with recyclable materials. A student volunteer says concise signs will help students sort items quickly. The editorial board is considering an opinion piece arguing that the school should also add a short recycling reminder to morning announcements. The package needs a factual news brief, a feature angle, an editorial claim with evidence and rebuttal, clear revision, publication conventions, vocabulary support, and a short oral briefing.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the factual news brief task. Readers need to know where the recycling bins will be placed and why the initiative matters.

Step 2: Identify the feature possibility. A feature angle could focus on student volunteers helping classmates sort items correctly.

Step 3: Identify the editorial task. The editorial board may argue for recycling reminders in morning announcements, but the claim needs evidence and a fair response to concerns about announcement time.

Step 4: Identify revision and conventions needs. The package must be clear, concise, accurate, and publication-ready.

Step 5: Identify vocabulary support. The term contamination needs a reader-friendly explanation.

Step 6: Identify oral briefing needs. A short announcement should tell students where bins are and how to sort items correctly.

Notebook Directions

Create a Unit 3 synthesis chart with six rows: News Brief, Feature Angle, Editorial Claim, Revision/Conventions, Vocabulary Support, and Oral Briefing. Fill each row using the embedded source.

Model Example

Vocabulary Support: Define contamination as wrong items being mixed with recyclable materials.

Why this works: The term is important to the story, and the definition helps readers understand the recycling problem.
