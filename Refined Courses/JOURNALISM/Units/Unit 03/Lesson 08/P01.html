📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 3 synthesis

This lesson brings together Unit 3 news, feature, editorial, revision, conventions, vocabulary, style, and oral presentation skills. You will apply the full Unit 3 writing and communication process to one journalism package scenario.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 3 Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.01, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.02, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.05, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.06, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.07, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.08, MLA.JOUR.COM.01.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.02 and MLA.JOUR.INT.02.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 3 writing skills work together in one journalism package.
  • How to choose the right writing approach for news, feature, editorial, and briefing tasks.
  • How revision, conventions, vocabulary, and style support publication readiness.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 3 Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the Unit 3 synthesis source.
  • Plan a factual news brief, feature angle, editorial claim, revision choices, vocabulary support, and oral briefing.
  • Complete guided synthesis practice and independent work.
  • Complete the Unit 3 Assessment. There is no Lesson 8 quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Apply multiple Unit 3 standards to one source scenario.
  • Choose the correct journalism form for the task.
  • Use evidence, revision, conventions, vocabulary, and presentation choices accurately.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 3 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use Unit 3 journalism skills together to create accurate, clear, publication-ready writing and communication.
