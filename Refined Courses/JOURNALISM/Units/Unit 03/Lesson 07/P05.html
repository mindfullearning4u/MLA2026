Embedded Source File: Broadcast Briefing Notes

The student newsroom will give a one-minute morning announcement about the school library staying open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays during a four-week pilot program. The librarian says the schedule gives students a quiet place to finish homework after clubs and tutoring. Students must sign in at the front desk, and activity buses leave at 5:10 p.m. The principal says sign-in numbers will be reviewed before the school decides whether to continue the schedule.

Guided Practice Strategy

Use this process before answering:

  1. Identify the main news.
  2. Check which detail listeners need next.
  3. Choose wording that is accurate and easy to hear.
  4. Reject answers that add opinion, change the facts, or overload the listener.

Oral reporting should sound clear, verified, and useful.
