P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Oral Presentation of Reporting

This lesson teaches how to turn verified reporting into a short oral briefing. A journalism presentation must be accurate, organized, audience-aware, and easy to follow.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.COM.01 - Oral Presentation of Reporting.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03 - Expository News Writing; MLA.JOUR.REP.02 - Evidence and Reasoning in Reporting.

What You Will Learn

  • How to select the most important facts for an oral briefing.
  • How oral reporting differs from a written news brief.
  • How to organize a short presentation for audience understanding.
  • How to use evidence and source attribution when speaking.

What You Will Do

  • Study a source file for a one-minute announcement.
  • Identify the lead information, supporting details, and closing reminder.
  • Practice writing speaker notes that are accurate and concise.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Create a clear oral reporting outline.
  • Use verified facts and attribution.
  • Organize information for listeners.
  • Submit checkpoint speaker notes aligned to MLA.JOUR.COM.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can present reported information out loud in a way that is accurate, organized, and useful for listeners.
