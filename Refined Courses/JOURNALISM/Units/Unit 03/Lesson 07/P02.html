P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Planning an Oral News Briefing

Vocabulary

  • Oral briefing: a short spoken report that gives listeners important information clearly.
  • Speaker notes: organized notes used to guide a presentation without reading a full article word for word.
  • Audience cue: wording that helps listeners know what information matters.
  • Pacing: the speed and rhythm of speech.
  • Attribution: naming the source of information.

Embedded Source

Embedded Source File: Broadcast Briefing Notes

The student newsroom will give a one-minute morning announcement about the school library staying open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays during a four-week pilot program. The librarian says the schedule gives students a quiet place to finish homework after clubs and tutoring. Students must sign in at the front desk, and activity buses leave at 5:10 p.m. The principal says sign-in numbers will be reviewed before the school decides whether to continue the schedule.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the announcement purpose. The purpose is to tell students that the library will stay open later on Tuesdays and Thursdays during a four-week pilot.

Step 2: Choose what listeners need first. Listeners need the schedule change before the reason or review process.

Step 3: Add practical details. Students need to know they must sign in and that buses leave at 5:10 p.m.

Step 4: Attribute source information. The librarian explains the purpose, and the principal explains how the pilot will be reviewed.

Step 5: Write speaker notes, not a crowded paragraph. Speaker notes should be short phrases in logical order so the presenter can speak clearly.

Step 6: End with a listener action. Remind students when the library is open and what they need to do if they stay.

Notebook Directions

In your notebook, create four headings: Opening, Key Details, Source Attribution, and Closing Reminder. Use the embedded source to add information under each heading.

Model Example

Opening: Library open until 5:00 p.m. Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Key Detail: Four-week pilot; students sign in; buses leave at 5:10 p.m.

Attribution: Librarian says the schedule supports homework after clubs and tutoring.

Closing Reminder: Sign in at the front desk before using the extended hours.
