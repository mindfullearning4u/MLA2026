P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your oral briefing sounds like a written paragraph, changes a source fact, or does not tell listeners what they need to know.

Submission Workflow

  • Complete P02 through P06.
  • Review your speaker notes for accuracy and order.
  • Submit your checkpoint response.
  • Complete the Lesson 7 quiz after submission.

Checkpoint Task

Submit your one-minute oral briefing plan. Include an opening sentence, organized speaker notes, a closing reminder, and a short explanation of how your briefing supports listener understanding.

Mastery Criteria

  • The briefing begins with the main news.
  • Speaker notes are accurate and organized.
  • Source attribution is used where needed.
  • The closing gives a clear listener action.
  • The explanation shows awareness of audience and oral delivery.
