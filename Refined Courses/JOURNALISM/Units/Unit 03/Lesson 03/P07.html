P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your claim is too broad, your evidence does not support your claim, or your rebuttal ignores a reasonable concern.

Submission Workflow

  • Complete P02 through P06.
  • Review your claim, evidence, counterclaim, and rebuttal for accuracy.
  • Submit your checkpoint response.
  • Complete the Lesson 3 quiz after submission.

Checkpoint Task

Submit your editorial argument plan. Include one claim, two evidence sentences with reasoning, one counterclaim, and one rebuttal. Each part must connect directly to the outdoor seating source.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim is clear and supportable.
  • Evidence is relevant and accurately used.
  • Reasoning explains the evidence.
  • The counterclaim is fair.
  • The rebuttal responds to the counterclaim with logic or evidence.
