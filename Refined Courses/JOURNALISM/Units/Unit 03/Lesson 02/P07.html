Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your feature opening sounds exaggerated, invents emotion, or does not clearly focus on a human-interest angle.

Submission Workflow

  • Complete P02 through P06.
  • Check your feature opening for accuracy and tone.
  • Submit your checkpoint response.
  • Complete the Lesson 2 quiz after submission.

Checkpoint Task

Submit your feature-opening plan. Include your human-interest angle, three supporting source details, your two- to three-sentence feature opening, and one explanation of how your point of view shapes the reader's understanding.

Mastery Criteria

  • The feature angle is clear and source-based.
  • The opening uses concrete reported details.
  • The writing maintains respectful tone.
  • The point of view is focused.
  • No unsupported or invented information appears.
