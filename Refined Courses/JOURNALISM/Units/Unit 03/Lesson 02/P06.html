📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 03 | Lesson 02

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the Maya Chen source to plan a feature opening. Your goal is to create reader interest while staying accurate and respectful.

Part A

Identify one human-interest angle from the source. Explain why that angle is stronger than simply saying the story is about bicycles.

Part B

Choose three source details that support your angle. For each detail, explain what it helps the reader understand.

Part C

Write a two- to three-sentence feature opening. Use a concrete detail, maintain a respectful tone, and avoid inventing feelings or facts.

Mastery Support Checklist

  • Did I choose a human-interest angle?
  • Did I use only reported details?
  • Did I avoid exaggeration?
  • Did my point of view guide the reader?
  • Did my tone respect the source?
