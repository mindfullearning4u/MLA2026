Embedded Source File: Feature Profile Notes

Senior Maya Chen repairs donated bicycles after school and gives them to students who need transportation. She started after noticing two classmates walking more than two miles home after practice. Maya says fixing the bikes takes patience because every repair teaches her something different. Her adviser says Maya keeps careful notes, asks for help when a repair affects safety, and treats each recipient respectfully. One student says receiving a bike made it easier to stay for tutoring.

Guided Practice Strategy

Use this process before answering:

  1. Identify the human-interest angle.
  2. Check whether the detail is actually in the source.
  3. Decide whether the tone is respectful and accurate.
  4. Choose the answer that creates interest without exaggeration.

Feature writing should be vivid, but it must remain reported journalism.
