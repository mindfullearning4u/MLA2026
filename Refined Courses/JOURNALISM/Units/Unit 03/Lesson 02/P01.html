P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Feature writing and point of view

This lesson teaches how feature writing uses a focused point of view, human detail, and accurate source information to help readers understand a person, issue, or experience.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.01 - Feature Writing and Point of View.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.08 - Voice, Tone, and Style; MLA.JOUR.INT.02 - Professional Source Communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How feature writing differs from a straight news brief.
  • How point of view focuses the reader on a human angle.
  • How to use descriptive detail without inventing facts.
  • How voice and tone support respectful source representation.

What You Will Do

  • Study a feature profile source file.
  • Identify the human-interest angle.
  • Practice writing a feature opening that stays accurate.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify the best feature angle from source details.
  • Write from a clear journalistic point of view.
  • Use tone that respects the source and audience.
  • Submit a checkpoint feature-opening plan aligned to MLA.JOUR.NEWS.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can write a feature opening that uses real source details to help readers understand a person or experience.
