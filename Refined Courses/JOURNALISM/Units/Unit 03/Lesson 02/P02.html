P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Finding the Feature Angle and Point of View

Vocabulary

  • Feature story: journalism that explores a person, experience, issue, or human angle using accurate reporting.
  • Point of view: the focused lens that shapes how the story is told.
  • Human-interest angle: the part of a story that helps readers understand people, choices, and impact.
  • Voice: the style or personality of the writing.
  • Tone: the writer's attitude toward the subject and audience.

Embedded Source

Embedded Source File: Feature Profile Notes

Senior Maya Chen repairs donated bicycles after school and gives them to students who need transportation. She started after noticing two classmates walking more than two miles home after practice. Maya says fixing the bikes takes patience because every repair teaches her something different. Her adviser says Maya keeps careful notes, asks for help when a repair affects safety, and treats each recipient respectfully. One student says receiving a bike made it easier to stay for tutoring.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the person or experience at the center. Maya Chen is the center of this feature because her repair work connects effort, skill, and student need.

Step 2: Identify the human-interest angle. The angle is not simply bicycles. The angle is how one student uses repair skills to help classmates with transportation.

Step 3: Choose point of view. A strong journalistic point of view could focus on Maya's careful process, the students helped, or the way a practical problem led to a service project.

Step 4: Select details that support the angle. Use details about walking home, repair notes, safety checks, and tutoring access.

Step 5: Keep voice respectful and accurate. Do not turn Maya into a superhero or invent private feelings. Let the source details create interest.

Notebook Directions

In your notebook, create three headings: Human-Interest Angle, Source Details, and Tone Choices. Use the embedded source to fill each section.

Model Example

Human-Interest Angle: A student turns bike repair into transportation support for classmates.

Source Detail: Maya noticed classmates walking more than two miles home after practice.

Tone Choice: Respectful and specific, not exaggerated.
