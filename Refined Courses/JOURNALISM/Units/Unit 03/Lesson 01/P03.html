P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now you will turn source notes into news structure. Strong expository news writing is not just a list of facts. It is a clear explanation organized around what readers need first.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Start with the change. Readers need to know the library schedule is changing.

Step 2: Add the time and start date. These details help readers act on the information.

Step 3: Explain the reason with attribution. The librarian says the goal is to give students a quiet place after clubs and tutoring.

Step 4: Add logistics. Sign-in and bus time are practical details that matter after the main news is clear.

Step 5: End with what happens next. The school will review sign-in numbers after the four-week pilot.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Source File: Library Hours News Brief Notes

The school library will stay open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays beginning next week. The librarian says the change is meant to give students a quiet place to finish homework after clubs and tutoring. The pilot program will run for four weeks. Students must sign in at the front desk, and activity buses will leave at 5:10 p.m. The principal says the school will review sign-in numbers before deciding whether to continue the schedule.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The library finally has a great schedule because students obviously needed it.

Correct: The school library will stay open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays and Thursdays beginning next week as part of a four-week pilot program.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect sentence adds opinion and assumes what students need. The correct sentence explains the verified change in a factual news style.

Notebook Directions

Draft one lead sentence for the library-hours story. Then write one sentence explaining why your lead uses facts instead of opinion.
