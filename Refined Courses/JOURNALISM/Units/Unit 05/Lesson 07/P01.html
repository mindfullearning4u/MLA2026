Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Digital Publication Package

This lesson teaches how to combine article text, accurate visuals, sequence, audio, and publication workflow into one coherent digital journalism package.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.PUB.01 - Online Collaborative Publication; MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 - Digital Equipment and Visual Accuracy.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.DIG.01 - Digital Presentations; MLA.JOUR.DIG.02 - Sequential Images and Media in Motion; MLA.JOUR.REP.05 - Time Management and Production Process.

What You Will Learn

  • How publication-ready digital journalism combines text, visuals, sequence, and layout.
  • How to judge whether a visual image is accurate enough to publish.
  • How sequence helps an audience understand a process.
  • How a production checklist prevents deadline and accuracy problems.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded digital package scenario.
  • Identify publication risks before release.
  • Practice choosing accurate visuals and logical sequence.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain why a digital package is or is not publication-ready.
  • Choose visual corrections that protect accuracy.
  • Organize media parts for audience understanding.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.PUB.01 and MLA.JOUR.DIG.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can prepare an online journalism package that uses accurate visuals, organized digital media, and a clear publication plan.
