P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now evaluate slide choices. A strong presentation slide does one clear job. It should not crowd the screen with every note from the reporting process.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Ask what the audience needs first. The audience needs to know the central finding.

Step 2: Add context. The audience needs to see where the crossing problem happens.

Step 3: Add evidence. Use the count and source comments to support the concern.

Step 4: Add recommendation. Explain the short-term cone solution and clearer markings.

Step 5: End responsibly. Include the source note and next step so the audience knows the report is evidence-based, not rumor.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Presentation Plan: Transit Safety Report

Reporting Topic: Students crossing the east parking lot after dismissal.

Source Notes: The school safety aide counted thirty-seven students crossing between parked cars on Tuesday. Two students said the marked crosswalk is hard to see when buses are lined up. The assistant principal said the school can add temporary cones while it reviews permanent signs.

Presentation Goal: Explain the safety concern clearly and recommend a short-term solution without exaggerating the problem.

Draft Slide Sequence: Slide 1: headline and central finding. Slide 2: photo of the crossing area with caption. Slide 3: key evidence from the count and student comments. Slide 4: recommendation for cones and clearer crosswalk markings. Slide 5: source note and next step.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Put every interview note on one slide so the audience sees all the information.

Correct: Use one slide for the main evidence and keep each detail connected to the safety concern and recommendation.

Teachable explanation: Crowding a slide makes the presentation harder to understand. A clear digital presentation organizes evidence so the audience can follow the report.

Notebook Directions

Choose two slides from the draft sequence. For each slide, write one sentence explaining what the slide should show and one sentence explaining why the audience needs it.
