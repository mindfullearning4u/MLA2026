Checkpoint Task

Submit a five-slide outline for the transit safety presentation. For each slide, include the slide purpose, one piece of content, and one sentence explaining how it supports audience understanding.

Mastery Criteria

  • Organizes slides logically.
  • Uses credible evidence from the embedded source.
  • Connects visual choices to audience understanding.
  • Avoids exaggeration and unsupported claims.
