P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Digital Presentations for Reporting

This lesson teaches how to turn reporting into a clear digital presentation. A journalism presentation is not a decoration project. It must communicate a focused perspective, use credible evidence, and guide the audience through information in a logical order.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.DIG.01 - Digital Presentations.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.COM.01 - Oral Presentation of Reporting; MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03 - Expository News and Feature Writing.

What You Will Learn

  • How to build a digital presentation from reporting notes.
  • How to organize slides around one clear perspective.
  • How to use evidence without overwhelming the audience.
  • How presentation choices support journalistic accuracy.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded transit safety presentation plan.
  • Identify the presentation goal, evidence, and slide sequence.
  • Practice choosing slide content that supports audience understanding.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose evidence that fits the reporting purpose.
  • Explain why a slide sequence is logical.
  • Connect visual choices to audience understanding.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.DIG.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can create a clear digital journalism presentation with organized ideas, credible evidence, and a focused perspective.
