P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Time Management and Production Workflow

This lesson teaches how journalism teams use time management to complete production work accurately before publication. A deadline does not remove the need for verification. A strong workflow helps the team finish on time while still protecting quality.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.REP.05 - Time Management and Production Process.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.ETH.02 - Collaborative Media Production; MLA.JOUR.PUB.01 - Online Collaborative Publication.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify urgent production tasks.
  • How to sequence workflow before a deadline.
  • How to protect accuracy while working quickly.
  • How team roles support final publication quality.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded broadcast package workflow.
  • Identify tasks, blockers, deadlines, and accuracy risks.
  • Build a priority list for publication readiness.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Prioritize workflow tasks logically.
  • Explain how verification fits into production timing.
  • Connect team roles to publication readiness.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.REP.05.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can manage a journalism production process so the team meets the deadline without sacrificing accuracy or quality.
