P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Production Workflow and Deadline Planning

Set up your notebook to track deadline, task, role, risk, and next action.

Vocabulary

  • Workflow: the ordered process a team follows to complete production.
  • Deadline: the time by which work must be complete.
  • Blocker: a problem that prevents the next task from being completed.
  • Verification: checking information for accuracy before publication.
  • Export: creating the final file for publication or release.

Embedded Source

Embedded Production Scenario: Friday Broadcast Package Workflow

Project: The newsroom must publish a two-minute Friday broadcast package about the school supply drive.

Deadline: Final package must be submitted by Thursday at 3:30 p.m. for Friday morning release.

Team Tasks: Reporter drafts the script, video editor cuts interview clips, photo editor adds two accurate still images, copy editor checks names and captions, and producer exports the final file.

Current Status: The interview clips are recorded, but one student name needs spelling confirmation. The script is missing a transition between the donation total and the volunteer quote. The still image caption does not identify the collection table location. The export settings have not been checked.

Workflow Need: The team must prioritize tasks, protect accuracy, meet the deadline, and avoid publishing before verification is complete.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the deadline. The package must be submitted Thursday at 3:30 p.m.

Step 2: List required roles. Reporter, video editor, photo editor, copy editor, and producer each own a different task.

Step 3: Identify blockers. The name spelling, missing transition, vague caption, and unchecked export settings all block publication readiness.

Step 4: Prioritize accuracy tasks first. Confirming the student name and fixing the caption protect factual accuracy.

Step 5: Prioritize flow next. The missing transition affects coherence, so the script must be revised before final recording or export.

Step 6: Export last. Export settings should be checked after content is verified and revised.

Notebook Directions

Create a workflow chart with four columns: task, role responsible, reason it matters, and when it should happen. Add at least five tasks from the embedded source.

Model Example

Task: Confirm student name spelling.

Role: Copy editor or reporter.

Reason it matters: Incorrect names damage credibility and should be corrected before export.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: If this journalism skill feels new, begin by defining the key term, identifying the audience and purpose, and finding one fact, source detail, or text feature that connects to the task.

Standard path: Complete the task in order: identify the journalistic choice, connect it to accuracy, fairness, clarity, or audience need, and explain why it strengthens or weakens the piece.

Accelerated extension: After your answer is complete, strengthen it by using precise journalism vocabulary, comparing two reporting or editing choices, or explaining how the choice affects credibility, ethics, or reader trust.
