Embedded Source

Embedded Production Scenario: Field Photo Accuracy Check

Assignment: Create accurate visual images for a school news brief about the new water bottle refill station outside the gym.

Available Images: Image A shows the actual refill station outside the gym with the blue installation sign visible. Image B shows an older hallway water fountain near the cafeteria. Image C is a close-up of a student filling a bottle, but the station label is cropped out. Image D shows the refill station after the caption writer added the location and date.

Reporting Notes: The station opened Monday. The facilities manager said it is the first refill station installed near the gym. The student government sponsor said the project was funded by a campus wellness grant.

Accuracy Requirement: The final visual must show the correct station, avoid misleading cropping, and include a caption that identifies what the audience is seeing.

Part A

Choose the best image for the news brief. Explain why it is the most accurate choice.

Part B

Identify one image that should not be used. Explain how it could mislead the audience.

Part C

Write an accurate caption for the selected image using the reporting notes.
