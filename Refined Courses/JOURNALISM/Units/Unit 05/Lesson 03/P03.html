P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now move from image selection to caption accuracy. A caption must identify what the audience sees and connect the image to the report.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: State what the image shows. The image shows the new water bottle refill station outside the gym.

Step 2: Add verified context. The station opened Monday and is the first near the gym.

Step 3: Avoid unsupported claims. Do not say the station will solve every water access problem unless the notes support that claim.

Step 4: Keep the caption specific. "New station outside gym" is clearer than "students use water."

Step 5: Check audience effect. A strong caption helps readers understand why the image is included.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Production Scenario: Field Photo Accuracy Check

Assignment: Create accurate visual images for a school news brief about the new water bottle refill station outside the gym.

Available Images: Image A shows the actual refill station outside the gym with the blue installation sign visible. Image B shows an older hallway water fountain near the cafeteria. Image C is a close-up of a student filling a bottle, but the station label is cropped out. Image D shows the refill station after the caption writer added the location and date.

Reporting Notes: The station opened Monday. The facilities manager said it is the first refill station installed near the gym. The student government sponsor said the project was funded by a campus wellness grant.

Accuracy Requirement: The final visual must show the correct station, avoid misleading cropping, and include a caption that identifies what the audience is seeing.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use Image B because it is clearer and brighter.

Correct: Use Image D because it shows the correct refill station and includes the location and date in the caption.

Teachable explanation: A clear image is not enough if it shows the wrong subject. Accuracy matters more than convenience or visual appeal.

Notebook Directions

Write one accurate caption for Image D. Then write one sentence explaining why Image B should not be used for this report.
