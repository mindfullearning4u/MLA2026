P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Digital Equipment and Accurate Visual Images

This lesson teaches how digital equipment and visual choices affect journalistic accuracy. A visual image is evidence. If the image shows the wrong location, crops out necessary context, or lacks a clear caption, it can mislead the audience.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 - Digital Equipment and Visual Accuracy.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.02 - Evidence and Reasoning; MLA.JOUR.ETH.01 - Format-Specific Quality Work.

What You Will Learn

  • How to select accurate visual evidence for a news brief.
  • How captions protect audience understanding.
  • How cropping and location errors can mislead readers.
  • How to justify visual choices with source evidence.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded field photo accuracy scenario.
  • Compare image choices for accuracy and usefulness.
  • Practice writing captions that identify what the audience is seeing.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Select the most accurate image for a report.
  • Explain why a visual choice is accurate or misleading.
  • Use evidence from reporting notes to justify visual decisions.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.DIG.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use digital images accurately so readers understand what really happened and where it happened.
