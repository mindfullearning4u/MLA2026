P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the embedded collaborative publication scenario to show that you can prepare online journalism for publication. Write in complete sentences.

Embedded Source

Embedded Publication Scenario: Collaborative Online Feature Package

Publication Task: A student newsroom team is preparing an online feature package about the school garden reopening.

Draft Elements: The reporter wrote a 450-word feature. The photo editor added three images with captions. The copy editor left comments asking for a clearer transition between the garden history and the reopening date. The web editor needs a headline, deck, image order, alt text, and export-ready layout.

Audience: Students, families, and community volunteers who may want to visit or help maintain the garden.

Feedback Notes: One paragraph repeats the same background detail twice. One caption says "students working" but does not identify the garden bed. The final paragraph needs a clearer call to action for volunteers.

Publication Requirement: The team must revise for clarity, respond to comments, maintain format-specific quality, and export a polished online article for the intended audience.

Part A

Identify three revision or publication tasks the team must complete before export.

Part B

Choose one feedback note and explain the exact revision action the team should take.

Part C

Write a final publication checklist for the web editor. Include headline, deck, captions, alt text, layout, and audience fit.
