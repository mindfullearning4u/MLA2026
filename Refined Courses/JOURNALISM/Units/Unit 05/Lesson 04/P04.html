📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 05 | Lesson 04

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Question: Which revision should address repeated background?

Modeled thinking: Repetition weakens clarity and cohesion. I should combine or remove the repeated detail.

Answer: The team should revise the repeated paragraph so the background appears once and leads smoothly into the reopening information.

Reasoning: This improves clarity and flow for readers.

Example 2

Question: How should the weak caption be fixed?

Modeled thinking: "Students working" is too vague. The audience needs to know what garden bed and what action the photo shows.

Answer: The caption should identify the garden bed and explain the student work connected to reopening.

Reasoning: A specific caption makes the image useful evidence.

Example 3

Question: Why include alt text before export?

Modeled thinking: Online publication must be accessible. Alt text helps readers who use screen readers understand images.

Answer: Alt text should be added before export so the online article is accessible and publication-ready.

Reasoning: Accessibility is part of quality online publication.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Online publication is ready when the article has pictures.

Correct: Online publication is ready when the writing is revised, captions are specific, alt text is added, layout is checked, and the article fits the audience.

Teachable explanation: Images are only one part of publication. Format-specific quality includes clarity, accessibility, and audience fit.
