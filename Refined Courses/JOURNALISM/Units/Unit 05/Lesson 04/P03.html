P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now prioritize revisions. In a newsroom, not all tasks are equal. Accuracy, clarity, audience use, and format requirements come before decorative preferences.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Fix repeated or unclear writing. Repeated background slows readers down and weakens cohesion.

Step 2: Fix transitions. A transition should help the reader move from garden history to the reopening date.

Step 3: Fix captions. Captions must identify the image and connect it to the story.

Step 4: Add accessibility. Alt text makes images usable for more readers.

Step 5: Check export readiness. Before export, the team should confirm headline, deck, image order, captions, alt text, and call to action.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Publication Scenario: Collaborative Online Feature Package

Publication Task: A student newsroom team is preparing an online feature package about the school garden reopening.

Draft Elements: The reporter wrote a 450-word feature. The photo editor added three images with captions. The copy editor left comments asking for a clearer transition between the garden history and the reopening date. The web editor needs a headline, deck, image order, alt text, and export-ready layout.

Audience: Students, families, and community volunteers who may want to visit or help maintain the garden.

Feedback Notes: One paragraph repeats the same background detail twice. One caption says "students working" but does not identify the garden bed. The final paragraph needs a clearer call to action for volunteers.

Publication Requirement: The team must revise for clarity, respond to comments, maintain format-specific quality, and export a polished online article for the intended audience.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Export the article first and fix comments later if someone notices.

Correct: Resolve comments, revise for clarity, check captions and alt text, and then export the article for publication.

Teachable explanation: Publication-ready work must be polished before it is shared. Exporting too early can publish errors to the audience.

Notebook Directions

Write a prioritized revision list with three items. For each item, explain why it matters to the audience or publication format.
