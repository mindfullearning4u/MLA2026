P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Planning a Sequential Journalism Video

Set up your notebook to track each shot, its purpose, its visual accuracy, and its narration.

Vocabulary

  • Storyboard: a plan that shows the order of images or shots.
  • Sequence: the order in which visual information appears.
  • Wide shot: a shot that shows the setting or full scene.
  • Close-up: a shot that focuses on a specific detail.
  • Visual accuracy: showing information correctly so the audience is not misled.

Embedded Source

Embedded Storyboard: Hallway Recycling Video

Project Goal: Create a 30-second news video showing how students should use the new hallway recycling stations.

Sequence: Shot 1: wide shot of the hallway station. Shot 2: close-up of the labels for paper, bottles, and trash. Shot 3: student holds a plastic bottle and pauses before choosing a bin. Shot 4: student places the bottle in the correct bin. Shot 5: final screen says Check the label before you drop it.

Sound Plan: Short narration explains each step. Light background sound is allowed only if it does not cover the narration.

Accuracy Note: The video must show the real labels and correct bin placement so viewers do not learn the wrong process.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the communication problem. Students need to know how to use the new hallway recycling stations.

Step 2: Start with context. A wide shot shows where the station is.

Step 3: Move to detail. A close-up of labels teaches the audience what choices are available.

Step 4: Show the decision moment. The student pauses before choosing a bin, which models checking the label.

Step 5: Show the correct action. The bottle goes into the correct bin.

Step 6: Reinforce the message. The final screen gives a short reminder that matches the video purpose.

Step 7: Protect sound clarity. Background sound cannot cover narration because the audience needs to hear the instruction.

Notebook Directions

Create a storyboard chart with columns for shot number, image, purpose, narration, and accuracy check. Fill in all five shots from the embedded source.

Model Example
