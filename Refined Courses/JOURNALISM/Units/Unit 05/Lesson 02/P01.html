P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Sequential Images and Media in Motion

This lesson teaches how sequential images and media in motion can solve a journalism communication problem. A video sequence must be accurate, logical, and useful to the audience.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.DIG.02 - Sequential Images and Media in Motion.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.MED.08 - Technology and Media Design Trends; MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 - Digital Equipment and Visual Accuracy.

What You Will Learn

  • How a sequence of shots teaches a process.
  • How motion, image order, and sound support journalism.
  • How visual accuracy protects audience understanding.
  • How to plan media in motion before recording.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded recycling video storyboard.
  • Analyze shot order, narration, and visual accuracy.
  • Practice selecting images that solve a communication problem.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain why a shot sequence is logical.
  • Identify accurate visual choices.
  • Connect sound and image choices to audience understanding.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.DIG.02.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can plan a sequence of images, motion, and sound that communicates journalistic information accurately.
