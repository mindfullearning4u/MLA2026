P04 Worked Example

Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

Embedded Source

Unit 5 Digital Publication Scenario. Project: The journalism staff is preparing a final online package called New Student Guide. Required parts: a digital presentation explaining three key findings, a sequential image strip showing how to find student services, an accurate feature photo, a revised article, and an online publication page. Team Needs: the package must be cohesive, visually accurate, captioned, reviewed, exported, submitted on time, and appropriate for new students and families. Production Risk: one image is outdated, one caption is vague, the article needs final revision, and the team has two days before publication.

Example 1: Outdated Image

Step 1: Identify the problem: one image is outdated.

Step 2: Connect to the standard: MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 requires accurate visual images.

Step 3: Correct the problem: replace the image or verify and recaption it.

Reasoning: A new student guide must not show old information as if it is current.

Example 2: Vague Caption

Step 1: Identify the problem: one caption is vague.

Step 2: Connect to publication readiness: readers need clear context.

Step 3: Correct the caption with who, what, where, and why it matters.

Reasoning: Captions are reporting, not decoration.

Example 3: Two-Day Deadline

Step 1: Identify remaining tasks.

Step 2: Assign order: revise article, verify visuals, check sequence, export, submit.

Step 3: Use teamwork to divide tasks without losing cohesion.

Reasoning: Time management protects quality when publication is close.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treat Lesson 8 as a place to add new standards or new skills that were not taught earlier.

Correct: Use Lesson 8 to synthesize Unit 5 skills: digital presentation, sequence, visual accuracy, publication workflow, time management, and collaboration.

Teachable explanation: A final unit synthesis should make students apply already-taught concepts in a connected way. It should not hide new expectations in the assessment.
