P01 Lesson Overview

Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

This lesson pulls together the Unit 5 digital journalism skills you have already learned. It does not introduce a new primary standard.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 5 Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.DIG.01, MLA.JOUR.DIG.02, MLA.JOUR.DIG.03, MLA.JOUR.PUB.01, MLA.JOUR.REP.05, MLA.JOUR.ETH.02.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04, MLA.JOUR.ETH.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 5 digital journalism skills work together.
  • How to evaluate a complete online publication package.
  • How to check visuals, sequence, revision, collaboration, and workflow before final submission.

What You Will Do

  • Review a final embedded digital publication scenario.
  • Practice identifying publication risks.
  • Complete guided practice and independent synthesis work.
  • Complete the Unit 5 Assessment instead of a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Evaluate whether a digital journalism package is publication-ready.
  • Use Unit 5 standards to justify corrections.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence and complete the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can combine digital presentation, image sequence, accurate visuals, teamwork, deadlines, and online publication into one responsible journalism package.
