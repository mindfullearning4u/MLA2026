Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Unit 5 Digital Journalism Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: combining several skills or ideas into one stronger judgment.
  • Cohesive package: media parts that feel connected in purpose, tone, order, and audience.
  • Publication risk: a problem that could weaken accuracy, clarity, fairness, deadline control, or audience trust.
  • Final review: the last check before a package is submitted or published.

Embedded Source

Unit 5 Digital Publication Scenario. Project: The journalism staff is preparing a final online package called New Student Guide. Required parts: a digital presentation explaining three key findings, a sequential image strip showing how to find student services, an accurate feature photo, a revised article, and an online publication page. Team Needs: the package must be cohesive, visually accurate, captioned, reviewed, exported, submitted on time, and appropriate for new students and families. Production Risk: one image is outdated, one caption is vague, the article needs final revision, and the team has two days before publication.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Name the package purpose. The package helps new students and families understand school services.

Step 2: Match each media part to its job. The digital presentation explains findings, the image strip shows a process, the feature photo adds context, the article explains details, and the publication page organizes everything.

Step 3: Find risks. Outdated images, vague captions, unrevised writing, and weak teamwork are not small details. They can make the package confusing or inaccurate.

Step 4: Connect each risk to a standard. Outdated image connects to visual accuracy. Vague caption connects to publication readiness. Article revision connects to revision. Deadline pressure connects to workflow and collaboration.

Notebook Directions

Create a Unit 5 synthesis chart with three columns: problem, standard connection, and correction. Add at least four rows from the embedded source.

Model Example

Problem: One image is outdated.

Standard connection: MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 because digital images must be accurate.

Correction: Replace the image or verify and recaption it before publication.
