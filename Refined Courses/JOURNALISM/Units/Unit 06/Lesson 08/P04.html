Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

Embedded Source

Final Journalism Capstone Scenario. Task: The student newsroom is submitting the final course portfolio and publication package. Required evidence: a revised article, a verified reporting note, a media-literacy audience check, a multimedia sequence, a collaborative publication workflow, a final online portfolio page, and a short oral presentation outline. Current Risks: one article transition is weak, one claim needs clearer evidence reasoning, one media headline overstates the audience opinion, one photo caption lacks date context, and the team has not assigned final export and submission roles. Goal: submit one accurate, revised, audience-ready, publication-quality journalism portfolio that demonstrates skills from the full course.

Example 1: Weak Article Transition

Step 1: Identify the problem: one transition is weak.

Step 2: Connect it to revision for clarity and cohesion.

Step 3: revise the transition so the article ideas connect smoothly.

Modeled thinking: A final article should guide the reader from one idea to the next.

Example 2: Overstated Headline

Step 1: Identify the problem: the headline overstates audience opinion.

Step 2: Connect it to media literacy and author purpose.

Step 3: revise the headline so it reflects the evidence fairly.

Explicit reasoning: A headline can mislead even when the article contains accurate details.

Example 3: Missing Team Roles

Step 1: Identify the workflow problem: final export and submission roles are not assigned.

Step 2: Connect it to collaboration and time management.

Step 3: assign roles before submission.

Modeled thinking: A strong team prevents last-minute errors by making responsibility clear.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treat Lesson 8 as a place to learn new skills for the first time.

Correct: Use Lesson 8 to synthesize already taught reporting, revision, media, multimedia, publication, collaboration, and presentation skills.

Teachable explanation: Final synthesis is about transfer. You show mastery by applying known skills accurately to a complete journalism problem.
