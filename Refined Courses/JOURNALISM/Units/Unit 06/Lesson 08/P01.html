Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

This lesson synthesizes previously taught Journalism skills. It does not introduce a new primary standard. You will use the full course skill set to evaluate a final portfolio and prepare for the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered

Previously taught course primary standards only: reporting, evidence, revision, media literacy, multimedia, collaboration, publication, and presentation standards used throughout the course.

Assessment: Unit Assessment only. Lesson 8 has no Lesson Quiz.

What You Will Learn

  • How to synthesize final journalism skills in one capstone scenario.
  • How to check a final portfolio for accuracy, revision, media responsibility, workflow, and audience readiness.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment without learning new standards.

What You Will Do

  • Study the embedded final capstone scenario.
  • Identify and correct remaining portfolio risks.
  • Complete guided practice and independent synthesis work.
  • Complete the Unit 6 Assessment instead of a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Apply previously taught journalism standards to a final portfolio problem.
  • Explain corrections using evidence and audience reasoning.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence and complete the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use everything I learned in Journalism to judge whether a final portfolio is accurate, revised, publishable, and ready to present.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify a final portfolio problem but cannot explain which course skill should be used to fix it.
