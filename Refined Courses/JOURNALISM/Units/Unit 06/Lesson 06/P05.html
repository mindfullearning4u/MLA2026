Newsroom Collaboration and Publication Readiness. Task: The staff is preparing the final portfolio publication page. Team Roles: editor checks revisions, designer checks layout, photo editor verifies captions, and project manager checks the deadline list. Conflict: the designer wants to publish now because the page looks polished, but the editor says two captions are unverified and one article still needs the final revision note. Deadline: final submission is tomorrow at noon. Publication Need: the team must use respectful compromise, complete the workflow, and submit one cohesive publication-ready package.
