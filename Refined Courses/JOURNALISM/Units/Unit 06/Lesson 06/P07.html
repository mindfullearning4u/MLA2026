Newsroom Collaboration and Publication Readiness

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record reviews your checkpoint evidence and provides feedback. Ask for help if your recommendation does not explain how the correction protects the audience.

Submission Workflow

  1. Complete Notebook Task Part 1 and Part 2.
  2. Complete Independent Work Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that each correction is specific.
  4. Submit your checkpoint evidence in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit your issue chart and final recommendation. Your work must identify the issue, connect it to the standard, and explain the correction before publication or submission.

Mastery Criteria

  • Issue is clearly identified.
  • Standard connection is accurate.
  • Correction protects audience understanding or publication readiness.
  • Reasoning uses the embedded source.
  • Writing is clear and complete.
