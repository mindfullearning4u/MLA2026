Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Newsroom Portfolio Planning

Vocabulary

  • Portfolio: a selected collection of work that shows skill and readiness.
  • Format rule: an accepted requirement for organizing or presenting work.
  • Publication-ready: revised, accurate, complete, and prepared for an audience.
  • Audience fit: the match between content choices and reader needs.

Embedded Source

Newsroom Portfolio Plan. Task: The student editor is preparing a final newsroom portfolio for publication review. Required items: one revised article, one published or publication-ready media package, one captioned visual, one reflection explaining audience purpose, and one checklist showing accepted format rules. Problem: The folder currently includes two rough drafts, one final article, one image without a caption, and a reflection that says the work is good but does not explain audience purpose. Audience: Teacher of Record, school publication team, and future readers who need to see quality journalism work.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the portfolio purpose. A newsroom portfolio is not a storage folder. It is a selected presentation of quality journalism work.

Step 2: Identify the required format. The format tells you what must be included, how it should be labeled, and what evidence of revision or publication readiness is needed.

Step 3: Check each item for audience fit. A portfolio for a school publication should show work that helps a real school audience understand reporting, decisions, and final products.

Step 4: Check revision status. Drafts should not be included as final work unless they are labeled as process evidence.

Notebook Directions

Create a two-column chart. In the left column, list the key decision from the source. In the right column, explain the evidence or format rule that should guide the decision.

Model Example

Decision: Do not submit work only because it exists.

Reason: Journalism work must be selected, checked, revised, and connected to audience purpose before it belongs in a final product.
