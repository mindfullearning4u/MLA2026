P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Newsroom Portfolio Planning

Embedded Source Reminder

Newsroom Portfolio Plan. Task: The student editor is preparing a final newsroom portfolio for publication review. Required items: one revised article, one published or publication-ready media package, one captioned visual, one reflection explaining audience purpose, and one checklist showing accepted format rules. Problem: The folder currently includes two rough drafts, one final article, one image without a caption, and a reflection that says the work is good but does not explain audience purpose. Audience: Teacher of Record, school publication team, and future readers who need to see quality journalism work.

Deeper Learning

Strong journalism decisions are not based on preference. They are based on audience need, format requirements, source evidence, and publication responsibility. When you explain a decision, name the rule or evidence first, then explain why it matters.

For a remedial learner, the first goal is to identify the required decision. For an on-level learner, the goal is to connect that decision to evidence. For an accelerated learner, the goal is to evaluate whether the decision protects credibility for a real audience.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Add every article, photo, and draft to the portfolio just because the work exists.

Correct: Select and organize work that follows the accepted portfolio format and shows publication-ready quality.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example skips the reasoning step. The correct example uses the standard to make a defensible journalism decision.
