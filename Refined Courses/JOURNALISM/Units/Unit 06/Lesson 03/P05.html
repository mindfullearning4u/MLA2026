Writing Package Revision

Embedded Source

Writing Package Revision. Task: The student newsroom is preparing a final article package about the spring club fair. Draft Problem: The article begins with a strong quote, but the second paragraph jumps to a list of clubs without explaining why the fair matters. Feedback: The editor says the article needs clearer purpose, stronger transitions, one sentence of evidence from attendance numbers, and corrected capitalization for club names. Available Evidence: last year had 18 clubs; this year has 27 clubs; student council says the fair was moved to the gym so more students could attend. Publication Need: The article must be clear, cohesive, accurate, and convention-ready before it appears in the final portfolio.

Guided Practice Directions

Complete the Guided Practice in Moodle. Each item includes the source. Read the standard, identify the specific issue, eliminate choices that are vague or unsupported, and choose the best journalism decision.
