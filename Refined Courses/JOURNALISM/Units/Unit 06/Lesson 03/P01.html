Writing Package Revision

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Writing Package Revision

This lesson teaches how to revise a journalism writing package for clarity, cohesion, evidence, and conventions before final publication.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.02, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.05.

What You Will Learn

  • How to revise a journalism package for clarity and cohesion.
  • How argument, expository writing, and conventions support publication quality.
  • How to use feedback without losing the reporting purpose.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded draft package and revision notes.
  • Identify unclear, unsupported, or inconsistent writing.
  • Practice choosing revisions that improve purpose, evidence, and conventions.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Recommend revisions that improve clarity and cohesion.
  • Explain how writing choices support the article purpose.
  • Use conventions to prepare work for publication.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can review journalism before publication and explain how the choices affect audience understanding and trust.
