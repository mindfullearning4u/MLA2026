Writing Package Revision

Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Writing Package Revision

Vocabulary

  • Revision: improving meaning, structure, clarity, and purpose.
  • Cohesion: the way ideas connect smoothly across a text.
  • Purpose: the reason the journalism piece exists for its audience.
  • Convention: a grammar, punctuation, capitalization, or spelling rule used for publication quality.

Embedded Source

Writing Package Revision. Task: The student newsroom is preparing a final article package about the spring club fair. Draft Problem: The article begins with a strong quote, but the second paragraph jumps to a list of clubs without explaining why the fair matters. Feedback: The editor says the article needs clearer purpose, stronger transitions, one sentence of evidence from attendance numbers, and corrected capitalization for club names. Available Evidence: last year had 18 clubs; this year has 27 clubs; student council says the fair was moved to the gym so more students could attend. Publication Need: The article must be clear, cohesive, accurate, and convention-ready before it appears in the final portfolio.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the purpose. Before revising, name what the article is supposed to help the audience understand.

Step 2: Find unclear or unsupported places. A sentence may be true but still unclear if it does not explain who, what, when, where, why, or how.

Step 3: Check cohesion. Each paragraph should connect to the next. If the writing jumps from one idea to another, add transitions or reorganize the information.

Step 4: Check conventions last. Grammar and punctuation matter, but they should support a clear message, not replace revision for meaning.

Notebook Directions

Create a three-column chart. Label the columns: issue, evidence from the source, and best correction. Complete at least three rows.

Model Example

Issue: The draft or media package includes a weak decision.

Evidence: A source detail shows that the audience may misunderstand the point.

Best correction: Revise the sentence, structure, evidence, or media choice so the audience receives accurate information.
