P01 Lesson Overview

Reporting Package Verification

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Reporting Package Verification

This lesson teaches how to verify a reporting package by citing evidence, explaining reasoning, and identifying what still needs confirmation before publication.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.REP.02.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.01, MLA.JOUR.REP.03, MLA.JOUR.REP.04.

What You Will Learn

  • How to verify a reporting package before publication.
  • How evidence explains and justifies reasoning.
  • How research questions, source comprehension, and inference support verification.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded reporting package.
  • Identify claims, evidence, and verification gaps.
  • Practice explaining which evidence justifies a reporting decision.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Cite evidence to justify reporting decisions.
  • Identify when a source is not enough to verify a claim.
  • Connect inference and comprehension to accurate reporting.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use journalism standards to prepare credible, organized work for a real audience.
