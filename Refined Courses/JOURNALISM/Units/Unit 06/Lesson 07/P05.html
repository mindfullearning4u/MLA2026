P05 Guided Practice

Integrated Journalism Portfolio Preparation

Embedded Source

Integrated Journalism Portfolio. Task: The student newsroom is preparing a final portfolio page for public review. Required parts: one revised article, one verified evidence note, one accurate feature photo with caption, one short oral presentation outline, one publication-ready media package, and one reflection explaining audience purpose. Current Status: the article is revised but missing a final transition, the evidence note cites attendance numbers but does not explain why they matter, the feature photo is accurate but caption needs the date, and the oral outline lists topics but lacks a clear order. Audience: Teacher of Record, publication team, families, and students who need to see credible final journalism work. Publication Need: the team must synthesize reporting, revision, accurate visuals, format rules, publication readiness, and oral presentation.

Guided Practice Directions

Complete the Guided Practice in Moodle. Each question includes the source. Read the standard, identify the portfolio issue, and choose the correction that best protects audience trust and final readiness.
