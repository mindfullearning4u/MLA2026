P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Integrated Journalism Portfolio Preparation

Embedded Source Reminder

Integrated Journalism Portfolio. Task: The student newsroom is preparing a final portfolio page for public review. Required parts: one revised article, one verified evidence note, one accurate feature photo with caption, one short oral presentation outline, one publication-ready media package, and one reflection explaining audience purpose. Current Status: the article is revised but missing a final transition, the evidence note cites attendance numbers but does not explain why they matter, the feature photo is accurate but caption needs the date, and the oral outline lists topics but lacks a clear order. Audience: Teacher of Record, publication team, families, and students who need to see credible final journalism work. Publication Need: the team must synthesize reporting, revision, accurate visuals, format rules, publication readiness, and oral presentation.

Deeper Learning

A final portfolio is a proof-of-learning product. It should show that you can report, revise, publish, use visuals responsibly, and present information clearly.

A remedial learner should first identify what is missing. An on-level learner should connect the missing part to a standard. An accelerated learner should explain how the correction improves audience trust and portfolio quality.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Submit the portfolio because every required part exists somewhere in the folder.

Correct: Submit only after each part is revised, verified, captioned, organized, and connected to the audience purpose.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect example treats a portfolio like a storage folder. The correct example treats the portfolio as a public demonstration of mastery. Every included part must be ready for its audience.

Notebook Directions

Write one paragraph explaining which portfolio correction is most urgent before submission. Use the source and at least one MLA standard in your explanation.
