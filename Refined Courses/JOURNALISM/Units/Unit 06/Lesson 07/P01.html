P01 Lesson Overview

Integrated Journalism Portfolio Preparation

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated journalism portfolio preparation

This lesson teaches how to prepare a final journalism portfolio by combining reporting, revision, accurate visuals, online publication, and oral presentation into one credible capstone product.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.PUB.01; MLA.JOUR.COM.01.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.02; MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04; MLA.JOUR.DIG.03; MLA.JOUR.ETH.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How to check a final portfolio for publication readiness.
  • How reporting evidence, revision, visuals, format rules, and oral presentation work together.
  • How to explain portfolio choices for a real audience.

What You Will Do

  • Study an embedded final portfolio scenario.
  • Identify what is complete and what still needs revision or verification.
  • Practice selecting the strongest correction for each portfolio part.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Evaluate whether portfolio parts are ready for publication.
  • Explain corrections using evidence and standard language.
  • Prepare checkpoint evidence that shows readiness for final course synthesis.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can prepare a final journalism portfolio that is organized, accurate, revised, publishable, and ready to present.
