P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Integrated Journalism Portfolio Preparation

Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Final Portfolio Readiness Chart

Vocabulary

  • Integrated portfolio: a final collection of journalism work that shows connected reporting, writing, media, publication, and presentation skills.
  • Publication readiness: the point when work is accurate, revised, organized, formatted, and ready for an audience.
  • Oral presentation outline: an organized speaking plan that uses a clear focus and credible evidence.
  • Format-specific quality: following accepted rules for the type of journalism product being submitted.

Embedded Source

Integrated Journalism Portfolio. Task: The student newsroom is preparing a final portfolio page for public review. Required parts: one revised article, one verified evidence note, one accurate feature photo with caption, one short oral presentation outline, one publication-ready media package, and one reflection explaining audience purpose. Current Status: the article is revised but missing a final transition, the evidence note cites attendance numbers but does not explain why they matter, the feature photo is accurate but caption needs the date, and the oral outline lists topics but lacks a clear order. Audience: Teacher of Record, publication team, families, and students who need to see credible final journalism work. Publication Need: the team must synthesize reporting, revision, accurate visuals, format rules, publication readiness, and oral presentation.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the required portfolio parts. The source lists six parts. A complete portfolio must include all six, but completion is only the first check.

Step 2: Check each part for readiness. The article needs a transition, the evidence note needs reasoning, the photo caption needs a date, and the oral outline needs order.

Step 3: Match each problem to a standard. Revision connects to MLA.JOUR.NEWS.04, evidence reasoning connects to MLA.JOUR.REP.02, accurate visual captioning connects to MLA.JOUR.DIG.03, and presentation organization connects to MLA.JOUR.COM.01.

Step 4: Decide the correction. The best correction is specific, realistic, and tied to audience trust.

Notebook Directions

Create a four-column chart: portfolio part, current status, standard connection, and correction before submission. Complete at least four rows from the embedded source.

Model Example

Portfolio part: evidence note.

Current status: cites attendance numbers but does not explain why they matter.

Standard connection: MLA.JOUR.REP.02.

Correction: add one sentence explaining how the attendance numbers support the article claim.
