P07 Checkpoint

Integrated Journalism Portfolio Preparation

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record reviews your checkpoint evidence and provides feedback. Ask for help if your portfolio recommendation names a problem but does not explain the standard connection.

Submission Workflow

  1. Complete Notebook Task Part 1 and Part 2.
  2. Complete Independent Work Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that every correction includes a standard connection.
  4. Submit your checkpoint evidence in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit your final portfolio-readiness chart and recommendation. Your work must identify portfolio issues, standards, corrections, and audience impact.

Mastery Criteria

  • All required portfolio parts are addressed.
  • Corrections are specific and source-based.
  • Standards are connected accurately.
  • Recommendation explains audience and publication readiness.
  • Writing is clear and complete.
