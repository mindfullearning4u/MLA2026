Media Literacy and Audience Check

Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Media Literacy and Audience Check

Vocabulary

  • Audience check: reviewing whether a media text works for its intended readers or viewers.
  • Rhetorical appeal: a method used to persuade or influence an audience.
  • Central idea: the main point supported across a text.
  • Opposing claim: a different position on the same issue.

Embedded Source

Media Literacy and Audience Check. Task: The newsroom is reviewing a media post about a proposed later start time for school. Package Parts: headline, short article, two statistics, a student quote, and a graphic showing students asleep at desks. Purpose: Explain the issue and help readers understand competing views. Concern: The headline says Everyone Wants Later Start Times, but the source notes show mixed opinions from students, families, and staff. Evidence: one survey shows 58 percent of students support the change, while staff comments mention transportation and activity schedule concerns. Publication Need: The package must use accurate purpose, fair claims, responsible rhetoric, and audience-aware media choices.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the intended audience. A media text should be evaluated based on who needs the information.

Step 2: Identify the author purpose. Ask whether the text informs, persuades, explains, warns, or calls people to act.

Step 3: Evaluate support. The central idea must be supported by evidence, not just strong wording or emotional design.

Step 4: Check opposing claims and rhetoric. If a piece discusses a public issue, it should avoid misleading appeals and should represent claims fairly.

Notebook Directions

Create a three-column chart. Label the columns: issue, evidence from the source, and best correction. Complete at least three rows.

Model Example

Issue: The draft or media package includes a weak decision.

Evidence: A source detail shows that the audience may misunderstand the point.

Best correction: Revise the sentence, structure, evidence, or media choice so the audience receives accurate information.
