Media Literacy and Audience Check

Embedded Source

Media Literacy and Audience Check. Task: The newsroom is reviewing a media post about a proposed later start time for school. Package Parts: headline, short article, two statistics, a student quote, and a graphic showing students asleep at desks. Purpose: Explain the issue and help readers understand competing views. Concern: The headline says Everyone Wants Later Start Times, but the source notes show mixed opinions from students, families, and staff. Evidence: one survey shows 58 percent of students support the change, while staff comments mention transportation and activity schedule concerns. Publication Need: The package must use accurate purpose, fair claims, responsible rhetoric, and audience-aware media choices.

Guided Practice Directions

Complete the Guided Practice in Moodle. Each item includes the source. Read the standard, identify the specific issue, eliminate choices that are vague or unsupported, and choose the best journalism decision.
