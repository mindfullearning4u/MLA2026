Final Clarity Check

Before you leave Media Literacy and Audience Check, confirm that your work shows the journalism decision, the evidence behind it, and the audience or publication reason for it. For MLA.JOUR.MED.03, MLA.JOUR.MED.01, MLA.JOUR.MED.02, MLA.JOUR.MED.04, a strong answer does not simply sound reasonable; it can be traced back to a verified source detail, a media feature, a quoted statement, a caption or image choice, an ethical rule, or a publication requirement from the lesson. If a passage, scenario, source note, headline, caption, or media example is provided, keep it beside your answer and use it directly. If your response depends on information that is not on the page or in the question, revise it so the reader can see exactly where your thinking comes from.
