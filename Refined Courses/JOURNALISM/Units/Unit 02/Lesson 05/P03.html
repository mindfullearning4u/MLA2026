P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Deeper Learning: Paraphrase Without Changing Meaning

A paraphrase should not copy the sentence exactly, but it must keep the meaning.

Step 1: Keep the important facts.

Step 2: Use your own sentence structure.

Step 3: Keep the source accurate by not adding blame or certainty.

Strong paraphrase: Coach Patel said soccer practice is indoors this week because lightning was reported near campus, and players should check team messages for daily updates.

Notebook Task: Write three source notes and one accurate paraphrase.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

I can change the source wording as long as the new sentence sounds smoother.

Why this is incorrect: Smoother writing is not enough. Journalism must keep the same meaning and show the evidence behind a claim.

Correct example:

I should keep the source meaning, identify the evidence, and explain only what the source supports.

Teachable Explanation: This protects accuracy and keeps the audience from being misled.
