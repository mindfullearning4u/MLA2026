P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Source Note

Step 1: Source: Coach Patel.

Step 2: Fact: practice moved indoors this week.

Step 3: Reason: lightning within ten miles.

Step 4: Audience use: players should check messages each afternoon.

Example 2: Accurate Paraphrase

Step 1: Keep the time frame: this week.

Step 2: Keep the reason: lightning near campus.

Step 3: Keep the action: check team messages.

Paraphrase: Soccer practice is indoors this week because of nearby lightning reports, and players need to watch team messages for updates.

Example 3: Quotation Choice

Step 1: Use a quote only when exact wording matters.

Step 2: The phrase "when conditions are safe" may be worth quoting because it explains the standard for returning outside.

Step 3: Surround exact words with quotation marks and identify the speaker.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The source gives some useful words, so I can use them without checking the full context.

Why this is incorrect: Pulling words out of context can change meaning and create inaccurate journalism.

Correct example:

I should read the full source, choose the relevant detail, and explain how it supports the reporting purpose.

Why this is correct: It keeps the report fair, accurate, and evidence-based.
