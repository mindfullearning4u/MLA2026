P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Paraphrase and Accurate Source Notes: Notebook Part 1

Write this title in your notebook. Show each step of your thinking.

Vocabulary

  • Paraphrase: restating source meaning accurately in your own words.
  • Source note: a brief record of useful source information and where it came from.
  • Quotation: exact source words copied with quotation marks.
  • Distortion: changing source meaning by adding, removing, or exaggerating details.
  • Attribution: identifying where information came from.

Embedded Source Material

Source: Interview excerpt: Coach Patel said, "The soccer team moved practice indoors this week because lightning was reported within ten miles of campus. We expect to return outside when conditions are safe, but players should check the team message each afternoon."

This source is included here so you do not need to look somewhere else.

Step-by-Step: Build Accurate Source Notes

Step 1: Identify who provided the information: Coach Patel.

Step 2: Identify the exact fact: practice moved indoors this week.

Step 3: Identify the reason: lightning was reported within ten miles.

Step 4: Identify the condition: outdoor practice returns when conditions are safe.

Step 5: Record the action readers need: players should check the team message each afternoon.

Notebook Directions
