📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 05

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Paraphrase and Accurate Source Notes

This lesson teaches paraphrase and accurate source notes through a journalism source. The instruction is step by step because you are expected to learn the skill from the lesson materials before working independently.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.MED.06.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.01, MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03.

What You Will Learn

  • How to protect source meaning.
  • How to turn source material into accurate notes or reasoning.
  • How to explain a reporting choice with evidence.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded source material.
  • Build notebook evidence in two parts.
  • Study three worked examples.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint submission, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use the lesson process without skipping steps.
  • Explain how source information supports your answer.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.MED.06.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use source information accurately so my reporting is clear, fair, and supported by evidence.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot explain how your answer preserves the source and supports accurate reporting.

Need Help?
