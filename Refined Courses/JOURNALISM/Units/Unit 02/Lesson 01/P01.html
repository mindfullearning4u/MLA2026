📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Reporting Questions and Scope

This lesson teaches reporting questions and scope as a journalism skill. You will learn the process step by step before you are asked to use it independently.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.REP.01.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03, MLA.JOUR.INT.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How journalists use reporting questions and scope to protect accuracy.
  • How to slow down and make each reporting decision in the correct order.
  • How to explain your reasoning using the MLA journalism standard.

What You Will Do

  • Read a journalism scenario embedded in the lesson.
  • Build notebook evidence in two parts.
  • Study three worked examples.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, a checkpoint submission, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use the lesson process without skipping steps.
  • Explain why one reporting choice is stronger than another.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.REP.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use reporting questions and scope to make reporting more accurate, focused, and useful for an audience.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot explain why a reporting decision protects accuracy, source trust, or audience understanding.
