📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Use complete sentences. Your answer must show your thinking, not just the final choice.

Part A

Write a focused reporting question for the embedded scenario. Make sure it can be answered with sources.

Part B

List two sources that could help answer the question and explain what each source can verify.

Part C

Write one scope sentence that explains what your story will cover and what it will not cover.
