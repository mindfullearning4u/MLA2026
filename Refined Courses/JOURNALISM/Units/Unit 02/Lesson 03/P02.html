P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Complex Source Comprehension: Notebook Part 1

Write this title in your notebook. Your notes should show the reading process and evidence, not only the final answer.

Vocabulary

  • Complex source: a source with multiple details, relationships, or conditions that must be read carefully.
  • Main point: the central information the source communicates.
  • Supporting detail: a fact or explanation that helps develop the main point.
  • Paraphrase: restating source information accurately in your own words.
  • Context: surrounding information that affects meaning.

Embedded Source Material

Source: Principal memo: Beginning next Monday, morning media center hours will shift from 7:15 a.m. to 7:45 a.m. because the building will use the first half hour for technology maintenance. Students who need a quiet workspace before 7:45 may report to Room 114. The memo says the change is temporary and will be reviewed after three weeks.

This is the source for the lesson. It is included here so you do not need to look elsewhere.

Step-by-Step: Reading a Complex Source

Step 1: Read the whole source once for the general topic.

Step 2: Identify the main point. Here, the media center opening time changes temporarily.

Step 3: Mark the exact details: old time, new time, reason, alternative location, and review period.

Step 4: Separate fact from interpretation. The memo states a maintenance reason; it does not say the media center is permanently closing early.

Step 5: Paraphrase the information without changing meaning.
