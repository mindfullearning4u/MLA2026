P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Complete all three parts in your notebook. Use complete sentences and cite the source details that support your thinking.

Part A

Write the main point of the embedded memo in one sentence.

Part B

List five supporting details that a student audience would need.

Part C

Write an accurate paraphrase that keeps the meaning of the memo.

Mastery Support: Source Comprehension Checklist

Before submitting independent work, check each answer.

Step 1: Does the main point name the schedule change and reason?

Step 2: Do the details come directly from the memo?

Step 3: Does the paraphrase keep the same meaning?

Step 4: Did you avoid adding blame, emotion, or permanent claims the source does not state?
