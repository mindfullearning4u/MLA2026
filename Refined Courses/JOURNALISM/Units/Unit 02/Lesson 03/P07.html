Checkpoint Task

Submit a source-comprehension note for the memo. Include the main point, three essential details, and a one-sentence paraphrase.

Mastery Criteria

  • The main point is accurate.
  • Details come directly from the source.
  • The paraphrase does not add or remove meaning.
  • The response is useful for a student audience.

Mastery Requirement: 80% or higher is required for mastery.

Resubmission: Corrections and resubmissions are allowed until mastery is demonstrated.

TOR Intervention: If your checkpoint shows that one or more lesson skills are not yet secure, your Teacher of Record may provide feedback, intervention, or additional support before you move forward.

Checkpoint Mastery Check

Use this checkpoint as quality control for Complex Source Comprehension. Compare your work to MLA.JOUR.REP.03, MLA.JOUR.MED.06, MLA.JOUR.REP.04, reread the mastery criteria, and confirm that your evidence is visible. If you cannot explain why your response is accurate, ethical, and audience-ready, use the TOR support process before moving forward. This course is self-paced, so use the page directions as your instruction. Read the embedded source, scenario, model, or newsroom task first. Then identify the exact journalism responsibility from MLA.JOUR.REP.03, MLA.JOUR.MED.06, MLA.JOUR.REP.04, decide what information is verified, explain why it matters to the audience, and keep the response inside the lesson focus. Do not invent facts, quotes, images, or source details. If the task asks for writing, draft in clear journalistic language. If it asks for analysis, point to the words, source details, media features, or evidence that prove the answer.
