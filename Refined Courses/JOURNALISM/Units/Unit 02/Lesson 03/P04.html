P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Main Point

Step 1: Ask what changed.

Step 2: The opening time changed from 7:15 to 7:45.

Step 3: The main point is not maintenance alone; it is the schedule change caused by maintenance.

Example 2: Supporting Detail

Step 1: Ask what helps readers use the information.

Step 2: Room 114 is an important supporting detail because it tells students where to go.

Step 3: A complete report should include that alternative.

Example 3: Accurate Paraphrase

Step 1: Keep the temporary review period.

Step 2: Keep the reason for the change.

Step 3: Write: The media center will open at 7:45 a.m. for three weeks during technology maintenance, with Room 114 available earlier for students.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

I only need one phrase from the source, even if the rest of the source changes the meaning.

Why this is incorrect: Quoting or paraphrasing one phrase without context can mislead the audience.

Correct example:

I should read the whole source, identify the key details, and explain how the evidence supports my reporting decision.

Why this is correct: It keeps the report accurate, fair, and tied to evidence.
