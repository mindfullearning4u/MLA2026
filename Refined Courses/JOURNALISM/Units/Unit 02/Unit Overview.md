# JOURNALISM - Unit 02 Overview: News Structure and Informational Writing

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops structured news writing. Students analyze how text structures and features shape meaning, study leads and the inverted pyramid, and write informational articles with clear organization, purposeful transitions, and appropriate tone and voice.

Emphasis is placed on leads, news value, inverted pyramid structure, clarity, concise organization, transitions, audience, tone, and voice. Students move from short news briefs to organized informational articles.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to selected Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards with an emphasis on news structure, informational writing, clarity, and audience communication. Students are expected to demonstrate writing fluency and conceptual understanding through structured news articles.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- text structures and features;
- leads and essential information;
- inverted pyramid organization;
- logical organization;
- purposeful transitions;
- tone and voice appropriate to the task;
- structured informational news writing.

Assessment items require students to analyze the impact of text structures and features and write expository news texts that explain and analyze information from multiple sources using logical organization, purposeful transitions, and a tone and voice appropriate to the task.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit asks students to solve information-structure problems. Students determine what readers need first, how supporting details should be ordered, and how structure affects understanding.
