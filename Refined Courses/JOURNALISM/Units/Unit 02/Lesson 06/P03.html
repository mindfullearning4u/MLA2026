P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Deeper Learning: Evidence Is Not Enough Without Reasoning

Evidence answers what supports the claim. Reasoning answers why that evidence matters.

Step 1: Write the claim.

Step 2: Choose the most relevant evidence.

Step 3: Explain the connection in your own words.

Step 4: Check the limit. The notes show demand and use; they do not prove long-term academic improvement.

Notebook Task: Write one claim, two evidence details, one reasoning sentence, and one evidence limit.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

I can change the source wording as long as the new sentence sounds smoother.

Why this is incorrect: Smoother writing is not enough. Journalism must keep the same meaning and show the evidence behind a claim.

Correct example:

I should keep the source meaning, identify the evidence, and explain only what the source supports.

Teachable Explanation: This protects accuracy and keeps the audience from being misled.

Deeper Journalism Reasoning Support
