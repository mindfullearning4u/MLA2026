P05 Guided Practice

Guided Practice

Complete the Guided Practice in Moodle. Use the feedback as part of the lesson.

Step 1: Read the prompt and identify what the reporter needs to preserve or prove.

Step 2: Return to the source details.

Step 3: Choose the answer that keeps the meaning and evidence accurate.

Step 4: If you miss a question, write the skipped step before continuing.

Mastery Check Before Moving On

You are ready for independent work when you can explain the difference between repeating source words, paraphrasing source meaning, and reasoning from evidence. If you cannot explain the difference, review P02-P04 first.

Guided Practice Strategy

Before choosing an answer, ask: Does this option connect evidence to a claim?

Step 1: Identify the claim or conclusion.

Step 2: Find the evidence that directly supports it.

Step 3: Check whether the answer explains why the evidence matters.

Step 4: Reject any option that overclaims, uses unrelated evidence, or repeats evidence without reasoning.
