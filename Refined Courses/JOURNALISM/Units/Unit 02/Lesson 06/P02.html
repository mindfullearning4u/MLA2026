Notebook Title

Evidence and Reasoning in Reporting: Notebook Part 1

Write this title in your notebook. Show each step of your thinking.

Vocabulary

  • Evidence: information that supports a claim or reporting conclusion.
  • Reasoning: the explanation of how evidence supports a claim.
  • Claim: a statement the report needs to prove or support.
  • Relevant evidence: evidence that directly connects to the claim.
  • Central idea: the main point developed by evidence across a source or report.

Embedded Source Material

Source: Reporting notes: The library opened a new quiet study room for ninth graders. The librarian says the room was requested after 42 students completed a survey about needing a calmer study space. During the first week, the room was reserved every afternoon. Two students said the room helped them finish assignments before practice.

This source is included here so you do not need to look somewhere else.

Step-by-Step: Connect Evidence to Reasoning

Step 1: Identify the claim: the quiet study room is meeting a student need.

Step 2: Find evidence that directly supports the claim: 42 students requested calmer study space.

Step 3: Add more evidence: the room was reserved every afternoon during the first week.

Step 4: Explain reasoning: if students requested the room and used it daily, the evidence suggests the room responds to a real need.

Step 5: Avoid unsupported claims, such as saying every student improved grades.
