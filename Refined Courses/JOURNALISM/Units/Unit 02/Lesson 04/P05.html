P05 Guided Practice

Guided Practice

Complete the Guided Practice in Moodle. Use the feedback as instruction.

Step 1: Read the question and identify what the reporter needs to know.

Step 2: Return to the embedded source or source idea.

Step 3: Choose the answer that uses source information accurately.

Step 4: If you miss an item, write the skipped step in your notebook before continuing.

Mastery Check Before Moving On

You are ready for independent work when you can explain the difference between what the source directly says and what a journalist may reasonably conclude from it. If you cannot explain that difference, review P02 and P03 first.

How to Use Feedback

If you miss a guided practice question, focus on the reasoning mistake.

Step 1: Decide whether the answer was a fact, inference, assumption, or overstatement.

Step 2: Locate the evidence in the interview notes.

Step 3: Ask whether the evidence proves the claim or only suggests it.

Step 4: Revise the wording so it is cautious and accurate.
