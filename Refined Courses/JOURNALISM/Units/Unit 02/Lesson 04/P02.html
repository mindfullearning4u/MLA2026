P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Inference in Reporting: Notebook Part 1

Write this title in your notebook. Your notes should show the reading process and evidence, not only the final answer.

Vocabulary

  • Inference: a reasonable conclusion based on evidence.
  • Evidence: source information that supports a conclusion.
  • Assumption: a belief that is not proven by the source.
  • Justification: an explanation of why evidence supports a conclusion.
  • Limit: what the evidence does not prove.

Embedded Source Material

Source: Interview notes: Three students say the new tutoring sign-up form is hard to find on the school website. The guidance counselor says tutoring appointments are available, but only eight students signed up during the first week. A teacher says several students asked for help after missing the online form.

This is the source for the lesson. It is included here so you do not need to look elsewhere.

Step-by-Step: Making a Reporting Inference

Step 1: Identify the stated facts. Three students had trouble finding the form, eight students signed up, and a teacher heard questions about the form.

Step 2: Ask what those facts suggest together.

Step 3: Make a careful inference: some students may not be finding the sign-up form easily.

Step 4: Name the evidence that supports the inference.

Step 5: Name the limit. The notes do not prove every student had trouble.
