📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts in your notebook. Use complete sentences and cite the source details that support your thinking.

Part A

Write one reasonable inference from the interview notes.

Part B

List two pieces of evidence that support your inference.

Part C

Write one sentence explaining the limit of your evidence.

Mastery Support: Inference Checklist

Before submitting independent work, test your inference.

Step 1: Is the inference based on more than one clue from the notes?

Step 2: Did you cite two specific pieces of evidence?

Step 3: Did you explain how the evidence supports the conclusion?

Step 4: Did you name what the evidence does not prove?

This final limit is important because it prevents journalism from turning a reasonable inference into an unsupported claim.
