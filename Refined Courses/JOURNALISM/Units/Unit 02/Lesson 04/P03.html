P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Learning: Inference Is Not Guessing

A guess can ignore evidence. A journalism inference must be tied to evidence and explained clearly.

Step 1: Start with a pattern in the notes.

Step 2: Connect the pattern to a cautious conclusion.

Step 3: Use words such as may, suggests, or appears when the evidence is not complete.

Step 4: Avoid words such as proves, everyone, always, or never unless the evidence truly supports them.

Notebook Task: Write one reasonable inference, two pieces of evidence, and one limit.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The source seems to suggest something, so I can report it as a fact.

Why this is incorrect: Journalism requires careful reading. A suggestion, pattern, or clue is not automatically a verified fact.

Correct example:

I can report stated facts as facts and label reasonable conclusions as inferences supported by evidence.

Why this is correct: The answer protects accuracy and shows the reader where the reasoning comes from.
