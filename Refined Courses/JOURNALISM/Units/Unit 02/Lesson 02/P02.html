P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Reliable and Valid Sources: Notebook Part 1

Write this title at the top of your notes. Your notes should show the decision process, not only the final answer.

Vocabulary

  • Reliable source: a source that can be trusted because it has direct knowledge, expertise, records, or accountability.
  • Valid source: a source that actually fits the reporting question.
  • Credibility: the degree to which a source is believable and useful.
  • Bias: a viewpoint or interest that may shape what a source says.
  • Corroboration: checking information against another reliable source.

Embedded Reporting Scenario

Scenario: The student newsroom hears that the school may add a second bus route after complaints about late arrivals. A student posted about it online, a bus driver mentioned changes might be discussed, and the transportation coordinator sent an email to administrators.

Everything you need for the lesson is included here. Do not search for another passage or source.

Step-by-Step: Testing a Source

Step 1: Identify what fact you need to verify.

Step 2: Ask whether the source has direct knowledge of that fact.

Step 3: Ask whether the source is current, accurate, and accountable.

Step 4: Ask whether the source fits the question. A source can be reliable for one question and not valid for another.

Step 5: Look for corroboration if the fact is important or disputed.
