📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 02

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Use complete sentences. Your answer must show your thinking, not just the final choice.

Part A

Identify the fact that must be verified in the embedded scenario.

Part B

Rank three sources from strongest to weakest. Explain reliability and validity for each.

Part C

Write one sentence explaining what additional corroboration would strengthen the report.

Mastery Support: How to Explain Your Source Ranking

When you rank sources, do not write only strongest or weakest. Explain the reason.

Step 1: Name the source role, such as coordinator, student witness, official record, or social media post.

Step 2: Explain what that source can actually know.

Step 3: Explain whether that knowledge matches the reporting question.

Step 4: State what additional source would confirm or challenge the information.

This explanation is what shows mastery. The ranking alone is not enough.
