Checkpoint Task

Submit a source-verification note for the embedded scenario. Include the fact being checked, your strongest source, one weaker source, and the reason each source is or is not valid.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response separates reliability from validity.
  • The strongest source has direct knowledge or a record.
  • The weaker source is explained clearly.
  • The response identifies when corroboration is needed.
