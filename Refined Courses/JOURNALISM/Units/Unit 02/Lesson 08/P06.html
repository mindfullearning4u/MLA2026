📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the tutoring sign-up scenario to complete a Unit 2 reporting synthesis plan. This is not a full article. It is a careful plan for accurate reporting.

Part A

Write three verified or attributed source details. For each one, identify the source and explain what the detail supports.

Part B

Write three verification gaps. For each gap, identify the source or evidence needed to answer it accurately.

Part C

Write a short reporting plan with two neutral interview questions, one accurate paraphrase, and one sentence explaining the limits of the current evidence.

Mastery Support Checklist

  • Did I avoid unsupported claims?
  • Did I attribute source statements?
  • Did I preserve meaning in my paraphrase?
  • Did I connect evidence to reasoning?
  • Did I plan follow-up interviews for missing information?
