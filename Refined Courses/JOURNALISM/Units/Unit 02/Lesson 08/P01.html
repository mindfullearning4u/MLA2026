📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 2 Reporting Synthesis

In this lesson, you will synthesize Unit 2 reporting skills by planning how to investigate, verify, paraphrase, reason from evidence, and prepare source follow-up before publication.

Standards Covered

Unit 2 Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.01, MLA.JOUR.REP.02, MLA.JOUR.REP.03, MLA.JOUR.REP.04, MLA.JOUR.MED.06, MLA.JOUR.INT.01.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.03 and MLA.JOUR.COM.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 2 reporting skills work together.
  • How to evaluate a reporting situation before drafting a story.
  • How to use source evidence without changing meaning.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 2 Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 2 reporting responsibilities.
  • Apply verification, paraphrase, inference, evidence, and interview planning.
  • Complete guided synthesis practice and independent work.
  • Complete the Unit 2 Assessment. There is no Lesson 8 quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use evidence accurately.
  • Identify what is verified and what needs follow-up.
  • Choose appropriate reporting and interview actions.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 2 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use Unit 2 reporting skills together to make accurate, verified, source-based journalism decisions.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

If you cannot explain why a reporting choice protects accuracy, ask your Teacher of Record before completing the Unit Assessment.
