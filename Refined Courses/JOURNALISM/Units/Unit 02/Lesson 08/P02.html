P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Unit 2 Reporting Synthesis: From Source Notes to Verified Story Plan

Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: using multiple skills together to solve a larger task.
  • Verification: checking whether information is accurate and supported.
  • Evidence: facts, source statements, data, or observations that support a claim.
  • Paraphrase: restating source information accurately in your own words.
  • Interview plan: a planned set of source questions tied to story needs.

Embedded Source

Embedded Unit 2 Reporting Scenario

The student newspaper is preparing a report about after-school tutoring sign-ups. The counselor says sign-ups increased after announcements were posted in advisory classes. A math teacher says some students still do not know where the form is located. Four students say reminders helped them remember the deadline. The reporter has not yet checked the sign-up data, interviewed the tutoring coordinator, or confirmed whether sign-ups increased in every grade level.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the story focus. The story is about after-school tutoring sign-ups and what may be affecting student awareness.

Step 2: Separate source statements from verified data. The counselor, teacher, and students provide useful statements. However, the reporter still needs actual sign-up data before making a broad claim.

Step 3: Look for evidence and limits. Four students say reminders helped, but four comments do not prove all students responded the same way.

Step 4: Plan source follow-up. The tutoring coordinator can confirm sign-up numbers. The counselor can explain the advisory announcements. Students can explain whether reminders were clear.

Step 5: Decide what can be published now. A reporter can publish that sources report increased awareness, but should not claim sign-ups increased in every grade until data confirms it.

Notebook Directions

Create a Unit 2 synthesis table with four rows: Verified Information, Needs Verification, Evidence Available, and Interview Follow-Up. Complete each row using the embedded source.

Model Example

Needs Verification: Whether sign-ups increased in every grade level.

Best follow-up source: Tutoring coordinator or school data record.

Why this works: The claim involves numbers and grade levels, so the reporter needs data rather than only comments.
