Guided Practice Instructions

Answer each guided practice question using the embedded cafeteria ordering scenario. The same source information appears in the Guided Practice GIFT file, so you do not need to search elsewhere.

Embedded Source

Embedded Source File: School Cafeteria Interview Planning Notes

The student newspaper is preparing a story about a new cafeteria ordering system. A cafeteria manager says the goal is to shorten lunch lines. Two students say the app is confusing during the first week. A technology assistant says the school will post a how-to guide by Friday. The reporter has not yet confirmed how many students have used the app or whether the line wait time has changed.

Guided Practice Strategy

Use this four-step process before each answer:

  1. Name the reporting focus.
  2. Identify what is verified.
  3. Identify what still needs evidence.
  4. Choose the interview or verification step that would most directly fill the gap.

If an answer sounds dramatic but is not supported, eliminate it. Journalism values accuracy over speed or drama.
