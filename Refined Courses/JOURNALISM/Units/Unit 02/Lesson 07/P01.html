P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Source Verification and Interview Planning

In this lesson, you will combine the Unit 2 reporting skills you have practiced: asking focused questions, checking source reliability, reading sources carefully, making reasonable inferences, paraphrasing accurately, and planning interview follow-up.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.01 - Reporting Questions and Source Verification; MLA.JOUR.INT.01 - Interview Planning and Active Listening.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.REP.02, MLA.JOUR.REP.03, MLA.JOUR.REP.04, MLA.JOUR.MED.06.

What You Will Learn

  • How to turn a story idea into a focused reporting plan.
  • How to decide what still needs verification before publication.
  • How to prepare interview questions that produce useful evidence.
  • How to connect interview planning to accurate source handling.

What You Will Do

  • Study a reporting scenario with incomplete source information.
  • Separate confirmed facts from information that needs follow-up.
  • Write interview questions that target missing evidence.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify what is verified and what still needs checking.
  • Choose interview questions that are clear, neutral, and useful.
  • Explain how source verification supports an accurate report.
  • Submit a checkpoint with a reporting and interview plan.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can plan reporting by asking focused questions, checking source information, and preparing interviews that help me verify the story before I publish it.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

If you cannot tell the difference between a verified fact, an inference, and a question that still needs reporting, ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting the checkpoint.
