📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Your work should show that you can prepare for an interview and use active listening to improve reporting accuracy.

Part A: Reporting Purpose

Choose one school journalism topic and write the reporting purpose. Explain what the audience needs to understand.

Part B: Interview Questions

Write four interview questions: two open-ended questions, one closed fact-checking question, and one follow-up question you might ask after listening.

Part C: Active Listening Plan

Write three actions you will take during the interview to listen actively, confirm facts, and mark details that still need verification.
