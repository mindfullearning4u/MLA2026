📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 1 Journalism Foundations

In this lesson, you will synthesize Unit 1 skills by making journalism decisions about format, technology, design, interviewing, tone, language access, and ethical collaboration.

Standards Covered

Unit 1 Standards: MLA.JOUR.ETH.01, MLA.JOUR.ETH.02, MLA.JOUR.MED.08, MLA.JOUR.MED.09, MLA.JOUR.INT.01, MLA.JOUR.INT.02, MLA.JOUR.INT.03, MLA.JOUR.INT.04.

Support: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.06 and MLA.JOUR.REP.05.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 1 journalism skills work together.
  • How format, media history, design, interviews, tone, and language access all support factual communication.
  • How to evaluate a journalism scenario using multiple standards.

What You Will Do

  • Review key Unit 1 concepts.
  • Apply standards to a newsroom scenario.
  • Complete a synthesis checkpoint.
  • Complete the Unit 1 Assessment. There is no Lesson 8 quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain decisions using Unit 1 journalism vocabulary.
  • Choose ethical, accurate, and audience-ready actions.
  • Submit synthesis evidence.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 1 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use Unit 1 journalism skills together to make accurate, respectful, and publication-ready choices.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help
