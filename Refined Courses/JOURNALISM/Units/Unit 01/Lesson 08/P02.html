P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Unit 1 Journalism Foundations Synthesis

Use this title for your notes. Your goal is to connect the Unit 1 skills rather than treat them as separate tasks.

Vocabulary Review

  • Format: the type or structure of a journalism product.
  • Verification: checking information before publishing.
  • Design hierarchy: arranging information by importance.
  • Active listening: listening and responding to what the source actually said.
  • Language access: communication choices that help people understand and participate.

Step 1: Read the Whole Newsroom Situation

In a synthesis task, do not grab the first standard you recognize. Read the whole situation. Ask what the newsroom is trying to produce, who the audience is, what information must be verified, and what communication choices affect trust.

Embedded Synthesis Scenario

Scenario: A student newsroom is preparing a digital story about the club fair. The team needs a news brief for the website, a photo caption, and a short message to ask the club sponsor for missing details. Some students may be new to the school and may not understand the phrase extracurricular expo.

Key idea: The team must use format rules, design clarity, interview norms, professional tone, and language access together.

Model Example

Question: What should the team do first?

Model answer: The team should verify the event time, location, and sponsor details before publishing. Then it should choose a clear news brief format, use an accurate photo caption, and revise confusing language so new students understand where to go.

Notebook direction: Label the sentence parts that show verification, format, caption accuracy, and language access.

Mastery Support: Unit 1 Synthesis Decision Map

When a Unit 1 question gives a newsroom scenario, use this map:

  1. Format: What kind of journalism product is needed?
  2. Verification: What fact must be checked before publishing?
  3. Design: What should the audience notice first?
  4. Source communication: What professional question should be asked?
  5. Language access: What wording helps the audience understand?

Do not answer from only one skill if the scenario requires several skills working together.
