P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Journalism Application

Unit 1 synthesis means making decisions in the correct order. Accuracy comes before speed. Source trust supports accurate information. Design supports reader understanding. Language access helps the audience use the information.

Step-by-Step: Unit 1 Decision Sequence

Step 1: Identify the journalism product and audience.

Step 2: Verify the facts and source information.

Step 3: Choose the format and design that support audience needs.

Step 4: Communicate with sources using professional tone and active listening.

Step 5: Revise language so the message is clear and accessible.

Notebook Directions

Part 1: Identify three Unit 1 standards that apply to the club fair scenario.

Part 2: Explain the first action the newsroom should take.

Part 3: Write one accessible sentence for new students.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The team should post quickly because digital journalism is fast.

Why this is incorrect: Speed is useful only when facts, captions, source communication, and audience language are accurate.

Correct example:

The team should verify the details first, then publish a clear digital news brief with accurate captions and accessible language.

Why this is correct: It uses multiple Unit 1 skills together to protect audience trust.

Mastery Support: How to Avoid One-Step Thinking

A common Unit 1 mistake is choosing a fast action without checking the journalism responsibility. For example, posting a mobile update is not automatically correct. It is correct only when the time, location, source, wording, and audience need are handled accurately.

Think: What action serves the audience and protects trust?
