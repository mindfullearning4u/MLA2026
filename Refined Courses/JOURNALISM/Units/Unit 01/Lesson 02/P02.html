P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Ethical Newsroom Collaboration

Vocabulary

Collaboration: working with others toward a shared goal.

Ethics: responsible choices that protect accuracy, fairness, and trust.

Active listening: listening carefully, asking clarifying questions, and responding to what the speaker actually said.

Respectful compromise: adjusting a plan while protecting the purpose, accuracy, and dignity of the group.

Workflow: the ordered steps a team follows to complete a task on time.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Know the shared goal. In a newsroom, the goal is not just to finish. The goal is to publish accurate, clear, audience-ready work.

Step 2: Define roles. A team may need a reporter, editor, fact checker, designer, photographer, or publication manager. Clear roles prevent duplicated work and missed tasks.

Step 3: Listen before deciding. Active listening helps teammates understand ideas, concerns, evidence, and deadlines before choosing a plan.

Step 4: Compromise responsibly. A good compromise does not remove accuracy or fairness. It solves a team problem while protecting the publication.

Step 5: Track time. A newsroom team needs checkpoints, deadlines, and a final review so the work is complete before publication.

Notebook Task

Create a five-step newsroom collaboration checklist in your notebook. Use these labels:

  1. Shared Goal
  2. Roles
  3. Active Listening
  4. Respectful Compromise
  5. Deadline Workflow

For each label, write one sentence explaining what a responsible journalism teammate should do.
