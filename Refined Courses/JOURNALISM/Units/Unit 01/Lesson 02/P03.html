P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply ethical collaboration to a newsroom problem.

Scenario

A journalism team is preparing a story about the school art show. One teammate wants to publish quickly. Another teammate says one quoted statement needs to be checked with the source. The deadline is tomorrow at noon.

Step-by-Step Thinking

Step 1: Identify the ethical issue. The team wants to meet the deadline, but accuracy must still be protected.

Step 2: Use active listening. Each teammate should restate the concern: one person is worried about time, and one person is worried about accuracy.

Step 3: Create a compromise. The team can assign one person to contact the source while another edits the rest of the story.

Step 4: Set a workflow deadline. The source check must be completed before the final review.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Publish the quote without checking it because the deadline is close.

This is incorrect because speed does not excuse weak accuracy. A rushed publication can damage trust.

Correct: Divide tasks so one teammate checks the quote while the rest of the team completes editing and layout.

This is correct because it protects accuracy, uses time wisely, and shows respectful compromise.

Notebook Task

In your notebook, write a short response with these four labels:

  1. Problem: What is the team conflict?
  2. Listening: What should each teammate listen for?
  3. Compromise: What should the team do?
  4. Deadline: What time-management step protects the final publication?
