P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can solve a newsroom teamwork problem ethically.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence using the course submission tool. Make sure your name, unit, lesson, and checkpoint title are visible.

Checkpoint Task

Write a newsroom collaboration plan for the fundraiser scenario from independent work. Use four labels:

  1. Ethical concern: Explain the accuracy or fairness issue.
  2. Active listening: Write one sentence that shows respectful listening.
  3. Compromise: Explain how the team should divide or adjust tasks.
  4. Workflow: List the next three steps before publication.

Mastery Criteria

  • The plan protects accuracy before publication.
  • The active-listening statement is respectful and specific.
  • The compromise keeps the team focused on shared quality.
  • The workflow includes clear next steps and deadline control.
