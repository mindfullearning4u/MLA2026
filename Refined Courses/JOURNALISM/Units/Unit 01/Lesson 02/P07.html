P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record checks whether your workflow protects factual reporting and ethical collaboration. Ask for help if you are unsure how to compromise without weakening accuracy.

Submission Workflow

  • Complete the P06 independent work.
  • Review your workflow for roles, deadlines, and ethical checkpoints.
  • Submit your checkpoint response in the assigned location.
  • Use feedback to revise your collaboration plan if needed.

Checkpoint Task

Task: Submit a newsroom collaboration plan for one journalism product. Your plan must include:

  1. The journalism product your team would create.
  2. At least three roles and responsibilities.
  3. A five-step workflow with deadline checkpoints.
  4. One active listening sentence for a team disagreement.
  5. One ethical compromise that protects accuracy.

Mastery Criteria

  • The plan stays focused on journalism and factual communication.
  • Roles and responsibilities are specific.
  • The workflow shows realistic time management.
  • The active listening sentence responds to a real concern.
  • The ethical compromise protects accuracy, fairness, and audience trust.
