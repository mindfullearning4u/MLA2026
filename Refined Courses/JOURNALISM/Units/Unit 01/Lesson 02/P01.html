Lesson 2: Newsroom Collaboration and Ethics

Standards Covered

  • MLA.JOUR.ETH.02: Create collaborative work that shows cohesiveness, team-building, respectful compromise, and time-management skills.
  • MLA.JOUR.INT.01: Use appropriate collaborative techniques and active listening skills in discussions.
  • MLA.JOUR.REP.05: Follow directions and use effective time-management skills during the production process.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how a newsroom team works ethically. In journalism, collaboration is not just dividing tasks. It means listening carefully, respecting roles, meeting deadlines, compromising when needed, and keeping the final publication accurate and consistent.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to explain what a responsible newsroom teammate does and how collaboration affects the quality of published journalism.

Why This Matters

A publication can fail even when each person has a good idea. If teammates ignore deadlines, talk over one another, change facts without checking, or refuse compromise, the final work may become confusing or inaccurate. Ethical newsroom collaboration protects the audience and the publication.

What You Will Do

  • Identify roles in a newsroom production task.
  • Practice active listening and respectful compromise in a journalism scenario.
  • Use a simple workflow to manage deadlines and responsibilities.
  • Evaluate a worked example of ethical and unethical collaboration.
  • Complete a checkpoint that explains how collaboration improves publication quality.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can describe an ethical newsroom response to a team problem, explain how active listening supports collaboration, and name a time-management step that helps a publication stay accurate and complete.

Student-friendly standard connection: I can work with a journalism team respectfully and responsibly so our final publication is accurate, organized, and cohesive.

Ask TOR for help if: you are unsure how to solve a teamwork problem without weakening accuracy or respect.
