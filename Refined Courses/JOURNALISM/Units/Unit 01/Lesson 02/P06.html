P06 Independent Work

Complete this newsroom collaboration task in your notebook.

Instructions

A team is preparing a short online story about a student fundraiser. The reporter has facts, the designer has a graphic, and the editor has ten minutes for final review. The designer wants to publish now, but the editor notices the date on the graphic does not match the date in the story.

Parts A, B, and C

Part A: Identify the ethical problem in one sentence.

Part B: Write two active-listening statements teammates could use.

Part C: Write a three-step workflow that solves the problem before publication.

Your response should protect accuracy, respect teammates, and manage the deadline.
