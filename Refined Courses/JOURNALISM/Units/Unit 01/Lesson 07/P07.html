Submission Workflow

  • Complete notebook and independent work.
  • Review the original and revised message.
  • Submit your checkpoint language-access revision.
  • Use feedback to revise if clarity or accuracy is weak.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one original confusing journalism message, one accessible revision, and a short explanation of how the revision supports clarity, respect, and factual accuracy.

Mastery Criteria

  • The barrier is clearly identified.
  • The revision uses clear language.
  • The verified fact stays accurate.
  • The explanation connects access to journalism purpose.
  • The tone is respectful and inclusive.
