P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Language Access in Newsroom Communication

In this lesson, you will learn how journalists communicate clearly and inclusively with sources and audiences who may need academic, social, or instructional language support.

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.INT.03 - Academic Language for ELLs; MLA.JOUR.INT.04 - Social and Instructional Communication for ELLs.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.INT.01 - Collaborative Techniques and Active Listening; MLA.JOUR.INT.02 - Voice and Tone in Source Communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How language access supports fair and accurate journalism.
  • How to clarify academic and newsroom vocabulary for sources and audiences.
  • How to use respectful communication when a source needs language support.
  • How plain language can improve audience understanding without lowering accuracy.

What You Will Do

  • Study newsroom messages that may confuse sources or readers.
  • Practice revising wording for clarity, respect, and access.
  • Analyze common mistakes in language support.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify communication barriers in journalism situations.
  • Revise source and audience messages for clarity.
  • Explain how inclusive language supports accuracy and trust.
  • Submit a checkpoint with a language-access revision.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can communicate journalism information clearly and respectfully so more people can understand, participate, and respond accurately.
