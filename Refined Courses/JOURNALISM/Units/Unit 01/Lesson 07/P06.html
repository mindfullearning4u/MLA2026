📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Your work should show that you can improve language access while protecting accuracy.

Part A: Identify a Barrier

Write one confusing newsroom message or audience notice. Identify the word, phrase, or structure that creates the barrier.

Part B: Revise Clearly

Rewrite the message using plain, respectful language. Keep all important facts accurate.

Part C: Explain the Revision

Explain how your revision helps sources or readers understand the information and participate more fully.
