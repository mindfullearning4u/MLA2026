P06 Independent Work

Complete this task in your notebook before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Read each newsroom situation. For each one, choose the best journalism format and explain two rules the writer should follow.

Situation A: The school board approved a new tutoring schedule that starts next week.

Situation B: A student photographer took a picture of volunteers cleaning the courtyard.

Situation C: A columnist wants to argue that students should have more quiet study spaces.

Parts A, B, and C

Part A: Write the best format for each situation.

Part B: List two accepted rules for each format.

Part C: Choose one situation and write one sentence that follows the format rules.

Check that your answers explain why the format fits the audience and purpose.
