📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Your work should show that visual design choices serve factual communication and audience understanding.

Part A: Design Choice

Choose one journalism product: digital article, printed news page, photo caption package, social post, or video thumbnail. Identify one design choice that would help the audience understand the information.

Part B: Accuracy Check

Explain how you would make sure the image, caption, headline, or layout accurately represents the verified facts.

Part C: Design Reasoning

Write one paragraph explaining how your design choice supports journalism purpose, audience understanding, and format-specific quality.
