📰 JOUR | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Complete this visual journalism task in your notebook.

Instructions

A student publication is preparing a story about a food drive. The staff can use a photo of stacked donation boxes, a photo of volunteers sorting cans, and a simple chart showing how many items were collected each day.

Parts A, B, and C

Part A: Choose the main visual and explain why it fits the story.

Part B: Write one accurate caption rule the staff should follow.

Part C: Decide whether the chart should be included and explain how to keep it accurate.

Use the words audience, accuracy, layout, and caption.
