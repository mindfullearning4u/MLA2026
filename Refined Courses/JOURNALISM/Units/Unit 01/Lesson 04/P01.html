P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Media Design and Visual Culture Trends

In this lesson, you will learn how media design choices help journalists present accurate information clearly for an audience.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.MED.08 - Technology and Media Design Trends.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.DIG.03 - Digital Equipment and Visual Accuracy; MLA.JOUR.ETH.01 - Format-Specific Quality Work.

What You Will Learn

  • How visual culture trends influence journalism layout, images, captions, and digital presentation.
  • How design choices can support or weaken factual communication.
  • How accurate images and captions protect audience trust.
  • How format-specific quality rules apply to visual journalism.

What You Will Do

  • Study embedded media design examples.
  • Identify how visuals, captions, hierarchy, and platform choices affect audience understanding.
  • Analyze common mistakes about decoration versus journalistic function.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, a checkpoint submission, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain how a media design choice supports journalism purpose.
  • Evaluate whether a visual or layout choice helps the audience understand verified information.
  • Identify visual accuracy responsibilities.
  • Submit a checkpoint design analysis connected to audience and format.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.JOUR.MED.08: I can investigate how technology and media design reflect visual culture trends in journalism.

In student language: I can explain how design choices help a journalism audience understand information accurately.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: If you are not sure whether a design choice informs the audience or merely decorates the page, ask your Teacher of Record before submitting your checkpoint.
