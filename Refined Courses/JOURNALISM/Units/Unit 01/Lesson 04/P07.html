P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can evaluate a visual design choice in journalism.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can describe a visual design choice but cannot explain how it affects audience understanding or accuracy.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence using the course submission tool. Make sure your name, unit, lesson, and checkpoint title are visible.

Checkpoint Task

Write a visual-design evaluation paragraph. Include:

  1. The design choice you are evaluating.
  2. The journalism format it appears in.
  3. What the audience may notice or believe.
  4. Whether the design supports accuracy or creates a risk.
  5. One revision if the design should be improved.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response names a specific design choice.
  • The response connects the design to audience understanding.
  • The response evaluates accuracy clearly.
  • The response recommends a revision when needed.
