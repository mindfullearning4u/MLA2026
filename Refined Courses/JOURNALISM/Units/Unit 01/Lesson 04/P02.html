P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Media Design and Visual Accuracy

Use this title for your notes. Your goal is to explain how visual design choices support accurate journalism.

Vocabulary

  • Visual culture: the shared habits and expectations people develop from seeing images, layouts, videos, and digital media.
  • Design hierarchy: the order of importance created by headline size, image placement, spacing, and layout.
  • Caption: text that identifies and explains an image accurately.
  • Visual accuracy: using images and media in ways that truthfully represent the subject.
  • Platform fit: matching design choices to the publication format and audience need.

Step 1: Design Has a Journalism Job

In journalism, design is not decoration first. Design helps the audience understand information. A headline signals the main idea. A photo provides visual evidence. A caption explains who, what, when, and where. A chart can make numbers easier to understand. A layout can guide readers through a story without confusion.

Strong design choices should support verified information. If a design choice makes a story look exciting but misleads the audience, it weakens journalism quality.

Step 2: Evaluate Visual Design Choices

Use three questions when you evaluate journalism design:

  1. What information should the audience notice first?
  2. Does the image, caption, or layout accurately support the story?
  3. Does the design fit the publication format and audience?

This keeps the analysis focused on journalism instead of personal taste.

Embedded Media Design Example

Design Card: Club Fair Digital Story

The student newsroom publishes a digital story about the club fair. The page uses a large headline, one photo of students talking with club leaders, a caption naming the club fair and date, and a short bullet list of event times. A bright graphic appears below the verified schedule instead of above it.

Design clue: The page gives priority to verified information and uses the image to show the event context.

Model Example

Question: Why is this design effective for journalism?

Model answer: The design is effective because it places verified event information where readers can find it quickly. The photo supports the story by showing students interacting at the fair, and the caption identifies the context accurately. The decorative graphic does not replace the factual schedule.

Notebook direction: Copy the model answer and underline the parts that mention verified information, photo support, caption accuracy, and design hierarchy.
