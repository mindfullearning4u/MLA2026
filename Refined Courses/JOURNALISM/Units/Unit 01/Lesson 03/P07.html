P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can explain how technology changed journalism.

TOR

Ask TOR for help if you can identify a media technology but cannot explain its effect on journalism technique, focus, or audience experience.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence using the course submission tool. Make sure your name, unit, lesson, and checkpoint title are visible.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph explaining a media technology transition in journalism. Include:

  1. The older medium and newer medium.
  2. One change in journalism technique.
  3. One change for the audience.
  4. One quality responsibility that remains important.

Mastery Criteria

  • The paragraph names a clear media transition.
  • The explanation connects technology to journalism technique or focus.
  • The explanation includes audience impact.
  • The response explains accuracy or clarity as an ongoing responsibility.
