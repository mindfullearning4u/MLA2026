P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Print to Website

Scenario: A school newspaper changes from a monthly printed issue to a website updated twice a week.

Step 1: Identify the transition: print distribution to web distribution.

Step 2: Identify the audience effect: readers can access updates sooner and from more places.

Step 3: Identify the journalist responsibility: writers must update facts, links, and corrections carefully.

Modeled thinking: The website is not automatically better. It is more flexible, but it demands stronger update habits.

Example 2: Radio to Podcast

Scenario: A student news team moves from live morning announcements to a weekly podcast.

Step 1: Identify the transition: live broadcast to on-demand audio.

Step 2: Identify the audience effect: listeners can replay interviews and hear tone of voice.

Step 3: Identify the journalist responsibility: audio clips must be edited fairly and not taken out of context.

Modeled thinking: Audio technology can make reporting more personal, but editing choices must stay truthful.

Example 3: Website to Mobile Alert

Scenario: The newsroom sends a mobile alert when a school event location changes.

Step 1: Identify the transition: web posting to direct mobile notification.

Step 2: Identify the audience effect: students can receive urgent information immediately.

Step 3: Identify the journalist responsibility: the alert must be brief, accurate, and confirmed before it reaches everyone.

Modeled thinking: Mobile alerts are powerful because they are immediate, so mistakes are also immediate.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

Technology changes journalism because new tools make every story more interesting.

Why this is incorrect: The answer is too general and does not explain a specific journalism function. Interest alone is not the main standard for journalism.

Correct example:

Technology changes journalism by changing how information is gathered, verified, designed, distributed, and updated for an audience.

Why this is correct: The answer names concrete journalism functions and keeps the focus on factual communication.
