P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Media Technology Timeline

Vocabulary

Media transition: a change from one communication tool or format to another.

Technique: the method used to create or present information.

Focus: what a journalism format emphasizes, such as speed, depth, visuals, sound, or interaction.

Platform: the place or system where journalism is published, such as print, broadcast, website, app, or social media.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the older medium. For example, printed newspapers used pages, columns, headlines, and photographs.

Step 2: Identify the newer medium. A news website can use links, video, image galleries, updates, and search tools.

Step 3: Explain what changed. The change might affect speed, audience reach, visual design, sound, or audience response.

Step 4: Connect the change to journalism quality. A faster tool still needs accuracy, but it may require shorter updates and careful corrections.

Notebook Task

Make a four-column timeline chart in your notebook.

Column 1: Media tool or platform

Column 2: What it allowed journalists to do

Column 3: What changed for the audience

Column 4: One quality concern journalists must still manage

Add these rows: printed newspaper, radio news, television news, news website, mobile alert, and social media post.
