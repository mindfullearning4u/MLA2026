📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Your work must stay focused on journalism, media tools, factual reporting, and audience communication.

Part A: Media Transition

Choose one media transition: print to website, radio to podcast, television to streaming video, website update to mobile alert, or printed photo to digital slideshow. Identify the older method and the newer method.

Part B: Benefit and Responsibility

Explain one benefit the newer method creates for the audience. Then explain one responsibility it creates for the journalist.

Part C: Balanced Explanation

Write one paragraph explaining how this technology change affects journalism. Include the words platform, distribution, and verification.
