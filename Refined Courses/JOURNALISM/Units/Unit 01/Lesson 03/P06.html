P06 Independent Work

Complete this task in your notebook before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Choose one journalism technology transition: print to radio, radio to television, television to website, website to mobile alert, or website to social media post.

Parts A, B, and C

Part A: Name the older and newer media tools.

Part B: Explain two things that changed for journalists or audiences.

Part C: Explain one quality concern that remains important after the technology change.

Your response should use the words transition, technique, audience, and accuracy.
