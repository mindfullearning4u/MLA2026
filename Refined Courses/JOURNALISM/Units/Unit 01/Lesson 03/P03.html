P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Journalism Application

Technology changes journalism decisions. A printed article may need a strong headline, concise paragraphs, and enough context for readers who see it later. A mobile alert needs immediate clarity, a verified fact, and sometimes a link to a fuller story. A podcast may use audio clips, but the reporter still needs accurate source notes.

Your job is to explain how the tool changes the reporting and presentation choices.

Step-by-Step: Explain the Effect of Technology

Step 1: Identify the older method and the newer method.

Step 2: Describe what changed for the audience. Did information become faster, more visual, more interactive, or easier to access?

Step 3: Describe what changed for the journalist. Did reporting require faster verification, stronger visuals, clearer audio, or updated design?

Step 4: Make a balanced judgment. Explain both the benefit and the responsibility.

Embedded Scenario

Scenario: The Rain Delay Update

The school baseball game is delayed because of rain. In the past, the school newspaper would report the delay in the next printed issue. Today, the student newsroom can post a website update and send a mobile notification within minutes. The athletic director confirms the new start time, but a student fan posts a different time on social media.

Think: The technology allows fast distribution, but the newsroom must rely on the confirmed source instead of the unverified social post.

Notebook Directions

Answer in four labeled parts.

Part 1: Identify the technology change in the rain delay scenario.

Part 2: Explain one benefit for the audience.

Part 3: Explain one responsibility for the journalist.

Part 4: Write one balanced sentence that includes both benefit and responsibility.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

Online news is better because it is faster, so reporters do not need to wait for confirmation.

Why this is incorrect: The answer notices speed but ignores verification. Faster publishing can help an audience, but it also creates a higher risk of spreading wrong information quickly.

Correct example:

Online news helps reporters update audiences quickly, but journalists still need to confirm facts before publishing because errors can spread immediately.

Why this is correct: The answer explains both the benefit of technology and the responsibility that comes with it.
