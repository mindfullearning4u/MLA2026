P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use a technology transition to explain a journalism change.

Scenario

A school newspaper used to publish once each month in print. Now it also publishes short updates on a website the same day events happen.

Step-by-Step Thinking

Step 1: Name the transition: monthly print publishing to same-day web publishing.

Step 2: Identify the technique change: writers may create shorter, faster updates.

Step 3: Identify the audience change: readers can receive information sooner.

Step 4: Identify the quality concern: speed must not replace verification.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Online journalism is better because it is faster.

This is incomplete. Faster publishing is one change, but quality still depends on accuracy, clarity, and format choices.

Correct: Online journalism can publish faster than monthly print, so journalists must verify information quickly and clearly before posting.

This is correct because it explains the technology change and the continuing responsibility.

Notebook Task

Choose one transition from your timeline. Write a five-sentence explanation:

  1. Name the older medium.
  2. Name the newer medium.
  3. Explain what changed in technique.
  4. Explain what changed for the audience.
  5. Explain one quality concern journalists must manage.
