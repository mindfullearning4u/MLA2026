P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now connect structure to purpose. A media text is designed for a reader who needs information quickly. The structure and features should support that reader.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Ask what the audience needs first. Students need to know that the bike rack area is open.

Step 2: Ask what the audience needs next. Students need to know where it is and how to use it safely.

Step 3: Decide which feature supports which need. The map supports location. The sidebar supports safety actions. The caption supports image understanding.

Step 4: Evaluate placement. A safety checklist works well as a sidebar because it is short, practical, and easy to scan.

Step 5: Explain impact. The features make the article more useful because readers can find action steps quickly.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Media Text: School Safety Web Article

Headline: New Bike Rack Area Opens Near East Gate

Subheading: The school adds marked parking spaces and a short safety checklist for student cyclists.

Body: The school opened a new bike rack area near the east gate on Monday. The article begins with the schedule change, then explains why the location was chosen, then lists safety reminders. A sidebar titled Before You Ride reminds students to lock bikes, walk bikes on sidewalks, and use helmets. A photo caption identifies the east gate entrance and notes that the rack area is visible from the front office. A map shows the route from the bike racks to the main courtyard.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The map is included because maps look good on a page.

Correct: The map helps readers locate the route from the bike racks to the main courtyard.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect answer describes decoration. The correct answer explains function: the map helps readers understand location and movement.

Notebook Directions

Choose two media features from the embedded source. For each one, write one sentence naming the feature and one sentence explaining its function for the audience.
