Embedded Unit 4 Synthesis Source: Student Media Critique File

Topic: A school proposal to replace printed club flyers with a weekly digital activity board.

News Brief: The activities office is testing a digital board that lists club meetings, tryout dates, volunteer events, and academic team deadlines. The board would appear on hallway screens and the school website every Monday.

Support Argument: Supporters say the digital board would reduce paper waste, update quickly, and help students find information in one place. They point to last month, when three club flyers had outdated room numbers after schedule changes.

Opposing Argument: Opponents say printed flyers are easier to notice during passing time and better for students who do not check the website. They argue that a digital board may be ignored if it contains too much information at once.

Media Features and Language: The draft package uses the headline One Board, Every Opportunity, a sidebar titled What Changes Monday, a screenshot caption, and the phrase a clearer path through the noise of school announcements.

Part A

Analyze one media feature and explain how it supports audience understanding.

Part B

Compare the support and opposing arguments. Explain which evidence is stronger and why.

Part C

Analyze one rhetorical or figurative language choice and explain its effect on the audience.
