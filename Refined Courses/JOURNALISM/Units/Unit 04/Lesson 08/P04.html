📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 04 | Lesson 08

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Question: How does the sidebar help the audience?

Modeled thinking: A sidebar separates action steps from the larger article.

Answer: The What Changes Monday sidebar helps readers find practical updates quickly.

Reasoning: This supports media structure and audience use.

Example 2

Question: Which opposing claim needs attention?

Modeled thinking: Opponents worry students may ignore the website or miss printed hallway reminders.

Answer: The package should address access and visibility because those concerns affect whether the digital board will reach all students.

Reasoning: This evaluates validity and audience impact.

Example 3

Question: How does language shape audience response?

Modeled thinking: The phrase clearer path through the noise of school announcements is figurative.

Answer: The phrase makes the digital board sound organized and helpful by comparing scattered announcements to noise.

Reasoning: This explains rhetoric and language effect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The headline is effective because it is short.

Correct: The headline is effective because it frames the digital board as opportunity and helps readers see the proposal positively.

Teachable explanation: Length is not enough. The analysis must explain effect.
