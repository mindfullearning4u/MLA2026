P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Use the embedded media post to show that you can analyze purpose, appeals, and language independently. Write in complete sentences and explain your reasoning.

Embedded Source

Embedded Media Text: Student Campaign Web Post

Headline: Join the Cafeteria Waste Challenge

Opening: Every lunch tray tells a story about our school choices. This month, the student newspaper invites every table to reduce wasted food and packaging.

Body: Last week, cafeteria staff counted twelve full trash bags after one lunch period. The environmental club says many unopened milk cartons and packaged snacks were thrown away. The post asks students to take only what they plan to eat, place unopened items on the share table when allowed, and sort bottles into recycling bins. It ends with the line, Small daily choices can make our cafeteria cleaner, calmer, and more responsible.

Sidebar: Three Ways to Help Today: take only what you need, use the share table correctly, and recycle bottles before leaving.

Part A

Identify the author purpose in one complete sentence. Your sentence must include what the writer wants readers to do or believe.

Part B

Choose one detail that creates a logical appeal. Explain how that detail supports the purpose.

Part C

Choose one word or phrase with a strong connotation. Explain how that language affects the reader and supports the purpose.
