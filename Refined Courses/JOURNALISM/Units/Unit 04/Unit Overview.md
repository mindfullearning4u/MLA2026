# JOURNALISM - Unit 04 Overview: Reporting, Interviews, and Information Gathering

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops reporting and information gathering. Students generate research questions, conduct interviews, gather notes, evaluate source relevance, refine the scope of questions, and synthesize information from reliable and valid sources.

Emphasis is placed on question development, interviews, note-taking, source integration, reliability, validity, relevance, synthesis, and responsible reporting. Students use information gathering to support accurate journalistic writing.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to selected Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards with an emphasis on reporting, research, source evaluation, synthesis, and real-world communication. Students are expected to demonstrate writing fluency and conceptual understanding through interview-based reporting and source-informed news writing.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- research question development;
- interview preparation;
- accurate note-taking;
- reliable and valid sources;
- refinement of scope;
- synthesis of information;
- interview-based reporting.

Assessment items require students to conduct research to answer a question, refine the scope of the question based on findings, synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources, and apply reporting processes to interview-based journalistic writing.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit asks students to solve information-gathering problems. Students determine what questions need to be asked, what sources are reliable, and how gathered information should be synthesized for accurate reporting.
