P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now move from identifying support to evaluating support. A detail is not strong just because it is true. It is strong when it clearly develops the central idea.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Test the detail. Ask: Does this detail explain why arriving on time helps students begin prepared?

Step 2: Look for a direct connection. Missing opening instructions directly connects to preparedness.

Step 3: Watch for weakly related details. A detail about the color of advisory posters might be true, but it would not strongly support preparedness unless the text explains why it matters.

Step 4: Explain the support in your own words. Do not just quote a detail. Explain how it develops the central idea.

Step 5: Consider structure. The article gives the central idea, then uses source details to develop it, then ends with the action advisory teachers will take.

Embedded Source Reminder

Embedded Media Text: Attendance Campaign Article

Headline: Advisory Campaign Encourages Students to Arrive on Time

Body: The article explains that the school is starting a two-week advisory campaign about arriving on time. The central idea is that consistent arrival helps students begin class prepared. The article supports this idea with three details: the assistant principal says students miss opening instructions when they arrive late, a teacher says the first five minutes often include warm-up practice, and a student mentor says reminders in advisory can help students plan morning routines. The article ends by explaining that advisory teachers will review arrival tips each Monday.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The central idea is attendance.

Correct: The central idea is that consistent arrival helps students begin class prepared.

Teachable Explanation: Attendance is only the topic. The central idea must state the message the article develops about attendance.

Notebook Directions

Choose the strongest supporting detail in the embedded source. Write two sentences: one naming the detail and one explaining exactly how it develops the central idea.
