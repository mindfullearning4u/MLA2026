Embedded Source

Embedded Media Text: Attendance Campaign Article

Headline: Advisory Campaign Encourages Students to Arrive on Time

Body: The article explains that the school is starting a two-week advisory campaign about arriving on time. The central idea is that consistent arrival helps students begin class prepared. The article supports this idea with three details: the assistant principal says students miss opening instructions when they arrive late, a teacher says the first five minutes often include warm-up practice, and a student mentor says reminders in advisory can help students plan morning routines. The article ends by explaining that advisory teachers will review arrival tips each Monday.

Guided Practice Strategy

Use this process before answering:

  1. Separate topic from central idea.
  2. Find the detail being tested.
  3. Ask how the detail develops the central idea.
  4. Choose the answer that explains the connection clearly.

Do not choose an answer just because it repeats a word from the source. Choose the answer that explains support.
