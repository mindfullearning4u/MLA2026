📰 JOURNALISM | Unit 04 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated media literacy and audience analysis

This lesson combines media structure, central idea support, author purpose, rhetoric, and opposing claims. You will analyze a media package as a whole instead of treating each piece separately.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.JOUR.MED.02 - Central Idea Support in Media Texts; MLA.JOUR.MED.04 - Opposing Arguments and Claims.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.MED.01, MLA.JOUR.MED.03, MLA.JOUR.MED.07.

What You Will Learn

  • How to analyze central idea support across a media package.
  • How to compare opposing claims on the same issue.
  • How structure and features guide audience understanding.
  • How rhetoric shapes audience judgment.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded lunch schedule media package.
  • Identify central idea, claims, evidence, and features.
  • Evaluate how effectively the package supports audience understanding.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain central idea support with source evidence.
  • Compare two opposing arguments accurately.
  • Evaluate evidence and rhetorical choices.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.MED.02 and MLA.JOUR.MED.04.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can analyze how a media package uses structure, evidence, and competing claims to help an audience understand an issue.
