P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Opposing Arguments and Claims

This lesson teaches how to compare two opposing arguments on the same topic. In journalism, readers need more than opinions. They need to know which claims are valid, which evidence is relevant, and which argument is more effective.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.MED.04 - Opposing Arguments and Claims.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.NEWS.02 - Argument and Editorial Writing; MLA.JOUR.REP.02 - Evidence and Reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify opposing claims on the same issue.
  • How to compare evidence used by each side.
  • How to evaluate claim validity and effectiveness.
  • How to explain which argument is stronger and why.

What You Will Do

  • Read two opposing editorial drafts.
  • Identify each main claim.
  • Compare evidence, reasoning, and limitations.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • State each opposing claim accurately.
  • Evaluate evidence from both arguments.
  • Explain effectiveness and validity without relying on personal preference.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.MED.04.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can compare two arguments about the same issue and explain which claims are better supported by evidence and reasoning.
