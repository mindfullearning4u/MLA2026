P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Rhetoric in media texts

This lesson teaches how rhetoric works in media texts. Rhetoric is not just fancy wording. It is the set of choices a writer uses to influence what an audience thinks, values, or decides.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.JOUR.MED.07 - Rhetoric in Media Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.JOUR.MED.03 - Author Purpose, Appeals, and Language; MLA.JOUR.MED.04 - Opposing Arguments and Claims.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify rhetorical choices in a media text.
  • How practical, safety, and fairness appeals influence readers.
  • How claim, evidence, and rhetoric work together.
  • How to explain rhetoric without simply agreeing or disagreeing.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded editorial media post.
  • Identify appeals and rhetorical choices.
  • Explain how rhetoric supports the author purpose.
  • Complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify rhetorical choices accurately.
  • Explain how appeals support a claim.
  • Use source evidence in your explanation.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence aligned to MLA.JOUR.MED.07.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can explain how a media writer uses rhetoric to influence an audience.
