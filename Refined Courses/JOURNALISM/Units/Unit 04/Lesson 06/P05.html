Embedded Source

Embedded Media Text: Editorial Media Post

Headline: Keep the Courtyard Open After School

Opening: The courtyard should remain open for one hour after dismissal because students need a supervised place to wait, study, and meet before activities.

Body: Last month, more than forty students waited outside the front office each afternoon before clubs, tutoring, or rides home. A supervised courtyard would move students away from crowded sidewalks and give staff a clear place to monitor. The proposal is not asking for unlimited campus access. It asks for one supervised hour, clear behavior rules, and a sign-in table.

Rhetorical choices: The post uses a practical appeal by naming the time limit and supervision plan. It uses a safety appeal by mentioning crowded sidewalks. It uses a fairness appeal by explaining that students waiting for activities need a reasonable place to be.

Guided Practice Strategy

Use this process before answering:

  1. Identify the claim.
  2. Find the rhetorical appeal named in the question.
  3. Connect the appeal to a source detail.
  4. Explain how the appeal influences the audience.

Do not choose based only on agreement. Choose the answer that explains rhetoric.
