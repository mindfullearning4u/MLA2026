P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question: What is the claim of the media post?

Modeled thinking: I look for the position the writer wants readers to accept. The opening sentence says the courtyard should remain open after dismissal.

Answer: The claim is that the courtyard should remain open for one supervised hour after school.

Reasoning: This answer includes both the position and the condition of supervision.

Example 2

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question: How does the writer use a safety appeal?

Modeled thinking: Safety appeal appears when the post mentions crowded sidewalks and staff monitoring.

Answer: The writer uses a safety appeal by arguing that a supervised courtyard would move students away from crowded sidewalks and into a monitored area.

Reasoning: This connects the evidence to audience concern about safety.

Example 3

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question: How does the writer answer a possible concern?

Modeled thinking: A reader may worry that open courtyard time means unlimited campus access. The writer directly says the proposal is not unlimited.

Answer: The writer answers a possible concern by limiting the proposal to one supervised hour with rules and sign-in.

Reasoning: This rhetorical choice makes the proposal seem controlled and realistic.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The post uses rhetoric because it has a headline.

Correct: The post uses rhetoric by combining safety, practical, and fairness appeals to make the courtyard proposal seem reasonable.

Teachable explanation: A headline is a feature, but rhetoric is the persuasive strategy. The correct answer explains how the strategy works.
