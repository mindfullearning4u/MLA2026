Worked example 1 - percent composition of CO2

  1. Molar mass = 12.01 + 2(16.00) = 44.01 g/mol.
  2. % C = 12.01 ÷ 44.01 × 100 = 27.29%.
  3. % O = 32.00 ÷ 44.01 × 100 = 72.71%.
  4. Check: 27.29 + 72.71 = 100.00%.

Worked example 2 - empirical formula from masses

  1. 2.40 g Mg ÷ 24.31 g/mol = 0.0987 mol Mg.
  2. 1.58 g O ÷ 16.00 g/mol = 0.0988 mol O.
  3. Divide both by 0.0987: Mg = 1.00 and O = 1.00.
  4. The empirical formula is MgO.

Worked example 3 - remove a fractional ratio

  1. After division, suppose N:O = 1.00:2.50.
  2. 2.50 is halfway between 2 and 3, so do not round it.
  3. Multiply both ratios by 2: 2.00:5.00.
  4. The empirical formula is N2O5.
