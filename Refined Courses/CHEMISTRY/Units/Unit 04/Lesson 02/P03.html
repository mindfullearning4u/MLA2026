Lesson summary

  • Fixed composition: a pure compound has a constant mass ratio of its elements.
  • Percent by mass: (mass of element in one mole of compound ÷ molar mass of compound) × 100.
  • Empirical formula: convert each element's mass to moles, divide all mole amounts by the smallest, then convert ratios to whole numbers.
  • Fractional ratios: multiply every ratio by the same small whole number; for example, 1:1.5 becomes 2:3.
  • Molecular formula: molecular formula = (empirical formula)n, where n = molecular molar mass ÷ empirical-formula mass.
Write in your notebook: the five summary statements above and this check: percent values should total about 100%, while empirical subscripts must be the smallest whole-number ratio.
