Unit 4 Lesson 02: Reaction Types

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.01

Florida alignment: SC.912.P.8.8

Learning target: I can classify synthesis, decomposition, single replacement, double replacement, acid-base, combustion, and redox reaction patterns.

Start Here

This lesson teaches the process step by step. Read the vocabulary, model, and examples in order before answering practice questions.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
synthesistwo or more reactants combine to form one main product
decompositionone reactant breaks into two or more products
replacement reactionatoms or ions switch partners
redoxa reaction involving electron transfer or oxidation-number change
Safety boundary: Reaction classification investigation using equation tables and flowcharts. No reaction performance is required. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
