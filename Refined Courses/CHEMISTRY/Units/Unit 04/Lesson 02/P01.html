Percent Composition and Empirical Formulas - Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: calculate percent composition from a formula and determine an empirical formula from mass or percent data.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.REA.02 and MLA.CHEM.LAB.02

Learning objectives

  • Use molar mass to calculate percent by mass.
  • Convert mass or percent data to moles.
  • Reduce mole ratios to smallest whole-number subscripts.
  • Explain the difference between empirical and molecular formulas.
