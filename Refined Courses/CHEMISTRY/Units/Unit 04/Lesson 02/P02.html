How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Count the number of reactants and products.
  2. Look for one product, one reactant, partner switching, oxygen involvement, or acid-base patterns.
  3. Match the equation form to a reaction type.
  4. Use evidence from the equation, not from the substance names alone.
  5. State the classification and the pattern that supports it.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
