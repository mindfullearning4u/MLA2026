Stoichiometry and Reaction Quantities: Worked Examples

Study each worked example. Unit 4 answers must show equation, data, or observation reasoning.

Worked Example: H2 to H2O

From 2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O, 3.0 mol H2 produces 3.0 mol H2O because the ratio is 2:2.

  1. Identify the evidence or quantity.
  2. Apply the lesson rule.
  3. State the conclusion with a reason.

Worked Example: H2 to NH3

From N2 + 3H2 -> 2NH3, 6.0 mol H2 x 2 mol NH3/3 mol H2 = 4.0 mol NH3.
