Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
stoichiometrycalculation of reactant and product quantities from a balanced equation
mole ratioa ratio from coefficients in a balanced equation
limiting reactantthe reactant that runs out first and limits product amount
theoretical yieldthe calculated amount of product possible from given reactants
Science Safety and Resource Note: Stoichiometry calculation and data interpretation using balanced equations and quantity tables. No reaction performance is required. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
