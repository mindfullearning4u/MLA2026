How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Start with a balanced equation.
  2. Identify the given substance and the target substance.
  3. Write the mole ratio from coefficients.
  4. Multiply the given moles by the target-over-given mole ratio.
  5. Check that the unit and substance label match the question.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
