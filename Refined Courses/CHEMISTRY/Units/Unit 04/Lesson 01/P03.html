Moodle lesson summary

  1. Mole: one mole represents 6.022 × 1023 particles, just as one dozen represents 12 objects.
  2. Particle name: elements use atoms, molecular compounds use molecules, and ionic compounds use formula units.
  3. Molar mass: add the atomic masses shown in a chemical formula; the unit is g/mol.
  4. Conversion direction: multiply by Avogadro’s number for moles → particles and divide for particles → moles.
  5. Mass relationship: multiply by molar mass for moles → grams and divide by molar mass for grams → moles.
Write in your notebook: copy the five summary statements. Then copy this example: H2O molar mass = 2(1.01) + 16.00 = 18.02 g/mol.
