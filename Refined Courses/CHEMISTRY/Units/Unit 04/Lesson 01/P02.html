Vocabulary

Write each term and its definition in your notebook. The definitions are provided here so you do not have to search across several Savvas screens.

  1. mole: the amount of substance containing 6.022 × 1023 representative particles.
  2. Avogadro’s number: 6.022 × 1023 particles per mole.
  3. representative particle: the particle being counted: an atom, molecule, formula unit, ion, or electron.
  4. molar mass: the mass of one mole of a substance, expressed in grams per mole (g/mol).
  5. conversion factor: a ratio equal to one that changes units without changing the amount represented.
  6. dimensional analysis: a calculation method that multiplies by conversion factors so unwanted units cancel.
