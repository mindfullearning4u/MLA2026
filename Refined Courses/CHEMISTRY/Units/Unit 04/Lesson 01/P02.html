How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the starting substance or substances.
  2. Look for evidence of a new substance: gas, precipitate, color change, odor, light, heat change, or new solid.
  3. Separate observation from inference. Observation is what is detected; inference is the explanation.
  4. Check whether a physical change could explain the evidence.
  5. State the claim and support it with at least two pieces of evidence.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
