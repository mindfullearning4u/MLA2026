The Mole Concept and Molar Relationships — Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: understand the mole as a counting unit and convert among moles, representative particles, and mass.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.REA.02 and MLA.CHEM.LAB.02.

Learning objectives

  • Explain why chemists use the mole and Avogadro’s number.
  • Identify the correct representative particle for an element, molecular compound, or ionic compound.
  • Calculate molar mass from a chemical formula.
  • Use dimensional analysis to convert moles, particles, and grams with correct units.
