Unit 4 Lesson 01: Evidence of Chemical Reactions

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.01; MLA.CHEM.MAT.02

Florida alignment: ELA.K12.EE.3.1

Learning target: I can identify and characterize evidence that a chemical reaction has occurred.

Start Here

This lesson teaches the process step by step. Read the vocabulary, model, and examples in order before answering practice questions.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
chemical reactiona process that rearranges atoms to form new substances
reactanta starting substance in a chemical reaction
producta substance formed by a chemical reaction
evidenceobservable or measurable information used to support a claim
Science Safety and Resource Note: Reaction evidence scenario/video/data analysis using observation tables. No unsupervised reactions are required. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.

Required Direct Simulation Link

Open this exact simulation/resource: PhET Reactants, Products and Leftovers

Direct URL: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/reactants-products-and-leftovers/latest/reactants-products-and-leftovers_all.html

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. After the simulation opens, use the reaction or molecules screen rather than the sandwich analogy if that screen is available.
  2. Set starting reactant amounts, then change only one reactant amount at a time.
  3. Observe the products formed and any leftover reactants after the model reaction.
  4. Record reactant amounts, product amounts, leftovers, and the ratio or conservation evidence used for Evidence of Chemical Reactions.

Evidence check: Observe the model, table, data, image, or source section carefully. Your notes must include the requested observations, data, or model evidence before you answer the lesson questions.
