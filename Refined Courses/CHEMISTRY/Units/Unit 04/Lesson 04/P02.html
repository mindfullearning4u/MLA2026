How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the starting unit.
  2. Identify the target unit.
  3. Choose the conversion factor that cancels the starting unit.
  4. Multiply across the top and divide by the bottom.
  5. Check whether the answer size is reasonable.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
