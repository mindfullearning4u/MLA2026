CHEMISTRY Unit 4 Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: The Mole Concept
Lesson Purpose: Mole-particle-mass conversion model using a road map and conversion table. No physical samples are required.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.CHEM.REA.02
Support Standards: MA.K12.MTR.3.1

What You Will Learn

You will learn the chemistry concept in this lesson by reading evidence, using models or data, and explaining the reasoning that connects the evidence to the mapped standard.

What You Will Do

  1. Read the vocabulary and teaching sequence carefully.
  2. Use the included tables, models, diagrams, scenarios, simulations, or data displays to make sense of the chemistry idea.
  3. Complete notebook evidence that shows your reasoning, not just your answer.
  4. Use the checkpoint to show independent mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining the lesson concept with accurate chemistry vocabulary, using evidence from the required stimulus, and writing reasoning that connects the evidence to the standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you work like a chemistry student: you will read evidence, use models or data, and explain how the evidence supports a scientific answer.

Lesson Content and Resource Directions

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.02

Florida alignment: MA.K12.MTR.3.1

Learning target: I can apply mole reasoning to particles, mass, and chemical quantities.

Start Here

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
mole6.022 x 10^23 particles of a substance
Avogadro constant6.022 x 10^23 particles per mole
molar massthe mass in grams of one mole of a substance
conversion factora ratio used to change units without changing the amount represented
Science Safety and Resource Note: Mole-particle-mass conversion model using a road map and conversion table. No physical samples are required. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
