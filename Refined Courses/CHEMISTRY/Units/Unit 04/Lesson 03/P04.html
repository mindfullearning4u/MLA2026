Example 1 - molarity from moles

0.750 mol KCl in 1.50 L: M = 0.750 ÷ 1.50 = 0.500 M.

Example 2 - molarity from grams

  1. Convert 11.7 g NaCl to moles: 11.7 g ÷ 58.44 g/mol = 0.200 mol.
  2. Divide by 0.400 L: 0.200 ÷ 0.400 = 0.500 M.

Example 3 - dilution

  1. Write M₁V₁ = M₂V₂.
  2. Substitute: (2.00 M)(V₁) = (0.200 M)(0.500 L).
  3. V₁ = 0.0500 L = 50.0 mL stock solution.
  4. Add solvent until the total solution volume is 500 mL.
Reasonableness check: a dilution must have a lower final molarity; the required stock volume must be less than the final volume.
