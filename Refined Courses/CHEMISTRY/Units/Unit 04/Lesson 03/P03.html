Lesson summary

  • Particle view: at the same volume, more solute particles means a more concentrated solution.
  • Molarity: M = moles of solute ÷ liters of solution.
  • Units first: convert milliliters to liters before using M = mol/L.
  • Finding moles: mol = M × L.
  • Dilution: M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ because adding solvent changes volume and molarity, not moles of solute.
