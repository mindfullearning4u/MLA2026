Notebook Task 1

Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your chemistry notebook. These meanings will be used throughout the lesson.

  1. solution: a homogeneous mixture in which particles are evenly distributed.
  2. solute: the substance that is dissolved.
  3. solvent: the substance that dissolves the solute and is usually present in the greater amount.
  4. concentration: the amount of solute in a specified amount of solution.
  5. molarity (M): moles of solute per liter of solution; M = mol/L.
  6. dilution: lowering a solution's concentration by adding solvent.
  7. stock solution: a concentrated solution used to prepare a less-concentrated solution.
  8. aqueous solution: a solution in which water is the solvent.
Notebook evidence: all eight terms and definitions must appear in your U4 L3 Notebook Evidence Submission.
