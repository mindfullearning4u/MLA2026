Unit 4 Lesson 03: Chemical Equations and Conservation of Mass

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.02

Florida alignment: SC.912.P.8.9; MA.K12.MTR.6.1

Learning target: I can use conservation of mass to represent reactions with balanced chemical equations.

Start Here

This lesson teaches the process step by step. Read the vocabulary, model, and examples in order before answering practice questions.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
chemical equationa symbolic statement showing reactants and products
coefficienta number placed before a formula to balance atom counts
balanced equationan equation with equal numbers of each atom on both sides
conservation of massmatter is not created or destroyed in a chemical reaction
Safety boundary: Conservation and balancing model analysis using atom inventory tables. PhET balancing may be used only as an assigned virtual resource. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
