Concentrations of Solutions - Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: describe concentration with particle models and calculate molarity and dilution quantities.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.SOL.01 and MLA.CHEM.LAB.02

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish dilute from concentrated solutions.
  • Calculate molarity using M = mol/L.
  • Rearrange the molarity equation to find moles or volume.
  • Use M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ for dilution.
