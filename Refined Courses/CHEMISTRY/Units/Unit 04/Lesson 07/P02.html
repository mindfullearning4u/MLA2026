How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the measured and calculated quantities.
  2. Check units before calculating.
  3. Use the balanced equation or percent-yield formula as appropriate.
  4. Compare the result to a reasonable range.
  5. Explain possible causes if the data do not match the expected result.
