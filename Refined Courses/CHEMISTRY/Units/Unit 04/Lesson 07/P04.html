P04 Worked Example

Common Mistake: Writing the final chemistry answer without showing the evidence or reasoning steps.
Incorrect: The answer is correct because that is the rule.
Correct: The answer is supported because the model, formula, table, data, or diagram shows the evidence and the reasoning explains how it supports the claim.

Teachable Explanation

Worked examples are useful because they show how to move from evidence to reasoning to a chemistry conclusion.

Template Reasoning Frame:
  1. Step 1: Identify the chemistry claim or question.
  2. Step 2: Select evidence from the model, data table, formula, diagram, or source.
  3. Step 3: Explain why the evidence supports the answer.

Interpretation: A complete worked example states what the answer means in chemistry terms, not just which option is correct.

Study each worked example. Unit 4 answers must show equation, data, or observation reasoning.

Worked Example 1: Percent yield

8.5 g / 10.0 g x 100 = 85 percent yield, which is possible.
