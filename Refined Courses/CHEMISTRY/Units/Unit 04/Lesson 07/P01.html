Unit 4 Lesson 07: Quantitative Reaction Data

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.02; MLA.CHEM.LAB.03

Florida alignment: MA.K12.MTR.6.1

Learning target: I can analyze reaction data for reasonableness, evidence, and calculation accuracy.

Start Here

This lesson teaches the process step by step. Read the vocabulary, model, and examples in order before answering practice questions.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
quantitative datanumerical evidence from measurement or calculation
percent yieldactual yield divided by theoretical yield, multiplied by 100 percent
reasonablenesswhether an answer makes sense based on units, size, and chemistry context
source of errora factor that can make measured data differ from the expected value
Science Safety and Resource Note: Reaction data reasonableness and limiting information analysis using data tables and calculation organizers. No reaction performance is required. Physical reactions, heating, mixing, chemicals, pressure, or nuclear/radiation materials are not part of this independent lesson and require separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.

Required Direct Simulation Link

Open this exact simulation/resource: PhET Reactants, Products and Leftovers

Direct URL: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/reactants-products-and-leftovers/latest/reactants-products-and-leftovers_all.html

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. After the simulation opens, use the reaction or molecules screen rather than the sandwich analogy if that screen is available.
  2. Set starting reactant amounts, then change only one reactant amount at a time.
  3. Observe the products formed and any leftover reactants after the model reaction.
  4. Record reactant amounts, product amounts, leftovers, and the ratio or conservation evidence used for Quantitative Reaction Data.

Evidence check: Observe the model, table, data, image, or source section carefully. Your notes must include the requested observations, data, or model evidence before you answer the lesson questions.
