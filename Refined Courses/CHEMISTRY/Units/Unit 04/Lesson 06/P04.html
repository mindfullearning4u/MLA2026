Chemical and Nuclear Reactions: Worked Examples

Study each worked example. Unit 4 answers must show equation, data, or observation reasoning.

Worked Example: Combustion

Combustion changes bonds and makes new compounds, but carbon nuclei remain carbon nuclei, so it is chemical.

Worked Example: Beta decay

A neutron changes into a proton and an electron is emitted, so the nucleus changes and the reaction is nuclear.

Worked Example: Dissolving salt

Ions separate in water; nuclei do not change, so this is not nuclear.
