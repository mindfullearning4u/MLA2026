How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Ask whether nuclei change.
  2. If only electrons, bonds, or ions rearrange, classify as chemical.
  3. If protons or neutrons change, classify as nuclear.
  4. Look for isotope changes, radiation emission, or element identity changes as nuclear evidence.
  5. State the classification and the particle-level evidence.
