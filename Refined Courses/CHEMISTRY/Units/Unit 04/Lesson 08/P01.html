CHEMISTRY Unit 4 Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Putting It All Together
Lesson Purpose: Unit synthesis across reaction evidence, equations, mole tables, stoichiometry data, and nuclear/chemical comparisons. No chemical or nuclear materials are required.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.CHEM.REA.01; MLA.CHEM.REA.02; MLA.CHEM.REA.03; MLA.CHEM.LAB.02; MLA.CHEM.LAB.03
Support Standards: ELA.K12.EE.1.1; MA.K12.MTR.6.1

What You Will Learn

You will learn the chemistry concept in this lesson by reading evidence, using models or data, and explaining the reasoning that connects the evidence to the mapped standard.

What You Will Do

  1. Read the vocabulary and teaching sequence carefully.
  2. Use the included tables, models, diagrams, scenarios, simulations, or data displays to make sense of the chemistry idea.
  3. Complete notebook evidence that shows your reasoning, not just your answer.
  4. Use the checkpoint to show independent mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining the lesson concept with accurate chemistry vocabulary, using evidence from the required stimulus, and writing reasoning that connects the evidence to the standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you work like a chemistry student: you will read evidence, use models or data, and explain how the evidence supports a scientific answer.

Lesson Content and Resource Directions

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.REA.01; MLA.CHEM.REA.02; MLA.CHEM.REA.03; MLA.CHEM.LAB.02; MLA.CHEM.LAB.03

Florida alignment: ELA.K12.EE.1.1; MA.K12.MTR.6.1

Required Direct Simulation Link

Open this exact simulation/resource: PhET Balancing Chemical Equations

Direct URL: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/balancing-chemical-equations/latest/balancing-chemical-equations_all.html

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. After the simulation opens, start with the introduction or balance-equation screen.
  2. Select one equation from the lesson activity and change coefficients only, not subscripts.
  3. Turn on atom counts, bar charts, or balance indicators if those tools are available.
  4. Record the unbalanced equation, final coefficients, atom counts on both sides, and the conservation rule shown by the model.

Evidence check: Observe the model, table, data, image, or source section carefully. Your notes must include the requested observations, data, or model evidence before you answer the lesson questions.

Do not leave this linked simulation/resource or use another one. Use this exact link so the model or source matches the lesson requirement.
