Water, Solutions, and Concentration Reasoning

Lesson focus: explain how water dissolves substances, identify solute and solvent, calculate concentration, and predict how adding solute, adding water, or evaporating water changes concentration.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.GAS.02 and MLA.CHEM.LAB.03

Learning objectives

  1. Use particle-level reasoning to explain dissolving in water.
  2. Distinguish solute, solvent, solution, dilute, concentrated, and saturated.
  3. Calculate concentration as solute amount divided by solution volume.
  4. Use controlled simulation evidence to explain concentration changes.
