Complete these steps exactly:
  1. Download the PDF. Enter your name, date, and “U3 L8.”
  2. Open PhET. On the opening screen, choose one solute from the solute menu.
  3. Before changing anything, record the starting volume and concentration as the baseline.
  4. Add solute without adding water. Wait for the concentration meter to stabilize; record the particle change and new concentration.
  5. Add water while keeping the amount and type of solute unchanged. Record the volume and concentration.
  6. Use evaporation to remove water. Do not add solute. Record the new volume and concentration.
  7. For each trial, draw the particle model shown on the PDF: solute dots, water level, and labels.
