Vocabulary

Words You Need

Write each term and definition in your notebook. These definitions are provided so you can focus on understanding and applying them.

  1. solution: a homogeneous mixture in which particles are evenly distributed.
  2. solute: the substance that is dissolved.
  3. solvent: the substance that does the dissolving and is usually present in the greater amount.
  4. aqueous: describing a solution in which water is the solvent.
  5. concentration: the amount of solute in a given amount of solution.
  6. dilute: having a relatively small amount of solute compared with solution volume.
  7. concentrated: having a relatively large amount of solute compared with solution volume.
  8. solubility: the maximum amount of a solute that can dissolve under stated conditions.
  9. saturated solution: a solution containing the maximum dissolved solute at a given temperature.
