Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning in this lesson
covalent bonda bond formed when atoms share one or more pairs of electrons
moleculea neutral group of atoms held together by covalent bonds
Lewis structurea diagram that shows valence electrons as dots and bonds as shared pairs
single bondone shared pair of electrons between two atoms
Science Safety and Resource Note: Covalent sharing and molecular structure model analysis using Lewis diagrams. No chemical handling is required. Any physical lab, chemical sample, heating, mixing, or synthesis activity requires separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
