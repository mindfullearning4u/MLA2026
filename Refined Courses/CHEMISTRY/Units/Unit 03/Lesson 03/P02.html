How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Count the atoms shown in the molecular formula.
  2. Identify which atoms need additional valence electrons to reach a stable outer level.
  3. Use shared electron pairs to connect atoms.
  4. Check that hydrogen has one bond and that typical nonmetals have enough shared electrons for a stable arrangement.
  5. Interpret the formula as the actual number of atoms in one molecule.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the case, key information, and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion. Then uncover the conclusion and compare.

Why This Matters

Chemical bonding is not memorizing labels. The goal is to connect particle structure, charge, formulas, and properties so a chemical explanation can be defended with evidence.
