Possible Part B answers

  1. Formula and model: each subscript states the number of that atom in one molecule. H2O therefore contains two H atoms and one O atom.
  2. Why covalent: the nonmetal atoms are held together by shared electron pairs. Each shared pair is a covalent bond.
  3. Molecule versus formula unit: H2O, CO2, and NH3 are separate molecules with actual atom counts. An ionic formula unit gives the simplest neutral ratio in a repeating lattice.
