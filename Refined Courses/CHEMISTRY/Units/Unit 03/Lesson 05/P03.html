Intermolecular Forces: Misconception Repair

Common Incorrect Idea

Boiling water does not break the O-H covalent bonds in water molecules. Boiling separates water molecules from each other by overcoming intermolecular attractions.

Step-by-Step Correction

  1. Identify the exact chemistry claim being made.
  2. Find the evidence in the model or table from Page 2.
  3. Decide whether the evidence describes particles, charges, formulas, names, or properties.
  4. Use one sentence to state the corrected claim.
  5. Use a second sentence to explain why the corrected claim is better.

Correction Frame

The correct explanation is ___ because the evidence shows ___.

Practice: Rewrite the misconception in your own words, then correct it using one detail from the model.
