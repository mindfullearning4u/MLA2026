Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning in this lesson
intermolecular forcean attraction between separate molecules or particles
intramolecular bonda bond within a molecule or compound particle
hydrogen bondinga strong intermolecular attraction involving H bonded to N, O, or F and a nearby N, O, or F
van der Waals forcesweaker attractions that can occur between particles because of temporary or uneven charge distribution
Science Safety and Resource Note: Intermolecular force comparison using models and boiling point/property data. No liquid testing is required. Any physical lab, chemical sample, heating, mixing, or synthesis activity requires separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
