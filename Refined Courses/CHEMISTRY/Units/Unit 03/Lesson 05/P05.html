Independent Application — PhET Molecule Polarity

Click here to open PhET Molecule Polarity

Click here to download the Independent Application answer sheet

Complete the simulation and PDF exactly:
  1. Open the PhET link. On the opening screen, select Two Atoms.
  2. Turn on Bond Dipole, Partial Charges, and Bond Character.
  3. Trial 1: Set the two electronegativity controls equal. Record the electronegativity difference and what happens to the dipole arrow and partial charges.
  4. Trial 2: Move only one electronegativity control to make a moderate difference. Record the evidence.
  5. Trial 3: Move the controls as far apart as possible. Record the evidence.
  6. Use the Home button, then select Three Atoms.
  7. Turn on Bond Dipoles, Molecular Dipole, and Partial Charges.
  8. Trial 4: Make a symmetric arrangement. Record whether the bond-dipole arrows cancel and whether a molecular dipole remains.
  9. Trial 5: Make an asymmetric arrangement. Record the molecular-dipole evidence.
  10. Answer all three analysis questions on the PDF. Use a specific trial as evidence in Question 3.

Important connection: PhET models bond and molecular polarity. Polarity helps you predict whether molecules can form dipole-dipole attractions; it does not display intermolecular forces directly.
