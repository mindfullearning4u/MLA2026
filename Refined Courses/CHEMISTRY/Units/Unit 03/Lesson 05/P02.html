NOTEBOOK TASK 1 — VOCABULARY

Vocabulary

Write every term and definition in your notebook. These definitions are provided so you do not need to search for them.

  1. intermolecular force: an attraction between separate molecules.
  2. intramolecular bond: a bond holding atoms together inside one molecule.
  3. London dispersion force: attraction caused by temporary, constantly changing electron distributions; it occurs in every atom and molecule.
  4. dipole-dipole force: attraction between the partially positive end of one polar molecule and the partially negative end of another.
  5. hydrogen bond: a strong dipole-dipole attraction involving H bonded to N, O, or F and a nearby N, O, or F.
  6. polar molecule: a molecule with an uneven charge distribution and a net dipole.
  7. nonpolar molecule: a molecule whose charge distribution has no net dipole.
  8. electronegativity: an atom’s ability to attract shared electrons in a bond.
  9. boiling point: the temperature at which a liquid’s vapor pressure equals the external pressure.
