## Notebook Evidence rubric — 100 points

### 1. Required Evidence: Vocabulary/Notes, Guided Practice, and Independent Work — 50 points

- **50:** Complete vocabulary/lesson notes, all guided-practice examples, and all independent-work questions are included.
- **40:** All three required components are present with minor omissions.
- **30:** Two required components are present but incomplete.
- **20:** Only one required component is attempted and major sections are missing.
- **0:** No required evidence submitted.

### 2. Chemistry Accuracy, Work, and Understanding — 40 points

- **40:** Accurate answers; complete models, calculations, tables, graphs, or explanations as required; correct chemistry notation; and clear understanding.
- **30:** Mostly accurate work with minor errors or gaps.
- **20:** Partial understanding with several errors or missing work.
- **10:** Major errors show limited understanding.
- **0:** No meaningful chemistry work.

### 3. Organization, Labels, and Submission Quality — 10 points

- **10:** Neat, readable, correctly labeled with course/unit/lesson, and all required pages submitted together in order.
- **7.5:** Clear and organized with only minor presentation issues.
- **5:** Partly organized, but several labels, pages, or submission requirements are missing.
- **2.5:** Difficult to follow; labels or pages are substantially missing.
- **0:** Unreadable, unusable, or not submitted correctly.

## Checkpoint rubric — 100 points

### 1. Completion of All Required Checkpoint Questions — 20 points

- **20:** Every checkpoint question and required part is completed and clearly labeled.
- **15:** One required question or part is incomplete.
- **10:** Several questions are attempted, but two or more required parts are incomplete.
- **5:** Only one required question or a small portion is completed.
- **0:** No checkpoint questions are completed.

### 2. Accuracy and Mastery of Lesson Objectives — 60 points

- **60:** Accurate responses demonstrate mastery of all assessed lesson objectives.
- **45:** Mostly accurate; minor errors do not obscure understanding.
- **30:** Partial mastery with several errors.
- **15:** Limited accuracy; major misconceptions remain.
- **0:** No correct understanding is demonstrated.

### 3. Reasoning, Evidence, Chemistry Notation, and Organization — 20 points

- **20:** Complete reasoning and evidence; required work shown; correct formulas, units, symbols, models, tables, or graphs; clear labels; organized presentation.
- **15:** Reasoning and work are mostly clear with minor gaps.
- **10:** Some reasoning is shown, but important steps, evidence, or notation are missing.
- **5:** Work is largely missing or difficult to follow.
- **0:** No work or reasoning is shown.

## TOR grading note

Grade only the evidence explicitly required in that lesson's Moodle pages and answer sheet. Do not deduct points for work the lesson did not request. When a student has corrected independent work after the Lesson Review, grade the corrected submission while using the original work and corrections as evidence of the student's process.
