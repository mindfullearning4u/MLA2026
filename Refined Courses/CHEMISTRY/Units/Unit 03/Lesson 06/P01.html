Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning in this lesson
chemical formulaa symbolic representation showing which elements are present and how many atoms or ions are represented
subscripta small number in a formula that tells how many atoms or ions of the preceding symbol are present
coefficienta number placed before a formula that multiplies the entire formula
polyatomic iona charged group of covalently bonded atoms that acts as one ion
