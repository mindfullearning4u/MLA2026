Vocabulary

Read each definition. These meanings are provided here so you do not have to search another page.

Chemical bond: an attraction that holds atoms or ions together in a stable arrangement.

Valence electron: an electron in the outer occupied energy level that can participate in bonding.

Electrostatic attraction: the force pulling opposite charges toward one another.

Electrostatic repulsion: the force pushing like charges away from one another.

Potential energy: stored energy associated with the positions of particles.

Stable arrangement: an arrangement with lower potential energy that requires added energy to separate.

Ion: an atom or group of atoms with a net charge because electrons were gained or lost.

Study direction: read the terms twice. On later pages, use the complete terms—not only “pull,” “push,” or “stable”—in your explanations.
