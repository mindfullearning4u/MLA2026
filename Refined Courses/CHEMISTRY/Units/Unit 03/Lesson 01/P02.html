How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Start with the outer electrons. Bonding depends mostly on valence electrons, not on inner electrons.
  2. Identify whether atoms can lower energy by losing, gaining, or sharing electrons.
  3. Track charges. Losing electrons makes a positive ion; gaining electrons makes a negative ion.
  4. Apply attraction. Opposite charges attract, and nuclei attract shared electron pairs.
  5. Explain the bond as a force of attraction, not as atoms wanting or choosing.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the case, key information, and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion. Then uncover the conclusion and compare.

Why This Matters

Chemical bonding is not memorizing labels. The goal is to connect particle structure, charge, formulas, and properties so a chemical explanation can be defended with evidence.
