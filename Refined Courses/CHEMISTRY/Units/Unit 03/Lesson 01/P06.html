Check Part A

  • +1e and −1e at 40 pm: opposite charges attract; the force arrows point toward one another.
  • +1e and +1e at 40 pm: like charges repel; the force arrows point away from one another.
  • +1e and −1e at 20 pm: the charges still attract, and the force magnitude is greater than at 40 pm because the charges are closer.

Check Part B

  1. A complete stability explanation states that a bond can form when electrostatic attractions create a lower-potential-energy arrangement than separated atoms.
  2. A complete NaCl comparison states that Na transfers one valence electron to Cl, producing Na⁺ and Cl⁻; opposite charges attract.
  3. If you wrote only “the atoms get eight electrons,” add that the octet is a prediction pattern and that electrostatic attraction and lower potential energy explain the bond.
