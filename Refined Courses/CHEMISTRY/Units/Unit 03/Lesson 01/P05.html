Use the simulation as evidence, then complete every item on the answer sheet.

Click here to open PhET Coulomb’s Law

Follow these steps exactly

  1. Download the PDF and enter your name and date.
  2. Open PhET. On the opening screen, click Atomic Scale (the picture on the right).
  3. Keep Force Values checked so the force is shown above each charge.
  4. Drag q₁ and q₂ so their separation is 40 pm.
  5. Use the Charge 1 and Charge 2 sliders or arrow buttons to set q₁ = +1e and q₂ = −1e. Record the force value and describe the direction of both arrows in row 1.
  6. Keep the distance at 40 pm. Change only q₂ to +1e. Record the force and arrow directions in row 2.
  7. Set q₂ back to −1e. Move q₂ until the separation is 20 pm. Record the force and arrows in row 3.
  8. Complete all Part B explanations. Use electrostatic attraction, electrostatic repulsion, potential energy, valence electrons, and stable arrangement where appropriate.
  9. Save the finished PDF. You will check and correct it on the next page.
