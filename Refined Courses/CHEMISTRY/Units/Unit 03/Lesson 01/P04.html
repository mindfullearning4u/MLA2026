Why Atoms Bond: Worked Examples

Study each worked example before attempting independent work. Each example shows the claim, evidence, and reasoning sequence.

Worked Example: Na and Cl

Na has 1 valence electron and Cl has 7. Na can lose 1 electron and Cl can gain 1. The resulting Na+ and Cl- attract, so an ionic bond can form.

  1. Identify the relevant particles or formula.
  2. Apply the lesson rule.
  3. State the chemistry conclusion with evidence.

Worked Example: H and H

Each H atom has 1 electron. The two nuclei both attract the shared pair of electrons between them. This shared attraction forms a covalent bond.

  1. Identify the relevant particles or formula.
  2. Apply the lesson rule.
  3. State the chemistry conclusion with evidence.

Worked Example: Mg and O

Mg can lose 2 electrons to form Mg2+. O can gain 2 electrons to form O2-. The 2+ and 2- ions attract strongly.
