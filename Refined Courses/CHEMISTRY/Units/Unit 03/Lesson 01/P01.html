Why Atoms Bond — Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: explain why atoms form bonds by connecting valence electrons, electrostatic attraction and repulsion, and lower potential energy.

MLA standard: MLA.CHEM.BON.01

Learning objectives

  • Identify the charged particles responsible for attraction and repulsion.
  • Explain why a bonded arrangement can be more stable than separated atoms.
  • Use a particle model as evidence for a bonding explanation.
