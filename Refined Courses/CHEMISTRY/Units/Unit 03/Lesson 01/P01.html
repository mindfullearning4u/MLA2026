Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning in this lesson
chemical bondan attraction that holds atoms or ions together in a stable arrangement
valence electronan electron in the outer occupied energy level of an atom
electrostatic attractionthe pull between opposite charges, such as a positive nucleus and negative electrons
stable arrangementa lower-energy arrangement that resists separating without added energy
