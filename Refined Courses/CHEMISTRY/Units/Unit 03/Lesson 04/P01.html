Metallic Bonding and Material Properties

Lesson focus: Use the sea-of-electrons model to explain electrical conductivity, malleability, ductility, melting point, and boiling point.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.BON.01 and MLA.CHEM.LAB.03

Learning objectives

  • Describe a metallic bond as attraction between positive metal cations and mobile valence electrons.
  • Use a particle model to explain why metals conduct electricity and can change shape without breaking.
  • Relate metallic-bond strength to melting and boiling points.
  • Use property evidence to identify metallic behavior.
