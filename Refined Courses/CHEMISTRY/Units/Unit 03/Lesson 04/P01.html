Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning in this lesson
metallic bondan attraction between metal cations and a mobile sea of valence electrons
conductivitythe ability to transfer electric charge or thermal energy
malleabilitythe ability to be hammered or pressed into shape
ductilitythe ability to be drawn into wire
Safety boundary: Structure-property evidence analysis using a metallic bonding model and property table. No heating or lab materials are required. Any physical lab, chemical sample, heating, mixing, or synthesis activity requires separate Teacher of Record assignment and safety controls.
