NOTEBOOK TASK 1 — VOCABULARY

Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your chemistry notebook. Keep this page with your U3 L4 notebook evidence.

  1. metallic bond: the electrostatic attraction between positive metal cations and freely moving valence electrons.
  2. sea of electrons: a model in which delocalized valence electrons move throughout an extended metal structure.
  3. delocalized electron: an electron that is not attached to one atom or one bond and can move through the metal.
  4. metal cation: the positive particle formed from a metal atom’s nucleus and core electrons after its valence electrons become delocalized.
  5. extended structure: a repeating arrangement of particles throughout a solid rather than separate molecules.
  6. electrical conductivity: the ability to carry electric current.
  7. malleable: able to be hammered or pressed into a new shape without breaking.
  8. ductile: able to be pulled into a wire.
  9. luster: the ability of a surface to reflect light.
