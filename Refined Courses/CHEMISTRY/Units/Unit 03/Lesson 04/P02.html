How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Picture metal atoms as positive metal ions surrounded by mobile valence electrons.
  2. Connect mobile electrons to conductivity because moving electrons can carry charge.
  3. Connect layered metal ions to malleability because layers can shift position.
  4. Explain ductility the same way: the structure can stretch while attractions remain.
  5. Use the bonding model as evidence for the property, not just the name of the material.

Why This Matters

Chemical bonding is not memorizing labels. The goal is to connect particle structure, charge, formulas, and properties so a chemical explanation can be defended with evidence.
