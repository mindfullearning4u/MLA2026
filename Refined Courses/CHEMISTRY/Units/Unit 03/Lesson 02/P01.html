Ionic Bonding and Formula Units — Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: explain ion formation, ionic attraction, charge balance, formula units, and repeating ionic lattices.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.BON.01 and MLA.CHEM.BON.02

Learning objectives

  • Determine an ion charge from protons and electrons.
  • Write a neutral ionic formula from cation and anion charges.
  • Explain why a formula unit is a ratio in a repeating lattice, not a separate molecule.

What you will submit

  1. Notebook Evidence: completed Independent Application PDF with review corrections.
  2. Checkpoint: completed Checkpoint PDF.
Sequence: learn first, study three worked examples, practice independently, correct your work, then complete the checkpoint.
