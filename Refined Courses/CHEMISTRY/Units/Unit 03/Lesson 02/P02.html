How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Write the cation first and the anion second.
  2. Record the charge on each ion.
  3. Choose the smallest number of each ion that makes total positive charge equal total negative charge.
  4. Write subscripts to show the ion ratio. Do not write the number 1 as a subscript.
  5. Check the formula by multiplying each ion charge by its subscript.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the case, key information, and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion. Then uncover the conclusion and compare.

Why This Matters

Chemical bonding is not memorizing labels. The goal is to connect particle structure, charge, formulas, and properties so a chemical explanation can be defended with evidence.
