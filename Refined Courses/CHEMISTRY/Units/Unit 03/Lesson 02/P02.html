Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your notebook. These definitions are provided because each term is required for the lesson.

  1. ion: an atom or group of atoms with a net electric charge.
  2. cation: a positively charged ion formed when electrons are lost.
  3. anion: a negatively charged ion formed when electrons are gained.
  4. ionic bond: the electrostatic attraction between oppositely charged ions.
  5. formula unit: the simplest whole-number ratio of ions in an ionic compound.
  6. ionic lattice: a repeating three-dimensional arrangement of positive and negative ions.
  7. charge balance: a ratio in which total positive charge and total negative charge add to zero.

Notebook evidence: include all seven terms and definitions.
