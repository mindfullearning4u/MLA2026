Part A — expected PhET ion results

  • Li+: 3 protons, 2 electrons, net charge +1; 3 - 2 = +1.
  • F-: 9 protons, 10 electrons, net charge -1; 9 - 10 = -1.
  • O2-: 8 protons, 10 electrons, net charge -2; 8 - 10 = -2.
Self-check: a cation has fewer electrons than protons. An anion has more electrons than protons. Neutrons affect mass number but not net charge.

Part B — expected formula results

  • NaCl: 1:1; (1 × +1) + (1 × -1) = 0.
  • MgCl2: 1:2; (1 × +2) + (2 × -1) = 0.
  • Al2O3: 2:3; (2 × +3) + (3 × -2) = 0.

Possible explanation: an ionic solid contains positive and negative ions arranged in a repeating lattice. Its formula unit gives the simplest whole-number ratio of ions that makes the total charge zero; it does not describe one separate molecule.
