How Ionic Compounds Form

This is the teaching page. Read and study; do not complete student work here.

1. Electrons create ions

An atom is neutral when protons equal electrons. Losing electrons leaves more protons than electrons and forms a cation. Gaining electrons creates more electrons than protons and forms an anion.

Cl-1718-1

2. Opposite charges attract

The cation and anion remain together because electrostatic attraction lowers the potential energy of the arrangement. Electron transfer produces the ions; attraction between the ions is the ionic bond.

3. Ionic formulas must be neutral

The subscripts show the least whole-number ratio that makes total charge zero. For Mg2+ and Cl-, one Mg2+ requires two Cl-: (+2) + 2(-1) = 0, so the formula is MgCl2.

4. Formula units describe lattices

Solid ionic compounds form repeating lattices. NaCl means a 1:1 ratio throughout the lattice; it does not mean one isolated NaCl molecule.
