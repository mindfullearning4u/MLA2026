Part A — exact PhET steps

  1. Select Atom.
  2. Turn on Net Charge.
  3. Build Na+ with 11 protons and 10 electrons; record the charge and evidence.
  4. Build Cl- with 17 protons and 18 electrons; record.
  5. Build Mg2+ with 12 protons and 10 electrons; record.

Part B — PDF steps

  1. Write the least whole-number ratio.
  2. Multiply charge by number of ions.
  3. Confirm the total is zero.
  4. Write the formula unit without charges.
  5. Complete the explanation.
