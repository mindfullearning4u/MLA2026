Build Ions and Write Formula Units

Important simulation limit: the particle buckets supply no more than 10 protons. You will build Li+, F-, and O2-. Do not try to build Na+, Cl-, or Mg2+ on this screen.

Part A — exact PhET steps

  1. Open the simulation. On the first screen, click Atom.
  2. On the right side, turn on Element, Net Charge, and Mass Number. Keep these displays open while you work.
  3. Build Li+: drag 3 red protons into the nucleus, 4 gray neutrons into the nucleus, and 2 blue electrons onto the electron shells. Check that the display says Lithium, net charge +1, and mass number 7. In the PDF, record 3 protons, 2 electrons, and +1 because 3 - 2 = +1.
  4. Clear the model before the next ion. Click the orange Reset All circular-arrow button in the lower-right corner and confirm the reset if asked. Then select Atom again and turn the three displays back on. If you do not reset, drag every particle back into its matching bucket.
  5. Build F-: drag 9 protons, 10 neutrons, and 10 electrons into the model. Check that the display says Fluorine, net charge -1, and mass number 19. Record 9 protons, 10 electrons, and -1 because 9 - 10 = -1.
  6. Reset the simulation again using the same method.
  7. Build O2-: drag 8 protons, 8 neutrons, and 10 electrons into the model. Check that the display says Oxygen, net charge -2, and mass number 16. Record 8 protons, 10 electrons, and -2 because 8 - 10 = -2.
  8. Before closing PhET, compare the models: a positive ion has fewer electrons than protons; a negative ion has more electrons than protons. Neutrons change mass, not charge.

Part B — complete the PDF

  1. For each pair of ions, write the least whole-number ratio that makes total charge zero.
  2. Multiply each ion's charge by the number of that ion in the ratio.
  3. Add the positive and negative totals. If the sum is not zero, correct the ratio.
  4. Write the formula unit without charge symbols.
  5. Complete the explanation using ions, total charge, formula unit, and lattice.
