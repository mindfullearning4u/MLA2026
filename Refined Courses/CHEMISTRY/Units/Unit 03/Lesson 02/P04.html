Three Worked Examples

Study how every answer is derived. You do not submit work from this page.

Example 1 — Na+ and Cl-

  1. Charges are +1 and -1.
  2. A 1:1 ratio gives (+1)+(-1)=0.
  3. Formula: NaCl.

Example 2 — Mg2+ and Cl-

  1. One Mg ion gives +2.
  2. Two chloride ions give 2(-1)=-2.
  3. Total charge is zero; formula: MgCl2.

Example 3 — Al3+ and O2-

  1. The smallest shared charge magnitude is 6.
  2. Two Al ions give +6; three oxide ions give -6.
  3. Total charge is zero; formula: Al2O3.
Model rule: a lattice repeats labeled ions with opposite charges adjacent. It is not one boxed molecule.

Self-check

Multiply each charge by its subscript and add the totals. A correct ionic formula totals zero.
