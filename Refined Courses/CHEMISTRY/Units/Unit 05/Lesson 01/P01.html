Unit 5 Lesson 01: Forms of Energy and Energy Transformations

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.ENE.01

Florida alignment: SC.912.P.10.1

Learning target: I can differentiate energy forms and transformations in chemical systems.

Start Here

This lesson is written to teach the concept step by step without a live presentation. Read the vocabulary, model, worked examples, and practice in order.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
energythe ability to cause change or do work
kinetic energyenergy of motion
potential energystored energy due to position or arrangement
energy transformationa change from one form of energy to another
Safety boundary: Energy transformation model analysis using energy flow diagrams and energy type charts. No heating or flame activity is required. Heat, flame, pressure, glassware, or solution testing is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.
