Forms of Energy and Energy Transformations: Misconception Repair

Common Incorrect Idea

Energy is not created from nothing during a chemical process. It changes form or is transferred between system and surroundings.

Step-by-Step Correction

  1. Identify the claim.
  2. Find the relevant data, graph region, particle model, or energy statement.
  3. Check whether the claim uses the correct relationship.
  4. Write the corrected claim.
  5. Explain why the evidence supports the correction.
Practice: Correct the misconception using one specific detail from Page 2.
