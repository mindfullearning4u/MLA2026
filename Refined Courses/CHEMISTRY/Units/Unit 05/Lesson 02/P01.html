Unit 5 Lesson 02: Temperature and Molecular Kinetic Energy

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.ENE.02

Florida alignment: SC.912.P.10.5

Learning target: I can relate temperature to average molecular kinetic energy.

Start Here

This lesson is written to teach the concept step by step without a live presentation. Read the vocabulary, model, worked examples, and practice in order.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
temperaturea measure related to average kinetic energy of particles
average kinetic energyaverage energy of particle motion in a sample
particle motionmovement of atoms, molecules, or ions
thermal energytotal energy associated with particle motion in a sample
Science Safety and Resource Note: Temperature vs particle motion data/model investigation using particle diagrams and temperature/kinetic energy data. No physical heating is required. Heat, flame, pressure, glassware, or solution testing is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.
