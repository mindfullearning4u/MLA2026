How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Read the temperature scale.
  2. Compare temperatures in kelvin when possible.
  3. Connect higher temperature to greater average particle kinetic energy.
  4. Do not assume all particles move at the same speed.
  5. Use the data trend to support the conclusion.
