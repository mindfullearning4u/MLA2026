How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Define the system.
  2. Look for temperature change in surroundings or energy diagram direction.
  3. If surroundings cool, energy entered the system.
  4. If surroundings warm, energy left the system.
  5. Classify the process and cite the evidence.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column, use the evidence and reasoning columns, and predict the conclusion before checking.
