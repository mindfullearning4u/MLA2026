This lesson is written to teach the concept step by step without a live presentation. Read the vocabulary, model, worked examples, and practice in order.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
solutionhomogeneous mixture of solute and solvent
solutesubstance dissolved
solventsubstance doing the dissolving
concentrationamount of solute in a given amount of solution
Safety boundary: Solution concentration and water property data/model analysis using particle diagrams, concentration tables, and graphs. No mixing or chemical testing is required. Heat, flame, pressure, glassware, or solution testing is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.

Required Direct Simulation Link

Open this exact simulation/resource: PhET Concentration

Direct URL: https://phet.colorado.edu/sims/html/concentration/latest/concentration_all.html

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. After the simulation opens, use the concentration container with solute, water, drain, and concentration display controls.
  2. Record the starting solute amount, solution volume, and concentration reading or color intensity.
  3. Change solute amount first, then reset or compare by changing water volume.
  4. Record how each change affects concentration and write the evidence-based concentration conclusion.
