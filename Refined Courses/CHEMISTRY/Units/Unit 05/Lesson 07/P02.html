How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify solute and solvent.
  2. Use amount of solute divided by amount of solution for concentration.
  3. Compare solutions only when units match.
  4. Use water polarity to explain dissolving of ionic or polar substances.
  5. Connect particle model to concentration data.
