How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify whether the question is about energy, particles, graphs, gases, phases, or solutions.
  2. Read the embedded model or data table first.
  3. Use the correct relationship: temperature-kinetic energy, graph-energy change, gas collisions, or concentration ratio.
  4. Show calculations when numbers are given.
  5. Write a claim with evidence and reasoning.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column, use the evidence and reasoning columns, and predict the conclusion before checking.
