Three Worked Examples

Example 1 - Oxygen

Atomic number 8 means 8 electrons. Arrangement = 2, 6. Two occupied levels place oxygen in Period 2; six outer electrons give six valence electrons.

Example 2 - Aluminum

Atomic number 13 means 13 electrons. Arrangement = 2, 8, 3. Three occupied levels place aluminum in Period 3; three outer electrons give three valence electrons.

Example 3 - Compare lithium and sodium

Lithium = 2, 1 and sodium = 2, 8, 1. Both have one valence electron, so they share a group pattern. Sodium has one more occupied level, so it is one period below lithium.

These examples model every reasoning step. Nothing is submitted from this page.
