P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1

Problem: Find the simple electron arrangement for carbon.

  1. Step 1: Carbon atomic number is 6.
  2. A neutral carbon atom has 6 electrons.
  3. Place 2 in the first level.
  4. Place the remaining 4 in the second level.
  5. Arrangement: 2, 4.
  6. Interpretation: Explain what the chemistry evidence shows and why it supports the conclusion.

Worked Example 2

Problem: Why are lithium and sodium in similar groups?

  1. Lithium arrangement is 2, 1.
  2. Sodium arrangement is 2, 8, 1.
  3. Both have 1 valence electron.
  4. Similar valence patterns support similar behavior.

Worked Example 3

Problem: What period clue comes from sodium's arrangement 2, 8, 1?

  1. Count occupied levels: first, second, third.
  2. There are 3 occupied levels.
  3. Sodium is in Period 3.
Common Mistake: Counting all electrons as valence electrons instead of only the electrons in the outer occupied level.
Incorrect: Sodium has 11 electrons, so it has 11 valence electrons.
Correct: Sodium has the arrangement 2, 8, 1, so it has 1 valence electron in the outer level.

Teachable Explanation

Each worked example shows the same learning pattern: identify the evidence, choose the correct atomic model or rule, apply it step by step, and interpret the result in context.
