Vocabulary

Write these terms and definitions in your notebook.

  1. electron arrangement: the distribution of electrons among occupied energy levels.
  2. energy level: a region around the nucleus where electrons with similar energy are found.
  3. valence electron: an electron in the outermost occupied energy level.
  4. period: a horizontal row of the periodic table; in this model it matches the number of occupied levels.
  5. group: a vertical column whose main-group elements share similar valence-electron patterns.
  6. neutral atom: an atom with equal numbers of protons and electrons.
  7. ion: an atom with unequal proton and electron counts and therefore a charge.
