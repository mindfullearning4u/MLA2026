P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 2 Lesson 04 - Electron Arrangement and Periodic Position

Vocabulary

  • Energy level: a region around the nucleus where electrons may be found.
  • Valence electron: an electron in the outer occupied energy level.
  • Group: a vertical column on the periodic table.
  • Period: a horizontal row on the periodic table.
  • Electron arrangement: how electrons are distributed in energy levels.

Start With the Big Idea

Relate electrons to periodic table position and properties. This lesson is hard if you try to memorize words without connecting them to evidence. Read each model or table slowly and ask what the evidence proves.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Use atomic number to find electrons in a neutral atom.
  2. Step 2: Place electrons into energy levels using the simplified model.
  3. Step 3: Count occupied energy levels to connect to period.
  4. Step 4: Count outer electrons to connect to group patterns.
  5. Step 5: Use valence electrons to predict similar chemical behavior.

How to Read the Visual

  1. Read the column headings before using any numbers or labels.
  2. Identify the evidence the table or model provides.
  3. Connect each row to the lesson vocabulary.
  4. Write one conclusion that the evidence supports and one conclusion it does not support.

Notebook Evidence

Copy the visual or data display in your notebook. Under it, write three evidence statements using the sentence frame: The evidence shows ___ because ___.
