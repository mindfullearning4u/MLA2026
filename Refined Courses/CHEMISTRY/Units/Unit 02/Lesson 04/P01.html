CHEMISTRY Unit 2 Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Electron Arrangement and Periodic Position
Lesson Purpose: Relate electrons to periodic table position and properties.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.CHEM.ATO.03
Support Standards: SC.912.P.8.5; MA.K12.MTR.5.1

What You Will Learn

  • You will explain how electron arrangement helps explain periodic table location and chemical behavior.
  • You will use the required model, data table, diagram, or spectrum stimulus from the Unit 2 mapping.
  • You will write Chemistry reasoning that connects evidence to atomic structure or atomic behavior.

What You Will Do

  1. Learn the vocabulary before using it in explanations.
  2. Read the model or data display step by step.
  3. Study three worked examples.
  4. Complete independent notebook work and a checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining the mapped Unit 2 atomic concept with accurate vocabulary, evidence from a model or data display, and step-by-step reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand atomic evidence and atomic patterns so later bonding and reaction lessons make sense.
