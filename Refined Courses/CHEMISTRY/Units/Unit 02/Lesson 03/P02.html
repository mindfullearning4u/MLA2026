Notebook Title: Unit 2 Lesson 03 - Isotopes, Atomic Mass, and Atomic Identity

Vocabulary

  • Isotope: atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons.
  • Mass number: protons plus neutrons.
  • Average atomic mass: weighted average mass of naturally occurring isotopes.
  • Percent abundance: how common an isotope is in a sample.
  • Weighted average: an average that accounts for how much each value contributes.

Start With the Big Idea

Apply atomic structure to isotope and mass reasoning. This lesson is hard if you try to memorize words without connecting them to evidence. Read each model or table slowly and ask what the evidence proves.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Check protons to confirm the element identity.
  2. Step 2: Compare neutrons to identify isotopes.
  3. Step 3: Use mass number = protons + neutrons.
  4. Step 4: Convert percent abundance to decimal form.
  5. Step 5: Multiply each isotope mass by its abundance and add the results.
