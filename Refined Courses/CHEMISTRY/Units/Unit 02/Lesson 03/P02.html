Vocabulary

Write all eight terms and definitions in your notebook.

  1. isotope: atoms of the same element with the same protons but different neutrons.
  2. atomic identity: the element determined by its number of protons.
  3. mass number: protons plus neutrons in one atom.
  4. atomic mass: the weighted average mass of naturally occurring isotopes.
  5. percent abundance: the percentage of a sample made of one isotope.
  6. decimal fraction: a percent divided by 100.
  7. weighted average: an average that accounts for each value’s abundance.
  8. amu: atomic mass unit, used for atomic-scale masses.
