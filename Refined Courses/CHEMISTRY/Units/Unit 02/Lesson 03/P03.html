Weighted average atomic mass

Step 1: Convert each percent to a decimal.

Step 2: Multiply isotope mass × decimal abundance.

Step 3: Add all contributions and report amu.

The average is closer to the mass of the more abundant isotope.

Common error: Do not add isotope masses and divide by two unless abundances are equal.
