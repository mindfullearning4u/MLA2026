Three Worked Examples

Example 1 - Find neutrons

chlorine-37 has atomic number 17. Neutrons = 37 - 17 = 20. It remains chlorine because it has 17 protons.

Example 2 - Weighted average

75% X-10 and 25% X-11:

(10 × 0.75) + (11 × 0.25) = 7.50 + 2.75 = 10.25 amu.

Example 3 - Interpret the result

10.25 amu is closer to 10 amu because X-10 makes up 75% of the sample. The average must fall between 10 and 11.
