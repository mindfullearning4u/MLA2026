Lesson Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: identify protons, neutrons, and electrons by charge, relative mass, and location; use particle counts to determine element identity, mass number, and ionic charge.

MLA standard: MLA.CHEM.ATO.02

Objectives

  • Describe each subatomic particle by charge, relative mass, and location.
  • Use atomic number and mass number to calculate protons and neutrons.
  • Determine electrons in neutral atoms and ions.
  • Explain which particle changes identity, mass, or charge.
