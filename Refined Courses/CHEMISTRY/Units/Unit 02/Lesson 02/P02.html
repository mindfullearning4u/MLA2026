Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your chemistry notebook. The definitions are provided because these exact meanings are required in this lesson.

  1. proton: a positively charged particle in the nucleus with a relative mass of about 1 amu.
  2. neutron: a neutral particle in the nucleus with a relative mass of about 1 amu.
  3. electron: a negatively charged particle in the electron cloud with very little mass.
  4. nucleus: the small, dense, positively charged center of an atom.
  5. atomic number: the number of protons; it identifies the element.
  6. mass number: the total number of protons plus neutrons.
  7. neutral atom: an atom with equal numbers of protons and electrons.
  8. ion: an atom with a net charge because it gained or lost electrons.
