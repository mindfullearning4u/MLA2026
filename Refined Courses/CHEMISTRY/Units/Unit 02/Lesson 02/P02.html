Notebook Title: Unit 2 Lesson 02 - Protons, Neutrons, Electrons, and Atomic Structure

Vocabulary

  • Proton: positively charged particle in the nucleus.
  • Neutron: neutral particle in the nucleus.
  • Electron: negatively charged particle outside the nucleus.
  • Nucleus: tiny dense center of the atom containing protons and neutrons.
  • Atomic number: number of protons in an atom.

Start With the Big Idea

Describe atomic particles by mass, charge, and location. This lesson is hard if you try to memorize words without connecting them to evidence. Read each model or table slowly and ask what the evidence proves.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Use atomic number to identify protons.
  2. Step 2: In a neutral atom, electrons equal protons.
  3. Step 3: Use mass number minus atomic number to find neutrons.
  4. Step 4: Remember that protons and neutrons make almost all atomic mass.
  5. Step 5: Use particle counts to explain identity, isotope, and charge.
