Example - How to Draw a Labeled Particle Model

Example atom: carbon-12 has 6 protons, 6 neutrons, and 6 electrons.

Electron cloud
e-     e-     e-
Nucleus
6 p+ (protons)
6 n0 (neutrons)
e-     e-     e-

Use this drawing process

  1. Draw a small center circle and label it nucleus.
  2. Write the correct proton and neutron counts inside the nucleus.
  3. Draw a larger surrounding circle or cloud and label it electron cloud.
  4. Place and label the correct number of electrons in the surrounding region.
  5. Write the element or ion name and charge beside the completed model.
