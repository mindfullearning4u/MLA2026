Check and Correct Your Independent Work

On the previous page, you completed the Rutherford Scattering Independent Application. Compare your work with the possible answers below. Then follow the correction rule.

Possible answers

  1. Mostly straight paths: Most of the atom is empty space.
  2. Rare large deflections: Positive charge and most mass are concentrated in a very small, dense nucleus.
  3. Model limitation: The simulation is not to scale and does not show exact electron positions, all dimensions, or every interaction in a real atom.
Correction rule: If an answer is missing, incorrect, or unsupported, draw one line through it, write the corrected answer beside it, and add one sentence explaining why the correction is scientifically stronger. Keep the original work visible.
