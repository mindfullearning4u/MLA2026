Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your chemistry notebook. These definitions are provided so you can focus on using the words accurately.

  1. atomic theory: the evidence-based explanation that matter is composed of atoms.
  2. scientific model: a representation used to explain observations and make predictions.
  3. experimental evidence: observations or measurements produced by an investigation.
  4. inference: a logical explanation drawn from evidence.
  5. electron: a negatively charged subatomic particle.
  6. nucleus: the small, dense, positively charged center of an atom.
  7. alpha particle: a positively charged particle used in Rutherford’s scattering experiment.
  8. line spectrum: a pattern of specific wavelengths emitted or absorbed by an atom.
  9. quantized: restricted to specific allowed values rather than any possible value.
  10. model limitation: something a scientific model cannot represent or explain accurately.
