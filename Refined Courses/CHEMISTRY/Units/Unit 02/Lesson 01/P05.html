Rutherford Scattering Investigation

Click here to open PhET Rutherford Scattering
Complete these steps exactly:
  1. Open the simulation and select the Rutherford atom model.
  2. Turn on or start the alpha-particle beam.
  3. Observe at least 30 particle paths.
  4. In your notebook, make a three-row table labeled: mostly straight, slightly deflected, and strongly deflected/backscattered.
  5. Tally each observed path in the correct row.
  6. Write three numbered conclusions: what the straight paths show; what the rare large deflections show; and one limitation of the model.
