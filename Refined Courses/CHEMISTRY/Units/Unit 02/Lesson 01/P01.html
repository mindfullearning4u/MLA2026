Atomic Theory and Experimental Evidence

Lesson focus: explain how experimental evidence caused scientists to revise atomic models.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.ATO.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.03.

Objectives

  • Connect cathode-ray, gold-foil, and line-spectrum evidence to changes in atomic models.
  • Distinguish evidence from an inference or model.
  • Explain why a useful scientific model may still have limitations.
