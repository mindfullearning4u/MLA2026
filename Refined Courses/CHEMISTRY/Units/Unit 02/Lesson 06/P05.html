Follow these steps exactly

  1. Save the PDF with your name and "U2 L6."
  2. Open PhET. On the opening screen, click Spectra (the picture on the left).
  3. On the Spectra screen, keep the top switch set to Model. The current PhET version uses the label “Model,” not “Prediction.”
  4. Press the red light-source button on the left to send photons toward the atom. The spectrometer is already displayed across the bottom.
  5. Select and compare two model choices from the list on the right. Record the electron behavior and the spectrum evidence shown for each model.
  6. Complete the absorption/emission table.
  7. Answer all analysis and correction questions.
  8. Upload the finished PDF to U2 L6 Notebook Evidence Submission.

The completed PDF is notebook evidence, not the checkpoint.
