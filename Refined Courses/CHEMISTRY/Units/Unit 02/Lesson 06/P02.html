Notebook Title: Unit 2 Lesson 06 - Quantized Energy and Atomic Energy Levels

Vocabulary

  • Quantized: limited to specific allowed values.
  • Ground state: the lowest energy arrangement for an electron.
  • Excited state: a higher energy arrangement after energy is absorbed.
  • Photon: a packet of light energy emitted or absorbed.
  • Emission: release of light when an electron drops to a lower energy level.

Start With the Big Idea

Explain quantized energy at the atomic level. This lesson is hard if you try to memorize words without connecting them to evidence. Read each model or table slowly and ask what the evidence proves.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Recognize that allowed electron energies are specific levels.
  2. Step 2: Absorbing energy can move an electron upward.
  3. Step 3: Emitting energy occurs when an electron drops downward.
  4. Step 4: The light energy equals the difference between levels.
  5. Step 5: Line spectra show only specific transitions, so energy is quantized.
