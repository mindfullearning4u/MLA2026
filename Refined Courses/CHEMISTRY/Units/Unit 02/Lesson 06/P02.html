Required vocabulary

  1. quantized energy: energy that can exist only in specific allowed amounts.
  2. energy level: an allowed electron-energy state in an atom.
  3. ground state: the lowest-energy arrangement of an atom's electrons.
  4. excited state: a higher allowed energy state reached after absorbing energy.
  5. absorption: transfer of energy into an atom that moves an electron to a higher allowed level.
  6. emission: release of energy when an electron moves to a lower allowed level.
  7. photon: a packet of electromagnetic energy.
  8. electron transition: movement between two allowed energy levels.
  9. line spectrum: a pattern of separate wavelengths produced by specific electron transitions.
