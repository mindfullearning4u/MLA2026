Vocabulary

Write each term and definition in your notebook. The definitions are provided so you can focus on using the words correctly.

  1. electromagnetic radiation: energy that travels through space as oscillating electric and magnetic fields.
  2. wavelength (λ): distance between corresponding points on consecutive waves.
  3. frequency (ν): number of wave cycles passing a point each second, measured in hertz.
  4. photon: a discrete packet of electromagnetic energy.
  5. amplitude: maximum displacement of a wave from its rest position; related to intensity.
  6. visible spectrum: electromagnetic wavelengths detectable by the human eye.
  7. emission spectrum: specific wavelengths emitted when excited electrons drop to lower energy levels.
  8. absorption spectrum: missing wavelengths produced when matter absorbs particular photon energies.
  9. spectroscopy: analysis of how matter emits, absorbs, or interacts with electromagnetic radiation.
