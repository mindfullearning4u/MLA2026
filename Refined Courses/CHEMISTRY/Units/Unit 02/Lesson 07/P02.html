Notebook Title: Unit 2 Lesson 07 - Electromagnetic Spectrum and Atomic Applications

Vocabulary

  • Wavelength: distance between matching points on a wave.
  • Frequency: number of wave cycles per second.
  • Electromagnetic spectrum: full range of electromagnetic radiation.
  • Spectrum: pattern of light separated by wavelength or frequency.
  • Energy: for light, energy increases as frequency increases.

Start With the Big Idea

Relate wavelength, frequency, energy, and spectrum applications. This lesson is hard if you try to memorize words without connecting them to evidence. Read each model or table slowly and ask what the evidence proves.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Identify wavelength and frequency relationship: as wavelength decreases, frequency increases.
  2. Step 2: Connect frequency to energy: higher frequency means higher energy.
  3. Step 3: Use spectrum lines as evidence for atoms.
  4. Step 4: Compare regions carefully; do not assume visible light is the entire spectrum.
  5. Step 5: Apply spectrum evidence to atomic identification and technology.
