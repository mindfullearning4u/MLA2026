Continue the Teaching

Periodic trends are patterns, not isolated facts. Atomic radius generally decreases from left to right because the nucleus has more protons pulling on electrons in the same main energy level. Atomic radius generally increases down a group because atoms have more occupied energy levels.

Ionization energy moves in the opposite general direction from radius. If an outer electron is held more tightly, it takes more energy to remove it.

Reasoning Steps for This Lesson

  1. Identify the specific atomic evidence, particle count, periodic pattern, energy change, or spectrum information.
  2. Name the Chemistry concept that matches that evidence.
  3. Apply the correct rule or model one step at a time.
  4. Check that the conclusion does not claim more than the evidence supports.
  5. Write the final explanation using lesson vocabulary.
Common Mistake: Memorizing arrows without explaining the atomic-structure reason behind the trend.
Incorrect: Fluorine is smaller than lithium because the chart says so, and no other reason is needed.
Correct: Fluorine is smaller than lithium across the same period because increased nuclear charge pulls electrons closer in the same main energy level.

Teachable Explanation

The correct example is stronger because it uses evidence and the specific Unit 2 concept. Atomic structure and periodic patterns are not guessed; they are reasoned from models, data, and particle relationships.
