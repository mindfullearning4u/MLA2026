Vocabulary

Vocabulary

  1. periodic trend: a predictable pattern in properties across periods or down groups.
  2. atomic radius: a measure of atomic size.
  3. ionization energy: energy required to remove an electron from a gaseous atom.
  4. electronegativity: an atom’s attraction for shared electrons in a bond.
  5. nuclear charge: the positive charge produced by protons in the nucleus.
  6. shielding: reduction of nuclear attraction caused by inner electrons.
  7. metallic character: the tendency to show metal-like behavior, including losing electrons.
  8. valence electron: an electron in the outermost occupied energy level.
