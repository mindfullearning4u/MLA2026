How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Decide whether the factor is temperature or catalyst.
  2. For temperature, connect faster particles to more energetic collisions.
  3. For catalysts, connect lower activation energy to more effective collisions.
  4. Do not say the catalyst is used up.
  5. Use graph or table evidence to support the rate claim.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
