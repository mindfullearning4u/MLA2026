How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the chemistry technology or application.
  2. List claimed benefits.
  3. List possible risks or costs.
  4. Check whether evidence supports each claim.
  5. Make a decision that explains tradeoffs.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
