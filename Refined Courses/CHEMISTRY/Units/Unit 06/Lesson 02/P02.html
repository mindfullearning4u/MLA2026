How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Locate the pH value.
  2. Compare the pH to 7.
  3. Lower than 7 is acidic; higher than 7 is basic; 7 is neutral.
  4. Use one pH unit as a tenfold change.
  5. State both classification and relative strength.
