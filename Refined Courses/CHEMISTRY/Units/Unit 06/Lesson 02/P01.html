Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
pHa logarithmic scale describing acidity or basicity
logarithmic scalea scale where each whole-number step represents a tenfold change
stronger acida solution with lower pH and greater hydronium concentration
stronger basea solution with higher pH and greater basicity
Science Safety and Resource Note: pH scale and concentration data reasoning using tables and graphs. No chemical testing is required. Household acid/base mixing, chemical testing, heating, product testing, or environmental sampling is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.
