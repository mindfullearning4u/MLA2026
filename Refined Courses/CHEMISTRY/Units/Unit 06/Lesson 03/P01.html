Unit 6 Lesson 03: Reaction Rates and Concentration

Standard(s): MLA.CHEM.GAS.04

Florida alignment: SC.912.P.12.12

Learning target: I can explain how concentration affects reaction rate.

Start Here

This final unit lesson is written for independent learning. Read the vocabulary, model, examples, and practice in order before taking an assessment.

Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
reaction ratehow fast reactants are converted to products
concentrationamount of particles in a given volume
collision modela model explaining reactions through particle collisions
effective collisiona collision with enough energy and correct orientation to react
Safety boundary: Rate vs concentration data investigation using rate graphs, concentration tables, and collision models. No chemical reaction performance is required. Household acid/base mixing, chemical testing, heating, product testing, or environmental sampling is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.
