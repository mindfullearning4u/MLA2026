How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the concentration level.
  2. Connect concentration to number of particles per volume.
  3. Connect particle number to collision frequency.
  4. Connect collision frequency to reaction rate.
  5. Use the data trend to justify the rate claim.
