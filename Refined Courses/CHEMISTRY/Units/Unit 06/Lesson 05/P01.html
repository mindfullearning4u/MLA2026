Vocabulary Before Use

TermMeaning
reversible reactiona reaction that can proceed forward and backward
dynamic equilibriumcondition where forward and reverse rates are equal
forward reactionreaction direction from reactants to products
reverse reactionreaction direction from products to reactants
Science Safety and Resource Note: Reversible process/equilibrium model investigation using dynamic equilibrium models and forward/reverse rate graphs. No chemical system manipulation is required. Household acid/base mixing, chemical testing, heating, product testing, or environmental sampling is not part of this independent lesson unless assigned by the Teacher of Record with safety controls.
