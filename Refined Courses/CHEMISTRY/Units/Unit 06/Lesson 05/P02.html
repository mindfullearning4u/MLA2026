How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the forward and reverse processes.
  2. Compare forward and reverse rates.
  3. If rates are equal, classify as dynamic equilibrium.
  4. Remember particles still react both ways.
  5. Use rate or concentration evidence to support the claim.
