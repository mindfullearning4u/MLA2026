How to Reason Through This Lesson

  1. Identify the claim.
  2. Identify the source type.
  3. Look for measurable chemistry evidence.
  4. Check whether the evidence actually supports the claim.
  5. Communicate a CER response using precise chemistry language.
Checkpoint: Cover the conclusion column. Use the evidence and reasoning columns to predict the conclusion, then compare.
