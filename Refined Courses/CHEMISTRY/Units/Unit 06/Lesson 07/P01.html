Required Direct Simulation Link

Open this exact simulation/resource: ACS Reactions: They Changed Coke and Didn't Tell Anyone

Direct URL: https://www.acs.org/pressroom/reactions/library/mexican-coke-problem-solved.html

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. After the ACS article opens, stay on this exact page and use the article section under the title.
  2. Read the paragraph that begins with the claim about Mexican Coke and the challenge from chemistry professors.
  3. Observe how the article connects a chemistry claim to lab testing, instruments, sources, and evidence.
  4. Record the chemistry claim, the evidence source named in the article, one source-quality feature, and whether the claim is supported.
  5. Write a CER conclusion that explains how the article's evidence supports or challenges the real-world chemistry claim.
