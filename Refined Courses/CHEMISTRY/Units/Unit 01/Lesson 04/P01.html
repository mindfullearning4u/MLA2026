CHEMISTRY Unit 1 Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Data Tables, Graphs, Precision, and Uncertainty
Lesson Purpose: Analyze data displays, uncertainty, patterns, and evidence.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.CHEM.LAB.03
Support Standards: MA.K12.MTR.2.1; MA.K12.MTR.6.1

What You Will Learn

  • organize chemistry data in a table
  • interpret a graph using labels, units, and trend
  • explain precision, accuracy, uncertainty, and outliers

What You Will Do

  1. Read the vocabulary and teaching sequence carefully.
  2. Use the included tables, models, diagrams, scenarios, or data displays to make sense of the chemistry idea.
  3. Complete notebook evidence that shows your reasoning, not just your answer.
  4. Use the checkpoint to show independent mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining the lesson concept with accurate chemistry vocabulary, using evidence from the required stimulus, and writing reasoning that connects the evidence to the standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you work like a chemistry student: you will read evidence, use models or data, and explain how the evidence supports a scientific answer.

Safety boundary: Lab/Data/Safety Connection: Data table and graph construction with provided data. No physical materials required.
Safety: Use teacher-provided or public data. Do not create chemical data by mixing substances without assignment.
