Independent Application — Percent Error

MLA standard: MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Problem: The accepted mass of a calibration sample is 25.00 g. A student measures the sample as 24.60 g. Calculate the percent error.
  1. Write the percent-error formula.
  2. Substitute the experimental and accepted values.
  3. Show the subtraction, absolute value, division, and multiplication by 100.
  4. Round appropriately and include the percent sign.
  5. Write one sentence stating whether the measurement is close to the accepted value.
