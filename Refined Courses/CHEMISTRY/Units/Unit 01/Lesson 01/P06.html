Complete the reading and analysis:
  1. Confirm the title says Nature of Science: Science and Society.
  2. Read the complete Savvas resource in order.
  3. In your notebook, make a table with these headings: Claim; Source or Author; Evidence Provided; Method Explained?; Possible Bias or Purpose; Reliability Decision.
  4. Select three different claims or examples from the resource and complete one row for each.
  5. For every row, quote or copy one exact evidence detail. Do not write only “the source had data.”
  6. Decide whether the evidence is relevant and sufficient. Explain your decision in one complete sentence.
  7. Finish with a four-sentence reflection explaining why chemistry decisions in society should be based on evidence from more than one reliable source.
