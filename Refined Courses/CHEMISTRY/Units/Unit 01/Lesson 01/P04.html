Complete the worksheet step by step:
  1. Write your name, date, and “U1 L1” at the top.
  2. Read each numbered item completely before responding.
  3. Study every diagram, scenario, table, and measurement included with the item.
  4. Answer every part of every numbered item. Use complete sentences unless the item requests a number, label, table entry, or calculation.
  5. When asked for evidence, copy the exact observation, value, unit, or source detail that supports your answer.
  6. When asked to explain, state the claim, cite the evidence, and explain the scientific connection.
  7. Review the worksheet and make sure no response area is blank.
