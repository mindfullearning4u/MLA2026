# Unit 1 Lesson 1 Foundation Evidence

Moodle activity: [Unit 1 Lesson 1 Foundation Evidence](https://mla.moodlecloud.com/mod/assign/view.php?id=17363)

## MLA standards assessed

- MLA.CHEM.SCI.01
- MLA.CHEM.LAB.01
- MLA.CHEM.LAB.02

## Required evidence

The student submits one PDF or clear ordered files/images containing:

1. The vocabulary table.
2. Inquiry and measurement notes.
3. Five laboratory safety rules.
4. The completed Savvas Data: Analysis and Calculations activity.

The student names the file `CHEM_U01_L01_Foundation_LastName`, opens the uploaded evidence to confirm readability, and submits it for Teacher of Record review. If feedback identifies missing evidence, the student corrects the named work and resubmits the complete file.
