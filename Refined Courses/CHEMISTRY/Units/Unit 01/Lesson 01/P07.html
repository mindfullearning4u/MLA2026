Investigation Design and Measurement

MLA standards assessed: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Scenario: A student investigates whether water temperature affects the time required for an effervescent tablet to dissolve. The student uses 200 mL of water, the same tablet brand and size, and the same container for every trial. Only the water temperature changes. Dissolving time is measured in seconds.

Complete these three tasks

  1. Investigation: Identify the investigation as descriptive, comparative, or experimental. Give one reason.
  2. Variables: Identify the independent variable, dependent variable, and two control variables.
  3. Percent error: A 25.00 g calibration sample is measured as 24.25 g. Calculate the percent error. Show the formula, substitution, work, unit values, and final percent.
Checkpoint submission: Put your name and “U1 L1 Checkpoint” on the page. Upload one clear photo, scan, or PDF containing the three numbered responses to U1 L1 Checkpoint Submission.

Mastery: 80% or higher. Your work must correctly classify the investigation, identify the variables, and show a complete percent-error calculation.
