CHECKPOINT — TEACHER OF RECORD GRADED

Which Evidence Supports the Claim?

MLA standards assessed: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Scenario:

A product advertisement claims, “Cleaner A removes mineral deposits better than Cleaner B.” The advertisement reports one demonstration in which Cleaner A removed 8.0 g of deposit and Cleaner B removed 5.0 g. An independent laboratory tested both products three times under the same conditions. Cleaner A removed 7.8 g, 8.1 g, and 7.9 g. Cleaner B removed 6.9 g, 7.1 g, and 7.0 g. The advertisement does not identify the starting deposit mass or product concentration. The independent laboratory identifies its procedure, quantities, instruments, and uncertainty.

Checkpoint Task

  1. Create a two-row table comparing the advertisement and the independent laboratory.
  2. For each source, record the claim, number of trials, exact data, missing information, possible purpose or bias, and reliability decision.
  3. Calculate the mean deposit removed by each cleaner in the independent test. Show the arithmetic and include grams.
  4. Write a CER response answering: Does the available evidence support the claim that Cleaner A removes mineral deposits better than Cleaner B?
  5. Your claim must answer the question directly. Your evidence must cite at least two exact numerical details. Your reasoning must discuss repeated trials, controlled conditions, missing information, and source reliability.
  6. End with one limitation: state what additional evidence would be needed before making a strong consumer decision.
