P07 Foundation Checkpoint and Submission

MLA Standards Assessed: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01; MLA.CHEM.LAB.01; MLA.CHEM.LAB.02

Submit in Moodle

  1. Click Unit 1 Lesson 1 Foundation Evidence.
  2. Upload your completed Savvas data activity as a file. If you completed it on paper or in a notebook, upload clear photographs or one PDF containing every completed page.
  3. Include your vocabulary, inquiry notes, and safety rules in the same upload or as additional pages.
  4. Before selecting Submit, open your uploaded file or images and verify that every page is readable.

Final Self-Check

  • I can use the direct Savvas links without searching for the activity.
  • I know where to record Savvas responses and how to submit worksheet evidence.
  • I know to stop and ask my Tor when safety instructions are unclear.

Revision: If feedback identifies missing evidence, correct the named page or response and resubmit the complete file.

Teacher of Record Support: Send your Tor the page number and exact issue if you need help with a link, safety expectation, notebook entry, or upload.
