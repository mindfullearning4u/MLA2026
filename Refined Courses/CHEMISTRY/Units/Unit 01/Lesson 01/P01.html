Scientific Inquiry and Measurement

Lesson focus: recognize investigation types and variables, use accurate measurement language, and calculate percent error.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Lesson sequence

  1. Find and record the verified Savvas vocabulary.
  2. Read all 12 pages of Scientific Inquiry and Measurement and copy only the page 12 percent-error sample problem.
  3. Complete the Savvas PDF worksheet.
  4. Review the key ideas—no written submission required.
  5. Solve one independent percent-error problem.
  6. Complete the short checkpoint.

Submit in U1 L1 Notebook Evidence Submission

  • All 19 vocabulary terms and Savvas definitions.
  • The page 12 Savvas percent-error sample problem.
  • The completed Scientific Inquiry and Measurement PDF worksheet or numbered notebook responses.
  • The independent percent-error problem.

Submit in U1 L1 Checkpoint Submission

Upload the single checkpoint page containing the investigation classification, variables, and percent-error calculation. A clear photo, scan, or PDF is acceptable.
