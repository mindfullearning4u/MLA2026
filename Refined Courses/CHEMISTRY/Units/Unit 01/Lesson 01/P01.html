What Counts as Chemistry Evidence? — Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: distinguish observations, inferences, claims, evidence, and reasoning; evaluate whether a source or conclusion is supported by checkable chemistry evidence.

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

What You Will Learn

  • How scientific inquiry turns observations and measurements into defensible conclusions.
  • How to separate a claim from the evidence used to support it.
  • How to judge whether evidence is relevant, sufficient, repeatable, and obtained from a reliable source.
  • How to write a claim-evidence-reasoning (CER) explanation.
