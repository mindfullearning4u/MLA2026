Key Ideas Check

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Read this short review before completing the independent problem. You do not need to copy this page.

  • Descriptive investigations describe an entity or process.
  • Comparative investigations look for similarities, differences, patterns, or relationships.
  • Experimental investigations test cause and effect by changing an independent variable, measuring a dependent variable, and holding control variables constant.
  • Accuracy describes closeness to an accepted value; precision describes closeness among repeated measurements.
  • Percent error compares an experimental value with an accepted value: |experimental − accepted| ÷ accepted × 100%.
  • Measurements must include a number, an appropriate unit, and the correct significant figures.
