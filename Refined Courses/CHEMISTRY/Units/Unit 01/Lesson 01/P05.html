Use the reading to answer all five questions below in your notebook. Number your responses 1–5 and use complete sentences.

  1. Why is “The liquid looked clear” an observation but not sufficient evidence that the liquid is water?
  2. Explain the difference between a measurement and an inference. Include one chemistry example of each.
  3. Why do repeated trials usually provide stronger evidence than a single trial?
  4. List four features that make a scientific source more reliable.
  5. Write a three-sentence CER response to this claim: “The unknown sample is pure water.” Use the evidence that its measured density was 1.00 g/mL and its boiling point was 100°C at standard pressure.
Common mistake: repeating a measurement without explaining how it supports the claim.
Correct approach: name the claim, cite the exact measured evidence with units, and explain why the measurement is consistent with the known property.
