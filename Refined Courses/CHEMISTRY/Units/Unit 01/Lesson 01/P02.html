P02 Vocabulary and Notebook Setup

MLA Standards: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01; MLA.CHEM.LAB.01; MLA.CHEM.LAB.02

Write This in Your Notebook

Draw a two-column table labeled Term and Meaning. Copy each term and definition below. Leave six blank lines after the table for the notes required on P03–P06.

TermStudent-friendly definition
observationA detail noticed or measured with tools or senses.
variableA condition that can change in an investigation.
measurementA number with a unit that describes an amount.
precisionHow consistently a measurement is recorded at the appropriate level of detail.
personal protective equipment (PPE)Safety clothing or equipment used to reduce laboratory risk.
safety data sheet (SDS)A document that explains a chemical's hazards, safe handling, and response steps.
