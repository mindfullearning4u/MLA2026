NOTEBOOK TASK 1

Vocabulary

MLA standards: MLA.CHEM.SCI.01 and MLA.CHEM.SCI.02

Write each numbered term and the complete definition below in your chemistry notebook. After writing, add one original example for terms 1, 2, 5, and 8.

  1. scientific inquiry: a planned investigation that uses scientific practices to answer a question about the natural world.
  2. observation: information obtained directly with the senses or a measuring instrument.
  3. measurement: a quantitative observation reported with a number and an appropriate unit.
  4. inference: a logical interpretation based on observations and prior knowledge.
  5. claim: a conclusion or answer that can be supported or challenged with evidence.
  6. evidence: relevant observations, measurements, data, or reliable source information used to evaluate a claim.
  7. reasoning: the scientific explanation that connects the evidence to the claim.
  8. reliable source: a source whose author or organization, methods, evidence, and purpose can be identified and checked.
  9. repeatability: the ability to obtain consistent results when a procedure is performed again under the same conditions.
  10. bias: an influence or preference that can unfairly affect how evidence is collected, interpreted, or communicated.
