P01 Lesson Overview

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Lab Safety, Tools, and Procedures
Lesson Purpose: Apply lab safety, apparatus expectations, and replicable procedures.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.CHEM.LAB.01; MLA.CHEM.SCI.01
Support Standards: ELA.K12.EE.5.1; ELD.K12.ELL.SC.1

What You Will Learn

  • connect common safety icons to safe lab behavior
  • identify basic chemistry tools and their purpose
  • explain why a procedure must be specific enough to repeat

What You Will Do

  1. Read the vocabulary and teaching sequence carefully.
  2. Use the included tables, models, diagrams, scenarios, or data displays to make sense of the chemistry idea.
  3. Complete notebook evidence that shows your reasoning, not just your answer.
  4. Use the checkpoint to show independent mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining the lesson concept with accurate chemistry vocabulary, using evidence from the required stimulus, and writing reasoning that connects the evidence to the standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you work like a chemistry student: you will read evidence, use models or data, and explain how the evidence supports a scientific answer.

Science Safety and Resource Note: Lab/Data/Safety Connection: Procedure critique and apparatus identification investigation using diagrams and scenarios only.
Safety: No unsupervised chemical handling. Any real lab tool use requires school/TOR assignment and proper safety controls.
