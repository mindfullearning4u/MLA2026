## P01 — Lesson Overview and Evidence Checklist

Focus: recognize laboratory hazards, select protective equipment, use common chemistry tools safely, and respond correctly to spills, exposure, broken glass, fire, and other emergencies.

Objectives:

- Identify hazards before beginning a laboratory activity.
- Select and correctly use PPE and safety equipment.
- Interpret chemical labels and Safety Data Sheet information.
- Apply safe procedures for heating, handling, cleanup, and disposal.
- Explain correct responses to common laboratory emergencies.

Notebook evidence includes vocabulary and five independent safety-decision responses. Checkpoint evidence includes four applied safety situations.

## P04 — Practice, Model, and Guided Explanation

No student work is required. The page demonstrates a four-step decision process:

1. Identify the hazard.
2. Determine who or what could be harmed.
3. Select the rule, equipment, or response that controls the hazard.
4. Explain why the action is safer than the alternatives.

Three complete models cover eye exposure, cracked glassware, and a corrosion pictogram.

## P05 — Independent Application

Students independently analyze five situations:

1. Heating a test tube pointed toward another person.
2. An unlabeled bottle containing a clear liquid.
3. Powder being brushed into the trash with a bare hand.
4. Removing goggles while chemicals remain in use.
5. Returning unused chemical to a stock bottle.

Each response uses three labels: **Hazard, Safest Action, and Why**.

## P06 — Lesson Review

Concise before/during/after laboratory summary. No new concepts or vocabulary are introduced.

## P07 — Checkpoint

Four applied situations assess MLA.CHEM.LAB.01:

1. Splattering liquid and missing goggles.
2. Chemical exposure to clothing and skin.
3. A chemical with flame and health-hazard pictograms.
4. Broken glass with chemical residue.

Each response must identify the hazard, state the safest action, and justify how the action reduces risk. Students submit the four responses in the separate U1 L1 Checkpoint Submission.
