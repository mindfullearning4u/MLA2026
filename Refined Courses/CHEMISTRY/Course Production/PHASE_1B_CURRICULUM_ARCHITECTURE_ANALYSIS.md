| Unit | Unit Title | Architecture Purpose |

| Unit 1 | Scientific Thinking, Measurement, and Matter | Establish scientific inquiry, lab safety, measurement, significant figures, scientific notation, states of matter, properties, and classification of matter. |

## Chemistry Course Boundaries

Chemistry is designed as a high-school laboratory science course centered on matter, atomic theory, bonding, reactions, energy, gases, solutions, acids and bases, equilibrium, quantitative relationships, and laboratory investigation.

- Biology content is included only where CPALMS Chemistry 1 includes water properties or chemistry applications relevant to living systems.
- Physics content is included only where CPALMS Chemistry 1 requires energy, electromagnetic spectrum, kinetic molecular theory, or gas behavior.
- Environmental Science content is included only when chemistry applications inform societal or environmental decision-making.
- Earth/Space Science content is limited to general scientific inquiry examples and matter/energy reasoning where required.
- Marine Science and Anatomy and Physiology content are not course drivers.

## MLA Chemistry Standards Framework

MLA Chemistry standards use this numbering format:

`MLA.CHEM.[DOMAIN].[NUMBER]`

Approved domains:

- `SCI` = Scientific Inquiry and Practices
- `LAB` = Laboratory and Data Skills
- `MAT` = Matter and Measurement
- `ATO` = Atomic Structure and Periodicity
- `BON` = Bonding and Compounds
- `REA` = Reactions and Stoichiometry
- `ENE` = Energy and Thermochemistry
- `GAS` = Gases, Solutions, and Equilibrium

## Architecture Controls

- Preserve the approved MLA mastery model.
- Preserve six units and eight lessons per unit.
- Preserve Lesson 8 as Putting It All Together in every unit.
- Preserve Unit Pretest, Lessons 1-7 quizzes, and Lesson 8 Unit Assessment structure.
- Keep scientific inquiry, lab safety, measurement, quantitative reasoning, data analysis, and evidence-based argumentation embedded across the course.
- Use CPALMS Chemistry 1 standards as the official Florida source.
- Cross-reference Common Core Literacy in Science and Technical Subjects, ACT Science, SAT science reading/data expectations, college readiness, workforce readiness, and scientific literacy.
- Do not create student-facing instructional lesson content during architecture.
- Do not create or populate HTML, JSON, GIFT, lesson, quiz, or LMS content during architecture.
