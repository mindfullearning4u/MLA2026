## Instructional design

- Vocabulary definitions are supplied directly.
- The Lesson page teaches the complete evidence-based investigation process without requiring student work.
- The guided page models variables, safety, data organization, graph choice, particle reasoning, and CER using a fully worked heating-ice example.
- The independent application uses the **Mystery Solid Investigation** data set. Students create a temperature-time graph and evidence-based conclusion in their notebook.
- The Lesson Review explicitly identifies the previous task, gives possible answers, and applies the correction rule.
- The Checkpoint uses a new copper-heating scenario to assess transfer.
- No unrelated Savvas resource was added; available results introduced content beyond the taught Unit 1 foundation. This lesson therefore uses MLA-created rigor and data while preserving the course workflow.
