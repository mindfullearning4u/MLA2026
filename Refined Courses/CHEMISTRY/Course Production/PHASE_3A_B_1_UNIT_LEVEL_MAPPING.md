## Required assessment and evidence sequence

Every unit begins with a zero-weight diagnostic Unit Pretest. Every lesson requires both a Notebook Evidence Submission and a Checkpoint Submission. Every lesson except the final lesson of the unit has a Lesson Quiz. The final lesson remains a substantive content lesson, keeps its notebook and checkpoint submissions, and is followed by the comprehensive Unit Assessment instead of a Lesson Quiz.
