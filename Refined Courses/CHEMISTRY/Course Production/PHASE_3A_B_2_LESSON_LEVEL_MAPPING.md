## Implementation notes

- Each lesson should use the applicable Investigative Phenomenon as its opening context and preserve direct Savvas resource placement.
- The Investigation Evaluation should be incorporated into the last applicable lessonâ€™s checkpoint/quiz or the unit assessment.
- Each unit begins with a zero-weight diagnostic pretest.
- Every lesson has a Notebook Evidence Submission and a separate Checkpoint Submission.
- Every lesson except the final lesson in a unit has a Lesson Quiz.
- The final lesson remains a substantive lesson with notebook and checkpoint evidence, but its quiz is replaced by the comprehensive Unit Assessment.
- No separate synthesis lesson is required unless the mapped content genuinely warrants one.
- Lesson counts are intentionally unequal. They follow prerequisite structure, standards depth, laboratory/data needs, and the adopted publisher program.
- This mapping must be reconciled with Moodle before the orientation pages state final unit or lesson counts.
