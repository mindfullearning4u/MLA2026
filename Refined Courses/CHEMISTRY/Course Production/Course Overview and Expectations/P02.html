How to Complete Chemistry Lessons

Moodle tells you exactly what to open, what to do, what to record, and where to submit evidence. Savvas provides the assigned eText, video, presentation, worksheet, lab, virtual lab, simulation, interactivity, or review activity.

Step 1 — Begin with the unit pretest

  1. Open the unit in Moodle.
  2. Select the Unit Pretest.
  3. Complete every question independently.
  4. The pretest is diagnostic. Use the results to identify topics that need attention.

Step 2 — Open the Moodle Lesson

  1. Select the lesson title shown on the Chemistry dashboard.
  2. Read the Lesson Overview before opening Savvas.
  3. Write the listed vocabulary terms and supplied definitions in your notebook.
  4. Follow every numbered direction in order. Do not skip a page.
  5. Use the blue Click here to open… button when a Savvas resource is assigned.

Click here to open the Savvas Chemistry program

After the link opens: remain signed in, use the exact resource title provided in Moodle, complete only the assigned screens or pages, and return to Moodle when finished.

Step 3 — Record the required evidence

  • If Moodle says record in your notebook, write the activity title and answer every assigned Savvas Notebook, Check Point, Key Ideas, Critical Thinking, analysis, or review prompt.
  • If Moodle assigns a worksheet, download or print it when Savvas provides that option. Complete it neatly. If downloading is unavailable, answer the numbered items on notebook paper.
  • For a lab, simulation, or virtual lab, record the question, variables, procedure evidence, observations, data, calculations, analysis, and conclusion requested by Moodle.
  • Do not assume work saved in Savvas has been submitted to MLA. Moodle is MLA’s official evidence record.

Step 4 — Submit and demonstrate mastery

  1. Upload the required learning evidence to Notebook Evidence Submission.
  2. Upload the final application task to Checkpoint Submission.
  3. Complete the Lesson Quiz.
  4. Continue only after Moodle releases the next activity.
  5. After the final lesson quiz, complete the Unit Assessment.
Never do this: Do not search Savvas for a different activity with a similar title, submit blank files, skip required data, or move ahead because a link appears open. Follow the exact Moodle lesson directions.
Need help? Contact your TOR if a Savvas link does not open or the assigned resource is not visible. Include the unit, lesson, page name, and resource title.
