P04 | READY TO BEGIN

Expectations, Support, and Readiness

Chemistry is an independent, mastery-based laboratory science course. Successful students follow directions in order, keep organized evidence, work safely, ask for help early, and revise when needed.

Materials and technology

  • A bound notebook or organized notebook paper reserved for Chemistry.
  • Pencil or pen, colored pencils, ruler, and calculator.
  • A computer or tablet with reliable internet access.
  • Access to Moodle and the assigned Savvas Chemistry program.
  • A camera or scanning method for clear evidence uploads.
  • Lab materials only when specifically listed in a Moodle lesson and approved for student use.

Laboratory safety is required

  • Read the complete activity and safety directions before beginning.
  • Use only the materials and procedures assigned in Moodle.
  • Wear required personal protective equipment.
  • Never taste chemicals, mix unassigned substances, or change a procedure.
  • Keep food and drinks away from laboratory work.
  • Stop immediately and contact the TOR or responsible adult if a material, procedure, spill, exposure, or disposal direction is unclear.
  • Virtual labs and simulations must still be completed carefully with accurate observations and data.

Independent-learning expectations

  1. Work through Moodle pages in order.
  2. Use the exact unit and lesson titles shown on the dashboard.
  3. Read all directions before clicking a resource.
  4. Record evidence while completing the activity—not afterward from memory.
  5. Use complete sentences, correct chemical vocabulary, units, labels, and calculations.
  6. Submit work by the assigned deadline and monitor feedback.
  7. Revise or resubmit when the TOR identifies missing evidence or incomplete mastery.

Academic integrity

Submit your own reasoning, calculations, models, observations, and explanations. You may use assigned course resources and approved tools, but you may not copy another student’s work, submit generated answers as your own, falsify laboratory data, or upload evidence you did not complete. When a source is required, identify it as directed.

When to contact the Teacher of Record

  • A Moodle or Savvas link does not open.
  • The exact assigned activity cannot be located.
  • You do not understand a direction after rereading the page and example.
  • You need clarification about notebook or checkpoint evidence.
  • You have a laboratory safety concern.
  • You received feedback requiring correction, remediation, or a retake.

Include your full name, course, unit and lesson code, page title, resource title, and a clear description or screenshot of the problem.

Readiness checklist

  • ☐ I can sign in to Moodle.
  • ☐ I can open the Savvas Chemistry program.
  • ☐ I have a Chemistry notebook and required basic materials.
  • ☐ I understand the Lesson → Notebook Evidence → Checkpoint → Lesson Quiz sequence.
  • ☐ I understand that the Unit Assessment follows the final lesson quiz.
  • ☐ I understand the 80% mastery expectation and revision process.
  • ☐ I know how to contact my TOR.
  • ☐ I agree to follow laboratory safety and academic-integrity expectations.
