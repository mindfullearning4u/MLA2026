P03 | EVIDENCE AND MASTERY

Evidence, Submissions, and Assessments

Your grade is based on evidence that you completed the learning process and can apply the lesson standards. Moodle is the official location for all MLA submissions and assessments.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Upload the work identified on the lesson’s overview and evidence checklist. Depending on the lesson, this may include:

  • Vocabulary terms and definitions.
  • Responses to assigned Savvas eText Notebook, Check Point, Key Ideas, Critical Thinking, or Lesson Review prompts.
  • Worked calculations, models, diagrams, data tables, graphs, annotations, or CER notes.
  • Completed worksheets or clearly numbered notebook responses.
  • Responses from assigned videos, presentations, simulations, virtual labs, or interactive activities.

Accepted evidence: a clear photo, scan, or combined PDF unless the activity gives a different direction. Your name and the unit/lesson code must be visible.

Checkpoint Submission

The checkpoint is the lesson’s independent application task. It may require a completed data table, calculation set, model, written explanation, lab analysis, or evidence-based claim.

  1. Read the entire checkpoint direction before starting.
  2. Complete every requested part.
  3. Show calculations and units when required.
  4. Use evidence from the assigned activity, model, or data.
  5. Upload one clear file unless Moodle gives another format.
  6. Check that the file opens before selecting Submit.

Lesson Quizzes

  • Each instructional lesson has a standards-aligned Lesson Quiz.
  • Complete the Moodle Lesson, evidence work, and checkpoint before attempting the quiz.
  • Read chemical symbols, units, tables, models, diagrams, and data carefully.
  • A minimum mastery level of 80% is required.
  • If mastery is not demonstrated, review the lesson, use feedback, contact the TOR when needed, and follow the correction or retake directions.

Unit Assessments

The Unit Assessment appears after the final instructional lesson and its Lesson Quiz. It measures the MLA standards taught across the entire unit.

  • Review notebook evidence, checkpoint feedback, lesson quizzes, formulas, vocabulary, and models.
  • Complete the assessment independently.
  • Use permitted tools only when the assessment directions allow them.
  • Contact the TOR for the approved remediation and retake process if mastery is not reached.

Before every upload

  • ☐ The correct unit and lesson code is shown.
  • ☐ Every requested item is complete.
  • ☐ Writing, numbers, graphs, and units are readable.
  • ☐ All pages are included and oriented correctly.
  • ☐ The uploaded file opens successfully.
  • ☐ The work is your own.
Need help? Contact your TOR before submitting if you are unsure which evidence belongs in Notebook Evidence or Checkpoint Submission. Include the unit and lesson code.
