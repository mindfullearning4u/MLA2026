Welcome to Chemistry

This is a 1.0-credit, mastery-based high school laboratory science course. You will study matter, measurement, atomic structure, periodic patterns, bonding, compounds, reactions, chemical quantities, energy, gases, acids, bases, equilibrium, organic chemistry, nuclear chemistry, and green chemistry.

You will learn independently through Moodle directions and assigned Savvas resources. Your Teacher of Record (TOR) provides clarification, feedback, intervention, and retake support, but every required learning step is explained in the course.

What you will learn to do

  • Use laboratory safety procedures, measurement, units, significant figures, data tables, and graphs.
  • Explain atomic structure, electron arrangement, periodic trends, bonding, formulas, and nomenclature.
  • Model chemical reactions and use conservation of mass, mole relationships, and stoichiometry.
  • Analyze energy changes, gases, phase transitions, reaction rates, equilibrium, acids, bases, and pH.
  • Interpret chemical evidence, models, diagrams, equations, and data.
  • Evaluate chemical technologies, environmental effects, and sustainable processes.
