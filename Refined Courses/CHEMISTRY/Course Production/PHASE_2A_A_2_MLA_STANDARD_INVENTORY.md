## MLA Chemistry Domains

| Domain | Name | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| SCI | Scientific Inquiry and Practices | Nature of science, scientific knowledge, claims, sources, models, and society. |
| LAB | Laboratory and Data Skills | Lab safety, measurement, tools, data displays, error, precision, and communication. |
| MAT | Matter and Measurement | Measurement, significant figures, scientific notation, states of matter, properties, and changes. |
| ATO | Atomic Structure and Periodicity | Atomic theory, atomic structure, electron arrangement, periodic table, energy quantization, and electromagnetic spectrum. |
| BON | Bonding and Compounds | Bonding forces, intermolecular attractions, molecular structure, formula representations, and compounds. |
| REA | Reactions and Stoichiometry | Reaction types, conservation of mass, mole concept, quantitative relationships, and chemical/nuclear distinctions. |
| ENE | Energy and Thermochemistry | Energy forms, temperature, energy transformations, potential energy diagrams, and endothermic/exothermic processes. |
| GAS | Gases, Solutions, and Equilibrium | Gases, kinetic molecular theory, water/solution behavior, acids/bases, rates, catalysts, and equilibrium. |
