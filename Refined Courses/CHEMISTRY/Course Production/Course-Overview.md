# CHEM - Chemistry

**Credit:** 1.0 High School Science Credit  
**Course Type:** Mastery-Based High School Laboratory Science Course  
**Dashboard Status:** Eight-unit authoritative structure

## Course Description

Chemistry develops understanding of matter, measurement, laboratory safety, atomic structure, periodic patterns, bonding, compounds, reactions, quantitative chemistry, energy, gases, acids, bases, equilibrium, redox, organic chemistry, nuclear chemistry, environmental chemistry, and sustainable chemical processes.

Instruction is designed for independent mastery in an online learning environment. Moodle gives complete student directions and is MLA's official evidence record. Assigned Savvas resources provide eText instruction, presentations, videos, worksheets, labs, virtual labs, simulations, interactives, and review activities. Teacher of Record support is available for clarification, feedback, intervention, remediation, and retake workflow.

## Standards Alignment

MLA standards are derived from the detailed Chemistry crosswalk and align course outcomes with Florida Chemistry expectations, CPALMS, Florida B.E.S.T. embedded literacy, Common Core Literacy in Science and Technical Subjects, SAT evidence/data expectations, and ACT Science readiness. Student-facing Moodle lessons list only the applicable MLA standards.

## Course-Level Learning Outcomes

Students will:

- use laboratory safety, measurement, units, significant figures, data tables, graphs, and uncertainty;
- explain atomic structure, electron arrangement, periodic trends, bonding, formulas, and nomenclature;
- model reactions and use conservation of mass, mole relationships, concentration, and stoichiometry;
- analyze energy changes, gases, phase behavior, reaction rates, equilibrium, acids, bases, and pH;
- interpret chemical evidence, models, diagrams, equations, and data;
- evaluate chemical technologies, environmental effects, and sustainable processes.
