# Chemistry Production MD Direct Resource Alignment Reaudit

Date: 2026-07-08
Course: Chemistry
Audit Focus: Course-production source-of-truth language for direct resources, simulations, and student-use directions

## Purpose

This reaudit corrects Chemistry course-production MD files that still reflected the older candidate-resource and approval-only language. That language conflicted with the global MLA science/lab requirement that routine free, student-safe, directly aligned trusted simulations, virtual labs, data sets, and resources must be inserted as exact direct student links with adjacent step-by-step student-use directions.

## Files Corrected

- `Course Production/PHASE_3A_A_MAPPING_FRAMEWORK.md`
- `Course Production/PHASE_3A_B_1_UNIT_LEVEL_MAPPING.md`
- `Course Production/PHASE_3A_B_2_LESSON_LEVEL_MAPPING.md`
- `Course Production/PHASE_3A_B_3_LAB_VISUAL_SIMULATION_MAPPING.md`

## Corrections Applied

- Removed source-of-truth language saying external resources are approval-only before lesson insertion.
- Replaced `Candidate Resource Location for Approval` language with required direct resource/simulation review and direct-link expectations.
- Added explicit requirement that every inserted resource link must include step-by-step student-use directions.
- Clarified that approval is required only for exceptions: login, payment, special hardware, unsafe activity/materials, non-public access, questionable source quality, mature content, or uncertain alignment.
- Clarified that Lesson 8 uses direct resources from Lessons 1-7 only unless a new unit-synthesis resource is needed and fully documented.

## Current Rule

For all science/lab courses, including Chemistry:

- exact direct links are required for routine free, student-safe, directly aligned trusted resources
- provider homepages, search pages, collection pages, and vague directions are not acceptable when a direct resource exists
- every link must tell students what to click, what controls/settings/sections/tables/figures to use, what to observe, what evidence to record, and how that evidence connects to the mapped standard
- missing direct links or missing student-use directions are Moodle-transfer and certification blockers

## Final Decision

PASS. Chemistry production MD source-of-truth files now defer to and align with the global MLA direct-resource and student-use-direction requirements. No Chemistry-specific simulation/lab policy is created by these files.
