# Final-Lesson Synthesis Standard

## Purpose

The final lesson in each unit must be a true synthesis lesson. It prepares students for the Unit Assessment by integrating the prior lessons in that unit.

For 1.0-credit courses, the final synthesis lesson is Lesson 8 and integrates Lessons 1-7.

For 0.5-credit courses, the final synthesis lesson is Lesson 5 and integrates Lessons 1-4.

## Final Synthesis Lesson Is Not

The final synthesis lesson is not:

- review only
- reteaching only
- assessment launch only
- isolated skill practice
- a place to introduce major new standards
- a page that displays backend assessment filenames

## Final Synthesis Lesson Must

The final synthesis lesson must:

- integrate the prior lessons in that unit
- combine unit standards, concepts, skills, vocabulary, reasoning, and communication
- use only standards taught in the prior lessons in that unit
- include synthesis tasks on P02-P07
- preserve the MLA workflow
- include Guided Practice before Unit Assessment
- prepare students for the Unit Assessment
- keep assessment references student-facing and not backend-facing

For 0.5-credit courses, Lesson 5 is the synthesis lesson. Lesson 5 includes Guided Practice and the Unit Assessment, but it must not include a Lesson Quiz.

## Final Synthesis Lesson Page Checks

Check:

- P01 identifies synthesis purpose
- P02/P03 include mixed unit tasks
- P04 includes three synthesis worked examples
- P05 prepares students for Guided Practice/Unit Assessment
- P06 includes independent synthesis work
- P07 includes checkpoint synthesis task and mastery criteria
