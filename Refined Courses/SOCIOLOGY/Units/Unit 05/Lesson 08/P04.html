Worked Example 1

Prompt: A review case asks students to combine MLA.SOC.INST.1, MLA.SOC.INST.2 without adding unmapped content.

Step 1: Identify each standard. This identifies the sociology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Name the exact mapped standard being used. This ties the answer to the embedded model, source, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Synthesis requires standard-by-standard evidence. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.SOC.INST.1.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A review case asks students to combine MLA.SOC.INST.1, MLA.SOC.INST.2 without adding unmapped content.

Step 1: Use unit evidence. This identifies the sociology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Connect the case to the correct chart or source. This ties the answer to the embedded model, source, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Do not borrow from another unit. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.SOC.INST.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A review case asks students to combine MLA.SOC.INST.1, MLA.SOC.INST.2 without adding unmapped content.

Step 1: Explain respectfully. This identifies the sociology object before reasoning.

Step 2: Avoid stereotypes, labels, or unsupported assumptions. This ties the answer to the embedded model, source, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Sociological explanations must be careful and evidence-based. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.SOC.INST.1.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the social pattern, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without stereotyping or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using sociological evidence to explain the social pattern within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a stereotype, personal opinion, or unsupported claim without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the social pattern or institution, read the evidence, apply the correct concept, and state a careful conclusion.
