P01 Lesson Overview

Economy, Work, and Social Life

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.SOC.INST.1: Analyze family, education, religion, economy, government, and health as social institutions. MLA.SOC.INST.2: Explain how institutions shape behavior, opportunity, inequality, belonging, conflict, and social stability/change.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how economy, work, and social life helps explain society and social interaction using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded work institution comparison chart, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped sociology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches social institutions. This lesson must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.SOC.INST.1, MLA.SOC.INST.2.

Embedded Sociology Support: work institution comparison chart

Step or EvidenceWhat the Student Should Notice
InstitutionOrganized part of society that meets needs and shapes behavior.

OpportunityAccess or barrier created by the institution.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the social pattern, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without stereotyping or guessing.
