P01 Lesson Overview

Leadership, Conformity, and Group Decision-Making

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.SOC.GRP.1: Analyze groups, networks, organizations, bureaucracy, leadership, conformity, deviance, and social control.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how leadership, conformity, and group decision-making helps explain society and social interaction using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded group influence decision table, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped sociology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches groups, organization, deviance, and change.
