Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Name the social behavior, pattern, group, institution, or community issue in the prompt.
  2. Identify the exact sociology concept involved: group, network, organization, deviance, social control.
  3. Read the embedded group and network comparison chart before choosing or writing an answer.
  4. Connect one piece of evidence to MLA.SOC.GRP.1 and stay inside the mapped lesson standard.
  5. Explain carefully: describe evidence, avoid stereotypes or unsupported claims, and state the sociological reasoning.
