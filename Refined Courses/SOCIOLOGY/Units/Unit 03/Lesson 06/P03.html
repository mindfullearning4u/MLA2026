P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A group or organization scenario shows conformity, leadership, deviance, labeling, or social change.

  1. Underline the social pattern, group, institution, category, or community issue.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: group, network, organization, deviance, social control.
  3. Use the embedded collective behavior comparison table to identify the evidence.
  4. Match the evidence to MLA.SOC.GRP.2.
  5. Write one sentence explaining the sociology reasoning without overclaiming.

Embedded Sociology Support: collective behavior comparison table

Step or EvidenceWhat the Student Should Notice
GroupPeople connected by interaction or identity.
NormExpected behavior.
ControlReward, sanction, law, or informal pressure.
ChangeHow group action or movements shift society.

Student check: Use this support before answering. Your explanation should name the social pattern, cite one piece of evidence, and connect it to the mapped standard without stereotyping or guessing.

Common Mistake

Correct: using sociological evidence to explain the social pattern within the mapped standard.

Incorrect: making a stereotype, personal opinion, or unsupported claim without evidence.

Teachable Explanation

The strongest answer starts with evidence, identifies the correct sociology concept, respects the limits of the evidence, and avoids stereotypes, labels, or assumptions not provided.
