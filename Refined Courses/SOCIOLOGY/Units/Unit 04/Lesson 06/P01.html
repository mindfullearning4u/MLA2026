P01 Lesson Overview

Stereotypes, Prejudice, Discrimination, and Bias

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.SOC.STR.2: Explain race, ethnicity, gender, age, ability, and other social categories using evidence, respectful language, and social-structure analysis.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how stereotypes, prejudice, discrimination, and bias helps explain society and social interaction using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded bias impact analysis chart, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped sociology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches stratification and social categories.

Embedded Sociology Support: bias impact analysis chart
