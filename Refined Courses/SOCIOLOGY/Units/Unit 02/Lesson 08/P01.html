P01 Lesson Overview

Synthesis: Culture and Socialization

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.SOC.CUL.1: Analyze culture, norms, values, symbols, language, sanctions, ethnocentrism, cultural relativism, and cultural change. MLA.SOC.CUL.2: Explain socialization, identity formation, agents of socialization, roles, status, and the life course.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how synthesis: culture and socialization helps explain society and social interaction using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded culture socialization evidence matrix, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped sociology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches unit synthesis using mapped sociology standards. This lesson must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.SOC.CUL.1, MLA.SOC.CUL.2.
