P01 Lesson Overview

Policy Choices and Community Impact

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.SOC.CIV.1: Apply sociological evidence to community issues, civic participation, policy choices, conflict resolution, and ethical social action. MLA.SOC.CIV.2: Create evidence-based explanations and action plans that remain within mapped sociology standards and avoid stereotypes or unsupported claims.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how policy choices and community impact helps explain society and social interaction using evidence.

What You Will Do: Study the embedded policy impact comparison table, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped sociology standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches civic sociology and social action. This lesson must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.SOC.CIV.1, MLA.SOC.CIV.2.
