# Florida, Common Core, SAT, and ACT Alignment

Health & Physical Education uses CPALMS/FDOE Health Education and Physical Education as the source layer and supports Florida B.E.S.T. literacy expectations through vocabulary, evidence, interpretation of charts/logs/labels, explanation, and written reflection.

Common Core support: reading informational text, writing explanations, citing evidence, speaking/listening, and vocabulary.

SAT support: evidence use, words in context, analysis of charts and data, and expression of ideas.

ACT support: reading comprehension, research summaries, conventions, and clear written communication.
