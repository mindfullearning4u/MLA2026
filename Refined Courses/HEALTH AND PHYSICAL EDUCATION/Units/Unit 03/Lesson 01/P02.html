Six Nutrient Categories

Carbohydrate, protein, and fat are macronutrients. Vitamins and minerals are micronutrients. Water is essential but is neither a macro- nor micronutrient in this comparison.

Hydration Needs Vary

Analyze five factors: physical activity, environment, duration, individual needs, and food/fluid intake. Warm conditions or longer, more intense activity may increase the importance of replacing fluid. One symptom alone does not prove dehydration, and there is no single amount that is correct for everyone.

Electrolytes participate in fluid balance, but a sports drink is not automatically required for every activity. The appropriate conclusion depends on the scenario.

Overall Dietary Pattern

Use four lenses: variety (different foods and nutrient sources), balance (reasonable relationships among choices), adequacy (needs are sufficiently supported), and context (culture, access, cost, preference, schedule, and activity). A single food cannot reveal an entire pattern.
