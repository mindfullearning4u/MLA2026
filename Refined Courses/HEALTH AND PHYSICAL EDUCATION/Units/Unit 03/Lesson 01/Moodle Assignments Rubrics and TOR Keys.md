### TOR notebook key

- Carbohydrate: important energy source.
- Protein: growth, maintenance, tissue repair, enzymes and other functions.
- Fat: energy, cell/body functions, absorption of certain vitamins.
- Vitamins/minerals: regulation, structural, and other essential functions.
- Water: fluid balance, transport, and temperature regulation.
- Fiber must be addressed as a carbohydrate component with digestive/other health functions.
- Accept legitimate secondary nutrients because foods contain multiple nutrients.
- Require revision for demonizing carbohydrate/fat, requiring supplements, equating nutrition with body weight, severe restriction, universal hydration claims, or unsupported conclusions.
