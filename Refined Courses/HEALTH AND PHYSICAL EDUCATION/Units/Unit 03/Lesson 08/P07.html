Checkpoint Task

Explain how a student should synthesize nutrition, hydration, and decision-making. Include the standard code, one piece of evidence, the decision or action step, and a verification check.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses correct health/fitness vocabulary.
  • Uses evidence from the lesson model.
  • Gives a realistic and safe action step.
  • Stays inside the lesson standard.
  • Explains why the action supports health, safety, or lifelong activity.
