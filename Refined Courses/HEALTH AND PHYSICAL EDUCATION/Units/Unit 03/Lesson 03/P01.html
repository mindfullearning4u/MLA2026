Essential Question

How can Nutrition Facts labels and credible information support informed choices without judging a food by one number, claim, or advertisement?

Overview and Why It Matters

You will learn to read standardized label evidence, adjust values for fictional amounts, interpret %DV, distinguish total from added sugars, and test package claims. Labels inform; they do not prescribe a perfect diet or classify foods or people as good or bad.

Learning Objectives

  • locate and interpret serving, calorie, nutrient, %DV, sugar, and micronutrient information;
  • calculate values for different serving amounts;
  • compare products using comparable servings and a stated goal;
  • evaluate claims, marketing, sources, evidence, and conflicts; and
  • state limitations without overgeneralizing.

MLA Standards

MLA.PEH.NUT.02: Interpret labels, dietary information, claims, and advertisements and distinguish evidence from unsupported claims.

MLA.PEH.CON.01: Evaluate credibility, evidence, qualifications, conflicts, safety, and reliability.

Supporting: MLA.PEH.NUT.01 and MLA.PEH.WEL.03.

Privacy

Use fictional labels only. Do not track personal intake, weight, BMI, allergies, diagnoses, or medical diets.
