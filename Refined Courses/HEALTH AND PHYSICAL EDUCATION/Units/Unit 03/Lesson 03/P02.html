Read the Label in Order

  1. Check serving size and servings per container.
  2. Match values to the fictional amount consumed.
  3. Read calories and nutrient amounts.
  4. Use %DV: 5% or less is generally low; 20% or more is generally high.
  5. Remember the guide is not individualized medical advice.
