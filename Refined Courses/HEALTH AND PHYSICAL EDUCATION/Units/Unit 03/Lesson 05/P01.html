Essential Question

How can nutrition knowledge, hydration needs, credible information, personal circumstances, and realistic goals be combined into a sustainable health-supporting plan?

Overview and Objectives

You will build and evaluate behavior-focused nutrition or hydration plans. By the end, you can create a realistic goal, connect it to evidence, match strategies to barriers, monitor the behavior, and revise the plan for sustainability.

MLA Standards: MLA.PEH.NUT.01, MLA.PEH.NUT.02, MLA.PEH.GOL.01; supporting MLA.PEH.NUT.03, MLA.PEH.CON.01, MLA.PEH.WEL.02, MLA.PEH.WEL.03, MLA.PEH.GOL.02.
