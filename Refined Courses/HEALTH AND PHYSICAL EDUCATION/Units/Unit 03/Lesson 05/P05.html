Fictional Option

Riley wants to use Nutrition Facts labels more consistently when comparing two similar packaged snacks for fiber and added sugars. Riley shops with a limited budget, has little weekday time, and currently checks labels only occasionally.

Required Notebook Response

  1. Approved focus and non-sensitive baseline.
  2. Specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, time-bound behavioral goal.
  3. At least two lesson-evidence connections.
  4. Two strengths and two or more barriers.
  5. One realistic strategy for every barrier, including an accessible alternative.
  6. Relevant variety, hydration/activity, or label considerations.
  7. One credible source type and why it is stronger than marketing/testimonial evidence.
  8. Exact behavior-monitoring tool and entries.
  9. Progress indicator, evaluation time, and adjustment rule.
  10. Sustainability analysis: time, cost, access, culture, preference, family context, schedule, flexibility, and safety.
  11. Final explanation of why the plan supports behavior rather than body outcome or perfection.
