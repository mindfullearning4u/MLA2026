Vocabulary

Required notebook directions: Write Unit 3 Lesson 2 Vocabulary. Copy all 17 bold terms and complete definitions.

Energy
Capacity to perform body functions and activity.
Calorie
A unit used to measure energy; in food contexts it generally means a kilocalorie.
Kilocalorie
The scientific unit equal to 1,000 small calories and commonly called a food Calorie.
Energy intake
Energy obtained from food and beverages.
Energy expenditure
Energy used for basic functions, activity, and other physiological processes.
Basal energy needs
Energy the body uses for essential functions at rest.
Physical activity
Body movement that requires energy.
Metabolism
The bodyâ€™s chemical processes that sustain life and use energy.
Energy balance
The relationship between energy intake and total expenditure over time.
Estimated value
An informed approximation rather than an exact measurement.
Variable
A factor that can change and influence a result.
Data
Recorded information used for calculation or analysis.
Serving
A standardized amount listed for nutrition information.
Portion
The amount actually selected or consumed.
Physical performance
How the body carries out an activity or task.
Recovery
The process of restoring capacity after activity or stress.
Energy availability
Energy remaining to support body functions after activity demands are considered.

Safety and Privacy

Use only fictional data. Do not report personal calories, weight, BMI, body-fat data, medical diets, or weight-loss goals.
