Activity Estimates and Variables

Estimated expenditure can vary with activity, intensity, duration, body size, physiology, efficiency, and measurement method. A device estimate is not an exact laboratory measurement.

Duration is how long activity lasts. Intensity is how demanding it is. Longer does not automatically mean more intense.
