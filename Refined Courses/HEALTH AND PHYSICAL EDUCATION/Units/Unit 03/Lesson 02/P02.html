Vocabulary

TermMeaning
EvidenceInformation from a chart, label, log, symptom, or observation that supports a decision.
Risk factorSomething that increases the chance of harm or an unhealthy outcome.
Protective factorSomething that lowers risk or supports health and safety.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Read the model first. Do not choose an answer from memory alone.
  2. Name the exact health, fitness, or safety concept in the lesson.
  3. Identify the evidence that supports the decision.
  4. Explain why the safest or healthiest choice fits the standard.
