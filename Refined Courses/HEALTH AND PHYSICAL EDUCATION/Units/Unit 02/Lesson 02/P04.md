P04 — Safe Physical Application
Purpose and Choices
Experience and monitor cardiorespiratory activity safely. Choose brisk walking, marching in place, low-impact aerobic movement, stationary cycling if safely available, dancing, or an approved seated/standing adaptive alternative.
Pre-Activity Check
Confirm safe space and surface, environment, clothing/footwear, water where appropriate, no known restriction ignored, and a modification option. Use moderate intensity as the default. Complete 10–15 minutes continuously or in approved modified intervals; no maximal effort.
Notebook — required activity record
Record: activity; duration; RPE; talk-test result; pulse/heart rate if safely obtainable (write “not used” if not); physiological observation; modification; and recovery observation. Use at least two monitoring methods. A wearable is not required.
Stop/Modify Rule
If effort is too high, slow down, reduce movement range, choose lower impact, or recover. Stop and seek appropriate help for concerning pain, dizziness, unusual breathing difficulty, unsafe conditions, or another serious warning sign. Never chase a target number.
