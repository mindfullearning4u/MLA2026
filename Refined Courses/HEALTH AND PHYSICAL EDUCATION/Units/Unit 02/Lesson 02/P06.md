Notebook Task — Four Fictional Profiles
Create four profiles labeled A–D. For each, the lesson provides/you must record: fictional age, activity, duration, activity heart rate, RPE, talk test, and physiological observation. Use these datasets: A age16 steady walk 20min HR140 RPE5 comfortable brief sentences controlled breathing; B age15 sprint intervals 6min HR178 RPE8 short phrases heavy but controlled breathing; C age17 cycling 15min HR105 RPE2 easy conversation; D age16 dance 8min HR150 RPE6 then dizziness.
Required Analysis for Each
Classify aerobic/anaerobic/mixed; calculate estimated maximum; calculate 60%–80% range; compare heart rate; interpret RPE and talk test; decide whether evidence agrees; choose maintain, safely increase, decrease, or stop/seek help; and justify with evidence.
Critical Thinking Response
After all four, explain in 4–6 sentences why multiple monitoring methods are more useful than one number. Note: Profile D must prioritize dizziness over target-zone data.
