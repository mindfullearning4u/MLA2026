P03 — Examples and Guided Practice
Target Heart-Rate Model
This is an estimate, not medical advice. Fictional age 16: 220 − 16 = 204 bpm. Lower boundary: 204 × 0.60 = 122.4 ≈ 122. Upper: 204 × 0.80 = 163.2 ≈ 163. Instructional range: 122–163 bpm. Calculate → interpret → adjust; safety overrides the number.
Modeled Multiple-Method Conclusion
Heart rate is within the assigned range, RPE is 5/10, speech is possible in brief comfortable sentences, and breathing is elevated but controlled. These independent clues agree with moderate intensity. The conclusion is derived by comparing all evidence—not by treating the device reading as exact.
Notebook Guided Practice
A. Classify six activities as primarily aerobic, anaerobic, or mixed: 30-minute walk; 10-second sprint; interval dance; steady cycling; brief explosive movement; stop-and-go court play. Cite duration/intensity evidence.
B. Calculate 60%–80% ranges for fictional ages 15, 16, and 17 using 220 − age; show every step and reasonable rounding.
C. For three profiles, compare heart rate, RPE, and talk test and choose low, moderate, vigorous, or excessive for the assigned scenario.
Misconception Check
Higher heart rate is not automatically better; sweating is not precise; 220 − age is only an estimate; trackers are optional; never push through warning signs to reach a number.
