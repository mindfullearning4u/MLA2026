P02 — Learn the Concept
Aerobic, Anaerobic, and Mixed
Aerobic activity is typically sustained and rhythmic, such as brisk walking or cycling. Anaerobic activity is typically brief with rapid high energy demand, such as a short sprint. Many activities are mixed. These terms describe energy demands—not “good/bad” or simply “easy/hard.”
Intensity Is Not Duration
Intensity means how hard the body works. Duration means how long. A 30-minute easy walk can be longer but less intense than a 10-second sprint.
Monitoring Triangle
Heart rate/pulse + talk test + RPE/physiological response → intensity decision. No single method is perfect. Heart rate is beats per minute; talk test uses speech comfort; RPE uses a 0–10 effort scale; responses include breathing, warmth, pulse, and controlled recovery.
RPE and Talk-Test Guide
RPE 0 = rest; 1–2 very light; 3–4 light to moderate; 5–6 moderate to somewhat hard; 7–8 vigorous/hard; 9–10 very hard to maximal and not deliberately required. Comfortable conversation suggests lower intensity; speaking with some effort suggests moderate; only short phrases suggests higher intensity.
