LESSON 2 — CARDIORESPIRATORY FITNESS: AEROBIC ACTIVITY AND EXERCISE INTENSITY
P07 — Mastery Checkpoint
Independent Checkpoint Scenario
Taylor, age 16, completes 14 minutes of low-impact aerobic movement for a moderate-intensity goal. Observed heart rate is 168 bpm, RPE is 7/10, speaking is limited to short phrases, breathing is elevated, and no emergency warning sign is reported.
Answer All Requirements
1 classify aerobic/anaerobic/mixed; 2 calculate 220 − age; 3 calculate 60%–80% range; 4 compare 168 with range; 5 interpret talk test; 6 interpret RPE; 7 interpret physiological response; 8 decide whether evidence agrees; 9 recommend maintain/increase/decrease/stop; 10 justify using at least three evidence forms; 11 explain one limitation of estimated heart rate alone.
Submission and Mastery
Submit structured calculations plus a 200–250 word analysis in U2 L2 Mastery Checkpoint Submission. Mastery is 16/20. A safe, evidence-supported alternative recommendation may earn credit; chasing a number despite warning signs does not.
