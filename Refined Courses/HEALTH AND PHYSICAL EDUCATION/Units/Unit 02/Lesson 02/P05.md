LESSON 2 — CARDIORESPIRATORY FITNESS: AEROBIC ACTIVITY AND EXERCISE INTENSITY
P05 — Analyze the Activity / Guided Application
Notebook — answer all nine questions
Using your P04 record: (1) Was activity primarily aerobic? (2) What evidence supports that? (3) What was estimated intensity? (4) Which two monitoring methods support it? (5) Did methods agree? (6) Was modification needed? (7) What happened during recovery? (8) What safe adjustment would reduce excessive intensity? (9) What modest adjustment could increase intensity if below the assigned goal?
Worked Reasoning Pattern
State evidence → compare methods → decide → justify adjustment. Example: RPE 7 and short-phrase speech both indicate vigorous effort, while the assigned goal was moderate; therefore slow pace or reduce range. Even if heart rate appears “in range,” two other methods and safety context justify reducing effort.
Notebook Transfer Check
Your notebook must include the complete activity record and nine numbered answers. Do not submit only a paragraph or only numerical data.
