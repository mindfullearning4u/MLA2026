Putting It All Together

Standards Covered

MLA.HPE.FIT.2, MLA.HPE.LIF.1, MLA.HPE.SAF.1

What You Will Learn

You will learn to synthesize a personal lifelong fitness plan. This lesson teaches the health or movement skill step by step so you can make safe, evidence-based choices without guessing.

What You Will Do

  • Study the model or organizer.
  • Learn the vocabulary.
  • Practice the decision or movement-planning process.
  • Use feedback to correct misunderstandings.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, guided practice, independent work, checkpoint submission, and the assigned quiz or unit assessment with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

The standard asks you to use health and physical education knowledge to make safer, healthier, and more active decisions.

Teacher of Record Support

If you are still unsure after using the lesson steps, examples, visuals, and checklist, contact your Teacher of Record for clarification, remediation support, checkpoint review, or retake guidance.
