Notebook Task — Independent Activity-to-Component Analysis
This entire response belongs in your notebook and will be submitted in the separate Notebook Submission.
Analyze These Five New Activities
1. Sustained easy swimming simulation using seated arm movements (no water required). 2. A brief controlled jump or no-impact rapid stand alternative. 3. A safe obstacle-path direction change or traced seated-hand pathway. 4. Repeated low-resistance band movement or equipment-free alternative. 5. A controlled mobility sequence.
Required for Each
Identify one primary component; at least two supporting components; category for each; two pieces of activity evidence; and a 2–3 sentence explanation. Then compare two activities and explain how different goals would change which activity is more appropriate.
Final Check
Do not claim that one activity develops every component equally. Do not use appearance, weight, or athletic superiority as evidence.
