PHYSICAL EDUCATION AND HEALTH
UNIT 2 — FITNESS SCIENCE, TRAINING, AND PERSONAL FITNESS DEVELOPMENT
LESSON 1 — HEALTH-RELATED AND SKILL-RELATED FITNESS
P01 — Lesson Overview and Vocabulary
Essential Question
How do health-related and skill-related fitness components help us understand what different physical activities develop and why those differences matter?
Quick Lesson Overview
Learn the five health-related and six skill-related fitness components, distinguish primary from supporting components, and use activity evidence to justify classifications.
Why This Lesson Matters
Fitness is not one ability. Knowing which capacities an activity requires helps you choose safe activities that match a goal without judging body size or athletic superiority.
Learning Objectives
Define and classify all eleven components; distinguish physical activity from exercise; identify primary and supporting components; analyze activities using evidence; and complete a safe introductory application.
MLA Standards Connection
MLA.PEH.FIT.01 — explain and apply health- and skill-related components. MLA.PEH.FIT.05 — safe varied lifetime activity. MLA.PEH.GOL.02 — collect and interpret evidence.
Notebook — copy these 18 terms and definitions
Physical fitness: capacities for physical activity. Health-related fitness: capacities supporting health and function. Skill-related fitness: capacities supporting movement performance. Cardiorespiratory endurance: support for sustained activity. Muscular strength: ability to exert force. Muscular endurance: repeated or sustained muscle effort. Flexibility: available joint range of motion. Body composition: relative proportions of body tissues. Agility: controlled direction change. Balance: body stability. Coordination: efficient use of body parts together. Power: rapid force production. Reaction time: response time to a stimulus. Speed: ability to move quickly. Physical activity: body movement using energy. Exercise: planned physical activity intended to develop fitness. Fitness component: one specific fitness capacity. Performance: execution of a task.

Write all 18 bold terms and their provided definitions in your notebook.

Use approved low-risk activity, clear space, appropriate clothing, modifications, and stop rules from Unit 1. Body composition is neutral: do not record weight, BMI, appearance, or body-fat measurements.
