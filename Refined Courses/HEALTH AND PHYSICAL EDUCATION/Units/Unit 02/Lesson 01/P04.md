UNIT 2 — FITNESS SCIENCE, TRAINING, AND PERSONAL FITNESS DEVELOPMENT
LESSON 1 — HEALTH-RELATED AND SKILL-RELATED FITNESS
P04 — Apply the Skill / Practice the Concept
Safe Introductory Physical Application
Choose one approved low-risk option: easy walking, gentle mobility, light marching in place, or an approved adaptive equivalent. Complete 5–8 comfortable minutes only after checking space, clothing, environment, and readiness. Stop for pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty beyond expected effort, unsafe conditions, or another concerning sign; seek appropriate help when warranted.
Notebook — required activity record
Record: activity; environment/safety check; approximate duration; modification used or “none”; one observed health-related demand; one observed skill-related demand; evidence for each; and whether each was primary or supporting.
Practice Scenarios
Analyze four fictional tasks: repeated chair rises using controlled form; a brief quick-response movement to a visual cue; a sustained easy walk; and controlled single-leg balance with a stable support available. For each, name the most relevant component and explain the movement evidence. Never grade performance level.
Success Criteria
Your classification names a component, quotes or paraphrases task evidence, distinguishes primary/supporting demand, and stays within safe Unit 1 procedures.
TOR Help

After using the model and directions, contact your Teacher of Record if you still need help. Identify the component or reasoning step that is confusing and show what you tried.
