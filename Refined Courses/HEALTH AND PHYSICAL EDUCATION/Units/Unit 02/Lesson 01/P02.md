LESSON 1 — HEALTH-RELATED AND SKILL-RELATED FITNESS
P02 — Learn the Concept
Health-Related Components
Component	What it means	Example
Cardiorespiratory endurance	Sustained heart/lung support	Brisk walking
Muscular strength	Force in an effort	Appropriate resistance movement
Muscular endurance	Repeated/sustained muscle effort	Repeated low-resistance movement
Flexibility	Available joint range	Controlled stretching
Body composition	Relative tissue proportions	Discussed neutrally; not measured here
Skill-Related Components
Agility changes direction; balance maintains stability; coordination organizes body parts; power produces force rapidly; reaction time responds to a stimulus; speed moves quickly.
Purpose and Overlap
Health-related components primarily connect to health, function, and lifetime capacity. Skill-related components primarily support efficient performance. Real activities often use both; classify by evidence rather than forcing one label.
Primary vs. Supporting
The primary component best matches an activity’s dominant demand or goal. Supporting components still contribute. Example: brisk walking primarily challenges cardiorespiratory endurance; muscular endurance, balance, and coordination support it.
Notebook — required summary
Create two labeled lists containing all five health-related and six skill-related components. Then write a three-sentence summary explaining category purpose, overlap, and primary versus supporting components.
