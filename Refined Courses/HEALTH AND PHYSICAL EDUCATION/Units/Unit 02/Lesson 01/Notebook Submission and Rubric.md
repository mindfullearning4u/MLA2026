TOR Key
Accept different primary/supporting classifications when the student cites defensible activity evidence. Full credit distinguishes strength from endurance and health-related from skill-related fitness. Do not grade athletic ability, weight, appearance, speed, or performance superiority. Revision triggers: missing evidence, appearance-based claims, unsafe activity, or treating every component as equally primary.
