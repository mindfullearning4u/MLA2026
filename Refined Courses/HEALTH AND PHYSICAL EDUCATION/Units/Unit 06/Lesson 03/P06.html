Mastery Checkpoint — Separate Submission

Scenario: A fictional social-media post shows a teen holding a vape beside the words “clean flavor, no real risk.” Comments say “everyone does it.” Another post says marijuana is natural and therefore cannot affect driving or school performance.

What you must submit

Write one 300–350 word analysis in the Lesson 3 Checkpoint Submission activity. Your response must answer all nine prompts:

  1. Identify two claims.
  2. Name one marketing technique.
  3. Explain why online comments do not prove a norm.
  4. Correct the “vapor” misconception.
  5. Explain nicotine dependence using a cause-and-effect chain.
  6. Correct the “natural = safe” claim.
  7. Explain two THC-related functions important for driving or school.
  8. Recommend two protective decisions tied to goals.
  9. End with an evidence → reasoning → conclusion statement.

This checkpoint is not the notebook task. Submit it separately.

Mastery reminder

Mastery is 80%. A response that gives opinions without evidence or fails to distinguish nicotine/vaping from marijuana/THC must be revised.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR for clarification before submitting; the TOR can guide your reasoning but cannot write the response for you.
