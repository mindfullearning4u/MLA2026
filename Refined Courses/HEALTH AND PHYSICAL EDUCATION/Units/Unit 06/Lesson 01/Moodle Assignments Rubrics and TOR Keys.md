# Unit 6 Lesson 1 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

## Notebook Submission — 20 points

- Vocabulary: 2
- Use / Misuse / Dependence / Addiction: 4
- Risk / Protective Factors: 4
- Health Consequences: 3
- Guided Practice: 2
- Independent Work: 4
- Organization: 1

TOR key: Use is broad and does not automatically mean misuse. Misuse includes prescription sharing, contrary-to-direction use, unsafe/unlawful use, and underage alcohol use. Dependence may involve adaptation or withdrawal. Addiction requires a broader problematic pattern and qualified assessment. Risk increases vulnerability but is not certainty. Require revision for unsupported diagnosis, tolerance-equals-safety claims, prescription-sharing approval, willpower/moral-failure explanations, or stigmatizing language.

## Checkpoint Submission — 20 points

- Protective Factors: 3
- Health/Life Consequences: 4
- Diagnosis Boundary: 2
- Prevention / Help-Seeking: 3

TOR key: Full mastery requires accurate classification, evidence-based risk/protection reasoning, multidimensional consequences, no unsupported diagnosis, realistic prevention/help response, and connection to goals. Return for revision if the student diagnoses addiction from one sign, treats risk as certainty, or uses stigmatizing explanations.
