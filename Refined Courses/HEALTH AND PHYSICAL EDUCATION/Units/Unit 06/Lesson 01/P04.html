Apply Substance-Use Health Reasoning

Part A — Classify

Classify each fictional example as use, misuse, dependence-related concern, addiction-related concern, or insufficient information: medication taken as directed; medication shared; one report of tolerance; withdrawal after stopping; repeated use interfering with responsibilities; a rumor about a classmate; underage alcohol use; a legal adult using a legal substance; craving plus repeated harm; one isolated poor decision.

Part B — Risk, Protective, or Context Dependent

Classify: trusted adult, chronic stress, clear goals, inaccurate perceived norm, healthy coping, substance access, supportive team, peer pressure, professional counseling, media glamorization, family history, accurate health information.

Part C — Myth or Fact

Correct these claims: “Any use means addiction.” “Tolerance means safety.” “Prescription drugs cannot be misused.” “Everyone uses substances.” “Addiction is weak willpower.” “Relapse means recovery permanently failed.”

Part D — Health Dimensions

For three fictional situations—impaired driving, repeated missed practices, and substance-based stress avoidance—identify possible physical, mental/emotional, social, performance, and legal/safety effects.

Required Notebook Task

Complete all four parts in your notebook. For every classification, add one sentence citing the evidence or concept that supports your answer. This page is guided application that prepares you for—not replaces—the independent notebook submission on Page 5.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR to review one uncertain classification. Revise any answer that treats risk as certainty, makes a diagnosis, or uses stigmatizing language.
