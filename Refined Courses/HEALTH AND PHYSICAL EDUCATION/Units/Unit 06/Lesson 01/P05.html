Independent Work — Notebook Submission

This entire page is the required Unit 6 Lesson 1 Notebook Submission.

Analyze four fictional profiles:

  1. A student takes a prescription exactly as directed and stores it securely.
  2. A student shares prescribed medication with a friend who reports similar symptoms.
  3. Repeated substance use is interfering with school, relationships, responsibilities, and goals.
  4. A student faces peer pressure but has healthy coping strategies, clear goals, a supportive adult, and accurate information.

For each profile, provide nine labeled responses:

  1. identify the substance-use concept;
  2. distinguish use from misuse;
  3. identify risk factors;
  4. identify protective factors;
  5. identify at least three possible health or life consequences;
  6. state what can be concluded;
  7. state what cannot be diagnosed;
  8. select a prevention or support strategy;
  9. explain how future goals may be affected.

Then write 150–200 words answering: Why is it more accurate and useful to treat addiction as a health condition than as a character flaw?

What to Submit

Submit your vocabulary, comparison, risk/protective matrix, health-dimensions work, guided practice, four independently completed nine-part profiles, and 150–200 word explanation in the Unit 6 Lesson 1 Notebook Submission activity.
