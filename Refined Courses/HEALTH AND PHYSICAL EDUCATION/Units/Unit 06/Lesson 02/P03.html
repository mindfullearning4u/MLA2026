Modeled Alcohol-Risk Analysis

Scenario: At a fictional event, the intended driver drank alcohol but says, “I feel fine.” Another student suggests coffee before driving.

EvidenceReasoningConclusion
Driver used alcoholJudgment and reaction can be impaired before obvious intoxication.Do not drive or ride with this driver.
“I feel fine”Self-perception is not reliable proof of unimpaired driving.Choose a sober safe ride.
Coffee suggestionCoffee does not quickly eliminate alcohol.Reject the myth; do not delay the safe decision.

Reasoning method: identify evidence, connect it to a taught health effect, then choose the option that removes the transportation danger.

Guided Practice

Analyze a new fictional situation: direct pressure to drink, an inaccurate “everyone drinks” claim, a student’s athletic goal, and a sober caregiver available. Identify the influence, write a concise refusal, cite two health/safety risks, select the safe transportation plan, and connect the decision to the goal.

Required Notebook Task

Copy the worked table. Create a second Evidence/Reasoning/Conclusion table for the guided-practice scenario and include the exact refusal statement you would recommend to the fictional student.
