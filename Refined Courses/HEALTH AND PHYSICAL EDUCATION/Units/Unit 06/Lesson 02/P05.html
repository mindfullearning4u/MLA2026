Independent Work — Notebook Submission

This entire page is required in the Unit 6 Lesson 2 Notebook Submission.

Analyze four fictional scenarios: underage drinking and peer pressure; an impaired-driving decision; repeated alcohol misuse affecting goals and health; and a possible alcohol-overdose emergency.

For each scenario, answer ten labeled prompts:

  1. primary alcohol-health issue;
  2. short-term risks;
  3. possible long-term risks where applicable;
  4. judgment/coordination consequences;
  5. legal/social/performance consequences;
  6. safest decision;
  7. refusal/prevention strategy;
  8. whether emergency help is needed;
  9. what must not be done;
  10. evidence-based justification.

Then write 150–200 words: Why is “I feel fine” not sufficient evidence that a person is unimpaired after alcohol consumption?

What to Submit

Submit vocabulary, alcohol/CNS notes, impairment analysis, driving tree, overdose pathway, guided practice, four ten-part scenarios, and the 150–200 word explanation.

TOR Help

Your TOR may clarify format. The decisions and reasoning must be independently completed.
