# Unit 6 Lesson 2 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

## Notebook — 20 points

Vocabulary 2; Alcohol/Impairment 4; Short-/Long-Term Effects 3; Driving/Safety 4; Emergency Recognition 3; Independent Work 3; Organization 1.

TOR key: full credit recognizes effects on judgment, coordination, reaction, attention, and decisions; never endorses driving/riding after alcohol; identifies serious poisoning signs as an emergency; rejects coffee/shower/walking and “sleep it off”; distinguishes acute and long-term risks. Return for unsafe emergency or driving advice and stigmatizing language.

TOR key: mastery requires prevention/refusal, impaired-driving avoidance, emergency recognition and immediate escalation, no “sleep it off,” long-term risk, and goal connection. Failure to classify the final stage as an emergency requires revision regardless of points.
