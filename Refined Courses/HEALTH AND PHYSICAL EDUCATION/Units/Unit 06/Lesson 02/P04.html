Apply Safety and Decision-Making

Part A — Term Match

Distinguish standard drink, BAC, impairment, intoxication, binge/excessive use, and alcohol poisoning.

Part B — Impairment Scenarios

For six fictional cases, identify effects on judgment, coordination, reaction time, attention, memory, or decision-making and cite the evidence.

Part C — Safe-Ride Decisions

Classify four transportation plans as safe or unsafe and replace every unsafe plan with a sober trusted driver, safe ride service, trusted adult, or emergency help.

Part D — Emergency or Not?

Classify six cases as prevention concern, impaired/risky, or emergency. Any case involving inability to wake, abnormal breathing, seizure, or severe overdose signs requires emergency services.

Part E — Myth Correction

Correct: coffee sobers quickly; walking proves driving safety; beer is automatically safer; vomiting ends danger; overdose can be slept off; underage use has only legal consequences.

Required Notebook Task

Complete all five parts. For every decision, add the evidence and one-sentence reasoning. This guided application prepares you for, but does not replace, Page 5 independent work.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR to review one emergency classification before you continue.
