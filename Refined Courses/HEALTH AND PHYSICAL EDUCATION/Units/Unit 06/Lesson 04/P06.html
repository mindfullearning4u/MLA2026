Mastery Checkpoint — Separate Submission

Scenario: A fictional student takes a pill purchased online that resembles a prescription medication. The student becomes unresponsive, makes gurgling sounds, and breathes slowly. A bystander suggests waiting or using a cold shower.

What you must submit

Write one 300–350 word analysis answering all ten prompts:

  1. Identify why the source is unsafe.
  2. Explain counterfeit and fentanyl risk.
  3. Identify three emergency signs.
  4. State the first action.
  5. Explain naloxone's role and boundary.
  6. State two dispatcher-directed/support actions.
  7. Reject waiting and the cold-shower suggestion.
  8. Explain why the person must not be left alone.
  9. Recommend one prevention/storage/disposal action.
  10. End with evidence → reasoning → conclusion.

This checkpoint is separate from the notebook submission.

Mastery is 80%. Missing the emergency call, recommending delay, or treating naloxone as a replacement for care requires revision.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR for clarification before submitting. In a real emergency, call emergency services immediately.
