Essential Question

How can prevention planning, assertive refusal, emergency action, and stigma-free help-seeking protect health and future goals?

Learning Objectives

  • apply risk and protective factors to prevention planning;
  • use assertive refusal and exit strategies;
  • recognize overdose and follow an emergency action sequence;
  • select credible school, family, medical, crisis, treatment, and recovery resources;
  • create a personal, privacy-safe prevention and help-seeking plan.

MLA Standards

MLA.PEH.SUA.03: Apply prevention, refusal, emergency response, and help-seeking strategies. MLA.PEH.SUA.04: Evaluate resources and develop a health-protective action plan.

Required Vocabulary Notebook Task

Write Unit 6 Lesson 5 Vocabulary and copy: prevention—action that reduces likelihood or harm; risk factor—condition increasing likelihood of harm; protective factor—condition reducing risk; refusal skill—communication that rejects unsafe behavior; assertive communication—clear, direct, respectful communication; boundary—limit protecting safety or values; exit strategy—planned way to leave; peer pressure—social influence toward a choice; support network—trusted people/resources; credible resource—qualified, current, evidence-based source; overdose—toxic exposure disrupting vital functions; naloxone—temporary opioid-overdose reversal medicine; recovery position—side position used when appropriate to help protect an unresponsive person's airway; treatment—professional care; recovery—ongoing improvement in health/function; harm reduction—evidence-based action reducing negative consequences; stigma—negative judgment that can block care; crisis resource—immediate support for urgent danger/distress; emergency services—professional rapid-response system; action plan—specific steps, resources, and backups for a goal.

Safety and Privacy

Use fictional or general examples. Do not disclose personal substance use. Immediate danger, breathing problems, unresponsiveness, seizure, or suspected overdose requires emergency services.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR for lesson clarification or help evaluating a resource. Emergencies go to emergency services first.
