Mastery Checkpoint — Separate Submission

Scenario: At a fictional gathering, one student pressures another to use an unknown pill. Later, someone is unresponsive with abnormal breathing. Peers fear getting in trouble and suggest leaving.

What you must submit

Create a two-part checkpoint in the Lesson 5 Checkpoint activity:

Part A — 150–200 words: Write an assertive refusal with reason/value, alternative, exit, and support; identify two risks and three protective factors; explain how the plan protects a goal.

Part B — 250–300 words: Identify emergency signs; state the emergency sequence; explain naloxone's role/boundary; reject leaving or delaying; identify information for responders; explain how stigma-free help-seeking improves safety.

End Part B with an evidence → reasoning → conclusion statement. This is separate from the notebook portfolio.

Mastery is 80%. Missing the emergency call, recommending delay/abandonment, or failing to provide a complete refusal and support plan requires revision.

No Lesson 5 quiz: after both submissions achieve mastery, proceed to the Unit 6 Final Assessment.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR for clarification before submitting. In a real emergency, call emergency services immediately.
