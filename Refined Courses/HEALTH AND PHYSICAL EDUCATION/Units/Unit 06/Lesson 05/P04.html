Apply the Skill — Build an Action Plan

A strong action plan answers: What is the risk? What warning sign triggers action? What will I say/do? Who is the first resource? What is the backup? What barrier may interfere? How will I overcome it? How does the plan protect a goal?

Worked Example

Risk: transportation with an impaired driver. Trigger: driver used alcohol/cannabis or shows impaired coordination/judgment. Action: refuse the ride and move to a safe public place. First resource: prearranged trusted adult/ride. Backup: another approved safe ride or emergency services if danger is immediate. Barrier: fear of upsetting peers. Solution: use a prepared sentence and text code. Goal: arrive safely and protect driving/school/athletic goals.

Required Guided-Practice Notebook Task

Create the eight-part action-plan table above for two fictional situations: (1) pressure to vape before practice; (2) concern that a classmate may be developing substance-related problems. Include credible, role-appropriate resources and a backup for each.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR to check that each resource can actually perform the role you assigned and that each backup is specific.
