5. Independent Work — Community Health Profile

Community and Environmental Health

Create a fictional or public-data community profile. Your notebook must include one health issue, two determinants, a small labeled data display, two evidence-based inferences, one limitation, two possible actions, and a justified priority action.

Checkpoint Submission

Answer the listed checkpoint prompts in the Moodle checkpoint activity. Use complete sentences and evidence. This is not a second notebook task.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.

TOR Help

If you are unsure, contact your TOR and identify the page and step where you are stuck. For this page, ask the TOR to check your summary and reasoning without completing the thinking for you.
