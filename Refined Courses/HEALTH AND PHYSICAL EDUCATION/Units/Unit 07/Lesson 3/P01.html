Physical Education and Health · Unit 7 · Lesson 3

1. Overview and Vocabulary

Health Communication, Advocacy, and Community Action

Essential Question

How can I use evidence, critical thinking, and responsible action to strengthen personal and community wellness?

Lesson Objectives

  • Explain and apply the lesson’s core framework.
  • Analyze evidence before reaching a conclusion.
  • Create a clear, ethical, and measurable response.

MLA Standard: MLA.PEH.COM.02 and MLA.PEH.COM.01 (primary); MLA.PEH.CON.01 and MLA.PEH.WEL.03 (supporting)

Design accurate, ethical, audience-centered health communication and an achievable advocacy action.

Vocabulary

TermDefinition
health communicationsharing health information so an audience can understand and use it
advocacysupporting a cause or policy through informed action
audiencethe people a message is designed to reach
call to actiona clear, feasible next step
stakeholdera person or group affected by or able to influence an issue
framinghow information is organized and emphasized
accessibilitydesign that allows people with varied needs to use information

Required Notebook Work

Write the lesson title, essential question, and every vocabulary term with its definition in your notebook. Then write one original sentence connecting two vocabulary terms.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.
