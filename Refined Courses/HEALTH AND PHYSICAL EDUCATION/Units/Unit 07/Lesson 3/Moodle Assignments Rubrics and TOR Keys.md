# Unit 7 Lesson 3 — Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

## Notebook activity
**Title:** U7 L3 Notebook — Health Communication, Advocacy, and Community Action

**Student submission:** Submit every component stated on Page 5. Label each part. Fictional information is acceptable.

**Mastery:** 80% | **Gradebook category:** Notebook | **Weight:** 20%

### Content-specific rubric (30 points)
- Specific audience, barrier, objective, and decision-maker — 8
- Accurate evidence and ethical core message — 8
- Accessible resource prototype with feasible call to action — 7
- Evidence → reasoning → conclusion justification for design choices — 7

### TOR expected evidence and answer guidance
Score the work, not the student's lifestyle. Accept different conclusions when evidence supports them. Look for every named component, accurate application of the lesson framework, and a visible evidence → reasoning → conclusion chain. A high-scoring response identifies limitations and does not claim causation or certainty beyond the evidence. Return for revision when a required component is absent.

## Checkpoint activity
**Title:** U7 L3 Checkpoint — Health Communication, Advocacy, and Community Action

**Student submission:** Answer the final-page prompts in complete sentences. This is separate from the notebook submission; do not resubmit the entire notebook product.

**Mastery:** 80% | **Gradebook category:** Checkpoint | **Weight:** 20%

### Content-specific rubric (30 points)
- Accuracy and audience fit evaluated — 8
- Accessibility and ethical framing evaluated — 8
- Call to action and stakeholder choice defended — 7
- Success measure aligns with objective — 7

### TOR expected evidence and answer guidance
Accept varied defensible answers. Require clearly identified evidence, explicit reasoning, conclusions that match the evidence, and completion of all prompts. For full credit, explanations must say why the evidence matters, not merely repeat it. Never require private disclosure, and do not grade a learner down for choosing a fictional scenario.
