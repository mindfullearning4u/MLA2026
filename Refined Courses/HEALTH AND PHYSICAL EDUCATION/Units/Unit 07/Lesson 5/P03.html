3. Reassessment and Adaptation

Lifetime Wellness: Adapting Your Plan Across Life Stages

Use a cycle: notice change, gather current evidence, reassess domains and priorities, preserve effective elements, revise goals/actions/supports, choose indicators, and schedule the next review.

Worked Reasoning

Evidence: Identify the exact fact, observation, or data point. Reasoning: Explain why that evidence supports or weakens an idea and name any uncertainty. Conclusion: State only what the evidence justifies.

Required Notebook Work

In your notebook, write the page heading and a 3–4 sentence summary. Include: (1) the most important idea, (2) one piece of evidence or example, and (3) why that evidence matters. Complete any framework or practice items named on this page.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.

TOR Help

If you are unsure, contact your TOR and identify the page and step where you are stuck. For this page, ask the TOR to check your summary and reasoning without completing the thinking for you.
