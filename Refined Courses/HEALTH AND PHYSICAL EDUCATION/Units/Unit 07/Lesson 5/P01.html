Physical Education and Health · Unit 7 · Lesson 5

1. Overview and Vocabulary

Lifetime Wellness: Adapting Your Plan Across Life Stages

Essential Question

How can I use evidence, critical thinking, and responsible action to strengthen personal and community wellness?

Lesson Objectives

  • Explain and apply the lesson’s core framework.
  • Analyze evidence before reaching a conclusion.
  • Create a clear, ethical, and measurable response.

MLA Standard: MLA.PEH.GOL.03 and MLA.PEH.GOL.02 (primary); MLA.PEH.GOL.01, MLA.PEH.FIT.04, MLA.PEH.FIT.05, MLA.PEH.WEL.03, MLA.PEH.NUT.01, MLA.PEH.MEH.01, MLA.PEH.MEH.02, MLA.PEH.REL.01, MLA.PEH.HLT.02, MLA.PEH.SUA.03, MLA.PEH.DIG.01, MLA.PEH.CON.01, MLA.PEH.COM.01, MLA.PEH.COM.02 (supporting)

Create a final lifetime-wellness capstone that can adapt across life stages and transitions.

Vocabulary

TermDefinition
life stagea period with typical roles and conditions
transitiona change that may alter needs or routines
protective factora condition that reduces risk or supports resilience
reassessmenta structured review of current evidence and priorities
continuitywhat remains stable across change
contingencya planned response if circumstances change
sustainabilitythe ability to maintain benefits over time

Required Notebook Work

Write the lesson title, essential question, and every vocabulary term with its definition in your notebook. Then write one original sentence connecting two vocabulary terms.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.
