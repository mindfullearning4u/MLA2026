Physical Education and Health · Unit 7 · Lesson 5

5. Guided Transition Scenarios

Lifetime Wellness: Adapting Your Plan Across Life Stages

Practice with two fictional transitions. For each, identify changed conditions, affected domains, protective factors, a goal to preserve, a strategy to revise, and evidence that would show the adaptation is working.

Required Notebook Work

In your notebook, write the page heading and a 3–4 sentence summary. Include: (1) the most important idea, (2) one piece of evidence or example, and (3) why that evidence matters. Complete any framework or practice items named on this page.

Required Notebook Work

The work described above is the required notebook submission for this lesson. Label every required component. Submit it in the separate Moodle notebook activity.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.
