3. Modeled Wellness Plan

Building and Monitoring a Lifetime Wellness Plan

Baseline: fictional student sleeps about 6 hours on school nights. Goal: average 7.5 hours for four weeks by beginning a wind-down routine at 10:00 p.m. Indicator: weekly average from a simple log. Barrier: late homework. Adaptation trigger: if average stays below 7 hours for two weeks, move the routine 20 minutes earlier and schedule homework blocks.
