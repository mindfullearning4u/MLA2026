## Assessment architecture
- Pretest: 10 questions, one attempt, gradebook weight 0%
- Lesson 1–3 quizzes: 25-question bank; 5 random released; one attempt; mastery 80%; Quiz category 30%
- Notebook: mastery 80%; Notebook category 20%
- Checkpoint: mastery 80%; Checkpoint category 20%
- Final Unit 7 assessment: 40-question bank, 8 per lesson; 10 delivered with exactly 2 per lesson; one attempt; mastery 80%; Unit Assessment category 30%
- Lessons 4–5: no lesson quiz
