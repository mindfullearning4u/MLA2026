## Assessment settings

| Activity | Attempts | Questions delivered | Mastery / grade to pass | Category | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Pretest | 1 | 10 | Diagnostic | Pretest | 0% |
| Notebook | As configured by course workflow | Submission | 80% | Notebook | 20% |
| Checkpoint | As configured by course workflow | Submission | 80% | Checkpoint | 20% |
| Lesson quiz | 1 | 5 random from the lesson's 25 | 80% | Quiz | 30% |
| Unit assessment | 1 | 10: exactly 2 random from each 8-question lesson subcategory | 80% | Unit Assessment | 30% |

Only the TOR may authorize an additional quiz or unit-assessment attempt.

## Release restrictions

- The pretest precedes Lesson 1.
- Each notebook follows its lesson and precedes its checkpoint.
- Each checkpoint precedes its lesson quiz when a quiz is required.
- A learner must achieve 80% mastery on the notebook, checkpoint, and required quiz before the next lesson releases.
- Lessons 4 and 5 have no lesson quiz; release uses notebook and checkpoint mastery.
- The Unit 7 Assessment follows mastery of the Lesson 5 notebook and checkpoint.
