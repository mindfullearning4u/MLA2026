Physical Education and Health · Unit 7 · Lesson 1

1. Overview and Vocabulary

Evaluating Health Information, Products, and Services

Essential Question

How can I use evidence, critical thinking, and responsible action to strengthen personal and community wellness?

Lesson Objectives

  • Explain and apply the lesson’s core framework.
  • Analyze evidence before reaching a conclusion.
  • Create a clear, ethical, and measurable response.

MLA Standard: MLA.PEH.CON.01 (primary); MLA.PEH.WEL.03, MLA.PEH.DIG.01, MLA.PEH.COM.02 (supporting)

Learn to judge health claims, products, services, technology, and media before acting on them.

Vocabulary

TermDefinition
health literacythe ability to find, understand, evaluate, and use health information
credible sourcea source with relevant expertise, transparent evidence, and accountable publishing practices
claima statement presented as true
evidenceinformation used to support or challenge a claim
biasa preference or interest that may shape a message
conflict of interesta relationship that could influence judgment
corroborationchecking whether independent reliable sources agree

Required Notebook Work

Write the lesson title, essential question, and every vocabulary term with its definition in your notebook. Then write one original sentence connecting two vocabulary terms.

Privacy Choice

You may use fictional scenarios or public information. Never disclose private health, family, financial, or identifying information.
