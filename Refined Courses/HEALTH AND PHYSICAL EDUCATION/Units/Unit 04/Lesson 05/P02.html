Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Read the model first. Do not choose an answer from memory alone.
  2. Name the exact health, fitness, or safety concept in the lesson.
  3. Identify the evidence that supports the decision.
  4. Explain why the safest or healthiest choice fits the standard.
