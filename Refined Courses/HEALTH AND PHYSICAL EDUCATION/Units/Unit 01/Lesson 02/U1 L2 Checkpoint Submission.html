Model direction: a strong response weighs friendship, sleep/concentration evidence, transportation limits, and available adult support. Leaving earlier, arranging verified transportation, or declining may all earn full credit when safe and justified. Collaboration is appropriate because transportation availability and risk require verification. Require revision for ignoring transportation, treating consequences as certain, using only one option, or claiming that requesting help shows failure.
