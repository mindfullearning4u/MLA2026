Body Systems During Activity

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Now apply the concept to a real student decision. A strong response does not say only what to do; it explains why the choice is appropriate, safe, realistic, and connected to the mapped standard.

  1. State the situation.
  2. Identify the health, movement, nutrition, wellness, or safety concern.
  3. Choose the evidence from the chart, checklist, label, plan, or scenario.
  4. Make the decision.
  5. Explain the likely outcome and what to monitor next.

Correct Approach

Green: The student uses evidence, names the standard concept, and chooses a realistic next step.

Common Mistake

Red: The student gives a vague answer such as "try harder" or "eat better" without explaining the specific action, evidence, or safety check.
