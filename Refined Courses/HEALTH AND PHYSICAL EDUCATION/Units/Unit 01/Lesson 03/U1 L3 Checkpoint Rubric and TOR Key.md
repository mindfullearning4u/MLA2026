# U1 L3 Checkpoint Rubric and TOR Key

20 points; mastery 16/20.

- Problem identification, 3: identifies specificity, measurement, and timeframe deficiencies.
- SMART rewrite, 6: behavior, measure, feasible/safe scope, and timeframe are explicit.
- Strengths/barriers, 3: uses profile evidence accurately.
- Strategies, 3: three actions directly address stated barriers/needs.
- Monitoring, 3: method and schedule can measure the rewritten goal.
- Adjustment reasoning, 2: states when evidence triggers change and proposes a reasonable modification.
