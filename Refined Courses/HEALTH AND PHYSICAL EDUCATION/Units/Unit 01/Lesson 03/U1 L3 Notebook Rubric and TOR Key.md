# U1 L3 Notebook Rubric and TOR Key

20 points; mastery 16/20.

- Vocabulary, 3: all required terms with accurate definitions.
- SMART analysis, 4: all five criteria evaluated using exact goal evidence.
- Barrier/solution work, 3: barriers are specific and strategies directly address them.
- Independent Goal Plan, 8: all 11 elements present; goal is safe, specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, time-bound; monitoring measures the stated goal; reasoning explains strategies and adjustment.
- Organization/identification, 2: P01, P02, P03, and P05 evidence is labeled and readable.

TOR accepts multiple safe goal domains and reasonable numerical values. Do not grade athletic ambition, body weight, appearance, or personal preference. Revision is required when the goal is unsafe, not measurable, has no timeframe, or uses monitoring that cannot measure the stated outcome.
