P04 — Guided Plan Development
Guided Profile
Maya wants a consistent bedtime routine. On three of seven school nights she began by 10:00 p.m. Late phone use is a barrier. A reminder and charging location outside the bedroom are resources. She must not change medication or treat a disorder through this task.
Notebook — complete this guided plan
Create a table with: wellness focus; baseline; SMART goal; reason; two strengths; two barriers; three strategies; safe behavior; safety consideration; monitoring method and frequency; support/resource; modification; evaluation criterion. Fill every row for Maya.
How to reason
Ask: What profile evidence supports this choice? Does the monitoring measure the goal? Is the choice realistic and safe? Example: a dated checklist of whether the routine began by 10:00 measures the target directly; total phone time alone does not prove the routine began on time.
Success Check
Include a measurable frequency and deadline, address the phone barrier, use matching evidence, and choose a safe workable modification.
