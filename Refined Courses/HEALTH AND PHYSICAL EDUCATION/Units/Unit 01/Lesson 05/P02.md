P02 — Learn the Planning Framework
Planning Cycle
GOAL → ACTION → MONITOR → EVALUATE → ADJUST
Goal: measurable change and deadline.
Action: safe realistic steps.
Monitor: evidence connected to the goal.
Evaluate: compare evidence with baseline and target.
Adjust: keep, modify, or replace a strategy for a stated reason.
Required Decisions
Select a wellness focus and baseline; establish a SMART goal; choose a behavior; identify barriers and safety concerns; choose monitoring; list steps and resources; plan a modification; and set an evaluation criterion.
Critical Analysis
If the goal is walking on four days weekly, a dated activity log measures frequency. A mood rating alone does not prove four walks occurred. Identify the measurable target first, then select evidence recording that same variable.
Notebook — required summary
Write the five-step cycle and one sentence explaining each step. Then write: A strong plan connects a measurable goal to safe actions, matching evidence, realistic support, and an adjustment rule.
