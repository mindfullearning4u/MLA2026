P03 — Modeled Integrated Plan
Fictional Student
Jordan completed two 15-minute easy walks last week. Homework varies; rain is a barrier; a safe indoor hallway is available with permission.
Worked Example
SMART goal: For 3 weeks, complete a 15-minute easy walk on 4 days weekly. This states action, duration, frequency, and deadline.
Strategies: schedule flexible time windows and prepare shoes. They address the named barriers.
Safety: check space/weather, warm up, and stop for concerning symptoms.
Monitor: record date and minutes after each walk because those data match the target.
Modification: use the safe indoor hallway during rain.
Evaluate/adjust: count completed days weekly; if fewer than four, revise time windows.
Notebook — modeled-plan table
Create a three-column table labeled Component, Jordan’s Plan, and Why It Fits. Include rows for goal, strategies, safety, monitoring, modification, and evaluation. Then explain in 2–3 sentences why a dated activity log is stronger evidence than “I felt healthier.”
