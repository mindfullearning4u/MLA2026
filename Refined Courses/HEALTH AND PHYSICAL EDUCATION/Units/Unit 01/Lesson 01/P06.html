P06 — Mastery Checkpoint

How do different dimensions of health interact to influence a person's overall wellness?

Independent Mastery Requirement

This checkpoint is separate from Notebook Evidence. Complete it independently using your own reasoning and lesson evidence. Do not submit AI-generated work as your own. Ask your Teacher of Record if you are unsure what assistance is permitted.

Checkpoint Scenario — Community Arts Program

Noor attends a free community arts program twice each week. Recently, road construction moved the nearest bus stop farther away, and the new route has little lighting after sunset. Noor has missed several sessions and feels disconnected from friends in the program. On nights Noor stays home, Noor works late on assignments and reports difficulty focusing the next morning. The program coordinator offers an earlier session, a family member can provide transportation once a week, and the program has an online group where members can share projects.

Checkpoint Task

Write 250–300 words or an equivalent structured analytical response that:

  1. identifies the three most relevant dimensions of health;
  2. explains how the dimensions interact;
  3. cites at least three specific scenario details;
  4. identifies one risk factor;
  5. identifies one protective factor;
  6. recommends two realistic actions;
  7. explains how each action could support more than one wellness dimension; and
  8. uses at least five required vocabulary terms accurately.

Do not diagnose Noor or add facts not provided in the scenario.

Success Criteria — 20 Points

  • Accurate health analysis — 6: appropriate dimensions and relationships.
  • Evidence — 4: at least three specific scenario details.
  • Reasoning — 4: explains interactions rather than listing dimensions.
  • Recommendations — 3: two realistic, appropriate actions.
  • Vocabulary — 2: at least five required terms used accurately.
  • Completion and clarity — 1: follows the required format.

Mastery threshold: 16 of 20 points (80%).

Submission Directions

  1. Review your response against all eight task requirements.
  2. Confirm that you used at least five vocabulary terms accurately.
  3. Submit only this response in U1 L1 Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Do not combine the checkpoint with your Notebook Submission.
  5. Review TOR feedback and revise if directed.
