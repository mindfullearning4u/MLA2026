New Scenario — A Changing Routine

Riley recently began a part-time after-school job. On workdays, Riley goes directly from class to the job, often forgets to bring a snack or water, and finishes homework late. Riley reports feeling tired in the morning and has stopped responding to friends' messages. The workplace supervisor offers a predictable break, and a friend suggests scheduling a short weekly check-in. Riley has access to a refill station and can prepare supplies the night before.

Required Response

  1. Identify at least three dimensions of wellness involved.
  2. Cite one specific scenario detail supporting each dimension.
  3. Explain at least two interactions among dimensions.
  4. Identify one protective factor.
  5. Identify one risk factor.
  6. Recommend one realistic health-supporting action.
  7. Explain why the recommendation is appropriate for the evidence.

Use the terms dimension, health behavior, protective factor, and risk factor accurately. You may use a structured table followed by two explanation paragraphs or a complete written response.
