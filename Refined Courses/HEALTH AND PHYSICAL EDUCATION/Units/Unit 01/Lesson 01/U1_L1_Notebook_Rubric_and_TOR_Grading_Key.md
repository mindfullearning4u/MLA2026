# U1 L1 Notebook Rubric and TOR Grading Key

Activity: U1 L1 Notebook Submission  
Maximum: 20 points  
Mastery: 16 points (80%)

## Required evidence

1. Thirteen vocabulary terms with the provided definitions.
2. Five-point P02 Quick Summary and one original wellness-interaction chain.
3. P03 Guided Practice table: copied worked Row A and completed Rows B and C.
4. P04 Transfer Check: one selected scenario, no more than four sentences.
5. P05 Independent Wellness Analysis.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Full-credit requirement | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Vocabulary | All 13 terms and provided definitions are accurate and complete. | 4 |
| Essential lesson evidence | Five accurate summary ideas and a defensible original interaction chain using cautious causal language. | 4 |

| Independent Wellness Analysis | Identifies 3+ dimensions with evidence, explains 2+ interactions, identifies one protective and one risk factor, recommends one realistic action, and justifies it. | 6 |

## TOR expected-answer guidance

### P02 interaction chain

Accept any evidence-based chain connecting at least two dimensions. It must avoid guaranteed or diagnostic claims. Example: insufficient sleep → reduced physical energy → difficulty concentrating → frustration during communication.

### P03 Row B — Online Comparison

- Likely dimensions: mental, emotional, and/or social.
- Evidence: edited fitness posts; belief that appearance proves health; use of a school resource; decision to limit the account.
- Defensible interaction: misleading media may influence beliefs or feelings; evidence-based information may improve decision quality.
- Protective factor/action: credible school resource, limiting misleading content, following evidence-based sources.

### P03 Row C — New School Routine

- Likely dimensions: social, emotional, mental, environmental.
- Evidence: misses friends, no activities, distracted in class, counselor support, interest group, quiet space.
- Defensible interaction: reduced connection may affect feelings or focus; supportive resources may increase connection and improve the learning environment.
- Protective factor/action: counselor, interest group, quiet lunchtime space, or another supported action.

### P04 Transfer Check

Accept any one of the three scenarios. Full credit requires two appropriate dimensions, one exact detail, one explained interaction, one risk/protective factor, and one realistic action. Do not require the sample wording.

### P05 Independent Wellness Analysis — Riley

- Defensible dimensions: physical (fatigue, water/snack, late work), mental (homework and morning functioning), social (not responding to friends), and environmental (job schedule, refill station, predictable break).
- Protective factors: predictable break, supportive friend, refill station, ability to prepare supplies, possible scheduling routine.
- Risk factors: skipped food/water preparation, late homework, fatigue, reduced social connection, demanding transition between school and work.
- Realistic actions may include preparing water/snack the night before, using the scheduled break, planning a homework routine, or maintaining the weekly friend check-in.
- Accept alternatives when the student cites scenario evidence and explains the reasoning.

## Revision triggers

Require revision when work omits a required component, treats health only as physical, relies on appearance, makes an unsupported diagnosis, lists dimensions without evidence or interaction, recommends an unsafe action, or is illegible.
