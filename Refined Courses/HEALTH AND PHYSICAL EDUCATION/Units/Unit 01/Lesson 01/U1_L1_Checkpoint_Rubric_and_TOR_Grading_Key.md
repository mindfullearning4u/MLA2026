# U1 L1 Mastery Checkpoint Rubric and TOR Grading Key

Activity: U1 L1 Mastery Checkpoint Submission  
Maximum: 20 points  
Mastery: 16 points (80%)

## Rubric

| Criterion | Full-credit requirement | Points |
|---|---|---:|
| Accurate health analysis | Identifies three appropriate dimensions and explains their relationships accurately. | 6 |
| Evidence | Uses at least three specific details from the Community Arts Program scenario. | 4 |
| Reasoning | Explains interactions rather than merely listing dimensions; avoids unsupported diagnosis or guaranteed claims. | 4 |
| Recommendations | Provides two realistic actions and explains how each could support more than one dimension. | 3 |
| Vocabulary | Uses at least five required vocabulary terms accurately. | 2 |
| Completion and clarity | Meets the 250–300-word or equivalent structured-response requirement and is clear and organized. | 1 |
| **Total** |  | **20** |

## TOR expected-answer guidance

### Strong-response elements

- Most relevant dimensions may include environmental, social, mental, emotional, and physical. Any three are acceptable when supported.
- Evidence may include the relocated bus stop, poor lighting, missed sessions, disconnection from friends, late assignment work, difficulty focusing, earlier session, family transportation, and online project group.
- Risk factors may include unsafe transportation conditions, reduced social connection, missed program participation, late work, or reduced focus.
- Protective factors may include the earlier session, family transportation, program coordinator, online group, or continued access to the arts program.
- Realistic actions may include attending the earlier session, using family transportation once weekly, participating in the online group, planning assignment time, or combining available options.

### Model reasoning, not required wording

The construction and poor lighting are environmental factors that affect safe access to the program. Missing sessions may reduce Noor's social connection, while late assignment work may affect sleep or concentration. Attending the earlier session could reduce the environmental safety risk while restoring social participation. Using the online group on other days could maintain connection and provide a manageable alternative when transportation is unavailable.

Accept different conclusions when the student cites evidence, uses cautious language, and explains how the dimensions interact.

## Revision triggers

Require revision when the response treats health only as physical, lists dimensions without interaction, uses fewer than three scenario details, makes an unsupported diagnosis, omits the risk/protective factors or actions, recommends an unsafe response, or uses fewer than five vocabulary terms.
