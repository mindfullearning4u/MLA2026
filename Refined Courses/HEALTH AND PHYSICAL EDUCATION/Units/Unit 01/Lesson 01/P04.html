P04 — Supported Practice

Classify evidence, explain interactions, and select a supported action.

This is guided practice—not Independent Work and not a separate assignment. Work through all three scenarios. In your notebook, record only the brief Transfer Check at the end. Page 5 contains the substantial independent response.

Scenario 1 — Schedule Pressure

Elena adds several activities to an already busy week. She skips her usual lunch break, stays online late, and has difficulty concentrating the next morning. A trusted adult helps her decide which commitments can be rescheduled.

Watch the conclusion being built

  1. Evidence: late schedule and difficulty concentrating.
  2. Classification: sleep or recovery is physical; concentration is mental.
  3. Connection: Elena's late schedule may reduce recovery, which could make concentration more difficult.
  4. Claim check: May and could avoid claiming a diagnosis or proven cause.
  5. Protection: the trusted adult is a resource, and rescheduling is a realistic action.

No written response is required. Explain the reasoning to yourself before continuing.

Scenario 2 — Unsafe Route

Dev wants to walk after sunset, but the route has poor lighting and no sidewalk. Dev chooses an indoor movement option and asks a family member to help identify a safer daylight route.

Think through the evidence: Poor lighting and no sidewalk are environmental hazards. The indoor option preserves the physical-health behavior while reducing risk. Asking for help uses an available protective resource.

No written response is required. Identify the environmental and physical interaction before continuing.

Scenario 3 — Team Communication

During an online project, Kai misunderstands a short message and responds angrily. After a break, Kai rereads the thread, asks what the classmate meant, and suggests a video meeting. The group then divides the work more clearly.

Think through the sequence: initial misunderstanding → emotional response → pause and clarification → improved communication. The pause, question, and meeting are protective strategies that may support mental, emotional, and social dimensions.

No written response is required. Check that your conclusion uses only scenario evidence and does not diagnose Kai.
