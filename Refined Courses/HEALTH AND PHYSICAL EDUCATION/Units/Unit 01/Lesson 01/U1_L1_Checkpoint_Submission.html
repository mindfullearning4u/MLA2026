Unit 1 Lesson 1

Mastery Checkpoint Submission

What to Submit

Submit only your P06 Community Arts Program analysis: 250–300 words or an equivalent structured response.

Your response must identify three dimensions, explain their interactions, cite three scenario details, identify one risk and one protective factor, recommend two realistic actions, explain how each action supports multiple dimensions, and use at least five required vocabulary terms accurately.

Do not attach unrelated Notebook Evidence.

Scoring — 20 Points

  • Accurate health analysis — 6
  • Evidence — 4
  • Reasoning — 4
  • Recommendations — 3
  • Vocabulary — 2
  • Completion and clarity — 1

Mastery threshold: 16 of 20 points (80%). Revision is required when the response treats health only as physical, makes an unsupported diagnosis, lacks evidence, lists dimensions without interaction, or recommends an unsafe action.

Academic Integrity

Use your own reasoning and lesson evidence. Do not submit AI-generated work as your own. Ask your Teacher of Record when unsure what assistance is allowed.
