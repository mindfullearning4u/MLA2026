Analyze the Activity / Guided Application

Analyze the activity

Fictional record: Eli planned 12 minutes of easy movement indoors. The first area contained a loose rug, so Eli moved to a clear room. Eli wore secure shoes, warmed up gradually, kept an easy pace, stopped after mild dizziness appeared, sat safely, told an adult, and did not resume.

How the conclusion is derived
Moving rooms was a MODIFY decision because it removed an environmental hazard.
Stopping after dizziness prioritized a warning sign over completing planned time.
Telling an adult was appropriate because the symptom required support rather than self-diagnosis.
Not resuming prevented an unsupported “push through” decision.
Supported Practice — No Additional Notebook Task

Read the record and orally identify preparation, environmental check, modification, stop condition, help-seeking, and recovery. Do not write or submit this page. P04 is your required physical-application record; independent written work begins on P06.
