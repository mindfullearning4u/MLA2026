Physical Education and Health

Unit 1 · Lesson 4 · Page 2

Safe Independent Physical Activity and Risk Reduction

Learn the Concept

Safety begins before movement
Prepare: choose appropriate clothing, footwear, space, and equipment.
Check the environment: surface, traffic, weather, heat, air quality, lighting, obstacles, and available help.
Warm up gradually: begin with easy movement appropriate to the planned activity.
Participate at an appropriate intensity: this lesson requires only easy, low-risk movement.
Monitor: notice conditions and warning signs without attempting diagnosis.
Modify or stop: reduce intensity, change location/activity, or stop when safety requires.
Cool down and recover: transition gradually and record observations.
CONTINUE / MODIFY / STOP / SEEK HELP

CONTINUE when conditions and response remain appropriate. MODIFY when a reasonable change can reduce manageable risk. STOP when continuing is unsafe. SEEK APPROPRIATE HELP when symptoms or circumstances exceed ordinary self-management.

Correct the misconceptions
Pain does not prove improvement.
Sweating does not by itself prove effectiveness.
Warm-up and cool-down are safety procedures, not optional decoration.
Motivation does not make unsafe weather safe.
Modification is responsible decision-making, not failure.
Required Notebook Task — P02 Safety Notes

Write the seven-step safety sequence, the four decision classifications with meanings, and the five misconception corrections. Do not copy the explanatory paragraphs.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record if you cannot distinguish MODIFIABLE risk from a condition that requires stopping or help.
