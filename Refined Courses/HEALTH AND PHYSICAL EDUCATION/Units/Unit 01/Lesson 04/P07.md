Mastery Checkpoint

Independent Mastery Checkpoint

Fictional profile: Sam plans a short independent walk after school. The forecast is hot, one familiar route has heavy traffic, Sam has water and secure shoes, an indoor corridor is available, and Sam knows to contact an adult if a serious or unusual warning sign occurs.

Exactly What to Submit in the Separate Checkpoint Activity

Create a numbered 1–12 safe activity plan:

activity selection;
location;
environmental check;
clothing/equipment;
preparation;
warm-up;
appropriate intensity;
hydration consideration;
modification;
stop conditions;
cool-down;
conditions requiring outside assistance.

Explain each safety choice with profile evidence. No actual strenuous exercise is required. Submit only in the Checkpoint activity. Mastery is 16/20.

Need Help?

The TOR may clarify directions but cannot supply your safety classifications or plan.
