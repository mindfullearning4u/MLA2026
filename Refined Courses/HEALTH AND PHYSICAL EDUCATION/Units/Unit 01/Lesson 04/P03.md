Safe Independent Physical Activity and Risk Reduction

Examples and Guided Practice

Worked safety reasoning

Scenario: A student planned an easy walk, but the route has standing water, poor lighting, and fast traffic.

Evidence: three environmental hazards are stated. Classification: MODIFY, because the student can choose a dry, well-lit indoor route. Derivation: the desired activity is low risk, but the planned location is not; changing location directly removes the stated hazards. If no safe alternative is available, STOP.

Evidence	Decision	Reason	Safe action
Loose object on floor	MODIFY	Removable space hazard	Clear area before movement
Required Notebook Task — P03 Guided Practice

Copy the four table headings and the worked row. Add rows for: excessive outdoor heat with no shade; shoes with an untied lace; sudden severe chest pain. For each, identify exact evidence, classify, explain why, and state the safe action. Expected reasoning: unsafe heat may require location/time modification or stopping; tie the lace before continuing; severe chest pain requires stopping and appropriate urgent help.

Need Help?

Use the safest defensible classification. When serious warning signs appear, do not propose pushing through.
