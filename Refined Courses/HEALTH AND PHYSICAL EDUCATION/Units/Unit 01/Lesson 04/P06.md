Required Notebook Task — P06 Scenario Analysis

For each scenario, write the classification (CONTINUE, MODIFY, STOP, or SEEK APPROPRIATE HELP), identify the risk, explain the decision, and propose a modification where appropriate.

A walking path is dry and clear; the student has appropriate shoes and feels as expected.
A resistance band has a visible tear before use.
A student develops sudden severe difficulty breathing during activity.
A planned movement requires more clear space than the room provides.
A beginner activity is too difficult to perform with control, but an easier low-impact version is available.

Success check: Your response must prioritize safety, use exact evidence, avoid diagnosis, and never recommend pushing through serious warning signs.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record for clarification of a scenario, but complete the classifications and reasoning independently.
