Safe Independent Physical Activity and Risk Reduction

Apply the Skill / Practice the Concept

Short Safe Physical Application

Choose one approved low-risk option: easy walking in a safe area, gentle mobility, light low-impact movement, or a TOR-approved equivalent. This is procedural practice, not fitness improvement.

Stop rule: Do not begin if the environment or equipment is unsafe. Stop if you experience a serious or unusual warning sign, and seek appropriate assistance when warranted. The course does not diagnose symptoms.
Inspect location, surface, obstacles, weather, lighting, and access to help.
Check clothing/equipment.
Begin with a gradual warm-up.
Complete only a short, easy activity at comfortable intensity.
Modify or stop if conditions change.
Cool down gradually and recover.
Required Notebook Task — P04 Safety Record

Record: activity selected; location/environment; completed safety check; preparation; approximate duration; whether modification was needed and why; recovery/cool-down; and one non-sensitive observation. A safer alternative earns full credit. Do not record heart rate, weight, diagnosis, or maximal performance.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record before beginning if you need an accessible alternative or cannot identify a safe low-risk option.
