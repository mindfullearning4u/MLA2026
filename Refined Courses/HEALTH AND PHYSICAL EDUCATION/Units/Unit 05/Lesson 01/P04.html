Apply the Risk and Help-Level Process

This supported practice prepares you for the independent notebook assignment.

  1. A loose cord crosses a busy walkway.
  2. A person has a small scrape and remains alert.
  3. A person has severe breathing difficulty after possible allergen exposure.
  4. A crash scene includes traffic and leaking fuel.

For each: identify the hazard, estimate the risk, name a prevention strategy, classify the help level, cite decisive evidence, and state one unsafe action to avoid.

Required Notebook Task

Create a four-row table with columns: Hazard, Risk, Prevention, Evidence, Help Level, Safe Action, Action to Avoid. Complete all seven columns for each situation.

TOR Help

Your TOR may review one completed row before you finish the remaining rows.
