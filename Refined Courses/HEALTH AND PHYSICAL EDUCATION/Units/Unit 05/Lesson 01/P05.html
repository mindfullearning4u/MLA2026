Six Fictional Safety Scenarios

  1. Distracted-driving risk.
  2. Minor activity injury.
  3. Severe breathing difficulty.
  4. Serious heat-related concern.
  5. Suspected poisoning.
  6. Unsafe accident scene.

For each scenario, answer all nine prompts: identify the hazard; potential risk; one prevention strategy; help level; scenario evidence; immediate action; what must not be done; whether emergency services are required; and why.

Then write 150–200 words: Why is knowing when not to manage a health emergency alone an important safety skill?

Submission Check

Your notebook must contain six labeled nine-part analyses and the synthesis. Never recommend entering danger, delaying emergency help, using a home poisoning remedy, or exceeding training.

TOR Help

Your TOR may clarify directions, but the analyses and synthesis must be completed independently.
