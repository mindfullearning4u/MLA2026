Mastery Checkpoint — Checkpoint Submission

Submit the timeline/decision table and 275–325 word explanation described below.

Scenario: During an unsupervised recreational activity in high heat, a participant trips near unsafe equipment. The initial scrape appears minor, but the participant later becomes confused, has difficulty breathing, and cannot stand. A bystander suggests giving an unknown medicine and moving the person through the hazardous area without calling for help.

  1. Identify the original hazard.
  2. Name a prevention strategy.
  3. Classify the initial situation.
  4. Cite evidence that it becomes more serious.
  5. State when professional help becomes necessary.
  6. State when emergency response becomes necessary.
  7. Give the correct response sequence.
  8. Identify the unsafe suggestion.
  9. Explain the scene-safety concern.
  10. Explain why a student must not exceed training.
  11. Use at least seven vocabulary terms accurately.

Submission Requirement

Submit one labeled timeline/decision table plus the 275–325 word explanation in the Lesson 1 Checkpoint activity. Mastery is 80%.

TOR Help

Your TOR may clarify the format but may not provide the safety decisions. For a real emergency, use emergency services immediately.
