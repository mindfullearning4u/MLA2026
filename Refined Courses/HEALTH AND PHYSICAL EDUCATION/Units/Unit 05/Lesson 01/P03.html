Modeled Safety Decisions and Guided Practice

Worked Example

Scenario: During activity in high heat, a participant becomes confused, has difficulty standing, and stops responding normally.

StepReasoning
Hazard/preventionHeat and inadequate hydration are hazards; planning breaks, fluids, shade, and supervision reduces risk.
EvidenceConfusion, inability to stand, and abnormal responsiveness show serious worsening.
Conclusion/actionBecause immediate serious warning signs are present, this is an emergency: ensure scene safety, call emergency services, follow dispatcher directions, and do not exceed training.

Guided Practice

Analyze a minor ankle injury that becomes increasingly painful and cannot bear weight. Explain why the initial situation may be non-emergency but later evidence supports professional evaluation.

Required Notebook Task

Copy the worked-example table. Add a second three-row analysis for the ankle scenario. Your final sentence must follow: Because [specific evidence], the appropriate help level is [level], so [response].

TOR Help

Your TOR may help distinguish evidence from assumptions. You must independently write the conclusion.
