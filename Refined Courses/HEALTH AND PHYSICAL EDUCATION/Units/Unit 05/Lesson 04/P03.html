Page 3 — Modeled Risk and Prevention Analysis

Worked Example

Claim: “If a person has no symptoms, they cannot have an STI.”

Step 1 — Identify the evidence: No symptoms are reported.

Step 2 — Identify the missing fact: Some STIs may be asymptomatic.

Step 3 — Test the conclusion: No symptoms cannot prove no infection.

Step 4 — Reach a safe conclusion: Possible exposure is evaluated through appropriate professional testing, not appearance or self-diagnosis.
