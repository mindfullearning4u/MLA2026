# U5 L4 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

## Notebook Evidence

Students submit vocabulary, reproductive-health notes, the pregnancy/STI comparison, prevention/testing analysis, guided decision work, four independent scenarios, and the reflection. Native rubric: **U5 L4 Reproductive Health Notebook Rubric** (area 4172). Mastery: 80%.

## Checkpoint

Students submit the Page 6 eight-row decision/evidence table and 300–350 word, 12-part analysis using at least eight vocabulary terms. Native rubric: **U5 L4 Reproductive Health Checkpoint Rubric** (area 4173). Mastery: 80%.

## TOR expected evidence

- Maintained abstinence avoids pregnancy from intercourse and sexual transmission from the avoided exposure.
- Pregnancy risk and STI risk are distinct; not every contraceptive method addresses STI risk.
- Some STIs may be asymptomatic; appearance does not determine status.
- Possible exposure requires appropriate professional testing, not self-diagnosis.
- Consent is voluntary; pressure or coercion invalidates healthy consent.
- A clear refusal can be repeated, followed by disengagement and trusted support.
- Sources should be evidence-based and qualified; stigma and moral judgment are unacceptable.
