# Unit 5 Assessment Configuration

## Pretest

- Activity: U5 PRETEST
- Bank: 10 questions
- Delivery: 10 questions
- Attempts: 1
- Gradebook weight: 0%

## Lesson Quizzes

- Lessons 1–5 include lesson quizzes as required by the unit map.
- Each quiz uses its lesson question bank, releases 5 random questions, allows 1 attempt, and requires 80% mastery.
- Lesson 6 has no regular lesson quiz. Its unused quiz shell is hidden from students and excluded from grading.

## Final Assessment

- Activity: U5 Final Assessment
- Bank: 40 questions distributed across all six lessons
- Delivery: 10 random questions
- Attempts: 1
- Mastery: 80%
- Release: Unit 5 Lesson 6 Checkpoint Submission must be completed and passed.

## Gradebook

- Notebook: 20%
- Checkpoint: 20%
- Quiz: 30%
- Unit Assessment: 30%
