# U5 L3 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

## Notebook Evidence Submission

Students submit the vocabulary, lesson summary, risk-analysis table, prevention and screening notes, four fictional profile analyses, and the 150–200 word reflection from Pages 1–5.

The native Moodle rubric is **U5 L3 Chronic Disease Notebook Rubric** (area 4170). It scores required evidence, risk-factor analysis and prevention reasoning, and clarity/vocabulary/organization. Mastery is 80%.

### TOR expected evidence

- Correctly distinguishes chronic/noncommunicable disease from communicable disease.
- Separates modifiable factors from nonmodifiable factors such as heredity and family history.
- Explains that a risk factor changes probability but does not prove a diagnosis.
- Connects primary prevention to lowering risk and secondary prevention to screening/early detection.
- Uses neutral, non-blaming language and refers diagnosis to a qualified healthcare provider.

## Checkpoint Submission

Students submit one document containing a risk matrix and a 300–350 word analysis addressing all 12 Page 6 requirements with at least eight vocabulary terms.

The native Moodle rubric is **U5 L3 Chronic Disease Checkpoint Rubric** (area 4171). It scores the matrix/12-part response, evidence-based chronic-disease reasoning, and vocabulary/clarity/submission quality. Mastery is 80%.

### TOR expected evidence

The response should identify and classify the family-history, environmental, behavioral, protective, screening, and access evidence; distinguish supported inferences from unsupported diagnosis; recommend appropriate primary and secondary prevention; explain when professional care is appropriate; and avoid blame.
