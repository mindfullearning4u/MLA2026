Learn Chronic Disease and Risk

Risk is a web, not a single cause. Heredity may change probability but does not determine outcome. Behavior, environment, access, age, and protective factors interact.

Primary preventionReduce risk before disease develops: activity, nutrition, avoiding tobacco, safer environments.
Secondary preventionScreen or detect concerns early and obtain professional follow-up.

A screening result indicates possible risk; it is not a diagnosis. A qualified provider interprets evidence and determines next steps.
