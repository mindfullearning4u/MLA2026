Mastery Checkpoint — Checkpoint Submission

Submit a transmission map plus a 300–350 word analysis.

Scenario: Several fictional community members attend the same indoor event. Some share food and objects. Over the next week, different people report symptoms; one tests positive, while others have only possible exposure. Public-health staff recommend ventilation, hygiene, testing for indicated people, and staying home when ill.

  1. Identify the likely communicable-disease concern and probable route from evidence.
  2. Distinguish exposure from confirmed infection and state what cannot be concluded.
  3. Give three personal and three community strategies.
  4. Explain when testing/professional evaluation is appropriate.
  5. Explain detection’s role and one treatment limitation.
  6. Correct an antibiotic or diagnosis misconception.
  7. Explain why stigma is inappropriate.
  8. Use at least eight vocabulary terms accurately.

Submission Requirement

Submit the map and 300–350 word analysis in the Lesson 2 Checkpoint activity. Mastery is 80%.
