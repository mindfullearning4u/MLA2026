Independent Work — Notebook Submission

This entire page is the required Lesson 2 Notebook Submission.

Analyze four fictional scenarios: respiratory transmission, foodborne illness, vector-borne disease, and contact-based infection.

For each, answer nine prompts: likely route; two prevention strategies; one individual strategy; one community strategy; exposure versus infection; evidence needed before diagnosis; when testing/professional support is appropriate; one misconception; and its correction.

Then write 150–200 words explaining why individual behavior and public-health strategies work best together.

Submission Check

Submit four labeled nine-part analyses and the synthesis. Do not diagnose fictional people or include personal medical information.
