Apply the Transmission-Interruption Process

  1. Respiratory droplets in a crowded poorly ventilated room.
  2. Improperly stored food served to a group.
  3. Mosquito exposure in an area with vector-borne disease.
  4. Shared athletic equipment and uncovered skin infection.

For each, identify the likely route, evidence, two personal strategies, two community strategies, and one conclusion that cannot yet be made.

Required Notebook Task

Create a four-row table with columns: Route, Evidence, Personal Prevention, Community Prevention, Limit on Conclusion. Complete every column.

TOR Help

Ask your TOR to check one row before completing the others.
