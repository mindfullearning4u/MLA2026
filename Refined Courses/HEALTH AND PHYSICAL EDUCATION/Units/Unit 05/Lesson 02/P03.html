Modeled Transmission and Prevention Analysis

Scenario: Several students share drinks. One later develops symptoms and receives professional testing.

QuestionReasoning
Possible routeShared drink containers create an indirect-contact/saliva exposure opportunity.
What is known?Possible exposure and later symptoms; infection is not confirmed for every student.
PreventionDo not share drinks; clean shared items; follow qualified testing and public-health guidance.

Guided Practice

Analyze a fictional foodborne event: identify source/route, two prevention strategies, what evidence supports possible exposure, and what cannot be concluded without testing.

Required Notebook Task
