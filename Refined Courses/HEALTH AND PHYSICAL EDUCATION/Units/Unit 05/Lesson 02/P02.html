Learn Communicable Disease and Transmission

Chain of Transmission

Pathogen/source → route → exposure → susceptible host. Prevention breaks one or more links. Hand hygiene and surface practices can interrupt contact spread; ventilation and respiratory precautions reduce some respiratory spread; safe food practices reduce foodborne spread; vector control and protective clothing reduce some vector-borne risk; vaccination can reduce susceptibility or disease impact.

Exposure is not confirmed infection. Symptoms alone are not a diagnosis. Testing and qualified evaluation may be required. Antibiotics treat certain bacterial infections—not viruses—and must be used only as directed.

Required Notebook Summary

Draw the four-link transmission chain. Under it, write one personal and one community strategy that could break each link. Then write 4–6 sentences distinguishing exposure, infection, symptoms, testing, and diagnosis.
