Page 4 — Apply the Resource-Selection Process

Six-step process

  1. Identify the health-service need without diagnosing.
  2. Classify urgency: routine/preventive, prompt non-emergency, or emergency.
  3. Match provider/service to the need.
  4. Identify access barriers and realistic strategies.
  5. Check credentials, evidence, scope, transparency, safety, and claims.
  6. Explain why the selected option fits better than alternatives.

Credibility red flags: guaranteed cure, secret treatment, no credentials, immediate payment pressure, impossible broad claims, hidden risks, or no transparent contact/service information.

Required Notebook Task

Apply all six steps to three fictional needs: a routine vaccination question; a timely mental-health support need without immediate danger; and severe breathing difficulty. For each, name one inappropriate alternative and explain why. The emergency scenario must prioritize emergency assistance without delay.

TOR Help: Do not let cost or appointment concerns delay the fictional emergency response.
