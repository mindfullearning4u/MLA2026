Page 2 — Learn Healthcare Types and Preventive Services

NeedTypical service levelReasoning
Checkup, vaccination, routine screeningPreventive/routine primary care or appropriate public-health serviceScheduled prevention or early detection
Prompt non-emergency concernPrimary care, appropriate urgent care, or qualified specialty serviceTimely evaluation without clear immediate danger
Severe breathing difficulty or immediate serious dangerEmergency services/emergency departmentEmergency response supersedes cost, scheduling, and routine navigation

Screening → Diagnosis → Treatment

Screening identifies a possible concern. Diagnosis determines a condition through professional evaluation. Treatment addresses an identified health need. A screening result is not automatically a diagnosis.
