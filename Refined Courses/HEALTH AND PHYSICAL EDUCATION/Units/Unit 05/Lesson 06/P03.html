Page 3 — Modeled Healthcare-Navigation Decisions

Worked Example

Scenario: A fictional family needs a routine blood-pressure screening but lacks transportation to its usual clinic. Options include a qualified nearby community health center, appropriate telehealth guidance, and a website selling a “guaranteed cure” with no credentials.

  1. Need: preventive screening, not diagnosis.
  2. Urgency: routine unless new emergency evidence appears.
  3. Provider fit: a qualified community service can perform or coordinate screening.
  4. Barrier: transportation; a closer qualified service may be realistic.
  5. Credibility: verified credentials, transparent scope, evidence, and realistic claims support trust. The guaranteed-cure site fails these checks.
  6. Conclusion: choose the qualified accessible service and follow professional guidance; do not self-diagnose.
