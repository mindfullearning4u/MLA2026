# U5 L6 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

Notebook rubric: **U5 L6 Healthcare Navigation Notebook Rubric** (area 4176). Checkpoint rubric: **U5 L6 Healthcare Navigation Checkpoint Rubric** (area 4177). Mastery: 80%.

TOR full-mastery evidence: accurate urgency classification; defensible provider/service selection; screening/testing/diagnosis/treatment distinctions; access barriers treated without blame; realistic strategies; credentials/evidence/scope/transparency checks; rejection of guaranteed-cure claims; and immediate emergency response when serious danger is present. Alternative provider choices may earn credit when scenario evidence supports them.
