Page 6 — Mastery Checkpoint

New fictional timeline

Stage 1: A contact presents a believable opportunity. Stage 2: The contact asks increasingly personal questions and requests location data. Stage 3: The contact demands secrecy. Stage 4: The contact tries to isolate and control communication. Stage 5: After a boundary is asserted, the contact makes a direct threat. A trusted adult is available, but a friend suggests continuing contact to collect proof.

Exactly What to Submit

Submit one checkpoint document, separate from Page 5.

Part A: A five-stage timeline naming evidence, concern level, and safest response at each stage.

Part B: A 300–350 word justification addressing all 12 requirements:

  1. first point of concern;
  2. at least five warning signs;
  3. force/fraud/coercion where supported;
  4. personal-security concerns;
  5. concern versus proof;
  6. why investigation is unsafe;
  7. disengagement response;
  8. help/resource level;
  9. point requiring emergency response;
  10. supportive friend response;
  11. one victim-blaming statement and correction;
  12. at least eight vocabulary terms.
TOR Help: Mastery is 80%. Immediate danger must receive emergency escalation, not ordinary follow-up.
