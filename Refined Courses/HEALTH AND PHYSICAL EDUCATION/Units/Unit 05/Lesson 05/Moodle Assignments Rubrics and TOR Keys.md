# U5 L5 Moodle Assignments, Rubrics, and TOR Keys

Notebook rubric: **U5 L5 Exploitation Prevention Notebook Rubric** (area 4174). Checkpoint rubric: **U5 L5 Exploitation Prevention Checkpoint Rubric** (area 4175). Both require 80% mastery.

TOR full-mastery evidence: scenario-based warning signs; concern distinguished from proof; accurate force/fraud/coercion concepts; privacy and boundary protection; disengagement; trusted help; emergency escalation after a direct threat; no personal investigation, confrontation, stereotypes, or victim blaming. The notebook includes all Page 1–5 evidence. The checkpoint includes the Page 6 timeline and 300–350 word 12-part justification using at least eight terms.
