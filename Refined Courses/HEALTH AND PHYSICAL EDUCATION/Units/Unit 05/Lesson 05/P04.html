Page 4 — Apply the Recognize/Respond Process

Safety pathway

RECOGNIZE CONCERN → PROTECT PERSONAL SAFETY → DISENGAGE → TRUSTED HELP → REPORT OR EMERGENCY RESPONSE AS APPROPRIATE

Fictional scenario: A friend says someone monitors their messages, controls transportation, discourages contact with family, and has made threats. Another friend suggests messaging the person to investigate.

Evidence: monitoring, isolation, transportation control, and threats form an escalating pattern.

Conclusion: The pattern warrants safety help; it does not require the student to determine a legal label.

Safe response: listen without blame, do not promise secrecy, do not confront or investigate, involve trusted professional/adult support, and use emergency help if danger is immediate.

Required Notebook Task

Create a five-step response plan for the fictional friend. For each step, explain why it reduces risk. Include one sentence correcting the victim-blaming claim “They should have left sooner.”

TOR Help: The plan must put safety before evidence collection.
