Page 3 — Modeled Warning-Sign Analysis

Fictional offer: An unknown contact offers a high-paying travel job, will not identify the business, demands secrecy, asks for location information, and becomes angry when questions are asked.

How the conclusion is derived

  1. List evidence: unverifiable business, travel alone, secrecy, location request, anger at questions.
  2. Classify evidence: fraud concern, isolation risk, privacy risk, and boundary pressure.
  3. Limit the claim: these signs raise serious concern but do not let a student prove a legal classification.
  4. Select action: do not proceed or meet; stop sharing information, disengage/block/report, and involve a trusted adult.
  5. Escalate if needed: a direct threat or immediate danger requires emergency assistance.

Required Notebook Table

Create four columns: Evidence; Warning category; What it supports; Safe response. Complete one row for each of the five clues above. Beneath the table, write two sentences explaining why continuing contact to collect proof would increase risk.

TOR Help: Reason from the fictional evidence and avoid stereotypes.
