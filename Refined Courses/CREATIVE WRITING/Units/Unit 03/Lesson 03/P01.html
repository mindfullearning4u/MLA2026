Lesson Overview: Responsible Source Adaptation

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.ADP.01 — adapt mythic, classical, or religious source material through purposeful transformation and responsible attribution.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish retelling, adaptation, allusion, and appropriation.
  • Identify a source’s structural DNA: conflict, roles, pattern, and consequence.
  • Transform context, viewpoint, stakes, and meaning rather than merely rename characters.
  • Research cultural or sacred context and state ethical limits.

Why This Matters

Source stories carry histories and living meanings. Strong adaptation enters a conversation with the source: it identifies what must remain recognizable, what will change, and why the new context produces a distinct insight.
