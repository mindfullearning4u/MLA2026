Notebook Title

Lesson 3: Adaptation of Mythic, Classical, or Religious Sources

Vocabulary

  • Adaptation: A new creative work that transforms elements of an earlier source.
  • Source material: The original story, myth, classical text, religious narrative, or tradition being adapted.
  • Source element: A character type, conflict, theme, symbol, structure, image, or setting pattern from the source.
  • Transform: To change a source element for a new purpose, audience, setting, or point of view.
  • Respectful adaptation: Adaptation that treats the source carefully and avoids mocking, stereotyping, or distorting sacred or cultural meaning.
  • Rationale: An explanation of why the writer made a craft choice.

Step 1: Identify the Source Element

Start by asking what part of the source you are adapting. Do not try to adapt everything. Choose one clear element.

Possible source elements: a journey away from home, a forbidden choice, a test of loyalty, a trickster figure, a guide, a symbolic object, a fall caused by pride, or a return with new wisdom.

Planning sentence: I will adapt the source element of a character receiving a warning before making a risky choice.

Step 2: Decide What to Preserve and What to Change

Adaptation works because something remains recognizable and something changes. If nothing changes, the writing is too close to copying. If nothing remains, the connection to the source disappears.

Preserve: A character is warned before making a risky choice.

Transform: Instead of a palace or ancient road, the setting becomes a school robotics lab. Instead of a magical warning, the warning comes from a teammate who notices a flaw in the code.

Step 3: Write a Craft Rationale

A rationale explains your adaptation choice. It should name the source element, name the transformation, and explain the effect.

Model rationale: I preserved the source pattern of a warning before a risky choice, but I transformed the setting into a robotics lab. This makes the old conflict feel modern and shows how pride can lead a character to ignore useful advice.

Notebook Task Part 1

Part A: Copy the vocabulary words and definitions.

Part B: Choose one source element from the list on this page.

Part C: Write what you would preserve, what you would transform, and why.
