Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

source textThe earlier work, tradition, or account an adaptation engages.
adaptationA new work that purposefully transforms recognizable elements of a source.
retellingA new presentation that largely preserves the source’s central sequence and meaning.
allusionA brief, indirect reference that depends on readers recognizing another work or tradition.
structural DNAThe source’s core pattern of roles, conflict, turning point, and consequence.
transformationA meaningful change in context, viewpoint, stakes, genre, structure, or theme.
intertextualityThe way a work gains meaning through relationships with other texts.
attributionClear acknowledgment of the source or tradition being used.
cultural contextThe histories, beliefs, practices, and power relationships shaping a source.
sacred narrativeA story held as spiritually authoritative or significant within a living tradition.
appropriationUse of cultural material in a way that removes context, exploits unequal power, or causes harm.
ethical boundaryA deliberate limit established to avoid distortion, disrespect, or exploitation.
