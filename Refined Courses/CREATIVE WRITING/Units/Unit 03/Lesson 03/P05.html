Independent Work: Adaptation of Mythic, Classical, or Religious Sources

Purpose

Transform a source pattern into an original work while preserving a meaningful connection.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Your work should show deliberate choices, not just completion.

Source for Your Work

Source pattern: A gifted figure receives a warning, ignores a boundary, and faces an unexpected consequence. Adaptation setting: a student programmer creates an artificial assistant that begins making decisions on its own.

1. Plan Before You Draft

DecisionYour Plan
Character goal and obstacleWrite one clear sentence.
Key turning pointState the choice or discovery.
Meaning readers should inferName the idea without writing a moral.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach would produce stronger creative writing? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Retell the old story with new character names but keep the same setting, events, and ending.
B. Keep the warning–boundary–consequence pattern, but change the setting, motivation, conflict, and outcome to create a new meaning.

3. Apply the Lesson

Draft a 300–400 word adaptation using the source pattern above. Establish a modern setting, give the protagonist a distinct motivation, and change at least two major story elements so the work is original.

4. Revise with Purpose

Identify the inherited source element and revise one choice so it creates a new theme or perspective rather than merely copying the source.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your creative work applies the lesson skill and uses the source provided.
  • Your explanation names a specific choice and its effect.
  • Your revision improves meaning or craft, not only spelling.
