Independent Work: Universal Themes in Creative Work

Purpose

Create a character choice that develops a universal theme without stating the theme directly.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Your work should show deliberate choices, not just completion.

Source for Your Work

At the edge of a crowded station, Mara finds a wallet containing enough money to solve a problem at home. No one saw her pick it up. On the identification card, the owner is smiling beside a child.

1. Plan Before You Draft

DecisionYour Plan
Character goal and obstacleWrite one clear sentence.
Key turning pointState the choice or discovery.
Meaning readers should inferName the idea without writing a moral.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach would produce stronger creative writing? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Mara is good, so she immediately turns in the wallet. The theme is honesty.
B. Mara closes her hand around the wallet, pictures the overdue bill on the kitchen table, and then studies the child in the photograph before walking toward the station office.

3. Apply the Lesson

Continue the scene for 250–350 words. Use Mara’s decision, its consequence, and at least two concrete details to develop a universal theme such as integrity, sacrifice, or responsibility.

4. Revise with Purpose

Revise one paragraph so the theme emerges more clearly through action, image, or consequence—not through a direct moral statement.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your creative work applies the lesson skill and uses the source provided.
  • Your explanation names a specific choice and its effect.
  • Your revision improves meaning or craft, not only spelling.
