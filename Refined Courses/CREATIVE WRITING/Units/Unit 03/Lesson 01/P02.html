Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
subjectA broad area a work explores, such as belonging or responsibility.The subject of the story is friendship.
themeA developed insight about human experience expressed through patterns and consequences.The story develops the theme that trust must be earned through honest choices.
thematic statementA complete, arguable sentence expressing a work’s theme.“Courage grows when people act despite fear” is a complete thematic statement.
universal themeA theme relevant across many contexts without pretending all experiences are identical.The desire to belong can function as a universal theme across cultures.
moralA direct lesson or rule about behavior, often stated as advice.“Always tell the truth” is a moral stated as a lesson.
motifA repeated image, phrase, object, or situation that gathers meaning.Repeated images of open doors form a motif of opportunity.
archetypeA recurring character, situation, or pattern recognizable across many traditions; not a rigid stereotype.The mentor archetype appears in the experienced neighbor who guides the protagonist.
cultural specificityConcrete practices, histories, language, places, and values rooted in a particular context.The festival’s foods, music, and customs give the story cultural specificity.
qualificationA condition or limit that makes a claim precise rather than absolute.The phrase “in some circumstances” adds a qualification to the claim.
clichéAn overused expression or idea that has lost precision and force.“Follow your heart” becomes a cliché when the story does not make the advice specific.
contradictionA meaningful tension between claims, actions, values, or outcomes.A character who values honesty but lies to protect a friend creates a contradiction.
resonanceThe extended emotional or intellectual significance a detail gathers.The final image echoes the opening and gives the theme emotional resonance.
