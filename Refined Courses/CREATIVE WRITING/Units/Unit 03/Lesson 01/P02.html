Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and complete definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

TermDefinition
subjectA broad area a work explores, such as belonging or responsibility.
themeA developed insight about human experience expressed through patterns and consequences.
thematic statementA complete, arguable sentence expressing a work’s theme.
universal themeA theme relevant across many contexts without pretending all experiences are identical.
moralA direct lesson or rule about behavior, often stated as advice.
motifA repeated image, phrase, object, or situation that gathers meaning.
archetypeA recurring character, situation, or pattern recognizable across many traditions; not a rigid stereotype.
cultural specificityConcrete practices, histories, language, places, and values rooted in a particular context.
qualificationA condition or limit that makes a claim precise rather than absolute.
clichéAn overused expression or idea that has lost precision and force.
contradictionA meaningful tension between claims, actions, values, or outcomes.
resonanceThe extended emotional or intellectual significance a detail gathers.
