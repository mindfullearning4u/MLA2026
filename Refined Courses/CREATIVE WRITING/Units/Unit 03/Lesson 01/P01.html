P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Universal Themes in Creative Work

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.FIC.02 - Universal Themes in Creative Work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.NAR.01 - Narrative techniques and point of view; MLA.CW.WR.08 - Evidence-based craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify a universal theme in a creative work.
  • How a theme is different from a topic, subject, summary, or moral.
  • How narrative choices help a reader understand a theme.
  • How to explain theme using evidence from a passage.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on theme vocabulary.
  • Practice separating topic from theme.
  • Analyze short creative passages for character conflict, change, and theme.
  • Draft a short explanation of how a writer develops a universal theme.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can read a short creative passage, name a universal theme as a complete idea, support it with specific evidence, and explain how the writer's choices develop that theme.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning how stories can explore ideas that many people understand across different places and times, such as courage, belonging, loss, justice, freedom, loyalty, identity, or change. You are not just naming the idea. You are proving how the writer builds the idea through character, conflict, setting, and narrative technique.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you can name a topic but cannot turn it into a complete theme statement, or if you are unsure what evidence best supports your theme.
