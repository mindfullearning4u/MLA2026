The Sharpening Stone

Every Saturday, Noor’s father sharpened the kitchen knives before preparing the family meal. He tested each edge against a sheet of paper and returned the knife to the same wooden slot.

After his arthritis worsened, Noor bought an electric sharpener. Arthritis is a condition that causes joint pain and stiffness. “This will be easier,” she said.

Her father thanked her but left the box unopened. The next Saturday, Noor found him struggling with the old stone. She reached for the knife. He pulled it back.

Neither spoke until the onion began to burn.

Noor opened the electric sharpener and asked him to show her how to judge the angle. He placed his hand over hers but did not guide it. Together they listened as the machine changed pitch.

When the blade cut the paper cleanly, her father returned it to the wooden slot. He placed the sharpening stone beside the new machine.

Analysis and Transfer

  1. Write an 80–100 word summary.
  2. Identify the subject and value conflict.
  3. Trace the sharpening motif.
  4. Explain the subtext of the unopened box.
  5. Analyze the burned onion as a turning point.
  6. Explain what the shared listening reveals.
  7. Write two qualified thematic statements.
  8. Support the stronger statement with three details.
  9. Identify one unsupported overgeneralization.
  10. Rewrite the final paragraph to earn a different theme.
  11. Explain how the passage remains culturally and personally specific.
  12. Write a 300–380 word narrative that develops a qualified theme through a repeated object, contradiction, choice, and consequence. Include a completed Visual Theme Bridge.
