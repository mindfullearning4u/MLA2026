P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue learning how to explain universal theme with evidence and craft reasoning.

Step 4: Build a Theme Explanation

A strong theme explanation has three parts.

First: State the theme as a complete sentence.

Second: Give evidence from the passage.

Third: Explain how the evidence develops the theme.

Passage: Amir found the lost wallet on the bus seat. His rent was due that night. He opened the wallet, saw the school photo tucked beside the money, and carried it to the driver before he could change his mind.

Theme statement: Integrity can require a person to do the right thing even when the wrong choice would solve an immediate problem.

Evidence: Amir needs rent money, but he returns the wallet after seeing the school photo.

Explanation: The conflict makes the honest choice difficult. The school photo reminds Amir that the wallet belongs to a real person, and his action develops the theme of integrity.

Notebook Task Part 2

Part A: Copy the Amir passage.

Part B: Write one topic and one complete universal theme statement.

Part C: Write two evidence sentences and one explanation sentence showing how the evidence develops the theme.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The theme is money.

This is incorrect because "money" is only a topic. It does not say what the passage suggests about life, choices, or human experience.

Correct: The passage suggests that integrity can be difficult when doing the right thing conflicts with personal need.

This is correct because it is a complete idea and can be supported by Amir's conflict and action.

Need help? Ask your TOR if your theme statement is only one word, sounds like a summary, or does not match the evidence.
