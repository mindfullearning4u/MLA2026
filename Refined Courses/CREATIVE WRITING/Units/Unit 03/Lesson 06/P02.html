Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

coming-of-age narrativeA story centered on a significant change in maturity, identity, responsibility, or understanding.
maturationGrowth in judgment or responsibility produced through experience and reflection.
worldviewA person’s organized assumptions about how people and society work.
perspectiveA position shaped by knowledge, experience, values, and interests.
conflicting perspectivesDifferent interpretations or priorities that cannot all be satisfied at once.
defensible positionA viewpoint supported by understandable evidence or values, even if imperfect.
counterevidenceEvidence that challenges or limits an initial belief.
recognitionA moment when a character understands something previously missed or resisted.
agencyA character’s capacity to make meaningful choices and affect outcomes.
costly choiceA decision that requires a real sacrifice, risk, or loss.
internal conflictStruggle among competing beliefs, desires, loyalties, or fears within a character.
earned changeA believable transformation prepared by pressure, evidence, choice, and consequence.
