The Regional Final

Leah planned to skip her family’s moving day so she could attend a regional robotics final. Her older sister, Cam, called the choice selfish. Leah argued that the competition could determine her scholarship.

On moving morning, Cam admitted she had rejected a job interview to help. Leah had assumed Cam simply valued family tradition more.

The moving truck arrived early. Leah could still reach the final if she left immediately, but their mother could not lift the medical equipment alone.

Leah called her team and explained the delay. They rearranged the presentation so she could join remotely after loading. The team lost points for the change but advanced.

That night, Cam apologized for calling her selfish. Leah did not apologize for caring about the competition. She asked why Cam had hidden the interview.

Analysis and Transfer

  1. Write an 80–100 word summary.
  2. State Leah’s initial belief.
  3. Complete the Perspective Matrix.
  4. Explain why both perspectives are defensible.
  5. Identify the counterevidence.
  6. Explain the costly choice and its real cost.
  7. Complete the Growth Arc.
  8. Analyze Leah’s agency.
  9. Explain why the ending is qualified rather than fully resolved.
  10. Rewrite one beat from Cam’s viewpoint.
  11. Revise the ending into false maturity and diagnose the failure.
  12. Write a 380–480 word coming-of-age scene with conflicting perspectives, counterevidence, costly choice, consequence, and revised behavior. Include both visual planners.
