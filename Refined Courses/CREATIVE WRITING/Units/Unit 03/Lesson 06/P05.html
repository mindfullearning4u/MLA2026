Independent Work: Coming of Age and Conflicting Perspectives

Purpose

Reveal growth by placing a character between competing, believable perspectives.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Your work should show deliberate choices, not just completion.

Source for Your Work

Sixteen-year-old Dani wants to accept an apprenticeship in another city. Dani’s older sibling believes leaving means abandoning the family during a difficult year.

1. Plan Before You Draft

DecisionYour Plan
Character goal and obstacleWrite one clear sentence.
Key turning pointState the choice or discovery.
Meaning readers should inferName the idea without writing a moral.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach would produce stronger creative writing? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Make Dani correct and the sibling selfish so the conflict is easy to resolve.
B. Give both characters valid concerns, then let Dani’s response show a more mature understanding of freedom and responsibility.

3. Apply the Lesson

Draft a 350–450 word scene in which Dani and the sibling confront the decision. Use dialogue, internal thought, and one meaningful action to show both perspectives and a step in Dani’s growth.

4. Revise with Purpose

Revise one exchange so each speaker’s reasoning is understandable while their voices remain distinct.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your creative work applies the lesson skill and uses the source provided.
  • Your explanation names a specific choice and its effect.
  • Your revision improves meaning or craft, not only spelling.
