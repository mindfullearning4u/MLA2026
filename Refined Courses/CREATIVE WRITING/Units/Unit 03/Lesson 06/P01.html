Lesson Overview: Coming of Age and Conflicting Perspectives

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.FIC.07 — develop coming-of-age change through conflicting perspectives, consequential choices, and qualified insight.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish maturation from simply getting older.
  • Build conflict between defensible perspectives rather than a correct person and a foolish one.
  • Trace a protagonist’s belief, challenge, choice, cost, and revised understanding.
  • Use viewpoint limits and counterevidence to avoid simplistic lessons.

Why This Matters

Coming of age is a change in responsibility, perception, or relationship. Strong stories do not require a young person to accept an adult’s view; they require the character to confront complexity and choose with fuller awareness.
