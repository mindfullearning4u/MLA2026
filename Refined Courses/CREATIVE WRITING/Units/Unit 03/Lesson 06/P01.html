P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Coming of Age and Conflicting Perspectives

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.FIC.07 - Coming of age and conflicting perspectives.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.FIC.03 - Narrator perspective and character viewpoint; MLA.CW.NAR.02 - Pace, tension, mood, and tone.

What You Will Learn

  • How coming-of-age fiction shows growth, realization, or changed understanding.
  • How conflicting perspectives create tension and deepen character development.
  • How narrator viewpoint can be limited, biased, uncertain, or changing.
  • How pacing and tone affect a character's moment of growth.

What You Will Do

  • Identify a character's starting belief and changed understanding.
  • Analyze conflict between two perspectives.
  • Explain how narration and pacing reveal growth.
  • Draft a coming-of-age scene plan.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can explain how a character's perspective changes and how conflicting viewpoints, narration, and pacing develop that change.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning how stories show a young or developing character seeing the world differently after conflict, pressure, or new understanding.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you can identify the conflict but cannot explain how the character changes.
