Checkpoint Task

Passage: Eli thought his grandmother's stories were only a way to delay chores. After he found the same street names in her old notebook and realized she had walked those roads before his family arrived, he asked her to tell the story again.

Write one paragraph explaining Eli's starting belief, changed understanding, and the evidence that creates the change.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies the character's starting perspective.
  • The response identifies the changed understanding.
  • The response explains the conflict or evidence that causes growth.
  • The response uses specific passage details.
