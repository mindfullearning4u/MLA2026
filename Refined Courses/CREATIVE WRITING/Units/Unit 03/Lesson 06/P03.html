P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by connecting perspective change to evidence.

Step 4: Explain the Growth

Passage: Marco thought his father's rules were only about control. After watching his younger sister nearly ride into traffic, Marco understood why his father always stopped at the corner and looked both ways twice.

Starting belief: The rules are about control.

New understanding: The rules are about care and safety.

Explanation: Marco's perspective changes because he experiences a moment that shows the reason behind the rule.

Notebook Task Part 2

Part A: Copy the Marco passage.

Part B: Identify the starting belief and new understanding.

Part C: Explain the evidence that causes Marco's change.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Marco changes because the passage says he understood.

This is incomplete because it does not explain what causes the understanding.

Correct: Marco changes because seeing his sister almost ride into traffic helps him understand the safety purpose behind his father's rule.
