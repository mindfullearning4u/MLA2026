Move the Bench

Neighbors:

The city plans to remove the only bench at the Route 8 stop because it blocks snow equipment. That concern is real. So is the fact that riders currently wait beside a six-lane road without shelter.

Last Tuesday, Mr. Alvarez missed one bus because standing worsened his knee pain and he sat on the curb where the driver could not see him. One story cannot establish frequency, but it shows the human consequence our counts should investigate.

I propose moving the bench twelve feet east for a thirty-day trial. Riders can report access problems through the posted number, and maintenance staff can document whether snow clearance improves.

Please attend Thursday’s transportation meeting or submit written comments by Wednesday.

Analysis and Transfer

  1. Summarize the proposal in 60–80 words.
  2. Name audience, purpose, and intended effect.
  3. Map the rhetorical situation.
  4. Complete the Visual Alignment Map.
  5. Analyze ethos and fair acknowledgment.
  6. Analyze logos and the limits placed on one story.
  7. Analyze pathos without labeling emotion alone.
  8. Explain the kairos.
  9. Identify two craft choices and their effects.
  10. Revise one paragraph for transportation engineers.
  11. Identify one manipulative revision the writer should avoid and explain why.
  12. Write a 350–450 word creative advocacy piece for a defined audience with mapped appeals, fair counterevidence, actionable purpose, and a 120–150 word ethical rationale.
