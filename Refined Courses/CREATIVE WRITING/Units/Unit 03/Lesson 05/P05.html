The Locked Community Room

To the library board:

At 4:05 each weekday, every seat near an outlet is occupied. Students sit beside walls, balancing laptops on backpacks while the community room remains locked for evening meetings. The current policy reserves that room after three, even when no meeting begins before six.

Last month, forty-seven students signed a request for supervised access. The director reasonably noted that staff cannot monitor another room continuously. A pilot can address that concern: open the room Tuesdays and Thursdays from 4:00–5:30, require sign-in, and place two tables within view of the service desk camera.

I am asking for a six-week trial, not permanent expansion. Attendance, incidents, and staff time can be reviewed before any longer decision.

Yesterday, a student ended a tutoring call when her laptop reached two percent. She packed without complaint and waited outside for her ride. Her quiet exit was easy to miss. That is precisely why a measured trial matters.

Rhetorical Analysis

  1. Write an 80–100 word objective summary.
  2. Name the exact audience.
  3. State the purpose as an action.
  4. Identify the intended effect.
  5. Map the rhetorical situation and constraints.
  6. Complete the Visual Alignment Map.
  7. Analyze ethos.
  8. Analyze logos.
  9. Analyze pathos.
  10. Analyze kairos.
  11. Explain how the writer represents the director fairly.
  12. Trace how scene and proposal work together.

Vocabulary and Revision

  1. Use context to define pilot as used in the proposal.
  2. Revise one paragraph for a student audience.
  3. Revise one paragraph for a facilities manager.

Original Rhetorical Creative Work

  1. Plan a real or fictional rhetorical situation with audience, purpose, constraints, appeals, evidence, and ethical risks.
  2. Write a polished 650–800 word creative advocacy piece combining scene, reasoning, fair acknowledgment, and an actionable purpose.
  3. Write a 180–230 word rationale mapping appeals to craft choices and explaining two ethical safeguards.
