Lesson Overview: Author Purpose, Rhetoric, and Craft Choices

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.RHT.01 — align purpose, audience, rhetorical strategy, and craft choices in creative work.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish topic, purpose, message, and intended effect.
  • Analyze how credibility, reasoning, emotion, and timing operate in creative texts.
  • Select craft choices that fit a specific audience and situation.
  • Revise manipulative, mismatched, or ethically weak persuasion.

Why This Matters

Creative writing always positions readers. A scene may invite sympathy, challenge assumptions, build trust, or make a familiar practice appear strange. Effective rhetoric coordinates that purpose with evidence, voice, form, and audience.
