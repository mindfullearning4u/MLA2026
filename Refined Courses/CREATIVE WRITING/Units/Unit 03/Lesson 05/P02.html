Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

author purposeThe result a writer intends to produce for a particular audience.
audienceThe specific readers or listeners a work addresses or anticipates.
rhetoricThe purposeful use of language, form, evidence, and arrangement to influence understanding or response.
ethosAn appeal based on credibility, character, or trustworthiness.
logosAn appeal based on reasons, evidence, patterns, or causal logic.
pathosAn appeal to emotion, values, or imagination.
kairosThe strategic use of timing, urgency, and situation.
rhetorical situationThe relationship among writer, audience, purpose, context, and constraints.
intended effectThe response or understanding a writer aims to create.
craft choiceA deliberate decision about structure, viewpoint, diction, syntax, imagery, detail, or pacing.
manipulationInfluence that distorts evidence, exploits vulnerability, or blocks informed judgment.
rhetorical alignmentA clear fit among purpose, audience, evidence, form, and style.
