Checkpoint Task

Central idea: Accepting help can be a form of strength.

Passage: Lena tried to carry all six boxes alone. When the smallest box slipped, she laughed too loudly and reached for it before anyone could move. Finally, she handed one box to her brother and kept walking beside him.

Write one paragraph explaining how two details support the central idea.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response states or uses the correct central idea.
  • The response identifies at least two relevant supporting details.
  • The explanation shows how the details develop the idea.
  • The response avoids unsupported or unrelated details.
