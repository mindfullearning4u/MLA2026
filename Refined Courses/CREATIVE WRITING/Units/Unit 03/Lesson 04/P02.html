Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
central ideaThe main concept or understanding a text develops across its parts.The central idea explains what the passage mainly says about preserving memory.
themeA qualified insight about human experience developed through pattern and consequence.The theme suggests that responsibility requires honest action.
essential detailA detail necessary to develop, complicate, or resolve the central idea.The broken bridge is an essential detail because it causes the town’s decision.
supporting detailRelevant evidence that strengthens understanding but may not be structurally necessary.The color of a minor character’s coat is only a supporting detail.
evidence chainA sequence in which details build, alter, and confirm an idea.The claim, quotation, and explanation form an evidence chain.
controlling ideaThe primary idea used to guide selection and organization during drafting.Every paragraph supports the controlling idea that change requires cooperation.
causal linkA relationship in which one event or choice produces another.The storm closes the road; that causal link explains why the meeting is delayed.
counterevidenceA detail that complicates, limits, or challenges an initial interpretation.A later document that disputes the witness provides counterevidence.
developmentThe process by which an idea gains clarity, depth, or qualification.Repeated choices develop the character’s growing independence.
relevanceA clear connection between a detail and the purpose being developed.A quotation about the central conflict has greater relevance than a decorative description.
coherenceLogical unity among details, events, and ideas.Transitions and logical connections give the explanation coherence.
narrative economyUsing details efficiently so each performs meaningful work.Removing repeated background details improves narrative economy.
