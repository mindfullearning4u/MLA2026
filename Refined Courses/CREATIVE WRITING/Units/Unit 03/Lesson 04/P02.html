Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

central ideaThe main concept or understanding a text develops across its parts.
themeA qualified insight about human experience developed through pattern and consequence.
essential detailA detail necessary to develop, complicate, or resolve the central idea.
supporting detailRelevant evidence that strengthens understanding but may not be structurally necessary.
evidence chainA sequence in which details build, alter, and confirm an idea.
controlling ideaThe primary idea used to guide selection and organization during drafting.
causal linkA relationship in which one event or choice produces another.
counterevidenceA detail that complicates, limits, or challenges an initial interpretation.
developmentThe process by which an idea gains clarity, depth, or qualification.
relevanceA clear connection between a detail and the purpose being developed.
coherenceLogical unity among details, events, and ideas.
narrative economyUsing details efficiently so each performs meaningful work.
