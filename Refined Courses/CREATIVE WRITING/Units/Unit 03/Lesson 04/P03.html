P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by learning how to explain support clearly.

Step 4: Explain the Support

A strong explanation does not just point to a detail. It explains why the detail matters.

Passage: Rae said she would answer the letter later. She slid it under her math book, then under her jacket, then finally under the loose floorboard beside her bed.

Central idea: Avoiding a hard truth can make fear grow.

Evidence: Rae keeps hiding the letter in more secret places.

Explanation: The repeated hiding shows that avoiding the letter does not solve the problem. Instead, Rae's fear becomes stronger and more secretive.

Notebook Task Part 2

Part A: Copy the Rae passage.

Part B: Identify the central idea and one supporting detail.

Part C: Write two sentences explaining why the detail supports the central idea.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The detail supports the idea because it is in the story.

This is incorrect because not every detail automatically supports the central idea.

Correct: The repeated hiding supports the central idea because it shows Rae's avoidance growing stronger instead of disappearing.

This is correct because it explains the relationship between evidence and idea.
