Common Mistake

Incorrect: My story has a theme, an object, and a conflict, so it is automatically integrated.

This is incorrect because pieces are not integrated unless they support the same purpose.

Correct: My story is integrated because the map, teamwork conflict, and changed understanding all develop the idea that leadership includes shared responsibility.
