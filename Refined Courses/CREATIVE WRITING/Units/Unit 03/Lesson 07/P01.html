Lesson Overview: Integrated Fiction Development

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.FIC.08 — plan, draft, and revise sustained fiction in which theme, character, conflict, structure, viewpoint, and style operate as one system.

What You Will Learn

  • Design a controlling effect before drafting scenes.
  • Coordinate character desire, conflicting perspectives, evidence chain, theme, and rhetoric.
  • Build setup and payoff across a sustained narrative.
  • Revise globally before polishing locally.

Why This Matters

Integrated fiction is more than a collection of good techniques. Each major choice should pressure, reveal, complicate, or resolve another part of the story. Revision tests the system rather than decorating isolated sentences.
