P04 Worked Example

Study how each example integrates multiple fiction skills.

Example 1: Central Idea

Step 1: Choose the central idea: responsibility is shared, not carried alone.

Step 2: Build a character who believes the opposite.

Step 3: Choose details that pressure the character to change.

Example 2: Source Influence

Step 1: Preserve a guide-object source pattern.

Step 2: Transform the object into a marked-up map from a teammate.

Step 3: Use the map to show that guidance helps the character mature.

Example 3: Growth Arc

Step 1: Starting belief: I must do everything myself.

Step 2: Conflict: A teammate insists that leadership requires trust.

Step 3: Changed understanding: The character lets the team carry part of the work and succeeds.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Adding a mythic object that does not affect the character's decision.

Correct: Using the adapted object to create pressure, reveal theme, and support the character's change.
