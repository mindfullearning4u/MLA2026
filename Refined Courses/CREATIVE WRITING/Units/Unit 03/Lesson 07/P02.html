Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

controlling effectThe primary experience or understanding the writer intends to create.
narrative systemThe interacting structure of character, conflict, setting, viewpoint, time, style, and meaning.
story premiseA concise statement of protagonist, pressure, situation, and stakes.
throughlineThe continuing desire, question, or conflict connecting a narrative’s parts.
scene sequenceAn ordered set of scenes in which each changes pressure or knowledge.
setupAn early element that prepares a later development.
payoffA later fulfillment, reversal, or transformation of a setup.
escalationAn increase or complication in pressure, cost, or uncertainty.
reversalA development that changes the apparent direction or meaning of events.
global revisionRevision of purpose, structure, viewpoint, causality, and development.
local revisionRevision of sentences, diction, imagery, rhythm, and conventions.
continuityConsistency of factual, temporal, spatial, and character information.
