Passage: “Borrowed Weather”

The colony’s climate dome produced rain every Thursday at four. Sera, apprentice keeper of the weather archive, knew the schedule better than her own pulse. Yet on Founders’ Day the ceiling brightened, thunder rolled, and dry silver leaves began falling through the market. Citizens cheered at the “miracle.” Sera recognized the leaves from a prohibited simulation created by her missing mother. The mayor ordered her to certify the event as natural before the evening broadcast. Sera opened the archive log and found her mother’s final entry: “A perfect system teaches people to stop looking up.” At 3:59, with the broadcast light blinking, Sera moved her hand from the certification seal to the manual weather controls.

Synthesis Tasks

  1. State the controlling effect and support it with two details.
  2. Identify Sera’s desire, opposition, stakes, and time pressure.
  3. Trace the passage’s causal chain.
  4. Create an access map for Sera, the citizens, mayor, and reader.
  5. Explain how the repeated schedule functions as setup.
  6. Analyze the silver leaves as world-building detail and motif.
  7. Define certify from context and identify the clue supporting your definition.
  8. State a theme as a defensible claim, then offer one counterreading.
  9. Predict two plausible choices Sera could make and the thematic consequence of each.
  10. Write a 180–220 word ending that pays off the setup without resolving the conflict through coincidence.
  11. Create a labeled story-system map for your ending and explain its weakest link.
  12. Write a 200–250 word craft rationale using two complete evidence chains to defend viewpoint, causality, motif, and ending.
