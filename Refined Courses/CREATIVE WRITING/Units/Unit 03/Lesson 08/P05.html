Independent Work: Unit 3 Portfolio Synthesis

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks. Submit your Page 2 vocabulary and this work as Notebook Evidence. Use one polished Unit 3 fiction draft of at least 1,000 words; it may be a substantially revised earlier draft or a new story.

Part A — Diagnose

  1. State the intended controlling effect.
  2. Write the throughline as desire → opposition → choices → consequence.
  3. Create a scene table naming purpose, pressure, choice, and result for every scene.
  4. Create an access map for the narrator, reader, and two major characters.
  5. Identify one broken or weak causal link and explain why it weakens the story.
  6. Trace one motif across at least three moments and explain its changing meaning.

Part B — Revise

  1. Make one global revision to structure, character logic, viewpoint, or ending.
  2. Revise the weak causal link so a character choice produces the next pressure.
  3. Strengthen one setup/payoff pair.
  4. Revise one passage so setting actively increases the cost of a choice.
  5. Revise one paragraph for pacing and one for sentence rhythm; show before and after.
  6. Check continuity of time, place, objects, knowledge, and character motivation; correct every inconsistency.

Part C — Defend and Transfer

  1. Write a 300–400 word craft defense using three evidence chains.
  2. Present one plausible counterreading of your theme and evaluate it fairly.
  3. Explain how the ending transforms the opening.
  4. Define coherence from its use in this lesson, then identify the strongest evidence of structural coherence in your draft.
  5. Create a one-page visual story-system map linking desire, pressure, choice, consequence, viewpoint, setting, motif, and theme.
  6. Write a 150–200 word transfer plan naming two techniques you will carry into Unit 4 and one craft risk you will take.

Quality Gate

The final draft must show purposeful integration, not simply mention every skill. Every analytical claim must name evidence. Every revision must improve the controlling effect.
