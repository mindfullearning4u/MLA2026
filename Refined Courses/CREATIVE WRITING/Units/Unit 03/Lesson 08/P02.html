Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Beside each term, add one Unit 3 example from your own work or a lesson model. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.
TermDefinition
synthesisCombining multiple craft elements into a coherent whole.
integrationMaking story elements support and influence one another.
controlling effectThe dominant experience or understanding a writer designs for the reader.
diagnostic readingReading to locate the specific cause of a draft problem.
craft claimA defensible statement explaining what a writer’s choice accomplishes.
evidence chainA sequence connecting textual detail, inference, craft function, and reader effect.
structural coherenceThe logical fit among scenes, causes, choices, and outcomes.
thematic coherenceThe alignment of recurring details and character choices with an emerging idea.
counterreadingA plausible alternative interpretation tested against evidence.
transferApplying a learned skill accurately in a new context.
global revisionChanging purpose, structure, character logic, or scene order.
local revisionImproving wording, rhythm, imagery, transitions, or conventions.
