Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
synthesisCombining multiple craft elements into a coherent whole.The final analysis synthesizes several craft choices into one interpretation.
integrationMaking story elements support and influence one another.Revision integrates character, structure, and theme instead of treating them separately.
controlling effectThe dominant experience or understanding a writer designs for the reader.Repeated silence and narrow spaces create a controlling effect of confinement.
diagnostic readingReading to locate the specific cause of a draft problem.Diagnostic reading identifies where the draft’s tension weakens.
craft claimA defensible statement explaining what a writer’s choice accomplishes.The writer makes a craft claim that the limited viewpoint increases uncertainty.
evidence chainA sequence connecting textual detail, inference, craft function, and reader effect.A claim, quotation, and explanation form an evidence chain.
structural coherenceThe logical fit among scenes, causes, choices, and outcomes.Each scene causes the next, creating structural coherence.
thematic coherenceThe alignment of recurring details and character choices with an emerging idea.Recurring choices about loyalty create thematic coherence.
counterreadingA plausible alternative interpretation tested against evidence.A counterreading argues that the ending suggests freedom rather than defeat.
transferApplying a learned skill accurately in a new context.The writer transfers a pacing strategy learned from one text into a new story.
global revisionChanging purpose, structure, character logic, or scene order.Reordering two scenes is a global revision.
local revisionImproving wording, rhythm, imagery, transitions, or conventions.Replacing a vague verb is a local revision.
