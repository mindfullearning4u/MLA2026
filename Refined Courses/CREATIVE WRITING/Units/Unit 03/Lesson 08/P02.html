Notebook Title

Lesson 8: Unit 3 Fiction Synthesis

Vocabulary Review

  • Universal theme: A complete idea that applies across people, cultures, or situations.
  • Adaptation: A transformed version of a source element.
  • Central idea: The controlling idea a passage develops.
  • Author purpose: The reason a writer makes craft choices.
  • Rhetoric: Language used to shape reader response.
  • Coming of age: Character growth through changed understanding.

Step 1: Use the Unit 3 Skill Chain

First: Identify the passage's central idea or theme.

Second: Look for source influence, adaptation, or theme comparison.

Third: Identify the character's perspective conflict or growth.

Fourth: Explain author purpose and reader effect.

Fifth: Support every answer with evidence.

Step 2: Practice with a Synthesis Passage

Passage: Eli thought the old map in his grandmother's drawer was clutter. When the city renamed his street, he found the same road marked in her handwriting and asked her to tell him where the map had been. She smiled and traced the route she had walked when she first arrived.

Theme: Memory can help a person understand where they belong.

Central idea support: The map connects place, family history, and Eli's changed understanding.

Coming-of-age change: Eli moves from dismissing the map to valuing its story.
