P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 3 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 3 Primary Standards: MLA.CW.FIC.02, MLA.CW.FIC.04, MLA.CW.FIC.06, MLA.CW.FIC.07, MLA.CW.WR.01, MLA.CW.WR.02, and MLA.CW.WR.04.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.NAR.01, MLA.CW.WR.08, and MLA.CW.WR.10.

What You Will Learn

  • How to review Unit 3 fiction skills together.
  • How to identify theme, adaptation, central idea, purpose, rhetoric, and coming-of-age change in short passages.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 3 assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 3 vocabulary and skill steps.
  • Practice with synthesis examples.
  • Complete guided practice.
  • Complete the Unit 3 assessment in Moodle.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by correctly analyzing Unit 3 fiction passages and explaining how evidence supports theme, adaptation, central idea, purpose, perspective conflict, and character growth.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson is your Unit 3 review. It does not introduce a new primary standard. It helps you combine everything you learned about fiction development.
