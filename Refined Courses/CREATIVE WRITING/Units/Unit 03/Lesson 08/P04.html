P04 Worked Example

Review each example before the Unit Assessment.

Example 1: Theme

Passage: A character gives up the winning speech slot to help a nervous friend prepare.

Step 1: Topic: generosity and ambition.

Step 2: Evidence: the character gives up a public opportunity.

Step 3: Theme: personal goals can still leave room for helping others.

Example 2: Adaptation

Source element: A guide gives an object before a journey.

Step 1: Preserve the guide-object pattern.

Step 2: Transform it into a coach giving a marked-up route card before a city race.

Step 3: Explain how the adapted object supports guidance and growth.

Example 3: Coming of Age

Passage: A narrator thinks family stories are delays until an old notebook proves the stories explain how the family arrived.

Step 1: Starting belief: the stories are delays.

Step 2: Evidence: the notebook connects the stories to real history.

Step 3: Changed understanding: the stories carry family identity.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treating a plot detail as a theme.

Correct: Use the plot detail as evidence for a complete theme statement.
