Lesson Overview: Comparative Universal Themes

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.THM.02 — compare how different creative works develop related universal themes through distinct contexts and craft choices.

What You Will Learn

  • Compare themes without treating different works as identical.
  • Separate shared subject from each work’s qualified thematic claim.
  • Analyze how context, genre, character choice, motif, and ending alter meaning.
  • Write balanced comparative claims supported by paired evidence.

Why This Matters

Two works may explore belonging but disagree about what belonging requires. Comparison becomes rigorous when it preserves each work’s context and explains how different craft paths lead to related, contrasting, or complicating insights.
