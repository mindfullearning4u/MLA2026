P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Comparative Universal Themes

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.FIC.06 - Comparative Universal Themes.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.FIC.02 - Universal themes in creative work; MLA.CW.WR.09 - Reading complex craft models.

What You Will Learn

  • How to compare universal themes in two creative passages.
  • How to identify similarities and differences in theme development.
  • How craft choices can develop a similar theme in different ways.
  • How to write a comparison using evidence from both passages.

What You Will Do

  • Review how to write a universal theme statement.
  • Read paired passages that share a topic or theme.
  • Compare character choices, conflicts, and outcomes.
  • Explain how each writer develops the theme.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can read two passages, identify a shared or related universal theme, use evidence from both passages, and explain how each passage develops the theme similarly or differently.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning to compare how different creative works explore big human ideas. Two works may both explore courage, but one may show courage through speaking up and another may show courage through leaving. Your job is to compare the idea and the writer choices that develop it.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you can understand each passage separately but are unsure how to compare them in one answer.
