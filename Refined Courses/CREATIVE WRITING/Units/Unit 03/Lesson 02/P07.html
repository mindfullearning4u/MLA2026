Checkpoint Task

Passage A: Rosa told the truth about the broken window before her cousin was blamed.

Passage B: Malik returned the prize ribbon when he realized the judges had recorded the wrong score.

Write one paragraph comparing how the passages develop a universal theme about honesty. Include evidence from both passages.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies a shared or related universal theme.
  • The response uses evidence from both passages.
  • The response explains at least one similarity or difference in theme development.
  • The response avoids surface-level comparisons such as names, length, or unrelated details.
