Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

comparisonAnalysis of meaningful similarities and differences using shared criteria.
contrastAnalysis of how elements differ and why the difference matters.
comparative claimAn arguable sentence stating a meaningful relationship between two works.
shared subjectA broad concern explored by multiple works.
thematic variationA distinct insight one work develops about a shared subject.
point of comparisonA common analytical category applied to both works.
paired evidenceSpecific evidence from each work selected to illuminate the same comparison.
contextThe cultural, historical, social, or situational conditions shaping meaning.
parallelA meaningful similarity in structure, image, character role, or development.
divergenceA point where works move toward different choices, consequences, or meanings.
synthesisReasoning that combines findings to produce a larger insight.
false equivalenceTreating importantly different situations as if they were the same.
