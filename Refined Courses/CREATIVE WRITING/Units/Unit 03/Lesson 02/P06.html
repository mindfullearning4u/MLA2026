Checkpoint: “Messages and Tools”

Directions

Read both micro-narratives and complete all 12 tasks independently. Submit only these responses and earn at least 80%.

Work A — “The Pronunciation”

Rina keeps her grandmother’s voice messages after the phone company announces an automatic deletion. She downloads them but never listens. When her younger cousin asks how their grandmother pronounced his name, Rina finally plays one. The recording becomes shared instruction rather than private protection.

Work B — “The Initials”

Mateo inherits his grandfather’s toolbox and refuses to let anyone use it. The tools remain polished but idle. After a neighbor’s roof leaks, Mateo lends one hammer and discovers his grandfather’s initials beneath another person’s worn grip.

Comparison Tasks

  1. Summarize each work in 50–70 words.
  2. State each qualified theme separately.
  3. Name the shared subject.
  4. Complete the Visual Comparison Bridge.
  5. Pair evidence about private possession.
  6. Explain a parallel in the protagonists’ initial choices.
  7. Explain the divergence in how sharing changes each object.
  8. Analyze how voice and physical tools create different forms of inheritance.
  9. Identify one false equivalence and correct it.
  10. Write a balanced comparative claim.
  11. Write a 150–180 word synthesis explaining what becomes visible only through comparison.
  12. Write two 180–220 word linked scenes that share a subject but develop contrasting themes. Include a comparative rationale.
