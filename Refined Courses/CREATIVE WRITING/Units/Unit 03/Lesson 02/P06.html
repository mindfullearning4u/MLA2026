P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using evidence from both passages.

Instructions

Passage A: Naya stayed after the meeting to pick up every chair, even though nobody thanked her.

Passage B: Mateo swept the stage after the concert because he wanted the next group to find it ready.

Part A: Write one theme statement for each passage.

Part B: Write one shared theme statement.

Part C: Write a comparison paragraph explaining one similarity and one difference in how the theme is developed.
