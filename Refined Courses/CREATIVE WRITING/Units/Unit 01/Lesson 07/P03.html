P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by revising unclear feedback into accessible workshop language.

Deeper Application

Draft line: "The hallway swallowed the last bell."

Clear feedback: "I noticed personification in 'hallway swallowed.' This makes the hallway feel powerful and gives the ending a dramatic tone. Could you add one sound detail to extend that effect?"

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "This is confusing and you should fix it."

Why this is not enough: The writer does not know what is confusing, where to revise, or what craft move to try.

Correct: "I got confused when the speaker changed from I to she. Could you clarify the point of view in the second paragraph?"

Teachable Explanation: The feedback identifies the location, names the craft issue, and asks a clear revision question.
