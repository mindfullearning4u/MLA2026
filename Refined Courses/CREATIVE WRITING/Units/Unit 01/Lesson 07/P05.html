Independent Work: Workshop Communication Case

Directions

Read the draft, writer goal, and workshop transcript. Answer all 16 questions in complete sentences. Submit these responses with Page 2 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Writer Goal and Draft

Writer goal: Amara wants the scene to show a daughter translating for her father while realizing that translation cannot carry every private meaning.

Draft: “At the pharmacy counter, Baba placed the folded prescription beneath my hand. The clerk spoke faster when I asked her to repeat the dosage. I translated the numbers, then the warnings. Baba nodded. In our language he said, Tell her the medicine is not the difficult part. The clerk waited. I translated, He understands. Outside, Baba unfolded the paper and traced the word I had not translated.”

Workshop Transcript

Reviewer 1: “The grammar is fine, but you should translate the other language into English so everyone understands.”

Reviewer 2: “I like it. Maybe add more details.”

Reviewer 3: “I think the untranslated sentence creates the conflict, but I am unsure what ‘the word’ refers to in the last line. Is the reader supposed to know?”

Amara: “Readers should understand that the daughter withholds something, but not know exactly which printed word matters.”

Part A — Diagnose the Communication

  1. Restate Amara’s goal in your own words.
  2. Identify the assumption in Reviewer 1’s feedback.
  3. Explain the difference between access and forced language replacement in this case.
  4. Why is Reviewer 2’s feedback unusable as written?
  5. Identify two strengths in Reviewer 3’s response.

Part B — Evidence-Based Feedback

  1. Quote one detail that clearly supports intentional withholding.
  2. Identify the exact source of unintended confusion in the final sentence.
  3. Write a confirmation check for Amara.
  4. Offer two revision options that preserve her goal.
  5. Explain the tradeoff of each option.
  6. Write a complete goal-aligned feedback paragraph using the lesson frame.

Part C — Inclusive Revision

  1. Revise Reviewer 1’s comment so it respects multilingual voice and addresses reader access.
  2. Revise Reviewer 2’s comment to include exact evidence and effect.
  3. Create an accessible three-step version of this prompt: “Analyze how linguistic mediation complicates filial agency.” Preserve the full reasoning demand.

Part D — Transfer

  1. Write a 180–220 word workshop exchange in which a misunderstanding about voice, dialect, register, or ambiguity is repaired through paraphrase, a focused question, specific evidence, two options, and a tradeoff.
  2. Annotate the exchange by identifying six protocol moves and explaining how the final response preserves rigor, dignity, and writer ownership.
