P06 Independent Work

Complete all parts in your writer's notebook or approved course document.

Instructions

Use respectful tone and clear craft vocabulary.

Part A

Write three sentence frames that help a peer discuss craft clearly.

Part B

Rewrite two vague comments into inclusive workshop feedback: "This is confusing" and "I liked it."

Part C

Write one paragraph explaining how inclusive communication helps workshop culture and revision.
