Notebook Title

Lesson 7: Inclusive Academic and Social Communication

Vocabulary

  • Inclusive communication: Clear and respectful language that helps all participants understand and respond.
  • Clarifying question: A question that asks for clearer meaning or more detail.
  • Sentence frame: A structure that helps a speaker organize academic language.
  • Accessible language: Language that is precise without being confusing or exclusionary.
  • Respectful tone: A tone that focuses on craft and protects the writer's dignity.

Step-by-Step Workshop Language

Step 1: Name the craft choice in plain academic language.

Step 2: Point to the exact place in the writing.

Step 3: Explain the reader effect.

Step 4: Ask a clarifying question or offer one revision option.

Model Frame: "I noticed ___ in the line ___. This creates ___. One question I have is ___."

Notebook Task Part 1

Part A: Copy the vocabulary.

Part B: Copy the model frame.

Part C: Write two original feedback sentence frames that a classmate could understand and use.
