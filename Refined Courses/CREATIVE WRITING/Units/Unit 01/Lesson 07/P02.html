Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
registerThe level and style of language chosen for a particular audience, purpose, and situation.
linguistic assetA language, dialect, expression, or communication strategy that expands a speaker’s or writer’s resources.
clarificationMaking meaning more understandable while preserving the original complexity and goal.
simplificationReducing complexity; useful sometimes, but harmful when it removes essential meaning or intellectual demand.
confirmation checkA brief question or paraphrase used to verify shared understanding.
repair moveA communication action that identifies and resolves misunderstanding without blame.
goal-aligned feedbackA response evaluated against the writer’s stated purpose rather than the reviewer’s personal preference.
specificityThe use of exact evidence, location, and effect instead of vague praise or criticism.
tone markerA phrase that signals stance or intent, such as curiosity, uncertainty, respect, or concern.
writer ownershipThe principle that feedback informs decisions while the writer retains authority over the work.
