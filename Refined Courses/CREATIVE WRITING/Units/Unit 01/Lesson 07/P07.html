P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can create clear, respectful, inclusive workshop feedback.

TOR Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your feedback sounds judgmental, vague, or confusing.

Submission Workflow

Complete the checkpoint in your notebook or approved course document. Submit it in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Step 1: Write one vague or unhelpful workshop comment.

Step 2: Rewrite it into inclusive academic feedback.

Step 3: Include a craft term, evidence from the draft, reader effect, and one clarifying question.

Step 4: Explain why the revision supports workshop culture.

Mastery Criteria

  • The feedback is clear and respectful.
  • The response uses accessible craft vocabulary.
  • The comment includes a specific revision path.
  • The explanation connects communication to workshop learning.
