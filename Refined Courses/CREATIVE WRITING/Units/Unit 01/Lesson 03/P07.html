P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can use academic and craft vocabulary accurately.

TOR Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your explanation uses general praise instead of specific craft vocabulary.

Submission Workflow

Complete the checkpoint in your notebook or approved course document. Review for accurate vocabulary and complete explanations. Submit it in the assigned Moodle submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Step 1: Write a four-to-six sentence original passage with a clear tone.

Step 2: Identify the intended tone.

Step 3: Explain three craft choices that create the tone. You must use at least three of these terms: diction, connotation, imagery, syntax, style, voice.

Step 4: Underline or clearly identify the exact words or phrases you are explaining.

Mastery Criteria

  • The passage has a clear tone.
  • The explanation uses accurate craft vocabulary.
  • Each explanation points to exact words or phrases.
  • The response connects craft choices to reader effect.
