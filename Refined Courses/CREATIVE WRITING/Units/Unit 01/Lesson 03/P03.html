P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by practicing how to move from vague reactions to precise craft explanations.

Using Vocabulary in Sequence

When you explain a craft choice, do not start with a general judgment. Start with evidence from the writing.

Step 1: Quote or identify the word, image, sentence, or detail.

Step 2: Name the craft term.

Step 3: Explain the connotation, tone, or reader effect.

Step 4: Connect the effect to the writer's purpose.

Embedded Mentor Sentence

Sentence: "The hallway exhaled the last of the students into the rain."

Craft Explanation: The verb "exhaled" is diction with a tired connotation. It also personifies the hallway. The effect is a weary tone, as if the school day itself is breathing out.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The sentence is descriptive and good."

Why this is not enough: The response gives a general opinion but does not identify a word, craft term, connotation, or effect.

Correct: "The verb 'exhaled' is diction that gives the hallway a tired, human quality. This creates a weary tone at the end of the school day."

Why this works: The response names the exact word, identifies the craft choice, and explains the tone created by that choice.

Notebook Task Part 2

Write one sentence that uses personification. Then write a craft explanation that names diction, connotation, and tone. Your explanation must include the exact word or phrase that creates the effect.
