Checkpoint Passage: “The Photograph”

The photograph waited beneath the loose floorboard, its corners curled inward. Theo wiped away the dust with his sleeve. Three people stood beside a boat, smiling into wind that had already carried their names away.

On the back, a date had been crossed out twice. Beneath it, in darker ink, someone had written Ask her what happened after the storm.

Theo heard his mother calling from downstairs. He returned the photograph to its hiding place—but not before taking a picture of it with his phone.

Checkpoint Requirements

  1. State the situation, dominant tone, and likely purpose.
  2. Analyze the diction and connotation of “waited.”
  3. Explain how the curled corners and dust function as selected details.
  4. Analyze the figurative effect of the wind carrying names away.
  5. Explain how the crossed-out date and darker ink create reader questions.
  6. Analyze the dash in the final sentence as a syntax or pacing choice.
  7. Identify one interaction among imagery, structure, and characterization.
  8. Rewrite the final paragraph to create a more hopeful tone without changing the basic action.
  9. Identify four craft choices in your revision using accurate terms.
  10. Explain in four to five sentences how those choices work together and why your terminology is precise.
