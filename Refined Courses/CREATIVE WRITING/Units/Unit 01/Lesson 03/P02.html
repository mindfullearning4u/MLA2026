Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and their complete definitions in your writer’s notebook. Submit these notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
dictionDeliberate word choice, including precision, formality, sound, and association.
denotationA word’s literal dictionary meaning.
connotationEmotional, cultural, or personal associations carried beyond literal meaning.
imageryLanguage that creates sensory experience through sight, sound, touch, taste, smell, or movement.
syntaxThe arrangement and rhythm of words, phrases, clauses, and sentences.
figurative languageNonliteral language—such as metaphor, simile, or personification—used to create comparison, emphasis, or layered meaning.
detailA selected fact, action, object, or observation that develops character, setting, tone, conflict, or meaning.
structureThe organization and sequence of parts in a sentence, passage, scene, poem, or complete work.
styleA recognizable pattern formed by recurring choices in diction, syntax, imagery, detail, voice, and structure.
reader effectThe thought, feeling, inference, expectation, or interpretation produced by a craft choice or pattern.
