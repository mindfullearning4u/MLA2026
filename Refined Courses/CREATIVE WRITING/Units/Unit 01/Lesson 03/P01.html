Lesson Overview: Academic and Craft Vocabulary

Lesson Focus

Lesson 3: Academic and Craft Vocabulary

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.05 — Academic and Craft Vocabulary.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03 — Voice and Tone; MLA.CW.WR.07 — Connotation, Denotation, and Style.

What You Will Learn

  • Use precise craft terms to identify, analyze, and revise writing choices.
  • Distinguish denotation from connotation and explain how word associations shape style.
  • Analyze interactions among diction, imagery, syntax, figurative language, detail, and structure.
  • Replace vague praise with evidence-based craft explanations.

Why This Matters

Precise vocabulary makes invisible writing decisions discussable. When writers can name a pattern and explain its effect, they can revise intentionally, compare alternatives, and communicate useful feedback instead of relying on “good,” “interesting,” or “better.”

What You Will Do

  • Record ten lesson-specific terms.
  • Practice a four-part evidence-to-effect explanation.
  • Study three complete models that combine multiple craft terms.
  • Analyze and revise a substantial passage.
  • Apply terminology independently to a different Checkpoint passage.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Submit Page 2 vocabulary and Page 5 responses as Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 separately as the Checkpoint.
  4. Correct work below 80% using TOR feedback and resubmit.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz with at least 80% mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Quote exact textual evidence.
  • Name craft choices accurately and explain how they interact.
  • Connect effects to tone, voice, meaning, pacing, or purpose.
  • Revise deliberately and defend the revision with precise terminology.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use accurate craft vocabulary to explain how writing choices create meaning and to justify my own revisions.
