P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Academic and Craft Vocabulary

In this lesson, you will learn how writers use precise craft vocabulary to explain creative choices, discuss voice and tone, and make stronger revision decisions.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.05 - Academic and Craft Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03 - Voice and Tone; MLA.CW.WR.07 - Connotation, Denotation, and Style.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use craft vocabulary accurately in writing and workshop discussion.
  • How connotation changes voice, tone, and reader effect.
  • How to explain diction, imagery, detail, syntax, and style without vague comments.
  • How vocabulary helps writers make intentional revision choices.

What You Will Do

  • Build a craft vocabulary notebook section.
  • Study mentor sentences for diction and connotation.
  • Revise vague feedback into precise craft language.
  • Complete a checkpoint using craft terms to explain your own writing choices.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook defines and applies required craft terms.
  • Your practice identifies diction and connotation accurately.
  • Your checkpoint explains creative choices with precise vocabulary.
  • Your quiz shows at least 80% mastery of academic and craft vocabulary.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.CW.WR.05 in student language: I can use correct writing vocabulary to explain creative choices in my own writing and in craft discussions.

Craft vocabulary gives you exact words for what writers do. Instead of saying a line is "good," you can explain that diction creates a tense tone, imagery makes a setting vivid, or syntax speeds up a moment.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help: Ask your TOR for help if you can sense the effect of a sentence but cannot name the craft choice creating that effect.
