Independent Work: Use Craft Language Precisely

Purpose and Directions

Apply academic and craft vocabulary to analyze a complete passage, make one focused writing choice, and explain it precisely. Complete every numbered task and submit this work with your vocabulary notes in Notebook Evidence Submission. Quality matters more than length.

Draft for Workshop: “The Hallway”

The final bell released the building all at once. Lockers snapped shut, shoes squeaked across the tile, and voices rushed toward the exits. Mara moved against the current with an unopened envelope pressed beneath her notebook. At Room 214, she stopped. The hallway emptied. The envelope seemed heavier when no one was watching.

2. Choose the Stronger Analysis

Record A or B, then explain in two or three sentences why your choice uses terminology and reasoning more accurately. The answer is not revealed on this page.

A. The passage has imagery, syntax, diction, and suspense because it uses many writing techniques.

B. The crowded sound details contrast with the short sentence “The hallway emptied,” shifting the pace and isolating Mara so the unopened envelope gains importance.

3. Apply the Craft in Your Writing

Write a six-to-eight-sentence micro-scene in which a character carries or hides an important object. Before drafting, name your intended reader effect. Use three lesson terms to plan three purposeful choices, but do not force techniques that do not serve the scene.

4. Revise and Explain

Replace one vague or unnecessary sentence with a more purposeful version. Show the original and revision, then explain in two or three sentences which craft term accurately names the change and how it strengthens the intended effect.
