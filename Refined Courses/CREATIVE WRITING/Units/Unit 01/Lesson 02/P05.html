Independent Work: Analyze, Revise, and Defend Craft Choices

Directions

Complete all requirements in complete sentences. Quote exact evidence when analyzing. Submit this work with Page 2 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Draft: “The Message”

At 6:14, the phone lit beneath the stack of unopened mail. Can we talk?

Rina read the message twice. The kitchen clock clicked with the confidence of something that had never made a mistake. She placed the phone face down, then turned it over again. Outside, the neighbor’s sprinkler swept silver arcs across an empty lawn.

She typed Sure, erased it, and typed About what? The second question looked too sharp. She erased that too. When the clock reached 6:16, Rina carried the phone to the window as if better light might change the words.

Part A — Analysis

  1. State the likely purpose of the passage and identify its dominant tone.
  2. Describe Rina’s voice or narrative presence. Distinguish voice from her immediate emotion.
  3. Quote three craft choices from at least two different categories—diction, syntax, imagery, detail, repetition, or point of view.
  4. Explain how the clock personification affects tone and characterization.
  5. Analyze the sprinkler image and decide whether it reinforces or complicates the dominant tone.
  6. Identify one possible tone shift or explain why the tone remains controlled throughout.

Part B — Revision for a New Purpose

  1. Rewrite the passage in 120–160 words for one new purpose: comic embarrassment, controlled anger, or hopeful anticipation.
  2. Keep the same basic event, but change at least four coordinated craft choices across diction, syntax, imagery, detail, and/or point of view.
  3. Underline or list the four most important changes.

Part C — Craft Rationale

  1. Name the new voice and tone using precise terms.
  2. Explain how each of the four changes contributes to the new reader effect.
  3. Compare the original and revised versions. Explain what stayed recognizable and what changed because the purpose changed.
  4. Identify one sentence you revised twice and explain why the final version is more effective.
