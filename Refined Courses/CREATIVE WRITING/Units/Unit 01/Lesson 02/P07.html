Checkpoint Task

Step 1: Choose a purpose: create humor, create suspense, create reflection, or show confidence.

Step 2: Write a five-to-seven sentence original passage that matches that purpose.

Step 3: Identify the intended voice and tone.

Step 4: Explain three craft choices that helped create the voice and tone. At least one explanation must use the word diction, point of view, or sentence rhythm.

Mastery Criteria

  • The passage has a clear writer purpose.
  • The voice and tone fit the selected purpose.
  • The explanation uses accurate craft vocabulary.
  • The response identifies specific craft choices rather than giving a general opinion.
