Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your writer’s notebook. Submit these notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
voiceThe distinctive personality, presence, or verbal identity communicated by a speaker, narrator, or writer across a piece.
toneThe attitude communicated toward a subject, character, situation, audience, or idea in a particular passage.
purposeThe intended result of a piece or craft choice, such as creating suspense, revealing character, questioning an idea, or producing humor.
dictionDeliberate word choice, including a word’s precision, formality, connotation, sound, and emotional association.
syntaxThe arrangement and rhythm of words, phrases, and sentences, including fragments, repetition, and sentence length.
point of viewThe perspective and position from which events are perceived and narrated.
connotationThe emotional or cultural associations a word carries beyond its literal definition.
tone shiftA purposeful change in attitude or emotional direction within a passage, often signaled by diction, syntax, imagery, or new information.
