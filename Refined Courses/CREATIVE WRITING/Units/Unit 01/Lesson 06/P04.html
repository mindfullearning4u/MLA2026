P04 Worked Example

Study three ways evidence can support craft reasoning.

Example 1: Evidence from Diction

Passage: "The word sorry sat between them, small and sharp."

Claim: The apology feels painful.

Evidence: "small and sharp."

Reasoning: The diction makes the word feel like an object that can hurt.

Example 2: Evidence from Action

Passage: "Eli folded the invitation once, then again, until it fit under his watchband."

Claim: Eli wants to keep the invitation hidden but close.

Evidence: He folds it until it fits under his watchband.

Reasoning: The action shows secrecy and attachment at the same time.

Example 3: Evidence from Setting

Passage: "The classroom lights stayed on, but every desk was empty except hers."

Claim: The setting creates isolation.

Evidence: "every desk was empty except hers."

Reasoning: The contrast between many empty desks and one occupied desk makes the character seem alone.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The setting creates isolation because I think so."

Why this is weak: It gives a claim but no evidence.

Correct: "The setting creates isolation because 'every desk was empty except hers' shows the character physically separated from everyone else."

Why this works: It uses exact evidence and explains how that evidence proves the claim.
