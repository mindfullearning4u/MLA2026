Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
craft claimA focused, debatable statement about how or why a writer’s choice functions.
evidenceSpecific textual language, pattern, structure, or revision result used to support a claim.
warrantThe reasoning that explains why the evidence supports the claim.
relevanceThe degree to which evidence directly addresses the exact craft claim.
sufficiencyWhether the amount and range of evidence are enough to justify the claim.
representativenessWhether selected evidence reflects the passage as a whole rather than an exceptional detail.
counterevidenceA detail or pattern that challenges, limits, or complicates a claim.
qualificationLanguage that narrows a claim so it matches the available evidence.
causal claimA statement that one craft choice produces or contributes to a particular reader effect.
revision rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of what a revision changes, why it is preferable, and how success will be judged.
