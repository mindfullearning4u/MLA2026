Independent Work: Defend a Craft Claim

Purpose and Directions

Apply the lesson skill through evidence-based reasoning, focused original writing, and purposeful revision. Complete every numbered task and submit this work with your vocabulary notes in Notebook Evidence Submission. Quality matters more than length.

Draft for Workshop: “Platform Three”

The departure board blinked from DELAYED to ON TIME. Niko folded the letter into a square small enough to disappear inside his glove. Across the tracks, a woman in a red scarf raised one hand. The train entered the station before he could decide whether she was waving to him.

1. Build the Reasoning Chain

Complete three rows, then write a three-to-four-sentence claim explaining how two choices work together.

Record your reasoning in the submission.

2. Choose the Stronger Reasoning

Record A or B, then explain in two or three sentences why your choice makes the stronger evidence-to-effect connection. The answer is not revealed here.

A. The scene is suspenseful because it has a letter, a train, and a mysterious woman.

B. The changing departure board compresses Niko’s decision time, while the letter hidden in his glove withholds its purpose; together these choices make the arriving train interrupt an unresolved choice.

3. Apply the Skill in Your Writing

Write a six-to-eight-sentence scene built around a decision interrupted by an arrival, sound, or deadline. State your intended reader effect and coordinate at least two craft choices. Then add a three-sentence rationale quoting those choices and explaining how they support your goal.

4. Revise and Test the Claim

Revise one sentence that does not fully serve your goal. Show the original and revision, then adjust one sentence of your rationale so the evidence and reasoning match the final draft precisely.
