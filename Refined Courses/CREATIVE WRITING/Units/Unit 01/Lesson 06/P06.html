📘 CW | Unit 01 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Complete all parts in your writer's notebook or approved course document.

Instructions

Use claim, evidence, and reasoning for every answer. Do not leave a claim unsupported.

Part A

Write three craft claims about one of your own draft sentences. Each claim must name a craft effect.

Part B

For each claim, cite exact evidence from your sentence or draft. Evidence may be a word, phrase, image, action, or sentence pattern.

Part C

Choose your strongest claim and write a workshop-ready feedback paragraph that includes the claim, evidence, reasoning, and one possible revision next step.
