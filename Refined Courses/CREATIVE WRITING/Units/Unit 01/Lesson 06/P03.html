P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by learning how to avoid unsupported craft claims.

Deeper Application

Passage: "The old stage lights hummed above the empty seats. Priya stepped to the mark taped on the floor and whispered the first line twice before speaking it aloud."

Claim: Priya is preparing herself for a vulnerable performance.

Evidence: She whispers the first line twice before saying it aloud.

Reasoning: The repeated whisper suggests rehearsal, nerves, and the need to gather confidence.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Priya is nervous because the passage is good."

Why this is not enough: The claim may be reasonable, but "the passage is good" is not evidence. It does not point to a word, action, or detail.

Correct: "Priya may be nervous because she whispers the first line twice before speaking it aloud."

Teachable Explanation: The corrected response supports the claim with a specific action from the passage.

Notebook Task Part 2

Write one claim about Priya, one exact piece of evidence, and two sentences of reasoning explaining how the evidence supports the claim.

Deeper Craft Reasoning Support

This page asks you to move from knowing the term to using the skill with judgment. For Evidence-Based Craft Reasoning, pause after each model or explanation and ask: What choice did the writer make? Why does that choice fit the purpose? What would be weaker if the choice were removed or changed? This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.WR.08, MLA.CW.WR.09, MLA.CW.COM.02, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose. If the task asks you to write, draft in complete sentences and make the craft move visible rather than hidden. If the task asks you to analyze, quote or describe the specific words that prove your answer. Your answer should show a chain of reasoning, not only a label.
