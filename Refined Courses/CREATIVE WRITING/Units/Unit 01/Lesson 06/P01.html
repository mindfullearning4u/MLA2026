P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Evidence-Based Craft Reasoning

In this lesson, you will learn how to support craft claims with specific evidence so your feedback, reflections, and revision decisions are clear and defensible.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.08 - Evidence for Craft Reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.CW.WR.09 - Complex Text Craft Models; MLA.CW.COM.02 - Peer Feedback and Collaborative Techniques.

What You Will Learn

  • How to make a craft claim about a piece of writing.
  • How to cite exact evidence from a model or draft.
  • How to explain why the evidence supports the claim.
  • How to use evidence-based reasoning in workshop feedback.

What You Will Do

  • Study claim-evidence-reasoning examples.
  • Practice selecting evidence that matches a craft claim.
  • Revise unsupported feedback into evidence-based feedback.
  • Complete a checkpoint explaining craft choices with evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook includes accurate claim-evidence-reasoning notes.
  • Your practice selects evidence that actually supports a claim.
  • Your checkpoint explains a craft choice with specific evidence and reasoning.
  • Your quiz shows at least 80% mastery of evidence-based craft reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.CW.WR.08 in student language: I can use specific evidence to explain and justify my thinking about writer choices.

Evidence-based reasoning keeps workshop feedback useful. It moves a response from "I think this works" to "This works because this specific craft choice creates this effect."

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help: Ask your TOR for help if your evidence does not clearly prove the craft claim you are making.
