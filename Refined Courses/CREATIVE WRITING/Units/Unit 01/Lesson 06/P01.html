Lesson Overview: Evidence-Based Craft Reasoning

Lesson Focus

Lesson 6: Evidence-Based Craft Reasoning

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.08 — support craft claims and revision decisions with precise evidence and reasoning.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.09 — analyze complex craft models; MLA.CW.COM.02 — evaluate and use feedback through collaborative reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • Construct complete claim–evidence–warrant chains.
  • Evaluate evidence for relevance, sufficiency, representativeness, and precision.
  • Address counterevidence and distinguish causation from coincidence.
  • Convert analytical reasoning into defensible revision decisions.

Why This Matters

Writers make hundreds of choices, but “I like it better” is not a reliable revision principle. Evidence-based reasoning helps writers explain how a choice functions, test whether it serves the intended effect, and respond thoughtfully to feedback.

What You Will Do

  • Record and apply advanced reasoning vocabulary.
  • Use a six-step method for craft claims and revision decisions.
  • Study three complete examples that test evidence and counterevidence.
  • Analyze and revise a substantial independent passage.
  • Complete a different checkpoint analysis and craft transfer.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Submit Page 2 vocabulary with all Page 5 responses in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback, revise, and resubmit work below 80%.
  5. Complete the randomized five-question quiz with at least 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use precise, representative evidence rather than isolated quotation.
  • State the warrant connecting evidence to a craft claim.
  • qualify claims when counterevidence limits certainty.
  • Justify a revision by predicting and evaluating its reader effect.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can prove how a writing choice works, consider evidence that challenges my claim, and use that reasoning to make a purposeful revision.
