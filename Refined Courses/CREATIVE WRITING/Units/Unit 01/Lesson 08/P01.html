P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 1 Synthesis

This lesson reviews Unit 1 skills and prepares you for the Unit Assessment. No new primary standard is introduced.

Standards Covered

Unit 1 Primary Standards: MLA.CW.COM.02, MLA.CW.COM.03, MLA.CW.COM.04, MLA.CW.COM.05, MLA.CW.WR.05, MLA.CW.WR.08, MLA.CW.WR.09, MLA.CW.WR.10.

Support Standards: MLA.CW.WR.03, MLA.CW.REV.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 1 skills work together in creative writing.
  • How voice, tone, vocabulary, inference, evidence, and workshop communication support revision.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 1 craft vocabulary and workshop skills.
  • Practice with a synthesis guided practice.
  • Complete independent review work.
  • Submit a checkpoint before the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your review work connects multiple Unit 1 skills.
  • Your checkpoint explains craft choices with evidence and revision thinking.
  • Your Unit Assessment demonstrates at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use Unit 1 craft and communication skills together to read like a writer, explain choices, revise, and participate in workshop.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help: Ask your TOR for help before assessment if any Unit 1 skill is still unclear.
