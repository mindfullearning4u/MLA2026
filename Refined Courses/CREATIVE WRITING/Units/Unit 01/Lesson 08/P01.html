Lesson Overview: Unit 1 Synthesis

Lesson Focus

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together — Unit 1 Synthesis

Unit 1 Primary Standards: MLA.CW.COM.02, COM.03, COM.04, COM.05, WR.05, WR.08, WR.09, and WR.10.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.03 and MLA.CW.REV.01.

What You Will Learn

  • Integrate voice, tone, craft analysis, inference, evidence, communication, and revision into one coherent process.
  • Diagnose which stage of a writing process needs attention rather than revising at random.
  • Defend craft decisions with evidence, counterevidence, and reader-impact criteria.
  • Transfer unit concepts into an original, polished scene and reflective rationale.

Why This Matters

Writers rarely use one skill at a time. A purposeful draft aligns audience and voice, controls what readers infer, uses evidence to guide revision, and improves through respectful feedback. Synthesis turns separate tools into a repeatable practice.

What You Will Do

  • Review and apply twelve high-leverage Unit 1 terms.
  • Learn an integrated seven-stage writing-and-revision cycle.
  • Study three complete synthesis examples.
  • Analyze, revise, and extend a substantial independent passage.
  • Complete a distinct checkpoint before the Unit 1 Final Assessment.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Submit Page 2 vocabulary and all Page 5 work in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback and resubmit work below 80%.
  5. After both submissions are complete and passed, take the Unit 1 Final Assessment. There is no separate Lesson 8 quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Analyze interacting craft choices with precise evidence.
  • Evaluate competing interpretations and revision options.
  • Create a polished original scene with controlled voice, inference, and structure.
  • Explain how feedback and evidence shaped final decisions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can plan, analyze, draft, receive feedback, revise, and defend a creative-writing piece by combining the major skills from Unit 1.
