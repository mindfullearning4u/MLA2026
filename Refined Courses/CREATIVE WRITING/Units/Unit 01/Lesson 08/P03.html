Continue reviewing by applying multiple Unit 1 skills to one short model.

Synthesis Passage

Passage: "The envelope waited under the lamp. Joel walked past it three times before turning the light off."

Step 1: Paraphrase the literal action.

Step 2: Infer what Joel may be avoiding.

Step 3: Cite evidence.

Step 4: Explain the craft effect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Joel is a terrible person."

Why this is not supported: The passage shows avoidance, but it does not give enough evidence for a personal judgment.

Correct: "Joel may be avoiding the envelope because he passes it three times and turns off the light instead of opening it."

Why this works: The inference is supported by exact actions.
