Synthesis Passage

“The last bus left Maren at the county line with a cardboard box and an address written in her own handwriting. The house beyond the field had one lit window. She crossed the ditch, stopped at the mailbox, and removed the nameplate before continuing. At the porch she heard a piano repeat the same unfinished scale. Maren placed the nameplate inside the box. She knocked only after the music stopped.”

Analysis

  1. Write a 70–90 word accurate paraphrase.
  2. State the intended reader effect and identify the audience knowledge boundary.
  3. Analyze voice and tone through two specific language choices.
  4. Trace the object pattern involving address, nameplate, and box.
  5. Make a calibrated inference about Maren’s relationship to the house using three details.
  6. Present counterevidence or an alternative interpretation.
  7. Write a claim–evidence–warrant analysis of the unfinished piano scale.
  8. Explain how sequence controls suspense.

Revision and Reflection

  1. Propose one revision to increase clarity and one to increase ambiguity. Explain each tradeoff.
  2. Write a goal-aligned peer-feedback message that includes evidence, two options, a confirmation check, and writer ownership.
  3. Revise one sentence and provide a hypothesis and measurable success criterion.
  4. Write a 180–220 word Unit 1 reflection explaining how voice, inference, evidence, feedback, and revision now function as one process. Cite specific work from this lesson.
