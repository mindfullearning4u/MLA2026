Step 1: Synthesis Model

Model: "The notebook stayed open on the desk, but every line after her name had been erased."

Craft Thinking: Diction, object detail, and inference work together. The erased lines suggest revision, uncertainty, or a change in identity without explaining the whole situation.

Notebook Teaching Support

Then write one sentence that tells how the term changes a draft or changes the reader's understanding. This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.WR.03, MLA.CW.REV.01, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose.
