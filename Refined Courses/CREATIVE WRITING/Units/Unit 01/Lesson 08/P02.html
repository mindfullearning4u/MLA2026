Vocabulary and Definitions: Unit 1 Review

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
audienceThe readers a writer anticipates and whose knowledge, expectations, and needs shape choices.
purposeThe intended work of a text, such as revealing, questioning, unsettling, persuading, or entertaining.
voiceThe distinctive presence created by diction, syntax, detail, perspective, and attitude.
toneThe writer’s or narrator’s attitude toward subject and audience.
craft interactionThe way multiple choices reinforce, complicate, or counteract one another.
subtextMeaning implied beneath the literal action or language.
calibrated inferenceA conclusion whose certainty matches the strength and limits of evidence.
warrantReasoning that connects textual evidence to a craft claim.
counterevidenceA detail that challenges, limits, or complicates an interpretation.
goal-aligned feedbackSpecific response evaluated against the writer’s intended effect.
revision rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of what changed, why, and how success is judged.
synthesisThe integration of multiple skills or ideas into a coherent new understanding or product.
