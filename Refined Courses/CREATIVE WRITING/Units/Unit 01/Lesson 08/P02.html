Step 1: Synthesis Model

Model: "The notebook stayed open on the desk, but every line after her name had been erased."

Craft Thinking: Diction, object detail, and inference work together. The erased lines suggest revision, uncertainty, or a change in identity without explaining the whole situation.

Self-Paced Learning Path

If you need remedial support: Review one lesson at a time. Start with vocabulary, then voice and tone, then inference, then evidence, then workshop feedback.

If you are ready for standard work: Use one synthesis passage to practice all four moves: paraphrase, infer, cite evidence, and give revision feedback.

If you are ready for accelerated work: Revise your own Unit 1 passage and explain how the revision improves voice, tone, evidence, and reader inference.
