P04 Worked Example

Review three synthesis examples before the Unit Assessment.

Example 1: Voice and Tone

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Line: "Obviously, the universe had scheduled my disaster for third period."

Explanation: The sarcastic voice and exaggerated diction create a humorous tone.

Example 2: Inference and Evidence

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Line: "The invitation stayed folded under his watchband."

Explanation: The evidence suggests the invitation matters because he hides it but keeps it close.

Example 3: Workshop Feedback

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Feedback: "The object detail creates mystery because the envelope is visible but unopened. You might add one action showing whether Joel wants to read it."

Explanation: The feedback names craft, cites evidence, explains effect, and gives a revision option.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The writing is good because I like it."

Correct: "The writing creates suspense because the envelope is shown but not opened, making the reader wonder what it contains."

Why this works: The corrected response uses evidence and explains reader effect.

Teachable Explanation: The correct example works because it applies the lesson skill deliberately and explains the reasoning instead of only naming an answer.
