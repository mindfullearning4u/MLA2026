Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
observationA directly verifiable detail from the text, stated without interpretation.
inferenceA conclusion supported by one or more textual observations and logical reasoning.
speculationA possibility that extends beyond what the available evidence can reasonably establish.
evidence convergenceThe strengthening of an inference when several independent details point toward the same conclusion.
evidence hierarchyA ranking of details by relevance, specificity, consistency, and explanatory power.
calibrated claimAn interpretation whose certainty matches the strength and limits of its evidence.
contradictionA detail or statement that conflicts with another and may reveal tension, concealment, or change.
omissionInformation deliberately withheld so absence itself shapes meaning or reader questions.
intentional ambiguityTwo or more supported meanings preserved deliberately without making the passage incoherent.
overdeterminationProviding so many explicit clues or explanations that the reader has no meaningful interpretive work to do.
