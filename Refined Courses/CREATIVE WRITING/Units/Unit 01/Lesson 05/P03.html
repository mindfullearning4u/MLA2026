P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by distinguishing supported inference from unsupported guessing.

Deeper Application

Craft Model: "Jonah laughed before anyone else did. Then he checked the doorway."

Literal Details: Jonah laughs early, then looks at the doorway.

Possible Inference: Jonah may be nervous about being watched or judged.

Evidence: The doorway check suggests he is aware of someone outside the immediate group.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Jonah is definitely a spy from another country."

Why this is not supported: The passage gives nervous behavior, but it does not include evidence about spying, countries, or secret missions.

Correct: "Jonah may feel nervous or watched because he laughs early and then checks the doorway."

Why this works: The correct inference stays close to the details that are actually present.

Notebook Task Part 2

Write a two-sentence scene that implies a character's emotion without naming the emotion. Under it, write the emotion you wanted the reader to infer and identify the details that support the inference.
