Independent Work: Read Like a Writer

Purpose and Directions

Trace a craft pattern across a complete model, choose the stronger analysis, and transfer one technique into your own writing. Complete every numbered task and submit this work with your vocabulary notes in Notebook Evidence Submission.

Craft Model: “The Last Light”

At closing time, Mr. Vale switched off each lamp except the one above the empty reading chair. The room narrowed into shadow, but the small circle of light remained.

June stood in the doorway with her coat buttoned. “You forgot one,” she said.

“No,” he answered, placing the returned book beneath the lamp. “That one remembers the way home.” Outside, the bus sighed at the curb. June stepped back into the room.

1. Trace the Pattern

Complete the organizer with three connected observations. Then write two or three sentences explaining how the passage changes from closing and departure toward return or possibility.

Pattern or contrastExact evidenceMeaning or effect
Record three connected observations in your submission.

2. Choose the Stronger Reading

Record A or B, then explain why your choice traces the whole model instead of isolating one detail. The answer is not revealed here.

A. The lamp means hope because light is always a symbol of hope.

B. The remaining lamp resists the room’s growing darkness, and June’s final movement back inside turns that contrast into a possible choice to stay or return.

3. Apply a Transferable Technique

Write a seven-to-nine-sentence scene about someone deciding whether to leave or return. Borrow one technique—not the characters or plot—from the model: a repeated image, a contrast that develops, or a final action that changes how an earlier detail is understood. State your intended effect before drafting.

4. Revise and Explain

Revise one sentence or final action so the craft pattern becomes clearer without explaining the meaning directly. Show the original and revised version, then explain how the revision strengthens the pattern and reader effect.

Before You Submit

  • I traced a pattern across the complete model.
  • I explained my A/B choice with evidence.
  • My original scene transfers a technique rather than copying the model.
  • I made and explained one purposeful revision.
