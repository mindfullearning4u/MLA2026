Independent Work

Instructions

Use the embedded model below for all parts. Do not look for another passage.

Craft Model: "Under the bridge, the river carried leaves, bottle caps, and one red glove. Tomas watched the glove spin away until it became just another bright thing the water had taken."

Part A

Paraphrase the model accurately in your own words. Keep the meaning and important details.

Part B

Identify two craft choices in the model. For each one, explain what the reader can infer from that choice.

Part C

Write a three-to-five sentence original imitation that uses one small object to suggest a larger feeling or situation. Then name the craft move you borrowed.

Independent Work Success Checklist

For the independent work in Reading Complex Craft Models, produce evidence that another reader can follow. Start by restating the task in your own words. Then plan the response, draft the required writing or analysis, and revise for the exact standard focus: MLA.CW.WR.09, MLA.CW.WR.03, MLA.CW.WR.10. This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.WR.09, MLA.CW.WR.03, MLA.CW.WR.10, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose. If the task asks you to write, draft in complete sentences and make the craft move visible rather than hidden. If the task asks you to analyze, quote or describe the specific words that prove your answer. Before submitting, check that your response includes the required passage, model, draft excerpt, or scenario evidence from this page and that every sentence helps prove mastery.
