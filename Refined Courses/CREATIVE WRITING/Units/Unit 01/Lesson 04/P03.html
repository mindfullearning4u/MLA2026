P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue by practicing paraphrase, inference, and craft reasoning.

Deeper Application

Craft Model: "The kitchen clock clicked through the silence. On the table, two mugs cooled beside a plate no one had touched."

Step 1: Paraphrase the meaning. Two people seem to be in or near a quiet kitchen, and untouched food suggests tension or absence.

Step 2: Identify craft choices. The writer uses sound imagery with the clock and visual imagery with the untouched mugs and plate.

Step 3: Infer purpose. The writer suggests conflict without stating it directly.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The passage means people are drinking coffee and nothing else."

Why this is not enough: This paraphrase is too shallow. It notices objects but misses the silence, the cooling mugs, and the untouched plate.

Correct: "The passage shows a quiet kitchen where two mugs and untouched food suggest that something tense or unresolved has happened."

Why this works: The correct version keeps the literal details and includes a reasonable inference based on those details.
