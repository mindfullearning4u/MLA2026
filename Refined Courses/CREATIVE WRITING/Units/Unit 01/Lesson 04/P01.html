Lesson Overview: Reading Complex Craft Models

Lesson Focus

Lesson 4: Reading Complex Craft Models

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.09 — analyze complex mentor texts as models of deliberate craft.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.03 — paraphrase craft models accurately; MLA.CW.WR.10 — draw evidence-based inferences about craft decisions.

What You Will Learn

  • Separate a model’s literal situation from its implied emotional or thematic meaning.
  • Trace how several craft choices work together rather than naming devices in isolation.
  • Explain why a writer’s choice is effective for a particular purpose and audience.
  • Transfer a craft principle into original writing without copying the model’s wording or subject.

Why This Matters

Strong writers read like apprentices. They study how published-quality passages create effects, test those techniques in new contexts, and make purposeful choices of their own. Accurate reading prevents imitation from becoming surface-level copying.

What You Will Do

  • Record and use lesson vocabulary.
  • Learn a five-stage method for analyzing complex craft models.
  • Study three complete worked examples with visible reasoning and original transfer.
  • Analyze a substantial independent passage and create a controlled imitation.
  • Complete a different checkpoint passage and transfer task.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Complete Page 5 and submit its responses with Page 2 vocabulary in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Complete Page 6 and submit those responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit below 80%.
  5. Complete the five-question randomized quiz; at least 80% is required.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Paraphrase complex models without adding unsupported meaning.
  • Build an evidence → craft choice → interaction → purpose chain.
  • Compare plausible interpretations and defend the stronger one.
  • Transfer a craft principle into original prose and explain the adaptation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read a challenging passage closely, explain how its choices work together, and adapt a craft principle in original writing.
