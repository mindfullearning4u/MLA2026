P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Reading Complex Craft Models

In this lesson, you will read short complex craft models as a writer. Your goal is not to complete literature analysis as an endpoint. Your goal is to notice how a model works so you can learn craft moves for your own writing.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.09 - Complex Text Craft Models.

Support Standards: MLA.CW.WR.03 - Paraphrase Craft Models; MLA.CW.WR.10 - Inference for Craft Decisions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to read a short complex passage as a craft model.
  • How to paraphrase a model's meaning accurately before discussing craft.
  • How to infer why a writer used a specific detail, image, sentence pattern, or point of view.
  • How to transfer a craft move into your own original writing.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded short craft models.
  • Paraphrase what each model says.
  • Identify craft choices and infer their purpose.
  • Write a short original imitation using one craft move from a model.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook paraphrases model passages accurately.
  • Your responses identify craft choices and infer writer purpose.
  • Your checkpoint applies one craft move in original writing.
  • Your quiz shows at least 80% mastery of reading craft models.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.CW.WR.09 in student language: I can read a challenging model closely enough to understand what it says and how its craft choices work.

Creative writers read models to study choices. A model can teach you how to open a scene, create tension, compress time, build imagery, or suggest emotion without explaining everything directly.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help: Ask your TOR for help if you can summarize what happens but cannot explain how the model creates its effect.
