P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can read a craft model, paraphrase it, infer craft purpose, and transfer a craft move into original writing.

TOR Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your paraphrase changes the meaning or if your inference is not supported by details in the model.

Submission Workflow

Complete the checkpoint in your notebook or approved course document. Make sure the model, paraphrase, inference, and original imitation are all included. Submit it in the assigned Moodle submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Craft Model: "The ticket stayed in her coat pocket all winter. By spring, the ink had faded, but Leah could still feel the route number pressed into the paper."

Step 1: Paraphrase the model accurately.

Step 2: Identify one craft choice and explain what the reader can infer from it.

Step 3: Write a three-to-five sentence original imitation using an object that carries emotional meaning.

Step 4: Explain the craft move you transferred from the model.

Mastery Criteria

  • The paraphrase keeps the model's meaning accurate.
  • The inference is supported by specific model details.
  • The original imitation uses a craft move from the model.
  • The explanation connects reading a model to creative writing practice.
