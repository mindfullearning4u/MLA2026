Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
craft modelA text studied not only for what it says but for how deliberate choices create meaning and reader effect.
literal situationThe observable action, setting, or circumstance stated directly in a passage.
subtextMeaning suggested beneath the literal words through implication, omission, contrast, or pattern.
compressionThe concentration of substantial meaning into few words or carefully selected details.
patternA meaningful recurrence of words, images, structures, sounds, or ideas.
contrastThe placement of unlike elements near each other to sharpen a difference or tension.
juxtapositionThe deliberate side-by-side placement of elements so their relationship produces meaning.
craft interactionThe way two or more choices reinforce, complicate, or counteract one another.
interpretive claimA defensible statement about meaning or purpose supported by specific textual evidence.
transferAdapting a craft principle to a new subject, situation, and wording in original writing.
