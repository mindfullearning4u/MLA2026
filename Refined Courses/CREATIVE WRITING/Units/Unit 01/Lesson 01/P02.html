Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your writer’s notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
creative writingOriginal writing shaped through deliberate choices in language, image, structure, voice, character, and form to create an intended effect.
writer’s notebookA working record of observations, questions, experiments, drafts, feedback, and revision plans—not merely a collection of finished work.
workshopA structured exchange in which writers share drafts, listen actively, discuss craft evidence, and use feedback to make their own revision decisions.
craft choiceA deliberate decision about diction, imagery, detail, structure, voice, tone, pacing, character, dialogue, or form.
feedbackA specific response that identifies draft evidence, explains its effect, and offers a focused possibility for revision.
active listeningAttentive listening that seeks to understand the writer’s purpose before responding, interrupting, or proposing changes.
reader effectThe thought, feeling, question, expectation, or interpretation produced by a particular craft choice.
revisionPurposeful re-seeing and reshaping of a draft to strengthen meaning, effect, structure, voice, or clarity; it is more than correcting errors.
