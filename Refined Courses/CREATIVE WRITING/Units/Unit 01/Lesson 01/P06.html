📘 CW | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your writer's notebook. Use complete sentences unless the direction asks for a list.

Instructions

Use your notes from P02-P04. Your goal is to show that you understand the writer's notebook, workshop norms, and useful feedback language.

Part A

Write five items that belong in a writer's notebook. For each item, explain how it could help a writer create, revise, or reflect.

Part B

Write one original four-to-six sentence scene starter. Then write two workshop questions you could ask a peer about that scene. The questions must focus on craft, not whether the peer likes the writing.

Part C

Rewrite the comment "I liked it" into a useful workshop response. Your revised response must name a craft choice, explain its effect, and suggest one possible next step.
