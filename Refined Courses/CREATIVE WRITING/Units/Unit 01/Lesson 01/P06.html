Checkpoint Draft: “Platform Nine”

The announcement board changed from ON TIME to DELAYED, but Nia did not sit down. She kept one hand on the handle of her suitcase and watched the tunnel where the rails disappeared.

Her father’s last message waited unopened on her phone. Around her, travelers rearranged bags, bought coffee, and complained into glowing screens. Nia counted the red tiles along the platform edge. Twenty-three. She counted again.

A train entered on the opposite track, pushing warm air and paper wrappers through the station. For one second, her reflection appeared in its dark windows beside rows of strangers. Then the cars moved on, and the glass returned her to herself.

Checkpoint Requirements

  1. State the situation and the decision or conflict the draft suggests.
  2. Quote two details that reveal Nia’s internal state without naming it directly.
  3. Explain the reader effect of the repeated counting.
  4. Analyze how the moving train and reflected image contribute to mood or meaning.
  5. Identify one ambiguity the writer should probably preserve and explain why.
  6. Write a complete workshop response about one strong craft choice using evidence, craft terminology, and reader effect.
  7. Write a second response identifying one focused area for development and offer a respectful, optional revision move.
  8. Write one clarifying question that would help you understand the writer’s purpose before recommending a major change.
  9. Rewrite the weak comment “This needs more emotion” into specific, usable feedback.
  10. Explain in three to four sentences why your revised feedback supports the writer’s ownership and is more effective than the weak comment.

80% Mastery Requirement

You must earn at least 80%. If you earn below 80%, review your TOR’s feedback, correct your mistakes, and resubmit. Meet with your TOR for additional help when needed.
