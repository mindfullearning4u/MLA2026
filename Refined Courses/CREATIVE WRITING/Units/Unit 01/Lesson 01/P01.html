P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Creative Writing Identity and Workshop Norms

In this lesson, you will begin the course by learning what creative writers do, how a writer's notebook supports creative growth, and how workshop norms help writers give and receive useful feedback.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.02 - Peer Feedback and Collaborative Techniques.

Support Standards: MLA.CW.COM.04 - Academic Language for ELLs; MLA.CW.COM.05 - Social and Instructional Communication for ELLs.

What You Will Learn

  • How creative writing differs from simply completing a school assignment.
  • How a writer's notebook becomes a place for ideas, observations, drafts, questions, and revision plans.
  • How workshop norms protect writer confidence while still helping writing improve.
  • How to use specific, respectful feedback language during a writing discussion.

What You Will Do

  • Set up your writer's notebook for course use.
  • Study model workshop comments and decide which ones are useful to a writer.
  • Practice naming craft choices instead of judging a writer personally.
  • Complete a checkpoint showing that you understand workshop expectations.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains what belongs in a writer's notebook and why.
  • Your practice responses use respectful workshop language.
  • Your checkpoint identifies helpful feedback and revises weak feedback into useful feedback.
  • Your quiz responses show at least 80% mastery of workshop norms and collaboration expectations.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.CW.COM.02 in student language: I can listen actively and use respectful feedback to help myself and other writers improve.

The workshop standard matters because creative writing grows through practice, response, revision, and reflection. A useful workshop comment focuses on the writing, names a specific craft choice, and gives the writer a clear next step.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you are unsure how to set up your notebook, how to respond respectfully to another writer, or how to turn a general comment into specific feedback.
