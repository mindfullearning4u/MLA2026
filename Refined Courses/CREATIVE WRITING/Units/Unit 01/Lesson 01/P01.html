Lesson Overview: Creative Writing Identity and Workshop Norms

Lesson Focus

Lesson 1: Creative Writing Identity and Workshop Norms

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.02 — Peer Feedback and Collaborative Techniques.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.04 — Academic Language for ELLs; MLA.CW.COM.05 — Social and Instructional Communication for ELLs.

What You Will Learn

  • Explain how creative writers collect, develop, test, and revise ideas.
  • Use a writer’s notebook as evidence of observation, experimentation, and reflection.
  • Distinguish useful craft feedback from praise, judgment, commands, and vague advice.
  • Use specific evidence and respectful academic language during a workshop.

Why This Matters

Creative writing helps you communicate ideas, experiences, and perspectives with purpose. Each lesson strengthens your ability to read like a writer, make deliberate craft choices, revise thoughtfully, and explain how those choices affect a reader. These skills build creative confidence, clear communication, and responsible participation in a writing community.

What You Will Do

  • Learn and apply the lesson vocabulary.
  • Follow step-by-step instruction and study worked examples.
  • Practice through interactive checks and guided application.
  • Complete Independent Work to apply the lesson skills.
  • Complete a separate Checkpoint to demonstrate your understanding.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Complete Page 5 and submit it with your Page 2 vocabulary in Notebook Evidence Submission.
  3. Complete Page 6 and submit it separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit any work scoring below 80%.
  5. Complete the assessment listed after the lesson. A score of at least 80% is required.
