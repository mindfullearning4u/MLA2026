📘 CW | Unit 05 | Lesson 04

P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can prepare a piece of writing in a format that supports quality and reader understanding.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you are not sure which format fits your piece, if your formatting makes the piece harder to read, or if you cannot explain why a format choice helps.

Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one short piece of creative writing.
  2. Identify the format you are using.
  3. Revise the format so it supports meaning and readability.
  4. Write an explanation of one format choice.
  5. Submit the formatted piece and explanation in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a formatted creative-writing excerpt and a short explanation. The explanation must name the format, identify one format choice, and explain how that choice improves publication quality.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies a clear creative-writing format.
  • Uses format features that fit the selected genre.
  • Improves clarity, pacing, tone, speaker clarity, or readability.
  • Avoids random decorative formatting.
  • Explains how one format choice improves publication quality.
