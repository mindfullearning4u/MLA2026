Worked Example: Three Format Transformations

Example 1 — Prose to Poem

Prose sentence: “At dawn, the empty school hallway seemed to wait for the first student.” Poem formatting breaks after “wait” to suspend the action and isolates “the first student” for emphasis. The words remain similar, but lineation changes pacing and attention.

Example 2 — Prose to Script

Narration: “Mina hides the letter when her brother enters.” Script:
INT. KITCHEN—NIGHT
MINA folds the letter beneath a towel.
JON enters.
JON
You’re still awake.
Action becomes visible and playable; no narrator explains motive.

Example 3 — Accessibility Repair

Weak portfolio page uses pale gray text, colored labels with no words, and “click here.” Repair increases contrast, adds written labels, uses descriptive link text (“Open audio performance”), preserves heading order, and supplies a transcript. Access improves without changing the creative work.

Verification Step

Always inspect the exported or uploaded version. A correct editor view can still produce broken page breaks, missing fonts, or inaccessible media.
