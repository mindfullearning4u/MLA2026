📘 CW | Unit 05 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one piece of your own writing or the model excerpt provided here.

Instructions

Choose a format that fits the piece. Your goal is not to make the page look unusual. Your goal is to make the format help the reader understand the writing.

Model excerpt: I waited outside the rehearsal room. The music stopped. I raised my hand to knock, then lowered it.

Part A: Identify the Format

Name the format you are using: poem, prose story, script, nonfiction reflection, or another clear creative-writing format. Write one sentence explaining why that format fits the piece.

Part B: Revise the Formatting

Revise at least three format features. You may revise line breaks, paragraph breaks, dialogue punctuation, stage directions, spacing, stanza breaks, headings, or another format feature that fits your piece.

Part C: Explain the Format Choice

Write a short explanation that names one format choice and explains how it improves reader understanding, pacing, tone, speaker clarity, or publication quality.
