Lesson Overview: Format-Specific Quality Work

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.PUB.02.
Supporting: MLA.CW.REV.02 and MLA.CW.WR.05. Prepare poetry, prose, scripts, and reflective material according to the conventions and reader needs of each format.

Learning Goals

  • Distinguish genre conventions from optional design preferences.
  • Use hierarchy, spacing, lineation, paragraphing, dialogue, and script cues purposefully.
  • Adapt one text across formats without losing meaning.
  • Audit readability, accessibility, consistency, and technical submission quality.

Workflow

Vocabulary → format audit and comparison map → three transformations → 18-part Independent Work → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz.

Why This Matters

Format is part of communication. It tells readers how to move through the work, identifies speakers and shifts, preserves poetic decisions, and signals professional care.
