Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and full definition. Add an original example for visual hierarchy, white space, lineation, stage direction, accessibility, and submission specification. Submit with Page 5.
formatThe structural and visual form used to present a work.
genre conventionA common expectation associated with a type of writing.
manuscriptA draft prepared for editing, submission, or publication.
visual hierarchyDesign cues showing the order and importance of information.
white spaceUnprinted area used to separate and organize content.
lineationIntentional shaping of poetic lines.
paragraphingDivision of prose into meaningful units.
dialogue tagWords identifying a speaker or manner of speech.
stage directionA script instruction for action, setting, or delivery.
consistencyReliable use of the same formatting rules.
accessibilityDesign enabling people with varied needs to use the work.
submission specificationA required technical or formatting condition.
