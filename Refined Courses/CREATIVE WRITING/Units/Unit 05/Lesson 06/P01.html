Lesson Overview: Author Presentation of Creative Work

Lesson Focus

Lesson 6: Author Presentation of Creative Work

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.01 — Author presentation of creative work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.08 — Reflection on creative process and product; MLA.CW.COM.03 — Communication of craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • Frame a creative work for an audience without explaining away its meaning.
  • Select two or three craft choices worth discussing.
  • Connect revision evidence to purpose and reader effect.
  • Deliver a concise, confident author introduction and reflection.

Six-Page Workflow

PagePurpose
1–2Orient and record vocabulary.
3–4Learn the presentation architecture and study three models.
5Create and submit your author presentation plan.
6Complete a distinct mastery checkpoint.

Mastery Target

I can introduce a creative work, explain precise craft decisions with evidence, and show how revision strengthened the intended audience effect. A score of at least 80% is required.
