P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can present creative work using purpose, craft reasoning, and revision reflection.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your presentation is mostly summary, if you cannot identify craft choices, or if your revision explanation does not show how the piece improved.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one revised creative piece or excerpt.
  2. Write the title, form, and purpose.
  3. Explain two craft choices.
  4. Explain one revision decision.
  5. Submit the author presentation plan in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit an author presentation plan that includes the title and form, intended purpose, audience frame, two craft explanations, and one revision reflection.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies title, form, and purpose clearly.
  • Frames the audience without over-explaining the piece.
  • Explains two craft choices with reader effects.
  • Describes one revision decision and why it improved the work.
  • Uses clear, respectful, presentation-ready language.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
