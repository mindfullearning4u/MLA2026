Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
author statementA brief explanation that frames a creative work and identifies purposeful craft decisions.
framingThe context an author gives an audience before presenting a work.
craft choiceA deliberate decision about language, structure, imagery, voice, pacing, or form.
audience effectThe response, question, feeling, or understanding a writer intends to create.
rationaleA clear explanation of why a decision was made.
excerptA short selected passage from a longer work.
deliveryThe use of pace, volume, emphasis, posture, and eye contact while presenting.
reflectionCareful thinking about a process, its results, and possible next steps.
