P04 Worked Example

Study how an author presentation moves from purpose to craft reasoning.

Example 1: Opening the Presentation

Creative work: A short prose piece titled "After the Rain."

Step 1: Name the title and form: "After the Rain" is a short prose piece.

Step 2: State the purpose: The piece shows a character who is safe but still unsure how to move forward.

Step 3: Avoid retelling every event. Give the audience a clear entry point.

Example 2: Explaining Craft Choices

Craft choice 1: The repeated umbrella image.

Reasoning: I repeated the umbrella image so the reader could see the character holding onto protection even after the rain stopped.

Craft choice 2: Short final sentence.

Reasoning: I used a short final sentence so the ending would feel quiet rather than fully resolved.

Example 3: Explaining Revision

Feedback: The first ending explained the character's feelings too directly.

Revision: I removed the sentence that named the feeling and ended with the character folding the umbrella halfway.

Why it improved the piece: The revised ending lets the action show uncertainty instead of telling the reader exactly what to think.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: My piece is about rain, then a driveway, then an umbrella, and then the character thinks about things.

This is incorrect because it lists events without explaining purpose, craft choices, or revision decisions.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: I used the umbrella image to show hesitation, and I revised the ending so the action suggests the character's uncertainty instead of explaining it directly.

This is correct because it explains craft reasoning and revision impact.
