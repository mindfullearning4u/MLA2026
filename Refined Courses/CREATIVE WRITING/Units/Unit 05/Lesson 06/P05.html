Independent Work: Author Presentation of Creative Work

Purpose

Present creative work with purposeful selection, clear context, expressive delivery planning, and respect for the audience.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Use precise evidence from your own work and the source provided.

Source for Your Work

Presentation situation: You have three minutes to introduce and read part of a piece to an audience unfamiliar with your work. Your goal is to create context without explaining away the piece.

1. Plan Before You Work

DecisionYour Plan
Purpose and intended audienceState each clearly.
Most important craft or communication needName the need and evidence.
Success indicatorDescribe what an effective result will do for a reader.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach demonstrates stronger decision-making? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Summarize every event and explain the theme before reading so the audience cannot misunderstand.
B. Offer only the context the audience needs, select a revealing excerpt, and plan delivery choices that support the writing.

3. Apply the Lesson

Create a three-minute author-presentation script containing a concise introduction, a 250–350 word excerpt or 20–28 lines, and a closing reflection. Add delivery annotations for pace, pause, emphasis, and eye contact.

4. Revise with Purpose

Read the script aloud. Revise one transition and two delivery annotations, then explain how the changes improve audience understanding or engagement.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your work applies the lesson skill to a specific creative product.
  • Your explanation identifies a decision and its effect on readers.
  • Your revision meaningfully improves the work, not only its appearance.
