P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one creative piece or excerpt you have revised.

Instructions

Prepare an author presentation plan. You are not writing a book report or a plot summary. You are explaining the purpose, craft, and revision behind your own creative work.

Part A: Purpose and Audience Frame

Write the title and form of your piece. Then write one sentence explaining the intended effect. Add one audience frame beginning with "As you read, notice..."

Part B: Craft Reasoning

Choose two craft choices. For each one, write a sentence that explains what you used and how it affects the reader.

Part C: Revision Reflection

Name one revision you made after feedback or rereading. Explain what changed and why the final version is stronger.
