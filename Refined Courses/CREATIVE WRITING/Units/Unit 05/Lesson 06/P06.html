Checkpoint: Presentation Reasoning Under Pressure

Directions

Complete this checkpoint independently. It uses a new scenario and is separate from the Independent Work. Answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

New Scenario

Writer Jules presents a flash-fiction piece about a night security guard who repeatedly passes a locked greenhouse. In the final draft, the sentence “At 2:17, the glass breathed once” appears three times. The first two repetitions are identical. In the third, Jules revises it to: “At 2:17, the glass breathed his name.” An earlier draft explained that the guard feared being forgotten. Jules deleted that explanation. During rehearsal, Jules reads every sentence at the same speed and spends most of the introduction retelling the plot.

Flash fiction is an extremely brief story. Cadence means the rhythmic rise, fall, and timing of spoken language.

Analysis

  1. State the likely creative aim in one precise sentence.
  2. Explain how repetition functions structurally.
  3. Analyze the effect of changing only the third repetition.
  4. Explain why deleting the fear explanation may strengthen the piece.
  5. Identify one risk created by the phrase “breathed his name.”

Presentation Decisions

  1. Write a two-sentence opening that frames the work without plot summary.
  2. Select the strongest excerpt Jules should read and justify the choice.
  3. Write one evidence-based craft explanation Jules could deliver.
  4. Mark a pause and a word for emphasis; explain how the delivery supports meaning.
  5. Recommend one cut from the introduction and explain why.

Synthesis

  1. Write a 75–100-word author statement connecting repetition, revision, and audience effect.
  2. Evaluate this claim: “If an author intended an effect, the effect is automatically successful.” Agree, disagree, or qualify it using evidence from the scenario.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review the feedback, revise misconceptions, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
