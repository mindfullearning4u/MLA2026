P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts. Use a draft excerpt from this lesson or one of your own creative drafts.

Instructions

Keep the original draft, feedback note, revision goal, revised draft, and explanation together so your revision process is visible.

Part A: Sort Feedback

Write three feedback notes: one adult-style note, one peer-style note, and one online-tool-style note. Label each as use now, adapt, or set aside.

Part B: Revise

Choose one feedback note and revise 4-8 lines or sentences from a creative draft.

Part C: Explain

Write 5-7 sentences explaining which feedback you used, what you changed, and how the revision improved purpose, clarity, structure, or style.
