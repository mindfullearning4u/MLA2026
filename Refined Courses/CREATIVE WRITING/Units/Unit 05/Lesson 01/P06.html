Creative Writing | Unit 5 | Lesson 1 | Page 6 of 6

Checkpoint: Feedback and Revision Decisions

Directions

Complete all 12 tasks independently and submit separately. At least 80% is required.

Draft and Feedback

Draft: “Mara entered the abandoned clinic. She was scared. She found the file and learned the truth.”
Adult: “Clarify why the file matters before she enters.” Peer: “Slow down the discovery.” Online: “Add a monster—it would be cooler.”

Tasks

  1. State the likely writing goal.
  2. Classify each comment.
  3. Evaluate each source’s relevance.
  4. Locate evidence for the adult concern.
  5. Locate evidence for the peer concern.
  6. Explain why the online advice may conflict with purpose.
  7. Identify a shared underlying problem.
  8. Write two revision hypotheses.
  9. Revise the passage to 180–220 words.
  10. Show three before/after choices.
  11. Create a concise change log.
  12. Write a 180–220 word defense of the decisions.

80% Mastery Requirement

Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit if needed.
