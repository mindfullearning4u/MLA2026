Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and full definition. Add one example for diagnosis, suggestion, criterion, revision hypothesis, and change log. Submit with Page 5.
feedbackInformation about how a reader experiences a draft.
reader responseA description of a reader’s reaction or understanding.
diagnosisAn explanation of the underlying craft problem.
suggestionA proposed way to revise.
criterionA standard used to judge effectiveness.
authorial purposeThe writer’s intended meaning or effect.
triageSorting feedback by urgency, value, and scope.
feedback patternA concern repeated by independent readers.
conflicting feedbackRecommendations pointing toward incompatible revisions.
revision hypothesisA testable prediction about how a change will improve the draft.
change logA record of feedback, decisions, revisions, and results.
source credibilityThe trustworthiness and relevant expertise of a feedback source.
