Independent Work: Revision Case File

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks using a substantial creative draft. Submit with Page 2 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Tasks

  1. State genre, audience, purpose, and controlling effect.
  2. Identify two nonnegotiable craft goals.
  3. Collect one adult, one peer, and one moderated/approved online or simulated feedback set.
  4. Record feedback exactly.
  5. Classify every comment.
  6. Evaluate each source’s credibility and expertise.
  7. Mark textual evidence for every diagnosed problem.
  8. Identify repeated patterns.
  9. Identify conflicting recommendations.
  10. Resolve one conflict through a named criterion.
  11. Write three revision hypotheses.
  12. Prioritize global before local changes.
  13. Make at least two global revisions.
  14. Make four local revisions.
  15. Show before/after excerpts.
  16. Retest with a reader or structured self-review.
  17. Create a complete change log.
  18. Write a 300–400 word reflection defending accepted, adapted, and rejected feedback.

Privacy Alternative

If live online feedback is unavailable or inappropriate, use the three teacher-provided feedback cases from Page 4 as simulated sources. Never post identifying information or private work publicly.
