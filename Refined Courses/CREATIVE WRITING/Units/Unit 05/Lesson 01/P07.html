P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can use feedback to make a targeted revision.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you cannot decide which feedback to use, or if a suggested change would remove your intended voice or purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review the original draft and feedback.
  2. Choose one targeted revision goal.
  3. Revise the draft excerpt.
  4. Submit the original, feedback, revision, and explanation in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one original draft excerpt, one useful feedback note, one revision goal, the revised excerpt, and a 5-7 sentence explanation of how feedback improved the draft.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies useful feedback accurately.
  • Creates a specific revision goal.
  • Makes a targeted revision rather than random changes.
  • Explains how the revision improves the draft.
  • Protects the writer's purpose and voice.
