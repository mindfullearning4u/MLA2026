P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Revision from Adult, Peer, and Online Feedback

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from adult, peer, and online feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.02 - Workshop collaboration and feedback; MLA.CW.WR.08 - Evidence-based craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to sort feedback into useful categories.
  • How adult, peer, and online feedback can help in different ways.
  • How to decide which feedback to accept, adapt, or set aside.
  • How to revise a creative draft with a clear purpose.

What You Will Do

  • Study feedback examples.
  • Identify feedback that is specific, useful, and aligned to the writing goal.
  • Make a revision plan.
  • Revise a short creative passage and explain the change.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can explain which feedback is useful, make a targeted revision, and justify the revision with evidence from the draft and the writing goal.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means revision is not just fixing mistakes. You are learning how to use feedback to make a creative work clearer, stronger, and closer to your purpose.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you receive feedback that conflicts, feels too general to use, or would change your draft in a way that no longer matches your purpose.
