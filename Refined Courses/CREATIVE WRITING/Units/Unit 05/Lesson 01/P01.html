Lesson Overview: Revision from Adult, Peer, and Online Feedback

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.REV.01.
Supporting: MLA.CW.COM.02 and MLA.CW.WR.08. Learn to evaluate feedback, protect authorial purpose, revise substantially, and document decisions.

Learning Goals

  • Separate reader response, diagnosis, suggestion, correction, and preference.
  • Evaluate authority, specificity, evidence, relevance, and risk.
  • Resolve conflicting feedback without surrendering ownership.
  • Use revision evidence to explain what changed and why.

Workflow

Vocabulary → feedback triage protocol and visual matrix → three worked cases → 18-part Independent Work → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz.

Why This Matters

Good revision is a reasoned decision, not automatic obedience. Writers listen for patterns, test advice against purpose and evidence, and remain responsible for the final work.
