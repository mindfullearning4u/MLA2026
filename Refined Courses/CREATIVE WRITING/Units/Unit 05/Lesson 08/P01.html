P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 5 Primary Standards: MLA.CW.REV.01, MLA.CW.REV.02, MLA.CW.PUB.01, MLA.CW.PUB.02, MLA.CW.COM.01, MLA.CW.COM.02.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03, MLA.CW.WR.05, MLA.CW.WR.08.

What You Will Learn

  • How the Unit 5 skills work together.
  • How to analyze feedback, revision, workshop, format, publication, and presentation decisions.
  • How to choose the strongest next step for a draft or portfolio piece.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 5 assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the full Unit 5 workflow.
  • Practice identifying the best revision or publication decision.
  • Use examples to prepare for assessment questions.
  • Complete the Unit 5 assessment in Moodle.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by answering assessment questions that require you to evaluate feedback, choose revision steps, identify workshop moves, prepare work for publication, and explain author decisions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson brings the unit together. You are showing that you can move from draft to feedback, revision, workshop, publication, portfolio preparation, and author presentation with clear reasons for each choice.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you cannot tell whether a question is asking about revision, workshop, publication, portfolio readiness, or author presentation.
