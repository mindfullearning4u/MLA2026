📘 CW | Unit 05 | Lesson 08

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts before taking the Unit 5 assessment.

Instructions

This review is meant to help you organize the full unit. Use your notes from Lessons 1-7 if needed.

Part A: Skill Sort

Create four headings: Revision, Workshop, Publication, and Author Presentation. Under each heading, list two actions a writer should take.

Part B: One Draft Problem

Write one draft problem, such as unclear ending, vague feedback, confusing format, or weak presentation. Then write the best next step and explain why it helps.

Part C: Assessment Readiness

Write three reminders you will use during the assessment. Each reminder should help you choose specific, purposeful, reader-focused answers.
