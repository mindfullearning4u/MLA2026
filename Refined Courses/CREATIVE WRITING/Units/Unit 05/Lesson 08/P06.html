Checkpoint: Unit 5 Transfer Challenge

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. It prepares you for the Unit 5 Final Assessment. A score of at least 80% is required.

New Case

Devon’s lyric essay alternates between recipes and memories of moving between countries. Workshop partners admire the form but report that the final recipe feels disconnected. Devon adds a paragraph explaining the entire theme, corrects every comma, and changes the page background to a photograph that makes several lines difficult to read. The online version embeds a family voice recording without a transcript or permission statement. Devon’s author presentation summarizes every memory but does not quote the essay.

Lyric essay is a nonfiction form that uses poetic language, fragments, imagery, and association. Embed means to place media directly within a digital page.

Diagnosis and Revision

  1. State the essay’s likely creative purpose.
  2. Explain why correcting commas does not resolve the central problem.
  3. Diagnose the structural weakness reported by readers.
  4. Evaluate the added explanatory paragraph.
  5. Propose a revision that connects the final recipe through image, pattern, or consequence.
  6. Name one strength the revision must preserve.

Publication and Presentation

  1. Identify three publication-readiness problems.
  2. Explain the ethical question surrounding the family recording.
  3. Recommend an accessible treatment for the recording and background.
  4. Rewrite the author-presentation opening in two sentences.
  5. Select one short excerpt Devon should quote and explain what craft reasoning should follow.

Final Synthesis

  1. Write a 150–200-word editorial recommendation that sequences the next four actions and justifies the order using Unit 5 principles.

Assessment Readiness

After your checkpoint is complete and passed, take the U5 Final Assessment. Review feedback and contact your TOR before retrying if you score below 80%.
