Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
synthesisThe integration of several ideas or skills into a new, coherent understanding or product.
diagnosisA reasoned identification of the underlying cause of a writing problem.
editorial judgmentA decision about what to revise, preserve, cut, or present based on purpose and evidence.
revision priorityA ranked change chosen for its likely effect on meaning and readers.
feedback loopA cycle of drafting, response, revision, testing, and further response.
transferThe application of a learned skill to a new situation.
publication readinessThe condition of being complete, polished, accessible, ethical, and usable by the intended audience.
defensibleSupported by clear reasons and relevant evidence.
constraintA limit or requirement that shapes a creative decision.
