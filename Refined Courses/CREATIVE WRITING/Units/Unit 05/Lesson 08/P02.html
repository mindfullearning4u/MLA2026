Notebook Title

Unit 5 Synthesis Review

Vocabulary

Revision: A meaningful change that improves purpose, clarity, structure, style, or reader effect.

Workshop: A respectful feedback process using listening, strengths, questions, and suggestions.

Publication-ready: Prepared for readers through revision, format, conventions, and appropriateness checks.

Author presentation: A clear explanation of purpose, craft choices, and revision decisions.

Step 1: Identify the Skill Being Tested

Before answering an assessment question, ask what the question is really testing. If the question includes peer comments, it may be about feedback or workshop. If it asks about line breaks, dialogue, or script labels, it may be about format. If it asks why a final piece belongs in a portfolio, it may be about portfolio readiness.

Step 2: Use the Full Workflow

The strongest answer usually follows the workflow: understand purpose, consider feedback, revise meaning or structure, check format, polish conventions, prepare readers, and explain author reasoning. Do not choose answers that skip straight from rough draft to final publication.

Step 3: Eliminate Weak Answers

Weak answers often use words like always, never, random, automatically, or no reason. Strong answers are specific, respectful, connected to purpose, and focused on reader effect.

Self-Paced Learning Path

If you need support: Identify whether each question is about revision, workshop, publication, or presentation.

If you are on pace: Explain why the correct answer fits the workflow.

If you are ready for a challenge: Explain why each incorrect answer fails.
