P04 Worked Example

Study how to answer synthesis questions that combine Unit 5 skills.

Example 1: Feedback to Revision

Question: A peer says the ending is confusing because the reader cannot tell why the character leaves. What should the writer revise first?

Step 1: Identify skill: revision from feedback.

Step 2: Identify problem: character motivation is unclear.

Step 3: Best answer: add or revise a moment that shows the character's reason for leaving.

Example 2: Format to Publication

Question: A script has no speaker labels. What should happen before publication?

Step 1: Identify skill: format-specific quality work.

Step 2: Identify problem: readers cannot track speakers.

Step 3: Best answer: add clear character labels and check stage directions before publication.

Example 3: Author Presentation

Question: Which author statement best explains craft reasoning?

Step 1: Look for a specific craft choice.

Step 2: Look for the reader effect.

Step 3: Best answer: I repeated the umbrella image so the reader could see the character holding onto protection even after the rain stopped.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Choose the answer that sounds most positive.

This is incorrect because positive language can still be vague. The correct answer must solve the writing problem or explain the craft effect.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Choose the answer that is specific, connected to the writer's purpose, and explains how the choice helps the reader.

This is correct because Unit 5 evaluates purposeful writing decisions.
