Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
portfolioA purposeful collection selected to demonstrate quality, range, and growth.The portfolio includes polished work and evidence of development.
curationThe deliberate selection, ordering, and framing of items for an audience.The writer opens with a strong piece and groups related work.
artifactA specific piece of work or evidence included in a portfolio.A marked draft is an artifact showing revision.
revision trailEvidence showing how a work changed from draft to final form and why.Drafts and notes create a revision trail.
editorial priorityA ranked need that guides which revisions should happen first.Repairing an unclear ending takes priority over punctuation.
cohesionThe clear connection among ideas, sections, or portfolio items.A recurring theme gives the collection cohesion.
accessibilityDesign that allows people with differing needs to perceive and use the work.Readable contrast and alt text improve accessibility.
metadataDescriptive information such as title, author, genre, date, or file label.Each file’s metadata identifies its genre and revision date.
proofreadTo inspect a near-final work for errors in language, formatting, and consistency.The writer proofreads after completing major revisions.

Make the Vocabulary Work

Which choice shows purposeful curation?

A. Include every draft in the order it was created.

This collects material but does not select, order, or frame it for an audience.

B. Select artifacts that show range and growth, then order them to create a clear reading path.

Correct. The writer makes deliberate choices about selection, sequence, and audience experience.
