Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
portfolioA purposeful collection of work selected to demonstrate quality, range, and growth.
curationThe deliberate selection, ordering, and framing of items for an audience.
artifactA specific piece of work or evidence included in a portfolio.
revision trailEvidence showing how a work changed from draft to final form and why.
editorial priorityA ranked need that guides which revisions should happen first.
cohesionThe clear connection among ideas, sentences, sections, or portfolio items.
accessibilityDesign that allows people with differing needs to perceive and use the work.
metadataDescriptive information such as title, author, genre, date, or file label.
proofreadTo inspect a near-final work for errors in language, formatting, and consistency.
