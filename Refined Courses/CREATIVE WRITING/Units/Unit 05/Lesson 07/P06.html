P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one creative piece or excerpt you are preparing for a portfolio.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

If you do not have a current piece ready, use this model excerpt: The last bus sighed past without opening its doors. I stayed under the shelter, holding a ticket that had already gone soft from rain.

Part A: Select and Diagnose

Name the piece, identify its form, and write a selection rationale. Then diagnose what the piece still needs before final submission.

Part B: Revise and Polish

Make or describe at least two revisions. At least one revision must improve meaning, structure, format, or reader effect. Then complete a final convention check.

Part C: Portfolio Note

Write a four-sentence portfolio note explaining why you selected the piece, what feedback or rereading showed, what you revised, and why the final version is ready for readers.
