Checkpoint: Portfolio Readiness Case Study

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Analyze the new portfolio case and answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

Case File

Mateo proposes a portfolio with three realistic stories written during the same month. Story A has polished dialogue but no saved draft. Story B includes an early ending that explains the theme and a final ending that replaces the explanation with a recurring train-whistle image. Story C contains ambitious shifts in point of view, but two readers reported that they could not tell who was speaking. Mateo’s website uses pale-gray text, links labeled “here,” and an audio reading without a transcript. His reflection says, “I chose these because they are my best work, and feedback helped.”

Transcript means a written version of spoken audio. Point of view means the narrative perspective through which a story is told.

Curation and Revision

  1. Evaluate whether the collection demonstrates sufficient range.
  2. Identify the strongest evidence of revision growth and explain why.
  3. Recommend whether Story A should remain; justify using portfolio criteria.
  4. Diagnose the highest-priority problem in Story C.
  5. Propose a revision test that would show whether Story C is clearer.

Publication Readiness

  1. Identify three accessibility problems on the website.
  2. Write a descriptive replacement for one “here” link.
  3. Explain why visual polish alone cannot make this portfolio publication-ready.
  4. Recommend a purposeful artifact order and justify it.

Reflection and Synthesis

  1. Rewrite Mateo’s reflection claim so it is specific and arguable.
  2. Write a 100–150-word model paragraph using one artifact and precise evidence to demonstrate growth.
  3. Give a final readiness judgment—ready, ready after revision, or not yet ready—and support it with at least four criteria.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review feedback, correct the identified weaknesses, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
