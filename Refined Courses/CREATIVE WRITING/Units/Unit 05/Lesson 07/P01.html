P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Integrated Publication-Ready Portfolio Preparation

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.01 - Online collaborative publication; MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.02 - Format-specific quality work; MLA.CW.REV.02 - Clarity, cohesion, and conventions; MLA.CW.COM.01 - Author presentation of creative work.

What You Will Learn

  • How to select writing for a publication-ready portfolio.
  • How to use feedback and revision evidence to improve a final piece.
  • How to check format, conventions, and reader readiness together.
  • How to prepare a short portfolio note that explains your author choices.

What You Will Do

  • Review a portfolio-readiness sequence.
  • Choose a piece or excerpt for final preparation.
  • Revise using feedback, format checks, and convention checks.
  • Write a portfolio explanation that connects revision to publication quality.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can prepare a portfolio-ready creative piece by selecting it intentionally, revising from feedback, checking format and conventions, and explaining why the final version is ready for readers.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson combines revision and publication. You are not just choosing your favorite draft. You are proving that the piece has been improved, checked, formatted, and prepared for a real reader.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you are unsure which piece is ready for portfolio preparation, if feedback conflicts, or if your final format does not match the genre.
