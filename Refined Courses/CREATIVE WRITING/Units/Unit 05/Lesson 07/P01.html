Lesson Overview: Integrated Publication-Ready Portfolio Preparation

Lesson Focus

Lesson 7: Integrated Publication-Ready Portfolio Preparation

Primary Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.01 — Online collaborative publication; MLA.CW.REV.01 — Revision from feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.02 — Format-specific quality work; MLA.CW.REV.02 — Clarity, cohesion, and conventions; MLA.CW.COM.01 — Author presentation of creative work.

What You Will Learn

  • Select work using evidence rather than attachment alone.
  • Translate feedback into purposeful revision.
  • Audit craft, conventions, accessibility, and presentation together.
  • Write a portfolio note that explains growth with specific evidence.

Portfolio Workflow

StageQuestion
SelectWhich work best demonstrates the standards?
ReviseWhich changes create the greatest reader benefit?
ProofIs every claim, sentence, and format choice ready?
PresentCan a new reader navigate and understand the portfolio?

Mastery Target

I can assemble a coherent portfolio, justify selection and revision decisions with evidence, and prepare each item for its intended audience and publication format. A score of at least 80% is required.
