P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can prepare a portfolio-ready creative piece.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you cannot choose between pieces, if your revision evidence is weak, or if your final piece is polished on the surface but still unclear in meaning.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one creative piece or excerpt.
  2. Write a selection rationale.
  3. Identify revision evidence.
  4. Complete format, clarity, cohesion, and convention checks.
  5. Submit the final piece or plan with a portfolio note in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a portfolio-ready preparation plan with a selected piece, selection rationale, revision evidence, final readiness checklist, and portfolio note.

Mastery Criteria

  • Selects a piece for a clear portfolio reason.
  • Uses feedback or rereading to guide revision.
  • Completes format, clarity, cohesion, and convention checks.
  • Explains revision evidence accurately.
  • Writes a portfolio note that shows why the final version is ready for readers.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
