P04 Worked Example

Study how a writer prepares one piece for a publication-ready portfolio.

Example 1: Selection Rationale

Piece: A short poem about an empty bus stop.

Step 1: Identify strength: the repeated bus stop image creates loneliness.

Step 2: Identify growth: feedback helped the writer add a clearer ending.

Step 3: Rationale: I selected this poem because it shows stronger imagery and a revised ending that makes the speaker's choice clearer.

Example 2: Revision Evidence

Before: I waited there and felt sad.

Feedback: Show the sadness through an image instead of naming it.

After: The last bus sighed past without opening its doors.

Why it works: The revision uses image and personification to show emotion.

Example 3: Final Readiness Check

Format: Line breaks create pauses at the strongest images.

Clarity: The speaker's situation is understandable.

Conventions: Capitalization and punctuation are intentional and consistent.

Author note: The writer explains the revised image without summarizing the whole poem.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: This piece is in my portfolio because I like it.

This is incomplete because it does not explain growth, revision, craft, or publication readiness.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: This piece is in my portfolio because the revised final image shows how feedback helped me replace telling with a stronger visual moment.

This is stronger because it connects selection to revision evidence and craft growth.
