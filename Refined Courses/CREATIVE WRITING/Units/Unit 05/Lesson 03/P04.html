Worked Example: Three Workshop Conferences

Example 1 — Replace Vague Praise

Weak: “Great description.” Observation: “The repeated heat images—sticky railing, white sky, buzzing asphalt—made the setting feel oppressive.” Question: “Do you want that pressure to explain why Lena leaves?” Feedforward: “If yes, echo one heat image at the decision point.”

Example 2 — Respect Agency

Reader: “Change first person to third person.” Writer asks, “What problem would that solve?” Reader clarifies that one memory arrives before the narrator could know it. The writer keeps first person and moves the memory after the discovery. The diagnosed access problem is solved without accepting the proposed method.

Example 3 — Receive Difficult Feedback

Two readers say the ending feels unearned. The writer paraphrases: “You understood the choice, but not why she makes it.” After confirming, the writer adds an earlier failed attempt and recurring object. The revision preserves the ending while building causality.

Asynchronous Alternative

When no live partner is available, use the provided simulated reader comments, write both sides of the protocol, and identify which clarifying question would be asked.
