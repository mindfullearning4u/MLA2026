Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and full definition. Add one original workshop example for observation, open question, craft feedback, feedforward, and uptake. Submit with Page 5.
workshopA structured conversation for studying and improving drafts.
active listeningAttending, paraphrasing, questioning, and confirming before responding.
writer’s purposeThe intended audience, meaning, and effect.
observationA neutral description of what appears in the draft.
interpretationAn explanation of how a reader understands a choice.
open questionA question inviting explanation rather than yes/no.
craft feedbackEvidence-based response about technique and effect.
feedforwardGuidance focused on a useful next action.
writer agencyThe writer’s authority over final decisions.
discussion normAn agreed rule supporting respectful participation.
uptakeHow a listener shows understanding and uses what was heard.
metacognitionAwareness and explanation of one’s own thinking.
