Lesson Overview: Workshop Collaboration and Active Listening

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.COM.02.
Supporting: MLA.CW.REV.01 and MLA.CW.COM.03. Practice ethical, purpose-centered workshop collaboration in live or asynchronous settings.

Learning Goals

  • Listen for the writer’s purpose before evaluating the draft.
  • Separate observation, interpretation, question, and suggestion.
  • Give specific, evidence-based feedback without taking ownership.
  • Receive feedback through paraphrase, clarification, and revision planning.

Workflow

Vocabulary → workshop cycle and visual protocol → three modeled conferences → 18-part Independent Work → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz.

Why This Matters

Workshop is collaborative inquiry, not a contest in taste. Strong participants make the draft’s effects visible, ask useful questions, and protect both honest critique and the writer’s agency.
