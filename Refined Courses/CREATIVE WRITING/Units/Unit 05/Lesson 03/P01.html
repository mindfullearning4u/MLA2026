P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Workshop Collaboration and Active Listening

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.02 - Workshop collaboration and active listening.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback; MLA.CW.COM.03 - Communication of craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How a writing workshop helps a draft improve.
  • How to listen actively before giving advice.
  • How to give feedback that is specific, respectful, and useful.
  • How to respond to feedback without becoming defensive.

What You Will Do

  • Study examples of helpful and unhelpful workshop comments.
  • Practice active listening moves.
  • Write feedback that names a strength, a question, and a revision suggestion.
  • Use workshop feedback to plan revision.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can give a peer useful feedback, explain the craft reason for that feedback, and respond to feedback with a revision plan.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means workshop is not casual opinion sharing. You are learning how to listen carefully, talk about craft, and help another writer improve while respecting the writer's purpose.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if workshop feedback feels too personal, too vague, or disconnected from the writer's stated purpose.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
