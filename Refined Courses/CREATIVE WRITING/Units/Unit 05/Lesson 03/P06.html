Creative Writing | Unit 5 | Lesson 3 | Page 6 of 6

Checkpoint: Workshop Collaboration

Directions

Complete all 12 tasks independently and submit separately. At least 80% is required.

Draft Excerpt

“Jon opened the message, deleted it, and drove to the bridge.” The writer wants restrained grief, but readers say the decision feels abrupt.

Tasks

  1. State the writer’s purpose.
  2. Write two neutral observations.
  3. Describe two reader effects.
  4. Write two open questions.
  5. Give two feedforward options.
  6. Identify one unhelpful command.
  7. Revise it into craft feedback.
  8. Model the writer’s paraphrase.
  9. Write a clarifying question.
  10. Create a revision hypothesis.
  11. Revise the excerpt to 160–200 words.
  12. Write a 180–220 word reflection defending the decision.

80% Mastery Requirement

Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit.
