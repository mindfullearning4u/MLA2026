Lesson: The Four-Pass Editorial Protocol

Pass 1 — Reader Orientation

Track who speaks or acts, where the reader is, when events occur, and what each pronoun refers to. Mark every point where a reader could form the wrong mental picture.

Pass 2 — Cohesion and Coherence

Map cause, contrast, sequence, comparison, and return. Use transitions only where relationships are not otherwise visible. Build lexical chains and place known information before new information when clarity requires it.

Pass 3 — Sentence Control

Check boundaries, coordination, subordination, modifier placement, parallelism, tense, and agreement. Preserve a fragment or unusual syntax only when its effect is identifiable and worth the cost.

Pass 4 — Publication Proof

Read aloud; inspect punctuation, capitalization, spelling, dialogue format, paragraphing, and house style. Compare the final version with the original purpose so correctness does not erase voice.
