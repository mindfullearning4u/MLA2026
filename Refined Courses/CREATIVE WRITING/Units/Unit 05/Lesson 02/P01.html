📘 CW | Unit 05 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Clarity, Cohesion, and Conventions

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.REV.02 - Clarity, cohesion, and conventions.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback; MLA.CW.COM.03 - Communication of craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How clarity helps readers understand the draft.
  • How cohesion connects ideas, scenes, lines, or stanzas.
  • How conventions support publication readiness.
  • How to revise without flattening creative voice.

What You Will Do

  • Identify unclear pronouns, missing transitions, and convention errors.
  • Revise a draft for clarity and flow.
  • Explain how conventions help the reader.
  • Prepare a short excerpt for a publication-ready portfolio.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can revise a creative excerpt so the meaning is clear, the parts connect smoothly, and conventions support the reader's understanding.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means your creative writing should still be readable and controlled. Readers should not have to guess who is speaking, when time changes, or what a sentence means unless ambiguity is intentional and crafted.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if a sentence seems unclear but you are not sure how to revise it, or if you worry that convention edits will change your creative voice.
