Lesson Overview: Clarity, Cohesion, and Conventions

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.REV.02.
Supporting: MLA.CW.REV.01 and MLA.CW.COM.03. Revise for reader orientation, meaningful connections, and publication-ready control without flattening voice.

Learning Goals

  • Diagnose ambiguity as intentional or accidental.
  • Repair unclear reference, chronology, spatial logic, and sentence boundaries.
  • Build cohesion through purposeful repetition, transitions, and information flow.
  • Edit conventions according to intended effect and defend deliberate deviations.

Workflow

Vocabulary → four-pass editorial protocol → three complete repairs → 18-part Independent Work → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz.

Why This Matters

Clarity is not simplicity. Complex writing succeeds when readers can follow who, where, when, and why while still encountering ambiguity, surprise, and distinctive voice.
