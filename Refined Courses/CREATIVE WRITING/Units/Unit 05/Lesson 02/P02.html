Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and definition. Add original examples for antecedent, transition, lexical chain, given-new flow, and intentional fragment. Submit with Page 5.
clarityEase with which intended meaning can be understood.
cohesionThe connections that make parts of a text function together.
coherenceThe overall logical and meaningful unity of a text.
antecedentThe noun to which a pronoun refers.
chronologyThe sequence of events in time.
transitionA signal showing relationship or movement.
lexical chainRelated words repeated or varied across a passage.
given-new flowMoving from known information toward new information.
sentence boundaryThe point where one complete sentence ends and another begins.
intentional fragmentAn incomplete sentence used deliberately for effect.
usageConventional ways words and structures are used.
house styleA publication’s consistent editorial rules.
