Creative Writing | Unit 5 | Lesson 2 | Page 5 of 6

Independent Work: Editorial Case File

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks using a substantial creative draft. Submit with Page 2 vocabulary.

Tasks

  1. State audience, purpose, and controlling effect.
  2. Identify every speaker and actor.
  3. Trace all pronoun antecedents.
  4. Map chronology.
  5. Map spatial movement.
  6. Mark accidental ambiguity.
  7. Separate intentional ambiguity.
  8. Diagram five logical relationships.
  9. Identify missing transitions.
  10. Trace two lexical chains.
  11. Revise given-new flow in one paragraph.
  12. Correct sentence-boundary errors.
  13. Check modifiers and parallelism.
  14. Check tense and agreement.
  15. Defend or revise three fragments.
  16. Complete a publication proofread.
  17. Create an edit log with eight before/after changes.
  18. Write a 300–400 word editorial rationale explaining how clarity and cohesion improved without flattening voice.
