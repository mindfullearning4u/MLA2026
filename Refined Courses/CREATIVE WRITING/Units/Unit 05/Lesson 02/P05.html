Independent Work: Clarity, Cohesion, and Conventions

Purpose

Improve clarity and cohesion while using conventions to support meaning and voice.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Use precise evidence from your own work and the source provided.

Source for Your Work

Draft: “Nia reached the platform late. The announcement echoed. Her sister had warned her. The ticket was damp. She made a decision. It changed everything.”

1. Plan Before You Work

DecisionYour Plan
Purpose and intended audienceState each clearly.
Most important craft or communication needName the need and evidence.
Success indicatorDescribe what an effective result will do for a reader.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach demonstrates stronger decision-making? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Combine every short sentence into one long sentence so the paragraph flows.
B. Clarify relationships among ideas, vary sentence patterns, and use transitions and punctuation where they guide the reader.

3. Apply the Lesson

Revise the draft into a coherent 180–250 word scene. Establish what Nia wants, connect the details logically, vary sentence structure, and maintain correct conventions without flattening the voice.

4. Revise with Purpose

Annotate three changes: one for clarity, one for cohesion, and one for conventions. Explain the specific problem each change solves.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your work applies the lesson skill to a specific creative product.
  • Your explanation identifies a decision and its effect on readers.
  • Your revision meaningfully improves the work, not only its appearance.
