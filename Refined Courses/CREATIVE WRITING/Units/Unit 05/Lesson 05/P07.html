P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can prepare a piece for online collaborative publication.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you are unsure whether the content is appropriate, if the publication format is unclear, or if collaboration changes the writer's purpose too much.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select a short creative-writing piece or excerpt.
  2. Identify the piece's purpose and intended reader effect.
  3. Explain one feedback decision.
  4. Check format, title, conventions, and audience appropriateness.
  5. Submit the publication plan in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a publication plan that includes the title, purpose, feedback decision, format check, online-reader preparation, and final readiness checklist.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies the purpose of the piece.
  • Uses feedback thoughtfully instead of automatically.
  • Checks format for the selected creative form.
  • Prepares the reader with a clear title and appropriate context.
  • Confirms the piece is appropriate and publication-ready.
