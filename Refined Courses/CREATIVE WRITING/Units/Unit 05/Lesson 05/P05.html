Independent Work: Online Collaborative Publication

Purpose

Prepare creative work for responsible, accessible, and audience-aware online collaboration and publication.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Use precise evidence from your own work and the source provided.

Source for Your Work

Publication scenario: A class literary site will feature student work. The page must be readable on a phone, credit collaborators and sources, protect private information, and invite useful reader response.

1. Plan Before You Work

DecisionYour Plan
Purpose and intended audienceState each clearly.
Most important craft or communication needName the need and evidence.
Success indicatorDescribe what an effective result will do for a reader.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach demonstrates stronger decision-making? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Post the first polished-looking version immediately and include personal details so readers know the author is real.
B. Revise for the intended audience and screen, confirm permissions and attribution, protect privacy, and invite focused feedback.

3. Apply the Lesson

Prepare a publication package for a 350–500 word creative piece or 24–36 line poem. Include the final text, title and short author note, accessibility choices, attribution/permission note, privacy check, and one focused reader-response question. Do not publish it.

4. Revise with Purpose

Test the package from a reader’s perspective. Revise one design or language choice that improves readability, access, or ethical publication.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your work applies the lesson skill to a specific creative product.
  • Your explanation identifies a decision and its effect on readers.
  • Your revision meaningfully improves the work, not only its appearance.
