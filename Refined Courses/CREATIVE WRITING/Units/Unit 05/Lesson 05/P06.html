P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one short piece of your own writing or the model excerpt provided here.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Prepare a publication plan. Do not post or share personal information. The goal is to show that the piece could be prepared for a safe, appropriate online class publication space.

Model excerpt: At the end of the driveway, the rain had stopped. I still held the broken umbrella above me, waiting for a reason to fold it.

Part A: Publication Purpose

Write the title of the piece and explain the purpose or intended reader effect in one or two sentences.

Part B: Collaborative Feedback Decision

Name one piece of feedback the writer should consider. Explain whether the writer should accept, revise, or reject that suggestion based on the purpose of the piece.

Part C: Online Publication Checklist

Write a checklist with at least five items. Include purpose, feedback, format, title, author note or context, audience appropriateness, and final conventions.

Independent Work Success Checklist

For the independent work in Online Collaborative Publication, produce evidence that another reader can follow. Start by restating the task in your own words. Then plan the response, draft the required writing or analysis, and revise for the exact standard focus: MLA.CW.PUB.01, MLA.CW.PUB.02, MLA.CW.REV.01. This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.PUB.01, MLA.CW.PUB.02, MLA.CW.REV.01, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose. If the task asks you to write, draft in complete sentences and make the craft move visible rather than hidden. If the task asks you to analyze, quote or describe the specific words that prove your answer. Before submitting, check that your response includes the required passage, model, draft excerpt, or scenario evidence from this page and that every sentence helps prove mastery.
