Checkpoint: Online Collaborative Publication

Directions

Complete all 12 tasks independently and submit separately. Do not publish the case. At least 80% is required.

Case

A student magazine page contains an author’s full personal email, a copyrighted photograph copied from a search result, pale text over animation, and an editor’s unapproved rewritten ending.

Tasks

  1. Identify four publication risks.
  2. Prioritize them by urgency.
  3. Assign each to an editorial role.
  4. Write the consent question for the author.
  5. Repair the privacy issue.
  6. Propose a lawful image solution.
  7. Write appropriate attribution metadata.
  8. Repair visual accessibility.
  9. Plan transcript/alt-text needs.
  10. Restore writer agency in the ending decision.
  11. Create a 10-step QA and approval checklist.
  12. Write a 200–250 word rationale defending the release decision.

80% Mastery Requirement

Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit.
