📘 CW | Unit 05 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one short piece of your own writing or the model excerpt provided here.

Instructions

Prepare a publication plan. Do not post or share personal information. The goal is to show that the piece could be prepared for a safe, appropriate online class publication space.

Model excerpt: At the end of the driveway, the rain had stopped. I still held the broken umbrella above me, waiting for a reason to fold it.

Part A: Publication Purpose

Write the title of the piece and explain the purpose or intended reader effect in one or two sentences.

Part B: Collaborative Feedback Decision

Name one piece of feedback the writer should consider. Explain whether the writer should accept, revise, or reject that suggestion based on the purpose of the piece.

Part C: Online Publication Checklist

Write a checklist with at least five items. Include purpose, feedback, format, title, author note or context, audience appropriateness, and final conventions.
