Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and full definition. Add one publication example for consent, attribution, license, metadata, alt text, and quality assurance. Submit with Page 5.
publication workflowThe ordered stages from submission through release and review.
editorial roleA defined responsibility in preparing a publication.
version controlA method for identifying and tracking revisions.
editorial approvalDocumented authorization for a change or release.
consentInformed, voluntary permission.
attributionClear identification of a creator or source.
copyrightLegal protection for original creative work.
licensePermission defining how work may be used.
metadataDescriptive information such as title, creator, date, and format.
alt textA concise description conveying an image’s relevant information.
content warningA notice preparing readers for potentially distressing material.
quality assuranceSystematic checking of content, access, and technical function.
