Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
publication workflowThe ordered stages from submission through release and review.The team tracks editing, approval, design, and release in one workflow.
editorial roleA defined responsibility in preparing a publication.The copy editor checks consistency and correctness.
version controlA method for identifying and tracking revisions.The filename Story_v4 distinguishes the current draft.
editorial approvalDocumented authorization for a change or release.The editor signs off before the issue is published.
consentInformed, voluntary permission.The writer obtains consent before publishing a student’s photograph.
attributionClear identification of a creator or source.The caption credits the photographer by name.
copyrightLegal protection for original creative work.The poem is protected by copyright when it is created.
licensePermission defining how work may be used.A license allows reuse with attribution but prohibits alteration.
metadataDescriptive information such as title, creator, date, and format.The digital file includes the author and publication date.
alt textA concise description conveying an image’s relevant information.Alt text describes the cracked compass that symbolizes uncertainty.
content warningA notice preparing readers for potentially distressing material.The editor places a warning before a story depicting violence.
quality assuranceSystematic checking of content, access, and technical function.The team tests links, captions, formatting, and screen-reader access.

Make the Vocabulary Work

A journal wants to publish a student’s photograph. Which action is ethically complete?

A. Credit the photographer after publication.

Attribution is necessary, but credit alone does not establish permission.

B. Obtain informed consent, confirm the license, and provide attribution.

Correct. This addresses permission, allowed use, and creator credit before release.
