Lesson Overview: Online Collaborative Publication

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.PUB.01.
Supporting: MLA.CW.PUB.02 and MLA.CW.REV.01. Prepare, review, and stage creative work for responsible collaborative online publication.

Learning Goals

  • Use defined editorial roles and a transparent approval workflow.
  • Protect consent, privacy, copyright, attribution, and writer agency.
  • Prepare accessible, format-specific digital content.
  • Perform content, technical, and rendered-view quality assurance before release.

Workflow

Vocabulary → publication pipeline and visual responsibility map → three cases → 18-part simulated publication packet → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz. No public posting is required.

Why This Matters

Online publication increases reach and permanence. Collaboration must make ownership, permissions, changes, accessibility, and final approval visible before anything becomes public.
