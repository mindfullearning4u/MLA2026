Worked Example: Three Style Decisions

Example 1

“The alley was thin” becomes “The alley pinched shut.” Denotation stays narrow; connotation, syntax, and hard sounds create pressure.

Example 2

Child narrator: “The residence deteriorated” is precise but unbelievable. “Our house sagged like it was tired” aligns register, image, and voice.

Example 3

A poem repeats clinical words—specimen, incision, sterile—then ends with “home.” The pattern makes the simple final word intimate and resistant.
