P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Connotation, denotation, and style

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.07 - Connotation, denotation, and style.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.POE.02 - Figurative language and mood; MLA.CW.COM.03 - Communication of craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How denotation and connotation differ.
  • How connotation changes mood and style in poetry.
  • How to choose words that fit a poem's purpose.
  • How to explain a style choice with evidence.

What You Will Do

  • Compare word choices with similar dictionary meanings.
  • Identify positive, negative, and neutral connotations.
  • Analyze how diction creates style.
  • Revise poem lines by selecting more precise words.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can explain how a word's denotation and connotation shape the mood, tone, and style of a poem or original line.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning that words do more than name things. Two words can have almost the same dictionary meaning but create very different feelings. Poets use those differences to control style.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you know a word's definition but cannot explain its feeling, or if your style explanation does not connect to evidence.
