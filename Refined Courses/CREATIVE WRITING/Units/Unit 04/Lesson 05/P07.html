P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can explain connotation, denotation, and style.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you are confusing denotation with connotation or if your explanation does not show how word choice creates style.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review P02 and P03 notes.
  2. Complete P06 Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that you explain both meaning and feeling.
  4. Submit your completed checkpoint work in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Write six original lines in a deliberate style. Then write a 4-6 sentence explanation naming three word choices, their connotations, and how they create the style.

Mastery Criteria

  • Defines denotation and connotation accurately.
  • Identifies positive, negative, or neutral connotations.
  • Explains how word choice creates style.
  • Uses evidence from specific words.
  • Creates original lines with intentional diction.
