Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
denotationA word’s direct dictionary meaning.“Winter” denotes the coldest season of the year.
connotationA feeling or idea associated with a word.“Winter” may suggest isolation, rest, or age.
styleA recognizable pattern of language choices.Short sentences and concrete nouns can create a spare style.
registerThe language level fitted to a context.A ceremonial speech uses a more formal register than casual dialogue.
lexical patternA recurring kind of word choice.Repeated machinery words form a lexical pattern of labor.
semantic prosodyPositive or negative coloring created by repeated context.A word repeatedly placed near danger can develop a threatening coloring.
dictionA writer’s deliberate word choice.“Clutched” creates more tension than the neutral verb “held.”
syntaxThe arrangement of words in a sentence.Placing the object first can emphasize what the speaker notices.
cadenceThe rhythmic movement of language.A series of balanced clauses creates a measured cadence.
euphonyA smooth or pleasing sound effect.Long vowels and liquid consonants can make a line flow.
cacophonyA harsh or jarring sound effect.Hard stops and clustered consonants can make a line grate.
stylistic coherenceThe consistent interaction of a writer’s choices.Diction, syntax, and sound all support the same restrained mood.

Make the Vocabulary Work

Which line uses connotation to create a threatening style?

A. “The vines curled around the abandoned gate.”

Best choice. “Curled” is literal enough to picture, while “abandoned gate” carries connotations of neglect and exclusion. Together they create unease.

B. “The plants were next to the old entrance.”

Not the best choice. The denotation is clear, but the neutral diction contributes little emotional coloring or distinctive style.
