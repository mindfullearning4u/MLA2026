P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

This page shows how to revise poetic diction and explain the effect of a word choice.

Step 4: Match Word Choice to Purpose

Basic line: The bird moved across the sky.

If the purpose is peace, revise to: The bird drifted across the pale sky. If the purpose is fear, revise to: The bird slashed across the dim sky. The action changes from neutral to gentle or sharp.

Step 5: Explain the Diction Choice

A complete explanation names the selected word, gives its meaning or connotation, and connects it to the poem's effect.

Example: The word slashed creates a threatening mood because it suggests a sharp, violent motion instead of a calm movement.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: I chose slashed because it sounds cool.

This is incorrect because it does not explain meaning, connotation, mood, or purpose. A diction choice must be justified with a craft reason.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: I chose slashed because its sharp connotation creates a tense mood and makes the bird's movement feel sudden and threatening.

This is correct because it connects the word choice to connotation, mood, and reader effect.

Notebook Task Part 2

Part A: Copy the basic line and both revisions.

Part B: Circle the diction that changes the mood.

Part C: Write one explanation of a diction choice using word + connotation + mood + purpose.
