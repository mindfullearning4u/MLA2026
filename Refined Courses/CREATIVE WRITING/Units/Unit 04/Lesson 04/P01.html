Lesson Overview: Word Origins and Poetic Diction

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.CW.WR.06—use word origins to deepen poetic diction.
Supporting: MLA.CW.WR.05 and MLA.CW.WR.07.

Learning Goals

  • Use roots, affixes, and etymology to infer meaning.
  • Compare denotation, connotation, register, sound, and history.
  • Build lexical patterns that support voice, mood, and theme.
  • Defend diction revisions with evidence.

Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Learn the six-lens diction protocol.
  3. Study three models.
  4. Complete all 18 Independent Work tasks.
  5. Complete the distinct 12-part Checkpoint.
  6. Earn at least 80% on the quiz.
