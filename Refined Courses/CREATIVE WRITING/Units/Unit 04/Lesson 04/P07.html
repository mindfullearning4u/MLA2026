P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can use and explain poetic diction.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if you are choosing words only because they sound interesting, or if you cannot explain how a word changes the mood or meaning.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your P02 and P03 notes.
  2. Complete P06 Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that your explanation includes denotation or connotation.
  4. Submit your completed checkpoint work in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Write four original lines of poetry that use precise diction to create a specific mood. Then write a 4-6 sentence explanation naming one important word, its denotation, its connotation, and how it shapes the poem's mood.

Mastery Criteria

  • Chooses precise diction for an intended mood.
  • Explains denotation or word-part meaning accurately.
  • Explains connotation clearly.
  • Connects word choice to mood, image, or meaning.
  • Creates original lines with intentional diction.
