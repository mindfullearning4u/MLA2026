Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and definition. For six terms, add an original poetic example. Submit these notes with Page 5.
etymologyThe history and development of a word.
rootThe core element carrying basic meaning.
prefixA word part added before a root.
suffixA word part added after a root.
denotationA word’s direct dictionary meaning.
connotationFeelings and associations attached to a word.
dictionA writer’s deliberate word choice.
registerLanguage level suited to audience, purpose, or context.
lexical fieldA group of words connected by meaning.
euphonyA smooth or pleasing sound effect.
cacophonyA harsh or jarring sound effect.
semantic precisionChoosing a word that expresses the exact intended meaning.
