Checkpoint: Word Origins and Poetic Diction

Directions

Complete all 12 tasks independently and submit separately. At least 80% is required.

Lines

Rain occupied the courtyard. / Each drain swallowed a silver argument. / Under the awning, we waited for the city / to remember its own name.

Tasks

  1. Paraphrase literally.
  2. Analyze “occupied.”
  3. Analyze “swallowed.”
  4. Explain “argument.”
  5. Identify the lexical field.
  6. Define awning from context.
  7. Compare occupied, covered, and flooded.
  8. Research one relevant root or origin.
  9. Revise for a hopeful mood.
  10. Defend two diction changes.
  11. Write an original eight-line stanza with a controlled lexical field.
  12. Write a 180–220 word rationale.

80% Mastery Requirement

Earn at least 80%; review TOR feedback and resubmit if needed.
