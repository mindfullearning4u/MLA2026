Creative Writing | Unit 4 | Lesson 4 | Page 5 of 6

Independent Work: Diction Laboratory

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks and submit them with Page 2 vocabulary.

Source Lines

The wind went through the vacant lot. / A plastic bag moved above the fence. / Behind the apartments, evening was dark.

Tasks

  1. Identify the imprecise verbs and adjective.
  2. Give three candidates for each.
  3. Compare denotations.
  4. Compare connotations.
  5. Analyze sound.
  6. Evaluate register.
  7. Create two different lexical fields.
  8. Revise for menace.
  9. Revise for wonder.
  10. Explain how one root or origin informs a choice.
  11. Identify and avoid an etymological fallacy.
  12. Complete a word-choice matrix.
  13. Read both versions aloud and compare rhythm.
  14. Write an evidence-based revision paragraph.
  15. Draft a 24–32 line poem with a controlled lexical field.
  16. Use five researched word origins meaningfully.
  17. Annotate ten diction choices and revise three.
  18. Write a 250–300 word craft rationale.
