Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Add an original example for ambiguity, subtext, enjambment, caesura, and paradox. Submit these notes with Page 5 as Notebook Evidence.
TermDefinition
layered meaningMeaning operating at literal, implied, symbolic, or structural levels.
productive ambiguityDeliberate openness that supports multiple evidence-based readings.
vaguenessInsufficient specificity that prevents a defensible interpretation.
inferenceA conclusion formed from evidence and reasoning.
subtextMeaning suggested beneath what is directly stated.
symbolic possibilityA plausible abstract meaning carried by a concrete detail.
referentThe person, object, or idea to which a word or pronoun refers.
syntaxThe arrangement of words and phrases in a sentence.
enjambmentA poetic sentence continuing beyond a line break without final punctuation.
caesuraA strong pause within a poetic line.
paradoxA seeming contradiction that reveals a possible truth.
evidence coverageHow fully an interpretation accounts for important details.
