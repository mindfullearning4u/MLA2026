📘 CW | Unit 04 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts. Your answers must be supported by evidence from the poem models.

Instructions

Use cautious interpretation language such as may suggest, could represent, and is supported by. Do not claim more than the evidence proves.

Part A: Literal Meaning

Choose one poem model from the lesson and write 2-3 sentences explaining what literally happens.

Part B: Implied Meaning

Write one possible deeper meaning. Use at least two details from the poem to support your interpretation.

Part C: Create

Write four original lines of poetry that include one object or image with layered meaning. Under the poem, explain the literal meaning and one possible implied meaning.
