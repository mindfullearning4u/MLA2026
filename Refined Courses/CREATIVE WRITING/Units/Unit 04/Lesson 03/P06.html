Poem: “Second Address”

The letter returns wearing my name,
though I have never lived there.
Someone has crossed out the street
but left the window in the stamp.
I hold it to the lamp—
the empty envelope grows heavy.

Tasks

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. Identify two uncertain details.
  3. Explain two possible meanings of “wearing my name.”
  4. Analyze the crossed-out street and remaining window.
  5. Define address in two ways relevant to the title.
  6. Explain the paradox in the final line.
  7. Develop and support Reading A with two details.
  8. Develop and support Reading B with two details.
  9. Test one limitation of each reading.
  10. Judge whether the ambiguity is productive.
  11. Write an original eight-line poem supporting two readings.
  12. Write a 180–220 word evidence-based rationale.
