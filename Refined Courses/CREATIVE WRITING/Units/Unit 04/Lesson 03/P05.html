Poem: “Receipt”

Your handwriting fades where the total should be.
I keep the paper folded in the blue cup,
beside three coins and a key that fits
either the old door or nothing now.
At midnight the refrigerator clicks off.
The kitchen becomes honest.
I unfold what you bought for us:
one blank line, still warm.

Analyze and Create

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. List three deliberately uncertain details.
  3. Identify possible referents for “you” and “us.”
  4. Explain the key’s symbolic possibilities.
  5. Analyze “the kitchen becomes honest.”
  6. Explain the paradox of a blank line being warm.
  7. Describe the effect of enjambment in lines 3–4.
  8. Develop Reading A as a complete claim.
  9. Support it with three evidence chains.
  10. Develop a genuinely different Reading B.
  11. Support it with three evidence chains.
  12. Identify one contradiction or limitation for each reading.
  13. Complete an interpretation matrix.
  14. Judge whether one reading is stronger or the tension is purposeful.
  15. Draft a 20–28 line poem with two supported interpretations.
  16. Use syntax, line breaks, and one recurring concrete object to control ambiguity.
  17. Annotate the evidence for both readings and revise one vague line.
  18. Write a 250–300 word rationale comparing evidence coverage and effect.
