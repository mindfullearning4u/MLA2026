Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. For each device, add one original example. Submit these notes with Page 5 as Notebook Evidence.
TermDefinition
figurative languageLanguage whose meaning extends beyond the literal words.
literal meaningThe direct, dictionary-level meaning.
moodThe emotional atmosphere a reader experiences.
toneThe speaker’s or writer’s attitude toward the subject.
simileA comparison using like or as.
metaphorA direct comparison stating one thing is another.
personificationGiving human action or qualities to something nonhuman.
imageryLanguage appealing to sight, sound, touch, taste, or smell.
symbolA concrete object or image carrying an additional idea.
connotationThe feelings or associations attached to a word.
denotationA word’s direct dictionary definition.
sound deviceA pattern such as repetition, alliteration, consonance, or onomatopoeia that shapes emphasis or mood.
