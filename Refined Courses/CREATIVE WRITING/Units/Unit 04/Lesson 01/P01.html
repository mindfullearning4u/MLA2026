Lesson Overview: Figurative Language and Mood

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.CW.POE.02—use and analyze figurative language to create mood.
Supporting standards: MLA.CW.WR.07 (connotation, denotation, style) and MLA.CW.COM.03 (craft reasoning).

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish literal meaning from figurative meaning.
  • Analyze how comparison, personification, imagery, symbol, sound, and connotation shape mood.
  • Select precise evidence and explain the device → association → reader effect chain.
  • Revise a poem so its details create a consistent but nuanced mood.

Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Learn the five-step mood analysis and visual sensory map on Page 3.
  3. Study three complete models on Page 4.
  4. Complete all 18 Page 5 tasks and submit them with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
  5. Complete the distinct 12-part Page 6 Checkpoint.
  6. Earn at least 80% on the lesson quiz.

Why This Matters

Mood is designed, not guessed. A skilled poet coordinates images, comparisons, sound, and connotations so readers experience an atmosphere while still discovering meaning for themselves.
