Independent Work: Mood Laboratory

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks. Submit them with Page 2 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Poem: “Platform at 5:12”

The station yawns beneath blue rain.
A vending machine hums to itself.
Tracks stitch darkness to the river,
and every signal holds one red eye.
My ticket softens in my hand.
Across the glass, dawn practices silver.

Analyze and Create

  1. Paraphrase the literal situation.
  2. Identify the speaker, setting, and possible purpose.
  3. Mark two examples of personification.
  4. Explain the metaphor in “tracks stitch darkness.”
  5. Analyze the connotations of “yawns,” “hums,” and “practices.”
  6. Name the opening mood and prove it with two details.
  7. Explain the red-eye image as symbol.
  8. Identify the mood shift in the final line.
  9. Distinguish mood from speaker tone.
  10. Read aloud and describe one sound or rhythm effect.
  11. Replace “softens” with a different verb and explain the changed mood.
  12. Offer a counterreading of the final image.
  13. Complete a four-column visual mood map.
  14. Write an evidence-chain paragraph using two quotations.
  15. Draft an 18–24 line original poem creating a precise mood.
  16. Use at least three different figurative devices and two sensory domains.
  17. Annotate each device and explain its intended effect.
  18. Write a 180–220 word craft rationale explaining diction, sound, imagery, mood shift, and revision.
