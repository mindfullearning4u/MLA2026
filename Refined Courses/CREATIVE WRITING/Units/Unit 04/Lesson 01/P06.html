Poem: “After the Power Returns”

The refrigerator clears its throat.
Room by room, the house remembers light.
Shadows fold themselves beneath the chairs,
but thunder keeps a key inside the walls.
We laugh too loudly in the kitchen,
counting seconds between each distant door.

Tasks

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. Identify two examples of personification.
  3. Explain the metaphor “the house remembers light.”
  4. Analyze the connotation of “clears its throat.”
  5. Define distant from context and explain its contribution.
  6. Name the dominant mood and support it with two details.
  7. Explain how “but” signals a mood complication.
  8. Analyze thunder as sound image and possible symbol.
  9. Distinguish the speaker’s tone from the reader’s mood.
  10. Revise one line to create relief; explain the craft changes.
  11. Write an original six-line stanza that shifts from tension to safety using two devices.
  12. Write a 150–180 word evidence-based rationale for your stanza.
