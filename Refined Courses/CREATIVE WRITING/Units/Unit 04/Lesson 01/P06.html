📘 CW | Unit 04 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts in your notebook or assignment document.

Instructions

Use complete sentences. Every answer must include evidence from the poem excerpt and explain how the language creates mood.

Part A: Identify

Write three examples of figurative language from the lesson models. Label each one as simile, metaphor, personification, imagery, or symbol.

Part B: Explain

Choose one example from Part A. Write a full explanation using this structure: device + quoted evidence + mood + reasoning.

Part C: Create

Write four original lines of poetry that use at least one example of figurative language to create a specific mood. Under your poem, name the mood and explain which words create it.
