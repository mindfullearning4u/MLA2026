Lesson Overview: Unit 4 Poetry Synthesis

Lesson Focus

Synthesize MLA.CW.POE.01–.03 and MLA.CW.WR.03, .06, .07 with revision, explanation, and communication standards.

Learning Goals

  • Diagnose how image, ambiguity, figurative pattern, diction, sound, and structure interact.
  • Paraphrase accurately before interpreting.
  • Revise poetry globally and locally.
  • Defend craft decisions through precise evidence chains.

Workflow

Vocabulary → synthesis protocol and visual diagnostic → three models → 18-part portfolio → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → Unit 4 Final Assessment. At least 80% is required.

Why This Matters

Poetic mastery is integration: a precise word changes sound, sound changes pace, lineation changes emphasis, images create patterns, and patterns carry layered meaning.
