Independent Work: Unit 4 Poetry Portfolio

Directions

Complete all 18 tasks and submit with Page 2 vocabulary. Use a polished poem of at least 30 lines.

Portfolio Tasks

  1. State literal situation.
  2. Paraphrase the complete poem.
  3. Name controlling effect.
  4. Map image system.
  5. Trace semantic fields.
  6. Analyze three figurative choices.
  7. Analyze denotation and connotation.
  8. Explain one word origin.
  9. Analyze register.
  10. Analyze sound and cadence.
  11. Explain two line breaks.
  12. Locate and defend the volta.
  13. Develop two readings.
  14. Evaluate evidence coverage.
  15. Make one global revision.
  16. Make four local revisions with before/after evidence.
  17. Create a one-page visual craft map.
  18. Write a 350–450 word craft defense using four evidence chains and a transfer goal.
