P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Step 3: Review Revision

Revision means improving craft choices. In poetry, revise for sharper diction, clearer images, stronger mood, and purposeful ambiguity.

Step 4: Synthesis Explanation

A synthesis explanation should connect several choices. Example: The fog creates uncertainty, the bridge suggests a crossing, and the feet answering the road show action despite not seeing everything clearly.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The poem is about fog and roads.

This is only topic naming. It does not explain craft or meaning.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The fog and hidden bridge create uncertainty, while the speaker's moving feet suggest persistence.

This is correct because it connects evidence to interpretation.
