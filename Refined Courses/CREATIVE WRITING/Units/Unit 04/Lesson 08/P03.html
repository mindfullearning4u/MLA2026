Lesson: The Poetry Synthesis Audit

Ten-Step Audit

  1. State literal situation.
  2. Paraphrase complex syntax.
  3. Name controlling effect.
  4. Trace image and semantic fields.
  5. Analyze figurative pattern.
  6. Map denotation, connotation, origin, register, and sound.
  7. Locate lineation effects and the volta.
  8. Test ambiguity with two evidence-based readings.
  9. Check architecture and ending payoff.
  10. Revise globally, then locally, and defend the changes.

Evidence Frame
