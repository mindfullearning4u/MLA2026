P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Step 3: Review Revision

Revision means improving craft choices. In poetry, revise for sharper diction, clearer images, stronger mood, and purposeful ambiguity.

Step 4: Synthesis Explanation

A synthesis explanation should connect several choices. Example: The fog creates uncertainty, the bridge suggests a crossing, and the feet answering the road show action despite not seeing everything clearly.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The poem is about fog and roads.

This is only topic naming. It does not explain craft or meaning.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The fog and hidden bridge create uncertainty, while the speaker's moving feet suggest persistence.

This is correct because it connects evidence to interpretation.

Deeper Craft Reasoning Support

This page asks you to move from knowing the term to using the skill with judgment. For Putting It All Together: Unit 4 Synthesis, pause after each model or explanation and ask: What choice did the writer make? Why does that choice fit the purpose? What would be weaker if the choice were removed or changed? This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.WR.05, MLA.CW.COM.03, MLA.CW.REV.01, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose. If the task asks you to write, draft in complete sentences and make the craft move visible rather than hidden. If the task asks you to analyze, quote or describe the specific words that prove your answer. Your answer should show a chain of reasoning, not only a label.
