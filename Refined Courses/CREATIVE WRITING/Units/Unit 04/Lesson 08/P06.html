Poem: “Museum of Rain”

In the dry city, we display umbrellas
beneath glass, each label naming a storm.
My mother points to the smallest one—
blue ribs, one torn wing—then says nothing.
Outside, sprinklers rehearse a weather
no forecast is brave enough to promise.

Tasks

  1. State literal situation.
  2. Paraphrase accurately.
  3. Analyze umbrella metaphor.
  4. Explain “torn wing.”
  5. Analyze the mother’s silence.
  6. Define forecast from context.
  7. Trace weather’s semantic field.
  8. Analyze “rehearse.”
  9. Develop two interpretations.
  10. Explain the controlling effect.
  11. Write a 12–16 line response poem transforming one image.
  12. Write a 220–280 word evidence-based craft rationale.
