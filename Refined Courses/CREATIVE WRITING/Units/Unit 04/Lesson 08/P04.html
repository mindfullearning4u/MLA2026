P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Paraphrase

Poem: The calendar still showed April in December.

Paraphrase: The calendar had not been changed and still displayed April even though it was December.

Example 2: Interpretation

The outdated calendar may suggest that emotional time has stopped around a past event or memory.

Example 3: Craft Explanation

The contrast between April and December creates layered meaning because the calendar shows one time while the real season shows another. This can suggest memory, grief, or being stuck in the past.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The poem proves the speaker hates winter.

This overclaims because the poem never states hatred.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The poem may suggest being emotionally stuck because the calendar remains in April while the actual time is December.
