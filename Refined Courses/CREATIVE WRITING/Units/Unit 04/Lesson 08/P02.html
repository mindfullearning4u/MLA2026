Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
synthesisCombining skills into a coherent whole.A final poem integrates image, sound, structure, diction, and revision.
controlling effectThe dominant reader experience.Every major choice develops a sustained feeling of uneasy anticipation.
diagnostic readingReading to locate the cause of a craft problem.A writer traces a flat ending to an image that never changes.
image systemA related, developing pattern of images.Seeds, roots, branches, and fruit develop a pattern of growth.
semantic fieldWords connected by meaning.“Ledger,” “debt,” “cost,” and “balance” create a field of accounting.
lineationIntentional poetic line shaping.A line break delays a key noun and briefly changes the sentence.
voltaA meaningful turn.The poem shifts from remembering a loss to accepting responsibility.
productive ambiguityMultiple supported readings.The final “door” may signal escape or return, and both readings fit the evidence.
paraphraseAccurate restatement in new words and structure.The writer preserves relationships while changing phrasing and syntax.
interpretationEvidence-based explanation of suggested meaning.The recurring bridge may represent a difficult transition.
global revisionA change to purpose, architecture, pattern, or ending.Replacing the ending changes the poem’s central realization.
local revisionA change to words, sounds, lines, or punctuation.Changing one vague verb sharpens a single image.

Make the Vocabulary Work

A poem’s ending feels unearned because its central image never changes. Which revision best addresses the problem?

A. Replace two ordinary adjectives with more vivid ones.

Not the best choice. This local revision may sharpen individual lines but does not repair the poem’s architecture or image development.

B. Revise the final section so the central image transforms after the volta.

Best choice. This global revision connects the turn, image system, and ending, strengthening synthesis and the controlling effect.
