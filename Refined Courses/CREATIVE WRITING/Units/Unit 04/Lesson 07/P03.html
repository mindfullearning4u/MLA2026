Lesson: The Integrated Poem Blueprint

Nine-Step Process

  1. Name the controlling effect.
  2. Select a concrete generative image.
  3. List literal details before symbolism.
  4. Build two related image or lexical fields.
  5. Choose speaker, audience, and situation.
  6. Plan a three-part architecture: establish, complicate, transform.
  7. Use sound and lineation to control movement.
  8. Create a turn caused by discovery, contrast, or re-seeing.
  9. Revise globally, then locally, testing every choice for coherence.

Visual Blueprint

IMAGE → PATTERN → COUNTERPRESSURE → TURN → TRANSFORMED IMAGE
Diction + sound + lineation shape every stage.

Learning Routes

Color-code patterns; perform the poem aloud; arrange lines on cards and test alternate architectures.
