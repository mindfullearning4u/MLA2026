P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Step 4: Check for Integration

A poem is integrated when its choices work together. If the mood is quiet determination, words like stubborn, through, and rose fit. Random words that create comedy or anger would weaken the poem.

Step 5: Revise One Word at a Time

Revision does not mean changing everything. Replace one vague word with a more precise word. Plant came up becomes green word rose. The revision adds image, metaphor, and style.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: My poem has a metaphor, so it is finished.

This is incorrect because one device is not enough. The metaphor must support the mood and layered meaning.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: My metaphor supports the mood because calling the shoot a "green word" makes growth feel like a quiet message breaking through pressure.

This is correct because it connects the craft choice to meaning.
