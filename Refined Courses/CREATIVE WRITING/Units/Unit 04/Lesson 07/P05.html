Independent Work: Integrated Poem Development

Purpose

Integrate image, sound, structure, diction, figurative language, and theme into a coherent poem.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Make deliberate choices that demonstrate the lesson skill.

Source for Your Work

A train platform at dawn: one traveler boards, one remains, and an unopened letter changes hands just before the doors close.

1. Plan Before You Draft

Poetic DecisionYour Plan
Speaker, situation, and intended effectState each precisely.
Image or language patternList two connected possibilities.
Turn or developmentDescribe what changes and why.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach demonstrates stronger poetic craft? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Add rhyme, metaphor, repetition, and unusual spacing everywhere so every poetic technique is visible.
B. Select and control the techniques that best shape the speaker’s experience, the poem’s turn, and the meaning readers infer.

3. Apply the Lesson

Develop the seed above or an equally complex original situation into a polished 24–32 line poem. Use a purposeful structure, recurring image, controlled sound, precise diction, and a meaningful turn.

4. Revise with Purpose

Complete two revisions: strengthen one structural choice and refine one language choice. Explain the effect of each revision on the whole poem.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your original writing applies the lesson skill and uses the supplied source or model.
  • Your explanation identifies a specific craft choice and its effect.
  • Your revision improves meaning or craft, not only spelling.
