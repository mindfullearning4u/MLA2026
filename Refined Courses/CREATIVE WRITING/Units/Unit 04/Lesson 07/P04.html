P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Planning

Goal: Create a lonely but hopeful mood.

Anchor image: one porch light in a dark street.

Step-by-step: Choose mood, choose image, list diction: small, steady, dark, waiting.

Example 2: Drafting

Draft:
One porch light held its breath
above the silent street,
a small gold answer
no one had asked for yet.

The personification and metaphor create a mood of quiet hope.

Example 3: Explaining

Explanation: The phrase "small gold answer" creates layered meaning because the light is literally visible, but it also suggests hope waiting before someone asks for it. The diction small and silent keeps the mood quiet.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The poem is good because it has light.

This is weak because it does not name the craft choice or explain mood.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The image of one porch light creates quiet hope because it gives the dark street a small but steady point of warmth.
