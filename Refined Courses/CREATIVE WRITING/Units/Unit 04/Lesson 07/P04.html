Worked Example: Three Integrated Poems

Example 1 — Image Transformation

Opening: a cracked cup signifies family fracture. Pattern: leak, stain, careful hands. Counterpressure: the speaker keeps using it. Turn: a gold repair line appears. Ending: the cup holds tea again. The image transforms damage into visible endurance; it is not merely repeated.

Pause and Choose: A or B

Which explanation best traces the cracked cup image across the poem?

A. The cup symbolizes a broken family because cracked objects always represent damage.
Weaker response. This gives the object one fixed meaning and ignores the speaker’s continued use and the repair at the turn.
B. The crack first carries family fracture, but continued use and the later gold repair transform the image into visible endurance; the cup’s meaning changes through action rather than repetition alone.
Stronger response. This explanation follows the image from damage through counterpressure to transformation and connects the ending to an earned meaning.

Retry: Close your first choice and open the other. Compare both explanations before continuing.

Example 2 — Sound and Lineation

A chase poem begins with short enjambed lines and hard consonants: “gate / clicked / heel struck.” At safety, longer vowel-rich lines slow the breath. Structure and sound enact the movement rather than simply describe fear and relief.

Example 3 — Productive Ambiguity

A speaker leaves a lamp burning for “you.” Concrete clues support both an absent loved one and the speaker’s future self. The turn—“I finally enter by the other door”—does not solve identity; it changes waiting into agency. Both readings support the controlling effect.

Your Turn Before Independent Work

Pause and try: Plan a three-stage image transformation for a worn coat: give it an opening meaning, a turn, and an ending meaning.

Check your thinking — open after you try
Teacher model: Opening: the coat hides the speaker. Turn: the speaker lends it during a storm. Ending: the returned coat carries two sets of stitched initials, transforming concealment into shared protection.
