Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and full definition. Add original examples for six terms. Submit with Page 5.
controlling effectThe dominant experience designed for the reader.
generative imageA central image capable of producing multiple related developments.
image systemA connected pattern of images.
poetic architectureThe deliberate organization of a poem.
lineationThe intentional shaping and breaking of poetic lines.
turnA meaningful change in thought, mood, or perspective.
compressionDense expression that carries substantial meaning in few words.
resonanceMeaning or feeling that continues beyond the line.
counterpressureA competing feeling or idea that complicates the poem.
global revisionChanging purpose, order, image system, or ending.
local revisionImproving words, sounds, lines, and punctuation.
coherenceThe meaningful fit among the poem’s choices.
