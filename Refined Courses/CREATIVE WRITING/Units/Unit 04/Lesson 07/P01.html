Lesson Overview: Integrated Poem Development

Lesson Focus

Primary: MLA.CW.POE.01 and MLA.CW.POE.03.
Supporting: MLA.CW.POE.02, MLA.CW.WR.06, MLA.CW.WR.07. Integrate image, figurative pattern, sound, diction, structure, ambiguity, and mood into one controlling effect.

Learning Goals

  • Design a poem as an interacting system.
  • Choose a generative image and transform it across the poem.
  • Use structure, sound, and diction to create a purposeful turn.
  • Revise globally before polishing locally.

Workflow

Vocabulary → integration protocol → three models → 18-part Independent Work → distinct 12-part Checkpoint → 80% quiz.
