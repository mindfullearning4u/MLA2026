Checkpoint: Paraphrase and Interpretation

Directions

Complete all 12 tasks independently and submit separately. At least 80% is required.

Poem: “Threshold”

Only after the train had gone
did the platform begin to speak:
a coin turning beneath the bench,
one paper cup rolling north,
and somewhere beyond the tracks,
a door practicing its hinge.

Tasks

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. Restore ordinary syntax in lines 1–2.
  3. Define platform from context.
  4. Identify each sound or movement.
  5. Paraphrase the poem in 70–90 words.
  6. Explain how your structure differs from the source.
  7. Identify one qualifier or relationship preserved.
  8. Correct a possible patchwritten phrase.
  9. Build an evidence chain for personification.
  10. Develop and support an interpretation.
  11. Offer one counterinterpretation.
  12. Write a 180–220 word comparison of the two readings.

80% Mastery Requirement

Earn at least 80%. Review TOR feedback, correct mistakes, and resubmit.
