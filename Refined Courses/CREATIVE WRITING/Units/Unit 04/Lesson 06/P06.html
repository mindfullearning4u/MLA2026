📘 CW | Unit 04 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts. Keep paraphrase and interpretation separate.

Instructions

Your paraphrase should be accurate and clear. Your interpretation should use cautious language and evidence.

Part A: Paraphrase

Choose one poem model from the lesson. Restate what literally happens in your own words without adding interpretation.

Part B: Interpret

Write one possible interpretation of the same poem. Use at least two details from the poem to support your idea.

Part C: Create

Write four original lines of poetry. Then write a paraphrase of your own lines and one interpretation explaining what your images may suggest.
