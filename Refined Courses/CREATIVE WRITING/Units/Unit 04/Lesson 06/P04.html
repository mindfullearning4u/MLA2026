Worked Example: Three Meaning Ladders

Example 1 — Inverted Syntax

Line: “Across the emptied square moved one blue song.”
Restore order: One blue song moved across the empty square.
Literal: A sound crosses a deserted place; “blue” blends color and sound.
Paraphrase: A solitary, melancholy sound traveled through the vacant plaza.
Interpretation: The synesthesia makes loneliness perceptible across senses.

Example 2 — Qualifier

Line: “Perhaps the locked gate remembers us.”
Trap: “The gate remembers us” removes uncertainty.
Accurate paraphrase: The speaker wonders whether the closed gate somehow retains a memory of them.
Interpretation: “Perhaps” reveals longing mixed with doubt; the speaker cannot know whether the past endures.

Example 3 — Patchwriting vs. Paraphrase

Source: “Winter stitched the river shut beneath a silver seam.”
Patchwriting: Winter sewed the river closed under a silver line.
Accurate paraphrase: Ice covered the river completely, leaving a narrow brightness across its frozen surface.
Interpretation: The sewing metaphor presents freezing as both careful construction and confinement.
