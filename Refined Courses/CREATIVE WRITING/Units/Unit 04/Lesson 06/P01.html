Lesson Overview: Paraphrase and Interpretation of Poetic Models

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.CW.WR.03—accurately paraphrase poetic models before interpreting them.
Supporting standards: MLA.CW.POE.01 and MLA.CW.WR.09.

Learning Goals

  • Reconstruct difficult syntax and resolve pronoun references.
  • Preserve literal relationships while changing wording and structure.
  • Separate paraphrase, inference, interpretation, and evaluation.
  • Use an accurate paraphrase as the foundation for evidence-based interpretation.

Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Learn the eight-step meaning ladder and visual evidence chain.
  3. Study three complete models.
  4. Complete all 18 Page 5 tasks and submit with vocabulary.
  5. Complete the distinct 12-part Checkpoint.
  6. Earn at least 80% on the quiz.

Why This Matters

Interpretation fails when it begins with a misreading. Accurate paraphrase makes the poem’s literal relationships visible, so deeper claims can be tested against what the text actually says.
