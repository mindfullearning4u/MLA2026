P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Paraphrase and Interpretation of Poetic Models

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.03 - Paraphrase and interpretation of poetic models.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.POE.01 - Poetic layers of meaning and ambiguity; MLA.CW.WR.09 - Comparative writing and thematic analysis.

What You Will Learn

  • How to paraphrase a poem model in clear everyday language.
  • How paraphrase differs from interpretation.
  • How to preserve important details without copying the original wording.
  • How to use paraphrase to support poetic interpretation.

What You Will Do

  • Read original poem models line by line.
  • Write accurate paraphrases.
  • Separate paraphrase from analysis.
  • Explain what a poem suggests after you understand what it says.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can accurately paraphrase a poem model and then explain what the model suggests through imagery, diction, or structure.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning to restate difficult poem lines accurately before interpreting them. Paraphrase helps you understand the surface meaning; interpretation explains the deeper meaning and craft effect.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if your paraphrase copies too much of the original wording, changes the meaning, or skips important details.
