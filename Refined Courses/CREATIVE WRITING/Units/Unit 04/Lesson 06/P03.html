Lesson: The Meaning Ladder

Eight-Step Method

  1. Read the complete poem for situation and speaker.
  2. Mark unfamiliar words and infer them from context.
  3. Restore inverted syntax to ordinary order.
  4. Identify every referent.
  5. Divide complex lines into literal propositions.
  6. Restate each proposition in new wording and structure.
  7. Check relationships, qualifiers, and omissions against the source.
  8. Only then build an interpretation through an evidence chain.

Visual Evidence Ladder

EXACT WORDS → LITERAL RELATIONSHIP → ACCURATE PARAPHRASE → INFERENCE → INTERPRETATION

If a higher step cannot be traced downward, the claim is unsupported.

Learning Routes

  • Visual: color-match source details to paraphrase clauses.
  • Auditory: speak the line in ordinary word order before writing.
  • Hands-on: cut a complex line into phrase cards and rearrange it without changing relationships.

Accuracy Checklist

Preserve who does what, time, cause, contrast, condition, degree, uncertainty, and image logic. Do not add motives or themes to the paraphrase.
