Poem: “Instructions for Returning”

What the road forgets, the shoes remember:
rain pressed into leather, red dust in every seam.
At the bridge, do not ask the river
which bank first taught it leaving.
Carry the key, though no door waits.
Say home only if the word still opens.

Tasks

  1. Identify speaker, audience, and situation.
  2. Define seam from context.
  3. Restore ordinary syntax in line 1.
  4. Identify referents.
  5. List the literal propositions.
  6. Explain the road/shoes contrast.
  7. Paraphrase lines 1–2.
  8. Paraphrase lines 3–4.
  9. Paraphrase lines 5–6.
  10. Check every qualifier and condition.
  11. Identify one tempting distortion.
  12. Show why a near-copy is patchwriting.
  13. Build two evidence chains.
  14. Develop a main interpretation.
  15. Offer a counterinterpretation.
  16. Evaluate evidence coverage.
  17. Write a 250–300 word analytical response.
  18. Transform the poem into a 300–400 word prose scene while preserving its relationships, ambiguity, and controlling effect; add a craft rationale.
