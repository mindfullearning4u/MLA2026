Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. For paraphrase, interpretation, inference, patchwriting, distortion, and qualifier, add an original example. Submit with Page 5.
paraphraseA restatement preserving meaning in genuinely new wording and structure.
interpretationAn evidence-based explanation of what a text suggests or means.
inferenceA conclusion formed from textual evidence and reasoning.
literal propositionA basic claim directly stated or logically contained in a line.
syntactic inversionAn unusual word order used for emphasis, rhythm, or form.
referentThe person, object, or idea a pronoun or phrase identifies.
context clueNearby information that helps determine meaning.
semantic relationshipA meaning connection such as cause, contrast, condition, or comparison.
patchwritingA near-copy that changes only a few words.
distortionA change that misrepresents the original meaning.
evidence chainDetail → literal meaning → inference → interpretation.
qualifierA word limiting certainty, scope, or intensity.
