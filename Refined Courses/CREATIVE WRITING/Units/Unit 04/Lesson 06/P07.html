P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can paraphrase and interpret poetic models accurately.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your paraphrase changes the poem, copies the poem too closely, or if your interpretation is not supported by evidence.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review P02 and P03 notes.
  2. Complete P06 Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that paraphrase and interpretation are separate.
  4. Submit your completed checkpoint work in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Choose one poem model from the lesson. Write a paraphrase, then write a separate interpretation using two pieces of evidence. Finally, write two original lines that use one image from your interpretation.

Mastery Criteria

  • Paraphrases accurately in original words.
  • Does not add interpretation inside the paraphrase.
  • Writes a supported interpretation.
  • Uses evidence from the poem model.
  • Creates original lines connected to the interpretation.
