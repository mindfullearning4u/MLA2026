This checkpoint confirms that you can analyze how figurative language develops mood across a poem.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your paragraph only lists devices, if you cannot find evidence from more than one part of the poem, or if your mood word does not match the language.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review P02 and P03 notes.
  2. Complete P06 Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Check that your paragraph includes a claim, evidence, and reasoning.
  4. Submit your completed checkpoint work in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Write one analytical paragraph explaining how a poem's figurative language develops or shifts mood. Use at least two pieces of evidence from different parts of the poem. Then write two original lines that show a similar mood shift.

Mastery Criteria

  • States a clear claim about mood development.
  • Uses evidence from more than one part of the poem.
  • Explains figurative language instead of only naming it.
  • Connects diction, imagery, or comparison to the reader's feeling.
  • Creates original lines that intentionally develop or shift mood.
