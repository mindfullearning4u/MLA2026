P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Advanced figurative language and mood

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.POE.03 - Advanced figurative language and mood.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.POE.02 - Figurative language and mood; MLA.CW.WR.05 - Organization and development of written explanation.

What You Will Learn

  • How to analyze more than one example of figurative language in a poem.
  • How mood can shift or deepen across lines.
  • How to explain a pattern of imagery, diction, and comparison.
  • How to organize a short analysis with a claim, evidence, and reasoning.

What You Will Do

  • Read poem models that contain multiple poetic devices.
  • Track how figurative language works across several lines.
  • Write analytical claims about mood.
  • Practice explaining how poetic choices develop a reader's experience.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can explain how several figurative language choices work together to create or shift mood in a poem.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are moving beyond naming a single device. You are learning to explain how a poet uses a pattern of figurative language, word choice, and images to shape the mood from beginning to end.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you can identify individual devices but cannot explain how they work together, or if your analysis sounds like a list instead of a connected explanation.
