Lesson Overview: Advanced Figurative Language and Mood

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.CW.POE.03—analyze and create patterns of advanced figurative language that deepen or shift mood.
Supporting standards: MLA.CW.POE.02 and MLA.CW.WR.05.

What You Will Learn

  • Trace an extended metaphor or image pattern across a poem.
  • Analyze how juxtaposition, ambiguity, sound, and a volta complicate mood.
  • Distinguish a mood shift from a random change in description.
  • Build a claim–evidence–reasoning analysis explaining interaction among devices.

Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Learn the six-step pattern-and-shift protocol on Page 3.
  3. Study three complete models on Page 4.
  4. Complete all 18 Page 5 tasks and submit them with vocabulary.
  5. Complete the distinct 12-part Checkpoint.
  6. Earn at least 80% on the quiz.

Why This Matters

Advanced poems rarely create one simple feeling. They establish expectations, complicate them, and use patterns whose meaning changes as the speaker’s understanding changes.
