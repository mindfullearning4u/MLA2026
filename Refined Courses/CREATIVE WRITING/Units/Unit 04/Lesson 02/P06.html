📘 CW | Unit 04 | Lesson 02

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts. Your work should show analysis, not only identification.

Instructions

Use a claim, at least two pieces of evidence, and reasoning that explains how figurative language develops mood.

Part A: Track

Create a chart with two columns: early mood and later mood. Add at least two words or images to each column from the lesson poem model.

Part B: Analyze

Write one paragraph explaining how the mood develops. Include a claim, early evidence, later evidence, and reasoning.

Part C: Create

Write six original lines of poetry that use at least two figurative language choices to develop or shift mood. Then write a 3-4 sentence explanation of your craft choices.
