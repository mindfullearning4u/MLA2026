Poem: “Signal”

All night the harbor practiced warning—
buoys blinking their red syllables,
fog folding ships into its coat.
I called the dark a locked room
until one horn crossed the water,
low and gold, opening no door.

Tasks

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. Identify the dominant semantic field.
  3. Trace the extended metaphor of language and communication.
  4. Analyze fog personification.
  5. Explain the synesthesia in “low and gold.”
  6. Define buoy from context and cite the clue.
  7. Locate the volta and explain its effect.
  8. Name the mood progression with evidence.
  9. Explain the ambiguity of the final line.
  10. Revise one line to make the ending hopeful; explain the change.
  11. Write an original eight-line poem with a pattern, volta, and nuanced mood.
  12. Write a 170–220 word evidence-based rationale.
