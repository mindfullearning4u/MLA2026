Poem: “Inventory of a Quiet House”

We counted cups asleep in the cupboard,
dust writing its gray alphabet on glass.
The clock chewed noon into smaller pieces;
floorboards whispered each borrowed name.
Still, the blue bowl held one summer peach—
a small sun refusing the weather.
Then the key turned, bright as a struck match,
and every shadow leaned toward the door.

Analyze and Create

  1. State the literal situation.
  2. Name the opening mood and support it.
  3. Identify the dominant semantic field.
  4. Trace the personification pattern.
  5. Analyze the clock metaphor.
  6. Explain “gray alphabet.”
  7. Analyze the peach as symbol and visual contrast.
  8. Locate the volta and justify the choice.
  9. Compare mood before and after the turn.
  10. Explain what emotional quality remains unchanged.
  11. Offer two interpretations of the final line.
  12. Evaluate which interpretation fits more evidence.
  13. Complete a visual pattern tracker.
  14. Write a claim–evidence–reasoning paragraph using three details.
  15. Draft a 20–28 line poem using an extended metaphor.
  16. Include a deliberate volta, one juxtaposition, and one pattern break.
  17. Annotate the craft choices and revise one weak link.
  18. Write a 220–280 word rationale explaining mood progression and cumulative effect.
