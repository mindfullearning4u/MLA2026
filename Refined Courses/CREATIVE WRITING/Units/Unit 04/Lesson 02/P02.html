Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Add an original example for extended metaphor, juxtaposition, synesthesia, motif, and volta. Submit these notes with Page 5 as Notebook Evidence.
TermDefinition
extended metaphorA comparison developed across multiple lines or sections.
conceitAn elaborate, often surprising comparison that organizes a poem.
semantic fieldA group of words connected by a shared area of meaning.
juxtapositionPlacing contrasting images or ideas together to reveal a relationship.
ambiguityLanguage supporting more than one reasonable interpretation.
synesthesiaImagery that blends two or more senses.
motifA recurring image, phrase, sound, or idea.
voltaA meaningful turn in thought, mood, argument, or perspective.
mood progressionThe way atmosphere develops across a text.
tonal complexityThe coexistence or tension of more than one attitude.
pattern breakA deliberate disruption that draws attention or signals change.
cumulative effectThe combined impact produced by multiple related choices.
