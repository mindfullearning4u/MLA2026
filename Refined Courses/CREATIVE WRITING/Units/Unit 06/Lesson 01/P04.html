P04 Worked Example

Study how a writer turns portfolio selection into a craft rationale.

Worked Example 1: Selecting a Piece

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Piece: A poem titled "Last Bus."

Step 1: Identify why it matters: the poem shows revision from telling to showing.

Step 2: Identify evidence: "I was lonely" became "The last bus sighed past without opening its doors."

Step 3: State selection reason: This poem belongs in the portfolio because it shows growth in imagery and emotional control.

Worked Example 2: Explaining Craft Evidence

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Claim: The final image is stronger than the original sentence.

Evidence: The bus "sighed past" uses personification and action.

Explanation: The reader understands the speaker's loneliness through a scene instead of a direct label.

Worked Example 3: Final Craft Rationale

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

I selected "Last Bus" because it shows my growth in using imagery to reveal emotion. In revision, I replaced the direct sentence "I was lonely" with the image of the last bus passing without stopping. That image helps the reader feel the speaker's loneliness through action and setting.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: This poem is my favorite, so it should go in the portfolio.

This is incomplete because a favorite piece still needs evidence of craft control, growth, or publication readiness.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: This poem belongs in the portfolio because the revised final image shows stronger control of imagery and reader effect.

This is correct because it gives a reason based on craft evidence.

Teachable Explanation: The correct example works because it applies the lesson skill deliberately and explains the reasoning instead of only naming an answer.
