📘 CW | Unit 06 | Lesson 01

P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can select a portfolio piece and explain its craft value.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your rationale only says that you like the piece, if your evidence is unclear, or if you cannot explain reader effect.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one creative piece or excerpt.
  2. Write a selection claim.
  3. Identify two pieces of craft or revision evidence.
  4. Explain the reader effect of the evidence.
  5. Submit your craft rationale in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a portfolio selection rationale with the selected piece title, selection claim, two pieces of evidence, and a reader-effect explanation.

Mastery Criteria

  • Selects a piece for a craft-based reason.
  • Uses specific evidence instead of vague praise.
  • Explains revision, craft choice, or growth accurately.
  • Connects evidence to reader effect.
  • Writes a clear rationale that could support a final portfolio.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
