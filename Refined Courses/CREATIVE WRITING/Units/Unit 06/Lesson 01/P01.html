P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Portfolio Selection and Craft Rationale

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.08 - Reflection on creative process and product.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.09 - Portfolio selection and evidence; MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback.

What You Will Learn

  • How to choose portfolio pieces for evidence-based reasons.
  • How to explain craft growth using specific revision evidence.
  • How to connect a selected piece to purpose, audience, and reader effect.
  • How to avoid vague portfolio explanations.

What You Will Do

  • Study weak and strong portfolio selection reasons.
  • Practice identifying useful craft evidence.
  • Write a rationale that explains why a piece belongs in a portfolio.
  • Use feedback and revision evidence to support your selection.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can select a portfolio piece for a clear reason and explain the craft choices, feedback, and revisions that make the piece meaningful evidence of your growth.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are not just collecting finished work. You are learning to prove why a piece belongs in your portfolio by explaining what it shows about your writing decisions and growth.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you cannot decide which piece best shows growth, if your rationale sounds vague, or if you cannot connect revision evidence to craft improvement.
