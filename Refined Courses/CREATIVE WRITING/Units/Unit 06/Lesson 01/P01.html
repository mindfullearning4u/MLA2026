Lesson Overview: Portfolio Selection and Craft Rationale

Lesson Focus

Lesson 1: Portfolio Selection and Craft Rationale

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.WR.08 — Reflection on creative process and product.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.09 — Portfolio selection and evidence; MLA.CW.REV.01 — Revision from feedback.

What You Will Learn

  • Choose portfolio pieces through evidence-based criteria.
  • Distinguish personal attachment from demonstrable craft quality.
  • Explain growth using precise draft-to-final evidence.
  • Write a defensible craft rationale for an unfamiliar audience.

Selection Lens

AskProve with
What does the piece do well?Exact language, structure, scene, or form
How did it grow?Before/after revision evidence
Why does it belong?A standard, portfolio purpose, and audience benefit

Mastery Target

I can select a creative work for a portfolio and defend the choice using specific craft, revision, audience, and growth evidence. A score of at least 80% is required.
