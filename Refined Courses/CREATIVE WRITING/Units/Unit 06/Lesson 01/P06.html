📘 CW | Unit 06 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one creative piece you may include in your final portfolio.

Instructions

If you do not have a piece ready, use this model excerpt: The last bus sighed past without opening its doors. I stayed under the shelter, holding a ticket soft with rain.

Part A: Selection Claim

Name the piece and write one sentence explaining why it belongs in the portfolio. The reason must connect to craft, growth, revision, or reader effect.

Part B: Evidence

Provide two pieces of evidence. At least one should show a revision, craft choice, feedback decision, or before-and-after improvement.

Part C: Craft Rationale

Write a complete craft rationale that includes a selection claim, evidence, and reader effect.
