P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

This lesson teaches how to choose portfolio pieces with evidence instead of preference alone.

Notebook Title

Portfolio Selection and Craft Rationale

Vocabulary

Portfolio selection: Choosing a piece because it shows growth, craft control, revision, or publication readiness.

Craft rationale: An explanation of why the piece matters and how specific writing choices affect the reader.

Evidence: A concrete detail, feedback note, revision, or craft choice that supports your claim.

Growth: A visible improvement in skill, control, purpose, or reader effect.

Step 1: Start with the Purpose of the Portfolio

A portfolio is not a storage folder. It is a collection that proves what you can do as a writer. Each selected piece should show something specific: stronger imagery, clearer structure, better dialogue, more controlled format, stronger revision, or deeper reflection.

Weak reason: I chose this because it was easy.

Strong reason: I chose this because the revised ending shows how I learned to use action instead of direct explanation.

Step 2: Choose Evidence Before You Write the Rationale

Do not begin with a general opinion. First find evidence. Evidence can be a before-and-after revision, a peer comment, a teacher comment, an online-tool suggestion you evaluated, a craft choice, or a reader effect.

Use this question: What can I point to in the piece that proves growth or craft control?

Step 3: Connect Evidence to Reader Effect

A strong rationale does not stop at naming a change. It explains why the change matters. If you revised a vague feeling into an image, explain how the image helps the reader infer emotion. If you changed line breaks, explain how the pacing changed.

Self-Paced Learning Path

If you need support: Choose one piece and identify one specific craft strength.

If you are on pace: Choose one piece, identify evidence, and explain reader effect.

If you are ready for a challenge: Compare two pieces and explain which one better proves your growth as a writer.

Notebook Task Part 1

Part A: Define portfolio selection, craft rationale, and evidence.

Part B: Write one weak selection reason and revise it into a strong selection reason.

Part C: Identify one piece you may include in a portfolio and list two pieces of evidence that support it.
