Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
craft rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of why a writer made or preserved particular artistic choices.
selection criterionA stated standard used to evaluate whether an item belongs in a collection.
representativeAccurately showing an important quality, skill, or pattern.
rangeMeaningful variety in genre, purpose, technique, voice, or challenge.
artifactA work or piece of evidence included in a portfolio.
revision evidenceSpecific before-and-after material showing how and why a work changed.
metacognitionAwareness and analysis of one’s own thinking and decision-making.
tradeoffA gain in one quality that may require giving up or limiting another.
defensible claimA conclusion supported by relevant reasons and evidence.
