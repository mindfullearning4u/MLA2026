📘 CW | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one narrative or fiction piece from your portfolio work.

Instructions

If you do not have a current piece, use this model situation: Ellis leaves an apology note under Mara's door instead of speaking to her directly.

Part A: Diagnose

Name one craft problem in the scene: conflict, motivation, pacing, point of view, structure, or character development.

Part B: Revise

Write or describe one targeted revision that directly solves the problem. Do not choose a random detail that leaves the main problem unchanged.

Part C: Explain

Explain how the revision improves reader understanding of character, conflict, pacing, point of view, or structure.
