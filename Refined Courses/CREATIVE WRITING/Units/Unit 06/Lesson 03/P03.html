P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

This page teaches how to revise narrative and fiction work without creating new problems.

Step 4: Revise the Scene, Not Just the Sentence

A story problem often needs more than a word change. If readers do not understand why a character leaves, changing one adjective will not solve it. You may need a beat of action, a line of dialogue, or a clearer consequence.

Step 5: Explain the Portfolio Value

After revising, explain what the final story now proves about your writing. Use this frame: I revised _____ so the reader could _____. This belongs in my portfolio because _____.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The ending was confusing, so I changed the character's shirt color.

This is incorrect because the revision does not solve the problem. A confusing ending usually needs clearer motivation, consequence, or sequence.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The ending was confusing, so I added one action showing the character choosing to leave before the final line.

This is correct because the revision addresses motivation and sequence.
