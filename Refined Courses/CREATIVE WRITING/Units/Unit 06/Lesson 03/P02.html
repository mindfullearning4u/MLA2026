Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
narrative arcThe shaped progression of conflict, choices, consequences, and change.
causalityThe relationship in which one event or choice meaningfully produces another.
character motivationThe desire, fear, belief, or need that explains a character’s actions.
sceneA dramatized unit of action occurring in a particular time and place.
summaryCompressed narration that covers time or information without dramatizing every moment.
narrative distanceHow close the narration feels to a character’s immediate thoughts and perceptions.
focalizationThe perspective through which narrative information is perceived or filtered.
turning pointA choice, discovery, or event that changes the direction or stakes of a narrative.
earned endingA conclusion prepared by earlier choices and patterns while still allowing surprise.
