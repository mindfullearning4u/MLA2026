Independent Work: Portfolio Narrative Revision

Directions

Select one narrative or fiction piece of at least 1,000 words. Submit a revised excerpt or complete work plus a 700–900-word revision memo and Page 2 vocabulary notes.

Part A — Diagnose

  1. State the story’s central desire, obstacle, choice, consequence, and change.
  2. Gather at least three pieces of reader feedback and separate symptoms from causes.
  3. Map five major scenes using “wants—but—so—causing.”
  4. Identify the highest-impact weakness and explain why it takes priority.
  5. If the narrative uses an unfamiliar or specialized term, define it in context or explain the clues that establish its meaning.

Part B — Revise

  1. Revise at least 500 words or one complete major scene.
  2. Strengthen one causal link between choice and consequence.
  3. Control focalization throughout the revised passage.
  4. Expand one consequential moment and compress one lower-value passage.
  5. Plant or refine one element that helps earn the ending.

Part C — Explain and Test

  1. Present 150–250 words of before-and-after evidence.
  2. Explain the diagnosis and the reasoning behind each change.
  3. Identify one piece of feedback you rejected or adapted.
  4. Use two targeted reader questions to test the revision.
  5. State one remaining limitation and measurable next step.

Required Visual

Include a labeled narrative chain: Desire → Obstacle → Choice → Consequence → Change, with exact scene references.
