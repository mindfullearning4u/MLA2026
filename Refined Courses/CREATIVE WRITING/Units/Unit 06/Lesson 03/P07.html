P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can revise narrative or fiction work for portfolio readiness.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your revision does not match the story problem or if your explanation does not name a reader effect.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select a narrative or fiction scene.
  2. Diagnose one craft problem.
  3. Make or describe one targeted revision.
  4. Explain the reader effect.
  5. Submit the revision explanation in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a narrative or fiction portfolio revision note that identifies the craft problem, describes the revision, and explains how the revision improves character, conflict, pacing, point of view, or structure.

Mastery Criteria

  • Correctly diagnoses a narrative or fiction craft problem.
  • Chooses a revision that directly addresses the problem.
  • Protects the story's purpose and voice.
  • Explains how the revision affects reader understanding.
  • Connects the revision to portfolio value.
