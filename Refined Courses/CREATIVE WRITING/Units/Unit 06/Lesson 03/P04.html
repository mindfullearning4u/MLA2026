P04 Worked Example

Study how a narrative scene is revised for portfolio readiness.

Example 1: Motivation Revision

Problem: Readers do not know why Ellis leaves.

Step 1: Identify the missing motivation.

Step 2: Add one action: Ellis folds the apology note and places it under Mara's door.

Step 3: The reader now sees that Ellis leaves after choosing indirect honesty.

Example 2: Pacing Revision

Problem: The door opens too quickly for suspense.

Revision: Add two action beats: Ellis listens to the floorboards and watches the light under the door shift.

Why it works: The added beats slow the moment and let tension build.

Example 3: Portfolio Explanation

I revised the final scene so Ellis's decision is shown through action instead of explanation. The note under the door clarifies motivation and keeps the quiet tone. This scene belongs in my portfolio because it shows stronger control of character choice and pacing.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: I added more description of the weather because the character's motivation was unclear.

This is incorrect because weather description does not automatically clarify character motivation.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: I added the note under the door so readers understand why Ellis leaves without a conversation.

This is correct because the revision directly solves the character-motivation problem.
