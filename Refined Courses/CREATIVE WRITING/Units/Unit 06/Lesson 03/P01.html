P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Narrative and Fiction Portfolio Revision

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.NAR.01 - Narrative development and structure; MLA.CW.NAR.02 - Narrative point of view, pacing, and detail; MLA.CW.FIC.06 - Fiction craft and character development.

What You Will Learn

  • How to revise a narrative or fiction piece for portfolio readiness.
  • How to diagnose character, conflict, point of view, pacing, and structure problems.
  • How to use feedback without damaging the story's purpose or voice.
  • How to explain narrative revision as evidence of growth.

What You Will Do

  • Study revision targets for narrative and fiction work.
  • Practice selecting the best revision for a story problem.
  • Revise one narrative or fiction excerpt for portfolio use.
  • Explain the craft reason for your revision.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can identify a narrative or fiction craft problem, choose a targeted revision, and explain how the revision improves the reader's understanding of character, conflict, pacing, or structure.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning to revise story work like an author. You will make changes that help readers follow what happens, understand why characters act, and experience the story's effect.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you cannot tell whether your story problem is about character, conflict, pacing, point of view, or structure.
