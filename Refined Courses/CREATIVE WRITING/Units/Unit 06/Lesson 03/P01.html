Lesson Overview: Narrative and Fiction Portfolio Revision

Lesson Focus

Lesson 3: Narrative and Fiction Portfolio Revision

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.REV.01 — Revision from feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.NAR.01 — Narrative development and structure; MLA.CW.NAR.02 — Point of view, pacing, and detail; MLA.CW.FIC.06 — Fiction craft and character development.

What You Will Learn

  • Diagnose story problems beneath reader symptoms.
  • Revise character desire, conflict, causality, point of view, and pacing as an integrated system.
  • Use feedback without flattening voice or intentional ambiguity.
  • Document narrative revision as evidence of growth.

Mastery Target

I can revise a narrative for portfolio readiness and defend how structural and sentence-level changes improve character, causality, pacing, viewpoint, and reader effect. A score of at least 80% is required.
