P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

This lesson reviews Unit 6 skills and prepares you for the Unit Assessment. No new primary standard is introduced.

Standards Covered

Previously Taught Standards: Portfolio selection and reflection, revision from feedback, format-specific publication quality, online collaborative publication, author presentation, and craft reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to review Unit 6 skills before the assessment.
  • How portfolio selection, revision, publication formatting, author statement, and presentation planning fit together.
  • How to answer synthesis questions by matching the situation to the correct craft or publication decision.

What You Will Do

  • Review key Unit 6 decision points.
  • Practice identifying strong portfolio and presentation choices.
  • Prepare for the Unit Assessment.
  • Submit the final checkpoint and complete the Unit Assessment in Moodle.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by completing the Unit Assessment and demonstrating that you can make strong decisions about portfolio evidence, revision, publication formatting, author statement voice, presentation planning, and reader or audience effect.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you are unsure how to access the Unit Assessment, if you do not understand a checkpoint requirement, or if you need clarification about final portfolio submission expectations.
