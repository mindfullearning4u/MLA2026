Independent Work: Final Portfolio Decision Brief

Directions

Complete a final quality review of your Unit 6 portfolio and submit a 1,000–1,200-word decision brief, corrected release candidate, evidence dashboard, and Page 2 vocabulary notes.

Part A — Establish and Rank

  1. List every nonnegotiable requirement and preference.
  2. Create a constraint hierarchy covering ethics, alignment, access, function, and polish.
  3. If any artifact uses an unfamiliar or specialized term, define it in context or explain the clues readers need.

Part B — Verify Evidence

  1. Construct five complete evidence chains across different standards.
  2. Verify every artifact, title, version, claim, and cross-reference.
  3. Test files, links, media, mobile display, and accessibility features.
  4. Document consent, rights, and privacy decisions.

Part C — Resolve and Decide

  1. Identify two conflicting or incomplete pieces of feedback.
  2. Generate and compare two response options for each.
  3. Correct all high-priority failures and record verification evidence.
  4. Write a final Ready/Ready after corrections/Not ready judgment.
  5. Acknowledge one remaining limitation and risk-mitigation plan.

Required Dashboard

Use columns: Requirement | Risk | Evidence | Verification | Status | Correction | Final result.
