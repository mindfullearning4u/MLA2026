Worked Example: Three Unit 6 Transfer Problems

Example 1 — Competing Priorities

Case: A polished portfolio contains an interview without consent and a poem with one typographical error.

  1. Rank risk: missing consent can block publication; the typo is lower risk.
  2. Pause publication of the interview.
  3. Request and document consent or remove the media.
  4. Correct the typo afterward.
  5. Judgment: not ready until the rights issue is resolved.

Pause and Choose: A or B

Which issue must be resolved before the portfolio is ready to publish?

A. Correct the poem’s typographical error first because visible mistakes always create the greatest risk.
Weaker response. The typo affects polish, but it does not outweigh a rights and consent problem that can block ethical publication.
B. Pause publication of the interview until consent is documented or the media is removed; correct the lower-risk typo afterward.
Stronger response. This response ranks risks, protects the interview subject, names two acceptable remedies, and sequences lower-risk polishing afterward.

Retry: Close your first choice and open the other. Compare both explanations before continuing.

Example 2 — Feedback Conflict

Case: One reader wants an essay’s ending explained; another values its ambiguity.

  1. Shared observation: the ending requires interpretation.
  2. Purpose: invite uncertainty while preserving emotional clarity.
  3. Option A adds explanation; Option B strengthens an earlier recurring object.
  4. Test whether B gives readers a stable emotional relationship without one fixed meaning.
  5. Choose B if the evidence supports purposeful ambiguity.

Example 3 — Weak Evidence Chain

Claim: “I grew in characterization.” Evidence: a final excerpt only.

  1. Identify the missing links: no earlier version, diagnosis, or revision cause.
  2. Add a before passage where motive is stated abstractly.
  3. Add the after passage where choice and consequence reveal motive.
  4. Explain how feedback exposed the problem.
  5. Verify that the after passage appears in the released artifact.

Transfer Frame

Requirement → risk → evidence → options → test → decision → verification.
