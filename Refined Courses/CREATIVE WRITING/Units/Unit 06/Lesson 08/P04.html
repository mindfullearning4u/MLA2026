P04 Worked Example

Use these examples to review how Unit 6 skills appear in assessment questions.

Example 1: Portfolio Selection

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question type: Which piece should the writer include?

Step 1: Look for evidence of craft growth.

Step 2: Avoid choices based only on favorite, longest, or easiest.

Best answer: Choose the piece that shows a clear revision and can be explained in the author statement.

Example 2: Publication Formatting

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question type: What should the writer fix before digital publication?

Step 1: Look for reader-facing issues such as missing title, inconsistent spacing, confusing labels, or draft comments.

Step 2: Choose the answer that improves readability and readiness.

Best answer: Preview the file, correct spacing and labels, and submit the final version.

Example 3: Author Presentation

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Question type: Which presentation plan is strongest?

Step 1: Look for title, genre, brief context, excerpt, listening focus, craft reasoning, and reflection.

Step 2: Avoid summary-only answers.

Best answer: A plan that introduces the piece, tells the audience what craft choice to notice, reads a focused excerpt, and explains effect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Pick the answer that says the writer worked hard."

Why this is incorrect: Effort matters, but the assessment measures specific writing, revision, publication, and presentation decisions.

Correct Version

Correct: Pick the answer that names the strongest action and explains why that action helps readers or listeners.

Teachable Explanation: Unit 6 synthesis is about purposeful author control from portfolio selection through final presentation.
