Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
synthesisThe integration of multiple skills or ideas into a coherent new judgment or product.The final defense combines craft analysis, evidence, and reflection.
constraint hierarchyA ranking of requirements according to importance, risk, and dependency.Permission and accessibility come before decorative design choices.
evidence chainA connected sequence from claim to artifact, exact evidence, reasoning, and verification.The defense links a growth claim to paired drafts and explains the change.
authenticityThe credible alignment of a writer’s voice, choices, values, and evidence.The statement honestly explains both success and unresolved risk.
editorial independenceThe ability to evaluate feedback and make reasoned decisions rather than obeying every suggestion.The writer rejects advice that would erase the intended ambiguity.
quality thresholdThe minimum defined level a product must meet before release.Every link must function and every source must have permission.
risk mitigationAn action that reduces the likelihood or impact of a known problem.Keeping a verified backup reduces the risk of losing the final files.
summative assessmentAn evaluation measuring integrated learning after instruction.The final portfolio defense is a summative assessment.
transfer taskA problem requiring learned skills to be applied in a new context.The writer applies revision criteria to an unfamiliar draft.

Make the Vocabulary Work

Which response builds a complete evidence chain?

A. “My portfolio shows growth because the final pieces are better.”

This gives a claim but no precise artifact, evidence, or reasoning.

B. “The first and final scene drafts show that I replaced summary with dialogue; the added conflict now makes the turning point earned.”

Correct. It identifies the artifacts, names the exact change, and explains how that evidence supports the claim.
