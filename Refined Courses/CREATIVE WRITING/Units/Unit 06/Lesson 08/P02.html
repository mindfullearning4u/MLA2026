Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
synthesisThe integration of multiple skills or ideas into a coherent new judgment or product.
constraint hierarchyA ranking of requirements according to importance, risk, and dependency.
evidence chainA connected sequence from claim to artifact, exact evidence, reasoning, and verification.
authenticityThe credible alignment of a writer’s voice, choices, values, and evidence.
editorial independenceThe ability to evaluate feedback and make reasoned decisions rather than obeying every suggestion.
quality thresholdThe minimum defined level a product must meet before release.
risk mitigationAn action that reduces the likelihood or impact of a known problem.
summative assessmentAn evaluation measuring integrated learning after instruction.
transfer taskA problem requiring learned skills to be applied in a new context.
