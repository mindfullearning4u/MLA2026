Notebook Title

Unit 6 Portfolio, Presentation, and Publication Synthesis Review

Vocabulary Review

  • Portfolio evidence: A piece or revision that proves a writer's craft growth.
  • Author statement: A reflective explanation of purpose, process, craft, and growth.
  • Publication readiness: Final formatting, organization, conventions, and file preparation for readers.
  • Presentation focus: The craft choice the audience should notice during an excerpt.

Step 1: Review Portfolio Selection

A strong portfolio choice is not just a favorite piece. It is a piece that shows craft, revision, growth, or audience impact. When a question asks which piece belongs in a portfolio, choose the option with the clearest evidence and explanation.

Write this rule in your notebook: Portfolio choices should be purposeful, supported by evidence, and connected to author growth.

Step 2: Review Revision and Formatting Decisions

Revision decisions improve meaning, clarity, style, structure, pacing, or reader effect. Formatting decisions prepare the piece for readers through title, spacing, line breaks, paragraphing, labels, conventions, and file readiness. On the assessment, ask whether the problem is about the writing itself or about the final reader-facing presentation.

Step 3: Review Author Statement and Presentation Decisions

An author statement explains the writer's process and growth. A presentation guides an audience through a selected excerpt. Both should use craft reasoning. If an answer only summarizes what happens, it is usually weaker than an answer that names a craft choice and explains effect.
