P06 Independent Work

Complete a final Unit 6 synthesis review before the Unit Assessment.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Use this review to prepare your thinking. You are not learning a new skill here. You are checking that you can apply Unit 6 skills together.

Part A: Skill Sort

Make four headings: portfolio selection, revision, publication formatting, and author presentation. Under each heading, write two decisions a writer must make.

Part B: Evidence and Effect

Choose one portfolio piece. Write the evidence it provides and the reader or audience effect it creates. Then explain which Unit 6 skill it best represents.

Part C: Assessment Readiness

Write three reminders for yourself before taking the Unit Assessment. Each reminder should begin with an action verb, such as identify, connect, choose, check, or explain.
