Checkpoint: Final Unit 6 Transfer Challenge

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. It prepares you for the U6 Final Assessment. A score of at least 80% is required.

New Case

Elena’s portfolio includes a polished short story, two similar poems, an author statement, and a presentation. The statement claims growth in dialogue, but none of the selected pieces contains sustained dialogue. The presentation uses a strong excerpt from an omitted essay. One poem includes a photograph copied from social media without credit or permission. Mobile testing shows accidental line wraps. The audio introduction has captions but no transcript, and the final download still contains teacher comments. Elena wants to publish tonight because the website colors are finished.

Analysis

  1. Identify two selection or range problems.
  2. Trace the broken evidence chain in the dialogue claim.
  3. Explain the presentation/portfolio alignment failure.
  4. Rank the rights, mobile-format, transcript, and comment problems.
  5. Explain why finished colors do not meet the quality threshold.
  6. State which issue blocks publication most clearly and why.

Repair and Synthesis

  1. Propose two defensible repairs for the dialogue-growth claim.
  2. Resolve the omitted-essay presentation problem.
  3. State the exact actions required for the photograph.
  4. Design a verification plan for poem lineation and audio access.
  5. Create a seven-row evidence-and-risk dashboard.
  6. Write a 180–220-word final release decision supported by at least six criteria.

Final Assessment Readiness

After this checkpoint is complete and passed, take the U6 Final Assessment. Review feedback and contact your TOR before retrying if you score below 80%.
