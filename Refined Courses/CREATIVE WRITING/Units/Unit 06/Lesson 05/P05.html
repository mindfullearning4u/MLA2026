Independent Work: Publication Formatting and Digital Preparation

Purpose

Prepare a coherent, readable, accessible digital portfolio whose design supports the creative work.

Complete each numbered part in your notebook. Support decisions with precise evidence from your portfolio work.

Source for Your Work

Publication scenario: A portfolio will be viewed on laptops and phones and may also be printed. It includes prose, poetry, headings, images, and links. Readers need consistent navigation and accessible presentation.

1. Plan Before You Work

Portfolio DecisionEvidence and Plan
Purpose and intended audienceState each precisely.
Strongest current evidenceName the piece, passage, or feature.
Most important improvementDescribe the needed change and intended effect.

2. Choose and Explain

Which approach demonstrates stronger author decision-making? Record A or B, then explain your choice in 2–3 sentences using a lesson concept.

A. Use many fonts, colors, and decorative elements so every page looks visually exciting.
B. Build a consistent hierarchy, protect readability, verify accessibility and links, and let design guide attention to the writing.

3. Apply the Lesson

Create a publication specification for your portfolio covering page hierarchy, typography, spacing, navigation, image treatment, alt text, file naming, attribution, privacy, and device/print checks. Apply it to a three-page sample.

4. Revise with Purpose

Test the sample at two screen widths and in print preview. Record one problem from each test and revise the design to solve both.

Before You Submit

  • All four numbered parts are complete.
  • Your work applies the lesson skill to specific portfolio evidence.
  • Your explanation identifies a decision and its effect on the audience.
  • Your revision meaningfully improves quality, coherence, or readiness.
