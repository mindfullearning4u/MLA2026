Lesson 5: Publication Formatting and Digital Preparation

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.PUB.02 — Format-specific quality work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.01 — Online collaborative publication; MLA.CW.REV.02 — Clarity, cohesion, and conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Translate genre and audience needs into defensible design choices.
  • Build readable hierarchy, consistent formatting, and accessible digital structure.
  • Separate editorial correction from unauthorized meaning changes.
  • Run a complete preflight check before publication.
