Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
typographyThe selection and arrangement of type to support readability and visual meaning.
visual hierarchyDesign cues that show the order and relative importance of information.
white spaceUnoccupied space that separates and organizes content.
accessibilityDesign that enables people with differing needs to perceive and use a work.
alternative textA concise written description communicating the purpose or meaning of an image.
descriptive linkLink text that identifies its destination or purpose without relying on “click here.”
metadataInformation such as title, author, date, genre, and file description.
version controlA method for labeling and preserving successive drafts or files accurately.
preflightA final systematic check of content, files, links, format, rights, and accessibility before release.
