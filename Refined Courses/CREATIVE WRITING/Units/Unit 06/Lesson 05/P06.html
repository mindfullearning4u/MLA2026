P06 Independent Work

Prepare one creative writing piece for digital publication. Use your own portfolio piece or the piece your TOR has assigned.

Instructions

Work in order. Do not begin with decoration. Begin with genre, reader access, and readiness. Your final work should look finished because it is clear, consistent, and easy to read.

Part A: Format Plan

Identify the piece, genre, audience, and publication format. Then write two formatting choices the reader will need. For example, a poem may need intentional line breaks and stanza spacing. A story may need paragraph breaks and dialogue formatting.

Part B: Digital Readiness Check

Check title, author label, spacing, paragraphing, line breaks, script labels if needed, conventions, and file name. Fix issues that would distract the reader or make the piece look unfinished.

Part C: Publication Note

Write a short publication note of four to six sentences. Explain what genre you prepared, what formatting choices you made, what convention or readability issue you corrected, and how the final version supports the reader.
