Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
author statementA concise explanation that frames a creative work, its purposes, craft choices, and development.
voiceThe distinctive presence or personality conveyed through language and perspective.
toneThe attitude a writer communicates toward the subject and audience.
registerA level and style of language suited to a context, such as conversational, academic, or professional.
credibilityThe quality of being trustworthy, knowledgeable, and honest.
specificityThe use of exact, concrete details rather than broad generalities.
rhetorical situationThe relationship among purpose, audience, context, message, and form.
qualificationA limit or condition added to make a claim more accurate.
curatorial noteA short text that helps an audience understand why a work was selected or arranged.
