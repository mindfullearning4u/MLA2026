Checkpoint: Repair an Author Statement

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Analyze the new statement and answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

Draft Statement

“My hybrid poem is super different and probably confusing, but that is the point. I put weather reports beside messages from my uncle because both are important. Everyone will realize that memory is unreliable. I changed a bunch of lines after workshop, and the final version is obviously much more powerful. Readers should understand exactly what I mean.”

A hybrid poem combines features from more than one form or medium. Juxtaposition means placing elements beside one another so their relationship or contrast produces meaning.

Diagnosis

  1. Identify two phrases with an overly casual register.
  2. Identify two inflated or universal claims.
  3. Explain why “both are important” fails as craft reasoning.
  4. Infer a plausible creative purpose from the juxtaposition.
  5. List the evidence missing from the revision claim.
  6. Describe the tone the author should aim for and why.

Revision and Adaptation

  1. Rewrite the opening in two credible sentences.
  2. Write one craft paragraph explaining the weather-report/message juxtaposition.
  3. Rewrite the reader-effect claim with appropriate qualification.
  4. Add a specific but plausible revision explanation.
  5. Write a final reader invitation that preserves interpretive freedom.
  6. Condense your repaired statement into a 75–100-word oral introduction.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review feedback, correct the identified weaknesses, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
