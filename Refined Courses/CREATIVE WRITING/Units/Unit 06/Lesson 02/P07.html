P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can write an author statement with effective voice and tone.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if the statement sounds like a plot summary, if the tone sounds defensive, or if the explanation does not include craft reasoning.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one creative piece or excerpt.
  2. Write an author statement using the four-part order.
  3. Revise for voice and tone.
  4. Check that the statement fits a portfolio or publication context.
  5. Submit the author statement in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a four-sentence author statement that explains purpose, craft choices, revision or growth, and reader effect. The statement should sound reflective, specific, and publication-ready.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses an author voice that sounds authentic and clear.
  • Creates a tone that is reflective and appropriate for publication.
  • Organizes the statement around purpose, craft, revision or growth, and reader effect.
  • Avoids defensive, vague, or exaggerated language.
  • Explains craft reasoning with specific evidence.
