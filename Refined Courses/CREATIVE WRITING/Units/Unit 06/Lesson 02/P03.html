P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

This page teaches how to organize an author statement so the tone stays focused.

Step 4: Use a Clear Statement Order

A strong author statement often follows this order: purpose, craft choices, revision or growth, final reader effect. This order helps the reader understand what the piece tries to do and how the writer shaped it.

Step 5: Remove Defensive or Empty Language

Defensive language argues with the reader. Empty language sounds positive but says little. Replace both with specific craft reasoning.

Defensive: People might not get it, but that is not my problem.

Specific: I used an open ending so readers could feel the speaker's uncertainty rather than receive a simple answer.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: This piece is really deep, and if people do not understand it, that is on them.

This is incorrect because the tone is defensive and the statement does not explain craft choices or reader effect.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: I used an open ending so the reader could experience the speaker's uncertainty. The final image is meant to leave the decision unresolved.

This is correct because the tone is reflective and the explanation focuses on craft.
