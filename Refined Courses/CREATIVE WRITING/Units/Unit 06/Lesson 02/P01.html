P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Author Statement Voice and Tone

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.03 - Communication of craft reasoning.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.05 - Organization and development of written explanation; MLA.CW.PUB.02 - Format-specific quality work.

What You Will Learn

  • What an author statement is and how it supports a portfolio.
  • How voice and tone change the way readers understand your reflection.
  • How to sound reflective, specific, and professional without sounding fake.
  • How to organize an author statement for publication or presentation.

What You Will Do

  • Compare weak and strong author statement tones.
  • Practice revising vague or overly casual statements.
  • Organize a statement around purpose, craft choices, and growth.
  • Write an author statement that fits a portfolio or publication context.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can revise an author statement so the voice sounds authentic, the tone fits the publication context, and the explanation clearly connects craft choices to reader effect.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you are learning to talk about your writing like an author. Your statement should sound like you, but it should also be clear, respectful, organized, and focused on craft.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if your author statement sounds too casual, too vague, too defensive, or disconnected from the creative work.
