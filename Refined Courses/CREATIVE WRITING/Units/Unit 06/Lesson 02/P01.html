Lesson Overview: Author Statement Voice and Tone

Lesson Focus

Lesson 2: Author Statement Voice and Tone

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.03 — Communication of craft reasoning.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.05 — Organization and development of written explanation; MLA.CW.PUB.02 — Format-specific quality work.

What You Will Learn

  • Shape a credible authorial voice for a real audience.
  • Control tone without becoming vague, inflated, or overly casual.
  • Explain purpose, craft, revision, and growth with exact evidence.
  • Adapt one core statement for portfolio, publication, and oral contexts.

Statement Architecture

Purpose
→
Craft evidence
→
Revision insight
→
Reader invitation

Mastery Target

I can write a specific, reflective, professional author statement whose voice and tone fit its audience and whose claims are supported by craft evidence. A score of at least 80% is required.
