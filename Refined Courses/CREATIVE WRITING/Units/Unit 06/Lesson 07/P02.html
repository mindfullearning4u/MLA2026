Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.
TermMeaningExample
coherenceThe quality of all parts connecting logically and purposefully.The artifact order and notes create one clear portfolio argument.
alignmentA clear match among goals, evidence, explanations, design, and criteria.The stated goal is supported by the selected drafts and reflection.
cross-referenceA direction connecting one component to related evidence elsewhere.The note directs readers to the marked revision on page six.
artifact mapA visual record showing what each portfolio item demonstrates and where evidence appears.The map links every criterion to a named artifact.
gap analysisA systematic search for missing evidence, skills, or requirements.The review reveals no artifact demonstrates collaboration.
quality assurancePlanned checking that confirms a product meets standards and functions as intended.The writer tests links and verifies labels before release.
provenanceDocumented information about the origin, ownership, and history of a work or media element.The image record names its creator, source, and license.
release candidateA near-final version considered ready pending a last verification pass.Version 8 becomes the release candidate after revisions.
readiness defenseAn evidence-based argument that a portfolio satisfies required criteria.The defense cites the map, revision trail, and accessibility check.

Make the Vocabulary Work

Which finding belongs in a gap analysis?

A. “The title page uses the correct font.”

This is a quality-assurance check, but it does not identify missing evidence.

B. “The portfolio claims growth in dialogue, but no paired drafts show a dialogue revision.”

Correct. It identifies a specific mismatch between a claim and the evidence needed to support it.
