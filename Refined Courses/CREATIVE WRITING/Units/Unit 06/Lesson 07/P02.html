Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
coherenceThe quality of all parts connecting logically and purposefully.
alignmentA clear match among goals, evidence, explanations, design, and assessment criteria.
cross-referenceA direction connecting one portfolio component to related evidence elsewhere.
artifact mapA visual record showing what each portfolio item demonstrates and where evidence appears.
gap analysisA systematic search for missing evidence, skills, or requirements.
quality assurancePlanned checking that confirms a product meets stated standards and functions as intended.
provenanceDocumented information about the origin, ownership, and history of a work or media element.
release candidateA near-final version considered ready pending a last verification pass.
readiness defenseAn evidence-based argument that a portfolio satisfies required criteria.
