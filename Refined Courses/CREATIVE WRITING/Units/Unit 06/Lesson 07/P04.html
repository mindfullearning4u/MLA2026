P04 Worked Example

These examples show how to prepare a complete author portfolio for publication and presentation.

Example 1: Checking Portfolio Completeness

Step 1: The student lists required parts: revised story, revised poem, author statement, publication formatting, and presentation plan.

Step 2: The student marks the story and poem present, the author statement present, and the presentation plan needs revision.

Step 3: The student writes the exact fix: "Add a listening focus and craft explanation for the story excerpt."

Result: The checklist gives a clear action instead of a vague reminder.

Example 2: Choosing Portfolio Order

Step 1: The student notices the portfolio includes a story, a poem, and an author statement about voice.

Step 2: The student places the story first because it shows dialogue voice, then the poem because it shows lyrical voice, then the statement to explain the growth across both pieces.

Step 3: The student writes: "This order shows how I developed voice in more than one genre."

Result: The order has a reader purpose.

Example 3: Linking Presentation to Portfolio

Step 1: The student selects a short excerpt from the revised story.

Step 2: The student gives context: "This scene happens when the narrator decides not to answer directly."

Step 3: The student names the listening focus: "Listen for how the dialogue avoids direct answers."

Step 4: The student explains: "I revised the dialogue so the reader can infer tension instead of being told what the character feels."

Result: The presentation points to craft, revision, and reader effect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "I put the files in this order because that is how I found them on my computer."

Why this is incorrect: File order should guide the reader through the portfolio. Random order does not show purpose or author control.

Correct Version

Correct: "I placed the story first because it shows my strongest revision of dialogue, then the poem because it extends the same voice work through image and rhythm."

Why this is correct: The writer explains how order supports reader understanding and portfolio purpose.
