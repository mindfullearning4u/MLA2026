P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Integrated Author Portfolio Preparation

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.01 - Online collaborative publication; MLA.CW.COM.01 - Author presentation of creative work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.REV.01 - Revision from feedback; MLA.CW.PUB.02 - Format-specific quality work; MLA.CW.WR.08 - Reflection on creative process and product.

What You Will Learn

  • How to assemble a final author portfolio for publication and presentation.
  • How to check that selected pieces, author statement, revision evidence, and presentation notes work together.
  • How to explain portfolio choices using craft reasoning and reader effect.
  • How to complete a final readiness pass before the Unit 6 synthesis assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the required parts of a final author portfolio.
  • Check each portfolio piece for revision, formatting, and publication readiness.
  • Connect your author statement and presentation plan to the pieces you selected.
  • Prepare a final checklist that shows your portfolio is ready for readers.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can prepare a complete author portfolio that includes publication-ready creative work, clear organization, evidence of revision, a reflective author statement, and a presentation plan that explains craft choices and audience effect.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson brings the unit together. You are preparing your work for real readers by organizing the portfolio, showing revision, formatting pieces clearly, and planning how to speak about your choices as the author.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you are unsure whether a piece is ready for publication, if your author statement does not connect to the portfolio, or if your presentation plan summarizes instead of explaining craft.
