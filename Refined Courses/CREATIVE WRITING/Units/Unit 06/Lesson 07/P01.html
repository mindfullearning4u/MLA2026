Lesson Focus

Lesson 7: Integrated Author Portfolio Preparation

Primary Standards: MLA.CW.PUB.01 — Online collaborative publication; MLA.CW.COM.01 — Author presentation of creative work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.REV.01 — Revision from feedback; MLA.CW.PUB.02 — Format-specific quality work; MLA.CW.WR.08 — Reflection on creative process and product.

What You Will Learn

  • Assemble a portfolio whose artifacts, statement, revision evidence, publication design, and presentation form one coherent argument.
  • Audit alignment across every portfolio component.
  • Resolve contradictions, gaps, and accessibility risks before submission.
  • Defend final readiness with evidence rather than confidence alone.

Mastery Target

I can assemble and defend a coherent, accessible, publication-ready author portfolio in which every component supports the same evidence-based account of craft and growth. A score of at least 80% is required.
