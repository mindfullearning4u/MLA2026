📘 CW | Unit 06 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Prepare your integrated author portfolio for publication and presentation.

Instructions

Work carefully and in order. This is the final preparation lesson before Unit 6 synthesis. Your goal is to make the portfolio coherent, reader-ready, and connected to your author presentation.

Part A: Portfolio Completeness Check

List every required portfolio part. Mark each one present, missing, or needs revision. For anything that needs revision, write the exact next action.

Part B: Portfolio Connection Check

Write one sentence explaining the order of the portfolio. Then write one sentence explaining how the author statement connects to the pieces. Finally, write one sentence explaining how the presentation plan connects to one selected excerpt.

Part C: Final Readiness Reflection

Write a six- to eight-sentence reflection explaining what is ready, what you revised, how the formatting supports readers, and how your presentation will explain author craft.
