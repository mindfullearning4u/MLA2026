Checkpoint: Portfolio Quality-Assurance Case

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Analyze the new case and answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

Case File

Samira’s portfolio contains a story, poem, lyric essay, author statement, revision trail, and presentation plan. The statement calls the story “The Blue Stair,” but the file is titled “Blue Stairs Final.” The revision trail compares an early poem with the final essay. The presentation excerpt is from a draft no longer included. The website has clear headings and contrast, but the poem wraps incorrectly on mobile, an interview recording has no consent record or transcript, and two links open private editing pages. Samira’s checklist marks every item complete.

A consent record documents permission to use a person’s words, image, voice, or identifying information.

Diagnosis

  1. Identify three alignment failures.
  2. Identify four publication, access, rights, or privacy risks.
  3. Explain why Samira’s completed checklist is not sufficient evidence.
  4. Rank the three highest-priority problems and justify the order.
  5. Explain how the mismatched revision trail weakens a growth claim.
  6. Describe how to verify the correct story version.

Repair and Judgment

  1. Create corrected cross-references among the story, statement, trail, and presentation.
  2. Propose a mobile-safe solution that preserves poetic lineation.
  3. State the steps required before publishing the interview recording.
  4. Replace the private links with a secure reader-access plan.
  5. Write a six-row quality-assurance dashboard.
  6. Give a final readiness judgment supported by at least six criteria.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review feedback, correct the identified weaknesses, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
