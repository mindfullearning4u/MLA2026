P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that your author portfolio is integrated and ready for final synthesis.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if a required portfolio part is missing, if your order does not have a reader purpose, or if the author statement and presentation plan do not connect to the selected pieces.

Submission Workflow

  1. Complete the portfolio completeness check.
  2. Correct any missing or unfinished required parts.
  3. Check publication formatting and file readiness.
  4. Connect the author statement and presentation plan to selected pieces.
  5. Submit the readiness reflection in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit an integrated portfolio readiness reflection that explains the required parts, the portfolio order, one major revision, publication formatting readiness, and how the presentation plan connects to one portfolio excerpt.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies all required portfolio parts.
  • Explains a purposeful portfolio order.
  • Shows evidence of revision and publication readiness.
  • Connects the author statement to selected pieces.
  • Connects the presentation plan to a specific excerpt and craft effect.
