Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
line breakThe point where one poetic line ends and another begins, creating pause, emphasis, or double meaning.
enjambmentA sentence or phrase continuing beyond a line break without terminal punctuation.
end-stopped lineA poetic line ending with a grammatical pause or punctuation.
caesuraA strong pause within a poetic line.
prosodyThe patterned rhythm, stress, pace, and sound of language.
consonanceRepetition of consonant sounds, especially within or at the ends of nearby words.
image systemA connected pattern of sensory details that develops meaning across a work.
figurative languageLanguage that creates meaning through comparison, association, or nonliteral expression.
syntactic variationPurposeful changes in sentence length, structure, or order.
