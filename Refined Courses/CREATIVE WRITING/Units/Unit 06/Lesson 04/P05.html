Independent Work: Poetry or Style Revision Laboratory

Directions

Select a poem of at least 20 lines or a style-focused prose passage of at least 500 words. Submit the revised work, a 700–900-word craft memo, comparison evidence, and Page 2 vocabulary notes.

Part A — Diagnose

  1. State the work’s central pressure and intended reader effect.
  2. Map its image system and identify one decorative or underdeveloped image.
  3. Mark line breaks or sentence patterns that create emphasis, speed, or pause.
  4. Read aloud and annotate three sound or rhythm patterns.
  5. If the work uses an unfamiliar or specialized term, define it in context or explain the clues that establish its meaning.

Part B — Run Controlled Experiments

  1. Create two versions of at least eight lines or 150 words.
  2. Change lineation or syntax in Version A.
  3. Change image or sound patterning in Version B.
  4. Annotate the intended effect of each change.
  5. Read both aloud and record what changed in breath, emphasis, ambiguity, and pace.

Part C — Revise and Defend

  1. Choose or combine the strongest effects into a final version.
  2. Quote three before-and-after pairs.
  3. Explain how each revision supports purpose.
  4. Identify one feedback suggestion you rejected or adapted.
  5. Acknowledge one tradeoff and one remaining limitation.

Required Visual

Create a four-column comparison chart: Feature | Draft effect | Revision effect | Decision.
