Independent Work

Complete all three parts using one poem or style-focused excerpt from your portfolio work.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

If you do not have a current poem, use this model line: I almost knocked but my hand stayed still.

Part A: Diagnose

Name one style or poetry problem: weak image, random line breaks, distracting repetition, unclear figurative language, flat rhythm, or vague word choice.

Part B: Revise

Make or describe one targeted revision. The revision must change image, line, sound, figurative language, rhythm, voice, or tone.

Part C: Explain

Explain how the revision changes reader effect and why the final version is stronger for a portfolio.

Independent Work Success Checklist

For the independent work in Poetry and Style Portfolio Revision, produce evidence that another reader can follow. Start by restating the task in your own words. Then plan the response, draft the required writing or analysis, and revise for the exact standard focus: MLA.CW.REV.01, MLA.CW.POE.01, MLA.CW.POE.03, MLA.CW.WR.07. This course is self-paced, so do not wait for a classroom explanation before you work. Read the prompt, model, scenario, or passage on the page first. Then name the exact craft skill from MLA.CW.REV.01, MLA.CW.POE.01, MLA.CW.POE.03, MLA.CW.WR.07, explain what the writer is doing, and connect that choice to reader effect, meaning, voice, mood, structure, or revision purpose. If the task asks you to write, draft in complete sentences and make the craft move visible rather than hidden. If the task asks you to analyze, quote or describe the specific words that prove your answer. Before submitting, check that your response includes the required passage, model, draft excerpt, or scenario evidence from this page and that every sentence helps prove mastery.
