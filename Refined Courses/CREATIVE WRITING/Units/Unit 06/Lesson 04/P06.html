Checkpoint: Revise a New Poem

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Analyze the new draft and answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

Draft Poem

I am sad in the empty station.
The lights are bright and very cold.
I remember my father leaving.
The train goes away quickly.
I feel alone and cannot speak.
The night is dark around me.

Station: a place where trains stop for passengers. Prosody: the rhythm, stress, pace, and sound pattern of language.

Diagnosis

  1. Identify three abstract or generic phrases.
  2. State a more precise central pressure for the poem.
  3. Propose an image system that could carry memory and departure.
  4. Explain how the current line endings affect emphasis.
  5. Identify one opportunity for sound patterning.
  6. Explain why adding random rhyme would not automatically improve the poem.

Revision

  1. Rewrite lines 1–3 using concrete imagery without naming sadness.
  2. Create two different lineations for the train’s departure.
  3. Explain the pacing and ambiguity of each version.
  4. Revise one sentence or line using purposeful syntactic variation.
  5. Write a complete 10–14-line revision.
  6. Write a 120–160-word defense citing image, line, sound, and reader effect.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review feedback, correct the identified weaknesses, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
