Lesson Overview: Poetry and Style Portfolio Revision

Lesson Focus

Lesson 4: Poetry and Style Portfolio Revision

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.REV.01 — Revision from feedback.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.POE.01 — Poetic form, line, and image; MLA.CW.POE.03 — Sound, figurative language, and meaning; MLA.CW.WR.07 — Style, voice, and sentence rhythm.

What You Will Learn

  • Diagnose poetic and stylistic problems without flattening voice.
  • Revise image, line, sound, figurative language, syntax, and rhythm as meaning-making choices.
  • Test competing versions aloud and on the page.
  • Defend revisions using precise textual evidence and reader effect.

Revision Lenses

Image
What is perceived?
Line
Where does attention turn?
Sound
What does the mouth/ear feel?
Syntax
How does thought move?

Mastery Target

I can revise a poem or style-focused passage so form, sound, imagery, and syntax work together, then defend the revision with evidence. A score of at least 80% is required.
