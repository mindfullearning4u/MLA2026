P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can revise poetry or style work for portfolio readiness.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your revision feels random, if your line breaks do not support meaning, or if your explanation does not name a reader effect.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one poem or style-focused excerpt.
  2. Diagnose one poetry or style problem.
  3. Make or describe one targeted revision.
  4. Explain the reader effect.
  5. Submit the revision explanation in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a poetry or style portfolio revision note that identifies the problem, describes the revision, and explains how the revision improves image, line, sound, figurative language, rhythm, voice, or tone.

Mastery Criteria

  • Correctly diagnoses a poetry or style problem.
  • Chooses a revision that directly supports the intended effect.
  • Uses precise language rather than random changes.
  • Explains how the revision affects the reader.
  • Connects the revision to portfolio value.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
