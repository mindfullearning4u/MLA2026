Lesson Overview: Author Presentation Planning

Lesson Focus

Lesson 6: Author Presentation Planning

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.01 — Author presentation of creative work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03 — Communication of craft reasoning; MLA.CW.WR.08 — Reflection on creative process and product.

What You Will Learn

  • Design a coherent presentation for a specific audience and time limit.
  • Select a representative excerpt through defensible criteria.
  • Integrate reading, craft analysis, revision evidence, and reflection.
  • Plan delivery and visual support without distracting from the creative work.

Mastery Target

I can plan a timed author presentation that centers the creative work, explains significant craft and revision decisions, and uses delivery choices to support audience understanding. A score of at least 80% is required.
