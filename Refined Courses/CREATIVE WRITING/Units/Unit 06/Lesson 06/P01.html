P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Author Presentation Planning

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.COM.01 - Author presentation of creative work.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03 - Communication of craft reasoning; MLA.CW.WR.08 - Reflection on creative process and product.

What You Will Learn

  • How to plan an author presentation that introduces a creative work clearly.
  • How to choose an excerpt that represents your purpose and craft choices.
  • How to explain craft decisions instead of only summarizing the plot.
  • How to connect revision, growth, and audience impact in a short presentation.

What You Will Do

  • Identify your presentation purpose and audience.
  • Select a short excerpt or portfolio piece to present.
  • Plan the order of your presentation notes.
  • Prepare craft reasoning that explains how your choices affect the reader or listener.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery when you can plan an author presentation that introduces the work, provides necessary context, explains selected craft choices, reflects on revision or growth, and prepares the audience to understand the piece.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you can speak as the author of your work. You are not only reading a piece aloud. You are helping the audience understand what you made, why you made it, how you revised it, and what craft choices shape the reader's experience.

Ask Your TOR (Teacher of Record) for Help

Ask your TOR if you are unsure which excerpt to present, if your notes summarize too much, or if your craft explanation does not yet connect to audience effect.
