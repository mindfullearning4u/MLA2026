P04 Worked Example

These examples show how an author presentation moves from context to excerpt to craft reasoning.

Example 1: Opening the Presentation

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Step 1: Name the title and genre: "My piece is a short story called The Last Bus."

Step 2: Give brief context: "The scene I will read happens after the narrator misses the last ride home."

Step 3: Name the listening focus: "Listen for the short sentences because they show panic and urgency."

Why it works: The audience knows what the piece is, what situation they are entering, and what craft choice to notice.

Example 2: Explaining Craft Reasoning

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Step 1: Name the craft choice: "I used repeated sound in the poem's final stanza."

Step 2: Point to the place: "The repeated s sound appears in the words still, street, and silver."

Step 3: Explain the effect: "That sound makes the ending feel quiet and slow, which matches the speaker's realization."

Why it works: The speaker explains how a specific writing choice shapes the audience's experience.

Example 3: Reflecting on Revision

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Step 1: Name the earlier problem: "My first draft explained the character's fear too directly."

Step 2: Name the revision: "I replaced the explanation with action and dialogue."

Step 3: Explain the growth: "Now the reader can infer the fear, which makes the scene more active and less repetitive."

Why it works: The presentation shows process, product, and reader effect instead of only saying the writer improved.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "I picked this excerpt because it is my favorite part."

Why this is incorrect: Personal preference alone does not prepare the audience. The presenter needs to explain why the excerpt represents craft, purpose, growth, or reader effect.

Correct Version

Correct: "I chose this excerpt because it shows the clearest example of my revised dialogue and the tension I wanted the reader to infer."

Teachable Explanation: The presenter connects excerpt selection to craft and audience understanding.
