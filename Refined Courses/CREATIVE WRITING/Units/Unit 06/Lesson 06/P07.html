P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can plan an author presentation of creative work.

TOR Support

Ask your TOR for help if your excerpt is too long, your context takes over the presentation, or your craft explanation sounds like plot summary.

Submission Workflow

  1. Select one portfolio piece or excerpt.
  2. Write the title, genre, audience, and purpose.
  3. Prepare brief context and a listening focus.
  4. Write two craft reasoning notes and one revision reflection.
  5. Submit the presentation plan in the LMS.

Checkpoint Task

Submit an author presentation plan that includes title and genre, brief audience context, selected excerpt, listening focus, two craft reasoning notes, revision or growth reflection, and a short closing statement.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies title, genre, audience, and presentation purpose.
  • Selects an excerpt that can be understood with brief context.
  • Provides a clear listening focus before the excerpt.
  • Explains craft choices with reader or listener effect.
  • Connects revision or growth to the final creative work.
