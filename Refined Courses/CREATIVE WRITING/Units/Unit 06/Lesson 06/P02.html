Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
presentation arcThe purposeful sequence that guides an audience through a presentation.
representative excerptA selected passage that accurately demonstrates important features of a larger work.
signpostingBrief language that shows where a presentation is going or how ideas connect.
annotationA note marking evidence, delivery, timing, or interpretation.
cadenceThe rhythmic rise, fall, stress, and timing of spoken language.
vocal emphasisPurposeful stress placed on a word or phrase during speech.
rehearsal protocolA planned method for practicing, timing, observing, and revising delivery.
visual supportA chart, slide, image, or text display that clarifies rather than replaces spoken reasoning.
audience calibrationAdjustment of context, language, and pacing to what listeners need.
