Checkpoint: Repair a Presentation Plan

Directions

Complete this distinct checkpoint independently. Analyze the new plan and answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. A score of at least 80% is required.

Draft Plan

Leah plans a five-minute presentation of a 2,400-word fantasy story. She spends two minutes summarizing the kingdom’s history, reads a 40-second battle excerpt, and says, “I used imagery because fantasy needs description.” Her only revision example is a corrected spelling error. Three slides contain full paragraphs in small gold text over a castle photograph. She has not timed the final minute, practiced the character names aloud, or prepared a conclusion.

Worldbuilding is the construction of a fictional setting’s places, rules, histories, cultures, and systems.

Diagnosis

  1. Identify the main imbalance in Leah’s time allocation.
  2. Explain why the excerpt may not represent her stated focus.
  3. Diagnose the weakness in her imagery claim.
  4. Explain why the spelling correction is insufficient revision evidence.
  5. Identify three visual-accessibility problems.
  6. Name two delivery risks created by the missing rehearsal.

Redesign

  1. Write a one-sentence presentation purpose.
  2. Create a five-part time budget totaling exactly five minutes.
  3. Describe a better excerpt and justify it using three criteria.
  4. Write one evidence-based craft explanation Leah could use.
  5. Redesign one slide and include accessibility features.
  6. Write a concluding reader invitation and a three-step rehearsal protocol.

80% Mastery Requirement

If you score below 80%, review feedback, correct the identified weaknesses, and resubmit according to your TOR’s directions.
