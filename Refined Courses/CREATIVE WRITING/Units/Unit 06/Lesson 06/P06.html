P06 Independent Work

Plan an author presentation for one portfolio piece or selected excerpt.

Instructions

Your presentation plan should help an audience understand your creative work without needing a teacher to explain it for you. Keep the plan focused. The goal is not to tell everything about the piece. The goal is to guide the audience toward the most important craft choices.

Part A: Presentation Frame

Write the title, genre, audience, presentation purpose, and one sentence of context. Then write what the audience should listen for during the excerpt.

Part B: Excerpt and Craft Notes

Select the exact excerpt you would present. Then write two craft notes. Each note must name the choice, point to where it appears, and explain the reader or listener effect.

Part C: Revision and Practice Plan

Write one revision or growth reflection. Then create a speaking-note sequence: title and genre, context, listening focus, excerpt, craft explanation, revision reflection, closing statement.
