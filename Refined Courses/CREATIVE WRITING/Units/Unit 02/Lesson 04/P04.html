Worked Example: Three Layered Craft Decisions

Example 1 — Symbol Through Change

First appearance: “Nora taped the cracked kitchen clock at 6:14 so her mother would not notice it had stopped.”

Later: After admitting she missed the appointment, Nora removes the tape and sets the clock on the table.

  1. Literal role: a broken clock hides missed time.
  2. Pattern: hiding becomes displaying.
  3. Changed context: confession alters the object’s function.
  4. Interpretation: the clock gathers meaning about avoidance and accountability.
  5. Limit: evidence supports responsibility, not unrelated ideas such as mortality.
  6. Style choice: precise “6:14” makes the concealment concrete.
  7. Revision principle: let action change the symbol; do not explain it.

Example 2 — Dialogue With Subtext

Scene: “‘You can keep the blue room,’ Eli said. He folded the lease along the same crease three times. ‘It gets better light.’”

  1. Literal statement: Eli offers a room.
  2. Behavioral evidence: repetitive folding signals controlled tension.
  3. Situation: the lease means the living arrangement is ending or changing.
  4. Mismatch: practical generosity conflicts with emotional strain.
  5. Plausible readings: guilt or an effort to avoid conflict.
  6. Boundary: the passage does not prove why Eli feels guilty.
  7. Stronger continuation: have the other character respond to the crease, not announce Eli’s hidden feeling.

Example 3 — Syntax Creates a Second Layer

Flat draft: “The council approved the demolition after hearing all objections.”

Revision: “They heard the custodian, the children, the woman whose wedding photograph still hung in the lobby; they thanked each speaker by name; they approved demolition before the microphone cooled.”

  1. Literal event: testimony occurs, then approval.
  2. Long accumulation: individual witnesses receive time and specificity.
  3. Semicolon turn: procedure pivots abruptly into decision.
  4. Understatement: no character is labeled uncaring.
  5. Juxtaposition: ceremonial attention contrasts with a predetermined outcome.
  6. Layered meaning: respectful procedure can coexist with institutional refusal.
  7. Tradeoff: compression sharpens critique but withholds the council’s reasoning.
