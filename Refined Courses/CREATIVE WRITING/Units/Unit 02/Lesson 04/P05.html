Instructions

You will revise a scene from Lesson 3 or create a new scene. Your goal is to add a layered detail using repetition, contrast, or placement.

Part A

Choose one layering strategy: repetition, contrast, or placement. Write the strategy at the top of your notebook page.

Part B

Write or revise a 6-8 sentence scene that includes one layered detail. The scene should still make literal sense even if the reader does not notice the deeper meaning immediately.

Part C

Write 4-5 sentences explaining the literal meaning, implied meaning, and evidence. Name whether your evidence uses repetition, contrast, or placement.
