Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and complete definitions. Submit these notes with Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
literal meaningThe direct, surface meaning of words, actions, or events.
implicationA meaning suggested by evidence rather than directly stated.
subtextThe unstated tension, motive, or meaning beneath dialogue and action.
symbolA concrete element that retains its literal role while gaining additional meaning through pattern and context.
resonanceThe extended emotional or intellectual significance a detail gathers across a work.
ambiguityA deliberate openness that permits more than one evidence-based interpretation.
motifA recurring image, object, phrase, or situation that develops significance through repetition.
juxtapositionPlacing contrasting elements near each other to sharpen difference or reveal a relationship.
dictionA writer’s purposeful word choices and their levels of formality, precision, and connotation.
syntaxThe arrangement and rhythm of words, phrases, and clauses in sentences.
connotationThe emotional or cultural associations a word carries beyond its dictionary meaning.
understatementDeliberately presenting something as less intense or important to create emphasis, irony, or restraint.
