Lesson Overview: Clearly Established Point of View

Lesson Focus

Lesson 2: Clearly Established Point of View

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.NAR.01 — use narrative techniques and a controlled point of view.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.FIC.03 — analyze narrator perspective, irony, and reliability; MLA.CW.COM.03 — control voice and tone.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish grammatical person from focalization and narrative distance.
  • Control what a narrator can observe, know, infer, remember, and misunderstand.
  • Use selective access, free indirect style, and reliability to shape reader judgment.
  • Diagnose and revise accidental point-of-view violations.

Why This Matters

Creative writing helps you communicate ideas, experiences, and perspectives with purpose. Each lesson strengthens your ability to read like a writer, make deliberate craft choices, revise thoughtfully, and explain how those choices affect a reader. These skills build creative confidence, clear communication, and responsible participation in a writing community.

What You Will Do

  • Learn and apply the lesson vocabulary.
  • Follow step-by-step instruction and study worked examples.
  • Practice through interactive checks and guided application.
  • Complete Independent Work to apply the lesson skills.
  • Complete a separate Checkpoint to demonstrate your understanding.
