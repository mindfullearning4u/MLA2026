Lesson Overview: Clearly Established Point of View

Lesson Focus

Lesson 2: Clearly Established Point of View

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.NAR.01 — use narrative techniques and a controlled point of view.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.FIC.03 — analyze narrator perspective, irony, and reliability; MLA.CW.COM.03 — control voice and tone.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish grammatical person from focalization and narrative distance.
  • Control what a narrator can observe, know, infer, remember, and misunderstand.
  • Use selective access, free indirect style, and reliability to shape reader judgment.
  • Diagnose and revise accidental point-of-view violations.

Why This Matters

Point of view is the story’s information system. It determines not only who speaks, but which details exist for the reader, how events are interpreted, whose uncertainty matters, and where dramatic irony can develop.

What You Will Do

  • Record and apply twelve point-of-view terms.
  • Learn a six-part POV control method.
  • Study three complete worked examples with revision comparisons.
  • Analyze a substantial narrative and rewrite it through another focalizer.
  • Complete a distinct checkpoint and original scene.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Submit Page 2 vocabulary and all Page 5 work in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback and resubmit work below 80%.
  5. Complete the five-question randomized quiz with at least 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Map a narrator’s knowledge and perceptual limits.
  • Explain how distance and reliability affect meaning.
  • Repair head-hopping and unsupported knowledge.
  • Use deliberate POV constraints in original writing.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can choose and control a point of view so every detail, thought, and inference belongs within the narrator’s established access.
