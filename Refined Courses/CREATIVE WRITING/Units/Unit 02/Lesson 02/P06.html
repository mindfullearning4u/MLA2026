P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your writer's notebook. Keep your point of view consistent unless the directions tell you to compare versions.

Instructions

You will write the same short moment from two points of view. This helps you see how narrator choice changes closeness, knowledge, and voice.

Part A

Write a 4-5 sentence first person scene about a character finding something hidden in a desk, backpack, closet, or locker. Use I, me, or my consistently.

Part B

Rewrite the same moment in third person limited. Use the character's name, he, she, or they. Stay close to only that one character's thoughts.

Part C

Write 3-4 sentences explaining which version feels closer to the character and why. Use point-of-view vocabulary in your explanation.
