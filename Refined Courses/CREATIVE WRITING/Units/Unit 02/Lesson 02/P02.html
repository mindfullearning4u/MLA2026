Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
point of viewThe position and information system through which a narrative is presented.
narratorThe constructed voice or consciousness that tells or presents the story.
first personA participant narrator using I or we, limited to that narrator’s experience, memory, and inference.
third person limitedNarration using third-person pronouns while restricting interior access primarily to one focal character.
omniscient narratorA narrator capable of moving beyond one character’s knowledge, often with a distinct organizing voice.
focalizationThe consciousness, perception, or position through which narrative information is filtered.
psychic distanceHow near or far narration feels from a character’s immediate thoughts and sensations.
interior accessThe degree to which narration reveals a character’s private thought, memory, or feeling.
free indirect discourseThird-person narration that temporarily adopts a character’s idiom or thought pattern without quotation tags.
narrator reliabilityThe degree to which a narrator’s account appears accurate, complete, and trustworthy.
head-hoppingAn uncontrolled shift between characters’ private thoughts within a scene.
dramatic ironyA condition in which the reader understands something important that a character does not.
