P07 Checkpoint

TOR Reminder

Ask your TOR for help before submitting if your narrator accidentally reports another character's private thoughts or if your draft switches from I to she or they without a clear reason.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your P02 and P03 notebook notes.
  2. Choose one point of view for your checkpoint scene.
  3. Check that your narrator's knowledge stays realistic for that point of view.
  4. Submit your checkpoint according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one original narrative scene of 6-8 sentences using either first person or third person limited. Your scene must keep one clear point of view from beginning to end. Under the scene, write a short explanation identifying the point of view and explaining what the narrator can and cannot know.

Mastery Criteria

  • The scene is 6-8 sentences and uses one clear point of view.
  • The narrator's knowledge is consistent and realistic.
  • The scene uses narrative techniques from Lesson 1.
  • The explanation correctly identifies the point of view.
  • The explanation explains what the narrator can and cannot know.
