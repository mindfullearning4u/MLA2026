Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
paceThe perceived speed and allocation of narrative attention.A chase uses short sentences and rapid actions to create a fast pace.
narrative timeThe span of story events represented by a passage.Three years pass between two chapters, compressing narrative time.
sceneA dramatized unit in which events unfold with immediate action, detail, or dialogue.In a scene, readers experience the argument through action and dialogue.
summaryCompressed narration that covers events more quickly than they occur.A single paragraph summarizes the character’s month-long journey.
pauseA moment when narration slows or stops event time for description, thought, or reflection.The story pauses while the narrator studies a faded photograph.
ellipsisThe omission of a span of story time that readers infer has passed.The phrase “By the following winter” creates an ellipsis that skips several months.
tensionReader concern created by competing outcomes, uncertainty, and stakes.The ticking clock increases tension because the character may miss the deadline.
suspenseForward-looking uncertainty about what will happen or be revealed.A locked door and unexplained footsteps create suspense about what lies beyond it.
stakesWhat may be gained, lost, damaged, or changed by an outcome.If the character fails, her family may lose its home; those consequences are the stakes.
moodThe emotional atmosphere a text creates for readers.Cold rain, empty streets, and dim lights create a lonely mood.
toneThe narrator’s or implied author’s attitude toward the subject.The narrator’s playful description of the mistake establishes an amused tone.
sensory fieldA coordinated pattern of sensory details selected to shape atmosphere.Salt air, gull cries, and rough rope form a sensory field that shapes the harbor atmosphere.
