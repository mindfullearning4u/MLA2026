Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and complete definitions. Submit them with Page 5 Independent Work.

TermDefinition
paceThe perceived speed and allocation of narrative attention.
narrative timeThe span of story events represented by a passage.
sceneA dramatized unit in which events unfold with immediate action, detail, or dialogue.
summaryCompressed narration that covers events more quickly than they occur.
pauseA moment when narration slows or stops event time for description, thought, or reflection.
ellipsisThe omission of a span of story time that readers infer has passed.
tensionReader concern created by competing outcomes, uncertainty, and stakes.
suspenseForward-looking uncertainty about what will happen or be revealed.
stakesWhat may be gained, lost, damaged, or changed by an outcome.
moodThe emotional atmosphere a text creates for readers.
toneThe narrator’s or implied author’s attitude toward the subject.
sensory fieldA coordinated pattern of sensory details selected to shape atmosphere.
