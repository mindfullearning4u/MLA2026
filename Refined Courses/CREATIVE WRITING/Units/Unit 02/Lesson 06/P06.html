Between Floors

The elevator stopped between floors with Mara and a sealed cake box inside. The emergency speaker clicked once but produced no voice.

Mara checked her phone: one bar, then none. On the box, blue icing had dried beside a card reading FOR ELI—DO NOT OPEN.

Something shifted inside.

She told herself it was a decoration sliding. The elevator lights dimmed. From the box came three soft scratches.

Mara pressed the alarm. Somewhere above, machinery answered with a heavy knock. The box moved again, this time against the wall.

She lifted one corner of the lid. A black nose pushed through.

The puppy sneezed icing onto her shoe. At that moment, the emergency speaker announced, “Please remain calm.”

Analysis

  1. Write an 80–100 word objective summary.
  2. State Mara’s goal, uncertainty, and stakes.
  3. Label each paragraph S, M, P, or E.
  4. Graph tension from 1–5 and explain the two largest changes.
  5. Analyze the repeated sound details as a sensory field.
  6. Explain how sentence and paragraph length control pace.
  7. Identify what readers know, suspect, and still do not know before the lid opens.
  8. Distinguish mood from tone before and after the reveal.

Revision and Transfer

  1. Expand one moment to increase suspense without withholding basic orientation.
  2. Compress another moment and explain why it is less consequential.
  3. Rewrite the last sentence to create a serious rather than comic tone.
  4. Write a 280–340 word original scene with a graphed tension curve, two narrative-time modes, a controlled sensory field, and a tonal turn. Annotate each.
