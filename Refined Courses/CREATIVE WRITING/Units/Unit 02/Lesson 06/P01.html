Lesson Overview: Pace, Tension, Mood, and Tone

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.NAR.03 — manipulate pacing and tension to shape reader experience.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.COM.03 — control tone and syntax; MLA.CW.FIC.04 — create atmosphere and layered effects.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish pace, tension, suspense, mood, and tone.
  • Control narrative time through scene, summary, pause, and omission.
  • Build tension from desire, uncertainty, stakes, and delay.
  • Coordinate syntax, sensory detail, and diction to create atmosphere.

Why This Matters

Pace is not simply fast or slow. Effective writers allocate attention: they expand consequential moments, compress routine events, and delay information only when the delay increases meaningful uncertainty.
