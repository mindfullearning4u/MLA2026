Two Labels

At closing, Sora found two labels for the same museum photograph. One called the crowd “celebrants”; the other called them “protesters.” Through the window, workers lowered the exhibit sign.

Her supervisor told her to choose the newer label. Sora placed both beneath the photograph and added a blank card: What evidence changes the name?

The next morning, the blank card held three handwritten answers—and one question asking who had been allowed to write the labels.
