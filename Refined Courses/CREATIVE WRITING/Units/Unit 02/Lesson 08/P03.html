Direct Teaching: The Narrative Integration Matrix

Nine-Step Synthesis Process

  1. Name the controlling effect.
  2. Build objective, pressure, turn, and consequence.
  3. Choose focalizer and map access.
  4. Make setting restrict choice.
  5. Plan subtext and a motif.
  6. Graph pace and tension.
  7. Coordinate tone, diction, syntax, and sensory field.
  8. Draft, then revise structure before sentences.
  9. Annotate evidence and defend tradeoffs.

Revision Order

Revise in this order: purpose → architecture → viewpoint → information → pacing → style → conventions. Sentence polish cannot repair a scene without change.

Quality Test

For each major choice, write: “Because I used ___ when ___, the reader can ___.” Remove or revise any element whose effect remains vague.
