Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
integrationThe purposeful coordination of craft elements toward one effect.The revision integrates character, setting, conflict, and structure into one coherent scene.
controlling effectThe primary experience or understanding a writer intends to create.Every detail contributes to a controlling effect of growing unease.
scene architectureThe organized relationship among objective, pressure, beats, turn, and consequence.The scene architecture moves from a quiet setup through confrontation to a decisive turn.
access mapA record of what the focalizer knows, infers, and cannot know.An access map tracks which character knows each fact and when that knowledge becomes available.
value shiftA meaningful change in condition or relationship.The scene moves from confidence to doubt, creating a clear value shift.
setupAn early detail that prepares a later development.A missing photograph in paragraph one serves as setup for a later discovery.
payoffA later fulfillment or transformation of a setup.Finding the photograph in the final scene provides the payoff.
subtextUnstated meaning beneath words and behavior.The words are polite, but the subtext reveals a warning.
motifA repeated detail that gathers significance.Repeated images of locked windows form a motif of confinement.
tension curveA visual representation of changing pressure and uncertainty.The tension curve rises with each failed attempt and peaks at the final choice.
revision priorityThe highest-impact problem a writer chooses to address first.Clarifying the character’s goal is the revision priority because it will improve the entire scene.
craft rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of how choices create effects.The writer’s craft rationale explains how the revised ending strengthens the controlling effect.
