Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5.

integrationThe purposeful coordination of craft elements toward one effect.
controlling effectThe primary experience or understanding a writer intends to create.
scene architectureThe organized relationship among objective, pressure, beats, turn, and consequence.
access mapA record of what the focalizer knows, infers, and cannot know.
value shiftA meaningful change in condition or relationship.
setupAn early detail that prepares a later development.
payoffA later fulfillment or transformation of a setup.
subtextUnstated meaning beneath words and behavior.
motifA repeated detail that gathers significance.
tension curveA visual representation of changing pressure and uncertainty.
revision priorityThe highest-impact problem a writer chooses to address first.
craft rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of how choices create effects.
