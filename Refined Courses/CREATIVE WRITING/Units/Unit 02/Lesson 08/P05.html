Independent Work: Synthesize Narrative Craft

Purpose and Directions

Apply the lesson skill through focused analysis, original writing, and purposeful revision. Complete every numbered task and submit this work with your vocabulary notes in Notebook Evidence Submission. Quality matters more than length.

Unit Synthesis Model: “Northbound”

The northbound train arrived without its usual announcement. Mara stepped aboard carrying her father’s camera and a ticket purchased that morning. Through the window, the station clock disappeared behind rain. She opened the camera case and found a photograph she had never seen: her father at the same platform, young and smiling beside an unfamiliar woman. When the conductor asked for her destination, Mara looked once more at the photograph before naming the final stop.

1. Map the Narrative System

Complete three rows, then write a four-to-five-sentence synthesis explaining how the elements and craft choices build toward Mara’s final response.

Record three integrated observations.

2. Choose the Stronger Reasoning

Record A or B, then explain in two or three sentences why your choice better connects evidence, craft, and effect. The answer is not revealed here.

A. The photograph proves Mara is traveling to find the unfamiliar woman and learn her father’s secret.

B. The newly discovered photograph changes the ticket from simple travel into a possible search, but the passage withholds Mara’s destination and motive, preserving more than one explanation for her journey.

3. Create a Polished Narrative Scene

Write an eight-to-ten-sentence scene in which a discovery alters a character’s immediate plan. Establish point of view, setting, character goal, sequence, pace, and tone through coordinated choices. Preserve one meaningful uncertainty rather than resolving the entire story.

4. Revise and Defend

Revise one sentence or short passage to improve the relationship among three craft choices. Show both versions, then write a three-to-four-sentence rationale explaining the reader effect, evidence, and one trade-off of the revision.
