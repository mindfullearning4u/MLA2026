Lesson Overview: Unit 2 Narrative Synthesis

Synthesis Focus

No new primary standard is introduced. You will integrate narrative technique, viewpoint, characterization, setting, meaning, style, irony, pacing, tone, and scene design.

What You Will Do

  • Review twelve synthesis terms.
  • Use a visual craft-integration matrix.
  • Study three complete revision models.
  • Plan, draft, annotate, and defend a sustained narrative.
  • Complete a separate checkpoint and the Unit 2 Final Assessment.

Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4.
  2. Submit Page 2 and Page 5 in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 separately.
  4. Use TOR feedback to meet 80%.
  5. Complete the Unit 2 Final Assessment with at least 80%.

Student-Friendly Standard

I can design, draft, and defend a coherent narrative in which major craft choices work together.
