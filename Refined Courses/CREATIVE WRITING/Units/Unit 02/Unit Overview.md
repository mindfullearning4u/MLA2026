# CREATIVE WRITING - Unit 02 Overview: Narrative Writing

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops narrative writing through plot, character, conflict, setting, point of view, pacing, tension, mood, and tone. Students draft original narratives and make craft choices that shape how a reader experiences a story.

Emphasis is placed on narrative techniques, varied transitions, a clearly established point of view, pacing, character motivation, conflict, dialogue, and scene development. Students move from brief narrative sketches to a developed narrative draft.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to selected Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards with an emphasis on creative production, narrative craft, revision readiness, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate writing fluency and conceptual understanding through original narrative writing.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- narrative techniques;
- transitions;
- point of view;
- pacing;
- tension, mood, and tone;
- original narrative writing.

Assessment items require students to write narrative scenes and a developed narrative draft using narrative techniques, varied transitions, a clearly established point of view, and pacing that creates tension, mood, and/or tone.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit asks students to solve creative problems related to character, conflict, pacing, and perspective. Students make intentional choices about what to reveal, when to reveal it, and how the reader should experience the story.
