P07 Checkpoint

TOR Reminder

Ask your TOR for help before submitting if your draft still reads like a list of events or if your transitions do not show how the moments connect.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your P02 and P03 notebook notes.
  2. Check that your scene uses at least two narrative techniques.
  3. Check that your transitions show time, place, cause, contrast, or focus.
  4. Submit your checkpoint according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one revised narrative scene of 6-8 sentences. Your scene must begin from one plain event list, include at least two narrative techniques, and include at least three transitions. Under the scene, write a short explanation naming the techniques and explaining how the transitions help the reader follow the story.

Mastery Criteria

  • The scene is 6-8 sentences and develops one clear moment.
  • The scene uses at least two narrative techniques intentionally.
  • The scene uses at least three transitions accurately.
  • The explanation names the techniques and explains how the transitions guide the reader.
  • The writing follows standard English closely enough that meaning is clear.
