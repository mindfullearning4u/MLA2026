Lesson Overview: Narrative Techniques and Transitions

Lesson Focus

Lesson 1: Narrative Techniques and Transitions

Primary Standard: MLA.CW.NAR.01 — use narrative techniques and point of view to develop coherent experiences and events.

Supporting Standards: MLA.CW.WR.05 — use academic and craft vocabulary; MLA.CW.REV.01 — revise through evidence and feedback.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish scene, summary, exposition, and reflection by their narrative functions.
  • Build scenes from goal, obstacle, action, change, and consequence.
  • Use temporal, spatial, causal, and associative transitions to control logic and pacing.
  • Choose dialogue, description, action, and interior thought based on purpose—not decoration.

Why This Matters

A narrative is more than a sequence of events. Readers need to understand where they are, why one moment leads to another, what changes, and which moments deserve attention. Technique and transition determine that experience.

What You Will Do

  • Record and apply twelve narrative terms.
  • Learn a six-stage scene-and-transition method.
  • Study three complete worked examples with revision comparisons.
  • Analyze a substantial narrative and create a controlled original scene.
  • Complete a distinct checkpoint and transfer task.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Submit Page 2 vocabulary and all Page 5 work together in Notebook Evidence.
  3. Submit Page 6 responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback and resubmit work below 80%.
  5. Complete the randomized five-question quiz with at least 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain why a narrative moment should be expanded or compressed.
  • Analyze how techniques interact to create movement and meaning.
  • Repair weak transitions without overexplaining.
  • Write and justify an original scene with purposeful pacing and coherence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can shape events into a coherent narrative by choosing the right techniques, controlling pacing, and guiding readers clearly from one meaningful moment to the next.
