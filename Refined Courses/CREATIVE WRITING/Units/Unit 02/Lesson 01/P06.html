The Last Rehearsal

During every rehearsal, Jalen entered through the stage door before anyone else and placed his father’s silver watch on the piano. The watch had stopped at 9:03, the time printed on the old concert program inside its case.

On opening night, the stage door would not unlock. Jalen circled the building, crossed the loading dock, and entered through the audience lobby. Through the curtains, he saw the piano already under the lights.

“Places in two minutes,” the director called.

Jalen reached into his pocket. The watch was missing.

He pictured it on the bus seat, beside the vending machine, beneath the locked stage door. The orchestra began tuning. For the first time, he could not place the watch on the piano before the opening chord.

Jalen stepped onto the stage. At 9:03, the conductor lifted her hands. The piano’s black surface held only the reflection of the lights.

After the final applause, Jalen found the watch inside the program case. He closed the case without removing it and left through the lobby.

Analysis

  1. Write an 80–100 word accurate summary.
  2. Identify the scene goal, resistance, turn, and consequence.
  3. Explain how the opening routine functions as exposition.
  4. Analyze the spatial transitions created by the locked stage door.
  5. Why does the narrative slow after Jalen discovers the watch is missing?
  6. Explain the transition from imagined locations to the orchestra tuning.
  7. Analyze the repeated time, 9:03, as structure and meaning.
  8. Why is the performance itself compressed?

Revision and Transfer

  1. Rewrite one transition to emphasize cause rather than chronology. Explain the effect.
  2. Identify one moment that should not be expanded and defend your judgment.
  3. Write a 180–220 word original scene using routine, disruption, spatial movement, a meaningful time marker, and a consequential ending.
  4. Annotate your scene by naming four narrative techniques and explaining how two transitions control coherence and pace.
