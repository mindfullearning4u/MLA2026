P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your writer's notebook. Use complete sentences where explanation is required.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

You will turn a plain event list into a short narrative scene. Your goal is not to write a full story yet. Your goal is to practice controlled movement from event to scene.

Part A

Choose one event list below:

  • I lost the ticket. I searched my bag. The doors opened.
  • Sam heard a whisper. Sam turned around. The hallway was empty.
  • The phone rang. I saw the name. I did not answer.

Copy the event list you chose.

Part B

Rewrite the event list as a scene of 6-8 sentences. Include at least three of these narrative techniques: action, dialogue, description, reflection, sensory detail.

Part C

Underline or label at least three transitions in your scene. Next to each transition, write what it shows: time, place, cause, contrast, or shift in focus.
