Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
sceneA dramatized unit of narrative in which characters pursue goals through action, dialogue, sensory detail, and change.
summaryCompressed narration that covers events or time without fully dramatizing each moment.
expositionBackground information necessary for understanding character, setting, conflict, or context.
reflectionNarration that interprets the meaning or significance of an experience.
narrative techniqueA deliberate method—such as dialogue, description, pacing, or interior thought—used to shape reader experience.
pacingThe perceived speed and duration of narrative events.
transitionLanguage or structure that clarifies movement between time, place, idea, action, or cause.
temporal transitionA transition that establishes movement or relationship in time.
spatial transitionA transition that guides the reader between locations or positions.
causal transitionA transition that reveals why one action, decision, or consequence leads to another.
associative transitionA movement triggered by memory, image, sound, object, or idea rather than simple chronology.
narrative coherenceThe meaningful connection among events, motives, transitions, and consequences so the narrative feels understandable and purposeful.
