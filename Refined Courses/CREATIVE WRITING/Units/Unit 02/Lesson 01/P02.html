Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
sceneA dramatized unit of narrative in which characters pursue goals through action, dialogue, sensory detail, and change.The argument unfolds moment by moment through action and dialogue.
summaryCompressed narration that covers events or time without fully dramatizing each moment.Three uneventful years pass in a single sentence.
expositionBackground information necessary for understanding character, setting, conflict, or context.The narrator briefly explains why the family left its hometown.
reflectionNarration that interprets the meaning or significance of an experience.Looking back, the narrator realizes the silence was an act of protection.
narrative techniqueA deliberate method—such as dialogue, description, pacing, or interior thought—used to shape reader experience.Interior thought reveals what the character cannot say aloud.
pacingThe perceived speed and duration of narrative events.Short sentences accelerate the chase; detailed description slows the discovery.
transitionLanguage or structure that clarifies movement between time, place, idea, action, or cause.“Later that evening” moves the narrative forward in time.
temporal transitionA transition that establishes movement or relationship in time.“By dawn” signals when the next event occurs.
spatial transitionA transition that guides the reader between locations or positions.“Across the courtyard” guides the reader to a new location.
causal transitionA transition that reveals why one action, decision, or consequence leads to another.“Because she hid the letter, the meeting ended without an answer” shows cause.
associative transitionA movement triggered by memory, image, sound, object, or idea rather than simple chronology.The smell of cinnamon triggers a childhood memory.
narrative coherenceThe meaningful connection among events, motives, transitions, and consequences so the narrative feels understandable and purposeful.Clear motives and transitions connect each event to its consequence.
