P07 Checkpoint

TOR Reminder

Ask your TOR for help before submitting if you cannot explain how your point of view, key element, and pacing support one purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your scene plan.
  2. Check that the scene is 8-10 sentences.
  3. Check that your explanation names point of view, key element, and pacing.
  4. Submit your checkpoint according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one 8-10 sentence integrated narrative scene and one 4-5 sentence craft explanation. The explanation must identify the scene purpose, point of view, key element, pacing choice, and intended reader effect.

Mastery Criteria

  • The scene is 8-10 sentences and focused on one moment.
  • The point of view stays clear and consistent.
  • The scene uses at least three narrative techniques.
  • A key element adds meaning or style.
  • Pacing creates a clear reader effect.
  • The explanation connects craft choices to scene purpose.
