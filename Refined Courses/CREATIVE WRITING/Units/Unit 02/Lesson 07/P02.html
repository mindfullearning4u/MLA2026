Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all twelve terms and definitions. Submit them with Page 5.

scene objectiveThe immediate result a focal character tries to obtain.
beatA small unit of action, reaction, decision, or change.
turnThe moment new information or choice redirects the scene.
value shiftA meaningful change in condition, such as trust to suspicion.
causal coherenceA clear relationship in which events and choices produce later effects.
setting pressureA condition of place, time, or society that restricts choices.
focalizationThe consciousness through which information is filtered.
subtextUnstated meaning beneath words and behavior.
motifA repeated detail that gathers significance.
pacingThe allocation and perceived speed of narrative attention.
payoffA later moment that fulfills or transforms an earlier setup.
craft rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of why specific writing choices create intended effects.
