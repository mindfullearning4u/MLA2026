Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every core term and its complete meaning in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence. Use the examples to see how each term works in context. Reference glossary terms do not need to be copied.

TermMeaningExample
scene objectiveThe immediate result a focal character tries to obtain.Mara’s scene objective is to persuade the principal to reopen the studio.
beatA small unit of action, reaction, decision, or change.A glance at the clock is a beat that prompts the character to act.
turnThe moment new information or choice redirects the scene.The discovery of a hidden key creates the turn that redirects the scene.
value shiftA meaningful change in condition, such as trust to suspicion.A friendly conversation shifts from trust to suspicion, creating a value shift.
causal coherenceA clear relationship in which events and choices produce later effects.Because the character lies, her friend leaves; that cause-and-effect link creates causal coherence.
setting pressureA condition of place, time, or society that restricts choices.An approaching storm and the last departing bus create setting pressure.
focalizationThe consciousness through which information is filtered.Through focalization, readers notice only the clues the anxious narrator understands.
subtextUnstated meaning beneath words and behavior.“I’m glad you remembered,” he says, while the subtext suggests disappointment.
motifA repeated detail that gathers significance.A recurring cracked mirror becomes a motif for the character’s divided identity.
pacingThe allocation and perceived speed of narrative attention.Short paragraphs and quick exchanges accelerate the scene’s pacing.
payoffA later moment that fulfills or transforms an earlier setup.The final revelation pays off the clue planted in the opening paragraph.
craft rationaleAn evidence-based explanation of why specific writing choices create intended effects.The writer explains that limiting the viewpoint increases suspense; that explanation is a craft rationale.
