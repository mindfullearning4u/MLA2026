Draft for Workshop: “The Return”

At sunset, Priya unlocked the greenhouse for the first time since winter. Most of the benches were bare. On the center table, one clay pot held a green shoot and a folded note. She recognized her brother’s narrow handwriting but did not open it. Instead, she filled the watering can and listened as the first drops struck the dry soil.

2. Choose the Stronger Reasoning

Record A or B, then explain in two or three sentences why your choice better connects evidence, craft, and effect. The answer is not revealed here.

A. The scene is about Priya finding a note, and the plant symbolizes hope.

B. The winter-bare greenhouse contrasts with the single green shoot, while Priya waters it before opening the note; setting, object, and action therefore connect renewal with a still-unresolved relationship.

3. Apply the Skill in Your Writing

Plan and write an eight-to-ten-sentence narrative scene with a clear point of view, purposeful setting, character goal, complication, and meaningful change. Coordinate at least three craft choices so they serve one intended reader effect.

4. Revise and Explain

Revise one passage where an element feels disconnected or overexplained. Show the before-and-after wording, then explain how the revision strengthens the interaction among at least two scene elements.
