Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Page 5 Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
narrative elementA component of a story—such as character, conflict, setting, plot, or point of view—that contributes to its total effect.
characterizationThe methods a writer uses to reveal a character through action, speech, thought, description, and response.
conflictA meaningful struggle between competing wants, values, forces, or obligations.
settingThe time, place, social conditions, and atmosphere in which events occur.
plotThe arranged sequence of causally connected events in a narrative.
turning pointA moment when new knowledge, choice, or consequence changes the direction of a narrative.
themeA developed insight about human experience expressed through the story’s patterns and consequences.
motifA recurring image, phrase, object, situation, or idea that accumulates significance.
toneThe narrator’s or implied author’s attitude toward the subject and audience.
styleA writer’s recognizable pattern of diction, syntax, imagery, figurative language, and rhythm.
syntaxThe arrangement and rhythm of words, phrases, and clauses in sentences.
juxtapositionThe placement of contrasting elements near one another to sharpen meaning.
