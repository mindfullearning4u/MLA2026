# Phase 2A-B: Creative Writing Crosswalk Draft

## Executive Summary

This crosswalk aligns the verified Creative Writing MLA standards to the official CPALMS `Creative Writing 1 (#1009320)` benchmark set and to broad college, workforce, SAT, ACT, and Common Core readiness categories. The alignments are planning-level architecture alignments, not assessment items or lesson content.

All 31 official CPALMS-listed standards appear exactly once.
