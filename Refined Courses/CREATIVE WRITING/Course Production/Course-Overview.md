# Creative Writing Course Overview

## Course Identity

Creative Writing is a high-school writing-production course built from the verified CPALMS `Creative Writing 1 (#1009320)` standards record. The course develops writing and language skills for creative expression in a variety of literary forms, with emphasis on genre study, craft modeling, narrative writing, poetry, fiction, revision, peer feedback, portfolio development, and publication-ready writing.

This overview is architecture only. It does not create lessons, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, LMS pages, or student-facing instructional content.

## Course Structure

| Unit | Unit Title | Core Focus |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of Creative Writing, Voice, and Craft | Writer identity, workshop norms, craft vocabulary, model reading, voice/tone, evidence-based craft discussion, and language access |
| 2 | Narrative Craft, Point of View, Character, and Setting | Narrative techniques, point of view, key elements, style, narrator effects, pacing, tension, mood, and tone |
| 3 | Fiction, Theme, Adaptation, and World Building | Universal themes, adaptation, central idea support, author purpose, rhetoric, coming-of-age patterns, and conflicting perspectives |
| 4 | Poetry, Figurative Language, Mood, and Style | Figurative language, mood, ambiguity, layers of meaning, poetic diction, connotation, denotation, and style |
| 5 | Revision, Workshop, Portfolio, and Publication | Feedback-based revision, conventions, workshop collaboration, format-specific quality, digital publication, and author presentation |
| 6 | Author Portfolio, Presentation, and Publication Synthesis | Portfolio selection, author statement, genre revision, publication formatting, author presentation, and final synthesis |
