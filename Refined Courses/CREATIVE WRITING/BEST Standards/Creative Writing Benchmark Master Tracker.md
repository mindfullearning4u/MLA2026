# CREATIVE WRITING Benchmark Master Tracker

---

## Standards Source

Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Standards, Grades 9-12 Band

Creative Writing does not have unique standards. Writing standards are officially coded within the Communication strand as ELA.x.C.1.x.

---

## Supporting Skills Progression

| Unit | Primary Focus | Recurring Supporting Skills |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Unit 01 | Foundations of Creative Expression | Writing habits, academic vocabulary, conventions, imagery, voice, and creative observation |
| Unit 02 | Narrative Writing | Voice, conventions, vocabulary, point of view, narrative techniques, pacing, mood, tone, and peer response |
| Unit 03 | Poetry and Language | Figurative language, connotation, denotation, word relationships, mood, symbolism, sound, and revision |
| Unit 04 | Creative Nonfiction | Research, reliable sources, explanatory structure, reflective writing, narrative craft, and source-based storytelling |
| Unit 05 | Style and Revision | Feedback, revision, sentence flow, clarity, style, vocabulary precision, and editing for publication |
| Unit 06 | Portfolio and Publication | Revision of multiple works, oral presentation, digital presentation, multimedia publication, audience awareness, and reflection |
