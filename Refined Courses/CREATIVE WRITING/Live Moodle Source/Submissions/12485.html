Checkpoint Submission

What to Submit

Submit your completed Checkpoint responses.

Before You Submit

Review your work for completeness, clarity, supporting evidence, and correct conventions before submitting.

Mastery Requirement

A score of at least 80% is required. If you score below 80%, review your TOR’s feedback, make corrections, and resubmit.

What to Include

At the top of your work, include:

  • Student Name
  • Course Name
  • Unit and Lesson

Example:
John Smith
Creative Writing – Unit 1, Lesson 4

Submission Format

  • PDF (preferred)
  • Microsoft Word: DOC or DOCX
  • Images: JPG, JPEG, PNG

If using Google Docs, download and submit your work as a PDF or Word file. Links are not accepted as the submission. Put dictionary and research URLs within your document. Number pages and submit all pages together in order.

🧑‍🏫 TOR Help
Contact your Teacher of Record (TOR) if you need clarification, feedback, or support.
