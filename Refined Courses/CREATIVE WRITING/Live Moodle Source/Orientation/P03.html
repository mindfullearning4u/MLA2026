Expectations and Policies

Student Responsibilities

  • follow the official Moodle sequence;
  • read all directions before beginning;
  • complete and submit original work;
  • use evidence and show reasoning;
  • review feedback and revise when required;
  • communicate promptly and respectfully;
  • protect assessment integrity and account security.

Grading and Feedback

Your TOR grades required evidence and assessments using the stated criteria. Feedback may identify missing evidence, an inaccurate interpretation, weak organization, a craft or delivery issue, or a need for remediation. Read all feedback before revising.

Work Evidence

Label evidence with the unit, lesson, and task. Upload the requested file type and confirm that text, annotations, planning, drafts, images, or recordings are complete and readable or playable.

Mastery Checkpoint Evidence

Checkpoint work must answer the task on the current page. Include the requested claim, evidence, reasoning, analysis, craft explanation, reflection, or presentation evidence. Do not submit unrelated notes.

Evidence and Communication Expectations

  • identify the source, speaker, text, or context accurately;
  • select specific and relevant evidence;
  • explain how evidence supports the response;
  • organize ideas logically for the purpose and audience;
  • use appropriate conventions, attribution, and citation;
  • revise content and edit presentation before submission.

Academic Integrity

Copied work, shared assessment content, unauthorized answer sites, fabricated evidence or sources, plagiarism, and unapproved AI-generated submissions are prohibited. You may be asked to explain your work or process.

Communication

Messages to your TOR should identify the course, unit, lesson, and page, explain what you attempted, and ask a precise question. For portfolio, presentation, audio/video, publication, or upload problems, include the device, file type, and error message.

Technical Requirements and Course Materials

  • reliable internet and a current browser;
  • Moodle and academy-account access;
  • a method to create readable documents or PDFs;
  • audio/video recording tools when the course requires them;
  • only the sources, tools, and media authorized on the current page.

Accessibility and Accommodations

Approved accommodations apply throughout the course. Contact your TOR if an accommodation is missing from a text, visual, recording, assessment, or submission activity.

Revision and Remediation

When directed, study the feedback, correct the underlying issue, preserve evidence of the revision process, and resubmit through the designated activity. Revision should improve meaning, evidence, organization, craft, delivery, or conventions—not merely replace a final answer.
