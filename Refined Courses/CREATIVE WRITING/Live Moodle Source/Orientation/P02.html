How This Course Works

Mastery-Based Learning

Progress follows this cycle: Learn → Analyze → Practice → Apply → Check Mastery → Review Feedback → Revise when required. Completion depends on demonstrated understanding, not simply viewing pages.

Where You Begin

Begin each unit with its ungraded Unit Pretest. Complete it honestly and independently so your TOR can identify prior knowledge and skills that may need support.

Unit Workflow

  1. Complete the Unit Pretest.
  2. Work through lessons in the Moodle order.
  3. Complete every page and required evidence submission.
  4. Complete each Mastery Checkpoint and Lesson Quiz.
  5. Review TOR feedback and revise when directed.
  6. Complete the Unit Assessment.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Lesson Overview: read the lesson focus, standards, learning goals, workflow, and success criteria.
  2. Vocabulary and Definitions: learn and record the key terms needed for the lesson.
  3. Lesson: study the content, craft explanations, models, visuals, and teacher guidance.
  4. Worked Example: follow step-by-step modeling that explains how a writer makes and evaluates craft choices.
  5. Independent Work: apply the lesson skills to a substantial reading, analysis, or original composition task.
  6. Checkpoint: independently demonstrate the lesson target and submit the required responses.

Text and Source Evidence

When a task requires a text, image, audio/video selection, chart, or source excerpt, the necessary material must appear or be linked on the same page. Cite or reference the evidence accurately and explain its significance. Do not rely on memory of an earlier page.

Worked Example

Study each model in order. Follow the teacher reasoning, notice how each craft decision affects the reader, and use the demonstrated process when you begin your independent work.

Independent Work

Complete the assigned response, composition, report, or presentation work independently. Address the purpose and audience, use relevant evidence or craft choices, and revise for clarity and correctness.

Checkpoint

Read the current checkpoint task, complete it independently, and submit the requested evidence. The checkpoint is separate from notebook or general practice work. Review feedback after grading and revise only when directed.

Lesson Quizzes and Unit Assessments

Lessons 1–7 include a Lesson Quiz; Lesson 8 is synthesis. Unit Assessments measure understanding, analysis, evidence use, application, and communication across the unit. Required stimuli appear with the question that uses them.

Completion, Mastery, and Help

The minimum mastery threshold is 80%. A page view alone is not completion. Contact your TOR with the course, unit, lesson, page title, what you attempted, and the exact assistance needed.
