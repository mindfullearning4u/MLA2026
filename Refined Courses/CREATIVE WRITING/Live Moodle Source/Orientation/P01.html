MINDFUL LEARNING ACADEMY • CREATIVE WRITING • COURSE ORIENTATION

Course Overview

Creative Writing

MLA Course Code: CW

Credit: 1.0 High School Elective or English Language Arts Credit, as assigned

Course Type: Online, asynchronous, mastery-based

Delivery: 100% online through Moodle

Instructional Model: Writer’s workshop, craft study, revision, and publication

Teacher Support: Teacher of Record (TOR)

Course Description

Creative Writing develops voice, narrative craft, fiction, poetry, workshop, revision, and publication. You will study models, analyze purpose and craft, practice with structured support, create original responses or products, and revise using evidence and feedback.

Moodle contains the official instructional sequence, readings and media, required submissions, quizzes, unit assessments, grades, TOR feedback, and progress tracking.

Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

  • read, view, or listen closely for central ideas, details, structure, and purpose;
  • use relevant evidence accurately and explain how it supports a claim or creative choice;
  • analyze audience, context, perspective, language, and craft;
  • plan, draft, revise, edit, and publish clear work for an identified purpose;
  • evaluate sources for credibility, relevance, bias, and appropriate use;
  • communicate ideas effectively in written, oral, visual, or digital forms;
  • apply feedback and reflect on growth toward mastery.

Prerequisite Knowledge and Skills

You should be able to follow multistep directions, read grade-appropriate texts with support, write complete responses, organize work, use basic digital tools, and communicate with your TOR when assistance is needed.

Course Organization

This course contains 6 units and 48 lessons. Each unit contains eight lessons. Lessons 1–7 develop content and communication skills; Lesson 8 serves as unit synthesis. Each unit begins with an ungraded Unit Pretest and ends with a graded Unit Assessment.

  •  
  • Unit 1 — Foundations of Creative Writing, Voice, and Craft
  •  
  • Unit 2 — Narrative Craft, Point of View, Character, and Setting
  •  
  • Unit 3 — Fiction, Theme, Adaptation, and World Building
  •  
  • Unit 4 — Poetry, Figurative Language, Mood, and Style
  •  
  • Unit 5 — Revision, Workshop, Portfolio, and Publication
  •  
  • Unit 6 — Author Portfolio, Presentation, and Publication Synthesis

How Lessons Work

Each lesson follows a clear six-page learning sequence:

  1. Lesson Overview
  2. Vocabulary and Definitions
  3. Lesson
  4. Worked Example
  5. Independent Work
  6. Checkpoint

Complete the required notebook or process evidence and checkpoint submission separately. Lessons 1–7 include a Lesson Quiz; Lesson 8 is a synthesis lesson.

Notebook and Process Evidence

Maintain organized evidence identified in each lesson. It may include annotations, vocabulary, notes, source analysis, outlines, planning, drafts, rehearsal notes, peer- or self-review, revisions, and reflection. Submit only the evidence requested on the current page.

Assessments and Mastery

  • ungraded Unit Pretests;
  • required notebook or process-evidence submissions;
  • Mastery Checkpoints;
  • Lesson Quizzes for Lessons 1–7;
  • graded Unit Assessments.

The minimum mastery threshold is 80%. Revision, remediation, or approved reassessment may be required before progression.

Standards Alignment

Creative Writing uses MLA-created standards as the controlling curriculum standards, crosswalked to applicable Florida B.E.S.T. ELA standards, communication and media-literacy expectations, and college- and career-readiness skills.

Teacher of Record Support

Your TOR monitors progress, grades required evidence, provides feedback, supports remediation, and assists with academic or technical problems. Include the course, unit, lesson, page title, and a specific question when requesting help.

Academic Integrity and Course Completion

Submit your own work. Do not copy, share assessment answers, fabricate sources or evidence, or submit unapproved AI-generated work as your own. The course is complete after all units, lessons, required evidence, checkpoints, quizzes, assessments, and directed revisions are finished.
