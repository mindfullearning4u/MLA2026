P04 Worked Example

This model shows how the unit skills work together in one complete synthesis response.

Example 1: Identify the Central Task

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: read the scenario. Step 2: identify the central claim, theme, issue, or communication purpose. Step 3: write one sentence naming what the response must explain.

Model: The response must explain how close reading, paraphrase, academic vocabulary, conventions, and oral explanation work together to create meaning for a reader or audience.

Example 2: Select Evidence and Reasoning

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: choose evidence that directly supports the central task. Step 2: explain how the evidence connects to the claim or interpretation. Step 3: use precise academic vocabulary.

Model: The strongest evidence is not just interesting; it is the detail that helps prove the claim and lets the explanation move beyond summary.

Example 3: Construct the Final Response

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: begin with a focused claim or interpretation. Step 2: include evidence and reasoning. Step 3: revise for vocabulary, style, and conventions.

Model: A complete synthesis response connects the unit skills in one polished explanation instead of answering each skill separately.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The response summarizes the scenario and mentions one unit skill without explaining how the skills work together.

Correct: The response uses evidence, vocabulary, reasoning, and conventions together to explain the unit focus clearly.

Teachable Explanation: A synthesis answer must show relationships among skills. The reader should see how evidence, analysis, language, and communication choices support one complete idea.
