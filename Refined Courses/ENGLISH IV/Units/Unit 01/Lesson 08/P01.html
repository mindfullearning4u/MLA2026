ENGLISH IV | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 1 Synthesis

This lesson synthesizes the Unit 1 standards already taught in Lessons 1-7. It does not introduce new primary standards and contains the Unit Assessment instead of a Lesson Quiz.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 1 skills work together in a synthesis task.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.
  • How to check your own work for paraphrase accuracy, vocabulary precision, conventions, and coherent explanation.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 1 skills using a synthesis passage.
  • Complete guided practice.
  • Complete independent synthesis work.
  • Submit the checkpoint and complete the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit a synthesis checkpoint.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment in Moodle.
  • Demonstrate accurate paraphrase, academic vocabulary, conventions, and coherent evidence explanation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can combine Unit 1 skills to understand, paraphrase, explain, revise, and present source-based ideas clearly.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help: If the synthesis checklist shows a gap, ask for support before completing the Unit Assessment.
