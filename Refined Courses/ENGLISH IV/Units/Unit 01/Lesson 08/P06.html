Passage: Independent Learning Habits

Independent learning does not mean working without support. Effective independent learners use resources deliberately, ask focused questions, evaluate feedback, and revise their work. A student who receives feedback but does not examine it carefully may repeat the same error, while a student who studies the feedback can identify the pattern behind the mistake. Strong independent learning therefore depends on both personal responsibility and thoughtful use of available support. In college and workplace settings, this habit matters because people are expected to improve work from evidence, feedback, and reflection.
