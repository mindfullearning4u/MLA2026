Embedded Passage

Passage: Independent Learning Habits

Independent learning does not mean working without support. Effective independent learners use resources deliberately, ask focused questions, evaluate feedback, and revise their work. A student who receives feedback but does not examine it carefully may repeat the same error, while a student who studies the feedback can identify the pattern behind the mistake. Strong independent learning therefore depends on both personal responsibility and thoughtful use of available support. In college and workplace settings, this habit matters because people are expected to improve work from evidence, feedback, and reflection.

Deeper Learning

Synthesis is not a pile of separate skills. It is one connected performance. If your paraphrase is accurate but your evidence explanation is vague, the response is incomplete. If your vocabulary is precise but your conventions confuse the sentence, the response loses credibility.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example: Independent learning means never using support.

Correct example: Independent learning means using support thoughtfully while taking responsibility for revision and improvement.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect example reverses the passage's first sentence. The correct example preserves the qualification and synthesizes the source's main idea.
