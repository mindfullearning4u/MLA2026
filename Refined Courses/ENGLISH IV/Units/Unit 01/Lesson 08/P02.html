P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Unit 01 Synthesis: Combining Skills from Lessons 1-7

Vocabulary

Use unit vocabulary accurately as part of your explanation. Focus on words that help you explain close reading, paraphrase, academic vocabulary, conventions, and oral explanation.

Passage / Scenario

A student speaker argues that careful reading matters after graduation. The speaker explains that workplace messages, college readings, and civic information often contain dense wording. Readers must identify the central meaning, paraphrase accurately, use academic vocabulary precisely, and communicate the meaning clearly to others.

Integrated Synthesis Task

Step 1: Read the scenario carefully. Step 2: identify the central claim, theme, issue, or task. Step 3: mark the details that matter most. Step 4: explain how those details connect to the unit skills from Lessons 1-7.

In your notebook, write a short planning note that connects at least three unit skills instead of treating them separately.
