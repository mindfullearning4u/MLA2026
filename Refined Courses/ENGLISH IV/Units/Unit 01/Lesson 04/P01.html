ENGLISH IV | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Advanced Analytical Written Response Foundations

This lesson teaches you how to build a clear analytical response from a paraphrased source idea, a focused claim, and precise reasoning.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.04 - Revise for Purpose, Clarity, Structure, and Style
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to turn paraphrased source meaning into an analytical claim.
  • How to connect evidence and reasoning in a focused response.
  • How to revise for purpose, clarity, structure, and style.
  • How conventions support credibility in advanced academic writing.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded passage about library learning spaces.
  • Paraphrase a source idea accurately.
  • Build an analytical response using claim, paraphrase, and reasoning.
  • Revise the response for clarity and style.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks and worked examples.
  • Complete guided practice and the lesson quiz.
  • Submit a checkpoint analytical response with a revision note.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG4.R.10: I can use accurate paraphrase as the foundation for a clear analytical written response.
