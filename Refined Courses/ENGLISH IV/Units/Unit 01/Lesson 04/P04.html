Passage: Libraries as Learning Spaces

A modern library is no longer only a storage place for printed books. In many communities, the library functions as a learning center where people attend workshops, use technology, meet with tutors, and access quiet study space. This expanded role does not make books less important; instead, it shows that reading, research, and community learning now support one another. A strong library program balances access to texts with access to guidance, tools, and collaborative spaces. When a library removes barriers to information and support, it strengthens independent learning.
