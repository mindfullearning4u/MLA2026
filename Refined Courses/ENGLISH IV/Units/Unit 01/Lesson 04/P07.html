Checkpoint Task

Use this embedded passage: A modern library is no longer only a storage place for printed books. In many communities, the library functions as a learning center where people attend workshops, use technology, meet with tutors, and access quiet study space. This expanded role does not make books less important; instead, it shows that reading, research, and community learning now support one another. A strong library program balances access to texts with access to guidance, tools, and collaborative spaces. When a library removes barriers to information and support, it strengthens independent learning.

Submit: one analytical paragraph with a claim, accurate paraphrase, and reasoning. Include a short revision note explaining one improvement you made for clarity, structure, or style.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response includes a focused analytical claim.
  • The paraphrase accurately preserves the passage's meaning.
  • The reasoning explains how the paraphrased idea supports the claim.
  • The revision note identifies a meaningful improvement.
  • The response follows standard English conventions.
