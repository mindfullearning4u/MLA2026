ENGLISH IV | Unit 01 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Academic Vocabulary for Advanced Analysis

This lesson teaches you how to choose, interpret, and use Grade 12 academic vocabulary precisely when analyzing a text and explaining your reasoning.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How academic words create precise distinctions in analysis.
  • How to choose vocabulary that matches what you actually mean.
  • How to paraphrase a passage while preserving the meaning of key academic terms.
  • How standard English conventions support clear academic expression.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded passage about precision in civic conversation.
  • Analyze the difference between related academic words.
  • Practice selecting precise vocabulary for interpretation and evaluation.
  • Write a short explanation using academic vocabulary accurately.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook vocabulary analysis and application tasks.
  • Complete guided practice aligned to the lesson standards.
  • Submit a checkpoint response that uses precise academic vocabulary.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG4.V.01: I can use Grade 12 academic vocabulary accurately in speaking and writing so my analysis is precise, credible, and clear.

In college and the workplace, precise vocabulary helps readers understand exactly what you claim, what evidence means, and why your reasoning is effective.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help: If two academic words seem similar and you cannot explain the difference, ask your Teacher of Record before submitting your checkpoint.
