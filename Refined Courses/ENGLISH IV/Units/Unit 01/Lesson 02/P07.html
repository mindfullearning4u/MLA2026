Checkpoint Task

Use this embedded passage: When people discuss a public issue, precision matters. A speaker who describes a policy as beneficial is making a broad positive judgment, but a speaker who calls it equitable is making a more specific claim about fairness. Likewise, describing evidence as relevant means it connects to the issue, while describing it as credible means it can be trusted. Academic vocabulary allows writers and speakers to make exact distinctions. Without precise words, an argument may sound confident while leaving the audience unsure about what is being claimed, how evidence supports it, or why the conclusion should be accepted.

Submit: a one-paragraph explanation that uses at least four lesson vocabulary words accurately. Explain how precise vocabulary improves the effectiveness of analysis in college, workplace, or civic communication.
