Checkpoint Task

Use this embedded passage: A community college review found that students with reliable evening transportation were more likely to attend tutoring sessions, library workshops, and study groups after work. The report did not claim that transportation alone caused higher grades. Instead, it concluded that dependable transit expanded students' access to academic support services. The strongest evidence came from attendance records showing that late-afternoon workshop participation increased after the college added a shuttle route to nearby bus stops. The review recommended continuing the shuttle program while also studying other barriers, including childcare, course scheduling, and internet access.

Submit: one claim, one paraphrased evidence sentence, and a three-sentence explanation of why the evidence is relevant. Include one sentence that could be used in a brief oral explanation.

Mastery Criteria

  • The evidence is relevant to the claim.
  • The paraphrase is accurate and does not overstate the passage.
  • The explanation clearly states how the evidence supports the claim.
  • The response uses academic vocabulary and clear organization.
