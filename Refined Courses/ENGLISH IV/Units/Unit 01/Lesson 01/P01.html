P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Grade 12 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

This lesson teaches you how to read a grade-level passage closely and paraphrase it accurately without distorting meaning, emphasis, or relationships among ideas.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the full meaning of a grade-level text before restating it.
  • How to preserve qualifications, cause-and-effect relationships, contrasts, and emphasis in a paraphrase.
  • How to use academic vocabulary and standard English conventions to write a clear paraphrase.
  • How to judge whether a paraphrase is accurate, incomplete, too close to the original, or distorted.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded passage about careful reading and paraphrase.
  • Break the passage into smaller meaning units.
  • Compare incorrect and correct paraphrases.
  • Write and revise your own accurate paraphrase.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks that show how you identify meaning before paraphrasing.
  • Complete guided practice aligned to the lesson standards.
  • Submit a checkpoint with a paraphrase and explanation of your decisions.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG4.R.10: I can restate a grade-level text in my own words while keeping the author's meaning, limits, relationships, and emphasis accurate.

In college, work, and civic settings, accurate paraphrase matters because people make decisions from written information. A distorted paraphrase can change what a source actually says.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help: If you cannot explain the difference between the original text and your paraphrase, pause and ask your Teacher of Record for support before moving forward.
