Checkpoint Task

Use this embedded passage: In professional communication, conventions are not decorative rules; they protect meaning. A misplaced comma can blur the relationship between ideas, and a sentence fragment can make a recommendation sound unfinished. Clear conventions also help readers evaluate a writer's reasoning. When grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling are controlled, the audience can focus on the claim, evidence, and explanation instead of struggling to decode the sentence. Strong writers revise conventions because accurate language supports credible thinking.

Submit: one accurate paraphrase of the passage's main idea, three corrected sentences, and one explanation of how conventions support clear academic reasoning.

Mastery Criteria

  • The paraphrase accurately preserves the passage's main idea.
  • Corrections follow standard English conventions.
  • Explanations connect conventions to clarity, credibility, or meaning.
  • The response uses academic vocabulary accurately.
