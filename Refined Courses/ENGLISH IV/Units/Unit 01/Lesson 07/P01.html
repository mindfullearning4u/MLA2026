ENGLISH IV | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice

This lesson integrates accurate paraphrase, precise academic vocabulary, standard conventions, and evidence-based explanation before the Unit 1 synthesis assessment.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine close reading, paraphrase, evidence, and vocabulary in one response.
  • How to keep academic voice clear, precise, and credible.
  • How to explain evidence in writing and in a brief oral script.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded passage about responsible technology use.
  • Paraphrase a key idea and select relevant evidence.
  • Use academic vocabulary to explain why the evidence matters.
  • Revise for conventions and prepare a brief oral explanation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete integrated notebook tasks.
  • Complete guided practice and the Lesson Quiz.
  • Submit a checkpoint with a written response and oral explanation script.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can accurately restate a complex source idea, use precise academic vocabulary, and explain evidence clearly in writing and speech.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help: If your response includes evidence but does not explain its function, ask for feedback before submitting.
