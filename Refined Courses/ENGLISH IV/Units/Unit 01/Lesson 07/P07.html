Checkpoint Task

Use this embedded passage: Digital tools can expand learning when students use them with purpose. A note-taking application can help a student organize source ideas, and a shared document can make peer feedback more efficient. However, technology does not automatically improve learning. If students copy information without paraphrasing, accept suggestions without evaluating them, or use digital tools to avoid careful thinking, the tool weakens rather than strengthens academic work. Responsible technology use requires students to preserve source meaning, evaluate support, and communicate conclusions clearly.

Submit: one integrated paragraph and one brief oral script. Include a claim, accurate paraphrase, relevant evidence, academic vocabulary, and clear conventions.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim preserves the source's qualification.
  • The paraphrase is accurate and connected to relevant evidence.
  • Academic vocabulary is precise.
  • The oral script is coherent and easy to follow.
  • Conventions support clarity and credibility.
