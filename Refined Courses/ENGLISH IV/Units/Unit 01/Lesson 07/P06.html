Passage: Responsible Technology Use

Digital tools can expand learning when students use them with purpose. A note-taking application can help a student organize source ideas, and a shared document can make peer feedback more efficient. However, technology does not automatically improve learning. If students copy information without paraphrasing, accept suggestions without evaluating them, or use digital tools to avoid careful thinking, the tool weakens rather than strengthens academic work. Responsible technology use requires students to preserve source meaning, evaluate support, and communicate conclusions clearly.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Integrate paraphrase, evidence, vocabulary, conventions, and oral organization.

Part A

Write one claim that preserves the passage's qualification about technology.

Part B

Paraphrase one relevant piece of evidence and explain its rationale.

Part C

Write a brief oral explanation script using at least three academic vocabulary words accurately.
