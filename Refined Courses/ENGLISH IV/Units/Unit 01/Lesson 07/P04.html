Embedded Passage

Passage: Responsible Technology Use

Digital tools can expand learning when students use them with purpose. A note-taking application can help a student organize source ideas, and a shared document can make peer feedback more efficient. However, technology does not automatically improve learning. If students copy information without paraphrasing, accept suggestions without evaluating them, or use digital tools to avoid careful thinking, the tool weakens rather than strengthens academic work. Responsible technology use requires students to preserve source meaning, evaluate support, and communicate conclusions clearly.

Example 1: Claim

Claim: The passage presents technology as useful only when students use it responsibly.

Modeled thinking: This claim keeps the qualification "only when" and avoids saying technology is always good or always harmful.

Reasoning: A Grade 12 claim should preserve the source's limits.

Example 2: Evidence and Rationale

Paraphrased evidence: The passage warns that copying information without paraphrasing or accepting suggestions without evaluation can weaken academic work.

Rationale: This evidence supports the claim because it shows that technology can become harmful when students stop thinking critically.

Reasoning: The evidence is paraphrased, relevant, and explained.

Example 3: Oral Version

Oral script: The passage does not argue against technology. Instead, it argues that digital tools help only when students use them responsibly. For example, copying without paraphrasing weakens academic work because the student avoids careful thinking.

Reasoning: The oral version is coherent because it moves from claim to evidence to explanation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example: The passage says technology weakens learning.

Correct example: The passage says technology weakens learning when students use it to avoid paraphrasing, evaluation, and careful thinking.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect example removes the condition. The correct example preserves the passage's full meaning.
