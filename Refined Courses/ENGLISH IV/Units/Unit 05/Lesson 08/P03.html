P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Learning: Integrated Revision Decisions

In final writing, skills rarely appear alone. A single revision may involve conventions, word meaning, style, and organization at the same time. The best revision is the one that improves meaning without adding unsupported ideas.

Embedded Writing Sample

Draft: The policy is not final and it is provisional however people are acting like it is permanent.

Revision: The policy is provisional, not permanent; however, many people are responding as if it will remain in place.

Step-by-step explanation: The revision uses the precise meaning of provisional, fixes the sentence structure, and uses however correctly to show contrast.

Notebook Application

  1. Explain the meaning of provisional in the sample.
  2. Identify the contrast in the revision.
  3. Explain how punctuation improves clarity.
  4. Write one final sentence explaining why the revision is stronger.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The policy is provisional, so it is weak and unimportant.

Correct: The policy is provisional, so it applies for now but may change later.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect response adds a judgment that the word does not support. The correct response preserves the precise meaning.
