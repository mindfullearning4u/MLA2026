ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 08

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Convention and Meaning

Draft: The evidence is current it strengthens the claim.

Revision: The evidence is current, so it strengthens the claim.

Reasoning: The revision fixes the sentence boundary and clarifies the cause-effect relationship.

Example 2: Word Meaning and Precision

Draft: The schedule is provisional, which means it is final.

Revision: The schedule is provisional, which means it is temporary and may change.

Reasoning: The revision uses the accurate meaning of the word.

Example 3: Revision and Polish

Draft: The author has good points and says stuff about costs.

Revision: The author presents a credible argument by connecting cost data to the proposal's long-term financial impact.

Reasoning: The revision improves style, precision, and purpose.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A final revision only checks spelling.

Correct: A final revision checks spelling, conventions, meaning, organization, vocabulary, style, and purpose.

Teachable explanation: Unit 5 mastery requires integrated revision, not isolated proofreading.
