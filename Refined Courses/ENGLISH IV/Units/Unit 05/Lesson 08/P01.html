ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

This lesson brings together Unit 5 language, vocabulary, style, and revision skills. You will review how conventions, word meaning, etymology, precision, and revision work together in polished Grade 12 writing.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Use Standard English Conventions
  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.02 - Analyze Etymology, Derivations, and Foreign Phrases
  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning
  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.W.04 - Develop and Strengthen Writing
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Accurately

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 5 skills work together in final writing.
  • How vocabulary precision supports style and clarity.
  • How conventions, organization, and revision improve reader understanding.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 5 Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review major Unit 5 language and vocabulary strategies.
  • Practice integrated revision decisions.
  • Complete Guided Practice and Independent Work.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence and complete the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use conventions to clarify meaning.
  • Determine word meaning from context, word parts, and reference information.
  • Revise writing for purpose, organization, style, and precision.
  • Complete the Unit 5 Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are showing that you can make final writing decisions independently: choosing precise words, correcting language, interpreting meaning, and revising for a real reader.
