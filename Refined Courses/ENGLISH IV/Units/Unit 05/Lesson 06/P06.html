ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Focus on paragraph structure, organization, transitions, and precise language.

Part A: Sentence Order

Sentences: A. This evidence explains why the policy may improve attendance. B. The district reported that transportation problems caused many absences. C. Reliable transportation is essential to school attendance.

Put the sentences in the strongest order and explain why.

Part B: Transition Choice

Draft: The plan reduces short-term costs. ____ it may create long-term maintenance problems.

Choose a transition that shows contrast and explain your choice.

Part C: Structural Revision

Write a three-sentence paragraph that moves from claim, to evidence, to explanation. Label each sentence by its function.
