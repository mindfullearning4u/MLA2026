P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Antonym Clue

Sentence: The policy was not permanent; it was an interim solution until the board completed its review.

Modeled thinking: The phrase not permanent gives an antonym clue. The phrase until the board completed its review confirms that interim means temporary or for the meantime.

Example 2: Word Family

Sentence: The committee wanted a comprehensive review, so it examined finances, staffing, public response, and legal impact.

Modeled thinking: The listed categories show that the review covers many parts. Related words such as comprehend and comprehensive connect to taking in or including a full range. The precise meaning is complete or broad in scope.

Example 3: Reference Entry

Sentence: The scientist used a control to compare the effect of the treatment.

Reference-style entry: control: power over something; a standard used for comparison in an experiment; a device that operates equipment.

Modeled thinking: The science context and the phrase compare the effect show that control means a standard used for comparison, not power or a device.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Control means power because that is the most familiar definition.

Correct: In this science context, control means a standard used for comparison in an experiment.

Teachable explanation: Familiarity can mislead readers. Use the subject, surrounding words, and reference entry to select the definition that fits the sentence.
