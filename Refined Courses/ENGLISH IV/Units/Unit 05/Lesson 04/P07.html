P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can find a definition but cannot decide which meaning fits the context.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook responses from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Choose one word from the lesson.
  3. Explain how context, word relationship, reference information, or derivation helped determine meaning.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one paragraph that explains a word's precise meaning. Include the sentence, the meaning you selected, the clue or reference evidence you used, and a paraphrase that preserves the meaning.

Mastery Criteria

  • Chooses the meaning that fits the context.
  • Identifies the clue, relationship, reference information, or derivation used.
  • Explains meaning without adding unsupported judgment.
  • Paraphrases accurately and clearly.
