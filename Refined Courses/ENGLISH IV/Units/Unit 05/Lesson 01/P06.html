ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C. Focus on preserving meaning while improving sentence control.

Embedded Source Note

A college writing center report found that students' drafts improved most when revision focused on one clear purpose at a time: clarifying the claim, strengthening evidence, or correcting sentence-level confusion.

Part A

Write one accurate paraphrase of the source note using standard conventions.

Part B

Write one sentence that uses a colon to introduce the three revision purposes named in the source note.

Part C

Revise one sentence from your own writing or create one new sentence that correctly uses a semicolon between two complete related ideas.
