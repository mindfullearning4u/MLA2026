ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Standard English Conventions in Advanced Writing

This lesson teaches you how conventions support clarity, credibility, and control in Grade 12 academic and professional writing.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Use Standard English Conventions
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Accurately
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.04 - Develop and Strengthen Writing

What You Will Learn

  • How conventions shape meaning in advanced writing.
  • How sentence boundaries, punctuation, and pronoun clarity affect reader understanding.
  • How to revise convention errors without changing source meaning.
  • How academic vocabulary and standard English support credibility.

What You Will Do

  • Study convention choices in academic sentences.
  • Revise unclear or incorrect sentences.
  • Practice paraphrasing with correct conventions.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, a checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify and correct sentence-boundary errors.
  • Use punctuation to clarify relationships between ideas.
  • Revise sentences for precise, conventional academic style.
  • Preserve meaning while improving clarity.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning that conventions are not just error rules. They help readers follow complex thinking, trust your message, and understand exactly what you mean.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you are unsure whether a sentence is unclear because of punctuation, word choice, or sentence structure.
