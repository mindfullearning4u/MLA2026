ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Etymology, Derivations, and Foreign Phrases

This lesson teaches you how word history, word parts, and common foreign phrases can help you read and write independently.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.02 - Analyze Etymology, Derivations, and Foreign Phrases
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Accurately

What You Will Learn

  • How etymology can clarify meaning.
  • How derivations and word families support vocabulary growth.
  • How common foreign phrases function in academic and professional writing.
  • How to paraphrase sentences containing unfamiliar or borrowed terms.

What You Will Do

  • Break words into meaningful parts.
  • Use word history to infer meaning.
  • Interpret common foreign phrases in context.
  • Apply vocabulary knowledge to paraphrase accurately.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Infer meaning from roots, prefixes, suffixes, and word families.
  • Explain how foreign phrases function in a sentence.
  • Choose accurate paraphrases for vocabulary-rich sentences.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to use word clues like a reader in college or the workplace. When a word is unfamiliar, you can use its parts, origin, context, and related words to make an informed decision.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if a word part suggests one meaning but the sentence context suggests another.
