ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 02

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C. Use word parts and context together.

Embedded Sentence Set

Sentence 1: The ad hoc committee met for two weeks and then dissolved after solving the issue.

Sentence 2: The speaker's credibility increased when she cited current evidence and acknowledged limitations.

Sentence 3: The new policy did not preserve the status quo; it changed the process students had followed for years.

Part A

Choose one foreign phrase from the sentence set. Write its meaning and explain which context clue confirms the meaning.

Part B

Choose one word with a root or word family clue. Write the clue, the meaning, and one related word.

Part C

Paraphrase one sentence from the set accurately. Preserve the meaning of the vocabulary word or foreign phrase.
