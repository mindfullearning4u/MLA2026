P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Learning: Context Controls Meaning

Context clues are not just hints for unknown words. They help you decide which meaning of a familiar word is being used. Many English words have several possible meanings. The correct meaning depends on the sentence, paragraph, topic, and tone.

Use this sequence: identify the word, read around it, locate clue words, decide the likely meaning, then explain how the meaning affects tone or interpretation.

Embedded Passage

Passage: The speaker praised the new policy as a bridge between private innovation and public responsibility. Her opponent dismissed the same policy as a mask for corporate influence.

Step-by-step thinking: First, both words are figurative. The policy is not literally a bridge or a mask. Second, bridge suggests connection and cooperation. Third, mask suggests hiding the truth. The contrast reveals two different tones: one hopeful and one suspicious.

Notebook Application

In your notebook, answer each question in complete sentences:

  1. What does bridge suggest about the policy?
  2. What does mask suggest about the policy?
  3. Which word creates a more suspicious tone? Explain why.
  4. Write one sentence explaining how figurative language changes the reader's interpretation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Bridge means a structure over water, so the policy probably has something to do with transportation.

Correct: In this context, bridge is figurative. It suggests the policy connects two groups or ideas that might otherwise remain separate.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect response uses only the literal denotation and ignores context. The correct response notices that the sentence is about policy, innovation, and responsibility, so the word functions figuratively.
