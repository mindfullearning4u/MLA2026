ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences. Include the passage wording in your answers so your reasoning is clear.

Part A: Denotation and Connotation

Passage: The report described the plan as flexible; the critic described it as unstable.

Explain the denotation the words share and the different connotations they create.

Part B: Tone

Passage: The speaker called the proposal a lifeline for small businesses.

Explain how the figurative word lifeline shapes tone and audience response.

Part C: Evaluation

Choose one word from Part A or Part B and evaluate whether it is effective. Your answer must explain the word's meaning, connotation, tone, and effect on the reader.
