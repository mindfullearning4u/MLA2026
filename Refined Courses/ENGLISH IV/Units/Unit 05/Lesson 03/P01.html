P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Context, Connotation, Denotation, and Tone

This lesson teaches you how word meaning changes when context, connotation, denotation, figurative language, and tone work together.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.09 - Evaluate Figurative Language
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.12 - Evaluate Rhetorical Choices
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How to separate denotation from connotation.
  • How context clues control the meaning of advanced words and phrases.
  • How word choice contributes to tone in literary and rhetorical passages.
  • How figurative language can create meaning beyond the literal definition.

What You Will Do

  • Read short literary and rhetorical passages.
  • Use context clues to determine meaning.
  • Compare literal meaning with emotional or cultural associations.
  • Explain how word choice affects tone and effectiveness.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Determine precise word meanings from context.
  • Distinguish denotation from connotation.
  • Explain how word choice shapes tone.
  • Evaluate why a writer's language choice is effective.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to read words as deliberate choices. At Grade 12, it is not enough to know what a word means. You must explain what the word does in the sentence, passage, argument, or literary moment.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify a word's dictionary meaning but cannot explain how the word affects tone, emotion, or rhetorical impact.
