ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Each part asks you to revise a sentence or short paragraph based on feedback. Write the original problem, the revision, and one sentence explaining why your revision improves the writing.

Part A: Clarify Purpose

Original: The essay is about leadership and it says useful things.

Feedback: Clarify the specific purpose of the sentence.

Revise the sentence so it identifies what the essay argues or analyzes.

Part B: Improve Clarity

Original: The data matters because it is connected to the point in a clear way.

Feedback: Explain the connection between data and point.

Revise the sentence so the relationship is specific.

Part C: Convention and Meaning

Original: The author uses examples they make the argument more persuasive.

Feedback: Correct the sentence boundary and clarify the relationship.

Revise the sentence so it is grammatically correct and clear.
