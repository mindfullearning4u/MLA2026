P05 Guided Practice

Guided Practice Instructions

Complete the Guided Practice questions in Moodle. Each question asks you to interpret feedback or choose the revision that best improves purpose, clarity, reasoning, or conventions.

Choose the answer that solves the actual writing problem, not the answer that only sounds more formal.

Before You Begin

Read the feedback first. Then ask what the revision must accomplish: clarify the claim, strengthen evidence explanation, improve analysis, or correct a convention error.
