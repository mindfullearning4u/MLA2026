Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one revised sentence or paragraph from P06.
  2. Check that it responds directly to the feedback.
  3. Explain the original problem and your revision choice.
  4. Submit the revised writing and explanation in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one original sentence or short paragraph, one revised version, and a brief explanation of how your revision improves purpose, clarity, reasoning, or conventions.

Mastery Criteria

  • Revision responds to the feedback.
  • Purpose or analysis is clearer.
  • Reasoning is more specific.
  • Conventions support meaning.
