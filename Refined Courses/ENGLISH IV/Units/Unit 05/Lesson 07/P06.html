ENGLISH IV | Unit 05 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Focus on final polish: style, precision, vocabulary, conventions, and readiness for a reader.

Part A: Style

Draft: The writer kind of shows that the idea works.

Revise for a formal academic register.

Part B: Precision

Draft: The report gives information about money.

Revise so the sentence identifies the exact kind of information and its purpose.

Part C: Publication Polish

Choose one sentence from your own recent writing or from this lesson. Revise it for final submission and explain what you changed.
