Example 3: Convention Polish

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: combine the lesson skill with evidence or revision choices. Step 2: check that the explanation stays focused on the assigned standard. Step 3: confirm that the final response is clear, accurate, and written in standard English.

Draft: The evidence is relevant it directly addresses the claim.

Revision: The evidence is relevant because it directly addresses the claim.

Reasoning: The revision corrects the sentence boundary and clarifies the relationship.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Make the sentence sound fancier by adding bigger words.

Correct: Make the sentence more precise by choosing words that match the exact meaning and audience.

Teachable explanation: Polish is not decoration. It is purposeful control of clarity, style, conventions, and meaning.
