P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use this embedded passage: When the council gathered to vote on closing the old reading room, Elias sat in the back beside the empty chair his mother had used every Thursday. The room smelled of paper, dust, and raincoats. On the wall, a cracked photograph showed children holding library cards like small flags. Several council members argued that the building was inefficient and that a digital archive would serve more people. Elias agreed that the town needed better access, yet he kept looking at the empty chair. When his turn came to speak, he did not ask the council to preserve every shelf. Instead, he asked them to carry the reading room's promise into whatever came next: a place where no child had to enter knowledge alone.

Part A

Identify two themes in the passage. Write each theme as a full thematic statement, not as a single topic.

Part B

Find one piece of evidence for each theme. Paraphrase each piece of evidence accurately.

Part C

Write one paragraph evaluating how the two themes interact by the end of the passage.
