P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Two or More Themes and Their Development

This lesson teaches you how to analyze more than one theme in a literary text and evaluate how those themes develop together.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.02 - Evaluate Development of Multiple Themes
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.01 - Evaluate Literary Elements and Functional Significance
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify two or more themes in the same text.
  • How to trace how themes develop from beginning to end.
  • How literary elements help themes grow or become more complex.
  • How to evaluate the relationship between themes.

What You Will Do

  • Read an original literary passage.
  • Identify two themes.
  • Track how details develop each theme.
  • Write an analytical response explaining how the themes interact.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify multiple themes accurately.
  • Use evidence to show development.
  • Explain how literary elements support theme development.
  • Evaluate how the themes relate to each other.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to explain how a story can develop more than one big idea at the same time. At Grade 12, you should be able to show how themes build, shift, complicate each other, or work together.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can name themes but need help proving how they develop across the passage.
