ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Classic Literature Influence on Contemporary World Texts

This lesson teaches you how to analyze how a classic literary pattern can influence a contemporary world text.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.11 - Analyze Classic Influence on Contemporary World Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.01 - Evaluate Literary Elements and Functional Significance
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How classic literature can influence later world texts.
  • How to identify adapted patterns such as journeys, tests, homecoming, exile, and recognition.
  • How to compare influence without claiming two texts are identical.
  • How to paraphrase the influence accurately.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded classic context from The Odyssey.
  • Read an original contemporary world-text passage.
  • Compare shared literary patterns.
  • Analyze how the contemporary text transforms the classic influence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify a classic pattern.
  • Explain how a contemporary text echoes and changes that pattern.
  • Use accurate paraphrase and academic vocabulary.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to see how old stories keep shaping new ones. The goal is to explain influence: what a newer text borrows, changes, questions, or reimagines from a classic source.
