P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Embedded Passage

By morning, the city wore a coat of broken branches. Rainwater whispered along the curb, carrying leaves like small, exhausted boats. In the park, the oldest oak lifted one split limb toward the pale sky, not in defeat but in accusation. People stepped around the puddles quietly, as if the storm had taken the street's voice and left everyone listening for what it would say next.

Deeper Learning

Evaluation asks whether the figurative language works and why. The phrase "not in defeat but in accusation" turns the damaged oak into a witness. That personification shifts the passage from simple storm description to a moral or emotional response to damage.

Use this frame: The phrase ___ is effective because it makes the reader see ___ as ___, which deepens ___.

Notebook Application

Choose one figurative phrase from the passage. Write one sentence explaining the literal meaning, one sentence explaining the figurative meaning, and one sentence evaluating the effect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Rainwater whispered" means the water was talking.

Correct: "Rainwater whispered" personifies the water to make the post-storm scene feel quiet, tense, and attentive.

Why this matters: The incorrect response treats figurative language literally. The correct response explains meaning and effect.
