P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Author Use of Figurative Language

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate an author's use of figurative language by explaining how it affects meaning, tone, and interpretation.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.09 - Evaluate Figurative Language
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.04 - Evaluate Major Poets in Historical Context
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify figurative language such as metaphor, personification, and imagery.
  • How to determine connotative meaning from context.
  • How to evaluate why a figurative choice is effective.
  • How figurative language can deepen theme, tone, and historical interpretation.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded poem-like passage.
  • Identify figurative language.
  • Explain literal and figurative meaning.
  • Evaluate how the language shapes interpretation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain what a figurative phrase means in context.
  • Evaluate why the author's figurative choice is effective.
  • Use precise academic vocabulary.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to explain why figurative language matters. A strong response does not stop at naming a metaphor. It explains how the metaphor changes what the reader understands or feels.
