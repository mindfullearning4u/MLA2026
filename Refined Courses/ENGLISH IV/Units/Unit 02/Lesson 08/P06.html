ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C to prepare for the Unit Assessment. Use your Unit 2 notebook evidence.

Part A: Literary Evaluation Review

Select one Unit 2 passage from your notes. Write three sentences explaining one literary element and its functional significance.

Part B: Theme and Perspective Review

Write one paragraph explaining how a character's perspective can help develop a universal theme. Use a specific example from your Unit 2 work.

Part C: Narrative Craft Review

Write a brief plan for a narrative scene that includes a conflict, two perspectives, and a universal theme. Revise one sentence for clarity or conventions.
