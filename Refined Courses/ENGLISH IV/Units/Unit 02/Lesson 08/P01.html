ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 2 Synthesis

This lesson brings together Unit 2 literary evaluation and narrative-writing skills. Lesson 8 has no Lesson Quiz. It contains the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered

  • Unit 2 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG4.R.01, MLA.ENG4.R.02, MLA.ENG4.R.03, MLA.ENG4.R.04, MLA.ENG4.R.09, MLA.ENG4.R.11, MLA.ENG4.W.01
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Use Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 2 literary skills work together.
  • How theme, perspective, figurative language, historical context, and classic influence support evaluation.
  • How narrative writing can apply literary craft intentionally.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment without learning a new primary standard.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 2 concepts through guided synthesis.
  • Analyze embedded literary passages.
  • Connect literary evaluation to narrative craft.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Evaluate literary elements and functional significance.
  • Analyze multiple themes, perspective, poetry context, figurative language, and classic influence.
  • Apply narrative craft concepts to perspective and universal theme.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment instead of a Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are showing that you can read literature like a senior: not just identifying features, but explaining how those features function and why they matter. You are also showing that you can use literary craft intentionally in narrative writing.
