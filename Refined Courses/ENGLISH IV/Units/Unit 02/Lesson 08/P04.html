P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Functional Significance

Question: What is the functional significance of Oren folding the letter repeatedly?

Step 1: Identify the action: Oren folds the letter more than once.

Step 2: Connect the action to emotion: repetition suggests hesitation and unresolved feeling.

Step 3: Explain function: the action reveals that Oren cannot easily express what happened to him.

Modeled answer: Folding the letter repeatedly shows Oren's hesitation and emotional conflict, emphasizing that his experience cannot be reduced to a simple statement of bravery.

Example 2: Theme Development

Question: How does the passage develop a theme about courage?

Step 1: Find repeated ideas: brave, survived, courage.

Step 2: Notice change: Oren rejects simple words and chooses a more complex statement.

Step 3: State the theme: courage may involve lasting cost and self-understanding.

Modeled answer: The passage develops the theme that courage is not only a heroic label; it can carry consequences that a person continues to understand after the public celebration ends.

Example 3: Narrative Craft Application

Question: How could this passage guide a complex narrative?

Step 1: Identify the character's perspective: Oren is conflicted about how to name his experience.

Step 2: Identify a concrete action: crossing out sentences.

Step 3: Identify a universal theme: people may need time to understand the cost of courage.

Modeled thinking: A writer could use repeated revision inside the story to show a character's changing self-understanding instead of explaining the theme directly.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The passage is about courage, so the answer is courage.

Correct: The passage evaluates courage as complicated because Oren's crossed-out statements show that public victory does not erase private cost.

Teachable explanation: A Grade 12 answer must move beyond topic identification. It should explain how the text develops, complicates, or evaluates the idea.
