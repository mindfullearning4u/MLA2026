Vocabulary

Passage / Scenario

A poem and a short narrative both present a speaker returning to a familiar place after many years. The poem uses images of weathered stone and evening light, while the narrative shows two characters remembering the same place differently. Together, the texts suggest that memory can preserve a place while also changing how people understand it.

Integrated Synthesis Task

Step 1: Read the scenario carefully. Step 2: identify the central claim, theme, issue, or task. Step 3: mark the details that matter most. Step 4: explain how those details connect to the unit skills from Lessons 1-7.

In your notebook, write a short planning note that connects at least three unit skills instead of treating them separately.
