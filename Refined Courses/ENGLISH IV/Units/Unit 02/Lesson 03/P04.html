ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P04 Worked Example

Embedded Passage

Nadia pressed her palm against the old orchard gate while her brother Tomas unfolded the developer's letter. The trees behind them were bent and hollow in places, but in spring they still filled the road with white blossoms. "The offer pays every debt," Tomas said. "It gives us a future." Nadia looked past him to the rows their grandmother had planted after arriving with two suitcases and no English. "It gives us money," she answered. "That is not the same thing." Tomas folded the letter carefully, as if neatness could make the decision merciful. "Grandmother wanted us safe," he said. Nadia opened the gate and stepped inside. "She also wanted us rooted."

Example 1: Tomas's Perspective

Step 1: Identify evidence: Tomas says the offer pays every debt and gives them a future.

Step 2: Interpret the evidence: Tomas connects the sale to financial survival.

Step 3: Evaluate significance: His perspective makes the conflict practical, not just emotional.

Example 2: Nadia's Perspective

Step 1: Identify evidence: Nadia looks to the rows their grandmother planted.

Step 2: Interpret the evidence: Nadia sees the orchard as family history.

Step 3: Evaluate significance: Her perspective makes the orchard a source of identity, not just property.

Example 3: Conflicting Perspectives

Step 1: Compare the views: Tomas values safety; Nadia values rootedness.

Step 2: Notice the shared source: both characters refer to their grandmother.

Step 3: Evaluate the conflict: the disagreement is powerful because both characters use family memory to defend different futures.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The conflict is about whether Tomas likes trees.

Correct: The conflict is about how the siblings interpret responsibility: Tomas sees responsibility as financial safety, while Nadia sees it as preserving family roots.

Feedback: The incorrect response stays at a surface detail. The correct response explains the deeper conflict between perspectives.
