Use this embedded passage: Nadia pressed her palm against the old orchard gate while her brother Tomas unfolded the developer's letter. The trees behind them were bent and hollow in places, but in spring they still filled the road with white blossoms. "The offer pays every debt," Tomas said. "It gives us a future." Nadia looked past him to the rows their grandmother had planted after arriving with two suitcases and no English. "It gives us money," she answered. "That is not the same thing." Tomas folded the letter carefully, as if neatness could make the decision merciful. "Grandmother wanted us safe," he said. Nadia opened the gate and stepped inside. "She also wanted us rooted."
