ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Character Perspective and Conflicting Perspectives

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate how a character's perspective develops and how conflicting perspectives create complexity in a literary text.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.03 - Evaluate Character Perspective
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify a character's perspective.
  • How perspective develops through dialogue, action, and reflection.
  • How conflicting perspectives reveal tension and complexity.
  • How to evaluate why a perspective matters to the meaning of the passage.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded literary passage.
  • Compare two characters' perspectives.
  • Track how one perspective develops.
  • Explain how conflict between perspectives affects interpretation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify and paraphrase evidence of character perspective.
  • Explain how perspective develops.
  • Evaluate the effect of conflicting perspectives.
  • Use precise vocabulary and clear oral or written explanation.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to explain how characters see a situation differently and why those differences matter. Strong Grade 12 analysis evaluates how perspective shapes conflict, theme, and reader understanding.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can tell what a character believes but need help explaining how that belief changes or conflicts with another character's view.
