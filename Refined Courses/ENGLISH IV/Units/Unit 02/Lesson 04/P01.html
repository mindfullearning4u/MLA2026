P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Major Poets in Historical Context

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate a major poet's work by connecting poetic choices to historical and literary context.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.04 - Evaluate Major Poets in Historical Context
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.02 - Evaluate Development of Multiple Themes
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How historical context can shape interpretation of poetry.
  • How a major poet's style can reflect or challenge literary expectations.
  • How to evaluate imagery, metaphor, and theme in context.
  • How to explain poetry using precise academic vocabulary.

What You Will Do

  • Read an Emily Dickinson excerpt with context.
  • Analyze metaphor and theme.
  • Connect poetic choices to 19th-century literary context.
  • Evaluate how context deepens interpretation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain how historical context affects interpretation.
  • Analyze poetic language and theme.
  • Evaluate why a major poet's choices are significant.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to read poetry as both art and historical expression. The goal is not to memorize a poet's biography. The goal is to use context to make a stronger interpretation of the poem.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can understand a poem's basic image but need help explaining how historical context changes or deepens the interpretation.
