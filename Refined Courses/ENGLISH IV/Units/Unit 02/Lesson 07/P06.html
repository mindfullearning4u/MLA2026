ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. You will use this work for the checkpoint and to prepare for the Lesson Quiz.

Part A: Narrative Planning

Create a brief plan for a complex narrative. Include a central situation, two perspectives, one conflict, and one universal theme.

Part B: Draft a Scene

Write one scene of 8-12 sentences. The scene should reveal at least one character's perspective through action, dialogue, or internal reflection. Do not simply state the theme.

Part C: Revise and Explain

Revise two sentences for clarity, detail, or conventions. Then write a short explanation of how your revision helps the narrative develop perspective or theme.
