Instructions

Use this embedded passage: Mara returned to the harbor after sunset, carrying the lantern her grandfather had left on the kitchen table. The town had installed electric lights along the pier, but she still lit the old lantern before walking to the water. Its flame shook in the wind, small but stubborn, while the new lamps shone with a clean, steady glare. At the edge of the dock, Mara held the lantern toward the dark bay. She knew no ship needed its light anymore. Still, the glow warmed her hands, and for the first time that week, the silence beside her felt less like absence and more like a place where memory could stand.

Part A

Identify two literary elements in the passage. For each one, quote or paraphrase the moment where it appears.

Part B

Explain the functional significance of one element. Use the frame: This element functions to ___ because ___.

Part C

Write a short analytical paragraph explaining how the author uses the chosen element to add a layer of meaning or style. Include evidence and academic vocabulary.
