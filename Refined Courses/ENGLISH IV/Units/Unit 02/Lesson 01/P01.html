ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Literary Elements and Functional Significance

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate how key literary elements add layers of meaning or style and how to explain what those elements do in the text.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.01 - Evaluate Literary Elements and Functional Significance
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify a literary element such as setting, symbol, imagery, conflict, structure, or tone.
  • How to move beyond identification into evaluation.
  • How to explain functional significance: what the element does for meaning, style, or interpretation.
  • How to use accurate paraphrase and academic vocabulary when writing about literature.

What You Will Do

  • Read an original literary passage.
  • Identify key literary elements.
  • Evaluate how those elements add layers of meaning or style.
  • Write short analytical responses that explain function, not just meaning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain how a literary element affects interpretation.
  • Use evidence from the passage.
  • Use academic vocabulary accurately.
  • Submit checkpoint work that evaluates functional significance.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to explain how an author's choices work. A strong Grade 12 response does not stop at "the lantern is a symbol." It explains what the symbol adds, how it shapes the reader's understanding, and why it matters to the whole passage.
