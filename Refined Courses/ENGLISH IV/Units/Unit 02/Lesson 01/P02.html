P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Evaluating What Literary Elements Do

Vocabulary

  • Literary element: a feature of a literary text, such as setting, symbol, imagery, conflict, characterization, structure, or tone.
  • Functional significance: the purpose or effect of an element in the text.
  • Layer of meaning: an added level of interpretation beyond the literal events.
  • Evaluate: judge how effectively something works and explain why.

Embedded Passage: "The Lantern Keeper"

Mara returned to the harbor after sunset, carrying the lantern her grandfather had left on the kitchen table. The town had installed electric lights along the pier, but she still lit the old lantern before walking to the water. Its flame shook in the wind, small but stubborn, while the new lamps shone with a clean, steady glare. At the edge of the dock, Mara held the lantern toward the dark bay. She knew no ship needed its light anymore. Still, the glow warmed her hands, and for the first time that week, the silence beside her felt less like absence and more like a place where memory could stand.

Detailed Teaching: Step by Step

Step 1: Identify the element. In this passage, the lantern is a key literary element. It is an object in the story, but it also carries symbolic meaning.

Step 2: Identify the literal role. Literally, Mara carries and lights an old lantern at the harbor.

Step 3: Ask what changes because the element is present. The lantern changes the mood of the passage. Without it, Mara would only be standing by water after a loss. With it, the scene becomes about memory, tradition, and emotional connection.

Step 4: Explain functional significance. The lantern functions as a symbol of connection to Mara's grandfather. It also contrasts with the electric lights, which are useful but emotionally cold. This contrast adds a layer of meaning: progress may replace an object's practical purpose, but it cannot replace its emotional significance.

Notebook Directions

In your notebook, create a two-column chart. Label the left column Literary Element and the right column What It Does. Add entries for the lantern, the electric lights, the harbor setting, and the silence.

Model Example

Element: lantern

What it does: The lantern symbolizes Mara's connection to her grandfather. Its light no longer has a practical purpose for ships, but it helps Mara transform silence into memory. This makes the passage about grief and remembrance, not just a walk to the harbor.
