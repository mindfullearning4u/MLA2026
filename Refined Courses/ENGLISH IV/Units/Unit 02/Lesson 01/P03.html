ENGLISH IV | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Embedded Passage

Mara returned to the harbor after sunset, carrying the lantern her grandfather had left on the kitchen table. The town had installed electric lights along the pier, but she still lit the old lantern before walking to the water. Its flame shook in the wind, small but stubborn, while the new lamps shone with a clean, steady glare. At the edge of the dock, Mara held the lantern toward the dark bay. She knew no ship needed its light anymore. Still, the glow warmed her hands, and for the first time that week, the silence beside her felt less like absence and more like a place where memory could stand.

Deeper Learning

Grade 12 analysis requires you to explain relationships among elements. The lantern, electric lights, and silence work together. The electric lights show that the lantern is outdated in a practical sense. The silence shows Mara's grief. The lantern connects those ideas by giving Mara a way to carry memory into the present.

When you evaluate functional significance, use this sentence frame: The element functions to ___ because ___.

Example: The contrast between the lantern and electric lights functions to show that emotional value can remain even after practical value is gone.

Notebook Application

Write three sentences. Sentence 1 should identify a literary element. Sentence 2 should paraphrase the relevant moment from the passage. Sentence 3 should explain the element's functional significance.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The lantern is a symbol. It means memory.

Correct: The lantern symbolizes memory, but its function is more specific: it helps Mara experience silence as a place where her grandfather's memory can remain present.

Why this matters: The incorrect response identifies meaning but does not evaluate function. The correct response explains how the symbol affects interpretation.
