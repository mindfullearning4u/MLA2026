# ENGLISH IV - Unit 02 Overview: Literature Across Time and Perspectives

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops advanced literary analysis across texts from different periods and perspectives. Students analyze two or more themes, evaluate character perspective, evaluate works of major poets in historical context, evaluate figurative language, analyze the influence of classic literature on contemporary world texts, and write complex narratives that establish multiple perspectives and convey universal themes.

Emphasis is placed on thematic development, perspective, conflicting perspectives, tone, meaning, poetry in context, literary influence, and evidence-based analysis. Students synthesize ideas across texts to explain how literature communicates complex meanings.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 12 Standards with an emphasis on evaluation, synthesis, argumentation, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through literary analysis, narrative writing, vocabulary application, and evidence-based explanation.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- analysis and evaluation of multiple themes;
- character perspective and conflicting perspectives;
- major poets in historical context;
- figurative language;
- classic literature and contemporary world texts;
- complex narrative writing with multiple perspectives and universal themes.

Assessment items require students to analyze two or more themes, evaluate character perspective, evaluate works of major poets in historical context, evaluate figurative language, analyze the influence of classic literature on contemporary world texts, and write complex narratives that establish multiple perspectives and convey universal themes.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit strengthens students’ ability to interpret literature across texts, periods, and perspectives. Students connect author choices, historical context, character perspective, figurative language, literary influence, and theme to explain how literature communicates complex meanings.
