P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Planning a Multimedia Text for Publication

Vocabulary

  • Multimedia text: a communication product that combines modes such as written text, visuals, audio, video, or interactive features.
  • Publication format: the digital form used to share a text with an audience.
  • Source credit: information that gives proper acknowledgment for borrowed evidence, images, audio, or ideas.
  • Audience access: how easily the intended audience can use and understand the product.
  • Ethical sharing: publishing in a way that is accurate, credited, respectful, and appropriate.

Embedded Scenario

Embedded Multimedia Publication Scenario: A senior project team is preparing to publish a multimedia text about youth financial literacy. The final product will include a short written introduction, an infographic showing common budgeting mistakes, a two-minute narration, source credits, and a shareable digital format for classmates and community partners. The team must decide how to combine words, visuals, audio, evidence, and publication choices so the message is accurate, accessible, ethical, and useful for the intended audience.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the purpose. The team wants to help viewers understand youth financial literacy.

Step 2: Match each mode to a function. The introduction sets context, the infographic shows budgeting mistakes, and narration explains the meaning.

Step 3: Check evidence and credits. A published product must make source use visible and ethical.

Step 4: Revise for audience access. The product should be readable, listenable, and easy to navigate.

Step 5: Choose a shareable format that fits classmates and community partners.

Notebook Directions

Create a multimedia planning chart with four columns: element, purpose, evidence or content, and audience benefit.

Model Example

Element: Infographic.

Purpose: Show common budgeting mistakes quickly.

Audience benefit: Viewers can recognize patterns without reading a long paragraph.
