Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you decide whether your multimedia product is publication-ready. Ask for help if you are unsure whether every element supports the message.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your multimedia planning chart.
  2. Check for accuracy, credits, access, and audience fit.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in the LMS.
  4. Use ToR feedback to revise your publication plan.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a multimedia publication plan. Include the purpose, intended audience, media elements, source-credit plan, access checks, and sharing format.

Mastery Criteria

  • Each media element has a clear purpose.
  • The plan explains how modes work together.
  • Source credits and ethical sharing are addressed.
  • The sharing format fits the audience.
  • The response shows publication-ready Grade 12 communication.
