ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 04

P04 Worked Example

Embedded Scenario

Embedded Design Scenario: A student team is preparing a digital presentation about community access to career certification programs. Their first draft contains twelve text-heavy slides, three unrelated photos, a chart without a title, and bullet points copied directly from a source. Their revised goal is to create a presentation that helps an audience understand the problem, compare two solutions, and remember the strongest evidence. The team must decide which visuals, text, layout choices, and speaker notes will make the presentation clear, credible, and effective.

Example 1: Revise Slide Focus

Problem: A slide includes six copied sentences from a source.

Modeled thinking: The audience cannot listen and read a paragraph at the same time. I need to keep only the key idea on the slide and explain the rest orally.

Revision: Use a short slide title, three paraphrased bullets, and speaker notes that explain the source with attribution.

Example 2: Choose an Effective Visual

Problem: The team includes a photo of students smiling, but the slide is about access barriers.

Modeled thinking: The image may look positive, but it does not explain barriers. A chart or diagram would do more instructional work.

Revision: Use a labeled chart comparing transportation, cost, and scheduling barriers.

Example 3: Evaluate Audience Impact

Problem: The slide deck has twelve text-heavy slides.

Modeled thinking: Too much text can weaken audience attention and make the main message hard to remember.

Revision: Combine related slides, create clearer section titles, and move details into speaker notes.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example: The best design is the one with the most information on each slide.

Correct example: The best design highlights essential information on the slide and uses speaker notes to explain the evidence in depth.

Teachable explanation: More information is not automatically more effective. A digital presentation must guide audience attention and support understanding.
