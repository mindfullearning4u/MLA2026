P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you evaluate whether your digital design choices support the message. Ask for help if your revisions focus on appearance but not audience understanding.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook revisions.
  2. Check that each revision improves clarity, credibility, or audience access.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in the LMS.
  4. Use ToR feedback to strengthen your digital presentation plan.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a digital-presentation revision plan. Include three weaknesses, three specific revisions, and one paragraph explaining how the revised design improves effectiveness for the intended audience.

Mastery Criteria

  • Design problems are accurately identified.
  • Revisions improve clarity, credibility, organization, or access.
  • Visual choices are connected to evidence and purpose.
  • Speaker support is addressed without overcrowding slides.
  • The explanation uses clear Grade 12 academic language.
