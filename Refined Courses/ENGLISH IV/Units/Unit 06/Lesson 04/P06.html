ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the digital presentation scenario from this lesson. Your task is to revise design choices so the presentation becomes clearer, more credible, and more useful for an audience.

Part A

Identify three design problems in the original slide deck. Explain how each problem affects audience understanding.

Part B

Write three specific revisions. Include at least one revision about slide text, one about visuals or data display, and one about speaker notes.

Part C

Explain which revision would have the greatest impact on effectiveness and why. Use precise academic vocabulary.
