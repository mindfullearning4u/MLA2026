ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

This lesson brings together oral presentation, digital presentation design, digital evaluation, and multimedia publication.

Standards Covered

  • Unit 6 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG4.C.01, MLA.ENG4.C.02, MLA.ENG4.C.03
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.04 - Revise and Edit Writing
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to synthesize oral, digital, and multimedia communication skills.
  • How to evaluate whether a final communication product is credible, accessible, and audience-ready.
  • How research, revision, vocabulary, and conventions support public communication.

What You Will Do

You will review a final project scenario, practice integrated communication decisions, and complete the Unit 6 assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery on the Unit 6 assessment by applying oral, digital, and multimedia communication standards to realistic Grade 12 scenarios.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson asks you to put the whole unit together: present clearly, design effectively, evaluate carefully, and publish responsibly.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help
