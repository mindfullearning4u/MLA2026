# ENGLISH IV - Unit 06 Overview: Capstone Synthesis, Presentation, and Application

---

## Unit Overview

This unit serves as the capstone for English IV. Students independently synthesize ideas across multiple texts and sources, present information orally, design and evaluate digital presentations, and demonstrate advanced communication skills for academic, professional, and real-world purposes.

Emphasis is placed on independent synthesis, audience awareness, organization, credible evidence, rhetorical effectiveness, digital presentation design, evaluation, and real-world communication. Students apply skills from the full course to demonstrate independent reading, writing, research, language, and presentation readiness.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 12 Standards with an emphasis on evaluation, synthesis, argumentation, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through independent synthesis, oral presentation, digital communication, rhetorical effectiveness, and final application.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- independent synthesis of evidence and ideas across multiple texts and sources;
- independent organization and communication;
- oral presentation with coherent focus and credible evidence;
- effective rhetorical devices where appropriate;
- digital presentation design and evaluation;
- audience-aware and purpose-driven communication.

Assessment items require students to independently synthesize information from multiple texts and sources, organize and present information orally with logical organization, coherent focus, credible evidence, and effective rhetorical devices where appropriate, and design and evaluate digital presentations for effectiveness. Students must demonstrate full-course integration of reading, writing, research, language, and communication skills.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit connects reading, writing, research, language, and communication into a final independent performance. Students independently synthesize evidence from multiple sources, organize ideas, adapt communication for audience and purpose, employ rhetorical devices where appropriate, and evaluate digital communication for effectiveness.
