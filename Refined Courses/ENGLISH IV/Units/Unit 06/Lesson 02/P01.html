ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Rhetorical Devices in Oral Presentation

This lesson teaches you how rhetorical devices can strengthen an oral presentation when they are purposeful, accurate, and connected to evidence.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present Information Clearly
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.12 - Evaluate Rhetorical Choices
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.08 - Evaluate Arguments and Claims
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How repetition, parallel structure, analogy, and rhetorical questions can support oral communication.
  • How rhetorical choices affect audience understanding.
  • How to keep rhetoric connected to valid reasoning.
  • How to use academic vocabulary to explain presentation choices.

What You Will Do

  • Study rhetorical devices in short presentation excerpts.
  • Evaluate whether a device strengthens or distracts from a message.
  • Revise oral statements for rhetorical effectiveness.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, a checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify rhetorical devices in oral presentation language.
  • Explain how rhetorical choices affect audience understanding.
  • Distinguish effective rhetoric from unsupported persuasion.
  • Use rhetorical language responsibly in a spoken response.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how speakers use language choices to guide listeners, emphasize ideas, and make evidence easier to remember.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify a rhetorical device but are not sure whether it strengthens the presentation.
