P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your rhetorical device sounds dramatic but does not clearly support your presentation's reasoning.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your rhetorical device from P06.
  2. Check that it supports a clear point and does not replace evidence.
  3. Submit the device and explanation in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one rhetorical device for an oral presentation and explain how it strengthens audience understanding while staying connected to evidence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Rhetorical device is identifiable.
  • Explanation connects device to audience understanding.
  • Reasoning remains evidence-based.
  • Academic vocabulary is used accurately.
