P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the telehealth presentation review from this lesson. Your independent work should evaluate effectiveness, not simply list preferences.

Part A

Identify two strengths and two weaknesses of the digital presentation.

Part B

For each weakness, explain which criterion it affects: clarity, credibility, accessibility, organization, or audience fit.

Part C

Write one evaluation paragraph recommending the two most important revisions and explaining why they would improve effectiveness.
