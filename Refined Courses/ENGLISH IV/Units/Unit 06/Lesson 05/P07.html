P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you strengthen your evaluation if your response sounds like opinion instead of evidence-based judgment.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your evaluation notes.
  2. Check that each judgment uses a clear criterion.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in the LMS.
  4. Use ToR feedback to revise your evaluation language.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a digital presentation evaluation. Include two strengths, two weaknesses, the criteria used to judge them, and a recommendation paragraph explaining the most important revision.

Mastery Criteria

  • Evaluation uses specific criteria.
  • Evidence from the scenario supports each judgment.
  • Recommended revisions are practical and audience-centered.
  • Academic vocabulary is precise and clear.
  • Standard English conventions support professional communication.
