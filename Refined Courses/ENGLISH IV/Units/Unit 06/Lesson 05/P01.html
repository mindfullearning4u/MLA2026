P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Evaluating Digital Presentations for Effectiveness

This lesson teaches you how to judge whether a digital presentation is clear, credible, accessible, and effective for its intended audience.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.C.02 - Design and Evaluate Digital Presentations
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to evaluate digital presentation effectiveness.
  • How to judge whether visuals, data, language, and organization support an audience.
  • How credibility and accessibility affect presentation quality.
  • How academic vocabulary and standard English improve digital communication.

What You Will Do

You will review a digital presentation scenario, identify strengths and weaknesses, and explain which revisions would make the presentation more effective for a specific audience.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by evaluating a digital presentation using evidence-based criteria: clarity, credibility, organization, accessibility, and audience fit.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you can explain why a digital presentation works or does not work and recommend revisions that make it stronger.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can name a design problem but cannot explain how that problem affects the audience.
