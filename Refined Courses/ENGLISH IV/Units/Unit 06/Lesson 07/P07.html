Teacher of Record Reminder

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your checklist and paragraph.
  2. Confirm that each judgment connects to audience, credibility, access, or coherence.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in the LMS.
  4. Use ToR feedback to revise your publication-readiness reasoning.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a publication-readiness review. Include your checklist, two strengths, two revision needs, and one paragraph identifying the top revision priority before sharing.

Mastery Criteria

  • Checklist categories are appropriate for multimedia publication.
  • Strengths and revision needs are specific.
  • Top revision priority is justified with audience-centered reasoning.
  • Academic vocabulary is accurate.
  • The response reflects Grade 12 publication readiness.
