ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 01

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Focus

Weak: I'm going to talk about jobs and transportation.

Strong: Reliable transportation helps workers keep stable employment because it reduces missed shifts.

Reasoning: The strong version gives listeners a clear point to follow.

Example 2: Evidence

Weak: People say transportation matters.

Strong: A local workforce report connects unreliable transportation to missed shifts and lost work hours.

Reasoning: The strong version names a credible source and the evidence it provides.

Example 3: Explanation

Weak: This is important.

Strong: This evidence is important because missed shifts can reduce income and make employment less secure.

Reasoning: The strong version explains why the evidence matters.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use evidence without explaining why it matters.

Correct: Present evidence and then explain how it supports the focus.

Teachable explanation: Oral communication requires explanation because listeners need help connecting evidence to the main point in real time.
