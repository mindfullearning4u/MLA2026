ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Oral Communication Organization, Focus, and Credible Evidence

This lesson teaches you how to organize a focused oral explanation or presentation using credible evidence and clear academic language.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present Information Clearly
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Accurately
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Use Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How to organize an oral response with a clear focus.
  • How to select credible evidence for speaking.
  • How to paraphrase evidence without losing meaning.
  • How academic vocabulary and standard English support credibility.

What You Will Do

  • Study a model oral explanation.
  • Identify focus, evidence, and explanation.
  • Practice planning a short oral response.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, a checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Organize a focused oral response.
  • Use credible evidence accurately.
  • Paraphrase source information clearly.
  • Use academic language appropriate for Grade 12 communication.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to speak in a way that is organized, evidence-based, and easy for a listener to follow.
