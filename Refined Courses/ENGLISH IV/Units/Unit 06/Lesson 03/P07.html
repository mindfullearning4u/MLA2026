P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you decide whether your evidence is strong enough for an oral presentation. Ask for help if your explanation sounds like a list instead of a connected argument.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook claim, evidence, and oral explanation.
  2. Revise unclear wording before submitting.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in the LMS.
  4. Use ToR feedback to improve your communication plan.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a research-presentation planning response. Include your focused claim, two credible pieces of evidence, explanation of significance, one research limitation, and a short note explaining how your presentation order helps the audience follow your reasoning.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim is focused and appropriate for oral presentation.
  • Evidence is credible and directly relevant.
  • Analysis explains significance rather than repeating facts.
  • The limitation is accurate and not exaggerated.
  • The response uses clear academic language and standard English.
