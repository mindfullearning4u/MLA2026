ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the research brief from this lesson. Your independent work should prepare you to explain research orally with a clear claim, credible evidence, and analytical significance.

Part A

Write one presentation claim about whether senior capstone presentations support college or workforce readiness.

Part B

Select two pieces of evidence from the research brief. For each one, explain why it is credible and relevant to your claim.

Part C

Draft a six-sentence oral explanation that includes a claim, two pieces of evidence, explanation of significance, one limitation, and a concluding sentence for the audience.
