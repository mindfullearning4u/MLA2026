ENGLISH IV | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Presenting Topical Research and In-Depth Analysis

This lesson teaches you how to turn research and analytical writing into a focused oral presentation that explains a claim, credible evidence, and significance for a real audience.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.02 - Write Arguments
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.03 - Write In-Depth Analysis

What You Will Learn

  • How to select research that belongs in an oral presentation.
  • How to organize a research-based presentation around one focused claim.
  • How to explain the significance of evidence instead of merely reading facts aloud.
  • How to connect research, argument, and analysis for college and workforce audiences.

What You Will Do

You will study a research brief, identify a presentation claim, choose credible evidence, and plan an oral explanation that moves step by step from claim to evidence to analytical significance.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining how research evidence supports a focused claim and by preparing checkpoint work that could become part of an oral research presentation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard means you can present research clearly, stay focused, use credible evidence, and make purposeful language choices so an audience understands why your analysis matters.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot tell the difference between a research detail that is interesting and evidence that actually supports your presentation claim.
