# ENGLISH IV - Unit 03 Overview: Rhetoric, Argument, and Critical Evaluation

---

## Unit Overview

This unit deepens rhetorical analysis and argument evaluation. Students evaluate text structures and features, evaluate central idea development and support, evaluate author purpose, compare arguments in related texts, evaluate rhetorical choices across multiple texts, and construct formal arguments supported by valid reasoning and credible evidence.

Emphasis is placed on reasoning, evaluation of claims, credibility, bias, rhetorical effectiveness, author purpose, validity, and how authors use the same information and rhetoric across related texts. Students apply rhetorical and argumentative analysis to formal evidence-based argument writing.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 12 Standards with an emphasis on evaluation, synthesis, argumentation, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through informational analysis, argument evaluation, rhetorical analysis, and formal evidence-based argumentative writing.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- text structures, features, and effectiveness;
- central idea development and support;
- author purpose and choices;
- related arguments, validity, and reasoning;
- rhetorical choices across multiple texts;
- formal argument writing.

Assessment items require students to evaluate structures and features, evaluate central idea development and support, evaluate author purpose, compare arguments in related texts, evaluate rhetorical choices across multiple texts, assess validity and reasoning, and write arguments using in-depth analysis, valid reasoning, credible evidence, elaboration, and thorough understanding of the subject.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit builds students’ ability to evaluate ideas and arguments. Students connect structure, evidence, rhetoric, author purpose, credibility, bias, and reasoning to determine how texts persuade, explain, and support claims.
