ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Related Arguments, Claims, Validity, and Reasoning

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate arguments by identifying claims, evidence, reasoning, and validity.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.08 - Evaluate Related Arguments
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.06 - Evaluate Central Idea Support
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.07 - Evaluate Author Purpose
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify an argument's claim.
  • How evidence and reasoning work together.
  • How to evaluate whether reasoning is valid.
  • How author purpose can shape argument choices.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded argument passages.
  • Identify claims, evidence, and reasoning.
  • Judge whether the reasoning supports the claim.
  • Write a checkpoint response evaluating argument validity.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify claims and evidence accurately.
  • Explain how reasoning connects evidence to a claim.
  • Evaluate whether reasoning is valid or incomplete.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to test whether an argument actually works. A strong argument does not only make a claim. It uses relevant evidence and valid reasoning that logically connects the evidence to the claim.
