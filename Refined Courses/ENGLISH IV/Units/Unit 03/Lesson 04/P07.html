ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your validity judgment is supported by evidence and reasoning.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your claim, evidence, and reasoning notes.
  2. Revise your validity judgment for clarity.
  3. Submit the checkpoint response.
  4. Submit notebook evidence if required.
  5. Complete the Lesson Quiz in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one paragraph evaluating an argument. Identify the claim, name one piece of evidence, explain the reasoning, and judge whether the reasoning is valid, partly valid, or weak.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies the claim accurately.
  • The response identifies relevant evidence.
  • The response explains reasoning rather than only quoting evidence.
  • The validity judgment is specific and defensible.
