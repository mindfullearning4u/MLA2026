Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences. Your answers should show that you can evaluate how rhetorical choices work across multiple texts.

Independent Texts

Text A: Library Technology Proposal

The library should extend laptop lending hours during final exams. Last semester, 42 students requested devices after the current checkout window closed. Extending lending hours by ninety minutes during exam weeks would address a documented need without requiring a permanent schedule change.

Text B: Student Appeal

A laptop should not decide whether a student can finish a research paper. When the library closes access early, students without personal devices lose more than convenience. They lose time, confidence, and equal opportunity. During exam weeks, extended lending hours would show that the school values every student's chance to succeed.

Part A

Identify the main rhetorical choice in Text A. Explain how that choice supports the author's purpose.

Part B

Identify the main rhetorical choice in Text B. Explain how diction and tone shape the reader's response.

Part C

Write one evaluative paragraph explaining which text is more effective for a school administrator. Use evidence from both texts and explain why the rhetorical choices fit or do not fit that audience.
