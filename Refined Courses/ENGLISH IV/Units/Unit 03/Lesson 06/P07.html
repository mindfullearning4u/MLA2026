Checkpoint Task

Using the library technology texts from P06, write one paragraph evaluating how the two writers use rhetorical choices differently. Your paragraph must identify one rhetorical choice in each text, explain the effect of each choice, and judge which approach is more effective for a school administrator.

Detailed Instructions

  • Begin with a claim about rhetorical effectiveness.
  • Use evidence from Text A and Text B.
  • Explain how diction, tone, evidence, or framing affects the audience.
  • Use precise vocabulary such as rhetorical choice, purpose, audience, tone, and effectiveness.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response identifies rhetorical choices in both texts.
  • Your response connects each choice to purpose and audience.
  • Your response evaluates effectiveness instead of only summarizing meaning.
  • Your response uses evidence accurately and explains your reasoning clearly.
