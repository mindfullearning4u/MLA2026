Guided Practice

Complete the Guided Practice questions for this lesson. These questions help you practice identifying rhetorical choices, connecting them to purpose, and evaluating effectiveness across texts.

Guided Practice File: ENG4_U03_L06_GuidedPractice.gift

How to Approach Each Question

  1. Read the embedded text carefully.
  2. Identify the author's purpose and audience.
  3. Find the rhetorical choice named or implied in the question.
  4. Explain the effect of the choice before selecting an answer.
  5. Choose the answer that connects choice, purpose, and audience.
