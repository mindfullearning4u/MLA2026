ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Rhetorical choices across multiple texts

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate how different authors use rhetorical choices to influence readers across multiple texts on the same issue.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.12 - Evaluate Rhetorical Choices Across Multiple Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.07 - Evaluate Author Purpose
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.08 - Evaluate Related Arguments
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Analyze Word Meaning and Tone

What You Will Learn

  • How rhetorical choices include diction, tone, emphasis, organization, appeals, and framing.
  • How to connect a rhetorical choice to an author's purpose.
  • How to compare rhetorical choices across texts about the same issue.
  • How to judge whether a rhetorical choice is effective for a specific audience.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded texts that address the same issue.
  • Identify rhetorical choices in each text.
  • Explain how word choice and tone shape audience response.
  • Write a checkpoint response evaluating rhetorical effectiveness across texts.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify rhetorical choices accurately.
  • Connect rhetorical choices to purpose, audience, and argument.
  • Evaluate which rhetorical approach is more effective and why.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to evaluate how writers persuade. Grade 12 analysis asks you to explain not only what a text says, but how the writer's rhetorical choices work across more than one text.
