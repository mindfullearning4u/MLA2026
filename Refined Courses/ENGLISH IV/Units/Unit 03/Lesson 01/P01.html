ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Text Structures, Features, and Effectiveness

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate how an informational text is organized and how its features help or limit the reader's understanding.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.05 - Evaluate Text Effectiveness
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How structure guides a reader through information.
  • How headings, transitions, examples, statistics, and comparisons function as text features.
  • How to judge whether a text's organization makes the author's point more effective.
  • How to explain a possible improvement to an informational text.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded informational passages.
  • Identify structure and text features.
  • Evaluate how effectively those choices help the reader understand.
  • Write a checkpoint response explaining one improvement.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain how structure affects the reader's understanding.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of a text feature.
  • Use precise academic vocabulary.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to judge whether an informational text works well. A Grade 12 reader does not only identify structure. A Grade 12 reader explains how the structure helps, limits, or could improve communication.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify a structure but are not sure how to evaluate whether that structure is effective.
