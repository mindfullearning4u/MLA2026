  4. Submit notebook evidence if required.
  5. Complete the Lesson Quiz in Moodle.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one paragraph evaluating an informational passage's structure or text feature. Your paragraph must identify the structure or feature, explain how it affects the reader, and recommend one specific improvement.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies a relevant structure or feature.
  • The response evaluates effectiveness, not just presence.
  • The improvement is specific and logical.
  • The writing uses clear conventions and academic vocabulary.
