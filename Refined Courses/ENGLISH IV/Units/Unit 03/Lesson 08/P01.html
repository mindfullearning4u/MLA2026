ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 3 Synthesis

This lesson helps you synthesize Unit 3 skills before completing the Unit Assessment. You will review informational text structure, central idea support, author purpose, related arguments, and rhetorical choices.

Standards Covered

  • Unit 3 Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.05, MLA.ENG4.R.06, MLA.ENG4.R.07, MLA.ENG4.R.08, MLA.ENG4.R.12
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10, MLA.ENG4.V.01, MLA.ENG4.L.01, MLA.ENG4.C.01

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 3 skills connect in a complete text evaluation.
  • How to use evidence, structure, purpose, and rhetoric together.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the Unit 3 evaluation process.
  • Practice with an integrated passage.
  • Complete independent synthesis work.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence and complete the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete synthesis practice.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence.
  • Complete the Unit 3 Unit Assessment.

Important: Lesson 8 does not include a lesson quiz. It includes the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are combining everything from Unit 3. A Grade 12 reader can evaluate not just what a text says, but how its structure, evidence, purpose, argument, and rhetoric work together.
