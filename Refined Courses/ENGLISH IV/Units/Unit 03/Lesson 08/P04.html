ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 08

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Structure and Central Idea

Modeled thinking: The emergency alert passage begins with a claim, explains the problem, gives reasons, and ends with a limited rollout.

Reasoning: This structure supports the central idea by moving from need to practical solution.

Example 2: Argument and Evidence

Modeled thinking: The passage supports text alerts by explaining that warnings must be received and understood quickly.

Reasoning: The evidence is relevant because it connects the proposed update to public safety outcomes.

Example 3: Rhetorical Effectiveness

Modeled thinking: The author uses practical language instead of dramatic language.

Reasoning: That choice is effective for government decision-makers because it presents the proposal as responsible, measurable, and manageable.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The text is effective because it has a topic and a claim.

Correct: The text is effective because its claim, evidence, structure, and practical rhetoric work together to persuade county leaders that the alert update is necessary and manageable.

Teachable explanation: Having a claim is not enough. A complete Unit 3 evaluation explains how multiple parts of the text work together.
