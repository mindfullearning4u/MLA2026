Independent Passage

Passage: Public Charging Stations

The transit authority should install public phone charging stations at major bus transfer centers. Riders increasingly use phones for digital tickets, route updates, job communication, and emergency contact. A dead battery can turn a delayed bus into a missed shift, a lost connection, or an unsafe wait. The authority can begin with three high-traffic stations and collect usage data before expanding the program.

Part A

Identify the central idea and text structure.

Part B

Evaluate the support and reasoning. Identify one strength and one possible limitation.

Part C

Write one synthesis paragraph evaluating the passage's effectiveness for transit authority leaders.
