ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 02

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Identify the Central Idea

Passage focus: Community repair cafes.

Step 1: Look for the sentence that states the broad point.

Step 2: Check which idea the details keep returning to.

Step 3: State the point in your own words.

Modeled answer: The central idea is that repair cafes can reduce waste while teaching residents practical skills.

Example 2: Evaluate Support

Support: One repair cafe reported that residents fixed 68 percent of the items they brought.

Step 1: Is it relevant? Yes, because it shows repair cafes lead to actual repairs.

Step 2: Is it specific? Yes, because it includes a percentage and time period.

Step 3: Is it sufficient alone? It is strong, but more examples from other cafes would make the support broader.

Modeled answer: The statistic is effective because it gives measurable evidence that repair cafes can reduce waste, although the passage would be stronger with data from more than one location.

Example 3: Recommend Stronger Support

Weak support: Many people like the program.

Stronger support: The program surveyed 120 participants, and 74 percent reported repairing at least one item at home within a month of attending.

Step 1: The stronger support is measurable.

Step 2: It connects directly to skill transfer.

Step 3: It helps prove the central idea more effectively.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The support is good because it is about the same topic.

Correct: The support is effective when it directly develops the central idea with specific evidence and explanation.

Teachable explanation: Same topic is not enough. Strong support must prove or clarify the central idea.
