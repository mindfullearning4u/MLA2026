Checkpoint Task

Submit one paragraph evaluating how effectively a passage supports its central idea. Include the central idea, one piece of support, a judgment about that support, and one recommendation for stronger support.

Mastery Criteria

  • The central idea is accurate.
  • The selected support connects directly to the central idea.
  • The evaluation explains relevance, specificity, sufficiency, or elaboration.
  • The recommendation would improve the reader's understanding.
