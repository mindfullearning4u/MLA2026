P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Author Purpose and Author Choices

This lesson teaches you how to evaluate why an author writes an informational text and how the author's choices support that purpose.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.R.07 - Evaluate Author Purpose
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.06 - Evaluate Central Idea Support
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify an author's purpose in an informational text.
  • How word choice, examples, organization, and emphasis reveal purpose.
  • How to evaluate whether author choices are effective for the intended purpose.
  • How to explain author purpose using precise academic vocabulary.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded informational passages.
  • Identify purpose and author choices.
  • Evaluate how choices shape reader understanding.
  • Write a checkpoint response about author purpose.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • State an author's purpose accurately.
  • Identify choices that support that purpose.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of those choices.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning to ask why the author made specific choices. Grade 12 readers do not stop at what the author says. They evaluate how the author tries to inform, persuade, warn, explain, or call readers to act.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify the topic but are not sure how to explain the author's purpose or evaluate the author's choices.
