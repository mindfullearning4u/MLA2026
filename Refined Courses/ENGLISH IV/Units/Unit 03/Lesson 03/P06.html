ENGLISH IV | Unit 03 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use precise language and explain effect, not just identification.

Part A: Purpose

Choose one embedded passage from this lesson. State the author's specific purpose in one complete sentence.

Part B: Author Choice

Identify one author choice, such as a list, example, tone, repeated phrase, or contrast. Explain how it supports the purpose.

Part C: Effectiveness Judgment

Write two to four sentences evaluating whether the author's choice is effective for the intended reader.
