Checkpoint Task

Using the neighborhood tool library passage from P06, write one paragraph evaluating how effectively the passage argues for the proposal. Your paragraph must include a judgment, evidence from the passage, and explanation of the author's rhetorical choices.

Detailed Instructions

  • Begin with a claim about effectiveness.
  • Identify the passage's structure.
  • Evaluate the evidence and reasoning.
  • Explain at least one rhetorical choice.
  • Connect your judgment to the intended audience.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response evaluates instead of summarizes.
  • Your response uses accurate evidence.
  • Your response explains structure, support, and rhetoric.
  • Your response connects effectiveness to purpose and audience.
