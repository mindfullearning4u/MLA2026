P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now you will practice writing a complete evaluation. A complete evaluation needs a claim about effectiveness, evidence from the text, explanation of how the evidence works, and a limitation or qualification when appropriate.

Embedded Passage

Passage: Library Career Workshops

The public library should offer monthly evening career workshops for adults changing jobs. Local workforce data show that applications for health care, logistics, and technology support positions increased during the past year. Evening workshops would help working adults learn resume revision, interview preparation, and online application skills without missing daytime work hours. The library already has computer labs and meeting rooms, so the program can begin with minimal new cost. A pilot series would allow the library to measure attendance before making a permanent schedule commitment.

Deeper Learning

This passage is strongest when judged as a policy recommendation. It gives a clear audience, a current need, a practical solution, and a low-risk implementation plan. The rhetoric is measured rather than emotional. That does not make it weak. It means the author is matching the style to decision-makers who may care about cost, feasibility, and measurable results.

Step-by-Step Explanation

Step 1: The claim is that the library should offer monthly evening career workshops.

Step 2: The evidence includes workforce data, adult scheduling needs, existing library resources, and a pilot plan.

Step 3: The rhetorical approach is practical and cautious.

Step 4: The passage is effective for library leaders because it connects community need to a feasible plan.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The passage is ineffective because it is not emotional.

Correct: The passage is effective for a policy audience because its measured tone, workforce data, and pilot plan make the proposal seem practical and low-risk.

Why this matters: Grade 12 evaluation requires audience awareness. Emotional language can be effective, but a calm practical style may be stronger for decision-makers.
