Complete the Guided Practice questions for this lesson. These questions help you evaluate how structure, evidence, argument, rhetoric, and audience fit work together.

How to Work

  1. Read the full passage in the question.
  2. Identify purpose and audience.
  3. Look for structure, support, reasoning, and rhetoric.
  4. Choose the answer that explains effectiveness, not just content.

Teacher of Record Reminder

If you are choosing answers based only on whether you agree with the topic, pause and ask your Teacher of Record for help. This lesson is about evaluating how a text works, not choosing a personal opinion.
