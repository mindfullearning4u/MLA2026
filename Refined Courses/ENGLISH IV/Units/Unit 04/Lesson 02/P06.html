Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use the embedded source set below.

Independent Source Set

Research Question: How do public charging stations affect transit riders?

Source A: A transit survey found that 41 percent of riders use phones for route updates or digital tickets.

Source B: A rider interview explains that a dead phone battery can make it difficult to contact work or family during delays.

Source C: A budget note says three charging stations could be tested at major transfer centers before expansion.

Part A

Identify what each source contributes to the research question.

Part B

Identify one limitation or missing piece of evidence in the source set.

Part C

Write one synthesis sentence that combines at least two sources to answer part of the research question.
