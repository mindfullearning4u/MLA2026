ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Variety of Sources and Source Synthesis

This lesson teaches you how to use different kinds of sources to build a more complete understanding of a topical issue.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.06 - Evaluate Central Idea Support
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.08 - Evaluate Related Arguments
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.03 - Analyze Word Meaning and Tone

What You Will Learn

  • How different source types contribute different kinds of information.
  • How to compare sources that address the same issue.
  • How to identify agreement, disagreement, and gaps between sources.
  • How word choice and tone reveal source perspective.

What You Will Do

  • Read short embedded source excerpts.
  • Identify what each source contributes.
  • Compare source claims and evidence.
  • Write a brief source synthesis statement.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify the usefulness of different source types.
  • Compare source perspectives accurately.
  • Explain how multiple sources build a fuller answer to a research question.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning that research is not collecting random facts. Strong research uses a variety of sources to compare evidence, understand perspectives, and build a careful answer.
