Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record will check whether your synthesis connects sources instead of listing them separately.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your source chart.
  2. Write your synthesis response.
  3. Check that you used at least two sources.
  4. Submit your checkpoint and notebook evidence.
  5. Complete the Lesson Quiz.

Checkpoint Task

Using the public charging station source set from P06, write one paragraph explaining what the sources together suggest. Include at least two sources, one limitation, and one sentence explaining what additional evidence would strengthen the research.

Detailed Instructions

  • Name each source you use.
  • Explain what each source contributes.
  • Combine source ideas into one synthesis statement.
  • Identify a gap or limitation.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response uses more than one source.
  • Your response explains relationships between sources.
  • Your response identifies at least one source limitation.
  • Your response uses precise research vocabulary.
