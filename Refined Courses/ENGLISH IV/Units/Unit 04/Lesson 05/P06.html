P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Passage / Scenario

A senior student is completing a task focused on Argument Elaboration and Thorough Subject Understanding. The response must show clear thinking, relevant evidence or details, precise academic language, and standard English conventions for a Grade 12 audience.

Part A

Identify the central skill or purpose in this lesson task. Explain what the response must prove or communicate.

Part B

Select one relevant detail, example, or evidence point from the scenario and explain how it supports the lesson focus.

Part C

Write a complete response that applies the lesson focus, explains your reasoning, uses precise vocabulary, and follows standard English conventions.
