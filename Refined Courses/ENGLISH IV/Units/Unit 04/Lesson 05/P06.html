ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the embedded argument situation to complete Parts A, B, and C. Your goal is to show thorough subject understanding through developed elaboration.

Embedded Argument Situation

Issue: Should schools provide a required workplace communication seminar for seniors?

Claim: Schools should provide a required workplace communication seminar because seniors need practice with professional email, feedback, and audience awareness before graduation.

Evidence: A local internship coordinator reported that students often understood job tasks but needed coaching on concise email updates, appropriate tone, and follow-up questions.

Part A

Write one context sentence that helps a reader understand why the evidence matters. Do not repeat the evidence word for word.

Part B

Write two elaboration sentences that explain the significance and consequence of the evidence. Show why communication skills matter beyond the classroom.

Part C

Revise the paragraph so it follows a clear structure: claim, evidence, context, significance, and consequence. Read it aloud once to check clarity.
