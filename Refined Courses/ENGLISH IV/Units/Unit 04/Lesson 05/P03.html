P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Deeper Learning

Elaboration also depends on structure. Strong argument paragraphs usually move from claim, to evidence, to explanation, to consequence. If the order is random, the reader may not see the argument develop.

You can also connect a topical issue to a broader theme or concern. For example, financial literacy is not only about a school assignment. It connects to independence, responsibility, and access to informed decision-making.

Embedded Practice Set

Issue: Should schools provide structured civic participation projects for seniors?

Claim: Schools should offer civic participation projects because students need practice applying communication and problem-solving skills to real community issues.

Evidence: A school report found that students who completed community problem-solving projects were more likely to revise proposals after receiving stakeholder feedback.

Step-by-Step Application

Step 1: Name the context. Civic projects require students to work with real audiences, not only complete classroom exercises.

Step 2: Explain the significance. Revising after stakeholder feedback shows that students are learning to respond to audience needs.

Step 3: Explain the consequence. This practice prepares students for college, workforce, and community situations where communication must adapt to real feedback.

Step 4: Revise for development. Add a sentence that links the project to a broader theme: responsible participation depends on listening, adapting, and communicating clearly.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: This evidence proves civic projects are good because students revised things.

Correct: This evidence supports civic projects because revision after stakeholder feedback shows students learning to adapt communication to real community needs.

Why this matters: The incorrect version repeats the evidence and gives a vague judgment. The correct version explains the evidence's significance and connects it to the claim.

Notebook Directions

Write a developed explanation of the civic participation evidence. Include one sentence for context, one for significance, one for consequence, and one that connects the issue to a broader theme.
