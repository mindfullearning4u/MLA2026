Checkpoint Task

Submit one developed argument paragraph about workplace communication seminars for seniors. Your paragraph must include a claim, evidence from the embedded source, and at least three elaboration sentences that explain context, significance, and consequence.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim is clear and arguable.
  • The evidence is accurately represented.
  • Elaboration explains context, significance, and consequence.
  • The paragraph is logically organized and revised for clarity.
