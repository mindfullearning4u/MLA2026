Deeper Review

Before the Unit Assessment, review the difference between evidence and explanation. Evidence gives the information. Explanation tells what the information means, how it supports a claim, or how it reveals the function of an author choice.

Embedded Passage

Passage: "The Open Door"

The workshop door stayed open after the last student left. On the tables, drafts of resumes, scholarship statements, and practice interview notes lay in small stacks. The room looked unfinished, but not abandoned. Each page carried a mark of revision: a crossed-out phrase, a stronger verb, a question written in the margin.

Ms. Alvarez turned off the projector and left the door open a little longer. She liked the room best at this hour, when the work was no longer being performed for anyone. The quiet showed what the day had been about: not perfect answers, but students learning how to make their next version clearer.

Review Application

Research connection: The passage connects to the topical issue of senior transition workshops.

Argument connection: It could support a claim that workshops should include revision time.

Analysis connection: The open door and marked drafts function as symbols of continued growth.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The passage is about papers on tables after students leave.

Correct: The passage uses marked drafts and an open door to show revision as an ongoing process of preparation and growth.

Why this matters: The incorrect version summarizes. The correct version explains how the details function.
