P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now apply the unit skills with less scaffolding. Your goal is to transfer what you learned in Lessons 1-7 into one connected response.

Passage / Scenario

A student researches how a modern author uses setting to reveal cultural conflict. The student has one primary-text passage about a neighborhood changing quickly and one research-source note about migration, identity, and public memory.

Deeper Application

Write a draft response that uses the scenario above. Make sure the response includes a clear claim or interpretation, relevant evidence, precise vocabulary, reasoning, and standard English conventions.

Then underline the part of your response that shows the strongest integration of more than one unit skill.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The response lists one skill from the unit but does not connect it to evidence, reasoning, or communication.

Correct: The response uses evidence, vocabulary, reasoning, and conventions together to explain the unit task clearly.

Teachable Explanation: A synthesis response is stronger than a list. It shows how skills work together to create accurate meaning and clear communication.

Deeper Reasoning Support

Use this second pass to strengthen your thinking before you move to practice. The goal is not just to get an answer; the goal is to explain why the answer is supported by the text, source, sentence, or communication situation.

  1. Reread the embedded passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt on this page and mark the most useful details.
  2. Separate evidence from opinion. Evidence is stated or clearly implied by the text; opinion is a reaction that still needs proof.
  3. Write the reasoning step that connects the evidence to the skill. Use because, therefore, this shows, or this matters because to make the connection visible.
  4. Revise vague words into precise academic language that matches the standard and lesson vocabulary.
