ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 4 Synthesis

This lesson helps you review and apply Unit 4 skills: topical research, argument writing, and complex text analysis.

Standards Covered

  • Primary Review: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Primary Review: MLA.ENG4.W.02 - Write In-Depth Arguments with Evidence
  • Primary Review: MLA.ENG4.W.03 - Write In-Depth Complex Text Analysis
  • Support Review: MLA.ENG4.R.10, MLA.ENG4.W.04, MLA.ENG4.L.01, MLA.ENG4.C.01

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 4 research, argument, and analysis skills connect.
  • How to select evidence for different purposes.
  • How to review synthesis, elaboration, revision, and communication expectations.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the major Unit 4 skill strands.
  • Practice deciding whether evidence supports research, argument, or analysis.
  • Complete a synthesis checkpoint.
  • Complete the Unit 4 Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use research evidence accurately.
  • Evaluate and revise argument reasoning.
  • Analyze author choices in a complex passage.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment. Lesson 8 does not include a Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are bringing the unit together. You will show that you can research a topical issue, build an evidence-based argument, and analyze how complex texts create meaning.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before the Unit Assessment if you are unsure how to distinguish research synthesis, argument elaboration, and complex text analysis.
