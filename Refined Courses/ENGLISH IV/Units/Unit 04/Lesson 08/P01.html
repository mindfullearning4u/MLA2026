P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 4 Synthesis

Standards Covered

MLA.ENG4.RS.01, MLA.ENG4.W.02, MLA.ENG4.W.03, MLA.ENG4.R.10, MLA.ENG4.W.04, MLA.ENG4.L.01, MLA.ENG4.C.01

What You Will Learn

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7. You will connect research questions, source use, literary analysis, synthesis, and documented writing in one integrated senior-level task.

What You Will Do

You will read a unit scenario, analyze the central issue, select relevant evidence, explain reasoning, apply academic vocabulary, and produce a polished synthesis response.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by combining multiple unit skills in notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint synthesis response.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

The standards work together here: you are not using one isolated skill. You are showing that you can read, think, explain, revise, and communicate like a prepared senior learner.
