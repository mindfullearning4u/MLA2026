Notebook Title

Unit 4 Synthesis Review: Research, Argument, and Analysis

Vocabulary Review

  • Research synthesis: connecting multiple sources to explain a larger idea.
  • Argument elaboration: explaining how evidence supports a claim and why it matters.
  • Complex text analysis: explaining how author choices create meaning.
  • Revision: strengthening writing through clearer meaning, organization, and language.
  • Communication: presenting ideas clearly for an audience and purpose.

Teach the Review

Unit 4 taught three connected skills. Research synthesis helps you understand a topical issue. Argument writing helps you defend a claim about that issue. Complex text analysis helps you explain how a writer builds meaning.

The skills overlap, but they are not the same. Research asks, "What do sources show together?" Argument asks, "What claim can I support with evidence and reasoning?" Analysis asks, "How does this text create meaning through choices?"

Embedded Review Set

Topical issue: Should schools provide structured senior transition workshops?

Source A: A counselor report found that students who attended transition workshops submitted more complete college, career, and scholarship applications.

Source B: A teacher survey noted that workshops were most effective when students received time to revise written materials after feedback.

Source C: A student focus group said workshops were useful when examples were specific to realistic post-graduation tasks.

Notebook Directions

Write one research synthesis statement using the three source notes. Then write one argument claim that could grow from that synthesis. Label each sentence clearly.
