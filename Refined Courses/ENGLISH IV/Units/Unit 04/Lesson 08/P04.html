P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Research Synthesis

Source relationship: The sources show that senior transition workshops help most when students receive realistic examples, feedback, and revision time.

Reasoning: This statement connects the sources rather than listing them separately.

Example 2: Argument Elaboration

Claim: Schools should offer senior transition workshops that include revision time and realistic post-graduation tasks.

Elaboration: The evidence matters because preparation is strongest when students practice tasks they will actually face and then improve their work after feedback.

Example 3: Complex Text Analysis

Analytical claim: The passage uses marked drafts and the open door to present revision as ongoing preparation rather than failure.

Reasoning: This analysis explains the function of details in the passage.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: All evidence works the same way, so I can use any detail for research, argument, or analysis.

Correct: Evidence must match the task: sources support research synthesis and argument, while passage details support analysis of author choices.

Teachable explanation: Unit 4 skills connect, but each task uses evidence for a specific purpose.
