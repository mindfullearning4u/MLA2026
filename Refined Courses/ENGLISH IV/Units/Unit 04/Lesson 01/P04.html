Example 1: Narrow a Topic

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify the specific lesson skill being modeled. Step 2: examine the words, evidence, sentence, or passage detail that matters most. Step 3: explain why the final response or revision demonstrates the skill accurately.

Broad topic: Artificial intelligence.

Focused issue: The use of artificial intelligence tools in college application essays.

Reasoning: The focused issue names a setting and a specific use, which makes research more manageable.

Example 2: Write a Research Question

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify what changed from the first example. Step 2: connect the example to the lesson focus. Step 3: explain how the response uses clearer reasoning, stronger evidence, or more precise language.

Weak question: Is AI good?

Strong question: How do AI writing tools affect the authenticity and fairness of college application essays?

Reasoning: The strong question is open-ended, focused, and answerable with evidence from multiple sources.

Example 3: Paraphrase Early Source Information

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: combine the lesson skill with evidence or revision choices. Step 2: check that the explanation stays focused on the assigned standard. Step 3: confirm that the final response is clear, accurate, and written in standard English.

Source idea: Some admissions officers worry that AI tools make it harder to determine how much help an applicant received.

Accurate paraphrase: Admissions officers may be concerned that AI writing tools make applicant authorship harder to judge.

Reasoning: The paraphrase keeps the meaning but uses different wording.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Should AI be allowed?

Correct: How should schools distinguish acceptable AI support from dishonest AI-generated writing?

Teachable explanation: The incorrect question is too broad. The correct question narrows the topic to a specific academic concern and invites evidence-based research.
