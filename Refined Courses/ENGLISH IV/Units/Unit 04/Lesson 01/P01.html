P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Topical Issue Research Questions and Inquiry Focus

This lesson teaches you how to begin research by turning a broad topical issue into a focused, researchable question.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.10 - Paraphrase Accurately
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How to distinguish a broad topic from a focused research question.
  • How to create a research question that can be answered with evidence.
  • How to narrow a topical issue by audience, cause, effect, policy, or solution.
  • How to paraphrase early source information without changing meaning.

What You Will Do

  • Examine a topical issue.
  • Practice narrowing research focus.
  • Write possible research questions.
  • Select the strongest question for independent inquiry.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify whether a question is too broad, too narrow, or researchable.
  • Revise a broad topic into a focused research question.
  • Explain why your question can guide evidence-based research.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning that research begins with a strong question. A strong question gives your reading a purpose and helps you decide what evidence is relevant.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your topic feels too large or if you cannot tell whether your question can be answered with evidence.
