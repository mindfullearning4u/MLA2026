ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: In-depth complex text analysis organization, tone, and voice

This lesson teaches you how to write analysis of a complex text by organizing ideas clearly and explaining the function of tone and voice.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.W.03 - Write In-Depth Complex Text Analysis
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.05 - Analyze Author Choices
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.L.01 - Use Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How complex text analysis differs from summary.
  • How to identify organization, tone, and voice as author choices.
  • How to explain the function of those choices.
  • How to write clear analytical sentences using standard conventions.

What You Will Do

  • Read an embedded complex passage.
  • Analyze how organization shapes meaning.
  • Evaluate tone and voice.
  • Write an analytical paragraph that explains author choices.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Distinguish analysis from summary.
  • Explain the effect of organization, tone, and voice.
  • Use evidence from the embedded passage.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to explain how a writer builds meaning. Instead of only saying what a text says, you will explain how the writer's choices affect readers.
