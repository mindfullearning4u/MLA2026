ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the embedded source set to complete Parts A, B, and C. Your work should show claim, evidence, and reasoning as separate but connected parts.

Embedded Source Set

Issue: Should local governments fund public charging stations for electric bikes and scooters?

Source A: A transportation report found that electric bikes and scooters reduced short car trips in neighborhoods with safe charging and parking areas.

Source B: A neighborhood association warned that charging stations can block sidewalks if placement rules are unclear.

Source C: A city pilot program found that stations near transit stops were used more often than stations placed in low-traffic areas.

Part A

Write one precise claim about public charging stations. Include a condition or limit so the claim matches all three sources.

Part B

Choose two pieces of evidence from the source set. For each piece of evidence, explain whether it supports the claim, limits the claim, or clarifies how the policy should be designed.

Part C

Write a short argument paragraph using your claim, evidence, and reasoning. Use at least one academic vocabulary word from the lesson, such as validity, relevance, sufficient, or implementation.
