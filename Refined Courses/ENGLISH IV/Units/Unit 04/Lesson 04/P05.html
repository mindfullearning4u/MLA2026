Practice Focus

You will practice evaluating claims, choosing relevant evidence, and identifying valid reasoning. Each question includes the information you need.

Before You Begin

Look for the answer that is precise, evidence-based, and logically connected. Avoid answers that overstate, ignore evidence, or rely on vague opinion.
