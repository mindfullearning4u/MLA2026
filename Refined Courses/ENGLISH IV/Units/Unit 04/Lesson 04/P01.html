ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: In-Depth Argument Claims, Valid Reasoning, and Evidence

This lesson teaches you how to build a Grade 12 argument by connecting a precise claim, valid reasoning, and relevant evidence.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.W.02 - Write In-Depth Arguments with Evidence
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.RS.01 - Conduct Topical Research
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.R.08 - Evaluate Rhetoric and Reasoning
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How to write a precise claim that can be supported with evidence.
  • How to connect evidence to reasoning instead of letting evidence stand alone.
  • How to evaluate whether reasoning is valid, relevant, and sufficient.
  • How to use academic vocabulary to make argument writing clear and professional.

What You Will Do

  • Study the parts of an in-depth argument paragraph.
  • Evaluate claims for precision and arguability.
  • Match evidence to reasoning.
  • Draft a focused argument paragraph from a source set.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose claims that are specific, arguable, and evidence-based.
  • Explain why evidence supports a claim.
  • Identify weak or invalid reasoning.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning that strong arguments do more than announce an opinion. They use research, reasoning, and precise language to help readers understand why the claim is valid.
