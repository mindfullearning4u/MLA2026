Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record if your claim is too broad, if your evidence does not fit, or if your reasoning repeats the evidence without explaining it.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your independent work paragraph.
  2. Underline the claim.
  3. Highlight the evidence sentences.
  4. Circle the reasoning sentences that explain why the evidence matters.
  5. Submit your checkpoint response using the course submission tool.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one argument paragraph about public charging stations for electric bikes and scooters. Your paragraph must include a precise claim, at least two pieces of evidence from the embedded source set, and reasoning that explains how the evidence supports or limits the claim.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim is specific, arguable, and aligned to the source set.
  • The evidence is relevant and accurately represented.
  • The reasoning explains why the evidence supports or limits the claim.
  • The paragraph uses clear academic vocabulary and precise language.
