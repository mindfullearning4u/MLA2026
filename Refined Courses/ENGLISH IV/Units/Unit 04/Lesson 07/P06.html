Embedded Passage

Passage: "The Weather Archive"

Boxes of handwritten weather logs filled the archive shelves. Some pages were neat, with temperatures lined in blue ink. Others were smudged by rain that had found its way through an open window decades earlier. Nina opened one book and saw a storm recorded in three sentences: wind before sunrise, water over the north road, children sent home before noon.

On her laptop, the same storm appeared as a point in a data set. The point was useful. It could be counted, compared, and graphed. Still, Nina copied the three handwritten sentences into her notes. Numbers showed the storm's size. The sentences showed where it had touched a life.

Part A

Write one analytical claim about how the passage contrasts data and lived experience.

Part B

Choose two details from the passage and explain the function of each detail.

Part C

Write one analysis paragraph. Then revise one sentence to make the vocabulary more precise.
