ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 07

P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Weak Claim to Strong Claim

Weak claim: The passage is about a stage after a play.

Strong claim: The passage uses backstage remnants to show that creative work continues to matter after the public performance ends.

Modeled thinking: The strong claim interprets the details instead of naming only the situation.

Example 2: Evidence and Function

Evidence: "one chair still facing the place where the story had ended"

Modeled thinking: This detail makes the finished performance feel present even after the audience leaves.

Reasoning: The chair functions as a reminder that stories and labor leave traces beyond the final curtain.

Example 3: Revision for Precision

Original: The details show the play was important.

Revised: The details of tape, dust, and the remaining chair show that the performance's meaning survives in the physical traces of shared labor.

Reasoning: The revised sentence uses precise nouns and explains why the details matter.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The author uses details because details are descriptive and interesting.

Correct: The author uses physical remnants on the stage to reveal how creative collaboration remains meaningful after the audience leaves.

Teachable explanation: The incorrect version names description but does not analyze function. The correct version explains what the details do.
