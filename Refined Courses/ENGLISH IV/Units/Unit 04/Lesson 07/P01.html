ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Complex Text Analysis with Thorough Subject Understanding

This lesson teaches you how to write a mature analysis paragraph that explains author choices with precise evidence, academic vocabulary, and revision.

Standards Covered

  • Primary: MLA.ENG4.W.03 - Write In-Depth Complex Text Analysis
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.02 - Write In-Depth Arguments with Evidence
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.W.04 - Develop and Strengthen Writing
  • Support: MLA.ENG4.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

What You Will Learn

  • How to build an analytical claim about a complex text.
  • How to use evidence without slipping into summary.
  • How to revise analysis for depth, precision, and academic tone.
  • How academic vocabulary strengthens analysis when used accurately.

What You Will Do

  • Read embedded passages.
  • Identify author choices and explain their function.
  • Draft and revise an analysis paragraph.
  • Evaluate the difference between shallow and thorough interpretation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Write an analytical claim that goes beyond summary.
  • Use evidence to explain author choices.
  • Revise analysis for clarity and depth.
  • Complete Guided Practice, the checkpoint, and the Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to make a strong interpretation and defend it with evidence. Your goal is not just to identify details, but to explain why those details matter.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your analysis retells the passage, uses vague vocabulary, or does not explain how the evidence supports your interpretation.
