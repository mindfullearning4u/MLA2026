ENGLISH IV | Unit 04 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the source set below to complete Parts A, B, and C. Work in order. Do not begin with a claim until you have paraphrased and synthesized the source notes.

Embedded Source Set

Research question: How should schools evaluate the use of AI study tools by seniors preparing for college or career training?

Source A: A college readiness survey found that students who used AI tools to generate practice questions reported more frequent review sessions.

Source B: A writing program warned that students who let AI draft full responses often struggled to explain their own reasoning during conferences.

Source C: A career training instructor reported that AI tools were useful when students used them to check terminology and revise unclear explanations.

Part A

Paraphrase each source note in your own words. Preserve the source meaning. Do not add a judgment yet.

Part B

Write one synthesis statement that explains what the three sources show together. Your synthesis should include both the useful role of AI study tools and the concern about overreliance.

Part C

Write one claim that could begin an argument paragraph. The claim must grow from your synthesis and include a clear condition, limit, or boundary.
