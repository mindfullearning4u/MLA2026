# ENG4 — English IV

**Credit:** 1.0 High School English Language Arts Credit  
**Course format:** Mastery-based online course  
**Structure:** 6 units, 8 lessons per unit  
**Mastery requirement:** 80%

---

## Page 1 — Course Overview

English IV is a senior-level course that strengthens close reading, literary and informational analysis, research, argument, revision, speaking, and multimedia publication.

### What Students Will Study

1. **Foundations of Grade 12 Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice**
2. **Advanced Literary Evaluation, Perspective, Poetry, and Narrative Synthesis**
3. **Advanced Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument Evaluation**
4. **Topical Research, Argument, and In-Depth Text Analysis**
5. **Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision**
6. **Synthesis, Presentation, Digital Evaluation, and Multimedia Publication**

By the end of the course, students will be prepared to read complex texts independently, communicate evidence-based ideas, conduct responsible research, and publish polished written, oral, and multimedia work.

---

## Page 2 — How This Course Works

Each unit contains eight lessons that move from direct instruction and modeling to practice, independent application, and mastery.

### Lesson Routine

1. **Learn:** Read lesson pages in order and study each model, text, visual, or example.
2. **Practice:** Complete guided practice and use feedback to correct misunderstandings.
3. **Apply:** Complete independent work and the checkpoint using the directions and evidence on the current page.
4. **Document:** Submit required notebook evidence and checkpoint work.
5. **Demonstrate mastery:** Complete the lesson quiz or unit assessment before advancing.

Students must earn **80% or higher** on mastery assessments. Students who do not yet meet mastery follow course directions and work with the Teacher of Record before another attempt.

---

## Page 3 — Expectations and Policies

Students are expected to:

- Complete work in order and read all directions before responding.
- Use specific textual or source evidence when required.
- Write in their own words unless quotation is required and cite sources as directed.
- Submit complete, readable work that reflects appropriate academic English.
- Communicate respectfully in written, oral, and multimedia work.
- Ask the Teacher of Record for help when directions, texts, or tasks are unclear.

Students must submit original work and may be asked to explain their reasoning, evidence, research process, or revisions. Before submitting, students should confirm that every part is answered, required evidence is included, writing is revised for clarity, and the stated format is followed.
