# Phase 3A-B-1: English IV Unit-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English IV unit-level mapping using the English IV Crosswalk Draft and Mapping Framework. It preserves the approved English I-III course-production structure while increasing Grade 12 rigor and maintaining vertical alignment.

The mapping preserves:

- 6 units
- 8 lessons per unit
- Lessons 1-7 with Lesson Quiz
- Lesson 8 = Putting It All Together
- Lesson 8 with Unit Assessment
- No new primary standards introduced in Lesson 8

## Workload Summary

| Unit | Workload Finding | Mapping Control Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Manageable foundation load | Focuses on paraphrase, vocabulary, evidence, academic voice, and conventions without early overload. |
| Unit 2 | High workload risk | Protects functional significance, themes, character perspective, major poets, figurative language, classic influence, and narrative synthesis. |
| Unit 3 | Moderate-high workload risk | Sequences structure/support effectiveness before purpose, related arguments, and rhetoric across texts. |
| Unit 4 | High workload risk | Treats topical research, in-depth argument, and in-depth complex analysis as one connected process. |
| Unit 5 | Manageable with integration risk | Uses authentic writing and reading contexts for language, vocabulary, style, and revision. |
| Unit 6 | Manageable if earlier reinforcement occurs | Uses prior communication reinforcement so presentation/publication is synthesis rather than first exposure. |
