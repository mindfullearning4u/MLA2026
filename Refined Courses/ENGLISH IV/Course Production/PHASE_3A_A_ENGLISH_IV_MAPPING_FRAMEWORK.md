# Phase 3A-A: English IV Mapping Framework

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the English IV Mapping Framework using the approved English I, English II, and English III production frameworks as the master template. It preserves the MLA mastery model, audit phases, naming conventions, documentation format, and course production artifact format while increasing rigor for Grade 12 English Language Arts.

This framework does not create lessons, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, or student-facing instructional content.

## Required Course Architecture

| Requirement | English IV Control |
|---|---|
| Course structure | 6 units |
| Unit structure | 8 lessons per unit |
| Lesson 8 | Putting It All Together |
| Lesson 8 assessment | Unit Assessment |
| Lessons 1-7 | Lesson Quiz included |
| Production mode | Structure-only mapping and documentation |
| Content control | No lesson content is created or modified |

## Approved English IV Unit Sequence

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Function |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Foundations of Grade 12 Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice | Establish Grade 12 paraphrase, academic vocabulary, evidence, conventions, and advanced analytical response habits. |
| Unit 2 | Advanced Literary Evaluation, Perspective, Poetry, and Narrative Synthesis | Develop functional significance, multiple theme evaluation, character perspective, major poets in historical context, figurative language, classic influence, and complex narrative writing. |
| Unit 3 | Advanced Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument Evaluation | Develop text effectiveness, central idea support, purpose, related argument evaluation, and rhetorical choices across multiple texts. |
| Unit 4 | Topical Research, Argument, and In-Depth Text Analysis | Connect topical issue research, source synthesis, in-depth argument writing, and in-depth complex text analysis. |
| Unit 5 | Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision | Refine conventions, academic vocabulary, etymology, derivations, foreign phrases, word meaning, purpose, clarity, structure, and style. |
| Unit 6 | Synthesis, Presentation, Digital Evaluation, and Multimedia Publication | Synthesize reading, research, writing, revision, oral presentation, digital presentation design/evaluation, and multimedia publication/sharing. |

## Vertical Alignment with English III

| Strand | English III Production Baseline | English IV Mapping Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Close reading | Grade 11 paraphrase, evidence, academic voice, literary research readiness. | Preserve early support while increasing Grade 12 evaluation, effectiveness, and synthesis readiness. |
| Literary analysis | Literary evaluation, themes across time/place, juxtaposition, poetry context, allegory, contemporaneous authors. | Advance to functional significance, multiple theme evaluation, character perspective development, major poets in historical context, and classic influence on contemporary world texts. |
| Informational text | Classical Period texts, multiple arguments, reasoning, validity, rhetoric evaluation. | Advance to effectiveness judgments, support improvement, author purpose, related arguments, and rhetoric across multiple texts. |
| Research | Literary research with primary and secondary sources. | Shift to topical-issue research using a variety of sources. |
| Writing | Literary analysis, complex text analysis, complex narratives with multiple perspectives. | Require in-depth arguments, in-depth complex text analysis, and narratives with universal themes. |
| Communication | Rhetorical oral presentation, audience-experience digital presentation, online/LAN publication. | Require digital presentation design/evaluation and creation, publication, and sharing of multimedia texts. |

## Benchmark Cluster Rules

| Cluster | Primary Unit | Mapping Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational Reading and Academic Response | Unit 1 | Must support every later unit and cannot become remedial-only content. |
| Advanced Literary Evaluation | Unit 2 | Must protect functional significance, multiple themes, character perspective, major poets, figurative language, classic influence, and complex narrative writing. |
| Advanced Informational Text and Rhetoric | Unit 3 | Must sequence structure/support effectiveness before purpose, related argument evaluation, and rhetorical choices across texts. |
| Topical Research and Analysis Writing | Unit 4 | Must integrate topical inquiry, source synthesis, in-depth argument, and in-depth complex text analysis. |
| Language, Vocabulary, and Revision | Unit 5 | Must use authentic reading, writing, research, and presentation contexts rather than isolated drill. |
| Oral, Digital, and Multimedia Communication | Unit 6 | Must synthesize previously taught skills and avoid introducing new major standards in Lesson 8. |

## Lesson Mapping Controls

- Lessons 1-7 may introduce, teach, practice, and assess primary standards.
- Lesson 8 must remain Putting It All Together.
- Lesson 8 must contain the Unit Assessment.
- Lesson 8 must not introduce a new primary standard.
- Most lessons should carry one primary standard.
- Two primary standards are allowed only when the standards are naturally paired.
- Support standards must reinforce instruction without becoming hidden extra lessons.
- Reading, writing, research, vocabulary, language, and communication must spiral across the course.

## Rigor Progression Controls

| Area | Required English IV Increase |
|---|---|
| Literary analysis | Move from Grade 11 evaluation to Grade 12 functional significance, multiple theme evaluation, major poets, and classic-to-contemporary influence. |
| Informational text | Move from Classical Period analysis to broader evaluation of effectiveness, support, purpose, related arguments, and rhetoric across multiple texts. |
| Research | Move from literary research to topical-issue research with varied sources. |
| Writing | Move from literary/complex analysis to in-depth argument and in-depth complex text analysis. |
| Vocabulary and language | Add commonly used foreign phrases and strengthen advanced precision. |
| Communication | Move from audience-experience presentation and publication to digital design/evaluation and multimedia sharing. |

## Assessment Architecture Controls

| Assessment Artifact | Mapping Requirement |
|---|---|
| Unit Pretest | Must align to unit standards and readiness prerequisites. |
| Notebook Evidence | Must document reading, writing, vocabulary, research, revision, and communication process work. |
| Checkpoint Submission | Must require application of the lesson's primary standard in context. |
| Lesson Quiz | Required for Lessons 1-7. |
| Unit Assessment | Required in Lesson 8 as synthesis of standards already taught. |
