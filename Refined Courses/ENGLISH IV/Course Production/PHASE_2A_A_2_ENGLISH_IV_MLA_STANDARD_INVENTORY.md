# Phase 2A-A-2: English IV MLA Standard Assignment

## Executive Summary

This report establishes the official MLA English IV standard inventory using the approved MLA English numbering framework:

`MLA.[COURSE].[DOMAIN].[NUMBER]`

For English IV, the course identifier is `ENG4`. Standards are numbered within the approved MLA English domains:

- `R` = Reading
- `W` = Writing
- `L` = Language
- `V` = Vocabulary
- `RS` = Research
- `C` = Communication

This inventory preserves the approved English I-III production frameworks while advancing the grade-level benchmark codes from Grade 11 English III to Grade 12 English IV. It does not create lessons, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, lesson JSON files, or student-facing instructional content.

Source-control note: Benchmark codes and wording were verified against the public CPALMS standards search endpoint before final certification. CPALMS returned benchmark cards for all 24 English IV benchmark codes.

## English III to English IV Comparison Findings

| Category | Finding |
|---|---|
| Course-code uniqueness | Every English IV standard is uniquely identified by `ENG4` and aligned to Grade 12 benchmark codes. |
| Structural uniqueness | English IV does not add new MLA domains beyond English I-III; rigor increases through evaluation, effectiveness judgments, in-depth analysis, topical research, and digital design/evaluation. |
| Literary progression | English IV moves beyond English III literary evaluation into functional significance, two or more themes, conflicting perspectives, major poets in historical context, and classic influence on contemporary world texts. |
| Informational progression | English IV generalizes beyond Classical Period texts into advanced evaluation of structure, support, purpose, related arguments, and rhetoric across multiple texts. |
| Writing progression | English IV emphasizes in-depth argument writing, in-depth complex text analysis, universal-theme narrative writing, and revision for purpose, clarity, structure, and style. |
