# Phase 2A-B: English IV Crosswalk Draft

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English IV Crosswalk Draft using the English IV MLA Standard Inventory as the source of record. MLA standard numbers, standard titles, and Florida benchmark codes are preserved exactly as assigned in the inventory.

This draft assigns each MLA English IV standard to an approved benchmark cluster, one primary unit, spiral reinforcement units, mastery/support status, and readiness-alignment tags. It preserves the approved English I-III production frameworks while increasing rigor for Grade 12 expectations.

## Potential Concerns

- Unit 2 and Unit 3 remain high-density evaluation units.
- Unit 4 must connect topical research to argument and in-depth analysis rather than fragmenting writing products.
- Unit 6 must remain synthesis and digital publication, not first exposure to oral or digital communication.
- English IV must not be assigned a literary tradition unless verified Grade 12 benchmarks require it.
