# English IV Unit 4 Lessons 3-4 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 4 Lessons 3 and 4 were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready assessment requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

The batch required one assessment-format correction during audit: the Lesson Quiz answer distributions had minor clustering. The correct answer letters were rebalanced mechanically while preserving the correct answer text. The audit was rerun and all checks passed.

Final decision:
