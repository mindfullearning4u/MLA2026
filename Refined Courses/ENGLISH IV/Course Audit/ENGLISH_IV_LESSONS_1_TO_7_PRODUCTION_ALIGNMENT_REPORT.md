# English IV Lessons 1-7 Production Alignment Certification Report

## Executive Summary

This certification reviewed English IV Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7 against the certified English IV curriculum foundation.

Source of truth:

- Certified Standards Inventory
- Certified Crosswalk
- Certified Unit-Level Mapping
- Certified Lesson-Level Mapping

The certified lesson mapping is the authoritative source. This audit reviewed both structure and actual instructional content. The review verified that produced lessons faithfully implement the approved mapping and that the instructional content is deep enough for Grade 12 independent mastery-based learning.

Initial validation showed that:

- Standards metadata matched the certified lesson mapping after accounting for English IV's snake_case `lesson.json` schema.
- Guided Practice and Lesson Quiz files were Moodle-ready.
- HTML structure was valid.
- Lesson focus language was present.
- Several P02-P03 instructional pages were still too compressed for the approved Grade 12 self-paced learning expectation.

Corrections were applied and the course was revalidated.

Final decision:

**PASS - STRUCTURE AND CONTENT ALIGN TO CERTIFIED LESSON MAPPING**

## Corrections Made

Mapped Grade 12 step-by-step instructional support boxes were added to all English IV P02 and P03 pages for Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7.

Total pages enhanced:

- 84 P02/P03 pages

Each added support box:

- Names the mapped lesson focus.
- Displays the mapped standard focus.
- Provides a Grade 12 thinking sequence tied to the lesson skill.
- Reinforces evaluation, functional significance, in-depth reasoning, academic voice, source synthesis, argument, complex analysis, digital evaluation, or multimedia publication as appropriate.
- Supports remedial learners through explicit process guidance.
- Supports standard learners through clear sequence and self-checking.
- Supports accelerated learners through emphasis on significance, effectiveness, audience, precision, polish, and independent judgment.

No standards were changed.

No mappings were changed.

No assessments were changed.

Existing lesson structure, examples, notebook tasks, independent work, checkpoints, visual shell, and TOR support boxes were preserved.
