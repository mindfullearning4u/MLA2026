# English IV Unit 5 Lessons 3-4 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 5 Lessons 3 and 4 were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready GIFT requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

Initial audit findings were identified in the GIFT assessment files:

- Lesson 3 Guided Practice contained one correct feedback line marked as a distractor.
- Lesson 4 Guided Practice contained one correct feedback line marked as a distractor.
- Lesson 4 Quiz contained several correct feedback lines marked as distractors and showed answer-letter clustering.

Required fixes were completed. The assessments were re-audited after correction.

**English IV Unit 5 Lessons 3-4 Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR NEXT LESSON BATCH**
