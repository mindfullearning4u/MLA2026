# English IV Unit 4 Lessons 1-2 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 4 Lessons 1 and 2 were reviewed for production readiness against the approved English IV architecture, English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, and Moodle-ready assessment requirements.

The batch initially required assessment-format corrections. The GIFT files contained correct feedback labels on some distractors and answer keys that needed normalization. Those issues were corrected, answer distribution was rebalanced, feedback was expanded beyond terse correctness labels, and the audit was rerun.

Final decision:

**English IV Unit 4 Lessons 1-2 Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR NEXT LESSON BATCH**
