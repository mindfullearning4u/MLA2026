# English IV Unit 4 Lessons 7-8 And Pretest Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 4 Lessons 7 and 8, the Unit 4 Unit Assessment, and the Unit 4 Pretest were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready assessment requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

Lesson 8 correctly functions as `Putting It All Together` and contains a Unit Assessment rather than a Lesson Quiz. The Unit 4 Pretest was created at the Unit 4 root level.

The first audit found answer-distribution imbalance in several assessment files. Correct answer letters were rebalanced mechanically while preserving answer text and feedback. The audit was rerun and all checks passed.

Final decision:

**English IV Unit 4 Lessons 7-8 and Pretest Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR UNIT 4 FINAL AUDIT**
