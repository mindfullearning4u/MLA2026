# English IV Unit 5 Lessons 5-6 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 5 Lessons 5 and 6 were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready GIFT requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

Initial audit findings were identified in the GIFT assessment files:

- Lesson 5 Guided Practice contained one correct feedback line marked as a distractor.
- Lesson 5 Quiz contained several correct feedback lines marked as distractors and showed answer-letter imbalance.
- Lesson 6 Guided Practice contained two correct feedback lines marked as distractors and showed answer-letter clustering.
- Lesson 6 Quiz contained several correct feedback lines marked as distractors and showed answer-letter clustering.

Required fixes were completed. The assessments were re-audited after correction.

**English IV Unit 5 Lessons 5-6 Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR NEXT LESSON BATCH**
