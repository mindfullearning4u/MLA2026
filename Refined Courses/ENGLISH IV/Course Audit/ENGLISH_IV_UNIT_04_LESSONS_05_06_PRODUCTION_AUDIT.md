# English IV Unit 4 Lessons 5-6 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 4 Lessons 5 and 6 were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready assessment requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

The batch required two assessment-format corrections during audit. Lesson 5 Quiz contained one correct-feedback answer that needed the correct Moodle marker, and Lesson 6 Quiz needed answer distribution balancing. Both issues were corrected and the audit was rerun.

Final decision:

**English IV Unit 4 Lessons 5-6 Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR NEXT LESSON BATCH**

- TOR reminders are included.
