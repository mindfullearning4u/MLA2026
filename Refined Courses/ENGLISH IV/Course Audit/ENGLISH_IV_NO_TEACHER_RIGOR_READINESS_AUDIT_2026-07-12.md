# English IV No-Teacher Rigor Readiness Audit

Audit date: 2026-07-12

Final decision: PASS

## Purpose

This audit was completed before Moodle transfer to confirm that English IV lessons can support independent, mastery-based student learning without live teacher instruction.

The review checked whether the course lessons provide:

- explicit step-by-step teaching
- enough detail for a student working independently
- modeled or worked examples
- common mistake correction
- embedded passage, source, scenario, or prompt context when needed
- independent work with clear parts
- checkpoint submission expectations
- mastery criteria
- Teacher of Record support language without relying on live-teacher instruction
- Grade 12 rigor aligned to the approved crosswalk and lesson mapping
