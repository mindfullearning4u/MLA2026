# English IV Unit 5 Lessons 1-2 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 5 Lessons 1 and 2 were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready assessment requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

The batch required assessment-format corrections during audit. Lesson 2 Quiz contained correct-feedback answers that needed Moodle marker correction, both quizzes needed answer distribution balancing, and Lesson 2 Quiz feedback needed expansion beyond terse correctness labels. All issues were corrected and the audit was rerun.

Final decision:

**English IV Unit 5 Lessons 1-2 Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR NEXT LESSON BATCH**

## Instructional Model Validation

The lessons support the MLA self-paced mastery model:

- Lesson 1 teaches conventions as tools for clarity, credibility, paraphrase accuracy, and revision.
- Lesson 2 teaches etymology, derivation, word-family clues, foreign phrases, context, and accurate paraphrase.
- Instruction is explicit, sequential, and student-facing.
- Embedded source notes and sentence sets appear directly in the lesson pages and assessment items.
- Students are not required to search elsewhere for the text needed to complete tasks.
- Checkpoints provide clear submission expectations and mastery criteria.
- TOR reminders are included.
