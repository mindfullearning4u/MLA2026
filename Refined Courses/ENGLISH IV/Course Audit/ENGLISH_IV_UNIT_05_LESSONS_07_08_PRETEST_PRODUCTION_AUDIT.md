# English IV Unit 5 Lessons 7-8 and Pretest Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English IV Unit 5 Lesson 7, Lesson 8, and the Unit 5 Pretest were built and audited against the approved English IV lesson-level mapping, MLA English instructional design standards, Moodle-ready GIFT requirements, and the established ALG1/English production visual model.

Lesson 8 was correctly built as **Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis** and uses a **Unit Assessment** instead of a Lesson Quiz.

Initial audit findings were identified in the GIFT files:

- Correct feedback lines were marked as distractors in multiple GIFT files.
- Answer-letter distributions were imbalanced in the Lesson 7 Quiz, Lesson 8 Unit Assessment, and Unit 5 Pretest.

Required fixes were completed. The assessments were re-audited after correction.

**English IV Unit 5 Lessons 7-8 and Pretest Production Batch: PASS - APPROVED FOR UNIT 5 FINAL AUDIT**
