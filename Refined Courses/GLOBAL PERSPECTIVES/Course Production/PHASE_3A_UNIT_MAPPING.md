# Global Perspectives Unit Mapping

Course structure: 1.0 credit, 6 units, 8 lessons per unit. Lesson 8 is synthesis and unit assessment.

| Unit | Unit Title | Purpose | Standards Covered | Required Visual/Source Supports | Simulation Required? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unit 01 | Global Inquiry, Geography, Sources, and Perspective | Build the evidence habits students need before studying complex global issues: question framing, maps, scale, source reliability, perspective, bias, and respectful multi-perspective reasoning. | MLA.GP.FND.1; MLA.GP.FND.2; MLA.GP.GEO.1 | world-region map, scale ladder, source reliability checklist, perspective matrix, claim-evidence-reasoning organizer | No simulation required |
| Unit 02 | Culture, Identity, Communication, and Representation | Teach students to analyze culture and identity carefully, avoid stereotypes, compare perspectives, and evaluate media/narratives that shape global understanding. | MLA.GP.CUL.1; MLA.GP.CUL.2; MLA.GP.FND.2 | culture iceberg model, identity web, media representation table, source perspective organizer, stereotype-vs-evidence chart | No simulation required |
| Unit 03 | Global Economies, Development, Inequality, and Technology | Use indicators, maps, charts, and scenarios to explain development, poverty, inequality, globalization, trade, labor, technology, and supply-chain tradeoffs. | MLA.GP.ECO.1; MLA.GP.ECO.2 | development indicator table, quality-of-life dashboard, supply-chain flow map, tradeoff matrix, inequality data display | No simulation required |
| Unit 04 | Governance, Human Rights, Conflict, and Cooperation | Analyze institutions, rights, international organizations, conflict causes, diplomacy, peacebuilding, and global cooperation using civic/process visuals and source evidence. | MLA.GP.GOV.1; MLA.GP.GOV.2 | human-rights framework chart, governance level diagram, conflict cause web, diplomacy pathway, international organization role table | No simulation required |
| Unit 05 | Environment, Population, Migration, Health, and Sustainability | Use environmental and demographic evidence to analyze sustainability, climate risk, resource use, migration, urbanization, public health, and resilience. | MLA.GP.ENV.1; MLA.GP.POP.1; MLA.GP.GEO.1 | climate-risk map, resource-use table, migration push-pull chart, population pyramid, resilience planning matrix | No simulation required |
| Unit 06 | Global Problem Solving, Research Synthesis, and Capstone Action | Synthesize the course by building a defensible global-issue research question, evaluating sources, comparing stakeholders, and creating an ethical action proposal. | MLA.GP.CAP.1; MLA.GP.CAP.2; MLA.GP.FND.1; MLA.GP.FND.2 | research planning board, stakeholder map, evidence synthesis matrix, action proposal logic model, impact evaluation checklist | No simulation required |

## Unit Mapping Validation

- Every MLA.GP standard appears in at least one unit.
- Unit 6 is capstone synthesis and does not introduce an unmapped outside-standard project.
- Social science source/visual supports are mandatory when mapped.
