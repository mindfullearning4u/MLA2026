# STATISTICS Course Overview

---

## Course Overview

Statistics develops students' ability to collect, represent, analyze, and interpret data in mathematical and real-world contexts. Students examine data displays, measures of center and variability, distributions, bivariate data, correlation, regression, probability, sampling methods, study design, statistical inference, random variables, probability distributions, expected value, and decision making.

The course is organized around competency-based progression. Students move through the course by demonstrating mastery of required instructional components, with emphasis on statistical reasoning, data interpretation, appropriate representation, probability reasoning, sampling decisions, inference, and real-world application.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Standards with a strong emphasis on mathematical reasoning, interpretation, justification, and application. Students engage in multi-step problem solving, data analysis, model interpretation, simulation-based reasoning, real-world application, and evaluation of statistical claims. Students are expected to demonstrate both procedural fluency and deep conceptual understanding.

---

## Course Structure

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Focus |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Unit 01 | Data Representation and Interpretation | Data types, univariate and bivariate displays, categorical and numerical data, components of displays, and segmented bar graphs |
| Unit 02 | Measures of Center, Variability, and Distributions | Measures of center, measures of variability, outliers, distribution shape, normal distributions, and population percentages |
| Unit 03 | Bivariate Data, Correlation, and Regression | Correlation and causation, bivariate numerical data, linear models, residuals, correlation coefficient, exponential models, and categorical association |
| Unit 04 | Probability Rules and Counting | Sample spaces, independence, conditional probability, empirical probability, addition and multiplication rules, permutations, and combinations |
| Unit 05 | Sampling, Bias, and Statistical Inference | Population parameters, sample statistics, sampling methods, study design, simulations, inference, experiments, treatment comparisons, and evaluating reports |
| Unit 06 | Random Variables and Decision Making | Random variables, probability distributions, expected value, binomial distributions, geometric distributions, payoff values, risk, and fair decisions |

---

## Mastery Model

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.
