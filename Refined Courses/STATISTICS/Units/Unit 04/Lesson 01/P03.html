P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now describe how events can be combined or excluded.

Union, Intersection, and Complement

  • A or B: outcomes in A, in B, or in both.
  • A and B: outcomes that are in both A and B.
  • Not A: outcomes in the sample space that are not in A.

If A = {2, 4, 6} and B = {4, 5, 6}, then A and B = {4, 6}.

Notebook Task

For the sample space {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}, let A = even numbers and B = numbers greater than 3. List A or B, A and B, and not A.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A or B means only outcomes that are in both events.
Correct: A or B means outcomes in A, in B, or in both. The word "and" means the overlap only.
