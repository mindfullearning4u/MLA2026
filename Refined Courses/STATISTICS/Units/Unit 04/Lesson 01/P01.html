Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Sample Spaces, Events, and Complements

Learn how probability begins by listing possible outcomes and describing events clearly.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.PSU.1 - I can describe events using sample spaces, subsets, unions, intersections, and complements.

What You Will Learn

  • List a sample space.
  • Identify events as subsets of a sample space.
  • Describe unions, intersections, and complements in words.
  • Use event language in real situations.

What You Will Do

  • Write possible outcomes for simple probability situations.
  • Sort outcomes into events.
  • Find what is included in A or B, A and B, and not A.
  • Explain event relationships in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using event notation and words.
  • Submit a checkpoint describing a sample space and complement.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.PSU.1: I can name all possible outcomes, describe which outcomes belong to an event, and explain what happens when events are combined or excluded.
