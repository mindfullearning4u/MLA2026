Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Interpreting Conditional Probability and Independence in Context

Learn how to recognize probability language in real situations and explain whether a condition changes a probability.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.PSU.5 - I can explain conditional probability and independence in everyday language and situations.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize words that signal conditional probability.
  • Identify the condition in everyday statements.
  • Explain independence without relying only on formulas.
  • Use context to decide whether a probability changes.

What You Will Do

  • Read probability statements carefully.
  • Compare conditional and overall probabilities.
  • Explain independence and dependence in complete sentences.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using everyday probability language.
  • Submit a checkpoint explanation with a condition and conclusion.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.PSU.5: I can explain what conditional probability and independence mean in situations people actually talk about.
