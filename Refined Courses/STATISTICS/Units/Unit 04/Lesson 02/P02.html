Notebook Title: Conditional Probability

Conditional probability means the probability of one event when another event is already known.

Vocabulary

  • Conditional probability: probability calculated under a given condition.
  • P(A | B): the probability of A given that B happened.
  • Independent events: events where knowing one event happened does not change the probability of the other.
  • Dependent events: events where knowing one event happened changes the probability of the other.

Choosing the Denominator

For P(A | B), the condition B becomes the total. If 12 of 30 students in Group B choose tutoring, then P(tutoring | Group B) = 12/30 = 40%.

Notebook Task

Explain why P(tutoring | online student) should divide by the number of online students, not by the grand total.
