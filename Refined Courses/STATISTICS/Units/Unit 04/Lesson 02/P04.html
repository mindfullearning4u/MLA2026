P04 Worked Examples

Use the examples to practice conditional probability and independence.

Worked Example 1

If 15 of 50 students in Group A choose tutoring, then P(tutoring | Group A) = 15/50 = 30%.

Worked Example 2

If P(A) = 0.25 and P(A | B) = 0.25, event B did not change the probability of A. The events may be independent.

Worked Example 3

If P(A) = 0.25 and P(A | B) = 0.60, event B changed the probability of A. The events are not independent.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: For P(A | B), divide by the total sample size every time.
Correct: For P(A | B), divide by the number of outcomes in condition B because B is the known group.
