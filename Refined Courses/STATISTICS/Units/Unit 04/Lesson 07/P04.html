P04 Worked Examples

Study all three examples. Each one shows the thinking process before the final answer.

Worked Example 1

A lunch combo has 3 sandwiches, 2 sides, and 4 drinks.

Step 1: The choices happen in stages.

Step 2: Multiply the choices: 3 × 2 × 4 = 24.

Conclusion: There are 24 possible lunch combos.

Worked Example 2

Six students are running for president, vice president, and secretary.

Step 1: The offices are different, so order matters.

Step 2: There are 6 choices for president, 5 for vice president, and 4 for secretary.

Step 3: 6 × 5 × 4 = 120.

Worked Example 3

A teacher chooses 3 students from 6 students for the same committee.

Step 1: Order does not matter because the committee is the same group.

Step 2: Use combination reasoning: 6C3 = 20.

Conclusion: There are 20 possible committees.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treat a committee as president, vice president, and secretary.
Correct: A committee with equal roles is a group, so order does not matter.
