P04 Worked Examples

Study all three examples. Each one shows the thinking process before the final answer.

Worked Example 1

A student guesses on two independent true-false questions. The chance of each correct answer is 1/2.

Step 1: The questions are independent.

Step 2: Multiply: 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4.

Conclusion: The chance of both answers being correct is 1/4.

Worked Example 2

A bag has 3 red tiles and 2 blue tiles. One tile is chosen and not replaced. What is the probability of red then blue?

Step 1: P(red first) = 3/5.

Step 2: After one red is removed, 4 tiles remain and 2 are blue, so P(blue second after red) = 2/4.

Step 3: Multiply: 3/5 × 2/4 = 6/20 = 3/10.

Worked Example 3

A weather app says P(rain) = 0.30. If it rains, P(game canceled) = 0.80.

Step 1: The question asks for rain and game canceled.

Step 2: Use the conditional probability after rain.

Step 3: 0.30 × 0.80 = 0.24.

Conclusion: The probability of rain and cancellation is 0.24.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treat drawing without replacement as independent.
Correct: Without replacement changes what remains, so the second probability must be updated.
