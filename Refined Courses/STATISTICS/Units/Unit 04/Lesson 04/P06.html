Instructions

Use the wording of each question to decide whether you need a joint, marginal, or conditional probability.

Part A

Explain the difference between a joint frequency and a marginal frequency in a two-way table.

Part B

A table shows 24 students are juniors who prefer paper notes out of 120 students total. Write the empirical probability for junior and paper notes.

Part C

Explain how you would use conditional relative frequencies to decide whether two characteristics are approximately independent.
