Notebook Title: Empirical Probability from Two-Way Tables

Build the foundation first. Read each explanation slowly and write the key ideas in your notebook.

Vocabulary

  • Two-way table: a table that organizes counts for two categorical variables.
  • Joint frequency: a count in the inside of the table showing both categories at once.
  • Marginal frequency: a row total or column total.
  • Conditional relative frequency: a percent found within a row or column group.
  • Empirical probability: probability based on observed data.

Reading a Two-Way Table

A two-way table lets you estimate probability from real data. Empirical means the probability comes from observations, not from a perfect theoretical model.
