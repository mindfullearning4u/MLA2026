Checking Approximate Independence

To decide whether two characteristics are approximately independent, compare conditional relative frequencies. If the percentages are close, the characteristics may be approximately independent. If the percentages are far apart, the condition appears to matter.

Always name what you are comparing. Do not just say "yes" or "no." Explain which percentages are close or different.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use the grand total for every probability in the table.
Correct: Use the total that matches the wording. Conditional probability uses the total from the condition group.
