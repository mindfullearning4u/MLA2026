Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Addition Rule for Probability

Learn how to find the probability that one event or another event occurs.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.PSU.7 - I can apply the addition rule for probability, including mutually exclusive and overlapping events, and interpret the result in context.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize when a probability question uses "or."
  • Identify whether events are mutually exclusive.
  • Subtract overlap when events can happen together.
  • Interpret the probability in context.

What You Will Do

  • Sort events as overlapping or mutually exclusive.
  • Use the addition rule correctly.
  • Explain why overlap is subtracted.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using the addition rule.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining overlap.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.PSU.7: I can calculate the probability of A or B and explain whether overlap affects the answer.
