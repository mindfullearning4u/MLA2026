Vocabulary

  • Addition rule: a rule for finding the probability of event A or event B.
  • Mutually exclusive events: events that cannot happen at the same time.
  • Overlap: outcomes that belong to both events.
  • A or B: outcomes in A, in B, or in both.

Why Overlap Matters

When a question asks for A or B, you are combining two event groups. If the groups overlap, the overlap gets counted twice unless you subtract it once.

The general idea is: probability of A or B equals probability of A plus probability of B minus the probability of both A and B.
