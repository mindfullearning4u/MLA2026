Random Sampling Versus Random Assignment

Random sampling helps support conclusions about a population. Random assignment helps support conclusions about treatment effects. These are different ideas. A study can have one, both, or neither.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Random sampling and random assignment mean the same thing.
Correct: Random sampling selects people from a population. Random assignment places subjects into treatment groups.

Teachable Explanation: When working with statistics, always connect the answer to the context, data type, and question being asked.
