Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Surveys, Experiments, and Observational Studies

Learn how to select and evaluate study designs based on the research question and the type of conclusion needed.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DCR.4 - I can choose an appropriate survey, experiment, or observational study design based on the research purpose.
  • MLA.STAT.DCR.5 - I can compare surveys, experiments, and observational studies and explain what conclusions each design supports.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify surveys, experiments, and observational studies.
  • Match study design to research purpose.
  • Explain when causal conclusions are limited.
  • Recognize confounding and random assignment.

What You Will Do

  • Classify study designs.
  • Compare strengths and limitations.
  • Explain what conclusions each design supports.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks about research design.
  • Submit a checkpoint comparing study designs.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DCR.4-DCR.5: I can choose a study design and explain what claims the design can and cannot support.
