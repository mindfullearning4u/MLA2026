Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Population Parameters, Sample Statistics, and Random Sampling

Learn how statisticians use samples to estimate population values and why random sampling matters for trustworthy conclusions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DCR.1 - I can distinguish a population parameter from a sample statistic.
  • MLA.STAT.DCR.2 - I can explain why random sampling helps produce representative data.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify populations and samples.
  • Distinguish parameters from statistics.
  • Explain why random sampling supports representativeness.
  • Describe why convenience samples can create bias.

What You Will Do

  • Classify numbers as parameters or statistics.
  • Compare random and non-random selection methods.
  • Explain conclusions using sampling vocabulary.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using population, sample, parameter, and statistic correctly.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining random sampling.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DCR.1-DCR.2: I can tell whether a number describes a full population or a sample, and I can explain why random sampling makes sample results more useful.
