Vocabulary

  • Population: the entire group a question is about.
  • Sample: the smaller group actually studied.
  • Parameter: a number that describes a population.
  • Statistic: a number calculated from a sample.
  • Random sample: a sample chosen so members of the population have a fair chance to be selected.

Population Versus Sample

Statistical inference starts with a practical problem: we often want to know something about a large population, but we cannot ask every person or measure every item. A sample helps us estimate what is true for the population.

  1. Identify the full group of interest. That is the population.
  2. Identify the smaller group that provided data. That is the sample.
  3. If the number describes the population, call it a parameter.
  4. If the number comes from the sample, call it a statistic.
