Why Random Sampling Matters

A random sample helps protect the study from hidden patterns in who gets selected. Random does not mean careless. It means the selection method is designed so the sample is not controlled by convenience, preference, or only one part of the population.

A random sample is more likely to be representative, which means it reflects the population well enough to support reasonable conclusions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A large sample is automatically representative.
Correct: A large sample can still be biased if it is selected poorly. Random sampling helps the sample represent the population.
