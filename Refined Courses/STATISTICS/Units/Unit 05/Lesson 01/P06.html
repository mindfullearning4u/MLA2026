📘 STATISTICS | Unit 05 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Use this page to show what you can do without step-by-step prompts.

Instructions

Answer each part using complete sentences. Focus on naming the group, the number, and how the sample was selected.

Part A

A city surveys 250 residents to estimate the percent of all city residents who use public transportation. Identify the population and sample.

Part B

The survey finds that 34% of the 250 residents use public transportation. Is 34% a statistic or a parameter? Explain.

Part C

Explain why randomly selecting residents from all neighborhoods is better than asking only people at one bus stop.
