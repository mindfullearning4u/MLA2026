📘 STATISTICS | Unit 05 | Lesson 01

P04 Worked Examples

Study all three examples. Each one shows the thinking process before the final answer.

Worked Example 1

A school wants to know the average homework time for all 900 students. It surveys 60 randomly selected students and finds an average of 42 minutes.

Step 1: The population is all 900 students.

Step 2: The sample is the 60 selected students.

Step 3: The 42 minutes is from the sample, so it is a statistic.

Worked Example 2

A report says 58% of all voters in a county support a proposal.

Step 1: The number describes all voters in the county.

Step 2: Because it describes the whole population, it is a parameter.

Conclusion: Population numbers are parameters.

Worked Example 3

A teacher chooses every student name from a complete class roster using a random number generator.

Step 1: The roster includes the full population for the class question.

Step 2: The random number generator gives students a fair chance of selection.

Conclusion: This method supports representativeness better than choosing only volunteers.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The sample average is a parameter because it estimates the population.
Correct: A sample average is still a statistic. It may estimate a parameter, but it is calculated from sample data.
