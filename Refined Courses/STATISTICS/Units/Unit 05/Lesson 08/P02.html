Unit 5 Review Vocabulary

  • Parameter and statistic: population value versus sample value.
  • Random sampling: fair selection from a population.
  • Margin of error: expected uncertainty around an estimate.
  • Simulation: repeated trials used to study variation.
  • Study design: survey, experiment, or observational study.
  • Inference: a reasoned conclusion based on data.
  • Report evaluation: checking whether evidence supports the claim.

How to Prepare for the Unit Assessment

Before choosing an answer, identify what part of inference the problem is testing. Ask: Is this about sampling, margin of error, simulation, study design, comparing groups, or evaluating a claim?

  1. Name the data collection method.
  2. Check whether random sampling or random assignment is used.
  3. Interpret the statistic, margin of error, or simulation result.
  4. State a conclusion that matches the evidence.
