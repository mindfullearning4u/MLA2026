Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

Review the major Unit 5 inference, sampling, study design, simulation, and claim-evaluation ideas before the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DCR.1-DCR.5, MLA.STAT.INF.1-INF.7, MLA.STAT.DMC.1 - I can use sampling, study design, simulation, inference, and report evaluation to make evidence-based statistical conclusions.

What You Will Learn

  • Connect sampling methods to inference.
  • Interpret margin of error and confidence level.
  • Use simulation to judge variability and significance.
  • Evaluate surveys, experiments, observational studies, and statistical reports.

What You Will Do

  • Review key Unit 5 decision points.
  • Complete mixed Guided Practice.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete the Lesson 8 Guided Practice.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence connecting Unit 5 ideas.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 5 Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can choose the correct inference or study-design reasoning by reading the situation carefully and matching the conclusion to the evidence.
