Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Evaluating Significance and Making Evidence-Based Decisions

Learn how to decide whether statistical evidence is meaningful and how to evaluate reports that make data-based claims.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.INF.7 - I can use simulations to decide whether differences between parameters are statistically meaningful.
  • MLA.STAT.DMC.1 - I can evaluate statistical reports by checking graphs, tables, sampling methods, arguments, and reported statistics.

What You Will Learn

  • Use simulation to judge whether a difference is typical or unusual.
  • Distinguish statistical significance from practical importance.
  • Evaluate statistical reports for design, sampling, graphs, and claims.
  • Explain evidence-based decisions with appropriate caution.

What You Will Do

  • Compare observed results to simulated results.
  • Check whether a report supports its conclusion.
  • Identify misleading graphs, weak sampling, and overclaims.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks about simulation and report evaluation.
  • Submit a checkpoint evaluating a statistical claim.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.INF.7 and MLA.STAT.DMC.1: I can decide whether a result is meaningful and whether a statistical report uses evidence responsibly.
