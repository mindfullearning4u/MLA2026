Evaluating Reports

A strong statistical report explains where the data came from, how the sample was chosen, what graph or table is being used, and what conclusion is reasonable. A weak report may use a misleading graph, hide the sampling method, or claim cause and effect from a design that does not support it.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: If two numbers are different, the difference must be significant.
Correct: Differences can happen because of sampling variation. Use simulation and context before deciding the difference is meaningful.
