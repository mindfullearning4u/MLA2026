Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Sample Surveys, Margin of Error, and Confidence Level

Learn how sample surveys estimate population values and how margin of error communicates uncertainty.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.INF.1 - I can estimate population totals, means, or percentages from survey data and use simulation to develop margin of error.
  • MLA.STAT.INF.2 - I can interpret margin of error and confidence level for a mean or percentage.

What You Will Learn

  • Use sample data to estimate population values.
  • Explain why estimates vary from sample to sample.
  • Interpret margin of error.
  • Interpret confidence level in everyday language.

What You Will Do

  • Build intervals from estimates and margins of error.
  • Connect simulation to sampling variability.
  • Explain uncertainty clearly.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using inference vocabulary.
  • Submit a checkpoint interpreting margin of error and confidence level.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.INF.1-INF.2: I can use sample results to estimate population values and explain how certain or uncertain those estimates are.
