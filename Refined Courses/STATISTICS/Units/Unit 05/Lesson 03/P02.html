Notebook Title: Sample Surveys, Margin of Error, and Confidence Level

Build the foundation first. Read each explanation slowly and write the key ideas in your notebook.

Vocabulary

  • Estimate: a sample-based value used to approximate a population value.
  • Margin of error: a range around an estimate that accounts for expected sampling variation.
  • Confidence level: how often the method is expected to capture the true population value in repeated sampling.
  • Simulation: a repeated model used to study how estimates vary.

From Sample Estimate to Uncertainty

A sample estimate is useful, but it is not exact. Margin of error reminds us that a different random sample could give a different estimate.

  1. Start with the sample estimate.
  2. Use simulation or repeated sampling to see how estimates vary.
  3. Report the estimate with a margin of error.
  4. Interpret the result as a reasonable interval, not a guarantee.
