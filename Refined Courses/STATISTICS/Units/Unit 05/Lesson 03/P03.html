Interpreting Confidence Level

A confidence level describes a long-run success rate for the method. For example, a 95% confidence level means that if the same sampling method were repeated many times, about 95% of the resulting intervals would capture the true population value.

It does not mean there is a 95% chance that one specific person answered correctly or that 95% of the sample agrees.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The margin of error means the survey made a mistake.
Correct: Margin of error describes expected sampling variability. It is part of honest statistical reporting.
