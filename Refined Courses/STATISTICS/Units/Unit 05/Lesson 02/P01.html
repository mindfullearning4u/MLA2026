Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Sampling Methods and Bias

Learn how different sampling methods work and how bias can affect statistical conclusions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DCR.3 - I can compare sampling methods and identify strengths, limitations, and possible bias.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify common sampling methods.
  • Compare strengths and limitations of sampling methods.
  • Recognize possible sources of bias.
  • Explain whether a method is likely to represent the population.

What You Will Do

  • Classify sampling situations.
  • Explain how samples are selected.
  • Evaluate whether a sampling plan may be biased.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks using sampling vocabulary.
  • Submit a checkpoint comparing sampling methods.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DCR.3: I can look at how data were collected and decide whether the sampling method is likely to give fair, useful results.
