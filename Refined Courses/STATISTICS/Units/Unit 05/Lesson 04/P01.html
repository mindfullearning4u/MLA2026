Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Simulated Samples and Data-Generating Processes

Learn how simulations show sampling variation and help decide whether data are consistent with a model.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.INF.3 - I can use repeated or simulated samples to examine variation in estimates or predictions.
  • MLA.STAT.INF.4 - I can evaluate whether a model is consistent with data from a data-generating process.

What You Will Learn

  • Describe why repeated samples vary.
  • Use simulated samples to study estimates.
  • Compare observed results to simulated distributions.
  • Judge whether a model is reasonable for a process.

What You Will Do

  • Interpret simulated sampling results.
  • Identify typical and unusual observed results.
  • Explain whether data support a model.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks about simulation and model checking.
  • Submit a checkpoint evaluating model consistency.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.INF.3-INF.4: I can use simulated samples to understand variation and decide whether observed data fit a proposed model.
