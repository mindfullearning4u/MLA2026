Vocabulary

  • Simulated sample: a sample generated by a model instead of collected directly from people.
  • Sampling variability: natural differences among sample results.
  • Data-generating process: the model or process believed to produce the data.
  • Consistent with a model: data that are reasonably expected under the model.

Why Simulate Samples?

Simulation lets us repeat a sampling process many times. Instead of relying on one sample, we can see the pattern of many possible sample results.

  1. Choose a model for the process.
  2. Generate repeated samples of the same size.
  3. Look at the distribution of simulated results.
  4. Decide whether the observed result is typical or unusual.
