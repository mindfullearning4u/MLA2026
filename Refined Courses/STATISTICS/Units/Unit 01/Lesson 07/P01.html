STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Selecting, Constructing, and Justifying Data Displays

Bring together data type, number of variables, display selection, interpretation, and justification.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.3 - I can construct and interpret segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.

What You Will Learn

  • Select displays that match numerical, categorical, univariate, and bivariate data.
  • Interpret components and quantities in data displays.
  • Use relative frequency tables and segmented bar graphs for categorical comparisons.
  • Justify display choices using evidence from the context.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze data contexts.
  • Choose and construct appropriate displays.
  • Explain what each display communicates.
  • Defend choices using standards-based reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint with display selection and justification.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1, MLA.STAT.STQ.2, and MLA.STAT.DVD.3: I can choose, interpret, construct, and justify data displays based on the data and the question.
