📘 STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P05 Guided Practice

Try each question before looking at the answer. Then read the feedback so you understand the reasoning, not just the final choice.

Question 1

Which display best fits one numerical variable such as daily reading minutes?

Answer: A. Histogram

Feedback: Correct. A histogram can show the distribution of one numerical variable.

Question 2

A scatterplot point represents one student with study time 4 hours and score 88. What does the point mean?

Answer: B. One case has two numerical values

Feedback: Correct. Each point in a scatterplot is a paired numerical observation.

Question 3

A relative frequency table shows Grade 9: bus 50%, car 30%, walk 20%. What should a segmented bar show?

Answer: C. A Grade 9 bar split into 50%, 30%, and 20% segments

Feedback: Correct. Segment lengths match the relative frequencies and total 100%.

Question 4

Which display best fits two numerical variables such as practice time and free throw percentage?

Answer: D. Scatterplot

Feedback: Correct. A scatterplot shows a relationship between two numerical variables.

Question 5

Why is justification important when selecting a display?

Answer: A. It explains how the display matches the data type, variables, and question

Feedback: Correct. A justification connects the display to the statistical purpose.
