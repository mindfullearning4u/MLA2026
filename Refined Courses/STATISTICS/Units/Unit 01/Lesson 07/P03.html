P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue your lesson notes. This page shows how to reason carefully before choosing or interpreting a data display.

Notebook Task Part 2

A complete display answer usually has three parts: the display choice, the reason it fits, and the conclusion it can support.

Model Reasoning

Question: How do study method preferences compare by grade?

Variables: grade and study method, both categorical.

Display: segmented bar graph using relative frequencies.

Reason: It compares percentages across grade groups.

Practice in Your Notebook

  1. Choose a display for one numerical variable.
  2. Choose a display for two numerical variables.
  3. Choose a display for two categorical variables with different group totals.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Any graph is acceptable if the data are correct."

Why this is wrong: A graph can contain correct data but still be a poor match for the question.

Correct: "The graph must match the data type, number of variables, and purpose."

Why this is correct: Use the decision process before constructing or interpreting a display.
