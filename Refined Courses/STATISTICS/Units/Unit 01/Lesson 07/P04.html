Worked Example

Context: A school compares transportation method by grade level using percentages.

Variables: grade level and transportation method, both categorical.

Display: segmented bar graph from a relative frequency table.

Justification: the display compares category proportions across groups and makes fair comparisons even if grade levels have different total numbers of students.
