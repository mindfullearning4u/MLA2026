Notebook Task Part 1

Create a decision guide for choosing displays.

  • One numerical variable: dot plot, histogram, or box plot.
  • Two numerical variables: scatterplot.
  • One categorical variable: frequency table, bar chart, or pie chart.
  • Two categorical variables: two-way table, relative frequency table, or segmented bar graph.

Key Idea

The best display depends on the data type, number of variables, and the question being investigated.
