Notebook Title

Selecting and Justifying Data Displays

Vocabulary

  • Display selection: choosing a graph or table that fits the data and question.
  • Justification: a reasoned explanation for a choice.
  • Interpretation: explaining what the display shows in context.
  • Limitation: something the display does not show or prove.
  • Relative frequency: a percent or proportion used for fair comparisons.

Step 1: Use a Display Decision Process

  1. Identify the variable or variables.
  2. Classify each variable as numerical or categorical.
  3. Decide whether the data are univariate or bivariate.
  4. Choose a display that fits the data type and purpose.
  5. Explain what the display will show.

Step 2: Justify the Display

A strong justification connects the display to the data. Do not write only, I picked a bar chart because it looks good. Explain what the chart helps compare or interpret.
