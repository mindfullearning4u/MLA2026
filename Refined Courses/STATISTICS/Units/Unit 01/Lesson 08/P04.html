P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Worked Example

Context: A school records study time, quiz score, favorite study method, and grade level.

Display choices: study time and quiz score use a scatterplot; favorite study method by grade level uses a two-way table or segmented bar graph.

Reasoning: the first question uses two numerical variables, while the second uses two categorical variables and can compare proportions.
