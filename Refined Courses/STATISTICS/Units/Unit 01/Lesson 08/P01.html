Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

Review and apply the full Unit 1 data representation and interpretation process before the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.3 - I can construct and interpret segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.
