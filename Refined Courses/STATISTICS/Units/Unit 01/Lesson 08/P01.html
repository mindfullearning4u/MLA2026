📘 STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will review the full Unit 1 learning path and prepare for the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.3 - I can construct and interpret segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.

What You Will Learn

  • Review numerical and categorical data.
  • Review univariate and bivariate displays.
  • Review display selection, construction, and interpretation.
  • Review relative frequency and segmented bar graphs.

What You Will Do

  • Complete synthesis notebook notes.
  • Practice mixed Unit 1 skills.
  • Use feedback to identify what to review before the Unit Assessment.
  • Submit a checkpoint readiness reflection.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete the Lesson 8 Guided Practice.
  • Submit Notebook Evidence and Checkpoint responses.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment covering all Unit 1 standards.
  • Demonstrate mastery across Unit 1 skills.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1, MLA.STAT.STQ.2, and MLA.STAT.DVD.3: I can bring together all Unit 1 skills to choose, construct, and interpret data displays accurately.
