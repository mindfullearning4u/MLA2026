Notebook Task Part 2

Write a short reflection explaining how to decide between a dot plot, histogram, scatterplot, bar chart, two-way table, and segmented bar graph.

Common Mistakes

  • Skipping the data type decision.
  • Confusing frequency with relative frequency.
  • Interpreting a display without reading labels and units.
  • Claiming causation from an association display.
