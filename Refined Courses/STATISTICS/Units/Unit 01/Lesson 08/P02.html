Notebook Title

Unit 1 Synthesis: Data Representation and Interpretation

Vocabulary

  • Numerical data: counts or measurements.
  • Categorical data: labels or groups.
  • Univariate: one variable.
  • Bivariate: two variables.
  • Relative frequency: percent or proportion of a total.
  • Supported conclusion: a claim backed by the display.

Step 1: Review the Unit 1 Decision Process

  1. Identify the variable or variables.
  2. Classify each variable.
  3. Decide whether the data are univariate or bivariate.
  4. Select a display.
  5. Interpret the display in context.
  6. State a supported conclusion.

Step 2: Prepare for the Unit Assessment

The Unit Assessment will ask you to use skills together. Expect questions that require classification, display selection, interpretation, and reasoning.
