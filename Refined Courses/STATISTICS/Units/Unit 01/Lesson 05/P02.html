Notebook Title

Interpreting Data Displays

Vocabulary

  • Title: names what the display is about.
  • Axis label: explains what each axis measures or represents.
  • Scale: shows the counting or measuring intervals.
  • Unit: tells what the numbers mean.
  • Conclusion: a statement supported by the data.

Step 1: Read the Display Before Answering

Start with the title, labels, scale, and units. These parts tell you what the numbers mean. Do not answer from the bars, dots, or points alone.

Step 2: Connect Quantities to Context

After reading a number, ask what it represents. A value of 12 might mean 12 students, 12 minutes, 12 points, or 12 percent depending on the display.
