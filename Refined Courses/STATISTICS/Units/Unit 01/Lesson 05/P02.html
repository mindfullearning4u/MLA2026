P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Task Part 1

Create a checklist titled How to Interpret a Data Display.

  • Read the title to identify the context.
  • Identify the variable or variables.
  • Decide whether the data are numerical or categorical.
  • Read axis labels, category labels, units, and scale.
  • Identify what quantities represent: counts, percentages, intervals, or data values.
  • State only conclusions supported by the display.

Key Idea

A display is not just a picture. Each label, scale, and quantity tells what the data mean.
