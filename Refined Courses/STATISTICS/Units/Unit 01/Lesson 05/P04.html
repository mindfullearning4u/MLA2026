P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example

Display: A bar chart shows favorite after-school activity. The tallest bar is sports with 18 students.

Variable: favorite after-school activity.

Data type: categorical.

Quantity: 18 is the frequency for the sports category.

Reasonable conclusion: Sports was chosen by more students than any other activity shown.
