P05 Guided Practice

Try each question before looking at the answer. Then read the feedback so you understand the reasoning, not just the final choice.

Question 1

In a bar chart of favorite apps, the vertical axis is labeled Number of Students. What do the bar heights represent?

Answer: A. Counts of students in each app category

Feedback: Correct. The axis label shows that each bar height is a frequency count.

Question 2

A histogram has intervals 0-10, 11-20, and 21-30 minutes. What do the intervals represent?

Answer: B. Ranges of a numerical variable

Feedback: Correct. Histogram intervals group numerical values into ranges.

Question 3

A scatterplot shows hours practiced and quiz score. What should each point represent?

Answer: C. One person or case with two numerical values

Feedback: Correct. Each point is a pair of values from the same case.

Question 4

A graph shows an upward pattern between study time and score. What conclusion is reasonable?

Answer: D. Higher study time is associated with higher scores in the data shown

Feedback: Correct. The display supports an association, not automatic causation.

Question 5

A pie chart shows 35% bus, 45% car, and 20% walk. What does 45% mean?

Answer: A. 45% of the group uses car transportation

Feedback: Correct. The slice represents that category share of the whole.
