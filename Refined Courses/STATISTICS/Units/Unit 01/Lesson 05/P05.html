STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Independent Work

Choose four data displays from class materials or a teacher-provided set. For each display, identify the title, variable or variables, data type, quantities, and one supported conclusion.

Checkpoint Prompt

A histogram shows daily homework time in intervals of 0-15, 16-30, 31-45, and 46-60 minutes. Explain what the intervals and bar heights represent, identify the data type, and state one conclusion the display can support.
