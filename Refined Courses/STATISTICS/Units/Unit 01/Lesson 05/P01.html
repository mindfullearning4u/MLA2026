Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Interpreting Components and Quantities in Data Displays

Learn how to read the title, labels, scales, categories, quantities, and context in a data display before making conclusions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify variables, units, labels, categories, and scales in displays.
  • Interpret frequencies, relative frequencies, percentages, intervals, and data values.
  • State whether a display shows numerical or categorical data.
  • Explain what conclusions are reasonable from a display.

What You Will Do

  • Read data displays closely.
  • Explain what each graph component represents.
  • Connect quantities back to context.
  • Separate supported conclusions from unsupported claims.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint interpreting a display in context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.2: I can explain what a data display shows, what each quantity means, and what conclusions the display supports.
