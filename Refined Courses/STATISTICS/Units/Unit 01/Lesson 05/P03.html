P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Task Part 2

For each display description, write what the quantities represent and one reasonable conclusion.

  1. A bar chart titled Favorite Study Location with categories home, library, classroom, and cafe.
  2. A histogram titled Daily Screen Time with intervals in hours.
  3. A scatterplot titled Practice Time and Free-Throw Percentage.
  4. A pie chart titled Transportation Method to School.

Common Mistakes

  • Reading category labels as numerical measurements.
  • Ignoring units or scale.
  • Making cause-and-effect claims from displays that only show patterns.
  • Confusing frequency with relative frequency.
