Notebook Task Part 1

Create a reference chart titled Categorical Data Displays. Include the display name, what it shows, and when it is useful.

  • Frequency table: lists categories and counts.
  • Bar chart: compares counts across categories.
  • Relative frequency table: compares proportions or percentages.
  • Pie chart: shows parts of a whole for one categorical variable.
  • Two-way table: organizes counts for two categorical variables.

Key Idea

For categorical data, displays usually compare groups using counts, proportions, or percentages.
