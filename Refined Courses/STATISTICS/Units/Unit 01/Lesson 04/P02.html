Notebook Title

Choosing Displays for Categorical Data

Vocabulary

  • Category: a group, label, or choice.
  • Bar chart: a display that compares counts or percentages across categories.
  • Segmented bar graph: a stacked bar that compares parts of a whole across groups.
  • Two-way table: a table showing counts for two categorical variables.
  • Relative frequency: a percentage or proportion of a total.

Step 1: Confirm the Values Are Categories

Category values name groups. Examples include favorite app, grade level, transportation type, and preferred music genre.

Key question: Are the values labels rather than measured amounts?

Step 2: Choose a Category Display

  • Bar chart: compare counts across one categorical variable.
  • Two-way table: organize two categorical variables.
  • Segmented bar graph: compare relative frequencies across groups.
