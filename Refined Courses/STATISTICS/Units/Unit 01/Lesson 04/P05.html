P05 Guided Practice

Try each question before looking at the answer. Then read the feedback so you understand the reasoning, not just the final choice.

Question 1

A survey records each student's favorite school lunch. Which display is appropriate?

Answer: A. Bar chart

Feedback: Correct. A bar chart compares counts across lunch categories.

Question 2

Which display lists categories with the number of responses in each category?

Answer: B. Frequency table

Feedback: Correct. A frequency table organizes categories and counts.

Question 3

A school compares preferred study location by grade level. Which display organizes the two categorical variables?

Answer: C. Two-way table

Feedback: Correct. A two-way table organizes counts for combinations of two categorical variables.

Question 4

A club wants to show the percent of members choosing each project type out of the whole club. Which display could fit?

Answer: D. Pie chart

Feedback: Correct. A pie chart can show how one categorical variable is divided into parts of a whole.

Question 5

Why is a histogram not appropriate for favorite music genre?

Answer: A. Genre is categorical, so category counts should be shown with a bar chart or table

Feedback: Correct. Histograms require numerical intervals, while genre names are categories.
