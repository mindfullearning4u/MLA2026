P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Worked Example

Question: A school wants to compare students' favorite cafeteria entrees.

Data: one categorical variable, favorite entree.

Display choice: a frequency table or bar chart.

Reasoning: both displays compare counts across categories, and a bar chart makes the largest and smallest categories easy to see.
