Instructions

Focus on whether the variable is a category and what comparison the display should support.

Part A: Identify Categorical Variables

  1. Favorite snack.
  2. Minutes spent exercising.
  3. Grade level and preferred club.

Part B: Choose Displays

Choose bar chart, two-way table, or segmented bar graph for each categorical situation.

Part C: Explain Reasoning

For two choices, explain what the display would help the viewer compare.
