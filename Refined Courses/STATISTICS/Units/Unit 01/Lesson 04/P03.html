P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue your lesson notes. This page shows how to reason carefully before choosing or interpreting a data display.

Notebook Task Part 2

For categorical data, the goal is usually comparison. Ask whether you are comparing categories within one variable or comparing two categorical variables across groups.

Model Reasoning

Question: Which lunch option is most popular?

Variable: lunch option.

Display: bar chart.

Reason: A bar chart compares counts across categories.

Practice in Your Notebook

  1. Favorite school subject by grade level.
  2. Transportation method used by students.
  3. Club membership and grade level.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "A histogram should be used for favorite music genre because many students answered."

Why this is wrong: A histogram is for numerical intervals, not category names.

Correct: "A bar chart should be used for favorite music genre."

Why this is correct: Use category displays when the data values are labels or groups.
