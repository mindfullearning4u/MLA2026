STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Representing Categorical Data

Learn how to choose displays for categorical data so categories, counts, and proportions are represented clearly.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize categorical data displays such as bar charts, frequency tables, relative frequency tables, and pie charts.
  • Choose a display for one categorical variable.
  • Choose a display for two categorical variables.
  • Explain how counts and proportions support interpretation.

What You Will Do

  • Identify categories from real contexts.
  • Match categorical questions to appropriate displays.
  • Distinguish counts from relative frequencies.
  • Justify display choices using the number of categorical variables.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining display choices for categorical data.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1: I can choose a display that matches categorical data and explain why it fits the question.
