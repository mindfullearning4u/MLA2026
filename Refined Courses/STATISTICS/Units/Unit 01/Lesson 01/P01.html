P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Numerical and Categorical Data

Learn how to tell whether data are numerical or categorical and how that choice affects display and interpretation.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify numerical data as measurements or counts.
  • Identify categorical data as labels, groups, or descriptions.
  • Choose displays that match data type.
  • Avoid treating category labels as quantities.

What You Will Do

  • Classify variables from real contexts.
  • Explain what is being measured or recorded.
  • Choose displays that match data type.
  • Interpret simple summaries in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint with classifications and justifications.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1 and MLA.STAT.STQ.2: I can decide what kind of data I have, choose a display that makes sense, and explain what the display shows in context.
