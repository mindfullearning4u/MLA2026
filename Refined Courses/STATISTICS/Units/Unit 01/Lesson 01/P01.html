P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Numerical and Categorical Data

In this lesson, you will learn how to tell whether data are numbers that measure or count something, or categories that name a group, label, or choice.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify numerical data as values that measure, count, or describe an amount.
  • How to identify categorical data as values that name groups, labels, choices, or descriptions.
  • Why a number can sometimes be a label instead of a quantity.
  • How data type helps you choose a display and explain what the data show.

What You Will Do

  • Read situations and identify the variable being recorded.
  • Decide whether each variable is numerical or categorical.
  • Explain your reasoning using the context, not just the appearance of the values.
  • Practice choosing displays that fit the data type.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes that define and compare numerical and categorical data.
  • Classify variables and justify each classification in guided and independent practice.
  • Submit a checkpoint that shows you can classify data in context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1 and MLA.STAT.STQ.2: I can look at a situation, decide what kind of data are being collected, choose a display that makes sense, and explain what the data show without making a claim that the data do not support.
