STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Independent Work

Choose five variables from a school, sports, job, or community context. For each variable, identify whether it is numerical or categorical and name one display that would fit the data type.

Checkpoint Prompt

A survey records students' grade levels, daily screen time in hours, favorite app category, and number of notifications received yesterday. Classify each variable and justify each classification.
