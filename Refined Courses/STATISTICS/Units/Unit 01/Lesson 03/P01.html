STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Representing Numerical Data

Learn how to choose displays for numerical data based on whether the data describe one variable or a relationship between two numerical variables.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize numerical data displays such as dot plots, histograms, box plots, and scatterplots.
  • Choose a display for one numerical variable.
  • Choose a display for two numerical variables.
  • Explain why the display matches the data and question.

What You Will Do

  • Sort numerical data contexts by display type.
  • Match statistical questions to dot plots, histograms, box plots, or scatterplots.
  • Describe what each display is designed to show.
  • Justify display choices using data type and number of variables.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining display choices for numerical data.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1: I can look at numerical data, decide what question is being asked, and choose a display that fits the data.
