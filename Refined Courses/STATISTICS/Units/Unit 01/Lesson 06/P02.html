Notebook Task Part 1

Create a reference page titled Relative Frequency and Segmented Bars.

  • Frequency: a count.
  • Relative frequency: a fraction, decimal, or percent of a total.
  • Segmented bar graph: a bar split into sections that show parts of a whole.
  • Each segmented bar should total 100% when using percentages.

Key Idea

Relative frequencies help compare groups fairly when the groups have different total sizes.
