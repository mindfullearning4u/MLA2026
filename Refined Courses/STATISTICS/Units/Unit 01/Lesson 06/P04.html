P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Worked Example

Relative frequency table: Group A has 40% yes and 60% no. Group B has 55% yes and 45% no.

Segmented bar graph: Draw two equal-length bars. Split Group A at 40% and 100%. Split Group B at 55% and 100%.

Interpretation: Group B has the larger yes segment, so a greater proportion of Group B answered yes.
