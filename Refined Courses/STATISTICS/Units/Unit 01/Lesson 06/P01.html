P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Relative Frequency Tables and Segmented Bar Graphs

Learn how to use relative frequency tables to construct and interpret segmented bar graphs for categorical data.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.3 - I can construct and interpret segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.

What You Will Learn

  • Read relative frequency tables using proportions and percentages.
  • Construct segmented bar graphs from relative frequencies.
  • Interpret category segments within each bar.
  • Compare groups using relative frequency instead of raw counts when group sizes differ.

What You Will Do

  • Convert category information into relative frequencies.
  • Build segmented bars that total 100% for each group.
  • Compare segment sizes across groups.
  • Explain conclusions in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint constructing and interpreting a segmented bar graph.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DVD.3: I can turn a relative frequency table into a segmented bar graph and explain what the segments mean.
