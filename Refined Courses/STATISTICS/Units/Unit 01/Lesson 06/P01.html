Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Relative Frequency Tables and Segmented Bar Graphs

In this lesson, you will learn how relative frequency tables and segmented bar graphs show percentages within categories.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.3 - I can construct and interpret segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.

What You Will Learn

  • Read relative frequencies as proportions or percentages.
  • Construct segmented bar graphs from relative frequency tables.
  • Compare groups using percentages instead of raw counts.
  • Interpret what each segment represents.

What You Will Do

  • Calculate or use relative frequencies.
  • Convert table values into segmented bar sections.
  • Compare category patterns across groups.
  • Explain results in context.

How You Will Show Mastery
