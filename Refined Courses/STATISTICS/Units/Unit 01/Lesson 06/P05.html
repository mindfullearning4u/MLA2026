P05 Guided Practice

Try each question before looking at the answer. Then read the feedback so you understand the reasoning, not just the final choice.

Question 1

A relative frequency table shows Group A: yes 40%, no 60%. How long should the yes segment be in a 100% segmented bar?

Answer: A. 40% of the bar

Feedback: Correct. Segment length matches the relative frequency for that category.

Question 2

Why are relative frequencies useful when comparing two groups of different sizes?

Answer: B. They compare proportions instead of raw counts

Feedback: Correct. Proportions make comparisons fair when totals differ.

Question 3

In a segmented bar graph, what should the total length of each bar represent?

Answer: C. 100% of that group

Feedback: Correct. Each segmented bar represents the whole group split into category parts.

Question 4

A table shows Grade 9: walk 30%, bus 50%, car 20%. Which segment should be longest?

Answer: D. Bus

Feedback: Correct. Bus has the largest relative frequency at 50%.

Question 5

A segmented bar graph uses the same category order in every bar. Why is this important?

Answer: A. It makes comparisons across groups clearer

Feedback: Correct. Consistent category order prevents misreading segment positions.
