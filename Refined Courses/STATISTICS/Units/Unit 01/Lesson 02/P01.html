P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Univariate and Bivariate Data Displays

Distinguish between univariate and bivariate data and connect each type to appropriate displays.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify one-variable data as univariate.
  • Identify two-variable data as bivariate.
  • Match univariate and bivariate data to appropriate displays.
  • Describe what a display can and cannot show.

What You Will Do

  • Sort statistical questions by number of variables.
  • Connect dot plots, histograms, bar charts, scatterplots, and two-way tables to contexts.
  • Interpret what each display communicates.
  • Explain why the number and type of variables matter.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks comparing one-variable and two-variable situations.
  • Submit a checkpoint selecting displays for contexts.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1 and MLA.STAT.STQ.2: I can tell how many variables a question uses, choose a matching display, and explain the meaning in context.
