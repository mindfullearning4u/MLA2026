P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Univariate and Bivariate Data Displays

In this lesson, you will learn how to decide whether a data set studies one variable or two variables, and how that decision affects the display you should use.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.STQ.1 - I can identify the type of data, the statistical question being investigated, and the most appropriate display for the context.
  • MLA.STAT.STQ.2 - I can interpret a data display by explaining the variables, data type, quantities, context, and what can reasonably be concluded.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify univariate data as data about one variable.
  • Identify bivariate data as data about two variables.
  • Connect one-variable and two-variable data to appropriate displays.
  • Explain what a display can and cannot show.

What You Will Do

  • Read situations and identify the variable or variables being studied.
  • Classify data as univariate or bivariate.
  • Match data sets to displays such as bar charts, histograms, dot plots, and scatterplots.
  • Interpret simple displays in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes on univariate and bivariate data.
  • Classify data sets and justify your decisions.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining display choices.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.STQ.1 and MLA.STAT.STQ.2: I can decide whether a question studies one variable or the relationship between two variables, then choose and interpret a display that matches the data.
