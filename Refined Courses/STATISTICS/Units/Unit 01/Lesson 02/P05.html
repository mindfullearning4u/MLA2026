STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 02

P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Independent Work

Write four statistical questions: two univariate and two bivariate. For each question, identify the variable or variables and choose an appropriate display.

Checkpoint Prompt

A school collects data about each student: minutes of exercise per day and preferred type of exercise. Explain whether each variable is numerical or categorical, whether the dataset is univariate or bivariate, and which display could help answer a relationship question.
