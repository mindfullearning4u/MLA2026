STATISTICS | Unit 01 | Lesson 02

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Task Part 2

For each statistical question, identify the variable or variables, decide whether the data are univariate or bivariate, and choose a reasonable display.

  1. How many minutes do students spend reading each night?
  2. Is there a relationship between hours of sleep and reaction time?
  3. Which after-school club is most common among ninth graders?
  4. Do grade levels relate to preferred study location?

Common Mistakes

  • Counting categories as variables.
  • Using a scatterplot for two categorical variables.
  • Using a histogram for categorical labels.
