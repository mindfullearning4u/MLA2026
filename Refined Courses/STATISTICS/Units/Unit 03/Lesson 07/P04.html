P04 Worked Examples

Use each example to practice relative-frequency reasoning.

Worked Example 1

In a class of 100 students, 35 prefer video notes. The joint relative frequency is 35/100 = 35% if the table cell represents one combined category.

Worked Example 2

If 45 of 120 students are seniors, the marginal relative frequency for seniors is 45/120 = 37.5%.

Worked Example 3

If 21 of 30 students in Group A prefer tutoring, the conditional relative frequency is 21/30 = 70%. This means 70% of Group A prefers tutoring.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Every relative frequency uses the grand total.
Correct: Joint and marginal relative frequencies use the grand total, but conditional relative frequencies use a row or column total.
