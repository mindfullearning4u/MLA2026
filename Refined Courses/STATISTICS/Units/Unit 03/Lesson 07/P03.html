📘 STATISTICS | Unit 03 | Lesson 07

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Conditional relative frequencies compare within a row or within a column. They are useful when group sizes are different.

Conditional Comparison

If 30 of 50 online students prefer video notes, the conditional relative frequency within online students is 30/50 = 60%. If 18 of 60 in-person students prefer video notes, the conditional relative frequency within in-person students is 18/60 = 30%.

The percentages are different, so learning format and note preference may be associated.

Notebook Task

Compare: 16 of 40 Group A students choose option 1, and 24 of 80 Group B students choose option 1. Which group has the higher conditional relative frequency?

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Group B has the stronger preference because 24 is greater than 16.
Correct: Group A has 16/40 = 40%, while Group B has 24/80 = 30%. Percentages give the fair comparison when group sizes differ.

Teachable Explanation: When working with statistics, always connect the answer to the context, data type, and question being asked.
