Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Interpreting Relative Frequencies and Categorical Relationships

Learn how relative frequencies help compare categorical groups fairly and solve real-world problems.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.BVM.8 - I can construct two-way relative frequency tables from marginal and conditional relative frequencies.
  • MLA.STAT.BVM.9 - I can interpret joint, marginal, and conditional relative frequencies in context.
  • MLA.STAT.BVM.10 - I can solve real-world problems involving univariate and bivariate categorical data.

What You Will Learn

  • Convert counts to relative frequencies.
  • Distinguish joint, marginal, and conditional relative frequencies.
  • Compare groups using percentages instead of only raw counts.
  • Use categorical data to support careful conclusions.

What You Will Do

  • Read two-way relative frequency tables.
  • Construct missing table entries from totals and percentages.
  • Interpret categorical relationships in context.
  • Explain whether data suggest an association.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks with relative-frequency interpretations.
  • Submit a checkpoint comparing categorical groups.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.BVM.8, MLA.STAT.BVM.9, and MLA.STAT.BVM.10: I can use percentages in two-way tables to compare groups and make careful conclusions from categorical data.
