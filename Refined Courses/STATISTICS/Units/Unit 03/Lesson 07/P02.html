Notebook Title: Relative Frequencies and Categorical Relationships

Relative frequency means a part compared to a whole. It is often written as a decimal or percent.

Vocabulary

  • Relative frequency: a count divided by a total.
  • Joint relative frequency: an inside-cell count divided by the grand total.
  • Marginal relative frequency: a row or column total divided by the grand total.
  • Conditional relative frequency: a count divided by a specific row or column total.

How to Calculate

If 18 out of 60 students prefer video notes, the relative frequency is 18/60 = 0.30 = 30%.

  1. Identify the part.
  2. Identify the correct total.
  3. Divide part by total.
  4. Interpret the result in context.

Notebook Task

Calculate and interpret: 24 of 80 students prefer online tutoring. Write the relative frequency as a decimal and percent.
