Notebook Title: Strength, Direction, and Correlation Coefficient

Correlation describes how closely two numerical variables follow a straight-line pattern.

Vocabulary

  • Positive correlation: y tends to increase as x increases.
  • Negative correlation: y tends to decrease as x increases.
  • Strength: how closely points follow a line.
  • Correlation coefficient: a technology-computed value, often called r, between -1 and 1.
  • Strong correlation: points are close to a line.
  • Weak correlation: points are spread out and do not closely follow a line.

Reading r

The correlation coefficient r tells both direction and strength.

  • r close to 1: strong positive linear relationship.
  • r close to -1: strong negative linear relationship.
  • r close to 0: weak or no linear relationship.

Notebook Task

Write one sentence for each value: r = 0.91, r = -0.84, and r = 0.12. Include direction and strength.
