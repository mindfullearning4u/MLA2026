P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now connect scatterplots, residuals, and correlation coefficients.

Combining Evidence

If a scatterplot slopes upward, residuals do not show a curve, and r = 0.88, then the data show a strong positive linear relationship. The coefficient is close to 1, and the graph supports a linear pattern.

If r = -0.78, the relationship is negative and fairly strong because the value is close to -1.

Notebook Task

A technology output gives r = -0.62 for commute time and attendance rate. Write a sentence describing the direction, strength, and context.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: r = -0.90 means there is almost no relationship because the number is negative.
Correct: r = -0.90 means a strong negative linear relationship. The negative sign gives direction; closeness to -1 gives strength.

Teachable Explanation: When working with statistics, always connect the answer to the context, data type, and question being asked.
