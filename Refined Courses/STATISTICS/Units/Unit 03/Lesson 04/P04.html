P04 Worked Examples

Use each example to practice interpreting direction, strength, and r.

Worked Example 1

Technology output: r = 0.93 for practice time and performance score.

  1. The sign is positive.
  2. The value is close to 1.
  3. The relationship is strong and positive.
  4. In context: more practice time is strongly associated with higher performance scores.

Worked Example 2

Technology output: r = -0.81 for commute time and attendance rate.

  1. The sign is negative.
  2. The value is close to -1.
  3. The relationship is strong and negative.
  4. In context: longer commute time is strongly associated with lower attendance rate.

Worked Example 3

Technology output: r = 0.08 for shoe size and quiz score.

  1. The value is close to 0.
  2. The relationship is weak or has no clear linear association.
  3. In context: shoe size and quiz score do not show a meaningful linear relationship in the data.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: r = 0.95 proves one variable caused the other.
Correct: r = 0.95 shows a strong positive linear association. It does not prove causation by itself.
