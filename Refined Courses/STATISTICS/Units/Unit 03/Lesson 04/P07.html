P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint shows whether you can interpret correlation strength, direction, and coefficient values.

TOR Information

The TOR should look for correct direction, strength, coefficient interpretation, context, and the reminder that correlation does not prove causation.

Submission Workflow

Submit your checkpoint response in the assigned Moodle location.

Checkpoint Task

Technology gives r = -0.74 for daily screen time and hours of sleep. Interpret the strength and direction of the relationship in context. Then explain whether this value proves screen time causes less sleep.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies the relationship as negative.
  • Describes the strength as moderately strong or strong.
  • Connects the interpretation to screen time and sleep.
  • Explains that correlation does not prove causation by itself.
