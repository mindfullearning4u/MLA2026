P04 Worked Examples

Use each example to practice reading and completing two-way frequency tables.

Worked Example 1

A cell says 22 students are seniors who prefer online tutoring. This is a joint frequency because it combines one grade category and one preference category.

Worked Example 2

If the row counts are 14 and 26, the row total is 40 because 14 + 26 = 40. Row totals are marginal frequencies.

Worked Example 3

If 30 of 40 online students prefer video notes and 15 of 40 in-person students prefer video notes, the preference differs by learning format. This suggests an association between learning format and note preference.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A marginal total is a count inside the table.
Correct: A marginal total is a row total or column total at the edge of the table. A count inside the table is a joint frequency.
