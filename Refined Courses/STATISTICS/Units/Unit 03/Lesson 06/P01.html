Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Two-Way Frequency Tables and Categorical Association

Learn how two-way frequency tables organize two categorical variables and help identify possible associations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.BVM.7 - I can build and interpret two-way frequency tables for bivariate categorical data.

What You Will Learn

  • Organize bivariate categorical data in a two-way table.
  • Identify joint frequencies and marginal totals.
  • Use row and column totals to check a table.
  • Describe possible association between categorical variables.

What You Will Do

  • Read and complete two-way frequency tables.
  • Find totals across rows and columns.
  • Interpret table cells in context.
  • Compare categories to decide whether an association may exist.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks building and interpreting tables.
  • Submit a checkpoint using a two-way table.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.BVM.7: I can use a two-way table to summarize two categorical variables and explain what the counts show.
