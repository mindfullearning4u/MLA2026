📘 STATISTICS | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Complete all parts independently. Your TOR can use this work to decide whether you are ready to move forward or need more support.

Instructions

Use table language carefully: joint frequency for inside cells and marginal frequency for row or column totals.

Part A

Create a two-way table with two row categories and two column categories. Include row totals and column totals.

Part B

Choose one inside cell from your table and explain it as a joint frequency in context.

Part C

Write one sentence explaining whether your table might suggest association between the two categorical variables.

Need Help?

If you are unsure about any step, contact your TOR for help or guidance before moving forward. Support is available, and asking early helps prevent small misunderstandings from becoming bigger problems.
