P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint shows whether you can interpret a two-way frequency table.

TOR Information

The TOR should look for correct table vocabulary, correct totals, and a careful association statement based on the counts.

Submission Workflow

Submit your checkpoint response in the assigned Moodle location.

Checkpoint Task

A table shows 24 of 40 online students prefer video notes, while 12 of 40 in-person students prefer video notes. Identify the variables, describe what one joint frequency means, and explain whether learning format and note preference may be associated.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies learning format and note preference as the two categorical variables.
  • Correctly explains an inside-cell count as a joint frequency.
  • Uses the counts to support a possible association statement.
  • Avoids claiming causation from the table alone.
