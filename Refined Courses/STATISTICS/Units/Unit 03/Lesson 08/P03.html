📘 STATISTICS | Unit 03 | Lesson 08

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now connect evidence to conclusions.

Conclusion Rules

  • Use "associated with" when evidence shows a relationship but not cause.
  • Use residuals to judge how well a linear model predicts.
  • Use r to describe linear strength and direction.
  • Use percentages to compare categorical groups fairly.

Notebook Task

Write one careful conclusion for a numerical relationship and one careful conclusion for a categorical relationship.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Strong association means one variable caused the other.
Correct: Strong association describes a relationship. Causation needs stronger study evidence and careful reasoning.
