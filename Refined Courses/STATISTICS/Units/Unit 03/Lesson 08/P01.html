Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

Review bivariate data, models, residuals, correlation, two-way tables, and categorical relationships.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.BVM.1-BVM.10 - I can analyze bivariate numerical and categorical data using association, models, residuals, correlation, and two-way tables.

What You Will Learn

  • Choose appropriate conclusions for bivariate relationships.
  • Interpret linear and exponential models in context.
  • Use residuals and correlation to judge model fit.
  • Use two-way tables and relative frequencies to compare categorical groups.

What You Will Do

  • Submit checkpoint evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit checkpoint evidence connecting Unit 3 ideas.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 3 Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use the correct tool for the data: careful association language, a model for numerical data, residuals and correlation for fit, and two-way tables for categorical data.
