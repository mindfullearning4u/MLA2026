Use the model and context carefully. Do not treat slope and y-intercept as just numbers; explain what they mean.

Practice Questions

  1. In y = 6x + 10, what is the slope?
  2. In y = 6x + 10, what does the y-intercept mean if x is hours and y is cost?
  3. Use y = 4x + 30 to predict y when x = 5.
  4. Why can prediction far outside the data range be risky?
  5. Which scatterplot pattern supports a linear model?
