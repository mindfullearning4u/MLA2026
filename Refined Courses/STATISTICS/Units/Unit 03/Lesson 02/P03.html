P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now use a linear model to make a prediction and decide whether the prediction is reasonable.

Using the Model

A model for total cost is y = 12x + 30, where x is the number of hours and y is the predicted cost in dollars.

  1. To predict the cost for 4 hours, replace x with 4.
  2. y = 12(4) + 30
  3. y = 48 + 30
  4. y = 78
  5. The predicted cost for 4 hours is $78.

Notebook Task

Use y = 7x + 15, where x is the number of sessions and y is predicted total cost.

  1. Predict the cost for 6 sessions.
  2. Explain what the slope 7 means in context.
  3. Explain what the y-intercept 15 means in context.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: In y = 7x + 15, the slope is 15 because it is the last number.
Correct: The slope is 7 because it is the number multiplied by x. The 15 is the y-intercept, or predicted starting value when x = 0.

Teachable Explanation: When working with statistics, always connect the answer to the context, data type, and question being asked.
