Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Linear Models for Bivariate Numerical Data

Learn how a line can model a relationship between two numerical variables and how slope and y-intercept are interpreted in context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.BVM.2 - I can fit a linear model to bivariate data and interpret slope and intercept in context.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize when bivariate numerical data suggest a linear model.
  • Use a linear equation such as y = 3x + 20 to make predictions.
  • Interpret slope as a rate of change in context.
  • Interpret the y-intercept as the predicted value when x = 0, when that value makes sense.

What You Will Do

  • Connect scatterplot patterns to linear models.
  • Read slope and intercept from a model equation.
  • Substitute values into a model to predict outcomes.
  • Explain whether a prediction is reasonable in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks explaining slope and intercept.
  • Submit a checkpoint using a linear model in context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.BVM.2: I can use a line to model two numerical variables and explain what the line means in the real situation.
