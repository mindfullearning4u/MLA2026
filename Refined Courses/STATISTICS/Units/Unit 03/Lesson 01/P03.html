P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will practice separating an association claim from a causation claim.

How to Read the Claim

Suppose a school finds that students who sleep at least 8 hours tend to have higher test scores. The data show an association between sleep time and test score.

  1. The two variables are sleep time and test score.
  2. The pattern is positive because higher sleep time is connected with higher scores.
  3. The data do not automatically prove sleep caused the higher scores.
  4. Other explanations could include study habits, health, schedule, or stress level.

Notebook Task

Write a careful conclusion for this situation: A city finds that neighborhoods with more parks also have higher reported exercise levels.

Use this structure: "The data show that ______ is associated with ______. The data do not prove ______ because ______."

Common Mistake

Incorrect: More parks caused people to exercise more.
Correct: More parks are associated with higher reported exercise levels. This is better because the data show a relationship, but another factor such as income, walkability, or community programs could also affect exercise.

Teachable Explanation: When working with statistics, always connect the answer to the context, data type, and question being asked.
