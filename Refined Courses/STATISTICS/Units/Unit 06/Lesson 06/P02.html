Notebook Title: Payoff Tables

A payoff is the gain or loss connected to an outcome. A payoff table organizes possible outcomes, probabilities, and values.

Vocabulary

  • Payoff: the gain or loss from an outcome.
  • Strategy: a choice or plan being compared.
  • Expected value: long-run average payoff.
  • Risk: chance of an unfavorable or highly variable result.
  • Decision rule: the reason used to choose one strategy over another.

Step 1: Organize Outcomes

Suppose Strategy A has a 0.70 chance of gaining $10 and a 0.30 chance of losing $5. The payoff table should list each outcome, its probability, and its payoff.

Step 2: Connect to Expected Value

Expected value is found by multiplying each payoff by its probability, then adding the products. Negative payoffs represent losses and must be included as negative values.

Notebook Task Part 1

Create a payoff table for a simple strategy with two possible outcomes: one gain and one loss. Include probability and payoff for each outcome.
