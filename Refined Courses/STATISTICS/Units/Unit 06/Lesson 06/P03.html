Notebook Title: Comparing Strategies

Expected value helps compare long-run averages, but risk helps explain how uncertain or costly the outcomes may be.

Expected Value Example

Strategy A: 0.70 chance of $10 and 0.30 chance of -$5.

Expected value = 10(0.70) + (-5)(0.30) = 7 - 1.50 = $5.50.

Risk Interpretation

A strategy can have a positive expected value and still include risk. If a strategy has a chance of a large loss, that risk should be mentioned when making a recommendation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Always choose the strategy with the highest possible payoff."

Correct: "Compare expected value and risk, not just the best possible outcome."

The highest payoff may have a very small chance or a large downside.
