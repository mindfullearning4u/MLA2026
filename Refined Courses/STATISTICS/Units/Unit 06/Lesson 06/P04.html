P04 Worked Example

Study each example step by step. The goal is to compare expected value and risk.

Example 1: Calculate Expected Value

A strategy has 0.80 probability of gaining $5 and 0.20 probability of losing $10.

Expected value = 5(0.80) + (-10)(0.20) = 4 - 2 = $2.

Example 2: Compare Two Strategies

Strategy A has expected value $2 with a possible $10 loss. Strategy B has expected value $1.50 with no loss. A has the higher long-run average, but B has lower risk.

Example 3: Recommendation

If the goal is the highest long-run average, choose Strategy A. If avoiding losses is more important, choose Strategy B. A strong recommendation states the decision rule.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Ignore negative payoffs because they are not winnings."

Correct: "Include negative payoffs as losses in the expected value calculation."

Leaving out losses makes a strategy look better than it really is.
