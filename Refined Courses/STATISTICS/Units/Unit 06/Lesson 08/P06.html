P06 Independent Work

Use this page to check whether your notebook is ready before the Unit Assessment.

Instructions

Answer each part in complete work. Include calculations and written interpretations.

Part A

Build a probability distribution for values 0, 1, and 2 with probabilities 0.25, 0.50, and 0.25. Calculate the expected value.

Part B

Decide whether this situation is binomial or geometric: a player shoots until making the first basket. Explain your choice.

Part C

Design a fair method for choosing one of five groups. State the probability for each group and explain why the method is fair.
