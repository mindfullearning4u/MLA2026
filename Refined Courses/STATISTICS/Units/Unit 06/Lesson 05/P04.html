P04 Worked Example

Study each example step by step. The goal is to identify the first-success pattern.

Example 1: Identify the Situation

A person calls customers until the first customer answers. Success is an answered call. This is geometric because the count stops at the first success.

Example 2: First Success on Trial 2

If p = 0.30, failure probability is 0.70. First success on trial 2 means failure, success: 0.70 × 0.30 = 0.21.

Example 3: First Success on Trial 4

If p = 0.50, failure probability is 0.50. First success on trial 4 means failure, failure, failure, success: 0.50 × 0.50 × 0.50 × 0.50 = 0.0625.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Use success probability for every trial before the first success."

Correct: "Use failure probability before the first success, then success probability on the final trial."

The trials before the first success must be failures.
