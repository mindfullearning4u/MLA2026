P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Title: Geometric Probability Patterns

For first success on a specific trial, multiply the probabilities for each failure before it and the success at the end.

The Pattern

If p is the probability of success, then 1 - p is the probability of failure. First success on trial 3 means failure, failure, success.

Example

If p = 0.20, then failure probability is 0.80. First success on trial 3 has probability 0.80 Ã— 0.80 Ã— 0.20 = 0.128.

This means there is a 0.128 probability of two failures followed by the first success.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "First success on trial 3 means success, success, success."

Correct: "First success on trial 3 means failure, failure, success."

The word first matters. Earlier successes are not allowed.
