Notebook Title: Recognizing Geometric Situations

A geometric situation counts the number of trials needed to get the first success.

Vocabulary

  • Geometric situation: repeated trials until the first success occurs.
  • Success: the outcome being waited for.
  • Failure: any trial before the first success.
  • p: probability of success on one trial.
  • First success: the first time the desired outcome happens.

Step 1: Ask What Is Being Counted

If the problem asks "How many tries until the first success?" it is usually geometric. For example, count how many calls are needed until the first person answers.

Step 2: Describe the Pattern

To get the first success on trial 4, the first three trials must be failures and the fourth trial must be a success.

The pattern is failure, failure, failure, success.

Notebook Task Part 1

Write one real-world situation where someone repeats an action until the first success. Identify the success, failure, and what number is being counted.
