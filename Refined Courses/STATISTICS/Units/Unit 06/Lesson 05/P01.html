Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Geometric Distributions

Learn how geometric situations count how many trials are needed until the first success occurs.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.PSU.15 - I can solve real-world problems involving geometric distributions.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize geometric situations.
  • Identify success, failure, and probability of success.
  • Explain why the first success matters.
  • Calculate and interpret simple geometric probabilities.

What You Will Do

  • Decide whether a context is geometric.
  • Use the pattern failures before first success.
  • Interpret probabilities in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook work identifying geometric features.
  • Answer guided practice questions about first success.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining a geometric context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.PSU.15: I can recognize when a problem asks how long it takes to get the first success and explain the probability pattern clearly.
