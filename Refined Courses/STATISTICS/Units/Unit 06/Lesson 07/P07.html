📘 STATISTICS | Unit 06 | Lesson 07

P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint lets your TOR see whether you can design and explain a fair probability-based decision method.

TOR Information

The student should be able to identify eligible outcomes, assign equal probabilities when equal chances are required, and explain why the method is fair.

Submission Workflow

Submit your checkpoint response in Moodle. Include your probability reasoning and a short explanation of fairness.

Checkpoint Task

Design a fair method to choose one student from eight students. You may use drawing lots or a random number generator. State each student's probability and explain why your method is fair.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies all eligible outcomes.
  • Assigns equal probability to each eligible student.
  • Uses a random method that matches the intended probabilities.
  • Explains fairness clearly using probability language.
