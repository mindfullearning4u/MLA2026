Notebook Title: Theoretical Probability Distributions

A theoretical probability distribution is built from what should happen based on the design of the chance process, not from collected trial results.

Vocabulary

  • Theoretical probability: probability based on the structure of a chance process.
  • Theoretical distribution: a probability distribution built from theoretical probabilities.
  • Expected value: the long-run average value of a random variable.
  • Weighted average: an average where values count more or less depending on their probabilities.
  • Long-run average: what the average tends to approach after many repetitions.

Step 1: Build From Possible Outcomes

Suppose a fair spinner has four equal sections labeled 1, 2, 3, and 4. Because the sections are equal, each value has probability 1/4, or 0.25.

This is theoretical because the probabilities come from the spinner design. You do not need to spin the spinner first.
