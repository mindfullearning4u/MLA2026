For mixed problems, follow this order: identify the data feature, choose the correct measure or model, calculate if needed, and write a conclusion in context.

Common Mistake

Problem: A normal model estimate says about 95% are within two standard deviations.

Incorrect: This is exact for every data set.

The normal model gives an approximate estimate and only fits appropriate data.

Correct: This is an approximate estimate when the normal model is appropriate.

Always connect the estimate to model appropriateness.
