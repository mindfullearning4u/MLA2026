Notebook Title

Putting It All Together

Vocabulary

Synthesis: Using several learned skills together in one task.

Distribution Summary: A complete description of center, spread, shape, and unusual features.

Normal Model Estimate: An approximate population percentage based on a fitted normal model.

Concept Development

Unit 2 started with center and variability. Center describes a typical value. Variability describes spread. Shape, skew, clusters, and outliers explain why one summary may be better than another.

Normal models add another layer. If a distribution is roughly symmetric and mound-shaped, mean and standard deviation can support percentage estimates using the empirical rule.
