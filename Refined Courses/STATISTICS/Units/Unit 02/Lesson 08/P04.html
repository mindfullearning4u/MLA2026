Worked Example 3: Estimate a Percentage

Problem: A normal model has mean 100 and standard deviation 10. Estimate the percent between 90 and 110.

Step 1: Identify 90 and 110 as one standard deviation from the mean.

Step 2: Use the empirical rule.

Step 3: State the estimate in context.

About 68%

Reasoning: One standard deviation on both sides contains about 68% in a normal model.

Common Mistake

Problem: A normal model estimate says about 95% are within two standard deviations.

Incorrect: This is exact for every data set.

The normal model gives an approximate estimate and only fits appropriate data.

Correct: This is an approximate estimate when the normal model is appropriate.

Always connect the estimate to model appropriateness.
