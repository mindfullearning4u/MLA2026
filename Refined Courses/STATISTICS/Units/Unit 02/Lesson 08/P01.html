STATISTICS | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 08: Putting It All Together

This lesson helps you use data summaries and distribution shape to make reasonable statistical conclusions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.4 - I can compare numerical data sets using center, variability, shape, and outliers.
    MA.912.DP.2.1
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.5 - I can decide when a normal model is appropriate and use mean and standard deviation to estimate percentages.
    MA.912.DP.2.2
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.6 - I can estimate population percentages using a data set that has been fit to a normal distribution.
    MA.912.DP.2.3

What You Will Learn

  • How all Unit 2 concepts work together.
  • How to compare distributions and evaluate normal models.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review center, variability, shape, outliers, normal models, and population estimates.
  • Practice mixed Unit 2 decision-making tasks.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete Lesson 8 Guided Practice and checkpoint.
  • Complete the Unit 2 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This synthesis lesson asks you to use every Unit 2 benchmark together so you can describe distributions, choose appropriate summaries, evaluate models, and estimate population percentages.
