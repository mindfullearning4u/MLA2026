P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Question 1

What is the mean of 4, 6, 8, 10?

Answer: A. 7

Feedback: Correct. The sum is 28, and 28 divided by 4 is 7.

Question 2

What is the median of 3, 5, 7, 9, 21?

Answer: B. 7

Feedback: Correct. The values are already ordered, and 7 is the middle value.

Question 3

A data set has an outlier much larger than the other values. Which measure of center is usually pulled upward the most?

Answer: C. Mean

Feedback: Correct. The mean uses every value, so a large outlier can pull it upward.

Question 4

For 2, 4, 4, 6, 9, what is the mode?

Answer: D. 4

Feedback: Correct. The value 4 occurs most often.

Question 5

A typical home price is being reported for a neighborhood with one extremely expensive mansion. Which measure of center is usually better?

Answer: A. Median

Feedback: Correct. The median is less affected by an extremely high outlier.
