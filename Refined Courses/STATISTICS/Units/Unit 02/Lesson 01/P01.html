Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Measures of Center for Numerical Data

Learn how mean, median, and mode describe typical values in numerical data sets and how context affects which measure is most useful.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.4 - I can compare numerical data sets using center, variability, shape, and outliers.

What You Will Learn

  • Calculate mean, median, and mode for numerical data.
  • Interpret each measure of center in context.
  • Choose an appropriate measure of center based on the data set.
  • Recognize how outliers can affect the mean.

What You Will Do

  • Compute measures of center from data sets.
  • Compare mean and median in real contexts.
  • Explain how an outlier changes a summary.
  • Justify which measure best represents a data set.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint interpreting center in context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DVD.4: I can use measures of center to describe and compare numerical data sets.
