STATISTICS | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Instructions: Complete the independent work in your notebook and show your reasoning clearly.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8, 10.
  2. Find the median of 9, 1, 5, 7, 3.
  3. Explain what mean measures.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. Find the mode of 2, 5, 5, 7, 9.
  2. Choose mean or median for 5, 6, 7, 8, 50.
  3. Explain why an outlier changes the mean.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct a median found before ordering.
  2. Explain when median is more useful than mean.
  3. Describe what center tells you about a data set.

Independent Work Guidance

For independent work, complete each part as evidence of mastery rather than as a list of short answers. In Part A, write the given data or scenario and identify the relevant variable, display, or statistic. In Part B, show the process: organize the table, read the graph, calculate the value, or compare the choices using the mapped lesson standard. In Part C, write a conclusion that a reader can understand without seeing your scratch work. Your response should answer the question, use the vocabulary from Measures of Center for Numerical Data, and avoid unsupported claims. If your answer depends on a graph or table, name the feature you used.
