Checkpoint Task: Measures of center

Complete all parts. Use clear labels and explain what your statistical values mean.

  1. Find the mean of 12, 14, 16, 18.
  2. Find the median of 20, 4, 12, 8, 16.
  3. Choose the better measure of center for 3, 4, 5, 6, 40.
  4. Explain your choice using the outlier.

Mastery Criteria

  • Calculates mean accurately.
  • Orders data before finding median.
  • Chooses appropriate center based on outliers.
  • Explains center in context.
