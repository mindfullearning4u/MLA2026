STATISTICS | Unit 02 | Lesson 05

Lesson 05: Mean, Standard Deviation, and Normal Models

This lesson builds the Statistics skills needed to describe distributions, make reasonable estimates, and explain what data values mean in context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.5 - I can decide when a normal model is appropriate and use mean and standard deviation to estimate percentages.
    MA.912.DP.2.2

What You Will Learn

  • How mean and standard deviation describe a normal model.
  • How to identify values one, two, and three standard deviations from the mean.
  • How to decide whether a normal model is reasonable for a data set.

What You Will Do

  • Calculate and interpret intervals around a mean.
  • Use the empirical rule as a model for roughly normal data.
  • Explain when a normal model may not be appropriate.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent work.
  • Answer Guided Practice and quiz questions with instructional feedback.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining mean, standard deviation, and model appropriateness.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to connect numerical summaries to the shape of a distribution. You are not just calculating mean and standard deviation; you are deciding whether those values can reasonably support a normal model.
