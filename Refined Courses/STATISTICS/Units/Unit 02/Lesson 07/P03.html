Deeper Reasoning

When evaluating a model, ask: What is the shape? Are there outliers? Is there one cluster or more than one? Does the context make the estimate reasonable?

A strong statistical conclusion names the data feature, explains the model choice, and connects the conclusion back to the real-world context.

Common Mistake

Problem: A data set is strongly skewed right.

Incorrect: Use mean and standard deviation because they are always best.

Mean and standard deviation can be distorted by skew and outliers.

Correct: Use median and IQR for a more resistant summary.

Match the summary to the data shape.
