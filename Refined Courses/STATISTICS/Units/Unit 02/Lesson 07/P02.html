Notebook Title

Evaluating the Appropriateness of Distribution Models

Vocabulary

Appropriate Model: A model that fits the data shape well enough to support reasonable estimates.

Resistant Measure: A statistic, such as the median or IQR, that is not strongly affected by extreme values.

Model Limitation: A reason a model may not accurately represent the data.

Concept Development

Choosing a statistical summary depends on the data. Symmetric data without extreme outliers can often be described well with mean and standard deviation. Skewed data or data with strong outliers often need median and IQR.

A normal model should be used only when the distribution is roughly symmetric, mound-shaped, and centered around one main cluster.
