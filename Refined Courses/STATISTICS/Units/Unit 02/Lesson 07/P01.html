STATISTICS | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

Lesson Title

Lesson 07: Evaluating the Appropriateness of Distribution Models

This lesson helps you use data summaries and distribution shape to make reasonable statistical conclusions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.4 - I can compare numerical data sets using center, variability, shape, and outliers.
    MA.912.DP.2.1
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.5 - I can decide when a normal model is appropriate and use mean and standard deviation to estimate percentages.
    MA.912.DP.2.2
  • MLA.STAT.DVD.6 - I can estimate population percentages using a data set that has been fit to a normal distribution.
    MA.912.DP.2.3

What You Will Learn

  • How to decide whether mean, median, IQR, or standard deviation best fits the data.
  • How outliers, skew, clusters, and gaps affect conclusions.
  • How to decide whether a normal model is appropriate.

What You Will Do

  • Compare data sets using center, variability, shape, and outliers.
  • Evaluate when a normal model should or should not be used.
  • Write defensible conclusions in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notes, independent work, Guided Practice, quiz, and checkpoint.
  • Explain model choices using data features.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will bring together center, variability, shape, outliers, and normal models to decide which statistical conclusions are reasonable.
