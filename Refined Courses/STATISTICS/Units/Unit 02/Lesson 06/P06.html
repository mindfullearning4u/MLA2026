Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. For mean 50 and standard deviation 5, identify the values one standard deviation from the mean.
  2. Estimate the percent within one standard deviation.
  3. Estimate the percent within two standard deviations.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. For mean 100 and standard deviation 12, estimate the percent above 112.
  2. For mean 40 and standard deviation 4, estimate the percent below 32.
  3. Write a population statement for an interval containing about 95%.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct this mistake: a value one standard deviation above the mean means 68% are above it.
  2. Explain why a tail estimate uses symmetry.
  3. Explain why normal-model estimates are approximate.
