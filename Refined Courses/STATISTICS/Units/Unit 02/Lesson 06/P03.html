Deeper Reasoning

A normal model is symmetric. If about 68% is within one standard deviation, then about 32% is outside that interval. Because the model is symmetric, about 16% is in each outside tail.

If about 95% is within two standard deviations, then about 5% is outside that interval, or about 2.5% in each tail.

Your final answer should say what population or context the percentage describes. A number alone is not enough in Statistics.

Common Mistake

Problem: A normal model has mean 60 and standard deviation 8. Estimate the percent above 68.

Incorrect: About 68%

The number 68 is a data value, not the percent. It is one standard deviation above the mean.

Correct: About 16%

Use the empirical rule: above one standard deviation is the upper tail, about 16%.
