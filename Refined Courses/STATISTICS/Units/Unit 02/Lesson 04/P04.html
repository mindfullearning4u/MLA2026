STATISTICS | Unit 02 | Lesson 04

P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Statistics is not just calculation; each answer must be interpreted in the context of the data.

Worked Example 1: Identify Symmetry

Problem: A dot plot has values balanced on both sides of the center.

Step 1: Look at the left and right sides of the display.

Step 2: Check whether the sides have similar shape.

Step 3: Name the shape.

Approximately symmetric

Reasoning: Symmetry means the distribution is balanced around the center.

Worked Example 2: Identify Skew

Problem: Most values are low, with a long tail to the right.

Step 1: Find where most data values cluster.

Step 2: Look for the direction of the tail.

Step 3: Name the skew by the tail direction.

Skewed right

Reasoning: A distribution is named by the direction of its tail.

Worked Example 3: Identify a Cluster

Problem: A dot plot has many values from 20 to 25 and few elsewhere.

Step 1: Look for where many values are grouped together.

Step 2: Name that interval as the cluster.

Step 3: Explain what it means.

Cluster from 20 to 25

Reasoning: Clusters show where data values occur frequently.

Common Mistake

Problem: Most values are small and the tail points right.

Incorrect:

Skewed left

Why it is wrong: Skew is named by the tail direction, not where most values are.

Correct:

Skewed right

Fix: Find the tail first, then name the skew.
