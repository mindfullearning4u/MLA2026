Worked Example

Data: 4, 5, 5, 6, 7, 22.

Most values are clustered between 4 and 7, with a high value at 22.

Shape: skewed right because the tail extends toward larger values.

Interpretation: the median may better describe the typical value because the high outlier can pull the mean upward.
