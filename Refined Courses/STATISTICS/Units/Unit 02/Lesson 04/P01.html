Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Shape, Skewness, Symmetry, and Clustering

Learn how distribution shape helps describe numerical data and supports comparisons beyond center and spread.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.STAT.DVD.4 - I can compare numerical data sets using center, variability, shape, and outliers.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify symmetric and skewed distributions.
  • Recognize clusters, gaps, peaks, and outliers.
  • Connect shape to reasonable measures of center.
  • Use shape to compare numerical data sets.

What You Will Do

  • Describe distribution shape from displays.
  • Interpret skewness in context.
  • Explain how clusters and gaps affect conclusions.
  • Compare distributions using shape, center, and variability.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks.
  • Submit a checkpoint describing distribution shape.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.STAT.DVD.4: I can describe the shape of numerical data and use that shape to compare distributions.
