P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Question 1

A distribution has a long tail toward larger values. What is its shape?

Answer: A. Skewed right

Feedback: Correct. A right-skewed distribution has a tail toward larger values.

Question 2

A distribution is balanced on both sides of the center. How should it be described?

Answer: B. Symmetric

Feedback: Correct. Symmetric distributions are balanced around the center.

Question 3

In 4, 5, 5, 6, 7, 22, where is the cluster?

Answer: C. Between 4 and 7

Feedback: Correct. Most values are grouped from 4 through 7.

Question 4

Which feature is an interval with no data values?

Answer: D. Gap

Feedback: Correct. A gap is a space in the distribution with no data values.

Question 5

Why might the median be preferred for a skewed distribution?

Answer: A. The median is less affected by the tail

Feedback: Correct. Skew and outliers can pull the mean, while the median is resistant.
