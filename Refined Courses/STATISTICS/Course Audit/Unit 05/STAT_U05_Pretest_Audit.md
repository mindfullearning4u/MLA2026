# Statistics Unit 5 Pretest Audit

## Executive Summary
Statistics Unit 5 Pretest was created and audited using the official MLA Audit Reporting Format.

Final Recommendation: PASS

## Strengths
- Pretest includes all mapped Unit 5 standards while maintaining the required 10-question count.
- Three items intentionally assess paired standards so the Pretest covers all 13 Unit 5 standards without increasing the assessment count.
- All questions include Question ID, MLA Standard, four answer choices, one correct answer, and feedback for every answer choice.
- Feedback is instructional and identifies misconceptions related to sampling, margin of error, simulation, study design, inference, significance, and report evaluation.
- No cross-course references or out-of-scope standards found.

## Findings
During audit, the initial Pretest draft did not represent all 13 Unit 5 standards because the Pretest count is locked at 10 questions. This was corrected by pairing related standards in three diagnostic items.
