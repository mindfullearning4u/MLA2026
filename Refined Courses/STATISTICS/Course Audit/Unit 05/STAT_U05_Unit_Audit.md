# Statistics Unit 5 Final Unit Audit

## Executive Summary

Statistics Unit 5 final audit is complete. Lessons 1-8, Guided Practice files, Lesson Quiz Banks, Unit Assessment, and Unit Pretest were verified against the Statistics mapping, MLA production requirements, Moodle-safe GIFT standards, and the stricter assessment quality standard.

Final Recommendation: PASS

## Strengths

Unit 5 follows the approved Statistics mapping for sampling, bias, simulation, inference, and data-based decision making.

All 8 lessons exist with P01.html-P07.html, lesson.json, and quiz.json.

Every lesson page includes the required student support reminder directing students to contact the TOR when help or guidance is needed.

Lesson 8 is correctly titled Putting It All Together.

Lesson 8 contains Guided Practice and the Unit Assessment.

Lesson 8 does not contain a Lesson Quiz.

The Unit Assessment covers all mapped Unit 5 standards.

The Unit Pretest covers all mapped Unit 5 standards within the required 10-question limit by using paired-standard diagnostic items where needed.

All assessment items include Question ID, MLA Standard, four A-D answer choices, one correct answer, and feedback for every answer choice.

All GIFT files use Moodle-safe formatting with no HTML tags, no inline braces, and no malformed answer blocks.

Cross-course scan found no ALG1, Geometry, ALG2, or incorrect course standard references.

## Findings

No blocking findings remain.

The stricter assessment audit verified balanced answer distribution, no duplicate answer choices, no missing feedback, no generic feedback pattern, and no parser-breaking GIFT formatting.
