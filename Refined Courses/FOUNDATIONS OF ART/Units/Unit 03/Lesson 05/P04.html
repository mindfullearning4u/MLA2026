Worked Example 1

  1. Look at the visual structure first.
  2. Name the strongest element or principle.
  3. Explain how it supports combine materials while preserving unity, craftsmanship, and intent.

Answer model: The repeated diagonal lines create movement because they guide the eye from one area to another.

Worked Example 2

  1. Find the focal point.
  2. Identify what creates emphasis.
  3. Explain why the viewer notices that area first.

Answer model: The focal point stands out because it has the strongest contrast and is placed near the center.

Worked Example 3

  1. Identify a possible revision.
  2. Connect the revision to a design goal.
  3. Explain how it improves communication.

Answer model: I would simplify the background so the main shape has more emphasis and the viewer is not distracted.

Common Mistake

A vague answer such as "make it better" does not show mastery. A strong revision names the exact visual choice and explains the effect.
