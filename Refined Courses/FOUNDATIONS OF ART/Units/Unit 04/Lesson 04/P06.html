FOA | Unit 04 | Lesson 04 | P06 Independent Work

Color and Symbolic Meaning

Instructions

Complete all three parts. Your work should show planning, visual evidence, and reflection.

Part A: Observe

Choose one example, object, image, or artwork. List five visible details using art vocabulary.

Part B: Create or Revise

Create a small study or revise an existing study to show interpret color meaning using evidence, context, and audience. Label at least three intentional choices.

Part C: Explain

Write a short explanation of what you changed or created, why you made those choices, and how the viewer should read the result.
