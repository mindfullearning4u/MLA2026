Notebook Title

Putting It All Together: First Look and Vocabulary

Vocabulary

TermStudent-Friendly Meaning
ElementA basic visual building block such as line, shape, color, value, texture, form, or space.
PrincipleA way artists organize elements, such as balance, contrast, emphasis, movement, pattern, rhythm, proportion, or unity.
EvidenceA visible detail that supports what you say about an artwork.
Putting It All Together color study

Use the swatches to compare hue, value, intensity, and contrast.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. First, identify what is visible. Do not decide if it is good or bad yet.
  2. Next, name the element or principle that is most important in the example.
  3. Then, explain what that choice does for the viewer.
  4. Finally, write one sentence connecting the visual choice to the lesson focus: synthesize color theory, meaning, and visual communication.

Notebook Task

Create a two-column notebook entry. On the left, list three visible details. On the right, explain what each detail helps the viewer notice.
