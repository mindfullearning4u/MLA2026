FOA | Unit 02 | Lesson 04 | P01 Lesson Overview

Movement and Rhythm

Standards Covered

MLA.FOA.COMP.1

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to guide the viewer's eye using repetition, direction, and visual pathways. The lesson teaches the skill step by step so you can use art vocabulary, visual evidence, and intentional choices instead of guessing.

What You Will Do

  • Study a visual model.
  • Practice noticing details before making judgments.
  • Create or revise a small artwork or planning study.
  • Explain your choices using evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will submit notebook evidence, complete guided practice, finish independent work, complete a checkpoint, and pass the lesson quiz or unit assessment when assigned.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to make and explain art choices clearly. You are learning to think like an artist: observe, plan, create, revise, and explain.
