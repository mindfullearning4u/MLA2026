Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. First, identify what is visible. Do not decide if it is good or bad yet.
  2. Next, name the element or principle that is most important in the example.
  3. Then, explain what that choice does for the viewer.
  4. Finally, write one sentence connecting the visual choice to the lesson focus: title, photograph, label, and organize work clearly.

Notebook Task

Create a two-column notebook entry. On the left, list three visible details. On the right, explain what each detail helps the viewer notice.
