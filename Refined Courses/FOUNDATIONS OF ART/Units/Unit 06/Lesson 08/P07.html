Putting It All Together

Teacher of Record Support

Before asking for help, reread the lesson steps, compare your work to the worked examples, and identify the exact part that is confusing. Then contact your Teacher of Record for clarification if needed.

Submission Workflow

  1. Check that your notebook evidence is complete.
  2. Check that your independent work includes Parts A, B, and C.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response.
  4. Use feedback to revise if required.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a response that explains how your visual choices support synthesize portfolio, presentation, reflection, and personal artistic voice. Include at least three art vocabulary terms and at least two visible details as evidence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses correct art vocabulary.
  • Gives visual evidence instead of unsupported opinion.
  • Explains choices step by step.
  • Stays aligned to the lesson standard.
  • Shows careful craftsmanship or planning.
