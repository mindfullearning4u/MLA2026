# Florida, Common Core, SAT, and ACT Alignment

Foundations of Art uses CPALMS Visual Art as the fine arts source and supports Florida B.E.S.T. literacy expectations through vocabulary, evidence, critique, presentation, and reflective writing.

Common Core support: reading informational/art-historical text, writing explanations, citing evidence, speaking/listening, and vocabulary.

SAT support: evidence use, words in context, analysis of visual information, and expression of ideas.

ACT support: reading comprehension, research summaries, conventions, and clear written communication.
