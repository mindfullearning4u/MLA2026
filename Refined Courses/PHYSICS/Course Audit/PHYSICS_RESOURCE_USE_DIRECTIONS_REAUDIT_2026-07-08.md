# Physics Resource Use Directions Reaudit

Date: 2026-07-08
Course: Physics
Audit Focus: Student-facing directions for direct lab, simulation, data, and resource links

## Purpose

This reaudit documents the new MLA science requirement that exact direct links are not sufficient by themselves. Every linked lab, simulation, data set, interactive, or resource must also include student-facing directions explaining what to do after the link opens.

## Standard Updated

The science resource standards and agent prompts now require:

- what to click after the page opens
- which controls, tabs, headings, tables, figures, models, or settings to use
- what to observe
- what evidence to record
- how the evidence connects to the mapped lesson standard

Missing step-by-step student-use directions are now a certification and Moodle-transfer blocker.

## Final Decision

PASS. Physics now includes exact direct resource links and adjacent student-facing directions that explain how to use the linked resource without relying on a live teacher or requiring students to search, browse, or guess what to do.
