## Root Cause Analysis

The initial Physics architecture package established a valid standards and mapping foundation, but some documents used compressed architecture language. Biology and Chemistry already established the fuller production model, including full crosswalk columns, detailed course overview sections, and lesson-level mapping with lesson purpose, assessment evidence, and rationale. Physics required normalization to that model.

Unit 6 originally referenced Earth/Space because CPALMS Physics 1 includes SC.912.E.5.2 and SC.912.E.5.6. The better Physics-preserving title is `Modern, Cosmic, and Applied Physics Systems`, because those benchmarks are addressed through forces, matter distribution, Newtonian relationships, physical models, and evidence rather than through an Earth/Space Science course identity.

Momentum was validly present in both Unit 2 and Unit 3, but the progression needed to be stated: Unit 2 introduces momentum as a force-interaction concept in Newtonian mechanics, and Unit 3 applies conservation of momentum within energy systems and collisions.
