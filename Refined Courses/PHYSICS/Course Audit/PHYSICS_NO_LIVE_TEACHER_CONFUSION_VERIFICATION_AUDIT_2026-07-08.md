# Physics No-Live-Teacher Confusion and Verification Audit

Date: 2026-07-08
Course: Physics
Audit Focus: Student independence, anticipated confusion, resource-use directions, and verification checks

## Purpose

This audit verifies that Physics lesson files assume there is no live classroom teacher guiding the student through the lesson. The lesson must provide the instruction, task directions, resource-use directions, common mistake support, evidence expectations, mastery criteria, and verification checks before Teacher of Record support is needed.

## Decision

PASS. Physics satisfies the no-live-teacher requirement at this audit gate. Student-facing lessons include directions, support boundaries, common mistake correction, resource-use instructions, evidence expectations, and checkpoint/mastery verification.
