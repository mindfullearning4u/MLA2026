# Physics Unit 1 Assessments

## U1 Pretest
- 10 multiple-choice questions covering all Unit 1 topics
- 1 attempt
- Diagnostic; gradebook weight 0%
- Scored on a 100-point scale for internal tracking
- 90% or higher indicates potential accelerated-pathway eligibility, subject to MLA policy

## U1 Final Assessment
- 40-question multiple-choice bank covering all seven lessons
- Moodle releases 10 randomized questions per attempt
- 1 attempt initially
- 80% mastery threshold
- Additional attempt is released by the TOR only after intervention
- Unit Assessment gradebook category weight: 30%

Correct-answer positions are intentionally distributed across A-D.
