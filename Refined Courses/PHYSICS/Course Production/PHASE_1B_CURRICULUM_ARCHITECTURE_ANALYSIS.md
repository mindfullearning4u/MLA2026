## Course Architecture Purpose

Physics is organized as a quantitative, laboratory-centered physical science course. The architecture emphasizes models, measurement, motion, forces, energy, waves, electricity, magnetism, modern physics, and application of physical principles to natural and technological systems.

## Major Architecture Controls

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Standards fidelity | Every CPALMS-listed Physics 1 standard is represented in the MLA inventory, crosswalk, unit map, or support alignment. |
| Unit coherence | Each unit has a distinct physics concept progression and avoids chemistry, biology, earth/space, environmental, marine, or anatomy identity drift. |
| Lesson coherence | Each unit has eight mapped lessons, and every primary unit standard is taught in Lessons 1-7 before Lesson 8 synthesis. |
| Lab science | Lab safety, measurement, procedures, tools, apparatus, data displays, error, interpretation, and communication are embedded across the architecture. |
| Virtual lab/data | Virtual investigation, simulation, graphical interpretation, and data analysis expectations are explicitly represented. |
| Readiness | Common Core, ACT Science, SAT science reading/data, college readiness, workforce readiness, and scientific literacy are present. |

## Unit Architecture

| Unit | Title | Primary Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Scientific Thinking, Measurement, and Motion | Establish scientific inquiry, lab/data practices, vectors, and kinematics. |
| 2 | Forces and Newtonian Mechanics | Develop force, Newtonian mechanics, gravitational, orbital, momentum, and fundamental-force reasoning. |
| 3 | Work, Energy, Momentum, and Thermal Systems | Build energy, conservation, work, power, heat, temperature, and system analysis. |
| 4 | Waves, Sound, Light, and Optics | Build wave, sound, electromagnetic, light, Doppler, lens, and mirror reasoning. |
| 5 | Electricity, Magnetism, and Matter | Build electrostatics, circuits, material behavior, and electromagnetism applications. |
| 6 | Modern, Earth/Space, and Applied Physics Systems | Synthesize atomic models, universe patterns, relativity concepts, models, and physics-informed decisions. |

## Architecture Decision

Physics uses 27 MLA Physics standards across eight domains. This count gives enough granularity for 48 lessons while keeping each standard teachable, mappable, and assessable at architecture level.

No lesson production begins in this phase.
