Vocabulary and Acceleration Representations

Open the Acceleration eText

Set up: title the page “U1 L2 — Vocabulary and Acceleration.” Number 1–14. For every term, write a definition in your own words and complete the required equation, unit, comparison, or visual.
  1. acceleration: define as rate of velocity change; include SI unit m/s².
  2. average acceleration: write a = Δv/Δt and define every variable.
  3. instantaneous acceleration: distinguish it from average acceleration.
  4. change in velocity: write Δv = vf − vi.
  5. positive acceleration: describe using a velocity-time graph.
  6. negative acceleration: explain why it does not always mean slowing down.
  7. constant acceleration: sketch a linear velocity-time graph.
  8. nonconstant acceleration: sketch a curved velocity-time graph.
  9. free fall: state the ideal assumption about air resistance.
  10. gravitational acceleration: identify g ≈ 9.8 m/s² downward near Earth.
  11. terminal velocity: define using balanced drag and weight.
  12. velocity-time graph: state what slope represents.
  13. acceleration-time graph: identify axes and what the sign shows.
  14. kinematic model: explain how equations, diagrams, and graphs represent motion.
Evidence checklist: 14 definitions; both equations; SI units; sign/direction explanations; and four requested graph sketches.
