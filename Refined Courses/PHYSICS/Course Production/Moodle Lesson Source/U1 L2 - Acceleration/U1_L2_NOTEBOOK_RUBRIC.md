## TOR guidance

Use `a = (vf − vi)/Δt`; velocity-time slope represents acceleration; `g ≈ 9.8 m/s²` downward; negative acceleration does not automatically mean slowing down. Accept equivalent correct methods when equations, substitutions, units, signs, directions, labels, and reasoning are shown. Return fabricated, copied, unreadable, or substantially incomplete evidence for revision.
