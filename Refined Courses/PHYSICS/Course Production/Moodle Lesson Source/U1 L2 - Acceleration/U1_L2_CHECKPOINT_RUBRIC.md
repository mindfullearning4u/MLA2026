## TOR guidance

Use `a = (vf − vi)/Δt`; velocity-time slope represents acceleration; `g ≈ 9.8 m/s²` downward; negative acceleration does not automatically mean slowing down. Verify any specific numeric response against the assigned Savvas Revisit prompt. Accept equivalent correct methods when equations, substitutions, units, signs, directions, labels, and reasoning are shown.
