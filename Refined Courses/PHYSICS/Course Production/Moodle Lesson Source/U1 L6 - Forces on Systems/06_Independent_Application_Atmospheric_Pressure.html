Independent Application

Atmospheric Pressure on a Sealed Container

Open the Savvas modeling activity

  1. Read the scenario about an empty plastic bottle sealed at sea level.
  2. Define the system as the bottle and enclosed gas; draw its boundary.
  3. Draw force arrows showing inside and outside pressure forces.
  4. Predict what happens when external pressure changes; state the direction of the net force.
  5. Create a before-and-after particle/force model.
  6. Write a CER: claim, at least two observations or physics principles, and reasoning connecting pressure imbalance to deformation.
  7. Identify one model limitation and one variable that should be controlled.

Evidence: submit the two labeled models and CER in Notebook Evidence.
