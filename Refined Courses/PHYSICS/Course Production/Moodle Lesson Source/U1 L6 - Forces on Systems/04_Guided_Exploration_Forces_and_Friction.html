Guided Exploration

Applied Force, Friction, and System Motion

Open the Forces on Systems PhET activity

  1. Read the complete activity directions before changing a control.
  2. Run a zero-applied-force trial and record friction, net force, and motion.
  3. Increase applied force in equal steps until the object begins moving; identify the maximum static-friction region.
  4. Record at least five trials after motion begins.
  5. For every trial, calculate net force and predict acceleration direction.
  6. Repeat one set with a changed mass and compare results.
  7. Create a graph of applied force versus net force and label the transition from static to kinetic friction.

Evidence: submit the data table, graph, two comparison statements, and one limitation.
