Scientific Reasoning

Internal Forces, Atwood Machines, and Torque

Watch “What Forces Are Acting On You?”

Then solve these three cases. For each: define the system, draw the boundary, draw a separate free-body diagram for every object, write component equations, solve, and check units and direction.

  1. Connected blocks: determine system acceleration and the internal tension.
  2. Atwood machine: determine acceleration and string tension for unequal masses.
  3. Balanced meter stick: use clockwise and counterclockwise torques to determine an unknown force or position.
Reasoning requirement: explain why internal forces cancel when the connected objects are treated as one system but remain necessary when one object is analyzed alone.
