# U1 L6 Notebook Evidence Rubric

| Criterion | 4 - Complete | 3 - Proficient | 2 - Developing | 1 - Limited | 0 - Missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vocabulary and system boundaries | 12 accurate definitions and 4 correct boundary analyses | Minor omission/error | Several errors | Minimal work | Missing |
| eText prompts and worked examples | All prompts plus 4 complete worked examples | One minor omission | Multiple omissions | Fragmentary | Missing |
| PhET data and graph | Complete trials, graph, comparisons, limitation | Minor error | Incomplete analysis | Minimal evidence | Missing |
| System problem reasoning | Correct diagrams, equations, units, and internal-force explanation | Minor error | Partial reasoning | Major errors | Missing |
| Atmospheric-pressure model and CER | Two accurate models, evidence-based CER, limitation/control | Minor omission | Partial model/CER | Weak/inaccurate | Missing |
