Vocabulary and Motion Representations

Open the Savvas eText. For each term, write the Savvas definition and add the representation requested below.

  1. projectile: sketch an object after launch and label its velocity.
  2. trajectory: sketch a parabolic path.
  3. range: mark the horizontal launch-to-landing distance.
  4. component: resolve one diagonal velocity vector into horizontal and vertical arrows.
  5. centripetal acceleration: draw the acceleration direction at three points on a circle.
  6. uniform circular motion: distinguish constant speed from changing velocity.
  7. relative velocity: identify the reference frame in one example.
Representation check: beneath the terms, create a two-column comparison titled “Projectile Motion” and “Circular Motion.” Include path shape, velocity direction, acceleration direction, and what remains constant.
