# U1 L3 Notebook Evidence Rubric — Circular and Projectile Motion

**Total:** 20 points  
**Mastery:** 16/20 (80%)

Each criterion is scored 0–4.

## 1. Vocabulary and representation table
- **4:** All seven terms use accurate Savvas-based definitions; required vector/path sketches and the projectile-versus-circular comparison are complete and correctly labeled.
- **3:** Complete and mostly accurate with minor labeling or definition errors.
- **2:** Several terms or representations are incomplete or contain repeated conceptual errors.
- **1:** Minimal, difficult to follow, or mostly inaccurate evidence.
- **0:** Missing.

## 2. eText reading, prompts, and worked examples
- **4:** Every required Notebook/Check Point response, table, component diagram, Hang Time example, and Artificial Gravity example is complete with reasoning and units.
- **3:** Nearly complete; minor omissions or calculation issues do not obscure understanding.
- **2:** Multiple prompts or worked steps are missing or inaccurate.
- **1:** Little traceable eText evidence.
- **0:** Missing.

## 3. Satellite circular-orbit data analysis
- **4:** Complete data table, correct calculations, unit use, trend analysis, evidence-based responses, and defensible conclusion.
- **3:** Complete and mostly accurate with minor calculation or explanation errors.
- **2:** Usable data but repeated omissions, calculation errors, or unsupported conclusions.
- **1:** Minimal or largely inaccurate analysis.
- **0:** Missing.

## 4. Projectile simulation evidence
- **4:** Three controlled trials, complete data, correctly labeled component models at three positions, accurate comparison, and evidence-based conclusion.
- **3:** Complete and mostly accurate with minor omissions.
- **2:** Partial trials or repeated model/analysis errors.
- **1:** Minimal or untraceable simulation evidence.
- **0:** Missing.

## 5. Independent practice and submission quality
- **4:** All three Savvas practice sets are complete; equations, substitutions, arithmetic, units, signs, directions, diagrams, and reference frames are shown; one readable ordered submission is labeled with student name and U1 L3.
- **3:** Nearly complete and organized with minor errors.
- **2:** Multiple problems or required work steps are missing.
- **1:** Difficult to grade or mostly incomplete.
- **0:** Missing.
