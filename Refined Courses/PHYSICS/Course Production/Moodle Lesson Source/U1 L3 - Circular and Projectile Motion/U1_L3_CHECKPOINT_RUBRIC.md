# U1 L3 Checkpoint Rubric — Circular and Projectile Motion

**Total:** 16 points  
**Mastery:** 13/16 (80% rounded up)

Each criterion is scored 0–4.

## 1. CER claim and evidence
- **4:** Claim directly answers the horizontal-motion question; at least two precise observations/data/model features are accurate and relevant.
- **3:** Clear claim and sufficient evidence with a minor precision issue.
- **2:** Claim is present but evidence is vague, incomplete, or partly inaccurate.
- **1:** Minimal claim/evidence connection.
- **0:** Missing.

## 2. CER reasoning, model, and limitation
- **4:** Reasoning explicitly connects independent components and gravity to the evidence; the equal-time/vector model is correct; air-resistance limitation is explained.
- **3:** Mostly accurate reasoning and model with a minor omission.
- **2:** Partial reasoning or repeated component/model errors.
- **1:** Reasoning does not connect evidence to physics principles.
- **0:** Missing.

## 3. Skills worksheet mastery
- **4:** Every section is complete and accurate; diagrams, equations, substitutions, arithmetic, units, signs, and directions are shown.
- **3:** Complete and mostly accurate with minor errors.
- **2:** Usable but multiple omissions or repeated errors.
- **1:** Major required sections are missing or difficult to grade.
- **0:** Missing.

## 4. Final comparison model and submission quality
- **4:** Projectile and circular panels accurately show path, velocity, and acceleration; 5–7 sentence comparison addresses all six required features and one limitation; submission is readable, ordered, and labeled with student name and U1 L3.
- **3:** Complete and mostly accurate with a minor omission.
- **2:** Partial comparison or repeated representation errors.
- **1:** Minimal, unreadable, or poorly organized submission.
- **0:** Missing.
