Displacement and Velocity — Overview and Evidence Checklist

Lesson focus: represent position, distance, displacement, speed, and velocity with words, vectors, equations, motion maps, and position–time graphs.

MLA standards focus: motion representation and quantitative reasoning. Supporting Florida correlations include SC.912.P.12.1, SC.912.P.12.2, and SC.912.N.1.1.

Submit in Notebook Evidence Submission — U1 L1

  1. Vocabulary: all assigned terms with physics-based definitions.
  2. eText: responses to all Notebook and Check Point prompts in the assigned Savvas experience.
  3. Guided Exploration: vector representations and observations from the simulation and explanation video.
  4. Scientific Reasoning: the completed position–time graph Claim–Evidence–Reasoning task.
  5. Independent Application: completed displacement-and-velocity skills practice.

Submit in Checkpoint Submission — U1 L1

Submit the motion representation requested on the final page, including a labeled position–time graph, displacement and average-velocity calculations, units, and a brief evidence-based explanation.
