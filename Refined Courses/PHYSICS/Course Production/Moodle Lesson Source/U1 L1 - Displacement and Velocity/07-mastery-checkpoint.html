Modeling Motion with Displacement and Velocity

Revisit the Modeling Motion phenomenon in Savvas

Create one final motion representation:
  1. Choose a motion interval from the phenomenon and define a reference point and positive direction.
  2. Draw and label a position–time graph for the interval.
  3. Calculate displacement and average velocity using v = Δx/Δt.
  4. Include substitutions, units, signs, and direction.
  5. Write a brief Claim–Evidence–Reasoning explanation connecting the graph’s slope to the object’s velocity.
