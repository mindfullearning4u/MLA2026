## TOR expected-answer guidance

- Q24 spacing equals `speed × camera interval`; equal spacing during equal intervals indicates uniform motion.
- Q25 average velocity equals total displacement divided by total elapsed time. The table and graph must support the uniform/nonuniform conclusion.
- Q26 displacement from 240 s to 300 s is the signed rectangular area under the velocity-time graph: `v × 60 s`.
- Verify source-specific numbers against the Savvas prompt and accept equivalent correct scales or methods.
- Require equations, substitutions, work, units, signs, directions, graph labels, and evidence-based conclusions.
