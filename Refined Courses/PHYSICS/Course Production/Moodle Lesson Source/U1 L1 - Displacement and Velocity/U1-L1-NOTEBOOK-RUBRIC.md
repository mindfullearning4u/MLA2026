## TOR expected-answer guidance

- Displacement is final position minus initial position and includes direction.
- Velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time and includes direction.
- Speed is scalar; velocity is vector.
- Slope of a position-time graph represents velocity.
- Equal dot spacing at equal time intervals represents uniform motion.
- For uniform motion, area under a velocity-time graph represents displacement.
- Accept mathematically equivalent work when equations, substitutions, units, signs, directions, labels, and reasoning are correct.
- Return fabricated data, copied answers, unreadable evidence, or missing major sections for revision.
