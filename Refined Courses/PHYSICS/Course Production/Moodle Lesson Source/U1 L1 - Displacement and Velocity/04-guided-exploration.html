Vector Addition, Speed, and Velocity

Part A — PhET: Displacement and Velocity

Open the PhET Simulation worksheet

  1. Open the linked Vector Addition PhET from the worksheet. Select Explore 2D and check Values.
  2. Complete Direction A exactly as written.
  3. Complete Direction B: place vector a at the origin; inspect each Components representation; record its two perpendicular components and confirm the vector length.
  4. Complete Direction C: add vector b with the specified horizontal and downward vertical components.
  5. Complete Direction D: move b head-to-tail with a; turn on Sum; position resultant s at the origin; record how original components combine and explain why the sum of two 10.0 m vectors is not necessarily 20.0 m.
  6. Complete worksheet Question 1: draw the requested two-vector component model.
  7. Complete worksheet Question 2: create two 18.0 m displacement vectors that produce a 30.0 m rightward resultant; draw the arrangement and translate it into walking directions.

Part B — Explain Video
