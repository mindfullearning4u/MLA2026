How Tall Can Mountains Be?

Watch “How Tall Can Mountains Be?”

  1. Record three direct observations or claims from the video.
  2. Draw a mountain cross-section showing weight, support/buoyancy, compressive stress, and erosion.
  3. Explain why increasing height increases stress at depth.
  4. Describe how rock strength, gravity, isostasy, and erosion limit height.
  5. Write a CER answering: Why do mountains not grow without limit?

Use at least two quantitative or model-based details and identify one limitation.
