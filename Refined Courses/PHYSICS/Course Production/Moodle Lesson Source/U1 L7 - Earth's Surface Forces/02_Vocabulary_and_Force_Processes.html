Vocabulary and Earth-System Force Processes

  2. For each, identify force directions and one surface feature it can produce.
  3. Compare mechanical weathering with erosion.
  4. Explain how uplift and erosion can operate at the same time.
  5. Use a force or pressure model to explain isostatic equilibrium.
