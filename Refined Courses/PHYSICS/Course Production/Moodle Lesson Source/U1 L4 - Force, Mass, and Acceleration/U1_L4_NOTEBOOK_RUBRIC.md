# U1 L4 Notebook Evidence Rubric — 20 points

1. Vocabulary, units, and diagrams — 4 points: nine accurate entries; F, m, a, p notation; balanced/unbalanced and interaction diagrams.
2. eText evidence — 4 points: every assigned prompt and worked example, labeled by heading, with complete reasoning.
3. PhET investigation — 4 points: two complete controlled-variable tables, accurate calculations, and recorded units.
4. Graph and conclusions — 4 points: correct graph conventions, force trend, mass trend, controlled variable, and limitation.
5. Independent problems and submission quality — 4 points: complete Mowing the Lawn and Will the Wire Break? work, diagrams, algebra, units, reasonableness checks, and readable labels.

Performance levels for each criterion: 4 Complete/accurate; 3 Mostly complete/minor errors; 2 Partial/conceptual gaps; 1 Minimal evidence; 0 Missing.
