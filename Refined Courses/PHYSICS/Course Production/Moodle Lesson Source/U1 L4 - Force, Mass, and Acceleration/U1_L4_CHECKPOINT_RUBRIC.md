# U1 L4 Checkpoint Submission Rubric — 16 points

1. CER claim and evidence — 4 points: answers the desk-chair question and cites at least two specific observations/data values.
2. CER reasoning and diagram — 4 points: correctly connects Newton’s second and third laws, labels two objects/forces, and states a reasonable limitation.
3. Skills Worksheet mastery — 4 points: complete visual, conceptual, and quantitative responses with equations and units.
4. Final comparison model — 4 points: all three required cases include arrows, equations, predictions, explanations, and readable labels.

Performance levels for each criterion: 4 Complete/accurate; 3 Mostly complete/minor errors; 2 Partial/conceptual gaps; 1 Minimal evidence; 0 Missing.
