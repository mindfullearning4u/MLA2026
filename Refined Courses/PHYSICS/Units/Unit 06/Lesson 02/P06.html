Physics Independent Work Self-Check

Before submitting, check that your work connects directly to MLA.PHYS.APP.01; MLA.PHYS.FOR.03. A complete physics response identifies the quantity or model, uses the provided data, graph, vector, diagram, or scenario as evidence, and explains the relationship in words.
