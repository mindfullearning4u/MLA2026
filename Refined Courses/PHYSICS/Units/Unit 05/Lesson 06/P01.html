P01 Lesson Overview

Electromagnetism in Devices

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary: MLA.PHYS.ELE.04

Support: MLA.PHYS.WAV.03

What You Will Learn

Apply electricity and electromagnetism concepts to devices.

What You Will Do

  • Read electrical, circuit, field, material, or device evidence slowly.
  • Copy vocabulary, diagrams, data tables, and rules into your notebook.
  • Use embedded models to answer without guessing.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, and the Checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use the correct Unit 5 model.
  • Interpret charge signs, field arrows, circuit paths, units, and diagrams accurately.
  • Explain why the evidence supports the answer.
  • Score at least 80% on required mastery checks.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use electricity and magnetism evidence the way a physicist uses diagrams, circuits, data, and models.

Science Safety and Resource Note

Use only embedded data, diagrams, and approved resources. Do not build circuits, handle electrical devices, use magnets near electronics, or perform physical investigations unless the Teacher of Record or school has approved the setup.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record if you are unsure how the charge, field, material, circuit, power, or device model connects to the standard.
