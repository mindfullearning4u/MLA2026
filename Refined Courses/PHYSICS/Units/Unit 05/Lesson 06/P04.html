Before the Worked Examples

Do not start by hunting for an answer choice. Set up the physics first. Identify the electrical evidence, read the labels, and choose the model or rule that belongs to that evidence.

Physics Unit 5 is about tracking charge, fields, paths, and energy transfer. Strong physics reasoning first points to the charge signs, field arrows, conductor table, circuit path, voltage-current-resistance values, power values, or device input-output model before choosing an answer.

Student reasoning move: First name the electrical evidence. Then state the rule. Then connect the evidence to the rule in one sentence.

Worked Example 1: Electromagnet

Problem: What happens when current flows through a coil?

  1. Step 1: Current moves through wire.
  2. Step 2: Moving charge creates a magnetic field.
  3. Step 3: The coil can act like a magnet.

Answer: An electromagnet forms.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 2: Motor

Problem: What energy transformation occurs in a motor?

  1. Step 1: The input is electrical energy.
  2. Step 2: Current interacts with a magnetic field.
  3. Step 3: The output is motion.

Answer: Electrical energy changes into mechanical motion.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 3: Generator

Problem: How is a generator different from a motor?

  1. Step 1: A generator begins with motion.
  2. Step 2: The motion changes magnetic interactions.
  3. Step 3: Electrical energy is produced.

Answer: A generator changes motion into electrical energy.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Motors and generators do the exact same energy transformation.

Correct: Motors use electrical input to make motion; generators use motion input to make electrical output.

Teachable Explanation: The correct version uses the mapped Physics visual evidence. The incorrect version skips the charge sign, field direction, circuit path, data value, or device system.
