Before the Worked Examples

Do not start by hunting for an answer choice. Set up the physics first. Identify the electrical evidence, read the labels, and choose the model or rule that belongs to that evidence.

Physics Unit 5 is about tracking charge, fields, paths, and energy transfer. Strong physics reasoning first points to the charge signs, field arrows, conductor table, circuit path, voltage-current-resistance values, power values, or device input-output model before choosing an answer.

Before SolvingReason
Choose the evidence typeCharge, field, material, circuit, power, and device questions use different models.
Identify labels and unitsTables and diagrams control the answer.
Use mapped standards onlyDo not pull in unrelated physics.
Explain with the visualThe diagram or table is part of the answer.

Student reasoning move: First name the electrical evidence. Then state the rule. Then connect the evidence to the rule in one sentence.

Worked Example 1: Charge interaction

Problem: A diagram shows positive and negative charges near each other. What happens?

  1. Step 1: The signs are opposite.
  2. Step 2: Opposite charges attract.
  3. Step 3: The force arrows point toward each other.

Answer: The charges attract.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 2: Circuit calculation

Problem: A circuit has 12 V and 3 A. What power is transferred?

  1. Step 1: Use P = IV.
  2. Step 2: Substitute P = 12 x 3.
  3. Step 3: Label the result watts.

Answer: Power is 36 W.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 3: Technology system

Problem: A motor takes electrical input and produces motion. What concept fits?

  1. Step 1: Identify input: electrical energy.
  2. Step 2: Identify electromagnetic interaction.
  3. Step 3: Identify output: motion.

Answer: This is an electromagnetism device model.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use the same electricity idea for every Unit 5 problem.

Correct: Choose the charge, field, material, circuit, power, or device model based on the embedded stimulus.

Teachable explanation: The correct version uses the mapped Physics visual evidence. The incorrect version skips the charge sign, field direction, circuit path, data value, or device system.
