Notebook Title

Lesson 5: Light Speed and Frames of Reference

Vocabulary

  • Frame of reference: viewpoint or coordinate system used to describe motion.
  • Relative motion: motion described compared with a chosen reference frame.
  • Light speed: speed of light in vacuum, represented by c.
  • Observer: person or device making a measurement.
  • Model limitation: condition where a model should not be extended beyond course evidence.

Step 1: Identify the Visual Evidence

Before answering, identify whether the question gives a wave graph, medium table, Doppler diagram, EM spectrum chart, reference-frame scenario, ray diagram, or lens/mirror model.

Step 2: Read Labels and Units

Labels and units tell you what the visual means. In Unit 4, most mistakes happen when students look at the picture but do not read the labels.
