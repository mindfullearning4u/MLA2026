P04 Worked Example

Before the Worked Examples

Do not start by hunting for an answer choice. Set up the physics first. Identify the visual evidence, read the labels, and choose the model or rule that belongs to that evidence.

Physics Unit 4 is visual. A veteran Physics teacher would not ask a student to guess from a word. The teacher would point to the crest, normal line, source-observer motion, spectrum row, frame label, focal point, or image point before choosing an answer.

Worked Example 1: Bus frame

Problem: A backpack sits on a bus seat. In the bus frame, is it moving?

  1. Step 1: Choose the bus as the frame.
  2. Step 2: The backpack stays in the same seat position.
  3. Step 3: In that frame, it is at rest.

Answer: It is at rest in the bus frame.

Interpretation: This example shows how a veteran Physics teacher would slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 2: Sidewalk frame

Problem: The same backpack passes a sidewalk observer. Is it moving in that frame?

  1. Step 1: Choose the sidewalk as the frame.
  2. Step 2: The bus and backpack change position relative to the sidewalk.
  3. Step 3: In that frame, the backpack is moving.

Answer: It is moving in the sidewalk frame.

Interpretation: This example shows how a veteran Physics teacher would slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Worked Example 3: Light model

Problem: What statement fits the course-level vacuum light-speed model?

  1. Step 1: Use the vacuum light model.
  2. Step 2: At this level, light speed in vacuum is treated as constant.
  3. Step 3: Do not add unsupported advanced claims.

Answer: Light in vacuum travels at c in the course model.

Interpretation: This example shows how a veteran Physics teacher would slow down, identify the diagram or data, use the correct rule, and explain the result.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Motion has only one description no matter who observes it.

Correct: Motion descriptions depend on the selected frame of reference, while the course-level vacuum light-speed model treats c as constant.

Teachable explanation: The correct version uses the mapped Physics visual evidence. The incorrect version skips the diagram, table, rule, or observer frame.
