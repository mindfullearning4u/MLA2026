Before the Worked Examples

Do not start by hunting for an answer choice. Set up the physics first. Identify the system, read the given stimulus, and choose the model or formula that belongs to that evidence.

Physics Unit 3 is about tracking energy and momentum carefully. First define the system. Next identify the quantity shown in the stimulus: energy form, force, distance, work, time, temperature, particle motion, or before/after momentum. Then choose the correct model or formula.

Worked Example 1: Calculate work

Problem: A 10 N force moves a box 2 m. How much work is done?

  1. Step 1: Write W = Fd. Why: work uses force and distance.
  2. Step 2: Substitute W = 10 N x 2 m. Why: use given quantities.
  3. Step 3: Multiply and label joules. Why: N*m equals J.

Answer: Work is 20 J.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the system, use the diagram/table/formula, and explain the result.

Worked Example 2: No distance

Problem: A student pushes a wall with 20 N, but the wall does not move. How much work is done on the wall?

  1. Step 1: Identify force: 20 N. Why: force alone is not enough.
  2. Step 2: Identify distance: 0 m. Why: wall does not move.
  3. Step 3: Calculate W = 20 x 0. Why: no distance means no work on the wall.

Answer: Work on the wall is 0 J.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the system, use the diagram/table/formula, and explain the result.

Worked Example 3: Compare trials

Problem: Why can 10 N over 2 m and 5 N over 4 m both equal 20 J?

  1. Step 1: Calculate first: 10 x 2 = 20. Why: force-distance product.
  2. Step 2: Calculate second: 5 x 4 = 20. Why: different factors can have same product.
  3. Step 3: Compare work values. Why: same energy transfer.

Answer: Both transfer 20 J of energy.

Interpretation: This example shows how to slow down, identify the system, use the diagram/table/formula, and explain the result.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Any force means work is done.

Correct: Work requires force and distance in the force direction.

Teachable Explanation: The correct version uses the mapped Physics evidence. The incorrect version skips the system, model, unit, rate, or conservation relationship.
