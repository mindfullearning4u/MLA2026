## Alignment Decision

U.S. History is governed first by the Florida CPALMS Social Studies standards assigned to course #2100310.

The course also includes college-readiness and literacy support alignment through:

- Florida B.E.S.T. / ELA Expectations included in the CPALMS course listing
- ELD Social Studies and instructional communication support
- Common Core literacy-in-history/social-studies skills where appropriate as support skills
- SAT reading, evidence, vocabulary-in-context, informational-text, chart/table, and argument-readiness skills
- ACT reading, social science passage, data interpretation, main idea, inference, and evidence-based reasoning skills

These support alignments do not replace the CPALMS Social Studies standards. They strengthen how students read, write, reason, and show mastery inside the U.S. History standards.

## Audit Requirement

During unit and course audits, agents must verify:

- CPALMS Social Studies standards are taught and assessed.
- Florida B.E.S.T., CPALMS, Common Core, ACT, and SAT were checked as part of crosswalk and mapping validation.
- Evidence, reading, writing, and source-analysis tasks are present.
- Maps, timelines, tables, charts, passages, photographs, political cartoons, and primary/secondary source stimuli appear when needed.
