# U.S. History Visual, Source, and Stimulus Mapping

U.S. History requires maps, timelines, excerpts, photographs, political cartoons, charts, graphs, comparison tables, and cause-effect organizers. Assessment questions must include the visual or stimulus inside the Moodle XML question when the question depends on it.

Students must not be required to search elsewhere for a passage, map, chart, image, or source needed to answer a question.

## Recommended Trusted Source Pools for Later Lesson Development

These are source pools for lesson developers. Any required source excerpt, visual, or data display must be embedded directly in the lesson or XML question after review.

- National Archives: founding documents, Reconstruction amendments, primary sources, photographs
- Library of Congress: photographs, maps, newspapers, political cartoons, oral histories
- Smithsonian / National Museum of American History: artifacts and historical context
- United States Holocaust Memorial Museum: Holocaust timelines and survivor/historical context
- Federal Reserve History / Bureau of Labor Statistics / Census: economic and demographic data when appropriate
- State Archives of Florida / Florida Memory: Florida-specific historical evidence

## Assessment Stimulus Rule

If a question asks students to interpret a chart, map, table, political cartoon, photograph, excerpt, timeline, graph, amendment, court decision, policy, or data display, that stimulus must appear directly inside the Moodle XML question. A question cannot say "refer to the lesson" or "look up" the source.
