## Crosswalk Rule

The CPALMS Social Studies standards are the controlling course standards. Support standards from ELA, ELD, MTR, Health/Public Policy, Common Core literacy, SAT, and ACT support evidence reading, maps/data interpretation, source analysis, historical writing, and student communication. Support standards do not create separate off-topic lessons.

## Accreditation and Compliance Notes

- Every lesson must trace to the unit and lesson mapping.
- Every mapped standard must be taught and assessed.
- Assessment XML must include passages, maps, charts, tables, images, or stimuli directly in the question when required.
- No question may require students to find a source elsewhere.
- No lesson may rely on live teacher delivery. TOR support is allowed for help, clarification, checkpoint feedback, intervention, and retake workflow.
