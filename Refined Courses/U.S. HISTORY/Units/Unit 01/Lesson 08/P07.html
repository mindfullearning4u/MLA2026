U.S. HISTORY • UNIT 1 • SYNTHESIS

P07 • Unit 1 Synthesis Mastery Checkpoint

Prompt

Which developments in Unit 1 most transformed freedom, political power, land, and economic life in the United States after the Civil War?

Product

Write a 425–500 word structured historical argument in four to five developed paragraphs. Select exactly three major developments.

Required evidence

  • Evidence from at least 5 different lessons
  • 2 primary-source details; 1 constitutional/legal source
  • 1 map inference; 1 quantitative inference
  • African American, Indigenous, and labor/industrial/immigrant perspectives
  • 2 cause/effect relationships; 2 continuity/change relationships
  • 1 direct comparison; significance criteria
  • 2 cross-lesson connections; 1 counterargument

Suggested structure

ParagraphPurpose
1Thesis naming and ranking exactly three developments
2Development 1 with context, sources, causation, change, significance
3Development 2 with geographic/affected-group evidence and comparison
4Development 3 with quantitative/industrial evidence and cross-lesson link
5 if usedCounterargument and final defended ranking

Final check

  1. Exactly three developments?
  2. At least five lessons represented?
  3. All required evidence types and perspectives?
  4. Explanation rather than a list?
  5. Chronology, causation, continuity/change, comparison, and significance?
  6. Counterargument answered?
  7. 425–500 words?

Assessment rule

There is no regular Lesson 8 quiz. After this synthesis checkpoint, complete the separate Unit 1 Assessment: 10 randomized questions from the mapped 40-question pool, one attempt, 80% mastery.
