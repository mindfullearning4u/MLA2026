Pause and Review — notebook task

Copy and answer: How can one development expand opportunity and produce conflict at the same time?

Model: Railroads connected markets and employment, but they also supported western settlement on Indigenous lands and concentrated corporate power.

Notebook Summary — copy this

Synthesis organizes evidence by themes and chronology, then explains cross-lesson relationships. Freedom expanded constitutionally while restrictions persisted; federal power protected some rights and advanced policies that harmed others; land and movement reshaped sovereignty and markets; labor changed from post-emancipation arrangements to industrial wage systems while inequality continued.
