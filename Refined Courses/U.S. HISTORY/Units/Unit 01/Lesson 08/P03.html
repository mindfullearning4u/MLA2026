Eight-source method

  1. Identify actor or institution.
  2. Name the Unit 1 lesson and theme.
  3. Record a precise evidence detail.
  4. Explain what changed and what continued.
  5. Judge significance.
  6. State a limitation.

Pause and Review — notebook task

Copy and answer: Why must the final matrix include different actors and evidence types?

Model: Legal text can show formal power, a primary account can show experience and agency, a map can reveal geography, and data can establish scale. Together they corroborate a broader conclusion.

A synthesis evidence matrix prevents a narrative based only on lesson summaries. It combines at least two written primary sources, legal evidence, maps, a timeline, quantitative evidence, and African American, Indigenous, and worker/immigrant/industrial perspectives.
