P01 • Unit 1 Synthesis Overview

Investigation Question

Which developments in Unit 1 most transformed freedom, political power, land, and economic life in the United States after the Civil War?

Purpose

This lesson introduces no new historical era. You will connect evidence and reasoning from Lessons 1–7, select significant developments, and build a cross-unit argument.

ThemeConnections
Freedom and citizenshipEmancipation, amendments, African American agency, political participation, Jim Crow, disenfranchisement
Federal powerReconstruction, enforcement, western land and Indian policy, railroads and industrial development
Land and movementWestern expansion, Indigenous dispossession, migration, railroads, immigration, urbanization
Labor and economic changeFreedpeople’s labor transition, sharecropping, western growth, wage labor, unions, immigration

Notebook on this page

Copy the investigation question and this overview: Unit 1 shows that freedom, federal power, land, labor, migration, race, technology, and economic opportunity changed together. Synthesis compares those changes, traces causes and consequences, identifies continuities, and ranks historical significance with evidence.
