Independent Investigation Question

Which Reconstruction Amendment produced the most significant constitutional transformation?

This page is a Historian’s Notebook task. Apply the exact process modeled on Page 5. Evaluate all three before choosing.

Worked Analogous Example — Method Only

The Emancipation Proclamation is not one of your three candidates. It shows the expected depth:

CategoryExample
Legal changeDeclared freedom in rebelling areas under wartime executive authority.
Source detailIts scope depended on rebellion and military authority.
Immediate effectChanged Union war aims and connected military victory to freedom.
LimitationDid not constitutionally abolish slavery throughout the nation.
SignificanceMajor wartime turning point, but narrower and less permanent than a constitutional amendment.
