Unit 1 • Lesson 3

Citizenship, Constitutional Change, and the Reconstruction Amendments

Investigation Question

How did the Reconstruction Amendments redefine freedom, citizenship, equal protection, and political participation, and why was constitutional change alone insufficient to guarantee those rights?

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish the Thirteenth, Fourteenth, and Fifteenth Amendments.
  • Explain what changed legally and what remained unresolved in lived experience.
  • Analyze authentic constitutional language and multiple Reconstruction perspectives.
  • Use continuity-and-change reasoning to evaluate constitutional transformation.

How This Seven-Page Lesson Works

  1. Overview: prepare the investigation.
  2. Historical Content: learn vocabulary and the three amendments in short sections.
  3. Evidence Lab: examine constitutional and historical sources.
  4. Historical Thinking: learn continuity-and-change reasoning.
  5. Guided Investigation: watch the reasoning process modeled.
  6. Independent Investigation: apply the process in your notebook.
  7. Mastery Checkpoint: submit an independent 325–375 word response.
