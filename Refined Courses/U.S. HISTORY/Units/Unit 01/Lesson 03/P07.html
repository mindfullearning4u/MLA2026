P07 — Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

How did the Reconstruction Amendments redefine freedom, citizenship, equal protection, and political participation, and why was constitutional change alone insufficient to guarantee those rights?

What to Submit

Write an independent response of 325–375 words in three or four developed paragraphs. Submit only the final response to the Mastery Checkpoint assignment. Your notebook is submitted separately.

Required Historical Content

  • Thirteenth, Fourteenth, and Fifteenth Amendments
  • One specific constitutional provision from each
  • At least one African American primary-source detail
  • At least one implementation challenge
  • One example of legal change and one example of continuity
  • An explanation of expanded federal constitutional authority
  • One meaningful comparison among the amendments
  • At least seven lesson vocabulary terms used accurately

Required Reasoning

  • Continuity and Change: distinguish constitutional transformation from conditions that persisted.
  • Comparison: explain how the purposes of the three amendments differ and connect.
  • Sourcing: identify what a primary source reveals and its limitation.
  • CER: make a claim, support it with evidence, and explain how the evidence proves the claim.

Paragraph Guide

  1. Paragraph 1 — Claim and constitutional sequence: explain why the amendments formed a connected transformation.
  2. Paragraph 2 — Freedom and citizenship: analyze the 13th and 14th with exact provisions.
  3. Paragraph 3 — Political participation and implementation: analyze the 15th, African American evidence, and an implementation challenge.
  4. Paragraph 4 — Significance: compare the amendments, explain continuity and change, and connect federal authority to lived experience. A strong three-paragraph response may combine Parts 3 and 4.

Final Review — Not a New Notebook Task

Check: 325–375 words; three or four paragraphs; all three amendments; three constitutional provisions; African American source detail; implementation challenge; legal change and continuity; federal authority; comparison; seven vocabulary terms; clear reasoning in your own words.
