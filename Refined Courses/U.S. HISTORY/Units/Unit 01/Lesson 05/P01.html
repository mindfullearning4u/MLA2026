Unit 1 • Lesson 5

Resistance to Reconstruction and the Rise of Jim Crow

Investigation Question

How did political resistance, violence, economic dependency, law, and court decisions weaken Reconstruction-era rights and contribute to the rise of Jim Crow?

Learning Path

  1. Learn the content and vocabulary.
  2. Analyze seven sources.
  3. Practice multi-cause reasoning.
  4. Study a complete guided model.
  5. Apply the process independently.
  6. Submit the checkpoint.

Notebook Setup

Title a new section U1 L5 — Rights Restricted. Copy all vocabulary. On teaching pages, write only the labeled Pause and Review responses and supplied summaries. Worked examples are instruction, not extra copying.

Primary Skill

Cause and Effect: explain how political, legal, economic, violent/social, and institutional causes interacted. Constitutional guarantees remained even when implementation was restricted.
