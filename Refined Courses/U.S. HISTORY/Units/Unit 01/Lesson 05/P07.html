Unit 1 • Lesson 5

P07 — Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Checkpoint Question

How did political resistance, violence, economic dependency, law, and court decisions weaken Reconstruction-era rights and contribute to the rise of Jim Crow?

Response Requirements

Write 350–425 words. Four developed paragraphs are recommended.

  1. Claim and constitutional continuity.
  2. Political violence, Enforcement Acts, and 1877 transition.
  3. Black Codes/Jim Crow, economic dependency, and a voting restriction.
  4. Plessy, African American perspective and resistance, interactions, and conclusion.

Required Evidence

  • Political violence
  • Enforcement Acts or federal enforcement evidence
  • 1877 political transition
  • Black Codes/Jim Crow comparison
  • Sharecropping or debt peonage
  • One voting restriction
  • Plessy v. Ferguson
  • One African American primary-source perspective
  • One continued-resistance example
  • Constitutional continuity versus practical restriction

Reasoning and Vocabulary

Use cause/effect, continuity/change, perspective, sourcing, and CER. Explain multiple interacting causes and use at least 8 vocabulary terms accurately.

What Goes Where?

Notebook Submission Checkpoint Submission
Vocabulary; essential notes; timeline; evidence lab; cause organizer; Black Codes/Jim Crow comparison; sharecropping analysis; independent investigation Only the polished 350–425 word response

Final Check

Do not submit until every required evidence item is present, the causes interact, the distinctions are accurate, African American agency is included, and the word count and vocabulary requirements are met.
