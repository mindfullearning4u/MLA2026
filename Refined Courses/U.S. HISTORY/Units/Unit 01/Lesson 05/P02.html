Core Content Mini-Lesson

Reconstruction was the national effort to rebuild the Union, define citizenship, protect rights, and decide who would control the postwar South. It involved presidents, Congress, freedpeople, white Southerners, political parties, and constitutional amendments.

Teaching moveWhat you should do
Move 1The 13th Amendment abolished slavery, but it did not by itself define citizenship or protect voting rights.
Move 2The 14th Amendment defined birthright citizenship and equal protection after Southern states tried to limit freedpeople's rights.
Move 3The 15th Amendment prohibited denying the vote based on race, color, or previous condition of servitude, but enforcement remained contested.
Move 4Presidents, Radical Republicans, freedpeople, Southern Democrats, and white supremacist groups fought over how much the federal government should protect Reconstruction changes.
