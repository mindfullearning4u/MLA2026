Unit 1 • Lesson 6

P07 — Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Checkpoint Question

How did geography, migration, federal policy, economic expansion, and Indigenous resistance reshape the American West in the nineteenth century?

Format

Write 350–425 words; four developed paragraphs recommended.

Required Evidence

  • Geographic region or feature
  • Homestead Act or land policy
  • Railroad expansion
  • Migration example
  • Treaty or reservation example
  • Dawes Act
  • Indigenous primary-source detail
  • Federal or settler detail
  • Map-based inference
  • Indigenous resistance or adaptation
  • Cultural continuity
  • Consequences for Indigenous nations and national growth

Reasoning and Vocabulary

Use geographic reasoning, perspective, causation, contextualization, and CER. Treat the West as inhabited, distinguish Indigenous nations, center sovereignty and agency, and use at least 8 vocabulary terms.
