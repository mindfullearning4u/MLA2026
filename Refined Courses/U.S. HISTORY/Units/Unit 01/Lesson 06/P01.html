Unit 1 • Lesson 6

Westward Expansion, Indigenous Nations, and the Transformation of the West

Investigation Question

How did geography, migration, federal policy, economic expansion, and Indigenous resistance reshape the American West in the nineteenth century?

Learning Goals

  • Explain how regions, resources, migration, railroads, and policy interacted.
  • Treat the West as inhabited Indigenous homelands and politically contested territory.
  • Compare Indigenous, federal, and settler perspectives.
  • Use geographic reasoning in evidence-based claims.
