Unit 1 • Lesson 4

P07 — Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Investigation Question

How did freedpeople use education, labor, community institutions, voting, and political office to define freedom during Reconstruction, and what limited their gains?

Response Format

Write 350–400 words. Four developed paragraphs are recommended. Submit only the final response to the Checkpoint assignment.

Required Evidence

  • One family or community example
  • One education example
  • The Freedmen’s Bureau
  • One labor/economic-freedom example
  • Voting and one African American officeholder
  • One Reconstruction-government institutional change
  • One African American primary-source detail
  • One federal/government record
  • One limitation on Reconstruction gains
  • At least two historical-significance criteria
  • At least seven vocabulary terms

Required Reasoning

  • Historical Significance: evaluate rather than list.
  • Perspective: center freedpeople’s goals and agency.
  • Continuity and Change: explain gains and continuing limits.
  • Corroboration: connect different source types.
  • CER: claim, evidence, and reasoning.

Paragraph Guide

  1. Claim and multidimensional freedom: explain how freedpeople actively constructed freedom.
  2. Family, education, churches, labor, and land: use evidence and limits.
  3. Voting, officeholding, and coalition government: explain political change and institutions.
  4. Significance and limits: evaluate scale/change/precedent or influence and explain opposition or weak implementation.

Final Review — Not a New Notebook Task

Check length, four developed paragraphs, all required evidence, two significance criteria, seven vocabulary terms, source limitations, and independent reasoning.
