P06 — Independent Investigation

Investigation Question

Which achievement of freedpeople during Reconstruction most significantly changed the meaning of American freedom?

This is a Historian’s Notebook task. Evaluate all six before selecting one.

Worked Analogous Example — Method Only

Freedom of movement is not one of the six ranked choices. Model: example—people could search for relatives and negotiate work; source—family notices and Bureau records; immediate effect—greater personal autonomy; limitation—violence and economic dependence restricted movement; significance—high degree of change from slavery even when protection was uneven.

Notebook Task 2 — Rank and Defend

  1. Rank all six achievements from 1–6.
  2. Identify the top three.
  3. Select one as most significant.
  4. Write a 7–9 sentence defense using at least three significance criteria and two source details.
  5. Explain one limitation and why it does not automatically erase significance.
  6. Write 2–3 sentences addressing a counterargument for another achievement.

Required Notebook Evidence

  • All six completed evaluation rows
  • Ranking 1–6 and top three
  • 7–9 sentence evidence-based defense
  • Limitation analysis
  • 2–3 sentence counterargument
