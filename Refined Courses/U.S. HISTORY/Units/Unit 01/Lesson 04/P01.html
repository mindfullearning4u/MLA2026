Unit 1 • Lesson 4

Freedpeople, Political Participation, and Reconstruction Governments

Investigation Question

How did freedpeople use education, labor, community institutions, voting, and political office to define freedom during Reconstruction, and what limited their gains?

What You Will Learn

  • Center freedpeople as historical actors who built freedom.
  • Examine family, education, churches, labor, land, voting, and officeholding.
  • Analyze Reconstruction governments as political coalitions.
  • Use historical-significance criteria to evaluate achievements and limitations.
