U.S. HISTORY • UNIT 1 • LESSON 7

P07 • Mastery Checkpoint Directions

Checkpoint question

How did industrialization transform the American economy, workplace, cities, immigration patterns, and relationships between workers and business?

Product

Write 375–450 words. Four developed paragraphs are recommended. This is separate from the notebook submission.

Required evidence checklist

  • At least two causes of industrial growth
  • Steel, oil, transportation, or communication example
  • One business-organization example
  • One industrial leader/business example
  • One worker primary-source detail and one labor-movement example
  • One European immigration example and one Asian immigration example
  • Urbanization evidence and one quantitative-data inference
  • At least one benefit/opportunity and one cost/conflict
  • One interaction among developments
  • At least 8 vocabulary terms used accurately

Recommended paragraph plan

ParagraphPurpose
1Claim plus multiple causes of industrial growth
2Business organization, leader example, worker/business relationship
3Labor, European/Asian immigration comparison, and urbanization/data
4Balanced conclusion: opportunity, conflict, interaction, continuity/change, and limitation

Final quality check

  1. Does every paragraph explain how or why?
  2. Are claims tied to specific evidence?
  3. Did you compare immigrant experiences without stereotyping?
  4. Did you interpret quantitative evidence without claiming it proves everything?
  5. Did you avoid a one-sided “all good” or “all harmful” narrative?
  6. Is the response within 375–450 words?

Mastery target

Score at least 80%. If remediation is required, use feedback to strengthen the missing reasoning or evidence before another attempt is authorized.
