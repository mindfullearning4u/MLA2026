Worked Example 1: Reading the standard

Problem: A student is studying Postwar Prosperity, Inequality, and Social Class and needs to explain this evidence detail: The GI Bill, defense spending, highway construction, and expanding industries helped many families enter the middle class

Worked Model: Begin by naming the historical setting, because the same word can mean different things in different eras. In this lesson, the important detail is The GI Bill, defense spending, highway construction, and expanding industries helped many families enter the middle class. Do not stop at naming it; ask what problem, decision, or conflict this detail helps explain.

Student Think-Aloud: The detail points toward this relationship: Suburban growth changed housing, transportation, schools, shopping, and family life, but segregation and lending discrimination limited access for many African American families. That tells you the answer needs cause-and-effect reasoning, not just a definition.

Model Answer: The GI Bill, defense spending, highway construction, and expanding industries helped many families enter the middle class. This matters because it shows this relationship: Suburban growth changed housing, transportation, schools, shopping, and family life, but segregation and lending discrimination limited access for many African American families. The answer is strong because it uses a specific detail, explains the historical relationship, and stays within Postwar Prosperity, Inequality, and Social Class instead of drifting into another lesson.

Incorrect shortcut: "GI Bill, defense spending, is enough because I remember seeing it in the lesson."
Correct thinking: The evidence must be connected to the historical relationship it proves: Suburban growth changed housing, transportation, schools, shopping, and family life, but segregation and lending discrimination limited access for many African American families.
Teachable correction: Do not describe the postwar era as equally prosperous for all Americans. The standard requires analysis of prosperity and social class. The fix is to pair the evidence with a cause, consequence, comparison, or policy impact from this exact lesson.
