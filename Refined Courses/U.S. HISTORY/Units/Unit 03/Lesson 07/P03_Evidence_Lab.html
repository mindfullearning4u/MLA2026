Source Method
  1. Identify creator, date, context, and source type.
  2. Determine audience, purpose, and perspective.
  3. Extract a precise evidence detail.
  4. Make a supported inference.
  5. Name a limitation.
  6. Corroborate with a different source type or perspective.
