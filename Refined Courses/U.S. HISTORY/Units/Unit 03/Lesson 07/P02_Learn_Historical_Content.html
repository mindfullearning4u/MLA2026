Vocabulary â€” Copy Every Term and Definition
New DealSeries of federal programs, policies, and reforms developed under Franklin D. Roosevelt in response to the Great Depression.
ReliefImmediate assistance intended to help people facing economic hardship.
RecoveryPolicies intended to restore economic activity, employment, production, and income.
ReformStructural change intended to correct problems or reduce future instability.
First Hundred DaysEarly period of Franklin D. Roosevelt's presidency in 1933 during which Congress enacted many major New Deal measures.
Bank holidayTemporary closure of banks intended to allow government inspection and restore confidence during the 1933 banking crisis.
FDICFederal Deposit Insurance Corporation, created to insure qualifying bank deposits and strengthen public confidence in banks.
SECSecurities and Exchange Commission, federal agency established to regulate securities markets.
CCCCivilian Conservation Corps, New Deal work program employing young men in conservation and public-land projects.
WPAWorks Progress Administration, major federal work-relief program providing jobs in construction, public works, arts, writing, and other projects.
TVATennessee Valley Authority, federal regional-development agency associated with dams, electricity, flood control, and economic development.
AAAAgricultural Adjustment Administration, New Deal program designed to support farm prices and agricultural income by influencing production.
Social Security Act1935 federal law establishing old-age insurance, unemployment-compensation structures, and selected assistance programs.
Wagner Act1935 law protecting many workers' rights to organize and bargain collectively and creating the National Labor Relations Board.
Collective bargainingNegotiation between workers or their representatives and employers over employment terms.
Court-packing planRoosevelt's unsuccessful 1937 proposal to change the size of the Supreme Court by allowing appointment of additional justices under specified conditions.
FederalismDivision and sharing of governmental authority between national and state governments.
Social insuranceGovernment-supported system designed to provide protection against certain economic risks such as unemployment, disability, or old age.
ConstitutionalityWhether a law or government action conforms to the Constitution.
