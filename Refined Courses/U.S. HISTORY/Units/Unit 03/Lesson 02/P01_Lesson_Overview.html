Unit 3 Lesson 2: Mass Culture, Consumerism, and Technological Change

Investigation Question

How did new technologies, advertising, mass media, and consumer culture change everyday American life during the 1920s?

Learning Goals
  • Explain radio, film, automobiles, household technology, and recorded sound.
  • Analyze advertising and media as persuasive historical evidence.
  • Distinguish shared national culture from cultural uniformity.
  • Evaluate change, continuity, unequal access, and significance.
