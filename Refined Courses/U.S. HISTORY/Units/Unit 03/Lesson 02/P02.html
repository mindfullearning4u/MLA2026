Core Content Mini-Lesson

U.S. expansion overseas involved economic interests, strategic concerns, ideology, military action, and debate over empire. Students must explain motives and consequences, not only identify territories.

Teaching moveWhat you should do
Move 1Economic motives included new markets, raw materials, investment opportunities, and access to trade routes.
Move 2Strategic motives included naval bases, coaling stations, the Panama Canal route, and influence in the Caribbean and Pacific.
Move 3Ideological motives included Social Darwinism, missionary arguments, nationalism, and belief in spreading American institutions.
Move 4The Spanish-American War, annexation debates, and territorial acquisitions affected Cuba, Puerto Rico, Guam, the Philippines, Hawaii, and Panama in different ways.
