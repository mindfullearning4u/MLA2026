P04 — Historical Thinking: Continuity and Change

Seven-Step Process
  1. Identify the earlier condition.
  2. Identify the technological development.
  3. Explain immediate behavioral or social change.
  4. Identify what continued.
  5. Compare groups or regions.
  6. Judge the degree of change.
  7. Defend the judgment with evidence.
Worked Radio Example

Earlier condition: much entertainment and information was locally limited. Development: broadcasting reached wide audiences. Change: common news, music, sports, advertising, and entertainment supported shared experiences. Continuity: segregation, regional traditions, income inequality, and different interpretations remained. Judgment: radio expanded mass culture without making society culturally uniform.

Page Summary — Copy

Continuity-and-change analysis compares conditions before and after a development, traces behavior and social effects, identifies persistence, and measures degree. Technology creates possibilities; it does not automatically determine identical outcomes.
