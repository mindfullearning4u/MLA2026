Unit 3 Lesson 1: The Postwar Economy and the Roaring Twenties

Investigation Question

Why did the United States experience rapid economic growth during much of the 1920s, and why was that prosperity uneven?

What You Will Learn
  • How demobilization and the 1920–1921 recession preceded recovery.
  • How productivity, mass production, automobiles, credit, and advertising interacted.
  • Why national growth did not mean equal prosperity for every group or region.
  • How historians use quantitative evidence, perspective, and source limitations.
Historical-Thinking Process

We will use cause and effect: identify conditions, separate immediate from long-term causes, trace interactions, test consequences, and recognize limits. You will also compare groups and interpret numerical evidence.
