Submission 2 — Mastery Checkpoint

Prompt: Why did the United States experience rapid economic growth during much of the 1920s, and why was that prosperity uneven?

Length: 375–450 words; four developed paragraphs recommended.

Required evidence: demobilization/postwar adjustment; productivity; mass production; automobile network; credit/installment buying; advertising; at least one consumer good or electrification example; agricultural distress; one quantitative trend; two unequal experiences involving groups or regions.

Reasoning: explain at least three interacting causes, distinguish opportunity from risk, address an alternative interpretation, and end with a qualified CER judgment. Use at least eight vocabulary terms accurately.

Checkpoint Structure
  1. Claim and context: define the boom and postwar transition.
  2. Growth explanation: show how production, demand, credit, and networks interacted.
  3. Uneven prosperity: use agricultural and group/regional evidence.
  4. Judgment: weigh the strongest explanation, address an alternative, and qualify the conclusion.
