Continue the Learning Sequence

Step 1: Separate claim from evidence. A claim is what you think the historical evidence shows. Evidence is the specific detail that supports the claim.
Step 2: Read the stimulus in layers. For turning-point evidence matrix, first read the title, then the categories, then the pattern, then the source or time period.
Step 3: Explain the relationship. Use words such as because, therefore, however, as a result, in contrast, and this shows to connect details.
Step 4: Verify scope. If your answer is interesting but does not connect to Unit 3 Synthesis: Expansion, War, Culture, Rights, and Economic Crisis, it does not meet this lesson's mapping.

What You May Need Clarified

Do not confuse a familiar term with the full historical explanation. The answer must explain what the evidence proves.

To avoid that confusion, always pair the historical term with a time period, a group or policy, and a consequence. That turns a memorized term into an explanation.
