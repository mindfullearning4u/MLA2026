Step-by-Step Process
  1. Define the historical development precisely.
  2. Select common analytical categories.
  3. Use evidence before judging.
  4. Compare perspectives, groups, or periods.
  5. Explain mechanism, change, consequence, or significance.
  6. Identify limits and an alternative interpretation.
  7. Write a qualified CER conclusion.
Worked Model

Claim: one development mattered because it changed more than one area of life. Evidence: a contemporary source and a contrasting or quantitative source support the pattern. Reasoning: the evidence shows how the development operated, while unequal effects and continuity qualify the conclusion.
