Worked Example 1: Reading the standard

Problem: A student is studying Foreign and Domestic Terrorism and needs to explain this evidence detail: Domestic terrorism includes attacks carried out within the United States by individuals or groups connected to domestic causes, such as the Oklahoma City bombing

Worked Model: Begin by naming the historical setting, because the same word can mean different things in different eras. In this lesson, the important detail is Domestic terrorism includes attacks carried out within the United States by individuals or groups connected to domestic causes, such as the Oklahoma City bombing. Do not stop at naming it; ask what problem, decision, or conflict this detail helps explain.

Student Think-Aloud: The detail points toward this relationship: Foreign terrorism includes attacks connected to international networks or conflicts, such as the September 11 attacks. That tells you the answer needs cause-and-effect reasoning, not just a definition.

Model Answer: Domestic terrorism includes attacks carried out within the United States by individuals or groups connected to domestic causes, such as the Oklahoma City bombing. This matters because it shows this relationship: Foreign terrorism includes attacks connected to international networks or conflicts, such as the September 11 attacks. The answer is strong because it uses a specific detail, explains the historical relationship, and stays within Foreign and Domestic Terrorism instead of drifting into another lesson.

Incorrect shortcut: "Domestic terrorism includes attacks is enough because I remember seeing it in the lesson."
Correct thinking: The evidence must be connected to the historical relationship it proves: Foreign terrorism includes attacks connected to international networks or conflicts, such as the September 11 attacks.
Teachable correction: Do not treat every act of violence as the same. Identify whether the question asks about domestic terrorism, foreign terrorism, response, or effect. The fix is to pair the evidence with a cause, consequence, comparison, or policy impact from this exact lesson.
