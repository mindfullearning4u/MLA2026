P04 • Integrated Historical Thinking

Unit 2 Synthesis: Reform, Empire, and America's Emerging Global Role

Nine-step synthesis process

  1. Identify a major development.
  2. Establish context.
  3. Identify causes.
  4. Identify consequences.
  5. Compare with another development.
  6. Identify continuity and change.
  7. Evaluate significance.
  8. Connect domestic and international history.
  9. Construct CER.

Worked example: Progressive reform + wartime mobilization

Both expanded expectations for federal action, but for different purposes. Progressives sought regulation of economic/social problems; wartime mobilization coordinated resources for military needs. Together they show a broader shift toward active national government, while wartime civil-liberties restrictions demonstrate that expanded power also created new conflict.

Pause and Review — notebook

What makes this synthesis rather than a list? Model: It uses a direct comparison, shared pattern, difference in purpose, continuity/change, and a counterpoint about power.
