Unit 2 Synthesis: Reform, Empire, and America's Emerging Global Role

Guided Question

How did the meaning and use of government power change from the Gilded Age through World War I?

Copy only the Pause and Review and summary.

Component Worked example
Claim National power became more active in regulation, diplomacy, and mobilization, but remained contested.
Domestic reform Progressives used government to regulate corporate/consumer problems.
Political/economic reform Amendments and process reforms altered accountability and participation unevenly.
Foreign expansion Territorial control, Panama, and diplomacy extended power beyond the continent.
WWI mobilization Federal agencies coordinated people, production, finance, and information.
Continuity Inequality, exclusion, sovereignty disputes, and limits on authority persisted.
Counterpoint League rejection shows that expanded global activity did not erase domestic limits.
