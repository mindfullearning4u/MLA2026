Progressive Reform and the Expansion of Democracy

Checkpoint Question

How did Progressive reformers attempt to change government, business, working conditions, and political participation, and why were their reforms uneven?

Length: 375–450 words; four developed paragraphs recommended.

Required evidence

  • One muckraker and one primary reformer-source detail
  • One consumer/business regulation example
  • One labor/workplace reform
  • One political-process reform plus the Seventeenth Amendment or direct-democracy reform
  • Women’s suffrage/Nineteenth Amendment
  • One African American reform source
  • One opposing or limiting perspective and one reform limitation
  • At least two significance criteria and 8 vocabulary terms
Paragraph Purpose
1 Claim, diverse Progressive context, and significance criteria
2 Government/business/consumer/labor reform with corroborated evidence
3 Political participation, suffrage, and African American activism
4 Achievements, exclusions, continuity/change, and significance judgment
