P01 • Lesson Overview

Progressive Reform and the Expansion of Democracy

Investigation Question

How did Progressive reformers attempt to change government, business, working conditions, and political participation, and why were their reforms uneven?

Learning Path

  1. Connect urban, labor, corporate, political, and participation problems to reform.
  2. Analyze reformers, opponents, laws, photographs, and interpretations.
  3. Measure historical significance through shared criteria.
  4. Study a complete guided evaluation.
  5. Rank six reform areas independently.
  6. Submit a 375–450 word checkpoint.

Standards and boundary

MLA.USH.PROG.02–03; supporting PROG.01 and inquiry HI.02, HI.03, HI.05, HI.06; Florida SS.912.A.3.12 with supporting 3.8 and 3.11. Imperialism and World War I are reserved for later lessons.
