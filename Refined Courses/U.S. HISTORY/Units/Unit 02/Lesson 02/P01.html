P01 • Lesson Overview

Labor Movements, Farmers, Populism, and Economic Reform

Investigation Question

How did workers and farmers respond to economic change, and why did their reform movements develop different goals and strategies?

Learning Path

  1. Learn labor and farm economic pressures.
  2. Analyze worker, employer, farmer, platform, rhetoric, and data evidence.
  3. Compare movements using the same categories.
  4. Watch a complete guided comparison.
  5. Evaluate organized labor and Populism independently.
  6. Submit a 375–450 word mastery response.

Standards

Notebook on this page

Copy the investigation question and this overview: Workers and farmers faced different positions in an industrial, national-market economy. Both challenged concentrated power, but workers emphasized wages, hours, safety, and bargaining, while farmers emphasized debt, crop prices, railroad rates, credit, and currency.
