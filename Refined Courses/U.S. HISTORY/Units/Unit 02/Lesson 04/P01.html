P01 • Lesson Overview

U.S. Imperialism and the Spanish-American War

Investigation Question

Why did the United States expand its influence overseas in the late nineteenth century, and how did the Spanish-American War change its global role?

Learning Path

  1. Separate long-term causes, immediate triggers, war, and consequences.
  2. Use expansionist, newspaper, Cuban, Filipino, government, and anti-imperialist evidence.
  3. Apply cause/effect and geographic reasoning.
  4. Evaluate six factors independently.
  5. Submit a 375–450 word checkpoint.

Verified Florida benchmarks

SS.912.A.4.1: Analyze major factors driving U.S. imperialism. SS.912.A.4.2: Explain motives for U.S. acquisition of territories. SS.912.A.4.3: Examine causes, course, and consequences of the Spanish-American War.

Panama Canal and later Roosevelt/Taft/Wilson diplomacy are reserved for Lesson 5.

Notebook on this page

Copy the question and this overview: Overseas expansion grew from interacting economic, strategic, ideological, political, and humanitarian/public-opinion pressures. The 1898 war accelerated territorial control and global involvement while creating sovereignty, citizenship, and self-government conflicts.
