P01 • Lesson Overview

World War I: Causes, Neutrality, and U.S. Entry

Investigation Question

Why did the United States move from neutrality to intervention in World War I?

Learning Path

  1. Separate European war causes from causes of U.S. entry.
  2. Trace neutrality from 1914 to 1917.
  3. Evaluate maps, diplomatic sources, trade evidence, and competing arguments.
  4. Rank six entry factors independently.
  5. Submit a 375–450 word checkpoint.

Standards

MLA.USH.WWI.01; MLA inquiry standards; SS.912.A.4.5.

Notebook on this page

Copy the question and overview: European causes and the Sarajevo trigger began the war, but the United States initially remained neutral. Submarine warfare, neutral rights, the Zimmermann Telegram, economic ties, security, and political ideals accumulated before entry in April 1917.
