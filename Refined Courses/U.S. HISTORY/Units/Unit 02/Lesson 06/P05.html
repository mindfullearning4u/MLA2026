World War I: Causes, Neutrality, and U.S. Entry

Guided Question

Which evidence best explains the shift from neutrality to intervention?

Copy only the Pause and Review and summary.

Factor Evidence and policy effect Limitation
Submarine warfare 1917 renewal directly threatened shipping and rights. Needs earlier context and political decision.
Zimmermann Telegram Raised a diplomatic/security threat. Proposal was not implemented by Mexico.
Economic ties Allied trade and finance shaped interests. Cannot alone explain timing or rhetoric.
Neutral rights Shipping disputes challenged claimed legal rights. Rights were interpreted amid wartime pressures.
Security Atlantic warfare and German strategy altered risk. Security claims require specific evidence.
Political ideals Wilson framed international order and democracy. Rhetoric may simplify mixed motives.

Pause and Review — notebook

How did submarine warfare and neutral rights reinforce each other? Model: Attacks created physical danger while also violating the legal principle U.S. leaders claimed to defend.
