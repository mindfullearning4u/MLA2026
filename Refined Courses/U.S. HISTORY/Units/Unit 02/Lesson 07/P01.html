P01 • Lesson Overview

World War I at Home and the Postwar Settlement

Investigation Question

How did World War I transform American society and government at home, and why did the United States reject part of the postwar settlement it had helped create?

Learning Path

  1. Trace military, economic, and civilian mobilization.
  2. Compare opportunity, discrimination, propaganda, and dissent.
  3. Evaluate continuity and change across six areas.
  4. Compare Wilson, Reservationists, and Irreconcilables.
  5. Submit a 400–475 word checkpoint.

Standards
