U.S. HISTORY • UNIT 2 • LESSON 1

P04 • Historical Thinking: Continuity and Change

Seven-step process

  1. Identify the earlier condition.
  2. Identify the later condition.
  3. Name specific changes.
  4. Name specific continuities.
  5. Judge degree and pace of change.
  6. Explain why change occurred.
  7. Support the conclusion with source and quantitative evidence.

Complete worked model

Move Reasoning
Earlier condition Smaller urban populations and earlier immigration patterns.
Later condition Rapidly expanding industrial cities with large immigrant populations.
Changes Neighborhoods expanded, transportation and infrastructure grew, and machines adapted to mobilize residents.
Continuities Prejudice, poverty, unequal power, community reliance, and political competition remained.
Cause and pace Industrial jobs, immigration, internal migration, transportation, and natural growth produced rapid change.
Evidence/limit Census trend plus immigrant or political evidence; categories and individual sources remain limited.

Pause and Review — notebook task

Copy and answer: Why is “cities changed” not enough?

Model: It lacks an earlier/later comparison, the degree and pace of change, causes, continuities, and evidence. Historical reasoning must explain all five.
