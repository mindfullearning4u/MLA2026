P01 • Lesson Overview

Panama, Roosevelt, and an Expanding U.S. Global Role

Investigation Question

How did strategic geography, economic interests, and presidential diplomacy expand U.S. influence in Latin America and the Caribbean during the early twentieth century?

Learning Path

  1. Read Panama's location as historical evidence.
  2. Connect trade, naval mobility, security, and political control.
  3. Evaluate Roosevelt, Taft, and Wilson with regional perspectives.
  4. Apply geographic reasoning independently.
  5. Submit a 375–450 word checkpoint.

Standards
