Lesson: Circle Constructions

Notebook Title

Circle Constructions

Purpose: Construct inscribed/circumscribed circles, regular polygons in circles, and tangent lines.

Vocabulary

  • Identify the mathematical object: the construction diagram with compass arcs, endpoints, and intersection points.
  • Use precise Geometry vocabulary from the lesson title and standards: MLA.GEO.CIR.1, MLA.GEO.CIR.2, MLA.GEO.CIR.3.
  • Record givens, markings, measurements, and conclusions separately.

Teaching Sequence

Step 1: Read the lesson task and underline the givens. A given may be a marked segment, angle measure, coordinate, radius, side length, scale factor, or construction step.

Why: Geometry conclusions must come from evidence, not from how a figure appears.

Step 2: Label the construction diagram with compass arcs, endpoints, and intersection points. Add names for points, sides, angles, arcs, coordinates, or dimensions before you calculate or justify anything.

Step 3: Choose the definition, theorem, construction property, or formula that matches the labeled evidence.

Step 4: Write one sentence that connects the evidence to the conclusion for Circle Constructions.

Student-Guided Thinking for Notebook Part 1

Use this section to model the first layer of the lesson. In Circle Constructions, do not begin by guessing from the diagram or choosing a formula too quickly. First name what the problem is asking, identify the givens, markings, measurements, coordinates, or relationships you can see, and connect the task to MLA.GEO.CIR.1, MLA.GEO.CIR.2, MLA.GEO.CIR.3.
