P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A central angle has the same measure as its intercepted arc. An inscribed angle has half the measure of its intercepted arc. Congruent chords in the same circle intercept congruent arcs.

Worked Example 1

Example: If an inscribed angle intercepts a 96° arc, the angle measures 1⁄2(96°) = 48°.

Example: An inscribed angle has the same measure as its intercepted arc.
