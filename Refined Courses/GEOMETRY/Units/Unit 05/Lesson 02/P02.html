P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Arcs, Angles, and Chords

Vocabulary

  • Arc: a connected part of a circle.
  • Minor arc: an arc measuring less than 180°.
  • Major arc: an arc measuring greater than 180°.
  • Central angle: an angle with vertex at the center of the circle.
  • Inscribed angle: an angle with vertex on the circle and sides that contain chords.
  • Chord: a segment with endpoints on the circle.

Notebook Task

  • Draw a circle with one central angle and one inscribed angle.
  • Label the intercepted arc for each angle.
  • Write what relationship is shown and what can be concluded.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

An inscribed angle has half the measure of its intercepted arc.

Correct:

The guided practice checks central angles, inscribed angles, intercepted arcs, and chord-arc relationships.
