P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Arc Length and Sector Area

Vocabulary

  • Arc length: the distance along part of a circle.
  • Sector: a region bounded by two radii and an arc.
  • Central angle: the angle that determines the fraction of the circle.
  • Circumference: the distance around a full circle, 2πr.
  • Circle area: the area of a full circle, πr2.

Notebook Task

  • Sketch a sector and label the radius, central angle, arc, and shaded region.
  • Write the arc length formula and sector area formula.
  • Explain how the central angle gives the fraction of the full circle.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

Sector area uses πr2 because it measures a region of the circle.

Correct:

The guided practice checks formula selection, substitution, arc length, sector area, and unit interpretation.
