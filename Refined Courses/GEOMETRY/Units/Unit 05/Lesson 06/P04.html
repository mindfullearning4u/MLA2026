P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Arc length is θ⁄360(2πr). Sector area is θ⁄360(πr2). The central angle determines what fraction of the full circumference or area is used.

Worked Example 1

Example: For a 90° sector with radius 8, arc length is 90⁄360(2π8)=4π, while sector area is 90⁄360(π82)=16π.

Worked Example 2

Example: Sector area uses 2πr because it measures around the circle.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Sector area uses πr2 because it measures a region of the circle.

Correct:

The guided practice checks formula selection, substitution, arc length, sector area, and unit interpretation.
