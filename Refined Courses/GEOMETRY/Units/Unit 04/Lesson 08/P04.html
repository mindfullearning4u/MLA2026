Key Concept

Unit synthesis problems require selecting the right tool. Similarity problems use corresponding parts and scale factors. Right-triangle problems use side relationships, the Pythagorean Theorem, and trigonometric ratios.

Worked Example 1

Similarity review: If all corresponding side ratios are equal, figures are similar and the common ratio is the scale factor.

Worked Example 2

Right-triangle review: Label the diagram first. Use a2 + b2 = c2 for side lengths, or sine, cosine, and tangent when a reference angle is involved.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Choose a formula or ratio before labeling the diagram.

Correct:

Label the diagram first, then choose the relationship that matches the given information.
