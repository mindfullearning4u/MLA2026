Key Concept

In a right triangle, the legs form the right angle and the hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. The Pythagorean Theorem, a2 + b2 = c2, connects the side lengths of right triangles.

Worked Example 1

Example: A right triangle has legs 9 and 12.

Equation: 92 + 122 = c2.

Solve: 81 + 144 = 225, so c = √225 = 15.

Worked Example 2

Example: A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 and one leg 5.

Equation: 52 + b2 = 132.

Solve: b2 = 169 - 25 = 144, so b = 12.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Use a side relationship without checking which sides are given.

Correct:

Label the right triangle first, then choose the equation or ratio that matches the given sides.
