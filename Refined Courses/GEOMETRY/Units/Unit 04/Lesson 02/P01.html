P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Dilations and Similarity Transformations

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.CGM.3: I can identify transformation sequences that map figures onto themselves or other figures.
  • MLA.GEO.CGM.4: I can identify reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry in geometric figures.
  • MLA.GEO.SIM.2: I can use transformations to justify that two figures are similar.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify transformation sequences that map figures.
  • Identify reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry.
  • Use dilations and transformations to justify similarity.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze diagrams using corresponding parts and proportions.
  • Write short mathematical explanations.
  • Connect similarity relationships to transformations.
