P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Dilations and Similarity Transformations

Vocabulary

  • Dilation: a transformation that changes size by a scale factor.
  • Similarity transformation: a dilation, or a dilation with rigid motions, that maps one figure to a similar figure.
  • Center of dilation: the fixed point from which distances are scaled.
  • Symmetry: a transformation that maps a figure onto itself.
  • Sequence: transformations performed in order.

Notebook Task

  • Draw or describe the given figure relationship.
  • Identify corresponding parts.
  • Write what evidence supports similarity or transformation reasoning.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A dilation with scale factor 2 proves congruence.

Correct:

A dilation with scale factor 2 changes size, so it can support similarity but not congruence.
