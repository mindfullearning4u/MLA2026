P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.TRI.4: I can define sine, cosine, and tangent ratios for acute angles in right triangles.

Key Concept

Trigonometric ratios compare side lengths in right triangles relative to a chosen acute angle. The reference angle determines which leg is opposite and which leg is adjacent.

Worked Example 1

Example: Relative to angle A, the opposite side is 7 and the hypotenuse is 25.

Ratio: sin A = opposite⁄hypotenuse = 7⁄25.

Worked Example 2

Example: Relative to angle B, the opposite side is 9 and the adjacent side is 12.

Ratio: tan B = opposite⁄adjacent = 9⁄12.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Use a side relationship without checking which sides are given.

Correct:

Label the right triangle first, then choose the equation or ratio that matches the given sides.
