# GEOMETRY - Unit 04 Overview: Similarity, Right Triangles, and Trigonometry

This unit follows the approved Geometry Unit 4 mapping. Students develop proportional reasoning, similarity transformations, similar triangles, right-triangle structure, trigonometric ratios, and right-triangle applications before moving into circles and measurement.
