# GEOMETRY - Unit 04 Overview: Coordinate Geometry and Quadrilaterals

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops coordinate geometry as a tool for proof, classification, and geometric modeling. Students determine weighted averages, use coordinate methods to justify properties and theorems, solve geometric problems involving lines, circles, triangles, and quadrilaterals, and prove relationships about parallelograms and trapezoids.

Emphasis is placed on slope, distance, midpoint, coordinate proof, quadrilateral properties, perimeter, area, and precise justification using algebraic and geometric representations.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Standards with an emphasis on mathematical reasoning, proof, structure, and application. Students are expected to demonstrate both procedural fluency and conceptual understanding consistent with state expectations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- coordinate proof and classification;
- quadrilateral reasoning;
- perimeter, area, and coordinate modeling.

All assessment items are aligned to the unit benchmarks and require students to use coordinate geometry to justify definitions and properties, solve problems involving geometric figures on the coordinate plane, and prove or apply parallelogram and trapezoid relationships.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit emphasizes coordinate proof, quadrilateral properties, and geometric modeling on the coordinate plane. Students connect algebraic tools to geometric reasoning across multiple representations.
