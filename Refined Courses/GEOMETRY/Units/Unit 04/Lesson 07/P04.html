P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.TRI.5: I can solve right-triangle problems using trigonometric ratios and the Pythagorean Theorem.

Key Concept

Right-triangle applications require matching the problem situation to the correct side relationship. Use the Pythagorean Theorem for side lengths and trigonometric ratios when an acute angle is part of the information.

Worked Example 1

Example: A ladder is 25 ft long and its base is 7 ft from the wall.

Equation: h2 + 72 = 252.

Solve: h2 = 576, so h = 24 ft.

Worked Example 2

Example: An angle of elevation has opposite side h and adjacent side 30.

Setup: tan θ = h⁄30 because tangent compares opposite and adjacent sides.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Choose a formula or ratio before labeling the diagram.

Correct:

Label the diagram first, then choose the relationship that matches the given information.
