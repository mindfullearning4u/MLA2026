P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.SIM.1: I can solve problems involving congruent and similar two-dimensional figures.

Vocabulary

  • Scale factor: the multiplier from one figure to a similar figure.
  • Proportion: an equation showing two ratios are equal.
  • Corresponding sides: sides that match in the same relative position.
  • Indirect measurement: using similar figures to find a length that is difficult to measure directly.

Notebook Task – Part 1

Draw two similar figures and label at least two pairs of corresponding sides. Set up a proportion to solve for a missing side length.

Circle the corresponding sides before solving so the ratio order stays consistent.

Observation and Evidence

Record what is shown in the diagram separately from what can be concluded. A conclusion must be supported by markings, measurements, ratios, transformations, or given information.
