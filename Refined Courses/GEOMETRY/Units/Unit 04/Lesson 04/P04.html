P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.SIM.1: I can solve problems involving congruent and similar two-dimensional figures.

Key Concept

Similar and congruent figures can be used to solve geometry problems. Similar figures use proportional side lengths, while congruent figures have equal corresponding side lengths and a scale factor of 1.

Worked Example 1

Example: A side of length 8 corresponds to a side of length 12. The scale factor is 12⁄8 = 1.5.

If another side is 10, the matching side is 10(1.5) = 15.

Worked Example 2

Example: A 6-foot person casts a 9-foot shadow. A flagpole casts a 27-foot shadow.

The shadow scale factor is 27⁄9 = 3, so the flagpole height is 6(3) = 18 feet.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The figures look similar, so no evidence is needed.

Correct:

A similarity conclusion must be supported by angle, side, proportional, or transformation evidence.
