P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Similar figures have congruent corresponding angles and proportional corresponding side lengths. Similarity must be supported by evidence, not appearance alone.

Worked Example 1

Example: Figure A has side lengths 3, 4, 5. Figure B has corresponding side lengths 6, 8, 10.

Scale factor: 6⁄3 = 8⁄4 = 10⁄5 = 2, so the figures are similar.

Worked Example 2

Non-example: Side ratios 4⁄2, 9⁄3, and 10⁄5 are 2, 3, and 5. Since the ratios are not equal, the sides are not proportional.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The figures look alike, so they must be similar.

Correct:

Figures are similar only when corresponding angles match and corresponding side lengths are proportional.
