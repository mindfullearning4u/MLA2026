P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Similar Figures

Vocabulary

  • Similar figures: figures with the same shape and proportional side lengths.
  • Corresponding parts: matching sides or angles.
  • Scale factor: the multiplier from one figure to a similar figure.
  • Proportion: an equation showing two ratios are equal.

Notebook Task

  • Draw or describe the given figure relationship.
  • Identify corresponding parts.
  • Write what evidence supports similarity or transformation reasoning.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

The figures look alike, so they must be similar.

Correct:

Figures are similar only when corresponding angles match and corresponding side lengths are proportional.
