GEO | Unit 04 | Lesson 03

P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.SIM.3: I can justify triangle similarity criteria using dilations and other similarity transformations.

Vocabulary

  • AA similarity: two pairs of corresponding angles are congruent.
  • SAS similarity: two pairs of corresponding sides are proportional and the included angles are congruent.
  • SSS similarity: all three pairs of corresponding sides are proportional.
  • Correspondence: the matching order of vertices, angles, and sides.

Notebook Task – Part 1

Sketch two triangles. Mark two pairs of congruent corresponding angles and write the similarity statement in corresponding order.

Explain what is shown and what conclusion can be made from the markings.

Observation and Evidence

Record what is shown in the diagram separately from what can be concluded. A conclusion must be supported by markings, measurements, ratios, transformations, or given information.
