P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.SIM.3: I can justify triangle similarity criteria using dilations and other similarity transformations.

Key Concept

Triangle similarity can be justified efficiently with AA, SAS, or SSS similarity. A complete argument names the evidence, matches corresponding parts, and states the correct similarity criterion.

Worked Example 1

Example: ∠A ≅ ∠D and ∠B ≅ ∠E.

Conclusion: ▵ABC ∼ ▵DEF by AA similarity because two pairs of corresponding angles are congruent.

Worked Example 2

Example: Corresponding side ratios are 6⁄4, 9⁄6, and 12⁄8.

Each ratio equals 1.5, so the triangles are similar by SSS similarity.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The figures look similar, so no evidence is needed.

Correct:

A similarity conclusion must be supported by angle, side, proportional, or transformation evidence.
