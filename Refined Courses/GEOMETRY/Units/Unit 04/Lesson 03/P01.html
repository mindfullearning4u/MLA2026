P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Similar Triangles

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.SIM.3: I can justify triangle similarity criteria using dilations and other similarity transformations.

What You Will Learn

  • Use AA, SAS, and SSS criteria to justify triangle similarity.
  • Write triangle similarity statements in correct corresponding order.
  • Connect similarity criteria to dilations and transformations.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze diagrams and mark corresponding parts.
  • Write short mathematical explanations that connect evidence to conclusions.
  • Use proportional reasoning to support similarity claims.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence.
  • Submit the checkpoint response.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson strengthens Unit 4 similarity reasoning by connecting diagrams, proportions, transformations, and written justification.
