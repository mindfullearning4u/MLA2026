Notebook Focus: Construction Evidence

Review how compass and straightedge constructions create evidence.

  • A copied segment preserves length: CD = AB.
  • A copied angle preserves angle measure: m∠DEF = m∠ABC.
  • A segment bisector creates equal parts: AM = MB.
  • A perpendicular bisector creates equal parts and right angles.

Connect the Ideas

For each construction, write what is shown and what can be concluded.

  1. Copying segment AB onto a new ray from point C.
  2. Constructing the perpendicular bisector of segment AB.
  3. Constructing an angle bisector of ∠ABC.

Common Misconception

Incorrect:

The construction is correct because it looks accurate.

Correct:

The construction is correct because the compass openings and arcs support the required equal lengths or equal angles.
