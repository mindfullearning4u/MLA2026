GEO | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this synthesis lesson, you will review and connect the major ideas from Unit 1 before completing the cumulative Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.FND.1: I can construct a copy of a segment or an angle using geometric tools.

MLA.GEO.FND.2: I can construct segment bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular bisectors.

MLA.GEO.RP.1: I can prove and apply relationships involving lines, angles, and angle pairs.

MLA.GEO.RP.4: I can interpret conditional statements and identify converse, inverse, and contrapositive statements.

MLA.GEO.RP.5: I can construct valid geometric proofs, including proofs by contradiction.

MLA.GEO.RP.6: I can evaluate arguments and use counterexamples to disprove false statements.

What You Will Learn

  • How construction, logic, and proof ideas connect across Unit 1.
  • How to choose evidence for line, angle, and construction conclusions.
  • How to identify common misconceptions before the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review construction procedures and construction evidence.
  • Review conditional statements and related logical forms.
  • Practice proof reasoning, counterexamples, and line-angle relationships.
  • Prepare for the cumulative Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete synthesis notebook tasks and guided practice.
  • Submit checkpoint evidence connecting multiple Unit 1 ideas.
  • Demonstrate cumulative mastery on the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This synthesis lesson asks you to connect Unit 1 skills: construction precision, conditional reasoning, valid proof, line and angle relationships, and counterexample reasoning.
