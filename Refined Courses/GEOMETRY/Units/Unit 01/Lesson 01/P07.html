Checkpoint Task: Geometry Readiness and Mathematical Thinking

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. A diagram shows points A, B, and C on one straight line, with B between A and C. Write one observation and one conclusion supported by the diagram.
  2. A diagram shows segment JK and segment LM with matching tick marks. Write the conclusion and explain the evidence.
  3. A diagram shows angle DEF with a square corner mark at E. Write the measure of angle DEF and explain how the marking supports your conclusion.
  4. A student claims that two lines are parallel because they look like they never meet.

    The lines are parallel because they look parallel.

    Identify the misconception. Correct the reasoning by explaining what evidence would be needed.

Mastery Criteria

  • Makes accurate observations from diagrams.
  • Supports conclusions using labels, markings, or given information.
  • Uses precise Geometry vocabulary and notation.
  • Distinguishes between what is shown and what can actually be concluded.
  • Explains and corrects diagram reasoning mistakes using mathematical evidence.
