P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Question 1

A diagram shows points A, B, and C on one straight line, with B between A and C. Which conclusion is supported?

Answer: A, B, and C are collinear.

Feedback: Points on the same straight line are collinear. The diagram description gives enough evidence for this conclusion.

Question 2

A diagram shows segment DE and segment FG with matching tick marks. What do the markings show?

Answer: DE = FG

Feedback: Matching tick marks show congruent segments, so the segment lengths are equal.

Question 3

A drawing shows two angles that look equal, but there are no angle measures or matching arc marks. What can be concluded?

Answer: The angles cannot be concluded congruent from appearance alone.

Feedback: A conclusion about congruent angles requires evidence such as equal measures, matching arc marks, or a given statement.

Question 4

A diagram shows angle RST with a square corner mark at S. Which statement is supported?

Answer: m∠RST = 90°

Feedback: A square corner mark shows a right angle, and a right angle measures 90°.

Question 5

A student says two lines are parallel because they look like they never meet. What is the best correction?

Answer: Parallel lines require markings, a given statement, or another valid reason.

Feedback: Appearance alone is not enough evidence. Geometry conclusions must be supported by given information, markings, or valid reasoning.
