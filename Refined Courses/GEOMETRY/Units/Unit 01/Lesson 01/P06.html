GEO | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Diagram Observations

  1. A diagram shows points L, M, and N on one straight line, with M between L and N. Write two observations that are supported by the diagram.
  2. A diagram shows segment AB and segment CD with matching tick marks. Write the conclusion and explain the evidence.
  3. A diagram shows angle PQR with a square corner mark at Q. Write the angle measure and explain how you know.
  4. A drawing shows two segments that look equal but have no labels, measurements, or tick marks. Explain what can and cannot be concluded.

Part B: Evidence and Explanation

Use complete sentences. Your explanation should make your thinking clear.

  1. A diagram states that line r is parallel to line s. Explain what evidence supports the conclusion that the lines are parallel.
  2. A student says angle A and angle B are congruent because they look the same size. Explain why this conclusion needs more evidence.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why labels and markings matter in Geometry.
  4. Write one sentence explaining what you should do when a diagram does not provide enough evidence for a conclusion.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student sees two lines that look parallel and writes:

    The lines are parallel because they look parallel.

    Explain the mistake. Then write a corrected statement about what evidence would be needed.
  2. A student sees two angles that look equal and writes:

    The angles are congruent.

    Explain the misconception. Then write what information would support the conclusion.
