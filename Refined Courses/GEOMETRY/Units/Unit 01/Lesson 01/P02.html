P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Lesson: Geometry Readiness Diagnostic and Mathematical Thinking

Notebook Title

Geometry Readiness Diagnostic and Mathematical Thinking

Purpose: Identify Geometry readiness strengths and establish diagram-based reasoning habits.

Vocabulary

  • Identify the mathematical object: the labeled diagram, formula, table, or written statement for this lesson.
  • Use precise Geometry vocabulary from the lesson title and standards: No new primary MLA standard, readiness/synthesis support only..
  • Record givens, markings, measurements, and conclusions separately.

Teaching Sequence

Step 1: Read the lesson task and underline the givens. A given may be a marked segment, angle measure, coordinate, radius, side length, scale factor, or construction step.

Why: Geometry conclusions must come from evidence, not from how a figure appears.

Step 2: Label the labeled diagram, formula, table, or written statement for this lesson. Add names for points, sides, angles, arcs, coordinates, or dimensions before you calculate or justify anything.

Step 3: Choose the definition, theorem, construction property, or formula that matches the labeled evidence.

Step 4: Write one sentence that connects the evidence to the conclusion for Geometry Readiness Diagnostic and Mathematical Thinking.
