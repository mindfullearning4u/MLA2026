📘 GEO | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Segment and Angle Constructions

In this lesson, you will use geometric tools to construct a copy of a segment and construct a copy of an angle while preserving the original length or angle measure.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.FND.1: I can construct a copy of a segment or an angle using geometric tools.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use a compass setting to copy a segment length.
  • How to use arcs to copy an angle measure.
  • How to keep the compass opening fixed when transferring a distance.
  • How to check that a constructed copy matches the original figure.

What You Will Do

  • Practice construction steps for copying a segment.
  • Practice construction steps for copying an angle.
  • Analyze common tool-use mistakes in constructions.
  • Complete a checkpoint explaining construction steps and evidence of accuracy.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice on segment and angle-copy constructions.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining how your construction preserves the original length or angle measure.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.FND.1: This standard asks you to make accurate copies of segments and angles using geometric tools, not estimation by sight.

In student language: I can use construction tools to copy a segment or angle so the copy matches the original.
