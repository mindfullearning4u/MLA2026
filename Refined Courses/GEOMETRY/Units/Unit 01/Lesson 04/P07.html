Checkpoint Task: Segment and Angle Constructions

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Describe how to construct a segment congruent to segment AB starting from a new endpoint C. Explain why the compass opening must stay fixed.
  2. Describe how to construct an angle congruent to ∠ABC from a new endpoint D. Explain how arcs help transfer the angle opening.

    I copied the angle because the new angle looks the same size.

    Identify the misconception. Correct the argument by explaining what construction evidence is needed.
