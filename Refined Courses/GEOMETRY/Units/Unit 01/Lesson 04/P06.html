📘 GEO | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Copying Segments

  1. Write the steps for constructing a segment congruent to segment AB starting from point C.
  2. Explain why the compass opening must stay fixed when transferring segment AB.
  3. Write a statement showing that segment CD is a correct copy of segment AB.
  4. Explain why estimating the copied length by sight is not a construction.

Part B: Copying Angles

Use complete sentences. Your explanation should make your thinking clear.

  1. Write the steps for constructing an angle congruent to ∠ABC from a new endpoint D.
  2. Explain why the same compass opening is used to draw arcs from the original vertex and the new vertex.
  3. Write a statement showing that ∠DEF is a correct copy of ∠ABC.
  4. Explain why drawing the second ray where it looks correct is not enough evidence.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student writes:

    I copied AB by drawing a segment that looked the same length.

    Explain the mistake. Then write the correct tool-based method.
  2. A student writes:

    I copied the angle by guessing where the second ray should go.

    Explain why the reasoning is incomplete. Then write what construction evidence should be used.
