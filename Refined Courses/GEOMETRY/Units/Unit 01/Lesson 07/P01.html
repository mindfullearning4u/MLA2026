P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Valid Arguments, Counterexamples, and Introductory Proof

In this lesson, you will evaluate geometric arguments, use counterexamples to disprove false claims, and write short introductory proofs using line and angle relationships.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.RP.1: I can prove and apply relationships involving lines, angles, and angle pairs.

MLA.GEO.RP.5: I can construct valid geometric proofs, including proofs by contradiction.

MLA.GEO.RP.6: I can evaluate arguments and use counterexamples to disprove false statements.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify valid and invalid mathematical arguments.
  • How to use a counterexample to disprove a false statement.
  • How to connect line and angle relationships to proof statements.
  • How proof by contradiction begins with assuming the opposite of what you want to prove.

What You Will Do

  • Evaluate whether conclusions follow from given evidence.
  • Write counterexamples for universal claims.
  • Complete short proof outlines involving vertical angles, linear pairs, and supplementary angles.
  • Explain why unsupported appearance-based arguments are invalid.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice on arguments, counterexamples, and proof structure.
  • Submit a checkpoint evaluating an argument and writing a short proof explanation.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.RP.1: This standard asks you to use line and angle relationships as evidence in reasoning.

MLA.GEO.RP.5: This standard asks you to build valid proof arguments, including contradiction reasoning.

MLA.GEO.RP.6: This standard asks you to judge whether an argument is valid and disprove false claims with counterexamples.
