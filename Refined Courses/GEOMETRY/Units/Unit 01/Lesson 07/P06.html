P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Teacher of Record Graded

Submit clear notebook evidence showing how you evaluate arguments, use counterexamples, and organize short proofs.

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Define valid argument, counterexample, and proof by contradiction.
  • Show one example of a line or angle proof statement with a valid reason.
  • Write one counterexample that disproves a false universal claim.
  • Explain why appearance alone is not enough proof.
  • Describe the first step in a proof by contradiction.

Evidence Quality

Your evidence should connect claims to reasons.

  • Claim: the conclusion being made.
  • Evidence: the given facts or relationships.
  • Reason: the definition, property, or theorem that connects the evidence to the claim.
