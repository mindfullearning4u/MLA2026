Checkpoint Task: Precision, Definitions, and Geometric Language

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. A figure has one endpoint at A and extends through point B in one direction. Identify the object and explain which definition supports your conclusion.
  2. A figure is straight, has arrows on both ends, and passes through points C and D. Write a precise description of the figure.
  3. Rewrite this vague description precisely: "There is a corner at E."
  4. A student writes:

    This is a line because it looks straight.

    Identify the misconception. Correct the reasoning by explaining what evidence is needed to identify a line.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses precise Geometry vocabulary and definitions.
  • Identifies points, lines, rays, segments, and angles from defining features.
  • Distinguishes observation from evidence.
  • Explains why appearance alone is not proof.
  • Corrects vague or inaccurate geometric descriptions.
