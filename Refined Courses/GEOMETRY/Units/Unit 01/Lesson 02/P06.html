P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Definitions and Precision

  1. Write the definition of a point, line, ray, segment, and angle in your notebook.
  2. A figure has endpoints M and N. Identify the object and explain which definition supports your answer.
  3. A figure begins at point P and passes through point Q with an arrow continuing beyond Q. Identify the object and explain the evidence.
  4. Rays CA and CB share endpoint C. Write a precise sentence that names the angle and its vertex.

Part B: Observation and Evidence

Use complete sentences. Your explanation should make your thinking clear.

  1. Explain the difference between an observation and evidence.
  2. Write one sentence explaining why appearance alone is not proof in Geometry.
  3. Rewrite this vague sentence precisely: "The figure has a corner at B."
  4. A student calls a figure a line because it is straight, but it has two endpoints. Explain the mistake.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student writes:

    A ray has two endpoints.

    Explain the mistake. Then write the correct definition of a ray.
  2. A student writes:

    The object is a segment because it looks short.

    Explain why this reasoning is not precise. Then write what evidence would support identifying a segment.
