Notebook Focus: Conditional Statements

A conditional statement is written in if-then form.

If p, then q.

  • p: the hypothesis, or condition.
  • q: the conclusion, or result.

Example

Statement: If a figure is a square, then it has four right angles.

  • Hypothesis: A figure is a square.
  • Conclusion: It has four right angles.

All and Not

The word all often signals a statement about every object in a group.

The word not changes the meaning by denying a condition or conclusion.

Example: All squares have four sides can be written as: If a figure is a square, then it has four sides.

Common Misconception

Do not switch the hypothesis and conclusion unless you are intentionally writing the converse.

Incorrect:

Hypothesis: It has four right angles.

Correct:

Hypothesis: A figure is a square.
