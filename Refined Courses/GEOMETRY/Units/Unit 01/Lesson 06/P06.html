P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Teacher of Record Graded

Submit clear notebook evidence showing how you interpret and rewrite logical statements.

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Define hypothesis and conclusion.
  • Explain the meaning of converse, inverse, and contrapositive.
  • Show one conditional statement and correctly write its converse, inverse, and contrapositive.
  • Explain how if and only if combines two conditional directions.
  • Include one note about how all or not changes the meaning of a statement.

Evidence Quality

Your evidence should distinguish between changing order and adding not.

  • Converse: Switch p and q.
  • Inverse: Keep the order and negate both parts.
  • Contrapositive: Switch the order and negate both parts.
