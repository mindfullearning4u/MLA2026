P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Conditional Statements, Converses, Inverses, and Contrapositives

In this lesson, you will interpret logical statements used in Geometry and rewrite conditional statements in related forms.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.RP.4: I can interpret conditional statements and identify converse, inverse, and contrapositive statements.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the hypothesis and conclusion in an if-then statement.
  • How to interpret biconditional statements written with if and only if.
  • How to interpret statements using all and not.
  • How to write the converse, inverse, and contrapositive of a conditional statement.

What You Will Do

  • Rewrite statements in if-then form.
  • Label hypotheses and conclusions.
  • Compare conditional, converse, inverse, and contrapositive statements.
  • Use precise logical language in short mathematical explanations.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice on logical statement forms.
  • Submit a checkpoint identifying and rewriting related conditional statements.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.RP.4: This standard asks you to read logical statements accurately and recognize how changing order or adding not changes the statement.

In student language: I can understand if-then statements and write their converse, inverse, and contrapositive forms.
