Checkpoint Task: Reasoning, Conjectures, and Mathematical Arguments

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. A diagram shows two segments with matching tick marks. Write a claim, the evidence, and a supported conclusion.
  2. A student says two angles are congruent because they look equal. Identify the flaw and explain what evidence would support the claim.
  3. Write a conjecture based on an observation. Then explain why the conjecture needs support before it can be accepted.
  4. A student writes:

    The lines are parallel because they look parallel.

    Identify the misconception. Correct the argument by explaining what evidence would be needed.

Mastery Criteria

  • Distinguishes claims, conjectures, evidence, and conclusions.
  • Supports conclusions with relevant evidence.
  • Recognizes flawed or incomplete reasoning.
  • Explains why appearance alone is not proof.
  • Revises weak reasoning into a clearer mathematical argument.
