📘 GEO | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Claims and Evidence

  1. Write the definition of claim, conjecture, evidence, argument, and flawed reasoning.
  2. A diagram shows two segments with matching tick marks. Write a claim, evidence, and conclusion.
  3. A diagram shows two angles with matching arc marks. Write a short argument that supports a congruence conclusion.
  4. Write one conjecture based on a diagram observation, then explain what evidence would be needed to support it.

Part B: Valid and Flawed Arguments

Use complete sentences. Your explanation should make your thinking clear.

  1. Explain why "because it looks true" is not strong mathematical evidence.
  2. A student says two lines are parallel because they look parallel. Explain what evidence would be needed.
  3. A student says one example proves a conjecture. Explain why this reasoning is incomplete.
  4. Write one sentence explaining how a valid mathematical argument connects evidence to a claim.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student writes:

    The angles are congruent because they look equal.

    Explain the flaw. Then write a corrected argument using evidence.
  2. A student writes:

    My conjecture is true because I found one example.

    Explain why the reasoning is incomplete. Then write what stronger support would include.
