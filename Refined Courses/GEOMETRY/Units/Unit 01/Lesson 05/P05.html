Before You Submit

  • Use equal segment notation such as AM = MB when a segment is bisected.
  • Use equal angle notation such as m∠ABD = m∠DBC when an angle is bisected.
  • Remember that a perpendicular bisector has both a midpoint relationship and a right-angle relationship.
