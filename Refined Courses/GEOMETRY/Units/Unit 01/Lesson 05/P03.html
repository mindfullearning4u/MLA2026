Notebook Focus: Angle Bisectors

An angle bisector divides an angle into two congruent angles.

If ray BD bisects ∠ABC, then:

m∠ABD = m∠DBC

Constructing an Angle Bisector

  1. Place the compass point on the angle vertex and draw an arc that intersects both sides of the angle.
  2. From each arc intersection, draw equal arcs inside the angle.
  3. Mark the point where the two interior arcs intersect.
  4. Use a straightedge to draw a ray from the vertex through the arc intersection.

Conclusion: The new ray bisects the original angle.

What Can Be Concluded?

  • Shown: Matching arcs are used from both sides of the angle.
  • Concluded: The constructed ray divides the angle into two equal angle measures.
  • Not enough: A ray that only appears centered is not enough evidence of an angle bisector.

Common Misconception

Do not draw the bisector by guessing the center of the angle.

Incorrect:

Draw a ray that looks halfway between the sides of the angle.

Correct:

Use equal compass arcs from both sides of the angle to locate the bisecting ray.
