P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Bisectors and Perpendicular Bisectors

In this lesson, you will use geometric tools to construct segment bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular bisectors.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.FND.2: I can construct segment bisectors, angle bisectors, and perpendicular bisectors.

What You Will Learn

  • How a segment bisector divides a segment into two equal parts.
  • How an angle bisector divides an angle into two equal angles.
  • How a perpendicular bisector crosses a segment at its midpoint and forms right angles.
  • How compass arcs provide construction evidence for bisectors.

What You Will Do

  • Practice construction steps for a segment bisector.
  • Practice construction steps for an angle bisector.
  • Identify how perpendicular bisectors show both midpoint and right-angle relationships.
  • Analyze common mistakes in bisector constructions.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice on bisector constructions.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining how construction arcs support equal parts or equal angles.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.FND.2: This standard asks you to use construction tools to divide segments and angles accurately, including constructing perpendicular bisectors.

In student language: I can use construction tools to split a segment or angle into equal parts and construct a line that is perpendicular at a segment midpoint.
