P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Teacher of Record Graded

Submit clear notebook evidence showing your construction reasoning for bisectors and perpendicular bisectors.

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Define segment bisector, angle bisector, and perpendicular bisector.
  • Show construction steps for a perpendicular bisector of segment AB.
  • Explain why matching compass arcs support AM = MB.
  • Show construction steps for an angle bisector of ∠ABC.
  • Explain why the constructed ray supports m∠ABD = m∠DBC.

Evidence Quality

Your evidence should distinguish between what is shown and what can be concluded from the construction.

  • Shown: Equal compass openings and intersecting arcs.
  • Concluded: Equal segment parts, equal angle parts, or perpendicular midpoint relationships.
  • Not enough: A statement based only on appearance.
