Checkpoint Task: Bisectors and Perpendicular Bisectors

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Describe how to construct the perpendicular bisector of segment AB. Explain why the compass opening must remain the same from both endpoints.
  2. Describe how to construct an angle bisector of ∠ABC. Explain how the equal arcs support the conclusion.
  3. A student marks a midpoint by sight and says the segment is bisected. Identify the mistake and correct the method.
  4. A student writes:

    The ray bisects the angle because it looks centered.

    Identify the misconception. Correct the argument by explaining what construction evidence is needed.
