Notebook Focus: Segment Bisectors

A segment bisector divides a segment into two congruent segments.

If point M is the midpoint of segment AB, then:

AM = MB

A perpendicular bisector is a line, ray, or segment that crosses a segment at its midpoint and forms right angles.

Constructing a Perpendicular Bisector

  1. Open the compass to more than half the length of segment AB.
  2. From point A, draw arcs above and below the segment.
  3. Without changing the compass opening, draw arcs from point B that intersect the first arcs.
  4. Use a straightedge to draw a line through the two arc intersections.

Conclusion: The constructed line bisects segment AB and is perpendicular to AB.

Observation and Evidence

  • Observation: The line crosses segment AB.
  • Evidence: Matching compass arcs from A and B locate points equally distant from both endpoints.
  • Conclusion: The line through the arc intersections is the perpendicular bisector of AB.

Common Misconception

Do not mark the midpoint by sight.

Incorrect:

Place M where the middle appears to be.

Correct:

Use equal compass arcs from both endpoints to locate the bisector.
