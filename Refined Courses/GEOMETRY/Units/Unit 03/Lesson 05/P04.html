P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A triangle congruence proof connects given evidence to a congruence conclusion using valid reasons such as definitions, shared sides, vertical angles, and congruence criteria.

Worked Example 1

Given: AB ≅ DE, AC ≅ DF, and ∠A ≅ ∠D.

Conclusion: triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by SAS.

Worked Example 2

Shared side: If two triangles share side BC, then BC ≅ BC by the Reflexive Property.

This shared side may be used as part of a congruence proof.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The triangles look congruent, so the proof is complete.

Correct:

The proof is complete only when each congruence conclusion follows from stated evidence and a valid reason.
