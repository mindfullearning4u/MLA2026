P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

In an isosceles triangle, congruent sides have congruent opposite angles. In an equilateral triangle, all sides are congruent and all angles measure 60°.

Worked Example 1

Given: AB ≅ AC and m∠B = 50°.

Conclusion: m∠C = 50° because base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent.

Worked Example 2

Given: triangle DEF is equilateral.

Conclusion: m∠D = m∠E = m∠F = 60°.

Error Check

Incorrect:

All isosceles triangles are equilateral.

Correct:

Equilateral triangles are isosceles, but many isosceles triangles have only two congruent sides.
