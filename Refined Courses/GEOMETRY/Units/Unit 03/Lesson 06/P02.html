P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Isosceles, Equilateral, and Triangle Applications

Vocabulary

  • Isosceles triangle: a triangle with at least two congruent sides.
  • Base angles: angles opposite the congruent sides of an isosceles triangle.
  • Equilateral triangle: a triangle with three congruent sides.
  • Equiangular triangle: a triangle with three congruent angles.
  • Vertex angle: the angle between the congruent sides of an isosceles triangle.

Notebook Task

  • Draw or describe the given triangle relationship.
  • Mark the evidence shown in the diagram.
  • Write what can and cannot be concluded from the evidence.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

All isosceles triangles are equilateral.

Correct:

Equilateral triangles are isosceles, but many isosceles triangles have only two congruent sides.
