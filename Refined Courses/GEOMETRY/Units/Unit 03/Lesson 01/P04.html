P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Triangles can be classified by side lengths and by angle measures. A classification must be based on markings, measurements, or stated information.

Worked Example 1

Given: AB ≅ AC and m∠A = 40°, m∠B = 70°, m∠C = 70°.

Classification: Triangle ABC is isosceles and acute because two sides are congruent and all angles are less than 90°.

Worked Example 2

Given: m∠D = 90° and all side lengths are different.

Classification: Triangle DEF is a right scalene triangle because it has one right angle and no congruent sides.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A triangle looks like it has two equal sides, so it must be isosceles.

Correct:

A triangle is isosceles only when side markings, measurements, or a valid reason show at least two congruent sides.
