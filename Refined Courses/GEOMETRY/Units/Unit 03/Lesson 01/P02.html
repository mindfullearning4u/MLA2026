P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Triangle Classification

Vocabulary

  • Scalene triangle: no congruent sides.
  • Isosceles triangle: at least two congruent sides.
  • Equilateral triangle: three congruent sides.
  • Acute triangle: all angles are less than 90°.
  • Right triangle: one angle is 90°.
  • Obtuse triangle: one angle is greater than 90°.

Notebook Task

  • Draw one triangle classified by sides and angles.
  • Mark side or angle evidence on the diagram.
  • Write a sentence explaining each classification.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A triangle looks like it has two equal sides, so it must be isosceles.

Correct:

A triangle is isosceles only when side markings, measurements, or a valid reason show at least two congruent sides.
