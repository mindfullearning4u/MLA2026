GEO | Unit 03 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.TRI.2: I can prove and apply triangle relationships and theorems in mathematical and real-world problems.
  • MLA.GEO.TRI.1: I can prove triangle congruence or similarity using valid triangle criteria.
  • MLA.GEO.TRI.3: I can justify triangle congruence criteria using rigid transformations.
  • MLA.GEO.RP.2: I can prove and apply relationships and theorems about parallelograms.
  • MLA.GEO.RP.3: I can prove and apply relationships and theorems about trapezoids.

What You Will Learn

  • Synthesize Unit 3 triangle and quadrilateral reasoning.
  • Review classification, angle relationships, correspondence, congruence, special triangles, and quadrilateral proof.
  • Prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Geometry diagrams using evidence.
  • Write short mathematical explanations.
  • Connect relationships to proof-ready reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence.
  • Submit the checkpoint response.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the required assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson builds proof and application skills used throughout later Geometry work.
