P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Putting It All Together

Vocabulary

  • Triangle relationships
  • Congruence criteria
  • Corresponding parts
  • Special triangles
  • Parallelogram relationships
  • Trapezoid relationships

Notebook Task

  • Draw or describe the diagram evidence.
  • Mark the given relationships.
  • Write what can be concluded from the evidence.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A conclusion is valid because the diagram looks correct.

Correct:

A conclusion is valid when it follows from markings, measurements, definitions, theorems, or congruence criteria.
