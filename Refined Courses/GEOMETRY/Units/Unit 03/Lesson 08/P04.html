P04 Guided Practice

Key Concept

Unit 3 connects triangle relationships, congruence reasoning, and quadrilateral proof. Strong solutions cite evidence and use the correct theorem or property.

Worked Example 1

Triangle synthesis: Use angle sums, congruence criteria, and corresponding parts to justify conclusions about triangles.

Worked Example 2

Quadrilateral synthesis: Use parallelogram and trapezoid properties to justify side, angle, diagonal, and midsegment conclusions.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A conclusion is valid because the diagram looks correct.

Correct:

A conclusion is valid when it follows from markings, measurements, definitions, theorems, or congruence criteria.
