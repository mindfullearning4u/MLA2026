P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A congruence statement tells which vertices, sides, and angles correspond. Correct order is essential because it determines which parts match.

Worked Example 1

Given: triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF.

Corresponding vertices: A ↔ D, B ↔ E, C ↔ F.

Corresponding sides: AB ≅ DE, BC ≅ EF, AC ≅ DF.

Worked Example 2

Given: triangle PQR ≅ triangle XYZ.

Conclusion: ∠Q ≅ ∠Y because Q and Y are in the same position in the congruence statement.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Triangle ABC ≅ triangle FDE means AB corresponds to DE.

Correct:

Triangle ABC ≅ triangle FDE means AB corresponds to FD because corresponding vertices are listed in order.
