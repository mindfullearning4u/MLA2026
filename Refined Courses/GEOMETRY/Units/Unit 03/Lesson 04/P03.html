P03 Notebook Task – Part 2

Diagram Reasoning Task

  • Choose one valid criterion and explain why it fixes the size and shape of the triangle.
  • Write one rigid-motion explanation for a triangle congruence conclusion.
  • Distinguish between evidence that proves congruence and evidence that only suggests similarity or appearance.

Observation vs. Conclusion

Write one sentence that describes what the diagram shows and one sentence that explains what can be concluded from the evidence.

Short Mathematical Explanation

Use this frame: The triangles are congruent by ___ because the diagram shows ___.
