P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Valid congruence criteria provide enough corresponding side and angle information to guarantee that one triangle can be mapped onto the other by rigid transformations.

Worked Example 1

Given: AB ≅ DE, AC ≅ DF, and ∠A ≅ ∠D.

Conclusion: triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by SAS because the congruent angle is included between the two congruent sides.

Worked Example 2

Given: right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs.

Conclusion: The triangles are congruent by HL because rigid motions can align the right angle, leg, and hypotenuse to determine the triangle.

Error Check

Incorrect:

AAA proves triangle congruence.

Correct:

AAA does not prove congruence because triangles can have the same angles but different sizes.
