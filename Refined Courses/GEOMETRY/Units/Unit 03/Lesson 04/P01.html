GEO | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Triangle Congruence Theorems

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.GEO.TRI.1: I can prove triangle congruence or similarity using valid triangle criteria.
  • MLA.GEO.TRI.3: I can justify triangle congruence criteria using rigid transformations.

What You Will Learn

  • Use SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and HL to establish triangle congruence.
  • Recognize information that is not sufficient for triangle congruence.
  • Connect congruence criteria to rigid transformations.

What You Will Do

  • Select the valid congruence criterion from diagram evidence.
  • Avoid invalid shortcuts such as AAA or SSA for congruence.
  • Explain why valid criteria give enough information to map one triangle onto another by rigid motion.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence.
  • Submit the checkpoint response.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Triangle congruence criteria help students prove when limited side and angle information is enough to establish congruence.
