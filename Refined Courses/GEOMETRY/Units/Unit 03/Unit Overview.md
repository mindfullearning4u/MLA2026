# GEOMETRY - Unit 03 Overview: Triangles, Congruence, and Quadrilaterals

This unit follows the approved Geometry Unit 3 mapping. Students develop triangle classification language, triangle angle theorem reasoning, congruent-figure correspondence, triangle congruence criteria, proof writing, special triangle applications, and quadrilateral relationships before later coordinate proof and similarity work.
