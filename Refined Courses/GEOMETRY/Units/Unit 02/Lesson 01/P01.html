P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Translations, Reflections, and Rotations on the Coordinate Plane

In this lesson, you will describe translations, reflections, and rotations using coordinate rules that connect each preimage point to its image point.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.1: I can describe transformations and write coordinate rules for images and preimages.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify preimage and image points.
  • How translations move points horizontally and vertically.
  • How reflections change coordinates across common lines of reflection.
  • How rotations around the origin can be described with coordinate rules.
