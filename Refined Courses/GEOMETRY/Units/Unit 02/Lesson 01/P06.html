P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit clear notebook evidence showing your transformation rules and worked examples.

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Vocabulary for preimage, image, prime notation, and coordinate rule.
  • Translation rule notes and at least one worked example.
  • Reflection rule notes for the x-axis, y-axis, and origin.
  • Rotation rule notes for 90 degree clockwise, 90 degree counterclockwise, and 180 degree rotations.
  • A short explanation connecting a coordinate rule to a transformation.

Evidence Quality

Your notebook evidence should show more than final answers. It should show how you used coordinate changes to identify the transformation.

Strong evidence: Includes a rule, a substituted point, an image point, and a short explanation.
