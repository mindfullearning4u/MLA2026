P05 Guided Practice

Complete the guided practice questions in Moodle. Use your notebook rules for translations, reflections, and rotations.

Guided Practice Preview

You will answer five questions that ask you to identify transformations, apply coordinate rules, and describe the movement from a preimage point to an image point.

  • Translations: compare how x and y change.
  • Reflections: look for sign changes.
  • Rotations: check whether coordinates switch places and change signs.

Sample Practice Item

Question: Point A(3, -2) maps to A'(-3, -2). Which transformation is shown?

Answer: Reflection across the y-axis.

Reasoning: The x-coordinate changes sign, and the y-coordinate stays the same.

Before You Submit

  • Check whether the question asks for a rule, a point, or a transformation name.
  • Substitute the original point into the coordinate rule.
  • Make sure the image point matches the transformation you selected.
