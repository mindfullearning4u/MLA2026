P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A conjecture may begin with a pattern, but a valid conclusion must be supported by diagram evidence, definitions, or logical reasons.

Worked Example 1

Step 1: Identify the given information.

Step 2: Match the information to a definition, rule, or relationship.

Step 3: Write the conclusion with a reason.

Worked Example 2

Question: What conclusion is supported?

Answer: The supported conclusion is the one that follows from the stated evidence, not from appearance alone.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The angles look equal, so they must be congruent.

Correct:

The angles are congruent only if markings, measures, or a valid reason support that conclusion.
