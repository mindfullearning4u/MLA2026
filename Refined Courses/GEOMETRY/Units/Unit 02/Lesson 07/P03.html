Notebook Title

Transformation Connections in Proof

Notebook Focus: Why Rigid Motion Matters

If a valid congruence criterion is met, one triangle can be matched to the other by rigid transformations. Rigid transformations preserve side lengths and angle measures, so the triangles are congruent.

Proof Sentence Frames

  • By _____, triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF.
  • The corresponding parts match because _____.
  • A rigid motion can map one triangle onto the other, so the triangles are congruent.

Example

Two right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs.

Proof statement: The triangles are congruent by HL. The marked hypotenuse and leg determine a matching right triangle, so a rigid motion can map one onto the other.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

AAA proves the triangles are congruent.

Correct:

AAA can show same shape, but it does not prove same size.
