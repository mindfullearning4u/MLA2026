GEO | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Triangle Congruence Proofs and Transformation Connections

In this lesson, you will connect rigid transformations, triangle congruence criteria, and proof reasoning.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.TRI.1: I can prove triangle congruence or similarity using valid triangle criteria.

MLA.GEO.CGM.6: I can use rigid transformations to justify that two figures are congruent.

MLA.GEO.TRI.3: I can justify triangle congruence criteria using rigid transformations.

What You Will Learn

  • How to choose a valid triangle congruence criterion from diagram evidence.
  • How rigid transformations explain why congruent triangles match exactly.
  • How to write short proof statements that connect evidence to a conclusion.

What You Will Do

  • Identify corresponding sides and angles.
  • Select SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, or HL when the evidence supports it.
  • Use rigid-motion language to justify congruence conclusions.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence connecting criteria and transformations.
  • Submit checkpoint work with a congruence proof explanation.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.TRI.1, MLA.GEO.CGM.6, and MLA.GEO.TRI.3: These standards work together when you prove triangles congruent and explain why the proof is supported by rigid motion.

In student language: I can use triangle evidence and rigid transformations to justify a congruence proof.
