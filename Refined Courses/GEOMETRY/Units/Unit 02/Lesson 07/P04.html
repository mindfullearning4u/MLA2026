P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Example 1: SAS Proof

AB ≅ DE, angle B ≅ angle E, and BC ≅ EF.

Conclusion: Triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by SAS because the angle is included between the two marked sides.

Example 2: AAS Proof

Angle A ≅ angle D, angle C ≅ angle F, and AB ≅ DE.

Conclusion: Triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by AAS because the side is not between the two given angles.

Example 3: HL Proof

Two right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs.

Conclusion: The triangles are congruent by HL.

Example 4: Rigid Transformation Explanation

After congruence is shown by a valid criterion, a rigid motion can move one triangle to match the other. The transformation preserves side lengths and angle measures.

Error Check
