Notebook Title

Unit 2 Synthesis: Congruence and Proof Tools

Notebook Focus: Congruence Reasoning

  • Rigid transformations preserve distance and angle measure.
  • Congruent figures have the same size and shape.
  • SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, and HL can prove triangle congruence.
  • Rigid transformations justify why valid criteria lead to matching triangles.

Evidence Review

  • Use labels, markings, and given information.
  • Do not rely on appearance alone.
  • Match corresponding parts in the correct order.

Example

If two right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one congruent leg, the triangles are congruent by HL.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

AAA proves congruence.

Correct:

AAA does not prove same size, so it does not prove congruence.
