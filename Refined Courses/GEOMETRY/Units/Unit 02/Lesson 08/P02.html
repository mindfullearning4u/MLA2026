Notebook Title

Unit 2 Synthesis: Transformation Tools

Notebook Focus: Coordinate Transformations

  • Translations add or subtract from coordinates.
  • Reflections change coordinate signs depending on the line of reflection.
  • Rotations use coordinate rules around the origin.
  • Sequences must be applied in order.

Vocabulary Review

  • Preimage and image
  • Rigid transformation
  • Symmetry
  • Transformation sequence
  • Congruent figures

Example

A point A(2, -3) translated by (x, y) → (x + 4, y - 1) maps to A'(6, -4).

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

Apply sequence steps in any order.

Correct:

Apply sequence steps in the order given.
