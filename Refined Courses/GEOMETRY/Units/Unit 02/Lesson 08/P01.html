GEO | Unit 02 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this synthesis lesson, you will review, connect, and apply Unit 2 concepts before completing the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.1: I can describe transformations and write coordinate rules for images and preimages.

MLA.GEO.CGM.2: I can determine whether a transformation preserves distance.

MLA.GEO.CGM.3: I can identify transformation sequences that map figures onto themselves or other figures.

MLA.GEO.CGM.4: I can identify reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry in geometric figures.

MLA.GEO.CGM.5: I can draw transformed figures on a coordinate plane after a sequence of transformations.

MLA.GEO.CGM.6: I can use rigid transformations to justify that two figures are congruent.

MLA.GEO.TRI.1: I can prove triangle congruence or similarity using valid triangle criteria.

MLA.GEO.TRI.3: I can justify triangle congruence criteria using rigid transformations.

What You Will Learn

  • How coordinate transformations, rigid motion, symmetry, and congruence criteria connect.
  • How to choose an efficient strategy for transformation and congruence problems.
  • How to prepare for cumulative mastery on the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review major Unit 2 concepts.
  • Practice mixed transformation and congruence tasks.
  • Complete checkpoint evidence before the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete synthesis notebook evidence.
  • Submit the final checkpoint for the unit.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Unit 2 asks you to describe and apply transformations, recognize rigid motion, analyze symmetry, draw transformed figures, and justify congruence using triangle criteria and transformation reasoning.
