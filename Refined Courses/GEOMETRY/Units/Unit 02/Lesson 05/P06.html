GEO | Unit 02 | Lesson 05

P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit clear notebook evidence showing rigid-motion congruence examples and justifications.

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Vocabulary for congruent figures, rigid transformation, and corresponding parts.
  • At least one translation congruence justification.
  • At least one reflection or rotation congruence justification.
  • A short explanation distinguishing rigid and non-rigid transformations.

Evidence Quality

Strong notebook evidence names the rigid motion and explains why distance and angle preservation support congruence.
