Lesson: Segment and Angle Proofs

Notebook Title

Segment and Angle Proofs

Purpose: Use segment, midpoint, line, and angle relationships in introductory proofs.

Vocabulary

  • Identify the mathematical object: the given statement, diagram evidence, and statement-reason proof table.
  • Use precise Geometry vocabulary from the lesson title and standards: MLA.GEO.RP.1.
  • Record givens, markings, measurements, and conclusions separately.

Teaching Sequence

Step 1: Read the lesson task and underline the givens. A given may be a marked segment, angle measure, coordinate, radius, side length, scale factor, or construction step.

Why: Geometry conclusions must come from evidence, not from how a figure appears.

StatementReason
Given relationship from the diagramGiven information
Conclusion supported by the relationshipDefinition, theorem, or valid argument

Step 2: Label the given statement, diagram evidence, and statement-reason proof table. Add names for points, sides, angles, arcs, coordinates, or dimensions before you calculate or justify anything.

Step 3: Choose the definition, theorem, construction property, or formula that matches the labeled evidence.

Step 4: Write one sentence that connects the evidence to the conclusion for Segment and Angle Proofs.
