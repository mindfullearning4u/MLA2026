P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Example 1: Congruence by Translation

Triangle ABC is translated 4 units right and 2 units down to land exactly on triangle DEF.

Conclusion: Triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF because translation is a rigid transformation.

Example 2: Congruence by Reflection

Quadrilateral JKLM reflects across the y-axis and lands exactly on quadrilateral PQRS.

Conclusion: JKLM ≅ PQRS because reflection preserves distance and angle measure.

Example 3: Congruence by Rotation

Triangle RST rotates 90° clockwise about the origin and lands exactly on triangle XYZ.

Conclusion: RST ≅ XYZ because rotation is a rigid transformation.

Example 4: Congruence by a Sequence

A figure is reflected across the x-axis and then translated 3 units left. The final image lands exactly on a second figure.

Conclusion: The figures are congruent because both steps are rigid transformations.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A dilation with scale factor 2 proves congruence.

Correct:

A dilation with scale factor 2 changes distance, so it does not prove congruence.
