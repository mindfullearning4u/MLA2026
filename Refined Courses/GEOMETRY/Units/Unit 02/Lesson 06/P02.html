P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Basic Transformations

Vocabulary

  • Preimage: the original figure.
  • Image: the transformed figure.
  • Translation: a slide.
  • Reflection: a flip.
  • Rotation: a turn.
  • Rigid transformation: preserves distance and angle measure.

Notebook Task

  • Make observations from the given diagram or statement.
  • Support each conclusion with evidence.
  • Distinguish what is shown from what can actually be concluded.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A dilation with scale factor 2 preserves distance.

Correct:

A dilation with scale factor 2 changes distances, so it is not rigid.
