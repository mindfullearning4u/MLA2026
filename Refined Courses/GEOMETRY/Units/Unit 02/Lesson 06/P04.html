P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Example 1: SSS

AB ≅ DE, BC ≅ EF, and AC ≅ DF.

Conclusion: Triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by SSS.

Example 2: SAS

AB ≅ DE, angle B ≅ angle E, and BC ≅ EF. The angle is included between the two sides.

Conclusion: Triangle ABC ≅ triangle DEF by SAS.

Example 3: ASA and AAS

ASA uses two angles and the included side. AAS uses two angles and a non-included side.

Conclusion: Both ASA and AAS can prove triangle congruence.

Example 4: HL

Two right triangles have congruent hypotenuses and one pair of congruent legs.

Conclusion: The right triangles are congruent by HL.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Two sides and any angle prove SAS.

Correct:

SAS requires the included angle between the two sides.
