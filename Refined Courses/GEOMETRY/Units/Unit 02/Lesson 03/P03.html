P03 Notebook Task – Part 2

Notebook Title

Symmetry as a Transformation

Notebook Focus: Types of Symmetry

A figure has symmetry when a transformation maps the figure exactly onto itself.

Vocabulary

  • Reflection symmetry: a line divides a figure into matching mirror halves.
  • Rotation symmetry: a rotation less than 360° maps a figure onto itself.
  • Translation symmetry: a repeated pattern maps onto itself by sliding.
  • Line of symmetry: the mirror line for reflection symmetry.

How to Support a Symmetry Claim

  1. State the type of symmetry.
  2. Name the transformation that maps the figure onto itself.
  3. Use diagram evidence such as matching halves or repeated parts.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

The figure has symmetry because it looks balanced.

Correct:

A symmetry conclusion needs a transformation that maps the figure onto itself.
