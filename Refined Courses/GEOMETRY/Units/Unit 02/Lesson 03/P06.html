P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Notebook Evidence Checklist

  • Vocabulary for transformation sequence and symmetry.
  • At least one ordered transformation sequence example.
  • At least one example of reflection, rotation, or translation symmetry.
  • A short explanation using evidence, not appearance alone.

Evidence Quality

Strong notebook evidence uses complete mathematical sentences and names the transformation evidence used to support each conclusion.
