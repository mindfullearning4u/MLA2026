P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Inductive and Deductive Reasoning

Vocabulary

  • Inductive reasoning: reasoning from examples.
  • Deductive reasoning: reasoning from accepted facts.
  • Counterexample: one example that disproves an always claim.
  • Argument: reasons used to support a conclusion.

Notebook Task

  • Make observations from the given diagram or statement.
  • Support each conclusion with evidence.
  • Distinguish what is shown from what can actually be concluded.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

Three examples prove an always statement.

Correct:

Examples suggest a conjecture; proof or a counterexample determines whether it is valid.
