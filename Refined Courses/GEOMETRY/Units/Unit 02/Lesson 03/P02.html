P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Transformation Sequences and Mapping Figures

Notebook Focus: Transformation Sequences

A transformation sequence is an ordered list of transformations. The order matters because each step starts with the image from the previous step.

Vocabulary

  • Sequence of transformations: two or more transformations performed in order.
  • Maps onto: moves a figure so its points match another figure or itself.
  • Onto itself: a figure lands exactly on its original position after a transformation.
  • Corresponding points: matching points in the preimage and image.

Example

A point moves from A(1, 2) to A'(5, 2), then to A''(5, -2).

Sequence: First translate 4 units right. Then reflect across the x-axis.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

The order does not matter.

Correct:

Apply the transformations in the order given.

Reversing a sequence can place the final image in a different location.
