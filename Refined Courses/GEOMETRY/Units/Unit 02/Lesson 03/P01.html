P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Symmetry and Mapping Figures Using Transformation Sequences

In this lesson, you will identify sequences of transformations that map figures onto themselves or onto another figure and determine reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.3: I can identify transformation sequences that map figures onto themselves or other figures.

MLA.GEO.CGM.4: I can identify reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry in geometric figures.

What You Will Learn

  • How transformation sequences map figures.
  • How a figure can map onto itself through symmetry.
  • How to identify reflection, rotation, and translation symmetry.

What You Will Do

  • Describe transformation sequences in order.
  • Use diagrams and coordinates to identify mappings.
  • Support symmetry conclusions with evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence on transformation sequences and symmetry.
  • Submit checkpoint work identifying mappings and symmetries.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
