P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Example 1: Identify a Transformation Sequence

Problem: Point A(1, 2) moves to A'(5, 2), then to A''(5, -2). Describe the sequence.

Answer: Translate 4 units right, then reflect across the x-axis.

Example 2: Map a Figure Onto Itself

A square rotated 90° about its center maps onto itself because each vertex lands on another vertex of the same square.

Example 3: Reflection Symmetry

A rectangle folded along a vertical line through its center has reflection symmetry if both halves match exactly.

Example 4: Translation Symmetry

A repeated border pattern that slides 6 units right and matches the next copy has translation symmetry.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A regular hexagon only has rotation symmetry at 360°.

Correct:

A regular hexagon maps onto itself at rotations less than 360°.
