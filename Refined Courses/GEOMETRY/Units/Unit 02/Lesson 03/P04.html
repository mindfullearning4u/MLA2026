P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Examples can suggest a conjecture, but deductive reasoning proves conclusions. One valid counterexample disproves an always statement.

Worked Example 1

Step 1: Identify the given information.

Step 2: Match the information to a definition, rule, or relationship.

Step 3: Write the conclusion with a reason.

Worked Example 2

Question: What conclusion is supported?

Answer: The supported conclusion is the one that follows from the stated evidence, not from appearance alone.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Three examples prove an always statement.

Correct:

Examples suggest a conjecture; proof or a counterexample determines whether it is valid.
