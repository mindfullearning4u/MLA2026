P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Distance Preservation and Rigid Transformations

In this lesson, you will determine whether a transformation preserves distance and identify translations, reflections, and rotations as rigid transformations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.2: I can determine whether a transformation preserves distance.

What You Will Learn

  • What it means for a transformation to preserve distance.
  • Why translations, reflections, and rotations are rigid transformations.
  • Why dilations with scale factor not equal to 1 do not preserve distance.
  • How to compare corresponding side lengths before and after a transformation.

What You Will Do

  • Classify transformations as rigid or non-rigid.
  • Use side lengths to determine whether distance is preserved.
  • Explain why a transformation does or does not preserve distance.
  • Complete a checkpoint using transformation evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and practice on distance preservation.
  • Submit checkpoint work explaining rigid and non-rigid transformations.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
