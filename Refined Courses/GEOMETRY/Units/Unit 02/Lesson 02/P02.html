Lesson 2: Distance Preservation and Rigid Transformations

Distance Preservation

A transformation preserves distance when the distance between any two points in the preimage equals the distance between the corresponding image points.

AB = A'B'

If every corresponding length stays the same, the transformation preserves distance.

Rigid Transformations

A rigid transformation preserves distance and angle measure. The image is congruent to the preimage.

  • Translations preserve distance.
  • Reflections preserve distance.
  • Rotations preserve distance.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A transformation preserves distance if the figure only looks similar.

Correct:

A transformation preserves distance if corresponding lengths are equal.
