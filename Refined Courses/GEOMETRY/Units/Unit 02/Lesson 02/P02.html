Lesson 2: Distance Preservation and Rigid Transformations

Notebook Focus: Distance Preservation

A transformation preserves distance when every distance in the preimage matches the corresponding distance in the image.

The key question is: Did the size and shape stay the same, or did at least one distance change?

Vocabulary

  • Distance preservation: Corresponding distances remain equal after a transformation.
  • Rigid transformation: A transformation that preserves distance and angle measure.
  • Non-rigid transformation: A transformation that changes at least some distances.
  • Corresponding parts: Matching parts of the preimage and image.
  • Congruent figures: Figures with the same size and same shape.

Rigid Transformations

Translations, reflections, and rotations are rigid transformations.

AB = A'B'

If corresponding lengths stay equal, distance is preserved.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A transformation preserves distance if the image only looks similar.

Correct:

A transformation preserves distance if corresponding lengths are equal.
