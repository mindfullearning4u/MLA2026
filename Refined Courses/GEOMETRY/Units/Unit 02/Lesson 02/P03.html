Notebook Focus: Rigid vs. Non-Rigid

Rigid transformations keep distance the same. Non-rigid transformations change at least some distances.

Changing location or orientation does not automatically mean distance changed.

Non-Rigid Transformations

A non-rigid transformation does not preserve distance. The image may be larger, smaller, stretched, or compressed.

A dilation with scale factor 2 changes every length by a factor of 2, so it does not preserve distance.

How to Check Distance Preservation

  1. Identify corresponding points such as A and A'.
  2. Compare a preimage length with the matching image length.
  3. If all corresponding distances are equal, distance is preserved.
  4. If any corresponding distance changes, distance is not preserved.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A dilation with scale factor 3 is rigid because the shape looks the same.

Correct:

A dilation with scale factor 3 is not rigid because distances change.
