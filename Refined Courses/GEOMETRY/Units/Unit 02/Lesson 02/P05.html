P05 Guided Practice

Complete the guided practice questions in Moodle. Use corresponding distances to decide whether each transformation is rigid.

Guided Practice Preview

You will answer five questions about rigid transformations, non-rigid transformations, and evidence for distance preservation.

  • Rigid transformations preserve distance.
  • Non-rigid transformations change at least some distances.
  • Strong evidence compares corresponding lengths.

Sample Practice Item

Question: Segment AB = 6 and A'B' = 6. Was distance preserved for this segment?

Answer: Yes.

Reasoning: The corresponding distances are equal.

Before You Submit

  • Identify the transformation type.
  • Check whether corresponding distances are equal.
  • Use distance evidence instead of appearance.
