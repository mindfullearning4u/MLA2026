P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Drawing Images from Transformation Sequences

In this lesson, you will draw transformed figures on the coordinate plane after a sequence of transformations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.5: I can draw transformed figures on a coordinate plane after a sequence of transformations.

What You Will Learn

  • How to apply transformation rules to every vertex.
  • How to draw an image after more than one transformation.
  • How to label intermediate and final images using prime notation.

What You Will Do

  • Apply translations, reflections, and rotations to coordinate points.
  • Graph transformed vertices on a coordinate plane.
  • Connect transformed vertices to draw the final image.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook examples drawing images from transformation sequences.
  • Submit checkpoint work with coordinates and a final transformed figure.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.GEO.CGM.5: This standard asks you to draw the image of a figure after a sequence of transformations on the coordinate plane.

In student language: I can transform every vertex in order and graph the final image accurately.
