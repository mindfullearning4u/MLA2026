GEO | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Two-Dimensional Area and Coordinate Area

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.10: I can use coordinate geometry to find perimeter and area of polygons in context.
MLA.GEO.CGM.13: I can solve mathematical and real-world area problems involving two-dimensional figures.

What You Will Learn

  • Find perimeter and area of polygons on the coordinate plane.
  • Use area formulas in mathematical and real-world contexts.
  • Select units and justify results from coordinate evidence.

What You Will Do

  • Break coordinate figures into familiar shapes.
  • Use distance or grid counting to find needed dimensions.
  • Write short explanations that connect formulas, units, and context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence.
  • Submit the checkpoint response.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson uses measurement and modeling tools to solve Geometry problems from diagrams, coordinates, and contexts.
