P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Two-Dimensional Area and Coordinate Area

Vocabulary

  • Perimeter: the total distance around a two-dimensional figure.
  • Area: the number of square units inside a two-dimensional figure.
  • Coordinate area: area found from a figure placed on the coordinate plane.
  • Composite figure: a figure made from two or more familiar shapes.
  • Units squared: units used to measure area, such as ft² or cm².

Notebook Task

  • Sketch or inspect a coordinate polygon and label key dimensions.
  • Choose an area method: formula, decomposition, or enclosing rectangle.
  • Find perimeter when side lengths are needed.
  • Explain why the chosen method matches the figure.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

The perimeter is 24 square units.

Correct:

Perimeter uses linear units; area uses square units.
