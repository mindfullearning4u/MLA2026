# GEOMETRY - Unit 06 Overview: Area, Surface Area, Volume, and Modeling

---

## Unit Overview

This unit synthesizes geometric measurement and modeling. Students analyze cross-sections, identify solids generated by rotations, determine how scale factors affect area, surface area, and volume, and solve real-world problems involving two-dimensional area and three-dimensional measurement.

Emphasis is placed on spatial visualization, units, formulas, modeling decisions, scale reasoning, and interpreting solutions in context.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Standards with an emphasis on mathematical reasoning, structure, and application. Students are expected to demonstrate both procedural fluency and conceptual understanding through multi-step problem solving and real-world modeling.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- spatial visualization;
- measurement reasoning with area, surface area, and volume;
- real-world modeling and interpretation.

Assessment items require students to analyze cross-sections, identify solids of rotation, determine scale effects, solve measurement problems, and interpret geometric models in context.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit synthesizes measurement, scale reasoning, spatial visualization, and real-world modeling. Students apply formulas, interpret units, and evaluate solutions across two-dimensional and three-dimensional contexts.
