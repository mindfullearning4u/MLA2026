P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Volume measures the space inside a solid. Prisms and cylinders use base area times height. Pyramids and cones use one-third of the matching prism or cylinder volume. Spheres use a formula based on the radius.

Worked Example 1

Example: A rectangular prism with length 8, width 3, and height 5 has volume V = lwh = 8(3)(5) = 120 cubic units.

Worked Example 2

Example: A cylinder with radius 4 and height 10 has volume V = πr²h = π(4)²(10) = 160π cubic units.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A cone has volume πr²h.

Correct:

A cone has volume 1⁄3πr²h because it is one-third of a cylinder with the same base and height.
