P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Coordinate proof uses calculations as evidence. Slope can justify parallel or perpendicular sides, distance can justify congruent sides, and midpoint can justify bisection. A classification is valid only when the needed properties are supported.

Worked Example 1

Example: Triangle ABC has A(0, 0), B(4, 0), and C(0, 3). Since AB = 4, AC = 3, and BC = 5, the side lengths support classifying triangle ABC as a right triangle because 3² + 4² = 5².

Worked Example 2

Example: Quadrilateral WXYZ has opposite sides with equal slopes. Equal slopes support opposite sides being parallel. If both pairs of opposite sides are parallel, the coordinate evidence supports classifying the figure as a parallelogram.

Error Check

Incorrect:

The quadrilateral looks like a rectangle, so it is a rectangle.

Correct:

Use slope to show adjacent sides are perpendicular and distance to support opposite-side relationships before classifying the quadrilateral.
