Guided Practice: Partition Points

  1. Find the midpoint of A(2, 6) and B(10, 14).
  2. Find the point 1/4 of the way from A(0, 0) to B(8, 12).
  3. Point P partitions AB in the ratio 1:3 from A to B. If A(0, 0) and B(12, 8), find P.

Show both coordinate calculations. A correct answer without the weighted-average setup is not enough for mastery.
