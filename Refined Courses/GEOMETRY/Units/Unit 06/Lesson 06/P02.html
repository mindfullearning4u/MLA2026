P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Scale Factors and Measurement Effects

Vocabulary

  • Scale factor: the multiplier that compares corresponding lengths.
  • Linear measure: a one-dimensional measure such as length, width, height, or radius.
  • Area scale factor: the square of the linear scale factor.
  • Volume scale factor: the cube of the linear scale factor.
  • Similar solids: solids with the same shape and proportional corresponding lengths.

Notebook Task

  • Record the linear scale factor from a pair of similar figures or solids.
  • Determine whether the problem asks about length, area, surface area, or volume.
  • Apply k, k², or k³ as appropriate.
  • Explain why the exponent matches the type of measurement.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

A scale factor of 4 makes volume 4 times as large.

Correct:

A scale factor of 4 makes volume 4³ = 64 times as large.
