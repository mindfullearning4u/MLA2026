P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A linear scale factor k changes lengths by k, areas and surface areas by k², and volumes by k³. The exponent matches the dimension of the measurement.

Worked Example 1

Example: If a rectangle is enlarged by a scale factor of 3, its area changes by 3² = 9. The new area is 9 times the original area.

Worked Example 2

Example: If a similar solid is enlarged by a scale factor of 2, its volume changes by 2³ = 8. The new volume is 8 times the original volume.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A scale factor of 4 makes volume 4 times as large.

Correct:

A scale factor of 4 makes volume 4³ = 64 times as large.
