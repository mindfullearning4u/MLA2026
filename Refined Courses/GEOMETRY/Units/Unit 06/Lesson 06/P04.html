Common Mistake

Incorrect: A scale factor changes length, area, and volume by the same amount.

Teachable Explanation: The correct approach works because it connects the answer to Geometry evidence, definitions, marked relationships, construction steps, or valid reasoning.

Correct: Length scales by k, area and surface area by k^2, and volume by k^3.

When checking your work, ask: What evidence proves this conclusion?
