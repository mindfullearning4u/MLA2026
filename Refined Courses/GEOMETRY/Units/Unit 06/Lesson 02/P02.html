P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Coordinate Relationships and Circle Equations

Vocabulary

  • Circle equation: an equation in the form (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r².
  • Center: the point (h, k) in a circle equation.
  • Radius: the distance from the center to a point on the circle.
  • Coordinate model: a coordinate representation of a real-world geometric situation.
  • Graph of a circle: all points a fixed distance from the center.

Notebook Task

  • Identify the center and radius from a circle equation.
  • Write a circle equation from a center and radius.
  • Use the distance formula to find a missing radius.
  • Explain what each part of the equation represents in context.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

The circle (x - 2)² + (y + 3)² = 49 has center (-2, 3).

Correct:

The center is (2, -3) because the equation uses x - h and y - k.
