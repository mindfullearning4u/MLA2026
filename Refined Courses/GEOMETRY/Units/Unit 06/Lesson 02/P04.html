P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

A circle with center (h, k) and radius r has equation (x - h)² + (y - k)² = r². Coordinate relationships such as distance, midpoint, and slope help solve problems involving lines, circles, triangles, and quadrilaterals.

Worked Example 1

Example: A circle has center (3, -2) and radius 5. Its equation is (x - 3)² + (y + 2)² = 25 because r² = 5² = 25.

Worked Example 2

Example: The equation (x + 4)² + (y - 1)² = 36 has center (-4, 1) and radius 6 because 36 = 6².

Error Check

Incorrect:

The circle (x - 2)² + (y + 3)² = 49 has center (-2, 3).

Correct:

The center is (2, -3) because the equation uses x - h and y - k.
