P02 Notebook Task – Part 1

Notebook Title

Geometric Modeling and Spatial Cross-Sections

Vocabulary

  • Cross-section: the two-dimensional shape made by slicing a three-dimensional figure.
  • Solid of rotation: a three-dimensional object made by rotating a two-dimensional figure.
  • Axis of rotation: the line around which a figure rotates.
  • Conic section: a curve formed by slicing a cone.
  • Modeling: using Geometry to represent and solve a real situation.

Notebook Task

  • Sketch a solid and a possible cross-section.
  • Describe one solid of rotation.
  • Identify one conic section from a cone slice.
  • Explain what is shown and what can be concluded.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect:

Any slice of a cylinder is a circle.

Correct:

Only slices parallel to the circular bases must be circles; other slices can form different shapes.
