P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Cross-sections, solids of rotation, and conic sections require spatial reasoning. The conclusion depends on the solid, the direction of the slice, or the two-dimensional figure being rotated.

Worked Example 1

Example: A plane slicing a cylinder parallel to its bases forms a circle. A plane slicing perpendicular to the bases can form a rectangle.

Worked Example 2

Example: Rotating a rectangle around one side creates a cylinder because the opposite side sweeps out a circular surface.

Error Check

Incorrect:

Any slice of a cylinder is a circle.

Correct:

Only slices parallel to the circular bases must be circles; other slices can form different shapes.
