P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Surface Area

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.GEO.CGM.15: I can solve surface area problems involving cylinders, pyramids, prisms, cones, and spheres.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify surface area as the total area covering a three-dimensional figure.
  • Use appropriate surface area formulas for prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, and spheres.
  • Interpret surface area answers with square units.

What You Will Do

  • Match a solid to its surface area formula or net.
  • Substitute dimensions carefully.
  • Check whether a context asks for total surface area or part of a surface.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence.
  • Submit the checkpoint response.
  • Complete Guided Practice and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson uses measurement and modeling tools to solve Geometry problems from diagrams, coordinates, and contexts.
