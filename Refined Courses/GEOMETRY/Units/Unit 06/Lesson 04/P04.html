P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Key Concept

Surface area measures the outside of a three-dimensional object. The method depends on the solid: prisms and pyramids can be connected to nets, while cylinders, cones, and spheres use formulas involving circular bases or curved surfaces.

Worked Example 1

Example: A rectangular prism with dimensions 4, 5, and 6 has surface area 2(4·5) + 2(4·6) + 2(5·6) = 40 + 48 + 60 = 148 square units.

Worked Example 2

Example: A cylinder with radius 3 and height 8 has surface area 2πr² + 2πrh = 2π(3)² + 2π(3)(8) = 18π + 48π = 66π square units.

Error Check

Incorrect:

A cylinder surface area is only πr²h.

Correct:

πr²h is volume; surface area adds the two circular bases and the curved side area.
