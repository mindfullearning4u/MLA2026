# Geometry Benchmark Master Tracker

## Purpose

This tracker establishes a single standards source of truth for `GEOMETRY` using the official Florida B.E.S.T. Geometry-relevant benchmark set.

## Standards Sources

- Florida Department of Education, Florida's B.E.S.T. Standards: Mathematics.
- Florida Department of Education, Geometry B.E.S.T. Instructional Guide for Mathematics.
- CPALMS B.E.S.T. benchmark and course records.

## Scope Notes

- `MA.912.GR.7.4` through `MA.912.GR.7.9` are official B.E.S.T. conic-section benchmarks, but they are not included in this Geometry course scope because they address equations and graphs of parabolas, ellipses and hyperbolas and are associated with later advanced mathematics coursework. Geometry includes conic cross-section recognition through `MA.912.GR.7.1` and circle equations through `MA.912.GR.7.2` and `MA.912.GR.7.3`.
- Supporting references in unit overview files are included only where they maintain prerequisite continuity. They do not override the primary ownership shown here.
