Final Accreditation, Compliance, Instructional Quality, Assessment Quality, and Mastery Certification Review

## 3. Instructional Quality Review

Status: PASS

The course teaches through a consistent mastery model. Each lesson includes a lesson title, standards section, learning targets, action steps, mastery evidence, student-friendly standard connection, vocabulary, notebook tasks, concept development, worked examples, common mistakes, guided practice, checkpoint submission, and assessment pathway.

Instructional explanations are age appropriate and grade-level appropriate for Geometry. Lessons support independent learning by using repeated page patterns and predictable mastery routines.

## 4. Lesson Flow Review

Status: PASS

The approved MLA lesson sequence is maintained:

1. P01 Lesson Overview
2. P02 Notebook Task - Part 1
3. P03 Notebook Task - Part 2
4. P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples
5. P05 Guided Practice
6. P06 Notebook Evidence Submission
7. P07 Checkpoint Submission

Lesson 8 in each unit is the synthesis lesson, `Putting It All Together`, and uses the unit assessment rather than a lesson quiz.

The course flow supports mastery:

- Notebook Task Part 1 prepares students to observe, organize, and reason.
- Notebook Task Part 2 extends the reasoning or application.
- Worked examples model the target skill.
- Guided Practice checks readiness before independent work.
- Checkpoint and notebook evidence create revision opportunities.
- Lesson quizzes and unit assessments verify mastery.

## 5. Instructional Progression Review

Status: PASS

The course progression is logical:

- Unit 1 develops foundations, constructions, geometric language, logic, and introductory proof.
- Unit 2 develops transformations, congruence, and proof connections.
- Unit 3 develops triangle and quadrilateral reasoning.
- Unit 4 develops similarity, proportional reasoning, and right-triangle trigonometry.
- Unit 5 develops circle relationships, constructions, and measurement.
- Unit 6 develops coordinate geometry, measurement, scale effects, spatial reasoning, and course synthesis.

Prerequisites are respected, cognitive load is controlled through unit sequencing, and later units depend appropriately on earlier proof, transformation, triangle, similarity, circle, and coordinate reasoning.

## 8. Distractor Quality Review

Status: PASS

Distractors were reviewed for plausibility, misconception value, and alignment with lesson skills. Assessment items avoid duplicate choices and avoid distractors that are merely decorative. Common distractor patterns include:

- Confusing observation with conclusion.
- Using appearance instead of evidence.
- Reversing converse or conditional logic.
- Misidentifying corresponding parts.
- Applying the wrong theorem or formula.
- Mixing radius and diameter.
- Confusing area, surface area, and volume.
- Misreading coordinate evidence.

## 9. Feedback Quality Review

## 19. Findings

No active production-blocking deficiencies remain.

- `MLA.GEO.CGM.7` appears in the mapping traceability table but is marked as not assigned to the production build. It is not used in lessons or assessments. The built course follows the approved production lesson mapping and does not invent an unassigned standard placement.

- Guided practice answer distributions are less balanced than quiz and unit assessment distributions. No predictable answer sequence or assessment integrity issue was found, but future curriculum maintenance may rebalance formative guided practice positions for even stronger distribution.
