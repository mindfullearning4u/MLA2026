## Excluded Benchmarks

The following official B.E.S.T. conic-section benchmarks are excluded from the MLA Geometry crosswalk because they are not part of the current MLA Geometry course scope. They are assigned to MLA Precalculus.

| Excluded Benchmark | Topic | Reason for Exclusion | Best MLA Course Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MA.912.GR.7.4` | Derive and create equations of parabolas using key features. | This is an advanced conic-equation standard requiring algebraic manipulation and function/coordinate readiness beyond the Geometry course scope. | Precalculus |
| `MA.912.GR.7.5` | Graph and solve real-world problems modeled by equations of parabolas; interpret key features. | This extends conic equations into graphing and modeling. MLA Precalculus Unit 05 explicitly includes parabola equations, graphs, and key features. | Precalculus |
| `MA.912.GR.7.6` | Derive and create equations of ellipses using key features. | Ellipse equation development is advanced conic work and belongs with conic sections in Precalculus. | Precalculus |
| `MA.912.GR.7.7` | Graph and solve real-world problems modeled by equations of ellipses; interpret key features. | Ellipse graphing and modeling require conic-equation fluency beyond the Geometry scope. | Precalculus |
| `MA.912.GR.7.8` | Derive and create equations of hyperbolas using key features. | Hyperbola equations are advanced conic-section content and are assigned in MLA Precalculus Unit 05. | Precalculus |
| `MA.912.GR.7.9` | Graph and solve real-world problems modeled by equations of hyperbolas; interpret key features. | Hyperbola graphing and modeling are Precalculus-level conic applications in the MLA sequence. | Precalculus |

This keeps Geometry focused on spatial reasoning, circles, coordinate geometry, proof, transformations, measurement, and right-triangle reasoning while placing advanced conic equation families in Precalculus.

The current workbook Sources and audit structure are sufficient, but adding the note would make the exclusion more visible during external review.
