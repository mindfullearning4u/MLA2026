Scenario: A state ends same-day registration and closes registration four weeks before an election. Officials cite administrative preparation; evidence shows the change disproportionately affects eligible citizens who move frequently.

Model Analysis

The state has a legitimate interest in accurate rolls and orderly administration, but the analysis must examine the burden, affected voters, evidence supporting the deadline, feasible alternatives, and governing constitutional or statutory protections. A turnout change alone does not prove unlawful intent, yet disparate burden is relevant evidence.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: “The government may do this because it has authority.” This does not identify the source, scope, procedure, evidence, or limit.
Correct: Low turnout does not have one cause. Separate legal eligibility, administrative access, resources, information, motivation, mobilization, and measurement before explaining a participation pattern.
