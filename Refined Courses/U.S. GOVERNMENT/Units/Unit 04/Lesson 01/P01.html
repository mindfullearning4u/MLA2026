P01 Lesson Overview

Voting Rights and Voter Participation

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.POL.1: Analyze voting rights, voter eligibility, voter turnout, barriers to participation, and expansion of suffrage.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how voting rights and voter participation works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the suffrage timeline, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches suffrage and participation through the assigned standard only.
