Common Mistake

Correct: matching the specific evidence to the specific standard.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar government word without proving it from the prompt.

Teachable Explanation

The teachable move is to prove the answer from the lesson evidence first, then name the standard. This keeps the work inside MLA.USG.POL.1, MLA.USG.POL.2, MLA.USG.POL.3, MLA.USG.POL.4 and prevents guessing. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.USG.POL.1, MLA.USG.POL.2, MLA.USG.POL.3, MLA.USG.POL.4.
