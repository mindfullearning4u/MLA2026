P01 Lesson Overview

Public Opinion, Polling, and Civic Discourse

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.POL.4: Evaluate civic discourse, polling, political messaging, media literacy, and evidence-based participation in a constitutional democracy.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how public opinion, polling, and civic discourse works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the poll interpretation table, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches public opinion and civic discourse through the assigned standard only.
