P01 Lesson Overview

Synthesis: Civic Decision-Making and Democratic Institutions

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.CMP.1: Compare democracy, republic, monarchy, dictatorship, authoritarianism, totalitarianism, oligarchy, theocracy, and parliamentary systems. MLA.USG.CMP.2: Analyze the Florida Constitution, Florida state government, county and municipal government, and the relationship between state and local authority. MLA.USG.CMP.3: Create evidence-based civic action plans using constitutional principles, stakeholder analysis, lawful advocacy, and practical implementation steps. MLA.USG.CMP.4: Evaluate contemporary constitutional issues using evidence, competing viewpoints, democratic safeguards, and civil civic discourse.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how synthesis: civic decision-making and democratic institutions works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the civic decision checklist, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches unit synthesis across all assigned standards through the assigned standard only. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.USG.CMP.1, MLA.USG.CMP.2, MLA.USG.CMP.3, MLA.USG.CMP.4.
