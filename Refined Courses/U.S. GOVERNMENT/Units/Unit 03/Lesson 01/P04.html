Worked Example 1

Prompt: A rights scenario table asks which freedom or protection is involved and what limit may apply.

Step 1: Name the right. This step matters because it prevents guessing.

Step 2: Identify the specific liberty in the scenario. This step ties the answer to evidence.

Step 3: Speech and search protections are not the same right. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.USG.RGT.1.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A rights scenario table asks which freedom or protection is involved and what limit may apply.

Step 1: Check limit. This step matters because it prevents guessing.

Step 2: Rights are protected but may have lawful limits. This step ties the answer to evidence.

Step 3: A limit must be justified by law. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.USG.RGT.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A rights scenario table asks which freedom or protection is involved and what limit may apply.

Step 1: Use evidence. This step matters because it prevents guessing.

Step 2: The scenario facts decide which right applies. This step ties the answer to evidence.

Step 3: Do not choose all rights at once. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.USG.RGT.1.
