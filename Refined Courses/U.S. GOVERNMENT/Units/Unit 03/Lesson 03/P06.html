Independent Work

Unit 3 · Lesson 3: Civil Rights Movements and Government Responses

Independent Work

Complete all parts without answer feedback. Your work must be understandable to a reader who has not seen the prompt.

Part A: Precision

Choose four vocabulary terms. For each, give a definition, an accurate example, a tempting non-example, and the evidence that distinguishes them.

Part B: Evidence analysis

Construct a rights-response timeline with at least six linked moments: inequality, rights claim, movement strategy, government response, resistance or implementation problem, and later outcome. Explain causation without claiming that sequence alone proves cause.

Part C: Competing claims

Write two plausible claims about the scenario from Page 3. Decide which claim is better supported, cite two authoritative details, and explain why the other claim is incomplete or incorrect.

Part D: Revise your opening hypothesis

Return to Page 1. Revise your response to the essential question in 5–7 sentences using at least two precise constitutional concepts.

Self-audit before Page 7

  • I identified the correct government actor and role.
  • I cited a relevant constitutional, statutory, procedural, or case detail.
  • I explained how the evidence supports my claim.
  • I addressed a limit, required step, or counterclaim.
  • I did not claim that any branch has unlimited power.
