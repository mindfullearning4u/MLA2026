P01 Lesson Overview

Citizenship, Responsibility, and Rule of Law

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.RGT.4: Explain citizenship, civic responsibility, civic virtue, rule of law, jury service, voting, and constructive civic participation.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how citizenship, responsibility, and rule of law works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the responsibility evidence chart, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches civic responsibilities through the assigned standard only.
