P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A responsibility chart compares required duties with voluntary civic responsibilities.

  1. Underline the civic action, government power, right, process, or problem.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: citizenship, jury service, voting, rule of law.
  3. Match that evidence to MLA.USG.RGT.4.
  4. For synthesis lessons, write one sentence for each mapped standard before writing the final conclusion.
  5. Write one sentence explaining why the evidence supports the answer.

Common Mistake

Correct: matching the specific evidence to the specific standard.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar government word without proving it from the prompt.

Teachable Explanation

The teachable move is to prove the answer from the lesson evidence first, then name the standard. This keeps the work inside MLA.USG.RGT.4 and prevents guessing.
