P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A unit review asks students to connect all standards in MLA.USG.RGT.1, MLA.USG.RGT.2, MLA.USG.RGT.3, MLA.USG.RGT.4 to one evidence-based civic explanation.

  1. Underline the civic action, government power, right, process, or problem.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: rights and responsibilities, constitutional institutions, political participation.
  3. Match that evidence to MLA.USG.RGT.1, MLA.USG.RGT.2, MLA.USG.RGT.3, MLA.USG.RGT.4.
  4. For synthesis lessons, write one sentence for each mapped standard before writing the final conclusion.
  5. Write one sentence explaining why the evidence supports the answer.

Common Mistake

Correct: matching the specific evidence to the specific standard.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar government word without proving it from the prompt.

Teachable Explanation

The teachable move is to prove the answer from the lesson evidence first, then name the standard. This keeps the work inside MLA.USG.RGT.1, MLA.USG.RGT.2, MLA.USG.RGT.3, MLA.USG.RGT.4 and prevents guessing. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.USG.RGT.1, MLA.USG.RGT.2, MLA.USG.RGT.3, MLA.USG.RGT.4.
