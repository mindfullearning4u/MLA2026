Model Analysis

Because a public school is a state actor and suspension affects recognized educational and reputational interests, due process requires an appropriately timed explanation and opportunity to respond. The Constitution does not demand a full criminal trial, but no meaningful notice or response procedure is constitutionally suspect.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: “The government may do this because it has authority.” This does not identify the source, scope, procedure, evidence, or limit.
Correct: Due process and equal protection are related but distinct. Due process asks about lawful deprivation and fairness; equal protection asks whether government has treated similarly situated people differently without adequate constitutional justification.

Teachable explanation: A strong answer is narrower than the topic. It matches one actor and action to one controlling rule, then explains how a printed fact changes the conclusion.
