P01 Lesson Overview

Due Process, Equal Protection, and Incorporation

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.RGT.2: Explain due process, equal protection, selective incorporation, and the relationship between federal courts and individual rights.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how due process, equal protection, and incorporation works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the rights pathway diagram, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches fairness and equal protection through the assigned standard only.
