Worked Example 1

Prompt: A budget table asks students to compare revenue, spending priorities, and tradeoffs.

Step 1: Read budget. This step matters because it prevents guessing.

Step 2: Identify where money comes from and where it goes. This step ties the answer to evidence.

Step 3: Revenue and spending are different columns. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.USG.PCY.4.

Worked Example 2

Worked Example 3
