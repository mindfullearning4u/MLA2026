P01 Lesson Overview

Budgeting, Taxing, Spending, and Tradeoffs

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.PCY.4: Explain taxation, budgeting, spending, opportunity cost, and public-policy tradeoffs in representative government.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how budgeting, taxing, spending, and tradeoffs works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the budget tradeoff table, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches budget and tradeoff reasoning through the assigned standard only.
