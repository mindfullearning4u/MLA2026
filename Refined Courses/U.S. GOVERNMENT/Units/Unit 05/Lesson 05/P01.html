P01 Lesson Overview

Unit 5 · Lesson 5: Synthesis: Policy, Courts, and Federalism

Lesson Title

Synthesis: Policy, Courts, and Federalism

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.USG.PCY.1: Explain how public policy problems are identified, debated, written into law, implemented, funded, and evaluated. MLA.USG.PCY.2: Compare federal, state, local, and tribal government roles and explain how federalism works in practical policy decisions. MLA.USG.PCY.3: Analyze landmark Supreme Court cases and explain how constitutional interpretation affects rights, powers, and public policy. MLA.USG.PCY.4: Explain taxation, budgeting, spending, opportunity cost, and public-policy tradeoffs in representative government.

What You Will Learn

You will learn to identify the correct government actor, apply the mapped rule, use relevant evidence, and explain a limit or competing consideration.

What You Will Do

  1. Learn six precise terms.
  2. Study the policy decision record.
  3. Watch three complete reasoning models.
  4. Practice with feedback.
  5. Complete self-contained independent work.
  6. Submit a self-contained mastery checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will earn at least 80% by making an accurate claim, citing two relevant details supplied with the task, explaining why each detail matters, and addressing a limit or counterclaim.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can answer “How can governments make, fund, administer, and review policy across institutions and levels of government?” with accurate civic vocabulary, evidence, and reasoning.

Evidence Set: Policy decision record

Scenario: Congress offers matching grants for states to reduce wildfire risk. A state accepts funds, delegates projects to counties, coordinates with a Tribal Nation, and faces a lawsuit alleging that one land-clearing rule exceeds statutory authority and burdens protected rights.

Policy principleWhat the evidence establishes
Authority recordIdentify each actor, source of power, jurisdiction, procedural duty, legal limit, and review mechanism.
Policy recordDefine the problem, baseline, alternatives, causal theory, stakeholders, distribution, and adoption decision.
Fiscal recordState revenue, appropriation, grant conditions, cost horizon, opportunity cost, reserves, and audit requirements.
Implementation recordAssign administrators, milestones, rules, procurement, data, coordination, communication, enforcement, and contingency plans.
Court recordAnticipate standing, jurisdiction, legal question, precedent, standard, facts, holding possibilities, and remedy.
Evaluation recordPredefine outputs, outcomes, comparison method, equity measures, unintended effects, reporting dates, and revision triggers.
