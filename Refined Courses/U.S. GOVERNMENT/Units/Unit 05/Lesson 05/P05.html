Complete the Moodle Guided Practice after using the evidence routine below. Feedback is part of instruction: after each missed item, identify whether the error involved vocabulary, source selection, procedure, or reasoning.

For every item

  1. Restate the question as a policy claim you must test.
  2. Eliminate choices that use the wrong actor or branch.
  3. Eliminate choices that skip a required procedure or invent an unlimited power.
  4. Select the answer best supported by the provided evidence.
  5. Explain why the correct choice is stronger than the most plausible distractor.

Guided-practice record

For at least three questions, record: question focus; your first answer; decisive evidence; feedback; corrected rule; and a new example showing that you can transfer the rule.

Do not memorize answer position. The quiz assesses application in new scenarios. Learn the constitutional rule and reasoning process.
