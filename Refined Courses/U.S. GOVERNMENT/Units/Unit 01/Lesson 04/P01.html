Federalism, Separation of Powers, and Checks and Balances

Standard for This Page

Student-friendly meaning: I can trace who has constitutional authority, identify the safeguard that controls it, and explain how the safeguard protects self-government and rights.

Essential Question

How does the Constitution make government powerful enough to govern but divided enough to remain accountable?

Five Connected Safeguards

PrincipleDesign questionConstitutional evidence
Popular sovereigntyWho authorizes government?“We the People,” elections, amendment and ratification processes
FederalismWhich level governs?Enumerated national powers, state authority, Supremacy Clause, Tenth Amendment
Separation of powersWhich branch performs the function?Articles I, II, and III vest different powers
Checks and balancesHow can another branch restrain misuse?Veto, override, appointments, confirmations, impeachment, judicial review
Individual rightsWhat may government not violate?Original constitutional protections and amendments, especially the Bill of Rights
