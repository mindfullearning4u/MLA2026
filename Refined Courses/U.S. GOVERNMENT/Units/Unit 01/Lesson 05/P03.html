Model Analysis

A complete analysis begins with legitimacy and constitutional authority, then traces institutional roles and federalism, and finally tests the law against rights and rule-of-law limits. The Articles experience explains the need for national capacity, but limited-government principles explain why emergency power is not automatically unlimited.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: “The government may do this because it has authority.” This does not identify the source, scope, procedure, evidence, or limit.
Correct: Effective government and limited government are not opposites. Constitutional design seeks enough lawful capacity to govern while preserving enforceable procedures, divided authority, and rights.
