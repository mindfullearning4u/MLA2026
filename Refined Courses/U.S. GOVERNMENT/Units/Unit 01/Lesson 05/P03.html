P03 Civic Evidence Lab – Cross-Standard Analysis

Synthesis: Foundations of Constitutional Government

Standards for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1–4: Synthesize foundational principles; founding-document and Enlightenment influences; the transition from the Articles to the Constitution; and federalism, separated powers, checks, popular sovereignty, and individual rights.

Student-friendly meaning: I can use evidence from the entire unit to explain how American constitutional government gets authority, protects rights, enables effective action, and limits power.

Evidence Directions

For each source, identify its direct meaning, the standard it best supports, and one valid cross-standard connection.

Source Set

  1. Declaration: rights are unalienable and just powers derive from consent.
  2. Articles, Article II: each state retains sovereignty except powers expressly delegated.
  3. Constitution, Preamble: “We the People” ordain a more perfect Union to establish justice and secure liberty.
  4. Constitution, Articles I–III: legislative, executive, and judicial powers are vested in separate institutions.
  5. Bill of Rights: amendments declare and restrict government power in relation to protected freedoms and legal guarantees.

Verify with the National Archives founding documents.

Cross-Standard Questions

  1. Use Sources 1 and 3 to explain continuity between revolutionary principles and constitutional authority.
  2. Use Sources 2 and 3 to explain the shift from a state-centered confederation to a federal union.
  3. Use Sources 3 and 4 to explain why popular sovereignty does not place all power in one institution.
  4. Use Sources 1 and 5 to distinguish a philosophical claim about rights from enforceable constitutional text.
  5. Explain how one Articles weakness could threaten the protection of rights.
  6. Construct a four-link chain: earlier influence → founding principle → constitutional structure → modern safeguard.

Accuracy Rule

State what a source directly says before making an inference. Label the inference and explain why the text supports it.
