P05 Guided Civic Investigation – Integrated Cases

Synthesis: Foundations of Constitutional Government

Standards for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1–4: Synthesize foundational principles; founding-document and Enlightenment influences; the transition from the Articles to the Constitution; and federalism, separated powers, checks, popular sovereignty, and individual rights.

Student-friendly meaning: I can use evidence from the entire unit to explain how American constitutional government gets authority, protects rights, enables effective action, and limits power.

Case 1: Elected but Unlimited?

An elected governor jails a peaceful critic without charge. Consent explains the governor’s legitimate office, but due process, individual rights, limited government, and rule of law constrain the action. Election is authority to govern constitutionally, not permission to act arbitrarily.

Case 2: Weak Confederation or Federal Response?

States impose retaliatory tariffs. The Articles comparison identifies the coordination weakness. The Commerce Clause provides federal authority; bicameralism, presentment, judicial review in proper cases, and elections constrain its exercise.

Case 3: Trace an Influence

A student says Montesquieu “wrote separation of powers into the U.S. Constitution.” Improve the claim: Montesquieu’s analysis influenced American thinking, while American framers adapted divided powers within their own constitutional context. Influence is not identical copying.

Case 4: Majority and Rights

A majority votes to prohibit an unpopular religion. Popular sovereignty supports representative authority, but the First Amendment restricts government. Constitutional democracy combines majority decision-making with protected rights.

Case 5: Compromise and Evaluation

The Great Compromise solved a representation dispute through bicameralism. The Three-Fifths Compromise increased slaveholding power while denying enslaved people rights. Both were institutional bargains, but their moral and human consequences were radically different. Accurate synthesis examines function and injustice.
