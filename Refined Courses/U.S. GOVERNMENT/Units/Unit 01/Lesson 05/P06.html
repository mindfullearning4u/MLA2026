U.S. Government | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Synthesis Investigation

Synthesis: Foundations of Constitutional Government

Standards for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1–4: Synthesize foundational principles; founding-document and Enlightenment influences; the transition from the Articles to the Constitution; and federalism, separated powers, checks, popular sovereignty, and individual rights.

Student-friendly meaning: I can use evidence from the entire unit to explain how American constitutional government gets authority, protects rights, enables effective action, and limits power.

Independent Directions

Complete all tasks in your own words and submit as Notebook Evidence.

Part A: Build Evidence Chains

  1. Natural rights → Declaration → individual rights: explain each link.
  2. Articles revenue weakness → taxing power → checks on lawmaking: explain each link.
  3. Montesquieu → separation of powers → a modern check: explain each link.
  4. Popular sovereignty → republicanism → civic virtue: explain each link.

Part B: Correct Misconceptions

  1. “States had no power after 1789.”
  2. “Winning an election makes every action constitutional.”
  3. “The Articles failed because they had no Congress.”
  4. “A source influence and a constitutional rule are the same thing.”

Part C: Constitutional Design Memorandum

A fictional country wants a government that can collect revenue, regulate trade among regions, protect rights, and prevent concentrated power. Recommend a design using at least one principle or evidence item from each FND standard. Include a counterargument about efficiency and explain the tradeoff.

Completion Check

  • Four evidence chains are complete.
  • Four misconceptions are corrected with specific evidence.
  • The memorandum uses all four standards and addresses a counterargument.
  • Historical injustice is described accurately.
  • Reasoning is original; assistance is verified and disclosed.

Get Targeted Help from Your Teacher of Record

Identify the standard, source, and reasoning step before contacting your TOR. Include what you tried, the evidence you selected, and the exact misconception or uncertainty.
