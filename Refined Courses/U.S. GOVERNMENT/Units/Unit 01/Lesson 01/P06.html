P06 Independent Civic Investigation

Natural Rights, Rule of Law, and Limited Government

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1: Explain natural rights, consent of the governed, republicanism, limited government, rule of law, and civic virtue as foundations of American constitutional government.

Student-friendly meaning: I can explain where government authority comes from, which rights government must protect, and why leaders and citizens must follow constitutional rules.

Independent Work Directions

  1. Complete Parts A-C in your notebook without copying from AI or another source.
  2. Use the matrix and lesson sources as support.
  3. Write complete sentences and label each response.
  4. Include this page with your Notebook Evidence.

Part A: Distinguish the Principles

  1. Define natural rights in your own words and give one original example.
  2. Explain the difference between consent of the governed and republicanism.
  3. Explain the difference between limited government and rule of law.
  4. Explain why civic virtue matters in a constitutional republic.

Part B: Analyze New Scenarios

  1. A legislature passes a law, but members exempt themselves from following it. Identify the principle most directly violated and explain using evidence.
  2. A governor follows an emergency-powers law, reports actions publicly, and accepts court review. Identify two principles demonstrated and explain each.
  3. Citizens select representatives but refuse to learn about issues, serve on juries, or follow lawful election rules. Which principle is weakest? Explain the likely effect on self-government.
  4. A leader claims that winning an election permits any action. Use two principles to explain why the claim is incorrect.

Part C: Evidence-Based Response

Prompt: Which is more important for preventing arbitrary government: limited government or rule of law?

Write one well-developed paragraph. You must acknowledge that the principles work together, choose the principle you believe is most directly protective, use at least two specific pieces of lesson evidence, explain your reasoning, and address one reasonable counterclaim.

Permitted Support

You may ask AI to explain a term differently, suggest research keywords, or challenge your reasoning. You may not ask it to draft the paragraph. Verify any factual claim with the National Archives or another official source. If AI materially helped, add the disclosure statement taught on P03.

  • Vocabulary is used accurately.
  • The work is written in your own words.
  • Any meaningful AI assistance is disclosed and verified.
