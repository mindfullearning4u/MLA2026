Natural Rights, Rule of Law, and Limited Government

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1: Explain natural rights, consent of the governed, republicanism, limited government, rule of law, and civic virtue as foundations of American constitutional government.

Student-friendly meaning: I can explain where government authority comes from, which rights government must protect, and why leaders and citizens must follow constitutional rules.

Teacher of Record Graded Checkpoint

Checkpoint task: A newly elected official argues, “Because voters chose me, every action I take represents consent of the governed and cannot violate constitutional government.” Evaluate the claim.

Your response must:

  1. State a clear judgment about the claim.
  2. Accurately explain consent of the governed.
  3. Use limited government and rule of law to identify why election victory does not create unlimited power.
  4. Explain how republicanism and civic virtue should shape the official's actions.
  5. Use at least two specific pieces of evidence from the lesson matrix, Declaration excerpt, source analysis, or scenarios.
  6. Address one reasonable counterclaim.
  7. End with a conclusion explaining how the principles work together.
