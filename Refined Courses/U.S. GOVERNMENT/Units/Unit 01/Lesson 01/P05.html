P05 Guided Civic Investigation

Natural Rights, Rule of Law, and Limited Government

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.1: Explain natural rights, consent of the governed, republicanism, limited government, rule of law, and civic virtue as foundations of American constitutional government.

Student-friendly meaning: I can explain where government authority comes from, which rights government must protect, and why leaders and citizens must follow constitutional rules.

Purpose

This investigation prepares you for the Moodle Guided Practice. Complete each step before opening the assessment.

Investigation: Can You Trust the Claim?

Sample AI-generated claim: “The Declaration of Independence says government creates citizens' rights and may use any power approved by elected officials.”

Your job is not to accept or reject the claim immediately. Your job is to verify it.

  1. Break the claim into two parts: Who creates rights? What limits government power?
  2. Identify search terms: Declaration natural rights; consent of governed; limited government.
  3. Use the official source: Open the National Archives transcript. Do not rely on a search-result summary.
  4. Locate evidence: Find the passage about rights and just powers.
  5. Record evidence: Write a short quotation or accurate paraphrase and the source name.
  6. Evaluate: Decide which part of the AI claim is unsupported or misleading.
  7. Correct: Write a two-sentence corrected explanation using natural rights, consent, and limited government.
  8. Disclose: If you used AI beyond the sample claim, state how it helped and how you verified it.
