Articles of Confederation and the Constitutional Convention

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.3: Compare the Articles of Confederation with the Constitution and explain why the Constitutional Convention created a stronger federal system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can compare the first national government with the Constitution and explain how specific weaknesses led delegates to design stronger—but still limited—federal authority.

Before You Begin

  1. Confirm P02, P03, and P06 Notebook Evidence is complete.
  2. Write independently in complete sentences.
  3. Submit the checkpoint and Notebook Evidence in their separate Moodle activities.

Teacher of Record Graded Checkpoint

Task: Evaluate this claim: “The Constitutional Convention solved the Articles of Confederation’s weaknesses simply by transferring sovereignty from the states to an unlimited national government.”

Your response must

  1. State a clear judgment.
  2. Use at least three specific Articles weaknesses.
  3. Connect each weakness to a constitutional response.
  4. Explain why federalism, separated powers, and checks prevent “stronger” from meaning “unlimited.”
  5. Address representation and at least one Convention compromise, including its consequences.
  6. Use evidence from the comparison table and an official founding document.
  7. Address a reasonable counterclaim and conclude with a synthesis.
