P02 Notebook Task – Vocabulary and Comparison

Articles of Confederation and the Constitutional Convention

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.3: Compare the Articles of Confederation with the Constitution and explain why the Constitutional Convention created a stronger federal system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can compare the first national government with the Constitution and explain how specific weaknesses led delegates to design stronger—but still limited—federal authority.

Notebook Directions

  1. Define every term in your own words.
  2. Add the constitutional problem each term helps explain.
  3. Complete the comparison questions. This page is required Notebook Evidence.

Vocabulary

TermAccurate meaningWhy it matters
confederationAn alliance in which member states keep most governing authorityDescribes the Articles’ state-centered structure
sovereigntySupreme governing authorityArticle II said each state retained sovereignty except powers expressly delegated
requisitionA request for money or resourcesCongress depended on states rather than collecting taxes directly
federalismConstitutional division of authority between national and state governmentsReplaced the loose confederation without abolishing state governments
enumerated powersPowers specifically granted by the ConstitutionStrengthened Congress while preserving limited government
bicameralHaving two legislative chambersThe Great Compromise created a House and Senate
ratificationFormal approvalNine state conventions were required for the Constitution to take effect among ratifying states

Notebook Check

  1. Why is a requisition weaker than a direct constitutional taxing power?
  2. How does federalism differ from a confederation?
  3. Why can stronger enumerated powers remain consistent with limited government?
  4. Explain how bicameralism addressed the dispute over state representation.
