Articles of Confederation and the Constitutional Convention

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.3: Compare the Articles of Confederation with the Constitution and explain why the Constitutional Convention created a stronger federal system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can compare the first national government with the Constitution and explain how specific weaknesses led delegates to design stronger—but still limited—federal authority.

Evidence-Lab Directions

Read each source feature, then answer Questions 1–6 in complete sentences. Cite the row or quotation that supports your answer. This page is required Notebook Evidence.

Primary-Source Evidence

“Each state retains its sovereignty, freedom and independence…” — Articles of Confederation, Article II
“The United States in Congress assembled shall never engage in a war… nor coin money… unless nine States assent…” — Article IX (excerpt)
“nor shall any alteration… be made… unless… confirmed by the legislatures of every state.” — Article XIII (excerpt)

Official source: National Archives, Articles of Confederation.

From Weakness to Constitutional Response

Observed problemWhy the Articles struggledConstitutional response
National debt and unreliable revenueCongress could request funds but lacked an independent taxing powerArticle I grants Congress power to lay and collect taxes
Trade barriers and competing state policiesNo effective national commerce powerCongress may regulate interstate and foreign commerce

Disputes requiring national lawNo general national judiciaryArticle III establishes federal judicial power

Notebook Questions

  1. What does Article II reveal about the balance between state and national authority?
  2. Why could a supermajority rule protect states yet obstruct urgent national action?
  3. Why did unanimity make formal amendment exceptionally difficult?
  4. Explain one connection between a specific Articles weakness and a constitutional power.
  5. Why was Shays’ Rebellion evidence of weakness rather than the sole cause of the Convention?
  6. Use two rows to explain why the Constitution created a federal system rather than simply strengthening a confederation.
