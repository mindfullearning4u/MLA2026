P01 Lesson Overview

Articles of Confederation and the Constitutional Convention

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.3: Compare the Articles of Confederation with the Constitution and explain why the Constitutional Convention created a stronger federal system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can compare the first national government with the Constitution and explain how specific weaknesses led delegates to design stronger—but still limited—federal authority.

Essential Question

Why did Americans replace a confederation of sovereign states with a federal constitutional system, and how did the new design strengthen national action without creating unlimited government?

What You Will Learn

  • The Articles created a “league of friendship” in which states retained broad sovereignty.
  • Congress lacked an independent power to tax and regulate interstate or foreign commerce.
  • There was no separate national executive or national judiciary under the Articles.
  • Economic disorder, interstate conflict, national-security problems, and events such as Shays’ Rebellion exposed weaknesses.
  • The Constitution created enumerated national powers, three branches, federal supremacy within constitutional authority, and a practical amendment process.
