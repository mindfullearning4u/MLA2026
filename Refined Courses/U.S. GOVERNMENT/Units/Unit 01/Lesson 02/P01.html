P01 Lesson Overview

Founding Documents and Enlightenment Influences

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.FND.2: Analyze how Magna Carta, English Bill of Rights, Mayflower Compact, Common Sense, Declaration of Independence, and Enlightenment political thought influenced the American constitutional system.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how founding documents and enlightenment influences works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the influence chain, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches founding document influence through the assigned standard only.
