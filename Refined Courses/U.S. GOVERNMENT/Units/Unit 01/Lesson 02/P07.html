Founding Documents and Enlightenment Influences

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.2: Analyze how Magna Carta, the English Bill of Rights, the Mayflower Compact, Common Sense, the Declaration of Independence, and Enlightenment political thought influenced the American constitutional system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can trace a constitutional idea to an earlier source, explain what the source contributed, and support the connection with evidence.

Teacher of Record Graded Checkpoint

Task: A student claims, “American constitutional government was created from one document, so earlier English traditions, colonial self-government, Enlightenment thinkers, and revolutionary writing are unimportant.” Evaluate the claim.

Your response must:

  1. State a clear judgment.
  2. Use at least three different sources or thinkers from the lesson.
  3. Explain each source's distinct contribution.
  4. Connect evidence to at least three constitutional principles.
  5. Address one counterclaim.
  6. Conclude by explaining how influences combined without being exact copies.

Mastery Criteria

Criterion Mastery evidence

Reasoning Each detail is connected to a later principle; the counterclaim is answered.
Integrity Writing is original and assistance is disclosed.
Communication Ideas are logically organized with precise civic vocabulary.
