Independent Work Workflow

Evidence Set: Influence-and-evidence comparison table

Scenario: Delegates propose a constitution with an elected assembly, separate executive, independent courts, protected jury trial, and a declaration that authority comes from the people.

Constitutional featureWhat the evidence establishes
Magna CartaHelped establish that rulers are subject to law and lawful procedures.
English Bill of RightsStrengthened legislative limits on executive power and listed protected rights.
Locke and social contractConnected legitimate authority to consent and protection of natural rights.
MontesquieuInfluenced the division of legislative, executive, and judicial functions.
Colonial self-governmentGave colonists experience with assemblies, compacts, and local participation.
Classical republicanismEmphasized civic responsibility and resistance to corruption.

Use this evidence only: Every fact, term, and rule needed for the task is printed in this panel. No other page or lesson is required.

Instructions

  1. Read the complete evidence set.
  2. Complete Parts A–C in order.
  3. Use only the printed vocabulary, scenario, and visual.
  4. Check each response against the completion criteria.

Part A — Precision

Choose four terms from this page’s visual or scenario. For each, write a definition, accurate example, tempting non-example, and the evidence that distinguishes them.

Part B — Evidence Analysis

Match each feature of the delegates' proposal to the strongest influence in the table, then explain two features that reflect more than one influence.

Part C — Competing Claims

Claim A: the proposed government action is adequately supported. Claim B: the action requires revision or more evidence. Choose the stronger claim, cite two details printed on this page, explain each link, and identify one fact that could reverse your conclusion.
