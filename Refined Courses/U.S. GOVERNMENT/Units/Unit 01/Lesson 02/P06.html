P06 Independent Civic Investigation

Founding Documents and Enlightenment Influences

Standard for This Page

MLA.USG.FND.2: Analyze how Magna Carta, the English Bill of Rights, the Mayflower Compact, Common Sense, the Declaration of Independence, and Enlightenment political thought influenced the American constitutional system.

Student-friendly meaning: I can trace a constitutional idea to an earlier source, explain what the source contributed, and support the connection with evidence.

Independent Directions

  1. Complete Parts A-C in your notebook in your own words.
  2. Use the influence chain and official sources.
  3. Label every response and cite the relevant source.
  4. Include this work with Notebook Evidence.

Part A: Trace the Influence

  1. Explain how Magna Carta and the English Bill of Rights contributed different limits on government.
  2. Explain how the Mayflower Compact illustrates consent and self-government.
  3. Trace one idea from Locke to the Declaration.
  4. Trace one idea from Montesquieu to the U.S. constitutional structure.

Part B: Compare and Evaluate

  1. Why was Common Sense influential even though it was not law?
  2. Compare the purposes of the English Bill of Rights and Declaration.
  3. Evaluate: “Every older document directly caused the exact wording of the Constitution.” Use two sources.
  4. Which source most strongly influenced limited government? Defend your choice and address one competing source.

Part C: Evidence-Based Response

Prompt: Which mattered more to American constitutional government: English legal traditions or Enlightenment political thought?

Write one developed paragraph with a defensible claim, at least two specific source details, reasoning, one counterclaim, and a conclusion recognizing that multiple influences interacted.

Submission Readiness

  • Eight numbered answers and Part C are complete.
  • Every influence claim names evidence and explains the connection.
  • Sources are identified.
  • Work is original; meaningful AI help is disclosed.
