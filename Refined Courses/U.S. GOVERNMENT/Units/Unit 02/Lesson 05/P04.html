Worked Examples

Unit 2 · Lesson 5: Synthesis: How the Constitutional System Works

Worked Example 1: Classify the power

Prompt: Congress passes a national data-privacy law under its commerce power. The President signs it, an agency issues enforcement rules, and a company challenges a rule as exceeding the statute and violating the Constitution.

  1. Underline the government action—not merely the topic.
  2. Identify the actor and its claimed authority.
  3. Locate the rule in the three-branch case pathway.
  4. Test the action against every required procedure or limit.
  5. Write a conclusion narrow enough to follow from the evidence.
Reasoned result: A complete analysis separates four questions: Congress’s constitutional authority and lawmaking procedure; the President’s role in presentment and execution; the agency’s delegated statutory authority; and the court’s jurisdiction and constitutional review. Calling the dispute simply “a checks-and-balances issue” is not enough without tracing each source of authority.

Worked Example 2: Diagnose weak reasoning

“This action is constitutional because the government has power.”

This response is inadequate. It does not identify which government actor, the source and scope of power, the required procedure, or a competing limit. Repair it by citing the relevant constitutional feature and explaining how the facts satisfy—or fail to satisfy—its requirements.

Worked Example 3: Handle an ambiguous fact

If a scenario omits a decisive fact, state a conditional conclusion: “If ___, then ___ because ___; however, if ___, then ___.” Constitutional analysis is stronger when it acknowledges what the record cannot establish.

Notebook repair: Rewrite the weak response as a four-sentence CER analysis. Sentence 4 must address a limit, counterexample, or missing fact.
Constitutional featureWhat the evidence establishes
Constitutional foundationBegin with the text: identify the branch, enumerated power, procedural rule, right, or structural limit at issue.
Legislative stageCongress investigates, drafts, deliberates, and must enact identical text through both chambers before presentment.
Executive stageThe President participates through approval or veto, then the executive branch administers valid law within constitutional and statutory limits.
Judicial stageA court with jurisdiction may resolve a concrete dispute about application or constitutionality, using precedent and reasoned interpretation.
Interbranch checksVetoes, overrides, appropriations, confirmations, oversight, judicial review, and jurisdictional rules distribute power without erasing each branch’s core function.
