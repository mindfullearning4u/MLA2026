Evidence Set: Three-branch case pathway

Scenario: Congress passes a national data-privacy law under its commerce power. The President signs it, an agency issues enforcement rules, and a company challenges a rule as exceeding the statute and violating the Constitution.

Constitutional featureWhat the evidence establishes
Constitutional foundationBegin with the text: identify the branch, enumerated power, procedural rule, right, or structural limit at issue.
Legislative stageCongress investigates, drafts, deliberates, and must enact identical text through both chambers before presentment.
Executive stageThe President participates through approval or veto, then the executive branch administers valid law within constitutional and statutory limits.
Judicial stageA court with jurisdiction may resolve a concrete dispute about application or constitutionality, using precedent and reasoned interpretation.
Interbranch checksVetoes, overrides, appropriations, confirmations, oversight, judicial review, and jurisdictional rules distribute power without erasing each branch’s core function.
Constitutional changeIf ordinary institutional tools cannot lawfully produce a desired rule, Article V provides the formal amendment path.
