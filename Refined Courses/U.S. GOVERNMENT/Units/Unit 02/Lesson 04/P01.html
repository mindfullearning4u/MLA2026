P01 Lesson Overview

Federal Courts and Judicial Review

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.CON.4: Explain the federal court system, judicial review, precedent, constitutional interpretation, and the role of the Supreme Court.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how federal courts and judicial review works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the court hierarchy diagram, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches judicial interpretation through the assigned standard only.
