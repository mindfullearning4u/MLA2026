P01 Lesson Overview

Congress and the Lawmaking Process

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.CON.2: Explain the structure, powers, representation, and lawmaking responsibilities of Congress.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how congress and the lawmaking process works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the bill-to-law flowchart, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches legislative power and lawmaking through the assigned standard only.

bill-to-law flowchart

StepStudent Look-For
IntroduceA bill is proposed in the House or Senate.
CommitteeMembers study, revise, hold hearings, or stop the bill.
VoteBoth chambers must pass the same version.
PresidentSign, veto, or allow the bill; Congress may override a veto.
