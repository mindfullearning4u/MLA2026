P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A bill moves from introduction to committee, floor votes, the other chamber, and the President.

  1. Underline the civic action, government power, right, process, or problem.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: House, Senate, bill, committee, veto override.
  3. Match that evidence to MLA.USG.CON.2.
  4. For synthesis lessons, write one sentence for each mapped standard before writing the final conclusion.
  5. Write one sentence explaining why the evidence supports the answer.

bill-to-law flowchart

StepStudent Look-For
IntroduceA bill is proposed in the House or Senate.
CommitteeMembers study, revise, hold hearings, or stop the bill.
VoteBoth chambers must pass the same version.
PresidentSign, veto, or allow the bill; Congress may override a veto.

Common Mistake

Correct: matching the specific evidence to the specific standard.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar government word without proving it from the prompt.

Teachable Explanation

The teachable move is to prove the answer from the lesson evidence first, then name the standard. This keeps the work inside MLA.USG.CON.2 and prevents guessing.
