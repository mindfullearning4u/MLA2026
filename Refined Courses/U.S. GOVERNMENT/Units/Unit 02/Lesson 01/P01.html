P01 Lesson Overview

Structure of the U.S. Constitution

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.CON.1: Analyze the Preamble, articles, amendment process, supremacy clause, and major constitutional principles of the U.S. Constitution.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how structure of the u.s. constitution works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the constitution map, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches constitutional structure through the assigned standard only.
