P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A constitution map asks students to locate where a power, process, or right appears.

  1. Underline the civic action, government power, right, process, or problem.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: Preamble, Articles, amendments, supremacy clause.
  3. Match that evidence to MLA.USG.CON.1.
  4. For synthesis lessons, write one sentence for each mapped standard before writing the final conclusion.
  5. Write one sentence explaining why the evidence supports the answer.

constitution map

StepStudent Look-For
PreambleStates purposes such as justice, defense, welfare, and liberty.
ArticlesOrganize branches, federalism, amendment process, and ratification.
AmendmentsAdd or change constitutional protections and rules.
SupremacyValid federal law is highest law when it conflicts with state law.

Common Mistake

Correct: matching the specific evidence to the specific standard.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar government word without proving it from the prompt.

Teachable Explanation

The teachable move is to prove the answer from the lesson evidence first, then name the standard. This keeps the work inside MLA.USG.CON.1 and prevents guessing.
