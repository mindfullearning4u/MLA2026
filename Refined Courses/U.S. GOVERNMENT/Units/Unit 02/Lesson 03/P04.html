Worked Example 1

Prompt: A President signs a bill, appoints officials, enforces laws, and responds to a foreign crisis.

Step 1: Identify role. This step matters because it prevents guessing.

Step 2: Ask what the President is doing. This step ties the answer to evidence.

Step 3: Signing, vetoing, enforcing, and commanding are different roles. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.USG.CON.3.
