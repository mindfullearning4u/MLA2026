P01 Lesson Overview

President and Executive Branch

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.USG.CON.3: Explain the constitutional powers, roles, limits, and responsibilities of the President and executive branch.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how president and executive branch works, how to prove it with evidence, and how it fits the assigned government standard.

What You Will Do: Read a government scenario, study the executive power organizer, answer practice questions, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will correctly connect evidence to the standard with at least 80% mastery on assessments.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches executive power through the assigned standard only.
