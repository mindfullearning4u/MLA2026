## Alignment Decision

U.S. Government is governed first by the Florida CPALMS civics/government standards assigned to course #2106310. The course uses local `MLA.USG.*` standards as the student-facing production numbering system, but those standards must stay bounded to the official CPALMS civic content listed in the external verification addendum.

The course also includes readiness and literacy support through:

- Florida B.E.S.T. ELA expectations for evidence, complex text, inference, format, and academic voice
- Common Core literacy in history/social studies skills used as support for civic source reading and evidence-based explanation
- SAT readiness for command of evidence, words in context, informational graphics, central ideas, and concise written reasoning
- ACT readiness for main idea, inference, evidence evaluation, social science passage reading, data interpretation, and clear usage
- ELD/accessibility support for academic vocabulary, clear directions, structured responses, and embedded visuals

These support alignments do not replace the CPALMS civics/government standards. They shape how students read, reason, interpret visuals/data, write, and explain evidence inside the mapped U.S. Government standards.

## Florida B.E.S.T. / ELA Support Alignment

| Support Skill | Course Use |
|---|---|
| Evidence citation and justification | Students explain civic reasoning using source evidence, constitutional language, cases, policy examples, charts, or scenarios. |
| Complex text comprehension | Students read founding documents, constitutional excerpts, case summaries, policy summaries, and civic scenarios in manageable steps. |
| Inference | Students infer civic principles, government limits, rights issues, institutional roles, and policy consequences from evidence. |
| Academic format | Students answer in clear civic explanations, source notes, decision records, and evidence-based checkpoints. |
| Voice and tone | Students use precise, civil, evidence-based language when discussing rights, civic action, public policy, and constitutional issues. |
