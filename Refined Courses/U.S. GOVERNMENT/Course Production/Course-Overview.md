# MLA U.S. Government Course Overview

Credit: 0.5

Structure: 6 units, 5 lessons per unit. Lesson 5 in every unit is the synthesis lesson and contains guided practice plus the unit assessment. Lesson 5 does not contain a lesson quiz.

Purpose: U.S. Government teaches constitutional principles, civic responsibility, rights, branches of government, federalism, elections, public policy, Florida government, comparative systems, and civic action through direct, student-facing instruction.

Instructional requirement: every lesson must teach step by step with no live-teacher dependency. Students are encouraged to contact the Teacher of Record for clarification, remediation, and retake readiness support.

Assessment format: Moodle XML only.
