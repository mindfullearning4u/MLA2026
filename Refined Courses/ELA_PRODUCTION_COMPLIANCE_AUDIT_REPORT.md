## Interpretation of Findings

### English I

English I passed the production compliance comparison. Lesson metadata and visible page standard references matched the approved lesson-level mapping.

### English II

English II has findings in Unit 6 Lessons 7 and 8.

- Unit 6 Lesson 7 metadata/page references include standards not assigned in the approved lesson-level mapping: MLA.ENG2.L.01 and MLA.ENG2.V.03.
- Unit 6 Lesson 7 is missing mapped standards MLA.ENG2.RS.01 and MLA.ENG2.W.03 from lesson metadata.
- Unit 6 Lesson 7 lists MLA.ENG2.C.03 as a primary standard even though the approved lesson-level mapping does not assign it as a primary standard for that lesson.
- Unit 6 Lesson 8 is missing mapped standard MLA.ENG2.V.01 and includes unmapped standards MLA.ENG2.C.03 and MLA.ENG2.V.03.

### English III

English III has findings in Unit 2 Lesson 8.

- Lesson 8 metadata and visible lesson pages reference MLA.ENG3.V.03, MLA.ENG3.W.04, and MLA.ENG3.C.01 even though those standards are not assigned in the approved Unit 2 Lesson 8 mapping.

### English IV

English IV passed the production compliance comparison. Lesson metadata and visible page standard references matched the approved lesson-level mapping.

### Creative Writing

Creative Writing has one metadata finding in Unit 6 Lesson 8.

- Unit 6 Lesson 8 lesson.json includes additional course-synthesis standards not represented in the parsed approved Lesson 8 mapping row: MLA.CW.WR.08, MLA.CW.REV.01, MLA.CW.PUB.01, MLA.CW.PUB.02, MLA.CW.COM.01, and MLA.CW.COM.03.
- No unauthorized visible page standard-reference finding was identified for this item in the automated page-reference scan.

### Journalism

Journalism has one metadata finding in Unit 6 Lesson 8.

- Unit 6 Lesson 8 lesson.json includes a broad set of course-synthesis standards beyond the parsed approved Lesson 8 mapping row, including ethics, reporting, writing, media, digital, publication, and communication standards.
- No unauthorized visible page standard-reference finding was identified for this item in the automated page-reference scan.

### Speech Communication

Speech Communication has two types of findings.

1. Metadata role granularity issue:
   - Units 2-6 Lessons 1-7 generally list standards in lesson.json but do not consistently separate primary standards from support standards using explicit primary/support fields.
   - The standards are present in the lesson metadata, but the primary/support role cannot be audited cleanly from lesson.json for those lessons.

2. Lesson 8 over-inclusion findings:
   - Unit 3 Lesson 8 includes MLA.SPEECH.LST.02 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.07 in metadata/page references even though those are not assigned in the approved Unit 3 Lesson 8 mapping row.
   - Unit 6 Lesson 8 metadata includes a broad set of course-synthesis standards beyond the parsed approved Unit 6 Lesson 8 mapping row.

## Unit Validation Findings

- Approved standards inventories, crosswalks, unit-level mappings, and lesson-level mappings are present for all reviewed ELA courses.
- All lesson-level mapped standards are traceable to the corresponding course inventory and crosswalk.
- All lesson-level mapped standards are traceable to the corresponding unit-level mapping standard set.
- Production-level mismatches are isolated to specific lesson metadata/page references listed in the Findings Detail table.

## Lesson Validation Findings

- English I and English IV show no automated standard-code mismatches against approved lesson-level mapping.
- English II, English III, Creative Writing, Journalism, and Speech Communication require targeted metadata or Lesson 8 synthesis review.
- Speech Communication requires metadata normalization if primary/support role auditing is expected from lesson.json.

## Recommendations

1. Do not modify files until the listed findings are reviewed against the intended approved mapping decisions.
2. For English II Unit 6 Lessons 7-8, reconcile lesson.json and visible page standard references with the approved lesson-level mapping.
3. For English III Unit 2 Lesson 8, confirm whether the extra Unit 5/6-style standards are intentional synthesis or should be removed from metadata/page references.
4. For Creative Writing and Journalism Unit 6 Lesson 8, confirm whether broad course-synthesis metadata is approved. If approved, update the lesson-level mapping wording to make full-course synthesis explicit and auditable.
5. For Speech Communication, normalize lesson.json fields to separate primary and support standards for Units 2-6 Lessons 1-7 if primary/support auditability is required.
6. For Speech Communication Unit 3 Lesson 8 and Unit 6 Lesson 8, reconcile extra metadata/page standards with approved lesson-level mapping.
