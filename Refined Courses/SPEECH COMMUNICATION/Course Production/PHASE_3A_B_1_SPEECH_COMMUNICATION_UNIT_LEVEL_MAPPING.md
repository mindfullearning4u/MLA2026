# Phase 3A-B-1: Speech Communication Unit-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document maps all 25 verified Speech Communication MLA standards to the approved six-unit course architecture. Every standard appears at the unit level, and no unit introduces student-facing lesson content, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, or LMS pages.

| 1 | Moderate; establishes course habits and communication access without overloading production demands. | Appropriate |

| 4 | High; research and source use require protected pacing. | Controlled by seven-lesson workflow |

| 6 | Moderate; synthesis and transfer only. | Appropriate |
