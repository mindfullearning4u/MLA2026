# Speech Communication — Course Overview

**MLA Course Code:** SPC  
**Credit:** 1.0 High School Communication Credit  
**Course Type:** Online, asynchronous, mastery-based  
**Delivery:** 100% online through Moodle  
**Teacher Support:** Teacher of Record (TOR)

## Course Description

Speech Communication develops effective listening, speaking, audience awareness, organization, research, argument, delivery, revision, and digital presentation skills. Students plan and deliver informative, persuasive, professional, and multimedia communication using accurate evidence and purposeful choices.

## Course Learning Outcomes

Students will learn to:

- adapt communication to audience, purpose, context, voice, and tone;
- organize clear informative and persuasive speeches;
- evaluate rhetoric, claims, reasoning, counterclaims, and evidence;
- research, evaluate, paraphrase, and cite sources responsibly;
- revise and deliver effective oral, digital, collaborative, and multimedia presentations.

## Course Organization

This course contains **6 units and 48 lessons**. Each unit begins with an ungraded Unit Pretest. Lessons 1–7 include a Lesson Quiz. Lesson 8 is a synthesis lesson followed by the Unit Assessment.

1. Foundations of Communication, Audience, and Listening
2. Speech Structure, Organization, and Informative Speaking
3. Rhetoric, Argument, and Persuasive Speaking
4. Research-Supported Speaking and Source Use
5. Delivery, Revision, Digital Presentation, and Publication
6. Professional, Multimedia, and Integrated Communication

## Mastery and Support

The minimum mastery threshold is **80%**. Students review TOR feedback and complete required revision, remediation, or approved reassessment before progressing. Students should contact the TOR when directions, resources, submissions, or feedback require clarification.
