P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

In a synthesis task, you are not practicing one isolated skill. You are showing that all source-use skills work together. A speaker should be able to explain what the source says, why it is useful, and how it supports the speech purpose.

Embedded Example

Research question: What preparation habits help students deliver clearer presentations?

Central idea: Organized preparation helps speakers reduce confusion and communicate clearly.

Source support: notes by main idea, timed rehearsal, room previewing, and purposeful visuals.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I found a source about presentations, so all of its details belong in my speech.

Correct: I should choose only the details that answer my research question and support my central idea.

The incorrect version treats all related information as useful. The correct version selects evidence based on purpose and audience need.
