How to Build a Complete Unit 4 Response

A complete synthesis response should name the communication situation, identify the audience, and explain the speaker or listener decision that fits the unit standards. Do not write a one-word answer or a general opinion. Use the standard language from the lesson to explain why the choice works.

Before submitting, check that your answer includes three parts: the exact communication skill, the evidence or scenario detail that proves it, and the effect on audience understanding. If the task asks for a sentence you could say aloud, make the sentence respectful, clear, and appropriate for the purpose. If the task asks for analysis, explain how organization, evidence, tone, source use, or digital presentation changes the message.

Use this frame when needed: The best communication choice is ___ because the audience needs ___, and the evidence from the scenario shows ___. This frame helps you show reasoning instead of guessing.
