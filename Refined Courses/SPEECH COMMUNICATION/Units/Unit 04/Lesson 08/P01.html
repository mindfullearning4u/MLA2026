What You Will Learn

You will synthesize Unit 4 by using research questions, source reliability, source comprehension, inference, structure, purpose, and central idea support in one speech task.

What You Will Do

You will review a full research-supported speaking scenario, practice source-use decisions, and prepare for the Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete synthesis practice, submit checkpoint evidence, and complete the Unit 4 assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson brings Unit 4 together so you can show that you can use sources responsibly and clearly in a speech.
