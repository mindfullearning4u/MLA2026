Submission Workflow

  1. Review the synthesis source.

  3. Select and explain source support.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a Unit 4 source-use plan with a research question, central idea, two supporting details, one supported inference, and one explanation of source purpose.

Mastery Criteria

  • The research question is focused.
  • The central idea is accurate.
  • Source details directly support the central idea.
  • The inference is supported by the source.
  • The source purpose explanation connects to speech use.
