Example 1

Task: Build the research frame.

  1. Research question: What habits help students deliver clearer presentations?
  2. Central idea: Preparation habits can reduce confusion and improve clarity.
  3. Audience: students preparing class speeches.

Example 2

Task: Choose source support.

  1. Detail: organize notes by main idea.
  2. Detail: practice with a timer.
  3. Explanation: both details support clearer organization and delivery.

Example 3

Task: Make a supported inference.

  1. Source detail: preview the room.
  2. Inference: knowing the space may reduce last-minute confusion.
  3. Speech use: recommend checking the presentation space before speaking.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source proves that visuals always make speeches better.

Correct: The source says visuals should clarify the message, so the speaker should choose visuals that help the audience understand.

The incorrect version overstates the source. The correct version keeps the source meaning accurate.
