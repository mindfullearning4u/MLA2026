Notebook Title

Unit 4 Research-Supported Speaking Synthesis

Vocabulary Review

  • Research question
  • Reliable source
  • Source comprehension
  • Inference
  • Source structure
  • Source purpose
  • Central idea
  • Supporting detail

Synthesis Scenario

Speech topic: preparing students for stronger presentations.

Source excerpt: "Presentation preparation improves when students organize notes by main idea, practice with a timer, preview the room, and choose visuals that clarify the message."

Step-by-Step Review

  1. Begin with a focused research question.
  2. Check that the source is reliable and relevant.
  3. Read the source accurately before using it.
  4. Make only supported inferences.
  5. Notice source structure and purpose.
  6. Choose details that support the central idea.
  7. Explain evidence for the audience.
