Speech Communication | Unit 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Central idea support in sources

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.02
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.02
  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.02

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to identify a source central idea and evaluate which details support it well enough to use in a speech.

What You Will Do

You will read source excerpts, identify the central idea, sort strong and weak support, and explain how evidence helps an audience understand the speech topic.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you choose evidence that supports a central idea.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you avoid random evidence. Strong speakers choose details that support the central idea and help the audience understand why the idea matters.
