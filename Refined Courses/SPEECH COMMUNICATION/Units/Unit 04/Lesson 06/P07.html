P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your evidence truly supports the source central idea.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the transition source.
  2. Identify the central idea.
  3. Select supporting details.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Submit the central idea, two supporting details, an explanation for each detail, and one sentence connecting the source to a speech about organization.

Mastery Criteria

  • The central idea is accurate.
  • Each detail directly supports the central idea.
  • Explanations show why the details matter.
  • The speech connection is clear and audience-focused.
Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the lesson, checking your notebook work, or preparing your submission.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Central idea support in sources

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.LST.02

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Start with the speech question or information need. Research should answer a purpose, not collect random facts.
  2. Check source reliability, relevance, and usefulness before adding information to speech notes.
  3. Paraphrase source information accurately so the speech sounds natural and avoids copying.
  4. Explain how the source evidence supports the speech point, central idea, or inference.

P07 checkpoint quality check: Your checkpoint should be clear enough for your Teacher of Record to see what communication skill you practiced, what choice you made, and why that choice meets the lesson goal. Before submitting, confirm that your response is complete, specific, audience-aware, and connected to the mapped standard.

  • Name the communication decision or skill you used.
  • Explain why your work fits the purpose and audience.
  • Revise any vague wording before checkpoint submission.
