Speech Communication | Unit 04

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

When you select a source, separate popularity from quality. A popular post may get attention, but it may not be accurate or useful for a speech. A less flashy source may be stronger if it gives clear information that matches the research question.

Deeper Communication Thinking

Think about your audience. If your speech asks listeners to trust your recommendation, they need to know your evidence came from a responsible source. Reliable sources help the speaker sound prepared and credible.

Embedded Example

Speech topic: improving club participation.

Reliable and valid source: a school activity report showing participation numbers and common reasons students join clubs.

Why it fits: it directly supports a speech about club participation and provides information beyond personal opinion.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I will use the first website I find because it mentions my topic.

Correct: I will choose a source that identifies the creator, connects to my research question, and gives information I can explain to my audience.

The incorrect approach values speed over quality. The correct approach checks reliability, validity, and audience usefulness.
