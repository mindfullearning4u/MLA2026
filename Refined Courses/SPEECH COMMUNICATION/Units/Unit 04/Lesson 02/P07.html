Speech Communication | Unit 04

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you decide whether a source is reliable, valid, and useful before you build a speech around it.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review the source evaluation steps.
  2. Write your research question.
  3. Compare two possible sources.
  4. Submit your source-selection checkpoint.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one research question, two source descriptions, and a source evaluation paragraph explaining which source is stronger for your speech and why.

Mastery Criteria

  • The research question is clear.
  • Each source is evaluated for reliability.
  • The stronger source is valid for the question.
  • The explanation connects source quality to audience understanding.
