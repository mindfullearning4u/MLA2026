Speech Communication | Unit 04

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Turn a broad topic into a research question.

  1. Broad topic: student clubs.
  2. Focused topic: helping new students join clubs.
  3. Research question: What club information helps new students choose a group that fits their interests?

Example 2

Task: Check whether a question is answerable.

  1. Question: Why are clubs the best?
  2. Problem: The question is opinion-based and unclear.
  3. Revision: How can club descriptions help students compare meeting times, activities, and goals?

Example 3

Task: Connect the question to speech purpose.

  1. Purpose: explain how students can choose a club.
  2. Audience: students who are unsure where to start.
  3. Research question: What details should students compare before joining a club?

Common Mistake

Incorrect: What about clubs?

Correct: What details should students compare before joining a club?

The incorrect question gives the speaker no direction. The correct question tells the speaker what information to research and explain.
