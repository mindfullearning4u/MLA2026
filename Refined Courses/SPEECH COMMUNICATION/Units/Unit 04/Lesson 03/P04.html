Speech Communication | Unit 04

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Identify the main point.

Source: "A community garden can help residents learn about nutrition while also creating a shared space for neighbors."

  1. Topic: community gardens.
  2. Main point: gardens can teach nutrition and build community.
  3. Speech use: this source could support a speech about local wellness projects.

Example 2

Task: Choose a useful detail.

Source: "Workshops at the garden teach families how to prepare simple meals using fresh vegetables."

  1. Useful detail: workshops teach meal preparation.
  2. Why it matters: it explains how the garden supports nutrition.
  3. Speech connection: the speaker can use this detail as evidence of a benefit.

Example 3

Task: Paraphrase accurately.

Original: "The garden creates a shared space for neighbors."

  1. Weak paraphrase: The garden fixes every neighborhood problem.
  2. Strong paraphrase: The garden gives neighbors a place to work and connect.
  3. Reasoning: The strong paraphrase keeps the meaning without exaggeration.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source says gardens solve all health problems.

Correct: The source says community gardens can support nutrition learning and neighborhood connection.

The incorrect example overstates the evidence. The correct example stays close to the source and is safer to use in a speech.
