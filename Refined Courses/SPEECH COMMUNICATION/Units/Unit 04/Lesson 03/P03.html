P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now think like a speaker. The source does not say movement breaks solve every study problem. It says they may help focus when they are short, planned, and connected to the study routine. Accurate speakers do not stretch a source beyond what it says.

Deeper Communication Thinking

When you use a source in a speech, your audience depends on you to explain it honestly. If you leave out an important limit, your audience may misunderstand the evidence. If you quote too much, the speech may sound like reading instead of speaking.

Embedded Source

Source: "Movement breaks should be brief, purposeful, and scheduled so they do not replace the study task."

Speaker explanation: This detail matters because it shows the source supports balanced study routines, not unlimited breaks.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source proves that students should take breaks whenever they want.

Correct: The source supports brief, planned movement breaks that help students return to studying with focus.

The incorrect version exaggerates the source. The correct version includes the source limitation and keeps the meaning accurate.
