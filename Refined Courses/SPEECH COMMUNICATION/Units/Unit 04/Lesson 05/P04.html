Speech Communication | Unit 04

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Identify source structure.

Source: A flyer lists four ways students can prepare for a class presentation.

  1. Structure: list of steps.
  2. Feature: numbered recommendations.
  3. Speech use: each item can become a main point or supporting detail.

Example 2

Task: Identify source purpose.

Source: A school article explains why rehearsal improves presentation timing.

  1. Purpose: to explain a benefit of rehearsal.
  2. Audience: students preparing presentations.
  3. Speech use: support a claim about practicing before speaking.

Example 3

Task: Match source purpose to speech purpose.

Speech purpose: explain how to plan group slides.

  1. Best source: a guide with steps for shared slide creation.
  2. Reason: the source purpose is instructional.
  3. Use: the speaker can explain the steps clearly.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source has a heading, so it must prove my point.

Correct: The heading helps me identify the topic, but I still need details that fit my speech purpose.

A feature helps understanding, but evidence must still connect to the speaker purpose and audience need.
