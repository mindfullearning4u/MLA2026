P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Structures, features, and source purpose

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.01
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.01
  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.03

What You Will Learn

You will learn how source structure, text features, and author purpose affect how a speaker uses source information in a speech.

What You Will Do

You will read short source excerpts, identify how they are organized, notice features such as headings or examples, and decide how the source purpose fits a speech purpose.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you explain how source structure and purpose guide speech evidence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you choose source information with a reason. You are learning to ask why the source was made and how its structure helps you explain it to an audience.
