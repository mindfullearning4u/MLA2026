Speech Communication | Unit 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Inferences That Support Source Understanding

Standards Covered

  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.04
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.03
  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.02

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to make reasonable inferences from source information and use those inferences to support speech understanding.

What You Will Do

You will read source excerpts, identify what the source states directly, make a careful inference, and explain how the inference can support a speech idea.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you connect source details to an inference for speech use.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you read between the lines without guessing. Strong speakers make inferences that are supported by source details.
