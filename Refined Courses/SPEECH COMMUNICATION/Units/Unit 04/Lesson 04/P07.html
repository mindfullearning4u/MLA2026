P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you decide whether your inference is supported by the source details.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the audience-questions source.
  2. Identify direct details.
  3. Write a supported inference.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Submit two direct source details, one supported inference, and a short explanation of how the inference could help a speaker improve a future presentation.

Mastery Criteria

  • Direct details are accurate.
  • The inference is reasonable and not exaggerated.
  • The explanation connects the inference to speech revision.
  • The response stays focused on source understanding.
