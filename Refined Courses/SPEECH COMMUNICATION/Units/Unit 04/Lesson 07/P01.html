Speech Communication | Unit 04

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Integrated Research-Supported Speech Planning

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.01
  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.02
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.03
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.04
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.02

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to combine research questions, source comprehension, inference, and central idea support into one speech plan.

What You Will Do

You will read a research scenario, choose useful source information, and plan how to explain evidence in a research-supported speech.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint that shows a complete research-supported speech plan.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you move from finding information to using it responsibly in a speech. You will show how sources support a clear central idea for an audience.
