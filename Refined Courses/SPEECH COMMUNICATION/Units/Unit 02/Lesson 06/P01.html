🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Informative speech support and transitions

In this lesson, you will learn how to support informative speech points and connect them with purposeful transitions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.02 - Expository Communication.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01 and MLA.SPEECH.PRES.02.

You will explain information with support, logical organization, purposeful transitions, and speech-ready format.

What You Will Learn

  • How support explains a speech point.
  • How examples, details, and reasoning help an audience understand.
  • How transitions show relationships between points.

What You Will Do

  • Build supported informative points.
  • Choose transitions that show clear relationships.
  • Revise weak support and weak transitions.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Select support that fits a speech point.
  • Explain why support helps the audience understand.
  • Write transitions that connect ideas logically.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can develop an informative speech by supporting each point and using transitions that guide the audience.
