P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Supporting Informative Points with Transitions

Vocabulary

Support: Evidence, examples, details, or reasoning that explain a point.

Reasoning: The explanation of why support matters.

Transition: A phrase that connects one idea to the next.

Purposeful transition: A transition that shows the relationship between ideas.

Informative point: A main idea that teaches or explains information.

Step 1: Build Support

A speech point needs support so the audience understands it. Support can be a specific example, a detail, or reasoning.

Point: Rehearsal improves speech timing.

Support: When speakers rehearse, they can notice sections that take too long.

Reasoning: This helps the speaker adjust the speech before presenting.

Step 2: Add a Transition

A transition should do more than say "next." It should show how ideas connect.

Weak: Next, I will talk about notes.

Stronger: After timing is under control, organized notes can help the speaker deliver each point clearly.

Notebook Directions

Write one informative point about preparing for a speech. Add one support detail, one reasoning sentence, and one transition to a second point.

Model Example: Point: Note cards help speakers remember order. Support: A note card can list each main point. Reasoning: This keeps the speaker from skipping a section.
