🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 06

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Submit one developed informative speech point with support, reasoning, and a transition.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record before submitting if you need help making your support specific or your transition purposeful.

Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one informative speech point.
  2. Add one specific support detail.
  3. Write one reasoning sentence.
  4. Write one transition to the next point.
  5. Submit your checkpoint in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Develop one informative speech point about preparing for a presentation. Include the point, support, reasoning, and a transition to a second point.

Mastery Criteria

  • The point is informative and clear.
  • The support is specific and relevant.
  • The reasoning explains why the support matters.
  • The transition shows a relationship between ideas.
