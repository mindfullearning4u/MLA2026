P06 Independent Work

Complete each part in your notebook.

Instructions

Write clearly. Label support, reasoning, and transition so your Teacher of Record can see each part.

Part A: Add Support

Point: Organized notes help speakers deliver information clearly. Write one specific support detail for this point.

Part B: Explain Reasoning

Using your Part A support, write one sentence explaining why that support helps the audience understand the point.

Part C: Write a Transition

Write one transition from the point about organized notes to a new point about rehearsing delivery.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if your transition does not show a clear relationship.
