P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Informative Speech Planning

In this lesson, you will combine organization, expository development, paraphrasing, support, transitions, and format into one informative speech plan.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.02.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.LST.05 and MLA.SPEECH.PRES.02.

You will plan an organized informative speech that explains information clearly, uses paraphrased support, and follows expected speech format.

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine Unit 2 skills into one speech plan.
  • How to move from focus to main points, support, reasoning, transitions, and conclusion.
  • How to check whether a speech plan is complete and presentation-ready.

What You Will Do

  • Review the parts of an informative speech plan.
  • Develop one focused speech outline.
  • Add paraphrased support and purposeful transitions.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Create a logical informative speech plan.
  • Use support that explains each point.
  • Include transitions that connect ideas.
  • Follow speech format expectations.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can plan an informative speech that is organized, supported, clearly explained, and ready to present.
