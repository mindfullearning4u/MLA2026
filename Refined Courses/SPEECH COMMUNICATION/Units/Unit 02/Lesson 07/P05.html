P05 Guided Practice

Use this page to prepare for the Moodle Guided Practice on integrated informative speech planning.

Before You Begin

The Guided Practice has 5 questions. You will identify complete speech planning choices, support, paraphrase, transitions, and format decisions.

Practice Thinking

Question: Is the plan complete?

Plan: Focus, three main points, support, reasoning, transitions, conclusion.

Answer: Yes. This plan includes the major parts needed for an informative speech.

How to Use Feedback

If you miss a question, identify which part of the plan failed: focus, support, reasoning, paraphrase, transition, or format.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you are unsure which part of your speech plan is missing.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
  • Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
