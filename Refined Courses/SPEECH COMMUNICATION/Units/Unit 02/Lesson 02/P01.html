P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Informative and Expository Speech Development

In this lesson, you will learn how to develop an informative speech that explains information clearly and supports ideas with evidence.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.02 - Expository Communication.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01 and MLA.SPEECH.RES.02.

You will explain and analyze information using logical organization, purposeful transitions, appropriate tone, and evidence that supports reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How informative speaking explains a topic rather than argues a personal opinion.
  • How to develop an explanation with details and evidence.
  • How to use transitions to guide the audience through information.
  • How evidence and reasoning work together in a speech.

What You Will Do

  • Study how speakers explain information step by step.
  • Practice matching evidence to a speech point.
  • Build a short informative speech section.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify the purpose of an informative speech.
  • Select evidence that supports an explanation.
  • Use transitions to organize expository information.
  • Create a clear informative speech plan with support.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can explain a topic clearly in a speech by organizing information, using helpful transitions, and supporting my ideas with evidence.
