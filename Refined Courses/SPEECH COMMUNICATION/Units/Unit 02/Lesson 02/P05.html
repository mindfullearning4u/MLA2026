P05 Guided Practice

Use this page to prepare for the Moodle Guided Practice questions on informative speech development.

Before You Begin

The Guided Practice checks whether you can tell the difference between a topic, an informative focus, evidence, reasoning, and purposeful transitions.

Remember: evidence gives support, but reasoning explains why the support matters.

Practice Thinking

Focus: Time management helps students complete assignments.

Point: Using a weekly schedule helps students see when assignments are due.

Evidence: A weekly schedule can show homework, project deadlines, and study sessions in one place.

Reasoning: This helps because students can notice busy days early and plan work before deadlines arrive.

How to Use Feedback

If you miss a question, identify whether the problem was purpose, evidence, reasoning, organization, or tone. Then return to the example above and compare the parts.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if evidence and reasoning still feel like the same thing.
