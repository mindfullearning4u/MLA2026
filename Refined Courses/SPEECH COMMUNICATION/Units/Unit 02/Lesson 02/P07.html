🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 02

P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint asks you to submit one developed informative speech section.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record before submitting if you are unsure whether your evidence and reasoning clearly support your informative point.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review P02-P06 and choose one school skill.
  2. Write one informative focus sentence.
  3. Develop one point with evidence and reasoning.
  4. Add one transition to a possible next point.
  5. Submit your checkpoint in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one developed informative speech section. Include a focus sentence, one speech point, one specific piece of evidence, one reasoning sentence, and one transition to the next idea.

Do not submit a full speech. This checkpoint measures whether you can develop information clearly and support it with evidence.

Mastery Criteria

  • The focus is informative and clear.
  • The point supports the focus.
  • The evidence is specific and relevant.
  • The reasoning explains why the evidence matters.
  • The transition logically connects to a next idea.
