Submit an explanation of how structure shapes meaning in a short speech plan.

Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one speech purpose.
  2. Choose a structure that fits the purpose.
  3. Name two features that would guide the audience.
  4. Explain how the structure and features shape meaning.
  5. Submit your checkpoint in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Create a short speech plan about preparing for a presentation. Choose chronological, cause and effect, or compare and contrast structure. Then explain how two signal words or features help the audience understand your meaning.

Mastery Criteria

  • The chosen structure matches the speech purpose.
  • Two features or signal words are identified.
  • The explanation connects structure to audience meaning.
  • The response stays focused on speech communication.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Structures and features that shape meaning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.LST.01

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Begin with the central focus of the speech. The audience should know what the speech explains and why it matters.
  2. Organize information in a logical order such as problem-solution, cause-effect, chronological order, or main-point order.
  3. Use transitions to show how ideas connect. Listeners need verbal signposts because they cannot reread a speech.
  4. Check whether each section supports the speech purpose instead of adding unrelated details.
