P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Structures and Features That Shape Meaning

In this lesson, you will learn how the structure and features of a speech or source text help an audience understand the speaker's meaning.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.01 - Structures and Features That Shape Meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.02 and MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01.

You will analyze how organization, headings, sequence, transitions, and emphasis affect meaning in communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How structure affects what listeners understand first, next, and last.
  • How features such as headings, transitions, examples, and repetition shape meaning.
  • How speakers choose structure to match purpose.

What You Will Do

  • Study examples of speech structures.
  • Identify features that guide audience meaning.
  • Explain how structure supports informative speaking.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify organizational structures in speech plans.
  • Explain how features shape meaning.
  • Choose a structure that matches a speech purpose.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can explain how the way information is organized changes what an audience understands.
