P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

How Structure Shapes Meaning

Vocabulary

Structure: The way ideas are arranged.

Feature: A tool that helps guide meaning, such as a heading, transition, repeated phrase, example, or signal word.

Chronological order: Ideas arranged by time or steps.

Cause and effect: A structure that explains why something happens and what results.

Compare and contrast: A structure that explains similarities and differences.

Step 1: Notice the Order

Structure affects meaning because listeners understand ideas in the order they receive them. If a speaker explains steps out of order, the audience may not understand the process.

Example: To explain how to prepare note cards, a speaker should first explain choosing key points, then writing short cues, then practicing with the cards.

Step 2: Notice the Features

Features guide the audience. Words like first, next, because, however, and as a result tell listeners how ideas connect. Repeated key words can also show what the speaker wants the audience to remember.

Model: "Because practice lowers uncertainty, students may feel more prepared." The word because signals cause and effect.

Notebook Directions

Write the vocabulary. Then write one example of chronological order, one example of cause and effect, and one example of compare and contrast for a speech topic.

Model Example: Topic: preparing for a speech. Chronological order: choose topic, outline points, rehearse delivery.
