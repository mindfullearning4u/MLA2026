P06 Independent Work

Complete each part in your notebook.

Instructions

Use speech examples from this page. Identify structure, features, and meaning. Write complete sentences so your Teacher of Record can see your thinking.

Part A: Identify Structure

Speech plan: choose a topic, write a focus, outline main points, practice delivery. Identify the structure and explain why it fits.

Part B: Identify Feature

Sentence: "However, practicing with a friend gives feedback that silent practice cannot provide." Identify the feature and explain what relationship it signals.

Part C: Choose Structure

You want to explain how rehearsal leads to stronger delivery. Choose a structure and explain why it fits that purpose.
