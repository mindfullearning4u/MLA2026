P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Paraphrase for Speech Notes and Summaries

In this lesson, you will learn how to restate source information accurately in your own words so it can be used in speech notes and oral summaries.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.05 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.RES.03 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.05.

You will paraphrase complex information, keep the original meaning, and use academic vocabulary accurately in speech notes.

What You Will Learn

  • What paraphrasing means in speech communication.
  • How paraphrase is different from copying and from summarizing.
  • How to keep the source meaning while changing the wording and sentence structure.
  • How paraphrased notes help a speaker explain information clearly.

What You Will Do

  • Read short speech-source passages included in the lesson.
  • Identify the important meaning of a source sentence.
  • Rewrite the idea in your own words for speech notes.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Recognize accurate and inaccurate paraphrases.
  • Paraphrase a sentence without copying the source wording.
  • Keep important academic vocabulary when it is needed for accuracy.
  • Create speech notes that restate source information clearly.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can restate source information in my own words so my speech notes are accurate and clear.
