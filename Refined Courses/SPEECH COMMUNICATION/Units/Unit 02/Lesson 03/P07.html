🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint asks you to paraphrase a short source for speech notes.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record before submitting if you are unsure whether your paraphrase keeps the same meaning as the source.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the checkpoint source sentence below.
  2. Write the source meaning in your own words.
  3. Write one accurate paraphrase.
  4. Create one short speech note from the paraphrase.
  5. Submit your checkpoint in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Source: Speakers who paraphrase source information accurately can explain borrowed ideas without sounding as if they are reading directly from the source.

Submit three labeled parts: meaning, paraphrase, and speech note.

Mastery Criteria

  • The meaning statement identifies the source idea accurately.
  • The paraphrase uses your own wording and sentence structure.
  • The paraphrase does not add, remove, or change important meaning.
  • The speech note is short enough to support speaking.
  • Academic vocabulary is used accurately when needed.
