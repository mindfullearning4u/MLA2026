Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Step 1: Meaning: missing transitions can confuse the audience.

Worked Example 3: Make a Speech Note

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each modeled step carefully and notice how the thinking moves from the task to evidence, reasoning, and a clear response.

Source: A concise note can remind the speaker of the idea without forcing the speaker to read a full sentence aloud.

Step 1: Paraphrase the idea: Short notes can help a speaker remember ideas without reading word for word.

Step 2: Turn it into a speech note.

Speech note: Use short notes to remember ideas; avoid reading full sentences.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Copying the source and replacing one word with a similar word is always a paraphrase.

This is incorrect because paraphrasing requires restating the idea in your own wording and structure, not swapping a few words.

Correct Example

Correct: A paraphrase keeps the source meaning but uses a new sentence pattern and wording that the speaker understands.

This is correct because the speaker must understand and accurately restate the information before using it in speech notes.
