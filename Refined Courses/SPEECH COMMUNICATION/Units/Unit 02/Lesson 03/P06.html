🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete each part in your notebook. This work shows whether you can paraphrase accurately before using source information in a speech.

Instructions

Use the source sentences provided below. Do not search for another passage. Write your answers in your own words and keep the meaning accurate.

Part A: Find the Meaning

Source: A speaker who pauses between main points gives the audience time to understand the shift in ideas.

Write one sentence that explains the meaning of the source without copying the exact sentence.

Part B: Write a Paraphrase

Source: Using academic vocabulary can make a speech sound more precise when the speaker explains a complex topic.

Write an accurate paraphrase. You may keep the terms academic vocabulary, speech, and topic if they are needed for accuracy.

Part C: Create Speech Notes

Turn your Part B paraphrase into a short speech note that would help you remember the idea during a presentation.
