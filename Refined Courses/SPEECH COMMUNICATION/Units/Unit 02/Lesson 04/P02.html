P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Speech Format and Quality Checklist

Vocabulary

Format: The expected structure or arrangement for a communication product.

Quality expectations: The features that make work complete, clear, accurate, and ready for its purpose.

Speech outline: A planned structure that lists the introduction, main points, support, transitions, and conclusion.

Conventions: Standard grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling rules.

Polished work: Work that has been checked and revised before submission or presentation.

Step 1: Know the Required Parts

A quality speech product usually includes an introduction, a clear focus, main points, supporting details, transitions, and a conclusion. These parts help the speaker prepare and help the audience follow the message.

If one part is missing, the speech may still contain information, but the format is incomplete.

Step 2: Check Quality, Not Just Completion

Having all parts is the first step. Quality means the parts are useful. A transition should connect ideas. A conclusion should close the speech focus. A note card should help speaking, not force the speaker to read every word.

Quality question: Does this part help the speaker communicate clearly to the audience?

Notebook Directions

Create a checklist with these headings: Required Parts, Organization, Evidence or Support, Transitions, Conclusion, and Conventions.

Model Example: Required Parts: introduction, focus, three main points, support, transitions, conclusion.
