P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Format is not decoration. Format is a tool that helps the speaker prepare and helps the audience understand. A strong format makes the speech easier to deliver because the speaker can see what comes first, what comes next, and how the ideas connect.

Step 1: Check that every required part is present.

Step 2: Check that each part has a clear purpose.

Step 3: Check conventions so the speech product is polished.

Embedded Example

Weak note card: "Talk about planning. Also stuff. End."

Stronger note card: "Focus: planning reduces missed work. Point 1: deadlines visible. Transition: after deadlines are visible, students can rank tasks."

The stronger note card follows a speech purpose and gives the speaker usable cues.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A speech product is high quality if it has many words on the page.

This is incorrect because quality is not the same as length. A long speech plan can still be confusing if it lacks format, organization, and clear conventions.

Correct Example

Correct: A speech product is high quality when it follows the required format, organizes ideas clearly, and uses standard English conventions.

This is correct because it checks both structure and clarity.
