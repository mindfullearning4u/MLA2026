P05 Guided Practice

Use this page to prepare for the Moodle Guided Practice on speech format and quality expectations.

Before You Begin

The Guided Practice has 5 questions. You will identify required format parts, quality revisions, speech organization choices, and standard English corrections.

Choose the answer that would make the speech product clearer and more presentation-ready.

Practice Thinking

Speech product problem: The outline has three main points but no conclusion.

Best revision: Add a conclusion that restates the focus and closes the speech.

This revision follows speech format expectations and improves quality.

How to Use Feedback

If you miss a question, identify the category of the mistake: missing format part, weak organization, unclear support, weak conventions, or poor note-card quality.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you are unsure which format part is missing from a speech product.
