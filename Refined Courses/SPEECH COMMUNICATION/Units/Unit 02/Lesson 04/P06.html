P06 Independent Work

Complete each part in your notebook to show that you can check speech products for format and quality.

Instructions

Use the speech product details below. Do not create a full speech. Your task is to check format, quality, and conventions.

Part A: Check Required Parts

A speech outline includes an introduction, focus, three main points, support, and transitions. It does not include a conclusion. Write the missing part and explain why it matters.

Part B: Revise a Weak Note

Weak note: "Talk about planning and all the things."

Rewrite the note so it gives the speaker a clear cue for one speech point.

Part C: Correct Conventions

Correct this sentence: "my speech describe why note cards helps speakers"

Write the corrected sentence using standard English.
