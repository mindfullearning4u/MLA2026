P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Identify the Focus

Speech plan: Practice helps speakers improve timing, confidence, and clarity.

Thinking: The focus is practice helps speakers improve. The three details all support that focus.

Example 2: Choose a Logical Order

Task: Organize points about preparing a speech.

Best order: Choose a topic, create an outline, practice delivery.

This order follows the process from planning to presenting.

Example 3: Add a Transition

Before: Practice helps timing. Practice helps confidence.

After: Practice helps timing. In addition, practice helps confidence.

The transition shows that the second point adds to the first.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: "First I will talk about clubs, then lunch, then homework because those are school topics."

These ideas share a broad setting, but they do not support one focused speech idea.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: "First I will explain how clubs help students meet people, then learn skills, then feel connected to school."

Each point supports the focus that clubs benefit students.
