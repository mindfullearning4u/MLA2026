P07 Checkpoint Submission

This checkpoint asks you to submit a short organized speech plan, not a full speech script.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record before submitting if you are unsure whether your speech plan has one clear focus or if your main points are in a logical order.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02-P06.
  2. Create your final speech organization plan.
  3. Check that every main point supports the same focus.
  4. Submit your checkpoint in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Create a short speech plan about a school-related topic. Your plan must include one focused speech sentence, three main points in logical order, two transitions, and one conclusion sentence.

Do not write a full speech. The purpose is to show that you can organize speech ideas clearly before presenting them.

Mastery Criteria

  • The speech focus is clear and specific.
  • All three main points support the focus.
  • The order of ideas is logical for an audience.
  • Transitions show how ideas connect.
  • The conclusion reminds the audience of the main idea.
