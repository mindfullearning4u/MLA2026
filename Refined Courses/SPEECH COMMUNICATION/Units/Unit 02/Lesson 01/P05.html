P05 Guided Practice

Use this page to practice recognizing focused speech organization before completing the Moodle Guided Practice questions.

Before You Begin

Read each speech plan carefully. Ask yourself: What is the one clear focus? Do the main points support that focus? Is the order easy for an audience to follow?

The Moodle Guided Practice has 5 questions. Each question checks a skill from this lesson: focus, order, transitions, conclusion, or voice and tone.

Practice Thinking

Question to ask: Which plan would help an audience follow the speech best?

Weak plan: School clubs are fun. Lunch should be longer. Clubs help students meet people.

Stronger plan: School clubs help students meet people, build skills, and feel connected to school.

The stronger plan works because every point supports the same focus. The audience can tell exactly what the speech is proving.

How to Use Feedback

If you miss a question, do not just choose another answer. Read the feedback and identify the exact issue: Was the focus unclear? Was the order confusing? Was the transition weak? Then connect the correction back to your notebook notes.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help
