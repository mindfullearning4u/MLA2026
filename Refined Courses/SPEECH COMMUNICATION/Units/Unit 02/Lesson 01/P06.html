🗣 SPEECH | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete each part in your notebook. This work helps your Teacher of Record see whether you are ready for the checkpoint.

Instructions

Use complete sentences. Keep your work focused on speech organization, not writing a full essay. Your goal is to show that you can plan a short speech with one clear focus and logical order.

Part A: Identify the Focus

Choose one topic: school clubs, phone use in class, preparing for a test, or helping a new student. Write one focused sentence that tells what your speech will explain or prove.

Check yourself: A focused sentence should be specific enough that every main point can connect back to it.

Part B: Organize Main Points

Write three main points for your speech. Place them in the order that would be easiest for an audience to follow. After each point, write one sentence explaining why that point belongs in that order.

Part C: Add Transitions and Closing

Write one transition between Point 1 and Point 2. Write one transition between Point 2 and Point 3. Then write a one-sentence conclusion that reminds the audience of the speech focus.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if your main points do not clearly connect to one speech focus.
