Notebook Title

Unit 2 Synthesis: Informative Speech Planning

Vocabulary

Coherent focus: One clear idea that all parts support.

Expository development: Organized explanation with support and reasoning.

Paraphrase: Accurate restatement of source information in your own words.

Structure: The order and relationship of ideas.

Quality format: Required speech parts arranged clearly for presentation.

Review: What a Strong Unit 2 Plan Includes

A strong informative speech plan includes a focus, logical structure, main points, support, reasoning, paraphrased source information when needed, transitions, conclusion, and standard English in speech materials.

Notebook Directions

Write a Unit 2 checklist with at least six items that would help you evaluate an informative speech plan.

Model item: Each main point supports the same informative focus.
