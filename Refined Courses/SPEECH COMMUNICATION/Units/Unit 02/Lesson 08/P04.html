Common Mistake

Incorrect: "If the speech has a topic, it is ready for assessment."

A topic alone is not enough. The speech plan must show organization, support, reasoning, transitions, and format.

Correct Example

Correct: "A speech is ready when its topic becomes a clear focus and each part of the plan supports that focus."

This is correct because mastery depends on complete communication planning.

The worked examples show how to apply the lesson skill step by step, check the communication choice against the audience and purpose, and correct a common mistake before independent work.
