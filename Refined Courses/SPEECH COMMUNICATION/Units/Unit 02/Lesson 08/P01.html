P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 2 Synthesis

In this lesson, you will synthesize Unit 2 skills by reviewing informative speech planning, organization, paraphrase, support, transitions, and format expectations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 2 Primary Standards: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01, MLA.SPEECH.COM.02, MLA.SPEECH.LST.05, MLA.SPEECH.PRES.02, and MLA.SPEECH.LST.01.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.04 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.05.

You will use all major Unit 2 standards to show readiness for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 2 skills work together in informative speaking.
  • How to check a speech plan for organization, support, paraphrase, transitions, and format.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 2 concepts.
  • Complete synthesis notebook tasks.
  • Complete Lesson 8 Guided Practice.
  • Complete the Unit 2 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify strong and weak informative speech planning choices.
  • Apply paraphrase, structure, support, transitions, and format rules.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment with evidence of Unit 2 mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can bring together all Unit 2 skills to plan and evaluate an informative speech.
