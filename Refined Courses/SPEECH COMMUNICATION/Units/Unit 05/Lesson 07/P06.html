Speech Communication | Unit 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Plan a collaborative online publication process for a four-slide speech presentation about preparing for class presentations.

Part A

Assign three group roles and explain what each person checks.

Part B

Write a final publication checklist with at least four items.

Part C

Explain how the checklist helps the audience receive a clearer final product.
