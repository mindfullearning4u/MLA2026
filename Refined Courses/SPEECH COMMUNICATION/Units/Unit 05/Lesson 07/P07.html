P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your publication plan is clear, complete, and audience-ready.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review the project purpose.
  2. Assign collaboration roles.
  3. Create a publication checklist.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Submit three collaboration roles, a four-item publication checklist, and one paragraph explaining how your process improves the final digital speech product.

Mastery Criteria

  • Roles are specific and useful.
  • The checklist addresses content, design, consistency, and audience fit.
  • The explanation connects collaboration to publication quality.
  • The response stays focused on online speech communication.
