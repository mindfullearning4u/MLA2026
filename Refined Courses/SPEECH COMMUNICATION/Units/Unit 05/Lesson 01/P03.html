P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Different feedback sources may focus on different parts of communication. An adult may notice organization. A peer may notice confusing wording. An online tool may identify grammar or repeated words. You should consider each comment, but you do not have to accept every suggestion automatically.

Deeper Communication Thinking

Good revision decisions ask: Does this feedback help my audience understand my message? Does it fit my speech purpose? Does it make the speech clearer, more organized, or more accurate?

Embedded Example

Peer feedback: "I understand your opinion, but I need an example."

Revision move: Add a brief example after the claim so listeners can picture the idea.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I will change every sentence because someone gave feedback.

Correct: I will choose the feedback that helps my audience understand the speech more clearly.

The incorrect approach treats feedback as automatic replacement. The correct approach uses judgment and keeps the speech purpose in focus.
