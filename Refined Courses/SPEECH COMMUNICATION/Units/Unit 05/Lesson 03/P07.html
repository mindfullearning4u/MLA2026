Speech Communication | Unit 05

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you decide whether your vocabulary is precise and still understandable.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the draft sentence.
  2. Identify vague wording.
  3. Choose precise vocabulary.
  4. Submit your revision and explanation.

Checkpoint Task

Submit two vague words or phrases, two academic vocabulary replacements, one revised sentence, and a short explanation of how your vocabulary helps the audience.

Mastery Criteria

  • Vague wording is identified accurately.
  • Vocabulary replacements are precise.
  • The revised sentence is clear.
  • The explanation connects vocabulary to audience understanding.
Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the lesson, checking your notebook work, or preparing your submission.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Academic vocabulary in speeches

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.COM.05

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Read the speech or notes as a listener would hear them. Mark any wording that may confuse the audience.
  2. Revise one layer at a time: clarity, organization, vocabulary, transitions, tone, and conventions.
  3. Choose words for audience impact. Denotation gives meaning; connotation shapes how the audience feels or responds.
  4. Check that revised wording is precise, natural to say aloud, and appropriate for the speaking situation.

P07 checkpoint quality check: Your checkpoint should be clear enough for your Teacher of Record to see what communication skill you practiced, what choice you made, and why that choice meets the lesson goal. Before submitting, confirm that your response is complete, specific, audience-aware, and connected to the mapped standard.

  • Name the communication decision or skill you used.
  • Explain why your work fits the purpose and audience.
  • Revise any vague wording before checkpoint submission.
