P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Using Academic Vocabulary Clearly

Vocabulary

  • Academic vocabulary: school or content-area words used to explain ideas precisely.
  • Precision: choosing the word that gives the exact meaning needed.
  • Audience-friendly definition: a short explanation that helps listeners understand a term.
  • Context clue: nearby words or examples that help explain meaning.

Speech Example

Draft: "Practice helps a speaker get better."

Revision: "Rehearsal improves delivery because the speaker can adjust pacing, volume, and transitions before presenting."

Step-by-Step Teaching

Academic vocabulary should make the speech more exact, not more confusing. First identify the idea. Then choose a precise word. Next, define or explain the word if the audience may need support. Finally, use the word in a sentence that connects to the speech purpose.

  1. Find vague wording.
  2. Choose a precise academic term.
  3. Explain the term when needed.
  4. Connect the term to the speech idea.
  5. Check that the audience can still follow.

Model Example

Term: pacing.

Audience-friendly explanation: Pacing means how quickly or slowly the speaker talks.

Speech use: Strong pacing helps the audience follow each point without feeling rushed.
