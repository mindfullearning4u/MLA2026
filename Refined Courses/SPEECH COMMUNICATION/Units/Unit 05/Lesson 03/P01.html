Speech Communication | Unit 05

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Academic Vocabulary in Speeches

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.05
  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.07
  • MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to choose and explain academic vocabulary so your speech sounds precise, clear, and appropriate for the audience.

What You Will Do

You will study vocabulary in speech sentences, replace vague wording, and explain terms so listeners can follow the message.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you revise speech wording using academic vocabulary.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you sound prepared without confusing the audience. Precise vocabulary makes speech ideas stronger when you explain it clearly.
