Draft: "You gotta make the speech more better."

  1. Problem: informal wording and nonstandard comparison.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Standard English means using the longest words possible.

Correct: Standard English means using clear, correct language that fits the audience and purpose.

Strong speech language is clear and appropriate, not unnecessarily complicated.

As you study the worked examples, look for how each model makes a communication decision and explains why that decision works.

  1. Read the speech or notes as a listener would hear them. Mark any wording that may confuse the audience.
  2. Revise one layer at a time: clarity, organization, vocabulary, transitions, tone, and conventions.
  3. Choose words for audience impact. Denotation gives meaning; connotation shapes how the audience feels or responds.
  4. Check that revised wording is precise, natural to say aloud, and appropriate for the speaking situation.
