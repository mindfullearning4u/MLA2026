P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Connotation, denotation, and audience impact

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.07
  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.09
  • MLA.SPEECH.LST.03

What You Will Learn

You will learn how word meanings affect audience reaction. You will distinguish denotation, the direct meaning, from connotation, the feeling or association a word creates.

What You Will Do

You will compare word choices, revise speech language for tone, and explain how word choice supports speaker purpose and audience awareness.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you revise speech wording for audience impact.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you choose words that say what you mean and create the right effect for listeners.
