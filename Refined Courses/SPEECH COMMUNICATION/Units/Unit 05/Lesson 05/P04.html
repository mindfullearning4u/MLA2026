Speech Communication | Unit 05

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Identify denotation.

Word: revise.

  1. Denotation: to improve or change a draft.
  2. Speech use: revise describes improving speech notes.
  3. Reasoning: the direct meaning fits feedback and improvement.

Example 2

Task: Compare connotation.

Words: stubborn and determined.

  1. Both suggest someone does not give up easily.
  2. Stubborn often sounds negative.
  3. Determined often sounds positive.

Example 3

Task: Revise for audience impact.

Draft: Our group was lazy.

  1. Problem: lazy sounds blaming.
  2. Revision: Our group needed a clearer work plan.
  3. Reasoning: the revision identifies the problem without attacking people.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I should use the strongest-sounding word even if it makes the audience defensive.

Correct: I should use a precise word that supports my purpose and keeps the audience willing to listen.

Strong wording should support communication, not damage it.
