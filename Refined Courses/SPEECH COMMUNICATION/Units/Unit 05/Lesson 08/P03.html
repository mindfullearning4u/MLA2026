P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Unit 5 skills work together. A presentation can have strong ideas but weak wording. It can have attractive slides but unclear order. It can have group effort but no final publication check. Synthesis means checking all parts before the final product reaches the audience.

Embedded Example

Problem: A slide says, "Stuff to do before speeches."

Revision: "Presentation Preparation Steps: organize notes, rehearse with a timer, and preview the room."

The revision improves vocabulary, clarity, slide focus, and audience usefulness.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The project is finished because every group member added something.

Correct: The project is finished when the group revises for clarity, checks slide coherence, and confirms the final version is publication-ready.

The incorrect version focuses on participation only. The correct version focuses on communication quality.
