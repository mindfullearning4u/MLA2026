Common Mistake

Incorrect: More text on slides means the audience gets more information.

Correct: Brief slide text plus clear speaker explanation helps the audience listen and understand.

Slides should support the speaker, not replace the speaker.

Teachable Explanation: The correct example works because it applies the lesson skill deliberately and explains the reasoning instead of only naming an answer.
