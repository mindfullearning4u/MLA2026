Speech Communication | Unit 05

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Use a word family.

Word family: present, presentation, presenter.

  1. Shared idea: showing or giving information to an audience.
  2. Presentation: the speech or display.
  3. Presenter: the person delivering it.

Example 2

Task: Choose the correct derived form.

Sentence: The speaker made a strong ____.

  1. Best form: presentation.
  2. Reason: the sentence needs a noun.
  3. Speech connection: the word names the communication product.

Example 3

Task: Explain a term to the audience.

Term: collaboration.

  1. Base meaning: working together.
  2. Speech explanation: collaboration is when group members share responsibilities to improve the final presentation.
  3. Reasoning: the explanation fits the speech topic.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The presenter presentationed the topic.

Correct: The presenter delivered the presentation.

The incorrect example uses a made-up form. The correct example chooses forms that fit standard usage.
