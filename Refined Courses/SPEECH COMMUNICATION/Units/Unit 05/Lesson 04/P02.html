Notebook Title

Using Word Parts to Explain Speech Vocabulary

Vocabulary

  • Etymology: the history or origin of a word.
  • Root: the main word part that carries meaning.
  • Prefix: a word part added to the beginning of a word.
  • Suffix: a word part added to the end of a word.
  • Derivation: a related word formed from the same root or base.

Speech Example

Word family: communicate, communication, communicator.

All three words connect to sharing meaning. A speaker can use the word family to explain how a message moves from speaker to audience.

Step-by-Step Teaching

When a speech topic includes unfamiliar vocabulary, word parts can help. First look for the base word or root. Then notice prefixes or suffixes. Next, connect the word to related forms. Finally, explain the word in a way the audience can use.

  1. Find the base word or root.
  2. Identify any prefix or suffix.
  3. Look for related words.
  4. Use context to confirm meaning.
  5. Explain the word in speech-friendly language.

Model Example

Term: collaboration.

Related words: collaborate, collaborative, collaborator.

Speech explanation: Collaboration means working with others to create or improve a shared product.
