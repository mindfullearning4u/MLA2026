Speech Communication | Unit 05

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your word forms fit the sentence and speech topic.

Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one word family.
  2. List related words.
  3. Explain the shared meaning.
  4. Submit two correct speech sentences.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one speech-related word family, three related words, an explanation of the shared meaning, and two sentences using different forms correctly.

Mastery Criteria

  • The word family is speech-related.
  • Related words are accurate.
  • The explanation uses meaning and context.
  • Sentences use word forms correctly.
