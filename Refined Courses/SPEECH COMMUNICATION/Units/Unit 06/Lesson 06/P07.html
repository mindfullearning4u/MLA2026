P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you evaluate whether your digital publication plan fits the audience, purpose, platform, and presentation setting.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your independent work.
  2. Choose your best digital publication plan.
  3. Submit your checkpoint response in Moodle.
  4. Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you need support before submitting.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a digital publication plan for a school communication need. Include the audience, purpose, chosen digital format, three organization or design choices, and an explanation of how each choice supports the audience.

Mastery Criteria

  • The plan names a clear audience and purpose.
  • The selected digital format fits the audience and message.
  • Design and organization choices improve clarity.
  • The explanation connects choices to communication goals.
