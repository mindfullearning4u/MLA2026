Speech Communication | Unit 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Audience-Aware Digital Publication

Standards Covered

  • MLA.SPEECH.DIG.01
  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.03
  • MLA.SPEECH.PRES.02

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to plan a digital communication product for a specific audience, purpose, platform, and presentation situation.

What You Will Do

You will evaluate audience needs, select a digital format, plan clear organization, and decide how visual and spoken elements should work together.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by creating a clear publication plan, explaining audience choices, completing guided practice, and submitting a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standards.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you make digital communication choices on purpose. The audience, setting, message, and platform should shape how your communication product looks, sounds, and is organized.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help deciding whether your digital publication fits its audience and purpose.
