P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

Now think like a digital communicator. Your job is to match the message to the audience. A strong digital publication is easy to scan, easy to understand, and appropriate for the setting.

Embedded Example

Audience: parents receiving a school email about a student presentation night.

Best communication choices: clear subject line, formal but friendly tone, date and location near the top, short explanation of what students will present, and contact information.

Step-by-Step Thinking

  1. Ask who needs the message.
  2. Ask what they need to know first.
  3. Choose the format that makes the message easiest to use.
  4. Check whether tone, visuals, and organization fit the audience.
  5. Remove anything that distracts from the purpose.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use bright colors, slang, and lots of animation because it will look exciting.

Correct: Use clear headings, readable contrast, direct language, and only visuals that help the audience understand the message.

The incorrect choice focuses on decoration instead of communication. The correct choice connects design decisions to audience needs, message clarity, and purpose.
