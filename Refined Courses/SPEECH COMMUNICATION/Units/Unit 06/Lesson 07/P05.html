Guided Practice Instructions

Complete the guided practice in Moodle. Each item asks you to match portfolio artifacts, audience, purpose, and reflection to communication skills.

Before You Begin

  • Check whether the artifact fits the portfolio purpose.
  • Choose reflection statements that explain communication choices clearly.
