What You Will Learn

You will learn how to plan a communication portfolio that shows speaking, presentation, audience awareness, research, and digital communication skills together.

What You Will Do

You will select communication artifacts, explain why each artifact belongs in the portfolio, and plan how the portfolio will demonstrate growth and readiness.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by planning a portfolio with clear purpose, organized evidence, audience-aware explanations, and reflection on communication choices.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

A portfolio is more than a folder of work. It is a planned communication product that shows what you can do, why your choices matter, and how your skills fit a real audience.
