Common Mistake

Incorrect: My portfolio is strong because it has many files.

Correct: My portfolio is strong because each artifact is selected to show a different communication skill and includes a clear explanation.

Quantity alone does not prove mastery. A strong portfolio uses purposeful selection and clear reflection.
