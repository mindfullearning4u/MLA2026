Common Mistake

Incorrect: My portfolio is strong because it has many files.

Correct: My portfolio is strong because each artifact is selected to show a different communication skill and includes a clear explanation.

Quantity alone does not prove mastery. A strong portfolio uses purposeful selection and clear reflection.

Worked Example Reasoning Support

Lesson focus: Integrated communication portfolio planning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01, MLA.SPEECH.PRES.01

As you study the worked examples, look for how each model makes a communication decision and explains why that decision works.

  1. Identify what the audience should understand after viewing the digital or multimedia product.
  2. Choose visuals, layout, audio, or media only when they clarify the message or improve audience understanding.
  3. Make sure digital elements support the speech rather than distract from the speaker's purpose.
  4. Review the final product for accuracy, organization, accessibility, and publication readiness.
