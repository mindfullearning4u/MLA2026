P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Planning a Communication Portfolio

Vocabulary

  • Portfolio: a purposeful collection of work that shows skill, growth, and readiness.
  • Artifact: a piece of work included as evidence, such as an outline, slide plan, speaking notes, or reflection.
  • Reflection: explanation of what the artifact shows and why it matters.
  • Audience: the person or group who will review the portfolio.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Identify the purpose of the portfolio.
  2. Name the audience who will review it.
  3. Select artifacts that show different communication skills.
  4. Write a short explanation for each artifact.
  5. Organize artifacts so the audience can follow your growth.

A strong portfolio does not include random work. Each artifact should prove a communication skill and help the audience understand your readiness.

Model Example

Artifact: presentation outline.

Skill shown: organizing ideas for a specific audience.

Reflection: This outline shows that I can move from purpose to main points and plan how the audience will follow the message.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Integrated communication portfolio planning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01, MLA.SPEECH.PRES.01

Use this sequence before completing the notebook task. It shows how to approach the communication skill without guessing or skipping steps.

  1. Identify what the audience should understand after viewing the digital or multimedia product.
  2. Choose visuals, layout, audio, or media only when they clarify the message or improve audience understanding.
  3. Make sure digital elements support the speech rather than distract from the speaker's purpose.
  4. Review the final product for accuracy, organization, accessibility, and publication readiness.
