P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Evidence, inference, and source explanation

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.02
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.03
  • MLA.SPEECH.RES.04

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to explain evidence, make a reasonable inference from that evidence, and connect a source to a professional communication purpose.

What You Will Do

You will read short source notes, identify what the source actually says, explain what can reasonably be inferred, and decide how the source should be used in a briefing or presentation.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by separating evidence from opinion, writing a clear inference, explaining why the evidence matters, and completing the guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use research responsibly. Strong speakers do not just quote information; they explain what the information means and how it supports the message.
