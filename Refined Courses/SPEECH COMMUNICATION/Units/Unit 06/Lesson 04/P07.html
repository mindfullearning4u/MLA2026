Speech Communication | Unit 06

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your evidence supports your professional recommendation.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the briefing scenario.
  2. Write a research question.
  3. Select evidence and recommendation.
  4. Submit your explanation.

Checkpoint Task

Submit one research question, one recommendation, one evidence sentence, and an explanation of how the evidence supports the recommendation.

Mastery Criteria

  • The research question is focused.
  • The recommendation is clear.
  • The evidence directly supports the recommendation.
  • The explanation is concise and professional.
Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the lesson, checking your notebook work, or preparing your submission.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Research-supported professional briefing

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.RES.01

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Identify the audience before choosing words, tone, or examples. A strong speaker adapts to what listeners need and expect.
  2. State the purpose in one clear sentence so every communication choice supports that goal.
  3. Choose voice and tone deliberately. Ask whether the language sounds respectful, appropriate, and useful for the situation.
  4. Check that the final message fits the audience, purpose, and setting without becoming too casual, too vague, or too formal.

P07 checkpoint quality check: Your checkpoint should be clear enough for your Teacher of Record to see what communication skill you practiced, what choice you made, and why that choice meets the lesson goal. Before submitting, confirm that your response is complete, specific, audience-aware, and connected to the mapped standard.

  • Name the communication decision or skill you used.
  • Explain why your work fits the purpose and audience.
  • Revise any vague wording before checkpoint submission.
