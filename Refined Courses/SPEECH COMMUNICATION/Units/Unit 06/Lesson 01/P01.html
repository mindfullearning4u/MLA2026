Speech Communication | Unit 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Professional Communication Purpose and Audience

Standards Covered

  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.09
  • MLA.SPEECH.COM.08
  • MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how professional communication changes based on purpose, audience, setting, voice, and tone.

What You Will Do

You will compare communication situations, identify audience needs, and revise messages so they fit professional settings.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook work, guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint where you adapt a professional message for an audience.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you speak and write in ways that match the situation, whether you are contacting a supervisor, asking for help, or explaining an idea in a workplace setting.
