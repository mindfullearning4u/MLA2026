Speech Communication | Unit 06

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Identify purpose and audience.

Situation: You need to ask a teacher about a missing assignment grade.

  1. Purpose: request clarification.
  2. Audience: teacher.
  3. Tone: respectful and specific.

Example 2

Task: Revise for professional tone.

Draft: "You forgot my grade."

  1. Problem: sounds accusatory.
  2. Revision: "Could you help me check whether my assignment was received?"
  3. Reasoning: the revision asks clearly without blaming.

Example 3

Task: Match message to setting.

Setting: job interview.

  1. Weak response: "I just need money."
  2. Stronger response: "I am interested in gaining experience and contributing to the team."
  3. Reasoning: the stronger response fits the professional setting.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: If I am honest, tone does not matter.

Correct: I can be honest while choosing a tone that helps the audience listen and respond.

Professional tone does not hide meaning; it helps the message work.
