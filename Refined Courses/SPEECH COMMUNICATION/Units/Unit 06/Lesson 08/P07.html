Checkpoint Task

Write a synthesis plan for the school-event communication scenario. Include audience groups, purpose, evidence, format choices, tone, and organization.

Mastery Criteria

  • The plan identifies audience groups and needs.
  • The plan uses evidence to support a recommendation.
  • Digital and presentation formats match audience and purpose.
  • Tone, organization, and visuals support clear communication.
