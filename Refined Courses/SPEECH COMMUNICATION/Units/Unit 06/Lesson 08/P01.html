Speech Communication | Unit 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 6 synthesis uses previously taught professional, collaborative, research, presentation, digital, and portfolio communication standards from this unit.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how the Unit 6 skills work together in one professional communication task: audience planning, discussion, research, digital publication, presentation, and reflection.

What You Will Do

You will plan a professional communication response, choose evidence, select a digital or presentation format, and explain choices for a specific audience.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete synthesis practice and a Unit Assessment. Lesson 8 has a Unit Assessment instead of a Lesson Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you combine the skills you have practiced so you can communicate clearly in professional, digital, research-supported, and audience-aware situations.
