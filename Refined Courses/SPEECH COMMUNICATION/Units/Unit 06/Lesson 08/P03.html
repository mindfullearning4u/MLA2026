P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

A synthesis response should not treat skills separately. Audience awareness shapes tone. Research shapes the recommendation. Digital format shapes how the message is received. Speaking choices shape how clearly the plan is delivered.

Embedded Example

Plan: Use a short slide briefing for staff and a digital announcement for families and students. The briefing explains the recommendation. The announcement gives event details clearly.

Reasoning: Staff need the reasoning behind the proposal, while families and students need clear action information.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I will make one message for everyone because it is faster.

Correct: I will adjust the message for each audience group because students, families, and staff need different information and levels of detail.

The incorrect plan values speed over communication. The correct plan uses audience awareness to improve clarity and usefulness.
