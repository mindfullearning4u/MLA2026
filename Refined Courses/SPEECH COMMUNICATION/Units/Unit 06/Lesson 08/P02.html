Notebook Title

Unit 6 Synthesis Communication Plan

Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: combining several skills into one complete communication task.
  • Professional context: a communication setting where tone, clarity, evidence, and audience awareness matter.
  • Communication plan: an organized plan for purpose, audience, message, evidence, format, and delivery.

Synthesis Scenario

Scenario: A student leadership team needs to present a proposal for improving communication about school events. The audience includes students, families, and school staff.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Define the communication problem.
  2. Identify each audience group.
  3. Select evidence that explains the need.
  4. Choose a digital or presentation format.
  5. Plan voice, tone, organization, and visuals.
  6. Explain how the plan supports audience understanding.
