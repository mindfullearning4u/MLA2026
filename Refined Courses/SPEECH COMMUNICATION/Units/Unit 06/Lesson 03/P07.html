Speech Communication | Unit 06

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your multimedia choices support your speech purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Write the purpose and audience.
  2. List slide titles in order.
  3. Add media choices.
  4. Submit your speaker-note explanation.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a four-slide multimedia plan with purpose, audience, slide titles, two media choices, and one speaker-note sentence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Slide order is coherent.
  • Media choices support the purpose.
  • Speaker notes add spoken explanation.
  • The plan fits the professional audience.
Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the lesson, checking your notebook work, or preparing your submission.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Multimedia presentation planning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.PRES.01

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Identify what the audience should understand after viewing the digital or multimedia product.
  2. Choose visuals, layout, audio, or media only when they clarify the message or improve audience understanding.
  3. Make sure digital elements support the speech rather than distract from the speaker's purpose.
  4. Review the final product for accuracy, organization, accessibility, and publication readiness.

P07 checkpoint quality check: Your checkpoint should be clear enough for your Teacher of Record to see what communication skill you practiced, what choice you made, and why that choice meets the lesson goal. Before submitting, confirm that your response is complete, specific, audience-aware, and connected to the mapped standard.

  • Name the communication decision or skill you used.
  • Explain why your work fits the purpose and audience.
  • Revise any vague wording before checkpoint submission.
