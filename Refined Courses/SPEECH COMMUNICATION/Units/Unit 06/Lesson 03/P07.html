Speech Communication | Unit 06

P07 Checkpoint Submission

Teacher of Record Reminder

Your Teacher of Record can help you check whether your multimedia choices support your speech purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Write the purpose and audience.
  2. List slide titles in order.
  3. Add media choices.
  4. Submit your speaker-note explanation.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a four-slide multimedia plan with purpose, audience, slide titles, two media choices, and one speaker-note sentence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Slide order is coherent.
  • Media choices support the purpose.
  • Speaker notes add spoken explanation.
  • The plan fits the professional audience.
Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help: Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the lesson, checking your notebook work, or preparing your submission.
