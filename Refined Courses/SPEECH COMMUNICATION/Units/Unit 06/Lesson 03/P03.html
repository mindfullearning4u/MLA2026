Speech Communication | Unit 06

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued Notebook Application

A strong multimedia plan explains why each media choice belongs. A chart should compare information. An image should clarify a point. A short audio or video clip should demonstrate something that words alone cannot show as clearly.

Deeper Communication Thinking

Before adding media, ask: Does this help my audience understand the point? Does it match my tone? Does it connect to the slide before and after it? Does it support what I will say aloud?

Embedded Example

Weak media choice: a decorative image of an office that does not connect to the interview point.

Stronger media choice: a two-column chart showing vague interview responses beside specific professional responses.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Multimedia is stronger when I add more images, animations, and text.

Correct: Multimedia is stronger when each media choice supports the purpose and helps the audience understand.

The incorrect version adds clutter. The correct version uses media with a communication purpose.
