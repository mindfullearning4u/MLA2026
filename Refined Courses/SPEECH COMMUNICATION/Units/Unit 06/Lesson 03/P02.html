P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Planning Multimedia That Supports Speaking

Vocabulary

  • Multimedia: communication using more than one mode, such as speech, slides, images, charts, or audio.
  • Media choice: the visual, audio, or digital element selected to support a point.
  • Coherence: how clearly all parts connect.
  • Speaker notes: notes that guide what the speaker says aloud.

Planning Scenario

Presentation topic: Preparing for a professional interview.

Possible media: a simple checklist slide, a short timeline of preparation steps, and one chart comparing weak and strong responses.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Start with the presentation purpose.
  2. Identify the audience and setting.
  3. Choose main points in logical order.
  4. Select media that clarifies each point.
  5. Plan what the speaker will say that is not already on the slide.

Multimedia should not repeat every spoken sentence. It should give the audience a structure for listening and a visual anchor for important ideas.

Model Example

Slide focus: Interview preparation checklist.

Media choice: checklist with three short items.

Speaker notes: explain why each item helps the interviewee prepare professionally.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Multimedia presentation planning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.PRES.01

Use this sequence before completing the notebook task. It shows how to approach the communication skill without guessing or skipping steps.

  1. Identify what the audience should understand after viewing the digital or multimedia product.
  2. Choose visuals, layout, audio, or media only when they clarify the message or improve audience understanding.
  3. Make sure digital elements support the speech rather than distract from the speaker's purpose.
  4. Review the final product for accuracy, organization, accessibility, and publication readiness.
