Speech Communication | Unit 06

P04 Worked Example

Example 1

Task: Choose media for a main point.

Main point: Prepare before an interview.

  1. Useful media: checklist slide.
  2. Why it fits: a checklist organizes preparation steps.
  3. Speaker role: explain each step briefly.

Example 2

Task: Plan coherent slide order.

  1. Slide 1: purpose and audience.
  2. Slide 2: preparation checklist.
  3. Slide 3: response examples.
  4. Slide 4: final professional reminder.

Example 3

Task: Match speaker notes to media.

  1. Slide shows a chart of weak and strong answers.
  2. Speaker notes explain why the strong answer is specific.
  3. Audience understands both the example and the reasoning.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: I will read every word on the slide aloud.

Correct: I will use the slide as a guide and explain the idea in my own spoken words.

Reading slides can weaken delivery. Speaker notes should add explanation.
