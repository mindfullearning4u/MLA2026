P05 Guided Practice

Complete the Moodle Guided Practice after reviewing the focus below.

Guided Practice Directions

You will identify claims, reasoning, credible evidence, and weak support.

  1. Find the claim in a speech argument.
  2. Choose reasoning that explains the claim.
  3. Choose evidence that is credible and connected.

Before You Begin

Read the scenario, identify the communication purpose, then choose the best answer. Use feedback as reteaching before continuing.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if the feedback shows that you are choosing evidence that is not connected to the claim.
