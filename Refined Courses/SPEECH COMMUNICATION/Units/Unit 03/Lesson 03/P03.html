Continued Notebook Application

A strong persuasive speech argument works like a chain. The claim is the main point. The reasoning is the link that explains the point. Evidence is the support that makes the point believable.

When you evaluate an argument, ask: Is the claim clear? Does the reasoning explain the claim? Is the evidence credible and connected?

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Students should practice speeches because practice is good.

The incorrect version repeats a general opinion. It does not explain how practice helps or why the audience should accept the claim.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Students should practice speeches because rehearsal helps speakers identify confusing parts before presenting.

This response uses the lesson skill in a clear, teachable way.
