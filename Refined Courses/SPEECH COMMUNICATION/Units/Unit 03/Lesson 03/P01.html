P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Claims, Reasoning, and Credible Evidence

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can use it in speech planning and response work.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.01.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.RES.02 and MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01.

These standards guide how you build, explain, and evaluate persuasive communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How to write a clear persuasive claim.
  • How reasoning explains why a claim makes sense.
  • How credible evidence supports a speech argument.

What You Will Do

  • Identify claims, reasoning, and evidence in speech examples.
  • Build a claim-reason-evidence set.
  • Explain why evidence is credible and useful.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Write a focused claim.
  • Connect reasoning to the claim.
  • Select credible evidence that supports the argument.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can build a persuasive speech argument using a claim, reasoning, and credible evidence.
