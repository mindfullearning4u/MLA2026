P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Rhetorical Appeals, Purpose, and Figurative Language

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can use it in your own speaking and listening work.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.03.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.LST.06 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.07.

You will use these standards to make purposeful communication choices as a speaker, listener, and evaluator.

What You Will Learn

  • How speakers use rhetorical appeals: ethos, pathos, and logos.
  • How purpose shapes a speaker’s word choice and examples.
  • How figurative language can make a message clearer or more memorable.

What You Will Do

  • Identify appeal, purpose, and figurative language in short speech examples.
  • Explain why a speaker might choose a specific appeal.
  • Practice choosing the best rhetorical move for a communication situation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Classify rhetorical appeals accurately.
  • Explain the purpose behind a speaking choice.
  • Use precise vocabulary when describing figurative language.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can identify how a speaker uses appeals, purpose, and figurative language to influence an audience.
