Checkpoint Task

Create a short speech paragraph, four to six sentences, encouraging students to prepare before presenting. Include one rhetorical appeal and one example of figurative language. After the paragraph, label the appeal and explain how it supports your purpose.

Mastery Criteria

  • The paragraph has a clear communication purpose.
  • One rhetorical appeal is used accurately.
  • One figurative language choice is included.
  • The explanation connects the choice to audience effect.
