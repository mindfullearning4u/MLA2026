Submission Workflow

  1. Review the Unit 3 standards.
  2. Check that your plan includes claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim, rebuttal, audience, and tone.
  3. Revise any unclear explanation.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a Unit 3 persuasive communication plan. Include the audience, purpose, claim, two evidence-and-reasoning pairs, one counterclaim, one rebuttal, and one rhetorical choice you would use.

Mastery Criteria

  • The plan synthesizes Unit 3 concepts.
  • The claim is supported by evidence and reasoning.
  • The counterclaim and rebuttal are fair and respectful.
  • The rhetorical choice fits the audience and purpose.
  • The response is clear enough for a reader to follow without extra explanation.
