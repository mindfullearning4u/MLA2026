P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Counterclaims and rebuttal

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can use it in speech planning and response work.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.01.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.LST.04 and MLA.SPEECH.RES.02.

These standards guide how you build, explain, and evaluate persuasive communication.

What You Will Learn

  • What a counterclaim is in persuasive speaking.
  • How a rebuttal responds to an opposing view.
  • How evidence can make a rebuttal respectful and convincing.

What You Will Do

  • Identify counterclaims in speech scenarios.
  • Write rebuttals that answer the counterclaim directly.
  • Use evidence and reasoning to keep disagreement respectful.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • State a counterclaim fairly.
  • Write a rebuttal that answers the counterclaim.
  • Use evidence or reasoning to support the rebuttal.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can respond to an opposing view with a respectful, evidence-based rebuttal.
