P06 Independent Work

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Instructions

Use complete sentences. Your answers should show your thinking, not just the final answer.

Part A

Write one claim about a communication habit that helps speeches improve.

Part B

Write one fair counterclaim someone could raise against your claim.

Part C

Write a rebuttal that answers the counterclaim using reasoning or evidence.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if your counterclaim or rebuttal feels incomplete.
