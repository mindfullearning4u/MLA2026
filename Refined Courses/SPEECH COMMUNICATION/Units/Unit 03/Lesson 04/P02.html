Begin your notebook carefully. The written lesson is your teacher for this skill.

Notebook Title

Counterclaim and Rebuttal Notes

Vocabulary

  • Counterclaim: an opposing viewpoint or objection.
  • Rebuttal: a response that explains why the original claim still works.
  • Respectful disagreement: responding to an idea without attacking the person.
  • Evidence-based response: a rebuttal supported by facts, examples, or reasoning.

Detailed Teaching

Step 1: Identify the original claim.

Step 2: State the counterclaim fairly. Do not exaggerate it.

Step 3: Write a rebuttal that answers the counterclaim directly.

Step 4: Add reasoning or evidence so the rebuttal is more than an opinion.

Model Example

Claim: Students should rehearse speeches with a partner.

Counterclaim: Some students may feel nervous practicing in front of someone else.

Rebuttal: Partner rehearsal can start with one short section, which lowers pressure while still giving useful feedback.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the vocabulary.
  2. Copy the model example.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why the model works.
