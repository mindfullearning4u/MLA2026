P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Explaining Rhetoric in Speeches and Texts

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can use it in your own speaking and listening work.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.06.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.LST.03 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.09.

You will use these standards to make purposeful communication choices as a speaker, listener, and evaluator.

What You Will Learn

  • How to explain rhetorical choices instead of only naming them.
  • How audience and purpose affect the meaning of a speech or text.
  • How to use evidence from a speech example to support rhetorical analysis.

What You Will Do

  • Read short speech and text examples included in the lesson.
  • Identify rhetorical choices and explain their effects.
  • Practice writing clear analysis statements.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain how rhetoric supports a speaker’s purpose.
  • Use evidence from the example in your explanation.
  • Connect the rhetorical choice to audience impact.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can explain how a speaker or writer uses rhetorical choices to affect an audience.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you can find a rhetorical choice but need help explaining its effect.
