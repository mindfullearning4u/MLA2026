P04 Worked Example

Each worked example shows how to explain the effect of a rhetorical choice.

Example 1

Example text: "We prepare not because speaking is easy, but because our audience deserves clarity."

Step 1: The phrase contrasts easy speaking with audience clarity.

Step 2: The contrast emphasizes responsibility.

Analysis: The speaker uses contrast to show that preparation is an audience-centered choice.

Example 2

Example text: "Think of your outline as a path. Without it, listeners may not know where your ideas are going."

Step 1: The outline is compared to a path.

Step 2: The image helps the audience understand organization.

Analysis: The metaphor explains why structure matters in a memorable way.

Example 3

Example text: "I have watched students transform uncertain notes into confident presentations through revision."

Step 1: The speaker shares observation and experience.

Step 2: Experience can build credibility.

Analysis: The speaker uses ethos to make the advice more trustworthy.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The speaker uses a metaphor. It is good.

The mistake weakens communication because it skips the thinking process behind the speaking choice.

Correct Example

Correct: The speaker compares the outline to a path so listeners understand that organization guides the audience through the speech.

The corrected response names the communication choice and explains its effect.
