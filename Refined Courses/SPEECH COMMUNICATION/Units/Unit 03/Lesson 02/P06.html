P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your notebook so your Teacher of Record can see your thinking.

Instructions

Answer each part in complete sentences. Use the lesson vocabulary and explain your thinking clearly enough that another person could follow it without hearing a teacher explain it.

Part A

Read this example: "A speech without structure is a hallway with no signs." Identify the rhetorical choice and explain what it helps the audience understand.

Part B

Read this example: "Why risk confusion when one revision can clarify your message?" Identify the rhetorical choice and explain its audience effect.

Part C

Write your own analysis sentence using this frame: The speaker uses ___ to help the audience ___.
