Checkpoint Task

Compare these opposing arguments: One speaker says full scripts are best because they prevent forgetting. Another speaker says note cards are best because they support memory and natural delivery. Explain which argument is stronger and why.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies both claims.
  • The response compares evidence or reasoning.
  • The response explains which argument is stronger.
  • The response uses respectful, neutral language.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Comparing opposing arguments

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.LST.04

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Identify the claim first. A persuasive speech needs a clear position before evidence or rhetoric can be evaluated.
  2. Connect reasons and evidence directly to the claim. Evidence should prove, clarify, or justify the position.
  3. Address opposing views fairly. A strong rebuttal explains why the speaker's position remains stronger.
  4. Evaluate audience effect. Rhetorical choices should strengthen credibility, emotion, logic, or urgency without weakening fairness.
