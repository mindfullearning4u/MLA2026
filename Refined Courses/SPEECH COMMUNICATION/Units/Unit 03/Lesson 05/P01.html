P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Comparing Opposing Arguments

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can apply it independently.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.04.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.01 and MLA.SPEECH.LST.02.

These standards support persuasive communication, listening, and evidence-based speaking.

What You Will Learn

  • How to compare two opposing arguments fairly.
  • How to evaluate claim, evidence, and reasoning in each argument.
  • How to decide which argument is more effective for an audience.

What You Will Do

  • Read two sides of a speech issue.
  • Identify each side’s claim and support.
  • Explain which argument is stronger and why.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Compare opposing claims accurately.
  • Evaluate evidence and reasoning.
  • Explain argument effectiveness without attacking a person.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can compare opposing arguments and explain which one is stronger using claims, evidence, and reasoning.
