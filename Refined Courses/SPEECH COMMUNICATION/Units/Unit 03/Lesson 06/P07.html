Checkpoint Task

Write a claim, one piece of evidence, and two explanation sentences showing how the evidence supports your reasoning. Topic: Students should revise speeches after practice feedback.

Mastery Criteria

  • The claim is clear.
  • The evidence is relevant.
  • The explanation shows how the evidence supports reasoning.
  • The response is clear enough for independent review.
