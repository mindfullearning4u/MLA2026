P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Evidence to Explain and Justify Reasoning

This lesson teaches the communication skill step by step so you can apply it independently.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.RES.02.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.01 and MLA.SPEECH.LST.04.

These standards support persuasive communication, listening, and evidence-based speaking.

What You Will Learn

  • How evidence supports reasoning in persuasive speeches.
  • How to explain evidence instead of simply naming it.
  • How evidence can justify a rebuttal or claim.

What You Will Do

  • Choose evidence that fits a claim.
  • Explain how evidence proves or supports reasoning.
  • Use evidence to strengthen persuasive speech responses.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Select relevant evidence.
  • Explain the evidence clearly.
  • Connect evidence to claims, reasoning, or rebuttals.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use evidence to explain and justify reasoning in a persuasive speech.
