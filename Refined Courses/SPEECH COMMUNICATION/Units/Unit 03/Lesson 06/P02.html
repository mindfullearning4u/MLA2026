Notebook Title

Evidence to Explain and Justify Reasoning Notes

Vocabulary

  • Evidence: support such as facts, examples, expert ideas, or observations.
  • Justify: to show why a claim or reason is valid.
  • Relevant: directly connected to the claim.
  • Explanation: the sentence that tells how the evidence supports the point.

Detailed Teaching

Step 1: Start with the claim or reason.

Step 2: Choose evidence that directly connects to that point.

Step 3: Explain the evidence. Tell the audience what the evidence proves.

Step 4: Check relevance. If the evidence does not help prove the point, choose stronger evidence.

Model Example

Claim: Peer rehearsal improves speeches.

Evidence: A peer can identify when a transition is unclear.

Explanation: This evidence supports the claim because feedback helps the speaker revise before presenting.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the vocabulary.
  2. Copy the model example.
  3. Write one explanation sentence in your own words.
