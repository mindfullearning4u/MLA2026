Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help paraphrasing the speaker's idea or choosing an appropriate response.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the checkpoint scenario.
  2. Write one paraphrase and one discussion response.
  3. Check that your tone is respectful and your meaning is clear.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Speaker: "I think our speech should include one short personal example because the audience may connect better to a real situation."

Write two parts:

  • A paraphrase of the speaker's idea.
  • A respectful response that either builds on the idea or disagrees with a reason.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your paraphrase keeps the speaker's meaning.
  • Your response connects to the speaker's idea.
  • Your tone is respectful and discussion-ready.
  • Your reason connects to audience, purpose, or clarity.
