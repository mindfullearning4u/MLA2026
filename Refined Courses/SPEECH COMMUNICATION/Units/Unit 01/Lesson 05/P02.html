P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Listening Before Responding

Vocabulary

  • Active listening: listening with attention so you can understand and respond thoughtfully.
  • Paraphrase: restating someone else's idea in your own words without changing the meaning.
  • Clarifying question: a question that helps you understand what someone means.
  • Discussion response: a spoken or written reply that connects to another person's idea.
  • Respectful disagreement: disagreeing with an idea while still treating the speaker with respect.

Step 1: Listen for the Main Idea

Active listening begins before you speak. Your first job is to understand the speaker's main idea. Do not plan your answer while the other person is still talking. Listen for the topic, the claim, and the reason the speaker gives.

Listening question: What is the speaker really trying to say?

Step 2: Paraphrase Before Responding

  1. State the speaker's idea in your own words.
  2. Keep the same meaning.
  3. Do not add your opinion yet.
  4. Ask if your paraphrase is accurate when needed.

Communication Example

Speaker: "Our introduction should start with a question because the audience may not know why the topic matters."

Paraphrase: "You are saying that a question at the beginning could help the audience understand the importance of the topic."

The paraphrase keeps the same idea but uses new words. It does not argue yet; it checks understanding first.

Notebook Directions and Model Example

In your notebook, copy the vocabulary and answer the prompts.

  1. What does it mean to paraphrase?
  2. Why should you paraphrase before disagreeing?
  3. Write your own paraphrase of the speaker example above.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

Step 1: Identify the communication skill, audience, and purpose before you begin the task.

Step 2: Locate the strongest detail, example, or speaking choice that connects to the skill.

Step 3: Explain how that detail affects the audience, speaker credibility, message clarity, or delivery.

Step 4: Revise your response so it uses precise communication vocabulary and answers the prompt directly.
