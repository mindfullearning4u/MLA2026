P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Active Listening and Discussion Response

In this lesson, you will learn how to listen actively, paraphrase a speaker's message, and respond in a way that keeps discussion respectful and productive.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.08 - Collaborative Communication.

Use appropriate collaborative techniques and active listening skills when engaging in discussions in a variety of situations.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.LST.05 supports paraphrasing content, and MLA.SPEECH.COM.09 supports appropriate voice and tone.

What You Will Learn

  • How active listening helps a discussion move forward.
  • How to paraphrase a speaker's idea before responding.
  • How voice and tone affect discussion responses.
  • How to disagree, clarify, and build on ideas respectfully.

What You Will Do

  • Study discussion examples that show strong and weak listening.
  • Practice paraphrasing a message in your own words.
  • Write responses that clarify, build, or respectfully disagree.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify active listening behaviors.
  • Paraphrase a speaker's idea accurately.
  • Choose discussion responses with appropriate tone.
  • Use discussion language that supports collaboration.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can listen carefully, restate what I heard, and respond in a way that helps a discussion continue.

This matters because strong speakers are also strong listeners. A discussion improves when people understand each other before they respond.
