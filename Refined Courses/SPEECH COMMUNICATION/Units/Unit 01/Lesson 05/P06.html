🗣 SPEECH | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Your work should show that you can listen, paraphrase, and respond in a discussion.

Part A

Explain active listening in your own words. Include two behaviors that show active listening during a discussion.

Part B

Speaker: "We should explain our claim before giving examples because the audience needs to know our point first."

Write an accurate paraphrase of the speaker's idea.

Part C

Write one respectful response that builds on the speaker's idea or respectfully disagrees with it. Include a reason.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help checking whether your paraphrase keeps the speaker's meaning.

Independent Work Planning Support

Lesson focus: Active listening and discussion response

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.COM.08

Use this checklist before completing Parts A, B, and C so your independent work stays aligned to the lesson standard.

  1. Listen for the speaker's main point before preparing your response. Active listening starts with understanding, not reacting.
  2. Use collaborative moves such as asking a clarifying question, building on an idea, summarizing a point, or respectfully disagreeing.
  3. Respond to the actual idea that was shared. Avoid changing the topic before the discussion goal is met.
  4. Check that your communication helps the group move forward and shows respect for the speaker and audience.

P06 independent work quality check: Before you submit, make sure Part A shows the basic skill, Part B applies the skill to a communication situation, and Part C explains your reasoning. A strong response does not only complete the task; it shows why your choices fit the audience, purpose, evidence, and communication goal.

  • Use complete, specific responses instead of one-word answers.
  • Connect each answer to the lesson focus and mapped standard.
  • Reread your work as if an audience, listener, or collaborator will use it.
