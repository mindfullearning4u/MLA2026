🗣 SPEECH | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 1 Synthesis

In this lesson, you will synthesize the Unit 1 communication skills and prepare for the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 1 Primary Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.08, MLA.SPEECH.COM.09, MLA.SPEECH.COM.10, MLA.SPEECH.COM.11, MLA.SPEECH.LST.06.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.05 and MLA.SPEECH.LST.05.

Lesson 8 introduces no new primary standard. It synthesizes the standards already taught in Unit 1.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 1 communication skills work together.
  • How to analyze a full communication situation.
  • How to prepare for the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review audience, purpose, tone, listening, academic language, and rhetoric.
  • Practice synthesis questions.
  • Complete the Unit 1 Guided Practice and Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Apply Unit 1 standards in integrated communication scenarios.
  • Explain communication choices using lesson vocabulary.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment with Moodle-ready questions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use all Unit 1 communication skills together to understand, respond, and speak with purpose.
