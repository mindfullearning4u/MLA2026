P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 1 Communication Synthesis Review

Vocabulary

  • Audience: the person or group receiving a message.
  • Purpose: the reason for communicating.
  • Active listening: listening to understand before responding.
  • Academic language: precise language used for learning and explaining.
  • Rhetoric: choices used to affect an audience.

Step 1: Read the Whole Communication Situation

Do not answer from one word alone. Identify the audience, purpose, speaker role, tone, and what the listener needs to understand.

Communication Example

Speech excerpt: "As your class representative, I have heard your concerns. If we practice twice this week, every group can improve timing before presentation day."

This uses trust through the speaker role and logic through the reason about practice and timing.

Notebook Directions and Model Example

In your notebook, identify the audience, purpose, tone, and appeal in the example.

Model answer: The audience is classmates, the purpose is to persuade them to practice, the tone is responsible, and the appeals are trust and logic.
