Submission Workflow

  1. Read the checkpoint task.
  2. Write a complete response.
  3. Check that your response addresses audience, purpose, tone, and listening.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Scenario: A classmate says, "We should use a funny opening for our speech about school safety."

Write a response that paraphrases the classmate's idea, chooses an audience-aware tone, and explains whether you agree or disagree with a reason.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response paraphrases the idea accurately.
  • Your tone is respectful and audience-aware.
  • Your reason connects to audience, purpose, or rhetorical choice.
  • Your response uses clear academic language.
