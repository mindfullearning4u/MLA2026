P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Audience-Aware Communication

In this lesson, you will combine audience awareness, voice, tone, active listening, academic language, and rhetoric so your communication choices work together.

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.09 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.08.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.10, MLA.SPEECH.COM.11, and MLA.SPEECH.LST.06.

You will use appropriate voice and tone while applying collaborative communication and active listening in audience-aware situations.

What You Will Learn

  • How audience and purpose guide communication choices.
  • How active listening improves group response.
  • How voice, tone, academic language, and rhetoric work together.
  • How to explain why a communication choice fits a situation.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze school communication scenarios.
  • Choose audience-aware words, tone, and listening responses.
  • Practice integrated communication decisions.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify audience, purpose, voice, and tone.
  • Use active listening and respectful discussion response.
  • Use academic language to explain communication choices.
  • Explain how a rhetorical choice supports a purpose.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can choose words, tone, listening responses, and rhetorical choices that fit my audience and purpose.
