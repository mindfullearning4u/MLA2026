P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continued notebook application: Explaining integrated choices.

Deeper Communication Thinking

A complete response should not only choose words. It should explain why those words fit. Use this frame: I chose ___ because the audience ___ and the purpose is ___.

Embedded Example

Scenario: Your group is speaking to younger students about hallway safety.

Response: "Let's use a friendly but firm tone because younger students need clear directions without feeling blamed."

This response connects tone to audience and purpose.

Notebook Directions

Write an integrated response for this scenario: A group member wants to use complicated academic vocabulary with a new audience. Explain what you would suggest and why.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: "Use big words so we sound smart."

This ignores the audience. Communication should help the audience understand, not impress them with confusing wording.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: "Use precise words and define any term the audience may not know."

This keeps academic language clear and audience-aware.

Deeper Learning Support

Lesson focus: Integrated audience-aware communication

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.COM.09, MLA.SPEECH.COM.08

Use this second-pass routine to check whether your thinking is specific, audience-aware, and connected to the lesson skill.

  1. Identify the audience before choosing words, tone, or examples. A strong speaker adapts to what listeners need and expect.
  2. State the purpose in one clear sentence so every communication choice supports that goal.
  3. Choose voice and tone deliberately. Ask whether the language sounds respectful, appropriate, and useful for the situation.
  4. Check that the final message fits the audience, purpose, and setting without becoming too casual, too vague, or too formal.
