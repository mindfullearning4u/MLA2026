P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help understanding the checkpoint task, choosing a respectful response, or clarifying what to submit.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the checkpoint task carefully.
  2. Write your response in complete sentences.
  3. Check that your response is respectful, specific, and helpful.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Scenario: You are in a group preparing a short class presentation. One group member says, "I do not know what I am supposed to do next."

Write a response you would say to the group member. Your response must:

  • Use respectful social communication.
  • Give or ask for clear instructional information.
  • Help the group move to the next step.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response is respectful and school-appropriate.
  • Your response names the task or next step clearly.
  • Your response helps the classmate or group understand what to do.
  • Your writing shows understanding of social and instructional communication.
