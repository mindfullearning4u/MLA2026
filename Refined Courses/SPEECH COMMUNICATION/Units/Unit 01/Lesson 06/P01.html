P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Rhetoric as a Communication Concept

In this lesson, you will learn how speakers use rhetoric to shape a message for an audience and purpose.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.LST.06 - Rhetoric in Text.

Explain an author's use of rhetoric in a text.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.09 supports voice and tone, and MLA.SPEECH.LST.03 supports purpose and rhetorical appeals.

What You Will Learn

  • What rhetoric means in speech communication.
  • How purpose, audience, voice, and tone shape a message.
  • How speakers use appeals to build trust, emotion, or logic.
  • How to explain a speaker's rhetorical choice in clear language.

What You Will Do

  • Study short communication scenarios and speech excerpts included in the lesson.
  • Identify purpose, audience, and tone.
  • Explain whether a speaker uses trust, emotion, or logic.
  • Complete Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify a speaker's purpose and audience.
  • Recognize rhetorical appeals in short speech examples.
  • Explain how tone supports a speaker's purpose.
  • Use evidence from the provided example to support your explanation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can explain how a speaker uses words, tone, and appeals to influence an audience.

This matters because every speaker makes choices. Understanding those choices helps you listen carefully and speak more intentionally.
