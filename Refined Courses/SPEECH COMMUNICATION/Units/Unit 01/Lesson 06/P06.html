P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use only the speech excerpt provided here.

Part A

Speech excerpt: "If every student brings one reusable bottle, our class can reduce trash every week and keep our campus cleaner."

Identify the speaker's purpose and audience.

Part B

Identify the rhetorical appeal in the excerpt. Explain which words show that appeal.

Part C

Explain how the speaker's tone supports the purpose. Use one detail from the excerpt.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help connecting the speaker's words to the rhetorical appeal.
