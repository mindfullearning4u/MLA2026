Teacher of Record Reminder

Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help identifying an appeal or explaining how it supports purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Read the checkpoint excerpt.
  2. Identify the rhetorical appeal.
  3. Explain how the appeal supports the speaker's purpose.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Speech excerpt: "As a member of the school safety team, I have seen how crowded hallways slow everyone down. If we walk on the right side, students can move faster and avoid bumping into each other."

Write a response that:

  • Identifies the speaker's purpose.
  • Identifies one rhetorical appeal.
  • Explains how a detail from the excerpt supports the purpose.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response identifies the speaker's purpose accurately.
  • Your response correctly identifies trust, emotion, or logic.
  • Your explanation uses a detail from the excerpt.
  • Your writing explains how the rhetorical choice affects the audience.
