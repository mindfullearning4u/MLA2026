P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Academic Language in Oral Communication

In this lesson, you will learn how to use precise academic language when speaking, discussing, explaining, and giving feedback.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.10 - Academic Language for ELLs.

English language learners communicate information, ideas, and concepts necessary for academic success in Language Arts.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.05 supports academic vocabulary, and MLA.SPEECH.COM.08 supports collaborative communication.

What You Will Learn

  • How academic language makes spoken ideas clearer and more specific.
  • How to replace vague words with precise communication vocabulary.
  • How to explain ideas using claim, reason, evidence, audience, purpose, and response.
  • How academic language supports discussion and collaboration.

What You Will Do

  • Study academic vocabulary used in speech communication.
  • Revise vague spoken responses into clear academic responses.
  • Practice explaining communication choices with precise vocabulary.
  • Complete notebook tasks, guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify academic vocabulary that fits oral communication.
  • Revise vague speech into clear academic language.
  • Explain why a word choice helps an audience understand.
  • Use academic language in your Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use precise academic words when I speak so my ideas are clear, focused, and easy to understand.

This matters because academic language helps a listener understand exactly what you mean instead of guessing what words like "stuff," "thing," or "good" are supposed to mean.
