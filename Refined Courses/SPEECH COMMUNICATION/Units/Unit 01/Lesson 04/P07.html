Checkpoint Task

Scenario: A classmate asks for feedback after practicing a short speech. The only feedback you wrote at first was: "It was good, but some parts were confusing."

Revise the feedback so it uses academic language. Your revised feedback must:

  • Name one specific part of the speech.
  • Use at least two academic vocabulary words from the lesson.
  • Explain one clear improvement the speaker can make.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your feedback replaces vague wording with precise academic language.
  • Your response names a speech section or communication skill.
  • Your explanation helps the speaker understand what to improve.
  • Your wording remains respectful and useful for collaboration.
