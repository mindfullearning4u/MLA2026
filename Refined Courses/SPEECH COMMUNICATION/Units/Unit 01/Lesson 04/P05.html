P05 Guided Practice

Complete the Guided Practice to check your understanding of academic language in oral communication.

Guided Practice Directions

Choose the answer that uses the clearest academic language for a speech communication situation.

  • Look for precise words instead of vague words.
  • Look for language that helps the audience understand.
  • Avoid answers that sound fancy but do not explain anything clearly.
