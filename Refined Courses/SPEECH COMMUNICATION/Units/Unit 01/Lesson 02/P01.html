P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Audience, Purpose, Voice, and Tone

In this lesson, you will learn how speakers adjust their words, voice, and tone based on who is listening and why the message is being delivered.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.09 - Voice and Tone.

Use appropriate voice and tone when speaking or writing.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.05 supports academic vocabulary, and MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01 supports organized oral presentation.

What You Will Learn

  • How audience and purpose shape communication choices.
  • How voice shows the speaker's personality and point of view.
  • How tone shows attitude toward the topic and audience.
  • How to revise a message so it fits a communication situation.

What You Will Do

  • Study audience, purpose, voice, and tone.
  • Analyze communication scenarios and speaker choices.
  • Revise messages to fit different audiences.
  • Prepare a checkpoint that explains your tone and audience choices.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify audience and purpose in a communication situation.
  • Choose voice and tone that fit the audience.
  • Explain why a communication choice is appropriate or inappropriate.
  • Demonstrate mastery through Guided Practice, Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can choose words, voice, and tone that fit my audience, purpose, and speaking situation.

This matters because the same message may need to sound different when you speak to classmates, a teacher, a family member, or a larger audience.
