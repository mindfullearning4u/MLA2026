P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notes about audience, purpose, voice, and tone.
  2. Write your checkpoint response in complete sentences.
  3. Check that your message matches the assigned audience.
  4. Submit your checkpoint in the assigned Moodle location.

Checkpoint Task

Situation: You are giving a short speech to classmates about why students should prepare before recording presentations.

Your task: Write a 5 to 7 sentence opening for the speech. Your opening must fit the audience, explain the purpose, and use an encouraging but responsible tone.

After the opening, write 2 to 3 sentences explaining how your voice and tone fit the audience and purpose.

Mastery Criteria

  • The opening clearly fits classmates as the audience.
  • The purpose of the message is clear.
  • The tone is encouraging, respectful, and responsible.
  • The explanation names at least one tone or voice choice and explains why it fits.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help before submitting your checkpoint.

Checkpoint Mastery Support

Lesson focus: Audience, purpose, voice, and tone

Mapped standard focus: MLA.SPEECH.COM.09

Use this checklist before submitting your checkpoint. Your work should show both the communication choice and the reason it meets the lesson goal.

  1. Identify the audience before choosing words, tone, or examples. A strong speaker adapts to what listeners need and expect.
  2. State the purpose in one clear sentence so every communication choice supports that goal.
  3. Choose voice and tone deliberately. Ask whether the language sounds respectful, appropriate, and useful for the situation.
  4. Check that the final message fits the audience, purpose, and setting without becoming too casual, too vague, or too formal.

P07 checkpoint quality check: Your checkpoint should be clear enough for your Teacher of Record to see what communication skill you practiced, what choice you made, and why that choice meets the lesson goal.
