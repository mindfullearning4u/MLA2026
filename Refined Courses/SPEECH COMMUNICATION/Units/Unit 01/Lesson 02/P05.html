P05 Guided Practice

Complete the Guided Practice questions in Moodle. The questions match the GIFT file for this lesson.

Before You Begin

Read the audience and purpose in each question. Then choose the message with the most appropriate voice and tone.

The Guided Practice focuses on MLA.SPEECH.COM.09 with support from MLA.SPEECH.COM.05 and MLA.SPEECH.SPK.01.

How to Think Through Each Question

  1. Identify the audience.
  2. Identify the purpose.
  3. Decide whether the situation needs a formal, friendly, serious, or persuasive tone.
  4. Choose the answer that best fits the situation without sounding rude, vague, or too casual.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help recognizing voice or tone in a message.
