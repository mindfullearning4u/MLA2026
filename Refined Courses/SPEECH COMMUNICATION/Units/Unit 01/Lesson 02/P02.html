P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Matching Message to Audience and Purpose

Vocabulary

  • Audience: the person or group receiving the message.
  • Purpose: the reason for communicating, such as to inform, explain, persuade, or request.
  • Voice: the speaker's style or personality in the message.
  • Tone: the attitude the message communicates.
  • Formal language: careful, respectful language used in academic or professional situations.
  • Informal language: casual language used with familiar audiences when appropriate.

Step 1: Identify Audience and Purpose First

Before deciding how to say something, identify who will hear it and why you are saying it. A message to a close friend can be casual. A message to a principal, employer, or public audience usually needs a more formal and respectful tone.

Purpose also matters. If your purpose is to inform, your tone should usually be clear and neutral. If your purpose is to persuade, your tone may be confident and respectful. If your purpose is to apologize, your tone should be sincere and responsible.

Thinking rule: Audience plus purpose should guide voice and tone.

Step 2: Choose Voice and Tone

  1. Name the audience.
  2. Name the purpose.
  3. Choose a voice that fits the situation, such as respectful, confident, friendly, or professional.
  4. Choose a tone that supports the purpose, such as serious, encouraging, calm, or persuasive.
  5. Check whether the message could be misunderstood as rude, careless, or too casual.

Communication Example

Situation: You need to ask your Teacher of Record for extra time because your internet stopped working.

Weak message: "My internet broke. I need more time."

Stronger message: "Hello, I had an internet problem while completing the assignment. May I please have extra time to submit it? I can finish it by tomorrow afternoon."

Notebook Directions and Model Example

In your notebook, copy the vocabulary and answer the prompts.

  1. What is the audience in the example above?
  2. What is the purpose?
  3. What tone does the stronger message use?
  4. Why is the stronger message more appropriate?

Model answer: The audience is the Teacher of Record. The purpose is to request extra time. The tone is respectful and responsible.
