P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Reminder

This checkpoint is reviewed by your Teacher of Record. Ask for help before submitting if you are unsure how to make your response respectful, specific, or collaborative.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notes from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Write your checkpoint response in complete sentences.
  3. Check that your response includes listening, collaboration, and a clear reason.
  4. Submit your checkpoint in the assigned Moodle location.

Checkpoint Task

Scenario: Your group is planning a short speech. One group member says, "I think we should start with a question for the audience." Another group member says, "No, we should just start with facts." The group is stuck.

Your task: Write a response you could say to help the group collaborate. Your response must show active listening, respect both ideas, and suggest a next step.

Length: Write 4 to 6 complete sentences.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response shows active listening by naming or paraphrasing the ideas.
  • Your tone is respectful and does not attack either person.
  • Your response suggests a clear next step for the group.
  • Your writing is clear enough for your Teacher of Record to understand your communication choice.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help before submitting your checkpoint.
