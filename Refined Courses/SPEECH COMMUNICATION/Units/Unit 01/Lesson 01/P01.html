P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Speech Communication Course Foundations

In this lesson, you will learn how strong communicators participate in discussions, listen actively, and respond in ways that help a group move forward.

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.SPEECH.COM.08 - Collaborative Communication.

Use appropriate collaborative techniques and active listening skills when engaging in discussions in a variety of situations.

Support Standards: MLA.SPEECH.COM.10 and MLA.SPEECH.COM.11 support academic, social, and instructional communication.

What You Will Learn

  • What collaboration means in a speech communication course.
  • How active listening is different from simply being quiet.
  • How to respond to another speaker with respect, evidence, and purpose.
  • How communication choices can help or block a discussion.

What You Will Do

  • Study the parts of active listening and collaborative discussion.
  • Analyze short classroom communication scenarios.
  • Practice choosing responses that build discussion instead of shutting it down.
  • Prepare a checkpoint response showing how you would communicate in a group.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks that explain active listening and collaboration.
  • Apply the lesson to communication scenarios in Guided Practice and Independent Work.
  • Submit a checkpoint that shows a clear, respectful, collaborative response.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can listen carefully, take turns, build on what others say, and communicate in a way that helps a discussion work.

This standard matters because speech communication is not only about speaking. It is also about listening, responding, and helping a group understand an idea more clearly.
