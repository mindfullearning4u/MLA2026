🗣 SPEECH | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete thoughts. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to see whether you understand active listening and collaborative communication.

Part A: Identify the Skill

Scenario: Sam says, "I think our group should explain the topic before giving examples." Lee replies, "You want the audience to understand the main idea first. That makes sense."

Write the active listening skill Lee uses. Explain how you know.

Part B: Improve the Response

Weak response: "That idea is boring."

Rewrite the response so it respectfully disagrees and gives a reason connected to the group's speech.

Part C: Apply Collaboration

Write a three-sentence plan for how you will communicate in a group discussion. Include how you will listen, how you will respond, and how you will invite others to participate.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help revising a weak response or explaining your communication plan.
