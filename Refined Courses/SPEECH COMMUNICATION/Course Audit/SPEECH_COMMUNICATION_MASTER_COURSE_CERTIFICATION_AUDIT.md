## 3. Instructional Quality Review

Result: PASS

The course provides a coherent Speech Communication learning experience across communication foundations, audience awareness, listening, collaboration, discussion, speaking, presentation design, research-supported speaking, digital communication, and final speech readiness. Lesson objectives are clear on P01 pages, standards are placed in the lesson overview, vocabulary appears in notebook tasks, worked examples support the lesson objective, and common mistakes address realistic student misconceptions.

The course is appropriate for a mastery-based adaptive communication course and supports independent online learning through consistent lesson page structure: overview, notebook development, worked example, guided practice, independent work, and checkpoint.

Lessons are written as direct student-facing instruction for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners. Course identity remains Speech Communication and does not drift into English Literature, Creative Writing, Journalism, or Math for College Readiness.

## 5. Instructional Progression Review

Result: PASS

The approved Speech Communication mapping creates a logical progression:

Communication foundations and audience awareness -> listening and collaboration -> discussion and interaction -> presentation planning -> research-supported speaking -> digital and final presentation readiness.

Prerequisites are respected across units. Students build foundational communication vocabulary before analyzing speakers and audiences, practice listening before collaboration, develop discussion skills before presentation delivery, and use research and digital communication skills after the required speaking and audience-awareness foundations have been introduced.
