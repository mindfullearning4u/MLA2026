## Findings

Initial rigorous unit audit finding:

Several Guided Practice and Lesson Quiz files reused identical answer-key sequences across the unit. Although the files were Moodle-safe and the answer distributions were balanced, the repeated answer sequences created a predictability risk.

## Corrections Applied

Answer positions were rebalanced across Unit 1 Guided Practice and Quiz files while preserving:

- question text
- answer choice text
- correct-answer meaning
- feedback
- MLA standards
- Moodle-safe syntax
- required question counts

The Unit Assessment and Pretest answer sequences were previously corrected during the Lesson 7-8 and Pretest production audit and were re-verified in this unit audit.
