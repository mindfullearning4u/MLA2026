## Corrections Made During Audit

The audit identified GIFT marker issues where several correct answer choices were initially marked with the incorrect-choice symbol. A mechanical correction was applied to align `#Correct` feedback with `=` markers.

The audit also identified short feedback and uneven answer-key distribution. Feedback was expanded to meet detail requirements, and answer placement was normalized to avoid pattern clustering.

A second audit pass confirmed all files, pages, JSON metadata, GIFT counts, answer choices, feedback, visual markers, and scope controls passed.
