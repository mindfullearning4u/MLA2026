The audit identified GIFT syntax issues where several correct C-answer choices were initially marked with the incorrect-choice symbol. These were corrected by changing the GIFT markers only. A second audit pass confirmed that all GIFT items now contain exactly one correct answer and valid feedback labels.

Lesson 2 quiz answer distribution was also rebalanced to avoid answer-key clustering.
