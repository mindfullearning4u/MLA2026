## Corrections Made During Audit

The audit identified GIFT syntax issues where several correct answer choices were initially marked with the incorrect-choice symbol. These were corrected by changing GIFT markers only. Short feedback items were expanded, and answer-key distribution was rebalanced to avoid clustering.

A second audit pass confirmed all files, pages, JSON metadata, GIFT counts, answer choices, feedback, visual markers, and scope controls passed.
