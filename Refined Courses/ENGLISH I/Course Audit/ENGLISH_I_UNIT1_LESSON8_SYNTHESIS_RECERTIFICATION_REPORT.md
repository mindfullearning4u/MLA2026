# English I Unit 1 Lesson 8 Synthesis Recertification Report

## Executive Summary

English I Unit 1 Lesson 8 was rebuilt under the authorized override for synthesis reconstruction. The certified Standards Inventory, Crosswalk, Unit Mapping, and Lesson Mapping were preserved. No new standards, concepts, skills, or vocabulary expectations were introduced.

The rebuilt Lesson 8 now functions as a true synthesis lesson. Students must combine and apply Unit 1 learning from Lessons 1-7 by reading, analyzing, paraphrasing, selecting evidence, using academic vocabulary, explaining reasoning, checking conventions, and preparing for the Unit Assessment.

## Findings Corrected

The previous version of Unit 1 Lesson 8 functioned primarily as a review/checklist lesson. The rebuild corrected this by:

- Replacing isolated vocabulary review with integrated vocabulary use inside passage-based tasks.
- Replacing isolated paraphrase/evidence checks with complete synthesis responses.
- Replacing a partial worked review with a full model of reading, analysis, evidence, vocabulary, response construction, and convention check.
- Replacing generic readiness work with approved P06 Independent Work that mirrors the ALG1 structure while using English I Unit 1 synthesis content.
- Replacing the assessment-launch workflow with the approved P07 Checkpoint workflow used across the academy.
