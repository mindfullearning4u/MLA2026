This certification was rerun at the deeper content-alignment level now required for ELA production review. The review covered English I Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7 only, using the certified curriculum foundation as the source of truth:

**PASS - STRUCTURE AND CONTENT ALIGN TO CERTIFIED LESSON MAPPING**

## Corrections Made During This Deep Content Pass

To strengthen independent learner support, mapped step-by-step instructional support boxes were added to all English I P02 and P03 pages for Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7.

Each added support box:

- Names the mapped lesson focus.
- Displays the mapped standard focus.
- Provides a step-by-step thinking routine tied to the lesson skill.
- Supports remedial learners by making the process explicit.
- Supports standard learners by clarifying the expected sequence of work.
- Supports accelerated learners by emphasizing quality, accuracy, and self-checking.
- Preserves existing lesson content, examples, notebook tasks, independent work, checkpoint tasks, assessments, and visual shell.

No lesson standards were changed.

No lesson mappings were changed.

No assessment files were changed during this English I deep content pass.
