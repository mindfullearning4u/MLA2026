# English I Unit 1 Full Unit Production Audit

## Executive Summary

Audit Date: 2026-06-19  
Course: English I  
Unit: Unit 01  
Unit Title: Foundations of Close Reading and Evidence-Based Writing  
Audit Scope: Lessons 1-8, Unit Pretest, Guided Practice files, Lesson Quiz files, Unit Assessment, HTML pages, JSON metadata, standards alignment, layout, visual consistency, and assessment integrity.

English I Unit 1 was audited against the approved English I lesson-level mapping, Unit 1 production requirements, MLA mastery model, and the Algebra I visual production model. The unit is structurally complete, standards-aligned, Moodle-ready, and appropriate for self-paced online learning.

Corrections were required and completed for assessment answer-key balance and pretest metadata alignment. After correction and revalidation, Unit 1 is approved to close before Unit 2 production begins.

Final Decision: PASS
