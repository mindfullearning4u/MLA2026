## Workload and Pacing Findings

Unit 4 remains a **moderate-high workload** unit because research, source evaluation, expository writing, and publication are all multi-step skills. The finished lesson sequence controls that risk by keeping the unit as one connected research-to-publication process rather than separate large products.

No overloaded individual lesson was identified after review. Lesson 8 appropriately functions as synthesis and assessment rather than new instruction.
