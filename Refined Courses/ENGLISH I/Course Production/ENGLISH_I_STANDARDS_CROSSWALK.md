# English I Standards Crosswalk Review

## Review finding

The repository contains 24 MLA English I standards aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Grade 9 ELA benchmarks and cross-referenced to Common Core, SAT, and ACT skill families. The Florida codes and wording below were independently checked against the official Florida Department of Education B.E.S.T. ELA standards PDF. All 24 MLA-to-Florida code relationships are valid. The required change is **instructional placement and evidence classification**, not renumbering.

## Alignment cautions

- Florida B.E.S.T. remains the controlling external framework; Common Core, SAT, and ACT are cross-references, not replacement standards.
- SAT/ACT domain alignment indicates skill relevance but does not mean a lesson directly reproduces test items.
- A standard listed in a lesson is marked **mastered** only when the student produces assessable evidence at the required complexity.
- Final authoritative publication requires academic-review acceptance of the independently verified benchmark wording and CPALMS course applicability.
