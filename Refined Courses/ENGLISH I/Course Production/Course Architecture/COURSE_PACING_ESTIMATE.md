Pacing is an estimate for a mastery-based course, not a seat-time rule.

| Unit | Lessons | Estimated instructional hours | Relative difficulty | Review/remediation placement |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 Literary and Informational Foundations | 8 existing | Existing course pacing | Complete | Out of scope; no changes required |
| 2 Inside the Nightmare | 9 | 26-36 | Moderate-high | Cluster reviews after Lessons 4 and 8; synthesis/assessment in Lesson 9 |

| **Units 2-6 production scope** | **51** | **158-216** | Savvas-aligned production scope | Mastery time varies by learner |

Each Units 2-6 estimate includes a pretest, appropriate clustered quizzes, and the unit assessment embedded in the final synthesis lesson.

## Typical lesson estimate

- Lesson overview/objectives: 10-15 minutes
- Vocabulary/source orientation: 20-40 minutes
- Core reading/viewing: 45-150 minutes depending on text length
- Guided analysis/modeling: 30-60 minutes
- Independent application/evidence: 30-90 minutes
- Checkpoint and revision: 20-45 minutes
- Help/TOR support: as needed

Extended-text lessons may span multiple work sessions. Students needing accessible text, language support, rereading, or revision should receive additional time without being treated as off pace.
