## Purpose of Unit 1

Unit 1 provides the prerequisite close-reading, paraphrase, vocabulary, evidence, literary/informational response, conventions, and communication routines required for success in the five Savvas units. Its lessons, content, pretest, assessments, and final synthesis/unit assessment are already complete. No Unit 1 work is required or authorized by this proposal.

## Major changes from the current repository

1. Preserve completed Unit 1 without modification.
2. Replace skill-domain Units 2-6 at the map level with the five Savvas thematic units.
3. Replace the fixed eight-lesson expectation with content-based unit sizes.
4. Embed vocabulary, conventions, revision, research, and communication across thematic units.
5. Protect extended texts with multi-session lesson clusters.
6. Use Savvas assessments as mapped sources without transferring questions during architecture.
7. Require each Unit 2-6 sequence to begin with a pretest, include clustered quizzes where instructionally appropriate, and end with a synthesis lesson containing the unit assessment.
