# Current vs. Proposed English I Map

## Summary

The current architecture is a six-unit **skill-domain course**. The proposed architecture is a six-unit **foundation-plus-Savvas thematic course**. Unit 1 remains foundational; Units 2-6 are replaced at the map level by the five Savvas thematic units.

| Current unit | Current title | Proposed unit | Proposed title | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Literary and Informational Foundations | 1 | Literary and Informational Foundations | No change; complete and out of scope |
| 2 | Literary Analysis and Narrative Craft | 2 | Inside the Nightmare | Replace skill-domain grouping with Savvas Unit 1 theme; distribute literary standards within authentic selections |
| 3 | Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument | 3 | Outsiders and Outcasts | Replace skill-domain grouping with Savvas Unit 2 theme; embed rhetoric/argument in unit texts and tasks |
| 4 | Research and Expository Writing | 4 | Crazy, Stupid Love | Replace skill-domain grouping with Savvas Unit 3 drama sequence; research/expository skills spiral elsewhere |
| 5 | Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision | 5 | Journeys of Transformation | Replace isolated language unit; language/revision become recurring supports within Savvas lessons |
| 6 | Synthesis, Presentation, and Digital Communication | 6 | World's End | Replace generic culmination; presentation and synthesis occur through Savvas Unit 5 tasks |

## Major pedagogical changes

1. Standards are taught through coherent texts and unit questions rather than grouped only by code/domain.
2. Language, vocabulary, conventions, and revision spiral through every unit instead of waiting for Unit 5.
3. Presentation and digital communication occur in multiple Savvas performance tasks instead of first appearing in Unit 6.
4. Research is concentrated in *Journeys of Transformation* but supported by informational-source work before and after it.
5. Extended works receive protected lesson sequences.
6. Units use variable lesson counts rather than a forced eight-lesson template.

## Materials moved or recontextualized

- Existing Unit 2 literary-analysis content becomes standards support across Savvas Units 2-6.
- Existing Unit 3 rhetoric and argument content is applied especially in Outsiders and Outcasts, Crazy Stupid Love criticism, and World's End media analysis.
- Existing Unit 4 research instruction supports Journeys of Transformation's research report.
- Existing Unit 5 language/revision content becomes embedded mini-instruction and Help/TOR support across all thematic units.
- Existing Unit 6 presentation instruction supports every oral, multimedia, advertisement, instructions, and poster task.

## Assessment impact

Existing skill-domain quizzes and unit assessments cannot automatically remain authoritative after the map changes. They must be audited against the proposed lesson sequence. Savvas assessments are mapped as sources, not copied. Existing production assessment files remain untouched pending approval.

## Required human decisions

- Approve the six-unit titles and lesson counts.
- Approve replacement of the skill-domain map after crosswalk review.
- Confirm that Unit 1 remains excluded from all replacement work.
- Approve any retirement or archival treatment of obsolete production lessons only after the replacement blueprint is accepted.
