## Traceability rule

Every production lesson must retain the MLA unit/lesson identifier, exact Savvas unit and selection, resource type, standards status (introduced/practiced/mastered/supporting), and assessment source. `LESSON_BLUEPRINT.md` is the controlling traceability document for lesson production.
