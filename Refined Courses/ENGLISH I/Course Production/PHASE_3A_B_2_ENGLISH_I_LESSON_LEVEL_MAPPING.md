# Phase 3A-B-2: English I Lesson-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English I lesson-level mapping using the approved English I Crosswalk, Unit-Level Mapping, Mapping Framework, Blueprint, MLA English Instructional Design Standards, and Phase 3B Unit-Level Mapping Validation Report.

This is mapping only. It does not create lesson objectives, lesson content, activities, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, or a Course Overview.

The mapping preserves the approved course structure:

- 6 units
- 8 lessons per unit
- Lessons 1-7 contain Lesson Quiz
- Lesson 8 = Putting It All Together
- Lesson 8 contains Unit Assessment

The mapping applies the required Phase 3B controls:

- Unit 2 prevents literary-analysis overload through natural sequencing and focused literary pairings.
- Unit 3 places informational reading and rhetoric before argument writing.
- Unit 4 treats research, expository writing, and collaborative publication as one connected source-based communication process.
- Communication reinforcement appears before Unit 6.
- Lesson 8 is not used as a catch-all for untaught standards.

## Spiral Reinforcement Summary

| Spiral Skill | Early Course Use | Mid-Course Use | Late Course Use | Increasing Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Language | Unit 1 short-response conventions | Units 2-4 conventions in analysis, argument, and research writing | Units 5-6 revision, style, and presentation polish | Moves from sentence clarity to authentic style and publication polish. |
| Vocabulary | Unit 1 academic vocabulary | Units 2-4 literary, rhetorical, argument, and research vocabulary | Units 5-6 etymology, connotation, precision, and audience-specific language | Moves from access vocabulary to independent word analysis and precise communication. |
| Revision | Unit 1 response clarity | Units 2-4 narrative, argument, expository, and publication revision support | Units 5-6 focused revision and communication polish | Moves from simple clarity checks to cohesion, style, and audience-aware revision. |
| Paraphrase | Unit 1 direct paraphrase instruction | Units 2-4 paraphrase for literary, informational, and research work | Units 5-6 paraphrase used in synthesis and presentation support | Moves from accurate restatement to source synthesis and communication support. |
| Evidence | Unit 1 evidence foundations | Units 2-4 evidence for analysis, argument, source evaluation, and research | Unit 6 credible evidence for presentation | Moves from selecting evidence to evaluating and presenting evidence. |
| Discussion | Unit 1 evidence-based explanation | Units 2-5 interpretation, claims/evidence discussion, collaboration, and feedback | Unit 6 oral and digital communication | Moves from low-stakes explanation to coherent presentation with audience awareness. |

## Workload Summary

| Unit | Workload Finding | Mapping Control Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Manageable foundation load | Repeats paraphrase and academic vocabulary in varied contexts without overloading with advanced analysis. |
| Unit 2 | High workload risk | Gives epic poetry and adaptation protected mapping points; connects narrative writing to literary craft. |
| Unit 3 | High workload risk | Places informational reading and rhetoric before argument writing; splits argument writing across two mapping points. |
| Unit 4 | Moderate-high workload risk | Treats research, expository writing, and collaborative publication as one connected source-based process. |
| Unit 5 | Manageable with integration risk | Keeps language, vocabulary, and revision connected to authentic reading/writing contexts. |
| Unit 6 | Manageable if earlier reinforcement occurs | Uses prior discussion, research, writing, revision, and evidence skills to support final communication. |

Most instructional lessons contain one primary mastery standard. Two primary standards appear only when naturally paired:

- Unit 1 Lesson 7: paraphrase and academic vocabulary integration.
- Unit 4 Lesson 7: expository writing and collaborative publication.
- Unit 6 Lessons 6-7: oral and digital communication integration.

Lesson 8 in each unit synthesizes standards already taught in Lessons 1-7 and introduces no new primary standard.

## Recommendations for Lesson-Level Mapping Validation

Phase 3C should validate this lesson-level mapping before lesson development begins.

Recommended validation checks:

- Confirm that every approved MLA English I standard appears in the lesson-level mapping.
- Confirm that no lesson is overloaded with unrelated primary standards.
- Confirm that Unit 2 protects epic poetry, adaptation analysis, and narrative craft.
- Confirm that Unit 3 sequences informational reading and rhetoric before argument writing.
- Confirm that Unit 4 remains one connected research-to-expository-writing process.
- Confirm that communication reinforcement appears before Unit 6.
- Confirm that Lesson 8 is never used to introduce new primary standards.
- Confirm that support standards reinforce instruction without becoming hidden extra lessons.
