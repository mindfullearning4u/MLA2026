# English I Lesson Blueprint: Units 2-6

**Status:** Architecture validated; ready for the required human approval gate. Not yet authorized for production.
**Rule:** Unit Production must follow the assigned page pattern and may not invent a different sequence without an approved blueprint revision.

## Blueprint scope

This blueprint covers **Units 2-6 only**.

**Unit 1: Literary and Informational Foundations is entirely complete.** Its pretest, lessons, instructional content, notebook evidence, checkpoints, clustered assessments where applicable, final synthesis lesson, and unit assessment already exist and require no review, redesign, replacement, or production work under this blueprint.

## Production safeguards

- Do not open, revise, rebuild, remap, or replace Unit 1 through this blueprint.
- Exact Savvas click paths and expected screens must be inserted when each lesson is built.
- No teacher-only file may be presented as a student assignment.
- Every checkpoint must name the standard, source, evidence expectation, and rubric.
- Notebook evidence is required only when it supports the objective; it is not filler.
- Every lesson must contain `lesson.json`, `quiz.json`, and exactly seven pages, `P01`-`P07`, in accordance with the repository's locked `AGENTS.md` standard.
- Page patterns may be revised only through an approved update to this blueprint.
