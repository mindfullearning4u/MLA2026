Lesson 4: Expository Organization from Research Findings

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.R.05 - Analyze Text Structure; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to organize research findings into an expository explanation.
  • How to group related notes under clear subtopics.
  • How to choose transitions that show relationships between ideas.

Why This Matters

Expository writing explains information clearly. Research notes only become useful writing when they are organized in a logical order for the reader.

What You Will Do

  • Sort research findings into related groups.
  • Create a short expository plan from source notes.
  • Use transitions to show how ideas connect.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can group related findings by subtopic.
  • You can choose a logical organization for an explanatory response.
  • You can write a clear topic sentence that introduces research-based information.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if your notes feel like a list of disconnected facts or if you cannot decide which information should come first.
