P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can organize research findings for expository writing.

Checkpoint Task

Research question: How can a school garden support biology learning?

  • Students can observe plant growth over time.
  • Students can connect vocabulary to real examples.
  • Hands-on activities can make abstract concepts easier to understand.

In your notebook, write a topic sentence and a three-step plan for an expository paragraph.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your topic sentence introduces an explanation.
  • Your plan uses source findings logically.
  • Your transitions show how ideas connect.
  • Your writing is clear and organized.
