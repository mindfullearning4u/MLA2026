P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now decide the best order for research findings.

Research Notes

Question: How can quiet study spaces affect homework completion?

  • Some students reported fewer distractions in quiet rooms.
  • Students with quiet study access reported finishing homework earlier.
  • The media center was most used before school and after school.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the best first sentence for an expository paragraph.
  2. Put the three notes in a logical order.
  3. Add one transition word or phrase between two ideas.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Quiet rooms are good. Media center. Homework. Students.

This is a list of fragments, not organized expository writing.

Correct Thinking

Correct: Quiet study spaces can support homework completion by reducing distractions and giving students useful times to work.

This sentence introduces the explanation and connects the research findings.

Deeper Learning Support

Lesson focus: Expository organization from research findings

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG1.W.03

Use this second-pass routine to check the quality of your thinking before you submit your notebook work.

  1. Identify the purpose of the task before choosing information. A research or publication task must answer a real question for a real audience.
  2. Separate information into useful categories: what the source says, why it matters, and how it will help the final product.
  3. Check whether each piece of information is reliable, relevant, and clear enough for the audience. If it does not help the audience understand the topic, revise or replace it.
  4. Use your own words when explaining source information, and keep the meaning accurate so the final communication remains trustworthy.
