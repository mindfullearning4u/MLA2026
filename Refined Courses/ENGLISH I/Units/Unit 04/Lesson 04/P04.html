P04 Worked Example

Use research findings to plan an expository paragraph.

Example 1: Identify the Main Explanation

Research question: How can school gardens support biology learning?

Main explanation: School gardens can give students hands-on examples of biology concepts.

Example 2: Group Findings

Group 1: plant growth and soil observations.

Group 2: student participation and hands-on learning.

The groups help the writer avoid jumping randomly from fact to fact.

Example 3: Use a Transition

Sentence: In addition, garden activities can help students connect classroom vocabulary to real observations.

The transition "In addition" shows that the writer is adding a related point.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Gardens, biology, plants, dirt, students, activities.

This does not explain how the ideas connect.

Correct Approach

Correct: School gardens can support biology learning because students can observe plant growth and connect class vocabulary to real examples.

This sentence organizes the information into a clear explanation.
