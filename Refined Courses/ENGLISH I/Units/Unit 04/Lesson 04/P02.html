P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Organizing Research Findings

Vocabulary

  • Expository writing: writing that explains or analyzes information.
  • Finding: important information learned from a source.
  • Subtopic: a smaller category within a larger subject.
  • Transition: a word or phrase that shows how ideas connect.

Direct Teaching

After research, you may have several notes. Do not place them randomly into a paragraph. First, sort them by what they explain.

Research question: How can peer tutoring affect student confidence?

Possible subtopics: asking questions, revising work, preparing for quizzes.

Each subtopic can become a part of the explanation.

Notebook Directions

Research findings:

  • Students used tutoring to ask questions they avoided in class.
  • Students who attended tutoring revised more incorrect answers.
  • Students said tutoring helped them feel more prepared for quizzes.

Group these findings into two or three subtopics. Then write a topic sentence for an expository paragraph about peer tutoring and student confidence.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Read the vocabulary and make sure you can say each term in your own words.
  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: Work with one sentence, term, or example at a time. Standard path: complete the full task and explain your reasoning. Accelerated path: add a second example, compare choices, or explain why a weaker answer does not meet the standard.
