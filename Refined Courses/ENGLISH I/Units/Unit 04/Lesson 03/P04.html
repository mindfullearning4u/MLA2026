P04 Worked Example

Follow the steps from source excerpt to note, paraphrase, and synthesis.

Example 1: Take a Useful Note

Source: Students who attended tutoring twice a week reported feeling more prepared for quizzes.

Note: regular tutoring may increase quiz confidence.

Example 2: Paraphrase Accurately

Paraphrase: Students who used tutoring regularly said they felt better prepared for quizzes.

This keeps the meaning and changes the wording.

Example 3: Synthesize Sources

Source A finding: Tutoring attendance increased quiz confidence.

Source B finding: Students liked tutoring most when it focused on current class assignments.

Synthesis: Tutoring may be most helpful when students attend regularly and receive support with current assignments.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Students who attended tutoring twice a week reported feeling more prepared for quizzes.

This copies the source sentence instead of creating a note or paraphrase.

Correct Approach

Correct: Students who used tutoring often said they felt more ready for quizzes.

This paraphrase preserves the source meaning in new wording.
