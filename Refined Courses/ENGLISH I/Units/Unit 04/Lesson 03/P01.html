Lesson 3: Note-Taking, Paraphrase, and Source Synthesis

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to take notes without copying whole source sentences.
  • How to paraphrase source information accurately.
  • How to combine related findings from more than one source.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Research is not copying. Strong researchers collect information, restate it accurately, and notice how sources connect. These skills prepare you to write from sources in the next lesson.

What You Will Do

  • Read short source excerpts included in the lesson.
  • Separate key information from minor details.
  • Write paraphrased notes and combine related findings.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can record useful notes from a source.
  • You can paraphrase without changing the source meaning.
  • You can synthesize two related source findings in one clear sentence.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you cannot tell what information is important or if your paraphrase sounds too close to the source wording.
