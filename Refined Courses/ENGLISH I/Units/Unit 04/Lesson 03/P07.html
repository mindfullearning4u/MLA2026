P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can take notes, paraphrase, and synthesize source findings.

Checkpoint Task

Source A: A media center sign-in report showed that the room was most used before school.

Source B: A student survey found that many students wanted a quiet place to finish work before first period.

In your notebook, write one note from each source, one paraphrase, and one synthesis sentence.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If you are unsure what to submit, ask your TOR before submitting the checkpoint.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your notes are short and useful.
  • Your paraphrase keeps the source meaning.
  • Your synthesis sentence connects the two sources.
  • Your writing uses clear standard English.
