P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now revise tone and cohesion.

Draft Sentences

Sentence 1: This source says tutoring is really, really helpful and stuff.

Sentence 2: Students who used tutoring reported stronger quiz confidence.

Sentence 3: Students also revised more incorrect work after attending tutoring.

Notebook Directions

  1. Revise Sentence 1 into academic tone.
  2. Add a transition between Sentence 2 and Sentence 3.
  3. Write one sentence explaining how the revised paragraph is clearer.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source is super good and proves a bunch of stuff.

This tone is informal and vague. It does not explain the source finding clearly.

Correct Thinking

Correct: The survey finding suggests that tutoring may support student confidence and revision habits.

This tone is formal, specific, and connected to research.
