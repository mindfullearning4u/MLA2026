P04 Worked Example

Watch how source information becomes clear expository writing.

Example 1: Introduce the Source

Finding: Students with online calendars missed fewer due dates.

Integrated sentence: A student survey found that online calendars helped students miss fewer due dates.

Example 2: Add a Transition

Sentence: In addition, online calendars gave students a place to check assignments after leaving class.

The transition shows that the writer is adding a related point.

Example 3: Match Tone to Task

Informal: Calendars are awesome because students forget stuff.

Academic: Online calendars may support students by making assignment information easier to review outside class.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The research says calendars are cool and students like them.

This is vague and informal. It does not clearly integrate a specific finding.

Correct Approach

Correct: A student survey found that online calendars helped students miss fewer assignment deadlines.

This sentence clearly introduces the source finding in academic tone.
