Lesson 5: Source Integration, Transitions, and Task-Appropriate Tone

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to introduce source information clearly.
  • How transitions connect findings in an expository paragraph.
  • How to keep a task-appropriate academic tone.

Why This Matters

A source-based paragraph should not feel like copied facts dropped into random places. Readers need to know where information comes from, how ideas connect, and why the tone fits the task.

What You Will Do

  • Practice introducing source findings.
  • Choose transitions that show addition, contrast, cause, and result.
  • Revise informal or unclear sentences into academic expository tone.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can integrate a source finding into your own sentence.
  • You can choose a transition that matches the relationship between ideas.
  • You can revise tone so it is clear, formal, and task-appropriate.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if your paragraph sounds like a list of copied facts or if you are unsure which transition fits between ideas.
