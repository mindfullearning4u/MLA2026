P06 Independent Work

Practice integrating findings into a clear expository paragraph.

Instructions

Research finding: Students who used online assignment calendars missed fewer due dates.

  1. Introduce the finding with a signal phrase.
  2. Add a second sentence using a transition.
  3. Revise both sentences for academic tone.

Independent Work Parts

Part A: Identify the skill, passage detail, or writing move the task is asking you to use.

Part B: Complete the assigned response using evidence from the lesson or passage.

Part C: Check your work for clarity, complete sentences, and a direct explanation of your reasoning.
