P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Integrating Sources with Transitions and Tone

Vocabulary

  • Source integration: placing source information into your own writing clearly.
  • Signal phrase: a phrase that introduces source information.
  • Transition: a word or phrase that shows how ideas connect.
  • Academic tone: clear, respectful, formal language that fits school writing.

Direct Teaching

A source finding should be introduced before it appears in your paragraph. A signal phrase tells the reader that the information comes from research.

Weak: Students finished homework earlier.

Stronger: A student survey found that students with quiet study access finished homework earlier.

The stronger sentence shows that the idea comes from a source.

Notebook Directions

Research finding: Students with quiet study access reported fewer homework distractions.

  1. Write one sentence that introduces this finding with a signal phrase.
  2. Write one transition sentence that connects this finding to another related finding.
  3. Revise your sentence so the tone is formal and clear.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Read the vocabulary and make sure you can say each term in your own words.
  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: Work with one sentence, term, or example at a time. Standard path: complete the full task and explain your reasoning. Accelerated path: add a second example, compare choices, or explain why a weaker answer does not meet the standard.
