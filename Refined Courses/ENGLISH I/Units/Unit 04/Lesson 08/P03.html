P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Review organization, source integration, and publication readiness.

Draft Product Note

A student-facing summary should explain that quiet study spaces may reduce distractions and help some students finish homework earlier. The product needs clear headings, accurate source language, and a formal tone.

  1. Write one audience-friendly heading.
  2. Write one source-integrated sentence.
  3. Write one publication-readiness check.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Quiet rooms always fix homework for everyone.

This exaggerates the source findings and is not publication-ready.

Correct Thinking

Correct: Source findings suggest quiet study spaces may help some students reduce distractions and complete homework earlier.

This keeps the wording accurate and evidence-based.
