P04 Worked Example

This example combines Unit 4 skills.

Example 1: Research Question

Question: How can digital announcements affect student awareness of club deadlines?

This question is focused, researchable, and connected to a possible publication audience.

Example 2: Source Synthesis

Source A: Students often miss hallway flyers.

Source B: Digital announcements can be updated quickly.

Synthesis: Digital announcements may improve student awareness because they can be updated quickly and may reach students who miss flyers.

Example 3: Publication Choice

Audience: students.

Product: a one-page digital summary with clear headings, brief evidence, and a recommendation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Make a thing for people about announcements.

This is too vague to guide source-based publication.

Correct Approach

Correct: Create a one-page student summary explaining how digital announcements can help students notice club deadlines.

This names audience, format, topic, and purpose.
