Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 4 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 4 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts; MLA.ENG1.C.03 - Collaborate and Publish Using Digital Tools.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Collaborate and Communicate Academic Ideas.

What You Will Learn

You will review and combine the research, expository writing, and publication skills from Lessons 1-7. No new primary standard is introduced in this lesson.

Why This Matters

The full Unit 4 process moves from a research question to reliable sources, notes, synthesis, expository organization, source integration, collaboration, and publication readiness.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can evaluate a research question and source quality.
  • You can paraphrase and synthesize source findings.
  • You can organize and revise source-based expository communication.
  • You can identify publication choices that fit audience and purpose.
