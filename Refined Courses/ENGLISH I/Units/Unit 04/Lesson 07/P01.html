Lesson 7: Publication-Ready Source-Based Communication

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts; MLA.ENG1.C.03 - Collaborate and Publish Using Digital Tools.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to prepare source-based writing for publication.
  • How to check research accuracy, organization, tone, and audience fit.
  • How expository writing and digital publication work together.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

A finished research product must be accurate, readable, and useful for its audience. Publication-ready work is more than a draft; it is revised communication prepared for readers.

What You Will Do

  • Review a source-based publication plan.
  • Identify what must be revised before publication.
  • Practice using a publication-readiness checklist.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify whether source-based writing is ready for an audience.
  • You can explain what revision would improve publication quality.
  • You can keep source accuracy and audience needs connected.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you cannot tell whether a source-based draft is accurate, organized, or ready for its audience.
