Lesson 6: Collaborative Publication and Audience Awareness

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.C.03 - Collaborate and Publish Using Digital Tools.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Collaborate and Communicate Academic Ideas.

What You Will Learn

  • How audience affects publication choices.
  • How collaborative roles support a shared source-based product.
  • How to revise communication for clarity before publication.

Why This Matters

Research writing often becomes something shared with an audience. A publication-ready product needs accurate information, clear organization, and communication choices that match the people who will read it.

What You Will Do

  • Identify audience needs for a research-based publication.
  • Choose collaborative roles for a shared product.
  • Revise a short publication plan for clarity and audience awareness.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can match publication choices to audience and purpose.
  • You can explain how team roles support collaboration.
  • You can improve a publication plan for clarity.
