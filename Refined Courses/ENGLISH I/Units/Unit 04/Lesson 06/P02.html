P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Audience and Collaborative Publication

Vocabulary

  • Audience: the people who will read, view, or use the published product.
  • Purpose: the reason the product is being created.
  • Collaborative role: a responsibility a team member has during shared work.
  • Publication: a finished product prepared to share with an audience.

Direct Teaching

Audience changes communication choices. A research summary for ninth-grade students may need short sections, clear headings, and simple explanations. A research summary for school leaders may need concise evidence and specific recommendations.

Collaboration also needs clear roles. A team might need a source checker, writer, editor, and design reviewer.

Notebook Directions

Publication task: Create a one-page research summary about how quiet study spaces can help students complete homework.

  1. Identify the audience.
  2. Write the purpose of the publication.
  3. List three collaborative roles that would help complete the product.
