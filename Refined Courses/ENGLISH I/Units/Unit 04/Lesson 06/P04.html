P04 Worked Example

Plan a publication product with audience and collaboration in mind.

Example 1: Identify Audience

Task: Share research about digital announcements with students.

Audience: students who need clear, quick updates about clubs and deadlines.

Example 2: Match Product to Audience

Product choice: a one-page digital summary with headings, brief evidence, and a clear recommendation.

This fits students because it is easy to scan.

Example 3: Assign Roles

Roles: source checker, writer, editor, layout reviewer.

Each role supports the shared publication goal.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Make something for people and put stuff in it.

This does not identify audience, purpose, product, or roles.

Correct Approach

Correct: Create a one-page digital summary for students that explains why digital announcements can help them notice club deadlines.

This plan identifies audience, format, purpose, and content focus.
