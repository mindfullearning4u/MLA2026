P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now revise a publication plan for audience awareness.

Draft Plan

We will make a page about tutoring. It will have some research and some words. Everyone will do whatever part they want.

Notebook Directions

  1. Identify what is unclear in the draft plan.
  2. Revise the plan so it names the audience, purpose, product, and team roles.
  3. Explain how your revision improves collaboration.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Everyone can just work on whatever.

This does not support collaboration because roles and responsibilities are unclear.

Correct Thinking

Correct: One student will check source accuracy, one will draft the explanation, and one will revise headings and clarity for a student audience.

This plan assigns roles that match the publication goal.
