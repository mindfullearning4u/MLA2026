P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now evaluate short source descriptions.

Source Descriptions

Source A: A school district report from this year explains how many students borrowed laptops and how often they used them for homework.

Source B: A personal blog post says, "School laptops are the worst idea ever, and no reasonable person could support them."

Source C: A five-year-old article explains how one high school repaired damaged laptops.

Notebook Directions

  1. Rank the three sources from strongest to weakest for the research question.
  2. Write one sentence explaining your strongest source.
  3. Write one sentence explaining your weakest source.
  4. Identify one source that may still be useful for a limited purpose.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Source B is best because it has a strong opinion.

A strong opinion is not the same as strong evidence. The language is one-sided and unsupported.

Correct Thinking

Correct: Source A is strongest because it is current, connected to the question, and based on district data.

This explanation uses source quality, not personal agreement, to make the decision.
