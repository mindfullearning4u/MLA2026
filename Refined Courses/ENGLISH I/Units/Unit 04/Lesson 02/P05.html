P05 Guided Practice

Answer the guided practice questions in Moodle. Each question asks you to evaluate source quality.

Directions

Read the research question and source description in each question. Choose the answer that best evaluates reliability, validity, credibility, or bias.

Do not choose a source just because it sounds interesting. Choose based on evidence and usefulness.
