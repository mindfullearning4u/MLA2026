P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can create and refine a research question.

Checkpoint Task

Topic: student access to quiet study spaces.

In your notebook, write one focused research question about this topic. Then write three bullets explaining what information you would need to find in order to answer the question.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area. Your response should show the question and the information you would need to research.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your question is focused.
  • Your question is researchable.
  • Your needed information matches the question.
  • Your writing is clear enough for another person to follow your inquiry plan.
