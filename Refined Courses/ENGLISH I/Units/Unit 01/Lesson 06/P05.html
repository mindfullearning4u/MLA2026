Question 1

MLA.ENG1.V.01

Passage: The article gives three details that prove the school garden saved water.

Question: Which sentence uses academic vocabulary correctly?

A. The article uses evidence to show the garden saved water.

B. The article uses stuff to be good.

C. The article evidences the garden with water things.

D. The garden was academic because water happened.
