P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will move vocabulary from a written response into an oral explanation plan.

Read the Passage

The teacher asked students to compare two responses. One response used vague words, while the other used specific evidence and academic vocabulary.

Direct Teaching

Academic vocabulary helps both writing and speaking. A written response might say, "The second response is more precise because it includes specific evidence." An oral explanation can use the same academic words: "I think the second response is stronger because its evidence is more precise."

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the academic words compare, specific, evidence, and precise.
  2. Write one sentence explaining why specific evidence improves a response.
  3. Write one sentence you could say aloud using at least two of the academic words.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The second one is better because it has good stuff.

This is too vague. It does not use academic vocabulary to explain the idea.

Correct: The second response is stronger because it uses specific evidence and precise vocabulary.

This is stronger because the academic words explain exactly why the response works.
