P06 Independent Work

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Passage

The school survey showed that students wanted more quiet places to study. The principal used the survey evidence to support opening an extra study room during lunch.

Part A: Vocabulary

Copy the words survey, evidence, and support. Write what each word means in the passage.

Part B: Written Response

Write two complete sentences explaining how the survey evidence supported the new study room.

Part C: Oral Explanation Plan

Write one sentence you could say aloud that uses at least two academic vocabulary words from Part A.
