Logical Fallacies

Notebook Task 1: Handwrite the table below.

Fallacy Definition
ad hominem attacks a person instead of the argument
ad populum claims something is true because it is popular
hasty generalization draws a broad conclusion from insufficient evidence
red herring introduces an unrelated distraction
slippery slope assumes one step will inevitably cause an extreme outcome
strawman misrepresents an opposing view to make it easier to attack
false analogy makes an invalid comparison
circular reasoning uses the conclusion as a premise
non sequitur offers a conclusion that does not logically follow
