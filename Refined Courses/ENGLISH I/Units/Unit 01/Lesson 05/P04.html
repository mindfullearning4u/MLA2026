Worked Example

Read and notice

Read the passage and study the labeled examples.

Our school should not buy new laptops because one student said computers are confusing. Besides, every successful school already has the newest technology, so we should buy them immediately. If we do not purchase new laptops this year, our school will soon fall behind every other school in the country.

Hasty generalization: one student’s opinion is treated as proof.
Ad populum: popularity is presented as evidence.
Slippery slope: the argument predicts an extreme outcome without support.

Follow the model

  1. Read the passage once for its overall meaning.
  2. Locate the labeled examples in the passage.
