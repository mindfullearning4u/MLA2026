Checkpoint

Fallacies in Reasoning

Identify three logical fallacies used in the argument.

Read the Passage

“Our school should not buy new laptops because one student said computers are confusing. Besides, every successful school already has the newest technology, so we should buy them immediately. If we do not purchase new laptops this year, our school will soon fall behind every other school in the country.”

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Hasty Generalization: Quote the evidence. Explain why it relies on insufficient evidence.
  2. Ad Populum (Bandwagon): Quote the evidence. Explain why popularity does not prove the claim.
  3. Slippery Slope: Quote the evidence. Explain why the predicted result does not necessarily follow.
Response pattern: Identify the concept, quote the evidence, and explain in 1–3 sentences why it is an example. Complete this work in your handwritten notebook and include it in your notebook submission.
