Before You Begin

A strong response should answer the question, use evidence, and explain the evidence clearly.

Question 1

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The school added a homework help table in the cafeteria before first period. Students could ask quick questions before class began.

Question: Which response best explains how the help table supported students?

A. The table was in the cafeteria and school started later.

B. The homework help table supported students because they could ask quick questions before class.

C. Students had homework, so the cafeteria was helpful.

D. The school had tables and questions.

Question 2

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The park added signs showing the length of each trail loop. Families used the signs to choose walks that matched the time they had available.

Question: Which sentence is the best explanation?

A. The signs helped families choose a trail because the signs showed the length of each loop.

B. The families walked because signs were outside.

C. The park had trails, and trails are good.

D. The signs were in the park because families had time.

Question 3

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The teacher gave students a checklist for revising paragraphs. The checklist reminded students to check topic sentences, evidence, and punctuation.

Question: Which response is clearest?

A. The checklist was a thing students used.

B. Students had paragraphs, so the teacher made them better.

C. The revision checklist helped students improve paragraphs by reminding them to check topic sentences, evidence, and punctuation.

D. The teacher gave something because students wrote.

Question 4

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The community center opened a quiet computer room for job applications. Visitors could complete forms without noise from the main lobby.

Question: Which response includes an accurate paraphrase and evidence?

A. The computer room was quiet, and forms exist.

B. The quiet computer room helped visitors complete job applications because they could work without lobby noise.

C. The lobby was noisy, so nobody completed anything.

D. The community center had computers near a room.

Question 5

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Question: Which response best explains the benefit of the video?

A. The video was slow, and students watched it.
