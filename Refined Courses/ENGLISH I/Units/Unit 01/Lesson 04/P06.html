Types of Logical Reasoning

Decide which type of reasoning is primarily used, then explain your answer.

Read the Passage

Every winter, the sidewalks near the school become icy after temperatures drop below freezing. This morning the temperature fell to 25°F, and the sidewalks were covered with ice. Since the forecast predicts freezing temperatures again tonight, it is reasonable to conclude that the sidewalks will likely be icy tomorrow morning as well.

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Identify the reasoning type: Is it deductive, inductive, or abductive?
  2. Quote the evidence: Copy the detail(s) the author uses to reach the conclusion.
  3. Explain: In 1–3 sentences, explain how the evidence leads to the conclusion.
Response pattern: Identify the concept, quote the evidence, and explain in 1–3 sentences why it is an example. Complete this work in your handwritten notebook and include it in your notebook submission.
