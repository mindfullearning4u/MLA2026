Passage

The animal shelter created a short form for visitors before they met adoptable pets. The form asked about home space, activity level, and pet experience so workers could suggest animals that matched each family.

Part A: Answer

Write one sentence that paraphrases how the form helped shelter workers and visitors.

Part B: Evidence

Copy one short detail that supports your answer.

Part C: Complete Response

Write a three-sentence response that includes your answer, evidence, and explanation. Then revise one sentence to make it clearer.
