Passage: The cafeteria posted ingredient cards beside each meal. Students with allergies could check the cards before choosing lunch.

Question: Which response is strongest?

A. The ingredient cards helped students with allergies choose lunch safely because they could check each meal's ingredients.

Question 3

MLA.ENG1.R.10; MLA.ENG1.V.01

Passage: The library added signs showing where to find fiction, nonfiction, and graphic novels. New students used the signs to locate books without asking for directions.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase "The signs helped students find books independently"?

A. The library had fiction.

B. New students used the signs to locate books without asking for directions.

C. Graphic novels were listed.

D. The signs were in the library.
