P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Academic Vocabulary in Context

Vocabulary

Academic vocabulary means words used in school reading, writing, and discussion.

Context means the words and ideas around a word that help you understand it.

Precise means exact and clear.

Response means your written or spoken answer to a text.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the sentence before and after the word. Do not guess from the word alone.

Step 2: Ask what job the word does. Does it name an idea, describe a change, show a comparison, or explain a reason?

Step 3: Replace the word with a simpler meaning and check whether the sentence still makes sense.

Step 4: Use the academic word correctly in your own response.

Read the Passage

The student council proposed adding shaded tables outside the cafeteria. Members explained that the change would benefit students who wanted a quieter place to eat, especially during crowded lunch periods.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the title: Academic Vocabulary in Context.
  2. Copy the words proposed and benefit.
  3. Use context to explain what each word means in this passage.
  4. Write one sentence that uses either word correctly.

Model Start

Proposed means suggested or recommended. I know this because the student council is asking for a change: shaded tables outside the cafeteria.
