P04 Worked Example

This worked example shows how to understand an academic word and use it in a clear response.

Example 1: Use Context

Passage: The science teacher displayed two plant samples side by side. By comparing the healthy plant with the wilted plant, students could identify how lack of water affected the leaves.

Academic word: identify

Step 1: Look near the word. Students are looking at two plant samples.

Step 2: Ask what they are doing. They are figuring out what changed in the leaves.

Meaning: In this passage, identify means to find or recognize something clearly.

Example 2: Choose Precise Vocabulary

Question: What did comparing the plants help students do?

Weak response: It helped them see plant stuff.

Think aloud: The response needs a precise academic word. The passage already gives a useful word: identify.

Stronger response: Comparing the plants helped students identify how lack of water affected the leaves.

Example 3: Check the Sentence

Check for meaning: Does the word fit the idea from the passage?

Check for grammar: Does the sentence sound complete and correct?

Final response: Comparing the plants helped students identify how lack of water affected the leaves.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The plants helped students identify water.

This is incorrect because the students did not identify water. They identified how lack of water affected the leaves.

Correct: The plant comparison helped students identify how lack of water affected the leaves.

This is correct because the academic word is used with the exact idea from the passage.
