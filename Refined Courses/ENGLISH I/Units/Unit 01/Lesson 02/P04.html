Worked Example

Read and notice

Read the passage and study the labeled examples.

Our community has always overcome challenges by working together. As your science teacher for the past fifteen years, I have watched students accomplish amazing things when they refuse to give up. Today, our school has the opportunity to build a community garden that will provide fresh vegetables for families in need. Imagine a child who goes to bed hungry finally having healthy food to eat. If every student volunteers just two hours this month, we can grow enough food to help dozens of families.

Ethos: “As your science teacher for the past fifteen years...” establishes credibility.
Pathos: “Imagine a child who goes to bed hungry...” appeals to emotion.
Logos: “If every student volunteers just two hours...” gives a logical plan.

Follow the model

  1. Read the passage once for its overall meaning.
  2. Locate the labeled examples in the passage.
