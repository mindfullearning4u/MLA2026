Checkpoint

Rhetorical Appeals

Identify three rhetorical appeals used in the passage.

Read the Passage

“Our community has always overcome challenges by working together. As your science teacher for the past fifteen years, I have watched students accomplish amazing things when they refuse to give up. Today, our school has the opportunity to build a community garden that will provide fresh vegetables for families in need. Imagine a child who goes to bed hungry finally having healthy food to eat. If every student volunteers just two hours this month, we can grow enough food to help dozens of families.”

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Ethos: Quote one example. Explain why it establishes the speaker’s credibility.
  2. Pathos: Quote one example. Explain the emotion it is meant to create.
  3. Logos: Quote one example. Explain the logical reasoning it provides.
Response pattern: Identify the concept, quote the evidence, and explain in 1–3 sentences why it is an example. Complete this work in your handwritten notebook and include it in your notebook submission.
