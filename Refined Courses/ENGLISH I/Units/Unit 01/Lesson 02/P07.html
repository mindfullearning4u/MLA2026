P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can use academic vocabulary accurately in a reading response.

Checkpoint Passage

A community survey revealed that many residents wanted safer bike routes. City planners evaluated the responses and proposed adding painted bike lanes near schools and parks. They explained that the change could improve safety for riders and drivers.

Checkpoint Task

In your notebook, write a four-sentence response that explains what city planners learned and what they proposed.

  • Use at least three academic vocabulary words from the passage.
  • Explain each word through your sentence meaning.
  • Use complete sentences and standard English conventions.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear image or typed copy of your checkpoint response. Make sure your name, unit, and lesson number are visible.

Checkpoint Submission Workflow

  1. Review your response for accurate vocabulary use.
  2. Check capitalization, punctuation, and complete sentences.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence in the checkpoint area.
  4. Ask TOR for help if you are unsure whether a word is used correctly.

Mastery Criteria

You show mastery when your response uses academic vocabulary accurately, explains the passage clearly, and follows standard English conventions.
