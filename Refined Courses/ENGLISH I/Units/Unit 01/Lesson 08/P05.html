P05 Guided Practice: Integrated Unit 1 Thinking

The guided practice checks whether you can combine Unit 1 skills in the same task. Read each passage carefully. Then choose the answer that best integrates paraphrase, evidence, vocabulary, reasoning, and conventions.

How to Think Through Each Question

  1. Read the passage and identify the main idea.
  2. Notice which detail is relevant evidence.
  3. Check whether the answer restates the meaning accurately.
  4. Look for precise academic vocabulary.
  5. Eliminate answers with vague language, copied wording, weak evidence, or convention errors.

Guided Practice Focus

Each guided practice item gives you the text you need. You should not answer by guessing one isolated skill. The best answer will usually be the one that keeps the passage meaning accurate, uses relevant evidence, and communicates clearly.

Before You Begin

If an answer only defines a word, only copies a sentence, or only checks punctuation, it may not be the strongest synthesis answer. A strong Unit 1 answer connects reading meaning, evidence, vocabulary, and conventions.
