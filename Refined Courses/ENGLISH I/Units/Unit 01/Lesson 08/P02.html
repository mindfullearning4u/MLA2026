P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 1 Synthesis: Reading, Paraphrase, Evidence, Vocabulary, and Conventions

Integrated Passage

Passage: The school library created a student recommendation shelf near the entrance. Students wrote short cards explaining why a book was useful, surprising, or enjoyable. The librarian noticed that more students stopped to browse when the cards included specific details instead of general praise. A card that said, "This mystery uses short chapters and strong clues, so it is easy to follow," helped readers more than a card that said, "This book is good." The librarian reminded students that precise language and clear evidence make recommendations more helpful.

This passage gives you enough information to combine Unit 1 skills. You will not answer one isolated question. You will build an explanation by reading, paraphrasing, selecting evidence, using academic vocabulary, and checking conventions.

Vocabulary Used in the Task

Paraphrase: Restate the passage meaning accurately in your own words.

Evidence: A detail from the passage that supports your explanation.

Relevant: Closely connected to the point you are making.

Precise: Exact and clear, not vague.

Conventions: Capitalization, punctuation, spelling, grammar, and complete sentences.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one accurate paraphrase of the passage's main idea.
  2. Select one relevant evidence detail that supports your paraphrase.
  3. Explain why that evidence is stronger than a vague detail.
  4. Use at least two academic vocabulary words from this page in your explanation.
  5. Check your response for capitalization, punctuation, and complete sentences.

Why This Is Synthesis

A correct response must do several things at once. If you paraphrase without evidence, the response is incomplete. If you quote evidence without explaining it, the reader may not understand your thinking. If you use precise vocabulary but ignore conventions, the response may be hard to read. Unit 1 mastery means using all of these skills together.
