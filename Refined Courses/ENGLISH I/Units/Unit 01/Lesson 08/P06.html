P06 Unit Assessment Readiness Check

Complete this readiness check before opening the Unit 1 Assessment.

Checklist

  • I can paraphrase a short passage accurately.
  • I can choose academic vocabulary that fits the passage.
  • I can identify relevant evidence.
  • I can recognize complete, correctly punctuated sentences.
  • I can choose the strongest integrated response.

Independent Work Parts

Part A: Identify the Unit 1 skill being reviewed.

Part B: Complete the response using evidence and precise vocabulary.

Part C: Revise the response for complete sentences, clear reasoning, and mastery criteria.
