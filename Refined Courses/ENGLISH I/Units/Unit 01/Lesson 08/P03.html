📘 ENG1 | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use the same Unit 1 skills in a short synthesis response. No new skill is introduced here.

Review Passage

The teacher posted sample responses before students submitted their own work. The samples showed how to answer the question, use evidence, choose precise vocabulary, and write complete sentences.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write a two-sentence response explaining how the sample responses helped students.
  2. Use the words evidence and precise correctly.
  3. Check capitalization and punctuation.

Review Reminder

Weak: The samples helped because they had stuff.

Strong: The samples helped students see how to use evidence, precise vocabulary, and complete sentences in their own responses.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a general answer without naming the specific evidence, skill, or reader effect.

Correct: Name the exact word, detail, structure, or choice from the lesson and explain how it supports meaning, clarity, or the reader.

Why this matters: English I mastery requires more than a correct-sounding answer. You need to show the thinking that connects evidence to explanation.
