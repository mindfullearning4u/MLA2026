P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will use the same Unit 1 skills with less scaffolding. This page increases complexity by asking you to create one complete response instead of separate notes.

Transfer Passage

Passage: A ninth-grade class created a wall of study tips before midterm exams. Students added notes explaining which strategies helped them remember information. The teacher observed that the most useful notes named a specific strategy and explained how it worked. One note said, "Making flashcards helped me practice definitions until I could explain them without looking." Another note said only, "Study more." The teacher used the wall to show that clear details and precise words make advice easier to follow.

Notebook Directions

Write one complete paragraph that answers this question:

Why were some study-tip notes more useful than others?

Your paragraph must:

  • Paraphrase the passage's main idea accurately.
  • Use one relevant evidence detail from the passage.
  • Use at least two academic vocabulary words correctly.
  • Explain how the evidence supports your answer.
  • Use complete sentences and standard English conventions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The notes were better because they were good and helped people study.

This response is weak because it is too general. It does not paraphrase the main idea clearly, does not use relevant evidence, and does not explain why precise language matters.

Correct: Some study-tip notes were more useful because they gave precise advice that students could follow. The note about flashcards was stronger than "Study more" because it named a specific strategy and explained that the student practiced definitions until they could explain them independently.

This response works because it combines paraphrase, evidence, academic vocabulary, reasoning, and clear conventions.
