Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 1 Synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7. It does not introduce new standards, new concepts, new vocabulary expectations, or new skills. You will use the Unit 1 skills together so you are ready to demonstrate mastery on the Unit Assessment.

Unit Standards

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

Unit Skills, Concepts, and Objectives

  • Read a short grade-level passage carefully enough to understand the main idea.
  • Paraphrase the passage accurately without copying the author's exact wording.
  • Select evidence that directly supports the paraphrase or explanation.
  • Use academic vocabulary such as paraphrase, evidence, relevant, precise, and conventions correctly.
  • Write a clear explanation that connects evidence to meaning.
  • Check capitalization, punctuation, sentence completeness, and word choice before submitting.

What You Will Do

You will work with one connected reading situation instead of separate review questions. Each page asks you to combine Unit 1 skills: read, analyze, paraphrase, select evidence, use academic vocabulary, explain reasoning, and check conventions.

The goal is not to remember one isolated skill. The goal is to show that you can use several English I skills together when a text-based task requires them.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery when you can read a passage, explain its meaning in your own words, support that explanation with relevant evidence, use academic vocabulary accurately, and submit a response that follows standard English conventions. The Unit Assessment mastery requirement is 80% or higher.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read a passage, restate its meaning accurately, choose helpful evidence, use precise academic words, and write a clear response that is ready for a reader.
