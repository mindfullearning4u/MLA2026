Vocabulary Examples

Study the examples

Copy the table into your handwritten course notebook. Include the word, definition, and example.

Word Definition Example
Metaphor Direct comparison of unlike things Life is a broken-winged bird.
Simile Comparison using like or as She ran like the wind.
Alliteration Repeated beginning consonant sounds Wild winds whistle.
Onomatopoeia A word that imitates a sound Buzz! Crash! Crunch!
Personification Human qualities given to a nonhuman subject The leaves waved goodbye.
Hyperbole Extreme exaggeration I have told you a million times.
Meiosis Intentional understatement A scratch, a scratch.
Allusion Reference to a well-known person, event, place, or text He met his Waterloo.
Idiom Expression whose meaning is not literal Spill the beans.
Imagery Language that appeals to the senses The warm cinnamon scent filled the kitchen.
Allegory A narrative with a secondary symbolic meaning A story about a farm may represent political events.
