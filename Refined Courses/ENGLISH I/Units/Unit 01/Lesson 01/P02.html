Table 1: Core Vocabulary

Vocabulary Word Definition
Figurative language Language that uses imaginative comparisons, descriptions, or expressions to create meaning beyond the literal words.

Table 2: Types of Figurative Language

Word Student-Friendly Definition
Metaphor A comparison that says one thing is another to show a shared quality.
Simile A comparison using like or as.
Alliteration The repetition of beginning consonant sounds in nearby words.
Onomatopoeia A word that sounds like the sound it names.
Personification Giving human qualities or actions to something nonhuman.
Hyperbole An extreme exaggeration used for effect.
Meiosis An intentional understatement that makes a situation seem less serious.
Allusion A brief reference to a familiar person, event, place, or work.
Idiom An expression whose meaning is different from the literal words.
Imagery Language that appeals to the senses to help a reader picture, hear, feel, taste, or smell something.
Allegory A symbolic narrative with a second level of meaning.
