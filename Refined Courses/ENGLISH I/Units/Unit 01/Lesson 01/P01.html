P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 1: Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase Foundations

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase Foundations

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to slow down and read a short grade-level passage closely.
  • How to tell the difference between copying and paraphrasing.
  • How to restate the main idea and important details in your own words.
  • How to keep the author's meaning accurate when you change the wording.

Why This Matters

Paraphrasing is one of the first skills you need for English I because it proves that you understand what you read. If you can explain a text accurately in your own words, you are ready to analyze, use evidence, write responses, and discuss meaning.

A strong paraphrase is not shorter because you removed important meaning. It is clearer because you understood the meaning and restated it accurately.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages that are included directly in the lesson.
  • Mark the main idea, key details, and words that need careful attention.
  • Write notebook paraphrases using your own sentence structure.
  • Compare incorrect and correct paraphrases so you can avoid common mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook responses identify the main idea and important details.
  • Your paraphrases keep the author's meaning accurate.
  • Your checkpoint response explains how you changed the wording without changing the meaning.
  • Your quiz answers show that you can choose accurate paraphrases and recognize weak ones.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.10 in student language: I can read a grade-level text and restate its meaning accurately in my own words.

Today, your job is not to guess what the passage means. Your job is to use the words on the page, understand them, and explain the same meaning clearly.
