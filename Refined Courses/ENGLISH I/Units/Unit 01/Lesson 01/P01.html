UNIT 1 • LESSON 1

Figurative Language

Lesson Overview

In this lesson, you will learn to recognize figurative language and explain how it shapes meaning, mood, and imagery.

Learning goals

  • Define common types of figurative language.
  • Identify each device in context.
  • Explain the effect of a writer’s language choice.

Lesson Workflow

Complete Pages 2 and 3 by hand in your notebook. Study the worked example on Page 4. Complete the independent practice on Page 5 in your notebook and submit it with your notebook evidence. Complete the checkpoint on Page 6 in the separate checkpoint activity.
