Identifying Figurative Language

Read the poem closely. Then complete the three short-response questions in your notebook.

Autumn Walk

The wind whispered secrets through the sleepy trees, Golden leaves danced across the ground with ease. The sun was a glowing lantern in the sky, Clouds sailed like ships drifting slowly by. Crunch, crunch went the leaves beneath my feet, The cool air wrapped around me like a sheet. I smiled as the forest waved goodbye, Until the stars blinked awake in the sky.

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Personification: Write one line from the poem that uses personification. Explain why it is personification.
  2. Simile: Write one line from the poem that uses a simile. Explain why it is a simile.
  3. Onomatopoeia: Write the word or line that uses onomatopoeia. Explain why it is onomatopoeia.
