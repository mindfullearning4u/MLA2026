P05 Guided Practice

Answer the five guided practice questions. Each passage is included inside the question so you do not need to search for another text. These questions match ENG1_U01_L01_GuidedPractice.gift.

Before You Begin

  1. Read the full passage before looking at the answers.
  2. Ask, "What is this mostly saying?"
  3. Choose the answer that keeps the meaning accurate in new words.

Question 1

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: During lunch, Maya noticed that many students threw away unopened milk cartons. She suggested placing a clearly labeled donation cooler near the cafeteria exit so unopened items could be saved for students who needed food later.

Question: Which choice best paraphrases the passage?

A. Maya wanted students to stop buying milk because the cafeteria had too many cartons.

B. Maya saw unopened food being wasted and suggested a way to save it for students who might need it.

C. Maya donated all of her lunch because other students forgot to bring food.

D. Maya asked the cafeteria to remove milk cartons from lunch because students disliked them.

Question 2

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The class aquarium became cloudy after students overfed the fish for several days. The teacher explained that extra food breaks down in the water and can make the tank unhealthy.

Question: What is the most accurate paraphrase?

A. The teacher wanted students to clean the aquarium because the fish had grown too large.

B. Students fed the fish because the aquarium water was already unhealthy.

C. The fish became unhealthy because the teacher forgot to explain how to feed them.

D. Too much fish food made the water cloudy because uneaten food can break down in the tank.

Question 3

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: A local trail reopened after volunteers repaired the wooden bridge and cleared branches from the path. The park manager said the repairs made the trail safer for walkers and cyclists.

Question: Which statement paraphrases the passage accurately?

A. Volunteers helped reopen a trail by fixing a bridge and removing branches so people could use it more safely.

B. The park manager closed the trail because walkers and cyclists damaged the wooden bridge.

C. Volunteers built a new park after a bridge was removed from the trail.

D. Walkers and cyclists repaired branches because the trail was unsafe for volunteers.

Question 4

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: Because the school play needed a city scene, the art class painted tall buildings on cardboard panels. The panels could be moved quickly between scenes without changing the stage floor.

Question: What is the main idea of the passage?

A. The art class painted the stage floor to make it look like a city.

B. The school play was canceled because the city scene was too difficult to build.

C. The art class made movable city panels to help create the setting for the play.

D. The cardboard panels were used because the play did not need a setting.

Question 5

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: When the debate team practiced, each speaker had two minutes to present a claim and one piece of evidence. The coach limited the time so students would learn to explain their ideas clearly and briefly.

Question: Which paraphrase is most accurate?

A. The coach stopped the debate team from using evidence during practice.

B. The coach used a time limit to help speakers practice making clear, brief arguments with evidence.

C. The debate team practiced for two minutes because the coach did not want students to speak.

D. Each speaker presented two claims because the debate team needed longer speeches.
