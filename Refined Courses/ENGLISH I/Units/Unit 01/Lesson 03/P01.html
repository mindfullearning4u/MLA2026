Rhetorical Devices

Lesson Overview

Learn the essential concepts of rhetorical devices, identify them in context, and explain how they shape meaning and persuasion.

Learning goals

  • Define the lesson vocabulary.
  • Study clear examples.
  • Analyze a worked example.
  • Apply the skill independently.

Complete Pages 2 and 3 by hand in your notebook. Study the worked example on Page 4. Complete the independent practice on Page 5 in your notebook and submit it with your notebook evidence. Complete the checkpoint on Page 6 in the separate checkpoint activity.
