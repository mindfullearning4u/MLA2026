P05 Guided Practice

Choose the answer that best connects an accurate paraphrase with relevant evidence. These questions match ENG1_U01_L03_GuidedPractice.gift.

Before You Begin

Read the passage, identify the meaning, and select the evidence that proves that meaning. Every passage is included in the question.

Question 1

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The art teacher placed labeled bins near each table so students could return markers, scissors, and glue without crossing the room. The new system helped cleanup finish faster.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase "The labeled bins made cleanup more efficient"?

A. The art teacher placed labeled bins near each table.

B. The room had tables.

C. Students used markers.

D. Glue was one supply.

Question 2

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The soccer coach moved practice to the shaded field during the hottest week of the season. Players were able to complete drills with fewer heat breaks.

Question: Which paraphrase is best supported by the passage?

A. The coach changed the practice location to help players work safely during hot weather.

B. The players stopped practicing because the field was too shaded.

C. The coach canceled every drill during the hottest week.

D. The players asked to remove shade from the field.

Question 3

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The club posted meeting reminders on the school website and near the front office. Attendance increased because students had more than one place to find the information.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase "The reminders helped more students attend meetings"?

A. The club had meetings.

B. The front office was near the reminder.

C. Attendance increased.

D. The school had a website.

Question 4

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The library created a shelf for books recommended by students. The librarian said the shelf encouraged students to try books their classmates enjoyed.

Question: Which explanation best connects the evidence to the paraphrase?

A. The shelf was in the library, so the library had books.

B. Student recommendations helped classmates find books other students liked.

C. The librarian said something, so the shelf was not useful.

D. The books were on a shelf, so students did not read them.

Question 5

MLA.ENG1.R.10

Passage: The science class used a clear jar to model erosion. When water was poured over the soil, students watched small pieces of dirt move away from the surface.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase "The model showed how water can move soil"?

A. The class was a science class.

B. The jar was clear.

C. Students watched small pieces of dirt move away from the surface.

D. The class used a jar.
