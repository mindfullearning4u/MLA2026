P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Paraphrase + Relevant Evidence

Vocabulary

Evidence is a detail from the text that supports your idea.

Relevant means directly connected to the idea you are explaining.

Support means to prove, explain, or back up an idea.

Paraphrase means restating the text accurately in your own words.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the passage and ask, "What is this mostly saying?"

Step 2: Write the meaning in your own words.

Step 3: Choose one detail from the passage that proves your paraphrase.

Step 4: Explain how the detail supports your paraphrase.

Read the Passage

The school garden club planted herbs near the cafeteria door. Cafeteria workers could clip small amounts of basil and mint during lunch preparation, which made fresh ingredients easier to use.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  2. Copy one text detail that supports your paraphrase.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why that detail is relevant.

Model Start

Paraphrase: The garden club made fresh herbs easier for cafeteria workers to use by planting them close to the cafeteria.

Relevant evidence: The passage says workers could clip basil and mint during lunch preparation.
