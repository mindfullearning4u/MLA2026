Word Definition Example
Antithesis contrasting ideas in parallel form We can choose fear or hope.
Asyndeton omission of conjunctions in a series Stronger schools, stronger neighborhoods, a stronger nation.
Chiasmus reversal of words or structure Ask what you can do for your community.
Irony contrast between expectation and reality The fire station burned down.
Metonymy related term stands for a larger idea The White House announced...
Rhetorical Question question asked for effect Will we let others decide our future?
Synecdoche a part represents the whole All hands on deck.
Zeugma one word governs two others differently She broke his car and his heart.
