P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will decide which evidence is relevant and which evidence is only nearby.

Read the Passage

A student group placed recycling signs above the trash cans in the gym. The signs listed examples of items that could be recycled, such as plastic bottles and clean paper. After the signs were posted, fewer recyclable items were found in the trash.

Direct Teaching

A detail is not relevant just because it appears in the passage. It must support the exact idea you are explaining.

Paraphrase: The signs helped students recycle correctly.

Relevant detail: The signs listed examples of recyclable items.

Why: This detail explains how the signs helped students know what to recycle.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write a paraphrase of the passage in one sentence.
  2. Write the most relevant evidence from the passage.
  3. Write one detail from the passage that is less useful for proving your paraphrase.
  4. Explain why the first detail is stronger evidence.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The strongest evidence is "in the gym."

This detail tells where the signs were placed, but it does not best prove that the signs helped students recycle correctly.

Correct: The strongest evidence is "The signs listed examples of items that could be recycled."

This detail is stronger because it directly explains how the signs helped students make better recycling choices.
