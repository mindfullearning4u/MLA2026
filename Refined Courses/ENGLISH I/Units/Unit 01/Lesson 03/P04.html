Worked Example

Read and notice

Read the passage and study the labeled examples.

We can choose fear or we can choose hope. We can remain divided, or we can stand together. Every voice matters because every citizen matters. Will we let others decide our future? Let us build stronger schools, stronger neighborhoods, and a stronger nation.

Antithesis: “fear” contrasts with “hope.”
Rhetorical question: “Will we let others decide our future?” prompts reflection.
Asyndeton: the closing series emphasizes the speaker’s goals.

Follow the model

  1. Read the passage once for its overall meaning.
  2. Locate the labeled examples in the passage.
