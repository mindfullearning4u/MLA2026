Passage

Heading: How School Recycling Works

Schools often begin recycling programs by placing labeled bins in hallways and cafeterias. Students then learn which materials belong in each bin. Custodial teams collect the materials, and local recycling centers sort paper, plastic, and metal for processing. A small chart beside each bin can show examples of accepted items.

Part A

Identify the text structure. Use one sentence to explain how you know.

Part B

Identify one text feature. Explain what it helps the reader do.

Part C

Write three to four sentences explaining how the structure and feature work together to help readers understand school recycling.
