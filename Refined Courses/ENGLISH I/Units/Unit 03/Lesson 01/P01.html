Lesson 1: Informational Text Structure and Features

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.05 - Analyze Informational Text Structure and Features.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How informational texts are organized to help readers understand ideas.
  • How headings, subheadings, captions, sidebars, charts, and other features guide attention.
  • How structure and features work together to make information clearer.

Why This Matters

Informational texts are built to explain real topics. If you understand the structure and features, you can find important ideas faster, paraphrase more accurately, and prepare for later lessons on central idea, rhetoric, and argument.

What You Will Do

  • Read short informational passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify common text structures such as cause/effect, problem/solution, compare/contrast, sequence, and description.
  • Explain how text features help a reader locate, organize, or understand information.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can name the structure of an informational passage.
  • You can identify a text feature and explain its purpose.
  • You can explain how organization helps the reader understand the topic.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.05 in student language: I can explain how an informational text is organized and how its features help readers understand information.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can find facts in a passage but cannot explain how the text is organized.
