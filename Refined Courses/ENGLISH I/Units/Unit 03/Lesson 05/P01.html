Lesson 5: Opposing Arguments, Claims, and Validity

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.08 - Evaluate Arguments, Claims, and Validity.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.06 - Analyze Central Idea Support and Evidence Quality; MLA.ENG1.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose and Rhetorical Appeals; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify a claim in an argument.
  • How to recognize opposing claims about the same issue.
  • How to judge whether evidence makes an argument valid and reasonable.

Why This Matters

Before you write an argument, you need to read arguments carefully. Strong readers can tell the difference between a claim, a reason, evidence, and an unsupported opinion.

What You Will Do

  • Read short arguments included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify claims, reasons, and evidence.
  • Compare opposing arguments on the same issue.
  • Decide which argument is better supported.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify a claim.
  • You can identify relevant support.
  • You can explain whether an argument is valid based on its reasoning and evidence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.08 in student language: I can evaluate arguments by identifying claims, checking the evidence, and deciding whether the reasoning is valid.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can find an opinion but cannot tell whether it is supported by evidence.
