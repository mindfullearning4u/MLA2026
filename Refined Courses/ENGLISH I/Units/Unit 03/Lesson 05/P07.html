Checkpoint Task

Passage: The school should add more outdoor trash bins near the athletic fields. After games, wrappers and bottles are often left near the bleachers because the nearest bins are across the parking lot. Adding bins near the exits would make cleanup easier and reduce litter. Another speaker says the school should not add bins because the field already looks fine from the street.

Submit one paragraph identifying both claims and explaining which argument is more valid based on evidence.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Write your checkpoint paragraph.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence and checkpoint response in the LMS.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies opposing claims accurately.
  • Uses evidence from the passage.
  • Explains validity based on evidence and reasoning.
  • Uses complete, clear sentences.
