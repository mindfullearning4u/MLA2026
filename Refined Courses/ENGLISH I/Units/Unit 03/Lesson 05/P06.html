P06 Independent Work

Use this task to practice before the checkpoint.

Passage

Argument A: The school should open the gym before first period because many students arrive early and wait outside. On rainy days, the front entrance becomes crowded, and staff often redirect students to keep the walkway clear.

Argument B: The school should keep the gym closed because opening it sounds annoying and nobody needs it.

Part A

Write each argument's claim.

Part B

Identify which argument has stronger evidence.

Part C

Write three to four sentences explaining which argument is more valid and why.
