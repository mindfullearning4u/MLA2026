P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now strengthen reasoning so the evidence does real work.

Direct Teaching

Weak reasoning only repeats evidence. Strong reasoning explains why the evidence proves the claim. If your evidence is a survey, explain what the survey reveals. If your evidence is an example, explain what the example shows.

Use reasoning stems such as: This matters because... This supports the claim because... This evidence shows...

Notebook Directions

Claim: The school should create a quiet study space during lunch.

Evidence: In a student survey, 61 percent of students said noise made it difficult to complete work during lunch.

  1. Write a weak reasoning sentence that only repeats the evidence.
  2. Write a stronger reasoning sentence that explains why the evidence supports the claim.
  3. Circle the stronger sentence in your notebook.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: This evidence supports the claim because 61 percent of students said that.

This repeats the evidence but does not explain why it matters.

Correct Thinking

Correct: This evidence supports the claim because it shows that noise is a common barrier for students who want to work during lunch, so a quiet space would address a real need.

This reasoning explains the connection between the evidence and the claim.
