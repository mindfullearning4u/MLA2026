Lesson 6: Argument Writing: Claims, Evidence, and Reasoning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.02 - Write Arguments with Claims, Evidence, and Reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.08 - Evaluate Arguments, Claims, and Validity; MLA.ENG1.R.06 - Analyze Central Idea Support and Evidence Quality; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to write a clear argument claim.
  • How to choose relevant evidence for the claim.
  • How to explain reasoning so the reader understands how the evidence proves the claim.

Why This Matters

A strong argument is not just an opinion. It needs a clear claim, evidence that fits, and reasoning that explains the connection. This lesson begins argument writing after you have practiced reading and evaluating arguments.

What You Will Do

  • Study argument parts in short examples.
  • Choose the strongest claim for a prompt.
  • Match evidence to a claim.
  • Write reasoning that explains why the evidence matters.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can write a focused claim.
  • You can select evidence that supports the claim.
  • You can explain reasoning in complete sentences.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.W.02 in student language: I can write an argument with a clear claim, relevant evidence, and reasoning that explains my point.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you have evidence but cannot explain how it proves your claim.
