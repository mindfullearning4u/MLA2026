P04 Worked Example

This example shows how to build a short argument response.

Prompt and Evidence

Prompt: Should the school add more outdoor seating?

Evidence: During lunch, many students sit on the ground because the current benches fill quickly. A student survey found that 64 percent of students would use outdoor seating if more tables were available.

Worked Example 1: Write the Claim

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify the specific lesson skill being modeled. Step 2: examine the words, evidence, sentence, or passage detail that matters most. Step 3: explain why the final response or revision demonstrates the skill accurately.

Claim: The school should add more outdoor seating for lunch.

Worked Example 2: Use Evidence

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify what changed from the first example. Step 2: connect the example to the lesson focus. Step 3: explain how the response uses clearer reasoning, stronger evidence, or more precise language.

Evidence: Many students sit on the ground because benches fill quickly, and 64 percent of surveyed students would use more outdoor seating.

Worked Example 3: Add Reasoning

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: combine the lesson skill with evidence or revision choices. Step 2: check that the explanation stays focused on the assigned standard. Step 3: confirm that the final response is clear, accurate, and written in standard English.

This evidence supports the claim because it shows both a current problem and student interest in a solution. If benches already fill quickly and most surveyed students would use more seating, additional tables would meet a real student need.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The school should add seating because I want it.

This is weak because it relies on personal preference instead of evidence and reasoning.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.

Correct Approach

Correct: The school should add outdoor seating because current benches fill quickly and survey results show many students would use additional tables.

This answer is stronger because it uses evidence to support the claim.
