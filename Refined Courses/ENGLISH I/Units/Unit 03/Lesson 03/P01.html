Lesson 3: Author Purpose and Rhetorical Appeals

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose and Rhetorical Appeals.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.06 - Analyze Central Idea Support and Evidence Quality; MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Analyze Word Choice and Meaning; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify an author's purpose in an informational or persuasive text.
  • How authors use ethos, pathos, and logos to influence readers.
  • How evidence, word choice, and appeal work together.

Why This Matters

Authors do not choose facts and words by accident. They write to inform, explain, persuade, warn, entertain, or call readers to act. When you can recognize purpose and appeal, you can read informational and argumentative texts more carefully.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify whether the author's purpose is mostly to inform, persuade, explain, warn, or call readers to act.
  • Identify rhetorical appeals: ethos, pathos, and logos.
  • Explain how an appeal supports the author's purpose.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify an author's purpose.
  • You can identify an appeal and explain how it works.
  • You can connect evidence and word choice to purpose.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.07 in student language: I can explain why an author wrote a text and how the author uses logic, credibility, or emotion to affect readers.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify what a passage says but cannot explain why the author wrote it.
