P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now separate the three rhetorical appeals and explain which one is strongest.

Direct Teaching

One passage may include more than one appeal. Do not assume that every emotional word is the main appeal. Ask which appeal does the most work for the author's purpose.

For example, if an author gives research results to persuade readers, logos may be the strongest appeal. If the author quotes a trusted expert, ethos may be strongest. If the author uses vivid language to make readers care, pathos may be strongest.

Notebook Directions

Passage: "When students volunteer at the food pantry, they see how a few hours of service can help families carry groceries home with dignity. Last month, student volunteers packed 420 bags of food. The pantry director says student help keeps weekend distribution running smoothly."

  1. Identify one example of pathos.
  2. Identify one example of logos.
  3. Identify one example of ethos.
  4. Write which appeal is strongest and explain why.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The appeal is logos because the passage is nonfiction.

This is not enough. Nonfiction can use ethos, pathos, and logos. You must point to the actual words or evidence.

Correct Thinking

Correct: The number "420 bags of food" is logos because it gives measurable evidence. The pantry director's statement is ethos because it comes from someone connected to the program.

This is stronger because it names the appeal and proves it with text evidence.
