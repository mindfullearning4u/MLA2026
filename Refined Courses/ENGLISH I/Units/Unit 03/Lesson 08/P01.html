Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 3 Synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7 in Unit 03: Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument. It does not introduce new standards, new concepts, new vocabulary expectations, or new skills. You will use the unit skills together so you are ready to demonstrate mastery.

Unit Standards

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.05 - Analyze Text Structure; MLA.ENG1.R.06 - Evaluate Central Idea Support; MLA.ENG1.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose and Rhetoric; MLA.ENG1.R.08 - Evaluate Opposing Arguments; MLA.ENG1.R.12 - Explain Rhetorical Choices; MLA.ENG1.W.02 - Write Arguments with Evidence

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally

What You Will Learn

  • Analyze how text structure and features support purpose.
  • Evaluate whether details actually support a central idea.
  • Identify author purpose and explain rhetorical appeals or choices.
  • Compare opposing claims for effectiveness and validity.
  • Write an argument with claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim response, organization, and appropriate tone.
  • Use academic vocabulary and conventions to make reasoning clear.

What You Will Do

You will work with one connected unit task instead of separate review questions. Each page asks you to combine Unit 03 skills, vocabulary, reasoning, and communication.

The goal is not to remember one isolated skill. The goal is to show that you can use several English I skills together when a text-based task requires them.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery when you can complete an integrated response that applies the Unit 03 standards, uses relevant evidence or support, explains reasoning clearly, uses academic vocabulary accurately, and follows standard English conventions. The Unit Assessment mastery requirement is 80% or higher.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can combine the skills from Unit 03 to read, think, write, revise, and communicate clearly for a specific purpose.
