Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 3 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 3 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.05, MLA.ENG1.R.06, MLA.ENG1.R.07, MLA.ENG1.R.08, MLA.ENG1.R.12, MLA.ENG1.W.02.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10, MLA.ENG1.V.01, MLA.ENG1.L.01, MLA.ENG1.C.01.

What You Will Review

  • Informational text structure and features.
  • Central idea support and evidence quality.
  • Author purpose, rhetorical appeals, and rhetorical choices.
  • Argument claims, validity, evidence, reasoning, counterclaims, organization, and tone.

Why This Matters

This lesson does not introduce new material. It gives you a final opportunity to connect informational reading, rhetoric, argument evaluation, and argument writing before the Unit 3 Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review Unit 3 reading and writing skills.
  • Practice synthesis questions.
  • Check readiness for the Unit 3 Assessment.
  • Complete ENG1_U03_L08_UnitAssessment.gift.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery by analyzing informational and argumentative texts, identifying rhetorical choices, evaluating claims and evidence, and writing argument responses with clear reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read informational and argumentative texts carefully, explain how authors use structure and rhetoric, evaluate evidence, and write an organized argument.
