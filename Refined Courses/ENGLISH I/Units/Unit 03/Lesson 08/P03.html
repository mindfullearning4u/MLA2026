P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Connect rhetoric to argument effectiveness.

Notebook Directions

Passage: Imagine trying to finish work while conversations echo around you. Imagine reading the same sentence three times because the room will not settle. A quiet lunch study room would not remove lunch conversation; it would give students who need focus a respectful choice.

  1. Identify one rhetorical choice.
  2. Explain its effect on the reader.
  3. Explain how it supports the author's purpose.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The author uses words to persuade.

This is too general. You need to identify the specific choice and explain its effect.

Correct Thinking

Correct: The repeated word "Imagine" asks readers to picture the problem, making the need for a quiet room feel personal and reasonable.

This identifies the technique and explains the effect.
