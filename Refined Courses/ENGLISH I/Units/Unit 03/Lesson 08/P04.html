P04 Worked Example

This review example combines Unit 3 skills.

Example Passage

The school should add more recycling bins in hallways. Custodians found paper and plastic bottles in hallway trash cans every day last week. Some may argue that students already have enough bins, but the evidence shows recyclable items are still going into trash cans, so more visible bins could help students sort waste correctly.

Example 1: Claim and Evidence

The claim is that the school should add more recycling bins in hallways. The evidence is that custodians found paper and plastic bottles in hallway trash cans every day last week.

Example 2: Validity

The evidence is valid support because it shows the problem the proposed bins would address.

Example 3: Counterclaim and Rebuttal

The counterclaim is that students already have enough bins. The rebuttal is that recyclable items are still going into trash cans, so more visible bins may still be needed.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The claim is correct because recycling is good.

This does not use the evidence or explain the argument's reasoning.

Correct Approach

Correct: The claim is supported because the evidence shows recyclable items are currently being thrown away, which more visible bins could help address.

This connects claim, evidence, and reasoning.
