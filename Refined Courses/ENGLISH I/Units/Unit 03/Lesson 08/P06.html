P06 Independent Work

Prepare for the Unit 3 checkpoint by reviewing the skills you practiced in Lessons 1-7.

Independent Task

Prompt: Your school is considering whether to replace printed announcements with digital announcements on classroom screens.

Write one organized argument paragraph in your notebook. Your paragraph must include a claim, one piece of evidence, reasoning, a respectful counterclaim, and a rebuttal.

Part A: Plan

  1. Write your claim.
  2. Write one fact, observation, or example that could support the claim.
  3. Write one possible counterclaim.

Part B: Draft

Write the paragraph in a formal academic tone. Use transition words such as because, however, although, or therefore when they help the reader follow your thinking.

Part C: Review

Check your paragraph for these five parts:

  • Claim
  • Evidence
  • Reasoning
  • Counterclaim
  • Rebuttal
