P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Unit 3 Synthesis Review

Review Passage

Passage: Schools should consider adding a quiet lunch study room. Many students use lunch to finish makeup work, but the cafeteria can be loud and crowded. A student survey found that 61 percent of respondents said lunch noise made it difficult to complete assignments. Some students may argue that the library is already available, but a dedicated room would give students a predictable quiet option during the busiest part of the day.

Notebook Directions

  1. Identify the claim.
  2. Identify the evidence.
  3. Identify the counterclaim.
  4. Explain whether the evidence supports the claim.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Read the vocabulary and make sure you can say each term in your own words.
  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: Work with one sentence, term, or example at a time. Standard path: complete the full task and explain your reasoning. Accelerated path: add a second example, compare choices, or explain why a weaker answer does not meet the standard.

Vocabulary Review

  • Evidence: words, details, or examples that support an answer.
  • Analysis: explanation of how evidence proves or develops an idea.
  • Mastery: showing that you can apply the lesson skill independently.
