P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint confirms that you can put the Unit 3 skills together before completing the Unit Assessment.

Checkpoint Task

Passage: The school newspaper argues that every student club should post a short weekly update online. The article claims that weekly updates would help students learn about events before deadlines pass. It supports the claim by explaining that three clubs had low event attendance after announcements were posted only on hallway flyers. The article also acknowledges that some club leaders may not have time to write updates, but it responds that updates could be two or three sentences long.

In your notebook, write a response that identifies the claim, evidence, counterclaim, rebuttal, and one rhetorical choice the writer uses to persuade readers.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notebook evidence using your course submission area. Your response should be complete enough for someone else to see how you analyzed the passage.

Checkpoint Submission Directions

  1. Read the checkpoint passage carefully.
  2. Write your response in your notebook.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence.
  4. Complete the Unit Assessment when directed in Moodle.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify the author's claim accurately.
  • You connect evidence to the claim.
  • You identify the counterclaim and rebuttal.
  • You explain one rhetorical choice using the passage.
  • You use clear, complete sentences.
