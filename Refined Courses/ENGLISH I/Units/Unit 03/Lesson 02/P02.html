P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Central Idea and Supporting Evidence

Vocabulary

  • Central idea: the main point an informational text develops.
  • Supporting detail: a fact, example, reason, or explanation that helps prove or explain the central idea.
  • Relevant evidence: evidence that clearly connects to the central idea.
  • Specific evidence: evidence that gives precise information instead of a vague statement.

Direct Teaching

The central idea is not just the topic. A topic might be "school gardens." A central idea says what the author wants readers to understand about that topic, such as "school gardens can support learning and healthy habits."

After you identify the central idea, look for details that support it. Strong support is relevant, specific, and connected. Weak support may be interesting, but it does not clearly help prove or explain the central idea.

A strong reader asks: What is the text mostly saying? and Which details help prove that idea?

Notebook Directions

Passage: School gardens can improve student learning beyond science class. Students may measure plant growth in math, write observations in journals, and learn responsibility by caring for living things. Some schools also use garden harvests in cooking lessons, helping students connect nutrition to daily choices.

  1. Write the central idea in one sentence.
  2. List two details that support the central idea.
  3. Choose the stronger detail and explain why it is more useful.

Model

The central idea is that school gardens can support learning in several subjects and habits. The detail about measuring plant growth supports this because it gives a specific example of how gardens connect to math.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.
