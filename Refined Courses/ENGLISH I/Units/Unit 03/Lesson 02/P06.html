P06 Independent Work

Use this task to practice central idea and evidence quality before the checkpoint.

Passage

Walking or biking to school can support student health and reduce traffic near campus. Students who walk or bike add movement to their day before classes begin. Fewer cars near the entrance can also make arrival safer and less crowded. Some schools support this habit by creating marked paths and organizing supervised walking groups.

Part A

Write the central idea in one complete sentence.

Part B

List two details that support the central idea.

Part C

Choose the stronger detail and explain why it supports the central idea well.
