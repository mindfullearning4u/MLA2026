Lesson 7: Argument Writing: Counterclaims, Organization, and Tone

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.02 - Write Arguments with Claims, Evidence, Reasoning, Counterclaims, Organization, and Tone.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.08 - Evaluate Arguments, Claims, and Validity; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to acknowledge a counterclaim without weakening your own claim.
  • How to organize an argument paragraph clearly.
  • How to use a respectful academic tone in argument writing.

Why This Matters

Strong arguments do not pretend other views do not exist. They address opposing ideas fairly, then explain why the writer's claim is still stronger. Organization and tone help readers follow and trust your argument.

What You Will Do

  • Review claim, evidence, and reasoning from Lesson 6.
  • Practice identifying counterclaims.
  • Write a response to a counterclaim.
  • Organize a short argument paragraph with respectful tone.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can write a clear claim with evidence and reasoning.
  • You can acknowledge a counterclaim fairly.
  • You can organize ideas in a logical order with academic tone.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.W.02 in student language: I can write an organized argument that supports my claim, responds to an opposing idea, and uses a respectful tone.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your counterclaim sounds disrespectful or if your paragraph order is hard to follow.
