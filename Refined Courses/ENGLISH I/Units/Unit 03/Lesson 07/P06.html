P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Prompt and Evidence

Prompt: Should the school add more recycling bins in hallways?

Evidence: Custodians found paper and plastic bottles in hallway trash cans every day last week.

Counterclaim: Some people may argue that students already have enough bins.

Part A

Write a clear claim that answers the prompt.

Part B

Use the evidence and explain your reasoning.

Part C

Write a respectful response to the counterclaim.
