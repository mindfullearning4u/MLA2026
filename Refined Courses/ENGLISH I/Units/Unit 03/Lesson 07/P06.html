P06 Independent Work

Use this task to practice before the checkpoint.

Prompt and Evidence

Prompt: Should the school add more recycling bins in hallways?

Evidence: Custodians found paper and plastic bottles in hallway trash cans every day last week.

Counterclaim: Some people may argue that students already have enough bins.

Part A

Write a clear claim that answers the prompt.

Part B

Use the evidence and explain your reasoning.

Part C

Write a respectful response to the counterclaim.
