P07 Checkpoint

Use this checkpoint to show mastery of MLA.ENG1.W.02.

Teacher of Record Support

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your counterclaim response sounds dismissive or if your paragraph order is unclear.

Checkpoint Task

Prompt: Should the school extend library hours one day each week?

Evidence: The library counted 180 students using the space during the final half hour before closing last month.

Counterclaim: Some may argue that later hours require extra staffing.

Submit one argument paragraph with a claim, evidence, reasoning, a counterclaim, and a respectful rebuttal.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Write and revise your checkpoint paragraph.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence and checkpoint response in the LMS.

Mastery Criteria

  • Writes a clear claim.
  • Uses evidence and reasoning.
  • Addresses a counterclaim respectfully.
  • Uses logical order and academic tone.
