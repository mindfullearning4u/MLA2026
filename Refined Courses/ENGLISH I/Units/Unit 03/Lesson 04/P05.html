P05 Guided Practice

Complete the five guided practice questions in ENG1_U03_L04_GuidedPractice.gift. Each question includes the passage you need.

Student Directions

  1. Read the full passage in each question.
  2. Identify the rhetorical choice.
  3. Ask what the choice makes the reader think, feel, or notice.
  4. Choose the answer that explains both the choice and its effect.
