P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Rhetorical Choices and Effect

Vocabulary

  • Rhetorical choice: a language or structure decision an author uses to affect readers.
  • Effect: what the choice makes readers think, feel, notice, or believe.
  • Repetition: repeating a word or phrase for emphasis.
  • Direct address: speaking directly to the reader using words such as "you" or "we."

Direct Teaching

Rhetorical analysis is more than naming a technique. If you say, "The author uses repetition," you have only started. You also need to explain what the repetition does.

Ask: Does this choice emphasize urgency? Build trust? Make the reader feel included? Make a problem seem serious? Make a solution seem practical?

A complete response follows this pattern: The author uses ___ to make readers ___ so that ___.

Notebook Directions

Passage: We can reduce hallway waste. We can carry reusable bottles. We can choose lunch containers that last. We can make small choices that keep our school cleaner every day.

  1. Identify the rhetorical choice used repeatedly.
  2. Copy the repeated words.
  3. Explain the effect on the reader.
  4. Explain how the effect supports the author's purpose.

Model

The author repeats "We can" to make the reader feel included and capable. This supports the purpose because the author wants students to believe they can help reduce waste.
