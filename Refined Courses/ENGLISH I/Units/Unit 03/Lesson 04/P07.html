P07 Checkpoint

Use this checkpoint to show mastery of MLA.ENG1.R.12.

Teacher of Record Support

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can name a rhetorical choice but cannot explain its effect on the reader.

Checkpoint Task

Passage: You know what a crowded hallway feels like. You know what it feels like to squeeze past backpacks, wait at blocked doors, and arrive late through no fault of your own. A one-way hallway plan would make passing periods calmer and safer.

Submit one paragraph that identifies a rhetorical choice, explains its effect, and connects the effect to the author's purpose.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Write your checkpoint paragraph.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence and checkpoint response in the LMS.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies a rhetorical choice accurately.
  • Explains the effect on the reader.
  • Connects the effect to author purpose.
  • Uses clear, complete sentences.
