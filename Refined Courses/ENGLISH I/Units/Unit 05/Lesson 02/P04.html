P04 Worked Example

Use word parts and word families step by step.

Example 1: Derivation

Word: revision

Related words: revise, revised, revising

Step 1: Notice the shared base vis, connected to seeing or looking.

Step 2: Connect the word family to writing. To revise means to look again at writing and improve it.

Meaning: Revision is the process of looking again and improving a piece of writing.

Example 2: Prefix

Word: reconstruct

Known word: construct means build.

Prefix: re- means again.

Meaning: Reconstruct means build again.

Example 3: Suffix

Word: credible

Related word: credit can connect to belief or trust.

Suffix: -ible can mean able to be.

Meaning: Credible means able to be believed or trusted.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Because construct means build, constructive must mean a building.

This ignores the suffix and the sentence. Constructive feedback means feedback that helps improve or build a better draft.

Correct Approach

Correct: Use the root meaning, the suffix, and the sentence context together.

The word-family clue starts your thinking, but the sentence confirms the meaning.
