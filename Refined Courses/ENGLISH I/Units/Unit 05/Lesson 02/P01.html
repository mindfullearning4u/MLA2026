Lesson 2: Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.V.02 - Apply Etymology and Derivations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How word parts can help you understand unfamiliar academic words.
  • How etymology shows where a word comes from.
  • How derivations and word families help you connect related words.

Why This Matters

You will not know every word you see in high school reading. Word families give you a way to make an informed meaning prediction instead of guessing randomly or stopping every time you meet a new word.

What You Will Do

  • Break academic words into roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Use word families to connect meaning across related words.
  • Apply etymology and derivation clues to sentences from school reading and writing.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify useful word parts.
  • You can explain how a related word helps with meaning.
  • You can choose a meaning that fits both the word parts and the sentence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to use word history and related word forms to figure out what academic words mean.
