P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply the revision skill to a second draft.

Draft for Revision

Draft: Students need support. The article gives examples. The examples are useful.

Notebook Directions

  1. Identify the clarity or connection problem.
  2. Write a revised version.
  3. Explain one exact revision choice.
  4. Check that the meaning stayed the same.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Rewriting the draft into a new idea instead of improving the original idea.

Revision should make the writer's meaning clearer, not replace it with a different message.

Correct Approach

Correct: Keep the original idea, then improve clarity, connection, and sentence control.
