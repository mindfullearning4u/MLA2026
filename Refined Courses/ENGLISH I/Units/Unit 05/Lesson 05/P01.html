P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 5: Feedback and Revision for Clarity

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Feedback and Revision for Clarity

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.W.02 - Write Arguments with Evidence; MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use feedback to revise writing for clarity.
  • How to keep the writer's meaning while improving the draft.
  • How to explain why a revision helps the reader.

Why This Matters

Revision is how writers make meaning easier to follow. Strong revisions do more than fix surface errors; they improve clarity, connection, and reader understanding.

What You Will Do

  • Study short drafts.
  • Choose useful revisions.
  • Explain revision decisions in academic language.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify a clarity or cohesion problem.
  • You can choose a revision that improves meaning.
  • You can explain how the revision helps the reader.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to revise writing so ideas are clear, connected, and easy for readers to follow.
