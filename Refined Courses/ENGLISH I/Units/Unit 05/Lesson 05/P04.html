P04 Worked Example

Follow the revision thinking step by step.

Example 1: Identify the Problem

Draft: The source is good and it helps.

Problem: The words good and helps are too vague for a reader.

Example 2: Revise with Purpose

Revision: The source is useful because it gives survey results about student homework habits.

This revision names why the source helps.

Example 3: Explain the Revision

The revision improves clarity because it replaces vague words with specific information.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The source is super amazing and helps everything.

This is still vague and adds exaggeration.

Correct Approach

Correct: The source is useful because it gives survey results about student homework habits.

The revision is specific and keeps the meaning controlled.
