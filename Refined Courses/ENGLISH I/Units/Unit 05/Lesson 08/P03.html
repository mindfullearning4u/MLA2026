P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continue the notebook thinking with a deeper application.

Draft for Application

Draft: The article gives some good information, and it is helpful because students can use it.

Notebook Directions

  1. Identify two vague or weak parts of the draft.
  2. Revise the draft for precision and clarity.
  3. Explain one word choice or sentence change.
  4. Check that your revision keeps the original meaning controlled.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Adding dramatic language that sounds stronger but changes the meaning.

Revision should improve meaning, not exaggerate it.

Correct Approach

Correct: Replace vague wording with exact wording that fits the evidence, audience, and purpose.
