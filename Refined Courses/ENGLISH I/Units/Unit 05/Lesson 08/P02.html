P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 05 Synthesis: Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision

Vocabulary: conventions, etymology, derivation, connotation, denotation, context clue, cohesion, transition, revision, style, precision

This notebook task asks you to use several skills from Lessons 1-7 at the same time. Do not answer by naming one isolated skill. Read the passage or scenario, decide what the task requires, and connect evidence, vocabulary, and reasoning in one response.

Integrated Text

Passage / Scenario: Draft Sentence Set: The club made a plan to conserve resources during the school festival. The idea was good because it helped. Students placed signs near the recycling bins, but some signs were unclear and did not explain what belonged in each bin. One volunteer suggested using more precise words, stronger transitions, and correct punctuation so visitors could follow the directions easily.

Synthesis Focus: Read the draft as a revision problem. Identify unclear wording, convention issues, weak transitions, and opportunities to use word knowledge for more precise meaning.

Notebook Directions

  1. Paraphrase the main situation or central idea in your own words.
  2. Identify at least two unit skills needed to respond well.
  3. Select one relevant detail as evidence or support.
  4. Use at least two unit vocabulary terms correctly.
  5. Explain how the evidence connects to your interpretation, claim, explanation, or communication purpose.

Model Start

A strong response begins by naming the task in your own words, then connecting the most important evidence to the unit skill being used. The response should sound like one complete explanation, not a list of unrelated answers.
