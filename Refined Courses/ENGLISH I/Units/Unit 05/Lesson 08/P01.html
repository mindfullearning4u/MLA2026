P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 5 Synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7 in Unit 05: Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision. It does not introduce new standards, new concepts, new vocabulary expectations, or new skills. You will use the unit skills together so you are ready to demonstrate mastery.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.V.02 - Apply Etymology and Derivations; MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts

What You Will Learn

  • Apply standard English conventions in authentic writing.
  • Use etymology, derivations, and word families to determine meaning.
  • Use context, connotation, denotation, and word relationships to choose precise words.
  • Revise writing for clarity, cohesion, style, transitions, and sentence control.
  • Use feedback to improve meaning without changing the writer's purpose.
  • Polish writing so it is ready for a reader.

What You Will Do

You will work with one connected unit task instead of separate review questions. Each page asks you to combine Unit 05 skills, vocabulary, reasoning, and communication.

The goal is not to remember one isolated skill. The goal is to show that you can use several English I skills together when a text-based task requires them.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery when you can complete an integrated response that applies the Unit 05 standards, uses relevant evidence or support, explains reasoning clearly, uses academic vocabulary accurately, and follows standard English conventions. The Unit Assessment mastery requirement is 80% or higher.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can combine the skills from Unit 05 to read, think, write, revise, and communicate clearly for a specific purpose.
