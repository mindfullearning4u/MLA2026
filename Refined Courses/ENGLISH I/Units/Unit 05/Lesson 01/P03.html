P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use conventions to improve a short authentic draft.

Read the Draft

Draft: Online calendars helps students track assignments they can check due dates after class. The reminders is useful because it shows what work are missing.

This draft has several convention problems. You do not need to rewrite every word. Your goal is to fix the problems that block clear meaning.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the heading Convention Revision Notes.
  2. List three convention problems you notice.
  3. Label each problem: agreement, sentence boundary, pronoun clarity, punctuation, or capitalization.
  4. Write a revised version of the draft.
  5. Underline or mark the words you changed.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Changing the meaning while fixing grammar.

If the draft says calendars help students track assignments, do not revise it into a claim that calendars solve every school problem. Correct conventions without inventing a new message.

Correct Approach

Correct: Revise for agreement, sentence boundaries, and clarity while keeping the original idea.
