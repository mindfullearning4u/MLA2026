P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Conventions Make Meaning Clear

Vocabulary

  • Convention: a standard rule for language, such as grammar, punctuation, capitalization, or sentence structure.
  • Subject-verb agreement: the subject and verb match in number.
  • Pronoun clarity: a pronoun clearly points to the noun it replaces.
  • Sentence boundary: the place where one complete sentence ends and another begins.

Direct Teaching

When you revise conventions, do not begin by asking, "What rule can I name?" Begin by asking, "What might confuse the reader?" Then use the rule to fix the confusion.

Step 1: Read the full sentence or short draft.

Step 2: Find the part that makes the meaning unclear.

Step 3: Decide which convention controls the problem.

Step 4: Revise only what needs to be revised. Keep the writer's meaning the same.

Notebook Task

Copy the sentence below. Then answer the three questions.

Sentence: The article explain how school gardens helps students understand science vocabulary.

  1. What part sounds incorrect?
  2. Which convention controls the problem?
  3. Write a corrected version of the sentence.

Model start: The verb explain does not match the subject article.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Read the vocabulary and make sure you can say each term in your own words.
  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: Work with one sentence, term, or example at a time. Standard path: complete the full task and explain your reasoning. Accelerated path: add a second example, compare choices, or explain why a weaker answer does not meet the standard.
