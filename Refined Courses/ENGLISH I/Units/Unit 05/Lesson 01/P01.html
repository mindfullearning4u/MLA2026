P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 1: Standard English Conventions in Authentic Writing

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Standard English Conventions in Authentic Writing

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion.

What You Will Learn

  • How standard English conventions help readers understand your ideas.
  • How to check subject-verb agreement, pronoun clarity, sentence boundaries, punctuation, and capitalization in real writing.
  • How to revise sentences without changing the writer's meaning.

Why This Matters

Conventions are not separate from meaning. A missing comma, unclear pronoun, sentence fragment, or incorrect verb can make a strong idea confusing. In this lesson, you will edit writing the way writers actually use conventions: to make ideas clear for readers.

What You Will Do

  • Study sentence-level convention problems in short authentic drafts.
  • Revise sentences for grammar, punctuation, and clarity.
  • Explain why a correction improves meaning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify a convention error in a sentence or short draft.
  • You can choose a corrected version that keeps the intended meaning.
  • You can explain how the correction makes the writing clearer.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to make your writing follow standard English so readers can understand your ideas without getting distracted by errors.
