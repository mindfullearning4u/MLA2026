P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Cohesion, Transitions, and Sentence-Level Control

Vocabulary

  • Cohesion: the way ideas connect smoothly.
  • Transition: a word or phrase that shows a relationship between ideas.
  • Sentence control: using sentence structure to make meaning clear.
  • Relationship: the connection between ideas, such as cause, contrast, example, or addition.

Direct Teaching

Read the whole draft before revising. First decide what the reader may not understand. Then choose a revision that solves that exact problem.

A strong revision keeps the original meaning but makes the sentence or paragraph clearer.

Notebook Task

Draft: Tutoring helps students. The survey showed higher confidence. In your notebook, add a transition and revise the two ideas into a clearer connection.

Label your response: problem, revision, explanation.
