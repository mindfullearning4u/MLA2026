Lesson 3: Context, Connotation, Denotation, and Tone

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.09 - Explain Figurative Language and Mood; MLA.ENG1.R.12 - Explain Rhetorical Choices; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How context helps you choose the best meaning of a word.
  • How denotation and connotation shape tone.
  • How word choice affects mood in literature and rhetoric in informational text.

Why This Matters

A word can be technically correct but still create the wrong feeling. Careful readers look at both definition and effect.

What You Will Do

  • Use context clues to determine word meaning.
  • Compare neutral, positive, and negative connotations.
  • Explain how word choice contributes to tone or mood.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify a word's denotation.
  • You can explain its connotation in context.
  • You can connect word choice to tone or mood.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how word meaning changes in context and how authors use word choice to create an effect.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can identify the dictionary meaning but cannot explain the tone or feeling created by the word.
