P04 Worked Example

Work from meaning to effect.

Example 1: Denotation

Sentence: The speaker called the plan ambitious.

Step 1: The denotation means the plan has a big goal or requires effort.

Step 2: This word is more positive than impossible or reckless.

Example 2: Connotation

Word set: curious, nosy, interested.

All three can describe wanting to know something. Nosy has a negative connotation because it suggests someone is interfering.

Example 3: Tone

Sentence: The article claims the proposal is a reckless experiment.

The word reckless creates a critical tone because it suggests danger and poor judgment.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Reckless means the same thing as ambitious because both describe a plan.

This ignores connotation. The words may describe plans, but they create different attitudes.

Correct Approach

Correct: Ambitious can sound admiring, while reckless sounds critical.
