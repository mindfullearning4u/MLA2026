P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Style, Precision, and Publication Polish

Vocabulary

  • Style: the way a writer uses language.
  • Precision: using exact words instead of vague ones.
  • Polish: final revision that prepares writing for readers.
  • Synthesis: combining related skills into one complete task.

Direct Teaching

Strong revision does not mean changing every sentence. It means finding the words, transitions, or conventions that affect meaning and improving those parts first.

When you revise, ask: Is the meaning clear? Is the wording precise? Does the tone fit the audience? Are the sentences controlled?
