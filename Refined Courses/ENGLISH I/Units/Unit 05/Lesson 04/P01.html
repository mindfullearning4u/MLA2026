Lesson 4: Word Relationships, Reference Materials, and Precise Meaning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.V.02 - Apply Etymology and Derivations; MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How synonyms, antonyms, and shades of meaning help you choose precise words.
  • How to use reference materials to confirm meaning and usage.
  • How precise meaning supports paraphrase and revision.

Why This Matters

Words that seem similar can carry different levels of strength, formality, or accuracy. Strong writers and readers choose the word that fits the exact meaning.

What You Will Do

  • Compare related words.
  • Use dictionary and thesaurus thinking responsibly.
  • Choose precise words that fit context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify precise meaning in context.
  • You can choose the best synonym for a sentence.
  • You can explain how reference materials support word choice.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to choose and explain exact word meanings instead of using words that are only close enough.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if two words seem similar but you cannot decide which one fits the sentence best.
