P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use reference-material thinking to confirm meaning.

Reference Entry

analyze verb: to examine something carefully by separating it into parts.

summarize verb: to state the main points briefly.

evaluate verb: to judge the value or quality of something.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the three definitions.
  2. Write one sentence explaining how analyze differs from summarize.
  3. Write one sentence explaining how evaluate differs from analyze.
  4. Choose the best word for this task: "_____ how the author builds the argument." Explain your answer.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Choosing a synonym because it sounds more advanced.

A word is only strong if it fits the task and sentence precisely.

Correct Approach

Correct: Use the definition, sentence context, and task purpose together before choosing a word.

Deeper Learning Support

Lesson focus: Word relationships, reference materials, and precise meaning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG1.V.03

Use this second-pass routine to check the quality of your thinking before you submit your notebook work.

  1. Read the whole sentence before deciding what a word means. Context often gives clues before and after the word.
  2. Separate literal meaning from feeling or tone. Denotation is the basic meaning; connotation is the feeling a word creates.
  3. Use word parts, roots, prefixes, suffixes, and related words when context alone is not enough.
  4. Choose the meaning that fits the passage, not just the first dictionary definition. Precise vocabulary must match the text and purpose.
