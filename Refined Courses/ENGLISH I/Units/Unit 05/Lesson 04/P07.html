P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can use word relationships and reference thinking to choose precise meaning.

Checkpoint Task

Sentence: The speaker _____ the plan because the evidence was weak.

Choices: praised, questioned, whispered, decorated.

In your notebook, choose the most precise word, explain why it fits the sentence, and explain why two other choices do not fit.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If you are unsure what to submit, ask your TOR before submitting the checkpoint.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your chosen word fits the sentence context.
  • Your explanation shows precise meaning.
  • Your response explains why weaker choices do not fit.
  • Your writing uses clear academic language.
