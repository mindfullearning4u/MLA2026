P06 Independent Work

Create a paired slide note and speaker note before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Presentation focus: Source checks improve research quality.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Write one concise slide note.

Part B: Write a 2-3 sentence speaker note that explains the slide.

Part C: Write one publication polish check for conventions, word choice, or audience fit.

Before You Continue

Check that the slide note is not a full paragraph and the speaker note does not simply repeat the slide.
