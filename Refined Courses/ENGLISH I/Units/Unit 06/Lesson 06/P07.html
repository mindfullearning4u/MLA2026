P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can integrate oral explanation and digital slide support.

Checkpoint Task

Presentation focus: Revision makes writing clearer for readers.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One concise slide note.
  2. One 2-3 sentence speaker note that explains the slide.
  3. One word-choice decision that helps the audience understand the idea.
  4. One publication polish check before sharing the presentation.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If your slide note and speaker note repeat each other or do not connect, ask your TOR before submitting.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your slide note is concise and clear.
  • Your speaker note explains rather than repeats the slide.
  • Your word choice fits the audience and purpose.
  • Your polish check supports publication-ready communication.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).
