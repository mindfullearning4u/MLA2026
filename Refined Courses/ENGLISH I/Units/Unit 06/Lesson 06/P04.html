P04 Worked Example

Watch how slides and oral explanation work together.

Example 1: Slide Text

Purpose: Explain why revision matters.

Slide note: Revision improves clarity and cohesion.

Why it works: The slide note is brief, precise, and connected to the presentation's focus.

Example 2: Speaker Note

Speaker note: Revision is not just fixing mistakes. It helps a writer make ideas easier to follow by improving word choice, transitions, and explanations.

The speaker note adds explanation that does not need to appear on the slide.

Example 3: Publication Polish

Check: The slide uses standard English, precise vocabulary, and a clear phrase. The speaker note explains the idea without repeating the slide word for word.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Put the entire speaker note on the slide and read it aloud.

This makes the slide crowded and gives the audience no reason to listen to the speaker's explanation.

Correct Approach

Correct: Use a concise slide note and a fuller speaker note that explains the meaning.

This lets the digital and oral parts support each other.
