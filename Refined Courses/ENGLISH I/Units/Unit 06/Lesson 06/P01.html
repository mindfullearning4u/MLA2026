P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally; MLA.ENG1.C.02 - Create Digital Presentations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning; MLA.ENG1.C.03 - Publish Collaborative Writing.

What You Will Learn

  • How oral delivery and digital slides work together.
  • How to decide what belongs on the slide and what belongs in the spoken explanation.
  • How word choice affects audience understanding.
  • How conventions and publication polish support communication.

Why This Matters

In a strong presentation, slides do not compete with the speaker. Slides guide the audience, and the speaker explains the meaning. When oral and digital communication match, the presentation is easier to follow and more professional.

What You Will Do

  • Compare slide notes with speaker notes.
  • Revise communication plans so slides and oral explanation align.
  • Choose precise words for slide text and spoken explanation.
  • Check digital work for publication-ready clarity.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can align slide text with oral explanation.
  • You can identify what information belongs on a slide and what should be spoken.
  • You can revise wording for clarity, precision, and audience understanding.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to make your spoken explanation and digital slides work together so your audience understands your message clearly.
