P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Use your notebook to separate slide text from oral explanation.

Notebook Title

Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Vocabulary

  • Speaker note: what the speaker plans to say aloud.
  • Slide note: short text that appears on a slide.
  • Integration: combining parts so they work together.
  • Publication-ready: polished enough for an intended audience.

Direct Teaching

A slide should not contain everything the speaker says. Instead, the slide gives the audience a clear anchor, and the speaker explains the idea.

Slide note: concise phrase or evidence point.

Speaker note: complete explanation, connection, or example.

If the slide and speaker note are identical, the speaker may only be reading. If they are unrelated, the audience may feel lost.

Notebook Directions

Slide note: Peer feedback reveals unclear claims.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One speaker note that explains this slide in 2-3 sentences.
  2. One word or phrase you would emphasize aloud.
  3. One reason the slide note should stay shorter than the speaker note.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Read the vocabulary and make sure you can say each term in your own words.
  2. Step 2: Study the direct teaching before you try the task.
  3. Step 3: Follow the model and notice how the thinking moves from evidence to explanation.
  4. Step 4: Complete the practice one part at a time instead of skipping ahead.
  5. Step 5: Check your answer against the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: Work with one sentence, term, or example at a time. Standard path: complete the full task and explain your reasoning. Accelerated path: add a second example, compare choices, or explain why a weaker answer does not meet the standard.
