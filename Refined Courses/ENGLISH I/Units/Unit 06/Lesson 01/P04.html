P04 Worked Example

Watch how a speaker turns a topic into a focused oral communication plan.

Example 1: Identify the Purpose

Task: Give a short presentation to classmates about the value of joining a school activity.

Step 1: Ask what the speaker is trying to do.

Answer: The purpose is to explain and encourage. The speaker wants classmates to understand the value of school activities and consider joining one.

Example 2: Name the Audience

Audience: classmates who may not participate in school activities.

Step 1: Think about what this audience may care about.

Step 2: Choose language that feels respectful and relatable.

Audience-aware choice: Use examples like meeting people, learning skills, and finding support at school.

Example 3: Create a Clear Perspective

Weak topic only: School activities.

Step 1: Add a point of view.

Step 2: Make the sentence specific enough to guide the whole presentation.

Clear perspective: Joining a school activity can help students feel connected while building skills they can use outside the classroom.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: My presentation is about clubs and activities.

This is only a topic. It does not show purpose, audience awareness, or a clear perspective. The listener cannot tell what point the speaker plans to make.

Correct Approach

Correct: I will explain to classmates how joining a school activity can help students feel connected and build useful skills.

This version names the purpose, audience, and perspective, so the presentation has a clear direction.
