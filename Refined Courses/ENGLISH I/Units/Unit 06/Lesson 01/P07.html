Checkpoint Task

Scenario: You will give a short presentation to classmates about one way students can improve their learning routine.

In your notebook, write:

  1. The audience.
  2. The purpose.
  3. A clear perspective statement.
  4. Two audience-aware language choices you would use.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your purpose clearly explains what the presentation will do.
  • Your audience is specific.
  • Your perspective statement is a complete, focused sentence.
  • Your language choices fit the audience and task.
