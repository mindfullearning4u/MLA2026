P06 Independent Work

Prepare a short oral communication plan before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Choose one topic: study habits, school lunch choices, phone use during homework, or joining a school activity. Then complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Write the audience for your presentation and explain why that audience matters.

Part B: Write the purpose of the presentation in one sentence.

Part C: Write a clear perspective statement that could guide a one-minute oral presentation.

Before You Continue

Check that your perspective statement is not just a topic. It should tell the listener what you believe, recommend, or want them to understand.
