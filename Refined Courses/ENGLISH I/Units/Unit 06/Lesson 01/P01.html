Lesson 1: Oral Communication Purpose, Audience, and Perspective

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the purpose of an oral presentation.
  • How to adjust a message for a specific audience.
  • How to state a clear perspective before adding details.
  • How academic vocabulary and standard English help a speaker sound clear and prepared.

Why This Matters

A strong presentation is not just talking in front of people. A speaker has to know why they are speaking, who is listening, and what perspective the audience should understand by the end. When purpose, audience, and perspective are clear, the presentation becomes easier to organize and easier to follow.

What You Will Do

  • Study short presentation situations.
  • Decide the speaker's purpose and audience.
  • Write a clear perspective statement for an oral response.
  • Practice choosing language that fits the audience and task.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify purpose, audience, and perspective in an oral communication task.
  • You can write a focused perspective statement for a short presentation.
  • You can explain how language choices should change for different audiences.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to prepare an oral presentation that has a clear reason, fits the audience, and communicates a focused point of view.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you are not sure who the audience is, what the presentation is supposed to do, or how to turn your idea into a clear perspective statement.
