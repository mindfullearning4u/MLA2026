P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can plan an oral presentation with a clear perspective and coherent focus.

Checkpoint Task

Topic: using feedback to improve writing.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One focused perspective about the topic.
  2. Three supporting details that stay connected to the perspective.
  3. One opposing view.
  4. One response that acknowledges the opposing view and returns to your perspective.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If you are not sure whether a detail supports your perspective, ask your TOR before submitting the checkpoint.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your perspective is clear and focused.
  • Your details support the same perspective.
  • Your opposing view is fair and brief.
  • Your response returns to the main focus instead of drifting away.
