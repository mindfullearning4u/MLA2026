P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Use your notebook to narrow a broad presentation topic into one focused perspective.

Notebook Title

Clear Perspective and Coherent Focus

Vocabulary

  • Perspective: the speaker's clear point of view.
  • Coherent focus: a presentation that stays connected to one main idea.
  • Rhetoric: the choices a speaker uses to achieve a purpose.
  • Opposing view: a different position on the same topic.

Direct Teaching

A focused presentation begins with one controlling perspective. Every major detail should answer this question: How does this help my audience understand my point?

Step 1: Start with a topic.

Step 2: Write one perspective about the topic.

Step 3: Keep only details that support that perspective.

Step 4: Remove or revise details that move the audience away from the main point.

Notebook Directions

Topic: student study groups

Broad idea: Study groups are useful.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One focused perspective about study groups.
  2. Three supporting details that match the perspective.
  3. One detail that might be interesting but does not belong because it would distract from the focus.

Model perspective: Study groups can help students prepare for quizzes because they allow students to explain ideas, ask questions, and compare notes.
