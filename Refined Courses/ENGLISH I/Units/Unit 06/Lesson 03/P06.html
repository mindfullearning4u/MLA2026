P06 Independent Work

Prepare a focused oral presentation plan before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Topic: students using class time wisely.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Write one focused perspective about the topic.

Part B: Write three details that support your perspective.

Part C: Write one opposing view and one sentence that responds while returning to your main perspective.

Before You Continue

Check every detail by asking: Does this help prove or explain my perspective? If not, revise or remove it.
