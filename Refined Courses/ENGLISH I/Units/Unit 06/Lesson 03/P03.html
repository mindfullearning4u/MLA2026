P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now practice keeping focus when an opposing view appears.

Deeper Application

A focused speaker can acknowledge a different view without letting that different view take over the presentation.

Perspective: Students should create a weekly study plan because it helps them prepare before deadlines arrive.

Opposing view: Some students say planning takes too much time.

A weak response would ignore the opposing view or switch topics. A strong response would connect back to the original perspective.

Notebook Task

In your notebook, write a three-part response:

  1. Acknowledge: Write one sentence that fairly states the opposing view.
  2. Respond: Write one sentence that answers the opposing view.
  3. Return: Write one sentence that returns to the main perspective.

Keep all three sentences connected to the same presentation focus.

Self-Check

If your response spends more time on the opposing view than on your perspective, revise it so your main point stays in control.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a general answer without naming the specific evidence, skill, or reader effect.

Correct: Name the exact word, detail, structure, or choice from the lesson and explain how it supports meaning, clarity, or the reader.

Why this matters: English I mastery requires more than a correct-sounding answer. You need to show the thinking that connects evidence to explanation.

Deeper Learning Support

Lesson focus: Presenting a clear perspective with coherent focus

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG1.C.01

Use this second-pass routine to check the quality of your thinking before you submit your notebook work.

  1. Start by identifying the audience and purpose. What the audience needs determines what information should be included.
  2. Organize ideas so listeners or viewers can follow the message without rereading. Use a clear beginning, connected evidence, and a focused ending.
  3. Choose words, visuals, and examples that support the message instead of distracting from it.
  4. Check that the final communication is accurate, respectful, and easy to understand for the intended audience.
