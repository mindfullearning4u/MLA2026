P06 Independent Work

Prepare evidence support for a digital presentation slide.

Instructions

Perspective: Feedback helps students improve final writing.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Choose one credible evidence source that could support the perspective.

Part B: Write a concise slide note using that evidence.

Part C: Write one sentence explaining how you would explain the evidence aloud for ninth-grade students.

Before You Continue

Check that your slide note is short enough to read quickly and specific enough to support the perspective.
