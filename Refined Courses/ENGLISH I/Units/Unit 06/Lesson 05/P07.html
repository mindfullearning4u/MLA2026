P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can support a digital presentation with credible, clear, audience-aware evidence.

Checkpoint Task

Perspective: Source checks help students choose stronger research evidence.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One credible evidence source that could support the perspective.
  2. One concise slide note using that evidence.
  3. One oral explanation sentence for a ninth-grade audience.
  4. One revision note explaining how you made the slide clearer.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If you are not sure whether your evidence is credible or your slide note is clear, ask your TOR before submitting.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your evidence source is credible and relevant.
  • Your slide note is concise and clear.
  • Your oral explanation fits the audience.
  • Your revision note explains how clarity improved.
