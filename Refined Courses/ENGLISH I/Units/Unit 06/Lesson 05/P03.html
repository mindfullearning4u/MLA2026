P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now revise a slide note for clarity and audience understanding.

Deeper Application

Weak slide note: The survey that happened with the students and calendars and stuff shows that calendars are useful for lots of reasons when assignments happen.

This note is too long, vague, and informal. A viewer may not know what the evidence proves.

Clearer slide note: Student survey: written due-date reminders help students track assignments.

Notebook Task

Weak slide note: Feedback from other people can kind of help because they tell you things and you can fix them later before turning it in.

In your notebook, write:

  1. A revised slide note with 12 words or fewer.
  2. One sentence explaining how your revision is clearer.
  3. One sentence explaining how the speaker should explain the slide orally.

Self-Check

If your slide note sounds like a paragraph, shorten it. If it sounds like a label with no meaning, add one precise detail.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a general answer without naming the specific evidence, skill, or reader effect.

Correct: Name the exact word, detail, structure, or choice from the lesson and explain how it supports meaning, clarity, or the reader.

Why this matters: English I mastery requires more than a correct-sounding answer. You need to show the thinking that connects evidence to explanation.
