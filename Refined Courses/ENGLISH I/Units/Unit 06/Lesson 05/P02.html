P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Use your notebook to choose evidence for a digital presentation slide.

Notebook Title

Digital Presentation Evidence, Clarity, and Audience Awareness

Vocabulary

  • Credible evidence: trustworthy information that supports a point.
  • Audience awareness: choosing words and details that fit the listeners or viewers.
  • Clarity: wording that is easy to understand.
  • Cohesion: ideas that connect smoothly.

Direct Teaching

A strong evidence slide does three things.

Step 1: It uses credible evidence from a reliable source.

Step 2: It keeps wording brief enough for a slide.

Step 3: It makes the evidence clearly support the presentation's perspective.

Do not place a full paragraph on a slide. Use a short evidence note, then explain the evidence orally.

Notebook Directions

Perspective: Weekly planning helps students manage long-term assignments.

Evidence choices:

  • A teacher reports that students who write interim due dates ask fewer last-minute questions.
  • A planner has a blue cover.
  • A student survey shows many students forget due dates when they do not record them.

In your notebook, write the two best evidence choices and explain why each one is credible or useful.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two possible answers, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
