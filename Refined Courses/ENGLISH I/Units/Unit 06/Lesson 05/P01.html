Lesson 5: Digital Presentation Evidence, Clarity, and Audience Awareness

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.C.02 - Create Digital Presentations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to choose credible evidence for a digital presentation.
  • How to revise slide text for clarity and audience understanding.
  • How to use academic vocabulary without overloading a slide.
  • How to make evidence useful instead of decorative.

Why This Matters

A digital presentation should help the audience understand the speaker's message. Evidence must be credible, clear, and connected to the perspective. If a slide is crowded, vague, or unsupported, the audience may miss the point.

What You Will Do

  • Evaluate evidence choices for digital slides.
  • Revise slide text so it is concise and clear.
  • Match vocabulary and explanation to the audience.
  • Explain how evidence supports a presentation perspective.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify credible evidence for a digital presentation.
  • You can revise slide text for clarity and cohesion.
  • You can explain why evidence fits the audience and perspective.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to create digital presentations that use reliable evidence and clear wording so an audience can understand your perspective.
