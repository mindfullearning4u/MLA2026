Lesson 7: Full-Course Synthesis for Presentation Readiness

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally; MLA.ENG1.C.02 - Create Digital Presentations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.W.03 - Write Expository Texts; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.C.03 - Publish Collaborative Writing.

What You Will Learn

  • How to check a presentation for readiness before final synthesis.
  • How research, explanation, revision, and publication polish support communication.
  • How to make oral delivery and digital slides work as one clear message.

Why This Matters

Before a presentation is ready, the speaker must check more than one part. The message needs a clear perspective, credible support, organized slides, useful speaker notes, and polished wording. This lesson helps you prepare for the final Unit 6 synthesis.

What You Will Do

  • Use a readiness checklist for a presentation plan.
  • Revise slide notes and speaker notes for clarity.
  • Check evidence and organization before final assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify whether a presentation is ready for an audience.
  • You can explain what revision is needed before publication or delivery.
  • You can connect slide support, oral explanation, evidence, and audience needs.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are learning how to check whether your oral and digital presentation work together clearly enough for an audience.

Ask TOR for Help
