P04 Worked Example

Watch how a presentation plan becomes ready for an audience.

Example 1: Check the Perspective

Weak: My topic is presentations.

Ready: A clear presentation uses concise slides and speaker notes that explain the message.

The ready version gives a perspective that can guide the full presentation.

Example 2: Check Slide and Speaker Note

Slide note: Concise slides guide the audience.

Speaker note: A concise slide gives listeners a main idea to follow, while the speaker explains details and examples aloud.

The two parts work together instead of repeating each other.

Example 3: Check Final Polish

Ready check: The slide uses standard English, precise vocabulary, and consistent formatting. The speaker note explains the slide clearly for the audience.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A presentation is ready because all slides have words on them.

Having words on slides is not enough. The slides must support a clear message, use credible evidence, and work with the speaker's explanation.

Correct Approach

Correct: A presentation is ready when the perspective, evidence, slide order, speaker notes, and polish all support the same audience-focused message.

This checks the whole communication product, not just one piece.
