P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can prepare a presentation for final synthesis.

Checkpoint Task

Presentation focus: Revision improves communication for a specific audience.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One clear perspective statement.
  2. One five-slide outline.
  3. One speaker note for a support slide.
  4. Three readiness checks: evidence, clarity, and publication polish.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If you cannot tell whether your presentation is ready for the final Unit 6 synthesis, ask your TOR before submitting.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your perspective is clear.
  • Your slide outline is organized and coherent.
  • Your speaker note explains a slide instead of repeating it.
  • Your readiness checks address evidence, clarity, and polish.

Checkpoint Submission: Submit your checkpoint response separately so your Teacher of Record can review your mastery evidence.
