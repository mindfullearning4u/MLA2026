P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Use your notebook to check presentation readiness.

Notebook Title

Presentation Readiness Checklist

Vocabulary

  • Readiness: prepared enough for the intended task and audience.
  • Synthesis: combining skills or ideas into one complete product.
  • Polish: final revision for clarity, conventions, and audience fit.
  • Delivery: how a speaker presents ideas aloud.

Direct Teaching

A presentation is ready when the parts work together. Use this sequence:

Step 1: Check that the perspective is clear.

Step 2: Check that evidence supports the perspective.

Step 3: Check that slides are concise and organized.

Step 4: Check that speaker notes explain the slides.

Step 5: Check final polish for audience and conventions.

Notebook Directions

Presentation plan: A student has a slide titled "Revision helps" and a speaker note that says, "Revision helps because it helps."

In your notebook, write three readiness problems and one revision for each problem.
