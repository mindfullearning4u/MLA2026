P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Use your notebook to plan the structure of a digital presentation.

Notebook Title

Digital Presentation Organization and Coherent Ideas

Vocabulary

  • Digital presentation: a presentation created with slides, visuals, or digital media.
  • Coherent ideas: ideas that connect clearly and support one purpose.
  • Slide sequence: the order of slides in a presentation.
  • Text structure: the way information is organized to create meaning.

Direct Teaching

Digital slides should help the audience follow the message. A strong slide order usually moves through a clear structure.

Step 1: Introduce the topic and perspective.

Step 2: Present the first major idea.

Step 3: Present the next major idea in a logical order.

Step 4: End by returning to the perspective.

The slide order should match the purpose. A cause-effect presentation needs a different order from a problem-solution presentation.

Notebook Directions

Digital presentation topic: how weekly planning helps students manage assignments.

In your notebook, write a four-slide sequence:

  1. Slide 1: topic and clear perspective.
  2. Slide 2: first major idea.
  3. Slide 3: second major idea.
  4. Slide 4: final takeaway.

Each slide note should be one sentence or phrase. Do not write full paragraphs for slides.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Use this sequence before you complete the notebook task. It shows how to approach the lesson skill without guessing or skipping steps.

  1. Start by identifying the audience and purpose. What the audience needs determines what information should be included.
  2. Organize ideas so listeners or viewers can follow the message without rereading. Use a clear beginning, connected evidence, and a focused ending.
  3. Choose words, visuals, and examples that support the message instead of distracting from it.
  4. Check that the final communication is accurate, respectful, and easy to understand for the intended audience.
