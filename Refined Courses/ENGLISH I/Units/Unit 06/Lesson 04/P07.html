P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can organize a digital presentation with coherent ideas.

Checkpoint Task

Digital presentation topic: helping students use feedback before submitting final work.

In your notebook, write:

  1. The best structure for this presentation.
  2. A clear perspective statement.
  3. A five-slide outline that follows the structure.
  4. One sentence explaining why the slide order is coherent.

Submission Workflow

Submit your notebook evidence in the course submission area.

Ask TOR for Help

If your slide outline feels like a list instead of a structured presentation, ask your TOR for help before submitting.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your structure fits the presentation purpose.
  • Your slide sequence develops coherent ideas.
  • Your outline supports one clear perspective.
  • Your explanation shows why the order makes sense.
