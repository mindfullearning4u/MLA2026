P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Claim: Students should use a planning tool to manage assignments.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A

Write one reason that supports the claim.

Part B

Choose one credible evidence source that could support the reason. You may choose a teacher statement, a student survey, a school policy, or an assignment calendar.

Part C

Write a four-sentence oral sequence: claim, reason, evidence, explanation.

Before You Continue

Read your sequence aloud once. If the evidence sounds disconnected, add explanation so the audience understands why the evidence matters.

Independent Work Success Checklist

Complete the work independently, but do not guess about what to use. Any passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt needed for the task must be on this lesson page or inside the assessment question itself.

  1. Answer every part of the task in order. If there is a Part A, Part B, and Part C, each part needs its own clear response.
  2. Use evidence or details from the same page when the task requires reading, analysis, research, or revision.
  3. Explain your reasoning step by step so the reader can see how you moved from evidence to answer.
  4. Before submitting, reread for accuracy, standard alignment, complete sentences, academic vocabulary, and standard English conventions.
