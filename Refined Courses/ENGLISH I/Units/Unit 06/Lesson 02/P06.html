P06 Independent Work

Build a short oral evidence plan before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Claim: Students should use a planning tool to manage assignments.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Write one reason that supports the claim.

Part B: Choose one credible evidence source that could support the reason. You may choose a teacher statement, a student survey, a school policy, or an assignment calendar.

Part C: Write a four-sentence oral sequence: claim, reason, evidence, explanation.

Before You Continue

Read your sequence aloud once. If the evidence sounds disconnected, add explanation so the audience understands why the evidence matters.
