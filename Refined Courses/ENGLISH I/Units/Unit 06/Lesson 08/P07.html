Checkpoint Task: Unit 06 Synthesis Response

Passage / Scenario: Presentation Scenario: The school library asks students to create a brief presentation encouraging classmates to use library resources before major projects. The audience is ninth-grade students. Available evidence includes a librarian interview, a list of research databases, and a student survey showing that many students wait too long to begin research. The presentation should combine oral explanation, digital organization, credible evidence, and clear audience-focused language.

  1. Create a synthesis presentation plan for the library scenario. Include a clear perspective, credible evidence, oral organization, digital slide plan, audience awareness, and revision notes.
  2. Use relevant evidence or support from the passage or scenario.
  3. Use at least two unit vocabulary words correctly.
  4. Explain your reasoning clearly.
  5. Check your response for standard English conventions before submitting.

Both submissions are required. Notebook Evidence and Checkpoint Submission are Teacher of Record graded.

Corrections and resubmissions are allowed until you demonstrate at least 80% mastery.

The Unit Assessment becomes available only after required mastery has been demonstrated.

Mastery Criteria

  • Creates a clear oral presentation purpose, perspective, and coherent focus.
  • Uses credible evidence to support presentation ideas.
  • Plans digital presentation content that supports rather than distracts from the message.
  • Revises language and organization for audience understanding.
  • Combines oral and digital communication with standard English conventions.
