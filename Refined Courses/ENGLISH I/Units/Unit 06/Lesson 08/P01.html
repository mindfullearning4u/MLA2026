Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 6 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally; MLA.ENG1.C.02 - Create Digital Presentations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.RS.01 - Conduct Reliable Research; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Review

  • How to present ideas orally with clear organization, credible evidence, and a clear perspective.
  • How to create digital presentations with coherent ideas and a clear perspective.
  • How evidence, revision, vocabulary, and conventions support final communication.

Why This Matters

This lesson brings together the Unit 6 skills you have already practiced. You will not learn a new primary standard here. You will show that you can synthesize oral and digital communication skills in a final assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review the readiness checklist from Lessons 1-7.
  • Practice identifying strong oral and digital presentation choices.
  • Complete the Unit 6 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can evaluate oral presentation purpose, audience, perspective, organization, and evidence.
  • You can evaluate digital slide organization, clarity, evidence, and audience fit.
  • You can identify revisions that make communication more coherent and publication-ready.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You are showing that you can combine oral and digital communication skills to make a clear, audience-ready message.
