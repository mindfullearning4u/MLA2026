P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 06 Synthesis: Synthesis, Presentation, and Digital Communication

Vocabulary: audience, purpose, perspective, coherent focus, credible evidence, digital presentation, oral communication, visual support, revision, delivery

This notebook task asks you to use several skills from Lessons 1-7 at the same time. Do not answer by naming one isolated skill. Read the passage or scenario, decide what the task requires, and connect evidence, vocabulary, and reasoning in one response.

Integrated Text

Passage / Scenario: Presentation Scenario: A student team will present a proposal for a school kindness campaign. Their audience is the student council. The team has survey data showing that students notice positive messages when they are specific, visible, and repeated. They also have examples of short announcements, hallway posters, and a digital slide plan. A strong presentation must organize the idea clearly, use credible evidence, and keep the digital slides focused instead of crowded.

Synthesis Focus: Plan the presentation as one connected communication task. Identify audience, purpose, perspective, credible evidence, digital organization, and oral explanation choices.

Notebook Directions

  1. Paraphrase the main situation or central idea in your own words.
  2. Identify at least two unit skills needed to respond well.
  3. Select one relevant detail as evidence or support.
  4. Use at least two unit vocabulary terms correctly.
  5. Explain how the evidence connects to your interpretation, claim, explanation, or communication purpose.

Model Start

A strong response begins by naming the task in your own words, then connecting the most important evidence to the unit skill being used. The response should sound like one complete explanation, not a list of unrelated answers.
