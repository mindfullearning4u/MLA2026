P04 Worked Example

Review how Unit 6 skills work together.

Example 1: Oral Focus

Strong choice: The speaker states a clear perspective and uses a speaker note to explain why evidence matters.

Example 2: Digital Focus

Strong choice: The slides use concise notes, coherent order, and credible evidence instead of crowded paragraphs.

Example 3: Revision Focus

Strong choice: The student checks conventions, precise vocabulary, audience fit, and slide-speaker alignment before sharing.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treating Lesson 8 as a place to add new presentation skills.

Lesson 8 is for putting together what was already taught. Adding new primary skills here would overload the final lesson.

Correct Approach

Correct: Use Lesson 8 to synthesize Unit 6 skills: oral clarity, digital coherence, credible evidence, revision, vocabulary, and conventions.

This keeps Lesson 8 aligned with Putting It All Together.
