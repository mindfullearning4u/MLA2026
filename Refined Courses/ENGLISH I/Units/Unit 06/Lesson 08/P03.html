P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply the review chart to a final synthesis scenario.

Synthesis Scenario

A student is creating a final presentation about why source checks improve research quality. The student has a clear perspective, one source checklist, a five-slide outline, and short speaker notes.

In your notebook, write:

  1. One strength in the oral communication plan.
  2. One strength in the digital presentation plan.
  3. One revision that would improve audience readiness.

Self-Check

Make sure your answer uses skills already taught in Lessons 1-7. Do not add a new requirement.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a general answer without naming the specific evidence, skill, or reader effect.

Correct: Name the exact word, detail, structure, or choice from the lesson and explain how it supports meaning, clarity, or the reader.

Why this matters: English I mastery requires more than a correct-sounding answer. You need to show the thinking that connects evidence to explanation.
