Lesson 4: Figurative Language and Mood

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.09 - Explain Figurative Language and Mood.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.03 - Analyze Narrator Perspective; MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning; MLA.ENG1.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How figurative language means more than the literal words.
  • How simile, metaphor, personification, and imagery affect mood.
  • How word choice and narrator perspective can strengthen mood.

Why This Matters

Authors often create feeling through language. When you explain how a comparison, image, or personified detail shapes mood, you are analyzing the author's craft.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included in the lesson.
  • Identify figurative language and mood.
  • Explain how the language creates or strengthens the mood.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify figurative language in context.
  • You can name the mood of a passage.
  • You can explain how the figurative language creates that mood.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.09 in student language: I can explain how figurative language helps create a feeling or mood in a text.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can find a comparison but cannot explain the feeling it creates.
