P04 Worked Example

Study how figurative language creates mood.

Worked Example 1: Find the Figurative Language

Passage: The morning sun spilled gold across the classroom floor, and the windows blinked awake.

Figurative language: "sun spilled gold" and "windows blinked awake."

Worked Example 2: Name the Mood

Mood: warm, hopeful, and peaceful. The gold light and waking windows make the room feel alive and welcoming.

Worked Example 3: Explain the Effect

The figurative language creates a hopeful mood by making the classroom seem bright and alive. The sun does not literally spill gold, but the image makes the light feel rich and positive.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The language means the sun is made of gold.

This reads figurative language too literally.

Correct: The phrase "spilled gold" creates a warm, hopeful mood by comparing sunlight to something bright and valuable.

This explains both the figurative meaning and the mood.
