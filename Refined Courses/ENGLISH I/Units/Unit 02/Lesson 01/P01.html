Lesson 1: Literary Elements and Layers of Meaning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.01 - Analyze Literary Elements.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How characters, setting, conflict, plot, and point of view work together in a literary text.
  • How a literary element can add a deeper layer of meaning instead of only giving basic information.
  • How to use evidence from a passage to explain what an element shows.

Why This Matters

Literary analysis begins with noticing what the author built into the story. When you can explain how one element affects meaning, you are ready for theme, narrator perspective, figurative language, and later literary comparison.

What You Will Do

  • Read short literary passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify the literary element being used.
  • Paraphrase important details before analyzing them.
  • Explain how the element adds meaning or style.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can name a literary element in a passage.
  • You can support your answer with accurate evidence.
  • You can explain how the element adds a layer of meaning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.01 in student language: I can explain how story parts such as character, setting, conflict, plot, or point of view help create deeper meaning or style.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify a story detail but cannot yet explain what that detail shows.
