P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your notebook before the checkpoint.

Passage

Jalen placed the cracked trophy on the shelf beside newer awards. The gold paint had faded, but he turned it so the engraved name still faced the room. When his sister asked why he kept it, Jalen said, "That was the first time I finished something hard."

Part A

Paraphrase what happens in the passage in one or two sentences.

Part B

Identify one literary element that matters most: character, setting, conflict, plot, or dialogue. Copy or paraphrase one detail that proves your choice.

Part C

Write a short explanation of how that element adds a layer of meaning to the passage.
