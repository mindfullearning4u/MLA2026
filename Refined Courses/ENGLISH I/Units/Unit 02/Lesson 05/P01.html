Lesson 5: Epic Poetry: Character, Structure, and Theme

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.R.04 - Analyze Epic Poetry.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.01 - Analyze Literary Elements; MLA.ENG1.R.02 - Analyze Theme Development; MLA.ENG1.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • How epic poetry uses a hero, a journey, elevated language, and major conflict.
  • How structure helps organize heroic action and important choices.
  • How character actions and obstacles develop theme.

Why This Matters

Epic poetry is older and more formal than many modern stories, but it uses the same literary tools you have practiced: character, conflict, structure, theme, and precise word meaning.

What You Will Do

  • Read short epic-style passages included in the lesson.
  • Identify epic traits and heroic choices.
  • Explain how structure and conflict develop theme.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can identify epic traits in a passage.
  • You can explain how a hero's choice or conflict matters.
  • You can connect epic structure to theme.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.R.04 in student language: I can analyze how epic poetry uses heroic characters, structure, and theme to create meaning.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if the elevated language makes the passage hard to paraphrase.
