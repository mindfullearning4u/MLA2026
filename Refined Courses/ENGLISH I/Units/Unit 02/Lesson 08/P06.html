P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Use complete sentences. When you explain your thinking, make sure your evidence and reasoning are clear enough for a reader to follow.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Passage / Scenario

Passage / Scenario: The director changed an old legend about a lost sailor into a modern school play. In the legend, the sailor follows a silver bird across a stormy sea and learns that pride can make a person ignore wise warnings. In the play, a student follows online praise and ignores friends who warn that the attention is becoming harmful. The bird becomes a glowing phone screen. The storm becomes a crowded hallway where rumors spread quickly. The ending still teaches that a person must listen before confidence becomes arrogance.

Part A: Integrated Understanding

  1. Explain how the adapted play preserves the original legend's theme while changing the setting and symbols.
  2. Identify two Unit 2 skills needed for a strong response.
  3. Select one relevant detail that supports your thinking.

Part B: Evidence, Vocabulary, and Reasoning

  1. Compare the silver bird and the glowing phone screen. Explain how each symbol affects mood and meaning.
  2. Use at least two unit vocabulary terms correctly.
  3. Explain how your evidence supports your response.

Part C: Complete Synthesis Response

  1. Write a brief narrative continuation in which the student makes a choice that reveals the theme. Use point of view, transitions, and precise language.
  2. Check your response for clarity, cohesion, and standard English conventions.
  3. Underline or label the part of your response that best shows Unit 2 synthesis.
