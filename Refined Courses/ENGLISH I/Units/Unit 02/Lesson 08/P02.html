Notebook Task Part 1: Review the Literary Evidence Chain

Notebook title: Unit 2 Synthesis - Literary Evidence Chain

Vocabulary Review

  • Literary element: a story part such as character, setting, plot, conflict, or point of view.
  • Theme: a message or insight about life, people, or choices.
  • Mood: the feeling a text creates for the reader.
  • Evidence chain: a clear path from claim to evidence to explanation.

Review Passage

Passage: "The Lantern Path"

Mara stood at the edge of the narrow path while the village lanterns trembled in the wind behind her. The forest ahead was dark, but not silent. Leaves scraped together like whispered warnings. In her pocket, the silver key felt heavier than it had that morning.

Her brother had said the old tower was only a story, something adults used to frighten children away from the cliffs. Mara had laughed then. Now, with the storm clouds folding over the moon, she wished she had listened more carefully.

She took one step forward. The lantern light slipped from her shoulders, and the path swallowed her shadow.

Step-by-Step Notebook Directions

  1. Write one sentence that identifies a possible theme from the passage.
  2. Copy one short piece of evidence that supports that theme.
  3. Explain how one literary element helps develop the theme.
  4. Name the mood and identify one word or image that creates it.

Short Model

A possible theme is that courage often begins when a person chooses to face uncertainty. The image of Mara stepping away from the lantern light supports this because she leaves safety behind even though the forest feels threatening.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two possible answers, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
