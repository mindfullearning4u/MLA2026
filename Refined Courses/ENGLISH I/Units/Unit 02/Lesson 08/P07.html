Checkpoint Task: Unit 02 Synthesis Response

Passage / Scenario: In the short story, Lina keeps a small compass from her father, who once told her that "a needle can point north, but it cannot choose your road." When Lina must decide whether to join a group that mocks a new student, she touches the compass in her pocket. The narrator says, "The hallway stretched like a narrow bridge, and every laugh behind her sounded like a wave striking stone." Lina walks toward the new student instead of the group. Later, a stage adaptation changes the compass into a song her father used to sing, but Lina still chooses kindness over approval.

  1. Write a synthesis response explaining how the original story and stage adaptation use character choice, symbol, figurative language, and mood to develop a shared theme.
  2. Use relevant evidence or support from the passage or scenario.
  3. Use at least two unit vocabulary words correctly.
  4. Explain your reasoning clearly.
  5. Check your response for standard English conventions before submitting.

Both submissions are required. Notebook Evidence and Checkpoint Submission are Teacher of Record graded.

Corrections and resubmissions are allowed until you demonstrate at least 80% mastery.

The Unit Assessment becomes available only after required mastery has been demonstrated.

Mastery Criteria

  • Explains how literary elements work together to create meaning.
  • Identifies a defensible theme and supports it with relevant evidence.
  • Analyzes narrator perspective, figurative language, and mood accurately.
  • Compares adaptation choices without treating every change as equally important.
  • Uses narrative or literary vocabulary correctly and follows standard English conventions.
