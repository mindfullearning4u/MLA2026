P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Part 2 increases the complexity. You will use the same unit thinking with less scaffolding, so your response should show that you can transfer the skills from Lessons 1-7 into a complete explanation.

Deeper Synthesis Task

Use the passage and your Unit 2 learning to write a literary synthesis response. Your response should connect literary elements, narrator perspective, figurative language, mood, and theme. Then add one sentence explaining how the passage could be adapted for stage or screen without losing the theme.

  1. Write a complete response that uses multiple Unit 02 skills together.
  2. Include relevant evidence, explanation, and unit vocabulary.
  3. Check that your response has a clear purpose and follows standard English conventions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The response names one unit skill and gives a general opinion without evidence.

Correct: The response combines the unit skills, uses specific evidence, explains the connection, and checks conventions.

Why this matters: Lesson 8 is synthesis. A correct response proves that you can use several skills together, not that you can remember one definition.
