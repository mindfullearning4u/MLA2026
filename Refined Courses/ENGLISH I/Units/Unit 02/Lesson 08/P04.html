Worked Example: Build a Unit 2 Synthesis Response

A synthesis response connects more than one skill. For Unit 2, that means you may need to connect theme, literary elements, perspective, mood, and narrative craft.

Example Passage

Passage: "The Lantern Path"

Mara stood at the edge of the narrow path while the village lanterns trembled in the wind behind her. The forest ahead was dark, but not silent. Leaves scraped together like whispered warnings. In her pocket, the silver key felt heavier than it had that morning.

Her brother had said the old tower was only a story, something adults used to frighten children away from the cliffs. Mara had laughed then. Now, with the storm clouds folding over the moon, she wished she had listened more carefully.

She took one step forward. The lantern light slipped from her shoulders, and the path swallowed her shadow.

Example 1: Identify the Question

Question: How does the author use setting to develop mood and theme?

Thinking: This question asks for three connected ideas: setting, mood, and theme. A strong answer should not discuss only one of them.

Example 2: Build the Evidence Chain

  1. Claim: The setting creates a fearful mood and supports a theme about courage.
  2. Evidence: The forest is dark, the leaves sound like "whispered warnings," and the lantern light disappears behind Mara.
  3. Explanation: These details make the path feel dangerous, so Mara's decision to move forward shows courage in the face of uncertainty.

Example 3: Write the Response

The author uses the dark forest setting to create a fearful mood and develop a theme about courage. The leaves sound like "whispered warnings," which makes the forest seem threatening. When Mara steps forward and the lantern light disappears, she leaves safety behind. These setting details show that courage does not mean fear is gone; it means moving forward even when the path is uncertain.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The setting is in a forest, and the theme is courage.

This answer names ideas, but it does not explain how the author's details connect setting, mood, and theme.

Correct Approach

Correct: The setting creates fear through dark forest imagery, and Mara's decision to keep walking develops a theme about courage during uncertainty.

This answer connects evidence to meaning, which is the goal of a Unit 2 synthesis response.
