Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 2 Synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7 in Unit 02: Literary Analysis and Narrative Craft. It does not introduce new standards, new concepts, new vocabulary expectations, or new skills. You will use the unit skills together so you are ready to demonstrate mastery.

Unit Standards

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.01 - Analyze Literary Elements; MLA.ENG1.R.02 - Analyze Theme Development; MLA.ENG1.R.03 - Analyze Narrator Perspective; MLA.ENG1.R.04 - Analyze Epic Poetry; MLA.ENG1.R.09 - Explain Figurative Language and Mood; MLA.ENG1.R.11 - Compare Literary Adaptations; MLA.ENG1.W.01 - Write Narrative Texts

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG1.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

Unit Skills, Concepts, and Objectives

  • Identify how literary elements work together instead of naming them separately.
  • Explain how character, conflict, setting, and structure help develop theme.
  • Analyze how narrator perspective shapes irony, tone, satire, or reader understanding.
  • Explain how figurative language creates mood and supports meaning.
  • Connect epic or classical features to theme, character, structure, and adaptation choices.
  • Use literary craft knowledge to make purposeful narrative choices.

What You Will Do

You will work with one connected unit task instead of separate review questions. Each page asks you to combine Unit 02 skills, vocabulary, reasoning, and communication.

The goal is not to remember one isolated skill. The goal is to show that you can use several English I skills together when a text-based task requires them.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery when you can complete an integrated response that applies the Unit 02 standards, uses relevant evidence or support, explains reasoning clearly, uses academic vocabulary accurately, and follows standard English conventions. The Unit Assessment mastery requirement is 80% or higher.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can combine the skills from Unit 02 to read, think, write, revise, and communicate clearly for a specific purpose.
