Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 2 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 2 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.01, MLA.ENG1.R.02, MLA.ENG1.R.03, MLA.ENG1.R.04, MLA.ENG1.R.09, MLA.ENG1.R.11, MLA.ENG1.W.01.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.10, MLA.ENG1.V.01, MLA.ENG1.L.01.

What You Will Review

  • How literary elements create layers of meaning.
  • How theme develops through character, conflict, setting, and plot.
  • How narrator perspective, irony, satire, figurative language, and mood shape meaning.
  • How epic structure and adaptation choices change a reader's understanding.
  • How narrative writing uses point of view, transitions, mood, and conventions.

Why This Matters

This lesson does not introduce new material. It helps you connect the skills from Lessons 1-7 before you complete the Unit 2 Assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Review a literary passage using Unit 2 reading skills.
  • Practice connecting analysis to narrative craft.
  • Check your readiness for the Unit 2 Assessment.
  • Complete the Unit 2 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

You show mastery by analyzing how literary choices shape meaning, explaining your thinking with evidence, and applying narrative craft in a focused written response.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read literature closely, explain how an author's choices affect meaning, compare related texts, and use narrative techniques to write clearly.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you are unsure how to connect evidence to a claim, explain figurative language, compare an adaptation, or organize your narrative response.
