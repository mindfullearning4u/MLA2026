P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Practice explaining how theme develops across a passage.

Read

Passage: At the beginning of the race, Darius sprinted ahead and ignored his coach's warning to pace himself. Halfway through, his legs shook and two runners passed him. Near the end, he slowed his breathing, matched his steps to the runner beside him, and finished with enough strength to smile.

Think in Order

Beginning: Darius rushes and ignores advice.

Middle: He struggles because of that choice.

End: He adjusts, controls his pace, and finishes stronger.

Theme: Learning to use patience and self-control can help a person recover from a mistake.

Notebook Task

Write a short response explaining how the theme develops. Include one beginning detail, one middle detail, and one end detail.

Use this frame if needed: At first, ___. Then, ___. By the end, ___. These changes develop the theme that ___.

Common Mistake Check

Incorrect: The theme is race.

This is only a topic. It does not state a message.

Correct: The theme is that patience and self-control can help people recover from early mistakes.

This is a complete message supported by the character's changing actions.
