P04 Worked Example

Follow the model from topic to theme to development.

Worked Example 1: Find the Topic

Passage: When the community garden flooded, Nora wanted to quit. Her neighbor brought one dry packet of seeds, and together they replanted a single row. By spring, that row had grown into the first green line in the muddy field.

Topic: perseverance.

Worked Example 2: Turn Topic into Theme

Theme statement: Small acts of perseverance can rebuild hope after a setback.

Worked Example 3: Explain Development

The theme develops through conflict and setting. At first, the flooded garden makes Nora want to quit. Then the dry packet of seeds gives her one possible next step. By spring, the single green row shows that hope can grow when people keep working after a loss.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The theme is garden.

This is only a topic or object. It does not state what the text suggests about life or experience.

Correct: The theme is that small steps can restore hope after disappointment.

This is a complete theme statement and can be supported by the character's choices and the changing garden.
