P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Set up your notebook to track theme development.

Notebook Title

Theme Development Through Literary Elements

Vocabulary

Topic: the subject a text is about, such as friendship, courage, honesty, or change.

Theme: a complete message about life or human experience.

Theme development: the way a theme grows through characters, conflicts, choices, and events.

Universal theme: a theme that many readers can understand across different places, times, or experiences.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Find the topic. A topic is usually one word or a short phrase.

Step 2: Ask what the text says about that topic. This turns a topic into a theme.

Step 3: Look for a literary element that develops the theme. A character's choice, conflict, or setting detail often gives evidence.

Step 4: Write the explanation: The theme develops when ___ because ___.

Notebook Task

Passage: At first, Talia refused to let anyone see her sketchbook. After her classmate showed her a messy first draft, Talia opened to a page filled with crossed-out lines and unfinished faces. "Mine looks like that too," she said.

In your notebook, write:

  1. Topic: one word or short phrase.
  2. Theme: one complete sentence.
  3. Evidence: one detail that develops the theme.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two possible answers, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
