P07 Checkpoint

Use this checkpoint to show mastery of MLA.ENG1.W.01.

Teacher of Record Support

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your narrative has events but lacks point of view, conflict, or mood.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a six-to-eight sentence narrative moment about a character making a difficult choice. Include clear point of view, at least two transitions, mood-building details, and standard English conventions.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Revise your narrative moment.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence and checkpoint response in the LMS.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses a consistent point of view.
  • Includes clear sequence and transitions.
  • Creates mood through detail or figurative language.
  • Uses standard English conventions.
