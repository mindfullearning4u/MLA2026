Lesson 7: Narrative Craft Connected to Literary Analysis

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG1.W.01 - Write Narrative Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG1.R.03 - Analyze Narrator Perspective; MLA.ENG1.R.09 - Explain Figurative Language and Mood; MLA.ENG1.W.04 - Revise for Clarity and Cohesion; MLA.ENG1.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How narrative writing uses point of view, transitions, and mood.
  • How to build a short narrative moment instead of listing events.
  • How revision improves clarity, cohesion, and sentence control.

Why This Matters

Writing narratives helps you use the same craft you analyze in literature. When you choose point of view, transitions, mood, and details on purpose, you understand how authors build meaning.

What You Will Do

  • Study a modeled short narrative moment.
  • Plan point of view, setting, conflict, and mood.
  • Write a short narrative response with transitions and sensory details.
  • Revise for clarity and conventions.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You can write a focused narrative moment.
  • You can use point of view and transitions clearly.
  • You can create mood through details or figurative language.
  • You can revise for clarity and standard English.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG1.W.01 in student language: I can write a narrative using clear point of view, purposeful transitions, and narrative techniques.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your narrative lists events but does not yet create a clear moment, mood, or point of view.
