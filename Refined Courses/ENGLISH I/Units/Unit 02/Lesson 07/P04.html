P04 Worked Example

Study how craft choices make a narrative stronger.

Worked Example 1: Weak Draft

I went to the door. I was nervous. I opened it. People looked at me.

Worked Example 2: Revision Choices

Point of view: first person.

Mood: nervous.

Transition: When.

Detail: cold doorknob, quiet room, eyes lifting.

Worked Example 3: Stronger Draft

When my fingers touched the cold doorknob, the room behind it fell silent. I opened the door slowly, and every face turned toward me before I could remember what I had planned to say.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: I was nervous and then everyone saw me and then I went inside.

This is a run-on sequence with weak pacing and unclear detail.

Correct: When I stepped inside, the quiet felt louder than applause. Every face turned toward me, and my first word disappeared.

This uses transition, mood, figurative language, and clear sentence control.
