P05 Guided Practice

Answer these five questions.

Questions

1. Passage: When the lights dimmed, I gripped the speech card until the corners bent. Question: Which point of view is used?

2. Passage: First, Lena heard the bell. Then she saw the empty hallway. Question: What do the transition words show?

3. Passage: The hallway swallowed my footsteps. Question: What mood does the figurative language create?

4. Passage: I walked in I dropped my books everyone stared. Question: Which revision is clearest?

5. Passage: A character must apologize after taking credit for a friend's idea. Question: Which detail would best build conflict?
