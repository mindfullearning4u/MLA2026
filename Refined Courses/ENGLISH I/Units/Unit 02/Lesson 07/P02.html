P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Set up your notebook to plan a focused narrative moment.

Notebook Title

Narrative Craft: Point of View, Mood, and Revision

Vocabulary

Narrative: writing that tells a story or story-like moment.

Point of view: the narrator position used to tell the story.

Narrative technique: a craft move such as dialogue, pacing, description, reflection, or sensory detail.

Transition: a word or phrase that shows time, order, or change.

Cohesion: the way ideas connect smoothly.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Choose one small moment. Do not try to tell a whole life story.

Step 2: Choose point of view. Decide whether the narrator says I or describes another character.

Step 3: Add a conflict or tension. Something should be uncertain, difficult, or meaningful.

Step 4: Use details to create mood.

Step 5: Use transitions so the reader can follow the sequence.

Notebook Task

Plan a narrative about this prompt: A character has to decide whether to speak up.

Write four planning lines: point of view, setting, conflict, and mood.
