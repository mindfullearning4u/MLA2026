P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Set up your notebook to study narrator perspective.

Notebook Title

Narrator Perspective, Irony, and Satire

Vocabulary

Narrator: the voice telling the story.

Perspective: the narrator's position, attitude, and way of seeing events.

First person: narration using I, me, my, or we.

Third person: narration using he, she, they, or character names.

Irony: a contrast between what is expected and what actually happens.

Satire: writing that uses humor, exaggeration, or irony to criticize a problem.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify who tells the story. Look for I, me, he, she, or they.

Step 2: Ask what the narrator knows. Does the narrator know only one mind, many minds, or only outside actions?

Step 3: Notice attitude. A narrator may sound proud, nervous, amused, angry, or doubtful.

Step 4: Explain how the narrator's perspective shapes meaning.

Notebook Task

Passage: I told everyone I was perfectly calm before the audition. Of course, perfectly calm people always drop their music, forget the first note, and bow to an empty practice room.

In your notebook, identify the point of view, paraphrase what happens, and explain what the narrator's tone reveals.
