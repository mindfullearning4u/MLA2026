P04 Worked Example

Study how narrator perspective changes the meaning of a passage.

Worked Example 1: Identify Perspective

Passage: I knew the shortcut was a brilliant idea, right until the mud reached my ankles and the bus passed me on the dry road.

Thinking: The narrator uses I and my, so the passage is first person. The narrator can tell us private thoughts and embarrassment directly.

Worked Example 2: Find Irony

Expected: A shortcut should help the narrator arrive faster.

Actual: The shortcut slows the narrator down and the bus passes by.

Meaning: The irony shows that the narrator's confidence was misplaced.

Worked Example 3: Write the Analysis

The first-person narrator makes the irony funny because readers hear the narrator admit the shortcut seemed "brilliant" before it clearly fails. The narrator's perspective helps readers understand both the mistake and the embarrassment.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The narrator is first person because the bus passed.

The bus detail is an event. It does not prove point of view.

Correct: The narrator is first person because the narrator uses I and my to describe private thoughts and events.

This answer uses evidence that actually shows perspective.
