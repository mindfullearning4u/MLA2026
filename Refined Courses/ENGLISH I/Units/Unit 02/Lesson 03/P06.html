P06 Independent Work

Complete this work in your notebook before the checkpoint.

Passage

I called my plan "perfect." First, I left my umbrella at home because the sky looked friendly. Then the clouds opened exactly when I reached the one street with no trees, no awnings, and no helpful buses.

Part A

Identify the narrator perspective and copy the words that prove it.

Part B

Explain the irony by naming what the narrator expected and what actually happened.

Part C

Write one paragraph explaining how the narrator's perspective makes the irony clearer.
