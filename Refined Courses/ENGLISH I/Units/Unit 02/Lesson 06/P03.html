P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now explain the effect of adaptation choices.

Read

Source: A proud hero refuses advice and loses the first battle.

Adaptation: A team captain ignores a teammate's strategy and loses the opening game.

Think in Order

Same: A leader is proud, rejects advice, and fails.

Changed: The battle becomes a sports game.

Effect: The adaptation makes the same theme easier to recognize in a modern team setting.

Notebook Task

Write one paragraph explaining how the adaptation changes the situation but preserves the theme about pride and listening to advice.

Common Mistake Check

Incorrect: They are different because one has a battle and one has a game.

This lists a difference but does not explain its importance.

Correct: The adaptation changes the battle to a game, but both versions show that pride can cause a leader to ignore helpful advice.

This compares and explains meaning.
