# Digital Citizenship and Online Safety Build Report

Date: 2026-07-13

## Scope

- Built production source-of-truth, standards inventory, crosswalk, unit mapping, lesson mapping, and full trace.
- Built 6 units, 30 lessons, 210 HTML pages, 30 lesson.json files, and 30 quiz.json files.
- Built 66 Moodle XML assessment banks with 1,050 multichoice questions.
- Lesson 5 in each unit is synthesis with Guided Practice and Unit Assessment only; no Lesson 5 quiz bank exists.

## Source Stack

- CPALMS Computer Science standards/report availability and course search were checked.
- CSTA PK-12 CS Standards and ISTE Student Digital Citizen standard were used as support frameworks.
- Florida B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, ACT, and ELD/accessibility support alignments are documented in the production files.
