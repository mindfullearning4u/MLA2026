Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to apply attribution and ethical remix decisions to digital creation. Your answer must use evidence, not just opinion.
  2. Read the scenario. A student remixes a chart, a quote, and a photo into an infographic.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the message, post, profile, app, source, or situation. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before taking digital action.
