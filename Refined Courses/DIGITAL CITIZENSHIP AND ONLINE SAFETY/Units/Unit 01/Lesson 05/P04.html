P04 Worked Example

Synthesis: Digital Identity Plan

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.ID.1 - Explain how digital identity, online choices, and audience shape a lasting digital footprint.; MLA.DCOS.ID.2 - Evaluate profile, posting, sharing, and privacy decisions for reputation and opportunity impact.; MLA.DCOS.ID.3 - Apply a decision routine before posting, resharing, or joining an online community.; MLA.DCOS.ID.4 - Build a personal digital identity plan that protects safety, dignity, and future goals..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to synthesize identity, footprint, posting, and positive-presence decisions into a digital identity plan. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Worked Example 1: plan evidence

Prompt: A student is preparing a safe, positive online presence for school, activities, and future opportunities.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on plan evidence.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the digital identity plan rubric and the scenario details before deciding what to do.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: The strongest answer connects the evidence to a safe, legal, ethical, or wellness-focused action.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.DCOS.ID.1, MLA.DCOS.ID.2, MLA.DCOS.ID.3, MLA.DCOS.ID.4 because it connects digital behavior to evidence-based responsibility.

Incorrect: Act quickly because the situation feels urgent or convenient.

Correct: Slow down, name the evidence, explain the risk or responsibility, and choose the action that best fits the standard.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because online choices often feel fast, emotional, or hidden, but safe digital citizenship requires deliberate evidence-based action.
