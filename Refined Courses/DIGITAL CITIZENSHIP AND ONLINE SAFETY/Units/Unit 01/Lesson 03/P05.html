P05 Guided Practice

Posting and Sharing Decisions

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.ID.2 - Evaluate profile, posting, sharing, and privacy decisions for reputation and opportunity impact.; MLA.DCOS.ID.3 - Apply a decision routine before posting, resharing, or joining an online community..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to apply a decision routine before posting, resharing, or joining an online group. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Guided Practice Preparation

Use this page to prepare for the Moodle Guided Practice questions. Do not look for a separate answer key. The practice questions will ask you to apply the same decision routine from the lesson.

  1. Read the scenario carefully.
  2. Identify the evidence, risk or responsibility, and safest next step.
  3. Use the post-before-you-share checklist to explain your reasoning.
  4. Revise any answer that depends only on opinion, speed, popularity, or fear.

post-before-you-share checklist

Evidence to checkWhat to look forSafe or ethical action
Source or signalWho is involved, what data appears, and what claim or request is being madeSlow down and identify the evidence before acting
Risk or responsibilityPrivacy, safety, reputation, rights, wellness, or community impactChoose the lowest-risk action that still meets the goal
Next stepVerification, consent, attribution, reporting, blocking, boundary setting, or help-seekingDocument the reason for the decision and use the proper support pathway
