P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Boundaries and Healthy Technology Routines

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.WELL.1 - Analyze digital wellness factors including attention, sleep, stress, ergonomics, and balance.; MLA.DCOS.WELL.3 - Set personal boundaries and technology routines that support wellness and learning..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to set boundaries and routines that support learning and wellness. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Continue the Teaching Sequence

  1. Match evidence to a risk or responsibility. Use the boundary routine planner to decide which part of the standard the evidence supports.
  2. Explain why the evidence matters. A complete digital citizenship answer does not only name an option; it explains why the option is safer, more ethical, or better supported.
  3. Check for missing information. If the evidence does not answer an important question, name that gap instead of pretending the decision is final.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example: I would do this because it seems fine and everyone online does it.

Correct example: I would choose the safer action because the evidence shows a clear connection between the scenario, the lesson standard, and the decision criteria in the organizer.

Teachable Explanation

The incorrect answer is too general. The correct answer names the evidence path: standard, scenario, organizer, and action. This prevents unsupported advice and helps protect privacy, safety, rights, and community well-being.

Self-Check: If another student could ask, "How do you know?", your answer must point to a specific detail in the scenario, table, organizer, or standard.
