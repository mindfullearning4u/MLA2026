P04 Worked Example

Misinformation and Manipulated Media

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.INFO.2 - Identify misinformation, manipulated media, persuasive design, and algorithmic amplification..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to identify misinformation, manipulated media, persuasive design, and amplification clues. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Worked Example 1: missing context

Prompt: A dramatic image is shared with a caption, but no source, date, or original context is provided.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on missing context.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the misinformation clue board and the scenario details before deciding what to do.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: The strongest answer connects the evidence to a safe, legal, ethical, or wellness-focused action.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.DCOS.INFO.2 because it connects digital behavior to evidence-based responsibility.

Incorrect: Act quickly because the situation feels urgent or convenient.

Correct: Slow down, name the evidence, explain the risk or responsibility, and choose the action that best fits the standard.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because online choices often feel fast, emotional, or hidden, but safe digital citizenship requires deliberate evidence-based action.
