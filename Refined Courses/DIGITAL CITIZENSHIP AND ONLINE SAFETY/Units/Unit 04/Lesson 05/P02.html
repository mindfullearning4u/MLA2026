Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to synthesize credibility, misinformation, corroboration, and AI-aware verification into an information decision. Your answer must use evidence, not just opinion.
  2. Read the scenario. A student must decide whether a viral claim is credible enough to use in a school project.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the message, post, profile, app, source, or situation. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before taking digital action.
