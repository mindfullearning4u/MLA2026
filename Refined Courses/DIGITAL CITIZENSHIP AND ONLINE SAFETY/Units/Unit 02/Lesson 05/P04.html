P04 Worked Example

Synthesis: Privacy and Security Plan

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.SEC.1 - Identify personal data, sensitive information, tracking signals, and data-collection risks.; MLA.DCOS.SEC.2 - Use secure account practices including strong passwords, multifactor authentication, updates, and backups.; MLA.DCOS.SEC.3 - Recognize phishing, scams, social engineering, malware, and unsafe links using evidence.; MLA.DCOS.SEC.4 - Create a privacy and security response plan for suspicious activity or account compromise..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to synthesize privacy, secure account, scam-detection, and response practices into a security plan. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Worked Example 1: privacy choice

Prompt: A student is setting up safe accounts and a response plan before using several online learning tools.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on privacy choice.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the privacy-security plan template and the scenario details before deciding what to do.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: The strongest answer connects the evidence to a safe, legal, ethical, or wellness-focused action.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.DCOS.SEC.1, MLA.DCOS.SEC.2, MLA.DCOS.SEC.3, MLA.DCOS.SEC.4 because it connects digital behavior to evidence-based responsibility.

Incorrect: Act quickly because the situation feels urgent or convenient.

Correct: Slow down, name the evidence, explain the risk or responsibility, and choose the action that best fits the standard.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because online choices often feel fast, emotional, or hidden, but safe digital citizenship requires deliberate evidence-based action.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
