Personal Data and Privacy Risks

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.DCOS.SEC.1 - Identify personal data, sensitive information, tracking signals, and data-collection risks..

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to identify personal data, sensitive information, tracking signals, and privacy risks. You will use evidence, safety reasoning, and a decision tool instead of guessing or relying on a live explanation.

Checkpoint Task

Write a short response explaining how to identify personal data, sensitive information, tracking signals, and privacy risks in the scenario: An app asks for a birthday, location, contacts, camera access, and permission to track activity.

Required Response Structure

  1. Claim: State the safest, most ethical, or most responsible action.
  2. Evidence: Cite a detail from the scenario or visual organizer.
  3. Reasoning: Explain how the evidence connects to the standard.
  4. Next Step: Name what you would do next, such as verify, revise, report, block, attribute, set a boundary, or ask for support.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses accurate vocabulary.
  • Names specific evidence.
  • Explains the risk, responsibility, or opportunity.
  • Chooses an action aligned to the mapped standard.
  • Meets at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
