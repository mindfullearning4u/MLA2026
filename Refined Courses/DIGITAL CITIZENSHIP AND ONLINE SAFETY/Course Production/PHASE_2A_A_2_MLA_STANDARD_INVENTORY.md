# Digital Citizenship and Online Safety MLA Standard Inventory

| Standard | Student Mastery Expectation |
|---|---|
| MLA.DCOS.ID.1 | Explain how digital identity, online choices, and audience shape a lasting digital footprint. |
| MLA.DCOS.ID.2 | Evaluate profile, posting, sharing, and privacy decisions for reputation and opportunity impact. |
| MLA.DCOS.ID.3 | Apply a decision routine before posting, resharing, or joining an online community. |
| MLA.DCOS.ID.4 | Build a personal digital identity plan that protects safety, dignity, and future goals. |
| MLA.DCOS.SEC.1 | Identify personal data, sensitive information, tracking signals, and data-collection risks. |
| MLA.DCOS.SEC.2 | Use secure account practices including strong passwords, multifactor authentication, updates, and backups. |
| MLA.DCOS.SEC.3 | Recognize phishing, scams, social engineering, malware, and unsafe links using evidence. |
| MLA.DCOS.SEC.4 | Create a privacy and security response plan for suspicious activity or account compromise. |
| MLA.DCOS.COM.1 | Use respectful, inclusive, and context-appropriate communication in digital communities. |
| MLA.DCOS.COM.2 | Analyze cyberbullying, harassment, hate speech, and exclusion scenarios and select safe responses. |
| MLA.DCOS.COM.3 | Apply reporting, blocking, documentation, and trusted-adult escalation pathways. |
| MLA.DCOS.COM.4 | Contribute positively to online communities while protecting self and others. |
| MLA.DCOS.INFO.1 | Evaluate digital sources for credibility, evidence, author purpose, currency, and bias. |
| MLA.DCOS.INFO.2 | Identify misinformation, manipulated media, persuasive design, and algorithmic amplification. |
| MLA.DCOS.INFO.3 | Use lateral reading, corroboration, and evidence comparison before believing or sharing information. |
| MLA.DCOS.INFO.4 | Explain responsible use of AI-generated or algorithmically recommended content. |
| MLA.DCOS.LAW.1 | Explain copyright, fair use, licensing, plagiarism, attribution, and creative ownership. |
| MLA.DCOS.LAW.2 | Identify illegal, unethical, or policy-violating technology behaviors and safer alternatives. |
| MLA.DCOS.LAW.3 | Apply ethical decision-making to digital creation, remixing, downloading, and sharing. |
| MLA.DCOS.LAW.4 | Create a responsible digital publishing and attribution plan. |
| MLA.DCOS.WELL.1 | Analyze digital wellness factors including attention, sleep, stress, ergonomics, and balance. |
| MLA.DCOS.WELL.2 | Use safety, help-seeking, and crisis-escalation routines for concerning online situations. |
| MLA.DCOS.WELL.3 | Set personal boundaries and technology routines that support wellness and learning. |
| MLA.DCOS.WELL.4 | Synthesize identity, privacy, communication, information, law, and wellness evidence into a digital citizenship portfolio. |
