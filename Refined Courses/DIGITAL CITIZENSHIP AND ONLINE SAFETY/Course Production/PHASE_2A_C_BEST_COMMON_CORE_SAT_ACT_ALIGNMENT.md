# Digital Citizenship and Online Safety BEST / Common Core / SAT / ACT Alignment

Digital Citizenship and Online Safety uses CPALMS/FDOE Computer Science standards as the Florida source family and MLA course-specific standards for this half-credit elective. Florida B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, and ACT are support alignments rather than replacement content standards.

| Support Framework | Course Use |
|---|---|
| Florida B.E.S.T. ELA | vocabulary, informational text, explanatory writing, evidence-based argument, presentation, and conventions |
| Common Core Literacy in Technical Subjects | source evaluation, technical procedures, claim/evidence/reasoning, research, synthesis |
| SAT Reading and Writing | Information and Ideas, Craft and Structure, Expression of Ideas, Standard English Conventions |
| ACT | reading comprehension, data/source interpretation, English conventions, clear writing choices |
| ELD/Accessibility | defined vocabulary, visual organizers, sentence frames, step-by-step routines, remediation and acceleration paths |
