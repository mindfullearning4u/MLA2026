# ELA Lesson Shell Compliance Audit and Certification Report

## Executive Summary

A comprehensive lesson-shell compliance audit was completed for the ELA Department courses listed below. The audit reviewed every unit, every lesson, and every lesson page P01-P07 for unauthorized learner-path or differentiation-path sections. The audit also applied the approved MLA lesson shell revision requiring the Teacher of Record (TOR) support reminder on every lesson page.

Final result: PASS.

All unauthorized learner-path sections identified in P01-P07 lesson pages were removed. The approved TOR reminder was added to every lesson page. A final re-audit confirmed that no unauthorized learner-path phrases remain in the reviewed lesson pages and that every reviewed lesson page includes the approved TOR reminder.

## Approved TOR Reminder Added

The following approved reminder was added in a consistent support box near the bottom of every P01-P07 lesson page:

**Need Help?**

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

Your TOR can:

- Clarify directions
- Explain concepts
- Review your work
- Answer questions
- Provide additional support

Do not wait until you are frustrated.

Ask for help as soon as you need it.
