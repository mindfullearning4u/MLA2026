# Career Planning and Portfolio Crosswalk Draft

## Crosswalk Rule

The primary content boundary is CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning. Florida B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, ACT, and ELD/accessibility standards support reading, research, writing, communication, data interpretation, and portfolio evidence. They do not replace or expand the primary career-planning content standard.
