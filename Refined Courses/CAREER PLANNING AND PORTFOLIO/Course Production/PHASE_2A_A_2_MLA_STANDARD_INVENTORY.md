# Career Planning and Portfolio MLA Standard Inventory

Primary content source: CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning standards. Support standards are used for literacy, research, communication, data interpretation, and readiness evidence only.

| MLA Standard | Student-Facing Requirement | CPALMS / Florida Career and Education Planning Alignment | B.E.S.T. / Common Core Support | SAT / ACT Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MLA.CPP.SELF.1 | Explain career interests using results from an interest assessment, personal experience, research, and reflection. | 01.01 Describe career interests based on assessment, experience, interviews, research, and/or reflection. | B.E.S.T. research, informational reading, vocabulary, explanatory writing; Common Core research and evidence writing. | SAT Information and Ideas; ACT Reading and Writing. |
| MLA.CPP.SELF.2 | Describe current and developable career-related skills using assessment evidence and reflection. | 01.02 Describe current or developable career-related skills based on skill assessment, experience, and/or reflection. | B.E.S.T. expository writing and vocabulary; Common Core explanatory writing. | SAT Expression of Ideas; ACT English/Writing. |
| MLA.CPP.SELF.3 | Explain lifestyle and workplace values that affect career decisions. | 01.03 Describe lifestyle and workplace values such as salary, benefits, work environment, nature of work, and work/life balance. | B.E.S.T. central idea, comparison, and evidence support; Common Core informational analysis. | SAT Craft and Structure; ACT Reading. |
| MLA.CPP.SELF.4 | Explain values related to positive impact on others and the world when weighing career choices. | 01.04 Describe values related to positively affecting others and the world during career decision making. | B.E.S.T. argumentative/explanatory writing; Common Core claim, evidence, reasoning. | SAT Information and Ideas; ACT Writing. |
| MLA.CPP.LMI.1 | Define career clusters, industries, occupations, regional poverty thresholds, average wages, and family-sustaining wages. | 02.01 Define career clusters, industries, and occupations; 02.02 define regional poverty, average, and family-sustaining wage thresholds. | B.E.S.T. vocabulary and informational reading; Common Core domain vocabulary. | SAT table/graph interpretation; ACT Reading/Data interpretation. |
| MLA.CPP.LMI.2 | Use local labor-market data to identify top regional occupations by employment, growth, and salary. | 02.03 Review local labor market data to identify the top five regional occupations for employment, growth, and salary. | B.E.S.T. research and data explanation; Common Core technical/informational literacy. | SAT informational graphics; ACT Reading/Science data reasoning as support. |

| MLA.CPP.PATH.4 | Compare training options and training-provider outcomes aligned to career assessment results and occupational exploration. | 03.04 Explain training options aligned with assessment results and occupational exploration; 03.05 review total cost, debt, earnings, and placement rates; 03.06 summarize provider research/tour/interview learning. | B.E.S.T. research synthesis and data explanation; Common Core research projects. | SAT informational graphics; ACT Reading/Data reasoning. |
