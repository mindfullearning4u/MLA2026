# Career Planning and Portfolio Unit Mapping

0.5-credit rule: six units, five lessons per unit. Lesson 5 is synthesis and unit assessment. Lessons 1-4 have lesson quizzes; Lesson 5 does not.

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Standards | Unit Purpose | Unit Assessment Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit 01 | Career Self-Knowledge: Interests, Skills, and Values | MLA.CPP.SELF.1-4 | Students identify career interests, current/developable skills, lifestyle/workplace values, and positive-impact values so career decisions begin with self-knowledge. | Interest/skills/values interpretation, self-knowledge profile, value tradeoff scenarios, synthesis reflection. |
| Unit 02 | Occupations, Industries, and Labor-Market Evidence | MLA.CPP.LMI.1-4 | Students define career clusters and labor-market terms, use regional data, compare occupations, and summarize occupational learning from credible sources or firsthand-style evidence. | Labor-market vocabulary, data table interpretation, occupation comparison, occupational learning summary. |
| Unit 03 | Postsecondary Pathways, Credentials, and Training Providers | MLA.CPP.PATH.1-4 | Students compare immediate post-high-school options, credential types, provider requirements, and cost/outcome data. | Pathway pros/cons, credential stack organizer, provider comparison, cost/debt/earnings explanation. |
| Unit 04 | Career Decisions, Skills, Credentials, and Paying for Training | MLA.CPP.PLAN.1-4 | Students use decision tools to choose an occupation, identify required skills/credentials, select a credential/provider, and explain funding options. | Decision matrix, career rationale, skills/credential plan, funding explanation. |
| Unit 05 | Academic Planning, Graduation Pathways, and Opportunity Alignment | MLA.CPP.ACAD.1-3 | Students connect Florida graduation pathways, accelerated credit, CTE/work-based learning, freshman scheduling, and extracurricular choices to career goals. | Graduation pathway explanation, aligned freshman schedule, extracurricular/work-based learning plan. |
| Unit 06 | Portfolio Synthesis and Career Readiness Presentation | MLA.CPP.PORT.1-4 plus integrated review of MLA.CPP.SELF/LMI/PATH/PLAN/ACAD | Students compile, revise, present, and reflect on a complete career and academic portfolio. | Final portfolio, plan presentation, evidence-quality revision, readiness gap reflection. |
