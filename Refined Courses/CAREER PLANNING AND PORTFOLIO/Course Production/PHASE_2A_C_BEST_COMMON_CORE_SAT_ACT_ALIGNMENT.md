# B.E.S.T., Common Core, SAT, and ACT Alignment

## Alignment Boundary

Career Planning and Portfolio uses CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning standards as the primary content source. The frameworks below support literacy, research, decision-making communication, data interpretation, and portfolio presentation.

## Florida B.E.S.T. Support Layer

| Support Area | B.E.S.T. Benchmark Support | Course Use |
|---|---|---|
| Informational reading and central idea | ELA.9.R.2.2; ELA.10.R.2.2; ELA.11.R.2.2; ELA.12.R.2.2 | Read career profiles, wage reports, provider information, scholarship/graduation information, and identify central ideas and support. |
| Argument and decision reasoning | ELA.9.C.1.3; ELA.10.C.1.3; ELA.11/12 argument-analysis support where appropriate | Explain and defend career, credential, provider, and funding choices with evidence. |
| Expository writing | ELA.9.C.1.4; ELA.10.C.1.4; ELA.11/12 expository-analysis support where appropriate | Explain labor-market findings, pathway requirements, portfolio evidence, and readiness gaps. |
| Research | ELA.9.C.4.1; ELA.10.C.4.1; ELA.11.C.4.1; ELA.12.C.4.1 | Conduct career, occupation, provider, scholarship, and pathway research using reliable sources. |
| Multimedia and digital communication | ELA.9.C.5.1; ELA.10.C.5.1; ELA.11.C.5.1; ELA.12.C.5.1; ELA.9.C.5.2; ELA.10.C.5.2 | Build, revise, and present a digital or structured career portfolio. |
| Vocabulary | ELA.9.V.1.3; ELA.10.V.1.3; ELA.11.V.1.3; ELA.12.V.1.3 | Use career, labor-market, credential, finance, and academic-planning vocabulary accurately. |

## SAT Support Layer

| SAT Reading and Writing Domain | Course Use |
|---|---|
| Information and Ideas | Interpret, evaluate, and integrate information from career texts and informational graphics. |
| Craft and Structure | Analyze word choice, purpose, and structure in career, scholarship, provider, and workplace texts. |
| Expression of Ideas | Revise explanations, rationales, portfolio statements, and presentations for clarity and purpose. |
| Standard English Conventions | Edit portfolio artifacts, summaries, and reflections for grammar, usage, punctuation, and sentence structure. |

## ACT Support Layer

| ACT Readiness Area | Course Use |
|---|---|
| Reading | Understand increasingly complex informational texts about occupations, pathways, requirements, and financial options. |
| English | Revise career summaries, portfolio statements, and planning explanations for clarity, organization, and conventions. |
| Writing | Support claims about career decisions with relevant evidence and reasoning. |
| Science/Data Reasoning as Support | Interpret wage, employment, growth, cost, debt, earnings, and placement data tables without treating ACT Science as a primary content standard. |

## ELD / Accessibility Support

Lessons and assessments must include explicit vocabulary, visual organizers, tables, models, sentence frames where appropriate, self-check criteria, and clear instructions so English learners and students needing access supports can complete the work without outside navigation or teacher-led explanation.
