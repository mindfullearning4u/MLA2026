P01 Lesson Overview

Credentials, Licenses, Certificates, and Degrees

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PATH.3. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 03.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain credentialing and stacking options. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to explain credentialing and stacking options using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the credential stack diagram, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
