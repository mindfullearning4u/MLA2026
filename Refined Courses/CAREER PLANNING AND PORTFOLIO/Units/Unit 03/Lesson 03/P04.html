P04 Worked Example

Credentials, Licenses, Certificates, and Degrees

Worked Example 1: Name the credential

Prompt: A student wants to become a practical nurse and later a registered nurse. The student must understand how certificates, licenses, and degrees may stack.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on name the credential.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the credential stack diagram and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: A license is different from a certificate because it may be legally required to practice.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.PATH.3 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not use all credential words as if they mean the same thing.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.
