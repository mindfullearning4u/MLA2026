P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Credentials, Licenses, Certificates, and Degrees

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PATH.3. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 03.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain credentialing and stacking options. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Credentials, Licenses, Certificates, and Degrees: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
credentialrecognized proof that a person has learned or can do specific work
licensepermission from an authority to perform regulated work
stackingearning credentials in a sequence that builds toward higher opportunity

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to explain credentialing and stacking options. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student wants to become a practical nurse and later a registered nurse. The student must understand how certificates, licenses, and degrees may stack.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.
