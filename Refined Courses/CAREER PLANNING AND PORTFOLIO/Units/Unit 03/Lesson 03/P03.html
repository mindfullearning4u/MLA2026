Credentials, Licenses, Certificates, and Degrees

Continue the Teaching Sequence

  1. Match evidence to the correct category. Use the credential stack diagram to decide which part of the standard the evidence supports.
  2. Explain why the evidence matters. A complete career-planning answer does not only name an option; it explains why the option fits the evidence.
  3. Check for missing information. If the evidence does not answer an important question, name that gap instead of pretending the plan is final.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example: I choose this because it seems like a good career.

Correct example: I choose this as the next option to research because the evidence shows a match between the lesson standard, the scenario details, and the planning criteria in the organizer.

Teachable Explanation

The incorrect answer is too general. The correct answer names the evidence path: standard, scenario, organizer, and decision. This keeps the lesson inside the mapped standard and prevents unsupported career advice.

Self-Check: If another student could ask, "How do you know?", your answer must point to a specific detail in the scenario, table, organizer, or standard.
