P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Options After High School

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PATH.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 03.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to evaluate the pros and cons of education, employment, entrepreneurship, or enlistment immediately after high school. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Options After High School: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
postsecondary pathwaya route after high school toward work, training, service, or education
entrepreneurshipstarting or operating a business or venture
enlistmentjoining a branch of military service

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to evaluate the pros and cons of education, employment, entrepreneurship, or enlistment immediately after high school. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student wants to earn income quickly but also needs a credential for the preferred long-term occupation.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
