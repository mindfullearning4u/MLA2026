P01 Lesson Overview

Synthesis: My Postsecondary Pathway Evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PATH.1; MLA.CPP.PATH.2; MLA.CPP.PATH.3; MLA.CPP.PATH.4. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 03.01-03.06.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to integrate pathway, provider, credential, and outcome evidence into a postsecondary pathway decision. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to integrate pathway, provider, credential, and outcome evidence into a postsecondary pathway decision using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the pathway decision organizer, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
