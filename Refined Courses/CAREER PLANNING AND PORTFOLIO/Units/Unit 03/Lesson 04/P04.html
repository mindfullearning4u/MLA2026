P04 Worked Example

Comparing Cost, Debt, Earnings, and Placement

Worked Example 1: Compare more than price

Prompt: A student compares three medical assisting programs. One is cheaper, one has stronger placement, and one has limited outcome data.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on compare more than price.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the cost/outcome comparison table and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: The lowest cost provider may not be best if completion or placement support is weak.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.PATH.4 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not choose by tuition alone.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
