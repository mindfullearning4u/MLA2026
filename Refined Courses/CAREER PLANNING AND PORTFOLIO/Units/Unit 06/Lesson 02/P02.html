P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Revising Portfolio Artifacts for Quality

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PORT.2. Portfolio evidence vehicle for CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.0-04.0.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to revise portfolio artifacts for clarity, accuracy, audience, and evidence quality. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Revising Portfolio Artifacts for Quality: Evidence Before Decision

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to revise portfolio artifacts for clarity, accuracy, audience, and evidence quality. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student's occupation comparison has good data but unclear labels, missing sources, and a conclusion that does not match the table.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
