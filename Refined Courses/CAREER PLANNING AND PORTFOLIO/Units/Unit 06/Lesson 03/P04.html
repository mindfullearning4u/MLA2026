P04 Worked Example

Presenting a Career and Academic Plan

Worked Example 1: Open with a claim

Prompt: A student has strong artifacts but presents them in random order. The audience cannot tell what the career goal is or how the plan supports it.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on open with a claim.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the presentation planner and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: My current goal is to explore diagnostic medical sonography because my evidence shows strong fit.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.PORT.3 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not begin by reading every artifact title.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.

Teacher of Record Support: Ask your Teacher of Record for help after you have used the lesson explanation, examples, visual organizer, and self-check criteria.
