P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Readiness Gaps and Next Steps

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PORT.4. Portfolio evidence vehicle for CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.0-04.0.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to reflect on readiness gaps and next steps for academic, career, financial, and portfolio growth. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Readiness Gaps and Next Steps: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
readiness gapa missing skill, requirement, experience, or information needed for the plan
next stepa specific action that moves the plan forward
reflection evidencedetails showing what was learned and what needs to happen next

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to reflect on readiness gaps and next steps for academic, career, financial, and portfolio growth. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student's plan is promising, but the student has not checked program prerequisites, scholarship requirements, or whether the chosen career includes shift work.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
