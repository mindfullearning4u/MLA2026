P01 Lesson Overview

Readiness Gaps and Next Steps

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PORT.4. Portfolio evidence vehicle for CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.0-04.0.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to reflect on readiness gaps and next steps for academic, career, financial, and portfolio growth. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to reflect on readiness gaps and next steps for academic, career, financial, and portfolio growth using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the gap action table, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
