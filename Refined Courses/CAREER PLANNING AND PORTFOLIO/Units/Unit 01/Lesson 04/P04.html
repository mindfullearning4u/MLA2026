P04 Worked Example

Career Choices and Positive Impact

Worked Example 1: Identify the beneficiary

Prompt: A student is comparing cybersecurity, nursing, and environmental technology. Each could help others, but the type of impact is different.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on identify the beneficiary.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the impact values organizer and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: Nursing often has direct impact on patients; cybersecurity may protect people indirectly by protecting systems.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.SELF.4 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not say only it helps people without naming how.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
