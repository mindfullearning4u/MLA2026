P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Career Choices and Positive Impact

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.SELF.4. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.04.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain values related to positive impact when weighing career choices. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Career Choices and Positive Impact: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
positive impacta helpful effect on people, communities, systems, or the environment
service valuea belief about helping others through work
impact evidencedetails showing who benefits and how

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to explain values related to positive impact when weighing career choices. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student is comparing cybersecurity, nursing, and environmental technology. Each could help others, but the type of impact is different.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

impact values organizer

Impact areaCareer evidence to look forQuestion for reflection
PeopleThe work supports health, safety, education, service, or well-beingWho is helped directly?
CommunityThe work improves local systems, access, infrastructure, or civic lifeWhat problem is reduced?
World or environmentThe work addresses larger needs such as sustainability, technology, or public healthWhat long-term result matters?
Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
