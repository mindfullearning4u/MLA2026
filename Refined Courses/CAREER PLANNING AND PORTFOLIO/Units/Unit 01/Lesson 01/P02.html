P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Career Interests and Personal Evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.SELF.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain career interests using assessment results, personal experience, research, and reflection. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Career Interests and Personal Evidence: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
career interesta type of work activity or problem that attracts your attention
assessment evidenceinformation from an inventory, survey, or reflection that supports a conclusion
reflectioncareful thinking about what evidence says about you

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to explain career interests using assessment results, personal experience, research, and reflection. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student completes an interest inventory and notices high scores in helping, organizing information, and solving practical problems. The student also enjoys tutoring classmates and planning club events.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
