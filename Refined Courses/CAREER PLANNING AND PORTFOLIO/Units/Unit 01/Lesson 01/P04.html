P04 Worked Example

Career Interests and Personal Evidence

Worked Example 1: Interest evidence

Prompt: A student completes an interest inventory and notices high scores in helping, organizing information, and solving practical problems. The student also enjoys tutoring classmates and planning club events.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on interest evidence.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the interest evidence table and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: The tutoring and club planning evidence points toward helping and organization interests.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.SELF.1 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not list a career just because it sounds impressive.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
