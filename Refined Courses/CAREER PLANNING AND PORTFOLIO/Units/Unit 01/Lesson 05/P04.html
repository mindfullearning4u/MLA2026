Synthesis: My Career Self-Knowledge Profile

Worked Example 1: Combine evidence

Prompt: A student has interests in helping and organizing, strengths in communication, a growth area in time management, and values a stable schedule and direct service.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on combine evidence.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the self-knowledge profile and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: Helping plus communication plus direct service may point toward education, health, counseling, or customer success careers.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.SELF.1, MLA.CPP.SELF.2, MLA.CPP.SELF.3, MLA.CPP.SELF.4 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not choose one result from one inventory as the final answer.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
