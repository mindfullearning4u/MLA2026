P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Synthesis: My Career Self-Knowledge Profile

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.SELF.1; MLA.CPP.SELF.2; MLA.CPP.SELF.3; MLA.CPP.SELF.4. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.01-01.04.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to integrate interests, skills, lifestyle values, workplace values, and impact values into a career self-knowledge profile. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Synthesis: My Career Self-Knowledge Profile: Evidence Before Decision

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to integrate interests, skills, lifestyle values, workplace values, and impact values into a career self-knowledge profile. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student has interests in helping and organizing, strengths in communication, a growth area in time management, and values a stable schedule and direct service.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
