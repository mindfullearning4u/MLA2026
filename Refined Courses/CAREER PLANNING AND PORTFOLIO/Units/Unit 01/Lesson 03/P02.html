P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Lifestyle and Workplace Values

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.SELF.3. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain lifestyle and workplace values that affect career decisions. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Lifestyle and Workplace Values: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
lifestyle valuea life condition that matters when choosing work
workplace valuea feature of the work setting or job role that matters to you
tradeoffa decision in which gaining one value may require giving up part of another

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to explain lifestyle and workplace values that affect career decisions. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student wants high earnings, a predictable schedule, and outdoor work. The student compares nursing, construction management, and accounting.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
