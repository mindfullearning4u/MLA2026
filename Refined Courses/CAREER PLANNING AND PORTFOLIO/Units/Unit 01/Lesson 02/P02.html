P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Career Skills: Strengths and Growth Areas

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.SELF.2. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 01.02.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to describe current and developable career-related skills using assessment evidence and reflection. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Career Skills: Strengths and Growth Areas: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
career-related skillan ability used in workplace tasks
strengtha skill you can already use with consistency
growth areaa skill that can improve with practice, training, feedback, or experience

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to describe current and developable career-related skills using assessment evidence and reflection. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student says communication is a strength because they lead group discussions, but time management is a growth area because long projects are often finished at the last minute.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
