Career Skills: Strengths and Growth Areas

Worked Example 1: Separate skill from personality

Prompt: A student says communication is a strength because they lead group discussions, but time management is a growth area because long projects are often finished at the last minute.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on separate skill from personality.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the skills growth chart and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: Communication is a skill because it can be observed in speaking, writing, listening, and feedback.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.SELF.2 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not write I am nice as the whole skill description.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
