P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Florida Graduation Pathways and Career Goals

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.ACAD.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 04.07.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to describe ways to meet Florida graduation pathways while preparing for career goals. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Florida Graduation Pathways and Career Goals: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
graduation pathwaythe required course, assessment, and credit route toward a diploma
diploma designationan added recognition based on meeting specific requirements
career preparationcourses and experiences that build readiness for a future occupation

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to describe ways to meet Florida graduation pathways while preparing for career goals. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student wants a health career and must choose electives while keeping graduation requirements on track.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
