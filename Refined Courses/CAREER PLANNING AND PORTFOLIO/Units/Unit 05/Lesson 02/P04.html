Accelerated Credit, CTE, Work-Based Learning, and Certifications

Worked Example 1: Align to goals

Prompt: A student interested in information technology compares a CTE program, an industry certification, and dual enrollment.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on align to goals.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the opportunity alignment chart and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: An IT certification may show technical readiness for entry-level support roles.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.ACAD.1 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not collect credentials that do not support the goal.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
