P07 Checkpoint

Synthesis: Occupation Research and Evidence Summary

Teacher of Record Graded: This checkpoint is reviewed for mastery evidence.

Checkpoint Task: Create a synthesis response that combines the unit evidence into one clear planning conclusion. Your response must include a claim, at least two pieces of evidence, reasoning that connects the evidence to the claim, and a final next-step statement.

Notebook Evidence Submission: Submit your P06 work and the checkpoint response according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Submission: Your submission should be clear enough that the Teacher of Record can see what evidence you used and how you reasoned from it.

Submission Workflow: Submit the checkpoint, review feedback, make corrections if required, and follow the retake or resubmission directions provided by the Teacher of Record.

Mastery Criteria: 80% mastery requires accurate vocabulary, correct use of evidence, complete reasoning, and alignment to MLA.CPP.LMI.1, MLA.CPP.LMI.2, MLA.CPP.LMI.3, MLA.CPP.LMI.4.

Correction / Resubmission: If your work does not meet mastery, revise the evidence, reasoning, or standard connection before resubmitting.

TOR Intervention: The Teacher of Record may require review, remediation, or a conference before another attempt.
