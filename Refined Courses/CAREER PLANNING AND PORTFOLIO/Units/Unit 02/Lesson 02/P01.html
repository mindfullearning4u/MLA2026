P01 Lesson Overview

Wages, Poverty Thresholds, and Family-Sustaining Income

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.LMI.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 02.02.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to define and compare poverty, average wage, and family-sustaining wage thresholds. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to define and compare poverty, average wage, and family-sustaining wage thresholds using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the wage threshold table, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
