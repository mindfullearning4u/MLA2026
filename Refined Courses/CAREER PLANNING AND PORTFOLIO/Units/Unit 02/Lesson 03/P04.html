P04 Worked Example

Reading Regional Labor-Market Data

Worked Example 1: Employment

Prompt: A student sees that one occupation has many current jobs, another is growing quickly, and a third pays more. The student must decide what the data actually means.

Step 1 - Identify the task: This example focuses on employment.

Step 2 - Find the evidence: Use the labor-market data table and the scenario details before writing the answer.

Step 3 - Explain the reasoning: High employment may mean many openings, but it does not always mean high pay.

Step 4 - Interpret the answer: This supports MLA.CPP.LMI.2 because it connects a planning decision to evidence.

Incorrect: Do not assume many jobs means best career.

Correct: Name the evidence, explain the connection, and state what the evidence suggests for the plan.

Teachable Explanation

All three worked examples use the same mastery routine: identify the task, locate evidence, explain reasoning, and interpret the decision. That routine matters because career planning can become opinion-based if the student skips evidence.

Mastery Check: Before moving on, make sure you can explain why each example answer is supported and why the incorrect example is not strong enough.
