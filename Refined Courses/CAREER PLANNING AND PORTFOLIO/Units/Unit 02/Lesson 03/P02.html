P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Reading Regional Labor-Market Data

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.LMI.2. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 02.03.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to use regional labor-market data to identify top occupations by employment, growth, and salary. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Reading Regional Labor-Market Data: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
employmentthe number of jobs or workers in an occupation
growthexpected increase in jobs over time
salarypay earned in an occupation

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to use regional labor-market data to identify top occupations by employment, growth, and salary. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student sees that one occupation has many current jobs, another is growing quickly, and a third pays more. The student must decide what the data actually means.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.
