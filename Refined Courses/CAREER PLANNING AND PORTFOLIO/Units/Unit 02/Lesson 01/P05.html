P05 Guided Practice

Career Clusters, Industries, and Occupations

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.LMI.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 02.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to define career clusters, industries, and occupations and explain how they are related. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Guided Practice helps you check whether you can apply the lesson evidence routine before graded mastery work. Read each question carefully, use the cluster hierarchy chart, and study the feedback for every answer choice.

Before Guided Practice

  • Review the vocabulary table from P02.
  • Review the worked examples from P04.
  • Use the organizer to identify evidence before choosing an answer.
  • Write down one question you still need to clarify.

After Guided Practice

After Guided Practice, you will complete the Lesson Quiz for this lesson.

Do not move to the next graded step until you can explain the correct answer and the misconception behind at least one incorrect answer.

Teacher of Record Support: Ask your Teacher of Record for help after you have used the lesson explanation, examples, visual organizer, and self-check criteria. The Teacher of Record can help you review feedback, identify a misconception, plan remediation, confirm checkpoint expectations, and determine when you are ready for another attempt.
