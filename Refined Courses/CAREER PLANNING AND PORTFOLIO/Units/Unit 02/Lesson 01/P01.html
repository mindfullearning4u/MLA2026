P01 Lesson Overview

Career Clusters, Industries, and Occupations

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.LMI.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 02.01.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to define career clusters, industries, and occupations and explain how they are related. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to define career clusters, industries, and occupations and explain how they are related using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the cluster hierarchy chart, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
