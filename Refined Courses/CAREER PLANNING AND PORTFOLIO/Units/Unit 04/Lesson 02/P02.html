P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Explaining the Career Choice with Evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PLAN.1. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 04.02.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to explain the reasoning for choosing a preferred occupation. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Explaining the Career Choice with Evidence: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
claimthe decision or position being explained
evidencefacts, data, or examples that support the claim
reasoningthe explanation that connects evidence to the claim

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to explain the reasoning for choosing a preferred occupation. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student chooses physical therapist assistant because it matches helping interests, uses communication skills, has clear training requirements, and shows regional growth.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
