P01 Lesson Overview

Paying for Postsecondary Training

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PLAN.4. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 04.06.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to define ways to pay for postsecondary training, including scholarships, grants, savings, work, work-study, private loans, and federal loans. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how to define ways to pay for postsecondary training, including scholarships, grants, savings, work, work-study, private loans, and federal loans using evidence instead of guesses.

What You Will Do: You will study a scenario, use the funding comparison chart, complete notebook work, practice with feedback, and prepare a checkpoint response.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will use accurate vocabulary, cite specific evidence, complete the lesson tasks, and reach at least 80% mastery on required assessments.
