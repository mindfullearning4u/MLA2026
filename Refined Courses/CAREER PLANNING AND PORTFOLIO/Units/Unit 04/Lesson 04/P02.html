P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Paying for Postsecondary Training

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PLAN.4. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 04.06.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to define ways to pay for postsecondary training, including scholarships, grants, savings, work, work-study, private loans, and federal loans. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Paying for Postsecondary Training: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
scholarshipmoney awarded for education that usually does not need to be repaid
grantfinancial aid that usually does not need to be repaid
loanborrowed money that must be repaid, often with interest

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to define ways to pay for postsecondary training, including scholarships, grants, savings, work, work-study, private loans, and federal loans. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student has a program cost of 8,000 dollars and considers a state scholarship, part-time work, a grant, and a small federal loan.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.

Notebook Check: Write one sentence naming the strongest evidence in the scenario and one sentence explaining what that evidence does and does not prove.
