P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Credentials, Technical Skills, and Employability Skills

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.CPP.PLAN.2; MLA.CPP.PLAN.3. CPALMS/FDOE Career and Education Planning 04.03-04.05.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches you to identify credentials, technical skills, employability skills, entrepreneurship skills, and prioritize credential/provider choices. The support skills are reading career information, using evidence, interpreting tables or organizers, and explaining decisions clearly.

Notebook Title

Credentials, Technical Skills, and Employability Skills: Evidence Before Decision

Vocabulary

VocabularyMeaning in this lesson
technical skilla specific work skill connected to tools, procedures, systems, or knowledge
employability skilla transferable workplace skill such as communication, reliability, teamwork, or problem solving
provider choicethe selected program or training source for a credential goal

Teaching Sequence

  1. Start with the standard. The lesson asks you to identify credentials, technical skills, employability skills, entrepreneurship skills, and prioritize credential/provider choices. That means your answer must show evidence, not just preference.
  2. Read the scenario. A student interested in web development compares an industry certification, a technical college certificate, and an associate degree pathway.
  3. Separate facts from assumptions. Facts are details given in the scenario or table. Assumptions are guesses that may sound reasonable but are not proven.
  4. Use the visual organizer. The organizer gives you a repeatable method for sorting evidence before writing an answer.
