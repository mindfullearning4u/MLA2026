Compliance Rule
Lesson audits are recorded as each lesson is built.
Unit audits are recorded after a full unit is built, fixed, re-audited, and locked.
Course audits are recorded after all 6 units are complete and the MLA Master Course Certification Audit is run.
The approved standards crosswalk, approved unit mapping, and approved course overview are the only approved source documents for standards, unit sequencing, and course-level structure.
Unit Overview.md files are ignored when they exist.
