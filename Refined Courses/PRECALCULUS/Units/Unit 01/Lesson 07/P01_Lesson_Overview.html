Systems and Piecewise Function Modeling

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.MOD.1 / MA.912.AR.9.3
  • MLA.PC.MOD.2 / MA.912.AR.9.10

What You Will Learn

  • How systems connect algebraic solutions to graph intersections.
  • How piecewise functions model relationships that use different rules on different intervals.

What You Will Do

  • Solve systems algebraically and graphically.
  • Interpret piecewise function rules and constraints.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook work, checkpoint reasoning, and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will show that you can solve systems and interpret piecewise models using equations, graphs, intervals, and context.
