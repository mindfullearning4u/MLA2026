Function Composition

Composition means one function's output becomes another function's input. The notation (f o g)(x) means f(g(x)).

Task

Use two functions from your lesson materials. Find f(g(x)) and g(f(x)), then compare the results and any domain restrictions.

Evidence

Your work should show correct substitution and explain why the order of composition matters.
