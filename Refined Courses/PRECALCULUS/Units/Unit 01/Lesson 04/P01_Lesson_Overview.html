Function Operations and Composition

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.FA.8 / MA.912.F.3.3
  • MLA.PC.FA.9 / MA.912.F.3.4
  • MLA.PC.FA.10 / MA.912.F.3.5

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine functions using addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • How to represent composite functions algebraically and from tables.
  • How domain restrictions affect combined and composite functions.

What You Will Do

  • Evaluate and simplify function operations.
  • Evaluate and write composite functions.
  • Track restrictions caused by denominators, radicals, and inner functions.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook tasks on operations and composition.
  • Submit checkpoint reasoning about a combined or composite function.
  • Complete the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will show that you can treat functions as objects that can be combined, composed, evaluated, and interpreted with attention to domain restrictions.
