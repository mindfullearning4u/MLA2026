Domain Restrictions

Some functions are not one-to-one over their full domain. Restricting the domain can produce an invertible function.

Task

Use a quadratic or absolute value function from your lesson materials. Identify why the full function is not invertible, choose a valid restricted domain, and describe the inverse relationship on that domain.

Evidence

Your work should explain how the restriction supports a one-to-one relationship.
