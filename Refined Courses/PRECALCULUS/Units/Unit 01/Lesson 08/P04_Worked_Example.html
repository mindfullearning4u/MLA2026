Common Mistake

Incorrect: Choose a method based only on the first formula you see.

Correct: Identify the function type, representation, and question target before choosing a method.

A formula may look familiar but still require a different strategy. For example, a composition problem and a multiplication problem can both show two functions, but the notation tells you which process is correct.
