Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use only the real part when converting a complex number to polar form.

Correct: Use both the real part and imaginary part to find the modulus and argument.

Polar and trigonometric forms describe both distance and direction. The real and imaginary parts work together to determine the complex number location.
