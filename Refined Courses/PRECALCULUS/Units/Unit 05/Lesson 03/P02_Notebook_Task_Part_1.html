Notebook Title

Dot Products, Projections, and Vector Applications

Vocabulary

  • Dot Product: A scalar result from multiplying vector components.
  • Projection: The shadow of one vector onto another.
  • Angle Between Vectors: The angle formed by two vectors from a common tail.
