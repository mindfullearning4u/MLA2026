P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Complex Coordinate Distance, Midpoint, and Problem Solving

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.VC.2 - MA.912.NSO.2.3: Calculate the distance and midpoint between two numbers on the complex coordinate plane.
  • MLA.PC.VC.3 - MA.912.NSO.2.4: Solve mathematical and real-world problems involving complex numbers represented algebraically or on the coordinate plane.

What You Will Learn

  • How to work with calculating distance and midpoint on the complex coordinate plane and solving problems involving complex numbers.
  • How to connect algebraic complex-number notation to points, movement, and meaning on the complex plane.
  • How to explain each step using clear mathematical reasoning.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes with vocabulary and step-by-step procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes before guided practice.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and the lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use accurate complex-number and coordinate-plane vocabulary.
  • Show step-by-step reasoning in notebook and independent work.
  • Complete guided practice, checkpoint, and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you see complex numbers as both algebraic expressions and geometric objects on a coordinate plane.
