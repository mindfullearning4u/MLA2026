P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Unit Assessment

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.VC.1 - MA.912.NSO.2.2: Represent addition, subtraction, multiplication and conjugation of complex numbers geometrically on the complex plane.
  • MLA.PC.VC.2 - MA.912.NSO.2.3: Calculate the distance and midpoint between two numbers on the complex coordinate plane.
  • MLA.PC.VC.3 - MA.912.NSO.2.4: Solve mathematical and real-world problems involving complex numbers represented algebraically or on the coordinate plane.
  • MLA.PC.VC.4 - MA.912.NSO.2.5: Represent complex numbers on the complex plane in rectangular and polar forms.
  • MLA.PC.VC.5 - MA.912.NSO.2.6: Rewrite complex numbers to trigonometric form. Multiply complex numbers in trigonometric form.
  • MLA.PC.PA.1 - MA.912.GR.7.1: Given a conic section, describe how it can result from the slicing of two cones.
  • MLA.PC.PA.2 - MA.912.GR.7.2: Given a mathematical or real-world context, derive and create the equation of a circle using key features.
  • MLA.PC.PA.3 - MA.912.GR.7.3: Graph and solve mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with an equation of a circle. Determine and interpret key features in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.PA.4 - MA.912.GR.7.4: Given a mathematical or real-world context, derive and create the equation of a parabola using key features.
  • MLA.PC.PA.5 - MA.912.GR.7.5: Graph and solve mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with an equation of a parabola. Determine and interpret key features in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.PA.6 - MA.912.GR.7.6: Given a mathematical or real-world context, derive and create the equation of an ellipse using key features.
  • MLA.PC.PA.7 - MA.912.GR.7.7: Graph and solve mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with an equation of an ellipse. Determine and interpret key features in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.PA.8 - MA.912.GR.7.8: Given a mathematical or real-world context, derive and create the equation of a hyperbola using key features.
  • MLA.PC.PA.9 - MA.912.GR.7.9: Graph and solve mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with an equation of a hyperbola. Determine and interpret key features in terms of the context.

What You Will Learn

  • How to work with all Unit 5 complex-number and conic-section standards.
  • How to identify key features, representations, equations, and context.
  • How to explain each step using precise mathematical language.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes with vocabulary and step-by-step procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes before guided practice.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use accurate vocabulary from the lesson.
  • Show step-by-step reasoning in notebook and independent work.
  • Complete guided practice, checkpoint, and the Unit Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use all Unit 5 complex-number and conic-section standards to solve and explain Precalculus problems.
