Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treat the number on the right side of a circle equation as the radius.

Correct: The right side of (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 is the radius squared, so take the square root to find the radius.

Circle equations store the radius as r^2. If the equation has 25 on the right side, the radius is 5, not 25.
