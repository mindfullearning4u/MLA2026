Notebook Title

Conic Sections from Cones and Circles

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Conic section: a curve formed by slicing a cone or double cone with a plane.
  • Circle: the set of all points in a plane the same distance from a center point.
  • Center: the fixed point inside a circle written as (h, k).
  • Radius: the distance from the center to any point on the circle.
  • Standard form of a circle: (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2.
  • Key features: the center, radius, graph, and contextual meaning of a circle.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on describing conic sections from slices of cones and deriving, graphing, and interpreting equations of circles.
