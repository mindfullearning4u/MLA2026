P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1

Problem: A right triangle has opposite side 9 and hypotenuse 15. Find sin(theta), then find theta to the nearest degree.

  1. Identify the trigonometric relationship needed.
  2. Rewrite or set up the expression carefully.
  3. Simplify or solve step by step.
  4. State the final answer and why it fits.

Worked Example 2

Problem: Solve tan(theta) = 1 for 0° <= theta < 180°.

  1. Choose the identity, ratio, or equation structure.
  2. Substitute values into the correct positions.
  3. Carry out the algebra or calculation carefully.
  4. Check restrictions, signs, and context before finalizing.

Worked Example 3

Problem: A ladder reaches a wall at a 65° angle with the ground and the ladder is 12 feet long. Find the height reached on the wall.

  1. Translate the expression or context into trigonometric language.
  2. Use the matching identity or ratio.
  3. Keep each step equivalent to the previous step.
  4. Explain the result in a complete sentence.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Solve a trigonometric ratio problem without checking whether the answer makes sense in the context.

Correct: Solve the ratio equation, then check the solution against the original equation and the real-world context.

Trigonometric ratio problems often involve lengths, angles, or restrictions. A correct solution must satisfy the equation and make sense for the situation.
