Common Mistake

Incorrect: Replace every trigonometric expression with a decimal approximation before simplifying.

Correct: Use identities to rewrite expressions exactly, then combine like factors or common denominators.

Simplifying with identities is exact reasoning. Decimals can hide structure, while identities show why two expressions are equivalent.
