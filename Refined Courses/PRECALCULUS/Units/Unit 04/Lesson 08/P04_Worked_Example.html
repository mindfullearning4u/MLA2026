P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1

Problem: Choose the correct law for a triangle problem and explain why it fits.

  1. Identify the problem type and the requested result.
  2. Choose the identity, formula, theorem, or ratio.
  3. Substitute or rewrite carefully.
  4. Solve or simplify and state the result.

Worked Example 2

Problem: Simplify a trigonometric expression using a reciprocal or Pythagorean identity.

  1. Identify the known information.
  2. Choose the method that matches the structure.
  3. Carry out each algebraic or trigonometric step carefully.
  4. Check signs, restrictions, and context.

Worked Example 3

Problem: Solve a one-variable trigonometric ratio problem and check the context.

  1. Translate the problem into trigonometric language.
  2. Use the matching identity, formula, theorem, or ratio.
  3. Keep each step equivalent to the previous step.
  4. Explain why the final answer fits the original problem.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Prepare for the Unit Assessment by memorizing formulas without checking which problem type each formula matches.

Correct: Identify the problem type, choose the matching tool, show the setup, and check the result.

The Unit Assessment requires method selection. You must decide whether the problem needs a triangle theorem, area formula, identity, angle formula, simplification, or ratio equation.
