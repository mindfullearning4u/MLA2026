P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Extend your notes by organizing the method into a sequence you can use independently.

Step-by-Step Method

  1. Read the problem and identify what representation is given.
  2. Match the representation to the correct definition, formula, or feature.
  3. Carry out the calculation or comparison one step at a time.
  4. Check that the answer fits the question and the standard.
  5. Write a final explanation in a complete sentence.

What To Write In Your Notebook

Write the method as a checklist, then add one sentence explaining how each step helps you avoid guessing. Include the standard code beside the notes so your evidence is connected to the approved mapping.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Using a shortcut before identifying what the question is asking.

Correct: Identify the representation, choose the matching method, and explain the conclusion with evidence.

Most errors happen when a student starts calculating before naming the feature or condition. The correction is to slow down, match the task to the standard, and then complete the work.

Teachable Explanation

The correct method works because it connects the given representation to the lesson relationship, checks the relevant restrictions or features, and explains the conclusion instead of relying on appearance or memorized steps.
