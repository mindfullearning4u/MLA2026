P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Conics and Complex Plane Relationships

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.PA.1: Identify conic sections and their defining features.
  • MLA.PC.PA.2: Analyze circles as conic sections.
  • MLA.PC.PA.3: Write and interpret circle equations.
  • MLA.PC.PA.4: Analyze parabolas as conic sections.
  • MLA.PC.PA.5: Write and interpret parabola equations.
  • MLA.PC.PA.6: Analyze ellipses as conic sections.
  • MLA.PC.PA.7: Write and interpret ellipse equations.
  • MLA.PC.PA.8: Analyze hyperbolas as conic sections.
  • MLA.PC.PA.9: Write and interpret hyperbola equations.
  • MLA.PC.VC.1: Represent complex numbers on the complex plane.
  • MLA.PC.VC.2: Use distance with complex numbers.
  • MLA.PC.VC.3: Use midpoint relationships with complex numbers.
  • MLA.PC.VC.4: Write complex numbers in polar and trigonometric form.
  • MLA.PC.VC.5: Use operations with complex numbers in polar form.

What You Will Learn

  • conic equations.
  • complex plane.
  • polar form.
