Notebook Title

Area of a Triangle Using Two Sides and the Included Angle

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Triangle area: the amount of space inside a triangle.
  • Included angle: the angle formed by the two known sides.
  • Area formula with sine: A = 1/2ab sin(C), where C is the included angle between sides a and b.
  • Sine of an angle: a trigonometric value that helps measure height indirectly.
  • Exact area: an area written with exact values instead of a decimal approximation.
  • Approximate area: an area rounded to a requested decimal place.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on finding triangle area when two side lengths and the included angle are known.

A triangle problem should begin with a careful reading of the given sides, angles, and what the question asks you to find.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Draw or label the triangle before calculating.
  2. Identify the given side lengths and angle measures.
  3. Choose the formula that matches the information given.
  4. Substitute values carefully and solve one step at a time.
  5. Write the answer with units or a clear explanation when the problem has context.
