Before You Start

For each Guided Practice item, identify whether the question is testing finding triangle area when two side lengths and the included angle are known.

Guided Practice Strategy

  1. Read the question and identify what is given.
  2. Choose the formula or relationship that fits.
  3. Substitute values in the correct places.
  4. Check that the answer matches the requested side, angle, or area.
