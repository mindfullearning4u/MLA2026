Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use A = 1/2bh even when no height is given, and choose either side as the height.

Correct: When two sides and the included angle are given, use A = 1/2ab sin(C). The sine factor accounts for the height created by the included angle.

The included-angle area formula is built from the base-height formula. The expression b sin(C) represents the height created by the angle, so the formula works even when the height is not directly given.
