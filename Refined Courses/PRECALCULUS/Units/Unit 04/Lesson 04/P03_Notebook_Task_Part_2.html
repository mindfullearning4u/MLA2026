Deepening the Concept

The main idea in this lesson is using angle sum, difference, double-angle, and half-angle formulas for sine, cosine, and tangent.

Identity work is not random algebra. Every replacement must come from a known identity, formula, reciprocal relationship, or ratio relationship.

Notebook Task

Create a three-column organizer labeled Expression, Identity or Formula Used, and Reason.

Use the organizer to explain each transformation instead of only writing calculations.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: sin(A + B) = sin(A) + sin(B).

Correct: sin(A + B) = sin(A)cos(B) + cos(A)sin(B).

Trigonometric formulas do not distribute like ordinary multiplication. Angle formulas have specific structures, and each term has a reason connected to unit circle and coordinate relationships.
