Notebook Title

Law of Sines and Law of Cosines

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Oblique triangle: a triangle that is not a right triangle.
  • Law of Sines: a relationship that connects side lengths to the sines of their opposite angles.
  • Law of Cosines: a relationship that connects three side lengths and one included angle.
  • Opposite pair: a side and the angle across from that side.
  • Included angle: the angle between two given sides.
  • Ambiguous case: a possible SSA situation where zero, one, or two triangles may fit the given information.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on choosing and applying the Law of Sines and the Law of Cosines to solve triangle problems.

A triangle problem should begin with a careful reading of the given sides, angles, and what the question asks you to find.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Draw or label the triangle before calculating.
  2. Identify the given side lengths and angle measures.
  3. Choose the formula that matches the information given.
  4. Substitute values carefully and solve one step at a time.
  5. Write the answer with units or a clear explanation when the problem has context.
