Deepening the Concept

The main idea in this lesson is choosing and applying the Law of Sines and the Law of Cosines to solve triangle problems.

The formula is not chosen by guessing. It is chosen by matching the structure of the information to the structure of the formula.

Notebook Task

Create a three-column organizer labeled Given Information, Formula Choice, and Reason.

For each practice situation, write what is known, which formula should be used, and why that formula fits.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use the Law of Sines for every triangle problem because it looks simpler.

Correct: Choose the formula based on the information given. Use Law of Sines when an opposite side-angle pair is known. Use Law of Cosines for SAS or SSS information.

The formula choice comes from the structure of the triangle information. A correct setup must match sides with opposite angles and must not assume a right triangle unless one is given.
