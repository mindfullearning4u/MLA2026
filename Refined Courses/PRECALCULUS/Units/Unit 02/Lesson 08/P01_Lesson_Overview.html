Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Unit Assessment

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.FA.1 - MA.912.AR.6.3: Explain and apply theorems for polynomials to solve mathematical and real-world problems.
  • MLA.PC.FA.2 - MA.912.AR.6.4: Given a table, equation or written description of a polynomial function of degree 3 or higher, graph that function and determine its key features.
  • MLA.PC.FA.3 - MA.912.AR.6.6: Solve and graph mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with polynomial functions of degree 3 or higher. Interpret key features and determine constraints in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.FA.5 - MA.912.AR.8.3: Solve and graph mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with rational functions. Interpret key features and determine constraints in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.EX.1 - MA.912.AR.5.7: Solve and graph mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with exponential functions. Interpret key features and determine constraints in terms of the context.
  • MLA.PC.EX.2 - MA.912.AR.5.9: Solve and graph mathematical and real-world problems that are modeled with logarithmic functions. Interpret key features and determine constraints in terms of the context.

What You Will Learn

  • How to synthesize all Unit 2 benchmarks through function model selection, key features, constraints, and interpretation.
  • How to choose the correct model, feature, restriction, or strategy.
  • How to explain answers using function evidence and context.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes with vocabulary and step-by-step procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes before guided practice.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use accurate function modeling vocabulary.
  • Show step-by-step reasoning in notebook and independent work.
  • Complete the guided practice, checkpoint, and required assessment.
