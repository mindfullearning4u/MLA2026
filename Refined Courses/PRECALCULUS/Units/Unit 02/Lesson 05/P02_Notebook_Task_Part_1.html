Notebook Title

Exponential Function Models and Constraints

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Exponential function: a function where the variable appears in the exponent.
  • Initial value: the output when the input is 0.
  • Growth factor: a multiplier greater than 1 that increases outputs over equal intervals.
  • Decay factor: a multiplier between 0 and 1 that decreases outputs over equal intervals.
  • Percent growth: repeated increase by a percent rate.
  • Percent decay: repeated decrease by a percent rate.
  • Horizontal asymptote: a horizontal line the graph approaches.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on exponential growth, exponential decay, initial value, growth factor, decay factor, asymptotic behavior, and contextual constraints.

A model should be interpreted by reading the formula, identifying the key features, and checking what the context allows.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the type of model and what each variable represents.
  2. Find the starting value, base, restriction, or boundary that matters.
  3. Connect the feature to the graph and to the context.
  4. Write a sentence explaining what the result means.
