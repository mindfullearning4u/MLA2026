Common Mistake

Incorrect: If a polynomial formula works algebraically, every x-value must make sense in the real-world model.

Correct: A real-world polynomial model may have a restricted domain based on the context.

The equation can often accept many x-values, but the situation may not. Time, length, number of items, and distance usually cannot be negative, so the domain must match the context.
