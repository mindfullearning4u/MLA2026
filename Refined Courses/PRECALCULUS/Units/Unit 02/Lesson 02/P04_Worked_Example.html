Common Mistake

Incorrect: Every zero of a polynomial graph must cross the x-axis.

Correct: A graph may cross or touch at a zero depending on multiplicity.

Odd multiplicity usually crosses the x-axis. Even multiplicity usually touches the x-axis and turns around. The zero is still an x-intercept, but the graph behavior is different.
