P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Angle Measure, Degrees, and Radians

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.TR.7 - MA.912.T.2.1: Given any positive or negative angle measure in degrees or radians, identify its corresponding angle measure between 0° and 360° or between 0 and 2π. Convert between degrees and radians.

What You Will Learn

  • How to work with angle measure, degrees, radians, coterminal angles, and conversion between degree and radian units.
  • How to connect angle or unit circle information to trigonometric meaning.
  • How to explain each answer using a clear mathematical reason.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes with vocabulary and step-by-step procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes before guided practice.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use accurate trigonometry vocabulary.
  • Show step-by-step reasoning in notebook and independent work.
  • Complete guided practice, checkpoint, and the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you build the foundation for trigonometry by connecting angle measure, circle structure, coordinates, and function values.
