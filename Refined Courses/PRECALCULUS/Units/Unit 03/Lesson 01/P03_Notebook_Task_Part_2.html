Common Mistake

Incorrect: 450° is already between 0° and 360° because it is positive.

Correct: 450° is coterminal with 90° because 450° - 360° = 90°.

A positive angle can still be larger than one full rotation. To find a matching angle between 0° and 360°, subtract 360° until the result is in the required interval.
