Notebook Title

Angle Measure, Degrees, and Radians

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Angle: a rotation formed by turning from one ray to another ray.
  • Degree: an angle unit where one full rotation is 360°.
  • Radian: an angle unit based on arc length on a circle.
  • Full rotation: one complete turn, equal to 360° or 2π radians.
  • Coterminal angles: angles that end at the same terminal side after adding or subtracting full rotations.
  • Terminal side: the ending ray after an angle rotation.
  • Standard position: an angle drawn with its starting side on the positive x-axis.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on angle measure, degrees, radians, coterminal angles, and conversion between degree and radian units.

Trigonometry begins with careful attention to meaning. Before calculating, decide what the angle, coordinate, or function value represents.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the given angle, coordinate, or trigonometric function.
  2. Choose the correct conversion, coterminal-angle process, or unit circle definition.
  3. Carry out the work one step at a time.
  4. Write a sentence explaining what the result means.
