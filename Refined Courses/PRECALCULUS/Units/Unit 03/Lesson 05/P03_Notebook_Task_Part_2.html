Common Mistake

Incorrect: tan(90°) = 1 because tangent is often 1 at special angles.

Correct: tan(90°) is undefined because cos(90°) = 0 and tangent is sine divided by cosine.

Tangent is sin(θ)/cos(θ). At 90°, sine is 1 and cosine is 0. Division by 0 is undefined, so tangent is undefined.
