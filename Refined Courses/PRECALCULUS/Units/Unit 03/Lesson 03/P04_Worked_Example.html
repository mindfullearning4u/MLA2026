Common Mistake

Incorrect: sin(π/6) = √3/2 because π/6 is close to the top of the unit circle.

Correct: sin(π/6) = 1/2 and cos(π/6) = √3/2.

The angle π/6 is 30°. In a 30-60-90 triangle, the side opposite 30° is the shorter leg, so the sine value is 1/2.
