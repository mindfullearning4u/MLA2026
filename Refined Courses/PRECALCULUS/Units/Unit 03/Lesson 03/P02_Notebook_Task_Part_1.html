Notebook Title

Special Triangle Values on the Unit Circle

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Special angle: an angle such as 0, π/6, π/4, or π/3 with exact trigonometric values.
  • Exact value: a trigonometric value written using fractions and radicals instead of decimals.
  • Reference triangle: a right triangle used to find unit circle coordinates.
  • 30-60-90 triangle: a special triangle with side ratios 1, √3, and 2.
  • 45-45-90 triangle: a special triangle with side ratios 1, 1, and √2.
  • Unit circle coordinate: a point written as (cosine, sine).
  • Reciprocal function: cosecant, secant, or cotangent, found from sine, cosine, or tangent.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on special triangle values, exact unit circle values, and the six trigonometric functions for common angles.

The unit circle is not a list to memorize only. It is a structure built from triangles, coordinates, reference angles, and quadrant signs.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the angle or relationship being used.
  2. Find the reference angle or special triangle.
  3. Use the correct coordinate, ratio, or reciprocal relationship.
  4. Check the sign using the quadrant or unit circle location.
