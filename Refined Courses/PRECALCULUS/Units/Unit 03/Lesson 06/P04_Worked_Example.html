Common Mistake

Incorrect: In y = 3sin(x), the period is 3 because the number 3 is in front.

Correct: In y = 3sin(x), the amplitude is 3 and the period remains 2π.

The number in front of sine or cosine changes amplitude. The period changes when x is multiplied inside the function, such as sin(2x).
