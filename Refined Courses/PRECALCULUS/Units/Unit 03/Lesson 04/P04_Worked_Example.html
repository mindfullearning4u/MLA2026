P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1

Problem: Express sin(π-x), cos(π-x), and tan(π-x) in terms of values for x.

  1. Identify the angle or expression.
  2. Choose the correct special triangle or symmetry relationship.
  3. Find sine, cosine, tangent, or the requested reciprocal value.
  4. State the exact answer and explain the reason.

Worked Example 2

Problem: Express sin(π+x), cos(π+x), and tan(π+x) in terms of values for x.

  1. Identify the reference angle.
  2. Use the unit circle or special triangle relationship.
  3. Check whether the value is positive or negative.
  4. Write the exact value in simplified form.

Worked Example 3

Problem: Express sin(2π-x), cos(2π-x), and tan(2π-x) in terms of values for x.

  1. Start with sine, cosine, or tangent.
  2. Use reciprocal relationships when needed.
  3. Check the sign and simplify.
  4. Write the conclusion in a complete sentence.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: sin(π-x), sin(π+x), and sin(2π-x) all have the same sign because they share reference angle x.

Correct: They share reference angle x, but their signs depend on the quadrant.

Reference angle gives the size of the triangle, but the quadrant gives the sign. Sine is positive in Quadrants I and II and negative in Quadrants III and IV.
