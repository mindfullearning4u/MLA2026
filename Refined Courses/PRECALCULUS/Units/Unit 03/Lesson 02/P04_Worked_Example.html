Common Mistake

Incorrect: On the unit circle, sine is the x-coordinate and cosine is the y-coordinate.

Correct: On the unit circle, cosine is the x-coordinate and sine is the y-coordinate.

A unit circle point is written as (cosine, sine). The first coordinate gives cosine, and the second coordinate gives sine. Mixing these coordinates reverses the function values.
