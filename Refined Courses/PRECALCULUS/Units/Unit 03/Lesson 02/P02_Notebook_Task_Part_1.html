Notebook Title

Unit Circle Definitions and the Six Basic Trigonometric Functions

Begin a new notebook page with this title. Leave space under the vocabulary because you will use these words while solving and explaining problems.

Vocabulary

  • Unit circle: a circle centered at the origin with radius 1.
  • Sine: the y-coordinate of a point on the unit circle.
  • Cosine: the x-coordinate of a point on the unit circle.
  • Tangent: the ratio sine/cosine when cosine is not 0.
  • Cosecant: the reciprocal of sine when sine is not 0.
  • Secant: the reciprocal of cosine when cosine is not 0.
  • Cotangent: the reciprocal of tangent, or cosine/sine when sine is not 0.

Lesson Foundation

This lesson focuses on unit circle coordinates, corresponding angle measures, and definitions of sine, cosine, tangent, cosecant, secant, and cotangent.

Trigonometry begins with careful attention to meaning. Before calculating, decide what the angle, coordinate, or function value represents.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Identify the given angle, coordinate, or trigonometric function.
  2. Choose the correct conversion, coterminal-angle process, or unit circle definition.
  3. Carry out the work one step at a time.
  4. Write a sentence explaining what the result means.
