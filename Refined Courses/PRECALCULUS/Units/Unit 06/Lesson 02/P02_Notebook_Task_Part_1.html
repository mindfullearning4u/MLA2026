Notebook Title

Geometric Sequences

Vocabulary

  • Geometric Sequence: A sequence where each term is multiplied by the same factor to get the next term.
  • Common Ratio: The repeated multiplication factor. It is written as r.
  • Growth: A pattern that increases by multiplying by a factor greater than 1.
  • Decay: A pattern that decreases by multiplying by a positive factor between 0 and 1.
  • Explicit Rule: A formula that finds a term directly when you know the term number.

Core Lesson Notes

A geometric sequence is built by repeated multiplication. The multiplier is called the common ratio. For 2, 6, 18, 54, the common ratio is 3 because each term is multiplied by 3. For 80, 40, 20, 10, the common ratio is 1/2 because each term is multiplied by 1/2.

The explicit rule is an = a1rn - 1. In this rule, an is the term you want, a1 is the first term, n is the term number, and r is the common ratio.

The exponent n - 1 appears for the same reason as in arithmetic sequences: the first term has not changed yet. To get to the second term, multiply by the ratio once. To get to the third term, multiply by the ratio twice.
