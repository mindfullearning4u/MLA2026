Notebook Title

Arithmetic Series

Vocabulary

  • Series: The sum of terms in a sequence.
  • Arithmetic Series: The sum of terms from an arithmetic sequence.
  • Sn: The sum of the first n terms.
  • Finite Series: A sum with a specific number of terms.

Core Lesson Notes

A sequence lists terms. A series adds terms. For example, 4, 7, 10, 13 is an arithmetic sequence. The expression 4 + 7 + 10 + 13 is an arithmetic series because it adds the terms.

The arithmetic series formula is Sn = n(a1 + an) / 2. This formula works because the first and last terms pair with the second and next-to-last terms to make equal sums. The formula multiplies the number of terms by the average of the first and last terms.
