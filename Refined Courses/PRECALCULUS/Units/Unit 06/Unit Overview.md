# PRECALCULUS - Unit 06 Overview: Sequences, Series, Vectors, and Mathematical Modeling

---

## Unit Overview

This unit synthesizes discrete and vector-based modeling. Students write and solve problems involving arithmetic and geometric sequences, apply finite and infinite series formulas, represent relationships using explicit and recursive notation, represent and manipulate vectors, perform vector operations algebraically and geometrically, and solve vector problems in context.

Emphasis is placed on pattern structure, notation, recursive and explicit definitions, series reasoning, vector representation, geometric interpretation, scalar multiplication, and modeling decisions.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Standards with an emphasis on mathematical reasoning, structure, justification, and application. Students are expected to demonstrate both procedural fluency and conceptual understanding through algebraic modeling, geometric interpretation, and multi-step problem solving.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- sequence and series reasoning;
- vector representation and operations;
- algebraic and geometric modeling in context.

Assessment items require students to write and solve problems involving sequences, apply series formulas, represent sequences explicitly and recursively, represent and manipulate vectors, perform vector operations, and solve two-dimensional vector problems in mathematical and real-world contexts.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit synthesizes sequence reasoning, vector modeling, and algebraic representation. Students use formulas, notation, and geometric interpretation to model relationships, analyze patterns, and solve multi-step problems across mathematical and real-world contexts.
