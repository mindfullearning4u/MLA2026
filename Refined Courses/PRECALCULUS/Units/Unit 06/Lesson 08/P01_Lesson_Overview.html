P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Sequences, Series, and Calculus Readiness

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.SEQ.1: Write and solve problems involving arithmetic sequences.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.2: Write and solve problems involving geometric sequences.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.3: Use finite arithmetic series formulas in mathematical and real-world contexts.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.4: Use finite and infinite geometric series formulas in context.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.5: Represent relationships using explicit and recursive sequence notation.
  • MLA.PC.FA.6: Interpret average rate of change and difference quotients as calculus readiness support.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 6 ideas connect through patterns, sums, notation, and rates of change.
  • How to decide whether a problem involves a sequence, a series, or function change.
  • How to explain your method clearly for a unit-level assessment.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes that explain the lesson vocabulary and formulas.
  • Study three worked examples with every step shown.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint submission.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence showing unit synthesis across sequences, series, and rates of change.
  • Complete Unit 6 Guided Practice and the Unit Assessment.
  • Submit a checkpoint response that explains multiple Unit 6 ideas together.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will combine arithmetic sequences, geometric sequences, recursive and explicit models, series, sigma notation, and rates of change to show readiness for course completion.
