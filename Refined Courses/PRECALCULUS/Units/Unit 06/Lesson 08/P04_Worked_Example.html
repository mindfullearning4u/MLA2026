Worked Example 1: Sequence or Series

Problem: A pattern begins 4, 7, 10, 13. Find the 20th term and the sum of the first 20 terms.

  1. The 20th term is a sequence question.
  2. Use a20 = 4 + (20 - 1)3 = 61.
  3. The total is a series question.
  4. Use S20 = 20(4 + 61) / 2 = 650.

Worked Example 3: Rate of Change

Problem: For f(x) = x2, find the average rate of change from x = 1 to x = 5.

  1. Find f(1) = 1 and f(5) = 25.
  2. Use (25 - 1) / (5 - 1).
  3. Simplify: 24 / 4 = 6.
  4. The average rate of change is 6.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Using one formula for every Unit 6 problem.

Correct: First identify what the question asks: term, sum, notation, or rate of change. Then select the matching method.

Unit synthesis requires choosing a method, not memorizing one rule for every situation.
