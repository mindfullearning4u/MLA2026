Notebook Title

Putting It All Together: Sequences, Series, and Calculus Readiness

Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: Combining several ideas in one solution.
  • Sequence: An ordered list of terms.
  • Series: A sum of terms.
  • Rate of Change: A comparison of output change to input change.

Core Lesson Notes

Unit 6 brings together patterns and change. Arithmetic and geometric sequences describe ordered terms. Arithmetic and geometric series add terms. Recursive and explicit rules describe patterns in different ways. Sigma notation writes sums compactly. Rates of change and difference quotients prepare you for future Calculus by measuring function change.
