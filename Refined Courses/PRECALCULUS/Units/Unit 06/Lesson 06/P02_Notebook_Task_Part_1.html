Notebook Title

Sigma Notation and Series Applications

Vocabulary

  • Sigma Notation: A compact way to show repeated addition.
  • Index: The variable that changes in the sum, often k or n.
  • Lower Bound: The first index value.
  • Upper Bound: The last index value.
  • Term Rule: The expression being evaluated and added.

Reading Sigma Notation

Read the sigma symbol as ï¿½add.ï¿½ Start with the lower index value, substitute into the term rule, continue one integer at a time, and stop at the upper index value.
