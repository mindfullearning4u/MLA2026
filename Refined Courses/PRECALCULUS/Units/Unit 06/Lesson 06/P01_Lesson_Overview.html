P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Sigma Notation and Series Applications

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.SEQ.3: Support extension of arithmetic series.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.4: Support extension of geometric series.
  • MLA.PC.SEQ.5: Support extension of sequence notation.

What You Will Learn

  • How sigma notation represents repeated addition.
  • How to expand a summation by substituting index values.
  • How to connect sigma notation to arithmetic and geometric series.

What You Will Do

  • Build notes on sigma notation and index values.
  • Study three worked examples.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, and quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Expand sigma notation correctly.
  • Identify whether a sum is arithmetic or geometric.
  • Explain the role of the index and bounds.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will use compact notation to represent series clearly and connect summation symbols to the sequence and series standards already learned.
