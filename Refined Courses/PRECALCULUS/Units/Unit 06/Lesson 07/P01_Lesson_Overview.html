P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Rates of Change, Difference Quotients, and Limit Foundations

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.FA.6: Support-only calculus readiness connected to average rate of change, difference quotients, and function behavior near a point.

What You Will Learn

  • How to calculate average rate of change and describe what a function value approaches without using full Calculus notation.
  • How difference quotients, tables, and graphs show function behavior near a point.
  • How end behavior and asymptotes connect to future limit thinking.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes that explain average rate of change, difference quotients, informal limit language, and function behavior.
  • Study three worked examples with every step shown.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, a checkpoint submission, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence explaining behavior from tables and graphs.
  • Complete independent work that uses precise approach language.
  • Submit a checkpoint response and complete the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will use rates of change, difference quotients, and function behavior to explain how outputs change and what values they appear to approach. This supports future Calculus work without turning this course into a Calculus course.
