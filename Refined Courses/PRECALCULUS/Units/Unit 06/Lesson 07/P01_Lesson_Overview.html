Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Rates of Change, Difference Quotients, and Limit Foundations

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.FA.6: Interpret average rate of change and difference quotients as calculus readiness support.

What You Will Learn

  • How average rate of change compares output change to input change.
  • How to write and simplify a difference quotient.
  • How informal limit language describes what function values approach.
