P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Recursive and Explicit Models

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.PC.SEQ.5
  • MA.912.AR.10.5: Represent relationships using explicit and recursive sequence notation.

What You Will Learn

  • How explicit notation and recursive notation describe the same pattern in different ways.
  • How to read recursive rules such as an = an - 1 + 4.
  • How to choose the notation that best fits a sequence problem.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes that explain the lesson vocabulary and formulas.
  • Study three worked examples with every step shown.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, a checkpoint submission, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence showing definitions, formulas, worked steps, and explanations.
  • Complete independent work that shows whether you can use the lesson method.
  • Submit a checkpoint response and complete the lesson quiz.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

You will show that you can describe a sequence in two ways: an explicit rule that finds a term directly and a recursive rule that uses the previous term.
