Lesson 4: Multiple Arguments, Claims, Validity, and Reasoning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.08 - Evaluate Multiple Arguments and Reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas in Classical Texts; MLA.ENG3.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose in Classical Texts; MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices.

What You Will Learn

  • How to compare multiple arguments on the same topic.
  • How to identify claims and reasoning.
  • How to evaluate whether reasoning is valid and effective.
  • How author purpose affects the way an argument is developed.

Why This Matters

Different authors can address the same issue and reach different conclusions. Strong readers compare not only what authors claim, but how they reason.

This lesson prepares you for deeper argument analysis and rhetorical evaluation later in Unit 3.

What You Will Do

  • Read paired argument passages about the same topic.
  • Identify each author's claim and reasoning.
  • Evaluate whether the reasoning is valid and effective.
  • Write a checkpoint comparison of two arguments.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will compare two arguments on the same topic and evaluate the effectiveness and validity of each author's reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to compare arguments and judge how well each author supports claims with reasoning.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can identify each claim but are unsure how to judge the reasoning behind it.
