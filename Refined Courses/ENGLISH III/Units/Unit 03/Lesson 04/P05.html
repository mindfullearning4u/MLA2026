Independent Work: Competing Arguments About the River Road

Directions

Read all three arguments and answer all 18 questions. For Question 2, infer and verify the passage word using a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source.

Argument A — Commerce

The river road should be widened to four lanes. Freight delays increased 18 percent after the distribution center opened, and three businesses report late deliveries. Wider lanes will improve reliability and attract employers.

Argument B — Safety and Environment

Widening may increase speed beside two schools and remove forty mature trees that stabilize the riverbank. Crash data show most delays result from the unsynchronized rail crossing, not lane capacity. Repair the crossing and add turning lanes at the distribution center before expanding the entire road.

Argument C — Conditional Pilot

Both sides assume traffic will remain constant. First synchronize the crossing, restrict freight turns during school arrival, and collect six months of travel-time, crash, and erosion data. If congestion remains chronic, widen the industrial segment only. Irreversible construction should follow evidence, not substitute for it.

Questions

  1. State each conclusion neutrally.
  2. Infer irreversible; verify it, record the source, and explain its importance.
  3. List A’s premises.
  4. Identify an assumption in A.
  5. Evaluate A’s evidence for sufficiency.
  6. List B’s premises.
  7. How does B challenge A’s causal reasoning?
  8. Identify a possible weakness in B.
  9. Map C’s line of reasoning.
  10. Explain C’s qualification.
  11. What evidence would strengthen each argument?
  12. Identify a relevant but insufficient detail.
  13. Test one argument for validity versus soundness.
  14. Compare how each treats uncertainty.
  15. Evaluate the arguments using efficiency and safety.
  16. Evaluate them using equity or environmental durability.
  17. Choose the strongest current argument and defend the judgment.
  18. Explain what new evidence could change your evaluation.
