Notebook Title

Comparing Multiple Arguments: Claims, Validity, and Reasoning

Vocabulary

  • Claim: the author's position or argument.
  • Reasoning: the explanation of why the claim makes sense.
  • Evidence: facts, examples, or details used to support a claim.
  • Validity: whether reasoning is logical and well-supported.
  • Effectiveness: how well the argument achieves its purpose for the audience.

Direct Teaching

When comparing arguments, do not stop at agreement or disagreement. Ask how each author builds the argument.

  1. Identify the shared topic.
  2. Find each claim.
  3. Explain each author's reasoning.
  4. Evaluate validity. Is the reasoning logical and supported?
  5. Evaluate effectiveness. Does the argument work well for its audience and purpose?
