📘 ENG3 | Unit 03 | Lesson 04

P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can compare arguments and evaluate reasoning.

Checkpoint Arguments

Argument A: The city should expand the public market because it gives local farmers more space to sell food and keeps money in the community.

Argument B: The city should improve the road to the existing market first because farmers and customers already struggle to reach it safely after heavy rain.

TOR Support

Ask TOR for help if you can identify the claims but need support evaluating validity or effectiveness.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit notebook evidence showing your vocabulary notes, comparison chart, P03 task, and P06 independent work.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph comparing the two arguments.

Your paragraph must include:

  • the shared topic,
  • each author's claim,
  • each author's reasoning, and
  • an evaluation of which argument is more effective for immediate access and safety.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify both claims accurately.
  • You explain reasoning for both arguments.
  • You evaluate validity and effectiveness using evidence.
  • You avoid relying only on personal preference.
