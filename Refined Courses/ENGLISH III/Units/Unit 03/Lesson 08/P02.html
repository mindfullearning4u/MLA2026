Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work.
Term Definition
synthesis claim A conclusion that explains a meaningful relationship among multiple sources.
point of convergence An idea, value, or conclusion on which sources meaningfully agree.
point of divergence A difference in claim, reasoning, evidence, assumptions, or proposed action.
source relationship The way sources support, complicate, qualify, extend, or contradict one another.
corroboration Independent support for a claim from another source or piece of evidence.
tension A meaningful conflict between ideas, values, evidence, or priorities.
nuance A subtle distinction that makes an interpretation more precise.
comparative reasoning Analysis that evaluates similarities and differences using consistent criteria.
contextualization Explanation of how historical or rhetorical circumstances shape a text.
qualified conclusion A conclusion limited by appropriate conditions, exceptions, or uncertainty.
