Notebook Title: Unit 3 Classical Text, Argument, and Rhetoric Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Central idea: the main point developed by a text.
  • Validity: the strength and soundness of reasoning.
  • Rhetoric: language choices used to influence an audience.

Synthesis Teaching

In Unit 3, structure is not separate from argument. The order of ideas helps the audience follow the claim. Purpose shapes what evidence is included. Rhetoric affects how strongly the audience responds. Synthesis means evaluating how all of these choices work together.

Notebook Task

Make a four-part organizer: structure, central idea, argument, and rhetoric. For each part, write what the reader should look for and why it matters.
