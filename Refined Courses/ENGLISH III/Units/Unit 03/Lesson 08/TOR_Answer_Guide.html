  4. Tension: maximum access versus risk of personal exposure.
  5. A links trust to usable evidence; B to visibility plus restraint.
  6. Antithesis distinguishes nominal from meaningful openness.
  7. Parallel contrast protects inspection of power without inspection of private lives.
  8. B qualifies publication methods, not transparency itself.
  9. Needed: privacy risk, data utility, accessibility, security, review standards.
  10. The relationship is qualification and extension.
  11. Synthesis: publish usable public data with privacy-by-design review and explained limits.
  12. Recommendation should include portal access, accessible formats, aggregation, review, and withholding explanations.
