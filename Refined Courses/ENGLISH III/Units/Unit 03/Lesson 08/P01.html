Lesson Overview: Putting It All Together — Unit 3 Synthesis

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.09 — Synthesize ideas, reasoning, evidence, and rhetorical choices across multiple texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Analyze ideas and their development; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze author purpose and rhetoric; MLA.ENG3.R.08 — Evaluate arguments and reasoning; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Primary skill: Synthesize analysis of classical ideas, argument structure, evidence, counterclaims, and rhetorical choices across multiple texts.

What You Will Learn

  • Compare how two texts address a shared civic question.
  • Distinguish similarities in topic from similarities in reasoning.
  • Integrate evidence from multiple sources.
  • Evaluate how context, audience, and rhetorical choices shape each argument.
  • Develop a qualified synthesis that preserves important differences.

Why This Matters

Synthesis is more than summary. It requires you to hold several lines of reasoning in view, test relationships among them, and form a conclusion that no single source supplies alone. This prepares you for the Unit Assessment and for the increasing rigor of Units 4–6.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Study the synthesis method and contrasting examples.
  3. Follow the paired-text model on Page 4.
  4. Complete Page 5 with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
  5. Submit Page 6 separately as the Checkpoint.
  6. Complete the Unit 3 Assessment: 10 random questions from a 40-question bank, one attempt, 80% mastery.
