P04 Worked Review

Review how a complete Unit 3 answer works.

Worked Review Passage

Friends, justice delayed is not justice resting; it is justice denied a voice. Let the court open its doors before silence becomes its verdict.

Review Example 1

The central idea is that delayed justice harms people by silencing their needs.

Review Example 2

The author uses personification by giving justice a voice. This makes delayed justice seem like a living need being ignored.

Common Mistake Review

Incorrect: The passage is about justice and doors.

Why it is weak: This identifies topics but does not explain central idea, purpose, or rhetoric.

Correct Review

Correct: The author personifies justice as having a voice, which strengthens the argument that delayed court action silences people who need justice.
