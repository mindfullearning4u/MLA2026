Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all lesson terms and definitions. Research passage vocabulary only when directed.

TermDefinition
framingPresentation choices that shape how information is interpreted.
selectionThe deliberate inclusion of particular details.
omissionThe exclusion of information that may affect interpretation.
emphasisSpecial prominence given to a detail or idea.
contextualizationPlacement of information within circumstances that clarify significance.
recontextualizationPlacement of existing information into a new context that changes its effect.
loaded languageWording carrying strong positive or negative associations.
statistical frameA choice of comparison, scale, baseline, or time period shaping numerical meaning.
representationThe way people, events, or evidence are portrayed.
fairnessBalanced treatment that accurately represents relevant evidence and alternatives.
