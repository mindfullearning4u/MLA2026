Checkpoint: Two Headlines, One Graduation Report

Directions

Read the shared data and accounts; answer 12 questions separately.

Shared Data

Graduation increased from 78% to 82%; the state average is 86%. The largest improvement occurred among students in a new mentoring program, though participation was voluntary.

Headline A

“Graduation Climbs as Mentoring Program Delivers Results.”

Headline B

“District Still Trails State Despite Four-Point Gain.”

Questions

  1. Infer trails in context.
  2. List shared facts.
  3. Identify A’s purpose and frame.
  4. Identify B’s purpose and frame.
  5. Analyze each headline’s verbs.
  6. What does A imply about causation?
  7. Why does voluntary participation matter?
  8. How does B’s baseline shape interpretation?
  9. Evaluate accuracy versus fairness.
  10. Write an objective synthesis.
  11. Create a more balanced headline.
  12. Explain how shared facts achieve different ends.
