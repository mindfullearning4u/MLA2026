Guided Practice Passage

Argument A: The town should keep the old market open because the market drew hundreds of visitors last summer. Those visitors support local sellers and keep the town's traditions alive.

Argument B: The town should move the market to a larger field because the market drew hundreds of visitors last summer. The crowd proves that the current square is too small for safe movement.

Before You Answer

  1. Find the information both arguments share.
  2. Identify what each author wants the town to do.
  3. Ask how the same visitor detail supports each different end.
