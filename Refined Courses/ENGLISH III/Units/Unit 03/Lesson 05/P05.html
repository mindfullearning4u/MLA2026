Independent Work: Three Accounts of the Harbor Project

Directions

Read all accounts and answer 18 questions. For Question 2, infer and verify the passage word with a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source.

Shared Facts

The city spent $4.2 million replacing a concrete seawall with wetlands and a pedestrian path. Construction took fourteen months, removed 22 parking spaces, and was followed by lower flood damage during two major storms.

Account A — City Release

“A resilient shoreline protected homes while creating public recreation. The project finished within the revised budget and demonstrated forward-looking climate leadership.”

Account B — Business Association

“Fourteen months of construction and lost parking burdened small businesses. Officials celebrate storm performance while ignoring revenue lost during disruption.”

Account C — Environmental Review

“Early storm results are promising, but two events cannot establish long-term resilience. Wetland survival, maintenance cost, habitat quality, and flood performance should be monitored for five years.”

Questions

  1. List all shared facts.
  2. Infer resilient; verify and record a source, then explain its use.
  3. State each account’s purpose and audience.
  4. Identify A’s selections and omissions.
  5. Analyze “climate leadership.”
  6. Identify B’s selections and omissions.
  7. Analyze “burdened.”
  8. What time frame does each account emphasize?
  9. How does C frame uncertainty?
  10. Explain “promising” as qualification.
  11. Which details are accurate but potentially misleading without context?
  12. What additional baseline is needed for the budget claim?
  13. What evidence would contextualize business losses?
  14. Compare effectiveness for three audiences.
  15. Evaluate fairness of each account.
  16. Write a synthesis preserving benefits, costs, and uncertainty.
  17. Design a fair heading for a combined report.
  18. Explain how the same facts achieve different ends.
