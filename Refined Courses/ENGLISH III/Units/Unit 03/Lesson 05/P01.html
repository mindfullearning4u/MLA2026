Lesson Overview: The Same Information Used to Achieve Different Ends

Purpose

Analyze how authors select, frame, sequence, and emphasize shared information to produce different conclusions or audience responses.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze how authors use selection, framing, sequence, emphasis, and omission to achieve different purposes.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze author purpose and rhetorical choices; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Learning Goals

  • Separate shared facts from interpretive framing.
  • Identify selection, omission, emphasis, labels, and visual/textual context.
  • Compare purposes without assuming one text must be false.
  • Evaluate fairness and effectiveness.

Workflow

Vocabulary, diverse models, worked analysis, longer Independent Work with targeted passage vocabulary, separate Checkpoint, and five-question quiz at 80% mastery.
