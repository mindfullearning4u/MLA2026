Lesson 7: Integrated Classical Text, Argument, and Rhetoric Analysis

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.12 - Evaluate Rhetorical Choices.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.05 - Evaluate Text Structures and Features; MLA.ENG3.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas in Classical Texts; MLA.ENG3.R.08 - Evaluate Multiple Arguments and Reasoning; MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices.

What You Will Learn

  • How structure, central idea, argument, and rhetoric work together.
  • How to move from identifying a rhetorical choice to evaluating its effect.
  • How to explain why a rhetorical choice strengthens or weakens an argument.
  • How to prepare for Unit 3 synthesis without learning a new standard.

Why This Matters

Advanced readers do not analyze rhetorical choices in isolation. They ask how structure, central idea, argument, and word choice combine to affect an audience.

This lesson prepares you for the Unit 3 Putting It All Together lesson.

What You Will Do

  • Read Classical-style argument passages.
  • Identify central idea, structure, claim, and rhetorical choices.
  • Evaluate how rhetoric supports purpose and reasoning.
  • Write a short integrated analysis paragraph.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain how a rhetorical choice strengthens a Classical-style argument by supporting the central idea, purpose, and reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to judge how rhetoric works inside an argument, not just name the device.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can identify a rhetorical choice but cannot connect it to purpose, central idea, or reasoning.
