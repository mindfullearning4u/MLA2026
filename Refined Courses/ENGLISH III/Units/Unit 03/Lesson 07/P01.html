Lesson Overview: Integrated Classical Text, Argument, and Rhetoric Analysis

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.09 — Integrate analysis of classical ideas, argument structure, evidence, and rhetoric across complex public texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Analyze ideas and their development; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze author purpose and rhetoric; MLA.ENG3.R.08 — Evaluate arguments and reasoning; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Primary skill: Integrate claims, reasoning, evidence, rhetorical choices, and historical context into a coherent analysis of a complex public text.

What You Will Learn

  • Reconstruct a text’s line of reasoning.
  • Explain how evidence and warrants support claims.
  • Analyze how rhetorical choices strengthen, qualify, or complicate an argument.
  • Compare multiple defensible interpretations.
  • Synthesize argument and rhetoric without listing disconnected devices.

Rigor Progression

This lesson combines the skills developed across Unit 3. You will move from comprehension to argument mapping, rhetorical analysis, evaluation, and synthesis. Strong responses will address both what a text argues and how its language makes that reasoning meaningful.
