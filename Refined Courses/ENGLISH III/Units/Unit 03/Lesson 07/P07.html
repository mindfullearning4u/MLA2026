📘 ENG3 | Unit 03 | Lesson 07

P07 Checkpoint

Show mastery of integrated argument and rhetoric analysis.

Checkpoint Passage

Citizens, when truth is kept behind a curtain, rumor becomes the public teacher. Open the records, open the doors, and open the path by which trust returns.

Checkpoint Task

Write one integrated analysis paragraph that includes:

  • the central idea,
  • the author's purpose,
  • one rhetorical choice,
  • the effect of that choice, and
  • an evaluation of how well the choice supports the argument.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your P02-P03 organizer and your independent work from P06 as evidence of preparation.

Submission Workflow

Submit your final paragraph in the checkpoint assignment area.

Ask TOR for help before submitting if your paragraph names a device but does not explain how it supports the argument.

Mastery Criteria

  • Connects central idea, purpose, and rhetoric.
  • Explains audience effect clearly.
  • Evaluates effectiveness with textual evidence.
  • Uses precise academic language.
