TOR ONLY — Answer Guide

Independent Work — Key Responses

  1. Impoverished: made poorer or limited; context calls the either/or choice falsely narrow.
  2. Speaker addresses residents/council after flood anniversary to support conditional memorial.
  3. Fund repairs first or alongside a transparent, limited memorial.
  4. Memory includes loss and cooperation; policy must protect living; memorial can preserve lessons; transparency resolves tension.
  5. Responsible remembrance must not replace safety.
  6. Flood experience supports preserving civic lessons and names.
  7. Balanced memories create complete, communal tone.
  8. Concession validates drainage concerns.
  9. Antithesis exposes hypocrisy of ceremony without safety.
  10. “Publish” demands transparency and builds cadence.
  11. Conditional syntax makes approval dependent on repairs.
  12. Lantern links memory to future guidance.
  13. Parallel “Build” sentences unite preparation and remembrance.
  14. Qualification: delay monument if repairs are delayed.
  15. Evidence is relevant but lacks costs/timelines.
  16. Needed facts include repair schedule, estimates, donations, survivor input.
  17. Rhetoric makes a compromise memorable while reasoning limits it.
  18. Integrated response should include claim, reasoning, two choices, and limitation.

Checkpoint — Key Responses

  1. Fallible: capable of error; supported by blurred dates and changing stories.
  2. Include contextualized oral histories in the archive.
  3. Archives reflect prior selection and exclusion.
  4. Memory can be biased or inaccurate.
  5. Missing voices cannot be recovered through documents alone.
  6. Contrast shows written sources also contain perspective.
  7. “When possible” acknowledges corroboration limits.
  8. Opening the door symbolizes broader participation.
  9. Selection problem → concession → safeguards → qualified conclusion.
  10. Safeguards substantially answer but do not eliminate accuracy concerns.
  11. Possible additions: consent, metadata, corroboration rules, preservation standards.
  12. Evaluation should balance inclusive evidence with verification limits.
