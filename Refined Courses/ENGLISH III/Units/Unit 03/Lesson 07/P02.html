Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work.
Term Definition
synthesis The combination of related ideas or evidence into a new, coherent understanding.
line of reasoning The ordered sequence connecting a claim, supporting reasons, evidence, and conclusions.
premise A statement or assumption on which an argument depends.
warrant The reasoning that explains why evidence supports a claim.
counterclaim A position that challenges or complicates the main claim.
rhetorical situation The interaction among speaker, audience, purpose, context, and constraints.
allusion A brief reference to a well-known person, event, text, or idea.
analogy A comparison used to clarify reasoning or reveal a relationship.
concession An acknowledgment that part of an opposing position has merit.
qualification Language that limits the scope or certainty of a claim.
