Guided Practice Passage

Citizens, a law hidden in difficult words is a locked gate. A law written plainly is a road. Let every person know the rule before being judged by it.

Before You Answer

  1. Find the central idea.
  2. Identify the rhetorical contrast.
  3. Explain how the contrast supports the argument.
  4. Evaluate the effect on the audience.
