Independent Work Passage

Leaders, a promise to the people is not a garland for ceremony; it is a debt. If you wear the promise but do not pay it, the crowd will remember the flowers and the emptiness beneath them.

Part A: Identify

Write the central idea, the audience, and one rhetorical choice.

Part B: Explain

Explain how the rhetorical choice affects the audience's view of promises.

Part C: Evaluate

Write three to five sentences explaining whether the rhetoric effectively supports the author's argument.
