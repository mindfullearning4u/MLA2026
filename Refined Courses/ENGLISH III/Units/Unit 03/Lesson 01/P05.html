Repairing the City Steps

The west steps outside the library cracked after years of rain. First, the city blocked the unsafe side with a sign. Next, engineers inspected the stone and found that water had weakened the base. Finally, the city added drainage channels before replacing the steps. The new design prevents rain from collecting under the stone.
