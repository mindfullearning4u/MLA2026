Independent Work Passage

Two Plans for the Square

Plan A would add benches and trees to the town square. Plan B would keep the center open for markets and public meetings. Plan A improves shade and rest. Plan B protects flexible space. Both plans support public use, but they define public need differently.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Identify

Identify the main structure of the passage and one feature that guides the reader.

Part B: Explain

Explain how the structure helps readers compare the two plans.

Part C: Evaluate

Write one paragraph evaluating whether the comparison structure is effective for the author's purpose.
