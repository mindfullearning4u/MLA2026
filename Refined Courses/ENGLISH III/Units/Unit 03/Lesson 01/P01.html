Lesson Overview: Evaluating Text Structures and Features

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.08 — Evaluate how structures and text features shape meaning, emphasis, and reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze author purpose and rhetorical choices; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify structural patterns in speeches, essays, and literary nonfiction.
  • Analyze how headings, repetition, chronology, comparison, and cause-effect guide readers.
  • Evaluate whether a structure fits purpose, audience, and evidence.
  • Research only the passage-specific context needed to interpret a feature accurately.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record lesson vocabulary.
  2. Study multiple examples and the fully modeled analysis.
  3. Complete the longer 18-question Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain not only what structure an author uses, but why it is effective or limited for a specific audience and purpose.
