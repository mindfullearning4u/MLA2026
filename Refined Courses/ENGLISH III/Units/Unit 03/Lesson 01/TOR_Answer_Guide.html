Suggested Answers and Scoring Notes

Independent Work

Central claim: the city should test targeted evening library hours rather than preserve inherited schedules or adopt unlimited expansion. Untenable means unable to be maintained or defended; acceptable sources include reputable dictionaries or encyclopedias. Structure: concrete problem, historical cause, concession/counterargument, definition of access, measured solution. Strong evaluations explain how this order builds urgency, credibility, and feasibility for officials.

Checkpoint

Privilege means advantage or favor in context and carries concern about unequal choice. Structure: problem, concession and limited solution, precedent/comparison, evaluation plan and synthesis. Strong answers link structural moves to skeptical commuter or agency audiences.

Research and AI Scoring

Credit requires a context-based inference, verification with a credible source, a recorded source, and an explanation in the student’s own words. AI alone is not verification. Apply visible rubrics; below 80%, require TOR-guided correction and resubmission.
