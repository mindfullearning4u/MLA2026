P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply your toolkit to a short informational passage.

Read the Passage

Keeping the Market Open

The town market began losing visitors after the bridge closed. Farmers could still sell produce, but customers from the west side had to travel an extra four miles. First, the council created a temporary shuttle. Next, vendors moved popular stalls closer to the north entrance. Finally, the council posted a weekly route map so visitors could plan ahead.

Step-by-Step Thinking

  1. Problem: The bridge closure makes the market harder to reach.
  2. Structure: The passage uses problem and solution with sequence words: "First," "Next," and "Finally."
  3. Features: The title previews the topic. The route map is a text-related feature inside the plan.
  4. Evaluation: The structure is effective because each solution responds to the access problem.

Notebook Task

  1. Identify the main structure of the passage.
  2. Quote one transition that helps the reader follow the structure.
  3. Identify one feature that helps reader understanding.
  4. Explain whether the structure is effective.
  5. Write one sentence that evaluates the passage's organization.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The passage is effective because it has words.

Why it is weak: It does not identify or evaluate structure.

Correct: The passage is effective because the problem-and-solution structure shows the access problem and then presents three steps that respond to it.

Why it works: It names the structure, explains how it works, and evaluates its effectiveness.
