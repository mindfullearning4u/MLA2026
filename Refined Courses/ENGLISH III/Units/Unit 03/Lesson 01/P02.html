Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all lesson terms and complete definitions. Passage-specific vocabulary will be studied separately through context and focused research.

TermDefinition
text structureThe organizational pattern through which ideas or events are arranged.
text featureA visible or verbal element that guides access to information, such as a heading, caption, preface, or repeated phrase.
chronological orderArrangement according to time sequence.
cause and effectOrganization linking reasons, actions, conditions, and consequences.
problem and solutionOrganization defining a problem and proposing or evaluating responses.
comparison and contrastOrganization revealing significance through similarities and differences.
classificationOrganization grouping information according to meaningful categories.
concessionAcknowledgment of a limitation or opposing point before advancing a claim.
refrainA repeated phrase or passage that creates emphasis and cohesion.
evaluation criterionA stated standard used to judge effectiveness.
