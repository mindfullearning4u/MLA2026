Checkpoint Passage

Why the Reading Room Moved

The old reading room sat beside the busy entrance, where visitors asked questions and carts rolled across the tile. Because students needed a quieter place, the library tested three locations. The back room reduced noise but had weak lighting. The balcony had sunlight but too few outlets. The east room offered quiet, light, and space for charging stations. Therefore, the library moved the reading room east.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that evaluates how the passage's structure and features support reader understanding.

Your paragraph must include:

  • the main structure,
  • one feature or transition,
  • evidence from the passage, and
  • a judgment about effectiveness.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify structure accurately.
  • You identify a useful feature or transition.
  • You explain how the structure supports understanding.
  • You make an evaluative judgment using evidence.
