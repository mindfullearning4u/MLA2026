Independent Work Essay

Essay on Courage

Courage is not the absence of fear. The sailor who feels no fear may simply be unaware of the storm. The courageous sailor sees the waves, understands the danger, and still ties the rope for another. Courage is fear governed by duty.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Topic and Central Idea

Identify the topic and write the central idea as a complete sentence.

Part B: Evidence

Quote one detail that supports the central idea.

Part C: Explanation

Explain how the detail develops the central idea.
