Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all lesson terms and definitions. Passage-specific vocabulary will be inferred from context and verified only when the analysis requires it.

TermDefinition
central ideaA major insight or proposition developed across a text.
subjectThe broad person, issue, event, or concept a text addresses.
claimAn arguable statement an author asks an audience to accept.
premiseA reason or proposition used to support a conclusion.
maximA concise statement expressing a general principle or rule of conduct.
exemplumA brief illustrative example used to support a moral or argumentative point.
antithesisParallel placement of contrasting ideas to sharpen a distinction.
amplificationExpansion of an idea through added detail, examples, or restatement.
qualificationA limit or condition that makes a claim more precise.
synthesisIntegration of several developed ideas into a larger understanding.
