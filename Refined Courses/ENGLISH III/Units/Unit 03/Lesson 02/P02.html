P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Learn how to separate topic, central idea, and supporting details.

Notebook Title

Central Ideas in Classical Speeches and Essays

Vocabulary

  • Central idea: the main idea the text develops.
  • Topic: the subject the text is about.
  • Supporting detail: evidence, example, or explanation that develops the central idea.
  • Classical Period text: a speech or essay connected to ancient civic, philosophical, or public reasoning traditions.
  • Paraphrase: restate an idea accurately in your own words.

Direct Teaching

A topic is usually one or two words. A central idea is a full thought about that topic.

Topic: leadership

Central idea: A leader earns trust by serving the common good before personal comfort.

To find a central idea, ask:

  1. What subject repeats across the passage?
  2. What claim or lesson does the author build about that subject?
  3. Which details support that claim?
  4. How does the structure move the idea forward?

Notebook Task

Copy this frame into your notebook.

  1. The topic of the passage is _____.
  2. The central idea is _____.
  3. One detail that supports the central idea is _____.
  4. This detail supports the central idea because _____.
