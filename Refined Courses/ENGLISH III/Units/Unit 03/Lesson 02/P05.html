Speech on Public Duty

A citizen does not serve the city only by speaking in the square. The quiet acts matter also: returning a lost tool, keeping a promise, and teaching a child to tell the truth. A city becomes honorable when ordinary duties are performed faithfully. Great laws cannot hold together a people who have forgotten small responsibilities.
