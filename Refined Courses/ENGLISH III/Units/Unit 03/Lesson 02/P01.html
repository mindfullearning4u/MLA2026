Lesson Overview: Central Ideas in Classical-Period Speeches and Essays

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.02 — Analyze how central ideas emerge, interact, and develop across speeches and essays.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze author purpose and rhetorical choices; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish subject, claim, and central idea.
  • Trace an idea through definition, example, contrast, concession, and conclusion.
  • Use essential historical or rhetorical context without replacing textual analysis.
  • Evaluate how several ideas combine into a larger argument.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record lesson vocabulary.
  2. Study multiple speech and essay examples.
  3. Complete the longer 18-question Independent Work with targeted passage vocabulary research.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will trace a central idea across an entire text and explain how structure, evidence, and rhetorical choices develop or complicate it.
