P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can analyze a central idea in a Classical Period-style speech or essay.

Checkpoint Speech

Speech on Counsel

No leader is made weaker by listening. The captain who hears the navigator avoids rocks; the judge who hears both sides avoids shame; the council that hears the poor avoids ruling only for the comfortable. Counsel does not steal authority. It teaches authority where to stand.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that identifies and analyzes the central idea of "Speech on Counsel."

Your paragraph must include:

  • the topic,
  • the central idea as a full sentence,
  • one quoted supporting detail, and
  • an explanation of how the detail develops the central idea.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify the central idea as a full thought.
  • You choose evidence that supports the central idea.
  • You explain how the evidence develops the idea.
  • You use clear academic language and conventions.
