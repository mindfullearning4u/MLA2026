P04 Worked Example

Study how to identify and support a central idea.

Worked Example Text

Essay on Wise Judgment

A ruler who answers every dispute quickly may appear strong, yet speed without listening is only noise in a finer robe. The wise judge asks what happened, who was harmed, and what repair is possible. Justice does not hurry because pride is impatient. Justice waits long enough to see clearly.

Example 1: Topic

The topic is wise judgment or justice.

Example 2: Central Idea

The central idea is that wise judgment requires careful listening rather than quick, prideful decisions.

Example 3: Supporting Evidence

The line "The wise judge asks what happened, who was harmed, and what repair is possible" supports the central idea because it shows judgment as careful inquiry.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The central idea is that rulers wear robes.

Why it is weak: The robe image is a detail, not the main idea.

Correct: The central idea is that justice requires patient listening before judgment.

Why it is correct: It captures the main idea developed across the entire essay.
