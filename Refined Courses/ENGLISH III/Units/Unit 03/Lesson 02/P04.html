Worked Example: “On the Uses of Disagreement”

Model Passage

Citizens often praise harmony as though a quiet council were proof of wisdom. Yet silence may arise from consent, fear, fatigue, or exclusion; its sound alone cannot reveal its cause.

Consider a ship whose officers agree not to wake the captain when clouds gather. Their agreement is complete, but their unity serves neither vessel nor crew. Responsible disagreement does not delight in conflict. It interrupts comfort when comfort conceals danger.

Some object that public dispute weakens confidence. The objection is reasonable when disagreement becomes performance rather than inquiry. Therefore criticism should name evidence, propose correction, and remain answerable to reply.

A city is not strongest when every voice repeats one sentence. It is strongest when difference can test a sentence before power turns it into law.

Trace the Development

  1. Opening: questions the assumption that harmony proves wisdom.
  2. Exemplum: ship analogy shows agreement can be dangerous.
  3. Qualification: disagreement must be evidence-based and reciprocal.
  4. Conclusion: redefines civic strength as tested rather than uniform thought.

Model Central Idea

Healthy civic unity depends on disciplined disagreement that tests claims with evidence, not on silence or automatic agreement.

Evidence and Reasoning

The ship exemplum demonstrates the danger of agreement without scrutiny, while the concession prevents the author from praising all conflict. The final antithesis—repetition versus testing—synthesizes both ideas.

Alternative Reading

A reader might argue the central idea concerns free speech. That reading is related but too broad unless it accounts for the author’s requirements that criticism offer evidence, correction, and reply.

Second Model — Compressed Maxim

“The law that cannot answer a question has already mistaken obedience for justice.” This maxim compresses a central idea, but a full essay would still need definitions, examples, and qualifications to develop it.
