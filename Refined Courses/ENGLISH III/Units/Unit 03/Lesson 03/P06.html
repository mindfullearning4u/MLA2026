Checkpoint: “On Repairing the Steps”

Directions

Read the different essay and answer all 12 questions. Submit separately.

Essay

The courthouse steps have been repaired four times in ten years, yet wheelchair users still enter through a service corridor beside the loading dock. Each repair preserves the appearance of the front entrance while preserving exclusion.

Officials argue that a ramp would alter the historic façade. History deserves care, but a public building is not a sculpture viewed from the street. Its purpose includes entry.

A reversible stone ramp can match existing materials without erasing the original stairs. More importantly, placing accessible entry at the front declares that accommodation is not a private exception.

Repair the steps if they are unsafe. But do not confuse maintaining stone with maintaining justice.
