Independent Work Passage

Essay on Wealth and Honor

Do not call a person honorable because his house is large. Stone can rise around a selfish heart. Honor is measured when a person uses abundance to repair roads, feed neighbors, and teach children. Wealth that serves only the owner grows heavy; wealth that serves the city becomes light.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Purpose

Identify the author's purpose as a complete sentence.

Part B: Author Choice

Quote one author choice that helps establish or achieve the purpose.

Part C: Explanation

Explain how the author choice helps the author achieve the purpose.
