Scoring Notes

Independent Work

Central idea: honest public memory must integrate achievement and injustice and remain revisable when evidence expands understanding. Amnesia literally refers to memory loss; metaphorically it describes selective civic forgetting. Trace praise/flattery, confidence/judgment, mature gratitude, unfinished monument, and revisable memory.

Checkpoint

Central idea: disciplined listening is an evidence-responsive method of inquiry, not automatic agreement or performed silence. Strong answers explain definition, examples, contrast, and final synthesis.

Research and Mastery

Require context inference, reputable-source verification when directed, source record, and original explanation. Apply visible rubrics; below 80%, require TOR-guided correction and resubmission.
