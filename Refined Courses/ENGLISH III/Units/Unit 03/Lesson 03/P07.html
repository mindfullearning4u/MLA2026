P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can analyze author purpose in a Classical Period-style text.

Checkpoint Passage

Speech on Courage

Young soldiers, courage is not noise before battle. The loudest voice may be the first to forget the wounded. Courage is the hand that steadies a frightened friend, the eye that sees danger clearly, and the will that moves when duty calls. Seek not to appear fearless. Seek to be faithful.

TOR Support

Ask TOR for help if your answer names the topic but does not explain what the author wants the audience to think or do.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit notebook evidence showing your vocabulary notes, purpose-analysis frame, P03 task, and P06 independent work.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that explains the author's purpose in "Speech on Courage."

Your paragraph must include:

  • the audience,
  • the author's purpose,
  • one quoted author choice, and
  • an explanation of how that choice helps achieve the purpose.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify purpose, not just topic.
  • You connect purpose to audience.
  • You use evidence from the passage.
  • You explain how an author choice helps achieve purpose.
