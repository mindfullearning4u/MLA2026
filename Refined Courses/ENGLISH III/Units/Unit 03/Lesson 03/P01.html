Lesson Overview: Author Purpose in Classical-Period Speeches and Essays

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Evaluate how authors of speeches and essays use content, structure, audience awareness, and rhetoric to achieve layered purposes.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Analyze ideas and their development; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish broad purpose labels from precise purpose statements.
  • Analyze primary, secondary, and evolving purposes.
  • Connect audience, occasion, appeals, structure, and diction.
  • Evaluate effectiveness and ethical implications.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study multiple contrasting examples and the model.
  3. Complete the longer Independent Work with passage-specific vocabulary research.
  4. Complete the separate Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will state a precise purpose and evaluate how several rhetorical decisions work together for a particular audience and occasion.
