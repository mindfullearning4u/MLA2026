Guided Practice Passage

Speech to New Citizens

You have received the city's protection; now learn the city's burden. Do not ask only what gates defend you. Ask what duties call you. A citizen keeps promises, speaks truth in the assembly, and guards the weak when comfort would be easier. Freedom is not a seat for resting; it is a task placed in honorable hands.
