Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all lesson terms and complete definitions. Passage-specific vocabulary will be inferred and researched separately when needed.

TermDefinition
author purposeThe intended effect an author seeks through a text.
occasionThe event or circumstances prompting a text.
audienceThe specific people or groups a text addresses or anticipates.
exigenceAn urgent issue or condition that invites rhetorical response.
ethosAppeal based on credibility, character, or shared values.
pathosAppeal engaging emotion, identity, or imagination.
logosAppeal using reasoning, evidence, and logical relationships.
rhetorical questionA question posed for effect rather than a direct answer.
call to actionA direct or implied request for audience response.
ethical appealA rhetorical choice invoking responsibility, fairness, or moral judgment.
