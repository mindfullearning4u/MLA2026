Notebook Title

Author Purpose in Classical Speeches and Essays

Vocabulary

  • Purpose: what the author wants the text to accomplish.
  • Audience: the people the author is addressing.
  • Author choice: a decision about structure, evidence, wording, example, or tone.
  • Establish purpose: make the goal of the text clear.
  • Achieve purpose: use choices that help the text accomplish its goal.

Direct Teaching

Author purpose is not the same as topic. A topic is what the text is about. Purpose is what the author wants the text to do.

Topic: leadership

Central idea: leaders should listen to wise counsel.

Purpose: to warn leaders against pride and persuade them to seek advice.

Use this process:

  1. Identify the audience.
  2. Identify the central idea.
  3. Ask what the author wants the audience to think, feel, or do.
  4. Find author choices that help accomplish that purpose.

Notebook Task

Copy this frame into your notebook.

  1. The audience is _____.
  2. The central idea is _____.
  3. The author's purpose is to _____.
  4. One author choice that supports this purpose is _____.
  5. This choice helps because _____.
