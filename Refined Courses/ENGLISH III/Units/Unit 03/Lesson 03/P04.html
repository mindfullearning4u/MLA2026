P04 Worked Example

Study how to connect purpose to author choices.

Worked Example Text

Essay on Public Speech

The speaker who seeks applause may win the hour and lose the city. Words should not be polished only to shine; they should be shaped to guide. If speech flatters weakness, it becomes noise. If speech calls citizens toward courage, it becomes service.

Example 1: Purpose

The author's purpose is to warn speakers against empty applause and persuade them to use speech for civic guidance.

Example 2: Author Choice

The author contrasts speech that "flatters weakness" with speech that "calls citizens toward courage."

Example 3: Effect

The contrast helps achieve the purpose because it shows readers two possible uses of speech: shallow praise or public service.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The author uses words because this is an essay.

Why it is weak: It does not identify a meaningful author choice.

Correct: The author uses contrast to warn that speech can either flatter weakness or guide citizens toward courage.

Why it is correct: It connects the author's choice to the author's purpose.
