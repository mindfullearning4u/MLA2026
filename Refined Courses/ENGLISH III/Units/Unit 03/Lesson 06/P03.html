P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply rhetorical evaluation to a short passage.

Practice Passage

Citizens, shall we sleep while the river rises? Shall we argue while the bridge cracks? Shall we wait until the road itself answers for us? We must repair the bridge now, not when fear has already crossed it.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the author's purpose.
  2. Identify one rhetorical choice.
  3. Explain the effect of that choice on the audience.
  4. Evaluate whether the choice is effective.

Be specific. "It makes the passage better" is not enough. Explain what the choice makes the audience feel, notice, or understand.

Model Start

Purpose: The speaker wants citizens to support immediate bridge repair.

Rhetorical choice: The speaker uses repeated questions.

Your turn: Explain the effect and evaluate the effectiveness.
