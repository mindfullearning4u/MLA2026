Lesson Overview: Rhetorical Choices and Their Effects

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze how diction, syntax, repetition, structure, and other rhetorical choices shape meaning and audience response.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Analyze ideas and their development; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Primary skill: Analyze how a writer’s choices in diction, syntax, repetition, and structure shape meaning, tone, emphasis, and audience response.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify specific rhetorical choices rather than labeling a passage generally “persuasive.”
  • Explain the effect of a choice in its exact context.
  • Trace how several choices work together across a passage.
  • Compare reasonable interpretations and determine which is best supported.

Why This Matters

Rhetorical analysis teaches you to notice how language guides attention and judgment. The same skill supports literary analysis, source evaluation, problem solving, and responsible communication.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Study the direct instruction and diverse examples on Page 3.
  3. Follow the fully modeled analysis on Page 4.
  4. Complete Page 5 and submit it with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
  5. Complete Page 6 and submit it separately as the Checkpoint.
  6. Complete the five-question randomized quiz. Mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will name precise rhetorical choices, cite or paraphrase evidence, explain effects without overclaiming, and connect several choices to a text’s purpose and audience.
