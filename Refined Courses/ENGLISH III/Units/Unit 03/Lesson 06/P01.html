Lesson 6: Rhetorical Choices and Effect in Text

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.12 - Evaluate Rhetorical Choices.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose in Classical Texts; MLA.ENG3.R.08 - Evaluate Multiple Arguments and Reasoning; MLA.ENG3.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify rhetorical choices in a text.
  • How repetition, contrast, questions, imagery, and direct address affect an audience.
  • How rhetoric supports author purpose and argument.
  • How to evaluate whether a rhetorical choice is effective.

Why This Matters

Authors do more than state ideas. They choose words, patterns, examples, and structures to move an audience toward a response.

Evaluating rhetoric helps you judge how a text works, not just what it says.

What You Will Do

  • Read short rhetorical passages.
  • Identify specific rhetorical choices.
  • Explain the effect of those choices on the audience.
  • Evaluate whether each choice helps the author achieve the purpose.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will identify a rhetorical choice, explain its effect, and evaluate how well it supports the author's purpose.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to judge how an author's rhetorical choices affect the reader or listener.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can name a rhetorical device but cannot explain its effect on the audience.
