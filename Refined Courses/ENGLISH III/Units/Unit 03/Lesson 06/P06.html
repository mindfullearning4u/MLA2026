Independent Work Passage

Citizens, a city without justice is a house built on sand. It may stand through one quiet morning, but the first storm will teach what the builders refused to learn. Let us repair the foundation before the roof collapses.

Part A: Identify

Write the author's purpose. Then identify one rhetorical choice from the passage.

Part B: Explain

Explain the effect of the rhetorical choice. What does it make the audience feel, notice, or understand?

Part C: Evaluate

Write two to four sentences judging whether the rhetorical choice effectively supports the author's purpose.
