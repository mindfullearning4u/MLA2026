Guided Practice Passage

People of the city, look at the empty granary, look at the silent mill, look at the children waiting at the market. If we delay the grain shipment, hunger will speak louder than any council bell.

Before You Answer

  1. Identify the author's purpose.
  2. Find one rhetorical choice.
  3. Explain what the choice does to the audience.
  4. Judge whether that choice helps the purpose.
