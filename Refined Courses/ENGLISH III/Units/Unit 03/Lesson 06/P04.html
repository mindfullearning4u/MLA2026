📘 ENG3 | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P04 Worked Example

Study how to move from device identification to evaluation.

Worked Example Passage

Friends, the law is not a locked gate; it is a lamp. It should not hide the path from the poor or the stranger. It should light the way so every citizen may walk without fear.

Example 1: Purpose

The speaker wants the audience to believe that law should be clear and accessible to everyone.

Example 2: Rhetorical Choice

The speaker uses metaphor by comparing law to a lamp. This image makes clear law seem helpful, guiding, and protective.

Example 3: Evaluation

The metaphor is effective because it turns an abstract idea, law, into a concrete image the audience can understand. The lamp image supports the purpose by making clear law feel necessary and humane.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The author uses a metaphor, so the rhetoric is good.

Why it is weak: Naming a device is not evaluation.

Correct: The lamp metaphor is effective because it makes clear law seem useful and protective, which supports the author's purpose of promoting accessible laws.

Why it is correct: It identifies the choice, explains the effect, and connects the effect to purpose.
