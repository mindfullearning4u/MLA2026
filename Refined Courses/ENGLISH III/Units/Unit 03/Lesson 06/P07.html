P07 Checkpoint

Show mastery of evaluating rhetoric in text.

Checkpoint Passage

Friends, the truth is not a whisper for locked rooms; it is a bell for the square. Let it ring where merchants bargain, where judges listen, and where children learn the names of justice.

Checkpoint Task

Write one organized paragraph that includes:

  • the author's purpose,
  • one specific rhetorical choice,
  • the effect of that choice on the audience,
  • an explanation of how the choice supports purpose, and
  • a judgment about whether the choice is effective.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your P02-P03 notebook chart and your independent work from P06 as evidence of preparation.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your final paragraph in the checkpoint assignment area.

Ask TOR for help before submitting if your paragraph names a device but does not explain its effect.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies a rhetorical choice accurately.
  • Explains audience effect clearly.
  • Connects rhetoric to author purpose.
  • Evaluates effectiveness using textual evidence.
