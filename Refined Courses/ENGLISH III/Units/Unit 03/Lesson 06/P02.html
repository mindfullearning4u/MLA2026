Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.
Term Definition
rhetorical choice A deliberate language or structural decision used to shape meaning or audience response.
diction A writer’s purposeful choice of words, including their connotations and level of formality.
syntax The arrangement of words, phrases, and clauses in sentences.
repetition The intentional reuse of a word, phrase, sound, or structure for emphasis or cohesion.
parallelism The use of similar grammatical structures to express related ideas.
anaphora Repetition of a word or phrase at the beginning of successive clauses or sentences.
antithesis The placement of contrasting ideas in balanced or parallel structures.
rhetorical question A question asked to prompt thought or emphasize a point rather than obtain an answer.
cadence The rhythmic flow created by sentence length, stress, punctuation, and sound.
cumulative effect The combined impact produced as multiple choices build across a text.
