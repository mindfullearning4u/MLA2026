📘 ENG3 | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Set up your notebook to evaluate rhetorical choices.

Notebook Title

Rhetorical Choices and Audience Effect

Vocabulary

  • Rhetoric: The way language is used to persuade, inform, or affect an audience.
  • Rhetorical choice: A deliberate author move, such as repetition, contrast, imagery, direct address, or a question.
  • Effect: What the choice does for the audience.
  • Purpose: What the author wants to accomplish.
  • Evaluate: Judge how well the choice works.

Direct Teaching

Do not stop at naming a device. A strong rhetorical evaluation has three parts: identify the choice, explain the effect, and connect the effect to purpose.

For example, if a speaker repeats "we must act," the device is repetition. The effect may be urgency. The purpose may be to move listeners toward action.

Use this frame: The author uses ____ to create ____, which helps achieve the purpose of ____.

Notebook Setup

Create a four-row chart:

  1. Author's purpose
  2. Rhetorical choice
  3. Effect on audience
  4. Evaluation of effectiveness

Leave space for one paragraph after the chart.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Rhetorical choices and effect in text

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.R.12

Use this sequence before you complete the notebook task. It makes the Grade 11 thinking process explicit so you do not have to guess what the skill requires.

  1. Identify the speaker or writer, audience, purpose, and occasion before judging the argument or rhetoric. These details shape what the choices are meant to do.
  2. Separate claim, evidence, reasoning, and rhetorical technique. A strong analysis names the move and explains how it affects the audience.
  3. Evaluate effectiveness, not just presence. Ask whether the structure, appeal, diction, repetition, or contrast actually helps the author achieve the purpose.
  4. Use precise academic language such as claim, validity, reasoning, appeal, effect, audience, and purpose so your analysis sounds mature and defensible.
