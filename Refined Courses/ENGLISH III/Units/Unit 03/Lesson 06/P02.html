P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Set up your notebook to evaluate rhetorical choices.

Notebook Title

Rhetorical Choices and Audience Effect

Vocabulary

  • Rhetoric: The way language is used to persuade, inform, or affect an audience.
  • Rhetorical choice: A deliberate author move, such as repetition, contrast, imagery, direct address, or a question.
  • Effect: What the choice does for the audience.
  • Purpose: What the author wants to accomplish.
  • Evaluate: Judge how well the choice works.

Direct Teaching

Do not stop at naming a device. A strong rhetorical evaluation has three parts: identify the choice, explain the effect, and connect the effect to purpose.

For example, if a speaker repeats "we must act," the device is repetition. The effect may be urgency. The purpose may be to move listeners toward action.

Use this frame: The author uses ____ to create ____, which helps achieve the purpose of ____.

Notebook Setup

Create a four-row chart:

  1. Author's purpose
  2. Rhetorical choice
  3. Effect on audience
  4. Evaluation of effectiveness

Leave space for one paragraph after the chart.
