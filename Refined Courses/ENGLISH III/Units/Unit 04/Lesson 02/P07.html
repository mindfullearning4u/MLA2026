P07 Checkpoint

Show mastery of selecting primary and secondary sources for literary research.

Checkpoint Scenario

Research question: How does the candle image in the poem develop the idea that a small act of witness can challenge darkness?

Source A: The poem that includes the candle image.

Source B: A scholarly article about light imagery and witness in poetry.

Source C: A general article about how candles are manufactured.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that:

  • identifies the primary source,
  • identifies the most useful secondary source,
  • explains why the third source is less useful, and
  • connects your source choices to the research question.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your source classification chart from P02-P03 and your independent work from P06.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your final paragraph in the checkpoint assignment area.

Ask TOR for help before submitting if you are unsure which source directly provides literary evidence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Correctly identifies primary and secondary sources.
  • Explains source relevance to the research question.
  • Uses literary research vocabulary accurately.
  • Chooses sources that support interpretation, not random background facts.
