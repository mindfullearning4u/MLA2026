Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work.
Term Definition
primary source An original text, artifact, record, or firsthand account examined directly as evidence.
secondary source A source that analyzes, interprets, or synthesizes primary sources.
tertiary source A reference source that summarizes established information and directs researchers to other sources.
scholarly source Research written by qualified specialists and supported by evidence, documentation, and disciplinary review.
authority The expertise, experience, or institutional position that makes a source qualified for a particular claim.
relevance The degree to which a source directly helps answer the research question.
currency How current a source is in relation to the information or scholarship required.
bias A perspective or preference that may shape selection, emphasis, interpretation, or omission.
corroboration Independent evidence that supports or confirms a claim.
provenance The documented origin, ownership, and history of a text, record, or artifact.
