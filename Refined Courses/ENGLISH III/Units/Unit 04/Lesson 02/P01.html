Lesson 2: Primary and Secondary Sources in Literary Research

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.RS.01 - Conduct Literary Research.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 - Evaluate Literary Elements; MLA.ENG3.R.11 - Compare Contemporaneous Authors; MLA.ENG3.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to distinguish primary and secondary sources in literary research.
  • How each source type supports interpretation differently.
  • How to choose sources that fit a literary research question.
  • How source context can help compare authors and literary elements.

Why This Matters

Literary research depends on using the right kind of evidence. The literary text itself is not the same as an article about the text.

Strong researchers know when to use a primary source, when to use a secondary source, and how both can support interpretation.

What You Will Do

  • Classify sources as primary or secondary.
  • Match source types to research purposes.
  • Explain how a source could support a literary interpretation.
  • Choose sources for a focused literary research question.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will identify primary and secondary sources and explain which sources best support a literary research question.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to conduct literary research using sources that help answer a question and support interpretation of texts.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can find a source but are unsure whether it is primary, secondary, or useful for your research question.
