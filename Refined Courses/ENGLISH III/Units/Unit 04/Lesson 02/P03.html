P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Apply source classification to a literary research question.

Research Question

How does the cracked map in the story develop the idea that memory preserves struggle after a problem is repaired?

Possible Sources

Source A: The short story that includes Mara and the cracked map.

Source B: A scholarly article explaining how maps often function as memory symbols in modern fiction.

Source C: A background article about road repair campaigns during the author's time period.

Notebook Directions

  1. Classify each source as primary or secondary.
  2. Explain how Source A could support direct interpretation.
  3. Choose which secondary source is more relevant to the question.
  4. Explain your choice in two or three sentences.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a broad answer without identifying the exact evidence, rhetorical move, literary choice, or author purpose.

Correct: Name the specific evidence or choice and explain how it supports meaning, argument, style, or reader effect.

Why this matters: English III mastery requires clear reasoning. The reader must see how your evidence proves the claim.
