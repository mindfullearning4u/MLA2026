P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Subject Understanding in Complex Text Analysis

Vocabulary

Subject: The main topic, issue, or concept a text addresses.

Subject understanding: Accurate knowledge of the subject shown through precise explanation.

Academic vocabulary: Formal words used to explain analysis clearly, such as develops, clarifies, emphasizes, contrasts, and qualifies.

Revision: Improving writing for clarity, structure, and style.

Complex Text Summary

An essay explains that community gardens are not only places to grow food. The essay describes neighbors sharing tools, older residents teaching planting methods, and students recording family recipes. The final paragraph argues that the garden preserves cultural memory while solving a practical need.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Name the subject of the text in one phrase.

Step 2: Write two details that show you understand the subject.

Step 3: Write one analytical claim about how the essay develops the subject.

Step 4: Add two academic vocabulary words you could use in your analysis.
