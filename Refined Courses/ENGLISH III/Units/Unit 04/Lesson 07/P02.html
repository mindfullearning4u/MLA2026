Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
subject knowledge Accurate information about the ideas, practices, events, or systems relevant to a text.
contextual lens A focused body of background knowledge used to clarify particular textual features.
historical context The conditions, events, beliefs, and institutions surrounding a text’s setting or production.
cultural context Shared practices, values, symbols, and assumptions that influence meaning.
disciplinary source A credible source created within a field of study, such as history, science, law, or art.
anachronism Placing an idea, object, practice, or value in a historical period where it does not belong.
presentism Judging or interpreting the past only through current assumptions without accounting for historical difference.
corroboration Confirmation of a claim through agreement among independent evidence or sources.
relevance The degree to which information directly helps answer the interpretive question.
context fallacy An error in which background information replaces, overwhelms, or contradicts analysis of the actual text.
