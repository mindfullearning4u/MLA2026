P04 Worked Example

Study how subject understanding strengthens complex text analysis.

Worked Example Text

A report explains that public libraries now provide books, internet access, job search help, and meeting rooms. The report uses headings for each service and ends by arguing that libraries function as civic infrastructure.

Example 1: Subject Understanding

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify the specific lesson skill being modeled. Step 2: examine the words, evidence, sentence, or passage detail that matters most. Step 3: explain why the final response or revision demonstrates the skill accurately.

The subject is the changing civic role of public libraries. The report does not only describe books; it explains how libraries support public needs through multiple services.

Example 2: Organized Analysis

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify what changed from the first example. Step 2: connect the example to the lesson focus. Step 3: explain how the response uses clearer reasoning, stronger evidence, or more precise language.

The headings organize the report by service, which helps readers see that the library's civic role is built from several connected functions.

Example 3: Revised Academic Voice

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: combine the lesson skill with evidence or revision choices. Step 2: check that the explanation stays focused on the assigned standard. Step 3: confirm that the final response is clear, accurate, and written in standard English.

Before: Libraries do a lot of stuff, and the headings make it easy.

After: The report's headings clarify the library's expanded civic role by separating each public service into a distinct section.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The report is about libraries being useful.

Why it is weak: This is too vague to show subject understanding.

Correct: The report analyzes public libraries as civic infrastructure by showing how their services support community needs.

Why it is correct: This names the subject accurately and explains the report's focus.
