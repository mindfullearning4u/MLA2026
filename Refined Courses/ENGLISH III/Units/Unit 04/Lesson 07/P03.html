P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Build a paragraph that shows subject understanding.

Paragraph Sequence

Sentence 1: State the subject and claim.

Sentence 2: Use evidence that shows the subject accurately.

Sentence 3: Explain how the evidence develops the subject.

Sentence 4: Use academic vocabulary to explain the larger meaning.

Model Paragraph

The essay develops the subject of community gardens as both practical and cultural spaces. Details about neighbors sharing tools show practical cooperation, while older residents teaching planting methods and students recording recipes show cultural memory. These examples clarify that the garden solves a food need while also preserving identity. The organization emphasizes that community spaces can meet physical needs and protect shared history at the same time.

Notebook Task

Write your own paragraph about the community garden text summary. Make sure the paragraph shows accurate subject understanding before it explains meaning.

After writing, revise one sentence to improve academic vocabulary.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The essay is about gardens and people like food.

Why it is weak: This is too general and does not show accurate understanding of the subject.

Correct: The essay presents community gardens as spaces that meet practical food needs while preserving cultural memory.

Why it is correct: This names the subject precisely and shows what the text explains about it.
