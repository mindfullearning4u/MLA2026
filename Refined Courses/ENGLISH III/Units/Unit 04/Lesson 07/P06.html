📘 ENG3 | Unit 04 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Practice writing complex text analysis that shows subject understanding.

Instructions

Use the text summary below. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Text summary: An article explains that school murals can preserve local history. It describes students interviewing elders, painting neighborhood scenes, and adding captions that identify important places. The article ends by arguing that public art can become a shared archive.

Part A

Write one sentence naming the subject of the article accurately.

Part B

Write one analytical claim about how the article develops the subject through examples.

Part C

Write one organized paragraph that shows subject understanding, uses academic vocabulary, and explains how the article's examples support its final claim.
