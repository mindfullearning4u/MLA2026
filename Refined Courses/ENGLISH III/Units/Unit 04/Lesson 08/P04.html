Example 1: Research Synthesis

The literary passage shows an empty market with sensory and physical traces of public life. The source note explains that marketplaces can represent community memory. Together, the sources support the interpretation that the market preserves evidence of a community even after people have left.

Example 2: Literary Analysis

The broom leaning against the wall symbolizes interrupted daily life because it looks as if someone has just stepped away. This detail makes absence feel recent rather than distant.

Example 3: Complex Text Analysis

The essay's organization moves from image, to examples, to final claim. This order helps readers understand the subject of civic memory by first seeing the empty market and then learning what public life it once held.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Why it is weak: This response is vague and does not explain research, literary elements, or organization.
