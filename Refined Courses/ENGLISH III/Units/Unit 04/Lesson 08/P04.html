📘 ENG3 | Unit 04 | Lesson 08

P04 Worked Example

A complete Unit 4 model asks a literary research question, selects sources, synthesizes evidence, and writes a formal analysis paragraph.

Example 1

Research question: How does setting reveal a character's conflict? This question is focused because it connects a literary element to meaning.

Example 2

The primary source shows the character choosing the quiet bridge, and the secondary source explains repeated places as emotional pressure.

Example 3

The analysis paragraph should connect both sources to a claim about transition, conflict, and setting.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Source A and Source B are about setting.

Correct: Source A shows setting in the text, while Source B explains how setting can reveal emotional pressure; together they support an interpretation of character conflict.
