Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 4 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 4 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG3.RS.01; MLA.ENG3.W.02; MLA.ENG3.W.03.

Unit 4 Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10; MLA.ENG3.W.04; MLA.ENG3.L.01; MLA.ENG3.C.01.

No New Primary Standard

This lesson reviews and synthesizes what you learned in Lessons 1-7. It does not introduce a new primary standard.

What You Will Review

  • Literary research questions, source selection, and source synthesis.
  • Literary analysis claims, evidence, reasoning, elaboration, and literary elements.
  • Complex text analysis organization, tone, voice, and subject understanding.
  • Revision for clarity, structure, style, and standard English conventions.

What You Will Do

You will complete a guided review, check your notebook evidence, and then complete the Unit 4 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by completing the Unit 4 Assessment on literary research, source-supported literary analysis, and complex text analysis.
