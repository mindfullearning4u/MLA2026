Lesson Overview: Putting It All Together—Unit 4 Synthesis

Lesson Focus

This synthesis lesson asks you to conduct a defensible literary investigation: frame an interpretive question, analyze a complex text, evaluate and synthesize sources, apply relevant subject knowledge, address competing interpretations, and communicate a precise conclusion.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.08 — Synthesize close reading, research, and contextual knowledge to produce a complex literary interpretation.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite strong and thorough evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze organization, tone, and voice; MLA.ENG3.R.07 — Integrate relevant subject knowledge; MLA.ENG3.W.07 — Conduct focused research; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Evaluate and synthesize sources; MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Draw evidence from literary and informational texts; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Turn ambiguity in a text into a focused, researchable interpretive question.
  • Build an evidence set containing textual details, credible sources, and relevant subject knowledge.
  • Synthesize sources by relationship rather than listing them separately.
  • Address limitations and competing interpretations.
  • Produce an interpretation whose reasoning can be traced and tested.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary and definitions.
  2. Study the synthesis protocol and diverse examples on Page 3.
  3. Follow the complete modeled investigation on Page 4.
  4. Complete Page 5 Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
  5. Complete Page 6 and submit it separately as the Checkpoint.
  6. Complete the Unit 4 Final Assessment: 10 questions drawn randomly from a 40-question bank, one attempt, 80% mastery.
