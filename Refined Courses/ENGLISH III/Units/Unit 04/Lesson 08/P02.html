Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
literary investigation A structured inquiry that uses close reading and relevant research to answer an interpretive question.
interpretive question A focused, debatable question about meaning, craft, or significance in a text.
evidence set The deliberately selected collection of textual details and credible research used to test a claim.
source relationship The way sources confirm, extend, complicate, qualify, or contradict one another.
synthesis matrix An organizer that compares sources by ideas, evidence, relationships, and usefulness.
line of reasoning The connected sequence showing how evidence and explanation support a conclusion.
counterinterpretation A plausible alternative explanation of the same textual evidence.
qualification A limitation or condition added to make a claim more accurate.
triangulation Testing a conclusion by comparing multiple independent forms of evidence.
defensible interpretation A conclusion supported by accurate evidence, logical reasoning, and responsible acknowledgment of uncertainty.
