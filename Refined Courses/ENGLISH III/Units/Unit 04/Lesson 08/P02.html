P02 Notebook Task Part 1: Unit Review

Notebook Title: Unit 4 Research and Analysis Review

Review Source Set

Literary passage: The closed market still smelled faintly of oranges. Painted signs hung above empty stalls, and a broom leaned against the wall as if someone had just stepped away.

Source note: Public marketplaces in literature can represent community memory because they connect labor, exchange, and everyday social life.

Complex text summary: An essay begins with the empty market, gives examples of vendors and customers who once gathered there, and ends by arguing that public spaces preserve civic memory.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Write one literary research question about the market passage.

Step 2: Paraphrase the source note in one sentence.

Step 3: Write one literary analysis claim about a symbol, image, or setting detail.

Step 4: Identify the organization of the complex text summary.

Vocabulary Review

  • Claim: the idea a response or argument tries to prove.
  • Evidence: words, details, or examples that support a claim.
  • Analysis: explanation of how evidence proves or develops an idea.
  • Purpose: the reason an author or speaker makes a choice.

Teacher-Guided Thinking Check

Before you answer independently, identify the skill, locate evidence, and explain the connection in your own words. If the response feels too general, return to the vocabulary and choose a more precise term.
