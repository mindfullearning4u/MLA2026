P03 Notebook Task Part 2: Synthesis Review

Use the same review source set from Part 1.

Review Sequence

Research: Ask a question that can be answered with the literary passage and source note.

Literary analysis: Make a claim about how a literary element creates meaning.

Complex text analysis: Explain how the essay's organization develops the subject.

Revision: Improve one sentence for clarity and academic voice.

Notebook Task

Write two review paragraphs. Paragraph 1 should synthesize the literary passage and source note. Paragraph 2 should analyze the organization of the complex text summary.

After writing, revise one sentence in each paragraph to improve precision and standard English conventions.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The market is empty, and the essay is about public places.

Why it is weak: This only lists ideas and does not synthesize or analyze.

Correct: The empty market develops community memory because its remaining smells, signs, and tools suggest everyday public life that has recently disappeared.

Why it is correct: This explains how evidence supports meaning.
