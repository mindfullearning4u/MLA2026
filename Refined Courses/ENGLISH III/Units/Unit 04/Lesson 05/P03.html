P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Turn literary element notes into source-supported analysis.

Analysis Sequence

Claim: Name the literary element and the meaning it creates.

Evidence: Use a specific detail from the literary text.

Source support: Add research or source information that helps explain the element.

Elaboration: Explain how the evidence and source support work together.

Model Analysis

The storm-damaged tree symbolizes endurance mixed with hidden harm. The new leaves make the tree appear strong, but the splitting roots reveal damage beneath the surface. The source note supports this interpretation by explaining that trees can show both visible growth and unseen damage. Together, the passage and source show that survival may look strong from the outside while still carrying internal strain.

Notebook Task

Write your own source-supported analysis paragraph about the tree passage. Your paragraph must include a claim, evidence, source support, and elaboration.

After writing, revise one sentence to make the literary element clearer.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The tree is damaged. The source says trees can mean endurance. This is about a storm.

Why it is weak: This lists details but does not explain the literary element or its meaning.

Correct: The tree symbolizes complicated endurance because its new leaves suggest survival while its splitting roots reveal hidden damage.

Why it is correct: This explains how the literary element creates layered meaning.
