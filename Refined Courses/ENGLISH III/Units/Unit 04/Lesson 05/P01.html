Literary Elements in Source-Supported Analysis

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.02 - Write literary analyses to support claims, using logical reasoning, credible evidence from sources, and elaboration, demonstrating an understanding of literary elements.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01, MLA.ENG3.R.09, MLA.ENG3.W.04

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to make literary elements the center of a source-supported analysis paragraph. You will connect evidence to elements such as symbol, imagery, figurative language, allegory, characterization, structure, and style.

A strong literary analysis does not just include a source. It uses the source to help explain how a literary element creates meaning.

Why This Matters

When you name and explain literary elements, your writing becomes more precise. Research can support your interpretation, but your paragraph must still prove how the text itself works.

What You Will Do

  • Identify the literary element that matters most in a passage.
  • Use source information to support, not replace, textual analysis.
  • Explain figurative language or allegory when it appears.
  • Revise analysis for clarity, structure, and style.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can write a literary analysis paragraph that makes a claim about a literary element, supports it with evidence, uses source information appropriately, and explains the meaning clearly.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can write analysis that explains how literary elements create meaning and support my ideas with evidence.

Ask TOR for Help: If you can name the evidence but not the literary element, ask TOR for help identifying the element before you write.
