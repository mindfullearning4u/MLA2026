Checkpoint: “The Red Thread”

Directions

Read and answer all 12 questions. Submit separately.

Passage

Amel stitched a red thread into every coat she repaired, hiding it beneath the lining. Customers never saw it. After she retired, her apprentice continued the practice but allowed one red knot to remain visible at each cuff.

Some customers requested the knot; others asked that it be removed. The apprentice kept a ledger of both choices, writing beside each name, “Seen” or “Carried.”

Questions

  1. Infer lining.
  2. State an interpretation.
  3. Analyze the thread motif.
  4. Compare Amel and the apprentice.
  5. Explain the effect of hidden versus visible placement.
  6. Analyze the ledger’s structural role.
  7. Identify counterevidence.
  8. Qualify the claim.
  9. Name one relevant craft source.
  10. Explain its limits.
  11. Compare two interpretations of “Seen” and “Carried.”
  12. Write a four-sentence analysis.
