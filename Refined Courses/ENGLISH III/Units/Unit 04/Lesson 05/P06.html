P06 Independent Work

Practice writing source-supported analysis of a literary element.

Instructions

Use the passage and source note below. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Passage: The lighthouse lamp turned even after the harbor closed. Each night, its beam crossed empty water and flashed against houses where no sailors lived anymore.

Source note: Light imagery in literature can suggest guidance, memory, warning, or hope, depending on what the light reveals and who responds to it.

Part A

Identify the literary element you will analyze. Then write one claim about what the lighthouse light suggests.

Part B

Select one detail from the passage and explain how the source note helps you interpret that detail.

Part C

Write one source-supported analysis paragraph. Then revise one sentence to make the claim, evidence, or elaboration clearer.
