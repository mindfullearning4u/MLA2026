Worked Example: Element Interaction with Sources

Passage

Each winter, Adra moved the family table closer to the window, though daylight lasted only minutes longer there. After her brother left, she set one place facing the glass. The narrator never states whether she expects his return.

In spring, the table remains by the window, but Adra removes the extra chair and plants beans in a box on its seat.

Close Reading

Setting, furniture, seasonal structure, and characterization interact. The window provides limited light; the empty place sustains uncertainty; the beans transform the chair from waiting into cultivation.

Contextual Source

A regional history explains winter household adaptations and window gardens. It clarifies literal practices but does not prove symbolism.

Critical Source

A scholarly essay argues that domestic objects in migration fiction often preserve unresolved relationships. This supports attention to the table but must be tested against the spring change.

Integrated Interpretation

The seasonal structure changes the table from a site of suspended return into a site of redirected care. Context clarifies the practical value of light; scholarship explains the object’s relational role; the beans and removed chair qualify a simple grief interpretation.

Alternative Reading

Planting on the chair may preserve the brother’s place rather than release it. The passage supports both continuity and change, so the strongest claim should acknowledge the tension.
