Independent Work: “The Room Above the Bakery”

Directions

Read the passage and answer all 18 questions. Analyze interacting literary elements, verify passage vocabulary, and use outside sources only where relevant. Submit with Page 2 vocabulary.

Passage

For twelve years, Mina rented the room above the bakery because the ovens warmed its floor before sunrise. She wrote at a desk pressed against the chimney wall, timing each paragraph by the scrape of bread trays below.

When the bakery closed, silence arrived before cold. Mina began hearing the building settle: pipes ticking, joists shifting, rain entering a gutter behind the wall. Her unfinished novel, once arranged by bakery seasons, lost its calendar.

She considered leaving. Instead, she moved the desk to the window and recorded every new sound. By winter, the novel no longer followed bread production; it followed the building’s slow exposure to weather. On the final page, her character opens a door not because someone knocks, but because the house has become too quiet to interpret alone.
