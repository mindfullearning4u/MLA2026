Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and definitions. Submit with Independent Work.
literary element A component of a literary work, such as character, setting, conflict, imagery, symbolism, point of view, or structure.
interaction The way two or more elements influence one another’s meaning or effect.
motif A recurring image, phrase, situation, or idea that develops meaning.
symbolism The use of a concrete detail to suggest meanings beyond its literal role.
point of view The position and perspective through which a narrative is presented.
setting The time, place, social environment, and conditions of a work.
characterization The methods used to develop a character’s traits, motives, relationships, and changes.
structural pattern An arrangement or recurrence that organizes a text.
critical lens A framework used to examine particular features or assumptions in a text.
contextual evidence Relevant outside information that clarifies circumstances without determining interpretation.
