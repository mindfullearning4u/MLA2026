📘 ENG3 | Unit 04 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Practice planning and writing organized complex text analysis.

Instructions

Use the complex text summary below. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Text summary: An essay opens with a description of a silent courthouse. It then presents three examples of citizens speaking at public meetings. It ends by arguing that democracy depends on ordinary people practicing public speech.

Part A

Identify the text's organization. Explain how the essay moves from opening image to examples to final claim.

Part B

Write one academic claim about how the organization supports meaning. Keep the tone formal and precise.

Part C

Write one organized analysis paragraph. Use a formal academic voice and standard English conventions.
