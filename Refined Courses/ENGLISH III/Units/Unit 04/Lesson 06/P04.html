Worked Example: A Layered Narrator

Passage

I told the committee that the archive room had always leaked. That was true, in the economical sense that it had leaked every year I worked there. I did not mention the box beneath the pipe, because no one asked where I had placed it.

Three weeks earlier, I had moved the box from the highest shelf. I remember thinking that important records should be accessible. I also remember the weather report. Memory is generous when it wants to become an alibi.

The committee’s final report called the damage unforeseeable. I corrected the spelling in their draft and left the adjective untouched.

Organization

The passage states the defense first, moves backward to the decisive action, then returns to the official report. This frame exposes gaps inside the opening claim.

Tone

“Economical sense” is dry and defensive. The tone shifts toward self-accusation in “Memory is generous…” and ends with controlled irony.

Voice and Reliability

The precise, bureaucratic voice initially appears credible, but its attention to wording becomes evidence of evasion. The narrator admits facts while managing their moral significance.

Integrated Interpretation

Organization forces the reader to revise the opening defense; tone moves from technical qualification to ironic admission; voice reveals reliability about events but unreliability about responsibility.

Alternative Reading

The narrator may be honestly uncertain rather than deliberately deceptive. The untouched adjective supports both guilt and institutional pressure, so the strongest analysis should remain qualified.
