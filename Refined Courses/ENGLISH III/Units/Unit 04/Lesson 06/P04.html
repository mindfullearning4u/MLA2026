P04 Worked Example

Study how organization, tone, and voice work together in complex text analysis.

Worked Example Text

A speech begins by describing a locked factory gate. It then lists the names of workers who returned each morning. It ends by arguing that labor gives dignity to a place even when ownership changes.

Example 1: Organization

The speech moves from a physical image, to specific people, to a broad claim. That order helps the audience move from concrete evidence to the speaker's larger argument.

Example 2: Tone and Voice

An academic analysis should use a serious, precise tone because the task is to explain how the speech is structured. The voice should avoid slang, exaggeration, and personal reactions.

Example 3: Analysis Sentence

The speech's structure moves from locked gates to named workers to a claim about dignity, which helps the audience understand labor as both personal and public.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The speech is really good because it talks about workers and factories in a powerful way.

Why it is weak: The tone is too informal, and the sentence does not analyze organization.

Correct: The speech uses a concrete-to-abstract structure, moving from the factory gate to workers' names before reaching its claim about dignity.

Why it is correct: This uses academic voice and explains the structure clearly.
