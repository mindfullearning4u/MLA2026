Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The speech is really good because it talks about workers and factories in a powerful way.

Why it is weak: The tone is too informal, and the sentence does not analyze organization.

Correct: The speech uses a concrete-to-abstract structure, moving from the factory gate to workers' names before reaching its claim about dignity.

Why it is correct: This uses academic voice and explains the structure clearly.
