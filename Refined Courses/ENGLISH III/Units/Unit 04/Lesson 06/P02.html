Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and definitions. Submit with Independent Work.
organization The deliberate arrangement and progression of ideas, scenes, evidence, or time.
tone A speaker’s or author’s attitude toward subject, audience, or material.
voice The distinctive presence created through diction, syntax, perspective, and style.
narrative distance The degree of closeness between narration and a character’s experience.
tonal shift A meaningful change in attitude or emotional stance.
juxtaposition Placement of elements near one another to create comparison or contrast.
frame narrative A structure in which one narrative contains or introduces another.
digression A temporary departure from the main line of discussion or action.
reliability The degree to which a narrator’s account appears accurate, complete, and trustworthy.
cadence The rhythmic movement of language produced by syntax, stress, and punctuation.
