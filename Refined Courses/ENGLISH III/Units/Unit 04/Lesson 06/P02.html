P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Organizing Complex Text Analysis

Vocabulary

Complex text: A text with layered ideas, formal structure, challenging language, or multiple features that require careful analysis.

Organization: The order and arrangement of ideas in writing.

Tone: The writer's attitude toward the subject and audience.

Voice: The writer's style and level of formality in communicating ideas.

Complex Text Excerpt

In the first paragraph, the essay describes a city at dawn with empty streets and closed market stalls. In the second paragraph, it shifts to workers arriving one by one. In the final paragraph, the writer argues that a city is not defined by buildings alone but by the repeated actions of people who make public life possible.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Write the subject of the excerpt.

Step 2: Identify how the excerpt is organized from beginning to end.

Step 3: Explain how the organization helps develop the writer's idea.

Step 4: Write one sentence describing the tone an academic analysis should use for this excerpt.
