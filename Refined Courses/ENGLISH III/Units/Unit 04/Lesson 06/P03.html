P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Build an organized analysis plan with academic tone and voice.

Logical Organization for Analysis

First: State the main analytical claim.

Next: Explain the structure or feature being analyzed.

Then: Use evidence from the text to show how the structure or feature works.

Finally: Explain why that structure or feature matters to the text's meaning.

Model Plan

Claim: The essay's structure moves from emptiness to activity to show that community is created through repeated human action.

Evidence: The excerpt begins with empty streets, shifts to workers arriving, and ends with a claim about public life.

Tone and voice: The analysis should be formal, precise, and focused on how the structure develops meaning.

Notebook Task

Create your own four-part analysis plan for the complex text excerpt from Part 1. Include a claim, structure explanation, evidence, and final significance statement.

Then revise one sentence so it sounds formal and academic.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The essay starts with streets, then workers come, and then it talks about people.

Why it is weak: This retells the order but does not analyze why the organization matters.

Correct: The essay's movement from empty streets to workers arriving helps show that public life is created by human action, not buildings alone.

Why it is correct: This explains the effect of the text's organization.
