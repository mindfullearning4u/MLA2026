Checkpoint: “The Second Signature”

Directions

Read and answer all 12 questions. Submit separately.

Passage

Every mural in the station carried one signature: M. Vero. During restoration, conservator Lian discovered a second name beneath the final layer of paint. The hidden letters belonged to Vero’s assistant, Tamsin, whose notebooks described mixing colors and redesigning damaged panels.

The museum added Tamsin’s name to the label but kept Vero’s signature visible on the wall. Lian argued that the two records served different purposes: one documented the artwork’s public history; the other corrected the history of its making.
