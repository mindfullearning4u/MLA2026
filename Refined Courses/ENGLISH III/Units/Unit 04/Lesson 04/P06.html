P06 Independent Work

Practice writing a complete literary analysis paragraph.

Instructions

Use the passage below to complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Passage: The old school clock had stopped at 3:12. Every afternoon, Julian waited for it to move, though the halls were empty and the bell had been removed years ago. He told himself he was only checking the time, but he stayed until the windows turned gold.

Part A

Write one claim about how the stopped clock develops meaning in the passage.

Part B

Select one detail from the passage and explain why it is the best evidence for your claim.

Part C

Write one paragraph with a claim, evidence, reasoning, and elaboration. Use at least one academic word such as symbolizes, emphasizes, suggests, develops, or contrasts.
