Lesson Overview: Literary Analysis Claims, Reasoning, Evidence, and Elaboration

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Construct evidence-based literary analysis with precise claims, logical reasoning, and substantive elaboration.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.09 — Synthesize source evidence; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Evaluate sources; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Develop a claim whose reasoning is visible, whose evidence is sufficient and relevant, and whose elaboration explains rather than repeats.

Learning Goals

  • Write qualified interpretive claims.
  • Distinguish evidence from the reasoning that makes it meaningful.
  • Build warrants and reasoning chains.
  • Select and integrate textual and source evidence.
  • Elaborate through analysis, implication, qualification, and counterevidence.

Workflow and Mastery

Vocabulary → multiple claim models → worked paragraph → Independent Work → separate Checkpoint → five randomized questions at 80% mastery.
