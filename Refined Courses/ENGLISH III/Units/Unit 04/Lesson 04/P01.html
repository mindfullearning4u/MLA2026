📘 ENG3 | Unit 04 | Lesson 04

Literary Analysis Claims, Reasoning, Evidence, and Elaboration

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.02 - Write literary analyses to support claims, using logical reasoning, credible evidence from sources, and elaboration, demonstrating understanding of literary elements.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.RS.01, MLA.ENG3.R.01, MLA.ENG3.V.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to turn literary research and reading notes into an analysis paragraph with a claim, reasoning, evidence, and elaboration.

A literary analysis does not simply retell the text. It explains how literary elements create meaning.

Why This Matters

Analysis writing is the bridge between understanding a text and proving an interpretation. Strong claims, evidence, reasoning, and elaboration help readers see why your interpretation is valid.

What You Will Do

  • Identify a precise literary analysis claim.
  • Select credible evidence from a literary text or source.
  • Explain reasoning that connects evidence to the claim.
  • Add elaboration using accurate academic vocabulary.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can write a literary analysis paragraph that makes a claim, supports it with evidence, explains the reasoning, and elaborates on how literary elements build meaning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can write a literary analysis that proves a claim with evidence, reasoning, and explanation.

Ask TOR for Help: If your paragraph sounds like summary, ask TOR for help turning the summary into an analytical claim.
