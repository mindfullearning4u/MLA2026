P04 Worked Example

Study how one paragraph moves from claim to evidence to reasoning to elaboration.

Worked Example Passage

The mill's windows were dark, but one lamp burned in the office. Nora stood outside and watched the light tremble against the glass. Her father said the machines would never run again. Nora kept looking at the lamp as if it had an answer.

Example 1: Claim

The lamp symbolizes Nora's hope that the closed mill still has meaning for her future.

Example 2: Evidence and Reasoning

The evidence is that the lamp burns while the rest of the mill is dark. This supports the claim because the contrast between darkness and light makes the lamp stand out as a sign of possibility.

Example 3: Elaboration

The word tremble makes the hope feel uncertain rather than simple. Nora's attention to the lamp shows that she wants meaning from a place others have already dismissed.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The mill is closed and Nora sees a lamp. This shows that the story is about a mill.

Why it is weak: This repeats plot information and does not explain the meaning of the lamp.

Correct: The lamp symbolizes uncertain hope because its light remains visible inside a dark, closed mill.

Why it is correct: This statement makes an interpretation and connects evidence to a literary element.
