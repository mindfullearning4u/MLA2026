Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The mill is closed and Nora sees a lamp. This shows that the story is about a mill.

Why it is weak: This repeats plot information and does not explain the meaning of the lamp.

Correct: The lamp symbolizes uncertain hope because its light remains visible inside a dark, closed mill.

Why it is correct: This statement makes an interpretation and connects evidence to a literary element.
