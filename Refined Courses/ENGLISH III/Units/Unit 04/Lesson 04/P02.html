Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and definitions. Submit with Independent Work.
interpretive claim A defensible statement explaining a text’s meaning, effect, or significance.
qualification A limitation that makes a claim more accurate and defensible.
reasoning chain An ordered sequence showing how evidence and warrants lead to a conclusion.
warrant The explanation connecting evidence to a claim.
elaboration Analysis that explains significance, relationship, implication, or limitation.
sufficiency The degree to which enough evidence supports a claim.
relevance The degree to which evidence directly bears on the claim.
counterevidence Evidence that challenges or complicates an interpretation.
commentary The writer’s explanation of how evidence supports the analysis.
implication A conclusion or significance suggested by evidence and reasoning.
