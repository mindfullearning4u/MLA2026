P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Build a synthesis statement from your source notes.

How to Build a Synthesis Statement

Step 1: State the interpretation you are supporting.

Step 2: Name what the primary source shows.

Step 3: Name what the secondary source adds.

Step 4: Explain how both sources answer the research question.

Model Synthesis

The abandoned square develops the idea that public places preserve community memory. The primary source shows the fountain holding traces of hands and names, while the secondary source explains that monuments connect personal experience to community history. Together, the sources show that the fountain matters because it carries evidence of the people who once gathered there.

Notebook Task

Write your own synthesis statement using the primary and secondary source excerpts from Part 1.

Your statement must include:

  • one interpretation of the literary text,
  • one accurate paraphrase from the primary source,
  • one accurate paraphrase from the secondary source, and
  • one explanation of how the sources connect.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The fountain has names on it. Monuments can show memory.

Why it is weak: This lists two source ideas but does not explain how they work together.

Correct: The fountain's carved names support the interpretation that public places preserve community memory because the secondary source explains that monuments connect personal traces to shared history.

Why it is correct: The sentence connects both sources to one interpretation.
