Lesson Overview: Synthesizing Sources to Support Text Interpretations

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Synthesize credible source evidence with close reading to support, complicate, or qualify a literary interpretation.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.09 — Analyze source relationships; MLA.ENG3.W.07 — Conduct focused research; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Evaluate sources; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Build interpretations that place the literary text and outside sources in a reasoned relationship instead of stacking quotations.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish summary, comparison, and synthesis.
  • Identify whether a source supports, complicates, qualifies, extends, or contradicts a claim.
  • Integrate sources by idea rather than discussing them one at a time.
  • Resolve apparent agreement by examining evidence and warrants.
  • Keep close reading at the center of source-supported analysis.

Workflow and Mastery

Vocabulary → synthesis method → multiple models → worked source conversation → Independent Work → Checkpoint → five-question quiz at 80% mastery.
