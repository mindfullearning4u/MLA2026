Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and definition. Submit with Independent Work.
Term Definition
synthesis The integration of ideas and evidence into a new, coherent understanding.
source relationship The way one source supports, complicates, qualifies, extends, or contradicts another claim.
interpretive claim A defensible statement explaining meaning or effect in a literary text.
signal phrase Words that introduce a source and identify its author, role, or relationship to the discussion.
paraphrase An accurate restatement using genuinely new wording and structure.
source conversation A reasoned presentation of agreements, tensions, methods, and limitations among sources.
qualification A limitation that makes a claim more precise.
counterevidence Evidence that challenges or complicates a claim.
conceptual category An organizing idea used to group evidence from several sources.
citation trail The connected record of citations used to locate evidence and trace how claims develop.
