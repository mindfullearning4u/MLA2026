P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Source Synthesis for Literary Interpretation

Vocabulary

Synthesize: To combine ideas from more than one source and explain how they work together.

Primary source: The literary text you are interpreting.

Secondary source: A source that explains, analyzes, or gives context for the literary text.

Paraphrase: To restate a source idea accurately in your own words.

Research Question

How does the image of an abandoned town square help develop the idea that public places can preserve community memory?

Primary Source Excerpt

The dry fountain kept the shape of water. Children no longer ran through the square, but the stone rim held the polish of every hand that had rested there. At dusk, the speaker touched the carved names and heard the town answer in silence.

Secondary Source Excerpt

In literature, public monuments often represent shared memory because they connect personal experience to a larger community history. A monument can become meaningful when characters notice traces of the people who used or protected that place.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Write the research question.

Step 2: Paraphrase the primary source in one sentence.

Step 3: Paraphrase the secondary source in one sentence.

Step 4: Write one sentence explaining what idea both sources share.

Model start: Both sources connect public places to memory because...
