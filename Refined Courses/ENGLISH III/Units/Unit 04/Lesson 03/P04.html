Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The station is dusty. Transit spaces are symbols in literature.

Why it is weak: The sentences sit beside each other, but they do not explain how the source ideas connect.

Correct: The dusty station and smooth benches suggest a place shaped by absence and memory, and the research source helps explain why transit spaces can symbolize loss and connection.

Why it is correct: The sentence uses both sources to support one interpretation.
