P06 Independent Work

Practice synthesizing sources independently before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Use the short source set below. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Primary source: A speaker sees a boarded theater. The stage is dark, but the floor still shows circles where performers once stood.

Secondary source: In literature, performance spaces can symbolize the tension between public identity and private memory because they preserve traces of past voices and audiences.

Part A

Paraphrase the primary source in one sentence. Focus on what the theater image suggests, not just what is physically present.

Part B

Paraphrase the secondary source in one sentence. Focus on the idea that helps interpret the theater image.

Part C

Write a three-sentence synthesis response. Sentence 1 states an interpretation. Sentence 2 connects the primary source to that interpretation. Sentence 3 explains how the secondary source strengthens the interpretation.

Independent Work Success Checklist

Lesson focus: Synthesizing Sources to Support Text Interpretations

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.RS.01 - Conduct Literary Research

Complete the work independently, but do not guess about what to use. Any passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt needed for the task must be on this lesson page or inside the assessment question itself.

  1. Answer every part of the task in order. If there is a Part A, Part B, and Part C, each part needs its own clear response.
  2. Use evidence or details from the same page when the task requires reading, analysis, research, or revision.
  3. Explain your reasoning step by step so the reader can see how you moved from evidence to answer.
  4. Before submitting, reread for accuracy, standard alignment, complete sentences, academic vocabulary, and standard English conventions.
