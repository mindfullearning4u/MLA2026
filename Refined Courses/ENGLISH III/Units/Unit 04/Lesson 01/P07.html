P07 Checkpoint

Show mastery of creating a focused literary research question.

Checkpoint Passage

At the end of the poem, the speaker leaves a candle in an empty window. The speaker says the flame is "small enough to tremble, bright enough to accuse the dark."

Checkpoint Task

Write a focused literary research question that includes:

  • a specific textual detail,
  • an interpretive focus,
  • a clear scope, and
  • academic vocabulary from the lesson.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your P02-P03 research question chart and your independent work from P06.

Submission Workflow

Submit your final research question in the checkpoint assignment area.

Ask TOR for help before submitting if your question is broad, factual, or not connected to the passage.

Mastery Criteria

  • Question is focused and researchable.
  • Question connects to interpretation, not just summary.
  • Question names or implies a specific textual detail.
  • Question uses precise academic vocabulary.
