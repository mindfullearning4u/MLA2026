Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work.
Term Definition
literary research question A focused, open-ended question that guides investigation of a literary text through textual and relevant outside evidence.
interpretive focus The specific aspect of meaning, craft, tension, or context an analysis investigates.
inquiry A disciplined process of questioning, investigating, evaluating, and revising understanding.
scope The boundaries that determine what a research question will and will not examine.
ambiguity Language, action, or meaning that reasonably supports more than one interpretation.
tension A meaningful conflict among ideas, values, motives, evidence, or expectations.
working question A provisional research question that can be refined as evidence is gathered.
subquestion A smaller question that helps answer part of a larger research question.
contextual source A source that supplies historical, cultural, biographical, linguistic, or theoretical information relevant to interpretation.
feasibility The degree to which a question can be responsibly answered with available time, evidence, and sources.
