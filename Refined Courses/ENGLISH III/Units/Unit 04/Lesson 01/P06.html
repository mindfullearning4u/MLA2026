Independent Work Passage

The narrator describes an old classroom where every desk has a carved initial. The narrator says, "The names are small, but they have outlived the voices that first carried them."

Part A: Identify

Write one broad topic and one specific textual detail from the passage.

Part B: Interpret

Write one possible interpretation of the carved initials.

Part C: Research Question

Write one focused literary research question that connects the textual detail to your interpretation.
