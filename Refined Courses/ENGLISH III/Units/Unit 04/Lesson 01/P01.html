Lesson Overview: Literary Research Questions and Interpretive Focus

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.07 — Develop and refine focused literary research questions that guide investigation and interpretation.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Cite relevant textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.R.09 — Synthesize information across texts and sources; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Evaluate and use credible sources; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Apply academic and passage-specific vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

Lesson Focus

Transform a broad literary topic into a researchable interpretive question whose answer requires close reading, relevant outside evidence, and reasoned judgment.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish topics, factual questions, and interpretive research questions.
  • Use textual tensions, patterns, ambiguities, and context to generate inquiry.
  • Set a manageable scope and identify evidence a question requires.
  • Revise questions that are too broad, leading, factual, or unsupported.
  • Use reputable outside sources without allowing research to replace literary analysis.

Why This Matters

A strong research question does not merely collect information. It directs attention, organizes evidence, exposes assumptions, and creates space for a defensible interpretation.
