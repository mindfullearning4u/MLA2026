Lesson 1: Literary Research Questions and Interpretive Focus

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.RS.01 - Conduct Literary Research.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to turn a broad literary topic into a focused research question.
  • How to connect a research question to an interpretation of a text.
  • How to refine the scope of a question so it can be researched.
  • How academic vocabulary makes literary research more precise.

Why This Matters

Literary research begins with a question. A weak question sends you looking for random information. A strong question helps you investigate a text with purpose.

In this lesson, you will learn how to ask a question that leads to interpretation, not just summary.

What You Will Do

  • Compare broad topics with focused literary research questions.
  • Read a short literary passage and identify possible interpretations.
  • Refine a question so it connects to a specific textual problem.
  • Write a checkpoint research question with an interpretive focus.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will create a focused literary research question that is tied to an interpretation of a text and narrow enough to guide research.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to conduct literary research by asking a clear question, narrowing the question, and connecting research to interpretation of a text.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if your question is too broad, only asks for a fact, or does not connect to an interpretation of the text.
