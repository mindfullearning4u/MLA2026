Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: What is memory?

Why it is weak: This question is too broad and does not connect to a specific literary text.

Correct: How does the cracked map develop the idea that memory preserves struggle after a problem is repaired?

Why it is correct: It names a textual detail, connects to interpretation, and gives research a clear scope.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.
