P04 Worked Example

Study how to turn a broad literary topic into a focused research question.

Worked Example Passage

At the end of the story, Mara keeps the cracked map even after the road is repaired. She says, "A straight road can still forget where people struggled to walk."

Example 1: Broad Topic

The broad topic is memory. This topic is useful, but it is too large for focused research.

Example 2: Interpretive Focus

The cracked map may represent the value of remembering struggle even after a problem is repaired.

Example 3: Focused Research Question

How does the cracked map in the story develop the idea that memory preserves struggle after public problems appear solved?

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: What is memory?

Why it is weak: This question is too broad and does not connect to a specific literary text.

Correct: How does the cracked map develop the idea that memory preserves struggle after a problem is repaired?

Why it is correct: It names a textual detail, connects to interpretation, and gives research a clear scope.
