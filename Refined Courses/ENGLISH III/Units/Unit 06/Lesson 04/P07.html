P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can revise a digital presentation for the audience.

TOR

If you are choosing visuals only because they look interesting, ask TOR how each choice helps the audience understand the message.

Submission Workflow

  1. Identify one digital presentation problem.
  2. Write one audience-centered revision.
  3. Explain how the revision improves understanding.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Revise this slide plan: "Full paragraph of analysis, four decorative images, tiny source note." Write a better slide plan and explain why it improves audience experience.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your revision reduces audience confusion.
  • Your slide plan supports the message.
  • Your visual choice has a clear purpose.
  • Your explanation connects design to audience experience.
