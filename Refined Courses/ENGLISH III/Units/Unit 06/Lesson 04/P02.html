Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Verify two with a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title. Submit these notes with Independent Work.

audience pathway The intended sequence through which users encounter and understand content.
multimodal Combining two or more modes such as text, image, audio, video, or interaction.
visual hierarchy Arrangement that signals importance and reading order.
cognitive load The amount of information working memory must process at one time.
accessibility Design that enables people with varied abilities and technologies to use content.
alternative text Concise written description conveying an image’s relevant meaning.
caption Synchronized text representing speech and meaningful sound.
contrast Difference in color, value, size, or form that supports visibility and emphasis.
source attribution Clear credit identifying where information or media originated.
usability test Observation of representative users completing tasks to reveal design problems.
