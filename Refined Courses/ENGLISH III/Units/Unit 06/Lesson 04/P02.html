P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Designing for Audience Experience

Vocabulary

Audience experience is how easy it is for viewers to follow and understand a presentation. Visual support is an image, chart, layout, or design choice that helps meaning. Cognitive load is the amount of information the audience must process at once.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Start with the audience's need. Ask what the audience must understand.

Step 2: Use slide text as support, not a script. The audience should not read a full essay on screen.

Step 3: Choose visuals that clarify the point.

Step 4: Remove design choices that compete with the message.

Notebook Task

Write three audience needs for a digital presentation about literary research. Then write one slide-design choice that would support each need.
