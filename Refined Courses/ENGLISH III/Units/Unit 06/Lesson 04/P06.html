Checkpoint: Redesign a Digital Poetry Walk

Scenario

A mobile poetry walk places poems, recordings, archival photographs, and historical notes at six park locations. The prototype uses tiny text, location-only instructions, unreviewed captions, decorative animations, and links labeled “click here.”

Questions

  1. State purpose and audience.
  2. Define prototype and verify it with a reputable source.
  3. Design a five-step pathway.
  4. Assign each mode a distinct job.
  5. Revise two link labels.
  6. Plan captions and transcripts.
  7. Write alternative text for one photograph.
  8. Replace location-only instructions with an accessible alternative.
  9. Explain how to reduce cognitive load.
  10. Write a 140–180 word design rationale.
  11. Create three usability tasks.
  12. Complete an accessibility and source-integrity check.
