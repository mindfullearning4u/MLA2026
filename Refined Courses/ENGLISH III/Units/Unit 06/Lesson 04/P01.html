Lesson Overview: Digital Presentation and Audience Experience

Standards and Goals

Primary: MLA.ENG3.SL.05 — use digital media strategically to clarify information and strengthen audience understanding. Support: MLA.ENG3.SL.01–04 and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

  • Design a coherent audience pathway.
  • Match visuals, audio, text, and interaction to purpose.
  • Apply accessibility, source integrity, and ethical design.
  • Test the experience rather than assuming it works.

Workflow and Mastery

Study vocabulary, direct teaching, and the worked model; complete the substantial Independent Work and separate Checkpoint. The lesson quiz draws five questions from 25, allows one attempt, and requires 80%. Below-mastery submissions require TOR feedback, correction, and resubmission.
