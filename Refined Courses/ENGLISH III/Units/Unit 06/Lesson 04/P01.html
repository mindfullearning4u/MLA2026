P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Digital Presentation and Audience Experience

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.02 - Create digital presentations to improve the experience of the audience.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.C.01, MLA.ENG3.W.03, MLA.ENG3.W.04

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to design a digital presentation that helps the audience understand the message, follow the organization, and stay focused on the content.

Why This Matters

Digital slides, images, and layout choices should make a presentation easier to understand. Visuals should guide the audience, not distract from the speaker's message.

What You Will Do

  • Identify audience needs in a digital presentation.
  • Choose slide text that supports oral delivery.
  • Use visuals to clarify complex analysis.
  • Revise design choices that distract or overload the audience.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can choose digital presentation features that improve audience understanding and revise features that make the presentation harder to follow.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can design digital presentation choices around the audience's experience.

Ask TOR for Help: If a slide looks interesting but does not help the audience understand, ask TOR how to revise it for purpose.
