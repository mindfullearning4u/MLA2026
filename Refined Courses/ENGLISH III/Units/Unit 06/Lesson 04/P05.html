Independent Work: Redesign “Voices from the Market Hall”

Source Scenario

A digital exhibit about a century-old market includes a six-minute video, 28 photographs, vendor interviews, price ledgers, and a floor plan. The current page autoplays video, shows all photographs at once, provides no captions or transcripts, labels a rising price line “proof of prosperity,” and hides source credits in a downloadable file.

Questions 1–18

  1. State the exhibit’s likely purpose and audiences.
  2. Define prosperity in context and verify it with a reputable source.
  3. Explain why the chart label overclaims.
  4. Identify four source-integrity concerns.
  5. Design a six-step audience pathway.
  6. Assign a specific job to video, photographs, interviews, ledgers, and floor plan.
  7. Choose what belongs on the opening screen.
  8. Write a descriptive heading and 50-word overview.
  9. Plan captions and transcripts.
  10. Write alternative text for the floor plan.
  11. Explain a high-contrast, non-color-only visual system.
  12. Revise the chart title and source note.
  13. Plan keyboard navigation.
  14. Identify two privacy or permission checks.
  15. Create three usability tasks.
  16. Write a 220–280 word design rationale.
  17. Create a revision log with six decisions.
  18. Complete an accessibility, attribution, usability, and final-publication check.
