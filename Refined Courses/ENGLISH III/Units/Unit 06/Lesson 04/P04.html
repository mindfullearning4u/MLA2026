P04 Worked Example

Revise a digital presentation for audience experience.

Example 1

Problem: A slide has seven bullet points with full sentences.

Revision: Use three short key phrases and explain the details aloud.

Why it works: The audience can listen instead of reading a full script.

Example 2

Problem: An image is decorative but unrelated to the claim.

Revision: Replace it with a simple visual that shows the relationship between claim, evidence, and explanation.

Example 3

Problem: The slide title says "Stuff" and does not preview the point.

Revision: Use the title "Research Evidence Supports the Interpretation."

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Add more colors, fonts, and images just to make the slide look busy.

Busy design can make the audience experience worse if it distracts from the message.

Correct: Use design choices that clarify the claim, guide attention, and support listening.

Audience-centered design makes content easier to understand.
