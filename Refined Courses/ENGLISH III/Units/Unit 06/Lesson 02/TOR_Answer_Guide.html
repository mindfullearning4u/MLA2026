TOR ONLY — U6 L2 Answer Guide

Rhetorical Devices in Oral Presentation

Independent Work

Strong responses qualify every source, define metadata as descriptive information about a record, and revise the opening so rhetoric strengthens rather than replaces evidence. Accept varied devices when students accurately name function, limitations, and ethical use. The 200–260 word speech must integrate three sources, three purposeful devices, two limitations, delivery cues, and a source-integrity check.

Checkpoint

Strong responses support a limited night-market pilot, qualify the four-day pedestrian count and voluntary business survey, define pilot as a temporary test, and use anaphora, antithesis, analogy, and kairos without false urgency. Require the 130–170 word excerpt, annotations, delivery cues, and ethical check.

Mastery

Require 80%. Use specific TOR feedback for correction and resubmission.
