Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Verify at least two terms with a reputable online dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title. Submit these notes with the Independent Work.

Term Definition
rhetorical device A purposeful language or structural technique used to influence meaning, emphasis, or audience response.
anaphora Repetition of a word or phrase at the beginning of successive clauses or sentences.
antithesis Placement of contrasting ideas in balanced or parallel structures.
parallelism Use of similar grammatical patterns to connect or emphasize related ideas.
rhetorical question A question posed primarily to prompt thought or create emphasis rather than obtain an answer.
analogy A comparison used to explain an unfamiliar idea through a more familiar relationship.
allusion A brief reference to a known text, event, person, or cultural idea.
tricolon A sequence of three parallel words, phrases, or clauses.
kairos The strategic use of timing and circumstances to make a message especially relevant.
ethical appeal A rhetorical appeal grounded in the speaker’s credibility, fairness, knowledge, or shared values.
