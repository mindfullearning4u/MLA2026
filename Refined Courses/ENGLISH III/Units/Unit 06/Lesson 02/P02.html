Notebook title: Rhetorical Devices for Speaking

Vocabulary

Rhetorical device is a language move used to affect an audience. Repetition repeats key words for emphasis. Parallel structure repeats a grammatical pattern. Rhetorical question asks a question to make listeners think rather than to get an answer. Contrast shows a difference between ideas.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Start with the presentation focus. A rhetorical device should support that focus.

Step 2: Decide what the audience needs: emphasis, clarity, comparison, or reflection.

Step 3: Choose the device that meets that need.

Step 4: Keep the device brief. In oral presentation, a device should help listeners, not distract them.

Notebook Task

Write one example of repetition, one example of parallel structure, and one rhetorical question that could fit a presentation about credible evidence.
