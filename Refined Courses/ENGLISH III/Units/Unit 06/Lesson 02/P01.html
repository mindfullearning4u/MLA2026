Lesson Overview: Rhetorical Devices in Oral Presentation

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.ENG3.SL.03 — analyze and use rhetorical devices strategically in oral communication.

Supporting standards: MLA.ENG3.SL.01 — organize focused presentations with credible evidence; MLA.ENG3.SL.02 — adapt content and delivery to audience and purpose; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — use precise, purposeful language.

What You Will Learn

  • Analyze how rhetorical devices shape emphasis, memory, emotion, credibility, and reasoning.
  • Select devices that serve a presentation’s claim instead of decorating it.
  • Balance rhetorical force with accurate evidence and ethical representation.
  • Plan vocal delivery so syntax and sound produce their intended effect.

Why This Matters

Rhetoric can clarify important reasoning or conceal weak evidence. Mature speakers recognize both possibilities and use memorable language without manipulating listeners or overstating what sources prove.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record and verify vocabulary.
  2. Study rhetorical functions through literary, civic, scientific, and historical examples.
  3. Follow the complete modeled speech revision.
  4. Complete the substantial Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary.
  5. Complete the separate Checkpoint.
  6. Complete the five-question random quiz from a 25-question bank; one attempt, 80% required.

Mastery

You will explain the effect and ethics of rhetorical choices, revise evidence-based speech passages, and justify delivery decisions. Work below 80% must be corrected using TOR feedback and resubmitted.
