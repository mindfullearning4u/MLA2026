P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Rhetorical Devices in Oral Presentation

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present information orally, with a logical organization, coherent focus, and credible evidence, while employing effective rhetorical devices where appropriate.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.12, MLA.ENG3.R.08, MLA.ENG3.V.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to use rhetorical devices in an oral presentation to clarify meaning, emphasize important ideas, and support audience understanding.

Why This Matters

Rhetorical devices help a speaker guide listeners. Used carefully, repetition, parallel structure, rhetorical questions, and contrast can make a presentation clearer and more memorable.

What You Will Do

  • Identify rhetorical devices that fit oral delivery.
  • Explain how a rhetorical choice affects listeners.
  • Choose devices that support a presentation focus.
  • Avoid devices that distract from credible evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can choose and explain rhetorical devices that strengthen an oral presentation without weakening the evidence or focus.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can use rhetorical devices to make an oral presentation clearer and stronger.

Ask TOR for Help: If a device sounds dramatic but does not support your focus, ask TOR whether it helps the audience understand.
