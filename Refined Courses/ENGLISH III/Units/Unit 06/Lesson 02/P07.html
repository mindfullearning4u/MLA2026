P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can use a rhetorical device purposefully.

TOR

If your rhetorical device sounds dramatic but does not support your focus, ask TOR to help revise it for audience understanding.

Submission Workflow

  1. Write one oral presentation line using a rhetorical device.
  2. Name the device.
  3. Explain how it helps the audience understand your focus.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Create one presentation line about why evidence matters in communication. Use one rhetorical device and explain why that device fits the audience and focus.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your line uses a clear rhetorical device.
  • The device supports the presentation focus.
  • Your explanation names the effect on the audience.
  • Your wording is appropriate for oral delivery.
