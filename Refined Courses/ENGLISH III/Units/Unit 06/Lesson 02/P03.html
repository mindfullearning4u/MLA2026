P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Match rhetorical devices to audience needs.

Presentation Situation

A student is presenting about why writers should revise before publishing. The audience already knows what revision is, but they need to understand why revision matters.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: The audience needs emphasis, so repetition may help.

Step 2: The audience needs a memorable structure, so parallel structure may help.

Step 3: A rhetorical question may help the audience reflect on their own writing habits.

Step 4: Each device must still connect to credible evidence and the presentation focus.

Notebook Task

Write three presentation lines for the situation: one using repetition, one using parallel structure, and one using a rhetorical question. Then write which one is strongest and why.
