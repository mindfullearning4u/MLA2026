P04 Worked Example

Organize an oral presentation plan.

Example 1

Topic: credible research in presentations

Focus: Credible research makes an oral presentation more trustworthy.

Why it works: The focus is narrow and can guide every point.

Example 2

Point: Listeners need evidence they can understand.

Evidence paraphrase: A source about revision explains that specific feedback helps writers clarify ideas.

Why it works: The evidence is paraphrased so it sounds natural in oral delivery.

Example 3

Logical order: focus, problem, evidence, explanation, closing.

This order helps listeners know the point before they hear the proof.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: I will talk about revision, vocabulary, research, school schedules, and my favorite book.

This plan has too many unrelated topics, so the audience cannot follow one focus.

Correct: I will explain how credible evidence helps listeners trust an oral presentation.

This focus is narrow, clear, and presentation-ready.
