Lesson Overview: Oral Communication Organization, Focus, and Credible Evidence

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.ENG3.SL.01 — organize and deliver focused oral communication supported by credible, relevant evidence.

Supporting standards: MLA.ENG3.SL.02 — adapt content and delivery to purpose and audience; MLA.ENG3.R.10 — evaluate evidence and reasoning; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — use precise standard English appropriate to the situation.

What You Will Learn

  • Develop a precise controlling idea for a speech or presentation.
  • Organize evidence so listeners can follow the reasoning without rereading.
  • Evaluate source credibility, represent evidence accurately, and distinguish fact from inference.
  • Use transitions, emphasis, pacing, and concise language to sustain focus.

Why This Matters

Listeners cannot pause and reconstruct a disorganized argument as easily as readers can. Effective speakers make the structure audible, select evidence responsibly, and show exactly how each detail advances the purpose.
