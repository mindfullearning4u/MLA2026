Checkpoint: “Should the Museum Add a Weekly Evening?”

Directions

Use the source set to answer all 12 questions and prepare a concise presentation plan. Submit these responses only in the separate Checkpoint Submission.

Source Set

Attendance record: During four special evening events last year, the museum averaged 146 visitors, compared with 72 visitors during the final three daytime hours on comparable Saturdays. The events included free admission and performances, so the record cannot isolate the effect of evening hours.

Budget estimate: The finance director estimates that one weekly evening would cost $27,000 annually for staffing, utilities, and security. The estimate assumes current wage rates and does not include possible sponsorships.

Visitor survey: Of 310 visitors who completed an optional survey, 61% said evening hours would make a future visit more likely. Because respondents were already museum visitors, the survey may not represent residents who never attend.

Education coordinator: The coordinator reports that teachers have requested evening family workshops but recommends a four-month pilot before a permanent schedule.

Analysis and Speaking Plan

  1. State a focused controlling idea for a three-minute recommendation.
  2. Identify the strongest evidence for demand and one important limitation.
  3. Using context, define isolate as used in the attendance record. Verify the definition with a reputable online dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title.
  4. Explain why the 61% survey result cannot represent all residents.
  5. Write an accurate oral citation for the budget estimate.
  6. Explain how the budget evidence is relevant to the decision.
  7. Write one concession and a reasoned response.
  8. Create a three-part sequence of reasons and write a signpost for each transition.
  9. Write a 120–160 word speaking excerpt that integrates two sources without overclaiming.
  10. Add four delivery cues.
  11. Describe one appropriate visual and one visual that would mislead listeners.
  12. Complete a final credibility and source-integrity check.
