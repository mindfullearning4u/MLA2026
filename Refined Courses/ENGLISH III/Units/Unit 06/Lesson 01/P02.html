P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Organizing an Oral Presentation

Vocabulary

Focus is the main message of a presentation. Logical organization is an order that helps listeners follow the speaker. Credible evidence comes from trustworthy information. Paraphrase means restating information in your own words while keeping the meaning.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Begin with one focus sentence. This tells the audience what the presentation is about.

Step 2: Choose two or three main points. Too many points can make listeners lose the focus.

Step 3: Place points in an order that makes sense, such as problem to solution or cause to effect.

Step 4: Match each point with credible evidence and a short explanation.

Notebook Task

Plan a short oral presentation about why revision improves writing. Write one focus sentence, two main points, and one piece of credible evidence for each point.
