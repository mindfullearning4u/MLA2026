Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Verify at least two terms with a reputable online dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title. Submit these notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
controlling idea The central claim or purpose that guides every part of an oral presentation.
audience analysis The process of considering listeners’ knowledge, needs, expectations, and possible questions.
signposting Words or phrases that make a presentation’s structure and movement clear to listeners.
credible evidence Information from a trustworthy, relevant source that can reasonably support a claim.
source qualification A brief explanation of who or what a source is and why it is trustworthy for the topic.
oral citation Spoken attribution that identifies the source of information during a presentation.
relevance The direct connection between evidence and the claim or purpose it supports.
concession Acknowledgment of a valid limitation, concern, or opposing point.
emphasis Deliberate stress placed on an idea through wording, repetition, pacing, volume, or position.
delivery cue A note in a speaking plan that directs a pause, change in pace, visual reference, or vocal emphasis.
