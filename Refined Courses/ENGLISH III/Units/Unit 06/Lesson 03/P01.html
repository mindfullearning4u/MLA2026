📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

Presenting Literary Research and Complex Analysis

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present information orally, with a logical organization, coherent focus, and credible evidence, while employing effective rhetorical devices where appropriate.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.RS.01, MLA.ENG3.W.02, MLA.ENG3.W.03

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to turn literary research and complex analysis into an organized oral presentation with a clear claim and credible evidence.

Why This Matters

Research becomes more useful when you can explain it clearly to listeners. A strong presentation helps an audience understand your interpretation, your sources, and your reasoning.

What You Will Do

  • State a literary research claim for oral presentation.
  • Select source evidence that supports the claim.
  • Explain literary analysis in listener-friendly language.
  • Organize the presentation so research and analysis work together.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can present a literary interpretation with a clear focus, credible research support, and explanation that listeners can follow.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can present literary research and analysis clearly to an audience.

Ask TOR for Help: If your research evidence is too long to say aloud, ask TOR to help paraphrase it accurately.
