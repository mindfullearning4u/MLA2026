Lesson Overview: Presenting Literary Research and Complex Analysis

Standards and Focus

Primary: MLA.ENG3.SL.04 — present literary research and complex analysis clearly, logically, and with appropriate evidence. Support: MLA.ENG3.R.10, MLA.ENG3.W.08, MLA.ENG3.SL.01, and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

Learning Goals

  • Transform a research-based literary interpretation into an audible argument.
  • Synthesize primary and secondary sources without source-by-source reporting.
  • Explain quotations, qualify scholarly claims, and distinguish interpretation from fact.
  • Design readable visuals and delivery cues that clarify complex reasoning.

Workflow and Mastery

Record vocabulary, study multiple models, follow the worked example, complete the 18-question Independent Work and separate 12-question Checkpoint, then take a five-question random quiz from a 25-question bank. One attempt and 80% are required; below-mastery submissions must be corrected using TOR feedback.
