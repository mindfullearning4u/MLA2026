Checkpoint Submission – Requirements

Complete the Lesson Checkpoint for this lesson.

📌 This submission is for your checkpoint responses only.

Notebook pages, lesson notes, and Independent Work should be submitted in the separate Notebook Evidence Submission activity.

What to Include

At the top of your work:

  • Student Name
  • Course Name
  • Unit and Lesson

Example:
John Smith
U.S. Government – Unit 1, Lesson 1

Checkpoint Response Requirements

Your checkpoint submission must include:

  • Responses to all checkpoint questions
  • Complete sentences
  • Clear explanation of your thinking
  • Use of lesson vocabulary when appropriate
  • Evidence that you understand and can apply the lesson concept

Submission Format

  • Typed responses only
  • PDF (preferred)
  • Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx)
  • Typed text submitted directly in the assignment text box (if enabled)

If using Google Docs, download and submit as a PDF or Word file. Links are not accepted.

Page Requirements

If your submission has more than one page:

  • Number each page
  • Submit all pages together and in order

Final Reminder

All work must be typed, clear, readable, and complete. If you have questions or need help, contact your TOR before submitting.
