Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Verify at least two with a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title. Submit these notes with Independent Work.

Term Definition
interpretive claim A defensible statement explaining the meaning or significance of a literary text.
research synthesis Integration of ideas from multiple sources into one coherent line of reasoning.
primary text The literary work or original document being analyzed.
secondary source A later work that interprets, explains, or contextualizes a primary text.
scholarly conversation The ongoing exchange of interpretations and evidence among researchers.
source framing Language that introduces a source, its perspective, and its relevance.
quotation analysis Explanation of how specific quoted language supports an interpretation.
counterinterpretation A plausible alternative reading of a text.
visual hierarchy Arrangement that guides viewers from the most important information to supporting detail.
speaker notes Concise prompts for evidence, transitions, citations, and delivery rather than a full script.
