📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 03

P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can plan a literary research presentation.

TOR

If your presentation plan is just a list of sources, ask TOR to help connect each source to your literary interpretation.

Submission Workflow

  1. Write your claim.
  2. Add primary-text evidence and research support.
  3. Write a short oral explanation.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Create a presentation plan with four parts: literary claim, primary evidence, research support, and oral explanation. Keep each part focused and listener-friendly.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your claim is interpretive and focused.
  • Your evidence supports the claim.
  • Your research support is paraphrased accurately.
  • Your explanation is clear for listeners.
