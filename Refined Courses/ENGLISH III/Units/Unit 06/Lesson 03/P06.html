P06 Independent Work

Plan a short literary research presentation.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Claim

Write one literary research claim that could be presented orally.

Part B: Evidence

List one primary-text detail and one research-source idea that support the claim.

Part C: Oral Explanation
