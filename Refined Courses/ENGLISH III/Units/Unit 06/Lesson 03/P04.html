Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Read a long research quote aloud without explaining it.

Listeners need a clear paraphrase and explanation, not a long copied passage.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.

Correct: Paraphrase the source and explain how it supports your interpretation.

This keeps the presentation focused and listener-friendly.
