Lesson 8: Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7. You will use the unit standards together in one connected synthesis process.

Standards Covered

MLA.ENG3.C.01, MLA.ENG3.C.02, MLA.ENG3.C.03

What You Will Learn

You will learn how oral communication, digital presentation, multimedia, credible evidence, revision, and publication work together.

What You Will Do

You will plan a communication product for an audience, choose evidence, improve multimedia support, and prepare publication-ready writing.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining how a complete communication product should be organized, revised, presented, and published.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student terms: you are making sure your message works for real listeners, viewers, and readers.
