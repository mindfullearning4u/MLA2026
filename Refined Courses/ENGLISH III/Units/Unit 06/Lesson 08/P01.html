Putting It All Together: Unit 6 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 6 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG3.C.01, MLA.ENG3.C.02, MLA.ENG3.C.03

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.RS.01, MLA.ENG3.W.04, MLA.ENG3.V.01, MLA.ENG3.L.01

Lesson 8 Note: This lesson reviews and synthesizes standards already taught in Lessons 1-7. No new primary standard is introduced.

What You Will Review

You will review how oral communication, digital presentation choices, multimedia, research, revision, and publication work together in a complete communication product.

Why This Matters

The end of the unit asks you to combine skills instead of using them separately. Strong communication requires clear speaking or presentation choices, strong writing, purposeful media, and audience awareness.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete review tasks and then take the Unit 6 Assessment. The assessment checks your ability to apply the communication standards taught across the unit.

Ask TOR for Help: Ask TOR before the assessment if you need help reviewing the difference between oral communication, digital presentation, multimedia support, and publication readiness.
