Lesson Overview: Unit 6 Synthesis

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.ENG3.C.03 — Create and deliver a coherent multimedia communication for a defined audience and purpose.

Support standards: MLA.ENG3.R.11 — synthesize evidence across complex sources; MLA.ENG3.W.07 — produce and revise digital writing; MLA.ENG3.SL.04 — present claims with credible evidence and purposeful media; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — apply Standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Synthesize claims and evidence across print, audio, visual, and digital sources.
  • Design a coherent publication package for a specific audience and purpose.
  • Audit accuracy, accessibility, ethics, permissions, usability, and editorial quality.
  • Defend final decisions in a concise reflective rationale.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Study the integrated workflow and diverse examples on Page 3.
  3. Follow the fully modeled reasoning on Page 4.
  4. Complete the 18-question Independent Work on Page 5.
  5. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint on Page 6.
  6. Submit Notebook Evidence and Checkpoint work separately. Correct and resubmit work below 80% using TOR feedback.

How You Will Show Mastery

Create evidence-based, accessible, ethical, publication-ready communication whose content, design, and media choices reinforce one another. Lesson 8 has no separate quiz; the Unit 6 Final Assessment draws 10 random questions from a 40-question bank, permits one attempt, and requires 80%.
