P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 6 Oral, Digital, and Publication Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Audience experience: how listeners or viewers understand and respond to a presentation.
  • Multimedia: audio, visual, or digital elements used to support meaning.
  • Publication-ready: revised and polished for an intended audience.

Synthesis Teaching

Communication synthesis means every part of the product supports the same purpose. The spoken claim, evidence, slide design, multimedia element, and published summary should work together instead of competing for attention.

Notebook Task

Create a communication plan with four sections: oral claim, credible evidence, multimedia support, and publication revision. Explain how each section supports the same audience purpose.
