Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these vocabulary notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
communication brief A concise statement of a project’s purpose, audience, central claim, required evidence, format, and constraints.
synthesis The integration of ideas or evidence from multiple sources into a new, coherent understanding.
publication package The complete set of content, media, credits, accessibility supports, and release documentation prepared for an audience.
editorial standard An agreed requirement for accuracy, clarity, organization, style, mechanics, and citation.
multimedia coherence The degree to which words, images, audio, video, and design work together toward one purpose.
accessibility conformance Documented evidence that content follows required practices for captions, transcripts, alt text, contrast, navigation, and readable structure.
source integrity The accurate representation, citation, context, and verification of information from sources.
usability evidence Observations or test results showing how effectively an intended audience can navigate and understand a product.
release documentation The change log, permissions, credits, approvals, version information, and archive record accompanying publication.
reflective rationale A reasoned explanation connecting final choices and revisions to purpose, audience, evidence, ethics, and quality standards.
