📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 08

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Connect Unit 6 skills to one final communication situation.

Review Situation

A student must share a research-based literary claim with classmates. The student will speak briefly, show a digital slide, and publish a short written summary to a class LAN page.

Review Process

Step 1: The spoken part needs a focused claim, credible evidence, and clear organization.

Step 2: The digital slide needs multimedia that helps the audience understand the claim.

Step 3: The published summary needs revised writing, source responsibility, and audience-aware formatting.

Step 4: All parts should support the same purpose.

Notebook Task

Write three sentences in your notebook.

Sentence 1: Explain what the speaker should do to communicate clearly.

Sentence 2: Explain what the digital slide should do for the audience.

Sentence 3: Explain what the published summary should include before it is ready to share.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving a broad answer without identifying the exact evidence, rhetorical move, literary choice, or author purpose.

Correct: Name the specific evidence or choice and explain how it supports meaning, argument, style, or reader effect.

Why this matters: English III mastery requires clear reasoning. The reader must see how your evidence proves the claim.
