Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Add a dramatic video because it looks impressive, even if it does not connect to the claim.

Impressive media can still distract the audience if it does not support understanding.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.

Correct: Choose a multimedia element that clarifies the claim or evidence for the audience.

Purposeful multimedia improves audience experience.
