Worked Example: Building a Coherent Multimedia Package

Scenario and Source Set

A student documentary page argues that the closing of the Moonlight Theater changed downtown social life, but community-led restoration created a different kind of gathering place. The source set includes attendance ledgers, a 1968 photograph, a 1997 demolition proposal, interviews with a former projectionist and a youth actor, present-day video, and a chart of event attendance after reopening.

Step 1 — Map Claims to Media

  • Historical importance: attendance ledger plus the 1968 photograph.
  • Threat of loss: demolition proposal and a brief explanatory quotation.
  • Change in community function: paired interviews and current program data.
  • Renewed participation: attendance chart with exact totals and source note.

No medium is assigned merely to create mood.

Step 2 — Evaluate Competing Choices

Choice A: use nostalgic music under the demolition section. Problem: it directs emotion and may substitute sentiment for evidence.

Choice B: use three seconds of room tone before the former projectionist speaks. Strength: it establishes place without interfering with the testimony.

Decision: choose B, add a transcript note, and allow users to pause audio.

Step 3 — Prevent Distortion

The first chart begins its vertical axis at 900, making an increase from 1,020 to 1,180 appear enormous. The team resets the axis to zero, labels years and units, and adds a sentence explaining that participation rose gradually. The 1968 photograph is uncropped so the half-empty neighboring storefronts remain visible.

Step 4 — Integrate Explanation

The page does not let media “speak for itself.” Before the chart, the narration asks whether restoration returned the theater to its former role. After the chart and youth interview, it explains that the building again gathers people, but now for performances, classes, and civic events rather than film exhibition alone.

Step 5 — Accessibility, Rights, and Fallback

The team supplies captions, transcripts, descriptive alt text, keyboard-operable controls, a high-contrast chart, and a text-only source summary. It verifies archival permission, credits the photographer, records the interview releases, and provides a still image plus transcript if video cannot load.

Final Reasoning

The finished package is coherent because each medium contributes a distinct kind of evidence, every element is interpreted in context, and the combination supports a qualified claim about change rather than a simplistic “decline and return” story.
