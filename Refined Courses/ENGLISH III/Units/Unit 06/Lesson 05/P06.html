Checkpoint: Multimedia for a Community Garden Report

Scenario and Questions

A report uses a before-and-after photo pair, harvest chart, volunteer audio, and irrigation video. The draft lacks consistent scale, chart units, transcript, alternative text, and source labels.

  1. State claim and audience.
  2. Define irrigation and verify it.
  3. Assign each medium a function.
  4. Identify one limitation per medium.
  5. Repair the photo comparison.
  6. Repair the chart.
  7. Plan captions and transcript.
  8. Write alternative text.
  9. Plan attribution and permissions.
  10. Write a 140–180 word rationale.
  11. Create three usability tasks.
  12. Complete a final ethics and accessibility check.
