P06 Independent Work

Plan one multimedia element for a presentation.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Purpose

Write the claim or evidence your multimedia element needs to support.

Part B: Multimedia Choice

Choose one multimedia element and explain why it helps the audience understand.

Part C: Caption and Source Note

Write a caption and a source note that use clear standard English.
