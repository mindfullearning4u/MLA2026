P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Purposeful Multimedia

Vocabulary

Multimedia combines modes such as text, image, audio, video, chart, or animation. Caption is short explanatory text for a visual. Source note identifies where information or media came from. Relevance means the element directly supports the message.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify what the audience needs to understand.

Step 2: Choose a multimedia element that makes that idea clearer.

Step 3: Add a caption or label so the audience knows what to notice.

Step 4: Include a readable source note when the element uses outside information.

Notebook Task

Choose one presentation claim. Then list one image, one chart, and one caption that could help the audience understand the claim.

Teacher-Guided Thinking Check

Before you answer independently, identify the skill, locate evidence, and explain the connection in your own words. If the response feels too general, return to the vocabulary and choose a more precise term.
