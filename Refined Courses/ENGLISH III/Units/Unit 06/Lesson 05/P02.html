Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Verify two using a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title.

multimedia element A component using image, audio, video, animation, data, or interaction.
explanatory function The specific job a media element performs in helping an audience understand.
data visualization A graphical representation of quantitative or categorical information.
annotation A label or note added to direct attention or explain evidence.
cropping Removing outer portions of an image, which may clarify focus or alter context.
audio cue A meaningful sound that signals information, transition, or emphasis.
transcript A written record of spoken and meaningful audio content.
media literacy Ability to analyze how media are created, sourced, framed, and interpreted.
licensing Rules governing permission to use, adapt, or share media.
redundancy Repetition that adds no new meaning or unnecessarily increases processing.
