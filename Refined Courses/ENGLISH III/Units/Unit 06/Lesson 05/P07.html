📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 05

P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can choose multimedia for audience understanding.

TOR

If your media choice is decorative but not explanatory, ask TOR to help connect the media to your evidence or claim.

Submission Workflow

  1. Choose one multimedia element.
  2. Write its purpose.
  3. Add a caption and source note.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Task

Plan one multimedia element for a digital presentation. Include the claim it supports, the media type, a caption, a source note, and a two-sentence explanation of how it improves audience understanding.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your multimedia choice supports the claim or evidence.
  • Your caption guides audience attention.
  • Your source note is clear and readable.
  • Your explanation connects multimedia to audience understanding.
