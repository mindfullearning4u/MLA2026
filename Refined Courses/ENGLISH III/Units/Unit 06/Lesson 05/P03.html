P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Evaluate multimedia for audience understanding.

Presentation Situation

A student is presenting research about how repeated images in a text develop the theme of isolation. The student wants to add a multimedia element.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: A decorative image of any closed door may be related, but it may not teach the audience much.

Step 2: A simple diagram showing repeated door images across three scenes may clarify the pattern.

Step 3: A caption should explain the connection to theme.

Step 4: The source note should be readable if the image or chart uses outside material.

Notebook Task

Write which multimedia element would best support the situation: decorative image, pattern diagram, audio clip, or unrelated video. Explain your choice in two sentences.
