Lesson: Selecting and Integrating Multimedia Evidence

The Function Test

Before adding any medium, complete this sentence: “This element helps the audience ___ by ___.” Strong functions include demonstrating change, clarifying scale, comparing patterns, preserving a speaker’s tone, documenting a place, or showing a process. If the sentence ends with “because it looks interesting,” the element is probably decorative.

Example 1 — Audio and Credibility

A podcast about school desegregation uses a witness’s recorded pause before describing her first day. Audio preserves tone and hesitation that a transcript alone cannot. The transcript remains necessary for access and precise quotation. The narrator identifies the interview date and distinguishes memory from archival fact.

Example 2 — Data Visualization and Scale

An article states that tree cover differs across neighborhoods. A proportional map shows where the difference occurs; a small comparison table supplies exact percentages. The map reveals spatial pattern, while the table prevents viewers from estimating values from color alone.

Example 3 — Image Sequence and Change

Three dated photographs of a theater show renovation over time. Cropping them differently creates a false impression of scale, so the editor aligns viewpoints, labels dates and sources, and explains what falls outside each frame.

Example 4 — When to Remove Media

A looping drone shot of a wetland adds atmosphere but provides no evidence for the claim about water quality. It also consumes bandwidth and distracts from the data. The editor removes it and uses a labeled diagram of runoff pathways instead.

Integration Checklist

  • What claim does the element support?
  • What can this medium communicate better than prose?
  • Is the element accurately framed, labeled, credited, and permitted?
  • Does the surrounding explanation tell the audience how to interpret it?
  • Are captions, transcripts, alt text, controls, and fallback content complete?
  • Could editing, cropping, scale, music, or sequencing distort meaning?
