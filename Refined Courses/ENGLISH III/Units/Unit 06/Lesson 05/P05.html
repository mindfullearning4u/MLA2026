Independent Work: Build “The Night Train Record”

Source Set

A presentation about a discontinued night train includes a route map, ridership chart, conductor interview, station photographs, and arrival audio. The current draft uses an unlabeled map, a truncated chart, a heavily cropped empty-platform image, music over the interview, and no transcript or permissions information.

Questions 1–18

  1. State the claim and audiences.
  2. Define truncated in context and verify it.
  3. Assign each medium an explanatory function.
  4. Identify two claims each medium cannot prove.
  5. Repair the map labels and source note.
  6. Repair the chart scale, units, dates, and title.
  7. Explain how cropping may distort the photograph.
  8. Write alternative text.
  9. Plan interview captions and transcript.
  10. Evaluate the background music ethically.
  11. Plan permissions and attribution.
  12. Write three annotations.
  13. Reduce redundancy across narration and slides.
  14. Create a seven-step media sequence.
  15. Write two transitions between modes.
  16. Write a 220–280 word multimedia rationale.
  17. Create four usability tasks.
  18. Complete an accessibility, source-integrity, and licensing check.
