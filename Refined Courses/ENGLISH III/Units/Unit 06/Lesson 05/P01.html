Lesson Overview: Multimedia Elements for Audience Understanding

Standards and Goals

Primary: MLA.ENG3.SL.05 — strategically integrate multimedia to clarify claims and strengthen audience understanding. Support: MLA.ENG3.SL.01–04, MLA.ENG3.R.10, and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

  • Match media to a specific explanatory function.
  • Interpret charts, images, audio, and video as evidence.
  • Avoid redundancy, distortion, and emotional manipulation.
  • Apply accessibility, attribution, permission, and testing.

Workflow and Mastery

Complete vocabulary, direct teaching, worked model, 18-question Independent Work, and separate 12-question Checkpoint. The quiz draws five of 25 questions, allows one attempt, and requires 80%. Below-mastery submissions require TOR feedback, correction, and resubmission.
