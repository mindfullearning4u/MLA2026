📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 05

Multimedia Elements for Audience Understanding

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.02 - Create digital presentations to improve the experience of the audience.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.RS.01, MLA.ENG3.V.01, MLA.ENG3.L.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to choose multimedia elements that clarify research, strengthen audience understanding, and support the purpose of a digital presentation.

Why This Matters

Multimedia should not be decoration. Images, charts, audio, and video should make evidence and analysis easier for the audience to understand.

What You Will Do

  • Match multimedia choices to audience needs.
  • Choose visuals that clarify research and evidence.
  • Use academic vocabulary to explain design choices.
  • Check conventions in captions, labels, and source notes.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can explain why a multimedia element helps the audience understand the presentation's claim, evidence, or research.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can choose multimedia that helps my audience understand.

Ask TOR for Help: If a multimedia element looks interesting but does not support the message, ask TOR how to revise or replace it.
