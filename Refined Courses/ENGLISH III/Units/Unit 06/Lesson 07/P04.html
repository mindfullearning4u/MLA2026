Worked Example: Final Audit of a Civic Story Map

Release Candidate

A story map about neighborhood wells combines claims, maps, photographs, interviews, charts, and downloads. The final audit finds one unsupported headline, two expired links, inconsistent dates, an interview without written permission, inaccurate auto-captions, missing chart text, and no low-bandwidth fallback.

Modeled Corrections

  1. Qualify the headline and link it to the strongest evidence.
  2. Repair links and add access dates.
  3. Standardize dates and version metadata.
  4. Hold the interview until permission is documented.
  5. Review captions and provide transcript.
  6. Add chart description and downloadable data.
  7. Create a text-first fallback.
  8. Retest, update change log, obtain sign-off, and archive the approved release.

Decision

Publication is delayed because permission and accessibility are release requirements, not optional improvements.
