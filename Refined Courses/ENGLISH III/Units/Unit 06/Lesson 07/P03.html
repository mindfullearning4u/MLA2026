P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Evaluate whether the parts of a communication product work together.

Practice Product Description

A student publishes a short research summary on a LAN page. The writing explains how conflict shapes a character's identity. The page includes a timeline of major events, a caption that connects the timeline to the claim, and a source note for the research.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the purpose. The product explains a research-based literary claim.

Step 2: Identify the audience. The LAN page is for class readers who need a clear summary.

Step 3: Test the multimedia. The timeline helps the audience follow events connected to the claim.

Step 4: Test the writing. The research summary must explain the claim and not only list facts.

Step 5: Test the final readiness. The product needs readable formatting, source notes, captions, and revised conventions.

Notebook Task

Write three labels in your notebook: Works Well, Needs Revision, and Final Check.

Under Works Well, identify one strong part of the product description.

Under Needs Revision, write one question you would ask before publishing.

Under Final Check, write one sentence explaining what must be checked before export.
