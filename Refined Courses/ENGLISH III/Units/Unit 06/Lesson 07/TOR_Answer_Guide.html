Independent Work — “After the Mill Closed”

  1. The package preserves community memory while documenting economic change and recovery efforts.
  2. The opening contrasts silence/closure with later civic activity.
  3. “Ambient” means surrounding background sound; accept a contextual definition supported by two reputable sources.
  4. The whistle, interviews, photographs, and redevelopment records provide complementary evidence.
  5. Claims must distinguish residents’ memories from verifiable records.
  6. Strong revisions identify speakers, dates, and source limitations.
  7. Audio adds tone and lived experience; captions/transcripts are required.
  8. Images need descriptive alt text and meaningful captions.
  9. Privacy review should address names, locations, consent, and sensitive recollections.
  10. Permission and credit lines must identify ownership and allowed use.
  11. Fallback content must preserve the essential claim if media fails.
  12. The content warning should be specific, brief, and non-sensational.
  13. The editorial audit checks accuracy, structure, clarity, mechanics, and citations.
  14. The media audit checks function, quality, accessibility, permission, and redundancy.
  15. A release candidate is the final testable version before publication.
  16. The eight-entry change log should name the issue, revision, and reason.
  17. Sign-off assigns responsibility for content, media, access, and release.
  18. The rationale must connect each final choice to audience, purpose, evidence, and ethical publication.

Checkpoint

  1. Identify the documentary page’s central claim and intended audience.
  2. Select the most credible evidence and explain why.
  3. Flag unsupported or overstated claims.
  4. Revise organization for a clear reading/viewing path.
  5. Explain the function of each medium.
  6. Require captions, transcript, alt text, and readable contrast.
  7. Verify source and credit information.
  8. Resolve privacy and permission risks.
  9. Create an accessible fallback.
  10. Provide six specific change-log entries.
  11. Recommend release, revision, or hold with evidence.
  12. The 140–180 word rationale should integrate audience, evidence, media, access, ethics, and quality assurance.

Mastery: 80%. Below mastery, give targeted TOR feedback and require corrections and resubmission.
