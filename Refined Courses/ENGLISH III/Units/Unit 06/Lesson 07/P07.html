P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can judge whether a communication product is ready to publish.

TOR

Ask TOR for help if you cannot explain how your writing, multimedia, research, and audience purpose connect.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook checklist from P02.
  2. Review your product evaluation from P03 and P06.
  3. Revise unclear writing or unsupported multimedia choices.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response using the course submission tool.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a publication-readiness review with four labeled sections:

  • Audience and Purpose: Identify who the product is for and what it communicates.
  • Writing Quality: Explain one revision that improves clarity or structure.
  • Multimedia Support: Explain how one media element helps the audience.
  • Final Publication Check: List two checks needed before export or submission.

Mastery Criteria

  • The response identifies a specific audience and purpose.
  • The writing revision improves clarity, structure, or audience fit.
  • The multimedia choice is explained as support, not decoration.
  • The final checks address publication quality and readiness.

Mastery Reminder: A product is publication-ready when every part helps the audience understand the message.
