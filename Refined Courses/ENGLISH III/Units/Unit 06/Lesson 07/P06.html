📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Prepare your publication-ready communication review.

Instructions

Use a draft from Unit 6. It may be an oral presentation plan, digital presentation, research summary, or publication paragraph.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Audience Fit

Write the audience and purpose. Then explain one way the final product helps that audience understand your message.

Part B: Writing and Research Quality

Choose one paragraph or section. Identify one sentence that is clear and one sentence that still needs revision. Rewrite the sentence that needs revision.

Part C: Multimedia and Publication Check

Name one multimedia element you plan to use. Explain how it supports the purpose.

Then list two final checks you must complete before publishing or submitting the product.

Independent Work Success Checklist

Complete the work independently, but do not guess about what to use. Any passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt needed for the task must be on this lesson page or inside the assessment question itself.

  1. Answer every part of the task in order. If there is a Part A, Part B, and Part C, each part needs its own clear response.
  2. Use evidence or details from the same page when the task requires reading, analysis, research, or revision.
  3. Explain your reasoning step by step so the reader can see how you moved from evidence to answer.
  4. Before submitting, reread for accuracy, standard alignment, complete sentences, academic vocabulary, and standard English conventions.
