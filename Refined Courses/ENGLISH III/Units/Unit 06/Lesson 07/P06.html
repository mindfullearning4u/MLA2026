Checkpoint: Final Audit of a Student Documentary Page

Scenario and Questions

A documentary page has a confident but unsupported summary, uncredited music, uneven audio, inaccurate captions, unidentified speakers, vague links, no transcript, and no mobile or low-bandwidth testing.

  1. Correct the summary.
  2. Define documentary and verify it.
  3. Plan music licensing and credit.
  4. Repair audio and fallback.
  5. Correct captions and transcript.
  6. Identify speakers and permissions.
  7. Rewrite links.
  8. Plan accessibility testing.
  9. Plan device/network testing.
  10. Write a 140–180 word audit rationale.
  11. Create a six-entry change log.
  12. Write release and sign-off requirements.
