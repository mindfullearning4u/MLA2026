P05 Guided Practice

Practice publication-readiness decisions.

Directions

Answer each guided practice question. Focus on whether the product is ready for a specific audience, uses multimedia purposefully, and communicates research clearly.

The questions match the Guided Practice GIFT file for this lesson.

Learning Support

Use this test: Does the choice make the final product clearer, more credible, and easier for the audience to use?

If a choice only makes the product look busy, it is not the best publication decision.
