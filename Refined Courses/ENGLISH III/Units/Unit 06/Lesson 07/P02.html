Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Verify two using a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title.

publication package The complete set of approved content, media, metadata, credits, and delivery files.
editorial audit Systematic review of meaning, organization, style, conventions, and accuracy.
media audit Systematic review of function, quality, sources, permissions, accessibility, and technical performance.
quality assurance Planned testing to confirm that a product meets stated requirements.
release candidate A near-final version submitted for last testing before publication.
change log Record of revisions, responsible editors, dates, and reasons.
fallback content Accessible alternative presented when a media element cannot load or be used.
content warning Brief notice that prepares audiences for potentially distressing material.
credit line Concise attribution naming a media creator, source, and license or permission.
sign-off Formal confirmation that required reviewers approve publication.
