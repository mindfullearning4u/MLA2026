P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Build a publication-readiness checklist.

Notebook Title

Publication-Ready Multimedia Communication Checklist

Vocabulary

Publication-ready: Prepared carefully enough to share with an intended audience.

Multimedia communication: A message that combines writing with media such as images, charts, video, audio, or digital design.

Audience fit: The match between the communication choices and what the audience needs.

Integration: Combining parts so they work together instead of feeling separate.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Start with the audience. Write who the product is for and what they need to understand.

Step 2: Check the writing. It should have a clear claim or purpose, strong evidence, explanation, and revised language.

Step 3: Check the multimedia. Each element should help the audience understand the message.

Step 4: Check the research. Source information should be accurate, relevant, and responsibly represented.

Step 5: Check the final format. The product should be readable, organized, and ready for an online or LAN setting.

Notebook Task

Create a five-part checklist in your notebook: audience, writing, multimedia, research, final format.

Under each heading, write one question you can use to test whether your product is ready to publish.
