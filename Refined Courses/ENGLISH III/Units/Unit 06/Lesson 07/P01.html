Lesson Overview: Publication-Ready Multimedia Communication

Standards and Goals

Primary: MLA.ENG3.SL.05 and W.06 — produce, evaluate, and publish integrated multimedia communication for a real audience. Support includes research, conventions, source integrity, accessibility, and revision standards.

  • Conduct a complete editorial and media audit.
  • Align claim, evidence, modes, navigation, and delivery.
  • Resolve privacy, permission, licensing, and accessibility.
  • Document testing and final publication decisions.

Mastery
