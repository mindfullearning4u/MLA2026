📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 07

Publication-Ready Multimedia Communication

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.03 - Create and export quality writing tailored to a specific audience, integrating multimedia elements, publishing to an online or LAN site.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.C.01, MLA.ENG3.C.02, MLA.ENG3.RS.01, MLA.ENG3.W.04, MLA.ENG3.V.01

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to check whether writing, multimedia, and presentation choices work together as one publication-ready communication product.

Why This Matters

A final communication product should not feel like separate pieces pasted together. The audience should see a clear purpose, readable writing, useful multimedia, and credible research working together.

What You Will Do

  • Check a communication product for audience fit.
  • Revise writing for publication quality.
  • Connect multimedia to evidence and purpose.
  • Prepare final communication choices for an online or LAN audience.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can explain why the final writing, multimedia, and communication choices are ready for a specific audience and publication setting.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can prepare a multimedia communication product that is clear, revised, audience-aware, and ready to publish.

Ask TOR for Help: If your writing, media, and audience purpose do not connect clearly, ask TOR to help you decide what to revise before publishing.
