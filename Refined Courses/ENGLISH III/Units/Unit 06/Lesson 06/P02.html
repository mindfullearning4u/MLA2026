Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Verify two with a reputable dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title.

LAN publication Content distributed through a local area network rather than the public internet.
screen readability Ease with which users can read and understand text on a digital display.
descriptive link Link text that identifies the destination or action without relying on “click here.”
information architecture Organization and labeling of content so users can find and understand it.
chunking Dividing information into manageable, logically labeled sections.
metadata Descriptive information that helps identify, manage, or retrieve digital content.
version control A method for tracking changes and identifying the current approved file.
privacy review Check for personal, sensitive, or identifying information before publication.
responsive design Layout that adapts to different screen sizes and devices.
publication audit Systematic final review of content, sources, accessibility, privacy, links, and display.
