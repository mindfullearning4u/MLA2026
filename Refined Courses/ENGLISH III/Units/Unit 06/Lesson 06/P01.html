Lesson Overview: Quality Writing for Online or LAN Publication

Standards and Goals

Primary: MLA.ENG3.W.06 — produce and publish clear, ethical, accessible digital writing. Support: MLA.ENG3.W.05, W.08, L.01, and SL.05.

  • Adapt organization, tone, and navigation for screen reading.
  • Edit links, headings, media, and sources for clarity and trust.
  • Protect privacy, permissions, and accessibility.
  • Test publication on intended devices and networks.

Mastery

Complete the six pages, two submissions, and quiz. Five questions are drawn from 25; one attempt and 80% are required. Below mastery, use TOR feedback to correct and resubmit.
