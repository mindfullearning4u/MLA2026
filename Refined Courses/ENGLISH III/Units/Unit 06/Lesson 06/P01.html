📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 06

Quality Writing for Online or LAN Publication

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.C.03 - Create and export quality writing tailored to a specific audience, integrating multimedia elements, publishing to an online or LAN site.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.W.02, MLA.ENG3.W.03, MLA.ENG3.W.04

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to prepare writing for publication by matching your words, structure, evidence, and multimedia choices to a specific audience.

Why This Matters

Published writing has real readers. Before writing is posted to an online or LAN site, it must be clear, accurate, organized, revised, and useful for the audience.

What You Will Do

  • Identify the audience and purpose for a published piece.
  • Revise writing so readers can follow the claim, evidence, and explanation.
  • Choose multimedia that supports the message.
  • Prepare a publication checklist for clarity, quality, and conventions.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can explain how a piece of writing has been revised, organized, and supported with multimedia for a specific online or LAN audience.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can prepare quality writing for publication by thinking about my readers, my purpose, and the media that will help them understand.

Ask TOR for Help: If you are not sure whether your writing is ready to publish, ask TOR to help you check audience, clarity, evidence, multimedia, and conventions.
