P07 Checkpoint

Show that your writing is ready for publication planning.

TOR

Before you submit, ask TOR for help if your audience is unclear, if your paragraph still sounds like a rough draft, or if your multimedia choice does not directly support the writing.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook work from P02, P03, and P06.
  2. Make sure your revised writing is clear for the audience.
  3. Check the multimedia caption or source note.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response using the course submission tool.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a publication readiness plan with four labeled parts:

  • Audience: Identify who will read the published writing.
  • Purpose: Explain what the audience should understand.
  • Revised Writing: Provide your revised paragraph or short section.
  • Multimedia Plan: Name the multimedia element and explain how it helps the audience.

Mastery Criteria

  • The audience and purpose are specific.
  • The writing is revised for clarity, structure, and publication quality.
  • The multimedia element supports understanding rather than decoration.
  • The response follows standard English conventions.

Mastery Reminder: Publication-ready writing is not just finished. It is prepared for real readers.
