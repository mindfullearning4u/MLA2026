📘 ENG3 | Unit 06 | Lesson 06

P05 Guided Practice

Practice preparing writing for publication.

Directions

Answer each guided practice question. Use the feedback to check whether the writing is audience-ready, revised clearly, and supported by useful multimedia.

The questions match the Guided Practice GIFT file for this lesson.

Learning Support

Before answering, ask yourself three questions: Who is the audience? What does the audience need to understand? Does the multimedia or revision help that audience?

If the answer choice adds decoration without purpose, it is probably not the best publication choice.
