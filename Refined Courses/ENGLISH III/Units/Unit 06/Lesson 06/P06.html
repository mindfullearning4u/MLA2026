P06 Independent Work

Prepare a short piece of writing for publication.

Instructions

Select one paragraph from your research, analysis, or presentation writing. You will prepare it for an online or LAN audience by revising it for clarity and planning multimedia support.

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook before the checkpoint.

Part A: Audience and Purpose

Write two sentences. First, identify your audience. Second, explain what the audience should understand after reading your published piece.

Part B: Publication Revision

Rewrite the paragraph so it is clearer for the audience. Add context if needed. Remove vague words. Make the claim, evidence, and explanation easy to follow.

Part C: Multimedia and Export Check

Choose one multimedia element that would support the published writing. Write a caption or source note for it.

Then write one export check sentence: "Before publishing, I need to check..." Complete the sentence with one specific quality check.
