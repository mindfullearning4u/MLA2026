Checkpoint: Publish a Local History FAQ

Scenario and Questions

A local-history FAQ has vague headings, long answers, “click here” links, uncredited images, names of living interviewees without permission, and an outdated file on the LAN.

  1. State audience tasks.
  2. Define archive and verify it.
  3. Create a heading structure.
  4. Revise two answers for screen reading.
  5. Rewrite three links.
  6. Plan image attribution and alternative text.
  7. Identify privacy and permission needs.
  8. Create version-control filenames.
  9. Plan keyboard, zoom, device, and LAN tests.
  10. Write a 140–180 word publication rationale.
  11. Create three usability tasks.
  12. Complete a final publication audit.
