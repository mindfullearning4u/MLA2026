P04 Worked Example

Prepare writing for publication by checking audience, revision, and multimedia support.

Example 1: Identify the Publication Audience

Draft purpose: Publish a short research summary on a class LAN page.

Step 1: The audience is classmates and TOR.

Step 2: The audience needs the claim, evidence, and explanation quickly.

Step 3: The writing should use a clear title, a direct lead sentence, and short paragraphs.

Example 2: Revise for Reader Understanding

Original: This proves the topic because it is important and shows change.

Step 1: The sentence is too vague. The reader cannot tell what "this" means.

Step 2: Name the evidence and explain the connection.

Revised: The character's decision to speak honestly supports the claim because it shows how personal conflict can lead to moral growth.

Example 3: Integrate Multimedia

Writing point: The presentation explains how setting shapes a character's choices.

Step 1: A map, setting image, or timeline could help the audience understand the context.

Step 2: The media must be labeled so readers know why it appears.

Caption: This timeline shows the sequence of pressures that influence the character's decision.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect Example:

I will paste the essay exactly as it is and add a random picture because published pages should have images.

This is incorrect because publication requires audience-aware revision. Random media can distract readers and weaken the purpose.

Correct Thinking

Correct Example:

I will revise the writing for my audience, check clarity and conventions, and include multimedia only if it helps readers understand the published piece.

This is correct because it connects quality writing, revision, audience, and multimedia to the purpose of publication.
