Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and complete definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

TermDefinition
figurative languageLanguage that creates meaning beyond a strictly literal interpretation.
metaphorA direct comparison in which one thing is described as another.
simileA comparison using like or as.
personificationAttributing human qualities or actions to something nonhuman.
symbolA concrete element that suggests meaning beyond its literal function.
allegoryA narrative in which a sustained pattern of characters, events, and settings develops a second level of meaning.
extended metaphorA comparison developed across multiple lines, sentences, or sections.
literal levelWhat directly happens within a text’s story or situation.
figurative levelThe additional conceptual meaning produced by comparison, symbol, or pattern.
ambiguityMeaning that remains open to more than one defensible interpretation.
