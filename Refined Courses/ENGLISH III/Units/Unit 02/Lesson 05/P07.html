Checkpoint Passage

The kingdom kept a map in the palace hall. The map showed only roads that had already been traveled. When a traveler asked to add a new path through the mountains, the king replied, "If it is not on the map, it cannot exist." That night, the traveler folded the map into a paper lantern and walked toward the dark ridge.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that answers this question:

How does the passage use allegory to communicate a deeper idea?

Your paragraph must include:

  • a clear claim about the deeper idea,
  • one quoted detail from the passage,
  • an explanation of the literal meaning, and
  • an explanation of the allegorical meaning.

Mastery Criteria

  • You identify a symbolic detail accurately.
  • You explain literal and deeper meaning.
  • You connect the symbol to a larger idea.
  • You use evidence from the passage.
