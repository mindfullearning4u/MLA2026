Lesson Overview: Figurative Language and Allegory

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.05 — Analyze figurative language and allegory as systems of meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • Interpret figurative language in context.
  • Distinguish a symbol from an extended allegorical pattern.
  • Trace literal and figurative levels without forcing one-to-one meanings.
  • Evaluate how allegory comments on social, moral, or political concerns.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study direct instruction and the model.
  3. Complete 18 Independent Work questions with vocabulary submission.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will trace a pattern of figurative details and explain how the literal story develops a defensible second level of meaning.
