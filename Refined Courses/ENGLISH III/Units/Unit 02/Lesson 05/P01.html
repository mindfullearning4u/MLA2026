Lesson 5: Figurative Language and Allegory

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.09 - Analyze Figurative Language and Allegory.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.04 - Analyze Poetry in Historical Context; MLA.ENG3.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify figurative language in a literary passage.
  • How to explain what figurative language means in context.
  • How allegory works when characters, objects, or events stand for larger ideas.
  • How to avoid stopping at identification when analysis is required.

Why This Matters

Authors often say one thing while asking readers to understand something deeper. A locked gate might be more than a gate. A storm might be more than weather. A journey might stand for growing up, resisting injustice, or searching for truth.

This lesson teaches you to move from noticing figurative language to explaining how it creates meaning.

What You Will Do

  • Read short original passages and poems.
  • Identify figurative language and explain its meaning.
  • Recognize examples of allegory.
  • Write a checkpoint response explaining how a symbolic element supports a larger idea.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain how figurative language and allegorical details reveal a deeper meaning in a text, using specific evidence from the passage.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to explain how an author's figurative language works and how an allegory uses story details to represent larger ideas.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can identify a symbol or comparison but are not sure how to explain its deeper meaning.
