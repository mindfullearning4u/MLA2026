P06 Independent Work

Practice explaining figurative language and allegory before the checkpoint.

Independent Work Passage

At the edge of the city stood a door with no handle. People walked past it every day and said, "Some doors are not meant to open." A painter sat beside it and drew a handle in blue chalk. By evening, the line of people waiting to touch the chalk handle stretched around the block.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences.

Part A: Identify

Identify one symbolic detail in the passage. Quote the exact words.

Part B: Explain

Explain the literal meaning of the detail and the deeper meaning it may suggest.

Part C: Analyze

Write one paragraph explaining how the passage works as an allegory about possibility, imagination, or social limits.
