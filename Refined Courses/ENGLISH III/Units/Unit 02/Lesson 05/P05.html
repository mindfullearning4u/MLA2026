P05 Guided Practice

Answer each question using the passage provided. The same questions are in the Guided Practice GIFT file.

Guided Practice Passage

The village kept a glass clock in the square. Its hands never moved, but the villagers bowed to it each morning. "It keeps us orderly," the elders said. A young apprentice noticed that the fields ripened whether the clock approved or not, and she began planting by sunlight instead.

Questions

  1. What detail most strongly suggests the clock is symbolic?
  2. What larger idea might the clock represent?
  3. Why is the apprentice's action important?
  4. Which sentence best explains the allegory?
  5. Which answer uses evidence correctly?

Learning Support

For each question, ask yourself: What does the object literally do? What pattern surrounds it? What larger idea does that pattern suggest?
