Independent Work: “The City of Sealed Wells”

Directions

Read the allegorical passage and answer all 18 questions. Submit these responses with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Passage

Every district in Orra possessed a well, but the council sealed each one beneath a glass floor. Citizens could see the water moving below their feet. To drink, they purchased numbered cups from the central tower. The cups grew smaller each year, while portraits of full rivers appeared on every public wall.

Apprentice mason Tavi was ordered to repair a crack in the oldest glass. Through it he heard voices rising from the well—not words, exactly, but echoes of rain, buckets, and people calling one another’s names. The tower inspector told him to pour silver mortar into the crack. Instead, Tavi lowered a thread through it. The thread returned wet.

At the next ration ceremony, Tavi held up the darkened thread. The council declared it counterfeit evidence because it carried no number. Citizens began pressing their own threads against the glass. By sunset, the floor looked covered in roots. The council opened the tower gates to stop the crowd, but the people walked toward the wells instead.

Tavi did not break the glass. He placed his hammer beside the crack and waited while the citizens argued about what freedom required next.
