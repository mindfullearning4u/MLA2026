Worked Example Text

The town built a wall to keep out the wind. At first, everyone praised the quiet. Soon the flowers leaned pale against the stones, and the children forgot the sound of leaves. One morning, a girl placed her ear to a crack in the wall and heard the forest breathing.

Text detail: "heard the forest breathing"

Step-by-step explanation: A forest does not literally breathe like a person. The phrase gives the forest human-like life. It suggests the outside world is alive, natural, and still present even though the wall separates the town from it.

Symbolic detail: the wall

Step-by-step explanation: Literally, the wall blocks the wind. Allegorically, it may represent fear-based protection or isolation. The town gains quiet, but it loses flowers, leaves, and connection to the forest. The detail suggests that avoiding discomfort can also remove life, growth, and awareness.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The wall is important because it is a wall and the town built it.

Why it is weak: It identifies an object but does not analyze figurative or allegorical meaning.

Correct: The wall represents isolation because it keeps the town quiet but also separates people from the living forest.

Why it is correct: It explains what the wall represents and connects the explanation to the passage.
