P04 Worked Example

Study how to explain juxtaposition step by step.

Worked Example 1: Passage

Marcus stood in the crowded courtroom while rain tapped softly on the roof. Everyone else watched the judge, but Marcus watched the empty chair beside the witness stand.

Worked Example 2: Identify the Contrast

Detail 1: Everyone else watches the judge.

Detail 2: Marcus watches the empty chair.

Contrast: The crowd focuses on authority, while Marcus focuses on absence.

Worked Example 3: Explain Perspective

The juxtaposition shows that Marcus sees the trial differently from the crowd. While others focus on the judge's power, Marcus focuses on the missing witness, which suggests doubt or concern.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Marcus is in court and it is raining.

Why it is weak: It summarizes details but does not explain the author's choice.

Correct: By contrasting the crowd's attention to the judge with Marcus's attention to the empty chair, the author reveals Marcus's concern about who is missing.

Why it is correct: It explains how juxtaposition defines perspective.
