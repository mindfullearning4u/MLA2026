P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Juxtaposition and Character Perspective

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.03 - Analyze Juxtaposition and Character Perspective.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG3.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning; MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices.

What You Will Learn

  • How authors place contrasting details side by side.
  • How juxtaposition can reveal a character's perspective.
  • How word choice and evidence help explain that perspective.
  • How to explain the author's choice clearly.

Why This Matters

Juxtaposition helps readers see contrast. In Grade 11 analysis, the key question is not only what is different, but why the author places those differences together.

This lesson prepares you to analyze perspective in more complex literary texts.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages with contrast.
  • Identify details placed side by side.
  • Explain what the contrast reveals about a character's perspective.
  • Write a checkpoint response using evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain how juxtaposed details define or reveal a character's perspective.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to notice contrasts and explain how those contrasts show the way a character sees a situation.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can see two contrasting details but are unsure what the contrast reveals about the character.
