Lesson Overview: Juxtaposition and Character Perspective

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.03 — Analyze how juxtaposition develops character perspective and meaning.

Support: close reading, evidence, academic vocabulary, and conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize purposeful juxtaposition rather than simple difference.
  • Analyze how contrasting settings, images, events, or viewpoints reveal perspective.
  • Trace how a character’s interpretation changes when placed beside another.
  • Write an evidence-based claim about the effect of juxtaposition.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study direct teaching and the worked example.
  3. Complete the 18-question Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question random quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain how an author places contrasting elements together to expose assumptions, shape character perspective, and deepen meaning.
