Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and complete definitions. Submit these notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
juxtapositionThe purposeful placement of elements side by side to highlight a meaningful relationship or contrast.
perspectiveA character’s or narrator’s way of understanding events, people, and ideas.
contrastA difference that becomes significant when elements are compared.
parallelA similarity in structure, situation, or development that invites comparison.
foilA character whose contrasting traits clarify another character’s qualities.
biasA preference or assumption that shapes interpretation, sometimes unfairly.
limited point of viewA perspective restricted to the knowledge and perceptions of one character.
shiftA noticeable change in perspective, tone, setting, or structure.
dissonanceTension created by elements that seem inconsistent or conflicting.
synthesisAn understanding formed by combining and interpreting multiple perspectives or ideas.
