P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Identify juxtaposed details.

Notebook Title

Juxtaposition and Perspective

Vocabulary

  • Juxtaposition: placing contrasting details side by side.
  • Perspective: the way a character understands or views a situation.
  • Contrast: a clear difference between two details.
  • Define: make clear or reveal.

Short Passage

Lena entered the banquet hall in a patched coat. The silver tables were crowded with flowers, but she noticed the kitchen door first, where a tired boy carried a tray too heavy for his arms.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Find the contrast. The hall is wealthy, but Lena notices labor and tiredness.

Step 2: Ask why the author places those details together.

Step 3: Connect the contrast to perspective. Lena sees the hidden work behind public luxury.

Step 4: Use evidence: "silver tables" contrasts with "tired boy."

Notebook Task

Copy the passage. Then list two contrasting details and write one sentence explaining what Lena notices that other guests may ignore.
