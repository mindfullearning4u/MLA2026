📘 ENG3 | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Write about juxtaposition independently.

Passage

The mayor praised the town's bright future from the steps of the new bank. Across the street, Inez watched a line of workers carry broken tools out of the closed factory.

Part A

Identify the two details being juxtaposed.

Part B

Explain what Inez notices that the mayor's speech may ignore.

Part C

Write a four-sentence response explaining how the juxtaposition defines Inez's perspective.
