P04 Worked Example

Study how to compare contemporaneous authors by reasoning and context.

Worked Example Context

Both authors write during a period when many young people are leaving small towns for factory jobs in cities.

Author A

Leaving home is not betrayal. A city job may teach a young worker skills that the town cannot offer. If the town values its children, it should bless their departure and welcome their return with new knowledge.

Author B

A town cannot thrive if every capable hand is trained to leave it. The city may offer wages, but it also teaches workers to forget the fields, roads, and neighbors who raised them. Progress that empties a home should be questioned.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The authors are different because one passage is longer.

Why it is weak: Length is not a meaningful comparison of reasoning.

Correct: The authors differ because Author A sees city work as skill-building, while Author B sees it as a threat to community survival.

Why it is correct: It compares the authors' reasoning about the same time-period issue.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.
