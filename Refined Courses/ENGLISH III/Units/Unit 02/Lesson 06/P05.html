P05 Guided Practice

Answer each question using the paired passages. The same questions are in the Guided Practice GIFT file.

Context and Passage A

Context: Both authors write during a time when public rail lines are expanding into rural areas.

Author A: The rail line will carry more than passengers. It will carry medicine, books, and letters to towns that have waited too long for connection. A community should not fear a road simply because it is made of iron.

Passage B

Author B: A rail line may bring letters, but it also brings buyers who see land only as numbers on paper. Before we cheer the engine, we should ask who owns the tracks and who must move when the price of progress rises.

Questions

  1. What shared topic do both authors address?
  2. How does Author A reason about the rail line?
  3. How does Author B reason about the rail line?
  4. What time-period concern connects both passages?
  5. Which comparison is most accurate?

Learning Support

Remember: a strong comparison names the shared topic, explains each author's reasoning, and connects both passages to the same time-period issue.
