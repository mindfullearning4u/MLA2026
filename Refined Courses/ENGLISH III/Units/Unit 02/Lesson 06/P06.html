P06 Independent Work

Practice comparing contemporaneous authors before the checkpoint.

Context and Author A

Context: Both authors write during a period when electric streetlights are replacing oil lamps in cities.

Author A: Electric lights lengthen the useful day. Streets that once emptied at dusk can now hold markets, readers, and safe passage. A city that lights its corners gives ordinary people more hours to live publicly.

Author B

Electric lights promise safety, yet they also teach the city to forget darkness. Under the new glare, shop windows shine while workers' rooms remain dim. A brighter street does not guarantee a fairer city.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Shared Topic

Identify the shared topic and time-period issue.

Part B: Reasoning

Explain Author A's reasoning and Author B's reasoning in your own words.

Part C: Comparison Paragraph

Write one paragraph comparing how the authors address the same issue. Include one detail from each passage.
