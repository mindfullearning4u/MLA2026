P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can compare contemporaneous authors by topic, reasoning, and context.

Checkpoint Context and Author A

Context: Both authors write during a time when newspapers are becoming cheaper and more widely available.

Author A: A cheaper newspaper gives workers a seat at the public table. When news costs less than bread, more citizens can follow debates, question officials, and understand decisions made in their name.

Author B

A cheaper newspaper can inform, but it can also hurry thought. When every headline competes for attention, rumor travels beside fact. Wider access matters, but speed without care may make citizens louder before it makes them wiser.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that answers this question:

How do the two contemporaneous authors address the same issue differently?

Your paragraph must include:

  • the shared topic,
  • Author A's reasoning,
  • Author B's reasoning,
  • one detail from each passage, and
  • a connection to the time-period context.

Mastery Criteria

  • You correctly identify the shared topic.
  • You compare reasoning instead of only comparing subjects.
  • You use evidence from both passages.
  • You connect the comparison to the time-period context.
