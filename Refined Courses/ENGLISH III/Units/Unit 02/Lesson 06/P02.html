Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

TermDefinition
contemporaneousExisting or occurring during the same general period.
related topicA shared subject or concern treated by more than one text.
reasoningThe logical connections linking evidence, assumptions, claims, and conclusions.
assumptionAn idea accepted as true within an argument, sometimes without direct proof.
line of reasoningThe ordered sequence through which a text develops and supports its ideas.
qualificationA limitation or condition added to make a claim more precise.
counterargumentA position that challenges or complicates another claim.
concessionAcknowledgment that part of an opposing position has merit.
genreA category of text with characteristic purposes, conventions, and forms.
comparative evaluationA judgment about meaningful similarities and differences supported by criteria and evidence.
