Set up a comparison system for contemporaneous authors.

Notebook Title

Comparing Contemporaneous Authors: Topic, Reasoning, Context

Vocabulary

  • Contemporaneous: existing or happening during the same time period.
  • Related topic: an issue or subject that both authors address.
  • Reasoning: the author's explanation of why a claim makes sense.
  • Context: the historical, cultural, or social situation surrounding a text.
  • Compare and contrast: explain meaningful similarities and differences.

Direct Teaching

When two authors are contemporaneous, they write from the same general time period. That does not mean they agree. It means they may be responding to shared conditions.

Use this process:

  1. Identify the shared topic. What issue are both authors addressing?
  2. Paraphrase each author's main idea. What is each author saying?
  3. Identify reasoning. Why does each author believe that idea?
  4. Connect to context. How does the time period help explain the concern?
