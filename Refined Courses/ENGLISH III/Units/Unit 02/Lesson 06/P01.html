Lesson 6: Contemporaneous Authors, Related Topics, and Reasoning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.11 - Compare Contemporaneous Authors.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.02 - Track and Analyze Universal Themes; MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • What it means for authors to be contemporaneous.
  • How authors from the same time period can address related topics differently.
  • How to compare reasoning, not just subject matter.
  • How to connect author choices to time-period context.

Why This Matters

People who live during the same period often respond to the same pressures: technology, migration, conflict, social change, economic hardship, or public debate. Their conclusions may differ because their reasoning, evidence, and perspective differ.

This lesson teaches you to compare how authors think, not just what they write about.

What You Will Do

  • Read paired short passages from the same fictional time period.
  • Identify each author's topic, claim, and reasoning.
  • Compare how the authors use context to shape their ideas.
  • Write a checkpoint response comparing contemporaneous authors.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will compare how two authors from the same time period address a related topic, explain their reasoning, and connect the comparison to the time-period context.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to compare authors from the same time period and explain how they reason about related issues in that context.

Ask TOR for Help
