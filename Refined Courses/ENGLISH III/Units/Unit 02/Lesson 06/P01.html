Lesson Overview: Contemporaneous Authors, Related Topics, and Reasoning

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Compare how contemporaneous authors develop related topics through distinct reasoning and literary choices.

What You Will Learn

  • Establish meaningful contemporaneity and context.
  • Compare treatments of a related topic without forcing identical themes.
  • Trace each author’s claims, assumptions, evidence, and reasoning.
  • Evaluate how genre and audience shape different approaches.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study the comparison process and model.
  3. Complete 18 Independent Work questions with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will make a comparative claim explaining how two authors from the same broad period reason differently about a related concern.
