P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can track and analyze a universal theme.

Checkpoint Text A

An old sailor taught children to read the stars even after ships began using machines. "Tools change," he said, "but people still need to know how to look up."

Checkpoint Text B

A coding mentor taught students to sketch their ideas on paper before opening a laptop. "Screens are useful," she said, "but thinking begins before the screen turns on."

Checkpoint Task

Write a five-sentence response explaining a universal theme both texts share and how each text develops that theme in a different setting.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notes showing the theme, evidence from Text A, evidence from Text B, and your comparison plan.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your final five-sentence response in the checkpoint area.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response states a complete universal theme.
  • Your response uses evidence from both texts.
  • Your response explains how each setting develops the theme differently.
  • Your academic vocabulary and conventions are clear.
