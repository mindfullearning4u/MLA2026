P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Track a theme across two different settings.

Passage A

The village bridge had fallen in the flood. An old carpenter set one plank across the gap and waited. By evening, a child brought a second plank, and by morning, every family had added one board.

Passage B

In the city tower, the elevator stopped between floors. One worker opened the emergency panel, another held the flashlight, and a third calmed the people inside. No one fixed the problem alone, but together they brought the elevator down safely.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Passage A develops the theme through a rural bridge and families adding boards.

Step 2: Passage B develops the theme through a modern city problem and workers sharing roles.

Step 3: The shared theme is cooperation.

Step 4: The theme appears in different places, but both texts show that shared effort solves a problem.

Notebook Task

Write a two-column chart. In the left column, list how Passage A develops cooperation. In the right column, list how Passage B develops cooperation.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Both texts are about things being broken.

Why it is weak: Broken objects are plot details, not the full theme.

Correct: Both texts develop the universal theme that cooperation can solve problems that one person cannot solve alone.

Why it is stronger: It states a complete theme and explains the shared idea across the two texts.
