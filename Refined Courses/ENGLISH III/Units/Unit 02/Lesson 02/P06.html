P06 Independent Work

Compare how two texts develop a shared universal theme.

Text A

In a mountain village, a baker saved one loaf each day for travelers who arrived after sunset. Years later, when snow blocked the pass, those same travelers returned with carts of flour.

Text B

In a crowded apartment building, a teenager repaired neighbors' phones without charging them. When her own computer broke before an interview, the neighbors found parts, tools, and a quiet room where she could prepare.

Part A

State one universal theme that both texts develop.

Part B

Choose one evidence phrase from each text that supports the shared theme.

Part C

Write a five-sentence comparison explaining how the two texts develop the same universal theme in different settings.
