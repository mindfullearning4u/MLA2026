Lesson Overview: Universal Themes Across Times and Places

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.02 — Compare how universal themes are developed across texts from different times and places.

Support: close reading, academic vocabulary, evidence, and Standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish a topic from a theme statement.
  • Identify universal themes without erasing cultural and historical differences.
  • Compare how characters, conflicts, symbols, and outcomes shape related themes.
  • Write a comparative claim supported by evidence from both texts.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study the comparison process and worked model.
  3. Complete the 18-question Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question random quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will compare how two texts develop a related universal theme through distinct settings, conflicts, literary elements, and cultural contexts.
