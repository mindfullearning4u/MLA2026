Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
topicA broad subject explored by a text, such as loyalty, freedom, or belonging.
themeA defensible statement about what a text suggests concerning a topic.
universal themeA recurring insight about human experience that appears across cultures, places, or time periods.
cultural contextThe beliefs, practices, values, and conditions shaping a text and its characters.
historical contextThe events, institutions, and social conditions of the period in which a text is set or produced.
archetypeA recurring character type, image, situation, or pattern recognized across many texts.
motifA repeated element that develops a text’s larger meaning.
parallelA meaningful similarity in structure, character, event, or idea across texts.
contrastA difference placed in relation to another element to clarify significance.
comparative claimAn arguable statement explaining a significant similarity or difference between texts.
