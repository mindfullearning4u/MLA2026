# ENGLISH III - Unit 02 Overview: American Literature and Perspective

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops literary analysis through American literature, classic literature, poetry, and contemporary world texts. Students track and analyze universal themes across time and place, analyze narrator perspective, irony, and satire, evaluate how poetry is shaped by the speaker, evaluate figurative language and allusions, analyze literary influence, and write complex narratives using multiple perspectives.

Emphasis is placed on historical and cultural context, perspective, thematic development, tone, meaning, literary influence, and textual evidence. Students connect literary analysis to purposeful narrative writing that establishes multiple perspectives.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 11 Standards with an emphasis on evaluation, synthesis, argumentation, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through literary analysis, narrative writing, vocabulary application, and evidence-based explanation.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- universal themes across time and place;
- narrator perspective, irony, and satire;
- poetry, speaker influence, and motivation;
- figurative language, tone, meaning, and allusion;
- literary influence across texts;
- complex narrative writing with multiple perspectives.

Assessment items require students to track and analyze universal themes, explain how narrator perspective shapes irony or satire, evaluate how poetry is influenced by the speaker, evaluate figurative language and allusions, analyze the influence of classic literature on contemporary world texts, and write complex narratives that establish multiple perspectives.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit strengthens students' ability to interpret literature across texts, periods, and perspectives. Students connect author choices, historical context, speaker influence, language, and theme to explain how literature communicates layered meanings.
