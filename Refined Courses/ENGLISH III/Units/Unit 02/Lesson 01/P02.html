Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

TermDefinition
characterizationThe methods an author uses to reveal a character’s traits, values, motives, and development.
settingThe time, place, and social conditions in which a text occurs.
conflictA struggle between opposing forces that drives action or internal change.
point of viewThe position and perspective from which a narrative is told.
imageryDescriptive language that appeals to the senses and creates an experience for the reader.
symbolismThe use of a person, object, place, or action to suggest meaning beyond its literal role.
motifA recurring image, idea, phrase, or situation that helps develop a larger meaning.
ironyA meaningful contrast between expectation and reality, appearance and truth, or stated and intended meaning.
toneThe author’s or speaker’s attitude toward the subject or audience.
styleThe distinctive way an author uses diction, syntax, imagery, structure, and other techniques.
