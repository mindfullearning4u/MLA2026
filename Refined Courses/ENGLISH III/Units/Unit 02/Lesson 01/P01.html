Lesson Overview: Literary Elements and Layers of Meaning or Style

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Evaluate how literary elements create layers of meaning or style.

Support: MLA.ENG3.R.10, MLA.ENG3.V.01, and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

What You Will Learn

  • Analyze characterization, setting, conflict, point of view, imagery, symbolism, tone, and style.
  • Explain how elements interact to create literal and deeper meanings.
  • Evaluate an author’s choices using precise evidence.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary and definitions.
  2. Study the direct teaching and worked example.
  3. Complete all 18 Independent Work questions and submit them with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
  4. Complete and separately submit all 12 Checkpoint responses.
  5. Complete the five-question lesson quiz. Mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will make a defensible claim about how multiple literary elements work together, support it with relevant evidence, and explain the resulting layer of meaning or style.
