Lesson 1: Literary Elements and Layers of Meaning or Style

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.01 - Evaluate Literary Elements.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How literary elements create more than one layer of meaning.
  • How setting, character, conflict, imagery, and symbolism can shape style.
  • How to evaluate whether a literary element adds depth to a text.
  • How to write a clear explanation using academic vocabulary.

Why This Matters

In English III, you are expected to move past identifying literary elements. You must evaluate how those elements work together to deepen meaning or style.

This skill prepares you for later analysis of theme, juxtaposition, poetry, allegory, and complex narratives.

What You Will Do

  • Review key literary elements.
  • Read short literary passages.
  • Identify how an element adds meaning or style.
  • Write a short evaluation using evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining how one literary element adds a layer of meaning or style in a short passage.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to explain how parts of a story or poem do important work, not just name those parts.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if you can identify a literary element but cannot yet explain how it changes the meaning or style of the passage.
