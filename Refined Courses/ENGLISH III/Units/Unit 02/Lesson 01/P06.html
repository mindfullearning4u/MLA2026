P06 Independent Work

Evaluate how a literary element adds meaning or style.

Passage

At the edge of the field, the abandoned well was covered with a door of new cedar. Leila ran her hand over the fresh wood but kept looking at the old stones beneath it. The village children used the well as a table now, spreading maps across the lid and planning roads no one had built yet.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use the passage, prompt, or task details on this page so your work is self-contained and ready for notebook evidence submission.

Part A

Identify one literary element in the passage. Choose from setting, symbol, imagery, character action, or contrast.

Part B

Choose one phrase as evidence. Explain what the phrase shows on the surface and what deeper meaning it suggests.

Part C

Write a four-sentence evaluation explaining how the literary element adds a layer of meaning or style.
