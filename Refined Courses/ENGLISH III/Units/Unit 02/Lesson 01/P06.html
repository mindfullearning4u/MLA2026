Checkpoint: “The Red Coat”

Directions

Passage

At dawn, Mei carried the theater’s final costume—a red coat—through the alley behind the stage. The building had been sold, and by noon the new owner would change the locks. For thirty years, Mei had repaired hems where audiences could not see them. She knew which heroes had knelt too quickly, which villains hid handkerchiefs in their sleeves, and which queens needed extra room to breathe.

The red coat belonged to no famous production. It had been made for an understudy named Tomas, who stepped onstage only once when the lead actor lost his voice. The archive listed that performance as a substitution. Mei remembered the audience rising before Tomas finished his final line.

Inside the costume room, empty racks drew long shadows across the floor. Mei placed the coat on the center hook. She could have taken it; no inventory sheet still named it. Instead, she stitched a narrow label beneath the collar: Worn once. Remembered longer.

When the buyer arrived, he spoke excitedly about glass offices and clean white walls. Then he noticed the coat. “Is that valuable?” he asked.

Mei looked at the faded cuffs and the careful label. “Only if you know what happened in it,” she said. She left before he decided whether to keep it.

Analysis

  1. State the central conflict.
  2. How does Mei’s work history characterize her?
  3. Explain how the empty costume room affects tone.
  4. What does the red coat symbolize? Support your interpretation.
  5. Analyze the contrast between the archive’s wording and Mei’s memory.

Layers of Meaning and Style

  1. Explain the significance of “where audiences could not see them.”
  2. Analyze the label “Worn once. Remembered longer.”
  3. How does the buyer’s question reveal his values?
  4. Explain the irony in the word valuable.

Synthesis

  1. Write a three- to four-sentence objective summary.
  2. Explain how characterization, setting, and symbolism work together.
  3. Evaluate the ending: how does leaving the buyer’s decision unresolved deepen the passage’s meaning?
