P04 Worked Example

Study how to evaluate a literary element instead of only naming it.

Worked Example 1: Passage

The river behind the mill had gone still, but under the ice, dark water kept moving. Tomas watched the frozen surface and tightened his grip on the letter in his coat.

Worked Example 2: Identify and Explain

Element: Imagery and setting.

Evidence: "under the ice, dark water kept moving."

Meaning: The river suggests that something hidden is still active, even when the surface appears frozen.

Worked Example 3: Evaluate

The river imagery adds a layer of meaning because it mirrors Tomas's hidden tension. The still ice suggests control, but the moving water suggests that emotion or conflict continues beneath the surface.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The river is part of the setting.

Why it is weak: This names the element but does not explain what it adds.

Correct: The river setting adds meaning because its hidden movement reflects Tomas's hidden conflict.

Why it is correct: It evaluates how the setting deepens the character's situation.
