P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Explain how a literary element adds style.

Same Passage

The house stood at the end of the road with every window lit. Mara paused outside the gate, holding the key her father had mailed without a note. Inside, the hallway clock ticked loudly enough to sound like footsteps.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Notice the sound detail: the clock ticks like footsteps.

Step 2: Name the element: imagery, because the detail helps the reader hear the scene.

Step 3: Explain the style: the sound makes the passage feel suspenseful.

Step 4: Evaluate the effect: the imagery turns an ordinary house into a place that feels uncertain and tense.

Notebook Task

Write a four-sentence explanation of how the clock detail adds style. Include the phrase "ticked loudly enough to sound like footsteps" as evidence.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The clock is a clock and it makes noise.

Why it is weak: This only identifies the detail. It does not evaluate how the detail adds meaning or style.

Correct: The clock imagery adds suspense because its sound resembles footsteps, making the house feel watched or occupied.

Why it is stronger: It names the element, uses evidence, and explains the stylistic effect.
