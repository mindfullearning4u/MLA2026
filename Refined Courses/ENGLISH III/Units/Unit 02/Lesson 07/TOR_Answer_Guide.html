Scoring Notes

Independent Work

Supported timeline: dispatch delay preceded flooding; bus transported twelve; school opened late through custodian action. Broadcast uses institutional certainty and omits delays. Driver offers contemporaneous operational detail but may not know policy intent. Resident memory is sincere but temporally unstable. Strong syntheses distinguish supported facts from unresolved timing and explain how multiple accounts expose the gap between official response and lived emergency.

Checkpoint

All accounts support a loud meeting, requested clarification, and the artist leaving before conclusion. They do not prove whether “remove it” referred to chair or scaffolding. Minutes may omit tone; artist interprets through displacement concerns; apprentice heard words without referent. Strong synthesis preserves ambiguity and shifts analysis from assigning blame to examining communication.

Mastery

Use visible rubrics. Below 80%, give TOR feedback and require corrected resubmission.
