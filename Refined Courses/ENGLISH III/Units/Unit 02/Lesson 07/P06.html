Checkpoint: “The Unfinished Mural”

Directions

Read all accounts and answer all 12 questions. Submit separately.

Account 1 — Artist

I stopped painting when the neighborhood committee demanded that I remove the empty chair. They said it made the mural sad. The chair represented families displaced before the new apartments opened.

Account 2 — Committee Minutes

The artist paused work after members requested clarification of one ambiguous image. No demand for removal was recorded. The project deadline was extended.

Account 3 — Apprentice

The meeting was loud. I heard “remove it,” but I do not know whether it referred to the chair or scaffolding blocking the sidewalk. The artist packed her brushes before the vote ended.

Questions

  1. Reconstruct a neutral timeline.
  2. Identify the central contradiction.
  3. How may the artist’s values shape her account?
  4. What limitation affects the minutes?
  5. What limitation affects the apprentice?
  6. Explain the ambiguity of “remove it.”
  7. Why does the artist leave before the vote ends?
  8. State the strongest supported conclusion.
  9. Name what remains uncertain.
  10. Write an objective summary.
  11. Write and support a synthesis claim.
  12. Evaluate how the structure shifts the story from blame to interpretation.
