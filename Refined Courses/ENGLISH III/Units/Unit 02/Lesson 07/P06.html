📘 ENG3 | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Draft a short multiple-perspective narrative plan before the checkpoint.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences and keep the shared event clear.

Part A: Choose a Shared Event

Choose one:

  • A community garden is replaced by a parking lot.
  • A school changes its mascot.
  • A family sells an old house.

Write the event in one sentence.

Part B: Plan Two Perspectives

Name two characters. For each character, list what the character notices, what the character wants, and what the character fears.

Part C: Draft

Write 8-10 sentences showing the same event from both perspectives. Use at least one transition or section break to make the shift clear.
