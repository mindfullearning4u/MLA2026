📘 ENG3 | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can write a short narrative with multiple perspectives.

TOR Support

Ask TOR for help if your two perspectives sound the same or if the shift between perspectives is confusing.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit notebook evidence showing:

  • your vocabulary notes,
  • your multiple-perspective planning chart,
  • your P03 perspective notes, and
  • your independent work draft from P06.

Checkpoint Task

Write a short narrative scene of 10-12 sentences using one shared event and two character perspectives.

Your narrative must include:

  • one shared event,
  • two clearly different character perspectives,
  • specific details that reveal what each character values,
  • at least one transition or clear shift between perspectives, and
  • correct grammar, punctuation, capitalization, and spelling.

Mastery Criteria

  • The narrative includes a clear shared event.
  • Each perspective is distinct and purposeful.
  • Details reveal character perspective instead of simply naming emotions.
  • The writing is clear enough for a reader to follow independently.
