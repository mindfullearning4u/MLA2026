Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and definition. Submit them with Independent Work.

TermDefinition
multiple perspectivesTwo or more distinct viewpoints presented within a text.
narratorThe voice or agent through which a narrative is told.
reliabilityThe degree to which a narrator’s account can be trusted as accurate or complete.
frame narrativeA story that contains or introduces another story.
nonlinear chronologyEvents presented outside straightforward chronological order.
flashbackA shift to an event that occurred earlier.
foreshadowingA detail that suggests or prepares for a later development.
narrative gapInformation omitted, delayed, or unavailable to a narrator or reader.
contradictionAn inconsistency between accounts, details, or claims.
synthesisAn interpretation combining evidence from multiple perspectives while preserving complexity.
