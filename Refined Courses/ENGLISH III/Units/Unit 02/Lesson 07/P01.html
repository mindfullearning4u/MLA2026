Lesson Overview: Complex Narratives with Multiple Perspectives

Standards and Purpose

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.07 — Analyze how multiple perspectives and complex narrative structures develop meaning.

What You Will Learn

  • Track narrator knowledge, reliability, bias, and chronology.
  • Compare accounts of shared events.
  • Analyze gaps, contradictions, and structural order.
  • Synthesize a claim without flattening uncertainty.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study direct teaching and the model.
  3. Complete 18 Independent Work questions with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the five-question quiz; mastery is 80%.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain how competing narrators and nonlinear structure shape what readers know, believe, and continue to question.
