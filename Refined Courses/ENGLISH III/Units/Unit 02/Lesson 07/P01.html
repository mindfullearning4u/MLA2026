Lesson 7: Complex Narratives with Multiple Perspectives

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.01 - Write Complex Narratives with Multiple Perspectives.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.03 - Analyze Juxtaposition and Character Perspective; MLA.ENG3.R.09 - Analyze Figurative Language and Allegory; MLA.ENG3.W.04 - Revise for Clarity, Structure, and Style; MLA.ENG3.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How complex narratives can include more than one perspective.
  • How point of view, sequence, and detail shape a reader's understanding.
  • How to use contrast and figurative language to reveal perspective.
  • How to plan a narrative without confusing the reader.

Why This Matters

Real conflicts rarely look the same to every person involved. Complex narratives help readers understand how different characters interpret the same event.

This lesson helps you write narrative scenes that show perspective through action, detail, and voice.

What You Will Do

  • Study how two perspectives can describe the same event differently.
  • Plan a short multiple-perspective narrative scene.
  • Practice choosing details that reveal character perspective.
  • Revise for clarity so readers can follow each perspective.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will plan and write a short narrative response that establishes multiple perspectives using clear structure, purposeful details, and correct conventions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to write a narrative that helps the reader understand more than one character's perspective.

Ask TOR for Help

Ask TOR for help if your narrative has two perspectives but the reader may not be able to tell whose perspective is being shown.
