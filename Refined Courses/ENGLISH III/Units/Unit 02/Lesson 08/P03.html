P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Review allegory, contemporaneous authors, and narrative craft.

Review Passage

The city kept an old fountain in the square, though no water had flowed there for years. Some citizens called it history. Others called it thirst carved in stone. When the council voted to repair it, one speaker praised the return of beauty, while another asked who would pay for water in the neighborhoods beyond the square.

Notebook Task

  1. What symbolic detail could support an allegorical interpretation?
  2. What larger idea might that detail represent?
  3. How do the two speakers address the same topic differently?
  4. How could this situation become a multiple-perspective narrative?
  5. Write one synthesis sentence connecting at least two Unit 2 skills.
