Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all terms and definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

TermDefinition
analytical lensA focused way of examining a text, such as perspective, structure, symbolism, or context.
synthesisIntegration of multiple ideas or sources into a new coherent understanding.
interpretive claimA defensible statement explaining how a text creates meaning.
complexityMeaning produced through tension, ambiguity, qualification, or interacting elements.
corroborationSupport created when independent details or accounts agree.
tensionA meaningful conflict between ideas, values, expectations, or textual elements.
nuanceA subtle distinction that makes an interpretation more precise.
counterinterpretationA reasonable alternative reading of the same evidence.
evaluationA judgment based on clear criteria and evidence.
elaborationReasoning that develops how and why evidence supports a claim.
