Lesson Overview: Unit 2 Synthesis

Purpose

This lesson integrates literary elements, universal themes, juxtaposition, poetry and historical context, figurative language and allegory, contemporaneous authors, and multiple-perspective narratives.

What You Will Learn

  • Select the most useful analytical lens for a complex text.
  • Combine multiple lenses without producing a device list.
  • Synthesize claims, evidence, context, and reasoning.
  • Evaluate ambiguity and competing interpretations.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record vocabulary.
  2. Study the synthesis method and worked example.
  3. Complete 18 Independent Work questions with vocabulary.
  4. Complete the separate 12-question Checkpoint.
  5. Complete the Unit 2 assessment: 10 random questions from a 40-question bank, one attempt, 80% mastery.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will produce a coherent interpretation that integrates several literary concepts while preserving textual complexity.
