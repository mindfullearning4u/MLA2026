P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Connect poem evidence to theme and issue.

Poem and Context

Context: This poem is set during rapid industrial growth, when families moved from farms to factory towns.

The field sleeps under cinders now,
the plow hangs still beside the door.
Mother counts the factory bells
and calls them weather anymore.

Response Frame

Sentence 1: Identify the time-period issue.

Sentence 2: Include poem evidence.

Sentence 3: Explain how the evidence reflects the issue.

Sentence 4: State the theme the poem develops.

Notebook Task

Write a four-sentence response using the frame. Use at least one phrase from the poem.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The poem is about a field and bells.

Why it is weak: It summarizes images but does not connect them to the time-period issue.

Correct: The poem reflects industrial change because "factory bells" replace natural signs of time and weather.

Why it is stronger: It connects diction to context.
