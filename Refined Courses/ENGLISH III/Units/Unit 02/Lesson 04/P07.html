Checkpoint Poem and Context

Context: This poem is set during a period of public protest over unequal working conditions.

Our shoes line up outside the mill,
dust-gray, split, and still.
Inside, the wheels sing louder than names,
but names are what we will.

Checkpoint Task

Write a five-sentence response explaining how the poem reflects themes and issues of its time period. Use at least one quoted phrase.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your notes showing the issue, theme, poem evidence, and explanation plan.

Submission Workflow

Submit your final response in the checkpoint area.

Mastery Criteria

  • Connect poem evidence to the context note.
  • Explain a theme or issue of the time period.
  • Avoid unsupported claims.
  • Use clear conventions and vocabulary.
