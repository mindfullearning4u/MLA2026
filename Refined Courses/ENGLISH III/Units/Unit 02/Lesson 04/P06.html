P06 Independent Work

Analyze a poem in historical context.

Poem and Context

Context: This poem is set during a period when families migrated to crowded cities for work.

We packed the moon in a paper sack,
left the porch light burning blue.
The city window held no stars,
but rent was due.

Part A

Identify the issue reflected in the context note.

Part B

Choose two phrases from the poem that connect to the issue.

Part C

Write a four-sentence response explaining how the poem reflects migration and economic pressure.
