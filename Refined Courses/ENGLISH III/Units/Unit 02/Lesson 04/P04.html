P04 Worked Example

Study how to use context without guessing beyond the evidence.

Worked Example Text

Context: This poem is set during wartime, when families waited for letters from soldiers.

The mailbox leans into the road,
paint peeled white as bone.
Each morning, Father lifts the latch
and lowers it alone.

Worked Example 1: Context

The context tells us the poem connects to wartime separation and waiting for news.

Worked Example 2: Evidence

The repeated action "lifts the latch / and lowers it alone" shows waiting and disappointment.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The father knows exactly what happened in the war.

Why it is weak: The poem shows waiting, not certainty.

Correct: The poem reflects wartime uncertainty because the father checks the mailbox each morning and finds no letter.

Why it is correct: It stays grounded in poem evidence and context.

Worked Example Review

Example 1: If the question asks for meaning, identify the claim or central idea first.

Example 2: If the question asks for evidence, choose the exact detail that proves the claim.

Example 3: If the question asks for analysis, explain how the detail affects the reader, argument, or interpretation.

Common Mistake: A response that only summarizes the topic does not show mastery because it does not explain the author's choices.
