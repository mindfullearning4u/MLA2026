Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work.

TermDefinition
speakerThe voice that speaks within a poem; not automatically the poet.
stanzaA grouped set of lines that functions as a structural unit in a poem.
line breakThe point where one poetic line ends, shaping pace, emphasis, or ambiguity.
enjambmentContinuation of a sentence or phrase beyond a line break without terminal punctuation.
caesuraA strong pause within a poetic line.
meterA recurring pattern of stressed and unstressed syllables.
allusionAn indirect reference to a known person, event, text, place, or idea.
historical contextThe period events and social conditions relevant to a text’s creation or setting.
social issueA condition or conflict affecting groups within a society.
enduring themeAn insight about human experience that remains significant across periods.
