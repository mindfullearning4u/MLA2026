P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Read a poem with context.

Notebook Title

Poetry and Time Period Context

Vocabulary

  • Context: background information about a time, place, or issue.
  • Diction: a poet's word choice.
  • Issue: a concern or problem people are facing.
  • Reflect: show or reveal something about a time period.

Context Note

This original poem is set during a period of rapid industrial growth, when many families moved from farms to factory towns.

Poem

The field sleeps under cinders now,
the plow hangs still beside the door.
Mother counts the factory bells
and calls them weather anymore.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the context. The poem connects farming and factory life.

Step 2: Notice diction: "cinders," "factory bells," and "plow."

Step 3: Connect diction to issue. The poem reflects the shift from farm labor to factory labor.

Step 4: State the theme: industrial change can reshape family life and people's relationship to nature.

Notebook Task

Copy the context note and poem. Then list two words from the poem that connect to the time period issue.
