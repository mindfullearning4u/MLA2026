P05 Guided Practice

Practice polishing style, precision, and conventions.

Directions

Read each draft sentence and choose the revision that best improves publication polish. The Moodle guided practice matches these skills.

  1. Look for vague wording.
  2. Check whether the tone fits academic analysis.
  3. Choose precise language.
  4. Make sure conventions support meaning.

Before You Start

The best polished revision is clear and precise. It does not add unrelated ideas or make the sentence overly complicated.
