Source Detail

After the final ferry route closes, residents continue placing handwritten schedules in the empty terminal window.

Paragraph to Polish

The continued placement of schedules by the residents is an indication of the fact that the terminal remains meaningful even though ferry transportation no longer occurs there. The schedules are handwritten by hand and they do not control actual departures. However, they create a repetition of the old public routine. This shows in a very clear way that the community is engaged in the preservation of memory through action.
