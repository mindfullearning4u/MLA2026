P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Publication Polish

Vocabulary

Style is the way writing sounds and feels. Precision means choosing exact words. Polish means preparing writing for a final reader. Publication-ready writing is clear, controlled, and carefully edited.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the sentence for meaning. Make sure the sentence says what you intend.

Step 2: Identify vague words such as thing, good, bad, stuff, many, or important.

Step 3: Replace vague words with precise words that match the subject and tone.

Step 4: Check conventions last so grammar and punctuation support the final meaning.

Notebook Task

Copy this sentence: The author's word choice makes the scene feel bad and important.

List two vague words. Then write a polished version that names the tone and explains the importance more precisely.
