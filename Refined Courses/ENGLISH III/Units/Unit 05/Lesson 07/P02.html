Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit the notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
style The characteristic choices in diction, syntax, rhythm, emphasis, and presentation that shape expression.
conciseness The quality of expressing necessary meaning without avoidable words or repetition.
nominalization A noun formed from another part of speech, often turning an action into an abstraction.
emphasis Special prominence given to an idea through placement, structure, repetition, or wording.
sentence rhythm The pattern created by sentence length, structure, stress, and punctuation.
variety Purposeful differences in sentence structure and length that support meaning.
redundancy Unnecessary repetition of meaning.
proofreading The final inspection for surface errors after substantive revision and editing are complete.
style sheet A record of publication-specific decisions about capitalization, spelling, formatting, and terminology.
publication readiness The condition in which content, evidence, organization, style, citations, format, and conventions meet final requirements.
