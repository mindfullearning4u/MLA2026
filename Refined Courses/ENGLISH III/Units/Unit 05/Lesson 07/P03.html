📘 ENG3 | Unit 05 | Lesson 07

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Polish a short analytical paragraph.

Draft Paragraph

The writer uses words that make the place seem scary. The hallway is described as dark and empty, and this is good because it helps the reader know the character feels nervous. This is important to the story.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify words that sound too general: words, scary, good, important.

Step 2: Replace them with precise literary language: diction, ominous, effective, reveals.

Step 3: Keep the same meaning, but make the style more academic.

Step 4: Check that punctuation and sentence boundaries are correct.

Notebook Task

Rewrite the paragraph in a more polished academic style. Keep the evidence about the dark, empty hallway. Then write one sentence naming two words you improved.
