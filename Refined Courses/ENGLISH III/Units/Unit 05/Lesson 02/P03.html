P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use derivations to reason through unfamiliar vocabulary.

Word-Meaning Process

Step 1: Look for a familiar root, prefix, or suffix.

Step 2: Think of related words in the same family.

Step 3: Use the sentence context to test your prediction.

Step 4: Restate the meaning in your own words.

Model

Word: audible

Related word: audio

Meaning prediction: able to be heard

Sentence: The speaker's final phrase was barely audible. Paraphrase: The audience could hardly hear the speaker's final phrase.

Notebook Task

Use the word-meaning process with these words: portable, revision, visible, and credibility. For each word, write the word part you recognize, a related word, a predicted meaning, and a sentence paraphrase.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Revision means vision because both words contain vision.

Why it is weak: This ignores the prefix re-, which means again, and does not use context.

Correct: Revision means seeing again or changing a piece of writing after looking at it again.

Why it is correct: The meaning uses the root and prefix together.
