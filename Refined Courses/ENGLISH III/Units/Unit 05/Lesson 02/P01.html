P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.02 - Apply knowledge of etymology and derivations to determine meanings of words and phrases in grade-level content.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.V.01, MLA.ENG3.R.10

What You Will Learn

You will learn how word origins, roots, prefixes, suffixes, and word families help you determine meaning in complex texts. You will use etymology as a reasoning tool, not as a memorization list.

Why This Matters

Grade-level texts often contain unfamiliar academic words. If you can break a word into meaningful parts and connect it to related words, you can make a strong meaning prediction before checking a dictionary.

What You Will Do

  • Break academic words into roots, prefixes, and suffixes.
  • Use derivations to connect words in the same family.
  • Apply word-family knowledge to paraphrase complex sentences.
  • Use academic vocabulary accurately in speaking and writing.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can use word parts and related words to make a reasonable meaning prediction, confirm it with context, and use the word accurately.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can use word origins and word families to understand unfamiliar academic words.

Ask TOR for Help: If you can identify a word part but cannot connect it to meaning, ask TOR for help finding related words.
