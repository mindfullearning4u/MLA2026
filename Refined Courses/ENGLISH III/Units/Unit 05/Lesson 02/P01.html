Lesson Overview: Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families

Lesson Focus

This lesson uses word history and morphology to develop precise vocabulary. You will analyze roots and affixes, trace changes in meaning, compare members of word families, and verify etymological claims with reputable references before applying them to complex texts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.02 — Use etymology, derivations, and word relationships to determine and refine meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Integrate academic vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Determine meaning in context; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Gather and evaluate information; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Separate a word into meaningful roots and affixes without inventing false divisions.
  • Distinguish etymology from current meaning.
  • Use derivational patterns to compare grammatical roles and shades of meaning.
  • Recognize semantic broadening, narrowing, and shift.
  • Verify word histories in reputable dictionaries or linguistic reference works.
