P04 Worked Example

Study how word parts and word families help determine meaning.

Example 1: Credible

Word family: credible, credit, credibility, incredible.

Reasoning: These words connect to belief or trust. A credible source is one that can be trusted.

Example 2: Portable

Word family: portable, transport, import, export.

Reasoning: The root port connects to carrying. Something portable can be carried or moved.

Example 3: Visible

Word family: visible, vision, visual, revise.

Reasoning: The root vis connects to seeing. A visible detail is one that can be seen.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Incredible means very credible.

Why it is weak: The prefix in- can mean not, so it changes the meaning.

Correct: Incredible can mean not believable or difficult to believe.

Why it is correct: The meaning uses both the prefix and the root.
