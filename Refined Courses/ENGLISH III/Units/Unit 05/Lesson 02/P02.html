Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these vocabulary notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
etymology The documented history of a word’s origin, earlier forms, and development.
morpheme The smallest meaningful unit in a language.
root The central morpheme that carries a word family’s core meaning.
affix A bound morpheme added before, after, or within a root to modify meaning or function.
derivation The formation of a new word by adding or changing morphemes.
inflection A grammatical change to a word that does not create a new lexical item, such as plural or tense.
word family A group of words related through a shared root and derivational history.
semantic shift A historical change in a word’s meaning.
cognate A word related to another through descent from a common ancestral form.
false etymology An appealing but unsupported explanation of a word’s origin or structure.
