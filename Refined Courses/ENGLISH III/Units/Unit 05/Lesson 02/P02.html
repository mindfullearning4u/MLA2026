P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Word Parts and Word Families

Vocabulary

Etymology: The history or origin of a word.

Derivation: A word formed from another word or word part.

Root: A core word part that carries meaning.

Word family: A group of related words that share a root or origin.

Word Study Set

Root: script means write.

Related words: manuscript, inscription, transcript, description, prescribe.

Sentence: The inscription on the monument clarified the event being remembered.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Copy the word study set.

Step 2: Underline the shared root in each related word.

Step 3: Predict the meaning of inscription using the root script.

Step 4: Paraphrase the sentence in your own words.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Etymology, derivations, and word families

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.V.02

Use this sequence before you complete the notebook task. It makes the Grade 11 thinking process explicit so you do not have to guess what the skill requires.

  1. Read the full sentence and surrounding sentences before deciding meaning. Advanced vocabulary often depends on context and tone.
  2. Use word parts, etymology, derivations, and related words when the meaning is not obvious from context alone.
  3. Separate denotation from connotation. In Grade 11 texts, a word may carry a historical, rhetorical, or emotional effect beyond its basic definition.
  4. Choose the meaning or word that best fits the author's purpose, audience, and tone.
