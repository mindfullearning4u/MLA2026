P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Word Parts and Word Families

Vocabulary

Etymology: The history or origin of a word.

Derivation: A word formed from another word or word part.

Root: A core word part that carries meaning.

Word family: A group of related words that share a root or origin.

Word Study Set

Root: script means write.

Related words: manuscript, inscription, transcript, description, prescribe.

Sentence: The inscription on the monument clarified the event being remembered.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Copy the word study set.

Step 2: Underline the shared root in each related word.

Step 3: Predict the meaning of inscription using the root script.

Step 4: Paraphrase the sentence in your own words.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or passage detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two interpretations, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
