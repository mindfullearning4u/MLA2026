Lesson Overview: Context, Connotation, Denotation, and Tone

Lesson Focus

This lesson examines how precise word choices operate simultaneously through denotation, connotation, context, register, and pattern. You will trace how diction creates and shifts tone without reducing tone analysis to a single adjective.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Determine how words and phrases function in context, including connotative and figurative meanings.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Integrate academic vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.V.02 — Use word relationships and etymology; MLA.ENG3.R.06 — Analyze tone and voice; MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Draw evidence from literary texts; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish denotation from context-specific connotation.
  • Analyze diction as a pattern rather than isolated “loaded words.”
  • Identify mixed, shifting, and layered tones.
  • Explain how register, syntax, imagery, and semantic fields interact with diction.
  • Use external references only when a passage-specific word or allusion genuinely requires verification.
