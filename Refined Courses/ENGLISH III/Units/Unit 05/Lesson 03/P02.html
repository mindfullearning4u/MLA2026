Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
denotation A word’s direct or conventional dictionary meaning.
connotation An association, attitude, or emotional implication attached to a word in context.
diction A writer’s purposeful selection of words and expressions.
tone The attitude or stance created by a text toward its subject or audience.
register A level or variety of language suited to a social situation, field, or audience.
semantic field A group of words connected by a shared area of meaning.
tonal shift A significant change in a text’s attitude or emotional stance.
valence The positive, negative, or neutral emotional direction associated with an expression.
euphemism A mild or indirect expression used in place of one considered harsh or uncomfortable.
dysphemism A deliberately harsh or disparaging expression used in place of a more neutral one.
