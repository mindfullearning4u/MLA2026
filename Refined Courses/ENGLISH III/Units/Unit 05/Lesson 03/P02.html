P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Denotation, Connotation, and Tone

Vocabulary

Denotation: The literal dictionary meaning of a word.

Connotation: The feeling or association a word suggests.

Tone: The writer's attitude toward the subject or audience.

Context clue: A nearby word, phrase, or sentence that helps reveal meaning.

Text Set

Sentence A: The speaker called the plan bold.

Sentence B: The speaker called the plan reckless.

Both words can describe risk, but bold suggests admiration while reckless suggests criticism.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Write the denotation of bold and reckless.

Step 2: Label each word's connotation as positive, negative, or neutral.

Step 3: Explain how each word changes the speaker's tone.

Step 4: Write one original sentence using a word with a clear connotation.
