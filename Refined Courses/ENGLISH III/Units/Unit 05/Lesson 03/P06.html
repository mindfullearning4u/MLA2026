P06 Independent Work

Practice analyzing word choice and tone.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Text: The official praised the citizens as persistent in one speech, but called the same citizens stubborn in a later letter.

Part A

Write the denotation and connotation of persistent and stubborn.

Part B

Explain how each word changes the official's tone toward the citizens.

Part C

Write a three-sentence analysis using the academic words connotation, tone, and context.
