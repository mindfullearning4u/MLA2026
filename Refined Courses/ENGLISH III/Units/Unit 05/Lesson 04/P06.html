P06 Independent Work

Practice choosing precise meaning with word relationships and reference tools.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A

Choose the more precise word for each pair: cautious/prudent, claim/insist, summarize/analyze.

Part B

Write a dictionary-style explanation for why your selected word fits the context.

Part C

Write a two-sentence paraphrase of a complex sentence using precise academic vocabulary.
