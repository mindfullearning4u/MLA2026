P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use reference materials to confirm precise meaning.

Process

Step 1: Read the context.

Step 2: Identify possible meanings or synonyms.

Step 3: Check a reference tool or usage note.

Step 4: Choose the word that fits meaning and tone.

Model

Context: The speaker gently offers one possible answer.

Best word: suggests.

Why: Suggests is less forceful than insists and more precise than says.

Notebook Task

Choose the most precise word for each context: analyze/evaluate/summarize; cautious/fearful/prudent; claim/insist/suggest. Explain your choice in one sentence each.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Analyze and summarize mean the same thing because both discuss a text.

Why it is weak: It ignores precise meaning.

Correct: Summarize means restate main points, while analyze means explain how parts create meaning.

Why it is correct: It uses word relationships to separate related but different academic tasks.
