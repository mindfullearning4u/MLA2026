P04 Worked Example

Study how precise word choice improves paraphrase and analysis.

Example 1: Analyze vs. Summarize

Summarize means restate main ideas. Analyze means explain how or why a text works. A prompt asking how structure creates meaning requires analyze, not summarize.

Example 2: Prudent vs. Fearful

Both words may involve caution, but prudent suggests wise care while fearful suggests anxiety. A careful policy choice is more likely prudent than fearful.

Example 3: Reference Check

A thesaurus may list cautious and fearful as related, but a dictionary or usage note shows their tones differ. Reference materials help choose the precise word.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Any synonym from a thesaurus will fit the sentence.

Why it is weak: Synonyms can differ in tone, strength, and exact meaning.

Correct: Check the context and usage note before choosing a synonym.

Why it is correct: Precision depends on meaning and context, not just similarity.
