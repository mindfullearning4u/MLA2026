Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit the notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
synonym A word sharing a similar core meaning with another but not necessarily identical use or connotation.
antonym A word expressing an opposite or contrasting meaning.
analogy A relationship in which corresponding similarities clarify how words or concepts are connected.
collocation A conventional pattern in which particular words commonly occur together.
usage label A dictionary marker identifying register, region, field, formality, or acceptability.
sense One distinct meaning of a word within a dictionary entry.
corpus A searchable collection of authentic spoken or written language used to study patterns.
thesaurus A reference work organizing words by related meanings; it must be checked against context and usage.
disciplinary glossary A field-specific reference defining specialized terminology.
precision The quality of expressing the intended meaning with exact, appropriate wording.
