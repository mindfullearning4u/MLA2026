P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Word Relationships and Reference Tools

Vocabulary

Synonym: A word with a similar meaning.

Antonym: A word with an opposite meaning.

Reference material: A tool such as a dictionary, thesaurus, glossary, or usage note.

Precision: Choosing the word that fits the exact meaning and tone.

Word Set

Words: argue, claim, insist, suggest.

Context: The writer does not force the reader to agree; she presents evidence and lets the reader consider it.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Rank the words from least forceful to most forceful.

Step 2: Choose the best word for the context.

Step 3: Explain why the other words are less precise.

Step 4: Write a paraphrase using your chosen word.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Word relationships, reference materials, and precise meaning

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.V.03

Use this sequence before you complete the notebook task. It makes the Grade 11 thinking process explicit so you do not have to guess what the skill requires.

  1. Read the full sentence and surrounding sentences before deciding meaning. Advanced vocabulary often depends on context and tone.
  2. Use word parts, etymology, derivations, and related words when the meaning is not obvious from context alone.
  3. Separate denotation from connotation. In Grade 11 texts, a word may carry a historical, rhetorical, or emotional effect beyond its basic definition.
  4. Choose the meaning or word that best fits the author's purpose, audience, and tone.
