Lesson Overview: Word Relationships, Reference Materials, and Precise Meaning

Lesson Focus

This lesson develops independent vocabulary judgment. You will compare related words, choose reference materials strategically, interpret dictionary entries and usage evidence, and justify the most precise word for a specific analytical context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.03 — Use word relationships and authoritative reference materials to determine and refine precise meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.04 — Determine meaning in context; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Integrate academic vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.V.02 — Apply etymology and derivation; MLA.ENG3.W.08 — Evaluate information sources; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Distinguish synonyms by connotation, register, intensity, collocation, and grammatical pattern.
  • Select the right reference for pronunciation, usage, etymology, disciplinary meaning, or historical evidence.
  • Read complete dictionary entries rather than choosing the first definition.
  • Use corpora or usage examples to test authentic patterns.
  • Defend a precise word choice using context and reliable evidence.
