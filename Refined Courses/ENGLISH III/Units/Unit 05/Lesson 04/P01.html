Word Relationships, Reference Materials, and Precise Meaning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.03 - Apply knowledge of context clues, figurative language, word relationships, reference materials, and background knowledge to determine connotative and denotative meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.V.02, MLA.ENG3.R.10

What You Will Learn

You will learn how synonyms, antonyms, word families, dictionaries, thesauruses, and context work together to help you choose precise meanings.

Why This Matters

Words that look similar are not always interchangeable. Precise meaning helps you paraphrase accurately and write with control.

What You Will Do

  • Use reference materials to confirm meaning.
  • Compare synonyms and related words.
  • Select precise words for paraphrase and analysis.
  • Explain why one word fits a context better than another.

What Mastery Looks Like

You can use context and reference tools to choose the most precise meaning or word.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can use word relationships and reference materials to understand and choose precise words.

Ask TOR for Help: If two synonyms both seem correct, ask TOR to compare their exact meanings and connotations.
