Checkpoint Task

Reorder the sentences into a clear analytical paragraph.

A. This detail shows that the setting mirrors the character's uncertainty.

B. The dim hallway creates a mood of hesitation before the character makes a choice.

C. The narrator describes the hallway as "half-lit and silent."

D. Because the setting feels incomplete, the character's decision also feels unresolved.

Write the correct order. Then add one transition phrase and explain why the order improves the paragraph.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your order begins with the claim.
  • Your evidence appears before the explanation.
  • Your transition clarifies the relationship between ideas.
  • Your explanation shows why the order improves coherence.
