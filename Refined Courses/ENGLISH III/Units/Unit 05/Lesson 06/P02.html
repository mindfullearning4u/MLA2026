Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit the notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
organization The purposeful arrangement of ideas and evidence to guide a reader through reasoning.
line of reasoning The connected sequence showing how claims, evidence, and explanations lead to a conclusion.
structural map A concise outline describing the actual function of each section in a draft.
paragraph function The specific job a paragraph performs within the larger argument.
cohesion The linguistic connections that link sentences and paragraphs.
coherence The overall logical clarity and consistency of a text’s ideas.
transition A word, phrase, sentence, or structural move that clarifies a relationship between ideas.
counterargument A plausible alternative position or interpretation addressed by the writer.
pivot A purposeful shift from one stage of reasoning to another.
synthesis conclusion An ending that combines the analysis and explains its broader significance or implication.
