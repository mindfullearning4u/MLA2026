P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook title: Revising Organization

Vocabulary

Structure is the way a piece of writing is built. Organization is the order of ideas. Transition is a word or phrase that shows how ideas connect. Coherence means the writing holds together clearly.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Label each sentence by its job. Common jobs include claim, context, evidence, explanation, and closing insight.

Step 2: Put the claim before the evidence. Readers need to know what the paragraph is trying to prove before they read the proof.

Step 3: Place context before quoted or specific evidence when the evidence would otherwise feel sudden.

Step 4: Put explanation after evidence. Evidence does not speak for itself; the writer must explain how it supports the claim.

Notebook Task

Copy these sentence jobs into your notebook in the order that usually creates a clear analytical paragraph: evidence, closing insight, claim, explanation, context.

Then write one sentence explaining why the evidence should not usually come before the claim.
