📘 ENG3 | Unit 05 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Revise a paragraph plan for organization.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Work carefully; this practice prepares you for the checkpoint.

Part A: Label Sentence Jobs

Label each sentence as claim, context, evidence, explanation, or closing insight.

A. This contrast shows that the narrator values independence more than approval.

B. The narrator refuses the invitation even though the crowd expects agreement.

C. The scene suggests that independence requires resisting public pressure.

Part B: Reorder

Put the sentences from Part A into the clearest order. Write the letters only first. Then rewrite the paragraph in that order.

Part C: Add a Transition

Add one transition phrase that helps the evidence connect to the explanation. Do not add a transition that changes the meaning.
