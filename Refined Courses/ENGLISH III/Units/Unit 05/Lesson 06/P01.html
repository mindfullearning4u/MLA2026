Lesson Overview: Revision for Structure and Organization

Lesson Focus

This lesson moves beyond sentence correction to architectural revision. You will analyze how introductions, paragraph sequence, transitions, counterarguments, and conclusions create a coherent line of reasoning for complex analytical writing.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.05 — Strengthen writing through revision of structure, organization, and development.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.W.04 — Produce clear, coherent writing; MLA.ENG3.W.01 — Develop claims and counterclaims; MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Use textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Map the existing structure of a draft before rearranging it.
  • Sequence paragraphs by reasoning rather than by the order evidence was discovered.
  • Use transitions to state relationships instead of hiding weak organization.
  • Place counterarguments where they genuinely test the claim.
  • Design conclusions that synthesize implications rather than repeat the introduction.
