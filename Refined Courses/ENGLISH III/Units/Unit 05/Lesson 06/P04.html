Worked Example: Reorganizing an Analytical Essay

Draft Structure

  1. Background on the abandoned station.
  2. Analysis of the final bell scene.
  3. Thesis: the story connects memory with renewed civic purpose.
  4. Analysis of Mara’s early dismissal of the station.
  5. Counterargument: the restoration may be nostalgic.
  6. Analysis of interviews and classroom display.
  7. Conclusion repeating the thesis.

Step 1 — Reverse-Outline Diagnosis

The thesis arrives after evidence, character development is out of sequence, and the final bell is interpreted before the interviews make its meaning possible. The counterargument is useful but arrives before the strongest evidence.

Step 2 — Governing Pattern

A developmental pattern best fits: initial dismissal → recovery of testimony → public reconstruction → counterinterpretation → qualified meaning of final bell.

Step 3 — Revised Structure

  1. Focused introduction and thesis.
  2. Mara’s initial view of the station as obsolete.
  3. Interviews transform private memories into a shared record.
  4. Classroom display gives the station a new civic function.
  5. Counterargument: restoration risks sentimental nostalgia.
  6. Final bell scene answers the concern by connecting memory with present action.
  7. Synthesis conclusion about preservation as active interpretation.

Step 4 — Relational Transitions

Development: “Mara’s interviews complicate her initial dismissal...”

Counterargument: “Yet transforming the station into a display could reduce history to comforting nostalgia...”

Response: “The final scene resists that simplification by...”

Step 5 — Revised Conclusion

The restored station matters not because it recreates the past unchanged, but because it turns surviving memories into a resource for present participation. The final bell therefore marks neither a returning train nor a completed history; it calls the community to continue interpreting what the place means.

Why the Revision Works

Every paragraph now has one primary function, evidence develops before it is tested, and the counterargument strengthens rather than interrupts the final claim.
