P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Putting It All Together: Unit 5 synthesis

This lesson combines the learning from Lessons 1-7. You will use the unit standards together in one connected synthesis process.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.ENG3.L.01, MLA.ENG3.V.02, MLA.ENG3.V.03, MLA.ENG3.W.04

What You Will Learn

You will learn how vocabulary knowledge, word precision, conventions, feedback, and revision work together.

What You Will Do

You will analyze a draft, improve word choice, revise structure and style, and explain the purpose of your changes.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by producing a polished revision with clear explanation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student terms: you are making writing clearer, more precise, and more polished without changing the intended meaning.
