Lesson Overview: Putting It All Together — Unit 5 Synthesis

Lesson Focus

Primary standard: MLA.ENG3.L.01 — apply standard English conventions, precise language, and purposeful style in publication-ready writing.

Supporting standards: MLA.ENG3.V.01 — determine and apply word meaning; MLA.ENG3.W.01 — develop clear, coherent writing through planning, revision, editing, and publication; MLA.ENG3.R.10 — analyze how language choices shape meaning and tone.

What You Will Learn

  • Integrate vocabulary, conventions, precision, tone, organization, and publication polish in one editorial workflow.
  • Use editorial judgment to decide which revisions improve meaning and which would erase an author’s voice.
  • Verify sources, quotations, formatting, and accessibility before publication.

Why This Matters

Publication-ready writing is not merely error-free. It communicates a defensible idea, guides a reader through evidence, and uses language that is accurate, ethical, accessible, and appropriate for its audience.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record and verify Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Study the integrated editorial process and diverse examples on Page 3.
  3. Follow the complete model on Page 4.

  5. Complete the separate Checkpoint on Page 6.
  6. Prepare for the Unit 5 Final Assessment: 10 questions drawn randomly from a 40-question bank, one attempt, 80% required.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will explain editorial decisions, revise substantial passages without distorting meaning, verify passage-specific vocabulary and sources, and produce clear publication-ready prose. Submissions require at least 80%; use TOR feedback to correct and resubmit work below mastery.
