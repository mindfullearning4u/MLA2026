P04 Worked Example

Synthesize Unit 5 skills in one revision.

Example

Draft: The character's conflict show that things are hard, it is important.

Step 1: Correct the convention error: conflict shows.

Step 2: Replace vague wording: things are hard.

Step 3: Fix the comma splice.

Revision: The character's conflict shows that loyalty becomes difficult when personal values clash with family expectations.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Fix only the spelling and ignore unclear meaning.

A final review should check meaning, word precision, structure, style, and conventions.

Correct: Review the draft for all Unit 5 skill areas before submitting the final version.

This gives the reader a clearer and more professional final draft.

Worked Example Review

Example 1: If the question asks for meaning, identify the claim or central idea first.

Example 2: If the question asks for evidence, choose the exact detail that proves the claim.

Example 3: If the question asks for analysis, explain how the detail affects the reader, argument, or interpretation.

Common Mistake: A response that only summarizes the topic does not show mastery because it does not explain the author's choices.
