Notebook title: Unit 5 Skill Review

Review List

Write these Unit 5 skills in your notebook: conventions, etymology, context clues, connotation, denotation, feedback, clarity, organization, style, precision, and publication polish.

Step-by-Step Review

Step 1: Decide whether a problem is about meaning, word choice, sentence correctness, or organization.

Step 2: Choose the strategy that fits the problem. Use roots for unfamiliar words, context for meaning, feedback for revision, and conventions for correctness.

Step 3: Revise only what needs to be revised. Do not change the meaning unless the task asks you to change the claim.

Notebook Task
