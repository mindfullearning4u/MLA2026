Notebook Title: Unit 5 Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Connotation: the emotional or suggested meaning of a word.
  • Denotation: the direct dictionary meaning of a word.
  • Revision: improving meaning, structure, style, and correctness.

Synthesis Teaching

Strong revision is more than fixing spelling. A writer must decide whether the issue is word meaning, tone, sentence structure, organization, or conventions. Then the writer chooses a revision that improves clarity while preserving the intended claim.

Notebook Task

Make a revision checklist with four categories: vocabulary precision, sentence structure, organization, and conventions. Under each category, write one question a writer should ask.
