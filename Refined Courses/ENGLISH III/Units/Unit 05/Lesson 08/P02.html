Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Verify at least two terms with a reputable online dictionary or encyclopedia and record the source title. Submit these notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
editorial workflow A planned sequence for reviewing meaning, organization, style, conventions, sources, and presentation.
substantive revision Large-scale revision that improves ideas, reasoning, evidence, or organization.
copyediting Editing for clarity, consistency, usage, grammar, and style.
proofreading The final review for surface errors after content and layout are stable.
style consistency Steady use of agreed conventions for wording, capitalization, citations, numbers, and formatting.
rhetorical effectiveness The degree to which communication achieves its purpose for a particular audience.
source integrity Accurate, ethical representation and attribution of information from sources.
publication standard A defined expectation for accuracy, clarity, accessibility, formatting, and readiness to share.
revision log A record of significant changes and the reasons for making them.
editorial judgment Reasoned decisions about what to change, preserve, verify, or question in a text.
