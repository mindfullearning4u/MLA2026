Lesson Overview: Standard English Conventions in Complex Analytical Writing

Lesson Focus

This lesson treats conventions as tools for controlling meaning in sophisticated analysis. You will revise sentence boundaries, punctuation, agreement, reference, capitalization, spelling, and parallel structure while preserving the complexity of a writer’s reasoning.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Demonstrate command of standard English grammar, usage, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling in complex writing.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.W.04 — Produce clear, coherent writing appropriate to task and audience; MLA.ENG3.W.05 — Strengthen writing through planning and revision; MLA.ENG3.R.01 — Use precise evidence; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Integrate academic vocabulary accurately.

What You Will Learn

  • Diagnose errors without flattening complex ideas into short, repetitive sentences.
  • Control independent and dependent clauses with correct punctuation.
  • Maintain subject–verb agreement, pronoun reference, and parallel structure across long sentences.
  • Use punctuation to signal logical relationships among claims, evidence, qualifications, and counterarguments.
  • Edit systematically at sentence, paragraph, and final-proof stages.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary and definitions.
  2. Study the diagnostic revision process and diverse examples on Page 3.
  3. Follow the fully modeled analytical revision on Page 4.
  4. Complete Page 5 Independent Work and submit it with vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.
