Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every lesson-specific term and its complete definition. Submit these vocabulary notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
independent clause A word group containing a subject and verb that expresses a complete thought.
dependent clause A word group containing a subject and verb that cannot stand alone as a complete sentence.
sentence boundary The grammatical point where one complete sentence ends and another begins.
comma splice An error created when independent clauses are joined only by a comma.
subordination The grammatical arrangement that marks one idea as dependent on or less central than another.
coordination The grammatical joining of elements with equal structural importance.
parallel structure The use of matching grammatical forms for ideas serving the same function.
pronoun antecedent The noun or noun phrase to which a pronoun refers.
modifier A word, phrase, or clause that describes or limits another element.
syntactic control Purposeful management of sentence structure so grammatical relationships and intended emphasis remain clear.
