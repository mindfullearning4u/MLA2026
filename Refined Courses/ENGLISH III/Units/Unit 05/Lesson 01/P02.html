P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Conventions That Clarify Analysis

Vocabulary

Sentence boundary: The place where one complete sentence ends and another begins.

Run-on sentence: Two or more complete sentences joined without correct punctuation.

Comma splice: Two complete sentences joined only by a comma.

Fragment: An incomplete sentence that is missing a subject, verb, or complete thought.

Analytical Draft

The author uses the empty station as a symbol it shows public memory because the benches are worn by many travelers. The source explains that transit spaces can represent movement and connection, this supports the interpretation.

Notebook Directions

Step 1: Copy the draft into your notebook.

Step 2: Underline the two sentence-boundary problems.

Step 3: Rewrite the draft as two or three correct sentences.

Step 4: Circle one academic vocabulary word that helps the analysis sound precise.
