P04 Worked Example

Study how to revise conventions without weakening the analysis.

Example 1: Run-On Sentence

Draft: The mural preserves history it shows neighborhood stores and family recipes.

Revision: The mural preserves history because it shows neighborhood stores and family recipes.

Reasoning: The word because joins the ideas into one clear sentence.

Example 2: Fragment

Draft: Because the author repeats the image of the locked gate.

Revision: The author creates emphasis by repeating the image of the locked gate.

Reasoning: The revision has a subject, verb, and complete thought.

Example 3: Academic Vocabulary

Draft: The source says the setting is a big deal.

Revision: The source clarifies the setting's symbolic importance.

Reasoning: Clarifies and symbolic are precise academic words.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The writer uses symbolism, because the clock stopped.

Why it is weak: The comma separates the verb from the explanation and makes the sentence choppy.

Correct: The writer uses symbolism because the stopped clock represents a past that will not move forward.

Why it is correct: The sentence is complete, correctly punctuated, and analytical.
