P06 Independent Work

Practice revising conventions in your own analytical writing.

Instructions

Use the short analytical draft below. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Draft: The image of the sealed well develops community memory, it shows that people still gather around the place. Because the source explains rituals can preserve connection.

Part A

Identify one comma splice and one fragment in the draft.

Part B

Rewrite the draft as two complete, correctly punctuated sentences.

Part C

Revise one word or phrase to make the analysis more academic and precise.
