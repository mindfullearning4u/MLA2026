Literary Passage

The harbor bell rang whenever fog erased the channel markers. For years, Tomas pulled its rope from the western pier while ships answered from beyond the breakwater. After an automated signal was installed, the bell remained silent but was not removed.

During the first heavy fog of autumn, the new signal failed. Tomas reached the pier before the harbor crew, yet he stood beneath the bell without pulling. The rope had frayed to a pale thread. One hard movement might ring the bell once and destroy it.

Instead, Tomas struck the iron housing with a wrench. The sound was smaller than the old bell, but a fishing boat answered. By morning, the harbor crew had replaced the rope. Tomas wrote no report about saving the boat. In the maintenance log, he entered: “Temporary method effective. Original system requires care.”
