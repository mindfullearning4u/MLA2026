📘 ENG3 | Unit 05 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Revise a short analytical draft for clarity.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. You will use your work on the checkpoint page.

Part A: Diagnose the Feedback

Draft: The writer's evidence is important because it proves the point.

Feedback: Name the evidence and explain what point it proves.

Write one sentence explaining what is unclear in the draft.

Part B: Revise for Clarity

Write a revised version of the draft sentence. Your revision must name a specific kind of evidence and explain what the evidence proves.

Part C: Explain the Revision

Write two sentences explaining how your revision improves clarity. Mention at least one vague word you replaced.
