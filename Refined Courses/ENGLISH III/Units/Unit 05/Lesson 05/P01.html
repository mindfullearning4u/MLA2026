Lesson Overview: Feedback and Revision for Clarity

Lesson Focus

This lesson teaches you to interpret feedback as evidence about a reader’s experience, diagnose the underlying writing problem, and revise at the level of ideas, organization, evidence, sentences, and conventions without surrendering authorship.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.05 — Develop and strengthen writing through purposeful revision based on feedback.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.W.04 — Produce clear, coherent writing; MLA.ENG3.W.09 — Use textual evidence; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Apply standard conventions; MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Use precise academic vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • Translate broad feedback into a specific revision diagnosis.
  • Separate global revision from sentence-level editing.
  • Prioritize changes by effect on meaning and reader understanding.
  • Evaluate conflicting feedback against task, evidence, and audience.
  • Use peer, TOR, and AI feedback responsibly while retaining final judgment.
