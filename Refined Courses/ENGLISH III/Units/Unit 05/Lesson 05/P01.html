P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Feedback and Revision for Clarity

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.W.04 - Improve writing by considering feedback from adults, peers, and online editing tools, revising to improve clarity, structure, and style.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.L.01, MLA.ENG3.W.02, MLA.ENG3.W.03

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to use feedback to revise analytical writing so the claim, evidence, explanation, and sentence meaning are clear to a reader.

Why This Matters

Strong writers do not revise only to fix mistakes. They revise so readers can follow the thinking without guessing what a sentence, example, or explanation is supposed to mean.

What You Will Do

  • Read feedback and identify what it asks you to improve.
  • Revise vague or confusing analysis for clarity.
  • Keep the original meaning while improving sentence precision.
  • Check conventions that affect reader understanding.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can explain what feedback is asking for and revise a draft so the analysis is more specific, readable, and connected to evidence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: I can use feedback to make my writing clearer.

Ask TOR for Help: If you are unsure whether feedback asks for clarity, structure, style, or conventions, ask TOR to help sort the feedback before you revise.
