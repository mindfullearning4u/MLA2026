Worked Example: Revising from Conflicting Feedback

Draft Paragraph

The station’s clock symbolizes memory. It stops after the final train, and residents later gather beneath it. The station was built in 1908 during a period of rail expansion. Clocks were important to railroad schedules. The gathering proves the town cannot move forward.

Reader Feedback

  • Reader A: “Add historical context about railroad time.”
  • Reader B: “The history interrupts the literary analysis.”
  • TOR: “The final claim overstates what the gathering proves.”

Step 1 — Diagnosis

The context is relevant but disconnected. The paragraph also moves from plausible symbolism to an absolute conclusion unsupported by the evidence.

Step 2 — Revision Priorities

  1. Qualify the central interpretation.
  2. Integrate only the historical fact that clarifies the clock’s function.
  3. Explain the relationship between stopped public time and communal memory.
  4. Polish transitions and wording last.

Revised Paragraph

The stopped station clock suggests that the town’s shared memory remains attached to a place whose original function has ended. Because standardized railroad time once coordinated arrivals and departures, the clock represented both mechanical schedules and public routine. Its stopping after the final train marks the loss of that function. When residents later gather beneath it, however, the object gains a different role: it coordinates remembrance rather than travel. The gathering does not prove that the town rejects change; it shows that moving forward can include deliberate attention to what shaped the community.

Revision Rationale

The revision uses focused context to explain symbolism, adds reasoning between evidence and claim, and qualifies the conclusion. It addresses all three readers without following either peer suggestion mechanically.

Alternative Choice

If the assignment prohibited outside research, the writer could omit railroad history and analyze only the passage’s internal contrast between stopped clock and later gathering. The best revision depends on task requirements.
