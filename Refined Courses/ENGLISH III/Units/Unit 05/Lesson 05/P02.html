Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and complete definition. Submit these notes with Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Definition
feedback Information about how a reader understands and responds to a draft.
revision Substantive rethinking and reshaping of ideas, organization, evidence, or expression.
editing Correction and refinement at sentence and word level after major reasoning is stable.
global concern A problem affecting the whole text, such as purpose, claim, organization, or evidence.
local concern A limited problem involving a sentence, phrase, word, citation, or convention.
diagnosis A precise explanation of the cause underlying a reader’s difficulty.
revision priority The ranked importance of a proposed change based on its effect on meaning.
reader expectation What the task, genre, organization, or previous statements reasonably lead a reader to anticipate.
feedback conflict A situation in which readers recommend different or incompatible changes.
revision rationale A clear explanation of why a change improves the draft’s purpose, evidence, logic, or audience fit.
