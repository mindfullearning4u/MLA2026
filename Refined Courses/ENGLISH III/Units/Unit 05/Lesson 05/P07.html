P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can use feedback to revise for clarity.

TOR

If your revision changes the original meaning instead of clarifying it, ask TOR for help keeping the claim stable while improving clarity.

Submission Workflow

  1. Complete your notebook work from P06.
  2. Write a final revised sentence or short paragraph.
  3. Write a brief explanation of how feedback shaped your revision.
  4. Submit your checkpoint response in the course submission area.

Checkpoint Task

Draft: The paragraph explains the theme because the quote connects to the lesson.

Feedback: Be more specific. Name the theme, identify what the quote shows, and explain how it supports the lesson.

Revise the draft into one clear analytical sentence. Then write one sentence explaining the change you made.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your revision responds directly to the feedback.
  • Your revision keeps the original analytical purpose.
  • Your revision replaces vague wording with specific language.
  • Your explanation names how clarity improved.
