Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and complete definitions. Submit them with the Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
independent clause A word group with a subject and verb that expresses a complete thought.
dependent clause A word group with a subject and verb that cannot stand alone as a complete sentence.
subordination The placement of one idea in a dependent structure to clarify its relationship to a main idea.
coordination The grammatical joining of ideas of equal importance.
parallel structure The use of the same grammatical pattern for items serving the same function.
pronoun reference The clear relationship between a pronoun and its antecedent.
modifier A word, phrase, or clause that describes or limits another element.
sentence boundary The point where one complete sentence ends and another begins.
active voice A structure in which the subject performs the action of the verb.
concision The expression of meaning clearly without unnecessary words.
