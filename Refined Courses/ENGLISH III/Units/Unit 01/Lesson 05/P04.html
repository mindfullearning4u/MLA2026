Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The image suggests pressure the narrator feels watched.

Why it is wrong: The sentence blends two ideas without a clear connection.

Correct: The image suggests pressure because the narrator feels watched.

Why it is correct: The word because clearly connects the evidence to the explanation.

Teachable Explanation: A strong answer does more than choose the correct wording. It names the specific issue, corrects it, and explains why the correction fits the lesson skill, audience, evidence, or meaning.
