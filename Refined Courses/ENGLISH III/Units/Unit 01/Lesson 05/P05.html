Independent Work: Editing “The Photograph Table”

Directions

Read the passage and complete all 18 editing and analysis tasks. Submit responses with Page 2 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Passage

At the reunion, families placed old photographs across three folding tables. Elena found a picture of the neighborhood before the highway was built, it showed gardens where the exit ramp now curved. Standing beside her grandfather, the names of former residents were explained. He remembered who repaired bicycles, who organized rent meetings, and delivering soup during the winter blackout.

Elena wanted to label the photograph “The Community That Disappeared.” Her grandfather objected, because several families still lived nearby and others returned for festivals. “The road changed the neighborhood,” he said. “It did not erase every connection.” After listening, the label was revised by Elena. She wrote that the photograph documented both displacement and the relationships residents continued to maintain.

Visitors gathered around the new label. Some pointed to houses they recognized; others added names to blank cards. By evening, the table was crowded with corrections. The photograph had not become more accurate by itself, the community made its context more complete.

  5. Change the unnecessary passive voice in “the label was revised by Elena.”
  6. Correct the final comma splice.

  6. Identify one sentence where active voice improves responsibility or agency.
