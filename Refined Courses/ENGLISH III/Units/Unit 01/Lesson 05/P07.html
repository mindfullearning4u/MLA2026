P07 Checkpoint

Show that you can revise conventions to make an analytical response clear.

Checkpoint Task

Draft: the narrator calls the letter "creased from being opened too many times" this detail suggests emotional weight

Revise the draft into one or two correct analytical sentences. Then write one revision note explaining the convention choices you made.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear photo, scan, or typed copy of your corrected response and revision note from your notebook.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your final corrected response in the checkpoint area. Make sure the response uses standard conventions, includes the quoted phrase accurately, and explains the meaning clearly.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response corrects capitalization and sentence boundaries.
  • Your response integrates the quoted phrase clearly.
  • Your revision note explains how conventions support meaning.
  • Your final answer is readable without a teacher explaining it for you.
