Draft Passage

The student radio station planned its final broadcast, the building would be renovated during summer. Walking into the studio, the dusty equipment surprised Jalen. He wanted the program to honor former hosts, current students, and to explain the renovation.

Jalen interviewed Ms. Orr and Marcus. She said the station taught her to speak with confidence, but the pronoun made the draft confusing. Marcus described producing weather alerts during a flood. After reviewing both interviews, the final script was written by Jalen with fewer repeated phrases.

The broadcast did not only describe the past. It also announced a digital studio that students would use next year. Because preserving a program can include changing the tools that carry it.
