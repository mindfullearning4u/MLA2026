P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title

Write this title: Paraphrase with Relevant Textual Evidence

Vocabulary

  • Textual evidence: words, phrases, or details from a text that support an idea.
  • Relevant: directly connected to the point being made.
  • Paraphrase: a restatement of meaning in your own words.
  • Explanation: the sentence or sentences that show how evidence supports your thinking.

Step 1: Read the Passage

Passage: The speaker begins by describing a quiet street after a storm, but the scene is not only about weather. The broken branches and scattered leaves help the audience understand that the community has been disrupted and must decide how to rebuild.

Read the passage twice. First, understand the literal scene. Second, notice the larger meaning connected to the community.

Step 2: Write the Paraphrase

In your notebook, write one or two sentences that restate the passage. Your paraphrase must include both parts:

  1. The speaker describes a street after a storm.
  2. The scene represents a disrupted community that needs to rebuild.

Step 3: Choose Evidence

Copy one phrase from the passage that supports your paraphrase. Then write one sentence explaining why that evidence is relevant.

Sentence frame: The phrase "_____" is relevant because it shows _____.
