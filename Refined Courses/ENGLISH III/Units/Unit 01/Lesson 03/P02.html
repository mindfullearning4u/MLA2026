Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and complete definitions. Submit them with the Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
claim A focused, defensible statement that an analysis will support.
relevant evidence Textual information that directly supports the specific claim being made.
context The circumstances surrounding evidence that help a reader understand its meaning and significance.
quotation The exact words of a source reproduced with quotation marks and citation.
paraphrase A faithful restatement in genuinely new wording and sentence structure.
integration The grammatical and logical blending of evidence into a writer’s own sentence.
reasoning The explanation that connects evidence to a claim.
attribution Language that identifies the source or speaker of information.
patchwriting A weak paraphrase that retains too much source wording or structure.
distortion A change that makes the source meaning less accurate, exaggerated, or incomplete.
