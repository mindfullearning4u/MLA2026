Independent Work

Core interpretation: Responsible history replaces single-hero narratives with attributed, corroborated, and qualified accounts of shared action. Relevant evidence includes the shopkeepers’ receipts, the mayor’s authorization, Mara’s organizing, and Eli’s differentiated captions. Eli grows from impulsive contradiction to disciplined synthesis. The lantern symbolizes shared service rather than individual glory.

Paraphrase model: Eli checks several source types: an interview credits Mara with coordinating deliveries, a clerk remembers the mayor securing warehouse access, and two newspapers provide incomplete or inconsistent coverage. Students must preserve these differences rather than merge them into certainty.

Checkpoint

Core interpretation: Amara demonstrates that the orchard has measurable food and instructional value while acknowledging diseased trees and space needs. Relevant evidence includes harvest distribution, classroom use, and required removals. A strong paraphrase preserves both benefits and limitations. The mixed-use proposal is best supported because it responds to the complete record rather than one metric or an absolute position.
