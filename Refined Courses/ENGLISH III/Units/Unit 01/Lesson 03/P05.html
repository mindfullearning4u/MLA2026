P05 Guided Practice

Answer the five guided practice questions in Moodle. Each passage is included inside the question.

Before You Begin

  1. Read the passage and question completely.
  2. Find the paraphrase or evidence that keeps the meaning accurate.
  3. Choose the answer that connects evidence to meaning.

Question 1

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The essay argues that public art can help a city remember difficult history, but only if the artwork invites honest conversation instead of simple celebration.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase that public art should encourage honest reflection?

A. "public art can help a city"

B. "remember difficult history"

C. "invites honest conversation"

D. "instead of simple celebration"

Question 2

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The narrator describes the old house as carefully repaired but still marked by cracks in the foundation. The description suggests that recovery has begun, but earlier damage has not disappeared.

Question: Which paraphrase is most accurate?

A. The house is fully repaired and has no signs of damage.

B. The house shows both repair and remaining evidence of past harm.

C. The narrator says the house should be destroyed.

D. The cracks prove no recovery has begun.

Question 3

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: A historian explains that a diary entry may reveal one person's experience, but it should be compared with other sources before broad conclusions are made.

Question: Which explanation best connects evidence to paraphrase?

A. The phrase "one person's experience" shows that a diary can give a personal view but not a complete picture by itself.

B. The phrase "broad conclusions" proves that diaries are useless.

C. The word "historian" means the passage is not about sources.

D. The phrase "diary entry" shows that all diaries are equally reliable.

Question 4

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The speaker admits that change is uncomfortable, yet argues that refusing to change can make a community less prepared for future challenges.

Question: Which evidence best supports the idea that change is necessary even when difficult?

A. "The speaker admits"

B. "change is uncomfortable"

C. "refusing to change"

D. "less prepared for future challenges"

Question 5

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The article states that a strong discussion depends on listening, evidence, and the willingness to revise a first impression when new information appears.

Question: Which paraphrase is most accurate?

A. Strong discussion requires listening, support, and openness to changing an early view.

B. Strong discussion means keeping a first impression no matter what evidence appears.

C. Strong discussion happens only when people avoid evidence.

D. Strong discussion depends on speaking first and listening later.
