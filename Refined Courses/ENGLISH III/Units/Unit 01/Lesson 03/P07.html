Checkpoint Task

Passage: The writer argues that a community should not treat history as a finished story. New documents, overlooked voices, and changing questions can help readers understand the past with greater responsibility.

Write an accurate paraphrase of the passage. Then provide one relevant piece of evidence from the passage and explain how that evidence supports your paraphrase.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear image or typed copy of your notebook work for P02, P03, and P06. Make sure your name, unit, and lesson are visible.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your final checkpoint response in the course submission area. Include the paraphrase first, the evidence second, and the explanation third.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your paraphrase keeps the passage meaning accurate.
  • Your evidence is quoted or copied accurately from the passage.
  • Your explanation shows why the evidence supports the paraphrase.
  • Your response uses standard English conventions.
