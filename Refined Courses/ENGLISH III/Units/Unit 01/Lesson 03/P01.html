P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Paraphrase with relevant textual evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices.

What You Will Learn

  • How to paraphrase a Grade 11 passage without losing the author's meaning.
  • How to choose evidence that actually supports the paraphrase.
  • How to explain the connection between a paraphrase and a quoted detail.
  • How to use precise academic language when explaining textual evidence.

Why This Matters

English III analysis depends on evidence. A paraphrase shows what the text says. Evidence shows where the text says it. When both parts work together, your response becomes more credible.

This lesson keeps the focus on comprehension while adding the habit of pointing to relevant textual support.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included inside the lesson.
  • Write paraphrases of central meaning and important details.
  • Select evidence that supports the paraphrase.
  • Explain why the evidence is relevant.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your paraphrase accurately restates the passage.
  • Your evidence directly supports the paraphrase.
  • Your explanation shows why the evidence is relevant.
  • Your checkpoint response uses clear academic language.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG3.R.10 in student language: I can restate a Grade 11 text accurately in my own words.

Today, you will add evidence to your paraphrase so your reader can see exactly where your understanding comes from.
