Lesson Overview: Paraphrase with Relevant Textual Evidence

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.R.10 — Paraphrase grade-level texts accurately and integrate relevant evidence.

Support: MLA.ENG3.V.01 and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

Learning Targets

  • Separate a claim, relevant evidence, and reasoning.
  • Paraphrase evidence without copying structure or changing meaning.
  • Select evidence that directly supports an analytical claim.
  • Integrate paraphrased evidence with context and explanation.
