P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Read the New Passage

Passage: The article explains that student debates improve when speakers listen closely before responding. A strong response does not simply repeat a prepared point; it addresses the actual claim, evidence, or concern raised by another speaker.

This passage is about debate, but the larger skill is responsive communication.

Notebook Task

Create a three-part response in your notebook:

  1. Paraphrase: Restate the whole passage accurately.
  2. Evidence: Copy one phrase or sentence that supports your paraphrase.
  3. Explanation: Explain why the evidence supports your paraphrase.

Deeper Application

Now add one sentence explaining how this passage connects to oral communication. Use the academic word relevant or evidence correctly.

Accelerated option: Explain why listening before responding can make a speaker more credible.

Common Mistake Check

Incorrect: Debaters should ignore other speakers and repeat their own ideas.

Why this is incorrect: The passage says a strong response addresses the actual claim, evidence, or concern raised by another speaker.

Correct: The article explains that debate responses improve when speakers listen carefully and respond to the specific point another person made.

Why this is correct: It keeps the meaning and identifies the relevant communication skill.
