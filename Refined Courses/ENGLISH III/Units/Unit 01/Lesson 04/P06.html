📘 ENG3 | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use the passage provided here.

Passage

The speaker describes the school auditorium as "filled with questions before anyone spoke." The description suggests that the audience arrives with uncertainty and expects the speaker to provide direction.

Part A: Response Parts

  1. Write an accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  2. Write a claim about what the auditorium description suggests.
  3. Copy one phrase as evidence.

Part B: Short Response

Write a four-sentence analytical response using your paraphrase, claim, evidence, and explanation.

Part C: Revision

Revise your response once. Improve one word choice, one sentence connection, and one convention issue if needed.

Independent Work Success Checklist

Lesson focus: Analytical Written Response Foundations

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts

Complete the work independently, but do not guess about what to use. Any passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt needed for the task must be on this lesson page or inside the assessment question itself.

  1. Answer every part of the task in order. If there is a Part A, Part B, and Part C, each part needs its own clear response.
  2. Use evidence or details from the same page when the task requires reading, analysis, research, or revision.
  3. Explain your reasoning step by step so the reader can see how you moved from evidence to answer.
  4. Before submitting, reread for accuracy, standard alignment, complete sentences, academic vocabulary, and standard English conventions.
