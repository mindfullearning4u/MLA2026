Strong response: The narrator describes the hallway as crowded with people whose attention makes the narrator feel tense. The phrase "a tunnel of waiting faces" creates a feeling of pressure because it makes the audience imagine the narrator surrounded by watchful people. This detail suggests that speaking will feel difficult before the narrator even begins.

Why it works: The response paraphrases, uses evidence, and explains the effect of the evidence.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The hallway is a tunnel, and people are there, so the author is good at details.

Why it is wrong: This response is vague and does not explain what the image suggests.

Correct: The phrase "a tunnel of waiting faces" suggests pressure because it makes the narrator feel surrounded by people who are watching.

Why it is correct: The response connects the evidence to a clear meaning.
