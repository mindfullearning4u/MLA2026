P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Write this title: Analytical Written Response Foundations

Vocabulary

  • Analytical response: writing that explains meaning, choices, or effects in a text.
  • Claim: the main point your response makes about the text.
  • Evidence: words or details from the text that support the claim.
  • Elaboration: explanation that shows how evidence supports the claim.

Step 1: Read the Passage

Passage: The narrator describes the classroom clock as "loud enough to interrupt every thought." This detail suggests that the room feels tense and that time is moving too slowly for the narrator.

Read for the literal detail first. Then think about what the detail suggests.

Step 2: Build the Response Parts

  1. Paraphrase: The narrator hears the clock as disruptive.
  2. Claim: The clock detail creates a tense mood.
  3. Evidence: "loud enough to interrupt every thought"
  4. Elaboration: The phrase shows that the sound is overwhelming and makes the narrator feel trapped in the moment.

Step 3: Write Your Own Sentence

Use this frame in your notebook:

The detail _____ suggests _____ because _____.

Your sentence should move from accurate paraphrase into analysis.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or passage detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two interpretations, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
