Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten lesson terms and complete definitions. Submit them with the Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
analytical claim A focused, defensible statement that interprets a text and answers the prompt.
topic sentence A sentence that states a paragraph’s controlling analytical idea.
context Information that prepares readers to understand evidence accurately.
textual evidence Quoted or paraphrased material used to support a claim.
reasoning Explanation of how and why evidence supports the analytical claim.
elaboration Additional explanation that develops significance, implications, or connections.
transition A word, phrase, or sentence that clarifies relationships between ideas.
coherence The clear and logical connection of ideas throughout a response.
objective summary A concise account of central ideas and essential details without judgment.
concluding insight A final statement that extends or clarifies the analysis without merely repeating it.
