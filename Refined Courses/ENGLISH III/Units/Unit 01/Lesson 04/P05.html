P05 Guided Practice

Answer the five guided practice questions. Each passage is included inside the question. These questions match ENG3_U01_L04_GuidedPractice.gift.

Before You Begin

  1. Read the passage and identify what it means.
  2. Look for the answer that builds a clear analytical response.
  3. Check whether the evidence and explanation match the paraphrase.

Question 1

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The narrator describes the courthouse steps as "worn smooth by generations of waiting." The detail suggests that many people have stood there hoping for decisions that affected their lives.

Question: Which sentence best begins an analytical response?

A. The steps are old and smooth, and that is interesting.

B. The courthouse steps show that many people have waited there for important decisions over time.

C. The narrator probably dislikes all courthouses.

D. The passage is mostly about building materials.

Question 2

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The speaker calls the audience "witnesses and builders," suggesting that they must both remember what happened and help shape what comes next.

Question: Which evidence best supports the idea that the audience has two responsibilities?

A. "The speaker calls"

B. "witnesses and builders"

C. "the audience"

D. "what comes next"

Question 3

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The author uses the phrase "a map with missing roads" to describe a history book that leaves out important voices.

Question: Which explanation is strongest?

A. The phrase suggests that incomplete history can mislead readers because it leaves out important paths of understanding.

B. The phrase proves the book is literally about travel.

C. The phrase shows that maps are always more useful than books.

D. The phrase means the author dislikes roads.

Question 4

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The narrator says the river "kept moving under the frozen surface," suggesting that change continues even when it cannot be seen.

Question: Which response best connects paraphrase and analysis?

A. The river is frozen, so nothing changes.

B. The river is moving, but the narrator does not notice it.

C. The river image suggests hidden change because movement continues under the ice.

D. The passage only describes winter weather.

Question 5

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Passage: The essay explains that a response becomes clearer when the writer places the paraphrase before the evidence and then explains the evidence.

Question: Which order best matches the passage?

A. Paraphrase, evidence, explanation

B. Evidence, unrelated opinion, conclusion

C. Explanation, no evidence, new topic

D. Opinion, copied passage, no explanation
