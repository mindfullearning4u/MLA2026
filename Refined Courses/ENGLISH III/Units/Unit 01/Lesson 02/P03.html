P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Continue Your Notebook Work

Now you will connect academic vocabulary to a short passage and explain why the word fits.

Passage

The speaker repeats the phrase "we must answer" after describing each problem facing the city. The repeated phrase makes the audience feel that action is both urgent and shared.

Notebook Task

Create a two-column chart. In the left column, write the vocabulary words repetition, urgency, audience, and shared responsibility. In the right column, explain how each word connects to the passage.

Deeper Application

Write a four-sentence explanation using all four vocabulary words correctly. Your explanation should identify the speaker's choice and its effect on the audience.

Accelerated option: Add the word rhetorical or coherent if it fits your explanation.

Common Mistake Check

Incorrect: The speaker says stuff again, and it is good.

Why this is incorrect: The sentence is vague and does not use academic vocabulary to explain the choice or effect.

Correct: The speaker's repetition creates urgency by reminding the audience that solving the city's problems is a shared responsibility.

Why this is correct: The sentence names the technique, effect, audience, and meaning precisely.
