Lesson Overview: Academic Vocabulary for Literary and Rhetorical Analysis

Lesson Focus

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.01 — Integrate grade-level academic vocabulary in literary and rhetorical analysis.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10 — Close reading and accurate paraphrase; MLA.ENG3.L.01 — Standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Determine precise meanings through morphology, context, denotation, and connotation.
  • Distinguish related literary and rhetorical terms.
  • Use academic vocabulary to make analytical claims more exact.
  • Explain how an author’s diction shapes tone, emphasis, and audience response.

Why This Matters

Precise vocabulary turns a vague reaction into an evidence-based interpretation. Terms such as juxtaposition, concession, and motif name relationships that readers must explain.
