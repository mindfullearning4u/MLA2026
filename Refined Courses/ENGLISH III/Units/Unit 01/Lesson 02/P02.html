Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record every term and its complete definition. Submit these vocabulary notes with the Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.

Term Complete Definition
denotation A word’s direct, dictionary meaning.
connotation The emotional or cultural associations a word carries beyond its denotation.
diction An author’s deliberate choice and arrangement of words.
register The level of formality and style appropriate to a purpose, audience, and situation.
motif A recurring image, idea, phrase, or situation that helps develop a larger theme.
juxtaposition The placement of unlike elements near each other to emphasize contrast or reveal a relationship.
rhetorical question A question posed for effect or emphasis rather than to obtain an answer.
concession An acknowledgment of a competing viewpoint before a writer qualifies or answers it.
qualifier A word or phrase that limits the scope or certainty of a claim.
nuance A subtle distinction in meaning, tone, or feeling.
