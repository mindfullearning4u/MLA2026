P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Write this title: Academic Vocabulary for Literary and Rhetorical Analysis

Vocabulary

  • Evaluate: judge the effectiveness or importance of something using evidence.
  • Rhetoric: the choices a writer or speaker uses to influence an audience.
  • Perspective: a viewpoint shaped by experience, position, or purpose.
  • Precise: exact and specific enough for the idea.

Step 1: Study the Sentence

Weak sentence: The author does a good thing with words to make readers feel something.

This sentence is vague. It does not name the author's choice, the effect, or the audience response clearly.

Step 2: Replace Vague Words

In your notebook, replace the vague words:

  • does a good thing could become uses an effective strategy.
  • words could become imagery, diction, repetition, or rhetorical appeals.
  • feel something could become creates sympathy, concern, urgency, or reflection.

Step 3: Write a Precise Sentence

Write a revised sentence using at least two academic vocabulary words.

Model: The author uses repeated images of darkness to create a concerned tone and emphasize the speaker's uncertainty.

Your sentence should be specific enough that a reader knows what choice the author made and what effect it created.
