Checkpoint: “A Public Place for Study”

Directions

Use the evidence and audience description to answer all 12 questions. Submit only these responses in the separate Checkpoint Submission. Earn at least 80%.

Evidence and Situation

A library survey found that 62% of teen respondents lacked a quiet study space at home. Evening attendance rose after a pilot kept one room open two hours later. Staffing the room every night is not currently affordable, but three evenings per week fit the proposed budget. You will address the library board and community members for two minutes.

Tasks

  1. Write a qualified central claim.
  2. Identify the audience’s likely knowledge needs.
  3. Select one discipline-specific term and define it naturally.
  4. Adapt the 62% statistic for oral delivery.
  5. Explain why the pilot evidence is relevant.
  6. Use a signpost to introduce the recommendation.
  7. Use a transition connecting need and evidence.
  8. Use emphasis without exaggeration.
  9. Explain why “every teen needs this every night” is inaccurate.
  10. Write a 120–160 word spoken recommendation.
  11. Explain two differences between the spoken and written versions.
  12. Explain how register, qualification, and precision strengthen credibility.
