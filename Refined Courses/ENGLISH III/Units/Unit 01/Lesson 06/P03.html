📘 ENG3 | Unit 01 | Lesson 06

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Use academic vocabulary in a short speaking note.

Short Text

The speaker repeats the phrase "we are responsible" after describing a shared problem. The repetition makes the audience feel included in the solution.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the key technique. Here, the technique is repetition.

Step 2: Identify the audience effect. The audience feels included and responsible.

Step 3: Use academic vocabulary to explain both parts.

Model: The speaker uses repetition as a rhetorical device to connect the audience to the shared purpose.

Notebook Task

Write a two-sentence speaking note about the short text. Use at least three vocabulary words from this lesson.

Your note should sound natural enough to say aloud but precise enough for English III.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The speaker says the same thing and it is good for people.

Why it is weak: The statement is too vague. It does not name the technique, the audience, or the purpose.

Correct: The speaker uses repetition to emphasize a shared responsibility and persuade the audience to act.

Why it is stronger: The revision uses academic vocabulary that identifies the technique and explains its effect.
