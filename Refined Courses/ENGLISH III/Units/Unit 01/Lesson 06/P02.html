P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Build a vocabulary bank for analytical speaking and writing.

Notebook Title

Academic Vocabulary for Clear Explanation

Vocabulary

  • Audience: the people a writer or speaker is trying to reach.
  • Purpose: the reason a writer or speaker communicates.
  • Evidence: information from a text or source that supports an idea.
  • Rhetorical device: a technique used to affect an audience.
  • Precise: exact and specific.
  • Credible: trustworthy and believable.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Start with the idea you want to explain.

Vague idea: The author says good things about responsibility.

Step 2: Identify what the author is doing. Is the author persuading, describing, warning, comparing, or proving?

Step 3: Choose academic vocabulary that fits the action.

Stronger version: The author uses positive diction to persuade the audience that responsibility is honorable.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary list. Next to each word, write one sentence that uses the word correctly in an English III explanation.

Then revise this vague statement: The speaker says important things about courage.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or passage detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two interpretations, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
