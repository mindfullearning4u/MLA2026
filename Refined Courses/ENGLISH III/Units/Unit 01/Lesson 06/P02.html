Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
audience The intended listeners or readers whose knowledge and expectations shape communication choices.
purpose The specific result a speaker or writer intends to achieve.
mode The form of communication, such as speech, essay, discussion, or multimedia presentation.
register The level of formality and style appropriate to a situation.
signposting Language that announces organization and guides an audience through ideas.
qualification Language that limits a claim’s scope or certainty.
emphasis Special prominence given to an idea through wording, placement, repetition, or delivery.
elaboration Explanation or example that develops an idea for an audience.
discipline-specific vocabulary Terms with specialized meanings within a field of study.
precision The use of exact language that communicates the intended meaning without ambiguity.
