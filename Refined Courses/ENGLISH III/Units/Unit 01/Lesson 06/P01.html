Lesson 6: Academic Vocabulary in Speaking and Writing

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices; MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How academic vocabulary makes literary and rhetorical thinking more precise.
  • How to replace vague words with accurate course vocabulary.
  • How to use the same vocabulary clearly in writing and speaking.
  • How to explain evidence without sounding memorized or unclear.

Why This Matters

Academic vocabulary helps you say exactly what a text is doing. Words such as tone, purpose, evidence, audience, and rhetorical device give your response focus.

In English III, you will often need to explain complex ideas in writing and aloud. Precise vocabulary helps both kinds of communication.

What You Will Do

  • Study key academic vocabulary for analysis and presentation.
  • Revise vague statements into precise academic statements.
  • Practice using vocabulary in written and spoken explanations.
  • Complete a checkpoint that connects vocabulary to a short response.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by choosing precise academic vocabulary and using it correctly in a written explanation and a short speaking note.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use the language of English III accurately so your thinking sounds clear, mature, and specific.
