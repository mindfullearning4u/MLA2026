Worked Example: Full Synthesis Response

Passage

The town restored the clock face but replaced its broken mechanism with a digital system. Some residents called the project imitation. Archivist Noor found records showing that the original mechanism had already been replaced twice. At the dedication, the old hands moved above a small display explaining each change.

Interpretation and Evidence

Initial claim: The restoration sacrifices authenticity. Counterevidence: Earlier mechanisms had also been replaced. Revised claim: The project presents authenticity as a transparent record of change rather than untouched material.

Model Paragraph

The clock project defines authenticity as honest continuity rather than physical permanence. Although critics describe the digital mechanism as imitation, Noor’s records reveal that previous generations also replaced the internal machinery. This corroborating evidence weakens the assumption that one mechanism represents an untouched original. Moreover, displaying the replacement history beneath the moving old hands makes change visible instead of disguising it. The project therefore preserves the clock’s public function and recognizable face while treating revision itself as part of the historical record.

Quality Check

The paragraph qualifies its interpretation, responds to counterevidence, integrates paraphrased evidence, explains relationships, uses coherent transitions, and ends with synthesis.
