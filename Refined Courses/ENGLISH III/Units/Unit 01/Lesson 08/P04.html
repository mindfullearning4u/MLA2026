P04 Synthesis Review Example

Review how a complete Unit 1 response works.

Review Passage

The rain had washed the road clean, but it had also erased the chalk arrows leading to the shelter. Nia knelt and drew them again, pressing hard enough for the lines to last through another storm.

Model Response

Nia's action shows responsibility because she restores directions that help others reach safety. The phrase "drew them again" is evidence that she responds to a problem with action. Her purpose is not only to replace the arrows but also to make them last "through another storm." The response uses paraphrase, evidence, academic vocabulary, and clear conventions.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Nia does arrows and it is good.

Why it is weak: It is too vague and does not show Unit 1 mastery.

Correct: Nia's action shows responsibility because she restores directions that help others reach safety.

Why it is correct: It paraphrases meaning, uses academic vocabulary, and explains the evidence clearly.

Worked Example Review

Example 1: If the question asks for meaning, identify the claim or central idea first.

Example 2: If the question asks for evidence, choose the exact detail that proves the claim.

Example 3: If the question asks for analysis, explain how the detail affects the reader, argument, or interpretation.

Common Mistake: A response that only summarizes the topic does not show mastery because it does not explain the author's choices.
