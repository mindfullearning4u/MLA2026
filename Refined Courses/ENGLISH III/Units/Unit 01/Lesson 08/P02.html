Notebook Title

Unit 1 Synthesis Review

Review Passage

The rain had washed the road clean, but it had also erased the chalk arrows leading to the shelter. Nia knelt and drew them again, pressing hard enough for the lines to last through another storm.

Review Task

  • Write one accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  • Choose one phrase as evidence.
  • Explain how the evidence supports the meaning.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Review the vocabulary and identify the skill being practiced.
  2. Step 2: Read the model or passage carefully before answering.
  3. Step 3: Find the exact evidence, word, sentence, or structural choice that supports your answer.
  4. Step 4: Explain how that evidence connects to meaning, effect, or purpose.
  5. Step 5: Check the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: work one step at a time. Standard path: complete the whole task with evidence and explanation. Accelerated path: compare alternatives or add a second evidence-based explanation.

Vocabulary Review

  • Claim: the idea a response or argument tries to prove.
  • Evidence: words, details, or examples that support a claim.
  • Analysis: explanation of how evidence proves or develops an idea.
  • Purpose: the reason an author or speaker makes a choice.
