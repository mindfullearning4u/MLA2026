Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
synthesis The combination of evidence or ideas into a supported new understanding.
interpretation A reasoned explanation of textual meaning.
corroboration Support created when independent details agree.
counterevidence Evidence that challenges or limits a claim.
qualification Language that limits a claim to the evidence’s scope.
integration The blending of evidence into a writer’s sentence and reasoning.
coherence The logical connection and progression of ideas.
academic voice Precise, formal, evidence-based expression.
concision Clear expression without unnecessary wording.
mastery Demonstrated achievement of the required knowledge and skills.
