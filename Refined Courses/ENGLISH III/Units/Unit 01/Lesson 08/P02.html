P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 1 Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice Synthesis

Vocabulary

  • Paraphrase: restating meaning accurately in your own words.
  • Evidence: text details that support an idea.
  • Academic voice: clear, precise language for school-based analysis.

Synthesis Teaching

A synthesis response does not complete paraphrase first, evidence second, and vocabulary third as disconnected steps. Instead, the skills work together: the paraphrase shows understanding, the evidence proves the understanding, the vocabulary makes the explanation precise, and conventions make the response readable.

Notebook Task

Create a two-column notebook chart. In the left column, list the unit skills: paraphrase, evidence, vocabulary, conventions, and explanation. In the right column, explain how each skill will appear in one connected response.
