Lesson Overview: Unit 1 Synthesis

Lesson Focus

This lesson integrates close reading, academic vocabulary, accurate paraphrase, relevant evidence, analytical organization, conventions, and academic voice.

Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10, V.01, W.01, L.01, and SL.01.

Mastery Targets

  • Develop and qualify an interpretation.
  • Select, paraphrase, integrate, and explain evidence.
  • Write coherent analytical responses in Standard English.
  • Adapt precise academic language to purpose and audience.

Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Complete the synthesis lesson and model.
  3. Submit Independent Work with vocabulary.
  4. Submit Checkpoint separately.
  5. Complete the Unit 1 Final Assessment: 10 random questions from a 40-question bank, one attempt, 80% mastery.
