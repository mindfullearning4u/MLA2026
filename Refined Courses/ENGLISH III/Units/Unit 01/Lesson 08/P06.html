Prepare for the Unit Assessment.

Readiness Checklist

  • I can paraphrase a short Grade 11 passage without changing its meaning.
  • I can choose evidence that supports an interpretation.
  • I can use academic vocabulary accurately.
  • I can recognize clear conventions in an analytical sentence.
  • I can explain how evidence supports meaning.

Independent Review Task

Review your notebook entries from Lessons 1-7. Mark one page where you improved paraphrase, one page where you improved vocabulary, and one page where you improved conventions.
