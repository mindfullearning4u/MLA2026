P05 Guided Practice

Answer each guided practice question by combining comprehension, evidence, vocabulary, and conventions.

Guided Practice Thinking

  1. Identify the central meaning of the scenario and choose one detail that proves it.
  2. Explain how an academic vocabulary word helps make the response more precise.
  3. Revise a vague sentence so it uses evidence and standard English conventions.

Before You Continue

Each guided practice question should require more than one unit skill. If you can answer using only one isolated skill, reread the passage or scenario and connect your answer to evidence, vocabulary, reasoning, and conventions.
