The Unfinished Mural

A school mural showed scientists, writers, and civic leaders from the region. Student researcher Tessa noticed an unpainted panel at the far end. Records revealed that the original artist had planned to add contemporary figures later, but funding ended.

Some students called the blank a failure. Others wanted to fill it immediately from memory. Tessa reviewed the artist’s sketches, interviewed community historians, and compared nominations with documented contributions. She proposed a rotating digital projection beside the protected blank panel. The projection could add researched figures without pretending the original artist had chosen them.

The board approved a one-year pilot. At the first display, the empty panel remained visible beside changing portraits. The arrangement distinguished inherited history from ongoing interpretation while allowing both to share the wall.
