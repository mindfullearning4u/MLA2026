📘 ENG3 | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Write an integrated response independently.

Passage

At sunrise, Jonah placed the repaired sign back above the library door. The paint was uneven, and one corner still showed a crack, but the word "OPEN" could be read from across the street. By noon, three neighbors had brought books to replace the ones lost in the storm.

Part A: Paraphrase

In your notebook, paraphrase what happens in the passage and what it suggests about the community.

Part B: Evidence and Vocabulary

Choose one phrase as evidence. Then choose two academic vocabulary words that will help explain the evidence.

Part C: Integrated Response

Write a four- to five-sentence response that paraphrases the passage, includes evidence, uses academic vocabulary, and follows standard conventions.
