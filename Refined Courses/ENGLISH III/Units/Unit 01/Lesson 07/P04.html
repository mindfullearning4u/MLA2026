P04 Worked Example

Study how one response combines Unit 1 skills.

Worked Example 1: Passage

When the town clock stopped, Mira did not panic. She climbed the courthouse steps, wound the old gears by hand, and waited until the bell struck noon. "A town can lose its rhythm," she told the crowd, "but it does not have to lose its will."

Worked Example 2: Build the Response

Paraphrase: Mira shows that a community can recover when someone chooses action instead of panic.

Evidence: She tells the crowd the town does not have to "lose its will."

Academic vocabulary: The clock can be explained as a symbol of the town's shared rhythm.

Worked Example 3: Final Response

Mira shows that a community can recover when someone chooses action instead of panic. The stopped clock symbolizes the town's lost rhythm, but Mira restores it by hand. Her statement that the town does not have to "lose its will" gives the audience evidence of resilience. Together, the action and dialogue show that determination can restore hope.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The clock is important and Mira is good because she fixes it.

Why it is weak: The response is vague and does not explain the symbolic meaning or evidence.

Correct: The stopped clock symbolizes the town's lost rhythm, and Mira's repair shows that purposeful action can restore order.

Why it is correct: The response uses academic vocabulary and explains how the evidence supports meaning.
