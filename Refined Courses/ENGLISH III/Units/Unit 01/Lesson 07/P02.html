P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Read, paraphrase, and identify evidence.

Notebook Title

Integrated Close Reading Response

Vocabulary

  • Integrated response: a response that combines several skills in one clear answer.
  • Academic voice: clear, precise, school-appropriate language used to explain ideas.
  • Evidence: words or details from a text that support an idea.
  • Paraphrase: a restatement of meaning in your own words.

Short Passage

When the town clock stopped, Mira did not panic. She climbed the courthouse steps, wound the old gears by hand, and waited until the bell struck noon. "A town can lose its rhythm," she told the crowd, "but it does not have to lose its will."

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify what happens. Mira repairs the town clock and speaks to the crowd.

Step 2: Paraphrase the larger meaning. Mira's action suggests that a community can recover when someone takes responsibility.

Step 3: Choose evidence. The phrase "does not have to lose its will" supports the idea of determination.

Step 4: Add academic vocabulary. Words such as symbol, evidence, audience, and purpose help explain the passage precisely.

Notebook Task

Copy the passage. Then write three notes:

  • One accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  • One piece of evidence that supports your paraphrase.
  • One academic vocabulary word you could use in a response.
