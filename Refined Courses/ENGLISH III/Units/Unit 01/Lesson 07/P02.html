Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task

Record all ten terms and complete definitions. Submit them with Independent Work.

Term Complete Definition
interpretation A reasoned explanation of a text’s meaning supported by evidence.
central idea The most important idea a text develops.
inference A logical conclusion based on textual clues and reasoning.
corroboration Support created when independent details or sources agree.
qualification A limitation that makes a claim appropriately precise.
integration The grammatical and logical blending of evidence into analysis.
academic voice A clear, precise, evidence-based style appropriate to formal analysis.
coherence The logical connection and progression of ideas.
counterevidence Textual information that challenges or limits an interpretation.
synthesis The combination of ideas or evidence into a new, supported understanding.
