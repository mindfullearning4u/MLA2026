P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Integrated Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG3.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG3.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG3.C.01 - Present with Rhetorical Devices.

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine close reading, paraphrase, evidence, and academic vocabulary in one response.
  • How academic voice stays clear, precise, and evidence-based.
  • How to move from a text detail to a complete explanation.
  • How to check clarity before submitting a response.

Why This Matters

Strong English III responses do more than answer a question. They restate meaning accurately, select useful evidence, use academic vocabulary, and explain the effect in a clear voice.

This lesson prepares you for the Unit 1 synthesis by putting the main Unit 1 skills together before the assessment lesson.

What You Will Do

  • Read a short passage closely.
  • Paraphrase the passage accurately.
  • Select evidence and explain why it matters.
  • Revise a response for academic vocabulary, conventions, and voice.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by writing a clear analytical response that paraphrases accurately, uses evidence, includes academic vocabulary, and follows standard conventions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson asks you to show the full thinking process: understand the text, restate it, support it, and explain it in academic language.

Ask TOR for Help
