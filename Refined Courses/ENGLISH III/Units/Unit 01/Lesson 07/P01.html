Lesson Overview: Integrated Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice

Lesson Focus

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG3.R.10, MLA.ENG3.W.01, MLA.ENG3.V.01, and MLA.ENG3.L.01.

This lesson integrates close reading, accurate paraphrase, relevant evidence, analytical organization, conventions, and academic voice.

Learning Targets

  • Develop a defensible interpretation from close reading.
  • Select, paraphrase, integrate, and explain relevant evidence.
  • Organize coherent analytical responses.
  • Maintain precise vocabulary, appropriate qualification, and Standard English.

Workflow

  1. Record Page 2 vocabulary.
  2. Study the integrated process and model.
  3. Submit Independent Work with vocabulary.
  4. Submit Checkpoint separately.
  5. Complete the quiz at 80% mastery.
