Checkpoint Task

Passage: A careful reader does not rush from a difficult sentence to a quick opinion. The reader first identifies the sentence's main idea, studies how the details connect, and then restates the meaning with precision.

Write an accurate paraphrase of the passage. Then write three to five sentences explaining how you preserved the author's meaning.

Mastery Criteria

  • The paraphrase uses your own wording and sentence structure.
  • The paraphrase keeps the author's central meaning.
  • The explanation identifies how you checked accuracy.
  • Standard English conventions support readability.
