Step 1: Identify the topic. The topic is what historians do.

Step 2: Identify the central meaning. Historians do more than gather facts; they interpret evidence and explain importance.

Historians interpret the past instead of only listing facts.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: Historians collect facts because old events do not matter to people today.

Why it is wrong: This reverses the meaning. The passage says historians explain why events mattered then and why they still matter today.

Correct: Historians study evidence and compare sources so they can explain the significance of past events for earlier audiences and current readers.

Why it is correct: The paraphrase keeps every required idea and uses a new sentence structure.
