P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Write this title: Grade 11 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

Vocabulary

  • Paraphrase: a restatement of a text's meaning in your own words.
  • Central meaning: the main point the author communicates.
  • Key detail: information that must stay because it supports the main point.
  • Accurate: faithful to the author's original meaning.

Step 1: Read the Passage

Passage: In many communities, public libraries have become more than places to borrow books. They also provide internet access, quiet workspaces, tutoring programs, and workshops that help residents complete school, job, and personal projects.

Read the passage twice. The first reading is for basic understanding. The second reading is for deciding what information must stay in your paraphrase.

Step 2: Break Down Meaning

In your notebook, answer these four questions:

  1. What is the topic?
  2. What is the central meaning?
  3. Which details must stay?
  4. Which words could be changed without changing meaning?

Step 3: Write a First Paraphrase

Write one sentence that restates the passage accurately. Do not copy the original sentence structure.

Frame: The passage explains that public libraries now...

Accelerated option: Write the paraphrase without the frame and include all four library services in a smooth sentence.

Step-by-Step Teaching Support

Lesson focus: Grade 11 close reading and accurate paraphrase

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG3.R.10

Use this sequence before you complete the notebook task. It makes the Grade 11 thinking process explicit so you do not have to guess what the skill requires.

  1. Identify the exact Grade 11 skill before beginning the task.
  2. Use textual evidence, source evidence, or reasoning instead of general impressions.
  3. Explain how the evidence proves the point, affects the audience, or develops meaning.
  4. Revise for clarity, precision, and academic voice before submitting your work.
