Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. Submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.
TermDefinition
close readingCareful, repeated examination of a text to determine what it says, how it develops meaning, and how its language and structure work.
central ideaThe most important idea a text develops; it is broader than a single detail and is supported across the passage.
essential detailA fact, event, quotation, or example that directly develops a central idea and is necessary for accurate understanding.
inferenceA logical conclusion formed by combining textual evidence with reasonable background knowledge.
textual evidenceSpecific words, details, or events from a text used to support an analysis or conclusion.
paraphraseA restatement of a source’s meaning in genuinely new wording and sentence structure, usually similar in length to the original.
patchwritingAn unacceptable near-copy that changes only a few words or rearranges parts of the original sentence.
objective summaryA concise statement of a text’s central idea and essential details without personal opinion or unnecessary information.
