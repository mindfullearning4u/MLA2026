Question 1

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Question: Passage: A community archive preserves letters, photographs, and oral histories so residents can study how local decisions shaped the present. Which paraphrase is most accurate?

A. A community archive removes old records so residents focus on the present.

B. A community archive keeps local historical materials so people can understand how earlier choices influenced today.

C. A community archive only stores photographs because letters are not useful.

D. A community archive helps residents avoid studying local decisions.

Question 2

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Question: Passage: The speaker repeats the phrase "we remember" to connect personal memory with public responsibility. What must an accurate paraphrase include?

A. The speaker forgets public responsibility.

B. The speech is only about private memories.

C. The repeated phrase links individual memory to shared duty.

D. The speaker repeats the phrase because the audience is confused.

Question 3

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Question: Passage: Although the article begins with a personal story, its larger purpose is to explain how reliable evidence can change public opinion. Which paraphrase preserves the contrast?

A. The article is only a personal story with no larger purpose.

B. The article argues that evidence never changes opinions.

C. The article begins with public opinion and ends with an unrelated story.

D. The article opens with an individual example but mainly explains how trustworthy evidence can influence what people believe.

Question 4

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Question: Passage: The poet uses images of winter fields to suggest both hardship and preparation for renewal. Which paraphrase is accurate?

A. The winter images suggest difficulty while also pointing toward future growth.

B. The poem says winter is pleasant and easy.

C. The poet only describes farming tools.

D. The winter fields prove renewal is impossible.

Question 5

MLA.ENG3.R.10

Question: Passage: The essay does not reject tradition; instead, it questions which traditions still serve the community well. Which paraphrase is strongest?

A. The essay rejects every tradition in the community.

B. The essay accepts all traditions without question.

C. The essay examines whether certain traditions continue to benefit the community.

D. The essay says communities should avoid traditions.
