P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete this work in your notebook. Use the passage provided here. Do not look for another passage.

Passage

A writer who studies a public monument must look beyond its size or materials. The writer should also consider who created it, whose story it tells, whose story it leaves out, and how viewers may understand it differently over time.

Part A: Meaning Breakdown

  1. Write the topic of the passage.
  2. Write the central meaning in one sentence.
  3. List four details that must stay in an accurate paraphrase.

Part B: Paraphrase

Write a complete paraphrase of the passage in your own words. Keep the meaning accurate and include all required details.

Part C: Accuracy Check

Under your paraphrase, write three sentences explaining how you checked accuracy. Name at least two details from the original passage that your paraphrase preserved.
