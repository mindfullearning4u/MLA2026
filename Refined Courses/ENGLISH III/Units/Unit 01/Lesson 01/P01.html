Lesson Overview: Grade 11 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

Lesson Focus

Lesson 1: Grade 11 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

Primary Standard: Analyze grade-level literary and informational texts closely, determine central ideas, and paraphrase meaning accurately using original wording and sentence structure.

Supporting Skills: academic vocabulary, relevant textual evidence, inference, and Standard English conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • Read a complex passage closely and identify its central idea and essential details.
  • Distinguish accurate paraphrasing from copying, patchwriting, summary, and interpretation.
  • Restate a source passage in genuinely new wording and sentence structure without changing its meaning.
  • Use brief, relevant evidence to explain an inference.

Why This Matters

Accurate paraphrasing proves that you understand a source. It allows you to integrate evidence ethically, discuss literature precisely, prepare research, and communicate another person’s ideas without distortion.

What You Will Do

  • Record the lesson vocabulary and definitions.
  • Study a step-by-step close-reading and paraphrasing process.
  • Follow a fully modeled literary example.
  • Analyze a substantial Independent Work passage and answer 18 questions.
  • Analyze a different Checkpoint passage and answer 12 mastery questions.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Complete Pages 1–4 in order.
  2. Complete Page 5 and submit its 18 responses with the Page 2 vocabulary in Notebook Evidence Submission.
  3. Complete Page 6 and submit its 12 responses separately in Checkpoint Submission.
  4. Review TOR feedback, correct errors, and resubmit any work below 80%.
  5. Complete the five-question lesson quiz. A score of at least 80% is required.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • State central ideas precisely.
  • Select details that genuinely develop those ideas.
  • Write paraphrases that preserve meaning while changing wording and structure.
  • Explain why evidence supports an interpretation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read a complex grade-level text, identify its essential meaning, and restate that meaning accurately in my own words.
