## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 03 provides direct instruction on juxtaposition and character perspective using embedded passages and step-by-step analysis. The lesson supports students in identifying contrasts, explaining how those contrasts reveal character perspective, and using textual evidence.

Lesson 04 provides direct instruction on poetry in historical and cultural context using embedded poems and context notes. The lesson supports students in connecting language, theme, and issue development to time period without requiring outside text access.

Both lessons are self-paced, scaffolded, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
