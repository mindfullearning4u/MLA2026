## Audit Method

English III was re-audited as a single course using the current MLA production expectations:

- Lessons must match the approved unit and lesson mapping.
- All mapped standards must be taught and assessed inside the correct lesson or unit.
- Lessons must teach directly to the student without depending on a live teacher.
- Teacher of Record language is allowed only for support, feedback, intervention, and checkpoint review.
- Lessons must be step-by-step, self-paced, and clear for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
- Passages, excerpts, scenarios, sources, and prompts must be embedded where the student needs them.
- Visual organizers must be present when they improve clarity, reasoning, or workflow.
- Production assessments must use Moodle XML, not GIFT.
