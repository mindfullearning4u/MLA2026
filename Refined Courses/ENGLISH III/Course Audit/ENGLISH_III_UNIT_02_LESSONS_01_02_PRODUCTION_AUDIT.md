# English III Unit 2 Lessons 1-2 Production Audit

## Executive Summary

English III Unit 2 Lessons 1 and 2 were reviewed after production for file completeness, standards alignment, lesson-shell compliance, GIFT assessment structure, JSON validity, visual consistency, student accessibility, and scope compliance.

Initial validation found minor assessment-quality warnings in Lesson 2: several GIFT answer choices had a leading space after the answer marker, and the Lesson 2 quiz answer distribution leaned too heavily toward one answer position. These issues were corrected and validation was rerun.

Final result: PASS.
