## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 01 teaches students to move from broad literary topics to focused, interpretive, researchable questions tied to specific textual details. The lesson supports Grade 11 literary research by preventing factual-only or overly broad inquiry.

Lesson 02 teaches students to distinguish primary and secondary sources in literary research and explain how each source type supports interpretation. The lesson appropriately connects source selection to literary elements, contemporaneous author comparison, and word meaning where relevant.

Both lessons are self-paced, embedded-context based, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
