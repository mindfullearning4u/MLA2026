# English III Unit 1 Rigorous Unit Audit

## Executive Summary

English III Unit 1 was audited for accreditation, compliance, LMS readiness, content alignment, visual consistency, and student-use readiness. The audit reviewed all eight lesson folders, all P01-P07 HTML pages, lesson metadata, quiz metadata, Guided Practice files, Lesson Quiz files, the Lesson 8 Unit Assessment, and the Unit 1 Pretest.

Initial audit found assessment-format concerns in GIFT files: several answer choices had a leading space after the answer marker, and the Lesson 8 Unit Assessment answer distribution was too heavily clustered. These were corrected, and the full validation was rerun.

Final result: PASS.
