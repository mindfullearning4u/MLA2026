## Findings and Corrections

### Finding 1

Lesson 03 Quiz Question 09 initially had no marked correct answer.

Why it was an issue: Moodle GIFT requires exactly one correct answer per multiple-choice item.

Correction: The correct answer marker was added to the source pairing that directly supports the research question about a broken clock and being trapped in the past.

Result after correction: PASS.

### Finding 2

Several GIFT items needed clearer embedded context labels for audit validation.

Why it was an issue: Every assessment item must stand alone and provide the context students need.

Correction: Context labels such as Draft and Scenario were added where needed without changing instructional meaning.

Result after correction: PASS.
