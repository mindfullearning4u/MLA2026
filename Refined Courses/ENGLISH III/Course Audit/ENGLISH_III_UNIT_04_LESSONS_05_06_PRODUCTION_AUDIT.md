## Findings and Corrections

### Finding 1

Lesson 06 Quiz Question 11 initially included correct-answer feedback but did not use the required GIFT correct-answer marker.

Why it was an issue: Moodle GIFT requires exactly one correct answer marker for each multiple-choice item.

Correction: The correct answer marker was applied to the grammatically clear sentence.

Result after correction: PASS.

### Finding 2

Several Lesson 06 Quiz items needed clearer embedded context labels for audit validation.

Why it was an issue: Assessment items must stand alone and provide the context students need without requiring outside lookup.

Correction: Scenario labels were added where needed.

Result after correction: PASS.

### Finding 3

Lesson 06 Quiz answer key initially contained a three-answer cluster.

Why it was an issue: Answer-key clustering can create a pattern risk.

Correction: One item was reordered so the key distribution remained balanced and no three-answer cluster remained.

Result after correction: PASS.
