## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 01 teaches students to evaluate informational text structures and features by identifying structure, explaining reader support, and judging effectiveness. The lesson prepares students for Classical Period analysis by building the structure-analysis foundation first.

Lesson 02 teaches students to distinguish topic, central idea, and supporting detail in Classical Period-style speeches and essays. The lesson uses civic and philosophical speech patterns appropriate to the Grade 11 benchmark without introducing later Unit 3 standards.

Both lessons are self-paced, embedded-passage based, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
