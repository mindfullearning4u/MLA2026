## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 03 teaches students to identify audience, distinguish topic from purpose, analyze author choices, and explain how those choices help achieve purpose in Classical Period-style speeches and essays.

Lesson 04 teaches students to compare multiple arguments by identifying shared issues, claims, evidence, reasoning, validity, and effectiveness. The lesson builds directly from author purpose into argument evaluation without moving into later research or synthesis tasks.

Both lessons are self-paced, embedded-passage based, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
