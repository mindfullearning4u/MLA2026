# English III Unit 2 Rigorous Unit Audit

## Executive Summary

English III Unit 2 was audited at the unit level for accreditation, compliance, student-readiness, standards alignment, assessment integrity, LMS formatting, visual consistency, Lesson 8 controls, and production completeness.

The audit reviewed all eight lessons, the Unit 2 Pretest, all Guided Practice files, all Lesson Quiz files, and the Lesson 8 Unit Assessment.

One metadata issue was found and corrected: `pretest.json` reported an answer distribution that did not match the actual Unit 2 Pretest GIFT file. The GIFT assessment itself was valid. The JSON metadata was corrected and validation was rerun.

Final validation returned no remaining issues.

## Content and Rigor Findings

Unit 2 provides a coherent Grade 11 literary progression:

1. Literary element evaluation begins the unit.
2. Theme analysis extends across times and places.
3. Juxtaposition and perspective receive protected instructional space.
4. Poetry is connected to time-period themes and issues.
5. Figurative language and allegory receive explicit instruction.
6. Contemporaneous authors are compared through topic, reasoning, and context.
7. Complex narrative writing applies perspective and literary craft.
8. Lesson 8 synthesizes the taught skills without adding new instruction.

The unit is appropriately rigorous for English III and does not regress into English I or English II expectations. The unit remains focused on literary analysis, contextual interpretation, allegory, contemporaneous author comparison, and multiple-perspective narrative craft.
