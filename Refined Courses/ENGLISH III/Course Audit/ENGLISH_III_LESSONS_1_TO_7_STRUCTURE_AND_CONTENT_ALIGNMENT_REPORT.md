# English III Lessons 1-7 Structure and Content Alignment Certification Report

## Executive Summary

This certification reviewed English III Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7 at the deeper content-alignment level required for ELA production review.

The review used the certified English III curriculum foundation as the source of truth:

- Certified Standards Inventory
- Certified Crosswalk
- Certified Unit-Level Mapping
- Certified Lesson-Level Mapping

The audit reviewed whether the actual lesson content teaches the mapped standard, not only whether the standard tags are present.

The review covered:

- P01 lesson overview alignment
- P02-P03 instructional text and notebook tasks
- P04 worked examples and common mistakes
- P05 Guided Practice alignment
- P06 independent work
- P07 checkpoint task and mastery criteria
- `lesson.json` standards
- Guided Practice and Lesson Quiz GIFT structure
- course identity and future-unit drift

Initial deeper review found that English III standards mapping, assessment structure, and HTML structure were correct, but many P02-P03 direct-instruction pages were too compressed for Grade 11 self-paced mastery. Corrections were applied.

Final decision:

**PASS - STRUCTURE AND CONTENT ALIGN TO CERTIFIED LESSON MAPPING**

## Corrections Made During This Deep Content Pass

Mapped step-by-step instructional support boxes were added to all English III P02 and P03 pages for Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7.

Each added support box:

- Names the mapped lesson focus.
- Displays the mapped standard focus.
- Provides a Grade 11 step-by-step thinking routine tied to the lesson skill.
- Supports independent learners without requiring live teacher explanation.
- Reinforces advanced English III expectations such as evaluation, rhetoric, Classical texts, literary research, complex analysis, presentation, and publication.
- Supports remedial learners through explicit process guidance.
- Supports standard learners through clear sequence and self-checking.
- Supports accelerated learners through emphasis on precision, evidence, significance, and audience effect.
- Preserves existing lesson structure, examples, notebook tasks, independent work, checkpoint tasks, assessments, and visual shell.

No lesson standards were changed.

No lesson mappings were changed.

No assessment files were changed during this English III deep content pass.
