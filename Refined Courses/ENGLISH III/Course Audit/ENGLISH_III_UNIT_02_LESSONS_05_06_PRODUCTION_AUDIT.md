## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 05 teaches students to analyze figurative language and allegory by separating literal meaning, symbolic detail, deeper meaning, and textual evidence. The lesson protects allegory as an explicit Grade 11 expectation and avoids reducing the work to device identification.

Lesson 06 teaches students to compare contemporaneous authors by topic, reasoning, and time-period context. The lesson avoids a generic comparison approach by requiring students to compare how authors from the same period address related issues through different reasoning.

Both lessons are self-paced, scaffolded, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
