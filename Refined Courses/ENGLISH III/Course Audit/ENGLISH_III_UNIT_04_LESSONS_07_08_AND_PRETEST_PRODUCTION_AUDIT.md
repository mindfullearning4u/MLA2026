## Findings and Corrections

### Finding 1

Lesson 07 Quiz initially contained two questions that needed clearer embedded context labels.

Why it was an issue: Each assessment item must stand alone with sufficient context for students.

Correction: Context labels were added to the affected questions.

Result after correction: PASS.

### Finding 2

Lesson 07 Quiz initially contained a three-answer cluster.

Why it was an issue: Answer-key clustering creates an avoidable pattern risk.

Correction: Answer choices were reordered without changing meaning or standards alignment.

Result after correction: PASS.

### Finding 3

Lesson 08 Unit Assessment initially contained three-answer clusters.

Why it was an issue: Unit assessments require balanced answer-key distribution and no obvious answer pattern.

Correction: Answer choices were reordered without changing meaning or standards alignment.

Result after correction: PASS.
