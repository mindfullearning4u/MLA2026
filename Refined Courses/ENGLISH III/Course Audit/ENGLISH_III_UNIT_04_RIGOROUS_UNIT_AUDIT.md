# English III Unit 04 Rigorous Unit Audit

## Executive Summary

English III Unit 04, Literary Research and Complex Text Analysis, was audited at full-unit level for accreditation, compliance, and student-use readiness.

Audit scope included all 8 lessons, all P01-P07 HTML pages, lesson metadata, quiz metadata, guided practice files, Lesson 1-7 quizzes, Lesson 8 Unit Assessment, Unit 4 Pretest, standards alignment, visual formatting, assessment answer integrity, embedded passage/source accessibility, and Lesson 8 synthesis controls.

Initial audit identified one assessment-quality warning in Lesson 05 Guided Practice answer distribution. The issue was corrected, and the full unit audit was rerun. Final validation returned no issues and no warnings.

## Final Certification Decision

English III Unit 4:

PASS - APPROVED FOR COURSE PRODUCTION CONTINUATION

## Findings and Corrections

### Finding 1

Lesson 05 Guided Practice initially had no A-position correct answer across its five questions.

Why it was an issue: Even small guided practice sets should avoid avoidable answer-position imbalance when possible.

Correction: One Lesson 05 Guided Practice item was reordered so the correct answer distribution became A=1, B=1, C=2, D=1.

Result after correction: PASS.
