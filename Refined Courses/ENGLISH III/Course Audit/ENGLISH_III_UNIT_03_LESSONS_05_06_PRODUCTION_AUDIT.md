## Content and Rigor Review

Lesson 05 teaches students to identify shared information across two arguments, explain each author's different end, and evaluate how effectively the same information supports different purposes. The lesson extends Lesson 4 without duplicating it by focusing specifically on shared information and different conclusions.

Lesson 06 teaches students to identify rhetorical choices, explain audience effect, and evaluate how rhetoric supports author purpose. The lesson appropriately follows argument comparison by asking students to evaluate how language shapes audience response.

Both lessons are self-paced, embedded-passage based, and appropriate for remedial, standard, and accelerated learners.
