## 3. Instructional Quality Review

Result: PASS

English III provides a coherent Grade 11 English Language Arts learning experience from close reading and paraphrase through literary evaluation, Classical Period rhetoric, literary research, language and revision, oral communication, digital presentation, and publication readiness.

Lessons are structured for independent online mastery. Each lesson includes overview, notebook development, modeled teaching, guided practice, independent work, and checkpoint evidence. The course supports remedial, standard, and accelerated learners through explicit explanation, scaffolded examples, common mistake feedback, and application tasks.

## 5. Instructional Progression Review

Result: PASS

The approved English III mapping creates a logical Grade 11 progression:

Close reading and academic language -> literary evaluation and narrative craft -> Classical Period rhetoric and argument -> literary research and analysis writing -> language, vocabulary, style, and revision -> oral, digital, and publication communication.

Prerequisites are respected across units. Unit 1 establishes close reading, paraphrase, vocabulary, and conventions. Unit 2 increases literary complexity. Unit 3 shifts to Classical Period informational/rhetorical analysis. Unit 4 develops literary research and complex text analysis. Unit 5 strengthens language, vocabulary, and revision. Unit 6 synthesizes communication, presentation, multimedia, and publication.
