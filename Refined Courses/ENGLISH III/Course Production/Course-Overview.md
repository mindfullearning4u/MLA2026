# English III — Course Overview

**MLA Course Code:** ENG3  
**Credit:** 1.0 High School English Language Arts Credit  
**Course Type:** Online, asynchronous, mastery-based  
**Delivery:** 100% online through Moodle  
**Teacher Support:** Teacher of Record (TOR)

## Course Description

English III develops Grade 11 literary, rhetorical, research, writing, language, and communication skills. Students evaluate complex literature, Classical Period speeches and essays, rhetoric and arguments; conduct literary research; write sophisticated analysis; revise for clarity and style; and publish oral, digital, and multimedia communication.

## Course Learning Outcomes

Students will learn to:

- read closely, paraphrase accurately, and support evaluation with evidence;
- analyze literary elements, universal themes, juxtaposition, poetry, allegory, and complex perspectives;
- evaluate Classical speeches, essays, arguments, and rhetorical choices;
- conduct literary research and write source-supported literary and complex-text analysis;
- revise for clarity, structure, style, and precision and communicate orally and digitally.

## Course Organization

This course contains **6 units and 48 lessons**. Each unit begins with an ungraded Unit Pretest. Lessons 1–7 include a Lesson Quiz. Lesson 8 is a synthesis lesson followed by the Unit Assessment.

1. Foundations of Grade 11 Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice
2. Literary Analysis, Juxtaposition, Poetry, and Complex Narrative
3. Classical Speeches, Essays, Rhetoric, and Argument
4. Literary Research and Complex Text Analysis
5. Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision
6. Synthesis, Oral Presentation, and Digital Publication

## Mastery and Support

The minimum mastery threshold is **80%**. Students review TOR feedback and complete required revision, remediation, or approved reassessment before progressing.
