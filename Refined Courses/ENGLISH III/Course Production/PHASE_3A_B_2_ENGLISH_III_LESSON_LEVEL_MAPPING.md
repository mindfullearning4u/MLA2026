# Phase 3A-B-2: English III Lesson-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English III lesson-level mapping using the English III Crosswalk Draft, Unit-Level Mapping, Mapping Framework, and approved English I and English II production structures.

This is mapping only. It does not create lesson objectives, lesson content, activities, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, or a Course Overview.

The mapping preserves the approved course structure:

- 6 units
- 8 lessons per unit
- Lessons 1-7 contain Lesson Quiz
- Lesson 8 = Putting It All Together
- Lesson 8 contains Unit Assessment

## Spiral Reinforcement Summary

| Spiral Skill | Early Course Use | Mid-Course Use | Late Course Use | Increasing Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Language | Unit 1 analytical response clarity | Units 2-4 conventions in literary, Classical, research, and analysis writing | Units 5-6 revision, style, and publication polish | Moves from clarity to complex analytical sentence control and publication polish. |
| Vocabulary | Unit 1 academic vocabulary | Units 2-4 literary, Classical, rhetorical, and research vocabulary | Units 5-6 derivation, connotation, precision, and audience-specific language | Moves from access vocabulary to independent vocabulary reasoning. |
| Revision | Unit 1 response clarity | Units 2-4 narrative, literary analysis, complex text analysis, and research writing support | Units 5-6 focused revision and communication polish | Moves from simple clarity checks to clarity, structure, style, and publication readiness. |
| Paraphrase | Unit 1 direct paraphrase instruction | Units 2-4 paraphrase for literary, Classical, and research work | Units 5-6 paraphrase for synthesis and presentation support | Moves from accurate restatement to research synthesis and communication support. |
| Evidence | Unit 1 evidence foundations | Units 2-4 evidence for evaluation, argument, literary research, and complex analysis | Unit 6 credible evidence for presentation and publication | Moves from selecting evidence to evaluating and presenting evidence. |
| Communication | Unit 1 evidence-based explanation | Units 2-5 interpretation, rhetoric, collaboration, and feedback | Unit 6 oral, digital, and published communication | Moves from explanation to rhetorical presentation and online/LAN publication. |
