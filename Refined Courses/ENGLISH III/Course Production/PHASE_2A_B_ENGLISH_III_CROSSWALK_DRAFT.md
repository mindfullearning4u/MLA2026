# Phase 2A-B: English III Crosswalk Draft

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English III Crosswalk Draft using the English III MLA Standard Inventory as the source of record. MLA standard numbers, standard titles, and Florida benchmark codes are preserved exactly as assigned in the inventory.

This draft assigns each MLA English III standard to an approved benchmark cluster, one primary unit, spiral reinforcement units, mastery/support status, and readiness-alignment tags. It preserves the approved English I and English II production frameworks while increasing rigor for Grade 11 expectations.

## Potential Concerns

- Unit 2 and Unit 3 remain the highest-density reading units.
- Unit 4 must not separate literary research from literary analysis writing.
- Unit 6 must remain synthesis and publication, not a first exposure to oral or digital communication.
- English III must not be misframed as a direct American literature continuation when verified Grade 11 benchmarks emphasize Classical Period texts and contemporaneous authors.
