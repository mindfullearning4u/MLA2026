# Phase 3A-A: English III Mapping Framework

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the English III Mapping Framework using the approved English I and English II production frameworks as the master template. It preserves the MLA mastery model, audit phases, naming conventions, documentation format, and course production artifact format while increasing rigor for Grade 11 English Language Arts.

This framework does not create lessons, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, quiz JSON files, lesson JSON files, or student-facing instructional content.

## Required Course Architecture

| Requirement | English III Control |
|---|---|
| Course structure | 6 units |
| Unit structure | 8 lessons per unit |
| Lesson 8 | Putting It All Together |
| Lesson 8 assessment | Unit Assessment |
| Lessons 1-7 | Lesson Quiz included |
| Production mode | Structure-only mapping and documentation |
| Content control | No lesson content is created or modified |

## Approved English III Unit Sequence

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Function |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Foundations of Grade 11 Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice | Establish Grade 11 paraphrase, academic vocabulary, evidence, conventions, and analytical response habits. |
| Unit 2 | Literary Analysis, Juxtaposition, Poetry, and Complex Narrative | Develop literary evaluation, theme tracking across times and places, juxtaposition, character perspective, poetry in context, allegory, contemporaneous author comparison, and complex narrative writing. |
| Unit 3 | Classical Speeches, Essays, Rhetoric, and Argument | Develop structure evaluation, Classical Period central idea and purpose analysis, multiple argument evaluation, and rhetorical evaluation. |
| Unit 4 | Literary Research and Complex Text Analysis | Connect literary research, primary and secondary sources, synthesis, literary analysis writing, and complex text analysis. |
| Unit 5 | Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision | Refine language conventions, vocabulary independence, word meaning, clarity, structure, style, and precision. |
| Unit 6 | Synthesis, Oral Presentation, and Digital Publication | Synthesize reading, research, writing, revision, rhetoric, oral presentation, audience-experience digital presentation, multimedia integration, and online/LAN publication. |

## Vertical Alignment with English II

| Strand | English II Production Baseline | English III Mapping Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Close reading | Grade 10 paraphrase, evidence, academic voice, and complex text readiness. | Preserve early support while increasing Grade 11 text complexity, evaluation, and literary research readiness. |
| Literary analysis | Comparative themes, coming-of-age/conflicting perspectives, poetic ambiguity, adaptation. | Advance to literary evaluation, universal themes across time/place, juxtaposition, poetry in context, allegory, and contemporaneous authors. |
| Informational text | Historical American speeches and essays, author purpose, rhetoric, and shared-information arguments. | Advance to speeches and essays from the Classical Period, multiple arguments, authors' reasoning, and rhetoric evaluation. |
| Research | Synthesis of multiple reliable and valid sources. | Require literary research with primary and secondary sources aligned to interpretations of texts. |
| Writing | Formal argument, expository analysis, narrative pacing, audience revision. | Require literary analysis writing, complex text analysis, complex narratives with multiple perspectives, and revision for clarity, structure, and style. |
| Communication | Oral/digital communication of findings, reasoning, and evidence. | Require oral presentation with rhetorical devices and digital presentation designed to improve audience experience. |

## Benchmark Cluster Rules

| Cluster | Primary Unit | Mapping Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Foundational Reading and Academic Response | Unit 1 | Must support every later unit and cannot become remedial-only content. |
| Literary Analysis and Craft | Unit 2 | Must protect literary evaluation, time/place theme tracking, juxtaposition, poetry in context, allegory, contemporaneous authors, and complex narrative writing from compression. |
| Classical Speeches, Essays, and Rhetoric | Unit 3 | Must sequence structure and central ideas before purpose, multiple arguments, and rhetorical evaluation. |
| Literary Research and Analysis Writing | Unit 4 | Must integrate literary inquiry, primary/secondary sources, synthesis, literary analysis, and complex text analysis. |
| Language, Vocabulary, and Revision | Unit 5 | Must use authentic reading, writing, research, and presentation contexts rather than isolated drill. |
| Oral, Digital, and Published Communication | Unit 6 | Must synthesize previously taught skills and avoid introducing new major standards in Lesson 8. |

## Lesson Mapping Controls

- Lessons 1-7 may introduce, teach, practice, and assess primary standards.
- Lesson 8 must remain Putting It All Together.
- Lesson 8 must contain the Unit Assessment.
- Lesson 8 must not introduce a new primary standard.
- Most lessons should carry one primary standard.
- Two primary standards are allowed only when the standards are naturally paired.
- Support standards must reinforce instruction without becoming hidden extra lessons.
- Reading, writing, research, vocabulary, language, and communication must spiral across the course.

## Rigor Progression Controls

| Area | Required English III Increase |
|---|---|
| Literary analysis | Move from Grade 10 analysis to Grade 11 evaluation, time/place comparison, juxtaposition, historical poetry context, allegory, and contemporaneous author comparison. |
| Informational text | Move from historical American text analysis to Classical Period speeches/essays and evaluation of multiple arguments and rhetoric. |
| Research | Move from general reliable-source synthesis to literary research using primary and secondary sources. |
| Writing | Move from formal argument and expository analysis to literary analysis and complex text analysis. |
| Vocabulary and language | Move toward independent interpretation of complex literary, rhetorical, Classical Period, allegorical, and research language. |
| Communication | Move toward rhetorical oral presentation, audience-experience digital presentation, and multimedia online/LAN publication. |

## Assessment Architecture Controls

| Assessment Artifact | Mapping Requirement |
|---|---|
| Unit Pretest | Must align to unit standards and readiness prerequisites. |
| Notebook Evidence | Must document reading, writing, vocabulary, research, revision, and communication process work. |
| Checkpoint Submission | Must require application of the lesson's primary standard in context. |
| Lesson Quiz | Required for Lessons 1-7. |
| Unit Assessment | Required in Lesson 8 as synthesis of standards already taught. |
