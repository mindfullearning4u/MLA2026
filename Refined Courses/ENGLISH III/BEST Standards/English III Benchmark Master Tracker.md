## Supporting Skills Progression

| Unit | Primary Focus | Recurring Supporting Skills |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Unit 01 | Advanced Analysis of Text and Meaning | Complex text reading, paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, and analytical response |
| Unit 02 | American Literature and Perspective | Literary analysis, textual evidence, paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, multiple perspectives, figurative language, and narrative writing |
| Unit 03 | Rhetoric, Argument, and Evaluation | Informational reading, rhetoric, claims, evidence, validity, bias, reasoning, conventions, academic vocabulary, and formal argument writing |
| Unit 04 | Research, Synthesis, and Argument Writing | Research questions, primary and secondary sources, synthesis, complex text analysis, citation, academic integrity, and publication-ready writing |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Strategic Revision | Reading interpretation, vocabulary precision, feedback, audience awareness, purpose, clarity, structure, style, and revision across extended writing |
| Unit 06 | Independent Synthesis, Presentation, and Application | Reading synthesis, research, argument or analysis, academic vocabulary, conventions, credible evidence, rhetorical devices, oral presentation, and digital presentation |
