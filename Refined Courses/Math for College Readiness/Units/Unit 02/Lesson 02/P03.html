Writing from a Table

A table shows cost of $19 for 2 miles and $25 for 5 miles.

Rate of change: (25 - 19) divided by (5 - 2) = 2 dollars per mile

Use C = 2m + b: 19 = 2(2) + b, so b = 15

Equation: C = 2m + 15

Constraints

In real-world linear functions, not every input makes sense.

For a delivery model, miles cannot be negative. A reasonable constraint is m is greater than or equal to 0.
