Worked Example 1

Problem: A table has x-values 0, 1, 2, 3 and y-values 5, 8, 11, 14. Write the equation.

  1. Step 1: Find the change in y: each y-value increases by 3.
  2. Step 2: The slope is 3.
  3. Step 3: Find the starting value: when x = 0, y = 5.
  4. Step 4: Write the equation: y = 3x + 5.
  5. Step 5: The relationship starts at 5 and increases by 3 each step.

Final check: The answer is connected back to the real-world situation.

Worked Example 3

Problem: For d = 100 - 8t, explain the meaning of 100 and -8.

  1. Step 1: The 100 is the starting amount when t = 0.
  2. Step 2: The -8 is the rate of change.
  3. Step 3: The negative sign means the amount decreases.
  4. Step 4: The model loses 8 units for each 1 unit of time.
  5. Step 5: In context, d starts at 100 and decreases by 8 each time t increases by 1.

Final check: The answer is connected back to the real-world situation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

y = 5x + 3

Why it is wrong: The student switched the slope and starting value. The repeated change is the slope, and the value at x = 0 is the intercept.

Correct:

y = 3x + 5

Correction: Use the relationship described in the problem, not just the numbers or symbols that appear first.
