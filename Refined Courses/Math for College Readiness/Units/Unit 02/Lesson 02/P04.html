Worked Example

A rental company charges $30 plus $12 per hour.

Equation: C = 12h + 30

Slope: $12 per hour

Intercept: $30 starting fee

Evaluate: C(5) = 12(5) + 30 = 90

Interpretation: A 5-hour rental costs $90.
