P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Study all three worked examples carefully. Each example shows the thinking, not just the final answer.

Worked Example 1

Problem: Plan A costs $20 plus $10 per month. Plan B costs $50 plus $5 per month. Find the break-even month.

  1. Step 1: Write the models: A = 20 + 10m and B = 50 + 5m.
  2. Step 2: Set them equal: 20 + 10m = 50 + 5m.
  3. Step 3: Subtract 5m: 20 + 5m = 50.
  4. Step 4: Subtract 20: 5m = 30.
  5. Step 5: Divide by 5: m = 6. The plans cost the same after 6 months.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 2

Problem: Solve the system y = 2x + 4 and y = 5x - 2.

  1. Step 1: Set the expressions equal: 2x + 4 = 5x - 2.
  2. Step 2: Add 2 to both sides: 2x + 6 = 5x.
  3. Step 3: Subtract 2x: 6 = 3x.
  4. Step 4: Divide by 3: x = 2.
  5. Step 5: Substitute: y = 2(2) + 4 = 8. The solution is (2, 8).

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 3

Problem: A student sells tickets. Online sales are y = 3x + 12 and door sales are y = 5x. Find when sales match.

  1. Step 1: Set the models equal: 3x + 12 = 5x.
  2. Step 2: Subtract 3x: 12 = 2x.
  3. Step 3: Divide by 2: x = 6.
  4. Step 4: Find y: y = 5(6) = 30.
  5. Step 5: The sales match at 6 tickets and $30.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

20 + 10m = 50 + 5m, so m = 30

Why it is wrong: The student stopped after finding the difference in fixed costs. The variable term still must be divided by the rate difference.

Correct:

20 + 10m = 50 + 5m, 5m = 30, m = 6

Correction: Keep the algebra connected to the wording of the situation.
