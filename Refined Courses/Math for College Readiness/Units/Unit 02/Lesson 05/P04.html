Worked Example 1

Problem: A savings account starts with $500 and grows by 4% each year. Write the exponential model.

Visual: growth by repeated 4% factors

  1. Step 1: Identify the initial value: 500.
  2. Step 2: Convert 4% growth to a growth factor: 1 + 0.04 = 1.04.
  3. Step 3: Use the form A = a(b)t.
  4. Step 4: Write A = 500(1.04)t.
  5. Step 5: The model shows the balance after t years.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 2

Problem: A car worth $20,000 loses 12% of its value each year. Write the decay model.

  1. Step 1: Identify the initial value: 20000.
  2. Step 2: Convert 12% decay to a decay factor: 1 - 0.12 = 0.88.
  3. Step 3: Use V = a(b)t.
  4. Step 4: Write V = 20000(0.88)t.
  5. Step 5: The model shows the value after t years.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 3

Problem: Evaluate A = 300(1.10)t when t = 2.

  1. Step 1: Substitute 2 for t: A = 300(1.10)2.
  2. Step 2: Calculate the power: 1.102 = 1.21.
  3. Step 3: Multiply: 300 × 1.21 = 363.
  4. Step 4: The amount after 2 years is $363.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

500(0.04)t

Why it is wrong: The student used the percent as the growth factor. A 4% increase means keep the original 100% and add 4%, so the growth factor is 1.04.

Correct:

500(1.04)t

Correction: Keep the algebra connected to the wording of the situation.
