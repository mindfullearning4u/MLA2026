P06 Independent Work

Instructions: Complete the independent work in your notebook and show your reasoning clearly.

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Identify and Represent

  1. List the quantities and units in the first guided practice situation.
  2. Define the variable or variables in the checkpoint situation.
  3. Write one sentence explaining which lesson skill is needed.

Part B: Solve and Interpret

  1. Write the algebraic model for the situation.
  2. Solve or interpret the model.
  3. Explain what the solution means in context.

Part C: Reasonableness and Error Analysis

  1. Check whether the answer is reasonable.
  2. Identify one possible mistake a student might make in this lesson.
  3. Correct the mistake and explain the correct reasoning.
