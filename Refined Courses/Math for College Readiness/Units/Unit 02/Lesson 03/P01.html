P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Inequalities and Constraints

In this lesson, you will learn writing, solving, graphing, and interpreting inequalities and constraints in real-world situations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.AR.4: I can write, solve, graph, and interpret inequalities and constraints.

Florida Benchmark: MA.912.AR.2.6

Common Core Alignment: HSA-CED.A.1; HSA-REI.B.3

What You Will Learn

  • How to recognize situations that require writing, solving, graphing, and interpreting inequalities and constraints in real-world situations.
  • How to identify the variables, constants, and constraints in context.
  • How to solve or interpret the model step by step.
  • How to explain whether an answer is reasonable.

What You Will Do

  • Write detailed notebook notes from the teaching pages.
  • Study step-by-step worked examples.
  • Complete guided practice and independent work.
  • Submit a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use correct algebraic notation and units.
  • Show organized work and explain each step.
  • Complete independent work for TOR review.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete TOR intervention.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.AR.4: I can write, solve, graph, and interpret inequalities and constraints.

In student language: I can use algebra to model a real-world decision and explain what the answer means.
