P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue writing the explanations, examples, and correction strategies into your notebook. This page shows how to explain the reasoning in a complete way.

How to Organize the Work

Organized work makes advanced algebra easier to follow. Each line should show one action and preserve the meaning of the context.

  1. Write what each variable or input means.
  2. Identify the starting value, rule, factor, or interval.
  3. Write or choose the model.
  4. Evaluate or interpret the model step by step.
  5. Write the answer in a complete sentence.

Worked Sequence

Situation: A parking garage charges $5 for up to 1 hour and $8 for more than 1 hour up to 2 hours. Interpret the cost at 1 hour.

  1. Identify the boundary: 1 hour.
  2. The phrase up to 1 hour includes 1 hour.
  3. Use the first rule for exactly 1 hour.
  4. The cost at 1 hour is $5.
  5. The endpoint is closed on the first interval.

Reasoning: The algebraic model and the real-world meaning must agree.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Common incorrect thinking:

A 2-pound package uses the over 2 pounds rule.

Why this is wrong: The phrase up to 2 pounds includes exactly 2 pounds. The word over means greater than 2, not equal to 2.

Correct thinking:

A 2-pound package uses the up to 2 pounds rule.

Correction: Read the context carefully before choosing the factor, rule, or interval.
