Checkpoint Task: Piecewise Relationships and Step Functions

A delivery service charges $5 for packages up to 1 pound, $9 for packages over 1 pound up to 3 pounds, and $14 for packages over 3 pounds up to 5 pounds. Identify the correct charge for 1 pound, 2.5 pounds, and 4 pounds, and explain the interval choices.

  1. List the quantities and units.
  2. Identify the model rule, factor, interval, or boundary.
  3. Write or choose the appropriate model.
  4. Evaluate or interpret the model.
  5. Explain why the answer is reasonable.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies quantities, units, and model features accurately.
  • Uses a model aligned to the lesson standard.
  • Shows organized step-by-step work.
  • Interprets the answer in context.
  • Checks reasonableness using the situation.

Alignment

MLA.MCR.AR.8: I can solve, graph, and interpret piecewise models with changing rules and constraints.
