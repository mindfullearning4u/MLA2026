Worked Example 1

Problem: A parking garage charges $5 for up to 1 hour and $8 for more than 1 hour up to 2 hours. Interpret the cost at 1 hour.

  1. Step 1: Identify the boundary: 1 hour.
  2. Step 2: The phrase up to 1 hour includes 1 hour.
  3. Step 3: Use the first rule for exactly 1 hour.
  4. Step 4: The cost at 1 hour is $5.
  5. Step 5: The endpoint is closed on the first interval.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 2

Problem: A shipping company charges $6 for packages up to 2 pounds and $10 for packages over 2 pounds up to 5 pounds. Find the cost for a 3-pound package.

  1. Step 1: Identify the package weight: 3 pounds.
  2. Step 2: Decide which interval contains 3.
  3. Step 3: 3 is greater than 2 and up to 5.
  4. Step 4: Use the second rule.
  5. Step 5: The shipping cost is $10.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Worked Example 3

Problem: A taxi charges $4 to start and $2 per mile after that. Write a rule for total cost after m miles.

  1. Step 1: Identify the starting fee: $4.
  2. Step 2: Identify the per-mile rate: $2 per mile.
  3. Step 3: For mileage after the trip starts, add 2m to 4.
  4. Step 4: The rule is C = 4 + 2m.
  5. Step 5: This rule applies for m = 0.

Final check: The solution is interpreted in the real-world situation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

A 2-pound package uses the over 2 pounds rule.

Why it is wrong: The phrase up to 2 pounds includes exactly 2 pounds. The word over means greater than 2, not equal to 2.

Correct:

A 2-pound package uses the up to 2 pounds rule.

Correction: Keep the algebra connected to the wording of the situation.
