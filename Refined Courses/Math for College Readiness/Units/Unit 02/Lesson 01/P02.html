Notebook Title

Lesson 1: Linear Equations in Context

From Context to Equation

A linear equation can model a situation with a starting amount and a constant change.

Example: A service costs $25 plus $15 per hour.

If h is the number of hours and total cost is $85, the equation is 25 + 15h = 85.

Solving the Equation

Equation: 25 + 15h = 85

Subtract 25: 15h = 60

Divide by 15: h = 4

Interpretation: The service lasts 4 hours.

Vocabulary

  • Variable: A symbol for an unknown quantity.
  • Linear equation: An equation with a constant rate of change.
  • Coefficient: The number multiplying a variable.
  • Constant: A value that does not change.
  • Solution: A value that makes an equation true.
