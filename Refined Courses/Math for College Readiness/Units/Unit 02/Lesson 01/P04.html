Worked Example

A phone repair costs $30 plus $18 per hour. The total cost is $102.

Variable: h = number of repair hours

Equation: 30 + 18h = 102

Solve: 18h = 72, so h = 4

Interpretation: The repair took 4 hours.

Check: 30 + 18(4) = 102.
