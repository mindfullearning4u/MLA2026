Equation Modeling Steps

  1. Define the variable.
  2. Identify the fixed amount and the repeated amount.
  3. Write the equation.
  4. Solve using inverse operations.
  5. Interpret the solution with units.
  6. Check the solution in the original situation.

Example

A gym charges a $40 sign-up fee plus $20 per month. The total paid is $180.

Variable: m = number of months

Equation: 40 + 20m = 180

Solve: 20m = 140, so m = 7

Answer: The membership lasted 7 months.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not solve for a number without explaining what it represents.

Incomplete: m = 7.

Complete: m = 7, so the membership lasted 7 months.
