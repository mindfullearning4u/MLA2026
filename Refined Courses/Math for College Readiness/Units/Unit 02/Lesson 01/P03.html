P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue writing the explanations, examples, and correction strategies into your notebook. This page teaches how to explain your reasoning without relying on a live teacher explanation.

How to Organize the Work

Organized work helps you and your Teacher of Record see whether your reasoning is correct. Use labels so each step has a purpose.

  1. Write what is given.
  2. Define the variable or input.
  3. Choose the equation or model.
  4. Show the solving or interpreting steps.
  5. Interpret the answer in a complete sentence.

Worked Sequence

Situation: A phone repair costs $30 plus $18 per hour. The total cost is $102. Find the number of hours.

  1. Define the variable: h = number of repair hours.
  2. Write the equation: 30 + 18h = 102.
  3. Subtract 30 from both sides: 18h = 72.
  4. Divide by 18: h = 4.
  5. The repair took 4 hours.

Reasoning: Each step connects the equation to the context instead of treating symbols like random letters.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Common incorrect thinking:

30 + 18h = 102, so h = 102 - 18

Why this is wrong: The student subtracted the rate instead of undoing the fixed fee first. In a two-step equation, undo addition or subtraction before undoing multiplication.

Correct thinking:

30 + 18h = 102, 18h = 72, h = 4

Correction: Match the equation to the meaning of the quantities and interpret the result.
