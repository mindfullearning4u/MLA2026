Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Conditional Probability

Vocabulary

  • Conditional probability: the probability of an event after a condition is already known.
  • Given: a word that tells you the group is restricted.
  • Restricted total: the smaller total that matches the given condition.
  • Joint count: a table count that fits two categories at once.
  • Two-way table: a table that organizes counts by two categories.

Learn the Idea

Conditional probability does not always use the grand total. The condition tells you which row, column, or group to focus on. The denominator becomes the total for that restricted group.

  1. Find the word given or the phrase that limits the group.
  2. Use that group total as the denominator.
  3. Find the count inside that group that also matches the event.
  4. Write part / restricted total and explain it in context.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Write one sentence explaining why the denominator changes in a conditional probability question.
