If a student is selected given the student is in Grade 11, the denominator is 30, not 60. The condition restricts the group to Grade 11 only.

Conditional Reading

The probability that a student prefers online review given the student is in Grade 11 is 18/30 = 3/5 = 60%.

This means that among Grade 11 students only, 60% prefer online review.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Use 18/60 because 60 is the grand total.

This ignores the condition. The problem already says the student is in Grade 11.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Use 18/30 because the Grade 11 condition restricts the total to 30.

In conditional probability, always make the denominator match the given condition.
