Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Risk, Uncertainty, and Decision Making

Vocabulary

  • Risk: the chance of an unwanted outcome.
  • Reward: the benefit of a successful outcome.
  • Uncertainty: not knowing which outcome will happen.
  • Tradeoff: giving up one benefit to gain another.
  • Assumption: a statement accepted as true for a decision.

Learn the Idea

Probability does not remove uncertainty. It helps you compare choices more clearly. A stronger decision explains the chance of each outcome, the possible benefit, the possible loss, and the assumptions used.

  1. Identify the choices.
  2. List possible outcomes.
  3. Estimate probability and payoff for each outcome.
  4. Compare risk, reward, and expected value.
  5. Explain the recommendation in context.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and five-step decision process. Then write one decision that includes uncertainty.
