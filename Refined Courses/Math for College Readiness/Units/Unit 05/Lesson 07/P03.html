Decision Evidence

A decision may have a higher reward but also a higher chance of loss. A careful explanation does not say a choice is good just because the possible reward is large. It compares probability, expected value, and the seriousness of the possible loss.

Example: A $100 reward with a 1/100 chance has expected value $1 before cost. If it costs $5 to try, the expected net value is negative.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Choose the option with the largest possible reward every time.

This ignores probability, cost, and possible loss.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Compare reward with probability, cost, and risk.

A strong decision explains why the evidence supports the recommendation.

Notebook Task

Write one example where the largest possible reward may not be the best decision. Explain why.
