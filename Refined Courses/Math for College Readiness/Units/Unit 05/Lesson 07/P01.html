P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Risk, Uncertainty, and Decision Making

In this lesson, you will use probability evidence to compare decisions when outcomes are uncertain.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Support-only extension of Unit 5 standards: I can compare risk, reward, uncertainty, and outcomes using probability evidence.

What You Will Learn

  • How probability supports decisions under uncertainty.
  • How risk and reward work together.
  • How assumptions can affect a decision.
  • How to explain a probability-based recommendation.

What You Will Do

  • Compare choices using probability, payoff, and risk.
  • Identify tradeoffs and limits of available evidence.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use probability evidence accurately.
  • Explain the decision, not just the calculation.
  • Complete independent work so your Teacher of Record can determine whether intervention is needed.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use probability evidence to explain which choice is more reasonable when there is risk.
