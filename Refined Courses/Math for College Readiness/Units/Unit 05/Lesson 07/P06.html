Instructions

Complete the three parts below in your notebook. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to see whether you can explain probability-based decisions clearly.

Part A

Find the expected value of a choice with a 1/2 chance to gain $8 and a 1/2 chance to lose $2.

Part B

Explain one risk and one reward for the choice in Part A.

Part C

Write a recommendation that uses your expected value and your risk explanation.

Independent Work Guidance

Before you write final answers for Risk, Uncertainty, and Decision Making, slow down and make the thinking visible. A complete response does not only give a number or a short phrase. It identifies the quantities, labels the units, chooses the correct tool, and explains why that tool matches the situation.

For each part, write the setup first, then the work, then a sentence interpreting the result in context. If a table, graph, formula, probability statement, financial comparison, or measurement model is involved, copy the important information into your notebook so the reasoning can be followed without guessing.

This synthesis work should connect back to the unit standards, so every answer must show the calculation, representation, or explanation that justifies the decision.

Check your work before submitting: units should be reasonable, percent or probability answers should fit the context, graph or table conclusions should match the displayed evidence, and written explanations should name the decision being made. If you find an error, correct it and briefly note what changed.
