P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Expected Value and Fair Decisions

In this lesson, you will use probability and payoff values to decide whether a strategy, game, or risk is reasonable.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.PR.8: I can use expected value to evaluate strategies and decisions.

MLA.MCR.PR.9: I can use probability to determine whether a decision or game is fair.

What You Will Learn

  • How to multiply each outcome value by its probability.
  • How to add weighted outcome values to find expected value.
  • How to decide whether a game is fair.
  • How to explain risk and reward using numbers.

What You Will Do

  • Build expected value tables.
  • Compare cost, payoff, probability, and long-run average result.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, and a checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Calculate expected value correctly.
  • Explain whether a decision is favorable, unfavorable, or fair.
  • Complete independent work so your Teacher of Record can determine whether intervention is needed.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can use probability to decide what a choice is worth on average and whether the decision is fair.
