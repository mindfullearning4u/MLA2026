P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1

Question: A number cube is rolled. Find P(even or greater than 4).

  1. Even outcomes: {2, 4, 6}.
  2. Greater than 4 outcomes: {5, 6}.
  3. Union: {2, 4, 5, 6}.
  4. Probability: 4/6 = 2/3.

Example 2

Question: Four students run for president and secretary. Count the results.

  1. President has 4 choices.
  2. Secretary has 3 remaining choices.
  3. Order matters because offices are different.
  4. There are 4 Ã— 3 = 12 results.

Example 3

Question: A decision has a 1/2 chance to gain $10 and a 1/2 chance to lose $4. Find expected value.

  1. (1/2)($10) = $5.
  2. (1/2)(-$4) = -$2.
  3. Expected value: $5 - $2 = $3.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Use the grand total, add every probability, or choose the largest prize without reading the context.

This ignores the structure of the problem.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Identify whether the question is about a condition, union, intersection, count, or decision.

The method must match the question.
