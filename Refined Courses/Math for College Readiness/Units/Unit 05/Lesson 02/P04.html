P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: A fair number cube is rolled once. What is the theoretical probability of rolling a 5?

  1. Step 1: A number cube has 6 equally likely outcomes.
  2. Step 2: Only one outcome is a 5.
  3. Step 3: The theoretical probability is 1/6.

Worked Example 2

Question: A coin was flipped 50 times and landed heads 28 times. What is the experimental probability of heads?

  1. Step 1: The favorable observed result is heads: 28.
  2. Step 2: The total number of trials is 50.
  3. Step 3: The experimental probability is 28/50 = 14/25 = 56%.

Worked Example 3

Question: In a survey of 80 students, 30 chose online practice and 50 chose paper practice. What is the relative frequency for choosing online practice?

Visual: survey frequency table

  1. Step 1: The count for online practice is 30.
  2. Step 2: The total number of students is 80.
  3. Step 3: The relative frequency is 30/80 = 3/8 = 37.5%.
  4. Step 4: This means 37.5% of the surveyed students chose online practice.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: A coin was flipped 50 times and got 28 heads, so the probability of heads must be exactly 1/2.

This confuses theoretical probability with experimental probability. The expected probability is 1/2, but the observed data was 28/50.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The theoretical probability of heads is 1/2, but the experimental probability from the data is 28/50.

When data is provided, use the observed count and the observed total unless the question specifically asks for the theoretical probability.
