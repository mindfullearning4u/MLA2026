P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: A spinner has four equal sections labeled red, blue, green, and yellow. What is the sample space?

Visual: four equal spinner outcomes

  1. Step 1: List every possible result of one spin.
  2. Step 2: Do not repeat a result unless it appears as a separate equal section.
  3. Step 3: The sample space is {red, blue, green, yellow}.

Worked Example 2

Question: A number cube is rolled. Event A is rolling an odd number. What is the complement of A?

  1. Step 1: The sample space is {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}.
  2. Step 2: Odd outcomes are {1, 3, 5}.
  3. Step 3: The complement is everything not odd: {2, 4, 6}.

Worked Example 3

Question: A card is chosen from cards numbered 1 through 8. Event A is choosing an even number. Event B is choosing a number greater than 5. Find A and B.

  1. Step 1: Sample space: {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}.
  2. Step 2: Event A: {2, 4, 6, 8}.
  3. Step 3: Event B: {6, 7, 8}.
  4. Step 4: A and B means the outcomes shared by both sets: {6, 8}.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: For Event A or Event B, add every outcome from both lists and count repeated outcomes twice.

This is incorrect because a sample space outcome should only be counted once in a union.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: In a union, list each outcome that belongs in either event, but do not duplicate an outcome that appears in both.

If A = {2, 4, 6, 8} and B = {6, 7, 8}, then A or B = {2, 4, 6, 7, 8}.
