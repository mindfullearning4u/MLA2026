P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Compound Events

In this lesson, you will learn how to find probabilities when a situation includes more than one event.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.PR.4: I can apply the addition rule for probability in context.

MLA.MCR.PR.5: I can apply the multiplication rule for probability in context.

What You Will Learn

  • How to recognize when a probability question uses or.
  • How to recognize when a probability question uses and.
  • How to use the addition rule for unions.
  • How to use the multiplication rule for independent events.

What You Will Do

  • Write organized notes about addition and multiplication probability rules.
  • Separate overlapping events from non-overlapping events.
  • Complete guided practice and independent work with step-by-step reasoning.
  • Submit a checkpoint that explains which rule matches the situation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose the correct probability rule from the wording and context.
  • Show correct fractions before simplifying.
  • Complete independent work so your Teacher of Record can determine whether intervention is needed.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.PR.4 and MLA.MCR.PR.5: I can use probability rules to find the chance of compound events.

In student language: I can decide whether to add or multiply probabilities and explain why that rule fits the situation.
