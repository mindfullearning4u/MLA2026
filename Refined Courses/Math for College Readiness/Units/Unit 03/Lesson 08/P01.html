P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will learn to synthesize data displays, distributions, bivariate data, claims, and responsible interpretation.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 3 standards synthesis: MLA.MCR.DL.1 through MLA.MCR.DL.9

What You Will Learn

  • How to connect multiple data literacy skills in one task.
  • How to identify evidence, claims, and limits.
  • How to explain data patterns in complete sentences.
  • How to avoid conclusions that are not supported by the data.

What You Will Do

  • Write organized notebook notes from the teaching pages.
  • Study step-by-step examples before trying practice problems.
  • Complete guided practice and independent work using clear reasoning.
  • Submit a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use correct data vocabulary and interpretation.
  • Complete independent work so your Teacher of Record can determine whether intervention is needed.
  • Submit a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.
  • Demonstrate mastery through the required assessment for this lesson.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Unit 3 standards synthesis: MLA.MCR.DL.1 through MLA.MCR.DL.9

In student language: I can use all Unit 3 data literacy skills together to read displays, compare data, judge claims, and explain limits responsibly.
