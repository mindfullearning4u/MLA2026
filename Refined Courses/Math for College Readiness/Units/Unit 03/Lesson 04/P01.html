P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Bivariate Data and Scatterplots

In this lesson, you will learn to fit and interpret linear models for bivariate numerical data.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.DL.5: I can fit and interpret linear models for bivariate numerical data.
MLA.MCR.DL.6: I can interpret correlation, slope, intercept, and residuals in context.

What You Will Learn

  • How to read a data situation before calculating or graphing.
  • How to connect vocabulary to data displays and summaries.
  • How to explain data patterns in complete sentences.
  • How to avoid conclusions that are not supported by the data.
