📘 MCR | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show your thinking clearly. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to decide whether you understand the lesson or need more support before moving forward.

Part A: Identify Quantities and Units

  1. A streaming service costs $12 each month for 6 months. List the quantities and units.
  2. A job pays $14 per hour for 8 hours. List the quantities and units.
  3. A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours. List the quantities and units.

Part B: Solve and Interpret

  1. Find the total cost of the streaming service for 6 months. Write a sentence that explains your answer.
  2. Find the total pay for working 8 hours at $14 per hour. Write a sentence that explains your answer.
  3. Find the speed of a car that travels 180 miles in 3 hours. Include the correct unit.

Part C: Reasonableness and Error Analysis

  1. Estimate 29 + 41. Then calculate the exact answer. Explain why the exact answer is reasonable.
  2. A student says 7 notebooks at $9 each cost $16. Identify the mistake and correct the answer.
  3. A student writes "60" as the answer to a speed problem. Explain what information is missing from the answer.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you need help, guidance, or another explanation before moving on.
