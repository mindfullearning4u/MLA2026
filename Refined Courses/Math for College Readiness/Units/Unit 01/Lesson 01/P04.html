Worked Example: Compare Two Choices

Problem: A tutoring center charges $18 per hour. A student needs 5 hours. Another center charges a flat $100 for the same amount of tutoring. Which option costs less?

Quantities and units: $18 per hour, 5 hours, $100 flat cost.

Assumption: Both centers provide the same amount of tutoring.

Solve: 18 x 5 = 90, so the hourly center costs $90.

Compare: $90 is less than $100.

Answer: The hourly center costs less by $10.

Reasonableness: $18 is close to $20, and 5 x 20 = 100, so $90 is reasonable.

Guided Practice Focus

  • Identify quantities and units.
  • Choose a strategy that matches the situation.
  • Write answers with meaning.
  • Use estimation to check reasonableness.
