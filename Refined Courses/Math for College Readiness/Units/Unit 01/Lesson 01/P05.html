P05 Guided Practice

Use these prompts to prepare for the Moodle Guided Practice. Try each one in your notebook before checking your reasoning.

Question 1

A streaming service costs $12 each month for 6 months. What are the quantities and units?

Answer: The quantities are $12 per month and 6 months.

Feedback: The unit "per month" tells you $12 repeats for each month.

Question 2

What is the total cost for the streaming service?

Answer: $72

Feedback: Multiply $12 by 6 months. The total is $72.

Question 3

A student completes 18 practice problems on Monday and 24 on Tuesday. What is the total number of problems?

Answer: 42 practice problems

Feedback: The question asks for a total, so add 18 + 24 = 42.

Question 4

A bus travels 210 miles in 5 hours. What does 210 divided by 5 represent?

Answer: 42 miles per hour

Feedback: Miles divided by hours gives miles per hour, so the division represents speed.

Question 5

A student says 7 notebooks at $9 each cost $16. What is the misconception?

Answer: The student added 7 and 9 instead of multiplying 7 groups of $9.

Feedback: "Each" signals equal groups. The correct total is 7 x 9 = $63.
