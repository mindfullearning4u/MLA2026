P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Complete the independent work in your notebook. Then prepare your checkpoint response.

Independent Work

  1. A streaming service costs $12 each month for 6 months. Find the total cost and explain the units.
  2. A student completes 18 practice problems on Monday and 24 on Tuesday. Find the total and check reasonableness with an estimate.
  3. A job pays $14 per hour. Identify the quantities and units if someone works 8 hours.
  4. A bus travels 210 miles in 5 hours. Explain what 210 divided by 5 represents.
  5. A student claims that $9 per item for 7 items is $16. Identify the misconception and correct the answer.

Checkpoint Task

A gym charges $30 per month. A student plans to use the gym for 4 months. Another option is a one-time $140 membership for the same 4 months.

  1. List the quantities and units in the situation.
  2. State one assumption needed to compare the options.
  3. Find the total cost of the monthly option.
  4. Decide which option costs less.
  5. Explain why your answer is reasonable.
