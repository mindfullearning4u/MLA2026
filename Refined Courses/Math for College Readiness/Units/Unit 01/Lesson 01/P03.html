Four-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Read: Identify the question being asked.
  2. Represent: List quantities, units, and the unknown.
  3. Solve: Choose a strategy that matches the situation.
  4. Interpret: Write the answer with units and check reasonableness.

Example 2: Use the Routine

Problem: A phone plan costs $25 each month for 4 months. What is the total cost?

Read: The question asks for total cost.

Represent: $25 per month, 4 months, unknown total dollars.

Solve: 25 x 4 = 100

Interpret: The total cost is $100. This is reasonable because four months at about $25 each should be about $100.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not ignore units. Units tell you whether to add, subtract, multiply, divide, compare, or convert.

Problem: A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours.

Incomplete response: 60

Complete response: 60 miles per hour, because 180 miles divided by 3 hours equals 60 miles for each hour.
