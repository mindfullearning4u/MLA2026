P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Estimation, Reasonableness, and Error Analysis

In this lesson, you will estimate, check units, identify errors, and explain whether answers make sense in real-world contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.MC.1: I can explain my mathematical reasoning clearly using words, numbers, tables, graphs, formulas, and units.

MLA.MCR.MC.2: I can choose appropriate strategies and representations to solve real-world problems.

MLA.MCR.MC.3: I can interpret results in context and check whether answers are reasonable.

Mapping Note: This is a support-only lesson in the approved Unit 1 mapping. It does not introduce new primary benchmark ownership.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use estimation before and after calculating.
  • How units help identify mistakes.
  • How to decide whether an answer is reasonable.
  • How to explain and correct mathematical errors.

What You Will Do

  • Estimate sums, products, rates, and percents.
  • Compare exact answers to estimates.
  • Identify operation, unit, and percent errors.
  • Write corrections with clear reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with estimates, unit checks, and error corrections.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining whether answers are reasonable.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Readiness Support: Estimation and error analysis help you decide whether calculations are useful and realistic.

In student language: I can check an answer, find mistakes, and explain how to correct them.
