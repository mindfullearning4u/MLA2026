MCR | Unit 01 | Lesson 04

P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Complete the independent work in your notebook. Then prepare your checkpoint response.

Independent Work

  1. Estimate 39 x 6 and explain whether 234 is reasonable.
  2. A pack of 8 pens costs $16. A student says the unit price is $24 per pen. Identify and correct the error.
  3. A $90 item is 10% off. A student says the discount is $10. Explain the mistake.
  4. A car travels 210 miles in 3 hours. Explain why 70 miles per hour is reasonable.
  5. A bill is $48 and a 20% tip is estimated as about $10. Explain whether the estimate is reasonable.

Checkpoint Task

A student compares two options: Option A costs $42 for 6 items. Option B costs $54 for 9 items. The student says Option A is better because $42 is less than $54.

  1. Estimate each unit price.
  2. Find the exact unit price for each option.
  3. Identify the student's misconception.
  4. Choose the better option using unit price.
  5. Explain why your answer is reasonable.
