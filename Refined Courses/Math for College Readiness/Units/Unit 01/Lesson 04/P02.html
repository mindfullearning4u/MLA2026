Notebook Title

Lesson 4: Estimation, Reasonableness, and Error Analysis

Vocabulary

  • Estimate: A close, reasonable value found by rounding.
  • Reasonable answer: An answer that makes sense in context.
  • Error analysis: Finding, explaining, and correcting a mistake.
  • Misconception: A misunderstanding that causes an incorrect method.

Why This Lesson Matters

This lesson builds college readiness because it teaches you how to read a situation, choose a method, and explain the meaning of the answer.

Do not rush to calculate. First identify the quantities, units, and the question being asked.

If an item costs $48 and another costs $31, estimate $50 + $30 = $80. An exact answer near $80 is reasonable.

Step-by-Step Thinking Process

  1. Read the problem and identify the question being asked.
  2. List every quantity with its unit.
  3. Name the unknown so you know what the answer should represent.
  4. Choose the operation or formula that matches the relationship.
  5. Calculate carefully and write the answer with units.
  6. Check whether the answer is reasonable in the situation.
