Lesson 4: Estimation, Reasonableness, and Error Analysis

Why Estimate?

Estimation gives a quick prediction before or after calculating. It helps you notice answers that are too large, too small, or in the wrong unit.

Example: 29 x 6 is close to 30 x 6 = 180. An exact answer near 180 is reasonable.

Reasonableness Checks

  • Size check: Is the answer too large or too small?
  • Unit check: Does the answer have the unit the problem asks for?
  • Context check: Does the answer make sense in the real-world situation?
  • Operation check: Does the operation match the question?

Vocabulary

  • Estimate: A reasonable approximation.
  • Reasonableness check: A check that compares an answer to context and estimate.
  • Error analysis: Finding, explaining, and correcting a mistake.
  • Misconception: An incorrect idea that leads to an error.
  • Correction: A revised solution with accurate reasoning.
