Worked Example 1

Problem: Estimate 49 + 32, then find the exact sum.

  1. Step 1: Round 49 to 50 and 32 to 30.
  2. Step 2: Estimate: 50 + 30 = 80.
  3. Step 3: Exact: 49 + 32 = 81.
  4. Step 4: The exact answer is reasonable because it is close to 80.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

198 + 305 is about 400

Why it is wrong: 198 is close to 200 and 305 is close to 300, so the estimate should be 200 + 300 = 500.

Correct:

198 + 305 is about 500

Correction: Use the relationship described in the problem, not just the numbers that appear first.
