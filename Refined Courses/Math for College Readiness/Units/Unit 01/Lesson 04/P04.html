Worked Example: Is the Answer Reasonable?

Problem: A student estimates 48 x 5 as about 250. Is the estimate reasonable?

Estimate: 48 is close to 50.

Multiply: 50 x 5 = 250.

Answer: Yes, the estimate is reasonable because 48 is close to 50, and 50 x 5 = 250.

Context check: Five groups of about 50 should be about 250.

Guided Practice Focus

  • Use rounding to estimate.
  • Check answer size and units.
  • Identify the misconception behind an error.
  • Correct work and explain the correction.
