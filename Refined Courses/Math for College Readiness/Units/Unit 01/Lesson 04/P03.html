Error Analysis Routine

  1. Identify what the problem asks.
  2. Estimate a reasonable answer.
  3. Review the operation and units.
  4. Name the misconception.
  5. Show the corrected work.
  6. Interpret the corrected answer in context.

Example: Correct an Error

Problem: A pack of 6 notebooks costs $18. A student says the unit price is $24 per notebook.

Estimate: $18 for 6 notebooks should be a few dollars per notebook, not $24.

Misconception: The student added 18 and 6 instead of dividing total cost by number of notebooks.

Correction: 18 divided by 6 = 3.

Answer: The unit price is $3 per notebook.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not accept an answer just because the arithmetic uses numbers from the problem.

An answer must match the operation, unit, estimate, and real-world context.
