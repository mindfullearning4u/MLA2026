Synthesis Routine

  1. Read the situation and identify the question.
  2. List quantities and units.
  3. Choose the correct tool: percent, rate, formula, function, rate of change, or weighted average.
  4. Solve with organized work.
  5. Interpret the result in context.
  6. Check reasonableness.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not choose a method just because a number looks familiar. The method must match the question, units, and context.
