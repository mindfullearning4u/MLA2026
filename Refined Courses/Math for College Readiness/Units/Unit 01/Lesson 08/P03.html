P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue writing the explanations, examples, and correction strategies into your notebook. This page teaches how to explain your reasoning without relying on a live teacher explanation.

How to Organize the Work

Organized work helps you and your Teacher of Record see whether your reasoning is correct. Use labels so each step has a purpose.

  1. Write what is given.
  2. Write what is being asked.
  3. Choose the strategy and explain why it fits.
  4. Show the calculation.
  5. Interpret the answer in a complete sentence.

Worked Sequence

Situation: A $50 item is discounted 20%. Find the sale price.

  1. Find the discount: 0.20 Ã¯Â¿Â½ 50 = 10.
  2. Subtract from the original price: 50 - 10 = 40.
  3. The sale price is $40.
  4. Check: 20% is one fifth, and one fifth of 50 is 10.

Reasoning: Each step connects the numbers to the context instead of treating them like random values.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Common incorrect thinking:

40

Why this is wrong: A final answer in a real-world problem needs units and context so the reader knows what the number means.

Correct thinking:

The sale price is $40 after a $10 discount.

Correction: Match the operation to the meaning of the quantities and write the answer with units.
