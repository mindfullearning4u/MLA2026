Worked Example

A delivery service uses C(m) = 2m + 15. A customer travels 12 miles and has a 10% discount.

Cost before discount: C(12) = 2(12) + 15 = 39

Discount: 10% of $39 is $3.90

Final cost: $39 - $3.90 = $35.10

Interpretation: The discounted delivery cost is $35.10.
