P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Study all three worked examples carefully. Each example shows the thinking, not just the final answer.

Example 1

Problem: A $50 item is discounted 20%. Find the sale price.

  1. Find the discount: 0.20 ï¿½ 50 = 10.
  2. Subtract from the original price: 50 - 10 = 40.
  3. The sale price is $40.
  4. Check: 20% is one fifth, and one fifth of 50 is 10.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Example 2

Problem: A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours. Find the rate and interpret it.

  1. Divide distance by time.
  2. 150 divided by 3 = 50.
  3. The rate is 50 miles per hour.
  4. This means the car travels an average of 50 miles each hour.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Example 3

Problem: A final grade uses tests 60% and homework 40%. Scores are 82 and 95. Find the weighted average.

  1. Convert weights: 0.60 and 0.40.
  2. Multiply: 82(0.60)=49.2 and 95(0.40)=38.
  3. Add: 49.2 + 38 = 87.2.
  4. The weighted average is 87.2.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

40

Why it is wrong: A final answer in a real-world problem needs units and context so the reader knows what the number means.

Correct:

The sale price is $40 after a $10 discount.

Correction: Use the relationship described in the problem, not just the numbers that appear first.
