P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will synthesize Unit 1 quantitative reasoning skills and complete the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 1 standards synthesis: MLA.MCR.QR.1, MLA.MCR.QR.2, MLA.MCR.QR.3, MLA.MCR.QR.4, MLA.MCR.QR.5, MLA.MCR.QR.6, and embedded MLA.MCR.MC standards.

Mapping Note: This synthesis lesson does not introduce new primary standard ownership.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 1 skills connect across real-world problems.
  • How units, rates, percents, formulas, functions, and weighted averages support decisions.
  • How to check answers for reasonableness.

What You Will Do

  • Review key Unit 1 reasoning tools.
  • Complete guided synthesis practice.
  • Submit notebook evidence and checkpoint work.
  • Complete the Unit 1 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence from the synthesis lesson.
  • Submit a checkpoint response explaining a multi-step decision.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 1 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Unit 1 Synthesis: This lesson asks you to combine the quantitative reasoning skills from the entire unit.

In student language: I can choose and use the right quantitative tools to solve real-world problems and explain my reasoning.
