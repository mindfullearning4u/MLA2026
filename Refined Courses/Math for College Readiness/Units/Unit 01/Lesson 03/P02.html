Lesson 3: Percent Reasoning and Percent Change

Percent Forms

A percent means a number out of 100. Percent reasoning often uses equivalent fraction or decimal forms.

  • 25% = 25/100 = 0.25
  • 50% = 50/100 = 0.50
  • 10% = 10/100 = 0.10
  • 5% = 5/100 = 0.05

Finding a Percent of a Quantity

Problem: Find 20% of $45.

Convert: 20% = 0.20

Multiply: 0.20 x 45 = 9

Answer: 20% of $45 is $9.

Reasonableness: 10% of $45 is $4.50, so 20% should be twice that, or $9.

Vocabulary

  • Percent: A number out of 100.
  • Discount: An amount taken off the original price.
  • Markup: An amount added to the original price.
  • Tax: An added percent of a cost.
  • Percent change: A percent increase or decrease from an original amount.
