P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Percent Reasoning and Percent Change

In this lesson, you will use fractions, decimals, percents, and percent change to solve real-world money and business problems.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.QR.1: I can use fractions, decimals, and percents to solve real-world money and business problems.

Florida Benchmark: MA.912.FL.1.1

Common Core Alignment: HSN-Q.A.1, HSN-Q.A.2

What You Will Learn

  • How to convert between fractions, decimals, and percents.
  • How to find a percent of a quantity.
  • How to calculate percent increase and percent decrease.
  • How to interpret percent results in money and business contexts.

What You Will Do

  • Use decimal and fraction forms of common percents.
  • Calculate discounts, markups, tax, tips, and percent changes.
  • Check whether percent answers are reasonable.
  • Explain percent calculations using units and context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with percent calculations and explanations.
  • Submit a checkpoint using percent reasoning in a real-world decision.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.QR.1: This standard helps you use percents in situations involving money, prices, sales, tips, tax, and business changes.

In student language: I can use percents to solve money problems and explain what the percent means.
