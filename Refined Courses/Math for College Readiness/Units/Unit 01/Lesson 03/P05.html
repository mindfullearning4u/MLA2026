P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Complete the independent work in your notebook. Then prepare your checkpoint response.

Independent Work

  1. Find 15% of $80.
  2. A $120 item is 25% off. Find the sale price.
  3. A restaurant bill is $45. Find an 18% tip.
  4. A price increases from $50 to $60. Find the percent increase.
  5. A price decreases from $90 to $72. Find the percent decrease.

Checkpoint Task

A store sells a $75 item with a 20% discount. After the discount, 6% sales tax is added.

  1. Find the discount amount.
  2. Find the sale price before tax.
  3. Find the tax on the sale price.
  4. Find the final cost.
  5. Explain why your final cost is reasonable.
