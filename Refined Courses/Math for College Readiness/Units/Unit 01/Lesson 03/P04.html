Worked Example: Discount and Tax

Problem: A $50 item is discounted by 20%. Then 6% tax is added to the sale price. What is the final cost?

Discount: 0.20 x 50 = 10

Sale price: 50 - 10 = 40

Tax: 0.06 x 40 = 2.40

Final cost: 40 + 2.40 = 42.40

Answer: The final cost is $42.40.

Reasonableness: The discount lowers the price below $50, and tax adds a small amount back.
