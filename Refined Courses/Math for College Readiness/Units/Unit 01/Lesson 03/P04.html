P04 Worked Example

Study all three worked examples carefully. Each example shows the thinking, not just the final answer.

Example 1

Problem: Find 15% of $80.

  1. Write 15% as 0.15.
  2. Multiply the decimal by the whole.
  3. 0.15 × 80 = 12.
  4. 15% of $80 is $12.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Example 2

Problem: A $60 jacket is discounted 25%. Find the sale price.

  1. Find the discount: 0.25 × 60 = 15.
  2. Subtract the discount from the original price.
  3. 60 - 15 = 45.
  4. The sale price is $45.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Example 3

Problem: A value increases from 40 to 50. Find the percent increase.

  1. Find the change: 50 - 40 = 10.
  2. Divide the change by the original value.
  3. 10 divided by 40 = 0.25 = 25%.
  4. The percent increase is 25%.

Final check: The answer includes the correct unit and matches the question.

Common Mistake

Incorrect:

30 × 90 = 2700

Why it is wrong: The percent must be changed to a decimal before multiplying. 30% means 0.30, not 30 whole groups.

Correct:

0.30 × 90 = 27

Correction: Use the relationship described in the problem, not just the numbers that appear first.
