Discounts and Sale Prices

A discount lowers the original price. First find the discount amount, then subtract it from the original price.

Problem: A $60 jacket is 25% off.

Discount: 0.25 x 60 = 15

Sale price: 60 - 15 = 45

Answer: The sale price is $45.

Percent Change

Percent change compares the amount of change to the original amount.

Formula: percent change = change divided by original amount

Problem: A price increases from $40 to $50.

Change: 50 - 40 = 10

Percent change: 10 divided by 40 = 0.25 = 25%

Answer: The price increased by 25%.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not subtract the percent number directly from the price.

Incorrect: 20% off $80 means 80 - 20 = 60.

Correct: 20% of $80 is $16, so the sale price is $64.
