Weighted Decision Example

A student compares two laptops using price score weighted 50%, battery score weighted 30%, and storage score weighted 20%.

Laptop A: 90, 70, 80

Weighted score: 90(0.50) + 70(0.30) + 80(0.20) = 45 + 21 + 16 = 82

The weighted score for Laptop A is 82.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not use a simple average when the categories have different weights.

If one category counts 60% and another counts 40%, they should not be treated equally.
