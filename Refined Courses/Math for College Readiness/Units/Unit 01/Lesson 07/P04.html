Study the worked example, then complete the guided practice questions in Moodle.

Worked Example

A scholarship score uses grades at 70% and service at 30%. A student earns 88 for grades and 95 for service.

Calculate: 88(0.70) + 95(0.30) = 61.6 + 28.5 = 90.1

Answer: The weighted scholarship score is 90.1.

Interpretation: Grades affect the result more because grades have the larger weight.

Guided Practice Focus

  • Convert percent weights to decimals.
  • Multiply each value by its weight.
  • Add weighted products.
  • Use the result to compare options.
