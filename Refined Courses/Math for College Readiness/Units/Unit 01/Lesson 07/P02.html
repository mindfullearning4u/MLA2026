Lesson 7: Weighted Averages and Multi-Step Reasoning

Weighted Average

A weighted average is an average where some categories count more than others.

Formula: weighted average = value times weight plus value times weight plus value times weight

Weights should total 100%, or 1.00 when written as decimals.

Example

A course grade uses quizzes at 40% and projects at 60%. A student has 80 on quizzes and 90 on projects.

Calculate: 80(0.40) + 90(0.60) = 32 + 54 = 86

Answer: The weighted average is 86.

Vocabulary

  • Weight: The amount a category counts.
  • Weighted average: An average that accounts for different category importance.
  • Decision score: A score used to compare options.
  • Spreadsheet reasoning: Organizing values, weights, and products in a table.
