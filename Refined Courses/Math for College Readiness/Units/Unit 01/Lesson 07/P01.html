P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Weighted Averages and Multi-Step Reasoning

In this lesson, you will calculate and interpret weighted averages using spreadsheet-style reasoning to support decisions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.QR.3: I can calculate and interpret weighted averages using spreadsheets or technology to support decisions.

Florida Benchmark: MA.912.FL.1.3

Common Core Alignment: HSN-Q.A.2, HSS-ID.A.2 support

What You Will Learn

  • How weighted averages are different from simple averages.
  • How weights affect a final score or decision.
  • How to multiply each value by its weight.
  • How to interpret weighted results in context.

What You Will Do

  • Calculate weighted averages.
  • Use weights written as decimals or percents.
  • Compare options using weighted scores.
  • Explain decisions with evidence and units.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with weighted average calculations.
  • Submit a checkpoint comparing options using weights.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
