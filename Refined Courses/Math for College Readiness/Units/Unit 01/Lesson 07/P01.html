Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Weighted Averages and Multi-Step Reasoning

In this lesson, you will learn using weighted averages to make decisions when values do not all count equally.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • How to identify the quantities, units, and unknown before calculating.
  • How to choose a strategy that matches the situation.
  • How to explain and check your answer in context.
