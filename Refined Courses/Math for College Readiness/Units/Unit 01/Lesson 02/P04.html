Worked Example: Compare Unit Prices

Problem: Store A sells 5 notebooks for $12.50. Store B sells 8 notebooks for $18.40. Which store has the lower price per notebook?

Store A unit rate: 12.50 divided by 5 = 2.50, so Store A costs $2.50 per notebook.

Store B unit rate: 18.40 divided by 8 = 2.30, so Store B costs $2.30 per notebook.

Compare: $2.30 is less than $2.50.

Answer: Store B has the lower price per notebook.

Reasonableness: Store B has a higher total price because it includes more notebooks, so the unit rate is needed for a fair comparison.
