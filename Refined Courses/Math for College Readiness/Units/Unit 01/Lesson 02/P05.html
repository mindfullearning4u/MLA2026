P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Complete the independent work in your notebook. Then prepare your checkpoint response.

Independent Work

  1. A pack of 10 folders costs $15. Find the cost per folder.
  2. A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours. Find the miles per hour.
  3. A store sells 4 shirts for $28. At the same rate, how much would 7 shirts cost?
  4. Compare $18 for 6 markers with $20 for 8 markers. Which is the better price per marker?
  5. A business uses 12 labels for every 3 packages. How many labels are needed for 10 packages at the same rate?

Checkpoint Task

A supply store offers two options for copy paper. Option A costs $24 for 6 packs. Option B costs $35 for 10 packs.

  1. Find the unit price for Option A.
  2. Find the unit price for Option B.
  3. Choose the better price per pack.
  4. Explain why total price alone is not a fair comparison.
  5. Use units in your final answer.
