P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Units, Rates, Ratios, and Proportions

In this lesson, you will use ratios, rates, proportional relationships, and units to solve real-world problems involving money, business, and everyday decisions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.QR.2: I can use ratios, rates, and proportional relationships to solve real-world money and business problems.

Florida Benchmark: MA.912.FL.1.2

Common Core Alignment: HSN-Q.A.1, HSN-Q.A.3

What You Will Learn

  • How ratios compare quantities.
  • How rates compare quantities with different units.
  • How unit rates support real-world decisions.
  • How proportions can solve equivalent relationship problems.

What You Will Do

  • Write ratios and rates from contexts.
  • Find unit rates such as dollars per item or miles per hour.
  • Use proportions to solve missing-value problems.
  • Explain answers with correct units and reasonableness checks.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with ratios, rates, unit rates, and proportional reasoning.
  • Submit a checkpoint using units and proportional relationships to justify a decision.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.QR.2: This standard helps you compare quantities in practical situations, especially when money, time, items, distance, or business costs are involved.

In student language: I can use ratios, rates, and proportions to compare choices and solve real-world problems with correct units.
