Checkpoint Task: Average Rate of Change

A tank has 90 gallons at 2 minutes and 54 gallons at 8 minutes. Find the average rate of change and explain what it means.

  1. List the quantities and units.
  2. Choose and show the strategy that matches the situation.
  3. Calculate carefully and label the answer.
  4. Explain what the answer means in context.
  5. Check whether the answer is reasonable.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies quantities and units accurately.
  • Uses a strategy aligned to the lesson standard.
  • Shows organized step-by-step work.
  • Interprets the answer in context.
  • Checks reasonableness using estimation, units, or context.

Alignment
