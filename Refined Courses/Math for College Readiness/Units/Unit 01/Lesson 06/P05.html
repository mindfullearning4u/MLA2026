P05 Independent Work & Checkpoint

Complete the independent work in your notebook. Then prepare your checkpoint response.

Independent Work

  1. A car travels 240 miles in 4 hours. Find the average speed.
  2. A balance changes from $150 in week 1 to $210 in week 5. Find the average rate of change.
  3. A delivery cost changes from $21 at 3 miles to $33 at 9 miles. Find the average rate of change.
  4. For C(m) = 4m + 12, identify the rate of change and its units if C is cost in dollars and m is miles.
  5. Explain why units matter when describing average rate of change.

Checkpoint Task

A delivery company charges $23 for 4 miles and $43 for 14 miles.

  1. Find the change in cost.
  2. Find the change in miles.
  3. Calculate the average rate of change.
  4. Interpret the rate in context.
  5. Explain why the answer is reasonable.
