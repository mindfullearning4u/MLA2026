Rate of Change from a Table

A delivery cost table shows 2 miles costs $19 and 7 miles costs $29.

Change in cost: 29 - 19 = 10 dollars

Change in miles: 7 - 2 = 5 miles

Average rate of change: 10 divided by 5 = 2 dollars per mile

Meaning: Over that interval, the cost increases by $2 for each additional mile.

Rate of Change from a Formula

Formula: C(m) = 2m + 15

The coefficient 2 shows the cost changes by $2 for each additional mile.

Average rate of change: $2 per mile

Common Mistake to Avoid

Do not divide output by input without checking the interval. Average rate of change uses change in output divided by change in input.

Use subtraction first when two points or table rows are given.
