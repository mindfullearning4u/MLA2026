Lesson 6: Average Rate of Change

Average Rate of Change

Average rate of change compares how much an output changes to how much an input changes.

Formula: average rate of change = change in output divided by change in input

Meaning: It tells how much the output changes for each 1-unit change in the input.

Example from a Context

A car travels 180 miles in 3 hours.

Change in output: 180 miles

Change in input: 3 hours

Rate: 180 divided by 3 = 60

Answer: The average rate of change is 60 miles per hour.

Vocabulary

  • Average rate of change: Change in output divided by change in input.
  • Input: The independent quantity.
  • Output: The dependent quantity.
  • Interval: The section over which change is measured.
  • Units of rate: Output units per input unit.
