P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Average Rate of Change

In this lesson, you will calculate and explain average rate of change from tables, graphs, formulas, and real-world contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.QR.6: I can calculate and explain average rate of change from tables, graphs, formulas, and contexts.

Florida Benchmark: MA.912.F.1.3

Common Core Alignment: HSF-IF.B.6

What You Will Learn

  • How to calculate average rate of change.
  • How to interpret change in output over change in input.
  • How to use units when describing rate of change.
  • How to compare rates from tables, formulas, and contexts.

What You Will Do

  • Find changes in input and output.
  • Divide change in output by change in input.
  • Explain the meaning of the rate in context.
  • Check whether the rate is reasonable.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with rate-of-change calculations and explanations.
  • Submit a checkpoint interpreting rate of change from a context.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
