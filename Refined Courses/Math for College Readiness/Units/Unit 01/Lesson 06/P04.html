Worked Example: Compare Change

A savings account has $120 in week 2 and $180 in week 6.

Change in output: 180 - 120 = 60 dollars

Change in input: 6 - 2 = 4 weeks

Average rate of change: 60 divided by 4 = 15 dollars per week

Interpretation: From week 2 to week 6, the balance increased by an average of $15 per week.

Guided Practice Focus

  • Find change in output.
  • Find change in input.
  • Divide output change by input change.
  • Interpret the rate with correct units.
