P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Formulas, Variables, and Function Notation in Context

In this lesson, you will rearrange formulas, evaluate function notation, and interpret quantities in real-world contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.QR.4: I can rearrange formulas to isolate meaningful quantities in real-world situations.

MLA.MCR.QR.5: I can evaluate function notation and interpret outputs in real-world contexts.

Florida Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.1.2; MA.912.F.1.2

Common Core Alignment: HSA-CED.A.4; HSF-IF.A.2

What You Will Learn

  • How variables represent meaningful quantities.
  • How to rearrange formulas to isolate a quantity.
  • How to evaluate function notation for a given input.
  • How to interpret function outputs with units and context.

What You Will Do

  • Substitute values into formulas and functions.
  • Rearrange formulas such as distance, rate, time, and cost formulas.
  • Connect inputs and outputs to real-world meaning.
  • Check units and reasonableness.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Submit notebook evidence with formula and function work.
  • Submit a checkpoint interpreting formulas and function outputs.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
