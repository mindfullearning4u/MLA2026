Worked Example: Formula and Function

Formula: d = rt

Question: A driver travels 180 miles at 60 miles per hour. How long does the trip take?

Rearrange: t = d/r

Substitute: t = 180/60 = 3

Answer: The trip takes 3 hours.

Function: C(h) = 12h + 5

Evaluate: C(3) = 12(3) + 5 = 41

Interpret: A 3-hour rental costs $41.

Guided Practice Focus
