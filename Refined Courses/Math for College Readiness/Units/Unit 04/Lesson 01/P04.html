P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1: Calculate gross pay

Problem: A worker earns $15 per hour and works 20 hours.

  1. Identify the hourly wage: $15 per hour.
  2. Identify the hours worked: 20 hours.
  3. Multiply: 15 Ã— 20 = 300.

Answer: Gross pay is $300.

Example 2: Calculate tax deduction

Problem: A paycheck has gross pay of $500 and estimated taxes of 10%.

  1. Write 10% as 0.10.
  2. Multiply: 500 Ã— 0.10 = 50.
  3. The deduction is $50.

Answer: The estimated tax deduction is $50.

Example 3: Calculate net pay

Problem: Gross pay is $500 and total deductions are $85.

  1. Start with gross pay.
  2. Subtract deductions: 500 - 85 = 415.
  3. Label the result as take-home pay.

Answer: Net pay is $415.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining whether it is gross pay, net pay, expense total, leftover money, or net worth.

Correct: Label the amount and explain what it means for the financial decision.
