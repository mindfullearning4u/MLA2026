P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Credit, Loans, and Debt

In this lesson, you will learn to analyze credit, finance charges, buying power, and debt payoff plans.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.FL.7: I can analyze credit card finance charges and buying power.
MLA.MCR.FL.8: I can analyze loan costs and repayment options.
MLA.MCR.FL.9: I can compare debt payoff plans.
MLA.MCR.FL.10: I can interpret credit, debt, and financial decision constraints.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the money amounts, rates, time, and constraints in a financial situation.
  • How to use percent and multiplication to compare financial options.
  • How to explain total cost or total growth in everyday language.
  • How to check whether a financial decision is reasonable.

What You Will Do

  • Write organized notebook notes from the teaching pages.
  • Study step-by-step examples before trying practice problems.
  • Complete guided practice and independent work using clear reasoning.
  • Submit a checkpoint aligned to the lesson standard.
