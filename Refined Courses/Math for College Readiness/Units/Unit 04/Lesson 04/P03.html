Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 2: Credit, Loans, and Debt

Build the Reasoning

Debt decisions should fit a budget. A lower monthly payment may look easier, but it can cost more over time if the loan term is longer or interest keeps adding up. A responsible comparison explains both short-term affordability and long-term total cost.

A complete financial answer includes the calculation, the label, and a short interpretation. The interpretation matters because money decisions are based on what the number means over time.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Choosing the smaller monthly payment or larger final balance without checking the total cost, time, or interest structure.

Correct: Compare the full situation: principal, rate, time, payments, total cost, and what the result means.

Notebook Task

Write one incorrect financial comparison and one corrected comparison. Explain why the corrected version gives better decision support.
