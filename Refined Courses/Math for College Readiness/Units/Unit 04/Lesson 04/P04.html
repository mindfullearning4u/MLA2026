P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1: Find total paid

Problem: A loan payment is $60 per month for 10 months.

  1. Identify monthly payment: $60.
  2. Identify number of months: 10.
  3. Multiply: 60 × 10 = 600.

Answer: The total paid is $600.

Example 2: Compare two payment plans

Problem: Plan A costs $80 for 6 months. Plan B costs $50 for 12 months.

  1. Plan A total: 80 × 6 = 480.
  2. Plan B total: 50 × 12 = 600.
  3. Compare totals: Plan A costs less overall.

Answer: Plan A costs $120 less overall.

Example 3: Interpret a finance charge

Problem: An item costs $700 cash, but the credit plan totals $840.

  1. Identify cash price: $700.
  2. Identify credit total: $840.
  3. Subtract: 840 - 700 = 140.

Answer: The finance charge is $140.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining whether it is interest, balance, total paid, finance charge, or amount saved.

Correct: Label the amount and explain what it means for the financial decision.
