Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Credit, Loans, and Debt

Vocabulary

  • Credit: the ability to borrow money and pay later.
  • Debt: money owed.
  • Finance charge: the cost of borrowing, often based on interest and fees.
  • Principal: the original amount borrowed.
  • Minimum payment: the smallest required payment for a billing period.
  • Loan term: the length of time used to repay a loan.
  • Buying power: what a person can afford based on income, credit, debt, and constraints.
  • Payoff plan: a strategy for reducing debt over time.

Learn the Idea

Credit can be useful, but borrowed money usually has a cost. A finance charge, interest rate, fee, or longer repayment time can make the total paid much larger than the original price. When comparing credit or loans, focus on total cost, payment amount, interest, and how long the debt lasts.

  1. Identify the financial question being asked.
  2. List principal, rate, time, payment, fee, or term when given.
  3. Choose the calculation that matches the situation.
  4. Write the answer with dollars and a sentence explaining what it means.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and the four-step process into your notebook. Add one original example involving saving, borrowing, credit, a loan, or debt repayment.
