P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1: Combine budget and saving

Problem: Net pay is $1,500 and monthly expenses are $1,250.

  1. Use leftover = net pay - expenses.
  2. Subtract: 1,500 - 1,250 = 250.
  3. The leftover can be saved, spent, or used for debt.

Answer: There is $250 left after planned expenses.

Example 2: Combine debt and total cost

Problem: A payment plan costs $60 per month for 18 months.

  1. Identify monthly payment: $60.
  2. Identify number of months: 18.
  3. Multiply: 60 x 18 = 1,080.

Answer: The total paid is $1,080.

Example 3: Combine insurance and risk

Problem: Plan A has lower monthly premium but a higher deductible than Plan B.

  1. Lower premium means lower regular cost.
  2. Higher deductible means more possible out-of-pocket cost.
  3. The better choice depends on expected use and ability to handle risk.

Answer: Plan A saves monthly money but carries higher deductible risk.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining what it means for the decision.

Correct: Label the amount and explain the tradeoff or constraint connected to it.
