P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1: Find annual contributions

Problem: A student saves $50 each month for 12 months.

  1. Identify monthly contribution: $50.
  2. Identify 12 months in one year.
  3. Multiply: 50 × 12 = 600.

Answer: The student contributes $600 in one year.

Example 2: Add growth to savings

Problem: A savings account receives $900 in contributions and earns $45 in growth.

  1. Start with contributions: $900.
  2. Add growth: 900 + 45 = 945.
  3. Label the result as ending balance.

Answer: The ending balance is $945.

Example 3: Compare two savings plans

Problem: Plan A ends with $1,200. Plan B ends with $1,350.

  1. Identify final balance for each plan.
  2. Compare 1,350 to 1,200.
  3. Plan B is higher by 150 dollars.

Answer: Plan B ends $150 higher.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining whether it is premium cost, deductible risk, contribution total, growth, or ending balance.

Correct: Label the amount and explain what it means for the financial decision.
