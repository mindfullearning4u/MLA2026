Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Retirement, Savings, and Long-Term Planning

Vocabulary

  • Savings plan: a plan for setting aside money over time.
  • Retirement: a long-term financial goal for future living expenses after working years.
  • Contribution: money added to a savings or retirement account.
  • Growth model: a rule or table that estimates how money changes over time.
  • Compound growth: growth based on the updated balance over time.
  • Time horizon: length of time money has to grow.
  • Future value: estimated amount at a future time.
  • Spreadsheet table: organized rows and columns used to track calculations over time.

Learn the Idea

Long-term planning depends heavily on time. Small contributions can become more powerful when they are made consistently and have more time to grow. A spreadsheet table helps track starting balance, contributions, growth, and ending balance over many years.

  1. Identify the financial question being asked.
  2. List regular costs, possible costs, time, growth, or risk.
  3. Choose the calculation that matches the situation.
  4. Write the answer with dollars and a sentence explaining what it means.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and the four-step process into your notebook. Add one original example involving insurance, risk, savings, retirement, or long-term planning.
