Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Insurance and Risk

Vocabulary

  • Insurance: a financial product that helps protect against large possible costs.
  • Premium: regular payment made to keep insurance coverage.
  • Deductible: amount paid out of pocket before insurance pays certain costs.
  • Copay: fixed amount paid for a covered service.
  • Out-of-pocket cost: money paid directly by the person.
  • Risk: the chance of a loss, accident, illness, or expensive event.
  • Coverage: what an insurance plan helps pay for.
  • Tradeoff: a choice where gaining one benefit may mean giving up another.

Learn the Idea

Insurance decisions involve both certain costs and possible costs. A premium is usually paid regularly whether or not a person uses the insurance. A deductible is paid only when certain claims or services happen. A plan with a lower premium may have a higher deductible, so the best choice depends on risk, expected use, and ability to handle a large cost.

  1. Identify the financial question being asked.
  2. List regular costs, possible costs, time, growth, or risk.
  3. Choose the calculation that matches the situation.
  4. Write the answer with dollars and a sentence explaining what it means.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and the four-step process into your notebook. Add one original example involving insurance, risk, savings, retirement, or long-term planning.
