Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 2: Insurance and Risk

Build the Reasoning

To compare insurance options, calculate the predictable cost first, such as monthly premiums for a year. Then consider possible out-of-pocket costs, such as deductibles or copays. A responsible comparison explains the financial tradeoff instead of choosing only the cheapest monthly price.

A complete financial answer explains the cost, the risk or growth, and the decision tradeoff. The number alone is not enough because financial planning depends on context.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Choosing the lowest monthly cost or largest single contribution without checking risk, time, growth, and total cost.

Correct: Compare the full decision: regular cost, possible cost, growth, time, and what the result means.

Notebook Task

Write one incomplete financial comparison and one corrected comparison. Explain why the corrected version gives better decision support.
