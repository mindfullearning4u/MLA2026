P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1: Simple interest

Problem: Find simple interest on $400 at 5% for 2 years.

Visual: simple interest inputs

PrincipalRateTime
$4005%2 years
  1. Step 1: Identify principal: $400.
  2. Step 2: Write 5% as 0.05.
  3. Step 3: Compute interest: 400 × 0.05 × 2 = 40.

Answer: The simple interest is $40.

Worked Example 2: Final balance with simple interest

Problem: A loan has principal $800 and simple interest $96.

  1. Step 1: Start with the principal.
  2. Step 2: Add the interest: 800 + 96 = 896.
  3. Step 3: Label the result as total amount owed.

Answer: The final balance is $896.

Worked Example 3: Compound interest idea

Problem: An account earns 3% interest each year and interest is added yearly.

  1. Step 1: After year 1, interest is added to the balance.
  2. Step 2: Year 2 interest is calculated on the new balance.
  3. Step 3: This is compound interest because interest earns more interest.

Answer: Compound interest uses the updated balance each period.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining whether it is interest, balance, total paid, finance charge, or amount saved.

Correct: Label the amount and explain what it means for the financial decision.
