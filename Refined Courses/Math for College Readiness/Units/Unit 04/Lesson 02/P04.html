P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1: Find total expenses

Problem: A budget lists rent $500, food $180, phone $60, and savings $100.

  1. Step 1: Add the planned amounts.
  2. Step 2: 500 + 180 + 60 + 100 = 840.
  3. Step 3: Compare total expenses to income if income is known.

Answer: Total planned expenses are $840.

Worked Example 2: Find net worth

Problem: Assets are $1,200 and liabilities are $450.

  1. Step 1: Identify total assets: $1,200.
  2. Step 2: Identify total liabilities: $450.
  3. Step 3: Subtract: 1,200 - 450 = 750.

Answer: Net worth is $750.

Worked Example 3: Check a budget constraint

Problem: Net income is $1,000 and planned expenses are $1,080.

  1. Step 1: Compare expenses to income.
  2. Step 2: 1,080 is greater than 1,000.
  3. Step 3: The budget is short by 80 dollars.

Answer: The budget is not balanced because expenses exceed income by $80.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining whether it is gross pay, net pay, expense total, leftover money, or net worth.

Correct: Label the amount and explain what it means for the financial decision.
