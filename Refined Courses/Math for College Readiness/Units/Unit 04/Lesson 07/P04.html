P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1: Calculate revenue

Problem: A fundraiser sells 30 items for $6 each.

Visual: revenue factors

ItemsPrice each
30$6
  1. Step 1: Identify number sold: 30.
  2. Step 2: Identify price per item: $6.
  3. Step 3: Multiply: 30 x 6 = 180.

Answer: Revenue is $180.

Worked Example 2: Calculate profit

Problem: Revenue is $450 and costs are $320.

  1. Step 1: Use profit = revenue - cost.
  2. Step 2: Subtract: 450 - 320 = 130.
  3. Step 3: Because the result is positive, there is profit.

Answer: Profit is $130.

Worked Example 3: Compare tradeoffs

Problem: Option A earns $200 profit and takes 10 hours. Option B earns $150 profit and takes 4 hours.

  1. Step 1: Option A has higher total profit.
  2. Step 2: Option B takes fewer hours.
  3. Step 3: The better choice depends on whether time or total profit matters more.

Answer: Option A earns more, but Option B may be better if time is the main constraint.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing only a dollar amount without explaining what it means for the decision.

Correct: Label the amount and explain the tradeoff or constraint connected to it.
