Solving for Mass or Volume

The density formula can be rearranged depending on what is missing. If density = mass / volume, then mass = density × volume. If volume is missing, volume = mass / density.

  1. Start by writing what the problem gives you.
  2. Identify what is missing: density, mass, or volume.
  3. Choose the relationship that solves for the missing quantity.
  4. Check whether the unit matches the missing quantity.

Reasonableness Check

Density tells how much mass is in one unit of volume. If a material has density 4 grams per cubic cm and the object has volume 10 cubic cm, the mass should be 4 grams repeated 10 times, or 40 grams.

The unit helps you decide: grams per cubic cm times cubic cm leaves grams.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: An object with mass 60 grams and volume 12 cubic cm has density 60 × 12 = 720 grams per cubic cm.

This multiplies mass and volume. Density is mass divided by volume.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Density = 60 / 12 = 5 grams per cubic cm.

The answer means each cubic centimeter has 5 grams of mass.

Notebook Task

In your notebook, write one example where density is missing and one example where mass is missing. Identify which operation is needed in each example.
