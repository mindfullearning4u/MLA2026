Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Density as a Measurement Ratio

Vocabulary

  • Density: mass divided by volume.
  • Mass: the amount of matter in an object, often measured in grams or kilograms.
  • Volume: the amount of space an object takes up.
  • Ratio unit: a unit that compares two measurements, such as grams per cubic centimeter.
  • Applied measurement: using units and ratios to solve a real-world problem.

Learn the Idea

Density compares how much mass is packed into a certain amount of space. The formula is density = mass / volume. The unit usually includes one mass unit and one volume unit, such as grams per cubic centimeter.

  1. Identify the mass.
  2. Identify the volume.
  3. Divide mass by volume.
  4. Write the ratio unit so the answer explains what each number means.

If an object has mass 40 grams and volume 8 cubic cm, its density is 40 / 8 = 5 grams per cubic cm.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then write one sentence explaining what grams per cubic centimeter means.
