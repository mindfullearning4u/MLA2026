P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: An object has mass 90 grams and volume 15 cubic cm. What is its density?

Visual: density inputs

MassVolume
90 g15 cubic cm
  1. Step 1: Density = mass / volume.
  2. Step 2: Substitute: 90 / 15.
  3. Step 3: Divide: 90 / 15 = 6.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 6 grams per cubic cm.

Worked Example 2

Question: A material has density 8 grams per cubic cm and volume 12 cubic cm. What is the mass?

  1. Step 1: Mass = density × volume.
  2. Step 2: Substitute: 8 × 12.
  3. Step 3: Multiply: 8 × 12 = 96.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 96 grams.

Worked Example 3

Question: A material has mass 45 grams and density 9 grams per cubic cm. What is the volume?

  1. Step 1: Volume = mass / density.
  2. Step 2: Substitute: 45 / 9.
  3. Step 3: Divide: 45 / 9 = 5.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 5 cubic cm.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: If density is 4 grams per cubic cm and volume is 7 cubic cm, mass is 4 / 7 grams.

This divides when mass should be found by multiplying density by volume.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Mass = density × volume = 4 × 7 = 28 grams.

The unit grams remains because grams per cubic cm multiplied by cubic cm gives grams.
