Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Units Tell the Meaning

Vocabulary

  • Unit: the label that tells what is being measured, such as feet, inches, minutes, or gallons.
  • Dimension: the type of measurement, such as length, area, volume, weight, or time.
  • Conversion factor: a ratio that compares two equal measurements in different units.
  • Equivalent measurement: two measurements that describe the same amount using different units.
  • Rate: a comparison of two different units, such as miles per hour.

Learn the Idea

A number by itself is incomplete in measurement. The number tells how many. The unit tells how many of what. For example, 12 could mean 12 inches, 12 feet, 12 minutes, or 12 gallons. Those are very different quantities.

  1. Read the number and the unit together.
  2. Identify the dimension. Inches and feet measure length. Square feet measure area. Cubic feet measure volume.
  3. Choose a conversion factor that uses equal measurements, such as 1 foot = 12 inches.
  4. Keep the unit label in the work so the answer remains connected to the situation.

Example: 3 feet and 36 inches are equivalent because each foot has 12 inches, and 3 × 12 = 36.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then write three measurements from real life and identify the dimension of each one.
