P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: Convert 7 feet to inches.

  1. Step 1: Use the conversion 1 foot = 12 inches.
  2. Step 2: Feet are larger than inches, so the number should increase.
  3. Step 3: Multiply: 7 × 12 = 84.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 7 feet = 84 inches.

Worked Example 2

Question: Convert 180 minutes to hours.

  1. Step 1: Use the conversion 60 minutes = 1 hour.
  2. Step 2: Minutes are smaller than hours, so the number should decrease.
  3. Step 3: Divide: 180 / 60 = 3.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 180 minutes = 3 hours.

Worked Example 3

Question: A walkway is 4 yards long. How many feet long is it?

  1. Step 1: Use the conversion 1 yard = 3 feet.
  2. Step 2: Yards are larger than feet, so multiply.
  3. Step 3: 4 × 3 = 12.
  4. Step 4: The walkway is 12 feet long.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: 2 yards = 2 feet because both measure length.

This ignores the unit size. Yards and feet measure length, but they are not the same size.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: 2 yards = 6 feet because 1 yard = 3 feet.

A unit conversion changes the number so the measurement keeps the same real-world size.
