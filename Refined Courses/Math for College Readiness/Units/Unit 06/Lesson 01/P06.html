P06 Notebook Evidence Submission

Instructions

Complete the three parts below in your notebook. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to decide whether you understand units and conversions or need extra help before moving forward.

Part A

Classify each measurement as length, area, volume, weight, or time: 8 feet, 24 square inches, 3 gallons, 15 pounds, and 45 minutes.

Part B

Convert 9 feet to inches and 150 minutes to hours. Show the conversion factor or explain the operation you used.

Part C

A room is 4 yards wide. Convert the width to feet, then explain why the number of feet is larger than the number of yards.
