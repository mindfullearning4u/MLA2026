P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Measurement, Units, and Conversions

In this lesson, you will learn how units help you measure real situations and how conversions let you compare measurements written in different units.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Support-only embedded MLA.MCR.MC standards: I can use units, dimensions, and conversions to prepare for applied measurement.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the unit attached to a measurement.
  • How to decide whether a measurement describes length, area, volume, weight, or time.
  • How to use a conversion factor to change from one unit to another.
  • How to check whether an answer makes sense by reading the unit.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes for units, dimensions, and conversion factors.
  • Practice one-step and multi-step unit conversions.
  • Use labels to keep track of each calculation.
  • Submit checkpoint work that shows clear unit reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose the correct conversion factor for the situation.
  • Write measurements with correct units throughout the work.
  • Explain why a converted measurement is reasonable.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Support-only embedded MLA.MCR.MC standards: I can use units, dimensions, and conversions to prepare for applied measurement.

In student language: I can tell what a measurement means, change it into another unit, and keep the label attached so the answer is useful.
