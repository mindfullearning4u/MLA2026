P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Building a Complete Response

A complete Unit 6 response has three parts: identify the tool, calculate carefully, and explain the answer in context. Do not skip the explanation. The explanation shows that the answer is not just a number.

Final Check

Before submitting, check units, reasonableness, and constraints. If the final question asks for a decision, state the decision clearly.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: Choosing a formula because it was used most recently, not because it matches the question.

This can lead to using area when the problem asks for volume or using a scale factor when the problem asks for density.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Choose the formula or relationship only after identifying what the question asks you to measure.

Tool choice should come from the context, the unit, and the diagram.
