P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will synthesize measurement, scale, geometry, right triangles, and applied modeling from Unit 6.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 6 standards synthesis: MLA.MCR.GM.1 through MLA.MCR.GM.7.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 6 measurement ideas connect.
  • How to choose between scale, area, volume, coordinate geometry, and right-triangle tools.
  • How to explain a complete modeling decision.

What You Will Do

  • Review the major Unit 6 tools.
  • Complete synthesis guided practice.
  • Submit notebook evidence and checkpoint work.
  • Complete the Unit 6 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose correct measurement tools.
  • Use units accurately.
  • Explain reasonableness and constraints.
  • Demonstrate mastery on the Unit 6 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Unit 6 standards synthesis: MLA.MCR.GM.1 through MLA.MCR.GM.7.

In student language: I can use all of the measurement and geometry tools from this unit to solve applied problems and explain my thinking.
