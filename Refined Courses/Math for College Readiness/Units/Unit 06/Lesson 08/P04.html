P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: A scale drawing uses 1 inch for 5 feet. What does 6 inches represent?

  1. Step 1: Identify the scale rate: 5 feet per inch.
  2. Step 2: Multiply: 6 × 5 = 30.
  3. Step 3: Answer: 30 feet.
  4. Step 4: Check: more inches on the drawing means more real feet.

Worked Example 2

Question: A rectangular prism is 6 feet by 4 feet by 3 feet. Find the volume.

  1. Step 1: The problem asks for inside space, so use volume.
  2. Step 2: Volume = 6 × 4 × 3.
  3. Step 3: Volume = 72.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 72 cubic feet.

Worked Example 3

Question: A support cable is 13 feet long and reaches 12 feet up a pole. Find the ground distance.

  1. Step 1: The cable is the hypotenuse.
  2. Step 2: Use a2 + 12 × 12 = 13 × 13.
  3. Step 3: a2 = 25.
  4. Step 4: a = √25 = 5 feet.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: A student writes 72 square feet for the prism volume.

The number may be correct, but the unit is wrong because volume uses cubic units.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: A rectangular prism volume of 72 should be written as 72 cubic feet.

The cubic unit shows that length, width, and height were used.
