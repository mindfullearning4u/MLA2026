Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Area and Formula Selection

Vocabulary

  • Area: the amount of flat space inside a two-dimensional figure.
  • Square units: units used for area, such as square feet or square meters.
  • Surface area: the total area of all outside faces of a three-dimensional object.
  • Volume: the amount of space inside a three-dimensional object.
  • Cubic units: units used for volume, such as cubic inches or cubic centimeters.

Learn the Idea

Before using a formula, decide what the question asks you to measure. Area is flat space. Surface area is outside covering. Volume is inside space. Choosing the measurement type first helps you avoid using the wrong formula.

  1. Read the context and underline what is being measured.
  2. Decide whether the question asks for area, surface area, or volume.
  3. Choose a formula that matches the shape.
  4. Substitute the given dimensions and keep the unit label.

For a rectangle, area = length × width. A floor that is 12 feet long and 9 feet wide has area 12 × 9 = 108 square feet.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then write one real-world question that asks for area and explain why square units are needed.
