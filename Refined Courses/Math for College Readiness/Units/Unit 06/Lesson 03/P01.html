P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Area, Surface Area, and Volume

In this lesson, you will learn how to choose a measurement formula, substitute values, and label answers with square or cubic units.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.GM.4: I can solve applied area problems with correct units.

MLA.MCR.GM.5: I can solve applied surface area problems with correct units.

MLA.MCR.GM.6: I can solve applied volume problems with correct units.

What You Will Learn

  • How area measures flat space in square units.
  • How surface area measures the outside faces of a three-dimensional object.
  • How volume measures space inside a three-dimensional object in cubic units.
  • How to choose formulas based on the shape and the question being asked.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes for area, surface area, and volume.
  • Practice formula selection before substituting numbers.
  • Show units at every step so the measurement meaning stays clear.
  • Submit checkpoint work that explains which formula fits the situation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Select the correct formula for area, surface area, or volume.
  • Substitute dimensions accurately.
  • Use square units for area and cubic units for volume.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.GM.4, MLA.MCR.GM.5, and MLA.MCR.GM.6: I can solve applied area, surface area, and volume problems with correct units.

In student language: I can decide whether a problem asks for flat space, outside covering, or inside space, then use the correct formula and unit label.
