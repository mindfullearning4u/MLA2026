P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Complete the three parts below in your notebook. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to decide whether you understand formula selection and measurement units or need extra help before moving forward.

Part A

Find the area of a rectangular wall that is 11 feet wide and 8 feet high. Use the correct unit label.

Part B

Find the volume of a rectangular storage bin that is 6 feet long, 3 feet wide, and 2 feet high. Use the correct unit label.

Part C

A cube has side length 5 inches. Find its surface area and explain why the answer uses square inches instead of cubic inches.
