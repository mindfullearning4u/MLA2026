P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: A rectangular garden is 14 feet long and 6 feet wide. What is the area?

Visual: rectangle dimensions

  1. Step 1: The question asks for flat space, so use area.
  2. Step 2: Area = length × width.
  3. Step 3: Substitute: 14 × 6 = 84.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 84 square feet.

Worked Example 2

Question: A rectangular prism is 5 inches long, 4 inches wide, and 3 inches high. What is the volume?

  1. Step 1: The question asks for inside space, so use volume.
  2. Step 2: Volume = length × width × height.
  3. Step 3: Substitute: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 60 cubic inches.

Worked Example 3

Question: A cube has side length 4 cm. What is its surface area?

  1. Step 1: A cube has 6 congruent square faces.
  2. Step 2: Area of one face = 4 × 4 = 16 square cm.
  3. Step 3: Surface area = 6 × 16 = 96.
  4. Step 4: Answer: 96 square cm.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The area of a 10-foot by 8-foot floor is 80 cubic feet.

The number is correct, but the unit is wrong. A floor is flat, so the answer needs square units.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The area of a 10-foot by 8-foot floor is 80 square feet.

Use square units for area because area measures two-dimensional flat space.
