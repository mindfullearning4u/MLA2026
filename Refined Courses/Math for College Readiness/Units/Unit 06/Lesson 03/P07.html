Checkpoint Task

A rectangular display case is 8 feet long, 3 feet wide, and 4 feet high. Find the volume of the display case. Then explain whether the answer should be written in square feet or cubic feet and why.

Mastery Criteria

  • The correct volume formula is selected.
  • The dimensions are multiplied accurately.
  • The explanation correctly connects cubic units to three-dimensional space.
