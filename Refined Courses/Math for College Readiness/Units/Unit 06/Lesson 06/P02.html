Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Right Triangles and Missing Sides

Vocabulary

  • Right triangle: a triangle with one 90-degree angle.
  • Legs: the two sides that form the right angle.
  • Hypotenuse: the longest side, across from the right angle.
  • Pythagorean Theorem: a2 + b2 = c2 for right triangles.
  • Square root: the value that gives a number when multiplied by itself.

Learn the Idea

The Pythagorean Theorem works only for right triangles. It connects the two legs and the hypotenuse. The hypotenuse is always c in a2 + b2 = c2.

  1. Identify the right angle.
  2. Label the hypotenuse across from the right angle.
  3. Substitute the known side lengths into a2 + b2 = c2.
  4. Use a square root when solving for a side length.

For example, if the legs are 3 and 4, then c2 = 3 × 3 + 4 × 4 = 25, so c = √25 = 5.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then draw a right triangle and label the two legs and the hypotenuse.
