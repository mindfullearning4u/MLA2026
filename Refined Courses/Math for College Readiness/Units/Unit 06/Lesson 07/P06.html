Instructions

Complete the three parts below in your notebook. Your Teacher of Record will use this work to decide whether you can combine measurement tools or need extra help before moving forward.

Part A

A rectangular patio is 15 feet by 10 feet. Concrete costs $6 per square foot. Find the total cost.

Part B

A box is 4 feet by 5 feet by 6 feet. It must have volume no more than 130 cubic feet. Does it meet the constraint?

Part C

A ramp rises 9 feet and has a horizontal run of 12 feet. Find the ramp length and explain why a right triangle model fits.
