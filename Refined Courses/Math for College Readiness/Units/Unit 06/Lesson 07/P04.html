P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the context, selects a strategy, calculates carefully, and interprets the result.

Worked Example 1

Question: A rectangular garden is 12 feet by 8 feet. Mulch costs $3 per square foot. What is the total cost?

Visual: area before cost

  1. Step 1: Find area: 12 × 8 = 96 square feet.
  2. Step 2: Multiply by cost: 96 × 3 = 288.
  3. Step 3: The total cost is $288.
  4. Step 4: Check: dollars make sense because the final question asks for cost.

Worked Example 2

Question: A box is 5 feet by 4 feet by 3 feet. Will it fit in a storage space with maximum volume 70 cubic feet?

  1. Step 1: Find volume: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cubic feet.
  2. Step 2: Compare to the constraint: 60 ≤ 70.
  3. Step 3: The box meets the volume constraint.
  4. Step 4: State the decision: it fits by volume.

Worked Example 3

Question: A ramp rises 6 feet and has a horizontal run of 8 feet. How long is the ramp?

  1. Step 1: The ramp forms a right triangle.
  2. Step 2: Use a2 + b2 = c2: 6 × 6 + 8 × 8 = 100.
  3. Step 3: Take the square root: c = √100 = 10.
  4. Step 4: The ramp is 10 feet long.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The box volume is 5 + 4 + 3 = 12 cubic feet.

This adds dimensions instead of multiplying length, width, and height.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: Volume = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 cubic feet.

Volume uses three dimensions and cubic units.
