P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1

Question: A rectangular garden is 12 feet by 8 feet. Mulch costs $3 per square foot. What is the total cost?

  1. Find area: 12 × 8 = 96 square feet.
  2. Multiply by cost: 96 × 3 = 288.
  3. The total cost is $288.
  4. Check: dollars make sense because the final question asks for cost.
