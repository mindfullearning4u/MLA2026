Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Similar Figures and Scale Factor

Vocabulary

  • Similar figures: figures with the same shape and proportional side lengths.
  • Corresponding sides: sides that match in the same position on similar figures.
  • Scale factor: the multiplier that changes one figure into a similar figure.
  • Dilation: a transformation that enlarges or reduces a figure by a scale factor.
  • Proportion: an equation showing two ratios are equal.

Learn the Idea

Similar figures have the same shape, but they may not have the same size. A photo and an enlarged poster of that photo are similar because every length is multiplied by the same number.

  1. Match corresponding sides. Do not compare sides that are in different positions.
  2. Find the scale factor by comparing new length / original length.
  3. Multiply by the scale factor to find a new length.
  4. Divide by the scale factor if you need to go back to the original length.

If a 4-inch side becomes a 12-inch side, the scale factor is 12 / 4 = 3. Every matching length is multiplied by 3.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then draw two rectangles that are similar and label the matching sides.
