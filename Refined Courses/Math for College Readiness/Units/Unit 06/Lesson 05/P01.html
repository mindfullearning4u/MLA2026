P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Coordinate Geometry in Context

In this lesson, you will use points on a coordinate plane to find side lengths, perimeter, and area in applied geometry situations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.MCR.GM.2: I can use coordinate geometry to solve perimeter and area problems involving polygons.

What You Will Learn

  • How to read ordered pairs on a coordinate plane.
  • How to find horizontal and vertical distances from coordinates.
  • How to find perimeter by adding side lengths.
  • How to find polygon area using coordinate information.

What You Will Do

  • Build notebook notes for coordinate geometry vocabulary.
  • Use coordinate differences to find side lengths.
  • Calculate perimeter and area for rectangles and polygons on a grid.
  • Submit checkpoint work that shows coordinate reasoning clearly.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Read and use ordered pairs correctly.
  • Find side lengths from coordinate differences.
  • Use correct formulas and units for perimeter and area.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.MCR.GM.2: I can use coordinate geometry to solve perimeter and area problems involving polygons.

In student language: I can use points on a grid to measure shapes and solve real-world perimeter and area problems.
