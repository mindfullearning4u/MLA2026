P04 Guided Practice / Worked Example

Example 1

Question: Find the distance from (2, 4) to (9, 4).

  1. The y-values are the same, so the distance is horizontal.
  2. Subtract the x-values.
  3. 9 - 2 = 7.
  4. The distance is 7 units.

Example 2

Question: A rectangle has vertices (1, 2), (7, 2), (7, 6), and (1, 6). Find the perimeter.

  1. Horizontal length: 7 - 1 = 6 units.
  2. Vertical length: 6 - 2 = 4 units.
  3. Perimeter = 6 + 4 + 6 + 4 = 20.
  4. The perimeter is 20 units.

Example 3

Question: A rectangle has vertices (0, 0), (5, 0), (5, 3), and (0, 3). Find the area.

  1. Horizontal length: 5 - 0 = 5 units.
  2. Vertical height: 3 - 0 = 3 units.
  3. Area = 5 × 3 = 15.
  4. The area is 15 square units.

Common Mistake - Incorrect

Incorrect: The area of the rectangle from (0, 0) to (5, 3) is 8 square units because 5 + 3 = 8.

This adds the side lengths. Area of a rectangle uses multiplication.

Common Mistake - Correct

Correct: The area is 5 × 3 = 15 square units.

Use coordinate differences to find side lengths, then apply the correct measurement formula.
