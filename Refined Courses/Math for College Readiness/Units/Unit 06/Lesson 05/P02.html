Notebook Title

Notebook Task Part 1: Measuring on the Coordinate Plane

Vocabulary

  • Coordinate plane: a grid formed by a horizontal x-axis and a vertical y-axis.
  • Ordered pair: a point written as (x, y).
  • Horizontal distance: distance left or right between x-values.
  • Vertical distance: distance up or down between y-values.
  • Polygon: a closed figure made from straight line segments.

Learn the Idea

Coordinate geometry lets you measure shapes drawn on a grid. If two points have the same y-value, the segment between them is horizontal. If two points have the same x-value, the segment between them is vertical.

  1. Read each point as (x, y).
  2. For horizontal distance, subtract the x-values.
  3. For vertical distance, subtract the y-values.
  4. Use the side lengths to find perimeter or area.

For example, the distance from (2, 3) to (8, 3) is 6 units because 8 - 2 = 6.

Notebook Task

Copy the vocabulary and four-step process into your notebook. Then write two horizontal points and two vertical points and find each distance.
