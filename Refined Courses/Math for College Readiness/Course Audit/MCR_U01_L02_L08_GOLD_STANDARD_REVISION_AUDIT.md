## Instructional Quality Findings

- Lessons now use foundation-first explanations appropriate for remedial, standard, and advanced learners.
- Written instruction is more self-contained for online delivery.
- Worked examples are step-by-step and include interpretation in context.
- Common mistakes use required red incorrect and green correct formatting.
- Independent work is organized for Teacher of Record intervention review.
- Checkpoint tasks align to the lesson focus and standards.

## Visual and Support Findings

- ALG1-style visual shell preserved.
- Color-block organization is consistent across Lessons 2-8.
- Every page includes a Teacher of Record support reminder.
- P07 Submission Workflow appears once per lesson.
- Lesson pages avoid flagged keyboard-style math notation patterns.
