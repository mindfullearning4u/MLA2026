## Unit Audits

Unit audits are completed only after all required unit components are built, audited, fixed, and re-audited.

Required unit components:

- Lessons 1-7
- Lesson 8: Putting It All Together
- Guided Practice for each lesson
- Lesson Quiz Bank for Lessons 1-7
- Unit Assessment
- Unit Pretest
- Unit-level audit, fix, re-audit, and lock

## Course Audit

The final course audit is completed only after all six units are built, audited, fixed, re-audited, and locked.

Required final course audit file:

`MCR_MASTER_COURSE_CERTIFICATION_AUDIT.md`

| Course Audit | Audit File | Status |
|---|---|---|
| MLA Master Course Certification Audit | MCR_MASTER_COURSE_CERTIFICATION_AUDIT.md | NOT READY - all six units must be complete first |

## Compliance Rule

No unit may be marked locked until its unit audit is complete.

No course may be marked certified until all six unit audits are complete and the MLA Master Course Certification Audit is complete.
