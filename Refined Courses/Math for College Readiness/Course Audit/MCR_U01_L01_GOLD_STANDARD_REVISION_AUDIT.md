## Instructional Quality Findings

- Lesson now starts from foundational reasoning and does not assume prior knowledge.
- Written explanation is more complete for online learners who may not have live teacher explanation.
- Examples use step-by-step reasoning, units, interpretation, and reasonableness checks.
- Common mistakes identify the misconception and show corrected thinking.
- Independent work is clear enough for TOR review and intervention decisions.
- Checkpoint aligns to MLA.MCR.MC.1, MLA.MCR.MC.2, and MLA.MCR.MC.3.

## Visual and Support Findings

- ALG1-style visual shell preserved.
- Color-block organization improved for learner readability.
- Red incorrect and green correct formatting included where required.
- Every page includes a Teacher of Record support reminder.
- Submission Workflow appears only once on P07.
