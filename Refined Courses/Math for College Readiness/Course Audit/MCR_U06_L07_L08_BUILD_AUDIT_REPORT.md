## Unit Focus

Unit 6 focuses on measurement, modeling, and applied geometry. Students use scale, coordinate geometry, area, surface area, volume, density, right-triangle relationships, constraints, and reasonableness checks to solve applied problems.

## Lesson Objectives

Lesson 7: Multi-Step Measurement and Modeling

Students solve multi-step measurement problems involving constraints, design choices, unit labels, formula selection, and reasonableness.

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

Students synthesize measurement, scale, coordinate geometry, area, surface area, volume, density, and right-triangle modeling across Unit 6.
