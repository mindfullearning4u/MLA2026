## Course Architecture Purpose

Earth/Space Science is organized as a laboratory science course centered on Earth systems, geologic processes, oceans, atmosphere, weather, climate, Earth history, astronomy, the solar system, stars, galaxies, space exploration, Earth-space relationships, models, and data analysis.

## Major Architecture Controls

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Standards fidelity | Every CPALMS-listed Earth/Space Science standard is represented in the MLA inventory, crosswalk, unit map, or support alignment. |
| Unit coherence | Each unit has a distinct Earth/Space Science progression and avoids Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Environmental Science, Marine Science, or Anatomy and Physiology identity drift. |
| Lesson coherence | Each unit has eight mapped lessons, and every primary unit standard is taught in Lessons 1-7 before Lesson 8 synthesis. |
| Lab science | Lab safety, measurement, data displays, maps, models, field-data reasoning, uncertainty, interpretation, and communication are embedded across the architecture. |
| Virtual lab/data | Virtual investigation, simulation, map-based investigation, satellite/weather/climate/geologic/astronomical data, graphical interpretation, and model analysis are explicitly represented. |
| Readiness | Common Core, ACT Science, SAT science reading/data, college readiness, workforce readiness, and scientific literacy are present. |

## Unit Architecture

| Unit | Title | Primary Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Scientific Thinking and Earth Systems Foundations | Establish inquiry, lab/data practices, Earth systems, models, and Earth-sphere interactions. |
| 2 | Geology, Plate Tectonics, and Earth's Structure | Develop Earth layers, surface processes, plate tectonics, geologic features, Florida geology, and ocean-basin development. |
| 3 | Weather, Climate, Oceans, and Atmosphere | Build cycles, ocean motion, atmosphere/hydrosphere relationships, weather prediction, severe weather, climate, and Florida influences. |
| 4 | Earth History and Change Over Time | Build fossil evidence, Earth history, origin-of-life explanations at course level, theory evidence, and change over time. |
| 5 | Astronomy, Solar System, and Stars | Build Big Bang evidence, universe matter distribution, astronomical distances, star evolution, the Sun, planetary systems, and Earth-Moon-Sun relationships. |
| 6 | Galaxies, Space Exploration, and Earth-Space Systems | Build space exploration, cosmic forces, electromagnetic evidence, radiation, waves, nuclear processes, heat transfer, motion, gravity, and applied Earth-space systems. |

## Architecture Decision

Earth/Space Science uses 29 MLA Earth/Space Science standards across seven domains. This count gives enough granularity for 48 lessons while keeping each standard teachable, mappable, and assessable at architecture level.

No lesson production begins in this phase.
