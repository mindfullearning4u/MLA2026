## Root Cause Analysis

Earth/Space Science includes benchmarks from Earth/space, life science, nature of science, and physical science. This creates a risk of identity drift if supporting Biology or Physics standards are treated as separate course units. The architecture prevents that by organizing the course around Earth systems, geology, atmosphere, climate, Earth history, astronomy, and space systems while using support science only where CPALMS requires it.

## Corrections Applied

No post-build corrections were required. The architecture was built using the corrected Science Department model established by Biology, Chemistry, and Physics:

- official-source verification before count reporting
- full MLA crosswalk format
- complete Course Overview format
- explicit primary/support classification
- lesson-level mapping with purpose, MLA assessment evidence, and rationale
- Lesson 8 validation
- dedicated consistency audit
