## 4. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Identity drift into Biology, Chemistry, or Physics | Supporting standards are framed only through CPALMS-required Earth/Space Science contexts. |
| Lesson production could over-expand source guidance into content | Matrices are explicitly guidance-only and do not create lesson or assessment content. |
| Weather/climate topics could drift into Environmental Science | Unit 3 remains focused on atmosphere, ocean, weather, climate systems, data, and Florida influences required by CPALMS. |
| Ocean topics could drift into Marine Science | Ocean content remains tied to Earth systems, ocean motion, energy transfer, and ocean-basin development required by CPALMS. |
