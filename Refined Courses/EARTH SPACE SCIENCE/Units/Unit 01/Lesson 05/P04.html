P04 Worked Examples: Earth as an Interacting System

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.ATM.03

Lesson boundary: Introduce Earth systems and interactions. Do not teach all weather, climate, geology, or ecology details yet.

Required visual/data/model: Earth-spheres interaction diagram and cause-effect systems table

Worked Example 1

Question: Which example best shows hydrosphere-atmosphere interaction?

Correct answer: Ocean water evaporates and adds water vapor to the air.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is listing Earth spheres without explaining interaction. The correction is to use an action verb such as evaporates, erodes, melts, releases, absorbs, or exchanges.

Correction

Slow down and tie every answer to the evidence, representation rules, and lesson boundary.
