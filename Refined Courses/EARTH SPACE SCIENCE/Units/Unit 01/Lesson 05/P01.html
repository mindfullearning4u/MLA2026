P01 Lesson Overview: Earth as an Interacting System

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.ATM.03

Lesson boundary: Introduce Earth systems and interactions. Do not teach all weather, climate, geology, or ecology details yet.

Required visual/data/model: Earth-spheres interaction diagram and cause-effect systems table

What you will learn: Introduce Earth systems and interactions. Do not teach all weather, climate, geology, or ecology details yet.

What you will do: Read the teacher-style explanation, use the required visual or data model, complete guided practice, complete independent work, and submit checkpoint evidence.

How you will show mastery: You will explain the lesson concept using accurate evidence, correct vocabulary, and reasoning that stays inside the mapped standard.

Student-friendly standard connection: This lesson teaches how Earth/Space Science claims are built from safe, reliable evidence instead of unsupported guesses.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, map/model interpretation, image evidence, public-source reasoning, or virtual resource review. Do not perform outdoor fieldwork, collect samples, use tools, observe hazardous weather, or complete any physical investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives explicit safety clearance.

Use the exact lesson resource links and embedded data displays so your evidence matches the lesson standard.
