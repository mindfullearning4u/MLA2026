Notebook Title

Unit 1 Lesson 03: Maps, Graphs, Models, and Scale

Vocabulary

TermStudent-friendly meaning
Map legendThe key that explains symbols or colors on a map.
ScaleThe relationship between a model or map distance and real distance.
AxisA labeled line on a graph showing the variable and unit.
Model limitationSomething a model leaves out or simplifies.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Earth/Space Science depends on representations because real Earth and space systems are often too large to view all at once. A map, graph, or model is useful only when you know how to read its labels, scale, symbols, and limits.
  2. Scale prevents a major misunderstanding. A one-inch line on a map is not one inch on Earth. It may represent one mile, ten miles, or hundreds of kilometers depending on the map scale.
  3. Graphs also require careful reading. The x-axis and y-axis tell what variables are being compared. The units tell what the numbers mean. A pattern is not valid unless it comes from the plotted evidence.
