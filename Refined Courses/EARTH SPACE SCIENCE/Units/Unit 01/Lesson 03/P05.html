P05 Guided Practice

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.LAB.02

Lesson boundary: Focus on interpreting representations. Do not teach full topographic surveying or advanced GIS beyond the mapped standard.

Required visual/data/model: map legend, scale bar, graph axes, and simplified model comparison

Use the guided practice to apply this lesson only. Each item should be answered from the visual, model, data, source, or reasoning process taught in this lesson.

Direct Resource Task

Resource: USGS Topographic Maps

  1. Open the exact linked resource in a new tab.
  2. Use only the section named in this lesson task; do not move to unrelated pages.
  3. Observe: the way map symbols, scale, and elevation evidence represent real locations
  4. Record: what a legend explains, what a scale bar does, what contour or elevation information means, and one model limitation
  5. Return to this lesson and use your recorded evidence in the notebook response, guided practice, and checkpoint.
