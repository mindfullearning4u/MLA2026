Notebook Title

Unit 1 Lesson 07: Earth Systems Investigation Design

Vocabulary

TermStudent-friendly meaning
Investigation questionA testable question that can be answered with evidence.
Independent variableThe factor being compared or changed.
Dependent variableThe measured result or response.
Evidence limitationA factor that limits what the data can prove.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. A strong Earth-system investigation begins with a question that can actually be answered with data. 'Is Earth interesting?' is not testable. 'How did monthly precipitation compare between two regions during the same year?' can be answered with data.
  2. Variables make the investigation clear. The independent variable might be location, month, or year. The dependent variable might be precipitation, temperature, wind speed, or another measured result.
  3. Good investigation design also names limits. If the data come from one station, one month, or one source, the claim must stay inside that evidence boundary.
