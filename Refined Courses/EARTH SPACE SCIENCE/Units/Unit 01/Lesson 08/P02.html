Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Lesson 8 does not introduce a new standard. It asks you to combine the Unit 1 skills: reliable evidence, safety, measurement, maps, graphs, models, scale, uncertainty, observation, inference, investigation design, and Earth-system interaction.
  2. A strong synthesis answer moves in order. First identify the evidence source. Then check safety and procedure. Next read the representation. Then separate observation from inference. Finally write a claim that stays within the evidence.
  3. If a question gives a map, graph, source excerpt, or system model, use it directly. Do not answer from memory when evidence is provided.
