Earth Space Science Unit 5 Lesson 7: Earth, Moon, and Sun Relationships

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Earth, Moon, and Sun Relationships
Lesson Purpose: Apply physical principles to Earth-Moon-Sun relationships.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ESS.AST.06

Support Standards: MLA.ESS.COS.03

What You Will Learn

  • Read Earth/space evidence connected to Earth, Moon, and Sun Relationships.
  • Use the required visual, data, map, model, or source to support a science claim.
  • Explain reasoning with accurate Earth/Space Science vocabulary.

What You Will Do

  1. Read the lesson pages in order.
  2. Use the direct resource and the embedded model or table to collect evidence.
  3. Complete notebook evidence before the checkpoint.
  4. Use TOR support when you need clarification after using the lesson supports.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by using evidence from NASA; PhET gravity/orbits if appropriate; OpenStax, explaining your reasoning step by step, and staying inside the lesson standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you think like an Earth/Space Science student: observe evidence, record data, compare the model to the claim, and explain what the evidence supports.

Safety boundary: Earth, Moon, and Sun Relationships. This lesson is completed with safe digital, map, model, image, source, or data evidence. Do not perform fieldwork, observe hazardous weather, view the Sun directly, handle unknown materials, or use special equipment unless the Teacher of Record assigns a separate approved safety-controlled activity.
