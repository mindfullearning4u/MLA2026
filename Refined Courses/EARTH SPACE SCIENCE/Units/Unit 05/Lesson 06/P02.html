Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Identify the science object. In this lesson, the science object is Planetary System Formation. Name the system, process, evidence source, or model before you try to explain it.
  2. Step 2: Read the visual or data display. Use Planetary system formation model analysis. Start with labels, units, arrows, map legend, scale, or source title.
  3. Step 3: Separate observation from inference. An observation is what the evidence directly shows. An inference is the explanation you build from the observation.
  4. Step 4: Connect evidence to the standard. Use MLA.ESS.AST.06 as the boundary. Do not add facts outside the mapped lesson.
  5. Step 5: Write the reasoning. Explain why the evidence supports the claim and name any limitation or uncertainty.
