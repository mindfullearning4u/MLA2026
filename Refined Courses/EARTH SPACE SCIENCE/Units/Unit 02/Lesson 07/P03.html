P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Standards Covered: MLA.ESS.EAR.02; MLA.ESS.EAR.04

Lesson boundary: Synthesize Unit 2 geologic evidence without introducing new astronomy or weather standards.

Required visual/data/model: geologic map, cross section, and process-feature model

Reasoning Sequence

  1. Name the evidence source.
  2. Describe what the visual, map, graph, model, table, or source shows.
  3. Explain the process connection: A model is stronger when it connects multiple evidence types to one process explanation.
  4. State a claim that fits the evidence.
  5. State one limitation or boundary.

Common Mistake

Correct Thinking

Use the evidence first, connect it to the process, and keep the claim inside the lesson standard.
