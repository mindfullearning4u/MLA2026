Earth Space Science Unit 6 Lesson 7: Earth-Space Systems and Scientific Decisions

P01 Lesson Overview
Lesson Title: Earth-Space Systems and Scientific Decisions
Lesson Purpose: Evaluate applied Earth-space systems and science-informed decisions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ESS.COS.01; MLA.ESS.COS.03

Support Standards: MLA.ESS.SCI.03

What You Will Learn

  • Read Earth/space evidence connected to Earth-Space Systems and Scientific Decisions.
  • Use the required visual, data, map, model, or source to support a science claim.
  • Explain reasoning with accurate Earth/Space Science vocabulary.

What You Will Do

  1. Read the lesson pages in order.
  2. Use the direct resource and the embedded model or table to collect evidence.
  3. Complete notebook evidence before the checkpoint.
  4. Use TOR support when you need clarification after using the lesson supports.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by using evidence from NASA; NOAA; USGS; Space Florida, explaining your reasoning step by step, and staying inside the lesson standard.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you think like an Earth/Space Science student: observe evidence, record data, compare the model to the claim, and explain what the evidence supports.

Safety boundary: Earth-Space Systems and Scientific Decisions. This lesson is completed with safe digital, map, model, image, source, or data evidence. Do not perform fieldwork, observe hazardous weather, view the Sun directly, handle unknown materials, or use special equipment unless the Teacher of Record assigns a separate approved safety-controlled activity.

Required Direct Resource

Open this exact resource: NASA Applied Sciences Disasters Program

Direct URL: https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/what-we-do/disasters

Click the link above. After the page opens, follow these steps exactly:

  1. Click the link and use the applied sciences page. Observe how Earth-space data support decisions. Record the decision area, the data source, and the evidence used.
  2. Observe the model, map, graph, data table, image, or source section named in the directions.
  3. Record the required evidence in your notebook before answering questions.
  4. Use the recorded evidence to write a claim-evidence-reasoning explanation connected to the lesson standard.

Evidence check: Your notes must include what you observed, what data or source detail you recorded, and how that evidence supports the lesson idea.
