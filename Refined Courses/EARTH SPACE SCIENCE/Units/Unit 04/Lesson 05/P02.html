P02 Notebook Task Part 1: Models of Change Over Time

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.HIS.03

Lesson boundary: Focus on modeling change over time.

Required visual/data/model: timeline model, change-over-time sequence, and limitation table

Vocabulary

TermMeaning
EvidenceA map, model, graph, table, image, source excerpt, observation, or data point that supports a claim.
ProcessThe Earth or space mechanism that explains why the evidence looks the way it does.
Model limitWhat a representation simplifies or cannot prove by itself.
Claim boundaryThe limit of what the evidence can validly support.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Start with the mapped standard: MLA.ESS.HIS.03.
  2. Read the required visual/data/model before making a claim: timeline model, change-over-time sequence, and limitation table.
  3. Learn the core idea: Models represent long-term Earth changes so students can reason about processes too slow or large to observe directly.
  4. Connect the process: A model should show sequence, scale, evidence, and limitation.
  5. State the boundary: Focus on modeling change over time.
