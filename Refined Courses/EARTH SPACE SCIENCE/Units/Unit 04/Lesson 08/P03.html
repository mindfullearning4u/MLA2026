P03 Notebook Task Part 2: Evidence and Reasoning

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.HIS.01; MLA.ESS.HIS.02; MLA.ESS.HIS.03

Lesson boundary: Use only Unit 4 mapped content.

Required visual/data/model: mixed fossil, timeline, theory, model, and source reliability packet

Reasoning Sequence

  1. Name the evidence source.
  2. Describe what the visual, map, graph, model, table, or source shows.
  3. Explain the process connection: A strong synthesis states evidence type, time scale, model role, and claim boundary.
  4. State a claim that fits the evidence.
  5. State one limitation or boundary.

Common Mistake

Treating all past-life or Earth-history claims as equally supported.

Correction

Use the evidence first, connect it to the process, and keep the claim inside the lesson standard.
