P01 Lesson Overview: Scientific Theories in Earth History

MLA Standard: MLA.ESS.HIS.03

Lesson boundary: Focus on theories and models as evidence-based explanations.

Required visual/data/model: theory-model-evidence chart

What you will learn: Scientific theories explain Earth history through evidence-supported models and repeated testing.

What you will do: Use the required visual/model/data, learn the process step by step, complete practice, and submit checkpoint evidence.

How you will show mastery: Explain the mapped concept using evidence, process reasoning, and a clear claim boundary.
