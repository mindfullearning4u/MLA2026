# ALG2 Units 3-4 Instructional, Assessment, and Notation Audit

## Executive Summary
ALG2 Units 3 and 4 were reviewed against the updated MLA instructional page standard, ALG2 mapping, Moodle-safe GIFT requirements, and mathematical notation expectations.

Final Recommendation: PASS WITH REVISIONS

## Strengths
- Unit 3 follows the approved ALG2 mapping for Radical and Rational Relationships.
- Unit 4 follows the approved ALG2 mapping for Exponential and Logarithmic Functions.
- All lessons contain P01-P07, lesson.json, quiz.json, Guided Practice, and the required lesson quiz or Unit Assessment structure.
- Lesson 8 in each unit remains Putting It All Together and includes Guided Practice plus the Unit Assessment instead of a lesson quiz.

## Findings
- Units 3 and 4 needed the updated TOR support reminder across lesson pages.
- P04 pages needed the required three worked examples and red/green common mistake structure.
- P06 pages needed the required Instructions, Part A, Part B, and Part C structure.
- P07 pages needed the updated checkpoint structure with a single Submission Workflow section.

## Required Corrections
Completed during audit:
- Added the required Need Help? TOR support reminder to lesson pages where missing.
- Rebuilt P04 pages for Units 3-4 Lessons 1-8 with exactly three worked examples and red/green common mistake support.
- Rebuilt P06 pages for Units 3-4 Lessons 1-8 with Independent Work instructions and Parts A-C.
- Rebuilt P07 pages for Units 3-4 Lessons 1-8 with TOR information, one Submission Workflow section, checkpoint task, mastery criteria, and support reminder.
- Converted Unit 3 radical-expression GIFT notation and Unit 4 logarithm-related symbol notation to Moodle-safe plain syntax where needed.

## Recommended Improvements
None required before production use for Units 3-4.
