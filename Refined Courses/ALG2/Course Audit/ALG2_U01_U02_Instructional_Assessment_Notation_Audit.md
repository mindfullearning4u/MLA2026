# ALG2 Units 1-2 Instructional, Assessment, and Notation Audit

## Executive Summary
ALG2 Units 1 and 2 were reviewed against the updated MLA instructional page standard, ALG2 mapping, Moodle-safe GIFT requirements, and mathematical notation expectations.

Final Recommendation: PASS WITH REVISIONS

## Strengths
- Unit 1 follows the approved ALG2 mapping for Advanced Expressions and Equations.
- Unit 2 follows the approved ALG2 mapping for Polynomial Functions.
- All lessons contain P01-P07, lesson.json, quiz.json, Guided Practice, and the required lesson quiz or Unit Assessment structure.
- Lesson 8 in each unit remains Putting It All Together and includes Guided Practice plus the Unit Assessment instead of a lesson quiz.

## Findings
- Several Unit 1 and Unit 2 lesson pages were missing the required TOR support reminder.
- Several P04 pages did not include exactly three worked examples.
- Several P06 pages did not consistently use Instructions, Part A, Part B, and Part C.
- Several P07 pages needed the updated checkpoint structure with a single Submission Workflow section.

## Required Corrections
Completed during audit:
- Added the required Need Help? TOR support reminder to lesson pages where missing.
- Rebuilt P04 pages for Units 1-2 Lessons 1-8 with exactly three worked examples and red/green common mistake support.
- Rebuilt P06 pages for Units 1-2 Lessons 1-8 with Independent Work instructions and Parts A-C.
- Rebuilt P07 pages for Units 1-2 Lessons 1-8 with TOR information, one Submission Workflow section, checkpoint task, mastery criteria, and support reminder.
- Converted Unit 1 complex-number GIFT notation from lesson-page square-root symbols to Moodle-safe `sqrt(...)` notation where needed.

## Recommended Improvements
None required before production use for Units 1-2.
