P04 Concept Instruction / Worked Examples

Concept Instruction

Financial growth models use the same exponential structure as growth and decay models. Savings and investment contexts often use growth factors, while depreciation and some debt contexts use decay or repeated balance changes. Always identify the starting value, rate, time unit, and meaning of the output.

Worked Examples

Example 1: 1500 dollars invested at 6% annual interest is A(t) = 1500(1.06)t.

Example 2: A 22000 dollar car depreciating 15% per year is V(t) = 22000(0.85)t.

Example 3: If A(t) = 800(1.04)t, then 800 is the starting balance and 1.04 means 4% annual growth.

Correct Reasoning Check

Correct: Comparing model outputs at the same time value supports a fair financial decision.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Comparing one account after 5 years to another after 10 years is not a fair same-time comparison.
