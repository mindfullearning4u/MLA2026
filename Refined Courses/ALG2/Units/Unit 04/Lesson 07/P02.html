Key Vocabulary

  • Model: a function that represents a real situation.
  • Doubling time: time needed for a quantity to double.
  • Half-life: time needed for a quantity to decrease to half.
  • Parameter: a value in a model with contextual meaning.

Correct Example

Correct: If P(t)=500(1.08)t, then 500 is the starting amount and 1.08 means 8% growth per time period.

Incorrect Example

Incorrect: Saying 1.08 means 108% growth is incorrect; it means the new amount is 108% of the previous amount, or 8% growth.
