P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Task

  1. Create a two-column chart for growth and decay factors.
  2. Convert 8% growth, 25% growth, 10% decay, and 35% decay into factors.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why decay factors are between 0 and 1.

Correct Reasoning

Correct: A 20% decrease has decay factor 0.80 because the new amount keeps 80% of the previous amount.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Writing a 20% decrease as a factor of 1.20 models growth, not decay.
