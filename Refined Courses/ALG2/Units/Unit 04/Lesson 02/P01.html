P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Growth and Decay Models

Analyze exponential growth and decay using rates, factors, and contextual parameters.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.EX.2; MLA.A2.EX.3

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.5.5; MA.912.AR.5.7

Analyze exponential growth and decay using rates, factors, and contextual parameters.

What You Will Learn

  • Convert percent change into growth and decay factors.
  • Write and interpret exponential growth and decay models.
  • Identify initial amount, rate, factor, and time interval in context.
  • Use models to make and interpret predictions.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on growth factors and decay factors.
  • Study worked examples involving percent increase and percent decrease.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Build models using y = a(1 + r)t and y = a(1 - r)t.
  • Interpret growth and decay factors in context.
  • Use exponential models to evaluate and explain predictions.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.EX.2; MLA.A2.EX.3: Analyze exponential growth and decay using rates, factors, and contextual parameters.

In student language: I can build and interpret growth and decay models from percentages and contexts.
