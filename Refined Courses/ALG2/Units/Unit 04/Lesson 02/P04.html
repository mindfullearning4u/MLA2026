P04 Concept Instruction / Worked Examples

Concept Instruction

Growth and decay models are exponential because the amount is multiplied by the same factor over equal time intervals. For growth, use y = a(1 + r)t. For decay, use y = a(1 - r)t, where r is written as a decimal.

Worked Examples

Example 1: A population begins at 500 and grows 6% per year. The model is P(t) = 500(1.06)t.

Example 2: A car worth 24000 decreases by 15% each year. The model is V(t) = 24000(0.85)t.

Example 3: In A(t) = 1200(1.03)t, 1200 is the initial amount and 1.03 means 3% growth per time period.

Correct Reasoning Check

Correct: The factor 0.92 means the amount keeps 92% each period, so the rate of decay is 8%.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Treating 0.92 as 92% decay is incorrect; it means 92% remains.
