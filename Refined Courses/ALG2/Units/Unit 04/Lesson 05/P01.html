P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Logarithmic Properties

Use logarithmic properties and base relationships to rewrite expressions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.LOG.4

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.NSO.1.6

Use logarithmic properties and base relationships to rewrite expressions.

What You Will Learn

  • Use product, quotient, and power properties of logarithms.
  • Expand logarithmic expressions into simpler parts.
  • Condense multiple logarithms into one logarithm.
  • Use change-of-base reasoning when a calculator or expression uses a different base.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on logarithmic properties and base relationships.
  • Study worked examples that expand and condense logarithmic expressions.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Rewrite logarithmic expressions accurately.
  • Choose the correct property for products, quotients, and powers.
  • Explain why an expanded or condensed form is equivalent.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.LOG.4: Use logarithmic properties and base relationships to rewrite expressions.

In student language: I can rewrite logarithmic expressions using properties while preserving equivalent value.
