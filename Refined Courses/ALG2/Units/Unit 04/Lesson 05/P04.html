P04 Worked Examples

Step-by-step explanation: Each worked example below models the lesson process one step at a time and explains the reasoning before the final answer.

Study each example carefully. Each one shows a different way the lesson idea appears in Algebra II work.

Worked Example 1: Product Property

Problem: Rewrite log_b(MN).

Step 1: Recognize multiplication inside the log.

Step 2: Use the product property.

Step 3: Split into a sum of logs.

log_b(MN)=log_b(M)+log_b(N)

Reasoning: Multiplication inside a log becomes addition outside.

Worked Example 2: Quotient Property

Problem: Rewrite log_b(M/N).

Step 1: Recognize division inside the log.

Step 2: Use the quotient property.

Step 3: Split into a difference of logs.

log_b(M/N)=log_b(M)-log_b(N)

Reasoning: Division inside a log becomes subtraction outside.

Worked Example 3: Power Property

Problem: Rewrite log_b(M^3).

Step 1: Recognize the exponent on M.

Step 2: Move the exponent to the front.

Step 3: Keep the same logarithm base.

log_b(M^3)=3log_b(M)

Reasoning: The power property turns exponents into coefficients.

Common Mistake

Problem: Rewrite log_b(M+N).

Incorrect:

log_b(M)+log_b(N)

Why it is wrong: The product property works for multiplication, not addition.

Correct:

No basic property separates log_b(M+N).

Fix: Check the operation inside the logarithm before applying a property.
