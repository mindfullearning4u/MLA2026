Pretest Title

Algebra II Unit 4 Pretest: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions

This pretest measures readiness, prior knowledge, and acceleration eligibility before you begin Unit 4 content.

Standards Covered

Cumulative MLA Standards: MLA.A2.EX.1; MLA.A2.EX.2; MLA.A2.EX.3; MLA.A2.LOG.1; MLA.A2.LOG.2; MLA.A2.LOG.3; MLA.A2.LOG.4; MLA.A2.FM.2; MLA.A2.FM.3; MLA.A2.FM.4

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.5.4; MA.912.AR.5.5; MA.912.AR.5.7; MA.912.AR.5.2; MA.912.AR.5.8; MA.912.AR.5.9; MA.912.NSO.1.6; MA.912.FL.3.1; MA.912.FL.3.2; MA.912.FL.3.4

The pretest samples the full Unit 4 skill set before instruction begins.

Purpose of the Pretest

  • Show what you already know about exponential and logarithmic relationships.
  • Identify readiness strengths and skills that need review.
  • Help determine whether acceleration or targeted support is appropriate.
  • Prepare you to begin Unit 4 with clear feedback.

Student Directions

  • Complete the pretest independently.
  • Use scratch paper or your notebook to show work when needed.
  • Choose the best answer for each multiple-choice question.
  • Read feedback carefully after submission.

Mastery and Readiness

The pretest is used to measure readiness before instruction. It is not a replacement for completing the Unit 4 learning sequence unless acceleration is approved through the course process.

Strong pretest performance may show readiness for accelerated pacing. Lower performance identifies skills to focus on during Unit 4.
