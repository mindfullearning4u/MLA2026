Common Mistake

Problem: Interpret 1.09 in a growth model.

Incorrect:

109% growth

Why it is wrong: The 1 represents the original amount; 0.09 is the growth.

Correct:

9% growth

Fix: Subtract 1 from the factor to get the growth rate.
