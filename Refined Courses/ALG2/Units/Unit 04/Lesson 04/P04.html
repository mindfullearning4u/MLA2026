P04 Worked Examples

Step-by-step explanation: Each worked example below models the lesson process one step at a time and explains the reasoning before the final answer.

Study each example carefully. Each one shows a different way the lesson idea appears in Algebra II work.

Worked Example 1: Connect Logs and Exponents

Problem: Rewrite log_2(8)=3 in exponential form.

Step 1: Identify base 2.

Step 2: The logarithm equals the exponent 3.

Step 3: Write base^exponent = result.

2^3 = 8

Reasoning: A logarithm answers an exponent question.

Worked Example 2: Evaluate a Logarithm

Problem: Find log_3(27).

Step 1: Ask 3 to what power equals 27.

Step 2: Use 3^3 = 27.

Step 3: State the log value.

log_3(27)=3

Reasoning: Logarithms undo exponentials.

Worked Example 3: Inverse Relationship

Problem: Why are y=2^x and y=log_2(x) inverses?

Step 1: Exponential form maps exponent to result.

Step 2: Logarithmic form maps result back to exponent.

Step 3: The inputs and outputs reverse.

They reverse each other.

Reasoning: Inverse functions undo each other.

Common Mistake

Problem: Evaluate log_2(16).

Incorrect:

8

Why it is wrong: The answer is an exponent, not half of 16.

Correct:

4

Fix: Ask what power of 2 equals 16.
