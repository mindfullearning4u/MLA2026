Worked Example 1: Build an Exponential Rule

Problem: A pattern starts at 3 and doubles each step.

Step 1: Identify the initial value 3.

Step 2: Identify the growth factor 2.

Step 3: Write a rule using x as the step number.

f(x)=3(2x)

Reasoning: Exponential functions multiply by a constant factor.

Visual support: Equal input steps multiply outputs by the same factor, which creates a curved growth graph.

Worked Example 2: Evaluate an Exponential Function

Problem: Find f(4) for f(x)=5(2x).

Step 1: Substitute 4 for x.

Step 2: Compute 24.

Step 3: Multiply by 5.

f(4)=80

Reasoning: The exponent controls repeated multiplication.

Worked Example 3: Interpret the Factor

Problem: In A=100(1.08)t, what does 1.08 mean?

Step 1: Compare the factor to 1.

Step 2: A factor above 1 means growth.

Step 3: Convert 0.08 to 8%.

Growth rate is 8%.

Reasoning: The growth factor contains the percent change.

Common Mistake

Problem: Interpret y=50(1.04)t.

Incorrect:

The amount increases by 104% each time.

Why it is wrong: The 1 is the original amount remaining; 0.04 is the increase.

Correct:

The amount increases by 4% each time.

Fix: Subtract 1 from the growth factor to find the rate.
