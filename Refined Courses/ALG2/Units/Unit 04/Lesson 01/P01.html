P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Exponential Functions

Build and interpret exponential functions from patterns, tables, graphs, and contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.EX.1

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.5.4

Build and interpret exponential functions from patterns, tables, graphs, and contexts.

What You Will Learn

  • Recognize exponential patterns from tables and contexts.
  • Write functions in the form f(x) = a(b)x.
  • Interpret the initial value and base of an exponential function.
  • Connect exponential rules to graph behavior.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on exponential vocabulary and structure.
  • Study worked examples from tables, equations, graphs, and contexts.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Write exponential functions from patterns.
  • Identify initial value, base, and multiplicative change.
  • Explain what an exponential model means in context.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.EX.1: Build and interpret exponential functions from patterns, tables, graphs, and contexts.

In student language: I can identify exponential change and write a function that represents it.
