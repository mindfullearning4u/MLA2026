P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each one shows a different way the lesson idea appears in Algebra II work.

Worked Example 1: Solve Logarithmic Form

Problem: Solve log_2(x)=5.

Step 1: Rewrite in exponential form.

Step 2: Use 2^5 = x.

Step 3: State the solution.

x = 32

Reasoning: Log equations can often be solved by rewriting as exponentials.

Worked Example 2: Solve Exponential Form

Problem: Solve 3^x = 81.

Step 1: Recognize 81 as a power of 3.

Step 2: Write 81 = 3^4.

Step 3: Match exponents.

x = 4

Reasoning: Common bases make exponential equations easier.

Worked Example 3: Check Domain

Problem: Solve log_5(x - 1)=2.

Step 1: Rewrite as x - 1 = 5^2.

Step 2: Solve x - 1 = 25.

Step 3: Check x - 1 is positive.

x = 26

Reasoning: Logarithm inputs must be positive.

Common Mistake

Problem: Solve log_3(x - 4)=2.

Incorrect:

x = 6

Why it is wrong: 3^2 is 9, so x - 4 must equal 9, not 2.

Correct:

x = 13

Fix: Rewrite correctly using base^exponent = argument.
