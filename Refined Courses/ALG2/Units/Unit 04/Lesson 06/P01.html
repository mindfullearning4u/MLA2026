P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Solving Logarithmic Equations

Solve logarithmic and exponential equations using inverse relationships.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.LOG.1

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.5.2

Solve logarithmic and exponential equations using inverse relationships.

What You Will Learn

  • Solve logarithmic equations by rewriting in exponential form.
  • Solve exponential equations by using logarithms or matching bases.
  • Check solutions against logarithmic domain restrictions.
  • Interpret equation solutions in context when appropriate.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on inverse solving strategies.
  • Study worked examples with logarithmic and exponential equations.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use inverse relationships to isolate variables.
  • Check logarithmic inputs for validity.
  • Explain whether a candidate solution is valid or extraneous.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.LOG.1: Solve logarithmic and exponential equations using inverse relationships.

In student language: I can solve equations involving exponents and logarithms and check that the answer is valid.
