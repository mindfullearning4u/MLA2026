Notebook Setup

Title your notes: Lesson 7: Advanced Function Analysis.

Write the standard: MLA.A2.FN.3; MLA.A2.FN.11; MLA.A2.FN.12.

Vocabulary

  • Key feature: an important graph or function characteristic such as an intercept, maximum, minimum, domain, range, or interval behavior.
  • Intersection: an input-output pair where two functions have the same value.
  • Viable solution: a solution that makes sense in the mathematical context and the real-world situation.
  • Average rate of change: the change in output divided by the change in input over an interval.

Notebook Task

  1. Create a two-column chart labeled Feature and How to find it.
  2. Add rows for intercept, increasing/decreasing interval, average rate of change, and intersection.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why different representations may be useful for different questions.
