P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Representation Comparison

Functions may be given as rules, tables, graphs, or descriptions. Before solving, identify what information is easiest to read from the representation.

Notebook Task

  1. Write the equation f(x)=x2+1 and g(x)=2x+4.
  2. Set up the equation f(x)=g(x).
  3. Solve x2+1=2x+4.
  4. Explain what each solution means as an intersection of the two functions.

Reflection Prompt

Why is solving f(x)=g(x) connected to finding where two graphs intersect?
