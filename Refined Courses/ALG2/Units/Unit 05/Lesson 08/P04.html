Worked Example 2: Transform a Function

Problem: Describe g(x)=f(x+2)-3.

Step 1: Inside +2 shifts left 2.

Step 2: Outside -3 shifts down 3.

Step 3: State both transformations.

Left 2 and down 3.

Reasoning: Synthesis problems often combine transformations.

Common Mistake

Problem: For f(x-3)+4, say left 3 and up 4.

Incorrect:

Left 3 and up 4

Why it is wrong: x-3 shifts right, not left.

Correct:

Right 3 and up 4

Fix: Inside transformations move opposite the sign.
