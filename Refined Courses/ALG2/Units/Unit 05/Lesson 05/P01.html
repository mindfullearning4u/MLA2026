Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Function Operations and Composition

Combine functions using operations and composition and interpret results.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.FN.7; MLA.A2.FN.8

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.F.3.2; MA.912.F.3.4

Combine functions using operations and composition and interpret results.

What You Will Learn

  • Add, subtract, multiply, and divide functions.
  • Evaluate function compositions such as f(g(x)).
  • Interpret combined functions and domain restrictions.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on the lesson vocabulary and procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Combine functions accurately.
  • Evaluate and simplify compositions.
  • State restrictions when division creates excluded values.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.FN.7; MLA.A2.FN.8: Combine functions using operations and composition and interpret results.

In student language: I can use function tools accurately and explain what the result means.
