P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each step shows the reasoning students should be able to reproduce independently.

Worked Example 1: Vertical Shift

Problem: Describe g(x)=f(x)+5.

Step 1: Compare g(x) to f(x).

Step 2: The +5 is outside the function.

Step 3: Move every output up 5.

Vertical shift up 5.

Reasoning: Outside changes affect outputs.

Worked Example 2: Horizontal Shift

Problem: Describe g(x)=f(x-3).

Step 1: Look inside the function input.

Step 2: x-3 shifts the graph right.

Step 3: State the horizontal movement.

Horizontal shift right 3.

Reasoning: Inside changes affect inputs in the opposite direction.

Worked Example 3: Reflection

Problem: Describe g(x)=-f(x).

Step 1: The negative is outside the function.

Step 2: Multiply every output by -1.

Step 3: Reflect across the x-axis.

Reflection over the x-axis.

Reasoning: Output signs control vertical reflection.

Common Mistake

Problem: Describe f(x-4).

Incorrect:

Shift left 4.

Why it is wrong: Inside transformations move opposite the sign.

Correct:

Shift right 4.

Fix: For f(x-h), the graph shifts right h units.
