Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Function Transformations

Analyze transformations of functions and connect changes in rules to graphs.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.FN.4; MLA.A2.FN.5; MLA.A2.FN.6

Analyze transformations of functions and connect changes in rules to graphs.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify vertical and horizontal shifts, reflections, and stretches.
  • Connect changes in function rules to graph changes.
  • Describe transformations using correct order and notation.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on the lesson vocabulary and procedures.
  • Study worked examples and common mistakes.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify transformations from function notation.
  • Write transformed rules from descriptions.
  • Use correct horizontal shift direction.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
