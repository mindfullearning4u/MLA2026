P01 Putting It All Together Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will synthesize Unit 1 expression, equation, complex number, polynomial division, and modeling skills before completing the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Unit 1 standards synthesis; no new primary MLA standard

Cumulative MLA Standards: MLA.A2.FN.1; MLA.A2.AE.1; MLA.A2.AE.2; MLA.A2.AE.3; MLA.A2.AE.4; MLA.A2.AE.5; MLA.A2.PF.1; MLA.A2.PF.7

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.F.1.1; MA.912.AR.1.1; MA.912.AR.1.3; MA.912.AR.1.5; MA.912.AR.1.6; MA.912.AR.1.8; MA.912.AR.3.2; MA.912.AR.6.1

This lesson reviews and connects all Unit 1 skills before the Unit Assessment.

What You Will Review

  • Function notation, domain, range, and graph interpretation.
  • Polynomial operations, factoring, and equivalent forms.
  • Complex numbers, quadratic solutions, and higher-degree equations.
  • Polynomial division, remainders, factors, and modeling interpretation.

Learning Objectives

  • I can choose an appropriate Unit 1 strategy for an algebraic task.
  • I can solve equations and interpret all real or complex solutions.
  • I can verify equivalent forms using structure and operations.
  • I can explain whether a solution is reasonable in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete synthesis notes and independent review work.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that show method choice and interpretation.
  • Submit notebook evidence from the full lesson review.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment in Moodle.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Unit 1 synthesis: This lesson does not introduce a new primary MLA standard. It helps you connect and apply all Unit 1 standards.

In student language: I can use the Algebra II tools from Unit 1 together and explain why my answer makes sense.

What You Will Learn

You will review how Unit 1 skills work together: function notation, polynomial operations, factoring, complex solutions, polynomial division, higher-degree equations, and modeling interpretation.

What You Will Do

You will complete synthesis review tasks, choose strategies for mixed Unit 1 problems, explain your reasoning, submit checkpoint evidence, and prepare for the Unit Assessment.
