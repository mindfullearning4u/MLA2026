ALG2 | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show enough work that your TOR can see your process, not just your final answer.

Purpose: This work helps your TOR decide whether you are ready to continue or whether you need intervention before moving ahead.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. Factor x2 + 6x + 8.
  2. Solve x(x - 3)(x + 5) = 0.
  3. Simplify √-100.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. Divide x2 + 4x + 3 by x + 1.
  2. Build a model for $18 plus $6 per hour.
  3. Interpret the meaning of a zero remainder.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct an error involving function notation.
  2. Explain how factoring supports equation solving.
  3. Describe one Unit 1 skill you would use first in a mixed problem.

Need Help?

If you are unsure about any step, contact your TOR for help or guidance before moving forward. Support is available, and asking early helps prevent small misunderstandings from becoming bigger problems.
