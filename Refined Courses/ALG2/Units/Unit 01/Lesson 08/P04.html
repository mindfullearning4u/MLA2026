Worked Example 2: Connect Division and Factors

Problem: A polynomial divided by x - 2 has remainder 0. What does that mean?

Step 1: A remainder of 0 means the divisor divides evenly.

Step 2: A divisor that divides evenly is a factor.

Step 3: Connect the factor to a zero.

x - 2 is a factor, and x = 2 is a zero.

Reasoning: Division, factors, and zeros are connected ideas.

Common Mistake

Problem: A student solves x(x + 4) = 0 and writes x = -4 only.

Incorrect:

x = -4

Why it is wrong: The factor x was ignored.

Correct:

x = 0 or x = -4

Fix: Set every factor equal to 0 during Unit 1 synthesis problems.
