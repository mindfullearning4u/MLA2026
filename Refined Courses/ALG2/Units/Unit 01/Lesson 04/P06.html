Part A: Complex Number Practice

  1. Simplify √-49, √-18, and √-75.
  2. Find the complex conjugate of 6 - 5i.
  3. Find the discriminant for x2 + 8x + 20 = 0 and classify the solutions.
  4. Find the discriminant for 2x2 - 4x + 9 = 0 and classify the solutions.

Part B: Quadratic Solutions

  1. Solve x2 + 25 = 0.
  2. Solve x2 - 10x + 29 = 0.
  3. Solve x2 + 2x + 17 = 0.
  4. Explain why the solutions to x2 - 10x + 29 = 0 are complex conjugates.
  5. Write one sentence explaining how the discriminant helps you predict the type of solutions before solving.
