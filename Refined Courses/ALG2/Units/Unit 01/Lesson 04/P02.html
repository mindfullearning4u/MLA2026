P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title

Title your notes: Lesson 4: Complex Numbers and Quadratic Solutions.

Write the standard: MLA.A2.PF.1.

Key Vocabulary

  • Imaginary unit: The number i, where i2 = -1.
  • Complex number: A number written in the form a + bi, where a and b are real numbers.
  • Complex conjugates: Numbers of the form a + bi and a - bi.
  • Discriminant: The expression b2 - 4ac in the quadratic formula.

Correct Example

Simplify: √-36

√-36 = √36 · √-1

√-36 = 6i

Correct: The negative is represented by i.

Incorrect Example

Simplify: √-36

√-36 = -6

Incorrect: Squaring -6 gives 36, not -36. The correct simplified form is 6i.

Notebook Task

  1. Write the definitions of imaginary unit, complex number, complex conjugates, and discriminant.
  2. Simplify √-9, √-20, and √-72.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why √-25 is not equal to -5.

Checkpoint Before Moving On

Before continuing, make sure your notes clearly show that i2 = -1 and that square roots of negative numbers use i.
