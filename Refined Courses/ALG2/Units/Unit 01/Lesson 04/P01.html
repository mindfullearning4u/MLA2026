P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Complex Numbers and Quadratic Solutions

In this lesson, you will extend quadratic solving into the complex number system. Complex numbers help you describe solutions when a quadratic equation has no real x-intercepts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.PF.1

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.3.2

This lesson focuses on solving quadratic equations with real and complex solutions and connecting those solutions to the discriminant.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use i, where i2 = -1.
  • How to simplify square roots of negative numbers.
  • How to identify real and non-real solutions using the discriminant.
  • How to solve quadratic equations that have complex solutions.

Learning Objectives

  • I can write a square root of a negative number using i.
  • I can classify quadratic equations by the value of the discriminant.
  • I can solve quadratic equations with complex solutions.
  • I can explain why complex solutions occur in conjugate pairs.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes about imaginary numbers, discriminants, and quadratic solutions.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that show solving steps and solution classification.
  • Submit notebook evidence from the lesson notes and independent work.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.PF.1: This lesson prepares you to solve quadratic equations and interpret when solutions are real or complex.

In student language: I can solve quadratic equations even when the solutions are not real numbers, and I can explain what the discriminant tells me about the solutions.
