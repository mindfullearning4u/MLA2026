If the discriminant of a quadratic equation is negative, what type of solutions does the equation have?

Answer: Two complex solutions

Feedback: A negative discriminant means the square root part of the quadratic formula is imaginary.

What are the solutions of x2 + 9 = 0?

Answer: x = 3i and x = -3i

Feedback: x2 = -9, so x = ±√-9 = ±3i.

What are the solutions of x2 - 4x + 8 = 0?

Answer: x = 2 + 2i and x = 2 - 2i

Feedback: The quadratic formula gives (4 ± √-16) divided by 2, which simplifies to 2 ± 2i.
