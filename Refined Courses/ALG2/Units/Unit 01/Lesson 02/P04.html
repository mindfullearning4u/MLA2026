P04 Worked Examples

Work through each example slowly. The goal is not only to get an answer, but to understand why each step is valid and how the idea connects to the lesson standard.

Worked Example 1: Combine Like Terms

Problem: Simplify (4x2 + 3x - 8) + (2x2 - 5x + 1).

Step 1: Group x2 terms together.

Step 2: Group x terms together.

Step 3: Group constant terms together and simplify.

6x2 - 2x - 7

Reasoning: Only like terms can be combined.

Worked Example 2: Subtract Polynomials

Problem: Simplify (7x2 - 4x + 6) - (2x2 + x - 9).

Step 1: Distribute the subtraction sign to every term in the second polynomial.

Step 2: Rewrite as 7x2 - 4x + 6 - 2x2 - x + 9.

Step 3: Combine like terms.

5x2 - 5x + 15

Reasoning: Subtraction changes every sign in the polynomial being subtracted.

Worked Example 3: Multiply Polynomials

Problem: Multiply (x + 4)(x - 3).

Step 1: Multiply each term in the first factor by each term in the second factor.

Step 2: Combine the middle terms -3x and 4x.

Step 3: Write the result in standard form.

x2 + x - 12

Reasoning: Polynomial multiplication uses the distributive property twice.

Common Mistake

Problem: Simplify (3x2 + 5x) + (2x2 + 7).

Incorrect:

5x4 + 12x

Why it is wrong: The student added exponents and combined unlike terms.

Correct:

5x2 + 5x + 7

Fix: Add coefficients only for like terms. Keep unlike terms separate.
