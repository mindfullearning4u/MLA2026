P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Polynomial Operations

In this lesson, you will add, subtract, and multiply polynomial expressions so you can work fluently with the algebraic structures used throughout Algebra II.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.AE.2

MLA.A2.AE.4

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.1.3; MA.912.AR.1.6

This lesson focuses on adding, subtracting, multiplying, and applying polynomial expressions with rational number coefficients.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify terms, coefficients, constants, and like terms in polynomial expressions.
  • How to add and subtract polynomials by combining like terms.
  • How to multiply polynomials using the distributive property.
  • How polynomial operations can represent area, perimeter, and other Algebra II expressions.

Learning Objectives

  • I can add polynomial expressions by combining like terms.
  • I can subtract polynomial expressions by distributing the subtraction sign and combining like terms.
  • I can multiply monomials, binomials, and polynomials using exponent rules and distribution.
  • I can use polynomial operations to model and solve mathematical or real-world problems.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent polynomial operation practice.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that show organized polynomial addition, subtraction, multiplication, and interpretation.
  • Submit notebook evidence from the lesson notes and independent work.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.AE.2 and MLA.A2.AE.4: This lesson prepares you to operate with polynomial expressions and use those operations in mathematical and real-world situations.

In student language: I can add, subtract, and multiply polynomials accurately, explain my steps, and use polynomial expressions to represent quantities.
