How to Subtract Polynomials

Example: Subtract (5x2 - 3x + 8) - (2x2 + x - 6).

Step 1: Distribute the subtraction sign to every term in the second polynomial.

5x2 - 3x + 8 - 2x2 - x + 6

Step 2: Combine like terms.

3x2 - 4x + 14

Answer: 3x2 - 4x + 14

How to Multiply Polynomials

Example: Multiply (x + 4)(x - 6).

Step 1: Distribute each term in the first binomial to each term in the second binomial.

x(x - 6) + 4(x - 6)

Step 2: Multiply and combine like terms.

x2 - 6x + 4x - 24 = x2 - 2x - 24

Answer: x2 - 2x - 24

Subtraction Sign Reminder

The most common subtraction mistake is changing only the first term after the subtraction sign.

Problem: (4x2 + 7x - 1) - (x2 - 5x + 3)

Incorrect:

4x2 + 7x - 1 - x2 - 5x + 3

Correct:

4x2 + 7x - 1 - x2 + 5x - 3

Why: Subtracting a polynomial means subtracting every term in that polynomial.

Why This Matters Mathematically

Polynomial operations let you rewrite expressions into equivalent forms. Equivalent forms help you recognize structure, factor expressions, divide polynomials, and interpret polynomial functions later in the unit.
