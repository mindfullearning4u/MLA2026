P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Factoring Practice

  1. Factor 8x3 - 12x2.
  2. Factor x2 - 64.
  3. Factor x2 + 10x + 21.
  4. Factor x2 - 3x - 28.

Part B: Structure and Explanation

  1. Explain why x2 - 36 is a difference of squares.
  2. Verify that (x - 2)(x + 9) is equivalent to x2 + 7x - 18.
  3. A student factors x2 + 9x + 20 as (x + 2)(x + 10). Explain the mistake and correct it.
  4. Explain why x2 + 25 does not factor over the real numbers and write its complex factored form.
  5. Write one sentence explaining how factored form supports solving equations in future lessons.

Checkpoint Submission

Complete the checkpoint below and submit your responses in the Checkpoint Submission activity.

  1. Factor 4x3 - 36x completely. Show the GCF step and any pattern used.
  2. Factor x2 - 2x - 35. Explain how your factor pair works.
  3. Verify your answer to one checkpoint problem by multiplying the factors back together.
