Question 5

ALG2_U01_L03_GP_Q05

MLA Standard:
MLA.A2.AE.1; MLA.A2.AE.5

Which factored form is equivalent to x2 + 8x + 12?

Answer: (x + 2)(x + 6)

Feedback: Multiplying (x + 2)(x + 6) gives x2 + 6x + 2x + 12 = x2 + 8x + 12.
