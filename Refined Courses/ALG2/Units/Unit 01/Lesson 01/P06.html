P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show enough work that your TOR can see your process, not just your final answer.

Purpose: This work helps your TOR decide whether you are ready to continue or whether you need intervention before moving ahead.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. If f(x) = 2x + 9, find f(5).
  2. For {(-1, 4), (0, 4), (3, 7)}, identify the domain.
  3. Explain what g(6) means in words.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. For {(1, 3), (2, 5), (2, 9)}, determine whether the relation is a function.
  2. Identify the range of {(-4, 8), (0, 2), (5, 8)}.
  3. A graph crosses the x-axis at x = 1 and x = 7. Identify the zeros.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. A student says repeated outputs mean a relation is not a function. Correct the misconception.
  2. Explain why f(3) is an output statement, not multiplication.
  3. Write one sentence connecting zeros to x-intercepts.

Need Help?
