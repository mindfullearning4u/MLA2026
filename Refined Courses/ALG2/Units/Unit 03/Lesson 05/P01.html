P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions

Add and subtract rational expressions using common denominators and equivalent forms.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Support-only extension of MLA.A2.AE.6; no new primary MLA standard

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: Support-only extension of MA.912.AR.1.9; no new primary benchmark

Extend rational expression operations by adding and subtracting with common denominators and equivalent forms.

What You Will Learn

  • Find least common denominators for rational expressions.
  • Rewrite rational expressions with equivalent denominators.
  • Add and subtract rational expressions and simplify results.
  • Keep restrictions from original denominators.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on common denominators and restrictions.
  • Study worked examples that combine rational expressions.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Use common denominators correctly.
  • Combine numerators and simplify final expressions.
  • State restrictions from original denominators.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Support-only extension of MLA.A2.AE.6: Add and subtract rational expressions using equivalent forms.

In student language: I can rewrite rational expressions with common denominators, combine them, simplify, and keep track of restrictions.
