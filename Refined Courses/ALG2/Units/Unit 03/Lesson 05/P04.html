P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each one shows a different way the lesson idea appears in Algebra II work.

Worked Example 1: Use a Common Denominator

Problem: Add 1/x + 3/x.

Step 1: Denominators already match.

Step 2: Add the numerators.

Step 3: Keep the denominator.

4/x

Reasoning: Like denominators allow direct numerator addition.

Worked Example 2: Build Equivalent Fractions

Problem: Add 1/x + 1/2.

Step 1: Find common denominator 2x.

Step 2: Rewrite 1/x as 2/(2x).

Step 3: Rewrite 1/2 as x/(2x) and add.

(x + 2)/(2x)

Reasoning: Common denominators create equivalent fractions.

Worked Example 3: Subtract Carefully

Problem: Simplify 5/x - 2/x.

Step 1: Denominators match.

Step 2: Subtract numerators.

Step 3: Keep the denominator.

3/x

Reasoning: Subtraction applies to the numerator expression.

Common Mistake

Problem: Add 1/x + 1/3.

Incorrect:

2/(3x)

Why it is wrong: The numerators cannot just be added before building equivalent fractions.

Correct:

(x + 3)/(3x)

Fix: Use a common denominator and rewrite both fractions first.
