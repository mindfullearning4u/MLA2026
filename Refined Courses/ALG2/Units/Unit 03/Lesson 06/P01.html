P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Rational Equations

Solve rational equations and identify restrictions and extraneous solutions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A2.RR.4

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.8.1

Solve rational equations while checking restrictions and solution validity.

What You Will Learn

  • Identify restrictions before solving rational equations.
  • Clear denominators using the least common denominator.
  • Solve the resulting equation and check possible solutions.
  • Reject extraneous solutions that violate restrictions or fail the original equation.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on rational equation solving.
  • Study examples that clear denominators and check solutions.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Solve rational equations accurately.
  • Identify and apply restrictions.
  • Explain why a candidate solution is valid or extraneous.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A2.RR.4: Solve rational equations and identify restrictions and extraneous solutions.

In student language: I can solve equations with rational expressions and check that my answers are allowed.
