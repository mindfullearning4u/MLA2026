Zeros, Roots, and Multiplicity

Zeros, roots, solutions, and x-intercepts describe related ideas. Multiplicity tells how a repeated factor affects graph behavior at the x-axis.

Worked Example 1

For f(x) = (x + 1)(x - 4)2, the zeros are -1 and 4. The zero 4 has multiplicity 2.

Worked Example 2

An odd multiplicity usually crosses the x-axis. An even multiplicity usually touches and turns around.

Correct Reasoning Check

Correct: The worked examples identify the relevant polynomial feature first, then connect that feature to the equation, graph, or context.

Common Mistake

Do not list the factor as the zero. The zero is the x-value that makes the factor equal 0.
