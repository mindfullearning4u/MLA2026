# ALG2 – Unit 02 Overview: Quadratic, Absolute Value, and Nonlinear Systems

---

## Unit Overview

This unit extends quadratic and absolute value reasoning into Algebra 2 contexts. Students solve quadratic equations over the real and complex number systems, analyze quadratic and absolute value inequalities, represent two-variable quadratic inequalities, and solve systems that combine linear and nonlinear relationships.

Emphasis is placed on viable solution sets, graphical and algebraic interpretation, constraints, and connections among equations, inequalities, systems, and contextual models.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. Standards with an emphasis on mathematical reasoning, structure, and application. Students are expected to demonstrate both procedural fluency and conceptual understanding consistent with state expectations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- symbolic reasoning and structure;
- function analysis and modeling;
- contextual interpretation of constraints and viable solutions.

All assessment items are aligned to the unit benchmarks and require students to solve, graph, interpret, and model quadratic, absolute value, and nonlinear system relationships in mathematical and real-world contexts.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.
