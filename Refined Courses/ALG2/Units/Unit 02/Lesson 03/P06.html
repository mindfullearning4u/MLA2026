ALG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show enough work that your TOR can see your process, not just your final answer.

Purpose: This work helps your TOR decide whether you are ready to continue or whether you need intervention before moving ahead.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. Describe end behavior of x4 + 3x.
  2. Describe end behavior of -2x4 + 1.
  3. Identify degree and leading coefficient of 5x3 - 2.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. Describe end behavior of 3x5 - x.
  2. Describe end behavior of -x3 + 8.
  3. Explain why the leading term matters most.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct an end-behavior description that ignores the leading coefficient.
  2. Explain the difference between even and odd degree.
  3. Write a rule for positive leading coefficients.
