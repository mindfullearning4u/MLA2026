Notebook Setup

Title your notes: Lesson 3: End Behavior.

Write the standard: MLA.A2.PF.8.

Key Vocabulary

  • End behavior: What a graph does as x moves far left or far right.
  • Degree: The highest exponent in a polynomial.
  • Leading coefficient: The coefficient of the highest-degree term.

Correct Example

f(x) = -2x4 + 3x - 1

Correct: Even degree and negative leading coefficient mean both ends fall.

Incorrect Example

f(x) = -2x4 + 3x - 1

Incorrect: Saying the right end rises ignores the negative leading coefficient.

Notebook Task

  1. Classify end behavior for an even-degree polynomial with positive leading coefficient.
  2. Classify end behavior for an odd-degree polynomial with negative leading coefficient.
  3. Explain why lower-degree terms do not control end behavior.
