What is the end behavior of f(x) = x4 - 3x + 1?

Answer: both ends rise

Feedback: Even degree with positive leading coefficient means both ends rise.

Question 2

Question ID:

MLA Standard:
MLA.A2.PF.8

What is the end behavior of f(x) = -x3 + 2x?

Answer: left rises, right falls

Feedback: Odd degree with negative leading coefficient rises left and falls right.

Which feature controls end behavior most directly?

Answer: degree and leading coefficient
