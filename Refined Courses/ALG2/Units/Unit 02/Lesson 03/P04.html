P04 Worked Examples

Work through each example slowly. The goal is not only to get an answer, but to understand why each step is valid and how the idea connects to the lesson standard.

Worked Example 1: Use Degree and Leading Coefficient

Problem: Describe the end behavior of f(x) = 2x4 - 3x + 1.

Step 1: Identify the degree as 4.

Step 2: Identify the leading coefficient as positive.

Step 3: Even degree with positive leading coefficient rises on both ends.

As x → -∞, f(x) → ∞; as x → ∞, f(x) → ∞.

Reasoning: The leading term controls end behavior.

Worked Example 2: Odd Degree Positive Leading Coefficient

Problem: Describe the end behavior of g(x) = x3 - 2x.

Step 1: Identify odd degree.

Step 2: Identify positive leading coefficient.

Step 3: Use opposite end directions for odd degree.

Left end down, right end up.

Reasoning: Odd-degree polynomial ends go in opposite directions.

Worked Example 3: Odd Degree Negative Leading Coefficient

Problem: Describe the end behavior of h(x) = -4x5 + x2.

Step 1: Identify odd degree.

Step 2: Identify negative leading coefficient.

Step 3: Reverse the positive odd-degree pattern.

Left end up, right end down.

Reasoning: A negative leading coefficient flips the end behavior.

Common Mistake

Problem: Describe the end behavior of -x4 + 2x.

Incorrect:

Both ends rise because degree 4 is even.

Why it is wrong: The leading coefficient is negative, so the graph is reflected.

Correct:

Both ends fall.

Fix: Use both degree and leading coefficient, not degree alone.
