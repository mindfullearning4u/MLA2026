P04 Concept Development and Worked Examples

End Behavior Rules

The degree and leading coefficient determine long-run polynomial behavior. Even degree means the ends move the same direction. Odd degree means the ends move opposite directions.

Worked Example 1

f(x) = 3x5 - x2 has odd degree and positive leading coefficient, so the left end falls and the right end rises.

Worked Example 2

g(x) = -4x6 + 2x has even degree and negative leading coefficient, so both ends fall.

Common Mistake

Do not use the constant term to determine end behavior. Use degree and leading coefficient.
