ALG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 01

P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show enough work that your TOR can see your process, not just your final answer.

Purpose: This work helps your TOR decide whether you are ready to continue or whether you need intervention before moving ahead.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. Identify the degree of f(x) = x2 + 7x - 9.
  2. Explain why every quadratic is a polynomial.
  3. Identify the zeros of (x + 2)(x - 6).

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. Compare the end behavior of x2 and x3.
  2. Explain how factored form helps graphing.
  3. Identify the leading term of 4x3 - x + 8.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct a student who identifies the degree from the first term only.
  2. Explain why quadratics are a helpful bridge into polynomial functions.
  3. Write one connection between zeros and intercepts.
