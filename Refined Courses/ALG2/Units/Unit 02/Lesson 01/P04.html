P04 Concept Development and Worked Examples

Quadratic Functions as Polynomials

A quadratic function is a degree 2 polynomial. Standard, factored, and vertex forms reveal different features.

Worked Example 1

f(x) = 2x2 - 8x + 6 has degree 2, leading coefficient 2, y-intercept 6, and zeros 1 and 3.

Worked Example 2

g(x) = -3(x - 2)2 + 5 has vertex (2, 5), opens downward, and has maximum value 5.

Common Mistake

The vertex of (x + 4)2 - 7 is (-4, -7), not (4, -7).
