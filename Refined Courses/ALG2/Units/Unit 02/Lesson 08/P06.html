P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Foundation Practice

  1. Describe end behavior of 3x5 - 2.
  2. Find zeros of (x + 4)(x - 1)2.
  3. Predict bounce or cross at each zero.

Part B: Skill Practice

  1. Solve x2 + x - 12 = 0.
  2. For an upward parabola with zeros -2 and 5, solve f(x) < 0.
  3. Interpret a zero in a profit context.

Part C: Explain and Correct

  1. Correct an end-behavior mistake.
  2. Correct a multiplicity mistake.
  3. Explain how Unit 2 polynomial tools work together.
