P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Task

  1. Copy the worked examples from the lesson.
  2. Label the feature or solution method used in each example.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why the incorrect example is incorrect.

Correct Reasoning

Correct: A complete analysis names the feature, connects it to the equation or graph, and explains its meaning.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: A partial analysis that lists numbers without identifying zeros, intervals, or context meaning is incomplete.
