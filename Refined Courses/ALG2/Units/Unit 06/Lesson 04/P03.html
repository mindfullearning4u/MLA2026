P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Read Regression Output

A regression output gives y=12.5(1.08)x for a population model.

Identify the initial value, the growth factor, and the percent growth rate. Then explain when the model may become unreliable.

Common Mistake to Watch

Incorrect: In y=12.5(1.08)x, the growth rate is 108%.

Correct: The growth factor is 1.08, so the growth rate is 8%.
