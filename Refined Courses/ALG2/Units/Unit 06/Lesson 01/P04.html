P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Worked Example 1: Compound Growth

A student invests $1,200 at 4% annual interest. Estimate the balance after 3 years.

Use A=P(1+r)t. Here P=1200, r=0.04, and t=3.

A=1200(1.04)3 = 1200(1.124864) = 1349.8368.

Correct: The balance is about $1,349.84.

Worked Example 2: Depreciation

A laptop costs $900 and depreciates by 18% each year. Estimate its value after 2 years.

Use V=900(1-0.18)2 = 900(0.82)2 = 900(0.6724) = 605.16.

Correct: The value is about $605.16.

Incorrect Example

Incorrect: For 18% depreciation, V=900(0.18)2. This uses the percent lost as the remaining factor.

Correct: The remaining factor is 1-0.18=0.82.
