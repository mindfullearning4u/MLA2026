P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each step shows the reasoning students should be able to reproduce independently.

Worked Example 1: Percent Increase

Problem: A $400 amount grows by 5%.

Step 1: Convert 5% to 0.05.

Step 2: Use factor 1.05.

Step 3: Multiply 400 by 1.05.

$420

Reasoning: Financial growth uses percent change as a multiplier.

Worked Example 2: Percent Decrease

Problem: A $600 balance decreases by 10%.

Step 1: Convert 10% to 0.10.

Step 2: Use factor 0.90.

Step 3: Multiply 600 by 0.90.

$540

Reasoning: A decrease factor is 1 minus the rate.

Worked Example 3: Compare Options

Problem: Compare 3% growth and 6% growth.

Step 1: Write factors 1.03 and 1.06.

Step 2: Compare the factors.

Step 3: Choose the larger growth factor.

6% grows faster.

Reasoning: Larger growth rates compound faster.

Common Mistake

Problem: Use a 7% growth factor.

Incorrect:

7

Why it is wrong: A percent growth factor is not the whole percent number.

Correct:

1.07

Fix: Use 1 plus the decimal rate.
