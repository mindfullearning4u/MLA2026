P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Compare Two Financial Situations

Situation A: A savings account starts with $800 and grows by 5% each year.

Situation B: A car starts at $18,000 and loses 12% of its value each year.

Write a model for each situation, identify the growth or decay factor, and explain why one model increases while the other decreases.

Common Mistake to Watch

Incorrect: A 12% decrease uses a factor of 0.12.

Correct: A 12% decrease uses a factor of 0.88 because 88% remains.
