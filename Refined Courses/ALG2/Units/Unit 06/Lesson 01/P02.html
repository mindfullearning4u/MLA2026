Set Up Your Notes

Title your notes: Lesson 1: Financial Mathematics.

Write the standard: Support-only review; no new primary MLA standard.

Write the learning target: I can use Algebra II models to make and explain financial decisions.

Vocabulary

  • Principal: the starting amount invested, borrowed, or saved.
  • Interest rate: the percent used to calculate growth or cost over time.
  • Growth factor: 1+r for an increase of r.
  • Decay factor: 1-r for a decrease of r.
  • Payment: a repeated amount used to pay down debt or add to savings.

Notebook Prompt

Create a two-column chart. In the left column, list financial quantities: principal, rate, time, payment, balance, interest, growth factor, and decay factor. In the right column, write what each quantity tells you in a real financial situation.
