ALG2 | Unit 06 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Financial Mathematics

Revisit and extend financial applications using prior exponential and modeling tools.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Support-only review; no new primary MLA standard

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: Support-only review of MA.912.FL.3.1; MA.912.FL.3.2; MA.912.FL.3.4

Use financial reasoning with percent, exponential growth, interpreting parameters, and calculator or spreadsheet tools.

What You Will Learn

  • Interpret financial models involving percent change, interest, savings, loans, and depreciation.
  • Identify principal, rate, time, payment, balance, growth factor, and decay factor.
  • Use exponential models such as A=P(1+r)t and A=P(1-r)t in financial contexts.
  • Explain what a financial answer means in context.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze financial vocabulary and formulas.
  • Work through examples involving interest, depreciation, loan payments, and savings goals.
  • Complete guided practice, independent work, checkpoint, notebook evidence, and a lesson quiz.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose appropriate financial models.
  • Calculate and interpret growth, decay, interest, and payment results.
  • Justify whether a result is reasonable for the financial situation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Support-only review: Use prior exponential and financial reasoning standards in authentic financial contexts.

In student language: I can use Algebra II models to make and explain financial decisions.
