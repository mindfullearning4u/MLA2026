P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Table Analysis

A data table shows hours studied and quiz score: (1, 62), (2, 68), (3, 74), (4, 80), (5, 86).

Describe the pattern, choose a reasonable model type, and predict the score for 6 hours. Explain whether the prediction is interpolation or extrapolation.

Common Mistake to Watch

Incorrect: Choose the model with the biggest prediction because it is most impressive.

Correct: Choose a model because its shape, residuals, and context fit the data.
