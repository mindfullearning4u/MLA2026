P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each step shows the reasoning students should be able to reproduce independently.

Worked Example 1: Compare Financial Models

Problem: Option A grows by 3%; Option B grows by 5%.

Step 1: Write factors 1.03 and 1.05.

Step 2: Compare the growth factors.

Step 3: State which grows faster.

Option B grows faster.

Reasoning: Financial comparisons depend on rates and factors.

Worked Example 2: Use a Regression Model

Problem: Use y=4x+10 to predict y when x=6.

Step 1: Substitute x=6.

Step 2: Compute 4(6)+10.

Step 3: State the prediction.

y=34

Reasoning: Regression models support prediction.

Worked Example 3: Choose and Justify a Model

Problem: Data increases by multiplying about 2 each step.

Step 1: Look for repeated multiplication.

Step 2: Identify geometric or exponential behavior.

Step 3: Choose a matching model.

Use an exponential model.

Reasoning: Model choice must match pattern structure.

Common Mistake

Problem: Choose a model only because it gives one close prediction.

Incorrect:

One close point proves the model.

Why it is wrong: A model should fit the overall pattern, not one point only.

Correct:

Use pattern, residuals, and context.

Fix: Evaluate the whole model, not a single result.
