Worked Review

For A=750(1.06)t, the starting amount is 750 and the growth rate is 6%. At t=3, A≈892.63.

Correct: The model predicts about $892.63 after 3 time periods.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Reporting only 892.63 without units or context.

Correct: State what the value represents and include units.
