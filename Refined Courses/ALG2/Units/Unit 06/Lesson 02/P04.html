P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Worked Example 1: Arithmetic Sequence

Find the 10th term of 4, 7, 10, 13, ...

The common difference is d=3. Use an=a1+(n-1)d.

a10=4+(10-1)(3)=4+27=31.

Correct: The 10th term is 31.

Worked Example 2: Geometric Sequence

Find the 6th term of 2, 6, 18, 54, ...

The common ratio is r=3. Use an=a1rn-1.

a6=2(3)5=486.

Correct: The 6th term is 486.

Incorrect Example

Incorrect: For 2, 6, 18, 54, use an=2+(n-1)4 because the terms increase.

Correct: The terms multiply by 3, so use a geometric rule.
