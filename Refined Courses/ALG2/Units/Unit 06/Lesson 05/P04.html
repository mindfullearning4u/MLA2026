P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Worked Example 1: Match Context to Model

A bacteria population triples every hour. Compare y=200+3t and y=200(3)t.

The phrase triples every hour means repeated multiplication by 3, so the exponential model fits.

Correct: y=200(3)t best matches the context.

Worked Example 2: Compare Residuals

Two models predict the same data. Model A residuals are -1, 2, -2, 1. Model B residuals are -8, 5, 10, -7.

Model A has smaller errors overall, so it is the better fit for the data.

Correct: Smaller residuals support Model A.

Incorrect Example

Incorrect: A model with a higher correlation always makes the most reasonable prediction.

Correct: Correlation matters, but context, residuals, and prediction range also matter.
