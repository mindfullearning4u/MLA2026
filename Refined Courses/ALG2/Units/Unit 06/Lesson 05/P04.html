P04 Worked Examples

Study each example carefully. Each step shows the reasoning students should be able to reproduce independently.

Worked Example 1: Compare at an Input

Problem: Model A gives f(5)=20 and Model B gives g(5)=25.

Step 1: Use the same input value.

Step 2: Compare the outputs.

Step 3: State which is greater at that input.

Model B is greater at x=5.

Reasoning: Fair comparisons use the same input.

Worked Example 2: Compare Growth Types

Problem: A linear model adds 5; an exponential model multiplies by 1.05.

Step 1: Identify additive growth.

Step 2: Identify multiplicative growth.

Step 3: Describe long-term behavior.

The exponential model can eventually grow faster.

Reasoning: Growth type affects long-term predictions.

Worked Example 3: Choose Better Fit

Problem: One model has smaller residuals across the data.

Step 1: Residuals measure errors.

Step 2: Smaller errors usually mean better fit.

Step 3: Choose the model with smaller residuals.

Choose the smaller-residual model.

Reasoning: Fit quality matters when selecting models.

Common Mistake

Problem: Compare f(3) with g(8).

Incorrect:

The larger output is the better model.

Why it is wrong: Different inputs do not make a fair comparison.

Correct:

Compare models at the same input or by fit measures.

Fix: Align inputs or compare residuals.
