P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Model a Context

A club has $500 and earns $35 per ticket sold. It must spend $1,200 on an event. Write an inequality for the number of tickets needed and solve it.

Common Mistake to Watch

Incorrect: The club needs 20.1 tickets, so 20 tickets is enough.

Correct: If the answer must be a whole ticket count, round up to meet the constraint.
