P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples

Worked Example 1: Linear Constraint

A fundraiser starts with $250 and earns $18 per item. It needs at least $700.

Model: 250+18x ≥ 700. Then 18x ≥ 450, so x ≥ 25.

Correct: The group must sell at least 25 items.

Worked Example 2: Exponential Revision

A population model P=1200(1.10)t predicts 1,932 after 5 years, but the habitat limit is 1,800.

The prediction exceeds the constraint, so the model may need a limited-growth revision or a warning about the prediction range.

Correct: A model result must be checked against context.

Incorrect Example

Incorrect: Once an equation gives an answer, the model is finished.

Correct: A model must be interpreted, checked, and revised when needed.
