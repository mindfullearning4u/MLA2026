P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Application Planning

A community garden starts with 20 plants and expects 15% growth each month, but the garden can hold at most 80 plants.

Write a model, predict the number of plants after 6 months, and explain whether the capacity constraint matters.

Common Mistake to Watch

Incorrect: A model answer is complete without units or context.

Correct: A complete answer states what the number means and whether it is reasonable.
