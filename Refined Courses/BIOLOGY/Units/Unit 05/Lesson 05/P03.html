What the Mapped Standards Require

  • SC.912.L.15.6: Domains and kingdoms of living organisms.
  • ELA.K12.EE.2.1: Read and comprehend grade-level complex texts proficiently.

Continue the Teaching Sequence

Classify by evidence. First determine whether cells are prokaryotic or eukaryotic. Then use traits such as nucleus, cell wall type, chloroplasts, nutrition, and multicellularity. Do not classify only by where an organism lives or whether it looks familiar.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the evolution concept, mechanism, relationship, or trend in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the graph, evidence chart, cladogram, classification table, or timeline. Why: evolution answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence and include any limit. Why: mastery requires careful reasoning, not overclaiming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "All microscopic organisms belong to the same domain."

Correct: "Microscopic organisms can belong to different domains; classification depends on cellular and molecular traits."

Teachable explanation: Size alone is weak classification evidence.
