Continue the Teaching Sequence

Read selection graphs by tracking trait frequency across generations. A trait increasing after a selection pressure suggests individuals with that inherited trait left more offspring. Do not say organisms changed because they wanted to; selection acts on existing variation.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the evolution concept, mechanism, relationship, or trend in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the graph, evidence chart, cladogram, classification table, or timeline. Why: evolution answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence and include any limit. Why: mastery requires careful reasoning, not overclaiming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Animals evolve because they need or want a better trait."

Correct: "Natural selection changes trait frequencies when inherited variation affects survival and reproduction."

Teachable explanation: Selection acts on existing inherited variation; it does not create traits because organisms want them.
