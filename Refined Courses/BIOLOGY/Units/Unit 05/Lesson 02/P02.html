First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Verify that individuals vary and that the relevant difference is heritable.
  2. Step 2: Identify the environmental pressure and compare survival and reproduction among variants.
  3. Step 3: Calculate trait-frequency change across generations.
  4. Step 4: Explain change at the population level; individuals survive, reproduce, or acclimate but do not genetically evolve during a lifetime.
