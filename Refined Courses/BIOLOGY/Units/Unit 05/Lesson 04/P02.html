First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Read the root, tips, nodes, and derived-trait marks before answering.
  2. Step 2: Find closest relatives by the most recent shared node, not by tip spacing.
  3. Step 3: Use a trait or DNA-distance matrix to propose grouping.
  4. Step 4: Revise a classification when stronger molecular or anatomical evidence conflicts with the old grouping.
