Notebook Title: Genetic Variation and Evolutionary Change

Vocabulary

  • Mutation: DNA change that can create new alleles.
  • Recombination: reshuffling alleles during sexual reproduction.
  • Genetic drift: random allele-frequency change, strongest in small populations.
  • Gene flow: movement of alleles between populations.
  • Allele frequency: how common an allele is in a population.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Evolutionary change depends on genetic variation. Mutation can create new alleles. Recombination creates new allele combinations. Genetic drift changes allele frequencies by chance, especially in small populations. Gene flow moves alleles between populations when individuals migrate and reproduce.
