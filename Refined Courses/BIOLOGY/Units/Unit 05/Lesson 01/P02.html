Vocabulary

  • Evolution: change in inherited traits of populations over generations.
  • Fossil: preserved evidence of past life.
  • Homologous structure: similar structure suggesting shared ancestry.
  • Molecular evidence: DNA or protein similarity used to compare organisms.
  • Biogeography: study of where organisms live and how distribution supports explanations.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Evolution is explained with multiple evidence lines. Fossils show organisms and transitions through time. Homologous structures show shared body plans. Embryology can show developmental similarities. Molecular evidence compares DNA and proteins. Biogeography shows how location and isolation connect to change.
