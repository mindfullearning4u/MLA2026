BIOLOGY | Unit 05 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Putting It All Together

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary standard(s): MLA.BIO.EVO.01, MLA.BIO.EVO.02, MLA.BIO.EVO.03, MLA.BIO.EVO.04

Support standard(s): ELA.K12.EE.1.1, MA.K12.MTR.5.1

What You Will Learn

Synthesize evolution evidence, mechanisms, classification, diversity, origin-of-life explanations, and hominid trends.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Unit-level mixed stimuli from taught lessons.
  • Use tables, diagrams, graphs, and process models for this investigation: Unit synthesis across evolution evidence, mechanisms, classification, and trends.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use evidence to explain biological change, relationships, diversity, and trends. Required visuals are included so you can reason from data instead of guessing from vocabulary alone.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless it is explicitly approved through your Teacher of Record or approved school workflow.

External simulations and resources are approval-only. Use the lesson content, embedded tables, diagrams, maps, models, and data displays as the required source for mastery unless an approved resource is later added.
