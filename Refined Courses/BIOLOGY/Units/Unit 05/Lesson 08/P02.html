Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: connecting several evidence-based ideas in one explanation.
  • Evolution evidence: fossils, anatomy, molecules, distribution, and observed change.
  • Evolution mechanism: process such as selection, drift, mutation, or gene flow.
  • Classification evidence: traits and molecular data used to group organisms.
  • Trend evidence: data pattern that supports a careful claim.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Unit 5 connects evidence and mechanisms. Evidence shows change, relatedness, and patterns. Mechanisms explain how populations change. Classification organizes biodiversity using evolutionary relationships. Scientific origin explanations and hominid trends require careful evidence and limits.
