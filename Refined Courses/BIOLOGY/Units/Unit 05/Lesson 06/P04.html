Worked Example 1: Miller–Urey model

Displayed evidence: Gases, water vapor, and electrical energy produce several organic molecules.

  1. Name what is being studied: Miller–Urey model.
  2. Observe: Restate the exact label, comparison, or value: Gases, water vapor, and electrical energy produce several organic molecules.
  3. Interpret: The result supports abiotic formation of building blocks under modeled conditions.
  4. Check the boundary: Do not claim more conditions, organisms, or causes than the displayed evidence supports.

Complete answer: The result supports abiotic formation of building blocks under modeled conditions. The supporting evidence is that gases, water vapor, and electrical energy produce several organic molecules.
