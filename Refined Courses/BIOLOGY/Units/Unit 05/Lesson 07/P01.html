P01 Lesson Overview

Hominid Evolution Trends

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary standard(s): MLA.BIO.EVO.04

Support standard(s): SC.912.L.15.10, ELA.K12.EE.3.1

What You Will Learn

Identify basic trends in hominid evolution.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Hominid trend chart/table.
  • Use tables, diagrams, graphs, and process models for this investigation: Trend analysis using fossil/trait data.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use evidence to explain biological change, relationships, diversity, and trends. Required visuals are included so you can reason from data instead of guessing from vocabulary alone.
