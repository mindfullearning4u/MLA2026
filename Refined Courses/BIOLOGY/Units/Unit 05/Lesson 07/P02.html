Investigation Notebook Setup

Evolution Evidence, Classification, and Hominid Trends Investigation: Question, Purpose, and Evidence Setup

Copy this title into your notebook. Under it, create these six labeled sections: Investigation Question, Claim Being Tested, Variables or Comparison Groups, Controlled Conditions, Data/Model Evidence, and CER Draft.

Vocabulary

  • Investigation question: the testable question the evidence is being used to answer.
  • Variable: a factor that changes or is measured in an investigation.
  • Control or controlled condition: what stays the same so the comparison is fair.
  • Evidence: specific data, observation, model detail, or source information that supports a claim.
  • CER: claim, evidence, and reasoning.

Step-by-Step Setup

  1. Step 1: Copy this investigation question: Which evidence best supports a scientific explanation of evolutionary relationships and change over time?
  2. Step 2: Copy this claim being tested: Evolutionary relationships are supported by patterns in fossils, anatomy, molecular data, population change, classification, and hominid trends.
  3. Step 3: Copy the variable or comparison focus: Evidence source being compared: fossil age, trait similarity, molecular similarity, population data, and cladogram position.
  4. Step 4: Copy the controlled conditions: Use the same group of organisms when comparing traits, molecular evidence, and classification.
  5. Step 5: Read the evidence table below. Put a star next to the two evidence rows that most directly answer the investigation question.
