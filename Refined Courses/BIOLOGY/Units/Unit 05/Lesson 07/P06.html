Independent Work Self-Check

Before you submit this independent work, check that each answer connects directly to MLA.BIO.EVO.04. A complete response should name the science idea, point to the data, model, diagram, source detail, or observation used as evidence, and explain why that evidence supports the answer.

  • Step 1: Reread the task and underline the science word or relationship being tested.
  • Step 2: Add the specific evidence you used. Do not write only a general opinion or memory statement.
  • Step 3: Explain the reasoning in one clear sentence so another student could follow your thinking without a live teacher.
  • Step 4: Check units, labels, arrows, categories, or vocabulary before moving to the checkpoint.
