Teacher of Record Graded

This checkpoint is reviewed by your Teacher of Record. It shows whether you can use biology evidence correctly before moving deeper into Unit 1.

Checkpoint Task

Submit a CER response using this scenario:

A student compares two sealed containers of yeast and sugar water. Container A is kept warm. Container B is kept cool. After 20 minutes, Container A has more bubbles than Container B.

  1. Write one testable question for this investigation.
  2. Write one claim about temperature and yeast activity.
  3. Identify one piece of evidence from the scenario.
  4. Write one reasoning sentence that connects the evidence to the claim.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Your notebook evidence should include:

  • Vocabulary from P02.
  • The observation, inference, claim, and evidence table.
  • The CER organizer.
  • Your independent work from P06.
  • Your checkpoint CER response.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit your CER response, completed evidence table, and one sentence explaining which part of CER was easiest or hardest.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review P02-P06 and complete all notebook evidence.
  2. Write your checkpoint response in complete sentences.
  3. Submit the checkpoint through the assigned course submission area.
  4. Use Teacher of Record review feedback to make corrections or resubmit when the workflow requires it.

Mastery Criteria

To show mastery, your checkpoint must meet at least 80% of the criteria below:

  • The question is testable and biology-based.
  • The claim clearly answers the investigation question.
  • The evidence comes from the scenario or data.
  • The reasoning explains why the evidence supports the claim.
  • The response uses science vocabulary accurately.

If your work does not meet mastery, review the feedback, complete Teacher of Record intervention when required, and resubmit corrections through the required workflow.

Need Help?
