Part A: Classify the Statements

Scenario: A student observes two samples of pond water under a microscope. Sample A has 12 moving organisms in one field of view. Sample B has 3 moving organisms in one field of view.

  1. "Sample A has 12 moving organisms in one field of view."
  2. "Sample A may contain more microorganisms than Sample B."
  3. "Sample A has more microorganisms because it has more nutrients."
  4. "The microscope count supports the idea that Sample A has more visible organisms than Sample B."

For each statement, label it as observation/data, inference, claim, or evidence/reasoning. Then explain one label.

Part B: Testable Question

Rewrite this non-testable question as a testable biology question:

Original question: Are pond organisms interesting?

Your rewritten question must include something that can be observed or measured.
