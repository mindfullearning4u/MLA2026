Term What It Means Biology Example
Observation What you directly notice or measure. The plant in Cup A is 12 cm tall.
Inference A logical explanation based on evidence. Cup A may have received more light.
Claim The answer or explanation you are trying to support. Light affects plant growth.
Evidence The data or observations that support the claim. Plants under light grew taller than plants kept in darkness.

How to use this source

Part A: Classify the Statements

Microscope observation record: The same microscope and magnification were used for both samples. Sample A had 12 moving organisms in one field of view. Sample B had 3 moving organisms in one field of view. The record contains no measurement of nutrients or water quality.

  1. "Sample A has 12 moving organisms in one field of view."
  2. "Sample A may contain more microorganisms than Sample B."
  3. "Sample A has more microorganisms because it has more nutrients."
  4. "Sample A has more visible organisms in this field of view than Sample B."

For each statement, use one label: observation/data, inference, or claim. A statement may serve as evidence only when it is used to support a claim. After labeling all four statements, choose one and explain which words helped you classify it.

Original question: Are pond organisms interesting?
