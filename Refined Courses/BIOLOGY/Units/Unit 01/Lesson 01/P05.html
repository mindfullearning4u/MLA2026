BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 01

P05 Guided Practice

Before You Start

Use your notes from P02, P03, and P04. The guided practice checks whether you can identify observations, claims, evidence, testable questions, and reasoning in biology situations.

Standard focus: MLA.BIO.SCI.01 and MLA.BIO.SCI.03.

What the Practice Will Ask You To Do

  • Choose whether a statement is an observation, inference, claim, evidence, or reasoning.
  • Identify which evidence best supports a biology claim.
  • Decide whether a question is testable.
  • Use data tables or short source excerpts to support a conclusion.

Strategy

  1. Read the question first.
  2. Underline or note the claim being made.
  3. Find the data, observation, or source information that matches the claim.
  4. Eliminate answer choices that are opinions, unrelated facts, or unsupported guesses.
