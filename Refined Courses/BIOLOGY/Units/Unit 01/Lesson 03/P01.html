BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P01 Lesson Overview

Lab Safety, Measurement, and Data Quality

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.LAB.01: Plan and carry out safe investigation procedures using appropriate controls and evidence.
  • MLA.BIO.LAB.03: Organize, analyze, and interpret measurement data, including precision, error, and data quality.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how safety decisions and careful measurement affect the quality of biology data. Reliable conclusions require safe procedures, clear units, repeated measurements, organized tables, and attention to possible error.

What You Will Do

  • Match safety choices to lab scenarios.
  • Record measurements with units and consistent precision.
  • Analyze a data table for error, outliers, and reliability.
  • Explain how data quality affects a scientific conclusion.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery through notebook evidence, guided practice, a checkpoint safety and data-quality analysis, and the lesson quiz. Your work must identify safe procedures and explain whether data are trustworthy.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Biology evidence is only useful when it is collected safely and measured carefully. This lesson prepares you to judge whether lab data can support a conclusion.
