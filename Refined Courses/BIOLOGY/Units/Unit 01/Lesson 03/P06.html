Independent Work Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Show complete sentences where you explain safety or data quality.

Part A: Safety Scenario

A procedure asks students to heat unknown liquid samples at home. Explain why this is not appropriate for this course format and rewrite it as a safe data-based analysis using provided temperature data.

Part B: Measurement Table

Copy and complete this table heading: "Trial | Seedling Height (___) | Notes". Choose the correct unit for height and explain why units are required.

Part C: Data Quality

Values for four trials are 3.2 cm, 3.3 cm, 3.1 cm, and 9.8 cm. Identify the possible outlier, give one possible error explanation, and write one sentence explaining how it affects the conclusion.

Independent Work Self-Check

Before you submit this independent work, check that each answer connects directly to MLA.BIO.LAB.01; MLA.BIO.LAB.03. A complete response should name the science idea, point to the data, model, diagram, source detail, or observation used as evidence, and explain why that evidence supports the answer.

  • Step 1: Reread the task and underline the science word or relationship being tested.
  • Step 2: Add the specific evidence you used. Do not write only a general opinion or memory statement.
  • Step 3: Explain the reasoning in one clear sentence so another student could follow your thinking without a live teacher.
  • Step 4: Check units, labels, arrows, categories, or vocabulary before moving to the checkpoint.
