Notebook Title

Lesson 3: Lab Safety, Measurement, and Data Quality

Vocabulary

  • Safety procedure: A required action that reduces risk during an investigation.
  • Measurement: A number with a unit collected using a tool or defined method.
  • Precision: How specific or repeatable measurements are.
  • Accuracy: How close a measurement is to the true or accepted value.
  • Outlier: A data point that is far from the pattern and may need explanation.
  • Error: A problem in measurement, procedure, or recording that can affect results.

Measurement Quality

Every measurement needs a number and a unit. A table of "5, 7, 8" is incomplete because the reader cannot tell whether the values are millimeters, centimeters, seconds, or cells per field of view.

Repeated measurements make data stronger because they reveal whether results are consistent or whether one trial looks unusual.
