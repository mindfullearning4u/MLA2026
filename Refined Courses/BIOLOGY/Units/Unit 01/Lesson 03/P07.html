P07 Checkpoint

Teacher of Record Graded

This checkpoint is submitted for Teacher of Record review and focuses on safe procedures, measurement quality, and interpretation of possible error.

Checkpoint Task

Scenario: A provided data set shows plant leaf lengths of 4.4 cm, 4.5 cm, 4.3 cm, and 12.0 cm for the same condition.

  1. State the correct safety format for this activity.
  2. Identify what is being measured and the unit.
  3. Identify the possible outlier.
  4. Give two possible explanations for the unusual value.
  5. Write a CER paragraph explaining whether the data are strong enough for a conclusion.
