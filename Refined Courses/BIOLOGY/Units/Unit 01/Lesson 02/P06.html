P06 Independent Work

Independent Work Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Show enough detail that someone can see your question, variables, controls, planned evidence, and reasoning.

Part A: Identify Variables

Scenario: A student uses a provided data set to compare how different salt concentrations affect brine shrimp movement rate.

  1. Identify the independent variable.
  2. Identify the dependent variable.
  3. List three control variables that should stay the same.

Part B: Improve the Question

Rewrite this weak question as a testable biology question: "Do plants grow better with different soil?" Include the changed factor, measured outcome, organism, and time frame.

Part C: Plan the Investigation

Create a safe data-based investigation plan for your Part B question. Include a purpose, safety note, independent variable, dependent variable, at least four controls, three procedure steps, and a blank data table with headings.
