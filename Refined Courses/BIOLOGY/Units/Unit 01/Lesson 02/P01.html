BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 02

P01 Lesson Overview

Questions, Variables, and Investigation Design

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.SCI.01: Use scientific inquiry to ask testable biology questions, collect evidence, analyze data, and communicate explanations.
  • MLA.BIO.LAB.01: Plan safe biological investigations by identifying variables, controls, procedures, and evidence.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to turn a biology question into an investigation plan. A strong plan identifies what will be changed, what will be measured, what must stay the same, and what evidence will answer the question.

What You Will Do

  • Write testable biological questions.
  • Identify independent, dependent, and control variables.
  • Use a planning flowchart to organize an investigation.
  • Design a safe data-based or provided biology investigation plan.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by completing notebook evidence, guided practice, an investigation design checkpoint, and the lesson quiz. Your work must show that your question, variables, controls, procedure, and evidence match each other.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Biologists do not just ask interesting questions. They ask questions that can be answered with evidence. This lesson helps you plan fair investigations so later lab results are trustworthy.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Required direct resource links are included when a free, student-safe resource supports the lesson. Use the exact lesson link provided so your evidence matches the lesson.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record after using the lesson if you are still unsure whether your question can be tested with evidence.

Bring your question and variable table when asking for clarification.
