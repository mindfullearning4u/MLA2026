Notebook Title

Lesson 2: Questions, Variables, and Investigation Design

Vocabulary

  • Testable question: A question that can be answered with observations, measurements, or provided data.
  • Independent variable: The one factor changed on purpose.
  • Dependent variable: The outcome measured or observed.
  • Control variables: Conditions kept the same so the comparison is fair.
  • Control group: A comparison group that does not receive the changed condition when one is appropriate.
  • Procedure: Ordered steps used to collect evidence safely and consistently.

From Biology Question to Investigation

A useful investigation question names a relationship that evidence can test. The question should include a factor that can change and an outcome that can be measured.

Weak question: Are plants better in sunlight?

Stronger question: How does the number of hours of light per day affect the height of radish seedlings after 10 days?

The stronger question is clearer because it identifies the changed factor, the measured outcome, the organism, and the time frame.

Investigation Planning Flowchart

Question -> Prediction -> Variables -> Controls -> Procedure -> Data Table -> CER Conclusion

Use this order because each part depends on the part before it. You cannot choose a data table until you know what outcome will be measured. You cannot make a fair conclusion unless the controls make the comparison trustworthy.
