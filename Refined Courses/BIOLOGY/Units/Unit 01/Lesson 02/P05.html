P05 Guided Practice

Guided Practice Readiness

Before starting the Moodle Guided Practice, review your notes for these skills: writing a testable question, identifying independent and dependent variables, listing controls, and choosing a matching data table.

You are ready when you can explain why changing one main factor makes an investigation stronger.

Practice Focus

  • Match variables to experimental scenarios.
  • Choose controls for a fair comparison.
  • Identify the best evidence for an investigation question.
  • Recognize unsafe or incomplete investigation plans.
