Checkpoint Task

Scenario: A virtual microscope image shows rectangular plant cells at 400x total magnification.

  1. Identify the microscope lens setup that could produce 400x total magnification.
  2. Name two microscope parts and explain their functions.
  3. Write two observations from the image.
  4. Write one interpretation using biology vocabulary.
  5. Explain one way a labeled model could help and one limit of relying only on a model.
