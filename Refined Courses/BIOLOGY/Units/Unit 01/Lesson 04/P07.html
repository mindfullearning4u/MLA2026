Teacher of Record Graded

This checkpoint is submitted for Teacher of Record review and focuses on microscope tools, magnification, models, and observation evidence.

Checkpoint Task

Scenario: A virtual microscope image shows rectangular plant cells at 400x total magnification.

  1. Identify the microscope lens setup that could produce 400x total magnification.
  2. Name two microscope parts and explain their functions.
  3. Write two observations from the image.
  4. Write one interpretation using biology vocabulary.
  5. Explain one way a labeled model could help and one limit of relying only on a model.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit P02 notes, P03 image/model notes, P04 worked examples, P06 independent work, and the checkpoint task.

Checkpoint Submission

Submit a clear photo, scan, or typed response in the course submission area.

Submission Workflow

Complete the checkpoint after the lesson pages and guided practice. Then submit it for Teacher of Record review.

Mastery Criteria

Mastery requires at least 80%. Your response must calculate magnification correctly, identify tool functions, separate observation from interpretation, and explain a model's usefulness and limit. If mastery is not met, use feedback to correct and resubmit. Repeated difficulty may require Teacher of Record intervention before another attempt.
