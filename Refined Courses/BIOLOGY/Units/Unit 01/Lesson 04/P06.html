Mapped Standards for This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.BIO.LAB.02

Support Standards: SC.912.L.14.4; ELA.K12.EE.3.1; MA.K12.MTR.2.1

Independent Work Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences for explanations and show all magnification calculations.

Part A: Microscope Parts

List the function of the eyepiece, objective lens, stage, focus knob, and light source. Then identify which part you would change to move from low power to higher power.

Part B: Magnification

Calculate total magnification for each setup: 10x eyepiece with 4x objective; 10x eyepiece with 10x objective; 10x eyepiece with 40x objective. Explain what changes as magnification increases.

Part C: Observation and Model

Use the sample micrograph-style image from P03. Write two observations, one interpretation, and one limit of the image. Then explain how a labeled model could help.

Independent Work Self-Check

Before you submit this independent work, check that each answer connects directly to MLA.BIO.LAB.02. A complete response should name the science idea, point to the data, model, diagram, source detail, or observation used as evidence, and explain why that evidence supports the answer.

  • Step 1: Reread the task and underline the science word or relationship being tested.
  • Step 2: Add the specific evidence you used. Do not write only a general opinion or memory statement.
  • Step 3: Explain the reasoning in one clear sentence so another student could follow your thinking without a live teacher.
  • Step 4: Check units, labels, arrows, categories, or vocabulary before moving to the checkpoint.
