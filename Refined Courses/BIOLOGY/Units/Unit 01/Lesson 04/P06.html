Independent Work Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences for explanations and show all magnification calculations.

Part A: Microscope Parts

List the function of the eyepiece, objective lens, stage, focus knob, and light source. Then identify which part you would change to move from low power to higher power.

Part B: Magnification

Calculate total magnification for each setup: 10x eyepiece with 4x objective; 10x eyepiece with 10x objective; 10x eyepiece with 40x objective. Explain what changes as magnification increases.

Part C: Observation and Model

Use the sample micrograph-style image from P03. Write two observations, one interpretation, and one limit of the image. Then explain how a labeled model could help.
