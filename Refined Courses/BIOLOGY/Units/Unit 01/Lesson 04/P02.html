Notebook Title

Lesson 4: Tools, Microscopes, Models, and Observations

Vocabulary

  • Tool: An instrument used to extend observation or collect measurements.
  • Microscope: A tool that magnifies small objects or structures.
  • Magnification: How many times larger an image appears.
  • Resolution: The ability to see detail clearly.
  • Model: A representation used to explain a structure, process, or relationship.
  • Micrograph: An image made using a microscope.

Microscope Diagram

PartFunction
EyepieceLens you look through; often 10x.
Objective lensLens near the specimen; may be 4x, 10x, or 40x.
StagePlatform that holds the slide.
Focus knobMoves the stage or lens to sharpen the image.
Light sourceShines light through or onto the specimen.

Magnification Model

Total magnification = eyepiece magnification x objective magnification.

If the eyepiece is 10x and the objective is 40x, then total magnification is 10 x 40 = 400x. This means the image appears 400 times larger than the actual object.
