Guided Practice Readiness

Before starting the Moodle Guided Practice, review microscope parts, total magnification, observations, models, and micrograph interpretation. You are ready when you can explain the difference between what an image shows and what a model explains.

Practice Focus

  • Match microscope parts to functions.
  • Calculate total magnification.
  • Choose tools for biological observation tasks.
  • Interpret simple micrograph-style evidence.
