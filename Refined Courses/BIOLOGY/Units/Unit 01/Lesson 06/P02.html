Vocabulary

  • Cell: the basic unit of structure and function in living things.
  • Cell theory: a well-supported explanation about cells and living things.
  • Scientific theory: a broad explanation supported by many lines of evidence.
  • Model: a representation that helps explain an object or process.
  • Microscope: a tool that makes small structures visible.

The Three Parts of Cell Theory

  1. All living things are made of one or more cells.
  2. The cell is the basic unit of structure and function in living things.
  3. All cells come from existing cells.

These statements are not guesses. They summarize evidence from observations of many organisms and from the repeated observation that cells divide to produce new cells.
