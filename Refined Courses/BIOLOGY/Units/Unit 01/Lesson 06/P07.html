Checkpoint Task

Use this evidence set: plant tissue observed under a microscope contains cells; animal tissue observed under a microscope contains cells; cell division images show one cell becoming two cells. Write an explanation of how this evidence supports cell theory.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit your timeline, cell theory evidence chart, model vs. theory table, and final explanation.

Checkpoint Submission

Your final explanation must include all three parts of cell theory and at least two specific pieces of evidence.

Submission Workflow

Submit the checkpoint to the Teacher of Record for grading. Review feedback, correct missing evidence or unclear reasoning, and resubmit according to the course workflow if needed.

Mastery Criteria

Mastery requires at least 80%. Your work must accurately state cell theory, connect evidence to the theory, and distinguish a model from a scientific theory. If you score below 80%, correction, resubmission, or TOR intervention may be required before another attempt.
