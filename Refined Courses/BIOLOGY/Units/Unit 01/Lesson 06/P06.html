Independent Work Workflow

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. This is an evidence and model analysis activity. No physical lab materials are required.

Part A: Timeline Evidence

Create a four-row timeline using these events: improved microscopes made cells visible; plant tissues were observed to contain cells; animal tissues were observed to contain cells; cell division was observed. For each event, write the cell theory statement it supports.
