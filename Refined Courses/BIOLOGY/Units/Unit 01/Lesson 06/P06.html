How to use this source

  1. Locate: Find the row, column, label, value, arrow, or relationship that directly matches the question.
  2. Record: Copy the exact relevant detail into your notebook. Include units and labels when they are shown.
  3. Explain: State what that detail shows using the lesson vocabulary and mapped biology standard.
  4. Connect: Explain why the detail supports your conclusion. Do not write only the source value or a definition.

For Parts A-C, each answer must name at least one exact feature from this source and explain how it supports the biology conclusion.

What Your Finished Independent Work Must Show

Write the heading Unit 1 Lesson 6: Cell Theory and the Process of Science. Then complete every prompt under Parts A, B, and C. Do not submit a copied table by itself.

  • Part A: Copy the requested labels, categories, sequence, or calculation and complete every item. Beside each answer, write the exact source row or feature you used.
  • Part B: Write complete sentences that compare or interpret the required evidence. Name both items being compared and the biological relationship between them.
  • Part C: Write a claim-evidence-reasoning response. Include one clear claim, at least two exact source details, and reasoning that explains why each detail supports the claim.

Completion check: A reader should be able to see what you answered, where your evidence came from, and how you reasoned without asking you to explain it aloud.
