BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 06

P01 Lesson Overview

Cell Theory and the Process of Science

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.CEL.01: Explain cell theory as a foundation for understanding living things.
  • MLA.BIO.SCI.03: Distinguish observations, models, theories, and evidence-based scientific explanations.

What You Will Learn

You will learn the three main ideas of cell theory and how evidence from microscopes, observations, and repeated investigations helped scientists build a scientific theory.

What You Will Do

  • Read an evidence timeline for cell theory.
  • Connect historical evidence to the three parts of cell theory.
  • Compare models and theories.
  • Use a cell image or observation to support an explanation.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining how evidence supports cell theory, completing a timeline and evidence chart, and submitting a checkpoint explanation with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Cell theory explains why cells matter in biology: living things are made of cells, cells are the basic unit of life, and new cells come from existing cells. The theory is strong because many lines of evidence support it.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless it is explicitly approved through your Teacher of Record or approved school workflow.

External simulations and resources are approval-only. Use the lesson content, embedded tables, diagrams, maps, models, and data displays as the required source for mastery unless an approved resource is later added.
