Model vs. Theory

IdeaPurposeCell Example
ModelRepresents something so it is easier to study.A labeled cell diagram.
TheoryExplains a broad pattern supported by evidence.Cell theory explains cells and living things.

A model can change when new evidence improves it. A scientific theory can also be refined, but it is not a casual guess. It is strongly supported.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Cell theory is just a guess because it has the word theory."

Correct: "Cell theory is a well-supported scientific explanation based on many observations of cells and living things."

Teachable Explanation: In science, a theory is a strong explanatory framework. It is different from an everyday guess.
