What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.CEL.01: Explain cell theory, the history of its discovery, and its connection to the process of science.
  • MLA.BIO.SCI.03: Distinguish observations, inferences, laws, theories, scientific questions, and non-scientific claims in biological contexts.
  • SC.912.L.14.1: Cell theory and history of discovery as process of science.
  • SC.912.N.3.1: Scientific theory as evidence-based explanation.
  • SC.912.N.3.4: Difference between theories and laws.

Before You Begin

Guided Practice will ask you to connect evidence to cell theory, identify models, and explain why scientific theories are evidence-based.

Standards: MLA.BIO.CEL.01 and MLA.BIO.SCI.03

Readiness Checklist

  • I can state the three parts of cell theory.
  • I can connect microscope evidence to cell theory.
  • I can tell a model from a scientific theory.
  • I can explain why theory does not mean guess in science.
