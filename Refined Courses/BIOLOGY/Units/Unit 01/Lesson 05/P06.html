BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P06 Independent Work

Independent Work Workflow

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. This is a data-based activity. No physical lab materials are required.

Part A: Source Reliability

Source 1: A university extension article reports that nitrate runoff can increase algae growth and includes pond nutrient data.

Source 2: A social media post says, "Algae blooms are always caused by hot weather," and gives no data.

Rate each source as strong, limited, or weak for explaining algae growth. Explain one reason for each rating.

Part B: Evidence Table

ObservationData
Heavy rain occurred before bloomRainfall: 8 cm in 3 days
Nitrate increased2 mg/L to 12 mg/L
Temperature changed slightly22 C to 23 C

Use the table to choose the stronger explanation: fertilizer runoff or warmer water. Write two evidence statements.

Part C: CER Response

Write a CER paragraph answering this question: Which explanation is better supported by the evidence, and why? Include one claim, at least two pieces of evidence, and reasoning about why the evidence supports your claim.
