BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 05

P01 Lesson Overview

Claims, Sources, and Scientific Argument

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.SCI.02: Evaluate scientific claims, sources, evidence, and alternative explanations in biology contexts.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to judge whether a biology claim is supported by reliable evidence. A strong scientific argument uses a clear claim, trustworthy evidence, and reasoning that connects the evidence to the claim.

What You Will Do

  • Use a source reliability checklist.
  • Compare competing explanations for the same evidence.
  • Build a claim-evidence-reasoning argument.
  • Identify weak evidence, unsupported claims, and overgeneralizations.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by evaluating a short source excerpt, rating evidence quality, comparing two explanations, completing guided practice, and submitting a checkpoint CER argument with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard helps you decide what to trust in science. When a source makes a biology claim, you should ask who made the claim, what evidence supports it, whether the evidence is current and relevant, and whether another explanation fits the evidence better.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record after you have reviewed the checklist and examples if you still need clarification.

Ask for support before the checkpoint if you cannot tell reliable evidence from an unsupported claim.
