Vocabulary

  • Macromolecule: a large molecule needed by living things.
  • Carbohydrate: molecule used for quick energy and structure in some organisms.
  • Lipid: molecule used for long-term energy storage, membranes, and insulation.
  • Protein: molecule that performs cell jobs such as structure, transport, movement, and enzymes.
  • Nucleic acid: molecule that stores and transmits genetic information.
