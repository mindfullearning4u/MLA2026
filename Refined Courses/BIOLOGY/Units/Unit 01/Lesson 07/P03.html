Water Polarity and Hydrogen Bonding

A water molecule has one oxygen atom and two hydrogen atoms. Oxygen pulls shared electrons more strongly, so the oxygen side is slightly negative and the hydrogen sides are slightly positive. This uneven charge is called polarity.

Water Polarity Diagram

H(delta+) - O(delta-) - H(delta+)

The slightly positive hydrogen side of one water molecule can attract the slightly negative oxygen side of another water molecule. That attraction is a hydrogen bond.

Why Water Properties Matter

Water PropertyCauseLife Connection
CohesionWater molecules attract each other.Helps water move as a connected column in plants.
Solvent abilityPolarity attracts charged or polar substances.Helps transport dissolved materials in cells and blood.
Temperature stabilityHydrogen bonds absorb and release heat.Helps organisms maintain stable conditions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Water is important only because organisms drink it."

Correct: "Water is important because its polarity and hydrogen bonding help dissolve substances, move materials, and stabilize temperature."

Teachable Explanation: Biology explanations should connect water's structure to its function in living systems.
