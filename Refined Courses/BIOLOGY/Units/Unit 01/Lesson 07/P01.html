BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 07

P01 Lesson Overview

Molecules and Water as Foundations for Life

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.01: Describe macromolecules and water properties that support life processes.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, and nucleic acids support living things. You will also learn why water's polarity and hydrogen bonding make water important for cells.

What You Will Do

  • Complete a macromolecule structure-function chart.
  • Analyze a water polarity and hydrogen bonding diagram.
  • Use molecule models to explain biological function.
  • Write evidence-based explanations about molecules and life.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by interpreting molecule and water models, completing notebook evidence, guided practice, and a checkpoint response with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Cells are made of matter. To understand life, you need to understand the molecules that build cells and the water properties that help cells maintain conditions for life.
