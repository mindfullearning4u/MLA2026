Laboratory Question

How do water's polarity and temperature stability help water support life, and how can structure and function evidence identify biological macromolecules?

This is a course-contained virtual laboratory. All simulated trials, observations, and data are provided on P03. No physical materials, website, login, or outside source is required.

What You Will Do

  1. Complete the background notes and prediction.
  2. Follow the simulated procedure one trial at a time.
  3. Record solubility observations and temperature data in provided tables.
  4. Calculate temperature change and construct a bar graph.
  5. Use molecular structure-function cards to classify macromolecules.
  6. Write a CER laboratory conclusion using exact data.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by interpreting molecule and water models, completing notebook evidence, guided practice, and a checkpoint response with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Cells are made of matter. To understand life, you need to understand the molecules that build cells and the water properties that help cells maintain conditions for life.
