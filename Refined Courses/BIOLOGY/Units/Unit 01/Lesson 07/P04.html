Investigation Evidence Is Included Here

Question: Which combination of cell observations, water-property evidence, and macromolecule functions best explains why cells can carry out life processes?

Claim being tested: Cells depend on water and carbon-based molecules for boundaries, transport, energy use, biological work, and information storage.
