Laboratory Checkpoint Task

Question: How do the simulation results show that water's properties support life?

Write a six- to eight-sentence CER conclusion. Your evidence must include one comparison from Data Table A and both temperature-change values from Data Table B. Your reasoning must connect polarity to particle dispersion and hydrogen bonding to temperature stability. End with one sentence explaining how these properties support cells or organisms.

Notebook Evidence Submission

  • Background vocabulary and macromolecule chart.
  • Prediction, Data Tables A and B, and both calculations.
  • Two-bar graph with title, labeled axes, scale, and values.
  • Completed P06 Parts A, B, and C.
  • Final CER laboratory conclusion.
