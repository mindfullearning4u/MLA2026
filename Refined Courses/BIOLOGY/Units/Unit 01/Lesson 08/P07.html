Checkpoint Submission

Submit the notebook evidence and final synthesis response in the required course location.

Mastery Criteria

To meet mastery, your checkpoint must earn at least 80% and show:

  • Correct identification of investigation question, variables, and controls.
  • Accurate use of measured data.
  • Clear connection to tool evidence, cell theory, and molecules/water.
  • A complete CER conclusion.

If your work does not meet mastery, complete Teacher of Record intervention when required and resubmit corrections through the required workflow.
