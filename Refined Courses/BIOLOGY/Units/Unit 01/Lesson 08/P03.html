Cell and Molecule Connections

Cell theory connection: A microscope image of algae cells supports that living things are made of cells.

Molecule connection: Living cells need water and molecules. Water's polarity helps dissolve and move materials, and macromolecules help cells store energy, build structures, do chemical work, and store genetic information.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The answer is fertilizer because Lesson 05 was about sources."

Correct: "The fertilizer runoff explanation is better supported because nitrate rose from 2 mg/L to 12 mg/L and algae coverage rose from 10% to 45%, while temperature changed only 1 C."

Teachable Explanation: Synthesis still requires evidence. Do not name a lesson topic as proof; use the data, model, or source detail.
