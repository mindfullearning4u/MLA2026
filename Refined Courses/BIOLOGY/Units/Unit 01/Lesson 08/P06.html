BIOLOGY | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Use the same pond-water scenario from P02-P04. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. This is independent synthesis work, so every answer must use evidence from the lesson pages.

Part A: Match the Skill

For each item, write the matching Unit 1 skill: variables, safety, data quality, tools, source reliability, cell theory, or molecules/water.

  1. Nitrate increased from 2 mg/L to 12 mg/L.
  2. The same pond location was sampled before and after rain.
  3. A microscope image showed many small algae cells.
  4. A sales website made a claim but gave no data.

Part B: Explain the Evidence

Choose two items from Part A. For each one, explain why it is useful evidence or why it improves the investigation.

Part C: Write a Synthesis CER

Write one CER paragraph answering this question: Did nutrient runoff likely affect the pond sample? Include at least two measured data points and one connection to either cells or molecules/water.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record if you completed Parts A and B but cannot organize Part C into claim, evidence, and reasoning.

Use the CER model from P04 before requesting support.
