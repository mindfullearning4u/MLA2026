Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: combining multiple ideas or evidence sources into one explanation.
  • Inquiry: asking testable questions and using evidence to answer them.
  • Data quality: how accurate, precise, organized, and useful data are.
  • Tool evidence: information gathered using tools such as microscopes, diagrams, or measuring devices.
  • Unit claim: a conclusion that uses evidence from more than one Unit 1 skill.

Mixed Stimulus Scenario

A student studies pond water after a heavy rain. The question is: Did nutrient runoff affect the pond ecosystem sample?

Unit 1 SkillStimulus
Inquiry and variablesCompare nitrate level and algae coverage before and after rain.
Safety and data qualityUse provided data only; no field collection or wet lab is required.
Tools and modelsMicroscope image shows many small living cells in the sample.
Sources and argumentA source with measured nutrient data is stronger than an opinion post.
