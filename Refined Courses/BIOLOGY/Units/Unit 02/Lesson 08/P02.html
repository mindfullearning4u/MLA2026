First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Identify the biological level shown: molecule, organelle, cell, tissue, organ, or system.
  2. Step 2: Interpret each diagram or graph before connecting across levels.
  3. Step 3: Link structure to function, transport to regulation, and regulation to health or organism function.
  4. Step 4: Test the explanation on a new case and state what the evidence cannot establish.
