First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Identify the substance and compare its concentration on both sides of the membrane.
  2. Step 2: Draw the net movement arrow from high to low concentration unless energy drives movement against the gradient.
  3. Step 3: Check whether the substance crosses the bilayer, uses a channel, or uses an ATP-powered pump.
  4. Step 4: Name the transport process and predict how the movement affects cell water or solute balance.
