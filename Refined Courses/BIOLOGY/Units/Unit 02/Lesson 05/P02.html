First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Trace water from soil into root hairs by osmosis.
  2. Step 2: Follow water and minerals upward through xylem to leaves.
  3. Step 3: At stomata, connect carbon dioxide entry with water-vapor loss and guard-cell regulation.
  4. Step 4: Trace sugars made in leaves through phloem to growing or storage tissues.
