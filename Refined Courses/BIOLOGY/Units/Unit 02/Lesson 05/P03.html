Continue the Teaching Sequence

Now deepen the idea by separating what the evidence shows from what the evidence means.

  1. Step 1: Locate one detail in the model, table, graph, or scenario.
  2. Step 2: Restate that detail as evidence using exact language.
  3. Step 3: Explain the biological meaning of the detail.
  4. Step 4: Decide whether the evidence supports, weakens, or is unrelated to the claim.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The model shows everything about plant structures and physiological."

This is too broad. A model only shows selected parts of a system.

Correct: "The model shows one relationship: how a structure, condition, or process connects to the function being studied."

Teachable Explanation: A strong science answer names the specific feature in the model and explains what it can and cannot show.
