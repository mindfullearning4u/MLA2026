First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Locate the organelle in the cell model.
  2. Step 2: Describe a visible structural feature before naming its function.
  3. Step 3: Connect the organelle’s function to a whole-cell need.
  4. Step 4: Predict what accumulates, decreases, or fails when the organelle malfunctions.
