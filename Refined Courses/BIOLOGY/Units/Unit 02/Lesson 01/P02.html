Vocabulary You Will Use

TermStudent-Friendly Meaning
prokaryotic cella cell without a nucleus or other membrane-bound organelles
eukaryotic cella cell with a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles
nucleoidthe non-membrane-bound region containing prokaryotic DNA
cell walla rigid outer layer that supports and protects some cells
chloroplasta plant organelle that captures light energy
central vacuolea large plant compartment that stores water and supports pressure

First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: First locate DNA: a nucleus means eukaryotic; a nucleoid without a nucleus means prokaryotic.
  2. Step 2: If the cell is eukaryotic, look for a cell wall, chloroplasts, and a large central vacuole to identify a plant cell.
  3. Step 3: Identify shared structures—membrane, cytoplasm, DNA, and ribosomes—before unique structures.
  4. Step 4: Classify with visible structural evidence, then connect at least one structure to its function.
