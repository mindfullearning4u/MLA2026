First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Trace a nervous pathway from receptor to processing center to motor response.
  2. Step 2: Trace blood through heart, arteries, capillaries, and veins while identifying material exchange.
  3. Step 3: Separate barriers, rapid nonspecific responses, and specific immune responses.
  4. Step 4: Explain how systems cooperate—for example, circulation transports oxygen and immune cells to tissues.
