Worked Example 1: Match system to function

Problem: Which system transports oxygen from the lungs to body cells?

  1. Step 1: Identify the material: oxygen. Why: the material tells you what needs transport.
  2. Step 2: Identify the transport pathway: blood. Why: oxygen travels through blood.
  3. Step 3: Choose cardiovascular system. Why: heart and vessels move blood.

Interpretation: Transport of materials is a cardiovascular function.

Worked Example 2: Analyze immune response

Problem: A model shows white blood cells recognizing a virus. Which system is represented?

  1. Step 1: Identify the structure: white blood cells. Why: cell type is the model clue.
  2. Step 2: Identify the job: recognizing a pathogen. Why: pathogen defense is immune function.
  3. Step 3: Choose immune system. Why: both structure and function match.

Interpretation: Immune models focus on defense and recognition.

Worked Example 3: Connect brain function

Problem: A student touches a hot surface and quickly pulls away. What role does the brain/nervous system play?

  1. Step 1: Identify the signal: heat/pain information. Why: the nervous system processes signals.
  2. Step 2: Identify the response: movement away. Why: coordination produces response.
  3. Step 3: Connect control to action. Why: the system helps the body respond to stimuli.

Interpretation: Control and coordination are nervous system functions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The immune system pumps blood because both are inside the body."

Correct: "The cardiovascular system pumps and transports blood; the immune system helps recognize and respond to pathogens."

Teachable explanation: Do not group systems by location. Match each system to its structures and job.
