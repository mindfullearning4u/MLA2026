P01 Virtual Laboratory Overview

Health, Environment, Pathogens, and Immunity

Lesson Title: Virtual Lab - Pathogen Transmission and Immune Response

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.CEL.05: Explain biological health relationships among genetics, environment, pathogens, immunity, prevention, detection, and treatment.
  • SC.912.L.14.6: Genetic, environmental, and pathogenic health factors.
  • HE.912.C.1.3: Evaluate environment and personal health relationships.
  • HE.912.C.1.5: Analyze prevention, detection, and treatment of communicable and chronic diseases.
  • HE.912.C.1.7: Analyze heredity and family history impacts on personal health.

What You Will Learn

Explain biological health factors using evidence.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Health case data or immune response diagram.
  • Use the lesson tables, diagrams, and models to explain health factor/pathogen/immunity evidence case analysis.
  • Practice writing evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This standard asks you to use biological structures, models, data, and evidence to explain how living systems work. The visuals are included so you can see the relationships instead of guessing.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Required direct resource links are included when a free, student-safe resource supports the lesson. Use the exact lesson link provided so your evidence matches the lesson.

Laboratory Question and Boundaries

How do prevention conditions affect simulated pathogen transmission, and how does prior immune exposure affect a simulated antibody response?

This course-contained virtual lab uses fictional population data. It does not ask for personal health information, vaccination status, family medical history, or medical decisions. Do not use the activity to diagnose or treat any condition.

Required: record both data tables, calculate transmission percentages, construct the antibody-response graph, answer P06, and submit the P07 CER conclusion. Optional: the free CDC and OpenStax readings.
