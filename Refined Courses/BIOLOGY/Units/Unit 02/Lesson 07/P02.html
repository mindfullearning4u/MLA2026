Vocabulary

  • Pathogen: organism or particle that can cause disease.
  • Transmission: movement of a pathogen from one host or place to another.
  • Immunity: ability to recognize and respond to pathogens.
  • Risk factor: condition or behavior that changes the chance of a health outcome.
  • Evidence-based health claim: health statement supported by data or reliable biological evidence.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Health claims in biology must be evaluated with evidence. Pathogens can spread through air, water, contact, vectors, or contaminated surfaces. The immune system responds by recognizing invaders and producing targeted defenses. Environmental factors and behaviors can change risk, but a claim must be supported by data, not fear or rumor.
