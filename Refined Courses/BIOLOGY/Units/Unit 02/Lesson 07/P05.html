📘 BIOLOGY | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P05 Guided Practice

Cell Transport, Homeostasis, and Health Investigation

What You Will Practice

The five Moodle questions use actual cell transport, homeostasis, and health investigation diagrams, data, and scenarios. They check whether you can:

  1. Station A: classify actual displayed cell models using nucleus, wall, chloroplast, and nucleoid evidence.
  2. Station B: compare solute concentrations, predict osmosis, and calculate red-blood-cell volume change.
  3. Station C: graph stomatal or transpiration data and connect the trend to plant water balance.
  4. Station D: calculate transmission and interpret first- versus second-exposure antibody graphs before writing a multi-source conclusion.
  5. Transfer the idea to a new but comparable example.

Before You Begin

Keep P02–P04 open. For every question: (1) read the visual or table title, (2) identify labels and units, (3) state the pattern, (4) choose the answer whose biological explanation matches the evidence. Feedback explains why each option is correct or incorrect.

Notebook: Correct every missed item by writing the evidence detail and the biological reason.

Teacher of Record Support

Help is available. If feedback does not resolve why an answer is incorrect, contact your Teacher of Record and share the exact step, label, value, or relationship that is unclear. Your Teacher of Record can clarify the idea and help you plan the next attempt.
