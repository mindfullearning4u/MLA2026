P01 Lesson Overview

Homeostasis in Cells and Organisms

Lesson Title: Homeostasis in Cells and Organisms

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.CEL.03: Explain cell membranes, selective transport, and homeostasis at the cellular level.
  • MLA.BIO.CEL.04: Relate plant, animal, and human system structures to biological functions and regulation.

What You Will Learn

Connect transport and regulation to homeostasis.

The lesson teaches the idea step by step, shows how to use evidence, and prepares you for guided practice, independent work, checkpoint submission, and quiz mastery.

What You Will Do

  • Read the standards and vocabulary before starting the work.
  • Use the included model, table, graph, or scenario as evidence.
  • Explain your reasoning in complete science sentences.
  • Complete guided practice, independent notebook work, and the checkpoint.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by completing notebook evidence, guided practice, a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint, and the lesson quiz. Mastery means you can use the correct science vocabulary, cite evidence, and explain why the evidence supports the answer.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson connects the Biology standard to the work you will actually do. You are not memorizing isolated terms. You are learning how to use evidence, models, and data to explain homeostasis in cells and in a way that can be checked.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Required data, models, or source excerpts are included directly in the lesson or Moodle assessment item when needed.

Need Help?

Contact your Teacher of Record after you have used the lesson steps, examples, and checks if you still need clarification.

Ask for help if you can locate the evidence but cannot explain how it supports the lesson standard.
