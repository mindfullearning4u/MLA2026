P01 Lesson Overview

Homeostasis in Cells and Organisms

Standards and Student Target

Primary: MLA.BIO.CEL.03; MLA.BIO.CEL.04

Supporting: ELA.K12.EE.1.1; MA.K12.MTR.6.1

I can: Connect transport and regulation to homeostasis.

Look Back

Lesson 3 showed how membranes move water and solutes. Homeostasis uses those movements and feedback to keep conditions within workable ranges.

Why This Matters

Temperature, blood glucose, and water balance must be regulated continuously for cells to function.
