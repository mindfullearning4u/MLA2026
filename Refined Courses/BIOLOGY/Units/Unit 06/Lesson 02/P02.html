Vocabulary

  • Succession: gradual ecosystem change after disturbance or new habitat formation.
  • Primary succession: succession where soil is not present at first.
  • Secondary succession: succession after disturbance where soil remains.
  • Seasonal change: predictable short-term environmental change.
  • Climate change: long-term shift in climate patterns.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Ecosystems change over time. Some changes are seasonal and repeat each year. Some are disturbances such as fires, storms, or floods. Succession describes how communities change after a disturbance or on new surfaces. Climate change involves long-term shifts that can alter habitat conditions and species distributions.
