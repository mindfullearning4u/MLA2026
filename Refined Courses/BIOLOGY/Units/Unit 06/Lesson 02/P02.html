Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Identify the disturbance, timescale, and whether soil remains.
  2. Step 2: Sequence colonization and changing abundance without assuming a fixed final endpoint.
  3. Step 3: Separate repeating seasonal cycles from long-term directional trends.
  4. Step 4: Use before/after data to predict distribution change and state alternative explanations.
