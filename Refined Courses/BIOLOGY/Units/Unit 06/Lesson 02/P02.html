Notebook Title

Ecosystem Change and Succession

Copy the title into your notebook. Leave room for vocabulary, model notes, a data/evidence table, and a short explanation.

Vocabulary

  • Evidence: a specific observation, number, model detail, or source statement that supports a scientific answer.
  • Model: a diagram, table, process drawing, or simplified representation used to explain a biological system.
  • Function: what a structure, process, or relationship does in a living system.
  • Reasoning: the science explanation that connects evidence to the answer.

Teaching Sequence

Start with the lesson question: How does evidence help explain ecosystem change and succession?

  1. Step 1: Name the structure, process, or relationship being studied. This prevents vague answers.
  2. Step 2: Identify the evidence source: model, data table, graph, scenario, or text evidence.
  3. Step 3: Explain what the evidence shows. Use exact terms from the standard.
  4. Step 4: Connect the evidence to the biological function or process.
