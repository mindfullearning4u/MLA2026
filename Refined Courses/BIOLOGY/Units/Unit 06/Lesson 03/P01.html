Lesson Title: Population Size, Limiting Factors, and Carrying Capacity

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.ECO.02: Analyze population size, carrying capacity, limiting factors, food webs, trophic energy transfer, and ecosystem interdependence.
  • SC.912.L.17.5: Population size, limiting factors, and carrying capacity.
  • MA.K12.MTR.7.1: Apply mathematics to real-world contexts.

What You Will Learn

Analyze population change using births, deaths, immigration, emigration, and limiting factors.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Population graph/scenario.
  • Use the visual/data display embedded in this lesson for this investigation: Population growth/carrying capacity data investigation.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use ecosystem data to explain organism distribution, population patterns, food web relationships, biodiversity, sustainability, monitoring, and human impact. Required visuals are included so you can reason from evidence instead of guessing.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Optional direct resource links may be included for further understanding. They are free, require no course submission, and do not replace the evidence displayed inside this lesson.
