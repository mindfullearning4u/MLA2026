Part C: Explain and Transfer

Write one concise, complete CER response that answers: How does the displayed evidence support the central idea of Food Webs and Energy Transfer? Include two different evidence details, the mechanism connecting them, and one boundary or limitation. Do not add a required sentence merely to make the response longer.
