BIOLOGY | Unit 06 | Lesson 08

P01 Lesson Overview

Putting It All Together

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary standard(s): MLA.BIO.ECO.01, MLA.BIO.ECO.02, MLA.BIO.ECO.03, MLA.BIO.ENE.05

Support standard(s): ELA.K12.EE.1.1, MA.K12.MTR.6.1

What You Will Learn

Synthesize ecosystems, populations, food webs, biodiversity, resources, monitoring, sustainability, and human impact.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Unit-level mixed stimuli from taught lessons.
  • Use the required visual/data display for this investigation: Unit synthesis across ecosystems, populations, food webs, biodiversity, resources, and monitoring.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you use ecosystem data to explain organism distribution, population patterns, food web relationships, biodiversity, sustainability, monitoring, and human impact. Required visuals are included so you can reason from evidence instead of guessing.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Required direct resource links are included when a free, student-safe resource supports the lesson. Use the exact lesson link provided so your evidence matches the lesson.
