BIOLOGY | Unit 06 | Lesson 08

P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue the Teaching Sequence

A strong Unit 6 synthesis response names the evidence source, explains the ecological relationship, and connects it to a human impact or decision when appropriate. It must stay inside the data instead of making broad claims that the evidence does not support.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the ecology concept, relationship, trend, or decision in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the map, graph, data table, food web, energy pyramid, timeline, or monitoring display. Why: ecology answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence and include any limit. Why: mastery requires careful reasoning, not overclaiming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Ecology questions can be answered with opinions because they involve human decisions."

Correct: "Ecology decisions should be supported by ecosystem evidence, data trends, costs, benefits, and clear limits."

Teachable explanation: Human-impact questions still require scientific evidence and reasoning.
