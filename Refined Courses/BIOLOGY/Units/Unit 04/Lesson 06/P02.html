  • SC.912.L.16.17: Mitosis/meiosis comparison and genetic variation consequences.

Vocabulary

  • Mitosis: cell division producing genetically identical body cells.
  • Meiosis: cell division producing gametes with half the chromosome number.
  • Gamete: reproductive cell such as sperm or egg.
  • Crossing over: exchange of DNA between homologous chromosomes.
  • Genetic variation: differences in genetic information among individuals.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Mitosis supports growth, repair, and asexual cell replacement by producing genetically identical cells. Meiosis produces gametes for sexual reproduction. Meiosis reduces chromosome number by half and creates variation through crossing over and independent assortment.
