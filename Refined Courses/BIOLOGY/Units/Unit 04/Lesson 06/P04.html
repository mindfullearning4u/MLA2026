Worked Example 1: Identify mitosis

Problem: A process makes two identical body cells for growth. Which process is shown?

  1. Step 1: Identify product: body cells. Why: product type is evidence.
  2. Step 2: Notice identical cells. Why: mitosis maintains genetic information.
  3. Step 3: Name mitosis. Why: mitosis supports growth and repair.

Interpretation: Mitosis makes identical body cells.

Worked Example 2: Identify meiosis

Problem: A process makes gametes with half the chromosome number. Which process is shown?

  1. Step 1: Identify product: gametes. Why: meiosis makes reproductive cells.
  2. Step 2: Notice chromosome number is reduced. Why: gametes need half the number.
  3. Step 3: Name meiosis. Why: meiosis creates gametes for sexual reproduction.

Interpretation: Meiosis makes haploid gametes.

Worked Example 3: Explain variation

Problem: How does crossing over increase variation?

  1. Step 1: Identify homologous chromosomes. Why: crossing over occurs between paired chromosomes.
  2. Step 2: Describe DNA exchange. Why: pieces are swapped.
  3. Step 3: Connect to new allele combinations. Why: exchanged DNA creates variation.

Interpretation: Crossing over creates new chromosome combinations.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Mitosis and meiosis both make identical body cells."

Correct: "Mitosis makes genetically identical body cells, while meiosis makes varied gametes with half the chromosome number."

Teachable explanation: The products and purpose of the two processes are different.
