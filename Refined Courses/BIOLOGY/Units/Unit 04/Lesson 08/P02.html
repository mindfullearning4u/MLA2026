Vocabulary

  • Synthesis: connecting multiple taught ideas with evidence.
  • Genetic information: DNA instructions that can be copied and expressed.
  • Inheritance: passing genetic information from parents to offspring.
  • Variation: genetic differences produced by mutation, recombination, or inheritance.
  • Application: using genetics knowledge in biotechnology or health decisions.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Unit 4 connects genetic information to inheritance and health. DNA is copied by replication. Genes can be expressed as proteins. Mutations can change DNA and sometimes phenotype. Inheritance models predict possible allele combinations. Meiosis produces variation in gametes. Biotechnology applies genetics knowledge to real cases.
