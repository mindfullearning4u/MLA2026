TermStudent-Friendly Meaning

First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Label nucleotide parts and the antiparallel DNA strands at the lesson’s required depth.
  2. Step 2: Separate strands and apply A–T and C–G pairing to each template.
  3. Step 3: Track parental and new strands through the replication fork.
  4. Step 4: Check the copy for mismatches and explain how semiconservative replication preserves sequence information.
