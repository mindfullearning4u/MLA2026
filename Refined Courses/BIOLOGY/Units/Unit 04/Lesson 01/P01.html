BIOLOGY | Unit 04 | Lesson 01

P01 Lesson Overview

DNA Replication and Genetic Information

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary standard(s): MLA.BIO.GEN.02

Support standard(s): SC.912.L.16.3, ELA.K12.EE.2.1

What You Will Learn

Explain DNA replication and conservation of genetic information.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze DNA model/replication diagram.
  • Use tables, diagrams, graphs, and process models for this investigation: DNA replication model analysis.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you explain how genetic information is copied, expressed, changed, inherited, or applied. Required visuals are included so you can use evidence instead of guessing from vocabulary alone.
