P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Continue the Teaching Sequence

Identify the pattern from evidence, not from the trait name. Ask what happens in the heterozygote, how many alleles are involved in the population, whether the pattern follows sex chromosomes, and whether the phenotype shows a continuous range.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the genetics process, pattern, model, or case claim in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the sequence, codon table, Punnett square, diagram, or case table. Why: genetics answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence. Why: mastery requires reasoning, not just naming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Every inheritance problem can be solved as simple dominant and recessive."

Correct: "Some traits follow non-Mendelian patterns, so the evidence must be checked before choosing a model."

Teachable explanation: Using the wrong inheritance model leads to wrong predictions.
