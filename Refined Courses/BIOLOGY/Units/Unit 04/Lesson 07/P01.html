P01 Lesson Overview

Genetics, Cell Division, and Biotechnology Investigation

Standards and Student Target

Primary: MLA.BIO.GEN.01; MLA.BIO.GEN.02; MLA.BIO.GEN.03; MLA.BIO.GEN.04; MLA.BIO.CEL.05; MLA.BIO.LAB.03

Supporting: SC.912.L.16.8; SC.912.L.16.10; SC.912.L.16.13; ACT Science

I can: Conduct a self-contained case investigation using DNA, mutation, inheritance, cell division, biotechnology, and health evidence.

Look Back

Lessons 1–6 traced genetic information from copying to expression, mutation, inheritance, and cell division.

Why This Matters

Genetic evidence can inform decisions, but probability, controls, privacy, consent, and model limits matter.

Your Learning Path

  1. Learn the vocabulary and read the labeled model.
  2. Follow the teaching sequence and complete the guided check.
  3. Study three fully solved examples through the investigation analysis sequence.
  4. Complete Guided Practice, independent work, and the checkpoint.

Mastery evidence: You can interpret a new visual or data set and explain the relevant biological relationship accurately.

Look Ahead

Next, you will synthesize how information is copied, expressed, inherited, varied, and applied responsibly.
