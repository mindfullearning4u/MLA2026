Notebook Title: Biotechnology, Reproduction, Cell Cycle, and Health

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.GEN.03: Explain mitosis, meiosis, cell cycle regulation, reproduction, and their effects on genetic variation and health.
  • MLA.BIO.GEN.04: Evaluate biotechnology and its impacts on individuals, society, ethics, medicine, and the environment.
  • SC.912.L.16.8: Mutation, cell cycle, uncontrolled growth, and cancer.
  • SC.912.L.16.10: Biotechnology impacts, including medical and ethical issues.
  • SC.912.L.16.13: Human reproduction and development from fertilization to birth.

Vocabulary

  • Biotechnology: use of biological systems or molecules to solve problems.
  • Genetic engineering: changing genetic material for a purpose.
  • Cell cycle: ordered stages of cell growth and division.
  • Cancer: disease involving uncontrolled cell division.
  • Evidence-based evaluation: judging claims using data, benefits, risks, and limits.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Biotechnology applies genetics knowledge to real problems, such as producing medicines, comparing DNA evidence, or modifying organisms. Cell-cycle regulation matters because cells must divide in a controlled way. When control fails, uncontrolled division can contribute to cancer. Strong evaluations use case evidence, not fear or excitement alone.
