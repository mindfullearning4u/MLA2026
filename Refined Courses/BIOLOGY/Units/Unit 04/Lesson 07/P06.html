Opening Independent Work Workflow

Complete this work in your notebook before the checkpoint. Your answers must be self-contained. A reader should understand your thinking without asking you to explain it verbally.

Part A: Investigation Setup

Investigation Setup: Copy the investigation question: How can DNA, mutation, inheritance, and biotechnology evidence explain variation in a fictional genetics case? Then copy the claim being tested and identify the comparison focus: Evidence source being compared: DNA sequence, codon effect, Punnett square, cell division model, and biotechnology case data.

Part B: Data and Model Analysis

Data and Model Analysis: Choose two evidence rows from the data table. For each row, copy the exact observation or data and write one sentence explaining how it supports, weakens, or clarifies the claim.

Part C: Complete CER Response

CER Response: Write one complete CER response. Begin with this claim if the evidence supports it: Genetic information can be copied, expressed, changed, inherited, and analyzed using evidence from DNA and inheritance models. Include two evidence sentences, one reasoning sentence, and this limitation or a better one you can justify: The case is fictional and simplified, so students should not use personal family or medical information.

Notebook Evidence Language

Your notebook evidence should include complete sentences, labeled data or model evidence, and a clear science explanation. Do not submit only a copied definition or a single answer choice.
