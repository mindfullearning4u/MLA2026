Part A: Identify and Label

  1. Copy a simplified version of the displayed visual.
  2. Label the parts, conditions, axes, or stages named in P02.
  3. For Cases A–C, name the vocabulary concept that best matches the evidence.

Part B: Analyze

  1. For each case, quote or copy the exact value, label, structure, or relationship used.
  2. Complete at least two evidence rows by explaining what the evidence shows and what it means biologically.
  3. When numbers are provided, show the calculation and include units.

Part C: Explain and Transfer

Write one concise, complete CER response that answers: How does the displayed evidence support the central idea of Putting It All Together? Include two different evidence details, the mechanism connecting them, and one boundary or limitation. Do not add a required sentence merely to make the response longer.

What to Submit

  • Your labeled visual
  • Completed Case A–C analysis
  • Calculations with units, when present
  • Your evidence-based explanation

Teacher of Record Support

Help is available. If you need clarification before completing a case or calculation, contact your Teacher of Record and share the exact step, label, value, or relationship that is unclear. Your Teacher of Record can clarify the idea and help you plan the next attempt.
