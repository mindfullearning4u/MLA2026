P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.01: Describe the structures and biological functions of major macromolecules and the properties of water that support life.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.02: Explain enzyme function, activation energy, and environmental effects on enzyme activity.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.03: Explain ATP and energy transfer within cells.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.04: Identify the reactants, products, and functions of photosynthesis and cellular respiration and explain their interdependence.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.05: Analyze the movement of matter and energy through biological systems, including water and carbon cycles.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1: Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • MA.K12.MTR.6.1: Assess reasonableness of solutions.

Continue the Teaching Sequence

A Unit 3 synthesis answer must connect matter and energy carefully. Matter cycles through molecules and Earth systems. Energy flows and changes form as it is captured, stored, and transferred.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the process, molecule, condition, or cycle in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the graph, table, diagram, or model. Why: biology answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence. Why: mastery requires reasoning, not just naming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "ATP, enzymes, photosynthesis, and cycles are connected because they are all science words."

Correct: "Enzymes help reactions, ATP transfers usable energy, photosynthesis stores energy in glucose, respiration releases usable energy, and cycles move matter through systems."

Teachable explanation: Synthesis requires specific relationships between concepts, not a list of terms.
