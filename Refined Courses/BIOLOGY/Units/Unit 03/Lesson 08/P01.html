P01 Lesson Overview

Putting It All Together

Lesson Title: Putting It All Together

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.01: Describe the structures and biological functions of major macromolecules and the properties of water that support life.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.02: Explain enzyme function, activation energy, and environmental effects on enzyme activity.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.03: Explain ATP and energy transfer within cells.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.04: Identify the reactants, products, and functions of photosynthesis and cellular respiration and explain their interdependence.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.05: Analyze the movement of matter and energy through biological systems, including water and carbon cycles.
  • ELA.K12.EE.1.1: Cite evidence to explain and justify reasoning.
  • MA.K12.MTR.6.1: Assess reasonableness of solutions.

What You Will Learn

Synthesize molecules, water, enzymes, ATP, photosynthesis, respiration, and cycles.

What You Will Do

  • Analyze Unit-level mixed stimuli from taught lessons.
  • Use tables, diagrams, graphs, and process models for this investigation: Unit synthesis across enzymes, ATP, photosynthesis, respiration, and cycles.
  • Write evidence-based explanations independently using the lesson pages.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will complete notebook evidence, Moodle Guided Practice, independent work, and a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you explain how molecules, energy, reactions, and cycles support living systems. Required visuals are included so you can see the process instead of guessing from words alone.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

Optional direct resource links may be included for further understanding. They are free, require no course submission, and do not replace the evidence displayed inside this lesson.
