Notebook Title: Water, Macromolecules, and Life Processes

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.01: Describe the structures and biological functions of major macromolecules and the properties of water that support life.
  • SC.912.L.18.1: Molecular structures and functions of biological macromolecules.
  • SC.912.L.18.12: Water properties that support life.

Vocabulary

  • Macromolecule: large biological molecule needed by living things.
  • Carbohydrate: molecule often used for quick energy.
  • Lipid: molecule used for long-term energy storage and membranes.
  • Protein: molecule that performs cell work such as enzymes.
  • Water polarity: uneven charge distribution that helps water interact with other substances.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Life processes depend on water and macromolecules. Water's polarity helps dissolve and move many substances. Carbohydrates provide quick energy, lipids store energy and form membranes, proteins perform cell work, and nucleic acids store genetic information.
