P01 Lesson Overview

Water, Macromolecules, and Life Processes

Standards and Student Target

Primary: MLA.BIO.ENE.01

Supporting: SC.912.L.18.1; SC.912.L.18.12

I can: Connect water and macromolecules to biological function.

Look Back

Unit 2 showed that cells need membranes, transport proteins, genetic information, and usable energy. Those functions depend on water and carbon-based molecules.

Why This Matters

Hydration, nutrition labels, body temperature, medicine transport, and cell survival all depend on molecular structure.

Your Learning Path

  1. Learn the vocabulary and read the labeled model.
  2. Follow the teaching sequence and complete the guided check.
  3. Study three fully solved examples.
  4. Complete Guided Practice, independent work, and the checkpoint.

Mastery evidence: You can interpret a new visual or data set and explain the relevant biological relationship accurately.

Look Ahead

Next, you will examine proteins that act as enzymes and learn why their shape and conditions control reactions.
