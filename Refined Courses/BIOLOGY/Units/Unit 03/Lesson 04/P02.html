First, Read the Biology

How to read this visual: Name each labeled part or condition. Follow arrows only in their stated direction. Then connect the visible feature to a biological function or process.

Step-by-Step Teaching Sequence

  1. Step 1: Locate photosynthesis in chloroplasts and distinguish matter inputs from light energy.
  2. Step 2: Read the equation: 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂.
  3. Step 3: Track carbon from carbon dioxide into glucose and oxygen atoms into products.
  4. Step 4: Interpret rate data for increase, plateau, and a possible limiting factor without overclaiming.
