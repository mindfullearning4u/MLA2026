Vocabulary

  • ATP: molecule cells use to transfer usable energy.
  • ADP: lower-energy molecule that can be recharged to ATP.
  • Phosphate: chemical group involved in ATP energy transfer.
  • Energy transfer: movement of energy from one molecule/process to another.
  • Cellular work: cell activities that require energy.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

ATP acts like a rechargeable energy-transfer molecule. ATP can lose a phosphate and become ADP, releasing usable energy for cellular work. ADP can be recharged back into ATP using energy from food breakdown or light-driven processes.

ProcessModel ChangeEnergy Meaning
ATP -> ADP + phosphatePhosphate removedEnergy released for work
ADP + phosphate -> ATPPhosphate addedEnergy stored for later use
