P01 Lesson Overview

Energy, Enzymes, and Cycling Matter Investigation

Lesson Title: Energy, Enzymes, and Cycling Matter Investigation

Standards Covered in This Lesson

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.01: Relate water, carbon, macromolecules, and molecular structure to the functions of living systems.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.02: Explain enzyme function and how conditions affect biological reactions.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.03: Explain ATP and energy transfer in cells.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.04: Explain photosynthesis, cellular respiration, and the relationship between those processes.
  • MLA.BIO.ENE.05: Analyze movement of matter and energy through biological systems and biogeochemical cycles.
  • MLA.BIO.LAB.03: Analyze biological data, tables, graphs, models, and investigation results to support scientific explanations.

What You Will Learn

Conduct a self-contained investigation using enzyme data, photosynthesis and respiration models, ATP evidence, and carbon/water cycle data.

This is the unit investigation lesson. You will use unit evidence, data, models, and CER reasoning to show that you can apply the standards without searching across pages for directions.

What You Will Do

  • Read the standards and vocabulary before starting the work.
  • Use the included model, table, graph, or scenario as evidence.
  • Explain your reasoning in complete science sentences.
  • Complete the lab/data investigation sequence one page at a time.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by completing notebook evidence, guided practice, a Teacher of Record graded checkpoint, and the lesson quiz. Mastery means you can use the correct science vocabulary, cite evidence, and explain why the evidence supports the answer.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson connects the Biology standard to the work you will actually do. You are not memorizing isolated terms. You are learning how to use evidence, models, and data to explain energy enzymes and cycling in a way that can be checked.

Science Safety and Resource Note

This lesson uses safe data analysis, model interpretation, virtual investigation planning, observation evidence, or public-data reasoning. Do not perform any hands-on experiment, field collection, chemical use, specimen handling, heat/flame activity, sharp-tool activity, or outdoor investigation unless your Teacher of Record or school workflow gives specific safety clearance.

This Lesson 7 investigation is self-contained. All required directions, data, tables, and evidence prompts are included on these pages.
