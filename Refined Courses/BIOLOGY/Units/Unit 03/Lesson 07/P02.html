Notebook Title: Matter and Energy in Biogeochemical Cycles

Vocabulary

  • Biogeochemical cycle: movement of matter through living and nonliving parts of Earth.
  • Carbon cycle: movement of carbon through atmosphere, organisms, water, and Earth materials.
  • Water cycle: movement of water through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff, and organisms.
  • Reservoir: place where matter is stored.
  • Flux: movement of matter from one reservoir to another.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Biogeochemical cycles show matter moving through living and nonliving systems. In the carbon cycle, photosynthesis moves carbon dioxide into sugars, respiration returns carbon dioxide, and decomposition and combustion also move carbon. In the water cycle, water moves through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, runoff, groundwater, and living organisms.
