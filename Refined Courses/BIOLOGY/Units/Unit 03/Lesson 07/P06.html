Independent Work Self-Check

Before you submit this independent work, check that each answer connects directly to MLA.BIO.ENE.05; MLA.BIO.LAB.03. A complete response should name the science idea, point to the data, model, diagram, source detail, or observation used as evidence, and explain why that evidence supports the answer.

  • Step 1: Reread the task and underline the science word or relationship being tested.
  • Step 2: Add the specific evidence you used. Do not write only a general opinion or memory statement.
  • Step 3: Explain the reasoning in one clear sentence so another student could follow your thinking. If anything is unclear, contact your Teacher of Record.
  • Step 4: Check units, labels, arrows, categories, or vocabulary before moving to the checkpoint.

Required Virtual-Lab Analysis

Part D - Water-Cycle Data

  1. Between which two temperatures did evaporation increase the most?
  2. How much more water evaporated at 35 C than at 15 C?
  3. Describe the graph pattern using all three values.
  4. Explain why temperature can affect evaporation rate.

Part E - Carbon-Cycle Data

  1. Show all three net-change calculations.
  2. Which scenario added the most carbon to the atmosphere? Cite its value.
  3. Which scenario removed the most? Cite its value.
  4. Explain how photosynthesis and decomposition move the same carbon atoms between different reservoirs.
  5. State one limitation of using simplified flux data.
