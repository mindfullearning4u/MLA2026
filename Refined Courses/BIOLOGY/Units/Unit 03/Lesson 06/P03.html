Continue the Teaching Sequence

The processes are not identical opposites. Photosynthesis captures and stores energy; respiration releases usable energy. The matter relationship is connected, but energy does not simply cycle the same way matter does.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the process, molecule, condition, or cycle in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the graph, table, diagram, or model. Why: biology answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence. Why: mastery requires reasoning, not just naming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Photosynthesis and respiration are the same process in reverse."

Correct: "Photosynthesis stores energy in glucose; respiration releases usable energy from glucose, and their reactants/products are connected."

Teachable explanation: The matter relationship is linked, but the energy roles are different.
