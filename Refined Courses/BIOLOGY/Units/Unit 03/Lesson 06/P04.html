P04 Worked Example

What the Mapped Standards Require

  • MLA.BIO.ENE.04: Identify the reactants, products, and functions of photosynthesis and cellular respiration and explain their interdependence.
  • SC.912.L.18.9: Interrelated nature of photosynthesis and respiration.
  • MA.K12.MTR.5.1: Use patterns and structure to connect concepts.

Worked Example 1: Compare inputs and outputs

Problem: How are glucose and oxygen related to both processes?

  1. Step 1: Locate glucose and oxygen in photosynthesis products. Why: they are made by photosynthesis.
  2. Step 2: Locate them in respiration reactants. Why: respiration uses them.
  3. Step 3: Explain the connection. Why: products of one process can be reactants of another.

Interpretation: Matter from one process can feed another process.

Worked Example 2: Explain energy roles

Problem: How does energy differ in the two processes?

  1. Step 1: Photosynthesis captures light energy. Why: light powers sugar formation.
  2. Step 2: Respiration releases usable energy from glucose. Why: cells need ATP transfer.
  3. Step 3: Compare roles. Why: one stores energy; the other releases usable energy.

Interpretation: Energy flow must be explained, not just reactants.

Worked Example 3: Use a model

Problem: A model shows arrows from respiration products to photosynthesis reactants. What does that mean?

  1. Step 1: Identify products: carbon dioxide and water. Why: respiration produces them.
  2. Step 2: Identify photosynthesis reactants. Why: photosynthesis uses them.
  3. Step 3: Explain matter cycling. Why: atoms move between processes.

Interpretation: Matter cycles through connected biological processes.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Photosynthesis and respiration are the same process in reverse."

Correct: "Photosynthesis stores energy in glucose; respiration releases usable energy from glucose, and their reactants/products are connected."

Teachable explanation: The matter relationship is linked, but the energy roles are different.
