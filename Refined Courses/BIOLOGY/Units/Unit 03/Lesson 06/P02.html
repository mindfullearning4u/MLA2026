Notebook Title: Photosynthesis and Respiration as Interdependent Processes

Mapped Standards for This Lesson

Support Standards: SC.912.L.18.9; MA.K12.MTR.5.1

Vocabulary

  • Interdependent: connected so processes affect or support each other.
  • Producer: organism that can make sugars by photosynthesis.
  • Reactant/product relationship: products of one process can become reactants of another.
  • Matter cycling: atoms move through processes.
  • Energy flow: energy changes form and moves through systems.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Photosynthesis and cellular respiration are connected. Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and water to make glucose and oxygen. Respiration uses glucose and oxygen to release usable energy and produce carbon dioxide and water. Matter cycles between the processes, while energy flows from light to chemical energy to usable cellular energy.
