Continue the Teaching Sequence

To interpret enzyme data, find the highest activity value first. Then identify the pH or temperature at that point. Values far from the optimum usually show lower activity because the enzyme shape or active site no longer works as well.

Step-by-Step Reasoning Routine

  1. Name the process, molecule, condition, or cycle in the question. Why: this prevents guessing from topic words only.
  2. Find the evidence in the graph, table, diagram, or model. Why: biology answers must be supported by evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence to the mapped standard vocabulary. Why: the answer must stay inside the lesson scope.
  4. Explain the conclusion in one complete sentence. Why: mastery requires reasoning, not just naming.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "The enzyme works best at the highest pH because bigger numbers are better."

Correct: "The enzyme works best at the pH with the highest activity value."

Teachable explanation: Graphs and tables must be read by the measured result, not by assuming bigger x-axis values are better.
