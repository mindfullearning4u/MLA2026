Notebook Title: Cellular Respiration Inputs, Outputs, and Function

Vocabulary

  • Cellular respiration: process that releases usable energy from glucose.
  • Mitochondrion: organelle where much respiration occurs.
  • Glucose: sugar used as a reactant.
  • Oxygen: reactant used in aerobic respiration.
  • Carbon dioxide and water: products of cellular respiration.

Detailed Teaching Sequence

Cellular respiration uses glucose and oxygen to release energy that can be transferred to ATP. Carbon dioxide and water are produced. The function is energy release for cellular work, not food production.
