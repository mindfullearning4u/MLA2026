## Rigor and Mastery Review

Unit 6 lessons are written for an asynchronous mastery-based model. The lesson pages provide the teaching sequence directly to the student and do not rely on a classroom teacher lesson. Teacher of Record language is limited to clarification, intervention, checkpoint review, corrections, resubmission, and retake workflow.

The lessons require students to use embedded ecological evidence:

- aquatic data maps and abiotic factor tables
- succession timelines and ecosystem change graphs
- population growth and carrying capacity graphs
- food webs, energy pyramids, and trophic tables
- biodiversity and invasive species data tables
- renewable/nonrenewable resource comparison tables
- monitoring maps/data tables and policy evidence organizers
- mixed Unit 6 synthesis evidence sets

## Resource and Simulation Control

Candidate resources are listed in `lesson.json` for approval only. No external simulation or resource link was embedded into student lesson pages without approval. All lesson tasks remain understandable from the content included directly in the page.
