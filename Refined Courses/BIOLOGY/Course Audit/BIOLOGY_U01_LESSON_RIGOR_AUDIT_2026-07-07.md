## Rigor and Mastery Review

Unit 1 lessons are written for an asynchronous mastery-based model. The lesson pages provide the teaching sequence directly to the student and do not rely on a classroom teacher lesson. Teacher of Record language is limited to clarification, intervention, checkpoint review, corrections, resubmission, and retake workflow.

The lessons require students to use embedded scientific evidence:

- observations, inferences, claims, and CER organizers
- investigation design tables
- safety and measurement quality data
- microscope/tool models
- source reliability and scientific argument evidence
- cell theory evidence and model/theory comparison
- macromolecule and water structure-function evidence
- Unit 1 synthesis across inquiry, lab skills, evidence, cell theory, and molecules

## Resource and Simulation Control

Candidate resources are listed in `lesson.json` for approval only. No external simulation or resource link was embedded into student lesson pages without approval. All lesson tasks remain understandable from the content included directly in the page.

PASS

Biology Unit 1 lessons meet MLA lesson structure, standards alignment, step-by-step rigor, visual/data evidence, asynchronous Teacher of Record boundaries, safety/resource boundaries, and mastery workflow requirements.
