# Biology Phase 1B: Curriculum Architecture Analysis

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the Biology curriculum architecture for Mindful Learning Academy using the approved MLA mastery model, the certified English and Communication Department production framework, the certified Math Department audit discipline, and the approved six-unit, eight-lesson course structure.

This is architecture only. It does not create lessons, assessments, HTML content, GIFT content, quiz JSON content, lesson JSON content, LMS pages, or student-facing instructional lesson content.

## Approved Unit Architecture

| Unit | Unit Title | Architecture Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Scientific Thinking and Biology Foundations | Establish scientific inquiry, experimental design, lab safety, data analysis, scientific argumentation, cell theory foundations, microscopy, and biological macromolecule readiness. |
| Unit 2 | Cells, Homeostasis, and Transport | Develop cell structure and function, plant/animal and prokaryotic/eukaryotic comparisons, membrane transport, homeostasis, and selected human body system connections used in Biology 1. |
| Unit 3 | Cellular Energy and Life Processes | Develop water and enzymes, ATP, photosynthesis, cellular respiration, energy transfer, biogeochemical cycles, and plant physiological connections. |
| Unit 4 | DNA, Genetics, and Heredity | Develop DNA replication, transcription, translation, mutation, Mendelian and non-Mendelian inheritance, mitosis, meiosis, biotechnology, reproduction, and genetic health connections. |
| Unit 5 | Evolution, Classification, and Diversity of Life | Develop evidence for evolution, natural selection, mechanisms of evolutionary change, origin-of-life explanations, classification, domains, kingdoms, and biodiversity. |
| Unit 6 | Ecology, Interdependence, and Human Impact | Develop aquatic systems, ecosystem change, population dynamics, food webs, biodiversity loss, environmental monitoring, resource use, sustainability, and human impact. |

## Biology Course Boundaries

Biology is designed as a high-school laboratory science course centered on living systems and biological explanations. The architecture intentionally preserves boundaries with other science courses:

- Chemistry topics are included only as biological chemistry needed for macromolecules, water, enzymes, ATP, photosynthesis, respiration, DNA, and cellular processes.
- Physics topics are included only as needed for biological energy transfer, measurement, and data analysis.
- Earth/Space Science is included only where Biology 1 standards require biogeochemical cycles.
- Environmental Science is included only where Biology 1 ecology, sustainability, and human-impact benchmarks require it.
- Marine Science is included only where aquatic ecosystem distribution appears in Biology 1.
- Anatomy and Physiology is included only where CPALMS Biology 1 includes immune, reproductive, brain, blood-flow, plant organ, and health support standards.

## MLA Biology Standards Framework

MLA Biology standards use this numbering format:

`MLA.BIO.[DOMAIN].[NUMBER]`

Approved domains:

- `SCI` = Scientific Inquiry and Practices
- `LAB` = Laboratory and Data Skills
- `CEL` = Cells and Homeostasis
- `ENE` = Energy and Metabolism
- `GEN` = Genetics and Heredity
- `EVO` = Evolution and Diversity
- `ECO` = Ecology and Systems

## Architecture Controls

- Preserve the approved MLA mastery model.
- Preserve six units and eight lessons per unit.
- Preserve Lesson 8 as Putting It All Together in every unit.
- Preserve Unit Pretest, Lessons 1-7 quizzes, and Lesson 8 Unit Assessment structure.
- Keep scientific inquiry, lab safety, measurement, data analysis, and evidence-based argumentation embedded across the course.
- Use CPALMS Biology 1 standards as the official Florida source.
- Cross-reference Common Core Literacy in Science and Technical Subjects, ACT Science, SAT science reading/data expectations, college readiness, workforce readiness, and scientific literacy.
- Do not create student-facing instructional lesson content during architecture.
- Do not create or populate HTML, JSON, GIFT, lesson, quiz, or LMS content during architecture.
