# Algebra 1 Lesson Blueprint

Status: Architecture-level blueprint. Page-level Savvas identifiers remain pending.

## Required architecture for every lesson

1. Lesson Overview — purpose, prerequisite link, and Savvas source.
2. Learning Objectives — measurable objectives and provisional standards status.
3. Notebook Task — retrieval or prediction when instructionally useful.
4. Vocabulary/Notation — only terms required for the lesson.
5. Concept Instruction — linked Savvas eText/teacher resource; worked examples as needed.
6. Guided Practice or Exploration — equations, graphs, tables, modeling, or technology based on the objective.
7. Independent Application — student evidence aligned to the objective.
8. Checkpoint — mastery evidence; formative unless designated otherwise.
9. Help & TOR Support — misconception-specific support and escalation path.

Pages 1, 2, 8, and 9 are mandatory. Pages 3-7 are included only when instructionally necessary.
