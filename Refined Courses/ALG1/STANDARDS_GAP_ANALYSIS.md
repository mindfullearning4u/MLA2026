# Standards Gap and Redundancy Analysis

## Likely gaps or uncertain coverage

- Florida financial-literacy benchmarks in the current MLA map are not visible as a dedicated Savvas topic and may require approved supplemental integration.
- Existing MLA lessons for broad financial literacy and multi-step algebra modeling do not yet have direct Savvas lesson traceability.
- SAT/ACT readiness claims are broad and need skill-level evidence rather than domain labels alone.
- CPALMS alignment has not yet been verified against current course descriptions and benchmark clarifications.
- Unit 1 prerequisite lessons intentionally may carry supporting rather than primary Algebra 1 benchmarks.

## Redundancy risks

- Linear relationships appear across Topics 2, 3, 4, and 7; progression must be explicit.
- Function analysis appears in Topics 3, 7, 9, and 10; repeated standards should advance from introduction to comparison and modeling.
- Polynomial operations and factoring should not be duplicated as detached early review and later core instruction.

## Recommended action

Use small approved supplements for financial literacy or Florida-specific expectations; do not invent a substantial parallel curriculum. Flag uncertain crosswalk claims for academic review.
