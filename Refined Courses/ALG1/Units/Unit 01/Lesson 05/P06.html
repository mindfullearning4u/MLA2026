P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Identify Expression Parts

  1. For 8x + 11, identify the variable, coefficient, constant, and terms.
  2. For 4m - 9, identify the variable, coefficient, constant, and terms.
  3. For 12 + 5p, identify the coefficient and explain why 12 is not the coefficient.
  4. For 6(a + 3), identify the grouped quantity.

Part B: Interpret in Context

Use complete sentences. Your explanation should make the meaning of each expression part clear.

  1. A tutor charges $18 per hour plus a $5 sign-up fee. In 18h + 5, explain what h, 18h, and 5 represent.
  2. A school sells shirts for $12 each and collects a $20 setup fee. In 12s + 20, explain what s, 12s, and 20 represent.
  3. A taxi charges $3 per mile plus a $4 starting fee. Write an expression using m for miles, then explain each part.
  4. In 2(x + 7), explain why x + 7 can be viewed as one quantity.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student says the coefficient in 9x + 2 is 2 because 2 is a number.

    9x + 2

    Explain the mistake. Then identify the coefficient and constant correctly.
  2. A student says 5p means p + 5.

    5p

    Explain the misconception. Then explain the correct meaning of 5p.
