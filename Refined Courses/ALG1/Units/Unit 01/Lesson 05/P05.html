Question 1

Question ID:
ALG1_U01_L05_GP_Q01

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
In the expression 5x + 8, what is the coefficient?

Answer: 5

Feedback: The coefficient is the number multiplied by the variable. In 5x + 8, the 5 is multiplied by x.

Question 2

Question ID:
ALG1_U01_L05_GP_Q02

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
In the expression 7n + 3, what is the constant?

Answer: 3

Feedback: A constant is a number that stands alone. In 7n + 3, the constant is 3.

Question 3

Question ID:
ALG1_U01_L05_GP_Q03

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
A delivery company charges $6 per package plus a $10 fee. In 6p + 10, what does p represent?

Answer: the number of packages

Feedback: Since the company charges $6 for each package, p represents the number of packages.

Question 4

Question ID:
ALG1_U01_L05_GP_Q04

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
In 4(x + 2), which part can be viewed as one grouped quantity?

Answer: x + 2

Feedback: The parentheses show that x + 2 should be viewed as one quantity being multiplied by 4.

Question 5

Question ID:

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
Which expression has two terms?

Answer: 3a + 5

Feedback: Terms are separated by addition or subtraction. The expression 3a + 5 has the terms 3a and 5.
