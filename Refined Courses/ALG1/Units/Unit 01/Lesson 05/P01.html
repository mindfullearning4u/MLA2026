P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Variables, Expressions, and Algebraic Language

In this lesson, you will learn how variables, quantities, terms, factors, coefficients, and constants help describe mathematical and real-world situations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FND.4

I can interpret the meaning of parts of expressions and equations in mathematical and real-world contexts.

What You Will Learn

  • How variables represent unknown or changing quantities.
  • How to identify terms, factors, coefficients, and constants.
  • How parts of an expression can represent quantities in context.
  • How to view grouped parts of an expression as one meaningful quantity.

What You Will Do

  • Label parts of algebraic expressions.
  • Connect variables and coefficients to real-world meanings.
  • Interpret expressions in context without solving equations.
  • Explain common mistakes in algebraic language.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence that defines and labels expression parts.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that interpret expression parts in context.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.A1.FND.4: This lesson introduces the language of algebra so you can explain what each part of an expression represents.

In student language: I can identify and explain the parts of an expression and describe what they mean in a real-world situation.
