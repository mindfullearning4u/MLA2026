P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. The goal is to interpret what each part means, not to solve an equation.

Example 1: Label Expression Parts

Expression: 7x + 9

Variable: x

Coefficient: 7

Constant: 9

Terms: 7x and 9

Reasoning: The coefficient is multiplied by the variable. The constant stands alone.

Example 2: Interpret a Context

Context: A gym charges $20 per month plus a one-time joining fee of $15.

20m + 15

m: the number of months

20m: the monthly cost for m months

15: the one-time joining fee

20m + 15: the total cost

Example 3: View a Part as One Quantity

Expression: 3(x + 4)

Grouped quantity: x + 4

Meaning: The expression represents 3 groups of the quantity x + 4.

Reasoning: The parentheses show that x + 4 should be viewed as one part of the expression.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is saying a variable is the answer instead of the quantity being represented.

Context: A student earns $12 per hour. The expression 12h represents the total pay for h hours.

Incorrect:

h means the total pay.

Why it is wrong: h represents the number of hours, not the total pay.

Correct:

h means the number of hours, and 12h means the total pay.

Reasoning: The coefficient 12 gives the pay rate, and h tells how many hours are worked.
