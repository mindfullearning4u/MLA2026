Checkpoint Task: Variables, Expressions, and Algebraic Language

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. For the expression 14r + 6, identify the variable, coefficient, constant, and terms.
  2. A park charges $7 per person plus a $10 parking fee. In 7p + 10, explain what p, 7p, and 10 represent.
  3. In 4(x + 5), explain why x + 5 can be viewed as one quantity.
  4. A student says the coefficient in 11x + 4 is 4.

    11x + 4

    Explain the misconception. Correct the statement using the words coefficient and constant.
