Checkpoint Task: Explain and Apply Rational Exponents

Complete all parts. Show your steps and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where requested.

  1. Evaluate 81 to the power of 3/4, which means 813/4. Show the root step and the power step.
  2. Generate a radical expression equivalent to 12 to the power of 5/2, which means 125/2. Explain what the denominator and numerator mean.
  3. Use exponent laws to simplify 4 to the power of 1/3 times 4 to the power of 2/3, which means 41/3 × 42/3. Explain why the result is equivalent to the original expression.
  4. A student claims 32 to the power of 2/5, which means 322/5, is the same as 322 ÷ 5. Identify the misconception, correct the work, and explain the correct meaning of the rational exponent.

Mastery Criteria

  • Correctly interprets the denominator of a rational exponent as a root.
  • Correctly interprets the numerator of a rational exponent as a power.
  • Uses exponent laws accurately to generate an equivalent expression.
  • Shows algebraic steps clearly and explains the reasoning behind the steps.
  • Addresses the misconception without repeating the same error.
