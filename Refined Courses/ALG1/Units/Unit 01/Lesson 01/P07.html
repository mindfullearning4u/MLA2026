Checkpoint Task: Explain and Apply Rational Exponents

Complete all parts. Show your steps and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where requested.

  1. Evaluate 81^(3/4). Show the root step and the power step.
  2. Generate a radical expression equivalent to 12^(5/2). Explain what the denominator and numerator mean.
  3. Use exponent laws to simplify 4^(1/3) · 4^(2/3). Explain why the result is equivalent to the original expression.
  4. A student claims 32^(2/5) means 32^2 / 5. Identify the misconception, correct the work, and explain the correct meaning of the rational exponent.

Mastery Criteria

  • Correctly interprets the denominator of a rational exponent as a root.
  • Correctly interprets the numerator of a rational exponent as a power.
  • Uses exponent laws accurately to generate an equivalent expression.
  • Shows algebraic steps clearly and explains the reasoning behind the steps.
  • Addresses the misconception without repeating the same error.
