P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show each root step, power step, or exponent-law step. This work is Notebook Evidence.

Part A: Evaluate Rational Exponents

  1. Evaluate 81 to the power of 1/2, which means 811/2. Show why your answer is correct.
  2. Evaluate 216 to the power of 1/3, which means 2161/3. Show why your answer is correct.
  3. Evaluate 25 to the power of 3/2, which means 253/2. Show the root step and the power step.
  4. Evaluate 32 to the power of 2/5, which means 322/5. Show the root step and the power step.

Part B: Generate Equivalent Expressions

  1. Rewrite 13 to the power of 1/2, which means 131/2, using radical notation.
  2. Rewrite 6 to the power of 4/3, which means 64/3, using radical notation.
  3. Simplify 7 to the power of 2/5 times 7 to the power of 3/5, which means 72/5 × 73/5.
  4. Simplify 10 to the power of 9/4 divided by 10 to the power of 5/4, which means 109/4 / 105/4.

Part C: Explain the Meaning

  1. A cube has volume 125 cubic units. The side length can be represented by 125 to the power of 1/3, which means 1251/3. Evaluate the expression and explain why the exponent makes sense in this context.
  2. A student writes 64 to the power of 2/3, which means 642/3, as 64² / 3. Explain the mistake and show the correct evaluation.
