P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1: Choose the Operation

Problem: A student reads 12 pages each day for 4 days. How many pages does the student read?

Step 1: Identify the question. The problem asks for a total number of pages.

Step 2: Identify the structure. There are 4 equal groups of 12 pages.

12 × 4 = 48

Answer: The student reads 48 pages.

Reasoning: Equal groups are represented with multiplication.

Worked Example 2: Check Reasonableness

Problem: A backpack costs $38. A calculator costs $22. About how much do they cost together?

Step 1: Estimate before calculating.

38 ≈ 40 and 22 ≈ 20

40 + 20 = 60

Step 2: Calculate exactly.

38 + 22 = 60

Answer: The items cost $60 together.

Reasoning: The exact answer matches the estimate, so it is reasonable.

Worked Example 3: Explain a Correction

Problem: Evaluate 18 - 6 ÷ 3.

Incorrect:

18 - 6 ÷ 3 = 12 ÷ 3 = 4

Why it is wrong: The subtraction was completed before division. Division must be completed first.

Correct:

18 - 6 ÷ 3 = 18 - 2 = 16

Reasoning: Division comes before subtraction in the order of operations.

Common Mistake

A common readiness mistake is writing an answer without explaining the meaning.

Problem: A runner completes 3 miles each day for 5 days. How many miles does the runner complete?

Incorrect:

15

Why it is incomplete: The number is correct, but the response does not show the operation or explain what 15 represents.

Correct:

3 × 5 = 15, so the runner completes 15 miles.
