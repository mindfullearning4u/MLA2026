P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Question 1

A student has 14 completed assignments and 5 missing assignments. How many assignments are there in all?

Answer: 19 assignments

Feedback: The question asks for the total, so add the completed and missing assignments. Since 14 + 5 = 19, there are 19 assignments in all.

Question 2

Evaluate 7 + 4 × 3.

Answer: 19

Feedback: Multiplication comes before addition. First 4 × 3 = 12, then 7 + 12 = 19.

Question 3

A student buys 6 folders for $2 each. How much does the student spend?

Answer: $12

Feedback: The situation has 6 equal groups of $2, so multiply. Since 6 × 2 = 12, the student spends $12.

Question 4

A student estimates 29 + 41 as about 70. Is the estimate reasonable?

Answer: Yes, because 29 is close to 30 and 41 is close to 40.

Feedback: Rounding 29 to 30 and 41 to 40 gives 30 + 40 = 70. This estimate is close to the exact sum, so it is reasonable.

Question 5

A student solves 20 - 8 ÷ 4 as 3. What is the correct value?

Answer: 18

Feedback: The student likely subtracted before dividing. Division comes first: 8 ÷ 4 = 2. Then 20 - 2 = 18.
