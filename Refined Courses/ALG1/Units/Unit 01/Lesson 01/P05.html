Question 1

Evaluate 36 to the power of 1/2.

361/2

Answer: 6

Feedback: The exponent 1/2 means square root. Since 6² = 36, 361/2 = 6. This supports MA.912.NSO.1.1 because you are evaluating a numerical expression with a rational exponent.

Question 2

Evaluate 64 to the power of 1/3.

641/3

Answer: 4

Feedback: The exponent 1/3 means cube root. Since 4³ = 64, the cube root of 64 is 4.

Question 3

Evaluate 16 to the power of 3/4.

163/4

Answer: 8

Feedback: The denominator 4 means fourth root: fourth root of 16 = 2. The numerator 3 means third power: 2³ = 8.

Question 4

Simplify 3 to the power of 1/5 times 3 to the power of 4/5.

31/5 × 34/5

Answer: 3

Feedback: Same base means add exponents: 1/5 + 4/5 = 5/5 = 1. So 3¹ = 3.

Question 5

Which radical expression is equivalent to 11 to the power of 2/3?

112/3

Answer: (∛11)²

Feedback: The denominator 3 means cube root, and the numerator 2 means square. This generates an equivalent numerical expression involving a rational exponent.
