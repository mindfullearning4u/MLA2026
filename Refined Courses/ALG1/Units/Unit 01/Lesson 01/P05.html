P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Question 1

Evaluate 36^(1/2).

Answer: 6

Feedback: The exponent 1/2 means square root. Since 6^2 = 36, 36^(1/2) = 6. This supports MA.912.NSO.1.1 because you are evaluating a numerical expression with a rational exponent.

Question 2

Evaluate 64^(1/3).

Answer: 4

Feedback: The exponent 1/3 means cube root. Since 4^3 = 64, the cube root of 64 is 4.

Question 3

Evaluate 16^(3/4).

Answer: 8

Feedback: The denominator 4 means fourth root: fourthroot(16)=2. The numerator 3 means third power: 2^3=8.

Question 4

Simplify 3^(1/5) · 3^(4/5).

Answer: 3

Feedback: Same base means add exponents: 1/5 + 4/5 = 5/5 = 1. So 3^1 = 3.

Question 5

Which radical expression is equivalent to 11^(2/3)?

Answer: (cuberoot(11))^2

Feedback: The denominator 3 means cube root, and the numerator 2 means square. This generates an equivalent numerical expression involving a rational exponent.
