Question 1

Evaluate 36¹ᐟ².

Answer: 6

Feedback: The exponent ¹ᐟ² means square root. Since 6² = 36, 36¹ᐟ² = 6. This supports MA.912.NSO.1.1 because you are evaluating a numerical expression with a rational exponent.

Question 2

Evaluate 64¹ᐟ³.

Answer: 4

Feedback: The exponent ¹ᐟ³ means cube root. Since 4³ = 64, the cube root of 64 is 4.

Question 3

Evaluate 16³ᐟ⁴.

Answer: 8

Feedback: The denominator 4 means fourth root: fourth root of 16 = 2. The numerator 3 means third power: 2³ = 8.

Question 4

Simplify 3¹ᐟ⁵ × 3⁴ᐟ⁵.

Answer: 3

Feedback: Same base means add exponents: ¹ᐟ⁵ + ⁴ᐟ⁵ = ⁵ᐟ⁵ = 1. So 3¹ = 3.

Question 5

Which radical expression is equivalent to 11²ᐟ³?

Answer: (∛11)²

Feedback: The denominator 3 means cube root, and the numerator 2 means square. This generates an equivalent numerical expression involving a rational exponent.
