P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Rational Exponents and Equivalent Numerical Expressions

In this lesson, you will connect roots, powers, and exponent rules so you can evaluate numerical expressions and generate equivalent forms.

What You Will Learn

  • How whole-number exponents review repeated multiplication.
  • How a fractional exponent connects to a root.
  • How to evaluate numerical expressions with rational exponents.
  • How to generate equivalent forms using exponent laws.

What You Will Do

  • Review exponent laws and square roots/cube roots.
  • Write rational exponents in radical form.
  • Evaluate rational exponent expressions step by step.
  • Explain why two numerical expressions are equivalent.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice with all steps shown.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining and applying rational exponents.
  • Score at least 80% on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
