P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Algebra Readiness Diagnostic and Mathematical Thinking

In this lesson, you will begin Algebra 1 by checking your readiness skills and practicing the habits that help mathematicians work accurately, explain clearly, and correct mistakes.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

This lesson focuses on foundational readiness skills and mathematical habits that support future MLA standards throughout the course.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify strengths and gaps before new Algebra 1 skills begin.
  • How to read a math problem carefully and decide what it is asking.
  • How to organize work so another person can follow your reasoning.
  • How to check whether an answer is reasonable.

What You Will Do

  • Practice readiness problems using arithmetic, simple expressions, and reasoning.
  • Write clear notebook notes about mathematical thinking habits.
  • Analyze common mistakes and explain how to fix them.
  • Complete a checkpoint that shows how you think through a problem.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice with organized work shown.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining your reasoning, correction process, and readiness needs.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Readiness Support: This lesson does not introduce a new primary Algebra 1 benchmark. It prepares you for later standards by building the habits needed for accurate problem solving, organized work, and mathematical communication.

In student language: I can show how I think through a math problem, check my answer, and explain what I need to review before moving deeper into Algebra 1.
