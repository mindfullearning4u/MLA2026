📘 ALG1 | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Convert Between Forms

  1. Write 1⁄5 as a decimal and a percent.
  2. Write 0.45 as a percent.
  3. Write 70% as a decimal and a fraction over 100.
  4. Write 7⁄10 as a decimal and a percent.

Part B: Percents and Unit Rates

  1. Find 10% of 90.
  2. Find 30% of 50.
  3. A student answers 18 out of 20 questions correctly. Write the score as a percent.
  4. A bicyclist travels 36 miles in 3 hours. Find the unit rate in miles per hour.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student writes 6% = 0.6. Explain the mistake and write the correct decimal.
  2. A student says 40 pages in 5 hours is 35 pages per hour because 40 - 5 = 35. Explain the mistake and find the correct unit rate.
