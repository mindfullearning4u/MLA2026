P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1: Convert a Fraction to a Percent

Problem: Write 3⁄5 as a percent.

Step 1: Divide the numerator by the denominator.

3 ÷ 5 = 0.6

Step 2: Convert the decimal to a percent.

0.6 = 60%

Answer: 3⁄5 = 60%

Worked Example 2: Find a Percent of a Number

Problem: Find 15% of 80.

Step 1: Write 15% as a decimal.

15% = 0.15

Step 2: Multiply.

0.15 × 80 = 12

Answer: 15% of 80 is 12.

Worked Example 3: Find a Unit Rate

Problem: A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours. Find the unit rate.

Step 1: Divide miles by hours.

150 ÷ 3 = 50

Answer: The unit rate is 50 miles per hour.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is treating 8% as 0.8 instead of 0.08.

Incorrect:

8% = 0.8

Why it is wrong: 0.8 is 80%, not 8%.

Correct:

8% = 8⁄100 = 0.08

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
