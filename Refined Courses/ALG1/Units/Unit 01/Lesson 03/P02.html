Lesson 3: Fractions, Decimals, and Percent Relationships

Why These Forms Matter

Fractions, decimals, and percents can describe the same amount. Different forms are useful in different situations.

A test score may be written as a fraction, decimal, or percent.

3⁄4 = 0.75 = 75%

Vocabulary

  • Fraction: A number written as a numerator over a denominator.
  • Decimal: A number written using place value and a decimal point.
  • Percent: A number out of 100.
  • Ratio: A comparison of two quantities.
  • Unit rate: A rate for one unit.

Fractions, Decimals, and Percents

To write a fraction as a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator.

1⁄2 = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5

To write a decimal as a percent, multiply by 100 or move the decimal point two places to the right.

0.5 = 50%

Common Equivalents

  • 1⁄4 = 0.25 = 25%
  • 1⁄2 = 0.5 = 50%
  • 3⁄4 = 0.75 = 75%
  • 1⁄5 = 0.2 = 20%
