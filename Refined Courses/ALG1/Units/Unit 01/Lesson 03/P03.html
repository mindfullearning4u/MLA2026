Percent Means Out of 100

A percent compares a quantity to 100.

35% = 35⁄100 = 0.35

This means 35 out of every 100.

Finding a Percent of a Number

To find a percent of a number, first write the percent as a decimal.

Example: Find 20% of 45.

20% = 0.20

0.20 × 45 = 9

So 20% of 45 is 9.

Ratios and Unit Rates

A ratio compares two quantities. A unit rate tells how much for one unit.

Example: A student reads 60 pages in 3 hours.

60 pages ÷ 3 hours = 20 pages per hour

The unit rate is 20 pages per hour.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is changing a percent to a decimal by moving the decimal point only one place.

Incorrect:

25% = 2.5

Correct:

25% = 25⁄100 = 0.25

Why: Percent means out of 100, so 25% is twenty-five hundredths.

Teachable Explanation: Algebra errors usually happen when a step is skipped or a rule is used without checking the expression, equation, graph, or context first.
