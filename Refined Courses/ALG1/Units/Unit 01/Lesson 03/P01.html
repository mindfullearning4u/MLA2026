P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Fractions, Decimals, and Percent Relationships

In this lesson, you will connect fractions, decimals, percents, ratios, and unit rates so you can move flexibly between forms and interpret quantities in context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

This lesson focuses on foundational readiness skills and mathematical habits that support future MLA standards throughout the course.

What You Will Learn

  • How fractions, decimals, and percents represent the same quantity in different forms.
  • How to convert between common fractions, decimals, and percents.
  • How ratios compare two quantities.
  • How unit rates describe a quantity for one unit.

What You Will Do

  • Rewrite fractions as decimals and percents.
  • Rewrite percents as decimals and fractions.
  • Use ratios and unit rates to compare quantities.
  • Explain common conversion and percent mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice with organized work shown.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining conversions, ratios, and unit-rate reasoning.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Readiness Support: This lesson does not introduce a new primary Algebra 1 benchmark. It prepares you for later standards by strengthening percent reasoning, unit-rate fluency, and rational-number flexibility.

In student language: I can rewrite and interpret fractions, decimals, percents, ratios, and unit rates in ways that make sense for the problem.
