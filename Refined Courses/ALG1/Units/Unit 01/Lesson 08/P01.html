P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will review Unit 1 foundation skills and demonstrate mastery on the Unit Assessment.

Standards Covered in This Unit

MLA.A1.FND.1

I can use rational exponents and exponent laws to evaluate and rewrite numerical expressions.

MLA.A1.FND.2

I can use exponent properties to create equivalent algebraic expressions.

MLA.A1.FND.4

I can interpret variables, quantities, terms, factors, coefficients, and expression parts in context.

What You Have Learned

  • How to use organized mathematical reasoning and check for reasonableness.
  • How to work with integers, rational numbers, fractions, decimals, percents, ratios, and unit rates.
  • How to evaluate expressions using order of operations and substitution.
  • How to interpret variables, terms, coefficients, constants, and expression parts.
  • How to use exponent laws and rational exponents.
  • How to simplify equivalent expressions using like terms and the distributive property.

How You Will Demonstrate Mastery

  • Complete final Unit 1 review tasks in your notebook.
  • Submit notebook evidence showing organized work and corrections.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment with at least 80% mastery.

Student Success Reminders

  • Read each question carefully before choosing an operation.
  • Show enough work to make your reasoning clear.
  • Use the order of operations, signs, and grouping symbols carefully.
  • Check whether your answer is reasonable before submitting.
