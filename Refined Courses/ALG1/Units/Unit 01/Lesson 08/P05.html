P05 Unit Assessment Preparation

Use this page to prepare for the Unit Assessment. The assessment will ask you to apply Unit 1 skills in mixed order.

Preparation Question 1

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.4

Question:
In 7x + 3, what is the coefficient?

Answer: 7

Feedback: The coefficient is the number multiplying the variable.

Preparation Question 2

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.1

Question:
Evaluate 42.

Answer: 16

Feedback: 42 means 4 · 4, which equals 16.

Preparation Question 3

MLA Standard:
MLA.A1.FND.2

Question:
Simplify 2(x + 5) + 3x.

Answer: 5x + 10

Feedback: Distribute first, then combine like terms.
