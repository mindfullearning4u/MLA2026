Notebook Title

Unit 1 Review: Foundations for Algebra

Review Focus 1: Signed and Rational Numbers

  • Use sign rules carefully when adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing.
  • Convert among fractions, decimals, and percents when the context requires it.
  • Use ratios, rates, and unit rates to compare quantities.

Review Example 1

Problem: A temperature changes from -6 degrees to 9 degrees. What is the change?

9 - (-6) = 15

Reasoning: Subtracting a negative is the same as adding its opposite.

Review Focus 2: Order of Operations and Substitution

  • Evaluate inside grouping symbols first.
  • Evaluate exponents before multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction.
  • When substituting, replace the variable and then follow the order of operations.

Review Example 2

Problem: Evaluate 2x + 5 when x = 4.

2(4) + 5 = 8 + 5 = 13

Reasoning: Substitute 4 for x, then multiply before adding.
