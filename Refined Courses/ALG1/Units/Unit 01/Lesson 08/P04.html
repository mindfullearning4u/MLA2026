P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study all three review examples before completing the Unit Assessment preparation work.

Worked Example 1: Numbers and Percents

Problem: Convert 0.35 to a percent.

Step 1: A decimal can be converted to a percent by multiplying by 100.

0.35 × 100 = 35

Answer: 0.35 = 35%.

Worked Example 2: Expressions

Problem: Evaluate 3x + 2 when x = 5.

Step 1: Substitute 5 for x.

3(5) + 2

Step 2: Multiply before adding.

15 + 2 = 17

Answer: The value is 17.

Worked Example 3: Exponents and Structure

Problem: Rewrite x3 · x2.

Step 1: The bases match, so use the product of powers rule.

x3 · x2 = x3 + 2

Step 2: Add the exponents.

x3 + 2 = x5

Answer: x5

Common Mistake

Incorrect: x3 · x2 = x6

Why this is wrong: This multiplies the exponents. Product of powers with the same base uses addition of exponents.

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
