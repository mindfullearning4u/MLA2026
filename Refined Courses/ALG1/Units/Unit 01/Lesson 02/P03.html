Adding Signed Numbers

Same signs: Add the absolute values and keep the sign.

-4 + (-7) = -11

Different signs: Subtract the absolute values and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value.

-9 + 5 = -4

Subtracting Signed Numbers

Subtracting a number means adding its opposite.

8 - (-3) = 8 + 3 = 11

-5 - 2 = -5 + (-2) = -7

Multiplying and Dividing Signed Numbers

Use the sign pattern:

  • Same signs make a positive result.
  • Different signs make a negative result.

(-6)(-4) = 24

-18 ÷ 3 = -6

Working with Rational Numbers

Rational numbers include integers, fractions, and terminating or repeating decimals.

Use the same sign rules with rational numbers.

-1⁄2 + 3⁄4 = 1⁄4

The positive value is farther from zero, so the result is positive.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is treating subtraction of a negative number as ordinary subtraction.

Incorrect:

6 - (-4) = 2

Correct:

6 - (-4) = 6 + 4 = 10

Why: Subtracting a negative means adding the opposite.
