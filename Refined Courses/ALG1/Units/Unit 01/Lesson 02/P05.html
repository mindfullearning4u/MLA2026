Guided Practice Directions

Answer each question. Then read the feedback to understand the exponent property being used.

Question 1

Simplify x to the power of 4 times x to the power of 3.

x4 × x3

Correct Answer:

x7

Feedback: The powers have the same base, so add the exponents: 4 + 3 = 7.

Question 2

Simplify m to the power of 8 divided by m to the power of 2.

m8 ÷ m2

Correct Answer:

m6

Feedback: The powers have the same base, so subtract the exponents: 8 - 2 = 6.

Question 3

Simplify b cubed raised to the fourth power.

(b3)4

Correct Answer:

b12

Feedback: A power raised to another power means multiply the exponents: 3 × 4 = 12.

Question 4

Simplify 2x squared raised to the third power.

(2x2)3

Correct Answer:

8x6

Feedback: The outside exponent applies to both 2 and x squared: 23 = 8 and (x2)3 = x6.

Question 5

Which expression is equivalent to p squared times q squared?

p2 × q2

Correct Answer:

p2q2

Feedback: The bases p and q are different, so the exponents do not combine.
