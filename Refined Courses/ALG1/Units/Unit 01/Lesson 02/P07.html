P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint is Teacher of Record graded. Corrections are allowed until you demonstrate at least 80% mastery.

Checkpoint Task

Complete all parts. Show every exponent-law step and explain your reasoning in complete sentences.

  1. Simplify x to the power of 4 times x squared. Name the exponent property used.

    x4 × x2

  2. Simplify 3y squared raised to the second power. Show how the outside exponent applies.

    (3y2)2

  3. Simplify a to the power of 9 divided by a to the power of 5. Explain why subtraction is used.

    a9 ÷ a5

  4. Rewrite 6x to the power of 3/2 using a square root. Explain why the new expression is equivalent.

    6x3/2

  5. A student writes:

    m2 × n3 = mn5

    Explain the mistake and write the correct equivalent expression.

What to Submit

  • Your simplified expressions.
  • The exponent property used in each simplification.
  • A written explanation for each reasoning prompt.
  • Corrections if your Teacher of Record asks you to revise.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses exponent properties correctly.
  • Shows clear algebraic steps.
  • Explains why expressions are equivalent.
  • Identifies and corrects the common mistake with unlike bases.
