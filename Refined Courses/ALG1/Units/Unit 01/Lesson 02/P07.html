Checkpoint Task: Integers and Rational Numbers

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Order these numbers from least to greatest: -4, -9, 3, -1.
  2. Evaluate -12 + 7 and explain why the answer is negative.
  3. Evaluate -6 - (-8) and explain how adding the opposite helps.
  4. A student says (-5)(-3) = -15 because multiplication always keeps the negative sign. Identify the misconception, correct the work, and explain the sign rule.

Mastery Criteria

  • Compares and orders signed numbers accurately.
  • Uses correct sign rules for addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • Shows organized mathematical work.
  • Explains mistakes using signed-number vocabulary.

Alignment: Readiness support for Algebra 1 signed-number fluency and rational-number reasoning.
