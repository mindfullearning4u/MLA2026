P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1: Compare Signed Numbers

Problem: Which number is less?

-8 or -3

Step 1: Think about number line position.

The number farther left is less.

Step 2: Compare the locations.

-8 is farther left than -3.

Answer: -8 is less than -3.

Worked Example 2: Add Signed Numbers

Problem: Evaluate -6 + 14.

Step 1: The signs are different, so subtract the absolute values.

14 - 6 = 8

Step 2: Use the sign of the number farther from zero.

14 is farther from zero than -6, so the result is positive.

Answer: -6 + 14 = 8

Worked Example 3: Subtract a Negative Number

Problem: Evaluate 5 - (-9).

Step 1: Rewrite subtraction as adding the opposite.

5 - (-9) = 5 + 9

Step 2: Add.

5 + 9 = 14

Answer: 14

Common Mistake

A common mistake is treating subtraction of a negative number as ordinary subtraction.

Incorrect:

6 - (-4) = 2

Why it is wrong: Subtracting a negative means adding its opposite.

Correct:

6 - (-4) = 6 + 4 = 10

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
