P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Notice which exponent property is used and why.

Example 1: Product of Powers

Problem: Simplify x to the power of 3 times x to the power of 5.

x3 × x5

Step 1: Identify the base. Both powers have base x.

Step 2: Identify the operation. The powers are being multiplied.

Step 3: Use the product of powers property. Add the exponents.

x3 × x5 = x3+5

Step 4: Simplify the exponent.

3 + 5 = 8

Final Answer:

x3 × x5 = x8

Reasoning: When multiplying powers with the same base, you add the exponents.

Example 2: Quotient of Powers

Problem: Simplify y to the power of 9 divided by y to the power of 4.

y9 ÷ y4

Step 1: Identify the base. Both powers have base y.

Step 2: Identify the operation. The powers are being divided.

Step 3: Use the quotient of powers property. Subtract the exponents.

y9 ÷ y4 = y9-4

Step 4: Simplify the exponent.

9 - 4 = 5

Final Answer:

y9 ÷ y4 = y5

Reasoning: When dividing powers with the same base, you subtract the exponents. The base cannot be 0.

Example 3: Power of a Product

Problem: Simplify 3a squared raised to the third power.

(3a2)3

Step 1: The outside exponent applies to each factor inside the parentheses.

(3a2)3 = 33(a2)3

Step 2: Evaluate the coefficient power.

33 = 27

Step 3: Use the power of a power property. Multiply the exponents.

(a2)3 = a2×3 = a6

Final Answer:

(3a2)3 = 27a6

Reasoning: A power outside parentheses applies to every factor inside the parentheses.

Example 4: Rational Exponent Algebraic Form

Problem: Rewrite 6x to the power of 3/2 in an equivalent form using a square root.

6x3/2

Step 1: Separate the exponent 3/2 into 1 and 1/2.

x3/2 = x1x1/2

Step 2: Rewrite x to the power of 1/2 as the square root of x.

x1/2 = √x

Final Answer:

6x3/2 = 6x√x

Reasoning: The expression keeps the same value because x3/2 can be written as x1x1/2.

Common Mistake

x to the power of 2 times y to the power of 3 means:

x2 × y3

Incorrect:

x2 × y3 = xy5

Correct:

x2 × y3 = x2y3

The bases are different. Exponents can be combined only when the bases match.
