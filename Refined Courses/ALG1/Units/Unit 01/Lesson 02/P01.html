P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Integers and Rational Numbers

In this lesson, you will strengthen your fluency with signed numbers and rational quantities so you are ready for equations, inequalities, slope, systems, and data calculations later in Algebra 1.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

This lesson focuses on foundational readiness skills and mathematical habits that support future MLA standards throughout the course.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify integers and rational numbers.
  • How to compare signed numbers using a number line.
  • How absolute value shows distance from zero.
  • How to add, subtract, multiply, and divide signed numbers accurately.

What You Will Do

  • Use number line reasoning to order positive and negative values.
  • Evaluate expressions involving integers and rational numbers.
  • Explain sign errors and correct them.
  • Complete readiness practice that prepares you for future Algebra 1 skills.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice with organized work shown.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining signed-number reasoning and error corrections.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

Readiness Support: This lesson does not introduce a new primary Algebra 1 benchmark. It prepares you for later standards by strengthening signed-number fluency and rational-number reasoning.

In student language: I can work accurately with positive and negative numbers and explain how signs affect a calculation.
