P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Equivalent Algebraic Expressions with Exponent Properties

In this lesson, you will use exponent properties to rewrite algebraic expressions into equivalent forms.

What You Will Learn

  • How exponent properties help rewrite algebraic expressions.
  • How to multiply powers with the same base.
  • How to divide powers with the same base.
  • How to raise a power or product to a power.
  • How to explain why two algebraic expressions are equivalent.

What You Will Do

  • Review bases, exponents, variables, coefficients, and like bases.
  • Apply product, quotient, and power properties of exponents.
  • Rewrite algebraic expressions step by step.
  • Explain the exponent property used in each equivalent form.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook notes and independent practice with all steps shown.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining and applying exponent properties.
  • Demonstrate at least 80% mastery on the lesson quiz or complete Teacher of Record intervention before another randomized attempt is released.
