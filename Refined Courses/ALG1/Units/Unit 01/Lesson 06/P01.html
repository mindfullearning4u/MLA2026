P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Properties of Exponents

In this lesson, you will use exponent laws to evaluate and rewrite numerical and algebraic expressions, including expressions with rational exponents.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FND.1

I can use rational exponents and exponent laws to evaluate and rewrite numerical expressions.

MLA.A1.FND.2

I can use exponent properties to create equivalent algebraic expressions.

What You Will Learn

  • How exponents represent repeated multiplication.
  • How to use product, quotient, and power properties of exponents.
  • How zero and negative exponents affect expression value.
  • How rational exponents connect to roots.

What You Will Do

  • Evaluate numerical expressions with exponents.
  • Rewrite algebraic expressions using exponent laws.
  • Connect rational exponents to square roots and cube roots.
  • Explain and correct common exponent mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence explaining exponent laws.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that evaluate and rewrite expressions.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
