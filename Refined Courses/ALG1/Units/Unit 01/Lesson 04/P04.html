P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the order matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Example 1: Grouping Symbols

Problem: Evaluate 18 - 3(4 + 2) ÷ 2.

18 - 3(4 + 2) ÷ 2

18 - 3(6) ÷ 2

18 - 18 ÷ 2

18 - 9 = 9

Answer: 9

Reasoning: Complete the grouping symbols first. Then multiply and divide from left to right before subtracting.

Worked Example 2: Exponents

Problem: Evaluate 5 + 23 × 4.

5 + 23 × 4

5 + 8 × 4

5 + 32 = 37

Answer: 37

Reasoning: Evaluate the exponent before multiplication and addition.

Worked Example 3: Substitution

Problem: Evaluate 3x + 5 when x = 4.

3x + 5

3(4) + 5

12 + 5 = 17

Answer: 17

Reasoning: Substitute 4 for x, then multiply before adding.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is subtracting before dividing.

Problem: Evaluate 12 - 4 ÷ 2.

Incorrect:

12 - 4 ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2 = 4

Why it is wrong: The subtraction was completed before division.

Correct:

12 - 4 ÷ 2 = 12 - 2 = 10

Reasoning: Division is completed before subtraction unless grouping symbols change the order.

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
