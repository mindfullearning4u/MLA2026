P04 Worked Example

Study each example. Pay attention to the order of each step.

Example 1: Grouping Symbols

Evaluate: 18 - 3(4 + 2) ÷ 2

18 - 3(6) ÷ 2

18 - 18 ÷ 2

18 - 9

9

Complete the grouping symbols first. Then multiply and divide from left to right before subtracting.

Example 2: Exponents

Evaluate: 5 + 23 × 4

5 + 8 × 4

5 + 32

37

Evaluate the exponent before multiplication and addition.

Example 3: Substitution

Evaluate 3x + 5 when x = 4.

3x + 5

3(4) + 5

12 + 5

17

Substitute 4 for x, then multiply before adding.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: 12 - 4 ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2 = 4

Correct: 12 - 4 ÷ 2 = 12 - 2 = 10

Division is completed before subtraction unless grouping symbols change the order.
