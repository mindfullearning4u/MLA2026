P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson 4: Order of Operations and Evaluating Expressions

In this lesson, you will evaluate numerical and algebraic expressions by using grouping symbols, exponents, multiplication, division, addition, subtraction, and substitution in the correct order.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

This lesson focuses on foundational readiness skills and mathematical habits that support future MLA standards throughout the course.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use the conventional order of operations.
  • How grouping symbols affect the value of an expression.
  • How to evaluate expressions that include exponents.
  • How to substitute values for variables before simplifying.

What You Will Do

  • Take notes on order of operations and expression evaluation.
  • Work through numerical and algebraic expression examples.
  • Practice substituting values into expressions.
  • Explain and correct common mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence with organized steps.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that show correct order and substitution.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

In student language: I can evaluate expressions accurately by following the correct order and showing each step clearly.
