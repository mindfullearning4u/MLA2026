P06 Independent Work

Complete the following work independently in the same notebook used for your lesson notes.

Show your thinking clearly. Answer in complete sentences whenever an explanation is required.

Important Important:

The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Part A: Numerical Expressions

  1. Evaluate 9 + 4 × 6.
  2. Evaluate (15 - 7)2.
  3. Evaluate 30 ÷ 5 + 32.
  4. Evaluate 24 - 6 × 2 + 8.

Part B: Algebraic Expressions

Substitute the given value first. Then simplify using the order of operations.

  1. Evaluate 4x + 7 when x = 3.
  2. Evaluate 6n - 5 when n = 4.
  3. Evaluate 3(y + 2) when y = 6.
  4. Evaluate b2 + 10 when b = 5.

Part C: Error Analysis

These questions require correction and explanation.

  1. A student says 10 + 23 = 123 = 1,728.

    10 + 23 = 123 = 1,728

    Explain the mistake. Then show the correct value.
  2. A student evaluates 18 - 6 ÷ 3 by subtracting first.

    18 - 6 ÷ 3 = 12 ÷ 3 = 4

    Explain the misconception. Then show the correct work.
