P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Equivalent Expressions and Simplifying Expressions

In this lesson, you will simplify expressions by combining like terms, using the distributive property, and recognizing expression structure.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

This lesson focuses on foundational readiness skills and mathematical habits that support future MLA standards throughout the course.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify like terms in an expression.
  • How to combine like terms without changing the value of an expression.
  • How to use the distributive property to rewrite expressions.
  • How to recognize when two expressions are equivalent.

What You Will Do

  • Sort terms by variable part.
  • Simplify expressions by combining like terms.
  • Use the distributive property to expand expressions.
  • Explain and correct common simplification mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing how to simplify expressions.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that combine like terms and use the distributive property.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson prepares you for later MLA standards by strengthening the algebra habits needed to rewrite expressions, solve equations, and work with polynomial expressions.

In student language: I can simplify expressions by combining like terms and using the distributive property while keeping the expression equivalent.
