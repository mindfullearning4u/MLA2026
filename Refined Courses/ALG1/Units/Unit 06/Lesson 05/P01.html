P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Scatterplots and Trend Analysis

In this lesson, you will analyze scatterplots, describe association, distinguish correlation from causation, and interpret linear models for data.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.DF.5: I can analyze association in scatterplots and describe trends.

MLA.A1.DF.7: I can fit and interpret linear models for data.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.DP.1.3; MA.912.DP.2.4

Common Core Alignment: HSS.ID.C.9; HSS.ID.B.6a; HSS.ID.B.6c

SAT Domain Connection: Problem-Solving and Data Analysis.

ACT Domain Connection: Statistics and Probability / Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How scatterplots show relationships between two numerical variables.
  • How to describe positive, negative, and no association.
  • Why correlation does not prove causation.
  • How to interpret slope and intercept in a linear trend model.

What You Will Do

  • Classify scatterplot association and strength.
  • Choose or interpret a line of fit.
  • Make predictions from a linear model.
  • Explain whether a claim is correlation or causation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence describing scatterplot trends.
  • Submit checkpoint work interpreting a linear model.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
