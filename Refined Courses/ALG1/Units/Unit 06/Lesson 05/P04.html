Example: Interpret a Line of Fit

A line of fit for bike rental cost is C = 8h + 12, where h is hours and C is cost in dollars.

  1. The slope is 8, so the cost increases by about $8 for each additional hour.
  2. The intercept is 12, so the model includes a starting cost of $12.
  3. For 5 hours, C = 8(5) + 12 = 52, so the predicted cost is $52.

Correlation and Causation

If two variables move together, they are correlated. To claim one causes the other, more evidence is needed, such as a controlled study or a clear mechanism.
