Trend Models

A line of fit can be used to estimate or predict values. The slope describes the predicted change in y for each 1-unit increase in x.

Context Connection

A line of fit for plant height is h = 2w + 5, where w is weeks and h is height in centimeters. The slope 2 means the plant is predicted to grow about 2 centimeters per week. The intercept 5 means the model predicts 5 centimeters at week 0.

Common Mistakes

  • Claiming causation from correlation alone.
  • Using a linear model far outside the data range.
  • Ignoring units when interpreting slope.
  • Drawing a line of fit that does not follow the center of the data pattern.
