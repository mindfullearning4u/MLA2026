P06 Independent Work

Independent Work

  1. Solve y = 3x - 2 and x + y = 10 by substitution.
  2. Solve 2x + y = 14 and 3x - y = 11 by elimination.
  3. Solve x - y = 4 and 2x + y = 14 using any algebraic method.
  4. Explain which method you prefer for problem 3 and why.

Check Your Work

For each solution, substitute the x-value and y-value into both original equations. A correct solution must make both equations true.
