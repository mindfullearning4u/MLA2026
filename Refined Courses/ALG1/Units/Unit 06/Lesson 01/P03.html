P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Interpreting the Graph

A system solution is not just where two lines look close. The solution is the exact ordered pair where the two lines cross. That point gives one x-value and one y-value that work in both equations.

Context Connection

Two phone plans are modeled by y = 20 + 5x and y = 10 + 7x, where x is the number of months and y is total cost in dollars.

  1. Graph both equations on the same axes.
  2. Find the intersection point.
  3. Write one sentence explaining what the x-coordinate means.
  4. Write one sentence explaining what the y-coordinate means.

Common Mistakes

  • Using the y-intercept as the solution before graphing both lines.
  • Reading the intersection from only one line.
  • Writing the answer as one number instead of an ordered pair.
  • Forgetting to check the ordered pair in both equations.
