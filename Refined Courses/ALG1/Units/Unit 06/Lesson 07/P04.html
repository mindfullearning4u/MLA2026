P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Residual

A model predicts 82 students will attend an event. The actual attendance is 76 students.

Residual = actual - predicted = 76 - 82 = -6.

The residual is -6, so the model overpredicted attendance by 6 students.

Example 2: Two-Way Table

In a survey, 18 ninth graders and 12 tenth graders chose online tutoring. A total of 40 ninth graders responded. The relative frequency of ninth graders who chose online tutoring is 18/40 = 0.45, or 45%.
