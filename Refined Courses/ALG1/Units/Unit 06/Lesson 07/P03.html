Residuals and Model Fit

A residual tells how far a prediction was from the actual value. A residual near 0 means the model prediction was close. A positive residual means the actual value was greater than the prediction. A negative residual means the actual value was less than the prediction.

Common Mistakes

  • Subtracting in the wrong order when finding residuals.
  • Treating a biased sample as if it represents the whole population.
  • Using a two-way table total when a conditional group total is needed.
  • Thinking simulation results are exact instead of estimates.
