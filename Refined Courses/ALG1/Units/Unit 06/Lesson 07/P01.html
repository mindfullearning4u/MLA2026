P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Multi-Step Algebra Modeling

In this lesson, you will use residuals, sampling, simulation, and two-way tables to support decisions in multi-step Algebra I modeling situations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.DF.6: I can use residuals to judge how well a model fits data.

MLA.A1.DF.8: I can use sampling and simulation to make reasonable conclusions.

MLA.A1.DF.9: I can interpret two-way frequency tables and relative frequencies.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.DP.1.4; MA.912.DP.2.6; MA.912.DP.3.1

Common Core Alignment: HSS.IC.B.4; HSS.ID.B.6b; HSS.ID.B.5

SAT Domain Connection: Problem-Solving and Data Analysis.

ACT Domain Connection: Statistics and Probability / Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How residuals measure the difference between actual and predicted values.
  • How sample design affects whether conclusions are reasonable.
  • How simulations can estimate outcomes for chance processes.
  • How two-way tables organize categorical data for comparison.

What You Will Do

  • Compute and interpret residuals from model predictions.
  • Identify representative and biased sampling methods.
  • Use simulation results to make an estimate.
  • Find totals, conditional frequencies, and relative frequencies in two-way tables.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence for residuals, sampling, simulation, and two-way tables.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining whether a model or conclusion is reasonable.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Multi-Step Algebra Modeling in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.DF.6, MLA.A1.DF.8, MLA.A1.DF.9. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
