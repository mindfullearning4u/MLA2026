P06 Independent Work

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C independently. Show enough work so your Teacher of Record can see your thinking and decide whether you are ready to continue or need another practice step.

Part A: Skill Check

  1. Solve a system in context.
  2. Write one sentence explaining the first step you used.

Part B: Explain the Reasoning

  1. Interpret data from a display or model.
  2. Explain why your answer follows the lesson idea instead of only listing an answer.

Part C: Apply the Lesson

  1. Compare two real-world options using algebraic evidence.
  2. Write a final sentence connecting your answer to the review standards for this synthesis lesson.

Need help?

If any step feels unclear, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR) before moving on. Your TOR can help you review directions, check your setup, and decide whether you need more practice.
