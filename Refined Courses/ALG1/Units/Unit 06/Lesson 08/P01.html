P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this synthesis lesson, you will demonstrate integrated Algebra I modeling across systems, data, and financial contexts.

Standards Reviewed in This Lesson

This lesson reviews Unit 6 standards for systems, constraints, financial literacy, data displays, scatterplots, residuals, sampling, simulation, and two-way tables.

MLA Standards: MLA.A1.AR.5; MLA.A1.AR.6; MLA.A1.AR.7; MLA.A1.DF.1; MLA.A1.DF.2; MLA.A1.DF.3; MLA.A1.DF.4; MLA.A1.DF.5; MLA.A1.DF.6; MLA.A1.DF.7; MLA.A1.DF.8; MLA.A1.DF.9

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra; Problem-Solving and Data Analysis.

ACT Domain Connection: Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How to select a strategy when a problem combines systems, data, and financial reasoning.
  • How to interpret answers in realistic contexts.
  • How to check whether a result is reasonable.

What You Will Do

  • Review systems, constraints, interest models, displays, scatterplots, and residuals.
  • Complete mixed modeling tasks that require method selection.
  • Prepare for the Unit 6 Unit Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete synthesis notebook evidence.
  • Submit a checkpoint explaining a complete modeling decision.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Putting It All Together in a way that connects directly to the review standards for this synthesis lesson. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
