P04 Worked Example

Example: Choose a Display

A teacher records quiz scores for one class: 72, 75, 75, 81, 84, 88, 90, 91, 96, 98. The teacher wants to show the distribution of scores.

Best choice: A dot plot or histogram, because the data are numerical and the question is about distribution.

Example: Interpret a Category Display

A bar graph shows favorite after-school activities. If sports has 18 students and music has 12 students, sports was chosen by 6 more students than music.
