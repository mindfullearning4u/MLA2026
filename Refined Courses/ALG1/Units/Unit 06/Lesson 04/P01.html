P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Interpreting Data Displays

In this lesson, you will select and interpret displays for numerical and categorical data, including how displays help answer different questions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.DF.3: I can select and interpret appropriate displays for numerical data.

MLA.A1.DF.4: I can select and interpret appropriate displays for categorical data.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.DP.1.1; MA.912.DP.1.2

Common Core Alignment: HSS.ID.A.1; HSS.ID.A.2

SAT Domain Connection: Problem-Solving and Data Analysis.

ACT Domain Connection: Statistics and Probability / Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How numerical and categorical data are different.
  • Which displays are useful for comparing categories.
  • Which displays show distribution, center, spread, or change.
  • How to interpret labels, scales, and units accurately.

What You Will Do

  • Identify whether data are numerical or categorical.
  • Choose an appropriate display for a data question.
  • Read values from tables, bar graphs, dot plots, histograms, and box plots.
  • Describe what a display shows and what it does not show.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence comparing display types.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that justify a display choice.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Interpreting Data Displays in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.DF.3, MLA.A1.DF.4. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
