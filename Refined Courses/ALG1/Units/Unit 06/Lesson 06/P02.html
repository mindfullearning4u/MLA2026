P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Lesson 6: Financial Literacy Applications

Vocabulary

Principal: the starting amount of money.

Interest: money earned or paid for using money over time.

Simple interest: interest calculated only on the principal.

Compound interest: interest calculated on the principal and previously earned interest.

Notebook Setup

Create a table with columns for Formula, Variable Meaning, and Growth Type.

Include I = Prt and A = P(1 + r/n)nt.

Notebook Task

For $500 invested at 6% simple interest for 3 years, identify P, r, and t. Then calculate I = Prt and the total amount A = P + I.
