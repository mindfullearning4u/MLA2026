P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Financial Literacy Applications

In this lesson, you will apply linear and exponential models to simple interest, compound interest, and continuously compounded interest.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.DF.1: I can apply simple interest and linear financial models.

MLA.A1.DF.2: I can apply compound and continuously compounded interest models.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.FL.3.2; MA.912.FL.3.4

Common Core Alignment: HSF.LE.A.1; HSF.LE.A.2; HSF.LE.A.3

SAT Domain Connection: Problem-Solving and Data Analysis.

ACT Domain Connection: Modeling / Integrating Essential Skills.

What You Will Learn

  • How simple interest grows by repeated addition.
  • How compound interest grows by repeated multiplication.
  • How compounding frequency affects total amount.
  • How to compare financial models in context.

What You Will Do

  • Calculate simple interest using I = Prt.
  • Calculate compound interest using A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt).
  • Interpret variables in financial formulas.
  • Compare linear and exponential growth in financial contexts.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence identifying formula variables.
  • Submit checkpoint calculations comparing interest models.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Financial Literacy Applications in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.DF.1, MLA.A1.DF.2. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
