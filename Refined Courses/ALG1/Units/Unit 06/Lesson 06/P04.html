P04 Worked Example

Example: Simple Interest

Find the simple interest on $600 at 4% for 5 years.

  1. Use I = Prt.
  2. Substitute: I = 600(0.04)(5).
  3. Calculate: I = 120.
  4. Total amount: A = 600 + 120 = $720.

Example: Compound Interest

For $600 at 4% compounded annually for 5 years, A = 600(1.04)^5, which is about $729.99. Compound interest is larger because interest earns more interest.
