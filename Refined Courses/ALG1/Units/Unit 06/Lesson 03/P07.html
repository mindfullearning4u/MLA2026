P07 Checkpoint

Checkpoint Prompt

A fundraiser sells shirts for $12 and hats for $8. The group wants to earn at least $240 and can sell no more than 25 items. Let s represent shirts and h represent hats. Write the system of constraints and determine whether (10, 15) is feasible.

Success Criteria

  • Variables are clearly defined.
  • Both constraints are written correctly.
  • The ordered pair is tested in both constraints.
  • The interpretation uses the context and units.
