P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Modeling with Systems

In this lesson, you will represent real-world constraints with systems of equations and inequalities, then interpret what the solutions mean in context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.AR.6: I can represent constraints using systems of equations and inequalities.

MLA.A1.AR.7: I can interpret solutions to systems and constraints in context.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.9.4; MA.912.AR.9.6

Common Core Alignment: HSA.REI.D.12; HSA.CED.A.3

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to translate context constraints into equations or inequalities.
  • How to identify feasible solutions for a system of constraints.
  • How to interpret ordered pairs using the units in a situation.
  • How systems can model limits, totals, costs, and combinations.

What You Will Do

  • Define variables for contextual quantities.
  • Write equations or inequalities from constraints.
  • Test whether ordered pairs satisfy all constraints.
  • Explain what a feasible solution means.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing variables, constraints, and interpretations.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that justify whether a solution is feasible.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
