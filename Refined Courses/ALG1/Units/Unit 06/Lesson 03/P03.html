P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Inequality Constraints

Many situations use phrases such as at most, at least, no more than, or no less than. These phrases create inequalities instead of equations.

For example, if a club can spend at most $60, then the cost expression must be less than or equal to 60.

Context Connection

A team needs at least 40 points. Small tasks are worth 4 points and large tasks are worth 10 points. Let x represent small tasks and y represent large tasks.

  1. Write an inequality for the point requirement.
  2. Test whether (5, 2) satisfies the requirement.
  3. Explain the meaning of the ordered pair in context.

Common Mistakes

  • Reversing the meaning of at least and at most.
  • Forgetting that a context may require whole-number solutions.
  • Checking only one constraint when the system has more than one.
  • Interpreting an ordered pair without using the variable definitions.
