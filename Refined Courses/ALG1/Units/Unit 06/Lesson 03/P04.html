P04 Worked Example

Example: Model a Constraint

A theater sells adult tickets for $8 and student tickets for $5. The theater wants to collect at least $200. Let a represent adult tickets and s represent student tickets.

  1. Write the inequality: 8a + 5s >= 200.
  2. Test (15, 20): 8(15) + 5(20) = 120 + 100 = 220.
  3. Since 220 >= 200, the ordered pair is feasible.

Interpretation: Selling 15 adult tickets and 20 student tickets meets the revenue goal.

Why It Works

Each coefficient tells how much one item contributes. The inequality compares the total contribution to the required limit.
