P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Vocabulary

Constraint: a condition or limit that a solution must satisfy.

Feasible solution: a solution that satisfies all constraints in a situation.

System of constraints: multiple equations or inequalities used together to model a situation.

Context interpretation: explaining what a mathematical answer means using the units and quantities in the problem.

Notebook Setup

Create a four-column table labeled Quantity, Variable, Constraint, and Meaning.

Use the table to keep the real-world quantities connected to the equations and inequalities you write.

Notebook Task

A school store sells pencils for $1 and notebooks for $3. A student spends exactly $15. Let x represent pencils and y represent notebooks.

  1. Write the equation that models the total cost.
  2. Explain why x and y should be whole numbers.
  3. Test whether (6, 3) is a feasible solution.
