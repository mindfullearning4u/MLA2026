P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study all three worked examples. Each example shows the steps and reasoning you should use before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Slope Synthesis

Problem: Find the slope through (1, 4) and (3, 10).

Step 1: Rise = 10 - 4 = 6.

Step 2: Run = 3 - 1 = 2.

Step 3: Slope = 6/2 = 3.

Mathematical Visual: Rise and Run from (1, 4) to (3, 10)

Worked Example 2: Equation Synthesis

Problem: Write a line with slope 3 and y-intercept (0, -2).

Step 1: Use y = mx + b.

Step 2: Substitute m = 3 and b = -2.

Step 3: y = 3x - 2.

Mathematical Visual: Substitute into y = mx + b

y = mx + b
m = 3 and b = -2
y = 3x - 2

Worked Example 3: Inequality Synthesis

Problem: Solve 3x - 5 ≤ 10.

Step 1: Add 5 to both sides: 3x ≤ 15.

Step 2: Divide by 3: x ≤ 5.

Step 3: Use a closed circle at 5 and shade left.

Mathematical Visual: x ≤ 5 on a Number Line

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Using one Unit 3 method for every problem.

Correct: Choose the method that matches the mathematical object: slope, equation, one-variable inequality, or two-variable inequality.

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
