P05 Unit Assessment Preparation

Prepare for the Assessment

Before the Unit Assessment, review your notebook evidence for slope, graphing, equation writing, and inequalities.

Readiness Checklist

  • I can calculate slope from two points.
  • I can write and graph a linear equation.
  • I can solve a one-variable inequality.
  • I can graph a two-variable inequality.
  • I can interpret equations and inequalities in context.

Final Review Prompt

Write one sentence explaining how slope connects tables, graphs, equations, and real-world rates.
