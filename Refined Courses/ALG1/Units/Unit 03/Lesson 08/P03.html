Inequalities Review

Review one-variable inequalities, two-variable inequalities, and solution regions.

Review Tasks

  1. Solve and graph: 3x - 5 ≤ 10.
  2. Solve and graph: -2x > 8.
  3. Describe the graph of y > x + 1.
  4. Test whether (2, 5) satisfies y ≤ 2x + 1.

Strategy Reminder

For one-variable inequalities, track whether the inequality symbol reverses. For two-variable inequalities, track boundary line type and shading region.
