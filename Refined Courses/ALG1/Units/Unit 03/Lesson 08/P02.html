P02 Unit Review - Part 1

Linear Relationships Review

Review the ways linear relationships appear across tables, graphs, equations, and contexts.

Review Tasks

  1. Find the slope between (1, 4) and (3, 10).
  2. Write the equation of a line with slope 3 and y-intercept (0, -2).
  3. Identify the slope and y-intercept of y = -2x + 7.
  4. Describe how to graph y = 1/2x - 4.

Notebook Evidence

For each task, show the relationship between the numbers and the representation. Label slope, intercept, and units when context is included.
