P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Solving and Representing Inequalities

Use inverse operations to solve. Then represent the solution set using a number line description.

Example A: Addition or Subtraction

Solve: x + 4 < 9

Subtract 4 from both sides: x < 5.

Representation: Open circle at 5, shade left.

Example B: Multiplication or Division

Solve: 3x - 2 ≥ 10

Add 2 to both sides: 3x ≥ 12.

Divide by 3: x ≥ 4.

Representation: Closed circle at 4, shade right.

Notebook Task

Solve each inequality and describe its graph.

  1. x - 6 ≤ 2
  2. 4x + 1 > 13
  3. -2x < 8

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Skipping a step or applying a rule before checking the structure of the expression, equation, graph, or situation.

Teachable Explanation: Algebra errors usually happen when a step is skipped or a rule is used without checking the expression, equation, graph, or context first.
