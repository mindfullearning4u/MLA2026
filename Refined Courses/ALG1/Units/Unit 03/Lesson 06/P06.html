P06 Independent Work

Independent Work

Solve each problem independently. Show each inverse operation and write a graph description.

Problems

  1. Solve and graph: y - 8 > 3
  2. Solve and graph: 4n + 1 ≤ 17
  3. Solve and graph: -5p > 20
  4. Solve and graph: 2 ≤ x - 1 < 8
  5. A club costs $10 to join plus $4 per visit. Write and solve an inequality for spending at most $34.

Reflection

Choose one problem where the graph matters. Explain how the inequality symbol tells you whether to use an open or closed circle and where to shade.
