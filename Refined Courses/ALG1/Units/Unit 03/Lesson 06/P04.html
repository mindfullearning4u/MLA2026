P04 Worked Example

Study all three worked examples. Each example shows the steps and reasoning you should use before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Linear Relationship

Problem: Use a table, graph, or equation to identify the starting value and rate of change.

Step 1: Identify what changes by a constant amount.

Step 2: Use the constant change to write or interpret the linear rule.

Worked Example 2: Graph or Equation

Problem: Connect a linear equation to its graph.

Step 1: Identify slope and intercept.

Step 2: Use them to graph or interpret the line.

Worked Example 3: Context Interpretation

Problem: Explain what a feature means in context.

Step 1: Identify the feature.

Step 2: Write a sentence using the units and situation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: "Divide by a negative number but keep the inequality symbol facing the same way."

Why this is wrong: Multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative reverses the order.

Correct: "Reverse the inequality symbol when multiplying or dividing by a negative number."

Correction: Track the operation carefully before graphing the solution.
