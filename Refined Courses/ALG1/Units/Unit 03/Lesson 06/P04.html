P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study all three worked examples. Each example shows the steps and reasoning you should use before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Solve x + 4 > 6

Problem: Solve x + 4 > 6.

Step 1: Subtract 4 from both sides: x + 4 - 4 > 6 - 4.

Step 2: Simplify: x > 2.

Step 3: Because the symbol is >, use an open circle at 2 and shade to the right.

Mathematical Visual: Example 1 Number Line for x > 2

Worked Example 2: Solve 3x - 4 ≥ 11

Problem: Solve 3x - 4 ≥ 11.

Step 1: Add 4 to both sides: 3x - 4 + 4 ≥ 11 + 4.

Step 2: Simplify: 3x ≥ 15.

Step 3: Divide both sides by 3: x ≥ 5.

Step 4: Because the symbol is ≥, use a closed circle at 5 and shade to the right.

Mathematical Visual: Example 2 Number Line for x ≥ 5

Worked Example 3: Solve -2x > 10

Problem: Solve -2x > 10.

Step 1: Divide both sides by -2.

Step 2: Because you divided by a negative number, reverse the inequality symbol.

Step 3: x < -5.

Step 4: Use an open circle at -5 and shade to the left.

Mathematical Visual: Example 3 Number Line for x < -5

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Dividing by a negative number and keeping the same inequality symbol.

Correct: When multiplying or dividing by a negative number, reverse the inequality symbol.

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
