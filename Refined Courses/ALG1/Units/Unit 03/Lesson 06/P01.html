P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Solving Linear Inequalities

In this lesson, you will solve and represent one-variable linear inequalities, including compound inequalities.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA Standards Covered: MLA.A1.AR.2

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks Covered: MA.912.AR.2.6

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.1; HSA.REI.B.3

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How an inequality represents a range of possible solutions.
  • How to solve one-variable linear inequalities using inverse operations.
  • When and why to reverse the inequality symbol.
  • How to represent solution sets on a number line.
  • How to solve and interpret compound inequalities.

What You Will Do

  • Solve inequalities with addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • Graph inequalities using open and closed circles.
  • Reverse the inequality symbol when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
  • Write and solve inequalities from real-world constraints.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing inequality solution steps and graphs.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that explain solution sets and boundary points.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection
