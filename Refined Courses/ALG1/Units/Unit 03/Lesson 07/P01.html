P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Graphing and Interpreting Inequalities

In this lesson, you will write and graph two-variable inequalities and interpret the solution regions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.AR.3: I can write and graph two-variable linear inequalities.

MLA.A1.AR.4: I can interpret solution regions for linear inequalities in context.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.2.7; MA.912.AR.2.8

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.3; HSA.REI.D.12

SAT/ACT Connection: Algebra; Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify the boundary line of a two-variable inequality.
  • When to use a solid boundary line and when to use a dashed boundary line.
  • How to shade the solution region for a linear inequality.
  • How to test ordered pairs to determine whether they satisfy an inequality.
  • How solution regions represent real-world constraints.

What You Will Do

  • Graph boundary lines from equations such as y = mx + b.
  • Use dashed and solid boundary lines correctly.
  • Shade above or below a boundary line based on the inequality symbol.
  • Interpret whether ordered pairs and regions are solutions.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing boundary lines and shaded solution regions.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that test points and interpret solution regions.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
