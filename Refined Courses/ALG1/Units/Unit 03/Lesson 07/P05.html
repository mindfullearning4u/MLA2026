Practice With Support

Use boundary lines, shading, and point testing to reason about each inequality.

Guided Practice Questions

  1. For y < 2x + 3, identify the boundary line, line type, and shading direction.
  2. For y ≥ -x + 4, identify the boundary line, line type, and shading direction.
  3. Determine whether (1, 5) satisfies y ≤ 2x + 3.
  4. Write an inequality for a budget of at most $18 when x items cost $3 each and y items cost $6 each.
  5. Explain what the shaded region means in a real-world constraint.
