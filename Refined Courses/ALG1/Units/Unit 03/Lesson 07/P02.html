Notebook Title: Lesson 7: Graphing and Interpreting Inequalities

Notebook Setup

Title your notes Graphing and Interpreting Inequalities. Create sections for vocabulary, boundary lines, shading, and point testing.

Vocabulary

  • Two-variable inequality: A statement comparing expressions with x and y.
  • Boundary line: The line that separates the coordinate plane into regions.
  • Solution region: The shaded set of ordered pairs that make the inequality true.
  • Dashed line: A boundary that is not included for < or >.
  • Solid line: A boundary that is included for ≤ or ≥.

Graphing Steps

  1. Replace the inequality symbol with an equals sign to graph the boundary line.
  2. Use a dashed boundary for < or > and a solid boundary for ≤ or ≥.
  3. Choose a test point not on the boundary, often (0, 0).
  4. Shade the side that makes the inequality true.
