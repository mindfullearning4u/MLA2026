Copy These Examples

Example 1: A streaming plan costs $4 each month.
Input: months. Output: total cost.
Table: (1, 4), (2, 8), (3, 12), (4, 16).
The output increases by 4 for each additional month, so the relationship is linear.

Example 2: A plant is 6 inches tall and grows 2 inches each week.
Table: (0, 6), (1, 8), (2, 10), (3, 12).
The starting value is 6 and the rate of change is 2 inches per week.

Example 3: The table (0, 3), (1, 7), (2, 11), (3, 15) is linear because the output increases by 4 each time the input increases by 1.

Common Mistakes

Do not decide a relationship is linear just because the numbers increase. The change must be constant when the input increases by equal amounts.
