P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Guided Practice

  1. A relationship has points (0, 2), (1, 5), (2, 8), (3, 11). Identify the rate of change.
  2. A table has outputs 4, 9, 14, 19 when inputs are 0, 1, 2, 3. Decide whether the relationship is linear.
  3. A parking garage charges $6 per hour. Write three ordered pairs for hours and cost.
  4. A plant starts at 10 inches and grows 3 inches each week. Identify the starting value and rate of change.
  5. Explain how a table can show whether a relationship is linear.
