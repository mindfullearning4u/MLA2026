P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Introduction to Linear Relationships

In this lesson, you will represent linear relationships from tables, graphs, descriptions, and contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.LR.1: I can represent linear relationships from tables, graphs, descriptions, and contexts.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.2.2

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.2

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How a linear relationship connects two quantities.
  • How to represent a linear relationship with words, tables, equations, and graphs.
  • How to identify a constant rate of change from a table or context.
  • How ordered pairs show input-output values in a relationship.

What You Will Do

  • Create tables from descriptions.
  • Read ordered pairs from tables and graphs.
  • Match equivalent representations of the same relationship.
  • Explain what the numbers in a relationship mean.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence with tables, ordered pairs, and explanations.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that connect representations of linear relationships.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can show the same linear relationship in more than one way and explain what the representations mean.
