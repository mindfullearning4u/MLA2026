P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. The Teacher of Record can use this work to decide whether you are ready to move forward or need support first.

Instructions

Show each step clearly. Use complete sentences when explaining reasoning or interpreting an answer.

Part A: Core Skill Practice

  1. Complete two basic problems from this lesson topic.
  2. Label the operation or rule you used.
  3. Check one answer by substitution or interpretation.

Part B: Explain Your Reasoning

  1. Choose one problem from Part A.
  2. Write the steps in order.
  3. Explain why each step keeps the equation, graph, or model valid.

Part C: Error Analysis

  1. Write one common mistake for this lesson.
  2. Show the incorrect work in one line.
  3. Correct the work and explain the misconception.
