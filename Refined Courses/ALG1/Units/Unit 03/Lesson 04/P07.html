Checkpoint Task: Slope-Intercept Form

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Identify the slope and y-intercept of y = 5x - 2.
  2. Graph y = -1/2x + 4 using slope and y-intercept.
  3. Write an equation for a line with slope 3 and y-intercept (0, 8).
  4. A student says the y-intercept of y = 7x + 2 is 7. Identify the misconception, correct it, and explain what 7 represents.

Mastery Criteria

  • Identifies slope and y-intercept from equations.
  • Graphs using slope and y-intercept.
  • Writes slope-intercept equations from given features.
