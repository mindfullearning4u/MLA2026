P04 Worked Example

Study how slope and y-intercept determine the line.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Graph y = 1/2x + 3.
Start at (0, 3). Use slope 1/2 to move up 1 and right 2. Plot another point at (2, 4).

Example 2: Write an equation with slope 4 and y-intercept (0, -1).
Use y = mx + b, so y = 4x - 1.

Example 3: In y = -2x + 9, the graph starts at (0, 9) and decreases by 2 for each 1-unit increase in x.

Interpretation Check

The slope describes repeated change. The y-intercept describes the output when x = 0.
