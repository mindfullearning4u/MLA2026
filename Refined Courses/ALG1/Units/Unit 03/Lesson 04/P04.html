Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study all three worked examples. Each example shows the steps and reasoning you should use before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Identify Slope and Intercept

Problem: Identify the slope and y-intercept in y = 2x + 5.

Step 1: Compare the equation to y = mx + b.

Step 2: m = 2, so the slope is 2.

Step 3: b = 5, so the y-intercept is (0, 5).

Mathematical Visual: y = 2x + 5 Compared to y = mx + b

y = 2x + 5
Slope: The coefficient of x is 2, so m = 2.
y-intercept: The constant is 5, so b = 5 and the point is (0, 5).

Worked Example 2: Graph from Slope-Intercept Form

Problem: Graph y = -3x + 4.

Step 1: Start at the y-intercept (0, 4).

Step 2: Use slope -3/1: move down 3 and right 1.

Step 3: Draw a line through the points.

Mathematical Visual: Graph of y = -3x + 4

Worked Example 3: Interpret a Context Model

Problem: A gym charges $12 to join plus $6 per visit. Interpret y = 6x + 12.

Step 1: The slope is 6, so each visit adds 6 dollars.

Step 2: The y-intercept is 12, so the starting fee is 12 dollars.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Calling 5 the slope in y = 2x + 5.

Correct: The coefficient of x is the slope; the constant is the y-intercept.
