Copy These Examples

Example 1: In y = 2x + 5, the slope is 2 and the y-intercept is (0, 5).

Example 2: To graph y = -3x + 4, start at (0, 4). Use slope -3, or -3/1, to move down 3 and right 1.

Example 3: A gym charges $12 to join plus $6 per visit. The model is y = 6x + 12. The slope is 6 dollars per visit, and the y-intercept is the $12 joining fee.

Common Mistakes

Do not confuse slope with y-intercept. The coefficient of x is the slope, and the constant term is the y-intercept.
