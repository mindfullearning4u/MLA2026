P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Notice how the work identifies the structure, applies the correct algebraic process, and checks the result.

Study all three worked examples. Each example shows the steps and reasoning you should use before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Slope Through Two Points

Problem: Find the slope through (1, 4) and (3, 10).

Step 1: Find the change in y: 10 - 4 = 6.

Step 2: Find the change in x: 3 - 1 = 2.

Step 3: Divide rise by run: 6/2 = 3.

Worked Example 2: Rate of Change in Context

Problem: A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours.

Step 1: Identify distance and time.

Step 2: Divide 150 by 3.

Step 3: The rate is 50 miles per hour.

Worked Example 3: Negative Slope

Problem: Find the slope through (2, 8) and (5, 2).

Step 1: Change in y is 2 - 8 = -6.

Step 2: Change in x is 5 - 2 = 3.

Step 3: Slope = -6/3 = -2, so the line decreases left to right.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Using change in x over change in y.

Correct: Slope is change in y divided by change in x.

Teachable Explanation: The worked examples model both the calculation and the reasoning needed to justify the answer.
