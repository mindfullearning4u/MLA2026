Notebook Title

Lesson 3: Slope and Rate of Change

Key Vocabulary

  • Slope: The ratio of vertical change to horizontal change.
  • Rate of change: How much one quantity changes compared to another.
  • Rise: The change in y-values.
  • Run: The change in x-values.
  • Unit rate: A rate for one unit of input.

Process Notes

  • Find the change in y-values.
  • Find the change in x-values.
  • Write slope as change in y divided by change in x.
  • Interpret the result using the units from the context.
