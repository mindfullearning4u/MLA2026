Continue writing examples, explanations, and correction strategies into your notebook.

Copy These Examples

Example 1: Find the slope through (1, 4) and (3, 10).
Change in y: 10 - 4 = 6.
Change in x: 3 - 1 = 2.
Slope = 6/2 = 3.

Example 2: A car travels 150 miles in 3 hours.
Rate of change = 150/3 = 50 miles per hour.

Example 3: A line through (2, 8) and (5, 2) has slope (2 - 8)/(5 - 2) = -6/3 = -2. The negative slope means the graph decreases from left to right.

Common Mistakes

Do not subtract x-values in one order and y-values in the opposite order. Use a consistent direction for both changes.
