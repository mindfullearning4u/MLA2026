# ALG1 Unit 03 - Linear Inequalities and Systems

---

## Unit Overview

This unit extends linear reasoning into inequality and systems work. Students write and solve one-variable inequalities, write and graph two-variable inequalities, solve systems of linear equations, graph solution sets for systems of linear inequalities, and interpret feasible solutions in real-world contexts.

Emphasis is placed on solution sets, constraints, graphical interpretation, algebraic reasoning, and the distinction between viable and non-viable options in modeling situations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- symbolic reasoning;
- graph interpretation;
- systems and feasible region analysis;
- real-world constraint modeling.

All assessment items require students to write, solve, graph, and interpret inequalities and systems in both mathematical and real-world contexts.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of **80% mastery** across all required components, including:

- Notebook Evidence
- Checkpoints
- Quizzes
- Unit Assessment

Students are not advanced until mastery is achieved, ensuring that inequality and systems reasoning is secure before the course moves into quadratic and absolute value function families.

No single assessment determines mastery; students must demonstrate consistent performance across all components.
