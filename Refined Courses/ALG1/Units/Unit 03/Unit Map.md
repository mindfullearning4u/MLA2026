# ALG1 Unit 03 – Functions and Linear Relationships

---

## Unit Overview

This unit introduces functions as relationships between quantities and extends students’ understanding of equations into graphical and functional representations. Students develop skills in identifying, representing, and analyzing functions using tables, graphs, and equations.

Emphasis is placed on understanding linear functions, interpreting rate of change, and modeling real-world situations using multiple representations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all standards addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- Conceptual understanding  
- Procedural fluency  
- Graphical and numerical interpretation  
- Real-world application  

All assessment items require students to analyze and represent functions using tables, graphs, and equations, interpret key features, and calculate and interpret average rate of change.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of **80% mastery** across all required components, including:

- Notebook Evidence  
- Checkpoints  
- Quizzes  
- Unit Assessment  

Students are not advanced until mastery is achieved, ensuring that foundational understanding of functions is solid before progressing to more complex relationships such as quadratic and exponential functions.

No single assessment determines mastery; students must demonstrate consistent performance across all components.
