Notebook Title

Lesson 5: Writing Linear Equations

Key Vocabulary

  • Slope-intercept form: y = mx + b.
  • Point-slope information: A point on a line and the slope of the line.
  • Parallel lines: Lines in the same plane that do not intersect and have equal slopes.
  • Perpendicular lines: Lines that intersect at right angles and have opposite reciprocal slopes.

Process Notes

  • If slope and y-intercept are known, substitute them into y = mx + b.
  • If a point and slope are known, substitute the point and slope into y = mx + b to solve for b.
  • For a parallel line, keep the same slope.
  • For a perpendicular line, use the opposite reciprocal slope.
