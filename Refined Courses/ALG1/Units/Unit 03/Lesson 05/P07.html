Checkpoint Task: Writing Linear Equations

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Write an equation with slope 6 and y-intercept (0, -3).
  2. Write an equation with slope -2 through (3, 1).
  3. Write an equation parallel to y = 4x + 9 through (0, 7).
  4. Write an equation perpendicular to slope 2 through (0, -5).
  5. A student uses (2, 10) as the y-intercept when writing an equation through (2, 10) with slope 3. Identify the misconception and write the correct equation.

Mastery Criteria

  • Writes equations from slope and y-intercept.
  • Finds y-intercepts from point-slope information.
  • Uses parallel and perpendicular slope relationships correctly.
  • Interprets linear equations in context.

Alignment: MLA.A1.LR.2; MLA.A1.LR.5.
