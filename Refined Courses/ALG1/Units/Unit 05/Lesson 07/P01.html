P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Comparing Linear, Quadratic, and Exponential Models

In this lesson, you will compare function families, identify key characteristics, determine best-fit models, and interpret real-world situations.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FN.6: I can compare key features of linear, quadratic, and exponential functions across representations.

MLA.A1.FN.7: I can select and interpret appropriate models for real-world situations.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.F.1.6; MA.912.F.1.8

Common Core Alignment: HSF.IF.C.9; HSF.LE.A.1; HSS.ID.B.6

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra / Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How linear, quadratic, and exponential models change differently.
  • How to compare key features from equations, tables, graphs, and contexts.
  • How to choose a model based on constant differences, second differences, or ratios.
  • How model choice affects interpretation of real-world situations.

What You Will Do

  • Classify models from tables and equations.
  • Compare rates of change and long-term behavior.
  • Match contexts to linear, quadratic, or exponential functions.
  • Justify model choice using mathematical evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence comparing model types.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that justify a best model.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Comparing Linear, Quadratic, and Exponential Models in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.FN.6, MLA.A1.FN.7. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
