P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Introduction to Quadratic Functions

In this lesson, you will recognize quadratic relationships and write quadratic functions from contexts, tables, and graphs.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.QE.2: I can recognize quadratic relationships and write quadratic functions from contexts, tables, and graphs.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.3.4

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.2; HSF.IF.C.8

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to recognize a quadratic function from an equation.
  • How to identify constant second differences in a table.
  • How to connect quadratic contexts to expressions and functions.
  • How parabolic graphs represent quadratic relationships.

What You Will Do

  • Classify relationships as linear, quadratic, or neither.
  • Write functions for area and patterned-growth contexts.
  • Use tables to look for constant first and second differences.
  • Match equations, tables, graphs, and contexts.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence that identifies quadratic structure.
  • Submit checkpoint responses explaining how a representation shows a quadratic relationship.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
