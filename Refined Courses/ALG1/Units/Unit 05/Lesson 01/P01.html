P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Polynomial Operations and Radical Readiness

In this lesson, you will add, subtract, multiply, and divide polynomial expressions by monomials while reviewing radicals, square roots, and perfect squares.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FND.3: I can use radical and square root reasoning with perfect squares.

MLA.A1.FND.6: I can add, subtract, and multiply polynomial expressions.

MLA.A1.FND.7: I can divide polynomial expressions by monomials.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.NSO.1.4; MA.912.AR.1.3; MA.912.AR.1.4

Common Core Alignment: HSN.RN.B.3; HSA.SSE.B.3; HSA.APR.A.1

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Number and Quantity; Integrating Essential Skills.

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine like terms in polynomial expressions.
  • How to multiply monomials and binomials using the distributive property.
  • How to divide polynomial terms by a monomial.
  • How perfect squares and square roots prepare you for quadratic work.

What You Will Do

  • Simplify polynomial sums and differences.
  • Use exponent rules when multiplying variable terms.
  • Divide each term of a polynomial by a monomial.
  • Identify perfect squares and simplify basic square roots.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing organized polynomial operations.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that explain operation choices.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Polynomial Operations and Radical Readiness in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.FND.3, MLA.A1.FND.6, MLA.A1.FND.7. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
