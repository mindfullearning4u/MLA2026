P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Task

Create a heading titled Graphing Quadratics. Define vertex, axis of symmetry, zeros, x-intercepts, y-intercept, minimum, and maximum.

For \(y=(x-2)^2-3\), identify the vertex and axis of symmetry. Explain how the equation shows each feature.

Key Reminder

In vertex form \(y=a(x-h)^2+k\), the vertex is \((h,k)\) and the axis of symmetry is \(x=h\).
