Zeros and Intercepts

Zeros are input values where the output is 0. On a graph, zeros appear as x-intercepts. In factored form, \(y=a(x-r_1)(x-r_2)\), the zeros are \(r_1\) and \(r_2\).

Notebook Task

For \(y=(x-5)(x+1)\), identify the zeros and write the x-intercepts as ordered pairs. Then find the axis of symmetry by locating the midpoint of the zeros.
