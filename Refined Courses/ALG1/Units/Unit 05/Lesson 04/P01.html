P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Graphing Quadratics

In this lesson, you will graph quadratic functions and interpret vertex, zeros, axis of symmetry, and other key features.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.QE.4: I can graph quadratic functions and identify key features.

MLA.A1.QE.5: I can interpret vertex, zeros, axis of symmetry, and other key features of quadratic graphs.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.3.6; MA.912.AR.3.7

Common Core Alignment: HSF.IF.B.4; HSA.SSE.B.3; HSF.IF.C.7a

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to graph a quadratic function as a parabola.
  • How to identify the vertex and axis of symmetry.
  • How zeros connect to x-intercepts.
  • How leading coefficient and graph features describe function behavior.

What You Will Do

  • Identify key features from vertex form and factored form.
  • Use symmetry to locate matching points on a parabola.
  • Interpret vertex and zeros in mathematical and real-world contexts.
  • Connect graph features to quadratic equations.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence that labels quadratic graph features.
  • Submit checkpoint responses explaining vertex, zeros, and axis of symmetry.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Graphing Quadratics in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.QE.4, MLA.A1.QE.5. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
