# ALG1 Unit 05 - Exponential Functions, Comparative Modeling, and Financial Literacy

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops exponential reasoning and comparative modeling across function families. Students classify exponential growth and decay, write and graph exponential functions, calculate and interpret average rate of change, compare linear and nonlinear behavior, determine the best model for a real-world situation, and apply these ideas to financial literacy contexts involving interest.

Emphasis is placed on model selection, feature comparison, and the connection between algebraic structure and real-world growth situations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- exponential representation and interpretation;
- comparative analysis of function families;
- rate of change reasoning;
- real-world model selection;
- financial literacy applications.

All assessment items require students to classify, represent, graph, compare, and apply exponential and comparative models using official course benchmarks.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of **80% mastery** across all required components, including:

- Notebook Evidence
- Checkpoints
- Quizzes
- Unit Assessment

Students are not advanced until mastery is achieved, ensuring that students can compare function families and apply exponential reasoning before the course culminates in data analysis and statistical modeling.

No single assessment determines mastery; students must demonstrate consistent performance across all components.
