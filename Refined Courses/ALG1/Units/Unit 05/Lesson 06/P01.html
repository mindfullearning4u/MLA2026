P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Exponential Functions: Growth and Decay

In this lesson, you will write, classify, graph, and interpret exponential growth and decay models.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.QE.7: I can write and classify exponential growth and decay functions.

MLA.A1.QE.8: I can graph exponential functions and interpret their features.

MLA.A1.QE.9: I can interpret exponential models in context.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.5.3; MA.912.AR.5.4; MA.912.AR.5.6

Common Core Alignment: HSF.LE.A.1; HSF.LE.A.2; HSF.IF.C.7e; HSF.IF.B.4

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to recognize multiplicative change in tables and contexts.
  • How to write exponential functions in the form \(y=a(b)^x\).
  • How to classify growth and decay from the base.
  • How to interpret initial value, growth factor, and decay factor.

What You Will Do

  • Identify exponential growth and decay from equations, tables, and contexts.
  • Write models using an initial amount and a common multiplier.
  • Evaluate exponential models for given input values.
  • Compare exponential change to linear and quadratic change.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing exponential model structure.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that interpret growth and decay factors.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
