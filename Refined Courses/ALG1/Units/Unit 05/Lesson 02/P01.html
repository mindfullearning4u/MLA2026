P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Factoring Quadratic Expressions

In this lesson, you will factor polynomial expressions and connect factors to zeros and x-intercepts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FND.8: I can factor polynomial expressions using structure.

MLA.A1.QE.3: I can connect factors of a quadratic expression to zeros and x-intercepts.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.1.7; MA.912.AR.3.5

Common Core Alignment: HSA.SSE.A.2; HSA.APR.B.3; HSF.IF.C.8; HSA.SSE.B.3

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Integrating Essential Skills; Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to factor out a greatest common factor.
  • How to factor quadratics of the form x2 + bx + c.
  • How to recognize simple difference of squares patterns.
  • How factors connect to zeros and x-intercepts.

What You Will Do

  • Identify factor pairs that multiply to a constant and add to a middle coefficient.
  • Rewrite quadratics as products of binomials.
  • Use factors to identify zeros.
  • Explain how factored form shows x-intercepts.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing factoring steps.
  • Submit checkpoint responses connecting factors to zeros.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Factoring Quadratic Expressions in a way that connects directly to MLA.A1.FND.8, MLA.A1.QE.3. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
