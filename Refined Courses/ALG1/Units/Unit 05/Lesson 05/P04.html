P04 Worked Example

Worked Example

Solve: \(x^2+2x-15=0\)

Factor: \((x+5)(x-3)=0\).

Use the zero-product property: \(x+5=0\) or \(x-3=0\).

Solutions: \(x=-5\) and \(x=3\).

Modeling connection: If this equation came from a length or time context, a negative solution may need to be rejected because negative length or negative elapsed time may not make sense.
