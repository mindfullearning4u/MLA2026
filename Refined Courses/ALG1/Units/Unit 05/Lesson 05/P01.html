P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Solving and Modeling Quadratics

In this lesson, you will solve quadratic equations and interpret quadratic models in context.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.QE.1: I can solve quadratic equations using appropriate methods and connect solutions to zeros.

MLA.A1.QE.6: I can interpret quadratic models and solutions in context.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks: MA.912.AR.3.1; MA.912.AR.3.8

Common Core Alignment: HSA.REI.B.4; HSA.CED.A.2; HSF.IF.B.4

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to solve quadratic equations by factoring and using the zero-product property.
  • How zeros connect to x-intercepts on a quadratic graph.
  • How to decide whether a solution makes sense in context.
  • How quadratic models represent height, area, and other nonlinear situations.

What You Will Do

  • Factor quadratic expressions before solving when possible.
  • Set each factor equal to zero to find solutions.
  • Interpret roots, zeros, and x-intercepts in real-world models.
  • Reject solutions that do not fit the context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing quadratic solving steps.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that explain solution meaning in context.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
