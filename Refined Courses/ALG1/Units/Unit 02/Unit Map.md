# ALG1 Unit 02 – Linear Equations and Inequalities

---

## Unit Overview

This unit builds on students’ understanding of algebraic expressions by introducing linear equations and inequalities as representations of relationships between quantities. Students develop skills in writing and solving linear equations and inequalities, representing solutions, and interpreting results in real-world contexts.

Emphasis is placed on understanding equations as balanced relationships, applying inverse operations, and analyzing and interpreting solutions within mathematical and real-world situations.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all standards addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- Conceptual understanding  
- Procedural fluency  
- Graphical representation  
- Real-world application  

All assessment items require students to write and solve linear equations and inequalities, represent solutions graphically, and interpret solutions within real-world contexts.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of **80% mastery** across all required components, including:

- Notebook Evidence  
- Checkpoints  
- Quizzes  
- Unit Assessment  

Students are not advanced until mastery is achieved, ensuring that foundational algebraic reasoning is solid before progressing to functions and more complex relationships.

No single assessment determines mastery; students must demonstrate consistent performance across all components.
