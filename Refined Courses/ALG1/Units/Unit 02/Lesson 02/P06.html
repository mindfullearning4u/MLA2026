The work completed below should be written in the same notebook as your lesson notes. Both your notes and your Independent Work will be included together when you submit your Notebook Evidence assignment.

Instructions

Show each step clearly. Use complete sentences when explaining reasoning or interpreting an answer.

Mathematical Visual: Two-Step Equation Flow

3x + 4 = 19
->
subtract 4
->
3x = 15
->
divide by 3
->
x = 5

Part A: Core Skill Practice

  1. Solve: 3x + 4 = 19.
  2. Solve: 5y - 6 = 24.
  3. Solve: n / 4 + 2 = 9.
  4. Solve: -2a + 7 = -11.
  5. Check your answer to Problem 1 by substitution.

Part B: Explain Your Reasoning

  1. Using Problem 1, explain why subtracting 4 must happen before dividing by 3.
  2. Using Problem 2, explain how the inverse operations undo the equation in reverse order.
  3. Write one sentence describing how a two-step equation is different from a one-step equation.

Part C: Error Analysis

A student solved Problem 2 this way:

5y - 6 = 24
5y = 18
y = 3.6

  1. Identify the mistake in the first solving step.
  2. Correct the solution for 5y - 6 = 24.
  3. Explain why the constant must be undone before the coefficient.
