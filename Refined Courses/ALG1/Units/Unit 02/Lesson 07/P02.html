P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Use your notebook to organize equation strategies from Lessons 1-6.

Vocabulary

  • Equation type: The structure of an equation, such as one-step, two-step, multi-step, variables on both sides, or literal.
  • Strategy selection: Choosing a solving method based on equation structure.
  • Application: A situation where an equation represents a real-world relationship.
  • Reasonableness: Whether a solution makes sense in the original problem.

Strategy Organizer

Create a table with four columns: Equation, Type, First Step, and Reason.

  1. Classify x + 7 = 19.
  2. Classify 3x - 5 = 22.
  3. Classify 4(x - 2) = 20.
  4. Classify 5x + 8 = 2x + 23.
  5. Classify A = bh when solving for h.

Key Idea

Mixed applications require decision-making. Before solving, identify what kind of equation you have and what operation or structure should be handled first.
