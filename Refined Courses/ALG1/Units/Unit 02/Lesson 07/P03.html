P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Practice choosing methods before solving.

Mixed Practice

For each item, identify the equation type, solve, and check your answer.

  1. n - 9 = 16
  2. 2x + 11 = 35
  3. 3(x + 4) = 30
  4. 7x - 5 = 4x + 16
  5. Solve d = rt for t.

Common Mistakes

  • Starting to solve before identifying the equation structure.
  • Forgetting to distribute before combining like terms.
  • Moving variable terms but not constant terms.
  • Solving a context problem without interpreting the answer.
