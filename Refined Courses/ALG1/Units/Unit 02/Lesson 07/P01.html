P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Mixed Equation Applications

In this lesson, you will select and solve appropriate equation types in varied contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA Standards Covered: No new primary MLA standard; cumulative support.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks Covered: No new primary benchmark.

Common Core Alignment: Review support.

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to decide whether a problem needs a one-step, two-step, multi-step, variables-on-both-sides, or literal-equation strategy.
  • How to choose an efficient first step based on equation structure.
  • How to interpret equation solutions in context.
  • How to check solutions for accuracy and reasonableness.

What You Will Do

  • Classify equations by structure before solving.
  • Write equations from application contexts.
  • Solve mixed equation types using inverse operations and equivalence.
  • Explain what each solution means.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing method selection and solving steps.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that solve mixed equation applications.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can choose an equation-solving strategy, solve accurately, and explain the solution in context.
