Checkpoint Task: Equations with Variables on Both Sides

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Solve 6x + 4 = 2x + 28 and check your solution.
  2. Solve 5x + 8 = 5x - 3 and classify the solution type.
  3. Solve 4(x - 2) = 4x - 8 and classify the solution type.
  4. A student says 3x + 7 = 3x + 2 has solution x = 0. Identify the misconception, correct the classification, and explain why.

Mastery Criteria

  • Moves variable terms correctly using inverse operations.
  • Simplifies both sides before classifying the equation.
  • Identifies one solution, no solution, and infinitely many solutions.
  • Explains solution-type reasoning clearly.
