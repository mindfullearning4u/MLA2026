P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Multi-Step Equations

In this lesson, you will solve multi-step linear equations in mathematical and real-world contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.AR.1: I can write and solve multi-step linear equations from real-world contexts.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.2.1

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.1; HSA.REI.B.3

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How to simplify each side of an equation before isolating the variable.
  • How to use the distributive property in an equation.
  • How to combine like terms while preserving equality.
  • How to write and solve a multi-step equation from a context.

What You Will Do

  • Distribute when parentheses appear.
  • Combine like terms before using inverse operations.
  • Solve equations with rational coefficients.
  • Interpret solutions in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence with organized multi-step equation work.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that include equation writing, solving, and interpretation.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can write and solve multi-step linear equations from real-world contexts and explain what the solution means.
