P04 Worked Example

Study each example carefully. Every step is shown because the reasoning matters as much as the final answer.

Worked Examples

Example 1: Solve 2(x - 3) + 5 = 17.
2x - 6 + 5 = 17
2x - 1 = 17
2x = 18
x = 9

Example 2: Solve 1⁄2x + 4 = 10.
1⁄2x = 6
x = 12

Example 3: A student buys a notebook for $3 and pens that cost $2 each. The total is $15. Solve 3 + 2p = 15.
2p = 12
p = 6 pens.

Common Mistake

A common mistake is distributing to only one term inside parentheses. In 2(x - 3), the 2 must multiply both x and -3.
