Checkpoint Task: Multi-Step Equations

Complete all parts. Show work clearly and explain your reasoning in complete sentences where required.

  1. Solve 4(x + 3) = 36 and check your solution.
  2. Solve 7x - 2x + 6 = 31 and check your solution.
  3. A rental costs $20 plus $8 per hour. The total is $68. Write and solve an equation for the number of hours.
  4. A student solves 2(x + 5) = 30 by writing 2x + 5 = 30. Identify the misconception, correct the work, and explain why the correction matters.

Mastery Criteria

  • Distributes accurately when parentheses are present.
  • Combines like terms correctly.
  • Uses inverse operations in a logical order.
  • Writes and interprets equations from contexts.
