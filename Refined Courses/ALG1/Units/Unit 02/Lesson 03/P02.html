Notebook Title

Lesson 3: Multi-Step Equations

Key Vocabulary

  • Multi-step equation: An equation that requires more than two actions to solve.
  • Distributive property: Multiplying a factor by each term inside parentheses.
  • Like terms: Terms with the same variable part and exponent.
  • Rational coefficient: A coefficient that may be an integer, fraction, or decimal.
  • Interpret: Explain what the solution means in the context.

Process Notes

  • Distribute first when parentheses appear.
  • Combine like terms on the same side of the equation.
  • Use inverse operations to isolate the variable.
  • Check the solution and interpret it if the problem has a context.
