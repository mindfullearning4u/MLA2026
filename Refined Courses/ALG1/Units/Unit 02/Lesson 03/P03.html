Copy These Examples

Example 1: Solve 3(x + 4) = 30.
Distribute: 3x + 12 = 30.
Subtract 12: 3x = 18.
Divide by 3: x = 6.

Example 2: Solve 5x + 2x - 8 = 20.
Combine like terms: 7x - 8 = 20.
Add 8: 7x = 28.
Divide by 7: x = 4.

Example 3: A gym charges a $10 fee plus $6 per class. If the total is $46, solve 10 + 6c = 46.
Subtract 10: 6c = 36.
Divide by 6: c = 6 classes.

Common Mistakes

Do not skip distribution or combine unlike terms. Simplify each side correctly before isolating the variable.

Incorrect: Skipping a step or applying a rule before checking the structure of the expression, equation, graph, or situation.

Teachable Explanation: Algebra errors usually happen when a step is skipped or a rule is used without checking the expression, equation, graph, or context first.
