Complete a mixed readiness set before the Unit Assessment.

Mixed Review

  1. Solve 2x + 9 = 31.
  2. Solve 4(x - 3) = 28.
  3. Solve 9x + 5 = 4x + 40.
  4. Solve A = bh for h.
  5. A subscription costs $12 plus $7 per month. The total is $82. Write and solve an equation.
  6. Decide whether 3x + 6 = 3(x + 2) has one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions. Explain.

Self-Check
