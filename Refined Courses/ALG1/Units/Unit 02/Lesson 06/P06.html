P06 Independent Work

Complete this work independently in your notebook. Show all variables, equations, solving steps, and interpretations.

Independent Work

  1. A concert ticket has a $9 service fee plus $18 per ticket. The total cost is $81. How many tickets were bought?
  2. A savings account starts with $40 and increases by $12 each week. The balance is $136. How many weeks have passed?
  3. Five more than three times a number is 44. Find the number.
  4. A rectangle has perimeter 54 meters and width 10 meters. Find the length.
  5. A student writes 7x + 3 = 31 for a situation but says the solution is 31. Explain the error and solve correctly.
