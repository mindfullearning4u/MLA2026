P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Problem Solving with Equations

In this lesson, you will translate real-world contexts into equations and interpret solutions.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA Standards Covered: No new primary MLA standard; application support.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks Covered: No new primary benchmark.

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.1 support.

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify quantities in a real-world context.
  • How to define a variable for an unknown quantity.
  • How to translate words and relationships into equations.
  • How to interpret a solution in the original context.

What You Will Do

  • Read a context carefully and identify the unknown.
  • Write an equation that models the situation.
  • Solve the equation using equation-solving skills from this unit.
  • Check whether the answer makes sense in the situation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing variables, equations, solving steps, and interpretations.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that model and solve real-world situations.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can write and solve an equation from a real-world situation and explain what the solution means.
