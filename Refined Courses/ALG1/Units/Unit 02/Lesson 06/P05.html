P05 Guided Practice

This practice is not graded. Try each question, then check the feedback to understand the reasoning.

Guided Practice

  1. A skateboard rental costs $8 plus $5 per hour. The total cost is $28. Write and solve an equation for the number of hours.
  2. A student has 12 stickers and buys packs with 4 stickers each. The student has 36 stickers total. Write and solve an equation for the number of packs.
  3. The sum of a number and 9 is 31. Write and solve an equation.
  4. A rectangle has perimeter 38 feet and length 12 feet. Write and solve an equation for the width.
  5. Explain why the final answer should include units or context.
