P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: One-Step Equations

In this lesson, you will solve equations using inverse operations and explain what a solution means.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA Standards Covered: No new primary MLA standard; prerequisite support.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks Covered: No new primary benchmark.

Common Core Alignment: HSA.REI.B.3 support.

SAT Domain Connection: Algebra.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Algebra.

What You Will Learn

  • How an equation represents two expressions with the same value.
  • How inverse operations undo addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • How to isolate a variable in a one-step equation.
  • How to check a solution by substituting it back into the original equation.

What You Will Do

  • Identify the operation connected to the variable.
  • Use the inverse operation on both sides of an equation.
  • Solve addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division equations.
  • Explain and correct common one-step equation mistakes.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence with organized equation-solving work.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that explain inverse operations and solution checks.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can solve equations by using inverse operations, show organized work, and check whether my solution makes the original equation true.
