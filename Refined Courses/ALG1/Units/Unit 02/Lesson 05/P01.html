P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Literal Equations and Formulas

In this lesson, you will rearrange formulas to isolate a specified variable.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA.A1.FND.5: I can rearrange formulas and equations to isolate a chosen quantity.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmark: MA.912.AR.1.2

Common Core Alignment: HSA.CED.A.4

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Integrating Essential Skills.

What You Will Learn

  • How a formula can be rearranged for different variables.
  • How inverse operations isolate a chosen quantity.
  • How to solve formulas involving addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • How formula rearrangement supports geometry, finance, and scientific applications.

What You Will Do

  • Identify the target variable.
  • Undo operations in a logical order.
  • Keep formulas equivalent while rearranging them.
  • Check rearranged formulas by substitution.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence showing formula rearrangement steps.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that isolate specified variables.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can rearrange a formula to isolate the variable I need.
