Copy These Examples

Example 1: Solve d = rt for r.
Divide both sides by t: r = d⁄t.

Example 2: Solve y = mx + b for b.
Subtract mx from both sides: b = y - mx.

Example 3: Solve P = 2l + 2w for l.
Subtract 2w: P - 2w = 2l.
Divide by 2: l = P - 2w⁄2.

Common Mistakes

Do not substitute numbers unless the problem asks you to evaluate. In a literal equation, the goal is to isolate a variable symbolically.
