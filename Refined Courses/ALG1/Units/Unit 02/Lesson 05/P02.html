Notebook Title

Lesson 5: Literal Equations and Formulas

Key Vocabulary

  • Literal equation: An equation with two or more variables.
  • Formula: A rule that relates quantities.
  • Target variable: The variable being isolated.
  • Equivalent formula: A rearranged formula with the same meaning.

Process Notes

  • Circle or name the target variable.
  • Treat every other variable as a known quantity.
  • Use inverse operations to isolate the target variable.
  • Keep both sides balanced.
