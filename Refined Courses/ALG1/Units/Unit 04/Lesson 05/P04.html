Worked Example 1: Identify increasing behavior

Problem: A function has values f(0)=2, f(1)=5, f(2)=8, and f(3)=11. Describe the behavior.

Step 1: Read the outputs in order: 2, 5, 8, 11.

Step 2: The outputs increase by 3 each time.

Step 3: The function is increasing over the shown interval.

Worked Example 2: Interpret intercept in context

Problem: A graph crosses the y-axis at (0, 12). The graph models dollars in an account after x weeks.

Step 1: The y-intercept occurs when x = 0.

Step 2: x = 0 means before any weeks have passed.

Step 3: The account started with 12 dollars.

Worked Example 3: Interpret absolute value behavior

Problem: The graph of y = |x - 2| has its lowest point at (2, 0). What does this key feature mean?

Step 1: The lowest point is the vertex.

Step 2: At x = 2, the output is 0.

Step 3: The function decreases until x = 2 and increases after x = 2.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Describing a graph as increasing just because one point is higher than another.

Correct: Describe behavior over an interval by tracking how outputs change as inputs increase.
