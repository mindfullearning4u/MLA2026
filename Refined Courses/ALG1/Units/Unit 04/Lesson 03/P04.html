P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Each example names the mathematical object, shows the work, and explains the decision a careful teacher would make.

Study all three worked examples before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Evaluate from an equation

Problem: Evaluate f(5) for f(x) = 2x - 3.

Step 1: f(5) means x = 5.

Step 2: Substitute: f(5) = 2(5) - 3.

Step 3: f(5) = 10 - 3.

Step 4: f(5) = 7.

Worked Example 3: Interpret the output

Problem: A(t) = 20t + 50 gives the amount saved after t weeks. Interpret A(4) = 130.

Step 1: The input 4 represents 4 weeks.

Step 2: The output 130 represents the amount saved.

Step 3: A(4) = 130 means after 4 weeks, 130 dollars have been saved.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Giving only the number without explaining what it means.

Correct: When there is a context, include units and explain the input and output.

Teachable Explanation: Check the exact function object first: ordered pairs, notation, equation, graph, table, or context. Then choose the method that matches that object.
