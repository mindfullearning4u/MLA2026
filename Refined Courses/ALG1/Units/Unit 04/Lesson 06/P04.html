P04 Worked Example

Worked Example

A gym charges a $10 sign-up fee plus $8 per class. The context can be represented by \(C(x)=8x+10\), where \(x\) is the number of classes.

Connect representations.

  • Equation: \(C(x)=8x+10\)
  • Table: \(C(0)=10\), \(C(1)=18\), \(C(2)=26\), \(C(3)=34\)
  • Graph: The y-intercept is 10 and the output increases by 8 for each 1-class increase.
  • Context: The $10 is the starting fee, and $8 is added for each class.
