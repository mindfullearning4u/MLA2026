Mixed Practice

Use \(f(x)=2x+3\), \(g(x)=|x-4|\), and a table with \(h(0)=5\), \(h(1)=9\), and \(h(2)=13\).

  • Evaluate \(f(6)\).
  • Identify the minimum output of \(g(x)\).
  • Find the rate of change of \(h(x)\).
  • Compare the y-intercepts of \(f\) and \(h\).
