Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together

In this lesson, you will synthesize function notation, representations, behavior, and comparisons.

Standards Covered in This Unit

MLA.A1.FN.1 Function notation and function relationships.

MLA.A1.FN.2 Evaluating functions and interpreting outputs.

MLA.A1.FN.3 Interpreting key features of functions.

MLA.A1.FN.4 Connecting multiple representations of functions.

MLA.A1.LR.4 Comparing key features of linear functions.

MLA.A1.AR.8 Interpreting absolute value behavior and related key features.

What You Have Learned

  • How to identify functions from mappings, tables, graphs, and contexts.
  • How to use and interpret function notation.
  • How to evaluate functions and identify domain and range.
  • How to interpret graph behavior and absolute value features.
  • How to connect and compare functions across representations.

How You Will Demonstrate Mastery

  • Complete final Unit 4 review tasks in your notebook.
  • Submit notebook evidence showing organized function reasoning.
  • Complete the Unit Assessment with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

This lesson helps you understand Putting It All Together in a way that connects directly to the review standards for this synthesis lesson. You will practice reading the problem, choosing the correct representation or method, and explaining what the answer means.
