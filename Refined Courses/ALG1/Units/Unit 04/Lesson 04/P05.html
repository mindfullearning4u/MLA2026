P05 Guided Practice

Practice With Support

Identify the domain and range. Explain whether each set is restricted by context.

Guided Practice Questions

  1. Find the domain of (1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 8).
  2. Find the range of (1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 8).
  3. For x: 0, 1, 2 and f(x): 10, 15, 20, identify domain and range.
  4. A function gives cost for whole numbers of tickets. What inputs are reasonable?
  5. A container holds at most 30 gallons. What range restriction makes sense for gallons in the container?
