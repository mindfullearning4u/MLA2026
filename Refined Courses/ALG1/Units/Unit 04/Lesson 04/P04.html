P04 Worked Example

Step-by-step explanation: Follow each example in order. Each example names the mathematical object, shows the work, and explains the decision a careful teacher would make.

Study all three worked examples before working independently.

Worked Example 1: Find domain and range from ordered pairs

Problem: Find the domain and range of {(1, 4), (2, 6), (3, 8)}.

Step 1: Domain means the input values, or x-values: 1, 2, 3.

Step 2: Range means the output values, or y-values: 4, 6, 8.

Step 3: Domain = {1, 2, 3}; Range = {4, 6, 8}.

Worked Example 2: Find reasonable domain in context

Problem: A movie ticket costs 9 dollars. C(t) = 9t gives the cost for t tickets. What domain makes sense?

Step 1: t is the number of tickets.

Step 2: Tickets cannot be negative or fractional in this context.

Step 3: Reasonable inputs are whole numbers such as 0, 1, 2, 3, and so on.
