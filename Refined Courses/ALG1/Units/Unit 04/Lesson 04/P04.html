P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1: Table

A table shows x: 0, 2, 4, 6 and y: 3, 7, 11, 15.

Domain: 0, 2, 4, 6

Range: 3, 7, 11, 15

Worked Example 2: Context Restriction

A water bottle is filled over time. Time cannot be negative, so the domain begins at 0.

If the bottle holds at most 24 ounces, the range is from 0 ounces to 24 ounces.

Common Mistakes

  • Reversing domain and range.
  • Listing outputs as inputs.
  • Ignoring context restrictions such as time, money, or item counts.
  • Using negative or fractional inputs when the situation requires whole-number counts.
