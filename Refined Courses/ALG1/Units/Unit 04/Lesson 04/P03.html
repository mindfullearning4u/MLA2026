P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Domain and Range From Representations

Look for input values to find domain and output values to find range.

Example A: Ordered Pairs

For (1, 4), (2, 7), (3, 10):

Domain: 1, 2, 3

Range: 4, 7, 10

Example B: Context

A movie ticket function gives total cost for whole numbers of tickets.

Reasonable inputs are whole numbers such as 0, 1, 2, 3, and so on. Negative tickets or half tickets are not reasonable.

Notebook Task

Find the domain and range for each set.

  1. (0, 5), (1, 8), (2, 11)
  2. (-2, 4), (0, 0), (2, 4)
  3. x: 1, 3, 5 and f(x): 10, 14, 18
