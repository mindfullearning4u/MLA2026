Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Domain and Range

In this lesson, you will identify reasonable inputs and outputs from graphs, tables, equations, and contexts.

Standards Covered in This Lesson

MLA Standards Covered: No new primary MLA standard; conceptual support.

Florida B.E.S.T. Benchmarks Covered: No new primary benchmark.

Common Core Alignment: HSF.IF.B support.

SAT Domain Connection: Advanced Math.

ACT Domain Connection: Preparing for Higher Math - Functions / Modeling.

What You Will Learn

  • How domain represents possible input values.
  • How range represents possible output values.
  • How to identify domain and range from tables and graphs.
  • How context can restrict reasonable inputs and outputs.

What You Will Do

  • List domain and range from ordered pairs and tables.
  • Read domain and range from graph descriptions.
  • Decide whether values are reasonable in context.
  • Explain domain and range using input-output language.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Complete notebook evidence identifying domain and range.
  • Submit checkpoint responses that explain contextual restrictions.
  • Complete the Lesson Quiz with at least 80% mastery.
