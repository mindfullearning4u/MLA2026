# ALG1 Unit 04 - Quadratic and Absolute Value Functions

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops nonlinear reasoning through quadratic and absolute value equations and functions. Students write, solve, graph, and interpret quadratic relationships; analyze vertices, zeros, and other key features; study functional transformations; and extend graph interpretation to absolute value functions.

Emphasis is placed on feature analysis, equivalent representations, contextual interpretation, and the relationship between algebraic forms and graphs.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- nonlinear function representation;
- feature interpretation;
- graphing and transformation analysis;
- contextual reasoning and constraints;
- equation solving.

All assessment items require students to interpret, represent, solve, and graph quadratic and absolute value relationships using official course benchmarks.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students must achieve an overall course grade of 80% or higher to demonstrate mastery of the unit.

Lesson quizzes require a minimum score of 80% to progress, with up to two attempts permitted.

The Unit Assessment must be completed but does not independently determine mastery; overall performance across Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment is used to determine advancement.
