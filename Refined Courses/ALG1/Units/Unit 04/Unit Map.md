# ALG1 Unit 04 – Quadratic Functions and Modeling

---

## Unit Overview

This unit extends students’ understanding of functions to include quadratic relationships. Students explore the structure and behavior of quadratic functions, represent them in multiple forms, analyze key features, and solve quadratic equations.

Emphasis is placed on interpreting graphs of quadratic functions, identifying vertex and zeros, understanding transformations, and modeling real-world situations using quadratic relationships.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all standards addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- Conceptual understanding  
- Procedural fluency  
- Graphical interpretation  
- Real-world modeling  

All assessment items require students to analyze quadratic functions, determine key features, solve quadratic equations, and interpret quadratic models within real-world contexts.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of **80% mastery** across all required components, including:

- Notebook Evidence  
- Checkpoints  
- Quizzes  
- Unit Assessment  

Students are not advanced until mastery is achieved, ensuring that understanding of quadratic relationships is solid before progressing to exponential functions and more advanced algebraic topics.

No single assessment determines mastery; students must demonstrate consistent performance across all components.
