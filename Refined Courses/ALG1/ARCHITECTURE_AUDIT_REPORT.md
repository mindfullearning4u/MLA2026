# Algebra 1 Course Architecture and Alignment Audit

Status: architecture approved on 2026-08-03; Moodle production remains blocked pending assessment-licensing and implementation authorization.

## Executive decision

Use the proposed eight-unit, 64-lesson architecture aligned to **enVision Florida B.E.S.T. Algebra 1 (2023)**. Each unit also begins with a separate ungraded pretest.

The verified comparison found all **46 required Florida Algebra 1 benchmarks** in at least one of the 50 audited Savvas lesson standards panels. Six additional Savvas-listed codes are supporting/enrichment: `MA.912.AR.4.2`, `MA.912.F.2.3`, `MA.912.F.3.1`, `MA.912.DP.2.5`, `MA.912.DP.3.2`, and `MA.912.DP.3.3`.

Direct metadata coverage is necessary but does not alone prove instructional depth, assessment quality, or reuse rights.

Every unit has an ungraded pretest before Lesson 1. It diagnoses support need; 90% or higher makes a learner eligible for a separately determined accelerated pathway. It is excluded from the final grade.

## Assessment architecture

- Formative evidence may use Savvas `Do You Understand?`, `Do You Know How?`, `Try It!`, MathXL practice/problem solving, mixed review, and adaptive practice.
- Quizzes may be lesson-level or clustered where Savvas supplies a coherent question source. Every quiz must name its source.
- The graded unit assessment is inside the final lesson of every unit.
- The final lesson is a genuine synthesis experience, never an assessment-only shell.
- Question transfer is outside this phase; licensing, answer-key access, and exportability require confirmation.

## Standards findings

1. Required inventory: 46 unique MLA rows mapped one-to-one to 46 Florida benchmark codes.
2. Savvas direct metadata coverage: 46 of 46 required codes; no code-level gap.
3. Financial literacy `MA.912.FL.3.2` and `.3.4` appear in Savvas linear-function and exponential-growth contexts.
4. Polynomial division `MA.912.AR.1.4` appears in Savvas Lesson 6-4 and must be explicit in Unit 5.
5. Common Core, SAT, and ACT mappings are secondary labels, not Florida compliance evidence.
6. Savvas Topic 2 has heading/objective inconsistencies. The displayed `2-1` page shows an absolute-value objective, and `2-2` shows slope-intercept language. Human confirmation is required before production.

## Moodle resource requirements

Each unit needs a landing page, zero-weight pretest, support-routing metadata, lessons, only mapped quiz objects, a final synthesis lesson, and its graded unit assessment. Each lesson record needs the MLA ID, sequence, objective, Florida benchmarks, Savvas source and URL, named resources, vocabulary decision, formative evidence, quiz relationship, notebook task, accessibility notes, and attribution/licensing status.

Gradebook controls must exclude pretests. The architecture does not define accelerated content; it only preserves a light-notebook-task field for learners routed separately.

## Pacing

The architecture contains 64 lessons plus 8 pretests. At five meetings weekly, 64 lessons equal 12.8 weeks before pretests, review, intervention, holidays, and EOC preparation. A full-year implementation should reserve 32–36 weeks; local bell schedules determine whether a lesson spans one or more meetings. Unit size is content-driven, not capped at eight lessons.

## Current versus proposed

The current v3 map uses six broad units and does not consistently expose the approved pretest, clustered-quiz, and final-synthesis model. The proposed eight units preserve coherent Savvas sequences. Existing production files remain unchanged until approval.

## Traceability and gate

Required trace: `Florida benchmark → MLA statement → proposed lesson → Savvas lesson/URL → objective → activity → formative/quiz evidence → synthesis/unit-assessment evidence`.
