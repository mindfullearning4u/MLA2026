## Unit 1 purpose

Unit 1 is diagnostic and prerequisite-focused, not a random review. Proposed lessons: mathematical habits and diagnostic; rational-number fluency; fractions/decimals/percents; ratios/rates/proportional reasoning; evaluating and simplifying expressions; coordinate-plane and graph-reading readiness. Skills are used later in equation solving, slope, exponentials, quadratics, systems, and data analysis. Diagnostic acceleration may permit demonstrated mastery to shorten practice, but the evidence gate remains.

## Assessment placement

- Unit 1: Algebra 1 Readiness Test plus targeted evidence checks.
- Units 2-8: corresponding topic readiness checks, lesson checkpoints, modeling/STEM performance evidence, topic assessments, and cumulative assessments at the existing Savvas breakpoints.
- EOC practice and progress monitoring remain course-level resources.
