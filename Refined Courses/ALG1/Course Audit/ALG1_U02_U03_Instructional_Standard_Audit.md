# ALG1 Units 2-3 Instructional Standard Compliance Audit

## Executive Summary
ALG1 Units 2 and 3 were checked and revised against the approved MLA online lesson instructional model established during the Statistics Lesson 1 standardization pass and first enforced on ALG1 Unit 1.

Final Recommendation: PASS WITH REVISIONS

## Strengths
- Units 2 and 3 retain their ALG1 lesson sequence, standards alignment, and assessment intent.
- All lessons include P01-P07.
- GIFT assessment files were not changed.
- Lesson pages now include consistent Teacher of Record support language.

## Findings
- Teacher of Record support reminders were missing from lesson pages.
- Several P04 pages did not expose three separate Worked Example headings.
- Common Mistake sections needed red/green correction structure.
- P06 pages needed consistent Instructions, Part A, Part B, and Part C structure.
- Lesson 8 pages needed normalization to the same P04/P06/P07 page roles used in the approved model.

## Required Corrections
Completed:
- Added the approved Teacher of Record help reminder to every Unit 2 and Unit 3 lesson page.
- Normalized P04 pages to exactly three worked examples plus a red/green Common Mistake section.
- Normalized P06 pages to Independent Work with Instructions, Part A, Part B, and Part C.
- Normalized P07 pages to include Teacher of Record information, one Submission Workflow title, checkpoint task, and mastery criteria.
- Normalized lesson.json page titles for Lessons 1-8 in both units.

## Recommended Improvements
- Continue applying this same instructional standard audit to ALG1 Units 4-6.
- Keep GIFT notation Moodle-safe; apply professional notation only on lesson pages.
