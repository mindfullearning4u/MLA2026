## Why Each Visual Was Added

| Lesson | Instructional Purpose |
|---|---|
| Lesson 03 | Students convert and compare fractions, decimals, percents, ratios, and unit rates. The tables make equivalence and per-one reasoning visible. |
| Lesson 04 | Students evaluate expressions and substitute values. The operation hierarchy and substitution table make sequence and replacement visible. |
| Lesson 05 | Students identify variables, coefficients, constants, and terms. The expression-part model connects vocabulary to symbols. |
| Lesson 06 | Students apply exponent properties. The expansion and rule tables make exponent structure visible before students select a rule. |
| Lesson 07 | Students combine like terms and distribute. The grouping and distribution visuals show why only like terms combine and how a factor applies to each term. |
| Lesson 08 | Students synthesize Unit 1 skills. The synthesis table reminds students which visual structure supports each kind of Unit 1 task. |
