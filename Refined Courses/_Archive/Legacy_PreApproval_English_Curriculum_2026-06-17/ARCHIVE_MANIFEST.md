# Legacy Pre-Approval English Curriculum Archive

## Archive Date

June 17, 2026

## Archive Purpose

This archive contains English I, English II, English III, and English IV curriculum documents that existed before the approved MLA English governance sequence was completed.

These documents were archived for compliance and accreditation traceability. They should not be used as active curriculum source documents unless formally reviewed, approved, and regenerated through the current MLA English curriculum development process.

## Archived Document Types

- Course overview files
- Unit overview files
- Benchmark master tracker files

## Reason for Archive

The archived documents include pre-approved curriculum decisions such as benchmark-to-unit placement, lesson breakdowns, assessment references, and primary mapping language.

Current MLA English governance requires approved benchmark inventories, MLA standard inventories, crosswalks, and mappings before active course overviews, unit overviews, and tracker documents are regenerated.

## Active Source of Record

For English I, the active source documents remain in:

`Refined Courses/ENGLISH I/Course Audit`

English II, English III, and English IV require their own approved analysis, numbering, crosswalk, and mapping phases before regenerated planning documents should become active.

## Archive Scope

No files were deleted. Files were moved only for governance control.
