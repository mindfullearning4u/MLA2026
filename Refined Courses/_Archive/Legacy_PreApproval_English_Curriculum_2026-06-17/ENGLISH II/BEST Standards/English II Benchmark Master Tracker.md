## Supporting Skills Progression

| Unit | Primary Focus | Recurring Supporting Skills |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Unit 01 | Advanced Foundations of Reading and Writing | Complex text reading, paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, and analytical response |
| Unit 02 | Comparative Literary Analysis and Theme | Paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, narrative writing, figurative language, and comparative literary analysis |
| Unit 03 | Informational Analysis, Rhetoric, and Argument | Paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, central idea analysis, rhetoric, claims, evidence, reasoning, and formal argumentative writing |
| Unit 04 | Research, Synthesis, and Expository Writing | Informational reading, central idea analysis, academic vocabulary, conventions, source evaluation, synthesis, expository writing, citation, and publication |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Advanced Revision | Reading interpretation, academic vocabulary, conventions, word choice, feedback, audience awareness, clarity, cohesion, and revision across writing types |
| Unit 06 | Synthesis, Presentation, and Independent Application | Reading synthesis, argument or exposition, academic vocabulary, conventions, credible evidence, oral presentation, digital presentation, and multimedia communication |
