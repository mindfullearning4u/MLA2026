# ENGLISH II - Unit 02 Overview: Comparative Literary Analysis and Theme

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops comparative literary analysis across multiple texts. Students analyze how authors develop meaning and style, analyze and compare universal themes, examine coming of age experiences and conflicting perspectives, analyze poetry, interpret figurative language, analyze adaptations, and write narratives using pacing to create tension, mood, and tone.

Emphasis is placed on comparing texts, analyzing author’s craft and style, evaluating perspective, interpreting ambiguity, and supporting analysis with textual evidence. Students connect literary reading to purposeful narrative writing.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through comparative literary analysis, narrative writing, vocabulary application, and evidence-based explanation.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- literary elements and layers of meaning;
- comparison of universal themes;
- coming of age and conflicting perspectives;
- poetic ambiguity and figurative language;
- adaptation analysis;
- narrative writing with pace, tension, mood, and tone.

Assessment items require students to analyze and compare literary texts, explain how authors create meaning and ambiguity, analyze theme development and perspective, interpret figurative language, analyze adaptations, and write narrative text using pacing to create tension, mood, and/or tone.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit strengthens students' ability to interpret complex literary meaning across texts. Students connect author choices, structure, perspective, language, and theme to explain how literature communicates layered meanings across genres and cultures.
