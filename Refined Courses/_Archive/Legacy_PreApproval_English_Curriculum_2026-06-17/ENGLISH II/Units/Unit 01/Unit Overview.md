# ENGLISH II - Unit 01 Overview: Advanced Foundations of Reading and Writing

---

## Unit Overview

This unit strengthens foundational literacy skills through the analysis of complex texts. Students annotate complex texts, analyze how key elements contribute to meaning, paraphrase accurately, evaluate textual evidence, integrate academic vocabulary, apply conventions, and develop structured analytical responses.

Emphasis is placed on close reading, annotation, textual evidence, vocabulary precision, conventions, and clarity in writing. Students move from comprehension toward deeper interpretation and supported analysis.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through close reading, analysis, paraphrasing, vocabulary application, conventions, and evidence-based analytical response.

| ELA.10.V.1.1 | Integrate academic vocabulary appropriate to grade level in speaking and writing. |

---

## Prerequisite and Supporting Concepts

Students are expected to have prior knowledge of:
- close reading and annotation strategies;
- paraphrasing and quoting accurately;
- using evidence to support written analysis;
- applying standard English conventions;
- using academic vocabulary in discussion and writing.

These concepts support complex text analysis, evidence-based response, and academic communication in this unit.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- close reading and analysis;
- paraphrasing and comprehension;
- academic vocabulary use;
- standard English conventions;
- evidence-based analytical response.

Assessment items require students to analyze how key elements contribute to meaning in texts, paraphrase accurately, integrate academic vocabulary, apply conventions, and write structured analytical responses supported by textual evidence.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit builds the analytical reading and writing habits used throughout English II. Students learn to interpret complex texts, analyze how meaning is constructed, restate ideas accurately, use academic language precisely, apply conventions, and support analysis with evidence.
