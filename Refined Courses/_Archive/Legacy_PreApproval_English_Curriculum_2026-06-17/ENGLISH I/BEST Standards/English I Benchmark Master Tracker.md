## Supporting Skills Progression

| Unit | Primary Focus | Recurring Supporting Skills |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Unit 01 | Foundations of Reading and Writing | Foundational reading, paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, and evidence-based response |
| Unit 02 | Literary Analysis and Theme | Paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, narrative writing, figurative language, and evidence-based literary analysis |
| Unit 03 | Informational Text and Argument | Paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, central idea support, rhetoric, claims, evidence, and argumentative writing |
| Unit 04 | Writing Development and Research | Informational reading, central idea support, academic vocabulary, conventions, source evaluation, expository writing, and audience-aware publication |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Revision | Reading interpretation, academic vocabulary, conventions, word choice, feedback, clarity, cohesion, and revision across writing types |
| Unit 06 | Synthesis, Presentation, and Application | Reading synthesis, argument or exposition, academic vocabulary, conventions, credible evidence, oral presentation, and digital communication |
