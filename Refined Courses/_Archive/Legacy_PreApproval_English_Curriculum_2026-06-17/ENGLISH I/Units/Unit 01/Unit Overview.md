# ENGLISH I - Unit 01 Overview: Foundations of Reading and Writing

---

## Unit Overview

This unit establishes the reading and writing foundation for English I. Students practice close reading of grade-level complex texts, annotate and identify key ideas, paraphrase accurately, integrate academic vocabulary in speaking and writing, follow standard English conventions, and develop evidence-based written responses.

Emphasis is placed on comprehension, accuracy, text-based evidence, academic language, conventions, and clear written expression. Students learn to move from literal understanding to supported interpretation while building habits that support literary analysis, informational analysis, research, and presentation throughout the course.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 9 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, interpretation, justification, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate both writing fluency and conceptual understanding through close reading, annotation, paraphrasing, vocabulary application, conventions, and structured written response.

| Standard Code | Standard Description |
|--------------|----------------------|
| ELA.9.R.1.1 | Explain how key elements enhance or add layers of meaning and/or style in a literary text. |

Students are expected to have prior knowledge of:
- identifying main ideas and relevant details;
- quoting and paraphrasing text accurately;
- using complete sentences and standard conventions;
- participating in academic discussion;
- applying vocabulary in reading and writing contexts.

These concepts support accurate comprehension, written response, and academic communication in this unit.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- close reading and annotation;
- paraphrasing and comprehension;
- academic vocabulary use;
- standard English conventions;
- evidence-based written response.

Assessment items require students to analyze grade-level texts, paraphrase content accurately, use academic vocabulary in context, apply grade-level conventions, and write structured responses supported by textual evidence.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit builds the literacy habits used throughout the course. Students learn to read closely, annotate texts, identify key ideas, restate meaning accurately, use academic language, apply conventions, and support written responses with clear evidence.
