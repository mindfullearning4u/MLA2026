## Supporting Skills Progression

| Unit | Primary Focus | Recurring Supporting Skills |
|------|---------------|-----------------------------|
| Unit 01 | Independent Analysis of Text and Meaning | Complex text reading, paraphrasing, academic vocabulary, conventions, and independent analytical response |
| Unit 02 | Literature Across Time and Perspectives | Literary interpretation, textual evidence, themes, perspective, poetry, figurative language, comparative reading, and narrative writing |
| Unit 03 | Rhetoric, Argument, and Critical Evaluation | Informational reading, rhetoric, validity, claims, reasoning, credibility, conventions, academic vocabulary, and formal argument writing |
| Unit 04 | Research, Synthesis, and Extended Writing | Research on topical issues, source synthesis, complex text analysis, citation, academic integrity, multimedia texts, and extended writing |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Purposeful Revision | Vocabulary precision, foreign phrases, feedback, audience awareness, purpose, clarity, structure, style, and revision across extended writing |
| Unit 06 | Capstone Synthesis, Presentation, and Application | Reading synthesis, research, argument or analysis, academic vocabulary, conventions, credible evidence, rhetorical devices, oral presentation, and digital communication |
