# ENGLISH IV Course Overview

---

## Course Overview

English IV develops students’ ability to independently analyze, evaluate, and synthesize complex literary and informational texts across multiple texts, periods, and perspectives. Students examine how authors construct meaning, evaluate arguments, conduct sustained research, and produce clear, well-structured writing for academic, professional, and real-world purposes.

The course is organized around competency-based progression. Students move through the course by demonstrating mastery of required instructional components, with emphasis on independent analysis, synthesis across texts, argument development, research, revision, and presentation.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 12 Standards with a strong emphasis on evaluation, synthesis, argumentation, and communication. Students engage in literary and informational analysis, argument writing, research-based writing, vocabulary development, revision, and synthesis across multiple sources. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency, independent reasoning, and evidence-based argumentation.

---

## Course Structure

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Focus |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Unit 01 | Independent Analysis of Text and Meaning | Advanced analysis, interpretation, author’s craft, paraphrasing, and independent analytical writing |
| Unit 02 | Literature Across Time and Perspectives | Literary analysis, thematic development, perspective, poetry, and cross-text comparison |
| Unit 03 | Rhetoric, Argument, and Critical Evaluation | Rhetorical analysis, argument evaluation, credibility, bias, and reasoning |
| Unit 04 | Research, Synthesis, and Extended Writing | Sustained research, synthesis across sources, complex text analysis, and extended writing |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Purposeful Revision | Style, tone, precision, audience awareness, and revision of extended writing |
| Unit 06 | Capstone Synthesis, Presentation, and Application | Independent synthesis, oral presentation, digital communication, and real-world application |

---

## Standards Progression

ELA standards develop through a spiral progression across the course. Each benchmark is assigned a primary unit for focused instruction and assessment, while reading, writing, communication, and vocabulary skills reappear as supporting skills in later units to deepen understanding and complexity.

The course progression emphasizes independence, depth of analysis, and synthesis across texts. Students move from advanced analysis to evaluation, argumentation, research, revision, and independent communication and application.

---

## Mastery Model

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.
