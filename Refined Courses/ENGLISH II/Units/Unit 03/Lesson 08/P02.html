Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
informational textWriting that explains, describes, or presents factual information about a subject.
text structureThe organizational pattern an author uses to arrange ideas and show relationships among them.
central ideaThe main point an informational text develops through relevant details and evidence.
author’s purposeThe reason an author creates a text, such as to inform, explain, persuade, analyze, or inspire.
rhetorical choiceA deliberate decision about language, evidence, structure, or appeal used to influence an audience.
argumentA position supported by claims, evidence, and reasoning.
validityThe quality of an argument whose reasoning logically connects its evidence to its conclusion.
claimA debatable statement that expresses a writer’s position or conclusion.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, or observations used to support or challenge a claim.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how evidence supports a claim and why a conclusion is logical.
counterclaimA reasonable opposing position that challenges the main claim.
objective toneA fair, measured style that emphasizes evidence and reasoning rather than emotional or biased language.
