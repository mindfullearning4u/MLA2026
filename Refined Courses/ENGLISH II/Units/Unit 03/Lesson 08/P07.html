Checkpoint Task

Using the Community Library Proposal passage, write one response that includes the following:

  • The passage's central idea.
  • The author's purpose.
  • One rhetorical choice or structural choice and its effect.
  • A formal argument paragraph with a claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim, and rebuttal.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your response accurately explains central idea and author purpose.
  • Your response uses specific evidence from the passage.
  • Your argument paragraph includes a fair counterclaim and evidence-based rebuttal.
  • Your writing uses formal tone and standard English conventions.
