📘 ENG2 | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can write a formal argument paragraph.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your paragraph has evidence but does not explain how the evidence proves the claim.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your claim, evidence, and reasoning.
  2. Revise for formal tone.
  3. Check capitalization, punctuation, and complete sentences.
  4. Submit according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Write one formal argument paragraph answering the P06 prompt. Include a precise claim, at least two pieces of evidence, and reasoning that explains why the evidence supports your claim.

Mastery Criteria

  • Includes a precise claim.
  • Uses relevant evidence from the prompt.
  • Explains reasoning clearly.
  • Maintains formal tone and standard English conventions.
