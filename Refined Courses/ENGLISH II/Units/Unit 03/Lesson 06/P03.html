📘 ENG2 | Unit 03 | Lesson 06

P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will revise weak reasoning into strong reasoning.

Argument Draft

Claim: The school should replace the auditorium sound system first.

Evidence: The sound system often fails during assemblies and presentations.

Weak reasoning: This is good evidence because microphones are annoying when they do not work.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: The claim supports replacing the sound system first.

Step 2: The evidence is relevant because it shows the sound system fails during school communication events.

Step 3: The weak reasoning uses casual language and only says the problem is annoying.

Step 4: Strong reasoning should explain why failed sound affects understanding, access, or communication.

Step 5: Revised reasoning: This evidence supports the claim because assemblies and presentations depend on clear sound, and repeated microphone failures can prevent students and staff from receiving important information.

Notebook Application

  1. Copy the weak reasoning.
  2. Underline the casual or vague language.
  3. Write a revised reasoning sentence in formal tone.
  4. Explain why your revision is stronger.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: This evidence proves my claim because it just does.

Correct: This evidence proves my claim because a leaking roof creates safety risks and can prevent the gym from being used for classes, teams, and school events.

Why this matters: Reasoning must explain the connection between evidence and claim. It cannot simply repeat that the evidence is good.
