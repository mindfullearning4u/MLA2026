Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
formal argumentA structured piece of writing that presents a clear position and supports it with logical reasoning and credible evidence.
claimA debatable statement that expresses the writer’s position on an issue.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, or other information used to support a claim.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how and why the evidence supports the claim.
counterclaimA reasonable opposing position that challenges the writer’s main claim.
rebuttalA response that explains why a counterclaim is less convincing or incomplete.
credibilityThe quality of being trustworthy, knowledgeable, and supported by reliable information.
relevanceThe degree to which evidence directly connects to and helps prove the claim.
logical organizationAn arrangement of ideas in a clear sequence that helps readers follow the argument.
transitionA word, phrase, or sentence that connects ideas and clarifies their relationship.
conclusionThe final section that reinforces the claim, synthesizes the reasoning, and leaves the reader with a clear understanding of the argument.
