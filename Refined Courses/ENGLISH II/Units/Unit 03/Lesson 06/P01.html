P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Formal Argument Writing: Claims, Evidence, and Reasoning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.02 - Write Formal Arguments.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.08 - Analyze Opposing Arguments and Validity; MLA.ENG2.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas in Historical American Texts; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to write a precise argument claim.
  • How to choose relevant evidence that supports the claim.
  • How to write reasoning that explains why the evidence matters.
  • How to use formal tone and standard English conventions.

What You Will Do

  • Read a short argument prompt with evidence included in the lesson.
  • Build a claim, evidence, and reasoning chain.
  • Revise weak reasoning into clear explanation.
  • Write a formal argument paragraph.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work includes a claim, evidence, and reasoning.
  • Your writing explains evidence instead of dropping it into a paragraph.
  • Your checkpoint paragraph uses formal tone.
  • Your assessment answers show you can identify and revise argument parts.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.W.02 in student language: I can write a formal argument that makes a clear claim, supports it with evidence, and explains my reasoning.

Today, your job is to make an argument that can be checked. A reader should see exactly what you claim, what evidence proves it, and why the evidence matters.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your evidence is included but your reasoning does not explain how it proves the claim.
