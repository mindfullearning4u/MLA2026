Notebook Evidence: Read the source packet and answer all 15 questions. Your final response will be a formal argument using claims, evidence, and reasoning.

Source Packet: Should Ridgeway High Adopt a Later Start Time?

Ridgeway High currently begins at 7:25 a.m. A twelve-week pilot moved the start to 8:00 a.m. During the pilot, first-period absences fell from an average of 46 per day to 34, and tardiness fell 21 percent. Teachers reported that more students participated during first period, although average grades changed by less than one percentage point.

The transportation director reported that a permanent change would require reorganizing elementary and high-school bus routes. The district estimates a one-time scheduling cost of $32,000 and an additional annual transportation cost of $18,000. Athletic directors warned that some practices would end later, especially during winter. In a family survey, 64 percent supported the later start, 23 percent opposed it, and 13 percent were unsure. Among families who opposed it, the most common concerns were child-care schedules and after-school jobs.

A local pediatrician explained that teenagers often experience biological changes that make early sleep difficult and that adequate sleep supports attention and emotional health. However, she cautioned that a schedule change alone will not help students who continue using screens late at night. Student council members proposed evaluating attendance, transportation, activities, and family impact after one full year.

Questions

  1. State the issue in one neutral sentence.
  2. Write a precise claim supporting or opposing the later start.
  3. Identify three facts that directly support your claim.
  4. Explain why the attendance evidence is relevant.
  5. Evaluate whether the grade data strengthens or limits the argument.
  6. Explain how the family survey should be interpreted.
  7. Identify one credible expert source and explain its value.
  8. Identify one limitation in the expert’s statement.
  9. Explain the significance of the transportation costs.
  10. Which counterevidence is strongest against your position?
  11. Write reasoning that connects your first fact to your claim.
  12. Write reasoning that connects your second fact to your claim.
  13. Explain why acknowledging a limitation can strengthen formal argument.
  14. Write a concluding sentence that follows logically from the evidence.
  15. Write a complete formal argument of at least two developed paragraphs using a precise claim, at least three facts, and clear reasoning.
