Lesson 5: Opposing Arguments, Validity, and Shared Information

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.08 - Analyze Opposing Arguments and Validity.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas in Historical American Texts; MLA.ENG2.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose in Historical American Texts; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify opposing arguments about the same issue.
  • How authors can use the same information to support different conclusions.
  • How to evaluate whether an argument is valid, relevant, and supported.
  • How purpose affects the way evidence is selected and explained.

What You Will Do

  • Read paired argument passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify each author's claim and purpose.
  • Compare how both authors use shared information.
  • Evaluate which argument is better supported and why.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work identifies both sides of an argument.
  • Your responses explain how evidence is used and whether it is valid.
  • Your checkpoint compares two arguments using shared information.
  • Your assessment answers show careful analysis of claims, evidence, and reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.08 in student language: I can compare opposing arguments and decide how well each author uses evidence and reasoning.

Today, your job is to read both sides fairly. Then you will explain which argument is better supported, not just which side you personally prefer.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can identify both claims but cannot explain which evidence is stronger.
