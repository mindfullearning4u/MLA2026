Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
argumentA position supported by claims, evidence, and reasoning.
claimA statement expressing a position or conclusion that can be supported and evaluated.
opposing argumentA position that disagrees with or challenges another argument’s claim or reasoning.
counterclaimA specific claim that presents an alternative or opposing position.
validityThe quality of an argument whose reasoning logically connects its evidence to its conclusion.
sound reasoningLogical, relevant, and well-supported thinking used to connect evidence with a claim.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, or observations used to support or challenge a claim.
shared informationFacts, sources, events, or background details used by more than one argument, sometimes interpreted differently.
biasA preference or viewpoint that may influence how information is selected, presented, or interpreted.
credibilityThe quality of being trustworthy, knowledgeable, and supported by reliable evidence.
rebuttalA response that challenges an opposing claim by explaining its weakness or presenting stronger evidence and reasoning.
