Historical Speech Passage: "The Public Road"

A road is more than packed earth and stone. It is the path by which a farmer brings food to market, a doctor reaches a sick child, and a letter travels from one county to another. When roads fail, the work of many citizens is slowed. When roads are kept open and safe, trade, health, and friendship move with them. For this reason, a community that repairs its roads repairs the connections among its people.

Part A

Write the topic of the passage and then write the central idea in one complete sentence.

Part B

Choose two details that develop the central idea. Explain what each detail adds.

Part C

Explain how the final sentence strengthens the central idea of the passage.
