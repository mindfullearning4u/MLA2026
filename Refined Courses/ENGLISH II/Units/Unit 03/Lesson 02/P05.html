P05 Guided Practice

Answer the guided practice questions in Moodle. Each question includes the passage or passage details you need.

Before You Begin

  • Read the whole passage before choosing an answer.
  • Separate the topic from the central idea.
  • Choose evidence that develops the central idea, not just a detail that appears in the passage.
  • Use feedback to revise your understanding before the quiz.
