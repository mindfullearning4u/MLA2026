Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
central ideaThe most important point an author develops about a subject throughout an informational text.
historical speechA spoken address from an earlier time that responds to people, events, beliefs, or issues in its historical setting.
historical essayA nonfiction prose work from or about an earlier period that explains, analyzes, or argues ideas.
historical contextThe events, conditions, beliefs, and social circumstances surrounding the creation of a text.
claimA statement expressing a position, interpretation, or conclusion that can be supported with evidence.
supporting detailA fact, example, explanation, quotation, or description that helps develop a central idea.
rhetoricThe purposeful use of language and techniques to inform, persuade, or affect an audience.
audienceThe specific person or group a speaker or writer intends to address.
purposeThe reason a speaker or writer creates a text, such as to inform, persuade, explain, or inspire.
textual evidenceA quotation, fact, detail, or example from a text used to support an analysis.
developmentThe process by which an author introduces, explains, supports, and connects an idea across a text.
