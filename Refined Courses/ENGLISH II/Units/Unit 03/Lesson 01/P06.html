Passage: "The Library Card Drive"

The library noticed that many students used public computers but did not have library cards. Without cards, students could not borrow books, use research databases from home, or reserve study rooms. To solve the problem, librarians visited the high school during lunch for three days. They brought simple forms, answered questions, and printed cards on the same day. By Friday, more than one hundred students had new cards and access to more resources.

Part A

Identify the main text structure. Then write one sentence explaining how you know.

Part B

Choose two details from the passage that show the problem and the solution.

Part C

Explain how the problem-and-solution structure helps the reader understand why the library card drive mattered.
