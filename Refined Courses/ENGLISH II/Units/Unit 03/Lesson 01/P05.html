P05 Guided Practice

Answer the guided practice questions in Moodle. Use the passage provided inside each question. Do not rely on memory or outside sources.

Before You Begin

  • Read the whole question before choosing an answer.
  • Ask yourself what structure the writer uses.
  • Choose the answer that explains the impact of the structure, not just the topic.
  • Use the feedback after each question to correct your thinking.
