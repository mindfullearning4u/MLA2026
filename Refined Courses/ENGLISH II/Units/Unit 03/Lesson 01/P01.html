P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 1: Text Structure Impact and Features

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Text Structure Impact and Features

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.05 - Analyze Text Structure Impact.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How informational texts are organized using structures such as cause and effect, problem and solution, compare and contrast, sequence, and description.
  • How text features such as headings, lists, dates, and signal words guide a reader.
  • How to explain the effect of structure instead of only naming it.
  • How structure shapes what the reader notices, understands, and remembers.

Why This Matters

Text structure helps readers find, organize, and remember information. Recognizing it supports efficient reading in school, work, news, instructions, and digital media.

What You Will Do

  • Read informational passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify the structure and text features used by the writer.
  • Paraphrase what the structure helps the reader understand.
  • Practice explaining why a structure is effective for the writer's purpose.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work identifies structure and explains its impact.
  • Your responses use accurate evidence from the passage.
  • Your checkpoint explains how a feature or structure supports understanding.
  • Your assessment answers show that you can connect structure, evidence, and effect.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection
