Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
text structureThe organizational pattern an author uses to arrange and connect ideas in an informational text.
chronological orderA structure that presents events or steps in the order in which they occur.
cause and effectA structure that explains why something happens and the results that follow.
compare and contrastA structure that examines meaningful similarities and differences between subjects.
problem and solutionA structure that presents a difficulty and explains one or more ways it may be addressed.
descriptionA structure that explains a topic by presenting characteristics, details, examples, or attributes.
sequenceThe specific order in which ideas, events, or steps are presented.
text featureA visual or organizational element, such as a heading, caption, diagram, or sidebar, that helps readers locate and understand information.
headingA title or label that identifies the topic of a section and signals how information is organized.
graphicA visual element, such as a chart, map, graph, photograph, or diagram, that communicates or clarifies information.
impactThe effect an organizational or visual choice has on a reader’s understanding of the text.
