Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
formal argumentA structured piece of writing that presents a clear position and supports it with logical reasoning and credible evidence.
counterclaimA reasonable opposing position that challenges the writer’s main claim.
concessionAn acknowledgment that part of an opposing viewpoint has merit or deserves consideration.
rebuttalA response that explains why a counterclaim is less convincing, incomplete, or outweighed by stronger evidence.
logical organizationAn arrangement of claims, evidence, reasoning, and counterclaims in a sequence that readers can follow.
objective toneA fair, measured style that emphasizes evidence and reasoning rather than emotional or biased language.
biasA preference or viewpoint that can influence how information is selected, described, or interpreted.
cohesionThe clear connection among ideas, sentences, and paragraphs that makes an argument function as a unified whole.
transitionA word, phrase, or sentence that connects ideas and clarifies how one part of an argument relates to another.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, or other reliable information used to support a claim or rebut a counterclaim.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how evidence supports a claim and why the argument’s conclusion is logical.
