Checkpoint Submission: Read the new source packet and answer all 10 questions separately.

Should the School Install Solar Panels or Replace Shade Trees?

Northview High has $480,000 for one campus improvement. A rooftop solar system would cost $470,000 and is projected to reduce electricity purchases by 18 percent annually. The installer estimates a twenty-year useful life, but roof repairs may require temporary panel removal. A tree-replacement plan would cost $310,000 and replace 86 diseased trees with native species, improve shaded outdoor areas, and reserve $170,000 for irrigation and maintenance.

In a student survey, 58 percent preferred solar panels, while 34 percent preferred trees and 8 percent were unsure. Facilities staff report that several walkways currently lack shade and that twelve diseased trees must be removed within two years. Solar supporters emphasize long-term energy savings; tree supporters argue that safety and daily outdoor comfort are more immediate. The board must choose one plan this year.

Questions

  1. Write a precise claim choosing one plan.
  2. Identify three facts supporting your claim.
  3. State the strongest counterclaim fairly.
  4. Acknowledge one valid concern from the opposing side.
  5. Write an evidence-based rebuttal.
  6. Organize your reasons from strongest to weakest and explain the order.
  7. Evaluate how the survey should influence the decision.
  8. Revise one possible biased statement into objective tone.
  9. Write a conclusion that follows logically from the evidence.
  10. Write a formal argument of at least three developed paragraphs using a claim, evidence, counterclaim, rebuttal, logical organization, and objective tone.
