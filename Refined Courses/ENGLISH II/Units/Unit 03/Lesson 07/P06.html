📘 ENG2 | Unit 03 | Lesson 07

P06 Independent Work

Complete Part A, Part B, and Part C in complete sentences.

Instructions

Use the prompt and evidence below to write an organized formal argument paragraph with a counterclaim and rebuttal.

Prompt and Evidence

Prompt: Should the town repair the river trail before adding festival lights?

Evidence 1: The river trail has broken pavement that creates safety risks for walkers and cyclists.

Evidence 2: Festival lights could attract evening visitors downtown.

Evidence 3: The town can fund only one project this year.

Part A

Write a claim and one piece of evidence supporting your claim.

Part B

Write a fair counterclaim and an evidence-based rebuttal.

Part C

Write the full paragraph in objective tone.
