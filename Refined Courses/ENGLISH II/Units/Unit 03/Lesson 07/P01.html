P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Formal Argument Writing: Counterclaims, Organization, and Objective Tone

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.02 - Write Formal Arguments.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.08 - Analyze Opposing Arguments and Validity; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise Writing for Audience and Purpose; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to include a counterclaim fairly in a formal argument.
  • How to organize claim, evidence, reasoning, counterclaim, rebuttal, and conclusion.
  • How objective tone makes an argument more credible.
  • How to revise informal or one-sided writing for an academic audience.

What You Will Do

  • Read argument drafts included in the lesson.
  • Identify counterclaims and rebuttals.
  • Revise biased or emotional wording into objective tone.
  • Write an organized argument paragraph that fairly handles an opposing view.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work identifies counterclaim and rebuttal accurately.
  • Your writing uses formal, objective tone.
  • Your checkpoint includes an organized argument with evidence and a fair rebuttal.
  • Your assessment answers show you can revise argument structure and tone.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.W.02 in student language: I can write a formal argument that supports my claim, responds fairly to an opposing view, and uses objective tone.

Today, your job is to sound credible. That means you do not ignore the other side, and you do not attack it. You answer it with evidence and reasoning.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your counterclaim sounds unfair, emotional, or disconnected from your evidence.
