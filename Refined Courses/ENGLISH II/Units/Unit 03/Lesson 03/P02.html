P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Author Purpose in Historical Texts

Vocabulary

Author purpose means the reason an author writes, such as to inform, persuade, explain, warn, honor, or call readers to act.

Audience means the people the author wants to reach.

Tone means the author's attitude toward the subject or audience.

Connotation means the feeling or association a word carries beyond its dictionary meaning.

Call to action means a statement that urges the audience to do something.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the topic. The topic tells what the passage is about.

Step 2: Identify the central idea. The central idea tells what the author says about the topic.

Step 3: Ask what the author wants the audience to think, feel, or do.

Step 4: Study word choice and tone. Strong, urgent, hopeful, or critical words often reveal purpose.

Step 5: Explain purpose with evidence. Do not stop at "to inform" if the author is also persuading, warning, or calling for action.

Read the Passage

Historical Speech Passage: "Keep the Public Library Open"

Neighbors, a library is not only a room of shelves. It is a doorway for the worker studying at night, the child searching for a first book, and the newcomer learning the language of the town. If we close that doorway, we do not merely save a line in a budget; we narrow the road by which people improve their lives. I ask this council to keep the library open, fund its evening hours, and remember that public knowledge is one of the safest investments a town can make.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title.
  2. Copy the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Write the topic and central idea of the speech passage.
  4. Identify the author's purpose.
  5. Copy one sentence that proves the purpose.

Model Example

Topic: Keeping the public library open.

Central idea: The library gives many community members access to knowledge and opportunity.

Purpose: The speaker wants to persuade the council to keep the library open and fund evening hours.

Evidence: The speaker says, "I ask this council to keep the library open, fund its evening hours."
