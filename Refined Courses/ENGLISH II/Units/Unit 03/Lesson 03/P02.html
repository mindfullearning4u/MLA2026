Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
author’s purposeThe primary reason an author creates a text, such as to inform, explain, persuade, inspire, or reflect.
informTo present facts, details, and ideas so an audience gains knowledge about a subject.
explainTo clarify how or why something happens by developing ideas, relationships, or processes.
persuadeTo influence an audience’s beliefs, attitudes, or actions through claims, evidence, and rhetorical choices.
inspireTo encourage an audience toward hope, commitment, reflection, or action.
audienceThe specific person or group a speaker or writer intends to address.
rhetorical situationThe relationship among the author or speaker, audience, purpose, subject, and context of a text.
historical contextThe events, beliefs, conditions, and social circumstances surrounding the creation of a text.
toneThe author’s or speaker’s attitude toward the subject and audience.
dictionAn author’s deliberate choice of words to create meaning, tone, and effect.
textual evidenceA quotation, fact, detail, or example from a text used to support an analysis of purpose.
