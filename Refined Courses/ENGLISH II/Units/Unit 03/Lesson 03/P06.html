Complete Part A, Part B, and Part C in complete sentences.

Instructions

Read the passage, then analyze the author's purpose using central idea, evidence, and word choice.

Historical Speech Passage: "Save the Town Hall"

This hall has held meetings, concerts, elections, and farewells. Its cracked steps do not make it useless; they remind us how many feet have crossed them for public life. I ask the town to repair the hall, not replace it, because a community should preserve the places where its memory and its future meet.

Part A

Write the topic and central idea of the passage.

Part B

Identify the author's purpose and copy one sentence that proves it.

Part C

Explain how the words memory and future help the author persuade the audience.
