P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 4: Rhetorical Choices and Effect

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Rhetorical Choices and Effect

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.12 - Analyze Rhetorical Choices and Effect.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose in Historical American Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Analyze Meaning, Connotation, and Tone; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How rhetorical choices shape audience response.
  • How repetition, contrast, rhetorical questions, emotional appeals, and inclusive language work.
  • How rhetorical choices connect to author purpose.
  • How to explain effect instead of merely naming a device.

Why This Matters

Rhetorical choices influence audiences in speeches, advertising, media, and public debate. Analyzing their effects helps you respond thoughtfully instead of being persuaded without noticing.

What You Will Do

  • Read historical speech and essay passages included in the lesson.
  • Identify rhetorical choices in context.
  • Explain how each choice supports purpose and tone.
  • Practice choosing evidence that proves rhetorical effect.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work identifies rhetorical choices and explains effect.
  • Your responses connect choices to author purpose.
  • Your checkpoint analyzes one rhetorical choice in a passage.
  • Your assessment answers show careful reading and reasoning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.12 in student language: I can explain how an author uses language choices to influence what the audience thinks, feels, or understands.
