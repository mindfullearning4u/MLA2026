Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
rhetoricThe purposeful use of language and techniques to inform, persuade, or affect an audience.
rhetorical choiceA deliberate decision about words, structure, evidence, or technique made to influence an audience.
rhetorical effectThe response, understanding, emotion, or action a rhetorical choice is intended to create in an audience.
ethosAn appeal based on the credibility, character, or trustworthiness of the speaker or source.
pathosAn appeal that uses language, examples, or imagery to influence an audience’s emotions.
logosAn appeal based on logic, evidence, facts, examples, or clear reasoning.
repetitionThe intentional reuse of words, phrases, sounds, or ideas to emphasize a point or create rhythm.
parallelismThe use of similar grammatical structures to connect ideas, create balance, or strengthen emphasis.
rhetorical questionA question asked for emphasis or effect rather than to receive a direct answer.
dictionA speaker’s or author’s deliberate word choices and their effect on tone and meaning.
syntaxThe arrangement and length of words, phrases, and sentences used to shape emphasis, rhythm, and meaning.
