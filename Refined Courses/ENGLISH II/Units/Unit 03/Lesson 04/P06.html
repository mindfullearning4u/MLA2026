Historical Speech Passage: "Open the Park Gates"

Open the gates for the child who needs a field. Open the gates for the worker who needs shade. Open the gates for the elder who needs a quiet bench. What kind of town locks away the very ground that could gather its people together?

Part A

Part B

Part C
