# ENGLISH II - Unit 03 Overview: Informational Analysis, Rhetoric, and Argument

---

## Unit Overview

This unit deepens informational analysis and argumentation. Students analyze the impact of text structures and features, analyze central ideas in speeches and essays, analyze author choices in achieving purpose, compare opposing arguments, analyze rhetoric, and construct evidence-based arguments.

Emphasis is placed on reasoning, evaluating evidence, rhetorical effectiveness, author purpose, credibility and bias, validity of claims, and synthesis of multiple sources. Students assess how authors use the same information to achieve different ends and apply that understanding to formal argument writing.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through informational analysis, argument evaluation, rhetorical analysis, and formal evidence-based argumentative writing.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- informational text structure and features;
- central ideas and author purpose;
- rhetoric and rhetorical strategies;
- opposing arguments and validity;
- evaluation of evidence, credibility, and bias;
- formal argumentative writing.

Assessment items require students to analyze informational texts, analyze central ideas and author choices, explain rhetoric, compare opposing arguments, evaluate effectiveness and validity of claims, assess credibility and bias, and write a formal argument using logical reasoning, credible evidence, counterclaim rebuttal, logical organization, elaboration, purposeful transitions, and an objective tone.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit builds students' ability to evaluate ideas and arguments. Students connect structure, evidence, rhetoric, author purpose, credibility, and reasoning to determine how texts persuade, explain, and support claims.
