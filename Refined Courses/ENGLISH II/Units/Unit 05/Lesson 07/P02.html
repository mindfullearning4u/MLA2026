Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
styleThe distinctive way a writer communicates through diction, syntax, tone, sentence patterns, detail, and organization.
precisionThe use of exact, specific language and carefully controlled sentences to communicate the intended meaning accurately.
audience-focusedDesigned with deliberate attention to the audience’s knowledge, needs, expectations, and response.
polishThe final refinement of language, organization, mechanics, formatting, and presentation so a text is clear and publication-ready.
dictionA writer’s deliberate choice of words to create meaning, tone, style, and effect.
syntaxThe arrangement of words, phrases, and clauses to create clear, meaningful, and effective sentences.
toneThe writer’s attitude toward the subject or audience as communicated through language and detail.
concisenessThe quality of expressing an idea clearly and completely without unnecessary words or repetition.
revisionThe process of improving ideas, organization, clarity, evidence, style, and effectiveness through meaningful changes.
editingThe process of correcting and refining sentence structure, grammar, usage, word choice, punctuation, and consistency.
proofreadingThe final careful review used to locate and correct surface errors, formatting problems, and minor omissions.
rhetorical effectThe response or impact produced in an audience by a writer’s choices in language, structure, tone, and presentation.
