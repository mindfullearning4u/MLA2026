P06 — Checkpoint: Polish a Grant Proposal

Complete this new task independently. Answer all 10 questions and submit them in the Checkpoint Submission.

Community Technology Workshop

Audience: local foundation reviewers. Purpose: request support to continue a student-led workshop.

Draft: Our tech workshop was super successful and helped a bunch of people. Six student tutors showed people stuff about email, online forms, and being safe online. The participants liked it a lot. Because of the fact that technology is important, giving us money would be a great thing. We can do more sessions and help everyone.

Verified facts: 42 community members attended; 6 student tutors led small groups; lessons covered email, online forms, and password security; 89% of survey respondents reported greater confidence; grant funds would purchase 10 loaner tablets and support four additional workshops.

Questions

  1. State the audience and purpose.
  2. Identify two vague or informal phrases.
  3. Select the two strongest facts for the proposal and explain why.
  4. Describe the tone foundation reviewers need.
  5. Revise the opening sentence precisely.
  6. Rewrite the sentence beginning “Because of the fact” concisely.
  7. Replace “help everyone” with a supportable statement.
  8. Write a conclusion that connects the request to measurable outcomes.
  9. Produce a polished 5–7 sentence proposal using only verified facts.
  10. Explain one precision choice, one audience choice, and one sentence-style choice.
