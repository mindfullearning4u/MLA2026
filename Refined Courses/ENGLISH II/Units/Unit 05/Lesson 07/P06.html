Independent Work

Instructions: Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook. Use complete sentences and explain your revision choices.

Part A: Identify the Problem

Read this sentence: "The library is a good thing because kids need somewhere nice." Identify two words or phrases that are too vague or informal for school leaders.

Part B: Polish the Sentence

Rewrite the sentence so it uses precise, formal wording and keeps the same basic meaning.

Part C: Explain Your Choices

Write three sentences explaining how your revision improves audience fit, precision, and conventions.
