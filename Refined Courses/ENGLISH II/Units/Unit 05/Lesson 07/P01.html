Lesson 7: Style, Precision, and Audience-Focused Polish

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.V.02 - Apply Etymology and Derivations; MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to polish a draft so the style, word choice, sentence control, and conventions fit a specific audience. Polishing is the final stage of revision. It does not mean decorating the writing with difficult words. It means making each sentence clearer, more precise, and more appropriate for the reader.

What You Will Do

You will study a short student draft, identify the audience, replace vague or informal wording, use word-family knowledge to choose precise terms, and correct conventions that affect clarity.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by explaining revision choices, selecting precise wording, correcting sentence errors, and polishing a short paragraph for an academic audience.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

These standards mean: I can revise my writing so my word choices, sentence structure, and grammar help my intended audience understand my message.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record if you cannot explain why a revision fits the audience, if you are unsure whether a word is formal enough, or if you need help correcting a sentence without changing its meaning.
