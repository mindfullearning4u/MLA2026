P04 — Worked Example: Polishing for a Scholarship Committee

Audience: scholarship committee. Purpose: demonstrate service, leadership, and growth.

Original: My volunteer work was good because I helped people and learned a lot. I went to the food pantry every week. It was awesome. I became a better person.

Model 1 — Add Precision

I completed 36 volunteer hours, sorted donations, and prepared food boxes for 75 families.

The measurable details prove service more effectively than the word good.

Model 2 — Match Audience and Tone

Weekly service taught me that dependable preparation can protect a family’s dignity.

This reflection is sincere and professional; it replaces casual praise such as awesome.

Model 3 — Create Cohesion

Polished paragraph: During 36 hours at the community food pantry, I sorted donations and prepared food boxes for 75 families. At first, I viewed each shift as a list of tasks. Over time, however, I learned that careful preparation helps families receive support efficiently and respectfully. This experience strengthened my reliability and showed me how consistent service can create meaningful change.

The final version uses evidence, transitions, varied sentences, and a conclusion tied to growth.

Quality Check

  • Does every detail serve the audience and purpose?
  • Are claims supported with precise evidence?
  • Is the tone professional but authentic?
  • Are sentences concise, varied, and connected?
