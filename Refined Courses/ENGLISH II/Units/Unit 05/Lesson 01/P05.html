Authentic Writing Draft: A Proposal for Evening Library Hours

The following student proposal intentionally contains convention and word-choice problems. Read it as a complete text before revising.

Many students depends on the school library after classes end. Some students wait for a late bus, others need a quiet place to work, and several do not have reliable internet at home. When the library closes at 3:15. Students often move into the crowded cafeteria or try to finish assignments on their phones.

Last month the student council surveyed 126 students, forty-eight percent said they would use the library at least twice each week if it stayed open until 5:00. The survey also found that students wanted access to computers, research databases and tutoring. These results shows that extended hours could support both academic work and student safety.

The plan do not require the library to remain open every evening. A six-week pilot could run on Tuesdays and Thursdays. One librarian and one rotating staff volunteer would supervise the space, students would sign in when they arrive. The school could compare attendance records, student feedback, and the number of completed tutoring sessions before deciding whether the program should continue.

Some people may argue that the plan is too expensive. However the school already pays for late buses on activity days. Keeping one additional room open may cost less than creating several separate study locations. The program would also be really good for students who participate in sports or clubs because they could use the library before their bus leaves.

Extending library hours gives students a safe, focused place to complete schoolwork. It also give the school measurable information about student needs. If the pilot is approved, the council will help advertise the schedule, collect feedback, and report the results to school leaders. This small change could make academic support more available and more equitable.
