P04 Worked Example

Each example shows how to revise conventions while keeping the writer's meaning.

Worked Example 1: Fix a Run-On

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify the specific lesson skill being modeled. Step 2: examine the words, evidence, sentence, or passage detail that matters most. Step 3: explain why the final response or revision demonstrates the skill accurately.

Draft: Some students ride late buses, they need a safe place to study.

Step 1: Notice that both sides of the comma are complete thoughts.

Step 2: Add a coordinating word that shows the relationship.

Revision: Some students ride late buses, so they need a safe place to study.

Worked Example 2: Fix a Fragment

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: identify what changed from the first example. Step 2: connect the example to the lesson focus. Step 3: explain how the response uses clearer reasoning, stronger evidence, or more precise language.

Draft: Because the library closes early.

Step 1: Notice that "because" begins a dependent idea.

Step 2: Add the complete thought that tells what happens.

Revision: Because the library closes early, students lose a quiet place to work.

Worked Example 3: Improve Word Choice

Step-by-step explanation: Step 1: combine the lesson skill with evidence or revision choices. Step 2: check that the explanation stays focused on the assigned standard. Step 3: confirm that the final response is clear, accurate, and written in standard English.

Draft: The library is good for students.

Step 1: Identify the vague word: "good."

Step 2: Replace it with precise academic language.

Revision: The library provides students with a quiet and reliable study space.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

The library should stay open, students need a quiet place.

Correct Example:

The library should stay open because students need a quiet place to work.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example is a comma splice because it joins two complete thoughts with only a comma. The correct example uses "because" to show the reason clearly.

Convention Editing Organizer

Use this organizer to apply Standard English conventions in authentic writing while preserving meaning and voice.

Analysis Step What to Record Quality Check
1. Locate the convention issue Identify the exact sentence and classify the issue: grammar, usage, capitalization, punctuation, or spelling. The diagnosis names the rule, not merely that the sentence “sounds wrong.”
2. Revise deliberately Write the corrected sentence and explain the change. The revision follows the convention without changing the intended meaning.
3. Check the whole passage Reread for consistency, clarity, and any new errors introduced by the revision. The final writing is correct, readable, and appropriate for its audience.
