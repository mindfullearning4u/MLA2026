Authentic Writing Draft: Community Technology Night

This school announcement intentionally contains convention and word-choice problems.

Jefferson High School invite families to Community Technology Night on October 18. The event begins at 6:00 p.m. in the media center, families should enter through the east doors. Because several workshops will occur at the same time.

Teachers will demonstrate the learning platform, online gradebook and digital library. Each family will receive a schedule when they arrives. Student volunteers is available to help visitors connect devices to the guest network and locate translation services.

The first workshop explains how families can review assignments and attendance records. The second workshop focus on protecting personal information online. During the final session, a librarian will show families how to borrow electronic books, evaluate websites, and use research databases from home.

Families should bring a charged phone, tablet, or laptop if possible however school devices will also be available. Childcare are provided for children ages five through twelve. Refreshments will be served in the cafeteria after the workshops ends.

This event will be super helpful for everybody. It gives families practical tools for supporting students and it creates an opportunity to ask questions directly. To reserve childcare or request an interpreter contact the family engagement office by October 15.
