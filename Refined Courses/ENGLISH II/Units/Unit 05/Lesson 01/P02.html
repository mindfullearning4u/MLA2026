Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
Standard EnglishThe widely accepted form of English used in formal academic, professional, and public communication.
conventionAn accepted rule or customary practice for grammar, usage, punctuation, capitalization, spelling, or formatting.
authentic writingCommunication created for a genuine purpose and audience rather than only to practice an isolated skill.
grammarThe system of rules that governs how words and phrases are formed and combined into meaningful sentences.
usageThe accepted way words, phrases, and grammatical forms are used in a particular language context.
syntaxThe arrangement of words, phrases, and clauses to create clear, meaningful, and effective sentences.
subject-verb agreementThe grammatical match between a subject and its verb in number and person.
pronoun referenceThe clear and grammatically correct connection between a pronoun and the noun or idea to which it refers.
modifierA word, phrase, or clause that describes, limits, or adds information about another element in a sentence.
punctuationMarks such as commas, semicolons, colons, apostrophes, and quotation marks used to clarify meaning and sentence structure.
capitalizationThe use of uppercase letters according to accepted rules for sentence beginnings, proper nouns, titles, and other specific purposes.
mechanicsThe technical features of writing—including spelling, capitalization, punctuation, and formatting—that support clarity and correctness.
