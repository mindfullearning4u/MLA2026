Draft: The New Recycling Stations

The school added recycling stations. They are in three hallways. This happened after the environmental club studied waste. The club found many bottles in trash cans. The stations have labels. The labels show paper, plastic, and landfill waste. Students still make mistakes. Teachers will explain the system. The club will collect data. The program may expand.

Audience: students and families reading the school newsletter. Purpose: explain how the system works and why it matters.

Facts: Stations are in the cafeteria, gym hallway, and library entrance. A two-week audit found 430 recyclable bottles in regular trash. Color-coded labels include pictures. Teachers will demonstrate sorting during advisory. The club will compare contamination rates after one month; if accuracy improves, stations will be added near classrooms.

Questions
