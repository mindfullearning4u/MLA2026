P04 — Worked Example

Follow the revision of a choppy paragraph for a school-news audience.

Original

The robotics team built a sorting machine. The machine uses a camera. The camera identifies colors. The team tested it. The first test failed. The team adjusted the sensor. The next test succeeded.

Model 1: Combine related ideas

“The robotics team built a sorting machine that uses a camera to identify colors.” The relative clause connects the machine, camera, and purpose without repetitive sentence starts.

Model 2: Signal contrast and cause

“The first test failed because the sensor was misaligned. However, after the team adjusted it, the next test succeeded.” Each transition names a different relationship.

Model 3: Final cohesive paragraph

The robotics team built a sorting machine that uses a camera to identify colors. The first test failed because the sensor was misaligned. However, after team members adjusted it, the next test succeeded. The sequence is concise, varied, and easy for a general school audience to follow.

Quality Check

The revision preserves every fact, clarifies relationships, removes unnecessary repetition, and varies sentence structure.
