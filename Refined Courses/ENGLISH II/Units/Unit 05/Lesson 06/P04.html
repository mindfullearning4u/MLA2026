Worked Examples

Each example models sentence control for audience clarity.

Example 1: Cause

Ideas: Some students ride late buses. They need a place to study.

Step 1: Identify the relationship: reason.

Revision: Because some students ride late buses, they need a place to study after school.

Example 2: Result

Ideas: The library closes early. Students work in loud hallways.

Step 1: Identify the relationship: effect.

Revision: The library closes early; as a result, students sometimes work in loud hallways.

Example 3: Contrast

Ideas: The library has limited staff. Students still need a supervised place after school.

Step 1: Identify the relationship: contrast or complication.

Revision: Although the library has limited staff, students still need a supervised place after school.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Although the library closes early, students work in loud hallways.

Correct Example:

Because the library closes early, students sometimes work in loud hallways.

Teachable Explanation: Although signals contrast, but the sentence needs cause. Because correctly shows why students work in hallways.
