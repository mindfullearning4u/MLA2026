P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Cohesion, Transitions, and Audience-Aware Sentence Control

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Cohesion, Transitions, and Audience-Aware Sentence Control

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Analyze Precise Meaning and Tone.

What You Will Learn

  • How cohesion helps readers follow ideas.
  • How transitions show relationships between sentences.
  • How sentence control improves clarity for a specific audience.

Why This Matters

Cohesion and sentence control guide readers through complex ideas. These skills improve reports, emails, applications, presentations, and digital communication.

What You Will Do

  • Revise a choppy paragraph.
  • Add transitions that show cause, addition, contrast, or result.
  • Combine or separate sentences for audience clarity.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your practice answers choose sentence revisions that improve flow.
  • Your checkpoint paragraph uses transitions and sentence control to guide the reader.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection
