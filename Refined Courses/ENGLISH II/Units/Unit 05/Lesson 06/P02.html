Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
cohesionThe clear connection among sentences and paragraphs created through transitions, repeated key ideas, pronoun reference, and consistent focus.
coherenceThe overall logical clarity of a text in which ideas are organized and developed so readers can follow the writer’s meaning.
transitionA word, phrase, clause, or sentence that shows the relationship between ideas or sections.
transitional phraseA group of words that connects ideas by signaling relationships such as addition, contrast, cause, sequence, or conclusion.
sentence controlThe deliberate management of sentence structure, length, emphasis, grammar, and punctuation to communicate clearly and effectively.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s needs, knowledge, expectations, and interests when making language and sentence choices.
sentence varietyThe purposeful use of different sentence lengths, beginnings, structures, and patterns to improve clarity, rhythm, and emphasis.
coordinationThe joining of words, phrases, or independent clauses that have equal grammatical importance.
subordinationThe joining of ideas so one clause is grammatically dependent on and less emphasized than the main clause.
parallel structureThe use of matching grammatical forms for ideas that have equal importance or function.
pronoun referenceThe clear and grammatically correct connection between a pronoun and the noun or idea to which it refers.
emphasisSpecial importance given to an idea through placement, repetition, sentence structure, punctuation, or word choice.
