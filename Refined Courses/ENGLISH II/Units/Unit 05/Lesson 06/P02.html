P02 Notebook Task - Part 1: Building Cohesion

Notebook Title: Unit 5 Lesson 6 - Cohesion, Transitions, and Sentence Control

Vocabulary

  • Cohesion: the way ideas connect smoothly in writing.
  • Transition: a word or phrase that shows how ideas are related.
  • Sentence control: the writer's ability to combine, separate, and punctuate sentences clearly.
  • Choppy writing: writing with short, disconnected sentences that do not show relationships.

Draft Paragraph

The library closes at four. Some students ride late buses. They wait in the hallway. The hallway is loud. Students have assignments to finish.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Read the paragraph for meaning. The writer wants to show why late library hours help students.
  2. Find related ideas. Late buses cause students to wait after school.
  3. Add a transition or connecting phrase to show cause.
  4. Combine sentences when the ideas belong together.
  5. Keep the writing clear for school leaders.

Notebook Directions

Copy the draft paragraph. Underline two sentences that should be connected. Then write a revised version using at least one transition.

Model Example: Because some students ride late buses, they often wait in loud hallways after the library closes.
