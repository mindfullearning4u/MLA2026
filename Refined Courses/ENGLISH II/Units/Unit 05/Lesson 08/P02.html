Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
Standard EnglishThe widely accepted form of English used in formal academic, professional, and public communication.
conventionAn accepted rule or customary practice for grammar, usage, punctuation, capitalization, spelling, or formatting.
etymologyThe study of a word’s origin and the historical development of its form and meaning.
word familyA group of related words that share a common root or base and have connected meanings.
contextThe words, sentences, situation, or background surrounding a word or passage that help determine its meaning.
connotationThe emotional, cultural, or associative meaning a word suggests in addition to its literal definition.
precise meaningThe exact meaning that best fits a word’s context, purpose, tone, and intended effect.
styleThe distinctive way a writer communicates through diction, syntax, tone, sentence patterns, detail, and organization.
cohesionThe clear connection among sentences and paragraphs created through transitions, repeated key ideas, and consistent focus.
sentence controlThe deliberate management of sentence structure, length, emphasis, grammar, and punctuation to communicate effectively.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s needs, knowledge, expectations, and interests when making language choices.
polishThe final refinement of language, organization, mechanics, formatting, and presentation so a text is clear and publication-ready.
