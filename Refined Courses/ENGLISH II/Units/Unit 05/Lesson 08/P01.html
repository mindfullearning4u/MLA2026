Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 5 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01, MLA.ENG2.V.02, MLA.ENG2.V.03, MLA.ENG2.W.04.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 and MLA.ENG2.R.10.

What You Will Learn

You will bring together the skills from Unit 5: conventions, etymology, word families, context clues, connotation, denotation, tone, feedback, revision, transitions, sentence control, style, precision, and audience-focused polish.

What You Will Do

You will revise a short draft by checking meaning, word choice, tone, sentence flow, and conventions in a clear sequence.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery on guided practice, independent synthesis work, and the Unit 5 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

These standards mean: I can understand and choose words carefully, apply conventions correctly, and revise writing so a specific audience receives the message clearly.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record if you are unsure whether a revision changes the meaning, if a word's tone is unclear, or if you need help preparing for the Unit 5 Assessment.
