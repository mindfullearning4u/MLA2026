P06 — Checkpoint: Independent Unit 5 Synthesis

Read the new draft and verified facts. Answer all 10 questions and submit them in the Checkpoint Submission.

A Request for a School Garden

Audience: school board. Purpose: request approval and limited start-up funding.

Draft: A garden would be super cool and students needs more things to do outside. It could teach science and healthy food and stuff. We asked people and almost everyone thought it was good. The empty courtyard is just sitting there, so obviously the board should give us money. This project will make every student healthier and fix the courtyard.

Verified facts: 186 students completed a survey; 78% supported a garden; science teachers identified four curriculum units that could use it; a local nursery offered $600 in donated materials; the request is for $900 and use of a 30-by-40-foot courtyard area.

Questions

  1. State the audience and purpose.
  2. Correct two convention errors.
  3. Identify three vague, informal, or exaggerated phrases.
  4. Explain the connotation of “obviously” and why it is unsuitable.
  5. Select two facts that best support approval and explain why.
  6. Write a transition connecting student support to curriculum use.
  7. Revise the funding request precisely and professionally.
  8. Write a supportable conclusion without overpromising.
  9. Produce a polished 7–9 sentence request using only verified facts.
  10. Explain four revision choices: conventions, vocabulary, cohesion, and audience/style.
