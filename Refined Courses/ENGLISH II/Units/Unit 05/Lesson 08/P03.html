P03 Notebook Task - Part 2: Explaining the Revision

In Lesson 8, you must do more than choose a better sentence. You must explain why it is better using Unit 5 language. A complete explanation names the problem, identifies the standard skill, and explains how the revision improves the writing.

Example Explanation: I replaced awesome with support because awesome is informal and has a casual connotation. Support is more precise and fits school leaders as the audience.

Notebook Directions: Choose two sentences from the synthesis draft. Revise each sentence. Then explain one Unit 5 skill used in each revision.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

I fixed it because the new sentence sounds better.

Correct Example:

I replaced "kids" with "students who wait for late buses" because the revision is more precise and gives school leaders clearer information.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect explanation is too vague. The correct explanation names the original wording, the revised wording, the skill used, and the reason the revision helps the audience.

Teacher-Guided Thinking Check

Before you move to independent work, pause and ask: What is the question asking me to prove? What evidence from the passage or model supports that answer? How can I explain the connection in my own words?

If your answer only names a topic, add analysis. If your answer only gives an opinion, add evidence. If your answer feels too general, return to the vocabulary and use the most precise lesson term.
