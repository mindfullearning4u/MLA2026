📘 ENG2 | Unit 05 | Lesson 03

Independent Work

Analyze how word choice affects tone in a short passage.

Instructions

Use the auditorium passage from P02. Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Part A: Identify

Choose two words from the passage that affect mood or tone. Write each word and its denotation.

Part B: Explain

Explain the connotation of each word. Decide whether each word feels positive, negative, or neutral.

Part C: Analyze Tone

Write three to four sentences explaining how the word choices create the author's tone and the reader's mood.
