P04 — Worked Example

Read the passage, then follow the complete model showing how word choice shapes tone.

The Reopened Theater

After months of repairs, the neighborhood theater opened its doors. Sunlight spilled across the polished floor, and volunteers welcomed visitors beneath the restored marquee. The lobby was small, but families called it cozy. An old piano offered a gentle melody while the first audience found its seats.

Model 1: cozy

  1. Denotation: small, warm, and comfortable.
  2. Connotation: positive; it suggests comfort and belonging.
  3. Tone effect: The writer sounds affectionate and approving. Replacing cozy with cramped would make the tone critical.

Model 2: spilled

  1. Context: Sunlight moves across the floor after the doors open.
  2. Connotation: Spilled suggests abundance and effortless movement.
  3. Tone effect: The image makes the reopening feel bright and generous. The neutral verb fell would be less vivid.

Model 3: welcomed and gentle

  1. Both words have positive connotations: welcomed suggests acceptance; gentle suggests calm.
  2. Together they establish a warm, hopeful tone.
  3. The reader is likely to experience a peaceful, inviting mood because the writer consistently selects comforting language.

Common Mistake

Weak: “The tone is positive because the passage is nice.”

Strong: “The words welcomed, cozy, and gentle carry comforting connotations, creating a warm and hopeful tone.”
