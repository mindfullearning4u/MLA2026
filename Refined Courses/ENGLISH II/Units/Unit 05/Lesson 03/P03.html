P03 Notebook Task - Part 2: Connotation and Tone

Now you will compare words with similar denotations but different connotations.

Word Pair

Thin and scrawny can both describe someone with little body fat.

The denotation is similar, but the connotation is different. Thin can be neutral. Scrawny often sounds negative or critical.

Step-by-Step Application

  1. Identify the literal meaning shared by the words.
  2. Ask what feeling each word creates.
  3. Decide whether the feeling is positive, negative, or neutral.
  4. Connect the word choice to tone.
  5. Explain how changing the word would change the reader's response.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Thin and scrawny mean the exact same thing, so they create the exact same tone.

Correct Example:

Thin and scrawny have similar denotations, but scrawny has a more negative connotation and can create a more critical tone.

Teachable Explanation: Words can share a basic definition but create different feelings. Tone depends on both literal meaning and emotional association.
