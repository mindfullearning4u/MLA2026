Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
contextThe words, sentences, situation, or background surrounding a word or passage that help determine its meaning.
context clueInformation near an unfamiliar or ambiguous word that helps a reader infer its meaning.
denotationThe direct, literal, or dictionary meaning of a word.
connotationThe emotional, cultural, or associative meaning a word suggests in addition to its denotation.
toneThe author’s or speaker’s attitude toward a subject or audience as communicated through language and detail.
dictionA writer’s or speaker’s deliberate choice of words to create meaning, tone, style, or effect.
nuanceA subtle difference in meaning, feeling, tone, or emphasis among words or ideas.
word choiceThe selection of particular words based on their precision, connotations, tone, and effect on an audience.
figurative languageLanguage that communicates meaning beyond the literal definitions of words through devices such as metaphor, simile, or personification.
literal meaningThe straightforward meaning of words without exaggeration, symbolism, or figurative interpretation.
registerThe level and style of language selected for a particular audience, purpose, and situation.
semantic relationshipA meaning-based connection between words, such as synonymy, antonymy, category, degree, cause, or association.
