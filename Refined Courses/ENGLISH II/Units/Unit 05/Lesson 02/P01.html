Lesson 2: Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.02 - Analyze Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Use Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How word origins can help you understand unfamiliar academic vocabulary.
  • How prefixes, roots, and suffixes create related words.
  • How to use word families to paraphrase and understand grade-level texts.

What You Will Do

  • Break words into meaningful parts.
  • Connect derivations such as construct, construction, constructive, and reconstruct.
  • Use word-family knowledge to choose precise meanings in context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains word parts and related word meanings.
  • Your practice answers use etymology and derivations to determine meaning.
  • Your checkpoint response explains how a word family helps you paraphrase a sentence.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use word origins, word parts, and related words to figure out academic vocabulary and explain meaning accurately.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot tell how a prefix, root, or suffix changes a word's meaning.
