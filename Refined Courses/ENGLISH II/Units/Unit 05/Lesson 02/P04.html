Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

All words with the same root mean exactly the same thing.

Correct Example:

Words in the same family share a base meaning, but prefixes, suffixes, and context change the exact meaning.

Teachable Explanation: Construct, constructive, and reconstruct all connect to the idea of building, but they are not interchangeable. Word parts and context determine the precise meaning.
