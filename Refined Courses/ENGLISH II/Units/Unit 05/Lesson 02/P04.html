Worked Examples

Each example shows how word families help you determine meaning.

Example 1: Construct

Sentence: The class will construct a model of the courtroom.

Step 1: The root struct means build.

Step 2: The sentence mentions a model, which can be built.

Meaning: Construct means build.

Example 2: Constructive

Sentence: The peer review gave constructive feedback that helped the writer improve the paragraph.

Step 1: Struct means build.

Step 2: The suffix -ive can mean having the quality of.

Meaning: Constructive means helpful or building toward improvement.

Example 3: Instruction

Sentence: Clear instruction helps students understand each step of the assignment.

Step 1: Instruction belongs to the struct word family.

Step 2: Instruction builds understanding by guiding someone through steps.

Meaning: Instruction means teaching, guidance, or directions.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

All words with the same root mean exactly the same thing.

Correct Example:

Words in the same family share a base meaning, but prefixes, suffixes, and context change the exact meaning.

Teachable Explanation: Construct, constructive, and reconstruct all connect to the idea of building, but they are not interchangeable. Word parts and context determine the precise meaning.
