Notebook Task Part 1: Word Origins and Word Parts

Notebook Title: Unit 5 Lesson 2 - Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families

Vocabulary

  • Etymology: the history or origin of a word.
  • Derivation: a word formed from another word or word part.
  • Prefix: a word part added to the beginning of a word.
  • Root: the base meaning of a word.
  • Suffix: a word part added to the end of a word.
  • Word family: a group of words connected by the same root or base meaning.

Word Family Focus

Root: struct means build.

Words: construct, structure, construction, constructive, reconstruct, instruction.

Key idea: If you know that struct means build, you can use that knowledge to understand several related words.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Start with the root. The root struct means build.
  2. Look at the prefix. Re- means again, so reconstruct means build again.
  3. Look at the suffix. -ive often means having the quality of, so constructive means having the quality of building or helping.
  4. Use context to confirm the meaning.
  5. Paraphrase the sentence using the meaning you built from word parts.

Notebook Directions

Create a word-family chart for struct. Include the words construct, construction, constructive, reconstruct, and instruction. For each word, write a short meaning in your own words.

Model Example: Reconstruct - to build again or put something back together.
