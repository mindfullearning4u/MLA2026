Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
etymologyThe study of a word’s origin and the historical development of its form and meaning.
derivationThe formation of a new word from an existing word, root, or base through the addition or change of word parts.
word familyA group of related words that share a common root or base and have connected meanings.
rootThe core word part that carries the primary meaning and may combine with prefixes, suffixes, or other roots.
base wordA complete word or foundational form to which an affix can be added to create a related word.
prefixA meaningful word part added to the beginning of a root or base to modify its meaning.
suffixA meaningful word part added to the end of a root or base to change its meaning, grammatical function, or word form.
affixA word part attached to a root or base, including a prefix or suffix, that changes meaning or grammatical role.
cognateA word related to a word in another language because both developed from the same historical origin.
morphologyThe study of how meaningful word parts combine and change to form words.
semantic changeA shift in a word’s meaning over time as language and culture develop.
part of speechA grammatical category—such as noun, verb, adjective, or adverb—that describes how a word functions in a sentence.
