Lesson 4: Word Relationships, Reference Materials, and Precise Meaning

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Analyze Word Relationships and Precise Meaning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.02 - Analyze Etymology, Derivations, and Word Families; MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How synonyms, antonyms, and shades of meaning help you choose precise words.
  • How dictionaries and thesauruses can support, but not replace, context-based thinking.
  • How to select a word that fits the sentence meaning and tone.

What You Will Do

  • Compare related words with different levels of intensity.
  • Use sample dictionary and thesaurus entries.
  • Revise sentences by choosing the most precise word for the context.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains word relationships and reference-material choices.
  • Your guided practice and quiz answers choose precise words in context.
  • Your checkpoint revises a sentence and explains why the selected word is the best fit.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use context, word relationships, and reference materials to choose the word that most clearly communicates my meaning.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if two words seem similar but you cannot decide which one fits the context better.
