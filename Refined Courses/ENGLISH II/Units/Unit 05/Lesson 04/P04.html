Worked Examples

Each example models how to choose a precise word.

Example 1: Synonym Choice

Sentence: The speaker gave a _____ explanation of the policy.

Choices: clear, loud, bright.

Step 1: The sentence is about explanation, not sound or light.

Answer: Clear fits because it describes an explanation that is easy to understand.

Example 2: Antonym Check

Sentence: The directions were precise, not vague.

Step 1: Precise and vague are opposites in this context.

Step 2: Precise means exact and clear.

Reasoning: The antonym helps confirm the meaning of precise.

Example 3: Reference Material

Dictionary Entry: Analyze - to examine carefully by separating into parts.

Sentence: Students will analyze the paragraph's structure.

Step 1: Use the dictionary meaning.

Paraphrase: Students will examine the paragraph's structure carefully by looking at its parts.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

The speaker gave a bright explanation of the policy.

Correct Example:

The speaker gave a clear explanation of the policy.

Teachable Explanation: Bright can be a synonym for clear in some contexts, such as bright light, but it does not fit an explanation as precisely as clear does.
