Notebook Task Part 1: Choosing Precise Words

Notebook Title: Unit 5 Lesson 4 - Word Relationships and Precise Meaning

Vocabulary

  • Synonym: a word with a similar meaning.
  • Antonym: a word with an opposite meaning.
  • Shade of meaning: a small difference in meaning, feeling, or intensity between related words.
  • Reference material: a tool such as a dictionary, glossary, or thesaurus.
  • Precise meaning: the exact meaning that best fits the context.

Reference Entries

Notice: to become aware of something.

Observe: to watch carefully, often to learn or understand.

Inspect: to examine closely for details or problems.

Context Sentence: During the science demonstration, the students had to _____ how the liquid changed color after each drop.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Read the sentence first. The students are watching a demonstration carefully.
  2. Check each word's precise meaning.
  3. Notice means become aware, which may be too general.
  4. Inspect means examine closely for problems, which does not fit the demonstration as well.
  5. Observe fits best because it means watch carefully to learn or understand.

Notebook Directions

Copy the three reference entries. Then explain why observe is the most precise word for the context sentence.

Model Example: Observe fits because the students are watching carefully to understand a change.
