Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
word relationshipA meaning-based connection between words, such as similarity, opposition, category, degree, part-whole, cause, or association.
synonymA word with the same or nearly the same meaning as another word in a particular context.
antonymA word whose meaning is opposite or strongly contrasted with another word.
analogyA comparison that shows a parallel relationship between two pairs of words, ideas, or situations.
categoryA group of words, ideas, or objects classified together because they share defining characteristics.
degree of meaningThe relative strength or intensity expressed by related words, such as annoyed, angry, and furious.
reference materialA resource used to locate, verify, or clarify information about words, language, or a subject.
dictionaryA reference source that provides information such as definitions, pronunciations, parts of speech, usage, and word origins.
thesaurusA reference source that organizes words by related meanings and provides synonyms, antonyms, and distinctions in usage.
glossaryAn alphabetical list of specialized terms and definitions associated with a particular text, field, or subject.
precise meaningThe exact meaning that best fits a word’s context, purpose, tone, and intended effect.
usage noteGuidance in a reference source explaining how a word or expression is correctly or appropriately used.
