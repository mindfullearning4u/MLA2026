Revise the new passage independently and answer all 10 questions in complete sentences.

Draft: Riverside Cleanup Notice

Audience: neighborhood volunteers. Purpose: explain how to participate safely in a Saturday cleanup.

We are cleaning the park Saturday. Come early and bring things. The river area is messy, so we need everyone to work hard. Kids can come too. We will finish sometime in the afternoon. Be careful near the water.

Coordinator feedback: Give exact time and meeting location; list required supplies and explain what will be provided; separate adult and youth tasks; replace vague safety language with clear directions; keep the tone encouraging rather than alarming.

Available facts: Check-in is 8:30 a.m. at the east picnic shelter. Gloves, trash bags, and water are provided. Volunteers should wear closed-toe shoes and sun protection. Adults will work along the riverbank. Youth volunteers will remain with group leaders on the playground and picnic paths. Work ends at noon.

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Identify the audience and purpose.
  2. Name three weaknesses in the draft.
  3. Sort the coordinator's feedback by revision focus.
  4. Which change should occur first, and why?
  5. Write a precise opening sentence.
  6. Revise “bring things” using the available facts.
  7. Revise the youth-participation information for clarity and safety.
  8. Replace “sometime in the afternoon” accurately.
  9. Write a polished five-sentence notice with an encouraging professional tone.
  10. Explain two revision choices and how they meet audience needs.
