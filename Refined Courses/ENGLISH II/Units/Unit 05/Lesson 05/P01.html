Lesson 5: Feedback and Revision for Audience Needs

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.W.02 - Write Arguments; MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Voice.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use feedback to revise for audience and purpose.
  • How to decide which feedback is actionable.
  • How to revise argument and expository writing without changing the main idea.

What You Will Do

  • Review feedback on an authentic draft.
  • Sort feedback into useful, vague, and off-task categories.
  • Revise wording, evidence, explanation, and conventions for a specific audience.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains how feedback guides revision.
  • Your practice answers choose revisions that fit audience needs.
  • Your checkpoint includes a revised paragraph and explanation of the revision choices.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use feedback to revise writing so it fits the audience, purpose, and task.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if feedback seems vague or if you are unsure how to revise without changing your main idea.
