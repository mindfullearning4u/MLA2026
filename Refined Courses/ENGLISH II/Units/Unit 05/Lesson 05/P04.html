P04 — Worked Example

Follow the complete revision of a message written for school leaders.

Original Draft

The library closes too early and that is bad. Lots of students need it. The school should do something because the hallways are loud and nobody can work.

Audience: principal and school leadership. Purpose: recommend extending library hours.

Feedback 1: Clarify the claim

Replace “do something” with the requested action: “Keep the library open until late buses depart.” The audience can now identify the exact proposal.

Feedback 2: Add audience-relevant evidence

Replace “lots of students” with a specific explanation: students waiting for transportation need a supervised study place. This evidence connects the proposal to safety and academic support.

Feedback 3: Adjust tone and wording

Replace blame and absolutes with respectful, precise language. “Nobody can work” becomes “hallway noise makes sustained study difficult.”

Final revision: The school should keep the library open until late buses depart because students waiting for transportation need a supervised place to complete assignments. Extended hours would support academic work and provide a quieter alternative to busy hallways.

Why It Works

The revision preserves the original purpose while strengthening claim, evidence, tone, precision, and audience awareness.
