Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
feedbackSpecific information about a text’s strengths, needs, and possible improvements provided to guide revision.
revisionThe process of improving ideas, organization, evidence, clarity, tone, and effectiveness through meaningful changes to a draft.
audience needsThe knowledge, expectations, interests, questions, and accessibility considerations a writer must address for intended readers.
peer reviewA structured process in which writers examine one another’s drafts and provide evidence-based feedback.
criteriaThe stated standards or qualities used to evaluate a text and guide improvement.
actionable feedbackClear, specific guidance that identifies what should change and gives the writer a practical direction for revision.
clarityThe quality of communicating ideas in a way that readers can understand accurately and without unnecessary confusion.
coherenceThe logical clarity of a text in which ideas are organized and connected to support the overall purpose.
toneThe writer’s attitude toward the subject or audience as communicated through language and detail.
purposeThe intended result of a text, such as informing, explaining, persuading, reflecting, or entertaining.
global revisionA substantial change to ideas, organization, development, evidence, or purpose that affects a large part of a draft.
local revisionA focused change to sentences, word choice, transitions, grammar, punctuation, or formatting within a smaller part of a draft.
