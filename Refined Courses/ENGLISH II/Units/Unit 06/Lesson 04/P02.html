Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
digital presentationA planned communication delivered through digital slides or another electronic format that combines spoken explanation with visual or multimedia support.
findingAn important conclusion, pattern, or piece of information discovered through research or analysis.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how evidence supports a claim, finding, or conclusion.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, images, or observations used to support a claim or explanation.
visual aidA chart, graph, image, diagram, map, or other visual element used to clarify and strengthen a presentation.
slide designThe deliberate arrangement of text, images, color, spacing, and other elements on a presentation slide.
data visualizationA visual representation of numerical or categorical information designed to reveal patterns, comparisons, or trends.
multimedia elementA component such as audio, video, animation, or an interactive feature used to support a digital message.
source attributionClear acknowledgment identifying the creator or source of borrowed information, data, images, audio, or video.
readabilityThe ease with which an audience can read and understand displayed text and visual information.
relevanceThe degree to which a visual, fact, or media element directly supports the presentation’s purpose and central message.
visual hierarchyThe arrangement of design elements to show the audience what is most important and in what order information should be viewed.
