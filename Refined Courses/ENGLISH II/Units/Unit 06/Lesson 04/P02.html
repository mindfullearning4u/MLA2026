P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Digital Presentation of Findings, Reasoning, and Evidence

Vocabulary

Digital presentation is a slide-based or screen-based presentation designed for an audience.

Finding is an important result or conclusion from evidence.

Visual feature is a design choice such as a heading, chart, image, label, or layout.

Slide sequence is the order of slides in a presentation.

Concise wording means using only the words needed for the audience to understand the point.

Presentation Context

Topic: Supervised after-school study space

Finding: Many students remain on campus after dismissal and need a supervised, productive place to wait.

Evidence: A survey of 120 students found that 68 remain on campus at least one day each week, and 49 of those students usually wait in hallways, outside the office, or near the bus loop.

Reasoning: A supervised study space would connect the evidence to a solution by giving students a safer and more productive location.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Put one main idea on each slide.

Step 2: Use headings that tell the audience what the slide proves.

Step 3: Match visual features to information. A simple chart can show survey results clearly.

Step 4: Keep wording concise. Slides should support the speaker, not replace the speaker.

Step 5: Sequence slides so finding, evidence, reasoning, and conclusion build logically.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title.
  2. Copy the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Write a four-slide sequence for the presentation context.
  4. Choose one visual feature for the evidence slide.
  5. Explain why the visual feature helps the audience understand.

Model Example

Slide 1: Finding - Many students remain on campus after dismissal.

Slide 2: Evidence - Survey results shown in a simple bar chart.

Slide 3: Reasoning - A supervised study space addresses the need.

Slide 4: Conclusion - Study space supports safety and learning.
