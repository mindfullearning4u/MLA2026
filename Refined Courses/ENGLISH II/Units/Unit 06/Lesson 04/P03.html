P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Focus: Choosing slide features that clarify rather than distract

Deeper Learning

A digital presentation is not stronger just because it has more decoration. Every slide feature should help the audience understand the finding, evidence, or reasoning. If a feature draws attention away from the message, it weakens the presentation.

Useful feature: A labeled chart comparing 68 students who remain on campus with 49 students who wait in informal areas.

Distracting feature: A busy background image that makes the survey number hard to read.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Put every sentence of the speech on one slide with bright colors and moving text.

Correct Example:

Use concise slide text, a clear heading, and one simple chart that supports the speaker's explanation.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example overloads the audience. The correct example helps the audience see the evidence while listening to the speaker's reasoning.
