Directions: Read the new data brief and answer all 10 questions. Submit separately as Checkpoint Evidence.

Data Brief: Classroom Electricity Use

Facilities staff compared electricity use in one classroom wing before and after a four-week shutdown routine. Average weekday use fell from 418 kilowatt-hours to 356 kilowatt-hours. The routine asked teachers to shut down display panels, student computers, and document cameras after the final class. The comparison used the same four-week period one year apart, but outdoor temperatures differed slightly. The school estimates a reduction of 62 kilowatt-hours per weekday and approximately $510 in savings over the four-week period.

Questions

  1. State the main finding for one slide.
  2. Choose the best visual form and justify it.
  3. Write a precise slide title.
  4. Identify the two values that must appear and their units.
  5. Calculate or state the change that should be emphasized.
  6. Write a source/limitation note about the comparison periods.
  7. Write concise slide text of no more than 20 words.
  8. Write the speaker’s two-sentence explanation of the evidence and reasoning.
  9. Describe one accessible design feature and one misleading feature to avoid.
  10. Describe the complete slide layout and how it supports audience understanding.
