P05 — Independent Work: Design Evidence for Audience Understanding

Directions: Read the complete research brief. Answer all 12 questions. Submit with your vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Research Brief: Student Study Habits

A school survey asked 360 students where they usually complete homework. Results: 144 at home in a quiet space, 90 at home with frequent distractions, 72 at school before or after classes, 36 while traveling or waiting, and 18 reported no consistent study location. Students could choose only one response. A follow-up question found that 214 students wanted access to a supervised quiet space at least twice per week. The survey was voluntary; 360 of 820 students participated.

The presenter’s finding is: Students use varied study settings, and many would benefit from reliable supervised study space.
