📘 ENG2 | Unit 06 | Lesson 04

Lesson 4: Digital Presentation of Findings, Reasoning, and Evidence

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.C.02 - Use digital presentation choices to improve audience understanding of findings, reasoning, and evidence.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Clearly; MLA.ENG2.R.05 - Analyze Structure and Features; MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Clear Organization.

What You Will Learn

  • How digital slide choices can clarify findings, evidence, and reasoning.
  • How headings, sequence, visuals, and concise wording support audience understanding.
  • How to avoid slides that distract from the message.

What You Will Do

  • Study a presentation plan about after-school study space.
  • Decide which slide choices make the information clearer.
  • Create a simple digital presentation sequence with claim, evidence, reasoning, and conclusion.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains effective slide structure and feature choices.
  • Your guided practice and quiz answers identify strong digital presentation decisions.
  • Your checkpoint outlines a digital presentation that improves understanding.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can design digital slides so my audience can understand my findings, evidence, and reasoning without being distracted.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot tell whether a slide feature clarifies or distracts from your evidence.
