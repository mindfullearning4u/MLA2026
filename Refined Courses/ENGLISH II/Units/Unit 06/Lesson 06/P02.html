Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
integrated communicationThe coordinated use of spoken language, digital media, visuals, evidence, and delivery choices to communicate one unified message.
oral deliveryThe presentation of a message through voice, pacing, volume, pronunciation, eye contact, posture, and gesture.
digital supportA slide, image, chart, audio clip, video, or other electronic element used to clarify and strengthen spoken communication.
multimodalUsing two or more modes—such as spoken language, written text, visuals, audio, gesture, or spatial design—to create meaning.
synchronizationThe deliberate timing and coordination of spoken explanation with visual or digital content.
coherenceThe logical clarity of a presentation in which spoken and digital elements work together to support the central message.
redundancyUnnecessary repetition of the same information in spoken and visual forms without adding meaning or support.
complementary informationContent in one mode that adds meaning, detail, or perspective rather than simply repeating another mode.
audience engagementThe degree to which a presentation gains, maintains, and responds to the audience’s attention and interest.
accessibilityThe practice of designing spoken and digital content so people with varied abilities and needs can perceive and understand it.
technical rehearsalA practice session used to test timing, equipment, media, transitions, visibility, sound, and delivery before presenting.
contingency planA prepared alternative for continuing a presentation if technology, media, timing, or another planned element fails.
