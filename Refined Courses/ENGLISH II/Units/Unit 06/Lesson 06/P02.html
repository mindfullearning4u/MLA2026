P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Vocabulary

Integrated communication means the spoken explanation and digital slides support the same message.

Speaker notes are short notes that guide what the presenter says.

Alignment means the slide content and oral explanation fit together.

Tone is the speaker's attitude toward the subject and audience.

Publication-ready means prepared carefully enough to share with an audience.

Presentation Pair

Slide heading: Survey Evidence Shows an After-School Need

Slide bullet: 68 of 120 students remain on campus after dismissal.

Weak speaker note: This number is here and it is kind of important.

Better speaker note: This survey result matters because it shows the need affects many students, so a supervised study space would address a real campus issue.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Check that the slide and speaker note address the same evidence.

Step 2: Make the slide concise and visual.

Step 3: Use the speaker note to explain reasoning aloud.

Step 4: Revise tone and conventions so the presentation sounds formal and clear.

Step 5: Use collaborator feedback to check whether the slide and spoken explanation work together.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title.
  2. Copy the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Explain why the weak speaker note does not fully support the slide.
  4. Copy the better speaker note.
  5. Write one sentence explaining how the slide and speaker note align.

Model Example

Slide: 49 students usually wait in hallways or similar areas.

Speaker note: This evidence shows why supervision matters; many students are waiting in informal spaces instead of a location designed for study and safety.

Why this works: The slide shows the evidence, and the speaker note explains the reasoning.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or passage detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two possible interpretations, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
