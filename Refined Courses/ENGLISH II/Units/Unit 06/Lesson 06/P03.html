P03 — Lesson: Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Direct Teaching

Oral and digital communication work together when the speaker provides the explanation and the visual makes essential evidence easier to understand. A slide should never replace the speaker or introduce an unrelated message.

Five-Step Integration Process

  1. State the audience, purpose, and central claim.
  2. Select the evidence listeners need to see.
  3. Choose a visual form that matches that evidence.
  4. Write concise slide text and a separate spoken explanation.
  5. Rehearse timing, transitions, citations, accessibility, and audience response.

Example 1 — Statistic

Slide: “62 kWh less per weekday.” Speaker: explains the before/after comparison and its temperature limitation.

Example 2 — Process

Slide: a three-step diagram. Speaker: explains why each step matters and how the audience should act.

Example 3 — Source Comparison

Slide: a simple two-column table. Speaker: explains which source is more credible and why.

Common Mistakes

  • Reading full paragraphs from slides.
  • Showing evidence without spoken reasoning.
  • Using visuals that conflict with the claim.
  • Missing citations, labels, or accessible contrast.
