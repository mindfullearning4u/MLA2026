Lesson 6: Integrated Oral and Digital Communication

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Clearly; MLA.ENG2.C.02 - Use Digital Presentation Choices to Improve Understanding.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Use Precise Word Meaning and Tone; MLA.ENG2.C.03 - Publish and Share Work Collaboratively.

What You Will Learn

  • How oral explanation and digital slides work together.
  • How conventions, word choice, and tone affect a presentation.
  • How collaborative review improves communication before publication.

What You Will Do

  • Match speaker notes to slide content.
  • Revise tone and conventions for audience clarity.
  • Use peer or collaborator feedback to prepare a publishable presentation.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook aligns oral notes with slide content.
  • Your guided practice and quiz answers select strong integrated communication choices.
  • Your checkpoint includes a revised slide-and-speaker-note pair.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can make my spoken words and digital slides work together so the audience understands one clear message.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your slide says one thing but your speaker notes explain a different message.
