P06 — Checkpoint: Integrated Communication Plan

Directions: Read the new brief and answer all 10 questions. Submit separately as Checkpoint Evidence.

Brief: Cafeteria Waste Trial

A five-day audit measured 642 pounds of cafeteria waste: 351 pounds of food, 174 pounds of recyclable containers, and 117 pounds of other trash. A proposed four-week trial would add clearly labeled sorting stations and a two-minute student announcement. The audience is the principal, cafeteria manager, and student council. The audit covers only one school week and may not represent the entire year.

Questions

  1. State the claim and purpose.
  2. Identify the audience’s likely concern.
  3. Choose the most useful visual and justify it.
  4. Write a finding-based slide heading.
  5. List all labels, units, and source information.
  6. State the audit limitation.
  7. Write concise slide text.
  8. Write three sentences of spoken evidence and reasoning.
  9. Describe an accessible proposal slide and the transition to it.
  10. Write a closing recommendation and measurement plan.
