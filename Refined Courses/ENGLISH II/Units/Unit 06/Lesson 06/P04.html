P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Slide and Speaker Note Match

Slide: Many students remain on campus after dismissal.

Speaker note: The survey result shows this issue affects a large group of students, not just one person.

Reason: The speaker note explains the same evidence shown on the slide.

Example 2: Tone Revision

Weak note: This is obvious and people should just fix it.

Revised note: This evidence suggests school leaders should consider a supervised study location for students who remain after dismissal.

Reason: The revision uses respectful, formal tone.

Example 3: Convention Revision

Weak note: Students needs somewhere safe.

Revised note: Students need a safe, supervised place to wait after school.

Reason: Correct conventions make the spoken message more credible.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Make the slide formal but use casual speaker notes that weaken the message.

Correct Example:

Use formal, precise language in both slide text and speaker notes.

Teachable Explanation: Integrated communication means all parts work together. Tone, conventions, and word choice should be consistent across the digital and oral parts.
