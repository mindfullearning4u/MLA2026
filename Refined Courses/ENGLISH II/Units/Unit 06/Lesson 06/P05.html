P05 — Independent Work: Integrate Speaking and Digital Evidence

Directions: Read the complete brief and answer all 12 questions. Submit your answers with the Lesson 6 vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Brief: Expanding Peer Tutoring

During one semester, the tutoring center recorded 286 visits. Mathematics accounted for 118 visits, English for 74, science for 61, and other subjects for 33. Of 132 students completing an exit survey, 103 said tutoring helped them understand an assignment, 21 were unsure, and 8 said it did not help. The voluntary survey did not include every visitor. The proposal is to add one Wednesday session using trained peer tutors and an adult supervisor. The intended audience is the school leadership team.

Questions

  1. State the presentation claim.
  2. Identify the audience’s likely concerns.
  3. Choose the strongest evidence of demand.
  4. State the survey’s limitation.
  5. Choose a visual for subject-area visits and justify it.
  6. Write its finding-based heading.
  7. Write a source note and required labels.
  8. Write no more than 18 words of slide text.
  9. Write three sentences of spoken reasoning for that slide.
  10. Write the transition to the proposal slide.
  11. Describe the proposal slide and its accessible design.
  12. Write a closing that integrates the evidence, recommendation, and measurement plan.

Independent Work Checklist

  • I answered all 12 questions in order.
  • I stated a clear claim and identified the school leadership audience’s likely concerns.
  • I used the visit and survey data accurately and explained the voluntary-survey limitation.
  • I selected an appropriate visual, wrote a finding-based heading, and included labels and a source note.
  • My slide text uses no more than 18 words and highlights only essential evidence.
  • My three spoken sentences interpret the evidence instead of repeating the slide.
  • I included a logical transition to the proposal.
  • My proposal slide is concise, readable, high-contrast, and understandable without color alone.
  • My closing connects the evidence, recommendation, and a way to measure the pilot’s results.
  • My responses are complete, readable, and written in standard English.
