P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 2: Organizing Oral Information with Credible Evidence

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Organizing Oral Information with Credible Evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present information clearly with organization, evidence, and audience awareness.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas; MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Use Credible Research; MLA.ENG2.W.02 - Use Claims, Evidence, and Reasoning.

What You Will Learn

  • How to organize oral information so listeners can follow your point.
  • How credible evidence supports a spoken claim.
  • How to connect evidence to reasoning instead of listing facts without explanation.

Why This Matters

Listeners need a clear sequence and credible evidence to follow and trust a message. This skill supports academic presentations, workplace proposals, and civic discussion.

What You Will Do

  • Read a short source set about after-school study support.
  • Identify a central idea and select credible evidence.
  • Build an oral outline using claim, evidence, reasoning, and closing statement.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook organizes oral information in a logical sequence.
  • Your practice and quiz answers choose credible evidence and clear reasoning.
  • Your checkpoint includes an oral outline that listeners could follow.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can organize what I say so my listeners hear a clear claim, credible evidence, and reasoning that explains why the evidence matters.
