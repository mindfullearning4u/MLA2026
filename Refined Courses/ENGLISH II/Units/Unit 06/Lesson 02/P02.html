Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
oral presentationA planned spoken communication delivered to an audience for a specific purpose.
organizational structureThe deliberate arrangement of ideas and evidence so listeners can follow and remember a spoken message.
introductionThe opening section that gains attention, establishes the topic and purpose, and prepares listeners for the message.
main pointA central idea that develops one important part of a presentation’s overall message.
supporting detailA fact, example, explanation, quotation, or statistic that develops and clarifies a main point.
credible evidenceTrustworthy, relevant, and accurate information from reliable sources used to support a spoken claim or explanation.
source attributionA spoken acknowledgment identifying the author, organization, or source of borrowed information.
signpostA spoken cue that tells listeners where the presentation is going or how one section relates to another.
transitionA word, phrase, or sentence that connects ideas and clarifies their relationship for listeners.
sequenceThe order in which ideas, events, steps, or evidence are presented.
conclusionThe closing section that reinforces the central message, synthesizes key points, and provides a sense of completion.
oral citationA brief spoken reference that gives listeners enough information to recognize and evaluate the source of evidence.
