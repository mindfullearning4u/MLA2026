P05 — Independent Work: Build a Credible Oral Presentation

Directions: Read the complete source set. Then answer all 12 questions in complete sentences. This work is submitted with your vocabulary as Notebook Evidence.

Source Set: Restoring the School Courtyard

For three years, the central courtyard at Franklin High has been closed during lunch because cracked pavement, poor drainage, and damaged benches create safety concerns. The student environmental council proposes a six-week restoration project. Its plan would replace two broken benches, add three native-plant beds, repaint faded boundary lines, and create a supervised outdoor reading area.

Source A — Facilities Report: A March inspection documented four pavement cracks wider than one inch, two unusable benches, and standing water near the east entrance after moderate rain. The report recommends repairing drainage before reopening the space. The facilities director estimates essential repairs at $6,800.

Source B — Student Survey: Of 312 students who responded, 226 said they would use a supervised courtyard for reading or quiet lunch at least once a week. Fifty-eight students said they were unsure, and twenty-eight said they would not use it. The survey was sent to all students, but participation was voluntary.

Source C — Principal Interview: The principal supports exploring the proposal but explains that supervision, accessibility, maintenance, and cost must be addressed. She suggests a limited trial after repairs so the school can measure use and behavior before committing additional funds.

Source D — Anonymous Comment: “Everybody knows outdoor spaces always improve grades, so the school should approve the plan immediately.” The comment provides no author, research, or data.

Questions

  1. State the central point of a presentation supporting a responsible courtyard reopening.
  2. Which source is most credible for explaining the safety problem? Explain why.
  3. Which source best shows likely student use? State one limitation of that evidence.
  4. Why is Source D weaker than the other sources?
  5. Group the credible evidence into two logical categories and label each category.
  6. Choose an organizational pattern and explain why it fits this topic.
  7. Write an opening that introduces the issue and central point.
  8. Write a transition from the safety problem to student benefits.
  9. Use one fact from Source A and add reasoning that explains why it matters.
  10. Use one fact from Source B or C and add reasoning that explains why it matters.
  11. Write a respectful response to the principal’s concern about supervision or cost.
  12. Create a six-part oral outline: opening, point 1, evidence and reasoning, point 2, evidence and reasoning, closing.
Success Check: Your responses distinguish evidence from opinion, explain credibility, organize ideas logically, and connect every fact to the central point.
