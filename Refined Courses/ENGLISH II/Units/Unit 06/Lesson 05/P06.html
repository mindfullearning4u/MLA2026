Presentation Situation

You will present to school administrators about whether to add another water-bottle filling station near the gym.

Source A — Refill Counter and Observation

The existing station’s electronic counter recorded 8,420 refills from August through December. During six after-school athletic events, student volunteers observed lines of five or more people 18 times. The counter does not identify who used the station, and the six events may not represent normal school days.

Source B — Administrator Requirement

Administrators will consider the request if the presentation distinguishes normal-use evidence from event observations, identifies the limits of both measures, provides a clear location rationale, and recommends how a short pilot or additional observation could test the need.

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Write a focused claim appropriate for the administrators.
  2. Select the most useful number from Source A and explain what it shows.
  3. Explain one limitation of the refill counter and one limitation of the event observations.
  4. Write a precise slide title that communicates the evidence takeaway.
  5. Choose and describe a visual that accurately represents the two different types of evidence without misleading the audience.
  6. Provide every label, caption, and source credit the visual needs.
  7. Write three complementary speaker-note sentences that interpret and qualify the evidence.
  8. Explain how your slide and speaker notes work together to improve audience understanding.
  9. Use Source B to recommend one practical next step or additional data collection method.
  10. Identify one clarity or accessibility revision you would make after testing the slide with an audience and explain why.
