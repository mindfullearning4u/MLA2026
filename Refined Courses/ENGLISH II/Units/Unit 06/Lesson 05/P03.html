P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Notebook Focus: Revising slides for audience understanding

Deeper Learning

Digital revision should protect the evidence while making it easier to understand. Do not delete the strongest evidence because it is hard to format. Instead, revise the slide design so the evidence is readable and meaningful.

Strong evidence statement: 68 of 120 surveyed students remain on campus after dismissal at least one day each week.

Audience need: Viewers need to know that the issue affects many students, not just one speaker.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Remove the survey numbers because they make the slide harder to design.

Correct Example:

Keep the survey numbers and display them in a clear labeled chart or concise evidence statement.

Teachable Explanation: Evidence should not disappear during digital revision. The correct revision keeps the evidence and improves how the audience sees and understands it.
