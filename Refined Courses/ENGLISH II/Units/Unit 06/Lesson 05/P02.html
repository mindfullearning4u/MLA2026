P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Digital Clarity and Evidence Understanding

Vocabulary

Clarity means the information is easy to see, read, and understand.

Evidence display means the way data, quotations, or source information appears on a slide.

Audience needs are what viewers must understand in order to follow the message.

Revision means changing a draft to improve communication.

Academic vocabulary means precise school language, such as evidence, finding, reasoning, and credible source.

Draft Slide Set

Slide 1 heading: Stuff We Found

Slide 2 text: A survey had 120 students and some stayed and some waited places and it means something about needing a space.

Slide 3 visual: A colorful background with small unreadable numbers.

Slide 4 closing: The end.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Replace vague headings with headings that state the finding.

Step 2: Break long slide text into concise evidence statements.

Step 3: Make numbers readable and label what they mean.

Step 4: Add reasoning so the audience knows why the evidence matters.

Step 5: Revise the closing so it reinforces the message.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title.
  2. Copy the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Identify three clarity problems in the draft slide set.
  4. Revise one heading and one evidence statement.
  5. Explain why your revision helps the audience.

Model Example

Weak heading: Stuff We Found

Revised heading: Survey Evidence Shows Students Need Supervised Space

Why this works: The revised heading uses academic vocabulary and tells the audience what the slide proves.
