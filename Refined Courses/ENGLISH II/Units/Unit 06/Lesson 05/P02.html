Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
clarityThe quality of communicating information in a way the audience can understand accurately and without unnecessary confusion.
evidence understandingThe audience’s ability to recognize what evidence shows, how reliable it is, and how it supports the speaker’s message.
visual evidenceInformation communicated through a chart, graph, image, map, diagram, table, or other visual form.
data labelText that identifies a value, category, unit, source, or feature within a chart or graph.
captionText that identifies, describes, or explains an image, chart, audio segment, or video content.
annotationA brief note or visual marker added to highlight and explain an important feature of evidence.
readabilityThe ease with which an audience can read and understand displayed text and visual information.
legibilityThe ease with which individual letters, numbers, symbols, and words can be visually distinguished.
visual hierarchyThe arrangement of elements to show what is most important and guide the order in which information is viewed.
signal-to-noise ratioThe balance between useful information and distracting or unnecessary elements in a visual display.
contextBackground information that helps the audience interpret evidence accurately and understand its significance.
interpretationAn explanation of what evidence means and how it connects to a claim, finding, or central message.
