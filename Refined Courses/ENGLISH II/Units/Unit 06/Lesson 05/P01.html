Lesson 5: Digital Presentation Clarity and Evidence Understanding

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.C.02 - Use digital presentation choices to improve audience understanding of evidence and reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Use Credible Evidence; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Use Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to revise digital slides so evidence is easy to understand.
  • How to make slide wording, labels, and visuals fit the audience.
  • How academic vocabulary helps viewers understand findings and reasoning.

What You Will Do

  • Study a draft slide set about after-school study space.
  • Find slide problems that make evidence unclear.
  • Revise digital presentation choices for clarity, audience, and evidence understanding.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook identifies unclear slide choices and explains revisions.
  • Your guided practice and quiz answers choose clear evidence-display decisions.
  • Your checkpoint revises a slide plan for stronger audience understanding.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can revise digital slides so my audience understands my evidence, my reasoning, and why the information matters.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot explain how a slide choice helps the audience understand evidence.
