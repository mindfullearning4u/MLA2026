P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 5: Digital Presentation Clarity and Evidence Understanding

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Digital Presentation Clarity and Evidence Understanding

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.C.02 - Use digital presentation choices to improve audience understanding of evidence and reasoning.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Use Credible Evidence; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Use Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to revise digital slides so evidence is easy to understand.
  • How to make slide wording, labels, and visuals fit the audience.
  • How academic vocabulary helps viewers understand findings and reasoning.

Why This Matters

Clear evidence displays help audiences make sense of findings and reasoning. Revising for audience needs prevents misleading, crowded, or inaccessible presentations.

What You Will Do

  • Study a draft slide set about after-school study space.
  • Find slide problems that make evidence unclear.
  • Revise digital presentation choices for clarity, audience, and evidence understanding.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz or unit assessment when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook identifies unclear slide choices and explains revisions.
  • Your practice and quiz answers choose clear evidence-display decisions.
  • Your checkpoint revises a slide plan for stronger audience understanding.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can revise digital slides so my audience understands my evidence, my reasoning, and why the information matters.
