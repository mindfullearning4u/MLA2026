P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 3: Presenting a Clear Perspective with Coherent Focus

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Presenting a Clear Perspective with Coherent Focus

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present a clear perspective with coherent focus for a specific audience and purpose.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.07 - Analyze Author Purpose; MLA.ENG2.R.08 - Analyze Arguments and Shared Information; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Use Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to present one clear perspective instead of several disconnected ideas.
  • How author purpose and argument choices can guide your own speaking choices.
  • How academic vocabulary helps listeners follow your focus.

Why This Matters

A coherent perspective helps an audience understand exactly what you believe and why. Staying focused strengthens leadership, collaboration, and public communication.

What You Will Do

  • Study a short speaking scenario with two possible perspectives.
  • Choose a clear focused perspective for an oral presentation.
  • Revise speaking notes so every detail supports the same message.

Lesson Workflow

  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook identifies a focused perspective and removes off-topic ideas.
  • Your practice and quiz answers choose coherent oral communication choices.
  • Your checkpoint presents a focused claim, supporting points, and clear academic language.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can speak from a clear point of view and keep my details focused so listeners understand my message.
