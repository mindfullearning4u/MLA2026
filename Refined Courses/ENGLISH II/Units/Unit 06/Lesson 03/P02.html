Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
perspectiveA particular viewpoint shaped by a person’s experiences, knowledge, values, and position.
central messageThe most important idea or understanding a speaker wants the audience to remember.
coherent focusA clear and consistent concentration on the central message, with every part logically connected to it.
claimA debatable statement that expresses the speaker’s position or conclusion.
supporting pointA major idea that develops, explains, or proves one part of the central message.
relevanceThe degree to which information or evidence directly connects to the speaker’s purpose and central message.
coherenceThe logical clarity of a presentation in which ideas are ordered and connected so the message makes sense.
unityThe quality of a presentation in which all ideas and details contribute to one controlling focus.
emphasisSpecial importance given to an idea through placement, repetition, vocal delivery, language, or visual support.
framingThe way a speaker introduces and presents an issue to shape how the audience understands its meaning and importance.
qualifierA word or phrase that limits or clarifies a claim so it is accurate and appropriately precise.
call to actionA statement that asks or encourages the audience to take a specific action after hearing the message.
