P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Clear Perspective and Coherent Focus

Vocabulary

Perspective means the point of view or position a speaker presents.

Coherent focus means all parts of the message connect to the same main point.

Purpose means the reason for speaking.

Argument means a claim supported with evidence and reasoning.

Relevant detail is information that directly supports the speaker's perspective.

Read the Speaking Scenario

Scenario: A student is preparing a two-minute presentation for a school meeting. The student wants school leaders to understand that an after-school study space would support students who remain on campus. The student has notes about hallway noise, late buses, student safety, favorite library books, cafeteria snacks, and homework completion.

The speaker cannot include every idea. A focused presentation should keep details that support the perspective and remove details that distract from it.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: State the perspective in one sentence.

Step 2: Identify details that directly support that perspective.

Step 3: Remove details that may be interesting but do not support the main point.

Step 4: Put the strongest details in a logical order.

Step 5: Use academic vocabulary that helps listeners understand the focus.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title.
  2. Copy the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Write one focused perspective for the speaking scenario.
  4. List three relevant details from the scenario.
  5. List two distracting details that should be removed.

Model Example

Focused perspective: A supervised after-school study space would help students remain safe and academically productive while waiting on campus.

Relevant details: hallway noise, late buses, student safety, homework completion.

Distracting details: favorite library books and cafeteria snacks do not directly support the presentation's focus.
