# ENGLISH II - Unit 06 Overview: Synthesis, Presentation, and Independent Application

---

## Unit Overview

This unit serves as the capstone for English II. Students independently synthesize ideas across multiple texts and sources, present information orally, create digital presentations, and demonstrate independent communication skills.

Emphasis is placed on independent synthesis, audience awareness, organization, credible evidence, coherent focus, multimedia communication, and effective oral and digital communication. Students apply skills from the full course to demonstrate independent reading, writing, research, language, and presentation readiness.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through independent synthesis, presentation, digital communication, multimedia use, and final application.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- independent synthesis of evidence and ideas across multiple texts and sources;
- independent organization and communication;
- oral presentation with coherent focus and credible evidence;
- digital presentation development;
- audience-aware and evidence-driven communication.

Assessment items require students to independently synthesize information from multiple sources, organize and present ideas orally with coherent focus, credible evidence, and a clear perspective, and create a digital presentation that improves understanding of findings, reasoning, and evidence. Students must demonstrate full-course integration of reading, writing, language, research, and communication skills.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit connects reading, writing, research, language, and communication into a final independent performance. Students independently synthesize evidence from multiple sources, organize ideas, adapt communication for audience and purpose, and present a clear, well-supported perspective.
