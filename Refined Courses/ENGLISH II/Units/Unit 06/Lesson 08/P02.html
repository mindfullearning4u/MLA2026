P02 Notebook Task - Part 1: Unit 6 Synthesis Checklist

Notebook Title: Unit 6 Lesson 8 - Oral and Digital Presentation Synthesis

Vocabulary Review

Purpose: the reason for communicating.

Audience: the people who will receive the message.

Perspective: the speaker's position or point of view.

Evidence: information used to support a claim.

Digital support: slides, charts, labels, and visuals that help the audience understand.

Synthesis: combining several skills into one complete performance.

Synthesis Presentation Plan

Audience: school leaders.

Purpose: explain why a supervised after-school study space would help students.

Claim: The school should provide a supervised study space after dismissal.

Evidence: In a survey of 120 students, 68 reported staying on campus after school at least twice a week.

Slide: Survey Evidence: More Than Half of Surveyed Students Stay After School.

Speaker Note: This evidence matters because it shows the need affects many students, so the proposal responds to a real campus issue.

Step-by-Step Synthesis Process

Step 1: Confirm audience and purpose. Every choice should help school leaders understand the proposal.

Step 2: Check the claim. The claim should state the speaker's position clearly.

Step 3: Check the evidence. The evidence should be specific and credible.

Step 4: Check the slide. The slide should make the evidence easy to see and understand.

Step 5: Check the speaker note. The note should explain why the evidence matters.

Notebook Directions: Copy the synthesis checklist and apply it to the presentation plan above.

Model Example

Question: Does the slide and note work together?

Answer: Yes. The slide shows the key survey evidence, and the speaker note explains why the evidence supports the proposal.

Why this works: The digital and oral parts are aligned, evidence-based, and focused on the intended audience.
