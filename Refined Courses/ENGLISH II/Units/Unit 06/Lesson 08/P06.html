📘 ENG2 | Unit 06 | Lesson 08

Independent Work

This work prepares you for the Unit 6 Assessment by requiring you to combine presentation skills.

Instructions

Part A: Write a claim, one credible evidence statement, and one slide title for a presentation to school leaders.

Part B: Write a speaker note that explains how the evidence supports the claim.

Part C: Revise your work for audience fit, formal tone, standard English conventions, and slide-note alignment.

Expectation: Your final answer should show that you understand the complete presentation process from planning to publication readiness.
