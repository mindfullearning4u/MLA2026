Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 6 Oral and Digital Presentation Synthesis

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01, MLA.ENG2.C.02, and MLA.ENG2.C.03.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01, MLA.ENG2.W.04, MLA.ENG2.L.01, and MLA.ENG2.V.03.

What You Will Learn

You will bring together Unit 6 skills: identifying audience and purpose, organizing oral ideas, using credible evidence, creating clear digital slides, aligning speaker notes, revising tone and conventions, and preparing work for publication.

What You Will Do

You will evaluate a complete presentation plan, revise weak parts, and prepare for the Unit 6 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery through synthesis notebook work, guided practice, independent work, the checkpoint, and the Unit 6 Assessment.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

These standards mean: I can communicate a clear message to an audience using spoken explanation, digital support, credible evidence, revision, and formal language.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you are unsure how to connect a slide, speaker note, evidence, and audience purpose before the Unit 6 Assessment.

Need Help?

If you are confused, stuck, or unsure about any part of this lesson, contact your Teacher of Record (TOR).

  • Clarify directions
  • Explain concepts
  • Review your work
  • Answer questions
