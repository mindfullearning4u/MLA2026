P03 Notebook Task - Part 2: Diagnose and Revise the Whole Presentation

In synthesis work, you do not check one skill alone. You check how the entire communication task works together.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: If the audience is school leaders, the language should be respectful, formal, and specific.

Step 2: If the purpose is to explain a proposal, the presentation needs a claim, evidence, and reasoning.

Step 3: If the evidence is numerical, the slide should label the number clearly.

Step 4: If the speaker note repeats the slide, revise it so it explains the meaning of the evidence.

Notebook Directions: Write one diagnosis statement and one revision for the slide or speaker note in the synthesis presentation plan.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The slide has a number, so the presentation automatically proves the claim.

Correct: The slide gives evidence, and the speaker must explain how that evidence supports the claim.

Why this matters: Evidence does not explain itself. A strong synthesis presentation uses the slide to show evidence and the speaker note to make the reasoning clear.
