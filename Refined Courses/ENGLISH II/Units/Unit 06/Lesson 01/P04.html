P04 Worked Example

Example 1: Identifying Purpose

Speaking task: Explain why the library should stay open after school.

Thinking: The speaker is not telling a story for entertainment. The speaker wants listeners to understand a school need.

Purpose: To explain and persuade.

Example 2: Identifying Audience

Audience: School leaders.

Thinking: School leaders need respectful wording, clear reasons, and specific student needs.

Best speaking note: Extended library hours would support students who need a quiet place to work while waiting for transportation.

Example 3: Identifying Perspective

Perspective: A student explaining a learning need.

Thinking: The speaker should not sound angry or careless. The speaker should explain what students experience and why it matters.

Strong statement: From a student perspective, the main concern is having a supervised place to complete assignments after regular school hours.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

Use the same words for every audience.

Correct Example:

Keep the main idea, but adjust details, tone, and vocabulary for the specific audience.

Teachable Explanation: Audience awareness does not mean changing your truth. It means presenting your message in the clearest and most appropriate way for the listeners.
