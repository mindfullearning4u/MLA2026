P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Notebook Focus: Matching the message to the listener

Deeper Learning

Oral communication changes when the audience changes. The main idea may stay the same, but the details, tone, and vocabulary should fit the listeners.

Scenario: You want to explain why students need a quiet study space after school.

If your audience is students: You might mention homework, noise, and waiting for rides.

If your audience is school leaders: You should mention supervision, academic support, student safety, and respectful evidence.

Notebook Task: Write two speaking notes for the same idea: one for students and one for school leaders.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

You all need to fix this because the hallway is annoying.

Correct Example:

School leaders should consider a supervised study space because students who wait after school need a quiet place to complete assignments.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example is too casual and demanding for school leaders. The correct example keeps the same concern but presents it with respectful tone, clear purpose, and audience-appropriate vocabulary.
