Later Bus Service Proposal

Situation: Give a two-minute statement to the district transportation committee supporting a six-week trial of one later bus route.

Draft: You should add a late bus because lots of kids need it and it would be awesome. Everybody knows activities are important, so this should be easy to approve.

Verified facts: 74 students participate in after-school tutoring or clubs at least twice weekly; 29 students reported missing activities because they lack transportation; the trial would run Tuesdays and Thursdays; estimated six-week cost is $2,400; the committee is concerned about cost, safety, and ridership.

Questions

  1. State the speaker, audience, occasion, and purpose.
  2. Identify two committee concerns.
  3. Write a clear, supportable perspective.
  4. Identify two phrases that weaken audience trust.
  5. Select the two strongest facts and explain why.
  6. Explain one fact that needs context.
  7. Write a respectful audience connection.
  8. Organize three speaking points.
  9. Write a polished 5–7 sentence opening using only verified facts.
  10. Explain one purpose choice, one audience choice, and one perspective choice.
