Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
oral communicationThe exchange of ideas and information through spoken language supported by delivery choices and audience interaction.
purposeThe intended result of a spoken message, such as informing, explaining, persuading, entertaining, or inspiring.
audienceThe specific listeners for whom a message is planned and delivered.
audience analysisThe process of identifying listeners’ knowledge, needs, expectations, values, and likely responses before communicating.
perspectiveA particular viewpoint shaped by a person’s experiences, knowledge, values, and position.
speakerThe person who creates and delivers an oral message to an audience.
messageThe central idea, information, or position a speaker intends the audience to understand.
contextThe situation, setting, occasion, and background conditions that influence how an oral message is created and interpreted.
toneThe speaker’s attitude toward the subject or audience as communicated through words and delivery.
registerThe level and style of language chosen for a particular audience, purpose, and situation.
deliveryThe way a speaker presents a message through voice, pacing, volume, pronunciation, eye contact, posture, and gesture.
point of viewThe position or angle from which a speaker presents and interprets information or experiences.
