P06 — Checkpoint: Presentation Readiness Revision Plan

Directions: Review the new draft and answer all 10 questions. Submit separately as Checkpoint Evidence.

Draft: Community Garden Trial

A student proposes a one-semester garden trial. Evidence includes 87 of 120 surveyed students expressing interest, a $1,750 supply estimate, and two teachers volunteering to supervise. The survey was voluntary. The opening is vague, one slide uses an unlabeled pie chart, another includes tiny source text, and the conclusion does not explain how success will be measured.

Feedback: Clarify the claim; acknowledge the survey limitation; explain cost; improve labels and contrast; propose measurable outcomes.

Questions

  1. Write the precise claim and purpose.
  2. Identify the audience’s likely concerns.
  3. Prioritize three revisions and justify the order.
  4. Rewrite the opening.
  5. Correct the survey-evidence slide.
  6. Write the spoken reasoning and limitation.
  7. Explain the cost and supervision evidence.
  8. List accessibility and citation corrections.
  9. Write a conclusion with measurable outcomes.
  10. Create a final five-point readiness checklist.
