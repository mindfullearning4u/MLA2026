P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Full-Course Synthesis for Presentation Readiness

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 and MLA.ENG2.C.02.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01, MLA.ENG2.W.03, MLA.ENG2.W.04, and MLA.ENG2.C.03.

What You Will Learn

You will learn how to rehearse an oral and digital presentation so the slide design, speaker notes, evidence, tone, and conventions work together before you submit or present the final version.

What You Will Do

You will use a rehearsal checklist, study feedback, revise a slide-and-speaker-note pair, and prepare a publication-ready presentation plan.

How You Will Show Mastery

You will show mastery by identifying effective rehearsal choices, revising weak presentation materials, and submitting a checkpoint that explains how feedback improved your final presentation.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

These standards mean: I can present a clear message, use digital tools to help my audience understand, and revise my work with feedback before I share it.

Ask Your Teacher of Record for Help

Ask your Teacher of Record if you are unsure whether your slides and speaker notes match, whether your tone is formal enough, or whether your final presentation is ready to submit.
