Directions: Review the complete draft and feedback. Answer all 12 questions. Submit with Lesson 7 vocabulary.

Draft: Later Library Hours

The presentation recommends an eight-week Tuesday/Thursday trial. It cites 96 after-school research-help requests, activity buses averaging 61% capacity, and an estimated $1,100 staffing cost. Slide 1 says “Libraries Are Great.” Slide 2 shows the 96 requests without a source label. Slide 3 contains a full 135-word paragraph about transportation. The conclusion says, “So that is why this idea is good.”

Feedback: Teacher—make the claim more precise and cite data. Student reviewer—explain cost and transportation aloud instead of crowding slides. Accessibility reviewer—increase contrast, label charts, and add alternative text.

Questions

  2. Rank the three most important revisions.
  3. Rewrite Slide 1’s heading.
  4. Design Slide 2 with a source label and finding statement.
  5. Reduce Slide 3 to concise text.
  6. Write the spoken explanation for Slide 3.
  7. Explain how the bus evidence supports feasibility.
  8. Address the $1,100 concern.
  9. Write a transition from need to feasibility.
  10. List accessibility corrections.
  11. Write a stronger conclusion with an audience action.
  12. Create a final rehearsal checklist covering accuracy, coherence, delivery, timing, citations, and technology.
