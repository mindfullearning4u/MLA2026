Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
synthesisThe process of combining ideas, evidence, and skills from multiple sources or learning experiences into a new, coherent whole.
presentation readinessThe state of being fully prepared in content, organization, evidence, visuals, delivery, timing, and technology for an audience.
central messageThe most important idea or understanding a presenter wants the audience to remember.
perspectiveA particular viewpoint shaped by a person’s experiences, knowledge, values, and position.
evidenceFacts, examples, data, quotations, images, or observations used to support a claim or explanation.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how evidence supports a claim, finding, or conclusion.
source credibilityThe trustworthiness of a source based on expertise, accuracy, evidence, transparency, and responsible purpose.
organizational structureThe deliberate arrangement of ideas and evidence so an audience can follow and remember the message.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s needs, knowledge, expectations, and interests when making content and delivery choices.
oral deliveryThe presentation of a message through voice, pacing, volume, pronunciation, eye contact, posture, and gesture.
digital supportA slide, image, chart, audio clip, video, or other electronic element used to clarify and strengthen spoken communication.
rehearsal and revisionThe repeated practice and improvement of presentation content, visuals, timing, and delivery based on evaluation and feedback.
