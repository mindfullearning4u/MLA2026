P03 — Lesson: Full-Course Synthesis for Presentation Readiness

Direct Teaching

Presentation readiness requires synthesis: combining reading, research, writing, vocabulary, oral communication, and digital design into one accurate, coherent message for a real audience.

Six-Step Readiness Process

  1. Confirm the claim, audience, and purpose.
  2. Verify every source, quotation, statistic, and limitation.
  3. Organize the presentation into a logical sequence.
  4. Align every slide with the spoken reasoning.
  5. Revise language, transitions, conventions, citations, and accessibility.
  6. Rehearse timing and use feedback to prioritize final changes.

Example 1 — Evidence Audit

Replace “everyone agrees” with a verified sample count and limitation.

Example 2 — Coherence Audit

Move cost evidence beside the feasibility claim instead of separating related ideas.

Example 3 — Delivery Audit

Shorten slide text, emphasize the key number, and explain its significance aloud.

Common Mistakes

  • Making cosmetic changes before correcting accuracy.
  • Ignoring contradictory feedback without evaluating it.
  • Adding new evidence during rehearsal without verifying it.
  • Ending without a clear audience action or takeaway.
