P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Collaborative Multimedia Publication and Audience Awareness

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Collaborative Multimedia Publication and Audience Awareness

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.C.03 - Publish Collaborative Multimedia Writing.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Voice; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to adapt research writing for a multimedia publication.
  • How audience needs affect wording, organization, and visual choices.
  • How collaborators can divide work while keeping one clear message.

Why This Matters

Multimedia communication combines accurate information, clear writing, visuals, and teamwork. These skills are common in school projects, workplaces, community organizations, and digital publishing.

What You Will Do

  • Review research findings for a student audience.
  • Plan a publication with text, evidence, and a visual element.
  • Revise content so it is clear, accurate, and audience-aware.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz or unit assessment when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains audience, purpose, message, evidence, and visual choice.
  • Your practice answers select publication choices that improve understanding.
  • Your checkpoint plan shows collaborative roles and audience-aware revision.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can work with others to turn research writing into a clear multimedia publication that fits a specific audience and purpose.
