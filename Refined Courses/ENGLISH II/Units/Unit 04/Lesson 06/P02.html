Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
multimedia publicationA published work that combines two or more forms of communication, such as written text, images, audio, video, charts, or interactive elements.
collaborationA process in which group members share responsibility, ideas, feedback, and tasks to create a common product.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s knowledge, needs, expectations, and interests when making communication choices.
purposeThe intended result of a publication, such as informing, explaining, persuading, documenting, or engaging an audience.
modeA form of communication used to convey meaning, such as linguistic, visual, audio, spatial, or gestural expression.
mediumThe platform or format through which information is delivered, such as a website, video, podcast, slideshow, or digital article.
design elementA visual or structural feature—such as color, typography, spacing, image placement, or layout—used to organize and communicate information.
accessibilityThe practice of designing content so people with varied abilities and needs can perceive, understand, navigate, and use it.
captionText that identifies, describes, or explains an image, chart, audio segment, or video content.
attributionClear acknowledgment of the creator or source of borrowed words, images, audio, video, data, or ideas.
revision protocolAn agreed-upon process for reviewing, improving, approving, and tracking changes to a collaborative publication.
publication platformThe digital service, site, or tool used to organize, present, and distribute a multimedia product to its intended audience.
