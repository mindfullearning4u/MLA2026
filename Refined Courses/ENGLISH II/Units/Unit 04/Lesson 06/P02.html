Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Audience-Aware Multimedia Publication

Vocabulary

Multimedia publication is a published piece that combines writing with visual or digital elements.

Audience is the group the publication is designed to reach.

Purpose is what the publication is meant to explain, show, or encourage.

Collaborative role is a specific responsibility a team member completes.

Visual evidence is a chart, image, icon, or layout choice that helps readers understand information.

Publication Scenario

Research finding: Pre-order lunch forms may reduce unwanted trays when students complete the form consistently and receive reminders.

Audience: Students who use the cafeteria.

Purpose: Explain how completing the form can help reduce food waste.

Publication format: A one-page digital flyer with a short explanation, one evidence statement, and a simple visual showing the steps.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the audience. Students need clear, direct language and practical steps.

Step 2: Identify the purpose. The flyer should explain why completing the form matters.

Step 3: Choose evidence. Use one short finding about reduced unwanted trays.

Step 4: Choose a visual. A three-step flow chart can show: choose lunch, staff prepare meals, fewer trays are wasted.

Step 5: Assign roles. One student can draft text, one can check evidence, and one can design the visual layout.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the audience, purpose, and research finding.
  2. Write one audience-aware sentence for the flyer.
  3. Choose one visual that would help students understand the finding.
  4. List three collaborative roles for a team creating the flyer.

Model Example

Audience-aware sentence: Completing the lunch form helps cafeteria staff prepare the meals students actually choose, which can reduce unwanted trays.

Visual choice: A three-step flow chart.

Collaborative roles: Writer, evidence checker, visual designer.
