P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now you will revise a publication plan for audience awareness.

Draft Publication Plan

Audience: Students using the cafeteria.

Draft text: "The pilot produced a measurable reduction in unwanted tray outputs due to advance meal-selection compliance."

Draft visual: A decorative image of random vegetables with no labels.

Problem: The text is too technical for the audience, and the visual does not explain the research finding.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Keep the evidence accurate. The pilot reduced unwanted trays.

Step 2: Translate technical wording for the audience. Students need direct wording.

Step 3: Choose a visual that supports understanding. A labeled process graphic is stronger than decoration.

Step 4: Make sure every team role supports the same message.

Notebook Application

  1. Rewrite the draft text for students.
  2. Choose a better visual and explain why it fits.
  3. Write one role for a teammate who checks evidence accuracy.
  4. Write one role for a teammate who checks audience clarity.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Use a random food picture because it looks nice.

Correct: Use a labeled flow chart showing how completing the form helps staff prepare fewer unwanted trays.

Teachable Explanation: Multimedia elements should improve understanding, not simply decorate the page.
