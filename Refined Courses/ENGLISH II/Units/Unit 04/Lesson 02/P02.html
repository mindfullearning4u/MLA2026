Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
source reliabilityThe degree to which a source consistently provides accurate, verifiable, and dependable information.
validityThe extent to which information, evidence, or a method accurately supports the conclusion for which it is used.
credibilityThe quality of being trustworthy because the author or source demonstrates expertise, accuracy, and responsible use of evidence.
biasA preference or viewpoint that may influence how information is selected, emphasized, omitted, or interpreted.
authorityThe expertise, qualifications, experience, or institutional standing that makes an author knowledgeable about a subject.
accuracyThe quality of being factually correct and supported by evidence that can be checked.
currencyHow recent and up to date a source is in relation to the research topic.
relevanceThe degree to which a source directly addresses the research question and provides useful information for the inquiry.
purposeThe reason a source was created, such as to inform, persuade, entertain, sell, or advocate for a position.
corroborationThe process of checking a claim against additional independent sources to determine whether the information agrees.
primary sourceOriginal evidence created during the time being studied or by a person directly involved in an event or investigation.
secondary sourceA source that analyzes, interprets, or summarizes information drawn from primary sources and other evidence.
