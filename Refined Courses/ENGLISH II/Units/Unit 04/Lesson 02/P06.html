Independent Work

Instructions

Use the source cards below to complete Parts A, B, and C.

Research Question

How could later bus service affect after-school job access for high school students in one neighborhood?

Source Cards

Source A: A city transportation report from 2025 lists evening bus routes, ridership numbers, and survey responses from high school students who work after school.

Source B: A social media comment says, "Buses are always terrible and nobody should rely on them." It gives no data.

Source C: A bus company advertisement describes comfortable seats but does not discuss student jobs or evening access.

Part A

Identify the most reliable source for the research question and explain why it is reliable.

Part B

Identify one source that is biased or not useful. Quote or paraphrase the detail that shows the problem.

Part C

Write a three-sentence source evaluation explaining which source you would use, which source you would avoid, and how your choice supports the research question.
