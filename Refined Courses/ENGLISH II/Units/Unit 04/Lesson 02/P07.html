Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph that evaluates Source A, Source B, and Source C from the Independent Work page. Identify the best source for the research question, identify one biased or weak source, and explain your reasoning with details from the source cards.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your paragraph names the best source and explains why it is reliable.
  • Your paragraph identifies bias or lack of usefulness in another source.
  • Your explanation connects the source choice to the research question.
  • Your writing uses precise academic vocabulary.
