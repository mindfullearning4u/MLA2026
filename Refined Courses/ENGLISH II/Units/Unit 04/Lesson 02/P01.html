Lesson 2: Source Reliability, Validity, Credibility, and Bias

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Conduct and Synthesize Reliable Research.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.06 - Analyze Central Ideas in Historical American Texts; MLA.ENG2.R.08 - Analyze Opposing Arguments and Validity; MLA.ENG2.V.03 - Determine Word Meaning, Connotation, and Tone.

What You Will Learn

  • How to decide whether a source is reliable, credible, and valid for a research question.
  • How to recognize bias and loaded language in research sources.
  • How to choose sources that help answer a focused inquiry question.

What You Will Do

  • Read source cards included in the lesson.
  • Evaluate authority, evidence, date, purpose, and bias.
  • Compare sources for usefulness and validity.
  • Practice selecting the best source for a research question.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains which source is most reliable for a research question.
  • Your practice answers identify bias, evidence, and credibility.
  • Your checkpoint justifies a source choice with specific evidence from the source card.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.RS.01 in student language: I can choose reliable sources by checking who created them, what evidence they use, whether they are current enough, and whether they are fair for my research question.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you cannot explain why one source is more reliable than another.
