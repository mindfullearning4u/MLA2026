Checkpoint Task

Using the school garden sources from P02, write two source notes, one accurate paraphrase, and one synthesis sentence that combines both sources to answer the research question.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your notes are relevant to the research question.
  • Your paraphrase is accurate and uses your own wording.
  • Your synthesis combines two sources in one connected explanation.
  • Your response uses clear standard English.
