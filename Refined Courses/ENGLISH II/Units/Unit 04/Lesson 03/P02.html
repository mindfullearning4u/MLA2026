Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
note-takingThe purposeful recording and organization of important information, ideas, and source details for later use.
paraphraseA complete restatement of a source idea in new wording and sentence structure while preserving the original meaning and crediting the source.
summaryA concise statement of a source’s main ideas and most important supporting points written in the reader’s own words.
direct quotationThe exact words of a source reproduced inside quotation marks and accompanied by appropriate source credit.
source synthesisThe process of combining related ideas or evidence from multiple sources to develop a new, coherent understanding.
main ideaThe most important point a source communicates about its subject.
supporting detailA fact, example, explanation, or quotation that develops or clarifies a main idea.
annotationA brief note added to a text or research record to identify an important idea, question, connection, or source use.
citationA formal acknowledgment identifying the source of borrowed words, ideas, facts, or evidence.
plagiarismPresenting another person’s words, ideas, or work as one’s own by failing to provide accurate source credit.
source relationshipThe way two or more sources agree, disagree, extend, qualify, or otherwise connect to one another.
synthesis statementA sentence that explains a meaningful connection among ideas or evidence from multiple sources rather than discussing each source separately.
