Teacher of Record Reminder

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook examples.
  2. Complete the checkpoint task below.
  3. Submit your response in the required LMS location.
  4. Use feedback to refine your question before the next research lesson.

Checkpoint Task

Choose one topic: school gardens, cafeteria food waste, public transportation, or library access. Write one focused research question. Then write three sentences explaining the scope and why the question can be answered with evidence.

Mastery Criteria

  • Your question is focused and researchable.
  • Your explanation identifies the scope.
  • Your question avoids biased wording.
  • Your writing uses clear academic vocabulary.
