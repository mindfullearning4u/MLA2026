P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 1: Research Questions, Inquiry Focus, and Scope Refinement

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Research Questions, Inquiry Focus, and Scope Refinement

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Conduct and Synthesize Reliable Research.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to turn a broad topic into a focused research question.
  • How to refine a question when it is too broad, too narrow, or too opinion-based.
  • How inquiry focus helps you decide what information you need to find.

Why This Matters

A focused research question saves time and leads to useful evidence. This skill supports school research, workplace problem-solving, and informed personal decisions.

What You Will Do

  • Study examples of broad and focused research questions.
  • Practice narrowing inquiry by audience, time period, location, cause, effect, or solution.
  • Write a research question that can be answered with reliable evidence.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz or unit assessment when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains why a question is focused enough for research.
  • Your practice answers distinguish topics from researchable questions.
  • Your checkpoint includes a refined question and a short explanation of the scope.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.RS.01 in student language: I can ask a focused research question, adjust the question based on what I find, and use reliable information to answer it.
