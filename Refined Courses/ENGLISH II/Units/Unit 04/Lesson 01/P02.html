Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
research questionA focused, open-ended question that guides investigation and requires evidence-based analysis rather than a simple factual answer.
inquiryA purposeful process of asking questions, gathering information, and developing understanding through investigation.
inquiry focusThe specific issue, relationship, or problem a researcher chooses to investigate.
scopeThe boundaries of a research project, including the topic, time period, population, location, or aspects that will be examined.
scope refinementThe process of narrowing or adjusting a topic so the investigation is manageable, specific, and meaningful.
open-ended questionA question that invites explanation, analysis, or evaluation and cannot be answered adequately with only yes, no, or one fact.
subquestionA smaller question that breaks the main research question into specific areas for investigation.
key conceptAn essential idea or term that defines the subject and helps organize a research investigation.
feasibilityThe degree to which a research question can be investigated successfully with the available time, sources, and resources.
relevanceThe degree to which information or a question directly connects to the inquiry focus and research purpose.
research purposeThe intended goal of an investigation, such as explaining a problem, comparing perspectives, evaluating causes, or proposing a solution.
