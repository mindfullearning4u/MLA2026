P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now you will refine a question after reviewing possible research findings.

Research Notes

Topic: School gardens

Note 1: Some schools use gardens for science observations about plant growth, soil, and ecosystems.

Note 2: Some schools use garden produce for cooking clubs or cafeteria taste tests.

Note 3: Garden programs can be difficult to maintain if no class, club, or staff member is responsible for watering and planning.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Read the notes and notice patterns. The notes mention science learning, food use, and maintenance.

Step 2: Choose one path. If you try to study every possible benefit and problem, the scope becomes too large.

Step 3: Refine the question around the path that best fits your purpose.

Step 4: A refined question should point toward the information you need next.

Notebook Application

  1. Choose one focus: science learning, food use, or maintenance.
  2. Write a refined research question using that focus.
  3. Write one sentence explaining why your question is narrow enough.
  4. List two types of information you would need to answer the question.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Why are school gardens the best thing ever?

Correct: How could a school garden support biology observations for tenth-grade students during one semester?

Why this matters: The incorrect question is biased and too broad. The correct question is focused, researchable, and limited by subject, grade level, and time frame.
