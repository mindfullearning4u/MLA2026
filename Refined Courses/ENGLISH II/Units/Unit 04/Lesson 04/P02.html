Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
expository writingWriting that explains, informs, or analyzes a subject through clear organization, accurate evidence, and logical development.
organizational structureThe deliberate arrangement of ideas and evidence so readers can follow how an explanation develops.
thesis statementA focused sentence that states the central explanation, insight, or controlling position developed throughout a text.
controlling ideaThe main concept that unifies the information and determines which details belong in an expository text.
research findingA conclusion, pattern, or important piece of information discovered through the examination of credible sources or collected evidence.
writer’s voiceThe distinctive presence created by a writer’s word choice, sentence patterns, tone, and approach to a subject.
formal styleA precise and objective manner of writing that avoids slang, unsupported opinion, and overly casual language.
coherenceThe logical clarity of a text in which ideas are ordered and connected so the overall explanation makes sense.
cohesionThe connection among sentences and paragraphs created through transitions, repeated key concepts, and consistent focus.
transitionA word, phrase, sentence, or structural cue that shows the relationship between ideas or sections.
source synthesisThe integration of related evidence and ideas from multiple sources into one unified explanation.
evidence integrationThe process of introducing, presenting, citing, and explaining source information so it supports the writer’s purpose.
