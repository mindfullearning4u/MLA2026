P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 4: Expository Organization and Voice From Research Findings

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Voice.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Conduct and Synthesize Reliable Research; MLA.ENG2.R.05 - Analyze Text Structure Impact; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to organize research findings in an expository paragraph.
  • How to choose a structure that fits the information you need to explain.
  • How to use a clear, task-appropriate academic voice.

What You Will Do

  • Review research findings from source notes.
  • Choose an organization pattern such as cause/effect, problem/solution, or compare/contrast.
  • Draft an expository paragraph that explains findings without sounding informal or biased.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook identifies an organization pattern for source findings.
  • Your writing explains research findings with clear evidence.
  • Your checkpoint uses academic voice and logical organization.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use research findings to write an organized explanation with a voice that fits an academic task.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your paragraph lists facts without explaining how they connect.
