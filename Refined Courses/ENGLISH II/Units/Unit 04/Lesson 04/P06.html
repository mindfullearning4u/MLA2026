Research Findings

Question: How could a school garden support science learning?

Finding 1: Gardens provide real observations of plant growth and soil conditions.

Finding 2: Journals help students record and compare observations over time.

Finding 3: Students can connect observations to science vocabulary when teachers guide the routine.

Part A

Choose the best organization pattern for these findings and explain why it fits.

Part B

Write one topic sentence for an expository paragraph using academic voice.

Part C

Write one organized expository paragraph that explains how the findings answer the research question.
