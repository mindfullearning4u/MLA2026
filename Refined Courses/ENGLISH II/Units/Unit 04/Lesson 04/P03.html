Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will turn organized research findings into an expository paragraph.

Draft Paragraph

Pre-order lunch forms may reduce cafeteria waste because they help staff prepare meals based on student selections. In the district pilot, fewer unwanted trays were prepared after students chose meals in advance. The system also needed support because some students forgot the form during the first week. In addition, reminders helped more students complete the form, which made the routine more effective.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: The first sentence states the explanation clearly.

Step 2: The second sentence gives source evidence about fewer unwanted trays.

Step 3: The third sentence adds a limitation, which keeps the explanation accurate.

Step 4: The final sentence explains how reminders improved the routine.

Step 5: The voice is academic because it is clear, precise, and not exaggerated.

Notebook Application

  1. Identify the topic sentence in the draft paragraph.
  2. Copy one transition from the paragraph.
  3. Explain why the paragraph uses academic voice.
  4. Write one sentence that could be added to clarify the limitation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: Lunch forms are awesome and fixed everything, so schools should just do them.

Correct: Pre-order lunch forms may reduce waste when they help staff prepare meals from student selections and are supported by reminders.

Why this matters: Expository voice explains research findings accurately instead of exaggerating or sounding informal.
