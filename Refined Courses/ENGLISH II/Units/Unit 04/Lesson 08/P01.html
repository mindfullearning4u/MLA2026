Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 4 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Unit 4 Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Conduct and Synthesize Reliable Research; MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Voice; MLA.ENG2.C.03 - Publish Collaborative Multimedia Writing.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How the Unit 4 skills connect from research question to publication.
  • How to synthesize reliable source evidence into expository communication.
  • How to prepare for the Unit 4 Assessment without learning a new primary standard.

What You Will Do

  • Review the full research-to-publication workflow from Lessons 1-7.
  • Practice choosing accurate, audience-aware, source-based revisions.
  • Complete the Unit 4 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook shows how research, synthesis, writing, and publication connect.
  • Your guided practice shows readiness for the unit assessment.
  • Your Unit 4 Assessment shows mastery of the standards taught in Lessons 1-7.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use reliable research to write, revise, and publish a clear source-based communication product for a real audience.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help before the Unit Assessment if you are unsure how to connect research, source synthesis, expository writing, or multimedia publication.
