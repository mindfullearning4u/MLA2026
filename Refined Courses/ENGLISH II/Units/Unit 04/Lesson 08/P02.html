Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
research questionA focused, open-ended question that guides investigation and requires evidence-based analysis.
scopeThe boundaries of a research project, including the topic, time period, population, location, or aspects examined.
credibilityThe quality of being trustworthy because a source demonstrates expertise, accuracy, and responsible use of evidence.
biasA preference or viewpoint that may influence how information is selected, emphasized, omitted, or interpreted.
paraphraseA complete restatement of a source idea in new wording and sentence structure while preserving the original meaning and crediting the source.
source synthesisThe integration of related evidence and ideas from multiple sources into one unified understanding or explanation.
expository writingWriting that explains, informs, or analyzes a subject through clear organization, accurate evidence, and logical development.
source integrationThe process of incorporating quotations, paraphrases, summaries, and source ideas smoothly into a writer’s own explanation.
purposeful transitionA word, phrase, or sentence chosen to clarify a specific relationship between ideas, evidence, or sections.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s needs, knowledge, expectations, and interests when making communication choices.
multimedia publicationA published work that combines written text with one or more additional forms such as images, audio, video, charts, or interactive elements.
publication-readyFully revised, edited, proofread, cited, formatted, and prepared for delivery to an intended audience.
