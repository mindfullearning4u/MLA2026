📘 ENG2 | Unit 04 | Lesson 08

Independent Work

Use this work to prepare for the Unit 4 Assessment.

Instructions

Complete Parts A, B, and C using the Unit 4 source set. Your work should show the full research-to-publication process.

Part A: Source Selection

Choose the two source details that best answer the research question. Explain why each detail is relevant.

Part B: Synthesis Paragraph

Write one paragraph that explains how the source details work together. Include evidence, explanation, and a clear academic voice.

Part C: Publication Plan

Name the audience, the purpose, one visual, and one revision you would make before publishing.
