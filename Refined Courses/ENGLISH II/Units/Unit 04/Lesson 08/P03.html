Notebook Task Part 2: Synthesis Practice

Use the Unit 4 source set to practice synthesis before the Unit Assessment.

Synthesis Task

Explain how Source A, Source B, and Source C work together to answer the research question: How can student pre-order forms help reduce unwanted lunch trays?

Step-by-Step Thinking

  1. Source A gives the main outcome: fewer unwanted trays.
  2. Source B explains one support that helped the process work: reminders increased form completion.
  3. Source C explains how to communicate the process to students: simple wording and a labeled chart improved understanding.
  4. Together, the sources show that the form can reduce waste when students understand it and complete it consistently.

Model Synthesis

The sources work together by showing the outcome, the support, and the communication need. Source A shows that the pre-order pilot reduced unwanted trays. Source B shows that reminders helped more students complete the form, which made the process more consistent. Source C shows that students understood the purpose better when the message used simple wording and a labeled chart. Together, the sources suggest that pre-order forms can reduce unwanted trays when students understand why the form matters and remember to complete it.

Common Mistake

Incorrect Example:

The sources are all about lunch, and they say forms are good.

Correct Example:

The sources show that pre-order forms reduced unwanted trays, reminders helped students complete the forms, and simple wording with a chart helped students understand the purpose.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example is too general. The correct example identifies what each source contributes to the answer.
