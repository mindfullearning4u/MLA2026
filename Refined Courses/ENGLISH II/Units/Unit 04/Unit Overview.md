# ENGLISH II - Unit 04 Overview: Research, Synthesis, and Expository Writing

---

## Unit Overview

This unit develops research and synthesis skills. Students conduct inquiry, refine research questions, evaluate sources, synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources, and produce structured expository writing.

Emphasis is placed on research design, credibility, synthesis, citation, academic integrity, logical organization, purposeful transitions, task-appropriate tone and voice, collaborative publication, and multimedia integration.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with an emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency and conceptual understanding through research, expository writing, source evaluation, synthesis, collaboration, and publication.

## Unit Assessment Alignment

The Unit Assessment evaluates student mastery of all benchmarks addressed in this unit. Students must demonstrate consistent proficiency across:

- conceptual understanding;
- research question refinement;
- reliable and valid source use;
- synthesis of information from multiple sources;
- expository organization and analysis;
- citation, academic integrity, tone, and voice;
- publication-ready writing with multimedia elements.

Assessment items require students to conduct research, refine a question based on findings, synthesize information from multiple reliable and valid sources, write expository text with logical organization and purposeful transitions, use a tone and voice appropriate to the task, and create publication-ready writing tailored to a specific audience with multimedia elements.

---

## Mastery Expectation

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.

---

## Problem-Solving Connection

This unit connects inquiry, reading, writing, and technology. Students learn to refine questions, evaluate sources, synthesize information, and communicate findings clearly and responsibly for a defined audience.
