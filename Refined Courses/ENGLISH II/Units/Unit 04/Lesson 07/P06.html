Draft: “Tutoring Fixes Everything!” The new program helped 126 students and everybody became confident. Teachers said attendance was a thing. The school definitely needs to expand it immediately. [tiny chart with no label] Learn more: click here.

Checkpoint Questions

  1. Identify two claims that exceed the evidence.
  2. Revise the headline for accuracy and audience.
  3. Integrate the 72% statistic with context, source credit, and analysis.
  4. Revise the sentence about attendance to preserve the teachers’ limitation.
  5. Write a measured recommendation supported by the findings.
  6. Reorder the content into a logical publication sequence and explain why.
  7. Specify how to repair the chart, including title, readable labeling, and source credit.
  8. Replace “click here” and add one accessibility improvement.
  9. List four final copyediting or technical checks.
  10. Rewrite the draft as a polished 7–9 sentence newsletter item that is accurate, cohesive, audience-centered, accessible, and publication-ready.
Before submitting: Verify evidence, organization, tone, correctness, accessibility, and technical function.
