📘 ENG2 | Unit 04 | Lesson 07

Independent Work

Use your notebook and the lesson source findings to complete all three parts.

Instructions

Your goal is to prepare a publication-ready message for a student audience. Keep the evidence accurate, explain why it matters, and make sure the final communication is clear enough to share.

Part A: Evidence and Explanation

Write two sentences. Sentence 1 must state one source finding accurately. Sentence 2 must explain why the finding matters for students.

Part B: Publication Choice

Choose one visual that would support the message. Explain what the visual would show and why it helps the audience understand the evidence.

Part C: Final Checklist

Create a checklist with five items: evidence accuracy, explanation, audience wording, visual support, and sentence conventions.
