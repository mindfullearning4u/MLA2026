P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 7: Publication-Ready Source-Based Communication

Lesson Title

Lesson 7: Publication-Ready Source-Based Communication

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.W.03 - Write Expository Texts with Voice; MLA.ENG2.C.03 - Publish Collaborative Multimedia Writing.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.RS.01 - Conduct and Synthesize Reliable Research; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Use Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to check whether source-based writing is ready for publication.
  • How to connect evidence, explanation, academic voice, and multimedia design.
  • How to revise final communication so the audience can understand and use the information.

Why This Matters

Publication-ready communication must be accurate, clear, polished, and useful. Final review protects your credibility and helps audiences act on the information.

What You Will Do

  • Review a source-based communication plan about pre-order lunch forms.
  • Identify revisions that improve accuracy, clarity, voice, and publication readiness.
  • Prepare a final checklist for a source-based multimedia product.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz or unit assessment when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains what makes writing publication-ready.
  • Your practice responses choose evidence-based, audience-aware revisions.
  • Your checkpoint checklist shows that your final communication is accurate, clear, and ready to publish.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can turn research and expository writing into a polished communication product that is accurate, clear, and useful for my audience.
