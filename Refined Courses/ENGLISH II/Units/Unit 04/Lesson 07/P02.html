Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
publication-readyFully revised, edited, proofread, formatted, and prepared for delivery to an intended audience.
source-based communicationWriting, speaking, or multimedia presentation that develops ideas using credible evidence and accurate source acknowledgment.
revisionThe process of improving ideas, organization, evidence, clarity, tone, and effectiveness by making substantial changes to a draft.
editingThe process of correcting sentence structure, word choice, grammar, usage, punctuation, and consistency.
proofreadingThe final careful review used to locate and correct surface errors, formatting problems, and minor omissions before publication.
coherenceThe logical clarity of a text in which ideas, evidence, and sections work together to support the overall purpose.
citationA formal acknowledgment that identifies the source of borrowed words, ideas, facts, images, data, or other material.
attributionClear identification of the creator or source connected to borrowed content within a publication.
multimodal elementA component that communicates through a mode other than or in addition to written language, such as an image, chart, audio clip, video, or layout feature.
audience awarenessThe understanding of an audience’s needs, knowledge, expectations, and interests when making communication and design choices.
purposeThe intended result of a communication, such as informing, explaining, persuading, documenting, or engaging an audience.
conventionsThe accepted rules of grammar, usage, capitalization, punctuation, spelling, citation, and formatting that make communication clear and credible.
