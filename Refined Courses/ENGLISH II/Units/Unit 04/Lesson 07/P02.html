P02 Notebook Task - Part 1: Publication-Ready Communication

Notebook Title: Unit 4 Lesson 7 - Publication-Ready Source-Based Communication

Vocabulary

  • Publication-ready: accurate, polished, organized, and clear enough to share with the intended audience.
  • Source-based communication: writing or multimedia that uses evidence from reliable sources to explain a message.
  • Precision: using exact words so the audience understands the idea without confusion.
  • Final check: the last review for evidence, explanation, audience fit, visual support, and conventions.

Source Context for This Lesson

A student team is preparing a short digital publication for students. The publication explains that completing pre-order lunch forms can help reduce unwanted trays.

Source Finding 1: A district pilot report found fewer unwanted trays after staff prepared meals from student pre-order selections.

Source Finding 2: A cafeteria manager explained that reminders helped more students complete the pre-order form after the first week.

Publication Goal: Create a clear student-facing message that explains the evidence, uses academic but readable language, and includes a visual that supports the finding.

Step-by-Step Teaching

  1. Check the evidence. Ask: Does the writing name or clearly refer to the source finding?
  2. Check the explanation. Ask: Does the writing explain why the evidence matters?
  3. Check the audience. Ask: Would students understand the language without an extra live explanation?
  4. Check the visual. Ask: Does the visual help explain the research finding?
  5. Check conventions. Ask: Are sentences complete, clear, and polished?

Notebook Directions

In your notebook, create a five-part checklist with these headings: Evidence, Explanation, Audience, Visual, and Conventions. Under each heading, write one sentence explaining what you must check before publication.

Model Example: Evidence - I must make sure the publication states that the pilot report found fewer unwanted trays after staff used student selections.
