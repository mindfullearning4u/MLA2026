Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
source integrationThe process of incorporating quotations, paraphrases, summaries, and source ideas smoothly into a writer’s own explanation.
signal phraseWords that introduce source information and identify the author, organization, or context of the evidence.
contextBackground information that helps readers understand the meaning, source, and importance of borrowed evidence.
citationA formal acknowledgment that identifies the source of borrowed words, ideas, facts, or evidence.
commentaryThe writer’s explanation of how evidence connects to the central idea, purpose, or reasoning of the text.
purposeful transitionA word, phrase, or sentence chosen to clarify a specific relationship between ideas, evidence, or sections.
toneThe writer’s attitude toward the subject or audience as communicated through word choice, detail, and sentence style.
voiceThe distinctive presence created by a writer’s language, perspective, sentence patterns, and approach to a topic.
formal styleA precise and objective manner of writing appropriate for academic audiences and supported explanations.
cohesionThe clear connection among sentences and paragraphs created through transitions, repeated concepts, and consistent focus.
quotationThe exact words of a source reproduced inside quotation marks and credited with an appropriate citation.
paraphraseA source idea restated fully in new wording and sentence structure while preserving its meaning and crediting the source.
