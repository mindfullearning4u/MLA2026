Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will revise source integration for transitions, tone, and voice.

Draft Sentence Set

Draft: The lunch form was awesome. A report said trays went down. Also, students forgot stuff at first. The manager said reminders helped.

Goal: Revise the draft so it sounds academic and clearly connects the source findings.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Replace vague words. "Awesome" does not explain the research finding.

Step 2: Add precise source lead-ins: "A district report found..." and "A cafeteria manager explained..."

Step 3: Use transitions that match meaning. Use "However" for a challenge and "In addition" for another related finding.

Step 4: Keep voice objective. Avoid exaggeration and personal reactions.

Notebook Application

  1. Identify two words or phrases in the draft that are too casual.
  2. Write one transition that shows a challenge or contrast.
  3. Rewrite the draft in three academic sentences.
  4. Underline the lead-in that introduces a source.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The report says lunch forms are awesome and fixed everything.

Correct: A district report found that the pre-order pilot reduced unwanted trays, while a cafeteria manager noted that reminders helped students complete the form.

Why this matters: The correct version integrates sources, uses a purposeful transition, and avoids exaggeration.
