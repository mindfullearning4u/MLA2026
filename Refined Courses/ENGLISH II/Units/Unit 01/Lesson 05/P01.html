P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 5: Conventions and clarity in short responses

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How standard English conventions make short responses clear.
  • How to check capitalization, punctuation, and complete sentences.
  • How to revise sentence errors without changing the meaning of a response.

Why This Matters

A strong idea can lose power if the sentence is incomplete or hard to follow. Conventions help your reader understand your paraphrase, evidence, and explanation.

What You Will Do

  • Review short responses based on included passages.
  • Identify sentence fragments, run-ons, capitalization errors, and punctuation errors.
  • Revise responses so the meaning stays accurate and the writing is clear.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your sentences are complete.
  • Your capitalization and punctuation are correct.
  • Your revisions improve clarity without changing the text meaning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.L.01 in student language: I can use standard English grammar, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling so my response is clear.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you know what you want to say but cannot make the sentence complete and clear.
