The Evening Technology Workshop

The public library offered an evening workshop for adults who wanted help using online job applications. Librarians prepared laptops, sample résumés, and a checklist for creating secure passwords. They expected twelve participants, but twenty-seven people arrived.

At first, everyone waited for the same librarian. Several participants had simple questions others needed help uploading documents. The room grew noisy, and the projected directions were difficult to see from the back tables.

The librarians changed the plan. One table focused on passwords and account setup. A second table helped with résumés and uploads, while a third offered individual support. A volunteer also printed the directions in large type. By the end of the evening, most participants had completed at least one application. The library scheduled another workshop and added a registration form so it could prepare enough equipment.
