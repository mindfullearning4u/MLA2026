P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will fix sentence boundaries in a short response.

Read the Passage

The library posted a calendar of book club meetings. Students checked the calendar to know which novel would be discussed each week.

Direct Teaching

A run-on sentence happens when two complete thoughts are pushed together without correct punctuation. A short response can be accurate but still confusing if sentence boundaries are wrong.

Run-on: The calendar helped students it showed which novel would be discussed.

Corrected: The calendar helped students because it showed which novel would be discussed.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy this run-on: The calendar helped students it showed the weekly novel.
  2. Explain why it is a run-on.
  3. Revise it into one clear sentence or two correct sentences.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The calendar helped students it showed the weekly novel.

This is incorrect because two complete ideas are joined without a connecting word or punctuation.

Correct: The calendar helped students because it showed the weekly novel.

This is correct because the sentence connects the idea and the reason clearly.
