Worked Example: Revising a Short Response

Read the Passage

On Thursday, students tested two locations for a quiet reading area. The corner near the windows had natural light, but hallway noise interrupted readers. The smaller room beside the library was less bright, yet it stayed quiet throughout lunch. After comparing both spaces, the group recommended the smaller room and suggested adding two lamps.

Draft Response

Draft: the group chose the smaller room because it was quiet they suggested lamps because it was less bright

Step-by-Step Revision

  1. Identify complete thoughts. Thought 1: the group chose the smaller room because it was quiet. Thought 2: they suggested lamps because it was less bright.
  2. Find the boundary problem. Two complete sentences are pushed together after quiet.
  3. Choose punctuation. A period clearly separates the recommendation from the solution.
  4. Correct capitalization. Capitalize The and They.
  5. Check meaning. The revision keeps both accurate passage details.

Final Revision

The group chose the smaller room because it was quiet. They suggested adding lamps because the room was less bright.

Second Worked Example: Fixing a Fragment

Fragment: Because the hallway noise interrupted readers.

  1. The word because introduces a reason but leaves the result unfinished.
  2. Add the main idea: The group rejected the window corner because hallway noise interrupted readers.
  3. Verify the revision matches the passage.

Third Worked Example: Fixing a Comma Splice

Comma splice: The smaller room was dim, it stayed quiet.

Revision: The smaller room was dim, but it stayed quiet.

The comma now works with but to join two complete, contrasting thoughts.
