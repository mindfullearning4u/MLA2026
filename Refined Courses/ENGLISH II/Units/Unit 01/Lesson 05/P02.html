Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
conventionAn accepted rule for spelling, capitalization, punctuation, grammar, or sentence construction.
clarityThe quality of expressing an idea so readers can understand it easily and accurately.
sentence boundaryThe point where one complete sentence ends and another begins.
capitalizationThe correct use of uppercase letters at the beginnings of sentences and for proper nouns and titles.
punctuationMarks such as periods, commas, apostrophes, and quotation marks that organize and clarify writing.
grammarThe system of rules governing how words and sentences are formed and used.
revisionThe process of improving ideas, organization, word choice, and clarity in a draft.
