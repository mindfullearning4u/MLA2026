Instructions

Complete Part A, Part B, and Part C in your notebook before moving to the checkpoint. Use the passage or sentence context on this page and write complete responses.

Passage

The school energy team analyzed electricity use in several classrooms. After reviewing the data, students recommended turning off unused projectors and lights. They concluded that small daily actions could reduce energy waste.

Part A: Identify Vocabulary

Copy these academic words: analyzed, recommended, concluded, and reduce. Write what each word means in the passage.

Part B: Use Vocabulary

Write two complete sentences about the passage. Each sentence must use at least one academic vocabulary word from Part A.

Part C: Short Response

Write a three-sentence response explaining what the energy team did and why it mattered. Use at least two academic vocabulary words accurately.
