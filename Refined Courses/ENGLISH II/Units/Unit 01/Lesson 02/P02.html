Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. You will submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.
Term Definition
academic vocabulary Words commonly used in school reading, writing, speaking, and discussion to explain ideas precisely.
context The words, sentences, and ideas surrounding a term that help clarify its meaning.
context clue A word, phrase, example, contrast, or explanation that helps a reader determine an unfamiliar word’s meaning.
precise language Exact, specific wording that communicates an idea clearly and avoids vague meaning.
academic voice A clear, formal, evidence-based way of speaking or writing that is appropriate for school tasks.
response A written or spoken answer that explains an idea about a text, question, or topic.
