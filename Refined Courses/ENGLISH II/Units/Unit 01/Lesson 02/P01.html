P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 2: Academic Vocabulary and Academic Voice

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Academic Vocabulary and Academic Voice

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.01 — Integrate Academic Vocabulary

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 — Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.L.01 — Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • Use context to determine the meaning of academic vocabulary.
  • Choose precise academic words for reading responses.
  • Use vocabulary to create a clear academic voice.

Why This Matters

Academic vocabulary gives you precise language for explaining ideas in school, careers, and public communication. It helps readers understand exactly what you mean.

What You Will Do

  • Read included passages and determine word meanings from context.
  • Replace vague language with precise academic vocabulary.
  • Write clear responses that use lesson vocabulary accurately.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Explain academic vocabulary using context.
  • Select vocabulary that fits the text and purpose.
  • Use academic words correctly in complete sentences and short responses.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use grade-level academic vocabulary accurately when I explain ideas in speaking and writing.
