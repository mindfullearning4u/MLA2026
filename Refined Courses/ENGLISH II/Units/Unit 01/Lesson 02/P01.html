P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Academic Vocabulary and Academic Voice

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • How academic vocabulary helps you explain ideas clearly.
  • How to use context to understand important words in a passage.
  • How to choose precise words when writing a reading response.

Why This Matters

Academic vocabulary gives you the words to explain what a text says, how it works, and why an idea matters. Words like significant, evidence, contrast, and conclude help your response sound clear, exact, and ready for high school work.

What You Will Do

  1. Read short passages with all text included.
  2. Use context to figure out academic words.
  3. Practice replacing vague words with precise academic vocabulary.
  4. Write a checkpoint response that uses academic vocabulary accurately.

How You Will Show Mastery

You can explain an academic word using context, choose vocabulary that fits a reading response, and write a clear sentence or short paragraph that uses the word correctly.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.V.01 means you are learning to use strong school vocabulary when you speak and write.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can understand the passage but cannot decide which academic word best explains your idea.
