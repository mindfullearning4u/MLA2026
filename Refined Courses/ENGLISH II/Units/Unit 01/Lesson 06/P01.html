Lesson 6: Academic Vocabulary in Speaking and Writing

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally; MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

What You Will Learn

  • How to use academic vocabulary in written responses.
  • How to use the same vocabulary in a short oral explanation plan.
  • How to choose precise words that match the text meaning.

Why This Matters

Academic vocabulary should not stay on a word list. You need to use it when you write about a text and when you explain your ideas aloud.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages with all text included.
  • Choose academic words that fit the passage meaning.
  • Write response sentences using precise vocabulary.
  • Plan a brief oral explanation using academic vocabulary accurately.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • You choose academic vocabulary that fits the text.
  • You use vocabulary accurately in written response sentences.
  • You prepare a clear oral explanation using the same vocabulary.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.V.01 in student language: I can use grade-level academic vocabulary correctly when I speak and write.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you know the idea but cannot choose the academic word that fits it.
