P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Academic Vocabulary in Speaking and Writing

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Academic Vocabulary in Speaking and Writing

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.V.01 — Integrate Academic Vocabulary

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.C.01 — Present Ideas Orally; MLA.ENG2.R.10 — Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts

What You Will Learn

  • Use academic vocabulary accurately in written responses.
  • Use the same vocabulary in a short oral explanation.
  • Choose precise words that match a text’s meaning.

Why This Matters

Precise vocabulary helps you communicate confidently in discussions, presentations, interviews, and written work.

What You Will Do

  • Read included passages and select vocabulary that fits their meaning.
  • Write response sentences with precise academic language.
  • Plan a brief oral explanation using the same vocabulary.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Choose vocabulary that fits the text.
  • Use academic words accurately in writing.
  • Prepare a clear oral explanation using precise vocabulary.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can use grade-level academic vocabulary correctly when I speak and write.
