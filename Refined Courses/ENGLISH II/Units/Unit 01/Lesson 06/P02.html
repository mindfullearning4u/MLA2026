P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Vocabulary for Writing and Speaking

Vocabulary

Precise means exact and clear.

Academic vocabulary means words used in school reading, writing, and discussion.

Oral explanation means a spoken explanation of your thinking.

Integrate means to use something naturally as part of your response.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the idea from the passage.

Step 2: Choose an academic word that matches the idea.

Step 3: Use the word in a complete written sentence.

Step 4: Plan how you would say the same idea aloud clearly.

Read the Passage

The school newspaper corrected an article after printing the wrong club meeting time. The editor explained that corrections help readers trust the information.

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the words corrected and trust.
  2. Write what each word means in the passage.
  3. Write one sentence using the academic word accuracy.
  4. Write one sentence you could say aloud to explain why corrections matter.
