P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Building an Evidence-Based Response

Vocabulary

Response is your written answer to a question about a text.

Evidence is a detail from the text that supports your response.

Explanation tells how the evidence supports your idea.

Revise means to improve writing so the meaning is clearer.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Answer the question by paraphrasing the important idea.

Step 2: Add a short piece of evidence from the passage.

Step 3: Explain how the evidence supports your answer.

Step 4: Reread and revise for clarity, complete sentences, and correct punctuation.

Read the Passage

The school library started a lunchtime pickup shelf for books students had reserved online. Students received a message when their books were ready, then picked them up without waiting in the regular checkout line.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the question: How did the pickup shelf help students?
  2. Write one sentence that paraphrases the answer.
  3. Copy one short piece of evidence from the passage.
  4. Write one explanation sentence.

Model Start

Answer: The pickup shelf helped students get reserved books more quickly.

Evidence: Students picked up books "without waiting in the regular checkout line."

Explanation: This evidence shows that the shelf saved time because students did not need to stand in the regular line.
