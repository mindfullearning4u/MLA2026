Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
evidence-based responseA written answer that uses relevant information from a text to support its ideas.
claimA clear statement that answers a question or expresses the main point a writer will support.
textual evidenceA quotation, fact, detail, or example from a text used to support a claim.
reasoningThe explanation that shows how and why the evidence supports the claim.
analysisCareful examination of a text or detail to explain its meaning, importance, or effect.
citationInformation that identifies where quoted or paraphrased evidence came from.
