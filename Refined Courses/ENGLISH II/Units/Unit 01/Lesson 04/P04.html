P04 Worked Example

This example builds a short written response step by step.

Example 1: Understand the Prompt

Passage: The school added water bottle refill stations near the gym and cafeteria. Students filled reusable bottles between classes instead of buying single-use plastic bottles.

Prompt: How did the refill stations help students reduce waste?

Think aloud: The prompt asks how the stations helped. I need to paraphrase the benefit and include evidence about reusable bottles.

Example 2: Build the Response

Answer sentence: The refill stations helped students reduce waste by making reusable bottles easier to use.

Evidence sentence: The passage says students filled reusable bottles instead of buying single-use plastic bottles.

Explanation sentence: This evidence shows that the stations gave students a way to avoid using extra plastic bottles.

Example 3: Revise for Clarity

Draft: The stations helped because bottles were better and students used them.

Revision: The refill stations helped students reduce waste because students could refill reusable bottles instead of buying single-use plastic bottles.

Why the revision is stronger: It explains the idea clearly, uses evidence from the passage, and avoids vague words.

Common Mistake to Avoid

Incorrect: The water things were useful because plastic is bad.

This is too vague and does not use the passage accurately. It also uses informal wording.

Correct: The refill stations helped reduce waste because students could refill reusable bottles instead of buying single-use plastic bottles.

This is correct because it paraphrases the passage, includes relevant evidence, and uses clear academic language.
