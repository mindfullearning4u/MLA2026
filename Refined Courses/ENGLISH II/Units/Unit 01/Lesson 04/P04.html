Worked Example: From Passage to Complete Response

Passage

Riverton Middle School replaced disposable cafeteria trays with washable trays. Students compared the dishwasher’s consumption with resources used to manufacture and transport disposable trays. The review found that reusable trays reduced annual waste while using only a small portion of cafeteria water. After a semester, trash pickups decreased from five per week to three.

Question and Claim

Question: Was the change effective?

Claim: Reusable trays reduced waste without creating a major increase in water use.

Evidence and Reasoning

Evidence: Pickups fell from five to three, while washing used a small share of water.

Reasoning: The lower pickup rate measures reduced waste, and the water comparison answers the main objection.

Completed Response

The reusable tray system effectively reduced cafeteria waste without creating a major increase in water use. After one semester, weekly trash pickups decreased from five to three, while washing required only a small share of cafeteria water. The lower pickup rate demonstrates less discarded material. The water data also answers the concern that reusables would create another serious resource problem. Together, the evidence supports continuing the program.

Revision Check

Confirm a direct claim, specific evidence, explanation rather than repetition, an audience-appropriate tone, and correct conventions.
