Checkpoint: “A Room for Study”

Directions

Read the passage and answer all 12 questions. Submit them only in the separate Checkpoint Submission.

Passage

After students reported difficulty finding quiet study space, Harbor Library tested opening its meeting room after school. Staff counted unused hours and surveyed preferred times. Most students requested access from three to six.

For eight weeks, the room opened four afternoons weekly. Students signed in and completed feedback cards. Average daily use rose from twelve students to thirty-one, and noise complaints in the main reading area declined.

On debate-club days the room was unavailable. Staff posted the schedule online and reserved a smaller room, and feedback improved.

Based on attendance, complaint records, and surveys, the director recommended continuing the program while reviewing staffing costs after another semester.
