Lesson 4: Evidence-Based Written Response Foundations

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How to turn a paraphrase into a short written response.
  • How to include evidence without copying too much of the passage.
  • How to explain evidence in your own words.
  • How to revise a response so it is clear and complete.

Why This Matters

In English II, many assignments ask you to explain what a text says and prove your explanation. A strong short response is built from three parts: an accurate paraphrase, relevant evidence, and a clear explanation.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included in the lesson.
  • Build written responses one sentence at a time.
  • Revise unclear responses into stronger ones.
  • Complete a checkpoint response using the response frame.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your response includes an accurate paraphrase.
  • Your evidence directly supports the paraphrase.
  • Your explanation uses clear academic language.
  • Your final response uses complete sentences and standard English.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.10 in student language: I can restate what the text says and use that understanding in a clear written response.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your response includes evidence but you are not sure how to explain it.
