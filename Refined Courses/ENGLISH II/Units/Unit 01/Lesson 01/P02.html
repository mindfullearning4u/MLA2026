Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook. You will submit these vocabulary notes with your Independent Work as Notebook Evidence.
Term Definition
close reading Reading carefully enough to notice meaning, details, and word choices.
main idea The most important point a passage communicates.
key detail An important fact, example, or idea that supports the main idea.
paraphrase A restatement of the same meaning using your own words and sentence structure.
