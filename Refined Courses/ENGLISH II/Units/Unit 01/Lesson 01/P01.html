P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 1: Grade 10 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

Lesson Title

Lesson 1: Grade 10 Close Reading and Accurate Paraphrase

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.10 — Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 — Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.L.01 — Apply Standard English Conventions

What You Will Learn

  • Read a grade-level passage closely and identify its main idea and key details.
  • Distinguish accurate paraphrasing from copying.
  • Restate meaning in your own words without changing the author’s ideas.

Why This Matters

Paraphrasing proves that you understand what you read. It prepares you to analyze texts, use evidence, discuss meaning, and write accurately in school, work, and everyday communication.

What You Will Do

  • Read and annotate short passages included in the lesson.
  • Identify the main idea, key details, and words that require careful attention.
  • Compare weak and strong paraphrases before writing your own.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Identify the main idea and important details.
  • Write an accurate paraphrase using original wording and sentence structure.
  • Explain how the paraphrase preserves the author’s meaning.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read a grade-level text and restate its meaning accurately in my own words.
