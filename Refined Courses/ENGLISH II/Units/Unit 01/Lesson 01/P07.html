Checkpoint Passage

Passage: The science class built a weather station behind the school. Students recorded temperature, wind direction, and rainfall each morning. After several weeks, they used the information to look for patterns in the local weather.

Use this passage for the checkpoint task.

Checkpoint Task

  1. Write the main idea of the passage.
  2. List two key details that support the main idea.
  3. Write a two-sentence paraphrase of the passage.
  4. Write one sentence explaining how you checked that your paraphrase stayed accurate.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear image or typed copy of your notebook work for P02, P03, P06, and this checkpoint. Make sure your name, unit, lesson, and page labels are visible.

Checkpoint Submission Workflow

  1. Complete the checkpoint in your notebook.
  2. Check that your paraphrase uses your own wording and keeps the meaning.
  3. Submit your notebook evidence in the lesson submission area.
  4. Take the Lesson Quiz after your checkpoint is complete.

Mastery Criteria

You show mastery when your paraphrase keeps the main idea, includes necessary details, uses your own wording, and does not add information that is not in the passage.

Need help? Contact your Teacher of Record if you cannot tell whether your paraphrase changed the meaning.
