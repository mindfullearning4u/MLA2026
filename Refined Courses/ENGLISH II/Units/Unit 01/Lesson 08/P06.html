The Breakfast Access Trial

Jefferson High served breakfast only in the cafeteria before first period. Staff noticed that students arriving on later buses often missed the meal. The school tested a grab-and-go breakfast cart near the main entrance for eight weeks.

Average daily breakfast participation rose from 118 students to 203 students. A survey of 260 participants found that 78 percent preferred the entrance location because it reduced waiting time. Custodians reported more packaging waste in the hallway, so the school added recycling bins and student cleanup volunteers.

Administrators approved the cart for the rest of the year. They concluded that the participation increase demonstrated improved access, while the waste response addressed an unintended consequence. The school will compare participation, cost, and waste data before deciding whether to add a second cart.
