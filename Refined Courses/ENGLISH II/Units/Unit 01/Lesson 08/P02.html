P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 1 Synthesis Review

Review Vocabulary

Paraphrase: Restate the text accurately in your own words.

Academic vocabulary: Precise school language used in writing and speaking.

Evidence: A text detail that supports an idea.

Conventions: Grammar, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling rules that make writing clear.

Review Passage

Passage: The school created a response checklist for short answers. The checklist reminded students to paraphrase the passage, include relevant evidence, use precise vocabulary, and check punctuation before submitting work.

Step-by-Step Review

  1. Find the main idea of the passage.
  2. Choose one relevant evidence detail.
  3. Name the academic vocabulary that helps explain the response.
  4. Check punctuation before submitting work.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  2. Copy one relevant evidence detail.
  3. List two academic vocabulary words from the passage.
  4. Write one convention you should check before submitting a response.

Step-by-Step Learning Path

  1. Step 1: Review the vocabulary and identify the skill being practiced.
  2. Step 2: Read the model or passage carefully before answering.
  3. Step 3: Find the exact evidence, word, sentence, or structural choice that supports your answer.
  4. Step 4: Explain how that evidence connects to meaning, effect, or purpose.
  5. Step 5: Check the mastery criteria before submitting.

Remedial support: work one step at a time. Standard path: complete the whole task with evidence and explanation. Accelerated path: compare alternatives or add a second evidence-based explanation.
