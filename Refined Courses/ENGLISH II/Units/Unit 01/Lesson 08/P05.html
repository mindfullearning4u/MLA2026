Before You Begin

Read each passage or sentence first. Then choose the answer that best uses Unit 1 skills: paraphrase, academic vocabulary, evidence, conventions, and integrated response.

Question 1

MLA.ENG2.R.10; MLA.ENG2.V.01

Passage: The school added a response checklist to the online classroom. The checklist reminded students to paraphrase the passage, include relevant evidence, use precise vocabulary, and check punctuation before submitting work.

Question: Which response best paraphrases the passage?

A. The checklist helped students review important parts of a short response before submitting it.

B. The checklist made students write longer answers with more words.

C. The checklist replaced all writing assignments in the online classroom.

D. The checklist was only about punctuation.

Question 2

MLA.ENG2.R.10; MLA.ENG2.V.01

Passage: The teacher posted sample responses before students submitted their own work. The samples showed how to answer the question, use evidence, choose precise vocabulary, and write complete sentences.

Question: Which academic word best describes "precise vocabulary"?

A. confusing

B. unrelated

C. exact

D. hidden

Question 3

MLA.ENG2.R.10; MLA.ENG2.V.01

Passage: The library added a display of student-recommended books. Students used the display to find titles their classmates enjoyed.

Question: Which evidence best supports the paraphrase "The display helped students find recommended books"?

A. The library had books.

B. Students used the display to find titles their classmates enjoyed.

C. The display was in the library.

D. Students were classmates.

Question 4

MLA.ENG2.R.10; MLA.ENG2.V.01

Passage: The school posted bus updates on a screen near the office. Students checked the screen before leaving.

Question: Which sentence uses standard English conventions correctly?

A. the screen helped students check bus updates before leaving

B. Because students checked the screen before leaving.

C. The screen helped students it had bus updates.

D. The screen helped students check bus updates before leaving.
