P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 8: Putting It All Together — Unit 1 Synthesis

Lesson Title

Lesson 8: Putting It All Together — Unit 1 Synthesis

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 — Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 — Integrate Academic Vocabulary

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 — Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.C.01 — Present Ideas Orally

What You Will Learn

  • Synthesize the close-reading, paraphrasing, evidence, vocabulary, and convention skills from Lessons 1–7.
  • Apply the complete Unit 1 response process independently.
  • Prepare for the Unit 1 assessment.

Why This Matters

Synthesis shows that you can transfer several connected skills to a new text—the kind of independent thinking expected in later courses, careers, and civic life.

What You Will Do

  • Review the Unit 1 response process.
  • Apply all Unit 1 skills to new included passages.
  • Complete the Independent Work, Checkpoint, and Unit 1 assessment.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Read and paraphrase accurately.
  • Use relevant evidence and academic vocabulary.
  • Write clearly using standard English conventions.
  • Complete the Unit 1 assessment successfully.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can read a text, explain its meaning accurately, support my thinking with evidence, and communicate clearly.
