📘 ENG2 | Unit 01 | Lesson 08

P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Use the same Unit 1 skills in a short synthesis response. No new skill is introduced here.

Review Passage

Passage: The teacher posted sample responses before students submitted their own work. The samples showed how to answer the question, use evidence, choose precise vocabulary, and write complete sentences.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write a two-sentence response explaining how the sample responses helped students.
  2. Use the words evidence and precise correctly.
  3. Check capitalization and punctuation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The samples helped because they had stuff.

This is too vague. It does not explain the passage with academic vocabulary, evidence, or complete sentence clarity.

Correct: The samples helped students see how to use evidence, precise vocabulary, and complete sentences in their own responses.

Teachable Explanation: This is stronger because it keeps the passage meaning, uses Unit 1 academic vocabulary, and explains the idea clearly.

Deeper Reasoning Support

Lesson focus: Putting It All Together: Unit 1 Synthesis

Mapped standard focus: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary

Use this second pass to strengthen your thinking before you move to practice. The goal is not just to get an answer; the goal is to explain why the answer is supported by the text, source, sentence, or communication situation.

  1. Reread the embedded passage, source, scenario, sentence, or prompt on this page and mark the most useful details.
  2. Separate evidence from opinion. Evidence is stated or clearly implied by the text; opinion is a reaction that still needs proof.
  3. Write the reasoning step that connects the evidence to the skill. Use because, therefore, this shows, or this matters because to make the connection visible.
  4. Revise vague words into precise academic language that matches the standard and lesson vocabulary.
