P04 Worked Example

This page reviews the Unit 1 response process. It does not add new material.

Example 1: Read and Paraphrase

Passage: The library added a display of student-recommended books. Students used the display to find titles their classmates enjoyed.

Modeled thinking: The passage is mainly about a display that helped students find books recommended by other students.

Paraphrase: The display helped students discover books recommended by other students.

Example 2: Add Relevant Evidence

Evidence: The passage says students used the display to find titles classmates enjoyed.

Why this evidence works: It directly supports the paraphrase because it shows how the display helped students find recommended books.

Example 3: Build the Final Response

Final response: The display helped students discover books recommended by other students. The passage says students used it to find titles their classmates enjoyed, which shows that the display organized helpful book suggestions.

Why this response works: It combines an accurate paraphrase, relevant evidence, academic vocabulary, and complete sentences.

Common Mistake Review

Incorrect: The display was good because books.

This is too vague, lacks evidence, and is not a complete explanation.

Correct: The display helped students find books their classmates recommended.

This keeps the passage meaning accurate and uses clearer vocabulary.
