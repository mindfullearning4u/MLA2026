Lesson 3: Paraphrasing with Relevant Evidence

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to choose evidence that actually supports a paraphrase.
  • How to tell the difference between relevant and unrelated details.
  • How to explain a paraphrase using a short text detail.
  • How to prepare a clear oral explanation of your thinking.

Why This Matters

A paraphrase is stronger when you can point to the exact part of the text that supports it. Evidence keeps your response accurate. It also helps another reader understand how you got your idea from the passage.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included in the lesson.
  • Write paraphrases that keep the meaning accurate.
  • Choose evidence that proves the paraphrase.
  • Practice explaining the connection between your paraphrase and the text.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your paraphrase restates the passage accurately.
  • Your evidence comes from the passage and directly supports your paraphrase.
  • Your explanation connects the evidence to the paraphrase.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.10 in student language: I can restate what a text says in my own words and use the text to prove my restatement is accurate.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if you can write a paraphrase but cannot decide which detail proves it.
