P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 3: Paraphrase with Relevant Textual Evidence

Lesson Title

Lesson 3: Paraphrase with Relevant Textual Evidence

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.10 — Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.V.01 — Integrate Academic Vocabulary; MLA.ENG2.C.01 — Present Ideas Orally

What You Will Learn

  • Choose evidence that directly supports a paraphrase.
  • Distinguish relevant evidence from unrelated details.
  • Explain how textual evidence confirms an accurate paraphrase.

Why This Matters

Evidence makes your explanation trustworthy. In school, work, and daily decisions, readers need to see how your conclusion connects to reliable information.

What You Will Do

  • Read and paraphrase passages included in the lesson.
  • Select details that directly support each paraphrase.
  • Explain the connection between the evidence and your interpretation.

Lesson Workflow

  1. Write the lesson vocabulary words and definitions in your notebook.
  2. Complete the Lesson and Worked Example pages.
  3. Complete the Independent Work. Submit your vocabulary and definitions plus the Independent Work in the Notebook Evidence Submission activity.
  4. Complete the Checkpoint and submit those answers separately in the Checkpoint Submission activity.
  5. Complete the lesson quiz when assigned.
  6. If you earn below 80%, review TOR feedback, correct your mistakes, and meet with your TOR when additional help is needed.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Restate passage meaning accurately.
  • Select relevant textual evidence.
  • Explain clearly how the evidence supports the paraphrase.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can restate a text accurately and use relevant details from the text to prove my understanding.
