📘 ENG2 | Unit 01 | Lesson 03

P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can paraphrase a short passage and choose relevant evidence.

Checkpoint Passage

The cafeteria started placing breakfast bags near the main entrance for students who arrived just before class. Each bag included fruit, a grain item, and milk. The new pickup table helped students get breakfast without waiting in the cafeteria line.

Checkpoint Task

  1. Write one accurate paraphrase of the checkpoint passage.
  2. Copy one relevant detail from the passage that supports your paraphrase.
  3. Write two sentences explaining how the evidence supports your paraphrase.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear image or typed copy of your checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Submission Workflow

  1. Check that your paraphrase uses your own words.
  2. Check that your evidence comes directly from the passage.
  3. Check that your explanation connects the evidence to the paraphrase.
  4. Submit your notebook evidence in the checkpoint area.

Mastery Criteria

You show mastery when your paraphrase is accurate, your evidence is relevant, and your explanation clearly connects the evidence to your paraphrase. Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your evidence does not clearly support your idea.
