P06 Independent Work

Complete Parts A, B, and C in your notebook.

Instructions

Complete Part A, Part B, and Part C in your notebook before moving to the checkpoint. Use the passage or sentence context on this page and write complete responses.

Passage

The school office placed a digital sign near the entrance. The sign displayed the day's schedule, club meeting changes, and bus updates. Students checked the sign before school so they could plan where to go first.

Part A: Paraphrase

Write one sentence that accurately paraphrases the passage.

Part B: Evidence

Copy one detail from the passage that directly supports your paraphrase.

Part C: Oral Explanation Plan

Write two sentences you could say aloud to explain how your evidence supports your paraphrase.
