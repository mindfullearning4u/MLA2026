Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
paraphraseA restatement of information from a source using your own words and sentence structure while preserving the original meaning.
textual evidenceA quotation, detail, fact, or example from a text used to support an idea or explanation.
relevantDirectly connected to the claim, idea, or question being addressed.
supportTo strengthen or prove an idea by providing appropriate evidence and reasoning.
source textThe original passage, article, story, or other material from which information and evidence are taken.
original meaningThe central idea or message expressed by the source before it is restated in new words.
