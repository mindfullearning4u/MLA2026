P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 7: Integrated Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions; MLA.ENG2.C.01 - Present Ideas Orally.

What You Will Learn

  • How to combine close reading, paraphrase, academic vocabulary, evidence, and academic voice.
  • How to write a clear response that uses Unit 1 skills together in an academic voice.
  • How to prepare a short explanation of your thinking.

Why This Matters

Strong English responses rarely use only one skill. You read carefully, restate meaning, choose precise vocabulary, use evidence, and write with an academic voice. This lesson helps you put those skills together before the Unit 1 synthesis lesson.

What You Will Do

  • Read short passages included in the lesson.
  • Paraphrase the meaning accurately.
  • Use academic vocabulary and standard English conventions to create an academic voice.
  • Support your response with relevant evidence.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your response includes an accurate paraphrase.
  • Your academic vocabulary fits the text meaning.
  • Your evidence directly supports your explanation.
  • Your sentences use standard English conventions and an academic voice.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.10: I can restate text meaning accurately in my own words.

MLA.ENG2.V.01: I can use academic vocabulary correctly when I explain my thinking in an academic voice.

Ask your Teacher of Record for help if one part of your response is strong but the paraphrase, vocabulary, evidence, or conventions do not work together yet.
