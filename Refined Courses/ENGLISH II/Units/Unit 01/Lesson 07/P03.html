P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will revise a response so paraphrase, vocabulary, evidence, and conventions work together.

Read the Passage

The city library added labeled shelves for beginner, intermediate, and advanced technology guides. Visitors could choose a guide that matched their experience level without asking for help.

Direct Teaching

A response can include evidence but still be weak if it uses vague words or unclear sentences. Revise by asking: Is my paraphrase accurate? Is my vocabulary precise? Does my evidence support the idea? Are my sentences correct?

Notebook Directions

  1. Copy the weak response: The shelves helped people because they had stuff for different people.
  2. List two problems with the response.
  3. Revise the response using the words organized, experience level, or specific.

Common Mistake

Incorrect: The shelves helped people because they had stuff for different people.

This is too vague. It does not use precise vocabulary or explain the passage clearly.

Correct: The labeled shelves helped visitors find technology guides that matched their experience level.

This is stronger because it paraphrases accurately and uses precise vocabulary from the passage.
