The School Garden Expansion

Lincoln High’s garden supplied herbs and vegetables for cooking classes, but teachers wanted space for science experiments. The garden committee proposed converting an unused courtyard into twelve raised beds.

The project would cost $9,600. A community foundation offered $6,000 if the school provided volunteer labor. In a survey of 380 students, 71 percent supported the expansion and 46 students volunteered for weekend construction.

Some staff worried about maintenance during school breaks. The committee responded with a rotating schedule involving families, teachers, and a local gardening club. The principal approved the project for one year and required the committee to report harvest totals, student participation, and maintenance hours.
