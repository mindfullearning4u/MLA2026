P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can combine Unit 1 skills in one response.

Checkpoint Passage

The school added a response checklist to the online classroom. Before submitting short answers, students used the checklist to confirm that they had paraphrased the text, included evidence, used precise vocabulary, and checked punctuation.

Checkpoint Task

Write a four-sentence response explaining how the checklist helped students improve short answers.

  1. Paraphrase the main idea.
  2. Use at least two academic vocabulary words.
  3. Include one relevant text detail.
  4. Use complete sentences and correct punctuation.

Notebook Evidence Submission

Submit a clear image or typed copy of your integrated checkpoint response.

Checkpoint Submission Workflow

  1. Check your paraphrase for accuracy.
  2. Underline your academic vocabulary.
  3. Check that your evidence supports your idea.
  4. Submit your notebook evidence in the checkpoint area.

Mastery Criteria

You show mastery when your response combines accurate paraphrase, academic vocabulary, relevant evidence, and standard English conventions. Ask your Teacher of Record for help if one part is missing or unclear.
