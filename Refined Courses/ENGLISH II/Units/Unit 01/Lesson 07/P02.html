P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Integrated Unit 1 Response

Vocabulary

Integrated means combined into one connected response.

Relevant evidence means a text detail that directly supports your idea.

Precise vocabulary means exact words that fit the meaning.

Conventions are grammar, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling rules that make writing clear.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the passage and paraphrase the main idea.

Step 2: Choose one academic word that fits the idea.

Step 3: Select relevant evidence from the passage.

Step 4: Write a clear response using complete sentences.

Step 5: Check that all parts work together.

Read the Passage

The school created a shared calendar for clubs, sports, and tutoring sessions. Students checked the calendar before making plans, which reduced scheduling conflicts and helped more students attend activities.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one accurate paraphrase of the passage.
  2. Copy one relevant evidence detail.
  3. Write one academic word that fits the passage.
  4. Write one sentence explaining how the evidence supports your paraphrase.
