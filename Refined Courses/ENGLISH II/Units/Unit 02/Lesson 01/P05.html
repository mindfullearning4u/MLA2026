The Last Lantern

For ninety years, a brass lantern hung beside the entrance to Bellweather Station. Trains no longer stopped there, but Mara’s mother polished the lantern every Friday. She said travelers once watched for its light during winter storms.

When the town announced plans to convert the station into a market, Mara volunteered to help clear the building. She wanted the empty station to feel useful again. Her mother objected when the planning committee suggested replacing the lantern with a bright electric sign. “New light is still light,” Mara argued.

During cleanup, Mara discovered a bundle of letters beneath the ticket counter. One described a night when the lantern keeper guided stranded families into the station after snow blocked the tracks. Another thanked him for leaving the light burning long after the final train was due.

At the next meeting, Mara did not demand that the entire station remain unchanged. Instead, she proposed restoring the lantern and placing it above the market entrance beside a small plaque. The electric sign could stand near the road. Her mother studied the sketch and slowly nodded.

On opening night, shoppers moved beneath the lantern’s warm circle while the new sign shone across the parking lot. Mara noticed her mother telling a child about the storm in the old letter. For the first time, Mara understood that preserving the lantern was not about refusing change. It was about carrying a light from one purpose into another.

Questions

  1. Describe the setting and its condition at the beginning.
  2. What does Mara initially want?
  3. Identify the central external conflict.
  4. Describe Mara’s internal conflict.
  5. How do the letters change her understanding?
  6. What compromise resolves the conflict?
  7. How does Mara change from beginning to end?
  8. What does the mother’s reaction reveal?
  9. Explain how the station setting shapes the plot.
  10. Identify the point of view and explain its effect.
  11. What does the lantern represent literally?
  12. Develop a supported symbolic meaning for the lantern.
  13. Cite two details supporting that meaning.
  14. Explain the contrast between the lantern and electric sign.
  15. State a theme as a complete sentence.
  16. Explain how Mara’s choice develops the theme.
  17. Analyze the layered meaning of “carrying a light from one purpose into another.”
  18. Write a four-sentence literary analysis connecting character, conflict, symbol, and theme.
