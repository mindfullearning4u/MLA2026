P05 Guided Practice

Answer each question by connecting the literary element to what it adds to the passage. The passage is included here so you do not need to look anywhere else.

Passage

Mara paused outside the storage room where the old brass lantern hung from a bent nail. During the day, the room smelled of dust and cardboard, but at night the lantern threw warm circles of light across the floor. Mara's grandfather had once used it during storms, lifting it high so neighbors could find the porch steps. Now Mara held the lantern by its handle and watched the flame tremble. Tomorrow she would speak at the town meeting about saving the library, and her notes shook in her other hand. She wanted to sound certain, but the empty room seemed to know she was afraid. When the flame steadied, Mara took a breath and read her opening sentence aloud.

Guided Practice Questions

1. Which literary element is most clearly developed by Mara's shaking notes? A. Internal conflict B. External setting C. Flashback D. Dialogue

2. What does the lantern most likely symbolize? A. A forgotten chore B. Mara's dislike of storms C. Guidance and courage D. The town's wealth

3. How does the setting affect the passage? A. It makes the story humorous. B. It creates a quiet place for Mara to face her fear. C. It proves Mara has already spoken at the meeting. D. It shows that the library has closed.

4. Which evidence best supports the idea that Mara is nervous? A. The lantern hung from a bent nail. B. The room smelled of dust. C. Her grandfather helped neighbors. D. Her notes shook in her hand.

5. Which statement best explains layered meaning in the passage? A. The lantern is old and made of brass. B. The lantern lights the room and also represents Mara learning to guide others. C. The storage room contains cardboard. D. Mara reads a sentence aloud.
