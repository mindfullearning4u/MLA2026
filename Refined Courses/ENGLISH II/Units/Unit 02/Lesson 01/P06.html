The Unfinished Map

Jonah’s older sister left an unfinished map pinned above his desk before moving overseas. Roads crossed the paper in careful blue lines, but the northern edge ended in blank space. Jonah avoided looking at it because the empty section reminded him that she was gone.

Months later, a storm closed the usual road to his grandmother’s farm. Jonah studied the map and noticed a faint path leading toward the unfinished edge. He packed the map, followed the marked route, and discovered that the path continued beyond the paper toward a wooden footbridge.

After reaching the farm, Jonah drew the bridge and remaining path into the blank section. He did not pretend that his sister had completed it. Instead, he wrote both of their names beneath the map’s title and pinned it above his grandmother’s kitchen table.

  2. Identify the conflict created by the storm.
  3. How does the map affect the plot?
  4. What choice shows Jonah beginning to change?
  5. What does the blank section represent literally?
  6. Develop a symbolic meaning for the blank space.
  7. Cite two details supporting that meaning.
  8. How does the setting contribute to Jonah’s growth?
  9. State a supported theme.
  10. Explain how Jonah’s final action develops the theme.
  11. Analyze the layered meaning of placing both names beneath the title.
  12. Write a three-sentence analysis connecting conflict, symbol, and theme.
