P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will move from identifying literary elements to explaining how those elements create layered meaning.

Passage

Mara paused outside the storage room where the old brass lantern hung from a bent nail. During the day, the room smelled of dust and cardboard, but at night the lantern threw warm circles of light across the floor. Mara's grandfather had once used it during storms, lifting it high so neighbors could find the porch steps. Now Mara held the lantern by its handle and watched the flame tremble. Tomorrow she would speak at the town meeting about saving the library, and her notes shook in her other hand. She wanted to sound certain, but the empty room seemed to know she was afraid. When the flame steadied, Mara took a breath and read her opening sentence aloud.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Choose one element that appears more than once or seems connected to the character's problem.

Step 2: Explain the surface meaning first. The lantern is a real object in the storage room.

Step 3: Explain the deeper meaning. The lantern also suggests guidance, courage, and continuing a family example.

Step 4: Connect the element to Mara's conflict. She is afraid to speak, but the lantern reminds her of helping others through darkness.

Step 5: Write a complete analysis statement that includes the element, evidence, and meaning.

Notebook Directions

  1. Choose one literary element from the passage.
  2. Write one piece of evidence that shows the element.
  3. Explain the surface meaning of the evidence.
  4. Explain the deeper meaning or stylistic effect.
  5. Write a final analysis sentence using the frame: The author uses ___ to show ___.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The lantern is a literary element because it is in the story.

Correct example:

The lantern is a symbol of guidance because Mara's grandfather used it to help neighbors during storms, and Mara holds it while preparing to help save the library.

Why this matters: The incorrect example only identifies an object. It does not explain what the object adds. The correct example names the element, uses evidence, and explains the deeper meaning connected to Mara's choice.
