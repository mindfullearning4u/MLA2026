Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
adaptationA new work that reshapes elements of an earlier source for a different form, audience, culture, or purpose.
source textThe original work from which an adaptation takes characters, events, themes, images, or structures.
mythA traditional story that explains beliefs, origins, natural events, or cultural values, often involving gods or extraordinary beings.
classical textA work associated with the literature, history, or culture of ancient Greece or Rome.
religious textA sacred or faith-based work that communicates beliefs, teachings, histories, or moral ideas.
archetypeA recurring character type, situation, symbol, or pattern found across texts and cultures.
allusionA brief reference to a well-known person, event, place, story, or text that adds meaning.
transformationA significant change made to a source element when it is reimagined in an adaptation.
cultural contextThe beliefs, values, customs, historical conditions, and social expectations surrounding a text or its audience.
