P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Source Text and Adaptation

Vocabulary

Source text means the original or earlier text an adaptation responds to.

Adaptation means a new version that changes a source text for a new purpose, audience, form, or setting.

Preserved element means something the adaptation keeps from the source.

Transformed element means something the adaptation changes in a meaningful way.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Read the source first and identify its central character, conflict, and lesson.

Step 2: Read the adaptation and identify what it keeps.

Step 3: Identify what the adaptation changes: setting, character role, conflict, outcome, or message.

Step 4: Explain the effect of the change. Ask, "What does this new version emphasize?"

Step 5: Use evidence from both texts in your analysis.

Read the Source and Adaptation

Source Summary: "The Fire Bringer"

In an old myth, a young messenger sees people shivering in darkness while fire is kept high on a mountain. Although the mountain keeper warns that fire is too powerful for ordinary people, the messenger carries a small coal down the path. People use the coal for warmth, cooking, and protection. The messenger is punished for disobeying, but the people remember the gift as the beginning of shared knowledge.

Adaptation: "The Password"

In a modern school, Lina notices that only a few students know the password to the library's digital archive. The archive contains tutorials that could help everyone prepare for exams. The technology captain says the files are too advanced and should stay restricted. Lina creates a student guide that explains how to use the archive responsibly and shares it with her classmates. She loses her captain position for breaking the rule, but more students learn how to research, study, and help one another.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the notebook title and vocabulary.
  2. Create a two-column chart: Source and Adaptation.
  3. List the central gift in each text.
  4. List who controls access in each text.
  5. Write one sentence explaining what the adaptation preserves.

Model Example

Preserved element: Both texts include a powerful resource being kept away from ordinary people.

Evidence: The source keeps fire on a mountain, while the adaptation restricts the library's digital archive.

Learner Support Path

Remedial support: reread the vocabulary, work with one sentence or passage detail at a time, and use the model before answering independently.

Standard path: complete the full task, cite or name the evidence, and explain how it proves your answer.

Accelerated path: add a second example, compare two possible interpretations, or explain why a weaker response does not meet mastery.
