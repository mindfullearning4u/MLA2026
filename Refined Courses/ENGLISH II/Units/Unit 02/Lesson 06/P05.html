P05 Guided Practice

Use the source and adaptation to answer each question.

Source and Adaptation

Source: A messenger carries fire from a guarded mountain to people who need warmth and protection. The messenger is punished, but the people gain shared knowledge.

Adaptation: Lina shares a guide to a restricted digital archive so classmates can research and study. She loses her captain position, but more students learn to use the archive responsibly.

Guided Practice Questions

1. What element is preserved in both texts? A. A character shares a restricted resource. B. A character builds a mountain. C. A character refuses to help anyone. D. A character hides all knowledge forever.

2. What is transformed in the adaptation? A. The mountain becomes a forest. B. Fire becomes access to digital information. C. The messenger becomes a cook. D. The people become animals.

3. What consequence does Lina face? A. She wins a mountain race. B. She loses her captain position. C. She loses the archive forever. D. She is given fire.

4. What is the effect of changing fire into a digital archive? A. It makes the story about cooking. B. It removes the idea of shared knowledge. C. It makes the old conflict relevant to modern learning. D. It makes Lina identical to the mountain keeper.

5. Which response is strongest? A. The two texts are different. B. Lina shares a restricted archive just as the messenger shares restricted fire, so the adaptation keeps the theme of access to knowledge. C. The adaptation has a school. D. The source has a mountain.
