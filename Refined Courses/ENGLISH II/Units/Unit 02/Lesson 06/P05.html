📘 ENG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 06

P05 — Independent Work

Directions

Read both texts. Answer all 16 questions with evidence from each.

Source: “The Sun-Bearer”

When winter refused to leave the valley, Mara climbed the eastern peak where the Sun Keeper guarded a burning coal. The Keeper warned that mortals would waste its power. Mara saw children shivering below and carried the coal away inside a clay bowl. Eagles tore at her cloak, yet she reached the village. The coal warmed every hearth, but Mara was forbidden to climb the mountain again. Each spring, villagers placed clay bowls at their doors to remember her choice.

Adaptation: “The Seed Vault Code”

After blight destroyed regional crops, intern Malik discovered that a private laboratory held climate-resistant seed data. Executives planned to sell access only to wealthy farms. Malik copied the code to a public agricultural network. Security blocked his career account, but small farms downloaded the designs and replanted. Years later, communities displayed empty seed envelopes at harvest festivals. Some called Malik a thief; others called him the reason the valley ate.

Questions

  1. Summarize the source conflict.
  2. Summarize the adaptation conflict.
  3. Compare protagonists.
  4. What does the coal symbolize?
  5. What does the code symbolize?
  6. Identify three preserved elements.
  7. Identify three changed elements.
  8. Compare consequences.
  9. Compare community responses.
  10. How do clay bowls/seed envelopes function?
  11. State a shared theme.
  12. How does the modern setting alter the theme?
  13. Why replace divine authority with corporate control?
  14. What ethical ambiguity does the adaptation add?
  15. Evaluate one gain and one loss caused by adaptation.
  16. Write a comparative paragraph explaining purpose and effect with evidence from both.
