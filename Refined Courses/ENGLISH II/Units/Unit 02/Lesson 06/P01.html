P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 6: Mythical, Classical, or Religious Text Adaptation

Lesson Title

Lesson 6: Mythical, Classical, or Religious Text Adaptation

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.11 - Analyze Literary Adaptations.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.04 - Analyze Poetic Ambiguity; MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How an adaptation changes an older source text for a new setting, audience, or purpose.
  • How to compare what stays the same and what changes.
  • How to explain the effect of adaptation choices.
  • How to use evidence from both the source and adaptation.

Why This Matters

Adaptations show how old ideas are reshaped for new audiences and purposes. Comparing versions helps you understand cultural change and make informed choices when adapting information yourself.

What You Will Do

  • Read a short source myth summary and a modern adaptation.
  • Identify preserved elements and changed elements.
  • Analyze why the adaptation changes the source.
  • Write an evidence-based comparison.
