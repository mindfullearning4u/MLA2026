📘 ENG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 06

P06 Independent Work

Complete each part to show that you can analyze adaptation choices.

Instructions

Use both the source summary and "The Password." Write in complete sentences and include evidence from both texts.

Part A: Identify

List two elements the adaptation preserves from the source and two elements it changes.

Part B: Explain

Choose one changed element and explain why the change matters. Use evidence from both the source and adaptation.

Part C: Extend

Write a paragraph explaining how "The Password" adapts "The Fire Bringer" for a modern audience. Include a claim, evidence from both texts, and explanation of effect.
