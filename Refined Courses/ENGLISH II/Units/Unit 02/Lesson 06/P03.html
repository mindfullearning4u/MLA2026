P03 Notebook Task Part 2

Now you will move from identifying changes to explaining why the adaptation changes the source.

Source and Adaptation

Source Summary: A messenger carries fire from a guarded mountain to people who need warmth and protection. The messenger is punished, but the people gain shared knowledge.

Adaptation: Lina shares a guide to a restricted digital archive so students can research and study. She loses her captain position, but more students gain access to learning.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: The adaptation preserves the pattern of a person sharing a restricted resource.

Step 2: The adaptation changes fire into digital information. This makes the older conflict relevant to modern students.

Step 3: The adaptation changes punishment from mythic punishment to losing a leadership role. This keeps the consequence realistic.

Step 4: The effect is a modern message about responsible access to knowledge.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one preserved element.
  2. Write one transformed element.
  3. Explain the effect of that transformation.
  4. Use evidence from both the source and adaptation.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The adaptation is different because it has a password.

Correct example:

The adaptation transforms fire into digital information, which shifts the older myth's focus on physical survival into a modern focus on access to learning.

Why this matters: The incorrect example names a difference. The correct example explains the effect of the difference.
