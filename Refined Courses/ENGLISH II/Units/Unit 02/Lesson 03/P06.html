📘 ENG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 03

P06 Independent Work

Complete each part to show that you can analyze coming-of-age growth and conflicting perspectives.

Instructions

Use "The Bridge at Maple Creek." Write in complete sentences and include evidence from the passage when asked.

Part A: Identify

Identify Elena's beginning perspective and her father's perspective. Use one piece of evidence for each perspective.

Part B: Explain

Explain the moment when Elena's understanding changes. Include the detail that causes her to hear her father's warning differently.

Part C: Extend

Write a short paragraph explaining how Elena's choice to stop Mateo shows a coming-of-age experience. Include evidence and explain the maturity shown by her decision.
