P04 Worked Example

Study how each example moves from evidence to analysis of growth and conflicting perspectives.

Example 1: Conflicting Perspectives

Question: What perspectives conflict at the beginning?

Step 1: Identify Elena's view. She sees the bridge as freedom.

Step 2: Identify her father's view. He sees the bridge as dangerous.

Step 3: Explain why they disagree. Elena values independence, while her father values safety.

Modeled response: Elena and her father have conflicting perspectives because Elena sees the bridge as a path to independence, but her father sees it as a place where she could be hurt.

Example 2: Coming-of-Age Moment

Question: Which moment shows Elena growing in maturity?

Step 1: Look for a change in understanding. Elena hears her father's warning in a new way.

Step 2: Look for a choice that proves the change. She tells Mateo, "Not today."

Step 3: Explain the growth. Elena realizes that responsibility can require patience.

Modeled response: Elena's coming-of-age moment occurs when she stops Mateo from crossing because she understands that waiting can be a responsible choice, not just a loss of freedom.

Example 3: Author's Representation

Question: How does the author represent the father's perspective?

Step 1: Notice that the father is not shown as controlling for no reason.

Step 2: The author gives specific safety details: slick boards, fast cars, and rising water.

Step 3: These details help the reader understand why the father says no.

Modeled response: The author represents the father's perspective as protective rather than unfair by giving concrete details about the bridge's dangers.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The father is wrong because Elena wants to go to the library.

Correct example:

The father and Elena disagree because Elena focuses on independence, while her father notices safety risks that Elena does not understand until she sees Mateo near the bridge.

Why this matters: The incorrect example takes one side without analyzing the conflict. The correct example explains both perspectives and how Elena's understanding changes.
