P05 Guided Practice

Use the passage to answer each question. Focus on Elena's growth and the conflicting perspectives.

Passage

Elena had waited all summer to ride her bike alone across the narrow bridge at Maple Creek. To her, the bridge meant freedom. Her father saw slick boards, fast cars, and a creek that rose without warning. Later, Elena found Mateo pushing his scooter toward the bridge. A truck rushed past the curve, and Elena heard her father's warning in a new way. She turned Mateo around and told him they could practice in the parking lot first.

Guided Practice Questions

1. What is Elena's perspective at the beginning? A. The bridge represents independence. B. The bridge should be destroyed. C. Mateo should cross alone. D. Her father wants her to stop reading.

2. What is her father's perspective? A. The bridge is harmless. B. The bridge is risky and requires caution. C. Elena should never leave home. D. Mateo should ride faster.

3. Which moment best shows Elena's coming-of-age experience? A. She waits all summer. B. She folds her arms. C. She stops Mateo after understanding the danger. D. She expects a lecture.

4. Which evidence best supports the father's perspective? A. Elena wants to visit the library. B. Mateo grins. C. Elena folds her arms. D. The bridge has slick boards, fast cars, and rising water.

5. Which statement best explains Elena's growth? A. She learns that maturity can mean waiting to keep someone safe. B. She learns that adults are always wrong. C. She learns that bridges are never useful. D. She learns that Mateo should make every decision.
