Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
coming-of-ageA literary pattern in which a young person gains maturity, insight, or a clearer sense of identity through significant experiences.
perspectiveThe particular way a character, narrator, or person understands and interprets events.
conflicting perspectivesDifferent or opposing interpretations, beliefs, or attitudes about the same person, event, or issue.
protagonistThe central character whose goals, choices, and conflicts drive a literary work.
characterizationThe methods an author uses to reveal a character’s traits, motivations, values, and development.
internal conflictA struggle within a character involving competing thoughts, feelings, desires, or values.
external conflictA struggle between a character and an outside force, such as another person, society, nature, or circumstance.
turning pointA significant event or decision that changes the direction of the plot or a character’s understanding.
maturationThe process of becoming more emotionally, intellectually, or socially developed.
