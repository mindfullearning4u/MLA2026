Directions

The House That Kept the Rain

By dusk, rain had erased the road behind Soren’s family. Their temporary house clicked and sighed around them. Water drummed on the roof like impatient fingers, and every dark window held a second night. Soren hated the place: its narrow rooms, its unfamiliar smells, the boxes that still wore labels from home.

When the power failed, his little sister began to cry. Their mother searched for candles while wind shoved at the walls. Soren found an old glass lantern in a cupboard. Its flame was small, but the kitchen gathered around it. The walls stopped looming and became a shelter cupping four people in warm hands.

They told stories until the storm softened to a whisper. In the morning, sunlight stitched gold across the wet floor. Soren opened a box and placed his books on the empty shelf. The house had not become home overnight, but it no longer felt like an enemy.

Questions

  1. Describe the opening mood.
  2. Identify two details that create it.
  3. Analyze “rain had erased the road.”
  4. Analyze “impatient fingers.”
  5. What effect do the dark windows create?
  6. How does setting reflect Soren’s conflict?
  7. Identify the turning point.
  8. What does the lantern symbolize?
  9. Analyze “the kitchen gathered.”
  10. How does “warm hands” change the house?
  11. Trace the sound imagery.
  12. Trace the light imagery.
  13. Explain the final metaphor.
  14. State a theme.
  15. Explain how mood develops that theme.
  16. Write a paragraph analyzing the mood shift using three quoted details.
