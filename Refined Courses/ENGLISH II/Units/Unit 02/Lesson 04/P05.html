P05 Guided Practice

Use the passage to answer each question. Focus on how figurative language creates mood.

Passage

The train station waited under a roof of gray clouds. Rain stitched silver lines down the windows, and the platform lights blinked like tired eyes. Nia pressed her ticket flat against her palm. The suitcase beside her felt like a stone. Every announcement echoed through the station like a question she could not answer. When the train finally appeared, its headlight cut a golden path through the rain.

Guided Practice Questions

1. What mood is created by "a roof of gray clouds"? A. cheerful B. tense and gloomy C. playful D. relaxed

2. Which phrase is an example of personification? A. "pressed her ticket" B. "arts program" C. "platform lights blinked like tired eyes" D. "in an hour"

3. What does the simile "the suitcase beside her felt like a stone" suggest? A. Nia feels emotionally weighed down. B. Nia packed too many books. C. The suitcase is broken. D. The train is late.

4. How does "a golden path through the rain" affect mood? A. It makes the scene colder. B. It removes all conflict. C. It shows Nia has returned home. D. It creates a more hopeful feeling.

5. Which answer best analyzes figurative language and mood? A. The announcement is loud. B. The phrase "like a question she could not answer" creates uncertainty because Nia is unsure about leaving. C. Nia has a ticket. D. The station has windows.
