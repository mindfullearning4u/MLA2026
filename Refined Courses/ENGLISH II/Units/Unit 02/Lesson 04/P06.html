Instructions

Use "At the Station Window." Write in complete sentences. Do not stop at naming a device; explain the mood and effect.

Part A: Identify

Find three examples of figurative language or vivid word choice. For each one, identify the type of language or describe the word choice.

Part B: Explain

Choose one phrase from Part A. Explain the mood it creates and how that mood connects to Nia's situation.

Part C: Extend

Write a paragraph explaining how the mood changes from the beginning to the end of the passage. Include at least two phrases from the passage and explain their effects.
