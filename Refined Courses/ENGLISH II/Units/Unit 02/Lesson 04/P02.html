Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
figurative languageLanguage that communicates meaning beyond the literal definitions of words to create images, comparisons, or emotional effects.
simileA comparison between unlike things that uses a word such as like or as.
metaphorA direct comparison that describes one thing as another to emphasize a shared quality or idea.
personificationA figure of speech that gives human qualities, actions, or emotions to something nonhuman.
imageryDescriptive language that appeals to the senses and helps readers imagine a scene, object, or experience.
moodThe feeling or emotional atmosphere a literary work creates for the reader.
toneThe author’s or speaker’s attitude toward a subject, character, or audience.
connotationThe feelings, associations, or ideas connected to a word beyond its dictionary definition.
interpretive effectThe influence a literary choice has on a reader’s understanding, emotions, or interpretation of a text.
