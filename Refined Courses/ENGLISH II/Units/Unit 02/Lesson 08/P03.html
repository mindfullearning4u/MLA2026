P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now you will connect Unit 2 reading skills to a writing decision.

Passage

Ari stood at the theater curtain, listening to rain tap the roof like impatient fingers. The role had belonged to his older sister last year, and everyone expected him to sound just like her. In his pocket, the lucky coin she gave him pressed a cold circle into his palm. He wanted to copy her confident voice, but the first line on the page seemed to ask for his own. The stage manager whispered, "Ready?" Ari closed his hand around the coin, then let it go. The curtain rose. He stepped into the light slowly, one breath at a time.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Ari's conflict is internal: copy his sister or use his own voice.

Step 2: The coin is a symbol. It connects Ari to his sister but also becomes something he releases.

Step 3: The rain simile creates an anxious mood.

Step 4: A narrative revision could slow the moment when he lets go of the coin to emphasize his growth.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one sentence analyzing the coin as a symbol.
  2. Write one sentence explaining Ari's coming-of-age moment.
  3. Write one sentence explaining mood.
  4. Revise the final two sentences to slow the pace.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The coin is in his pocket, and then he lets it go.

Correct example:

The coin symbolizes Ari's connection to his sister, and letting it go shows that he is ready to perform in his own voice.

Why this matters: The incorrect example summarizes the object. The correct example explains symbolic meaning and character growth.
