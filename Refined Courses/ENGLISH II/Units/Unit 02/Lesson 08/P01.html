Lesson 8: Putting It All Together - Unit 2 Synthesis

Standards Covered

Primary Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.01, MLA.ENG2.R.02, MLA.ENG2.R.03, MLA.ENG2.R.04, MLA.ENG2.R.09, MLA.ENG2.R.11, MLA.ENG2.W.01.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.10, MLA.ENG2.V.01, MLA.ENG2.L.01.

What You Will Learn

  • How Unit 2 literary analysis and narrative craft skills work together.
  • How to move between reading analysis and writing decisions.
  • How to use evidence to support interpretation, comparison, adaptation analysis, and writing choices.

What You Will Do

  • Review literary elements, theme, perspective, ambiguity, figurative language, adaptation, and pacing.
  • Read passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Complete synthesis practice and a checkpoint.
  • Prepare for the Unit 2 Assessment.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains which standard each task uses.
  • Your checkpoint combines reading evidence with a writing choice.
  • Your Unit Assessment shows mastery of Unit 2 standards.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

I can analyze literary craft and use narrative writing choices to explain or create meaning. This lesson reviews what you learned in Unit 2 and asks you to apply the skills together.
