The Bell Under Water

Before the reservoir covered the old town, Mara’s grandfather said its bell would keep ringing beneath the water. Years later, Mara piloted a survey boat across the flooded valley. Her supervisor called the story nostalgia and ordered her to record only measurable data. At dusk, the sonar traced a tower below. Then the receiver pulsed with a regular metallic echo.

Mara could label it equipment noise and finish on time. Instead, she repeated the scan from three angles. The echo remained. She logged the evidence carefully, but beside the numbers she wrote the town’s original name. When she played the recording for her grandfather, he did not say the legend was proven. He only whispered, “Now the water remembers too.”
