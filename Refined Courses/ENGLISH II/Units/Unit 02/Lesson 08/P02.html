P02 Notebook Task Part 1

Notebook Title: Unit 2 Synthesis Review

Vocabulary

Synthesis means bringing multiple skills or ideas together.

Evidence-based analysis means explaining a claim with specific details from a text.

Craft choice means a decision an author makes about structure, language, character, point of view, or pacing.

Revision means improving writing to better meet a purpose or audience need.

Step-by-Step Review

Step 1: Identify the skill the question requires: element, theme, perspective, ambiguity, mood, adaptation, or pacing.

Step 2: Find evidence that directly matches that skill.

Step 3: Explain the effect. Do not stop at naming the device or summarizing the plot.

Step 4: When writing, choose craft moves that create the intended reader experience.

Passage

Ari stood at the theater curtain, listening to rain tap the roof like impatient fingers. The role had belonged to his older sister last year, and everyone expected him to sound just like her. In his pocket, the lucky coin she gave him pressed a cold circle into his palm. He wanted to copy her confident voice, but the first line on the page seemed to ask for his own. The stage manager whispered, "Ready?" Ari closed his hand around the coin, then let it go. The curtain rose. He stepped into the light slowly, one breath at a time.

Notebook Directions

  1. Identify one literary element and explain its effect.
  2. Identify one phrase that creates mood.
  3. Explain how pacing creates tension near the end.
  4. Write one theme statement supported by the passage.

Model Example

Pacing: The phrase "one breath at a time" slows Ari's movement onto the stage and creates a tense but focused mood.
