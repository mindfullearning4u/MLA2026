Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
literary elementA basic component of a literary work, such as character, setting, plot, conflict, or theme.
themeA central insight or message about life, society, or human experience developed through a literary work.
perspectiveThe particular way a character, narrator, or person understands and interprets events.
figurative languageLanguage that communicates meaning beyond literal definitions to create comparison, imagery, or emotional effect.
ambiguityThe quality of allowing more than one reasonable meaning or interpretation.
adaptationA new work that reshapes elements of an earlier source for a different form, audience, culture, or purpose.
narrative pacingThe speed at which a story’s events unfold and information is revealed.
interpretationA reasoned explanation of a text’s meaning supported by specific textual evidence.
synthesisThe process of combining ideas, evidence, and literary-analysis skills to form a complete understanding or response.
