P04 Worked Example

Each example uses a different Unit 2 skill.

Example 1: Literary Element

Question: What does the coin add to the passage?

Step 1: The coin is a concrete object connected to Ari's sister.

Step 2: Ari lets it go before stepping into the light.

Modeled response: The coin works as a symbol of Ari's dependence on his sister's example, and letting it go shows he is ready to use his own voice.

Example 2: Mood and Figurative Language

Question: How does "rain tap the roof like impatient fingers" affect mood?

Step 1: The simile compares rain to impatient fingers.

Step 2: The image creates pressure and nervousness.

Modeled response: The simile creates an anxious mood because the rain seems to rush Ari toward a decision.

Example 3: Narrative Pacing

Question: How does "one breath at a time" affect pacing?

Step 1: The phrase slows Ari's movement.

Step 2: It makes the reader feel each moment before he performs.

Modeled response: The pacing creates focused tension because Ari enters the light slowly instead of rushing through the important moment.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The passage has rain, a coin, and a stage.

Correct example:

The rain creates anxious mood, the coin symbolizes Ari's connection to his sister, and the slow pacing shows his tense movement toward independence.

Why this matters: Synthesis requires explaining how details work together, not listing them separately.
