P05 Guided Practice

Use the passage to answer each synthesis question.

Passage

Ari stood at the theater curtain, listening to rain tap the roof like impatient fingers. The role had belonged to his older sister last year, and everyone expected him to sound just like her. In his pocket, the lucky coin she gave him pressed a cold circle into his palm. He wanted to copy her confident voice, but the first line on the page seemed to ask for his own. The stage manager whispered, "Ready?" Ari closed his hand around the coin, then let it go. The curtain rose. He stepped into the light slowly, one breath at a time.

Guided Practice Questions

1. What does the coin most likely symbolize? A. Ari's connection to his sister and the choice to find his own voice. B. The price of the ticket. C. The theater's lights. D. The sound of rain.

2. How does the rain simile affect mood? A. It creates a calm mood. B. It creates an anxious mood. C. It creates a humorous mood. D. It removes tension.

3. Which line best shows pacing? A. "The role had belonged to his older sister." B. "The stage manager whispered." C. "He stepped into the light slowly, one breath at a time." D. "everyone expected him"

4. What coming-of-age idea is suggested? A. Copying others is always best. B. Performing is never difficult. C. Rain means failure. D. Growing up can mean choosing your own voice.

5. Which synthesis statement is strongest? A. The passage has a coin and rain. B. The coin, anxious rain, and slow pacing all show Ari's movement toward independence. C. Ari is near a curtain. D. The stage manager speaks.
