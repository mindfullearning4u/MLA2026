P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now you will explain how each passage develops the shared theme in a different situation.

Passages

Passage A: "The Seed Tray"

Jonas wanted the school garden to look perfect before visitors arrived. When he saw that Mina had planted the tomato seeds unevenly, he almost pulled them from the tray. Then he noticed Mina standing nearby with soil on her sleeves and hope in her eyes. Jonas remembered his own first garden, when half the beans never grew. He handed Mina a marker and said, "Let's label this row together, and we can watch what happens." Weeks later, the crooked row grew taller than any other.

Passage B: "The Spare Uniform"

Talia kept an extra debate uniform in her locker because she liked being prepared. On competition day, her teammate Andre arrived with rain soaking his jacket and panic tightening his voice. Talia thought about how long she had saved for the spare blazer. Then she opened her locker and handed it to him. Andre's shoulders relaxed before he walked onstage. Talia wore her old sweater that day, but when the team won, Andre pointed to her first.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: Start with a theme statement that fits both passages.

Step 2: Explain how Passage A develops the theme through Jonas's choice. Jonas gives up control over the perfect garden.

Step 3: Explain how Passage B develops the theme through Talia's choice. Talia gives up the blazer she saved for.

Step 4: Compare the development. Both characters help someone else, but Jonas gives up perfection while Talia gives up personal comfort.

Step 5: End by explaining what the comparison reveals about the theme.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write one theme statement that applies to both passages.
  2. Write one sentence explaining how Passage A develops the theme.
  3. Write one sentence explaining how Passage B develops the theme.
  4. Write one comparison sentence that explains an important similarity or difference.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

The theme is helping. Both stories have helping.

Correct example:

Both passages develop the theme that helping others may require personal sacrifice. Jonas gives up his perfect garden plan, while Talia gives up the blazer she saved for so Andre can compete confidently.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example gives only a topic and a vague comparison. The correct example states a complete theme, uses evidence from both passages, and explains how the theme develops.
