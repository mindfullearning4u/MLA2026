P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can compare how two literary texts develop a shared theme.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your response names a topic but does not state a complete theme, or if it uses evidence from only one passage.

Submission Workflow

  1. Review your notebook chart and worked examples.
  2. Complete the checkpoint paragraph in complete sentences.
  3. Confirm that you used both passages.
  4. Submit your checkpoint according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Write one paragraph comparing how "The Seed Tray" and "The Spare Uniform" develop the theme that helping others may require sacrifice. Include evidence from both passages and explain one important similarity or difference in how the theme develops.

Mastery Criteria

  • States or uses a complete theme statement.
  • Includes evidence from both passages.
  • Explains how each passage develops the theme.
  • Compares the passages instead of summarizing them separately only.
