P01 Lesson Overview

Lesson Title

Lesson 2: Comparative theme development across literary text

Standards Covered in This Lesson

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.R.02 - Analyze and Compare Theme Development.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.01 - Analyze Literary Elements; MLA.ENG2.R.10 - Paraphrase Grade-Level Texts; MLA.ENG2.V.01 - Integrate Academic Vocabulary.

What You Will Learn

  • How to identify a universal theme in a literary text.
  • How to explain how a theme develops through character choices, conflict, and details.
  • How to compare the way two literary texts develop a similar theme.
  • How to use evidence from both texts in a comparative response.

What You Will Do

  • Read two short literary passages included directly in the lesson.
  • Identify a shared theme and track how each passage develops it.
  • Compare character choices and story details across the passages.
  • Write a response that uses evidence from both passages.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook work states a theme as a complete idea, not a one-word topic.
  • Your responses explain how the theme develops in each passage.
  • Your checkpoint compares the two passages with evidence from both.
  • Your assessment answers show accurate comprehension, application, and analysis.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.R.02 in student language: I can explain a theme in a literary text and compare how that theme develops across texts.

Today, your job is not just to name a topic like courage or responsibility. Your job is to explain what the text teaches about that topic and how the author develops that message.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your theme sounds like one word, if you cannot find evidence from both passages, or if your comparison explains only one text.
