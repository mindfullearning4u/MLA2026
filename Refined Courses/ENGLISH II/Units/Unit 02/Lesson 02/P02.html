Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
themeA central insight or message about life, society, or human experience developed through a literary text.
thematic statementA complete sentence that expresses a text’s theme as a general insight rather than a single-word topic.
theme developmentThe way a theme becomes clearer through characters, conflicts, events, settings, symbols, and outcomes.
comparisonAn examination of two or more texts that identifies meaningful similarities.
contrastAn examination of two or more texts that identifies meaningful differences.
literary textA creative work, such as a story, novel, drama, or poem, that uses literary elements to communicate meaning.
textual evidenceA quotation, detail, or example from a text used to support an interpretation.
motifA recurring image, idea, phrase, object, or situation that helps develop a theme.
interpretationA reasoned explanation of a text’s meaning supported by evidence.
