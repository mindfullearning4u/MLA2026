P02 Notebook Task - Part 1

Notebook Title: Comparing Theme Development

Vocabulary

Theme means a message or insight about life, people, choices, or society.

Universal theme means a theme that can apply to many people, places, or time periods.

Development means the way an idea grows, changes, or becomes clearer across a text.

Compare means to explain important similarities and differences.

Step-by-Step Teaching

Step 1: Identify the topic. A topic is usually one word or phrase, such as responsibility, courage, or friendship.

Step 2: Turn the topic into a complete theme statement. A theme statement explains what the text suggests about the topic.

Step 3: Track how the theme develops. Look at the beginning, middle, and end of the passage.

Step 4: Find evidence from each text. A comparison must use both passages.

Step 5: Explain the similarity or difference in development. Do not simply summarize both plots.

Read the Passages

Passage A: "The Seed Tray"

Jonas wanted the school garden to look perfect before visitors arrived. When he saw that Mina had planted the tomato seeds unevenly, he almost pulled them from the tray. Then he noticed Mina standing nearby with soil on her sleeves and hope in her eyes. Jonas remembered his own first garden, when half the beans never grew. He handed Mina a marker and said, "Let's label this row together, and we can watch what happens." Weeks later, the crooked row grew taller than any other.

Passage B: "The Spare Uniform"

Talia kept an extra debate uniform in her locker because she liked being prepared. On competition day, her teammate Andre arrived with rain soaking his jacket and panic tightening his voice. Talia thought about how long she had saved for the spare blazer. Then she opened her locker and handed it to him. Andre's shoulders relaxed before he walked onstage. Talia wore her old sweater that day, but when the team won, Andre pointed to her first.

Notebook Directions

  1. Write the title: Comparing Theme Development.
  2. Write the vocabulary words and meanings.
  3. Identify one topic shared by both passages.
  4. Write a complete theme statement about that topic.
  5. Create a chart with evidence from Passage A and Passage B.

Model Example

Topic: Helping others

Theme statement: Helping others sometimes requires giving up control or comfort, but it can strengthen the whole group.

Evidence: Jonas lets Mina keep the uneven row, and Talia gives Andre the spare blazer even though she saved for it.
