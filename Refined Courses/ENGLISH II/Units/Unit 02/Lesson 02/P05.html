P05 Guided Practice

Use both passages for the guided practice. The passages are included here so you do not need to look anywhere else.

Passages

Passage A: "The Seed Tray"

Jonas wanted the school garden to look perfect before visitors arrived. When he saw that Mina had planted the tomato seeds unevenly, he almost pulled them from the tray. Then he noticed Mina standing nearby with soil on her sleeves and hope in her eyes. Jonas remembered his own first garden, when half the beans never grew. He handed Mina a marker and said, "Let's label this row together, and we can watch what happens." Weeks later, the crooked row grew taller than any other.

Passage B: "The Spare Uniform"

Talia kept an extra debate uniform in her locker because she liked being prepared. On competition day, her teammate Andre arrived with rain soaking his jacket and panic tightening his voice. Talia thought about how long she had saved for the spare blazer. Then she opened her locker and handed it to him. Andre's shoulders relaxed before he walked onstage. Talia wore her old sweater that day, but when the team won, Andre pointed to her first.

Guided Practice Questions

1. Which is the best shared theme statement for both passages? A. Helping others may require giving up something we value. B. Gardening is easier than debate. C. Prepared people never need help. D. Teams always win competitions.

2. How does Passage A develop the theme? A. Jonas wins a garden contest. B. Mina teaches Jonas how to plant. C. Jonas chooses to support Mina instead of fixing the imperfect row. D. The visitors cancel their trip.

3. How does Passage B develop the theme? A. Talia refuses to help Andre. B. Talia gives Andre the spare blazer even though she saved for it. C. Andre decides not to compete. D. The team loses because of the rain.

4. Which comparison is most accurate? A. Both passages show characters avoiding responsibility. B. Passage A is about competition, and Passage B is about gardening. C. Both passages show that sacrifice is always punished. D. Both passages show characters choosing to help someone else, but Jonas gives up control while Talia gives up a personal item.

5. Which evidence from Passage B best supports the theme? A. Talia kept an extra uniform in her locker. B. Talia handed Andre the blazer when he arrived soaked and nervous. C. Andre walked onstage. D. Talia wore an old sweater.
