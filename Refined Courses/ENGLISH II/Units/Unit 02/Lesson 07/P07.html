P07 Checkpoint

This checkpoint shows whether you can write a narrative moment with controlled pacing.

Teacher of Record Reminder

Ask your Teacher of Record for help before submitting if your writing tells the event but does not create tension, mood, or tone.

Submission Workflow

  1. Draft your scene.
  2. Revise one sentence for stronger pacing.
  3. Check that the scene creates a clear feeling.
  4. Submit your checkpoint according to your course directions.

Checkpoint Task

Write a five- to seven-sentence narrative scene in which a character faces a tense moment. Use at least one short sentence, one sensory detail, and one clear action that reveals the character's choice.

Mastery Criteria

  • Uses pacing intentionally.
  • Creates tension, mood, or tone.
  • Includes sensory detail and clear action.
  • Uses standard English conventions.
