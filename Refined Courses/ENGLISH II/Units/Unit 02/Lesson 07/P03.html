P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Now you will revise pacing to create a stronger narrative effect.

Revision Passage

Jalen walked to the door and felt nervous. He opened it and said he was ready.

Deeper Learning

Step 1: The revision passage summarizes too quickly. It tells the reader Jalen is nervous but does not make the reader feel it.

Step 2: To slow the moment, add physical details: cold hands, bent cards, a loud clock, a hand on the knob.

Step 3: Use short sentences to create pressure: "Ten steps. Five steps."

Step 4: End with a clear action that shows growth: Jalen opens the door and speaks.

Notebook Directions

  1. Rewrite the revision passage in four to six sentences.
  2. Add at least one sensory detail.
  3. Use at least one short sentence for tension.
  4. End with a clear action that shows Jalen choosing courage.

Common Mistake

Incorrect example:

Jalen was nervous and then he went inside.

Correct example:

Jalen's fingers tightened around the bent note cards. Three steps. Two. The clock clicked above him, and he opened the door before fear could answer for him.

Teachable Explanation: The incorrect example summarizes emotion. The correct example uses pacing, detail, and action to create tension.
