P01 Lesson Overview: Lesson 7: Narrative Pacing for Tension, Mood, and Tone

Standards Covered

Primary Standard: MLA.ENG2.W.01 - Write Narrative Texts with Pace and Tone.

Support Standards: MLA.ENG2.R.03 - Analyze Coming-of-Age Perspectives; MLA.ENG2.R.09 - Analyze Figurative Language and Mood; MLA.ENG2.W.04 - Revise for Audience Needs; MLA.ENG2.L.01 - Apply Standard English Conventions.

What You Will Learn

  • How pacing controls how quickly or slowly a narrative moment feels.
  • How sentence length, detail, dialogue, and paragraph breaks create tension, mood, and tone.
  • How to revise a narrative scene for stronger effect.
  • How pacing can support character growth and conflict.

What You Will Do

  • Read a model narrative scene included in the lesson.
  • Identify pacing moves that slow down or speed up a moment.
  • Revise sentences to create tension, mood, and tone.
  • Draft a short narrative moment using intentional pacing.

How You Will Show Mastery

  • Your notebook explains how pacing affects a scene.
  • Your revisions use sentence length, detail, and paragraphing intentionally.
  • Your checkpoint writes a paced narrative moment with clear mood or tension.
  • Your quiz answers identify effective narrative pacing choices.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection

MLA.ENG2.W.01 in student language: I can write a narrative scene that uses pacing to create tension, mood, or tone.

Today, your job is to control the reader's experience by choosing when to slow down, when to move quickly, and which details to emphasize.

Need help? Ask your Teacher of Record for help if your scene tells what happened but does not create a clear feeling for the reader.
