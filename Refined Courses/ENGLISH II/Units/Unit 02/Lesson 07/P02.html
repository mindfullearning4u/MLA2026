Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
narrative pacingThe speed at which a story’s events unfold and information is revealed to the reader.
tensionA feeling of pressure, uncertainty, or conflict that keeps readers concerned about what may happen.
suspenseAn anxious sense of anticipation created when readers wait to learn the outcome of an uncertain situation.
moodThe emotional atmosphere a narrative creates for the reader.
toneThe narrator’s or author’s attitude toward the subject, characters, or audience.
sceneA section of narrative that presents events in detail as they unfold in a specific time and place.
summaryA condensed account that covers events or time quickly by presenting only the most important information.
syntaxThe arrangement and length of words, phrases, and sentences, which can influence rhythm and pacing.
sensory detailDescription that appeals to sight, sound, touch, taste, or smell and makes a narrative moment more vivid.
