📘 ENG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 07

P06 — Checkpoint

Directions

Read the flat draft, revise it into a 180–250-word suspense scene, and answer the reflection questions.

Flat Draft

Maya entered the archive room to get a file. The door locked. She heard someone outside. She found another exit and left.

Required Revision Elements

  • Establish a specific setting and reason the file matters.
  • Slow one crucial moment with sensory detail and internal thought.
  • Use at least three short sentences to accelerate action.
  • Delay one piece of information to create suspense.
  • Maintain a consistent mood and tone.
  1. Where did you slow the pace, and why?
  2. Where did you accelerate it?
  3. Which diction choices establish tone?
  4. How does your ending resolve or sustain tension?
