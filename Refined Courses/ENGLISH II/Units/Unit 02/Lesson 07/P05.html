P05 Guided Practice

Use the narrative scene to answer each question.

Passage

Jalen reached the end of the hallway just as the final bell rang. The classroom door stood twenty steps away. Inside his backpack, the presentation cards bent against his folder. Ten steps. The clock above the door clicked once, loud as a lock. Five steps. Jalen stopped with his hand on the knob. He could still turn around. Instead, he breathed in, opened the door, and said, "I'm ready."

Guided Practice Questions

1. What is the effect of "Ten steps. Five steps."? A. It slows the pace and builds tension. B. It summarizes the whole story. C. It removes all suspense. D. It changes the setting.

2. Which detail most clearly creates tension? A. The hallway is long. B. The clock clicked "loud as a lock." C. Jalen owns a backpack. D. The class has a desk.

3. Which revision best improves pacing? A. Jalen was nervous and went in. B. Jalen did a thing at school. C. Jalen's fingers paused on the cold knob. One breath. Then he opened the door. D. Jalen gave a presentation.

4. Which choice would speed up unimportant action? A. Add a full page about tying shoes. B. Stop before the scene begins. C. Repeat the same detail six times. D. Summarize the walk to school in one sentence.

5. What tone is created when Jalen says, "I'm ready"? A. careless B. determined C. mocking D. confused
