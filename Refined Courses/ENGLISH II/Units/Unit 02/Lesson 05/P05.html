📘 ENG2 | Unit 02 | Lesson 05

P05 Guided Practice

Use the poem to answer each question. Focus on evidence-supported ambiguity.

Poem

A blue door waits at the end of the alley,
paint peeling like old maps in the rain.
No house holds it. No key turns near it.
Still, someone has polished the brass knob clean.
I stand with my notebook under my coat,
listening to water write on the stones.
The door does not open. The door does not close.
It only shines where my hand might go.

Guided Practice Questions

1. Which detail best supports the interpretation that the door represents uncertainty? A. "No house holds it. No key turns near it." B. "brass knob clean" C. "my notebook" D. "blue door"

2. Which detail best supports the interpretation that the door represents opportunity? A. "No key turns near it" B. "where my hand might go" C. "old maps" D. "water write"

3. Why is the poem ambiguous? A. It has no images. B. It has no speaker. C. The door can reasonably suggest both opportunity and uncertainty. D. Every word has only one meaning.

4. Which response is strongest? A. The poem can mean anything. B. The door is just a door and nothing else. C. The poem is wrong. D. The door may represent a choice because the speaker notices where a hand might go.

5. What does "water write on the stones" suggest? A. The rain creates a reflective, thoughtful mood. B. The speaker is underwater. C. Stones can literally write letters. D. The notebook is ruined.
