P05 — Independent Work

Directions

Read “Two Shores.” Answer all 15 questions with evidence.

Two Shores

At low tide, the river leaves a silver road
between the town I know and fields unnamed.
My mother calls it mud, says wait for rain;
my brother calls it passage, dares my feet.

I place one shoe where water used to be.
The earth remembers currents underneath.
A heron lifts—departure or return?—
and drops one feather at the border grass.

By dusk the road is river once again.
I stand on neither shore inside my dream,
holding a feather dry as an old promise,
hearing two homes answer to my name.

Questions

  1. Describe the literal situation.
  2. Who is the speaker?
  3. Identify three ambiguous images.
  4. What may the silver road symbolize?
  5. Give a second interpretation.
  6. Compare the mother’s and brother’s views.
  7. Analyze “earth remembers.”
  8. Explain the heron question.
  9. What may the feather symbolize?
  10. How does the returning river affect meaning?
  11. Interpret “neither shore.”
  12. Explain “two homes.”
  13. State one theme.
  14. State a different supported theme.
  15. Write a paragraph explaining how ambiguity creates multiple layers of meaning using three details.
