P06 — Checkpoint

Directions

Read “The Unsent Letter.” Answer all 10 questions separately.

The Unsent Letter

Your letter sleeps beneath my lamp,
sealed, addressed, but never sent.
At dawn I turn it toward the window;
the paper brightens, words stay dark.
I could call silence mercy.
I could call it fear.
The envelope waits between those names,
a small white boat without a shore.

Questions

  1. Describe the literal situation.
  2. Analyze “letter sleeps.”
  3. Why is bright paper/dark words important?
  4. Explain the two meanings of silence.
  5. What does the envelope symbolize?
  6. Analyze the boat metaphor.
  7. Give one interpretation of the speaker’s choice.
  8. Give a different supported interpretation.
  9. Explain how ambiguity affects the mood.
  10. Write a paragraph comparing both interpretations with evidence.
