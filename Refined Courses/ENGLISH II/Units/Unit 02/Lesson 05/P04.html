P04 — Worked Example

Poem: “The Key”

I found a key beneath the winter tree—
too warm for snow, too small for any gate.
I carried it home inside my sleeve.
At midnight, every locked room seemed awake.
By morning, the key had left no mark,
but one door stood open in my mind.

Step-by-Step Analysis

  1. Literal situation: the speaker finds and carries a mysterious key.
  2. Interpretation one: the key symbolizes opportunity; the open mental door suggests discovery.
  3. Interpretation two: the key symbolizes memory or insight; it opens no physical lock but changes thought.
  4. Evidence: unusual warmth makes the key feel alive; “every locked room” suggests possibilities; “in my mind” moves the meaning inward.
  5. The ambiguity matters because the poem asks whether change comes from an external opportunity or an internal realization.

Modeled response: The key can represent either a new opportunity or a recovered insight. Its warmth and the imagined locked rooms support possibility, while the final mental door supports an inward realization. The poem leaves both readings open to show that discovery can begin outside us yet become meaningful within us.

Second Worked Example: “The Unopened Window”

“All winter the window stayed closed / though spring kept leaving green notes on the sill.”

  1. Literal layer: A window remains shut as seasons change.
  2. Figurative layer: The closed window may represent emotional withdrawal.
  3. Personification: Spring “leaving notes” suggests repeated invitations to reconnect.
  4. Ambiguity: The poem never explains whether the speaker is grieving, afraid, or unwilling to change.
  5. Effect: The uncertainty invites readers to infer the emotional barrier from the contrast between the closed window and returning spring.

Modeled response: The window can symbolize the speaker’s emotional isolation, while spring’s “green notes” represent opportunities for renewal. Because the poem does not name the cause of the isolation, readers must use imagery and seasonal contrast to construct a supported interpretation.
