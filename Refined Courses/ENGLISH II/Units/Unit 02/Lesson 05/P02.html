Vocabulary and Definitions

Notebook Task: Record every term and its complete definition in your notebook.
TermDefinition
poetryA literary genre that uses carefully chosen language, sound, rhythm, imagery, and structure to express ideas and experiences.
ambiguityThe quality of allowing more than one reasonable meaning or interpretation.
speakerThe voice that communicates the poem; the speaker is not automatically the poet.
imageryLanguage that appeals to the senses and helps readers imagine an experience, scene, or emotion.
symbolA person, object, image, place, or event that represents an idea beyond its literal meaning.
connotationThe feelings, associations, or ideas connected to a word beyond its dictionary definition.
denotationThe direct, literal dictionary meaning of a word.
interpretationA reasoned explanation of a poem’s meaning supported by specific textual evidence.
layered meaningMeaning created when literal details, figurative language, symbols, sound, and structure communicate several connected ideas.
