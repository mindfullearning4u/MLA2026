# ENGLISH II Course Overview

---

## Course Overview

English II develops students' ability to analyze increasingly complex literary and informational texts, evaluate how authors develop ideas across texts, and produce clear, well-structured writing for a variety of purposes and audiences. Students examine themes across genres and cultures, analyze author’s craft and rhetorical strategies, evaluate arguments, conduct research, refine writing through revision, and communicate ideas effectively in oral and digital formats.

The course is organized around competency-based progression. Students move through the course by demonstrating mastery of required instructional components, with emphasis on close reading of complex texts, comparative analysis, evidence-based writing, academic discussion, research, revision, and presentation.

Instruction and assessment are aligned to Florida B.E.S.T. ELA Grade 10 Standards with a strong emphasis on analysis, evaluation, synthesis, and communication. Students engage in literary and informational analysis, argument writing, expository writing, research, vocabulary development, revision, and synthesis across texts. Students are expected to demonstrate advanced writing fluency, deeper textual interpretation, and independent reasoning supported by textual evidence.

---

## Course Structure

| Unit | Unit Title | Primary Focus |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Unit 01 | Advanced Foundations of Reading and Writing | Complex text analysis, annotation, evidence-based response, vocabulary, and conventions |
| Unit 02 | Comparative Literary Analysis and Theme | Cross-text analysis, theme comparison, author’s craft, perspective, poetry, adaptation, and narrative writing |
| Unit 03 | Informational Analysis, Rhetoric, and Argument | Historical and contemporary speeches and essays, argument evaluation, rhetorical analysis, and evidence-based writing |
| Unit 04 | Research, Synthesis, and Expository Writing | Multi-source research, synthesis, structured writing, citation, and publication-ready writing |
| Unit 05 | Language, Style, and Advanced Revision | Style, tone, precision, vocabulary, audience awareness, and purposeful revision |
| Unit 06 | Synthesis, Presentation, and Independent Application | Independent synthesis, oral presentation, digital presentation, multimedia communication, and audience-focused communication |

---

## Standards Progression

ELA standards develop through a spiral progression across the course. Each benchmark is assigned a primary unit for focused instruction and assessment, but reading, writing, communication, and vocabulary skills reappear as supporting skills in later units to deepen understanding and complexity.

The course progression emphasizes increasing text complexity, deeper analysis, stronger evaluation of ideas, and more independent writing. Students move from structured responses to comparative analysis, argument evaluation, research-based writing, revision for audience, and independent synthesis and presentation.

---

## Mastery Model

Students are expected to demonstrate a minimum of 80% mastery across all required instructional components, including Notebook Evidence, Checkpoints, Quizzes, and the Unit Assessment.

Students are not advanced to the next unit until mastery is achieved. Mastery is determined through consistent performance across multiple measures and is not based on a single assessment.

The Unit Assessment is required for all students and contributes to the overall mastery determination.

Students participating in the Accelerated Path must meet additional expectations. Please refer to the official Accelerated Path Overview document for full eligibility, requirements, and continuation criteria.
