# English II Lessons 1-7 Structure and Content Alignment Report

## Executive Summary

This audit reviewed English II Units 1-6, Lessons 1-7 only, against the certified English II curriculum foundation:

- Certified Standards Inventory
- Certified Crosswalk
- Certified Unit-Level Mapping
- Certified Lesson-Level Mapping

The audit included lesson standards, lesson objectives, instructional content, examples, notebook tasks, guided practice, independent work, checkpoint tasks, progression, HTML structure, JSON metadata, and Moodle-ready GIFT assessment structure.

One content-alignment issue was identified and corrected:

- Unit 1 Lesson 7 was mapped as "Integrated close reading, evidence, and academic voice," but the produced lesson emphasized close reading, vocabulary, and evidence without explicitly teaching or assessing academic voice.

After correction and revalidation, English II Lessons 1-7 are aligned to the certified mapping and approved for continued implementation review.
