Course Overview

English 2

MLA Course Code: ENG2
Credit: 1.0 High School English Language Arts Credit, as assigned
Course Type: Online, asynchronous, mastery-based
Delivery: Moodle with Teacher of Record support

Course Purpose

English 2 develops close reading, literary analysis, rhetoric, research, revision, academic language, writing, and oral/digital communication. You will analyze substantial texts and source packets, study multiple models, apply skills independently, and revise using evidence and feedback.

Course Organization

6 Units • 48 Lessons
Each unit contains eight lessons. Lessons 1–7 teach and assess lesson-specific skills. Lesson 8 synthesizes the full unit. Every unit begins with an ungraded 10-question Pretest and ends with a graded Unit Assessment.
  1. Unit 1 — Foundations of Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice
  2. Unit 2 — Literary Analysis, Perspective, and Narrative Craft
  3. Unit 3 — Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument Development
  4. Unit 4 — Research, Source Synthesis, and Expository Writing
  5. Unit 5 — Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision
  6. Unit 6 — Synthesis, Presentation, and Digital Communication

P06 CheckpointA different reading or scenario and an independent mastery task submitted separately.

Required Lesson Activities

  • Notebook Evidence Submission: P02 vocabulary and definitions plus completed P05 Independent Work.
  • Checkpoint Submission: only the completed P06 responses.
  • Lesson Quiz: Lessons 1–7 use five random questions from a 25-question lesson-specific bank, one attempt, and 80% required.
  • Lesson 8: synthesis lesson with no separate lesson quiz.
  • Unit Assessment: ten random questions from a 40-question unit-specific bank, one attempt, and 80% required.

Mastery and Support

The minimum mastery threshold is 80%. Notebook and Checkpoint work below 80% must be corrected using TOR feedback and/or additional TOR guidance before resubmission.
