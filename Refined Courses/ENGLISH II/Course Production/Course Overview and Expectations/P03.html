📘 MINDFUL LEARNING ACADEMY | ENGLISH 2 | COURSE ORIENTATION

Expectations and Policies

Student Responsibilities

  • Follow the official Moodle sequence and read every direction.
  • Complete and submit original work.
  • Use specific textual or source evidence and explain your reasoning.
  • Review TOR feedback and correct work when required.
  • Communicate promptly and respectfully.
  • Protect assessment integrity and account security.

Required Submission Evidence

Notebook Evidence Submission: Submit only all P02 vocabulary terms with definitions and all completed P05 Independent Work responses.

Checkpoint Submission: Submit only all completed P06 Checkpoint responses.

Do not combine the two submissions or add unrelated notes. Number answers and cite the passage or source letter when requested.

Identification and File Requirements

At the top of each submission include:

  • Student Name
  • Course Name
  • Unit and Lesson

Accepted formats: PDF (preferred), Word (.doc or .docx), or readable JPG/JPEG/PNG images. Download Google Docs as PDF or Word files. Links are not accepted. Number multiple pages and submit them together in order.

Grading, Feedback, and 80% Mastery

Student-visible rubrics explain the grading criteria. Lesson-specific answer guidance is available only to the TOR. Notebook and Checkpoint submissions require at least 80%. If you do not earn 80%, correct your mistakes using TOR feedback and/or meet with your TOR for extra help and guidance, then resubmit.

Academic and Assessment Integrity

Copied work, shared assessment content, unauthorized answer sites, fabricated evidence or sources, plagiarism, and unapproved AI-generated submissions are prohibited. Complete Pretests, quizzes, Checkpoints, and Unit Assessments independently. You may be asked to explain your reasoning or work process.

Evidence and Communication Quality

  • Identify the text, source, speaker, or context accurately.
  • Select relevant evidence and explain how it supports the response.
  • Organize ideas logically for the assigned purpose and audience.
  • Use appropriate attribution, academic vocabulary, and standard English.
  • Revise content, clarity, and presentation before submitting.

Technical Requirements and Accessibility

You need reliable internet, a current browser, Moodle access, and a way to create readable documents or images. Approved accommodations apply throughout the course. Contact your TOR if an accommodation or required content is missing.

🧑‍🏫 TOR Help
Identify English 2, the unit, lesson, and page; explain what you attempted; and ask a precise question. For upload problems, include the device, file type, and error message.
