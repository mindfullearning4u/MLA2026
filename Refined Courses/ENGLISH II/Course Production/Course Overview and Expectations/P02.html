📘 MINDFUL LEARNING ACADEMY | ENGLISH 2 | COURSE ORIENTATION

How This Course Works

Mastery Cycle
Preview → Learn → Study a Worked Example → Apply Independently → Demonstrate Mastery → Review Feedback → Correct and Resubmit when required.

Unit Workflow

  1. Complete the ungraded 10-question Unit Pretest honestly and independently.
  2. Complete Lessons 1–8 in Moodle order.
  3. For Lessons 1–7, complete the lesson, two submissions, and lesson quiz.
  4. For Lesson 8, complete the synthesis lesson and two submissions; there is no separate Lesson 8 quiz.
  5. Review TOR feedback and correct work below 80%.
  6. Complete the Unit Assessment.

Lesson Workflow

  1. P01 — Lesson Overview: read the lesson title, goals, lesson-specific standards, workflow, mastery expectations, and why the learning matters.
  2. P02 — Vocabulary and Definitions: record every term and complete definition in your notebook. This is the only page labeled Notebook Task.
  3. P03 — Lesson: study the direct instruction, steps, multiple examples, common mistakes, and TOR Help.
  4. P04 — Worked Example: study the substantial model or passage and follow each explanation step.
  5. P05 — Independent Work: read the assigned passage or source packet and answer every question.
  6. P06 — Checkpoint: complete the different passage/scenario and mastery questions independently.

Two Separate Submissions

Notebook Evidence Submission
Submit only: (1) every P02 vocabulary term and definition and (2) all completed P05 Independent Work responses.

Checkpoint Submission
Submit only the completed P06 Checkpoint responses. Do not combine Checkpoint work with Notebook Evidence.

Submission Identification and Formats

At the top of every submission include your Student Name, Course Name, and Unit and Lesson. Submit PDF (preferred), Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx), or readable JPG/JPEG/PNG images. Download Google Docs as PDF or Word files. Links are not accepted.

Assessments

  • Lesson quizzes: Lessons 1–7 only; five random lesson-specific questions, one attempt, 80% required.
  • Unit Assessment: ten random unit-specific questions, one attempt, 80% required.
  • Any passage needed to answer an assessment question appears inside that question.
80% Mastery Requirement
Notebook Evidence and Checkpoint work below 80% must be corrected using TOR feedback and/or a TOR support meeting before resubmission.
