# Phase 3A-B-1: English II Unit-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English II unit-level mapping using the English II Crosswalk Draft and Mapping Framework. It preserves the English I course-production structure while increasing Grade 10 rigor and maintaining vertical alignment.

The mapping preserves:

- 6 units
- 8 lessons per unit
- Lessons 1-7 with Lesson Quiz
- Lesson 8 = Putting It All Together
- Lesson 8 with Unit Assessment
- No new primary standards introduced in Lesson 8

## Workload Summary

| Unit | Workload Finding | Mapping Control Applied |
|---|---|---|
| Unit 1 | Manageable foundation load | Focuses on paraphrase, vocabulary, evidence, academic voice, and conventions without early overload. |
| Unit 2 | High workload risk | Protects coming-of-age/conflicting perspectives, figurative language, poetic ambiguity, adaptation, and narrative pacing with discrete lesson placements. |
| Unit 3 | High workload risk | Sequences informational reading and rhetoric before opposing arguments and argument writing. |
| Unit 4 | Moderate-high workload risk | Treats research, synthesis, expository writing, and publication as one connected process. |
| Unit 5 | Manageable with integration risk | Uses authentic writing and reading contexts for language, vocabulary, style, and revision. |
| Unit 6 | Manageable if earlier reinforcement occurs | Uses prior communication reinforcement so presentation is synthesis rather than first exposure. |
