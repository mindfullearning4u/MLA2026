# Phase 3A-B-2: English II Lesson-Level Mapping

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English II lesson-level mapping using the English II Crosswalk Draft, Unit-Level Mapping, Mapping Framework, and completed English I production structure.

This is mapping only. It does not create lesson objectives, lesson content, activities, assessments, HTML files, quizzes, GIFT files, or a Course Overview.

The mapping preserves the approved course structure:

- 6 units
- 8 lessons per unit
- Lessons 1-7 contain Lesson Quiz
- Lesson 8 = Putting It All Together
- Lesson 8 contains Unit Assessment

## Spiral Reinforcement Summary

| Spiral Skill | Early Course Use | Mid-Course Use | Late Course Use | Increasing Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Language | Unit 1 short-response clarity | Units 2-4 conventions in analysis, argument, and research writing | Units 5-6 style and presentation polish | Moves from clarity to complex sentence control and publication polish. |
| Vocabulary | Unit 1 academic vocabulary | Units 2-4 literary, rhetorical, argument, and research vocabulary | Units 5-6 derivation, connotation, precision, and audience-specific language | Moves from access vocabulary to independent vocabulary reasoning. |
| Revision | Unit 1 response clarity | Units 2-4 narrative, argument, expository, and publication revision support | Units 5-6 focused revision and communication polish | Moves from simple clarity checks to cohesion, style, and audience control. |
| Paraphrase | Unit 1 direct paraphrase instruction | Units 2-4 paraphrase for literary, informational, and research work | Units 5-6 paraphrase for synthesis and presentation support | Moves from accurate restatement to source synthesis. |
| Evidence | Unit 1 evidence foundations | Units 2-4 evidence for analysis, argument, source evaluation, and research | Unit 6 credible evidence for presentation | Moves from selecting evidence to evaluating and presenting evidence. |
| Communication | Unit 1 evidence-based explanation | Units 2-5 interpretation, claims/evidence discussion, collaboration, and feedback | Unit 6 oral and digital communication | Moves from explanation to coherent presentation with audience awareness. |
