# Phase 2A-B: English II Crosswalk Draft

## Executive Summary

This document creates the English II Crosswalk Draft using the English II MLA Standard Inventory as the source of record. MLA standard numbers, standard titles, and Florida benchmark codes are preserved exactly as assigned in the inventory.

This draft assigns each MLA English II standard to an approved benchmark cluster, one primary unit, spiral reinforcement units, mastery/support status, and readiness-alignment tags. It preserves the completed English I production framework while increasing rigor for Grade 10 expectations.

## Potential Concerns

- English II must not duplicate English I at the same rigor level.
- Unit 2 and Unit 3 remain the highest-density units and require protected sequencing.
- Unit 4 must treat research, source synthesis, expository writing, and collaborative publication as one connected process.
- Unit 6 must synthesize previously taught skills rather than introduce communication for the first time.
