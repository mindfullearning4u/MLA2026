# English II — Course Overview

**MLA Course Code:** ENG2  
**Credit:** 1.0 High School English Language Arts Credit  
**Course Type:** Online, asynchronous, mastery-based  
**Delivery:** 100% online through Moodle  
**Teacher Support:** Teacher of Record (TOR)

## Course Description

English II strengthens Grade 10 reading, writing, research, language, vocabulary, and communication. Students analyze complex literary and informational texts, evaluate rhetoric and argument, write for varied purposes, synthesize sources, revise for audience, and communicate through oral and digital presentations.

## Course Learning Outcomes

Students will learn to:

- read closely, paraphrase accurately, and support analysis with evidence;
- analyze literary craft, perspective, theme, informational structure, rhetoric, and argument;
- write narrative, argument, and research-based expository texts;
- use language and vocabulary precisely and revise for audience and purpose;
- present findings, reasoning, and evidence orally and digitally.

## Course Organization

This course contains **6 units and 48 lessons**. Each unit begins with an ungraded Unit Pretest. Lessons 1–7 include a Lesson Quiz. Lesson 8 is a synthesis lesson followed by the Unit Assessment.

1. Foundations of Close Reading, Evidence, and Academic Voice
2. Literary Analysis, Perspective, and Narrative Craft
3. Informational Text, Rhetoric, and Argument Development
4. Research, Source Synthesis, and Expository Writing
5. Language, Vocabulary, Style, and Revision
6. Synthesis, Presentation, and Digital Communication

## Mastery and Support

The minimum mastery threshold is **80%**. Students review TOR feedback and complete required revision, remediation, or approved reassessment before progressing.
