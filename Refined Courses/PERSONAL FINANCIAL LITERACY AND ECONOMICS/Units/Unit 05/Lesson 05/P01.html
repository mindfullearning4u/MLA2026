P01 Lesson Overview

Risk Management and Emergency Planning

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.RSK.1: Explain risk management, emergency planning, insurance, deductibles, premiums, coverage limits, and claims.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how risk management and emergency planning works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the risk response matrix, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches risk protection and consumer safety using only the assigned standard.
