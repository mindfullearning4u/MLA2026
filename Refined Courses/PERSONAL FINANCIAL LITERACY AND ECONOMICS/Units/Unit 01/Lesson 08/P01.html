P01 Lesson Overview

Synthesis: Economics Behind Financial Decisions

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.ECO.1: Apply scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, incentives, and tradeoffs to personal, household, business, and government decisions. MLA.PFLE.ECO.2: Analyze supply, demand, price, competition, and market signals in product, labor, and financial markets. MLA.PFLE.ECO.3: Explain economic systems, productive resources, entrepreneurship, specialization, and voluntary exchange. MLA.PFLE.ECO.4: Use economic indicators, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, and business-cycle evidence to explain personal financial decisions.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how synthesis: economics behind financial decisions works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the economic decision checklist, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches unit synthesis across all mapped financial standards using only the assigned standard. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.PFLE.ECO.1, MLA.PFLE.ECO.2, MLA.PFLE.ECO.3, MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.
