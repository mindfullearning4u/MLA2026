Worked Example 3

economic decision checklist

Evidence PointStudent Look-For
Standard 1: MLA.PFLE.ECO.1Apply scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, incentives, and tradeoffs to personal, household, business, and government decisions.
Standard 2: MLA.PFLE.ECO.2Analyze supply, demand, price, competition, and market signals in product, labor, and financial markets.
Standard 3: MLA.PFLE.ECO.3Explain economic systems, productive resources, entrepreneurship, specialization, and voluntary exchange.
Standard 4: MLA.PFLE.ECO.4Use economic indicators, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, and business-cycle evidence to explain personal financial decisions.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
