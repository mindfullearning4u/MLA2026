P01 Lesson Overview

Scarcity, Choice, and Opportunity Cost

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.ECO.1: Apply scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, incentives, and tradeoffs to personal, household, business, and government decisions.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how scarcity, choice, and opportunity cost works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the opportunity cost decision table, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches scarcity and opportunity cost reasoning using only the assigned standard.
