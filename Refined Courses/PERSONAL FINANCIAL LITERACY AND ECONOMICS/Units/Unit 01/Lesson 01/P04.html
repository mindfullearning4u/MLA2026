Worked Example 1

Prompt: A student has limited money and time and must choose one option while giving up another.

Step 1: Name scarcity. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Identify the limited resource. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Scarcity creates the need to choose. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.ECO.1.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
