P01 Lesson Overview

Entrepreneurship, Specialization, and Voluntary Exchange

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.ECO.3: Explain economic systems, productive resources, entrepreneurship, specialization, and voluntary exchange.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how entrepreneurship, specialization, and voluntary exchange works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the specialization exchange flowchart, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches evidence-based financial decision-making using only the assigned standard.
