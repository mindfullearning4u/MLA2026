Worked Example 1

Prompt: A unit review asks students to combine MLA.PFLE.INC.1, MLA.PFLE.INC.2, MLA.PFLE.INC.3, MLA.PFLE.INC.4 in one realistic financial decision.

Step 1: Identify standard. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Choose which mapped standard is being tested. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Synthesis requires standard-by-standard evidence. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A unit review asks students to combine MLA.PFLE.INC.1, MLA.PFLE.INC.2, MLA.PFLE.INC.3, MLA.PFLE.INC.4 in one realistic financial decision.

Step 1: Make decision. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: State the financially responsible action. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: A final decision must be supported by evidence. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.1.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
