Worked Example 1

Prompt: A student compares two career pathways with training time, cost, wages, and benefits.

Step 1: Compare total path. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Look beyond starting pay. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Training costs and benefits matter. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.1.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
