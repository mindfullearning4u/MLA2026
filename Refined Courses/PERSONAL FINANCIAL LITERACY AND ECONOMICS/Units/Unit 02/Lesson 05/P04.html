P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1

Prompt: A student compares financial choices and must choose the decision best supported by the evidence.

Step 1: Identify the choice. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Name what decision is being made. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: A financial answer starts with the decision. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.3.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A student compares financial choices and must choose the decision best supported by the evidence.

Step 1: Use numbers first. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Read the table before choosing. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Evidence prevents guessing. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.3.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A student compares financial choices and must choose the decision best supported by the evidence.

Step 1: Explain the tradeoff. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: State the cost, benefit, and risk. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: A strong answer explains why the choice fits the standard. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.INC.3.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.

Student Clarity and Mastery Check

Lesson focus: Benefits, Workplace Compensation, and Total Pay. The goal is to make evidence-based financial and economic decisions using costs, benefits, risk, scarcity, incentives, and tradeoffs while staying inside MLA.PFLE.INC.3.
