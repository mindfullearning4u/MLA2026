Worked Example 1

Prompt: A monthly budget table shows income, expenses, savings, and a remaining balance.

Step 1: Read income. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Start with money available. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: A budget begins with income. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.BUD.1.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A monthly budget table shows income, expenses, savings, and a remaining balance.

Step 1: Classify expenses. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Separate fixed, variable, and periodic. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Correct categories prevent surprises. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.BUD.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A monthly budget table shows income, expenses, savings, and a remaining balance.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
