P01 Lesson Overview

Synthesis: Budget, Banking, Saving, and Spending

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.BUD.1: Create and evaluate budgets using income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, periodic expenses, savings, and emergency planning. MLA.PFLE.BUD.2: Explain checking accounts, savings accounts, digital payments, bank statements, fees, reconciliation, and account safety. MLA.PFLE.BUD.3: Apply saving strategies, emergency funds, short-term goals, long-term goals, compound growth, and inflation protection. MLA.PFLE.BUD.4: Evaluate consumer spending decisions using needs, wants, comparison shopping, opportunity cost, and total cost.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how synthesis: budget, banking, saving, and spending works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the money management checklist, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches unit synthesis across all mapped financial standards using only the assigned standard. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.PFLE.BUD.1, MLA.PFLE.BUD.2, MLA.PFLE.BUD.3, MLA.PFLE.BUD.4.
