P01 Lesson Overview

Entrepreneurship and Personal Financial Planning

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.INC.4: Create income plans that connect career goals, employability skills, taxes, and long-term financial stability. MLA.PFLE.CAP.1: Apply personal finance and economics evidence to major life decisions such as housing, transportation, education, employment, taxes, and entrepreneurship.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how entrepreneurship and personal financial planning works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the entrepreneurship finance plan, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches evidence-based financial decision-making using only the assigned standard. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.PFLE.INC.4, MLA.PFLE.CAP.1.
