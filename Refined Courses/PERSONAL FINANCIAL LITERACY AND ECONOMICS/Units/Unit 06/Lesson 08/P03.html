P03 Notebook Task - Part 2

Scenario: A unit review asks students to combine MLA.PFLE.CAP.1, MLA.PFLE.CAP.2, MLA.PFLE.ECO.4 in one realistic financial decision.

  1. Underline the money choice, economic condition, risk, or consumer problem.
  2. Circle the concept from this lesson: MLA.PFLE.CAP.1, MLA.PFLE.CAP.2, MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.
  3. Use the embedded capstone financial plan checklist to identify the evidence.
  4. Match the evidence to MLA.PFLE.CAP.1, MLA.PFLE.CAP.2, MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.
  5. Write one sentence explaining the financially responsible decision.

capstone financial plan checklist

Evidence PointStudent Look-For
Standard 1: MLA.PFLE.CAP.1Apply personal finance and economics evidence to major life decisions such as housing, transportation, education, employment, taxes, and entrepreneurship.
Standard 2: MLA.PFLE.CAP.2Create an evidence-based financial plan using goals, budget, saving, credit, investing, insurance, consumer protection, and economic conditions.
Standard 3: MLA.PFLE.ECO.4Use economic indicators, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, and business-cycle evidence to explain personal financial decisions.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

The strongest answer starts with evidence, checks the cost, benefit, risk, or tradeoff, and then connects that evidence to the assigned standard. This prevents guessing and keeps the answer inside the lesson scope.
