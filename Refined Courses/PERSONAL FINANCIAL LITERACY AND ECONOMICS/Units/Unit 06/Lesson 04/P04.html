P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1

Prompt: A paycheck table shows gross income, deductions, taxes, and take-home pay.

Step 1: Start with gross. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Find total earnings before deductions. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Gross pay is not take-home pay. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A paycheck table shows gross income, deductions, taxes, and take-home pay.

Step 1: Subtract deductions. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Identify required and optional deductions. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Each deduction changes net pay. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A paycheck table shows gross income, deductions, taxes, and take-home pay.

Step 1: Use net pay. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Budget with take-home income. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Budgets must use realistic income. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.ECO.4.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
