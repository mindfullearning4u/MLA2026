P01 Lesson Overview

Taxes, Public Goods, Inflation, and Household Budgets

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.ECO.4: Use economic indicators, inflation, unemployment, interest rates, and business-cycle evidence to explain personal financial decisions. MLA.PFLE.CAP.1: Apply personal finance and economics evidence to major life decisions such as housing, transportation, education, employment, taxes, and entrepreneurship.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how taxes, public goods, inflation, and household budgets works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the household economy impact chart, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches net income and tax reasoning using only the assigned standard. This synthesis must use evidence from all mapped standards: MLA.PFLE.ECO.4, MLA.PFLE.CAP.1.
