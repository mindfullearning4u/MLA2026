P04 Worked Example

Worked Example 1

Prompt: A total-cost table compares upfront cost, monthly cost, fees, insurance, maintenance, and opportunity cost.

Step 1: Find all costs. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Include upfront and ongoing costs. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Sticker price is incomplete. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.CAP.1.

Worked Example 2

Prompt: A total-cost table compares upfront cost, monthly cost, fees, insurance, maintenance, and opportunity cost.

Step 1: Check budget. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Use net income and priorities. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Affordability is evidence-based. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.CAP.1.

Worked Example 3

Prompt: A total-cost table compares upfront cost, monthly cost, fees, insurance, maintenance, and opportunity cost.

Step 1: Explain tradeoff. This identifies the decision before solving.

Step 2: Name what is gained and given up. This ties the answer to the table, chart, or scenario evidence.

Step 3: Major purchases affect future choices. This is the teachable conclusion for MLA.PFLE.CAP.1.

Evidence PointStudent Look-For

AffordabilityWhether the choice fits the budget and goals.

Common Mistake

Correct: using the financial evidence to choose the best-supported decision.

Incorrect: choosing a familiar money word without checking the evidence.

Teachable Explanation

Each example uses the same mastery path: define the decision, read the evidence, compare cost and benefit, account for risk, and explain the final choice.
