P01 Lesson Overview

Interest, APR, Fees, and Loan Terms

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.CRD.2: Calculate and compare borrowing costs using interest, APR, fees, minimum payments, loan terms, and total repayment.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how interest, apr, fees, and loan terms works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the borrowing cost table, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches borrowing cost and debt management using only the assigned standard.
