P01 Lesson Overview

Credit Reports and Credit Scores

Standards Covered in This Lesson: MLA.PFLE.CRD.1: Explain credit reports, credit scores, creditworthiness, credit rights, and actions that build or damage credit.

What You Will Learn: You will learn how credit reports and credit scores works and how to use evidence to make a financially responsible decision.

What You Will Do: Study the credit factor chart, follow the step-by-step lesson, complete guided practice, and explain your reasoning.

How You Will Show Mastery: You will connect evidence to the mapped standard with at least 80% mastery.

Student-Friendly Standard Connection: This lesson teaches creditworthiness using only the assigned standard.
