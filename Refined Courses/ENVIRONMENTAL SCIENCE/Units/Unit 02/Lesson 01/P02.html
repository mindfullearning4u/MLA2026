Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Ecosystem Change and Succession. The standard is MLA.ENV.ECO.01, so the work must stay inside this lesson: ecosystem change and succession evidence.

A strong environmental science answer does more than state an opinion. It reads the required map, graph, table, model, source, or case evidence; names the pattern; explains the environmental relationship; and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the disturbance or starting condition.
  2. Place pioneer, intermediate, and later communities in order.
  3. Connect abiotic changes to biotic changes.
  4. Use the model to explain ecosystem recovery or shift.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: thinking succession is random replacement with no pattern

Correct Thinking

Correct: succession follows evidence-based patterns as communities change after disturbance or over time
