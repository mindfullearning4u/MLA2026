Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Invasive Species and Biodiversity Loss. The standard is MLA.ENV.ECO.05, so the work must stay inside this lesson: biodiversity change, extinction risk, and invasive species impact.

A strong environmental science answer does more than state an opinion. It reads the required map, graph, table, model, source, or case evidence; names the pattern; explains the environmental relationship; and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the species or biodiversity measure.
  2. Compare before-and-after evidence.
  3. Explain the ecological impact.
  4. Connect the impact to stability, extinction, or management.

Common Confusion to Avoid

Incorrect: counting only the number of organisms instead of species variety and ecosystem role

Correct Thinking

Correct: biodiversity includes variety, balance, and ecosystem function, not just total organism count
