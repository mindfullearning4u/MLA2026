Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Births, Deaths, Immigration, and Emigration. The standard is MLA.ENV.POP.02, so the work must stay inside this lesson: population structure, population change, and carrying capacity.

A strong environmental science answer does more than state an opinion. It reads the required map, graph, table, model, source, or case evidence; names the pattern; explains the environmental relationship; and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Read the population graph or table.
  2. Identify the factor changing the population.
  3. Connect the factor to carrying capacity or resource use.
  4. State whether the evidence shows growth, decline, stability, or overshoot.
