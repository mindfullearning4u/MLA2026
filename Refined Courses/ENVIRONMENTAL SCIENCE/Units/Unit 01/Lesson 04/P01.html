Science Safety and Resource Note

This Environmental Science lesson uses classroom-safe models, maps, graphs, public data, source excerpts, or case evidence. Do not collect personal health information, enter unsafe outdoor areas, handle unknown substances, or conduct unsupervised field sampling. Use only the provided or linked student-safe evidence.
