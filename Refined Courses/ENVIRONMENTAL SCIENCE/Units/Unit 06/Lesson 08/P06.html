Instructions

Complete all three parts using only this lesson standard, the required evidence source, and your notebook evidence.

Part A

Define the environmental system, process, impact, or decision in your own words.

Part B

Use the visual, map, table, graph, model, source, or case to explain how the evidence supports the standard.

Part C

Write one claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph. Include a claim, two evidence details, and reasoning that connects the evidence to the environmental system, impact, tradeoff, or decision.
