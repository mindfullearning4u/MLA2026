Teacher-Style Explanation

This lesson focuses on Technology and Environmental Quality. The standard is MLA.ENV.PCH.05, so the work must stay inside this lesson: climate, pollution, waste, technology, and environmental quality evidence.

A strong environmental science answer does more than state an opinion. It reads the required map, graph, table, model, source, or case evidence; names the pattern; explains the environmental relationship; and limits the conclusion to what the evidence can support.

  1. Identify the pollutant, climate factor, technology, or waste pathway.
  2. Read the graph, map, or case data.
  3. Connect source, pathway, and effect.
  4. Separate evidence-based conclusion from unsupported claim.

Correct Thinking

Correct: environmental trends require data patterns, source reliability, and careful limits on what the evidence proves
