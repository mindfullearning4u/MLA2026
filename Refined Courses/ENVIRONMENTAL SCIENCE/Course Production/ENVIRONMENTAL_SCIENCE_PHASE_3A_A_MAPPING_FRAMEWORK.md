# Environmental Science Phase 3A-A: Mapping Framework

## Mapping Rules

1. Every MLA Environmental Science standard must appear in the crosswalk, unit-level mapping, and lesson-level mapping.
2. A standard listed as a primary unit standard must be taught as a primary standard in at least one of Lessons 1-7 for that unit.
3. Support standards remain support unless explicitly listed as primary.
4. Lesson 8 is always titled `Putting It All Together`.
5. Lesson 8 synthesizes only standards already taught in Lessons 1-7.
6. Lesson 8 includes Unit Assessment evidence.
7. No lesson, assessment, HTML, JSON, GIFT, or LMS content is created by this architecture package.
