# ENV Text and Data Source Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix provides production guidance for future Environmental Science lesson development. It identifies recommended text, data, model, case study, and monitoring source categories by unit. It does not create lesson content, assessment content, HTML content, JSON content, legacy assessment-bank content, or LMS pages.

## Source Category Matrix

| Unit | Unit Title | Recommended Source Categories | Production Guidance |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Environmental Science Foundations and Scientific Inquiry | Environmental monitoring datasets; inquiry scenarios; safety case studies; data tables and graphs | Use sources that support scientific inquiry, safe monitoring practices, source reliability, environmental evidence, graphs, tables, models, and decision-making foundations. |
| 2 | Ecosystems, Energy Flow, and Biodiversity | Ecosystem datasets; biodiversity datasets; food web models; invasive species case studies | Use sources that support ecosystem change, biotic/abiotic systems, organism relationships, energy transfer, trophic levels, biodiversity loss, extinction, and invasive species impacts. |
| 3 | Population Dynamics and Human Impact | Population datasets; carrying capacity models; environmental health data | Use sources that support population structure, limiting factors, carrying capacity, human population size, resource use, environmental quality, and environmental health connections. |
| 4 | Natural Resources and Resource Management | Resource-use datasets; energy-production case studies; land-use maps | Use sources that support renewable and nonrenewable resource tradeoffs, resource production and renewal, energy technologies, sustainable land use, forests, wildlife, water, and fossil fuels. |
| 5 | Pollution, Climate, and Environmental Change | Climate datasets; pollution datasets; Florida environmental case studies; waste-management examples | Use sources that support climate change, ocean-atmosphere-hydrology interactions, Florida environmental impacts, pollution, waste management, technology, and biotechnology impacts. |
| 6 | Sustainability, Environmental Policy, and Future Solutions | Sustainability case studies; environmental policy examples; conservation programs; monitoring reports | Use sources that support environmental monitoring, policy decisions, lifestyle impacts, sustainability, conservation, stewardship, and evidence-based future solutions. |

## Source Controls

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Source authority | Prefer official or authoritative environmental data sources, government agencies, university extensions, peer-reviewed summaries, and CPALMS-aligned resources. |
| Data integrity | Data should include units, dates, location, collection method, scale, and limitations whenever available. |
| Identity control | Sources must preserve Environmental Science identity. Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth/Space Science, Marine Science, or Anatomy and Physiology sources may be used only as CPALMS-required support. |
| Guidance-only boundary | This matrix does not create student-facing lesson content or assessments. |
