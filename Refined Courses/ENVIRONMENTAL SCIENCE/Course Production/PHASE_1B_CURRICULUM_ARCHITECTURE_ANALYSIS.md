# Environmental Science Phase 1B: Curriculum Architecture Analysis

## Source Verification

| Category | Count |
|---|---:|

Embedded/support standards include 6 ELA expectations, 2 ELD expectations, 2 health expectations, and 7 Mathematical Thinking and Reasoning expectations.

## Architecture Purpose

Environmental Science is organized around environmental systems, ecosystems, biodiversity, population dynamics, human impact, natural resources, pollution, climate change, environmental monitoring, policy, sustainability, and solution design.

## Architecture Controls

| Control | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Standards fidelity | Every CPALMS-listed Environmental Science standard is represented in the MLA inventory, crosswalk, unit map, or support alignment. |
| Identity | Environmental Science remains centered on ecosystems, biodiversity, resources, pollution, sustainability, policy, conservation, and decision-making. |
| Lesson coherence | Every primary unit standard is taught in Lessons 1-7 before Lesson 8 synthesis. |
| Lab science | Monitoring, measurement, field/lab safety, data tables, graphs, models, and evidence-based reasoning are embedded across the course. |
| Virtual lab/data | Ecosystem, population, climate, pollution, resource, GIS/map, and policy data experiences are represented. |
| Readiness | Common Core, ACT Science, SAT data/reading, college readiness, workforce readiness, scientific literacy, and sustainability literacy are present. |

## Unit Architecture

| Unit | Title | Primary Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Environmental Science Foundations and Scientific Inquiry | Inquiry, safety, monitoring, evidence, models, and environmental decisions. |
| 2 | Ecosystems, Energy Flow, and Biodiversity | Ecosystems, relationships, energy flow, biodiversity, extinction, and natural selection support. |
| 3 | Population Dynamics and Human Impact | Population dynamics, carrying capacity, human population, resource use, and environmental health. |
| 4 | Natural Resources and Resource Management | Renewable/nonrenewable resources, energy production, land use, availability, and management. |
| 5 | Pollution, Climate, and Environmental Change | Climate conditions, ocean-climate interactions, pollution, waste, technology, and change. |
| 6 | Sustainability, Environmental Policy, and Future Solutions | Monitoring, policy, sustainability, conservation, lifestyles, solutions, and decision-making. |

## Architecture Decision

Environmental Science uses 28 MLA Environmental Science standards across seven domains. This count supports 48 lessons while preserving traceability, teachability, and audit readiness.
