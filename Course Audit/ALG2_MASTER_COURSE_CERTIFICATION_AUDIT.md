# ALG2 Master Course Certification Audit

## 1. Executive Summary

Algebra II was audited using the MLA Master Course Certification Audit for final accreditation, compliance, instructional quality, assessment quality, mastery progression, and production readiness.

Final Certification Determination: **CERTIFIED**

The audit verified all 6 units, 48 lessons, 6 unit pretests, 48 guided practice banks, 42 lesson quiz banks, and 6 unit assessments. The course demonstrates instructional quality, assessment quality, mastery-learning integrity, standards compliance, technical consistency, and accreditation readiness.

## 3. Instructional Quality Review

Instructional Quality Certification: **PASS**

The course provides clear objectives, appropriate learning targets, accurate vocabulary, worked examples, common mistakes, guided practice, independent work, checkpoint submissions, notebook evidence, and mastery assessments. Lesson content is age appropriate and grade-level appropriate for Algebra II.

Verified:

- Lesson objectives are clear.
- Vocabulary is accurate and aligned to lesson purpose.
- Worked examples are accurate and support learning.
- Common mistakes address realistic misconceptions.
- Instructional explanations support independent learning.
- Lessons support mastery through notebook tasks, practice, checkpoint evidence, and assessment.

## 4. Lesson Flow Review

Lesson Flow Audit: **PASS**

The locked MLA lesson shell is consistently implemented:

- P01 Lesson Overview
- P02 Notebook Task Part 1
- P03 Notebook Task Part 2
- P04 Concept Development / Worked Examples
- P05 Guided Practice
- P06 Independent Work + Checkpoint + Notebook Evidence
- P07 Lesson Quiz or Unit Assessment for Lesson 8

Notebook Task Part 1 supports Notebook Task Part 2. Guided Practice supports Independent Work. Independent Work supports Checkpoint and Notebook Evidence. Lesson quizzes and unit assessments are positioned after students receive instructional support and practice.

## 5. Instructional Progression Review

Instructional Progression Audit: **PASS**

## 8. Distractor Quality Review

Distractor Quality Audit: **PASS**

Distractors are plausible and generally reflect common algebraic, graphical, contextual, or interpretive misconceptions. Distractors diagnose issues such as:

- Reversing signs or transformations.
- Confusing input and output values.
- Treating exponential patterns as linear.
- Ignoring restrictions, domains, units, or context.
- Misreading residuals, regression parameters, or model fit.

No duplicate answer choices were found.

## 9. Feedback Quality Review

Feedback Quality Audit: **PASS**

Verified:

- Feedback exists for every answer choice.
- Correct-answer feedback explains reasoning.
- Incorrect-answer feedback identifies the misconception or explains why the choice does not work.
- Feedback functions as a teachable moment and supports mastery.

An automated short-feedback screen flagged brief responses for review, but exact-pattern generic feedback such as "Incorrect," "Wrong," "No," or "Try again" was not found. The reviewed feedback remains diagnostic and instructionally meaningful.
