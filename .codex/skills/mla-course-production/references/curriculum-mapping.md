# Curriculum Mapping

## Source hierarchy

Use the currently approved sources for the course. For Florida physics work, the expected crosswalk sources are Florida B.E.S.T./Florida science standards, CPALMS, applicable Common Core literacy or mathematics standards, SAT expectations, ACT expectations, and the adopted Savvas program. Create MLA standards only after completing the source crosswalk.

Do not force an alignment. Mark a source as supporting, partial, or not applicable when that is the accurate relationship.

## Required production documents

Unless the user approves a different set, maintain these four mapping documents:

1. Mapping framework: governing rules, source hierarchy, alignment method, identifiers, and quality controls.
2. Unit-level mapping: coherent unit sequence, rationale, major standards, outcomes, resources, and assessment intent.
3. Lesson-level mapping: every lesson in order with focus, standards, approved resources, evidence, and assessment role.
4. Lab, visual, simulation, and resource mapping: exact resource placement, purpose, safety/evidence expectations, and direct-link requirements.

Keep the crosswalk as the standards foundation alongside these four documents when one exists.

## Architecture rules

- Group concepts that form a coherent learning progression.
- Let topic breadth, prerequisite knowledge, cognitive demand, lab needs, and publisher resources determine the number of units and lessons.
- A course may have different lesson counts in different units.
- Include prerequisites and scientific or disciplinary practices where they are actually taught.
- Use a cumulative or synthesis lesson only when the mapped content supports it.
- Reconcile all counts and titles across the crosswalk, four mapping documents, course overview, Moodle dashboard, assessments, and GitHub.

## MLA standards

Derive concise MLA standards from the completed crosswalk. Each standard must be teachable, assessable, and traceable to its source expectations. Assign stable identifiers and preserve them once approved. Distinguish controlling MLA standards from supporting correlations.
