# Moodle Production

## Dashboard sequence

For each unit, use this order unless an approved course map requires a documented variation:

1. Unit pretest.
2. Lesson activity.
3. Lesson notebook evidence submission.
4. Lesson checkpoint submission.
5. Lesson quiz.
6. Repeat the lesson sequence for every mapped lesson.
7. Unit assessment after the final lesson sequence.

Use the approved content-specific unit and lesson names. Keep unit and lesson numbers consistent everywhere.

## Release restrictions

- The first unit pretest follows completion of Course Overview and Expectations.
- Each lesson follows completion of the preceding required activity.
- Each notebook and checkpoint submission follows completion of its lesson.
- Each lesson quiz follows the required lesson evidence sequence.
- The next lesson follows the preceding lesson's required completion point.
- The unit assessment follows the final required lesson activity.
- The next unit pretest follows completion of the prior unit assessment.

After adding, deleting, renaming, or reordering activities, inspect every affected restriction. Verify both the activity name and the actual referenced activity; do not rely on visible order alone.

## Student-facing lesson pages

Use a consistent seven-part learning flow when it matches the approved course shell:

1. Lesson overview and evidence checklist.
2. Vocabulary/notebook task.
3. Publisher eText or core-content task.
4. Model, worked example, guided analysis, laboratory, simulation, or visual exploration.
5. Lesson review/scientific reasoning.
6. Independent application.
7. Mastery checkpoint.

The exact page purpose may vary with the subject and lesson. Do not invent a lab, simulation, or synthesis task merely to fill a slot.

Every page must state:

- course, unit, and lesson identifier;
- page purpose and student-facing title;
- exact task directions and stopping point;
- what evidence is required and where it is submitted;
- direct help instructions naming the unit, lesson, and page;
- applicable standards when useful.

## Savvas links

- Link directly to the exact eText lesson, video, presentation, worksheet, interactivity, lab, simulation, review, or checkpoint needed on that page.
- Never substitute the Savvas front page, dashboard, program table of contents, or an approximate resource.
- Open and verify each link while signed in. Confirm the resource title and destination before saving Moodle.
- Use `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener"` in Moodle HTML.
- If an exact link cannot be verified, mark it unresolved and do not claim completion.

## Orientation pages

Maintain four student-facing pages: Course Overview, How This Course Works, Expectations and Policies, and Course Acknowledgement. Keep course name, course code, credit, unit count, lesson count, learning outcomes, assessment language, mastery threshold, and official workflow synchronized with the approved mapping and dashboard.
